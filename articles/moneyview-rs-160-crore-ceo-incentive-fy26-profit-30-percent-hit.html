Moneyview Pays Rs 160 Crore CEO Incentive Ahead of IPO; FY26 Profit Takes 30% Hit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Moneyview, the digital lending platform preparing for its stock market debut, paid Managing Director and CEO Puneet Agarwal a one-time performance-based incentive of Rs 160 crore earlier this year. The payment, disclosed in the company's red herring prospectus (RHP), significantly reduced its FY26 reported profit.

The incentive was disclosed as an exceptional item in Moneyview's FY26 financial statements. Expenses increased and profit decreased by nearly Rs 120 crore after tax, representing more than 30% of the firm's profit for the year before exceptional items. The consequential tax impact of over Rs 40 crore was included within tax expense.

One-Time and Non-Recurring

Moneyview clarified in its RHP that the one-time incentive was paid to Agarwal for his continued leadership, performance and contributions to the company's overall growth. The company stated the payment is non-recurring and it currently has no plans for similar incentives in the future.

The incentive has been factored into the pre-offer net worth and does not impact the objects of the offer, the company said.

Financial Performance

Moneyview reported a net profit of Rs 242.7 crore for FY26 ended March 31, 2026, compared to Rs 240 crore in FY25 and Rs 171 crore in FY24. Profit before tax stood at Rs 397 crore in FY26, sharply higher than Rs 240 crore in the prior year. Before the exceptional item, profit after tax was approximately Rs 400 crore.

IPO Details

Moneyview has reduced its IPO size to Rs 1,092 crore from Rs 1,500 crore earlier. The price band is Rs 32-34 per share. The IPO opens for subscription on September 24 and closes on September 28, with listing scheduled for October 1.

The IPO comprises a fresh issue of Rs 750 crore and an offer for sale of 10.05 crore equity shares valued at Rs 342 crore at the higher end. Promoters Puneet Agarwal and Sanjay Aggarwal will each sell up to 1.35 crore shares. Accel, Tiger Global and Ribbit Capital are also selling stakes.

"We are a profit-making company. We have shown a very strong consistent trajectory of profit growth with about Rs 400 crore of profit after taxes before exceptional items in FY26," Agarwal said.
