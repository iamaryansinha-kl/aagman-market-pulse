India Imports 2.5 Million Tonnes Urea at $935-$959/tonne in Govt Tender

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India has finalised imports of 2.5 million tonnes of urea through a government tender at prices of $935 and $959 per tonne
  • 02/The purchase is aimed at ensuring adequate fertiliser supply for the upcoming kharif sowing season across key agricultural states
  • 03/India remains heavily dependent on urea imports despite domestic production capacity, with import volumes fluctuating based on monsoon demand

The government has decided to import 2.5 million tonnes of urea. The tender has been awarded at two price points: $935 and $959 per tonne.

The purchase is targeted at ensuring adequate fertiliser availability. It covers the kharif sowing season starting June 2026.

India is one of the world's largest urea consumers. Annual demand exceeds 35 million tonnes across the agricultural sector.

Domestic production stands at approximately 25 million tonnes. The gap of 8-10 million tonnes is met through imports each year.

The import prices reflect firm international markets. Global urea prices have remained elevated due to supply chain constraints.

The government bears the subsidy burden on fertiliser imports. The difference between import cost and farmer price is funded by the exchequer.

Adequate urea supply is critical for food security. It supports production of rice, wheat, and other staple crops.
