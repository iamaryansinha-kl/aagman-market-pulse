Nestle India Hits 52-Week High After Strong Q4 Results, Brokerages Raise Targets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nestle India reports strong Q4 FY26 results with 12% YoY revenue growth and margin expansion
  • 02/Brokerages raise target prices; stock hits 52-week high of ₹2,845, up 3% on results day
  • 03/Growth driven by volume recovery in Maggi noodles, Kitkat, and premium coffee portfolio

Nestle India Ltd reported better-than-expected fourth quarter earnings on April 22, 2026. Revenue rose 12% year-on-year to ₹4,823 crore, while net profit increased 15% to ₹746 crore.

The company's operating margin expanded by 80 basis points to 24.3%. Cost optimisation and premiumisation efforts offset input cost pressures.

Volume growth returned after two quarters of flat to negative movement. Maggi noodles, the company's flagship brand, posted mid-single-digit volume growth.

The premium coffee segment, including Nescafe Gold and Nespresso-compatible pods, grew 18% YoY. Nestle is expanding its cold coffee portfolio ahead of the summer season.

Brokerages responded positively. Morgan Stanley raised its target price to ₹3,000 from ₹2,750. CLSA maintained a buy rating with a ₹2,950 target.

Nestle India shares surged 3% to hit a 52-week high of ₹2,845 on April 22. The stock has outperformed the FMCG index by 8 percentage points over the past year.

The board declared a final dividend of ₹35 per share, taking the total FY26 payout to ₹85 per share. The record date is May 5, 2026.
