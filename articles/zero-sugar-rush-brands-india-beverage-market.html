Zero-Sugar Rush: Brands Race to Capture India's ₹30,850 Cr Beverage Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's no-sugar soft drinks segment is expected to reach ₹30,850 crore in 2026, nearly triple since 2021
  • 02/Varun Beverages reports low and no-sugar products at 63% of consolidated sales volume in Q1 2026
  • 03/Coca-Cola, Dabur, PepsiCo and startups like Zyro, Zumi, Lahori Zeera are launching zero-sugar variants

India's no-sugar soft drinks segment is set to reach ₹30,850 crore in 2026. This is nearly triple its size since 2021, according to Euromonitor International data.

The overall soft drinks market is estimated at ₹60,000-65,000 crore. Zero and low-sugar options have surged from roughly 5% of volume in 2020 to about 30% by 2025.

Varun Beverages Ltd, PepsiCo's bottler, said low and no-sugar products hit 63% of consolidated sales volume in the March 2026 quarter. This rose from 53% in CY2024 and 42% in CY2023.

Coca-Cola India's zero-sugar portfolio now contributes double-digits to total sales. The line includes Thums Up X Force, Sprite Zero, Diet Coke and Coca-Cola Zero Sugar.

Dabur India's Real Activ no-added-sugar portfolio is growing at 26% year-on-year. CEO Mohit Malhotra said the company is scaling up the brand while cutting sugar in its nectar juice range.

Startups are piling in. Zyro by Karan Aujla and VRB Consumer Products offers zero-calorie fizzy drinks. Beannery Foods' Zumi makes lactose-free, no-refined-sugar milkshakes.

Archian Foods' Lahori Zeera brand will launch a stevia-based zero-sugar variant. CEO Saurabh Munjal said the formulation is cracked and launch is imminent.

Tetra Pak reports booming orders as brands shift portfolios. Marketing Director Saurabh Sinha said low and reduced sugar is fast becoming the new baseline across all categories.
