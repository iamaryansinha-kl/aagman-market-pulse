India's Festive Food Exports See Demand Surge Ahead of Diwali: TPCI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ A TPCI survey projects average festive-season growth of around 6% for Indian F&B exports.
  • •- 02/ A notable segment of overseas buyers expects growth above 15% versus last year.
  • •- 03/ Ready-to-eat and cooked food exports jumped 16% to $2.4 billion in 2025-26.
  • •- 04/ The GCC is the leading destination, with Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain especially active.
  • •- 05/ Traditional categories — sweets, savouries, spices, pickles, ghee — remain the backbone of demand.

## The festive rush

Driven by appetite for traditional delicacies and convenience foods, India's food and beverage export sector is seeing strong demand ahead of Diwali, with international buyers keenly interested in traditional products alongside ready-to-consume items, a survey by the Trade Promotion Council of India found.

The survey projects an upbeat seasonal outlook with average growth of around 6%. A notable segment of overseas buyers expects expansion above 15% compared with last year.

## Traditional and modern

Demand remains robust across core traditional categories: sweets, savouries, namkeens, rice, spices, masalas, pickles, ghee, pulses and other staples. Value-added convenience products are moving even faster.

Official figures show exports of ready-to-eat and cooked food items surged 16% to $2.4 billion in 2025-26, from $2.1 billion the previous year.

"The combination of strong demand for traditional Indian products such as sweets, savouries, rice, spices and pickles with the rapid emergence of ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook foods" is what stands out, TPCI Chairman Mohit Singla said.

TPCI sees the opening extending to frozen snacks, prepared meals, festive mixes, instant mixes, curries, Indian breads, cooked rice and shelf-stable convenience foods.

## The Gulf leads

Geographically, the GCC has established itself as the leading destination for India's festive food shipments. Procurement activity is particularly active among importers and distributors in Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain across sweets, dry fruits, savouries, staples, spices, ghee, pickles, condiments and convenience foods.
