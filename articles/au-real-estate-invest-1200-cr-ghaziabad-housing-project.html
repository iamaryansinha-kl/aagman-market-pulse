AU Real Estate to Invest ₹1,200 Cr in Ghaziabad Housing Project

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/AU Real Estate will invest ₹1,200 crore to develop Cosmos Corner, a 5.6-acre housing project in Siddharth Vihar, Ghaziabad
  • 02/The project will comprise approximately 831 residential units with projected revenue of ₹1,600 crore upon completion
  • 03/Siddharth Vihar is emerging as a key NCR residential destination; project underscores AU's expansion in Delhi-NCR

AU Real Estate on Monday announced it will invest ₹1,200 crore to develop a housing project in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The company has launched Cosmos Corner in Siddharth Vihar.

The project spans 5.6 acres and will comprise around 831 residential units. The estimated total investment is approximately ₹1,200 crore for development.

The project carries a projected revenue potential of ₹1,600 crore upon full completion. This marks a major expansion for AU Real Estate in the Delhi-NCR market.

"Siddharth Vihar is rapidly emerging as one of NCR's most promising residential destinations." - Ashish Agarwal, Director, AU Real Estate

AU Real Estate is actively developing projects across Delhi-NCR. The company has built a presence in key micro-markets of the national capital region.

Ghaziabad has emerged as a high-growth corridor within Delhi-NCR. Infrastructure upgrades and metro connectivity have boosted residential demand in Siddharth Vihar significantly.

The ₹1,600 crore revenue target represents a 33% premium over the investment cost. Investors will watch sales velocity and construction timelines closely.
