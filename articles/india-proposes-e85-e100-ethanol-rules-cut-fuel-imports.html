India Proposes E85 and E100 Ethanol Rules to Cut Fuel Imports

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoRTH issued a draft notification on April 28 proposing amendments to Central Motor Vehicles Rules to include E85 and E100 fuels
  • 02/India achieved its 20% ethanol blending target (E20) in petrol in 2025 and now aims to expand to higher blends
  • 03/The draft rules are open for public comments before a final decision; automakers are already preparing flex-fuel vehicles

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a draft notification late on April 28, 2026. It proposes amendments to the Central Motor Vehicles Rules to formally incorporate higher ethanol-blended fuels.

The draft includes provisions for E85 fuel, an 85% ethanol blend with petrol. It also covers E100, which allows vehicles to run on nearly pure ethanol.

E20 fuel will continue as the standard monofuel under the proposed rules. The notification encourages vehicle manufacturers to introduce higher ethanol blends.

India achieved its target of 20% ethanol blending in petrol during 2025. The country now aims to push beyond E20 to cut costly petroleum imports.

India imports between 88% and 91% of its crude oil requirements. The oil import bill touched approximately USD 137 billion (₹11.5 lakh crore) in FY2025 alone.

Since 2014, the Ethanol Blended Petrol program has saved India over ₹1.36 lakh crore in foreign exchange. Over ₹1.18 lakh crore has been disbursed directly to farmers for ethanol feedstocks.

All vehicles manufactured after April 1, 2023 are already E20-compliant. These vehicles can handle ethanol blends of up to 30% without modification.

Maruti Suzuki is preparing the Fronx FFV for a 2026 launch. Flex-fuel vehicles can run on ethanol blends ranging from E20 to E85.

India currently produces over 1,000 crore litres of ethanol annually. The national capacity stands at approximately 2,000 crore litres, providing room for higher blending targets.

The draft rules have been opened for public comments. The government will take a final decision after reviewing stakeholder feedback.
