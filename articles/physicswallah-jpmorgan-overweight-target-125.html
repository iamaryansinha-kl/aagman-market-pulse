JPMorgan Initiates Physicswallah at Overweight, Sets ₹125 Target Price

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JPMorgan initiates coverage on Physicswallah with Overweight rating and price target of ₹125
  • 02/The target implies approximately 18% upside from the current market price of ₹106.35
  • 03/Brokerage cites attractive online education model, large addressable market and disruptive pricing

JPMorgan has initiated coverage on Physicswallah Ltd with an Overweight rating. The global brokerage has set a price target of ₹125 per share.

The target price implies an upside of around 18% from the current market price of ₹106.35. This marks the first major institutional coverage since the company's IPO.

Physicswallah listed on Indian stock exchanges in November 2025. It was the first edtech IPO in India since Byju's collapse.

JPMorgan highlighted the company's online education strength. The brokerage noted Physicswallah's large addressable market and disruptive pricing model.

The online business is the profitable core with attractive growth and margins. The company also offers offline value through its 303 centers across 152 cities.

Physicswallah reported revenue of ₹2,890 crore in FY25. Revenue grew 49% year-on-year with 4.46 million paid students.

The company had a net loss of ₹243 crore in FY25. However, EBITDA margin improved to 6.7% from prior losses.

The stock has faced pressure since listing, hitting an all-time low of ₹81.39 in March 2026. JPMorgan's coverage could provide a sentiment boost.
