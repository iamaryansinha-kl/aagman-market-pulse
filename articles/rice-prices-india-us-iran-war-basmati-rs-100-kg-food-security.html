Rice Gets Pricier in India as US-Iran War Triggers Global Food Stockpiling; Basmati Hits Rs 100/kg

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Basmati rice prices have risen 15-20% year-on-year in Q1 FY27, reaching around Rs 100 per kg from Rs 85 per kg a year earlier
  • 02/Non-basmati varieties are up 20-25%, with Sona Masoori climbing from Rs 51-52 per kg to Rs 63-64 per kg
  • 03/The US-Iran war has triggered aggressive food stockpiling by Gulf and other countries, boosting export demand for Indian rice
  • 04/India shipped a record 6.52 million tonnes of basmati in FY26 to 154 countries, with Saudi Arabia the largest buyer
  • 05/Prices are expected to stay elevated until the new kharif crop arrives in October-November

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
How much have rice prices risen in India?
A/01/ Basmati is up 15-20% year-on-year to around Rs 100 per kg, while non-basmati varieties have risen 20-25%. Sona Masoori has jumped from Rs 51-52 to Rs 63-64 per kg.
Why are rice prices rising?
A/02/ The US-Iran war has triggered global food stockpiling, with Gulf countries buying aggressively from India. Deficient rains in southern states and government procurement have tightened domestic supply.
When will rice prices come down?
A/03/ Traders expect prices to remain elevated until the new kharif crop arrives in October-November, roughly two to three months away.
How much rice does India export?
A/04/ India shipped a record 6.52 million tonnes of basmati in FY26 to 154 countries, with Saudi Arabia as the largest buyer.
