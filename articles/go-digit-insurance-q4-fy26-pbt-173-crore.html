Go Digit Insurance Q4 FY26: PBT Jumps 49% to ₹173 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Go Digit General Insurance Q4 FY26 PBT under IGAAP surged 49.1% to ₹173 crore from ₹116 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/FY26 PAT rose 28% to ₹544 crore; gross direct premium grew 21.3% to ₹2,402 crore in Q4; stock trades at ₹317
  • 03/Watch IRDAI forbearance on expense limits and IndAS transition from April 2026; pending merger with Infoworks

Go Digit General Insurance reported Q4 FY26 profit before tax of ₹173 crore under IGAAP. This marks a 49.1% jump from ₹116 crore in Q4 FY25. The board approved results on April 28, 2026.

Profit after tax for the quarter rose 28.4% to ₹149 crore. For the full year, PAT climbed to ₹544 crore from ₹425 crore in FY25. The company crossed ₹10,000 crore in total income.

Gross direct premium income grew 21.3% year-on-year to ₹2,402 crore in Q4. Gross written premium for FY26 reached ₹11,294 crore, up 9.8%. Assets under management rose 16.3% to ₹22,922 crore.

The solvency ratio improved to 2.42x from 2.24x, well above the IRDAI minimum of 1.5x. Net worth under IGAAP expanded 13.7% to ₹4,586 crore. This signals adequate capital to absorb underwriting risks.

Despite the strong earnings, the stock has underperformed this year. It is down 8.39% year-to-date, worse than the Nifty 500's 3.83% decline. Shares closed at ₹317.30 on April 29, up just 0.16%.

Investors are weighing regulatory risks alongside the profit growth. The company's insurance expenses exceeded IRDAI limits in FY26, and management is seeking forbearance. A negative ruling could mean penalties or forced operational changes.

Go Digit is also awaiting approvals for its merger with Go Digit Infoworks Services. BSE and NSE issued no-adverse-observation letters on April 22, 2026. The company must file with NCLT within six months.

The mandatory shift to IndAS accounting from April 2026 adds another variable. Under IndAS, FY26 PBT was ₹1,021 crore and net worth stood at ₹7,610 crore. Clarity on this transition will shape investor sentiment in coming quarters.
