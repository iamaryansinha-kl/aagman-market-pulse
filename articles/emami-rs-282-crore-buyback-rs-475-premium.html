Emami Announces Rs 282 Crore Share Buyback at Rs 475 Apiece, a 29% Premium

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ The buyback is worth up to Rs 282 crore at Rs 475 per share, a ~29% premium.
  • •- 02/ Up to 59.36 lakh shares, or a 1.36% stake, will be repurchased via the open market.
  • •- 03/ The company must deploy at least 75% of the amount — about Rs 211.5 crore.
  • •- 04/ Promoter holding rises to ~56% without promoters participating.
  • •- 05/ The stock is down 28% in 2026 and 37% over one year.

## Board approves open-market buyback

Emami's board on Thursday approved a share buyback of up to Rs 282 crore at a maximum price of Rs 475 per share — a premium of nearly 29% over the previous closing price. The repurchase will run through the open-market route.

The buyback covers up to 59.36 lakh shares, equivalent to a 1.36% stake in the company. Emami has committed to deploying at least 75% of the earmarked amount, which works out to roughly Rs 211.5 crore or a minimum of 44.53 lakh shares. A dedicated committee will oversee the programme.

This is Emami's first buyback since 2023.

## Promoter math

Before the buyback, promoters and the promoter group held nearly 55% of the company, with public shareholders holding 45%. Because the share count shrinks while promoters sit out, their stake rises to roughly 56% after the repurchase. Public shareholding falls to 44%.

Buybacks at a premium reward shareholders who tender while signalling that management views the stock as undervalued.

## A beaten-down stock

The market liked the news: Emami shares surged more than 7% to an intraday high of Rs 396 on Thursday, before paring gains to trade about 2% higher at Rs 378.

The stock has had a rough year. It has declined around 28% in 2026 so far and is down more than 37% over the past twelve months. Over three and five years, the stock has fallen 31% and 35% respectively. The company's market capitalisation stands at a little over Rs 16,480 crore.

Against that backdrop, a Rs 282 crore repurchase at a 29% premium is a confidence vote — though one sized modestly relative to the company's value.
