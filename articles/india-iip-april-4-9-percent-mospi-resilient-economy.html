India IIP Grows 4.9% in April: MoSPI Says Economy Resilient

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's IIP grew 4.9% in April 2026, signaling continued economic resilience despite global headwinds, MoSPI Secretary Saurabh Garg said on June 29
  • 02/Garg described the last quarter as "extremely positive" and said all indicators point to the economy doing well despite external challenges
  • 03/MoSPI is focusing on data harmonization, CAPI-based field collection, and AI/ML deployment to modernize official statistics for Viksit Bharat 2047

India's Index of Industrial Production (IIP) grew 4.9% in April 2026. MoSPI Secretary Saurabh Garg shared the data during an exclusive interview with ANI on June 29, 2026.

Garg said the previous quarter was "extremely positive." He noted all available indicators point to continued resilience despite global headwinds.

The IIP data for May 2026 will be released later on June 29 afternoon. Garg declined to anticipate the figure but expressed strong optimism.

Garg spoke on the sidelines of the 20th Statistics Day celebrations in New Delhi. The theme was "Unlocking the Potential of Administrative Data for Decision-Making."

India's Digital India growth over the past decade has generated vast administrative data. This can be leveraged for evidence-based, data-driven policymaking.

"The Hon'ble Prime Minister has been focusing a lot on data-driven decision-making. If we are talking of Viksit Bharat by 2047, the policies that we take have to be so much more focused and more data-driven," Garg said.

MoSPI has completely moved away from pen-and-paper surveys. All field data collection now happens through Computer Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI).

Frontier technologies like Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are being deployed. Portals and mobile apps are making official statistics more accessible and intuitive.

Garg said data harmonization remains the biggest challenge. MoSPI is working with state governments and ministries to build a stronger, interoperable data ecosystem.
