Global Crude Oil Supply Deficit Hits 4.8 mbpd Amid West Asia Crisis

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PL Capital report estimates global oil supply deficit of 4.8 mbpd due to West Asia disruptions and Strait of Hormuz restrictions
  • 02/IEA strategic releases of 400 mn barrels and alternative routes covering 6.2 mbpd only partly offset the 15 mbpd supply loss
  • 03/Demand destruction expected to rebalance markets as IEA sees 1.5 mbpd demand contraction in Q2 2026 and 2.3 mbpd in April

The PL Capital report estimates a global oil supply deficit of 4.8 million barrels per day. Disruptions in West Asia and restricted flows through the Strait of Hormuz have tightened global supply.

Crude flows through the Strait of Hormuz have dropped by nearly 15 mbpd. The International Energy Agency has released about 400 million barrels from strategic reserves to ease the crunch.

Alternative export routes bypassing Hormuz now account for around 6.2 mbpd. These measures have only partly offset the total supply loss.

"Supply deficit of 4.8mbpd needs to be compensated... such expected demand destruction will partially rebalance market dynamics further, putting a downward pressure on crude prices" - PL Capital report.

The IEA expects global oil demand to contract by 1.5 mbpd in Q2 2026. April 2026 could see a sharper decline of 2.3 mbpd against industry estimates of 4.0 mbpd.

Demand slowdown is likely to hit the Middle East and Asia-Pacific first. Governments in oil-consuming nations may introduce mandates to curb consumption.

PL Capital remains positive on oil marketing companies. It expects lower crude prices to improve marketing margins for Indian Oil, BPCL, and HPCL.

Crude oil prices have risen amid the supply tightness. Early signs of demand destruction are already visible as higher prices weigh on global consumption.

THE DISRUPTION OF CRUDE FLOWS THROUGH THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ HAS RESULTED IN A SUPPLY LOSS OF NEARLY 15 MBPD.
WHAT MEASURES HAVE BEEN TAKEN TO OFFSET THE SUPPLY LOSS?
THE INTERNATIONAL ENERGY AGENCY (IEA) HAS RELEASED ABOUT 400 MILLION BARRELS FROM STRATEGIC RESERVES. ALTERNATIVE EXPORT ROUTES BYPASSING HORMUZ NOW ACCOUNT FOR AROUND 6.2 MBPD.
HOW WILL THE SUPPLY DEFICIT BE ADDRESSED?
THE IMBALANCE IS EXPECTED TO BE ADDRESSED THROUGH DEMAND DESTRUCTION AS HIGHER OIL PRICES WEIGH ON CONSUMPTION. THE IEA EXPECTS GLOBAL DEMAND TO CONTRACT BY 1.5 MBPD IN Q2 2026 AND 2.3 MBPD IN APRIL 2026.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR OIL MARKETING COMPANIES?
PL CAPITAL REMAINS POSITIVE ON OIL MARKETING COMPANIES SUCH AS INDIAN OIL CORPORATION, BHARAT PETROLEUM, AND HINDUSTAN PETROLEUM. LOWER CRUDE PRICES DRIVEN BY DEMAND DESTRUCTION ARE EXPECTED TO IMPROVE THEIR MARKETING MARGINS.
