ED Attaches ₹3,034 Crore Assets in Anil Ambani Reliance Group Probe

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ED attaches ₹3,034 crore of RCOM and R-Infra assets under PMLA, taking total group attachments to ₹19,344 crore
  • 02/Attached assets include a Mumbai flat, Khandala farmhouse, Sanand land, and 7.71 crore R-Infra shares
  • 03/RCOM remains under insolvency since 2019; Anil Ambani faces SEBI ban and fraud classifications by multiple banks

The Enforcement Directorate has provisionally attached assets worth ₹3,034 crore. The properties belong to Reliance Communications (RCOM) and Reliance Infrastructure Ltd (R-Infra).

The attachment order was issued under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). It covers a flat in Mumbai, a farmhouse in Khandala, land parcels in Sanand (Ahmedabad), and 7.71 crore shares of R-Infra.

With this action, total ED attachments against the Reliance Anil Ambani Group (RAAG) now stand at ₹19,344 crore. The agency is probing bank fraud and fund diversion across multiple group companies.

RCOM has been under Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) since June 2019. The company owes over ₹40,185 crore to lenders, with nine banks declaring its loan accounts as fraud.

Reliance Infrastructure clarified that Anil Ambani is not on its board for over 3.5 years. The company said the attachment has no impact on operations, shareholders, or employees.

R-Infra shares trade around ₹87 with a market cap of roughly ₹3,004 crore. The stock has fallen 66% over the past year amid regulatory overhang.

Anil Ambani faces a five-year securities market ban imposed by SEBI in August 2024. The Bombay High Court in February 2026 allowed banks to proceed with fraud tagging against him.
