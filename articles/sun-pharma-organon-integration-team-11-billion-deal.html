Sun Pharma Sets Up Integration Team for $11.75 Billion Organon Acquisition

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sun Pharma is forming a dedicated integration committee to oversee Organon’s global operations, expected to be operational in less than a month.
  • 02/The $11.75 billion acquisition creates a combined entity with $12.4 billion in revenue, catapulting Sun into the top 25 global pharma companies.
  • 03/The deal is expected to close in six to nine months by early FY 2027, with Organon carrying $8.6 billion in debt.

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (NSE: SUNPHARMA) is putting in place an integration team to oversee the merger of Organon’s global operations. The committee will be operational in less than a month.

Managing Director Kirti Ganorkar said the integration office will have representation from both companies. Planning can begin now, but formal decisions must wait until the deal closes officially.

The transaction is expected to take six to nine months to complete. Sun Pharma aims to finish the acquisition by early FY 2027.

Sun Pharma’s Chief Financial Officer Jayashree Satagopan said the committee will evaluate portfolios, people, functions, and commercial footprint. Licensing opportunities will also be reviewed.

Organon has about 10,000 employees and annual revenues of $6.2 billion. The combined entity will post revenues of $12.4 billion.

The deal will expand Sun Pharma’s presence in women’s healthcare, innovative products, biosimilars, and key markets like China. It will push Sun into the top 25 global pharmaceutical companies.

Organon was spun off from Merck (MSD) in 2021. It carries $8.6 billion in debt, which analysts have flagged as a concern.

Satagopan cited founder Dilip Shanghvi’s stance that Sun is “debt-averse, not risk averse.” She said the combined business will generate sizable cash to repay debt at an early stage.

Both companies will remain independent initially. Sun eventually plans to delist Organon from the NYSE.
