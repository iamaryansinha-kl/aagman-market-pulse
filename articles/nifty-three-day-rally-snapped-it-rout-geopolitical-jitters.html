Nifty Snaps 3-Day Rally as IT Sell-Off and Geopolitical Fears Drag Markets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 falls 0.8% to close at 23,450, ending a three-day consecutive rally
  • 02/IT sector plunges 2.5% on fears of US recession and potential trade curbs; all 10 Nifty IT stocks decline
  • 03/India VIX jumps 12% to 16.8 as geopolitical tensions in West Asia and US-China trade concerns spike risk aversion

Indian equity markets snapped a three-day rally on April 22, 2026. The Nifty 50 index fell 0.8% to settle at 23,450.

The BSE Sensex dropped 510 points to close at 77,120. Both benchmarks had gained over 2% in the preceding three sessions.

Information technology stocks led the decline. The Nifty IT index plunged 2.5%, its steepest single-day fall in three weeks.

All 10 constituents of the Nifty IT index ended in the red. Infosys fell 3.1%, TCS dropped 2.8%, and Wipro declined 2.4%.

US recession fears and potential restrictions on tech services exports weighed on the sector. Analysts fear tightening US visa norms could impact margins.

Broader markets underperformed. The Nifty Midcap 100 fell 1.2%, while the Nifty Smallcap 100 dropped 1.5%.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, surged 12% to 16.8. This marked the highest level in two weeks.

Foreign portfolio investors sold shares worth ₹1,850 crore. Domestic institutional investors provided partial support with purchases of ₹920 crore.

"Markets are pricing in elevated geopolitical risk and slowing global growth," a senior fund manager at a domestic AMC said."We expect volatility to remain high near term."
