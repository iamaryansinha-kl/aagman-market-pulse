Housing Sales Hit Rs 9.33 Lakh Cr in FY26, Up 16%: Liases Foras

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Housing sales value rises 16% to Rs 9.33 lakh crore in FY26 across 75 cities despite 1% unit decline to 7,09,793
  • 02/New supply jumps 10% to 6,20,842 units; unsold inventory climbs 13% to around 12 lakh units
  • 03/MMR leads with 23.7% market share and 4% growth; Pune sees 25% decline amid rising supply accumulation

Housing sales across 75 major Indian cities rose 16 per cent in value to Rs 9.33 lakh crore during FY2025-26. The data was released by Liases Foras Real Estate Rating & Research on May 25, 2026.

Unit sales fell marginally by 1 per cent to 7,09,793 homes from 7,19,029 in the previous fiscal. The value surge reflects higher average ticket sizes across markets.

New residential supply increased 10 per cent to 6,20,842 units during the year. This supply push lifted unsold inventory by 13 per cent to roughly 12 lakh units.

Among the top eight metros, Mumbai Metropolitan Region accounted for 23.7 per cent of pan-India sales. MMR also recorded a 4 per cent year-on-year growth in volumes.

Bengaluru ranked second with a 9.9 per cent share of the national market. Pune, however, saw a sharp 25 per cent annual decline in housing sales despite ample supply.

Tier II and III cities reported a 4 per cent drop in sales to 2,01,943 units from 2,09,818. The top eight cities saw volumes slip to 5,07,850 from 5,09,211.

The housing price index rose 3 per cent annually during FY26. There are nearly 18,000 active developers across these 75 tracked cities, according to Liases Foras.

Rising unsold inventory amid flat demand signals pricing pressure ahead. Buyers may find more negotiation room as supply continues to outpace absorption in key markets.

WITH UNSOLD INVENTORY RISING 13 PER CENT AND UNIT SALES STAYING FLAT, BUYERS MAY GAIN NEGOTIATING POWER. THE HOUSING PRICE INDEX ROSE ONLY 3 PER CENT ANNUALLY, SUGGESTING LIMITED PRICING MOMENTUM IN THE NEAR TERM.
