India-Russia Energy Ties Widen to Gas, Nuclear, Petrochemicals

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India and Russia are in advanced talks to expand energy cooperation into gas supplies, CNG infrastructure and petrochemical facilities
  • 02/Russia supplied 33.3% of India's crude oil imports in 2025, with US sanctions waivers continuing to enable trade
  • 03/Civil nuclear cooperation is gaining momentum via Kudankulam and the SHANTI Act, while Rosneft's 49.13% stake in Nayara anchors downstream ties

India and Russia are in talks to broaden energy cooperation beyond crude oil. The discussions cover gas supplies to Indian cities, expansion of CNG filling stations, and new oil, gas and petrochemical facilities.

Roman Babushkin, Russia's deputy chief of mission in India, disclosed the plans on Thursday. He was speaking at an event organised by the Indo-Russia Innopratika Technology Hub.

The push comes as the West Asian crisis has disrupted energy supply chains. Russia remains India's top crude oil supplier, and the US has extended sanctions waivers on Russian oil exports to India.

In recent weeks, India and Russia have held high-level discussions on purchasing LPG from Russia. India is also exploring new hydrocarbon assets in the eastern part of the country.

Civil nuclear cooperation is gaining fresh momentum. Babushkin cited the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Project as a showcase of Russian technology, including capabilities for small modular reactors.

The recently adopted Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India (SHANTI) Act is expected to enable further cooperation in the nuclear sector.

Russia supplied 33.3% of India's crude oil imports in 2025. The relationship extends beyond trade: Rosneft holds a 49.13% stake in Nayara Energy, which operates a 20 million tonne per year refinery at Vadinar.

Indian companies are also present upstream in Russia. ONGC Videsh holds a 20% interest in Sakhalin-1 and a 26% stake in Vankorneft.

"Energy remains the most visible pillar of recent growth, and it will continue to matter," said Harsh Vardhan Shringla, Rajya Sabha MP and former foreign secretary. "India-Russia energy ties are not transactional — they include investment, equity, refining and infrastructure."

Russia has also become a steady supplier of sunflower oil (51%), fertilisers (25%) and beans (10%) to India. Joint ventures are being set up in agriculture alongside the energy push.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT ARE INDIA AND RUSSIA DISCUSSING BEYOND CRUDE OIL TRADE?
THE TWO COUNTRIES ARE IN TALKS TO EXPAND COOPERATION INTO GAS SUPPLIES TO INDIAN CITIES, EXPANSION OF CNG FILLING STATIONS, AND NEW OIL, GAS AND PETROCHEMICAL FACILITIES. HIGH-LEVEL DISCUSSIONS ON LPG PURCHASES FROM RUSSIA HAVE ALSO TAKEN PLACE IN RECENT WEEKS.
HOW MUCH OF INDIA'S CRUDE OIL DOES RUSSIA SUPPLY?
RUSSIA SUPPLIED 33.3% OF INDIA'S CRUDE OIL IMPORTS IN 2025, MAKING IT THE COUNTRY'S TOP SUPPLIER. THE US HAS EXTENDED SANCTIONS WAIVERS ON RUSSIAN OIL EXPORTS TO INDIA, ALLOWING THIS TRADE TO CONTINUE.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE KUDANKULAM NUCLEAR POWER PROJECT?
KUDANKULAM IS INDIA'S ONLY NUCLEAR POWER PROJECT ESTABLISHED WITH ANOTHER COUNTRY. IT FEATURES CUTTING-EDGE RUSSIAN TECHNOLOGIES, INCLUDING THOSE APPLICABLE TO SMALL MODULAR REACTORS (SMRS), AND REPRESENTS A KEY PILLAR OF CIVIL NUCLEAR COOPERATION.
WHAT ARE THE MAJOR INVESTMENT AND EQUITY TIES BETWEEN INDIA AND RUSSIA IN ENERGY?
ROSNEFT HOLDS A 49.13% STAKE IN NAYARA ENERGY, WHICH OPERATES A 20 MILLION TONNE PER YEAR REFINERY AT VADINAR. INDIAN COMPANIES ARE ACTIVE UPSTREAM IN RUSSIA: ONGC VIDESH HAS A 20% INTEREST IN SAKHALIN-1 AND A 26% STAKE IN VANKORNEFT.
WHAT ROLE DOES THE SHANTI ACT PLAY IN INDIA-RUSSIA NUCLEAR COOPERATION?
THE SUSTAINABLE HARNESSING AND ADVANCEMENT OF NUCLEAR ENERGY FOR TRANSFORMING INDIA (SHANTI) ACT, RECENTLY ADOPTED BY INDIA, IS EXPECTED TO ACT AS AN ENABLER FOR FURTHER CIVIL NUCLEAR COOPERATION BETWEEN THE TWO COUNTRIES, INCLUDING POTENTIAL SMALL MODULAR REACTOR PROJECTS.
