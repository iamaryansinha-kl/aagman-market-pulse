Adani Enterprises to Invest ₹17,000 Cr in Airports for FY27

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Enterprises Ltd (NSE: ADANIENT) will invest ₹17,000 crore in airports in FY27, representing 42% of its total ₹40,000 crore capex plan
  • 02/FY26 airport EBITDA surged 55% to ₹5,394 crore on total income of ₹13,081 crore, with aero revenue up 26% and non-aero revenue up 31%
  • 03/Key projects include a new Ahmedabad terminal ahead of 2030 Commonwealth Games and accelerated Phase II expansion at Navi Mumbai International Airport

Adani Enterprises Ltd (NSE: ADANIENT) will invest ₹17,000 crore in its airports business during FY27. This represents 42% of the company's total ₹40,000 crore capital expenditure plan for the year. CFO Jugeshinder Singh confirmed the allocation to investors recently.

The remaining capex is split across PVC (₹9,000 crore), new energy including hydrogen (₹10,000 crore), and mining (₹4,000 crore). This reflects AEL's pivot toward infrastructure-led businesses with long-term contracted earnings.

A new terminal is planned at Ahmedabad airport ahead of the 2030 Commonwealth Games. Navi Mumbai International Airport will see Phase II expansion accelerate, with capacity expected in 12-18 months. City-side development is also planned at Mumbai, Lucknow, and Jaipur airports.

The airport business reported total income of ₹13,081 crore in FY26, up 28% year-on-year. EBITDA surged 55% to ₹5,394 crore, driven by tariff revisions and strong non-aero growth. Aero revenue rose 26% and non-aero revenue climbed 31%.

AEL has shifted to an infrastructure-led model with 80% of EBITDA now from mature, long-term-contracted businesses. FY26 consolidated EBITDA stood at ₹16,464 crore on total income of ₹102,943 crore. The company said its incubation journey is moving past the heavy capex phase.

"We were close to 95 percent of our target capex this year. We expect the capex for next year to be at the same level around ₹40,000 crore," Singh told investors. The company expects Navi Mumbai, Kutch Copper, and Ganga Expressway to add over ₹3,000 crore in FY27 EBITDA.

Analysts at Jefferies maintain a 'Buy' rating on Adani Enterprises with a target price of ₹2,600, implying 18% upside. However, the company carries a debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 3.9x, which demands careful capital management amid the ₹40,000 crore spending plan.
