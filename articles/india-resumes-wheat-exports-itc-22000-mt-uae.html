India Resumes Wheat Exports After 4-Year Ban: ITC Ships 22,000 MT to UAE

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ITC begins loading 22,000 metric tons of wheat at Kandla port for shipment to UAE, marking India's first wheat export in four years
  • 02/Government approved 2.5 million tonnes of wheat exports in February 2026 on the back of bumper crop and record 18.2 MT FCI stocks
  • 03/Record wheat production expected to surpass 117.94 MT in 2025-26; acreage up 3% to 33.41 million hectares

ITC has started loading 22,000 metric tons of wheat at Kandla port. The shipment is bound for the United Arab Emirates.

This marks India's first wheat export in four years. Traders had been barred from overseas shipments since May 2022.

The government lifted the export ban in February 2026. It approved 2.5 million tonnes of grain and 0.5 million tonnes of processed wheat products.

The decision follows a record harvest outlook and comfortable stocks. Private grain stocks stand at 7.5 million tonnes, up 3.2 million tonnes year-on-year.

Food Corporation of India wheat stocks are projected at 18.2 million tonnes on April 1. This is well above the buffer norm of 7.6 million tonnes.

Wheat production in 2025-26 is expected to surpass the previous record of 117.94 million tonnes. Acreage has expanded to 33.41 million hectares, up 3% from last year.

Current domestic wheat prices are around Rs 2,675 per quintal. Global prices hover at $220-$240 per tonne, against India's domestic price of about $280 per tonne.

"With ample wheat in the open market and a robust harvest expected, the government has opted for a calibrated release of excess stocks. This move safeguards farmers' interests by stabilising wheat prices." - Navneet Chitlangia, President, Roller Flour Millers' Federation of India
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE FIRST WHEAT EXPORT SHIPMENT FROM INDIA AFTER THE BAN?
ITC HAS STARTED LOADING 22,000 METRIC TONS OF WHEAT AT KANDLA PORT FOR SHIPMENT TO THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES. THIS IS THE FIRST PHYSICAL EXPORT AFTER THE FOUR-YEAR BAN.
WHEN WAS THE WHEAT EXPORT BAN LIFTED AND HOW MUCH WAS APPROVED?
THE GOVERNMENT LIFTED THE NEARLY FOUR-YEAR BAN ON FEBRUARY 13, 2026. IT APPROVED EXPORTS OF 2.5 MILLION TONNES OF WHEAT AND AN ADDITIONAL 0.5 MILLION TONNES OF PROCESSED WHEAT PRODUCTS.
WHY DID INDIA BAN WHEAT EXPORTS IN 2022?
INDIA IMPOSED RESTRICTIONS ON WHEAT EXPORTS IN MAY 2022 AFTER AN UNEXPECTED HEATWAVE REDUCED CROP YIELDS AND TRIGGERED A SHARP RISE IN DOMESTIC PRICES. THE DECISION WAS TAKEN TO SAFEGUARD FOOD SECURITY AND CONTROL INFLATION AMID GLOBAL SUPPLY DISRUPTIONS FOLLOWING THE RUSSIA-UKRAINE CONFLICT.
WHAT ARE INDIA'S CURRENT WHEAT STOCKS AND PRODUCTION OUTLOOK?
PRIVATE GRAIN STOCKS STAND AT 7.5 MILLION TONNES, NEARLY 3.2 MILLION TONNES HIGHER THAN LAST YEAR. THE FOOD CORPORATION OF INDIA PROJECTS WHEAT STOCKS OF 18.2 MILLION TONNES ON APRIL 1, AGAINST A BUFFER OF 7.6 MILLION TONNES. PRODUCTION IS EXPECTED TO SURPASS THE PREVIOUS RECORD OF 117.94 MILLION TONNES.
WHAT SHOULD TRADERS AND FARMERS WATCH FOR NEXT?
GLOBAL WHEAT PRICES CURRENTLY HOVER AROUND $220-$240 PER TONNE, AGAINST INDIA'S DOMESTIC PRICE OF ABOUT $280 PER TONNE. THIS PRICE GAP MAY LIMIT EXPORT VIABILITY DESPITE THE BAN LIFTING. FARMERS SHOULD MONITOR PROCUREMENT PRICES AS THE NEW HARVEST ARRIVES IN MARKETS FROM MID-MARCH.
