Cabinet Approves ₹23,437 Crore Railway Projects Across Six States

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approves three multi-tracking railway projects worth ₹23,437 crore across 19 districts in six states
  • 02/Projects will add 901 km to Indian Railways network by 2030-31, boosting freight capacity by 60 MTPA and cutting carbon emissions by 185 crore kg
  • 03/New lines on Nagda-Mathura, Guntakal-Wadi and Burhwal-Sitapur sections to improve connectivity for 4,161 villages and key tourist destinations

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved three railway multi-tracking projects on May 5, 2026. The total estimated cost is ₹23,437 crore.

The projects include third and fourth lines on the Nagda-Mathura, Guntakal-Wadi and Burhwal-Sitapur sections. These are high-density railway routes.

The expansion will add approximately 901 km across 19 districts. It covers Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said the additional lines will decongest busy routes. He expects improved mobility and service reliability.

The projects are planned under the PM-Gati Shakti National Master Plan. They will benefit around 4,161 villages with nearly 83 lakh people.

Tourist access will improve to Mahakaleshwar, Ranthambore, Keoladeo, Mathura and Vrindavan. Freight capacity will rise by about 60 million tonnes per annum.

The government estimates oil import savings of 37 crore litres annually. Carbon dioxide emissions will drop by roughly 185 crore kg.

Projects are expected to be completed by 2030-31. Prime Minister Narendra Modi welcomed the approval, calling it a boost to infrastructure.
