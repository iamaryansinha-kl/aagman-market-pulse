Srichakra Polyplast to Invest ₹425 Crore in rPET, Targets ₹1,000 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Srichakra Polyplast to invest ₹425 crore in rPET capacity, expanding from 90,000 to over 113,000 tonnes by 2026
  • 02/Company targets ₹1,000 crore revenue by FY27-28, up from ₹227 crore in FY25; FY26 target is ₹400 crore
  • 03/EPR mandate from April 2026 requires 40% recycled content; industry faces structural supply constraint in food-grade rPET

Srichakra Polyplast has lined up ₹425 crore in capital expenditure. The Hyderabad-based company will expand its food-grade recycled PET capacity from around 90,000 tonnes to over 113,000 tonnes by 2026.

The expansion adds over 23,000 tonnes of processing capacity. It will be executed through a distributed model anchored by a new Krishnagiri facility in Tamil Nadu.

The company reported revenue of ₹227 crore in FY25. It expects to scale to ₹400 crore in FY26 and target ₹1,000 crore by FY27-28.

Srichakra operates EFSA- and US FDA-approved processes. It supplies global brands across beverage and packaging segments and ships around $1.5 million in exports monthly.

From April 2026, India's EPR framework mandates 40 per cent recycled content in PET bottles. This is up from 30 per cent earlier, tightening compliance requirements.

Industry estimates indicate food-grade rPET availability at around 3.54 lakh tonnes. This falls short of the 6.84 lakh tonnes required to meet FY27 compliance targets.

"The future of recycling in India will be defined not by how much plastic is collected, but by how much of it can be certified, traced, and reintegrated," said Ravindra P V, co-founder and managing director.

Competitors including Banyan Nation, Ganesha Ecosphere, and Indorama Ventures are also scaling up. The market is shifting from volume-based models to certification and traceability.
