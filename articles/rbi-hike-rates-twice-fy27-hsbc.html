RBI May Hike Rates Twice in FY27 to 5.75% Amid Inflation, El Nino Risks: HSBC

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HSBC projects two RBI rate hikes in FY27, taking repo rate from 6.5% to 5.75% by 1Q27
  • 02/Headline inflation forecast at 5.6% for FY27; El Nino temperature channel could add 0.5ppt
  • 03/India's FY27 GDP growth forecast slashed to 6% from 7.4%; informal sector to bear the brunt

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) may raise interest rates twice in FY27, according to an HSBC report. The repo rate is expected to climb to 5.75 per cent from the current 6.5 per cent.

HSBC expects the first hike in the fourth quarter of 2026 (4Q26). The second increase would follow in the first quarter of FY27 (1Q27).

The report projects headline inflation at 5.6 per cent for FY27. This factors in higher energy prices and temperature-related food inflation from El Nino weather disruptions.

"With the energy and El Nino shocks coinciding, the FY27 outlook needs attention. Our model suggests the El Nino/temperature channel can add 0.5ppt to inflation over a year," the HSBC report stated.

HSBC has lowered India's GDP growth forecast for FY27 to 6 per cent. This is a sharp downgrade from its earlier projection of 7.4 per cent.

The impact of these shocks is expected to hit the informal sector hardest. Rural households and small businesses will face the brunt of rising costs and slower growth.

Higher foodgrain stocks and full granaries could ease some inflationary pressure. However, a severe El Nino event could still trigger sharper food price spikes.

The RBI will likely balance inflation control with growth concerns. Policymakers face a difficult macroeconomic environment with overlapping energy, industrial and climate shocks.

THE HSBC REPORT PROJECTS THE REPO RATE WILL RISE TO 5.75 PER CENT AFTER THE TWO EXPECTED HIKES, UP FROM THE CURRENT LEVEL.
