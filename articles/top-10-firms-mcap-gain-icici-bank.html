6 of India's Top-10 Firms Add Rs 88,678 Cr; ICICI Bank Biggest Gainer

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Six of India's top-10 most valued firms added Rs 88,678.1 crore in combined market cap in the holiday-shortened week
  • 02/ICICI Bank gained the most at Rs 29,588.75 crore, followed by HDFC Bank (Rs 24,718.3 cr) and Reliance Industries (Rs 12,043.96 cr)
  • 03/Bharti Airtel lost the most at Rs 35,615.21 crore; markets await crude oil and FII flow trends for next week

The combined market valuation of six of India's top-10 most valued companies rose by Rs 88,678.1 crore in the holiday-shortened trading week. ICICI Bank emerged as the biggest gainer among the pack.

The BSE Sensex climbed 297.57 points (0.38 per cent) and the NSE Nifty advanced 42.9 points (0.17 per cent). Markets gained in three of the four trading sessions.

"Markets ended the holiday-shortened week with modest gains, advancing in three of the four trading sessions. Sentiment remained constructive, supported by easing crude oil prices, improving geopolitical developments in West Asia, and selective buying by foreign institutional investors (FIIs)," Ajit Mishra, SVP, Research, Religare Broking Ltd, said.

ICICI Bank's market valuation jumped Rs 29,588.75 crore to Rs 9,95,610.74 crore. It ranked fourth in the top-10 list.

HDFC Bank added Rs 24,718.3 crore to reach Rs 12,25,981.44 crore. It retained the second position.

Reliance Industries' valuation surged Rs 12,043.96 crore to Rs 17,83,926.92 crore. It stayed at number one.

Bajaj Finance climbed Rs 11,580.28 crore to Rs 6,10,081.53 crore. State Bank of India rallied Rs 9,322.93 crore to Rs 9,64,738 crore.

Larsen & Toubro edged up Rs 1,423.88 crore to Rs 5,80,550.83 crore. However, four firms lost ground.

Bharti Airtel shed Rs 35,615.21 crore to Rs 11,27,348.09 crore. It was the worst performer among the top-10.

LIC eroded Rs 21,188.74 crore to Rs 5,35,537.56 crore. TCS declined Rs 11,143.71 crore to Rs 7,58,206.42 crore.

Hindustan Unilever dipped Rs 5,321.83 crore to Rs 5,10,624.92 crore. The top-10 ranking remained unchanged.
