Umiya Buildcon FY26 Revenue Up 49%: Products Segment Hits Decade-High Sales

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Umiya Buildcon Ltd posts consolidated FY26 revenue of ₹7,236.5 lakh, up 48.9% from ₹4,860.7 lakh in FY25
  • 02/Products segment records decade-high quarterly revenue of ₹1,278.1 lakh in Q4 FY26, up 141% YoY, driven by MRO-TEK networking demand
  • 03/Real estate revenue surges 70.4% to ₹2,725.3 lakh; EPS more than doubles to ₹7.80; stock up 36.68% over past year

Umiya Buildcon Ltd reported its full-year and fourth-quarter results for FY26 on Tuesday. Consolidated annual revenue stood at ₹7,236.5 lakh, up 48.9 per cent from ₹4,860.7 lakh in FY25.

The products segment delivered the standout performance. Q4 FY26 revenue hit ₹1,278.1 lakh, the highest quarterly figure in a decade, compared to ₹529.4 lakh in Q4 FY25.

For the full year, the products segment grew 79.5 per cent to ₹3,267.3 lakh from ₹1,820 lakh in FY25. The growth was driven by demand for high-capacity switches and data centre networking products under the company's indigenous MRO-TEK brand.

The real estate segment also gained ground. Annual revenue surged 70.4 per cent to ₹2,725.3 lakh from ₹1,599.6 lakh, supported by two active projects — Umiya Bricklane in Candolim, Goa, and Umiya Prism on Cunningham Road in Bengaluru.

The Solutions segment saw annual revenue slip to ₹1,243.9 lakh from ₹1,441.1 lakh in FY25. However, Q4 revenue was marginally higher at ₹373.8 lakh against ₹337 lakh a year ago.

Earnings per share grew 146 per cent year-on-year, from ₹3.17 to ₹7.80. This reflects improved profitability across the business.

The stock was trading at ₹87.01 on the NSE on Wednesday, down 0.51 per cent intraday. Its market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹162.57 crore. The stock has returned 36.68 per cent over the past year, significantly outperforming the Nifty 50.

The company expects to sustain growth momentum into FY27 on the back of a healthy order pipeline in its Products business.
