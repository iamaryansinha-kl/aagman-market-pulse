India Factory Output Growth Slows to 4.1% in March 2026 on Weak Electricity

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Index of Industrial Production (IIP) grew 4.1% YoY in March 2026, down from 5.2% in February
  • 02/Manufacturing expanded 4.3% and mining 5.5%, while electricity output grew just 0.8%
  • 03/Capital goods output jumped 14.6%, signalling resilient investment demand despite the headline slowdown

India's factory output growth slowed to 4.1% in March 2026. The Index of Industrial Production (IIP) moderated from 5.2% growth recorded in February, the Ministry of Statistics reported on April 28.

Manufacturing output, which carries the highest weight in the IIP, expanded by 4.3%. Mining activity performed better at 5.5%, supported by steady demand for minerals and metals.

Electricity generation grew by just 0.8% in March. This marked a sharp slowdown from the 6.3% expansion in electricity generation recorded in March 2025. Lower incremental demand weighed on power generation.

The top three positive contributors were motor vehicles at 18.1%, basic metals at 8.6%, and machinery at 11.2%. Auto components, commercial vehicles, and axles drove the automobile segment.

Capital goods output surged 14.6% under the use-based classification. Infrastructure and construction goods also recorded solid growth of 6.7%, reflecting ongoing project execution.

Consumer durables grew 5.3%, while consumer non-durables expanded just 1.1%. The gap between investment-linked and consumption-oriented segments continued to widen.

For the financial year 2025-26 (April-February), IIP growth averaged 4.1%. The data points to a resilient but uneven industrial recovery, with investment demand outpacing consumption.
