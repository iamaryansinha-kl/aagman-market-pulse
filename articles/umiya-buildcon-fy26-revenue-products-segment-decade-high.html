Umiya Buildcon FY26 Revenue Up 49% to ₹7,237 Lakh; Products Hit Decade High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Umiya Buildcon posts FY26 consolidated revenue of ₹7,236.5 lakh, up 48.9% from ₹4,860.7 lakh in FY25
  • 02/Products segment records decade-high quarterly revenue of ₹1,278.1 lakh in Q4 FY26, up 141% YoY
  • 03/EPS surges 146% to ₹7.80; stock has returned 36.68% over the past year versus Nifty 50's -0.23%

Umiya Buildcon Ltd reported its full-year FY26 results on April 28, 2026. Consolidated revenue rose 48.9% to ₹7,236.5 lakh from ₹4,860.7 lakh in FY25.

The products segment was the standout performer. Quarterly revenue hit ₹1,278.1 lakh in Q4 FY26, the highest in a decade. This compares to ₹529.4 lakh in Q4 FY25.

For the full year, the products segment grew 79.5% to ₹3,267.3 lakh. Growth was driven by demand for high-capacity switches and data centre networking products under the indigenous MRO-TEK brand.

The real estate segment also gained ground. Annual revenue surged 70.4% to ₹2,725.3 lakh from ₹1,599.6 lakh in FY25.

Two active projects supported real estate growth. Umiya Bricklane in Candolim, Goa secured RERA registration and initial bookings. Umiya Prism, an ultra-luxury project on Cunningham Road in Bengaluru's CBD, received RERA approval during the quarter.

The solutions segment saw annual revenue slip to ₹1,243.9 lakh from ₹1,441.1 lakh in FY25. However, Q4 revenue rose marginally to ₹373.8 lakh from ₹337 lakh a year ago.

Earnings per share grew 146% year-on-year. EPS stood at ₹7.80 versus ₹3.17 in FY25, reflecting improved profitability.

The stock was trading at ₹87.01 on the NSE on April 29, down 0.51% intraday. Market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹162.57 crore.

The stock has returned 36.68% over the past year. It significantly outperformed the Nifty 50, which declined 0.23% over the same period. The five-year return stands at 264.82%.

The company expects to sustain growth momentum into FY27. This outlook is backed by a healthy order pipeline in its products business.
