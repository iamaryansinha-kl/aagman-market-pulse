Jammu-Srinagar Vande Bharat Flagged Off: Services Begin May 2, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the 20-coach Jammu-Srinagar Vande Bharat Express on April 30, 2026
  • 02/Commercial services begin May 2 on the 267-km route, operating six days a week with no trains on Tuesdays
  • 03/The extension connects the twin capitals for the first time via rail on the ₹43,780-crore Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla link

Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the Jammu-Srinagar Vande Bharat on April 30. The ceremony was held at Jammu Tawi Railway Station. Chief Minister Omar Abdullah and Union MoS Jitendra Singh attended.

This marks the first direct train between the twin capitals of Jammu and Kashmir. It extends the Katra-Srinagar route that Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated on June 6, 2025.

The inaugural train covers 267 kilometres between Jammu Tawi and Srinagar. Commercial services begin on May 2, 2026, operating six days a week. There will be no service on Tuesdays.

The Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla Rail Link cost ₹43,780 crore. It includes 36 tunnels spanning 119 kilometres and 943 bridges across difficult Pir Panjal mountain terrain.

Work on connecting Kashmir by rail began in the late 1990s. Udhampur was linked by 2005. The first train ran in the Kashmir Valley in October 2008.

The 20-coach train features indigenous technology and the Kavach safety system. It has GPS-based passenger information and rotating seats. Senior Divisional Commercial Manager Uchit Singhal confirmed the world-class amenities.

"Constructed using indigenous technology, this Vande Bharat Express will offer passengers a world-class travel experience," Uchit Singhal said. The extension is expected to boost tourism and the local economy significantly.

Stations at Jammu Tawi, Katra, Udhampur, and Budgam are being redeveloped under the Amrit Bharat Station Scheme. The dedicated Jammu Railway Division was created in January 2025.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN DOES THE JAMMU-SRINAGAR VANDE BHARAT SERVICE BEGIN?
COMMERCIAL SERVICES BEGIN ON MAY 2, 2026. THE TRAIN WAS FLAGGED OFF BY RAILWAYS MINISTER ASHWINI VAISHNAW ON APRIL 30, 2026, AT JAMMU TAWI RAILWAY STATION.
WHAT IS THE DISTANCE AND SCHEDULE OF THE JAMMU-SRINAGAR VANDE BHARAT?
THE TRAIN COVERS 267 KILOMETRES BETWEEN JAMMU TAWI AND SRINAGAR. IT WILL OPERATE SIX DAYS A WEEK WITH NO SERVICE ON TUESDAYS.
HOW MUCH DID THE UDHAMPUR-SRINAGAR-BARAMULLA RAIL LINK PROJECT COST?
THE PROJECT WAS BUILT AT A TOTAL COST OF ₹43,780 CRORE. IT INCLUDES 36 TUNNELS SPANNING 119 KILOMETRES AND 943 BRIDGES ACROSS THE PIR PANJAL MOUNTAIN RANGE.
WHAT SAFETY AND COMFORT FEATURES DOES THE TRAIN OFFER?
THE 20-COACH TRAIN FEATURES INDIGENOUS TECHNOLOGY INCLUDING THE KAVACH SAFETY SYSTEM, A GPS-BASED PASSENGER INFORMATION SYSTEM, AND ROTATING SEATS FOR COMFORT.
WHO ATTENDED THE FLAGGING-OFF CEREMONY AND WHAT DOES THIS EXTENSION MEAN?
JAMMU AND KASHMIR CHIEF MINISTER OMAR ABDULLAH, UNION MOS JITENDRA SINGH, AND BJP STATE PRESIDENT SAT PAUL SHARMA ATTENDED. THE EXTENSION PROVIDES THE FIRST DIRECT RAIL LINK BETWEEN THE TWIN CAPITALS AND IS EXPECTED TO BOOST TOURISM AND THE LOCAL ECONOMY.
