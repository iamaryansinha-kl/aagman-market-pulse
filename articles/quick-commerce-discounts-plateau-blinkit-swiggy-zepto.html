Quick Commerce Discounts Plateau as Blinkit, Swiggy, Zepto Eye Profits

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Blinkit turned EBITDA positive at ₹4 crore in Q3 FY26, marking the first profitable quarter for a major Indian quick commerce player
  • 02/Swiggy Instamart reported an adjusted EBITDA loss of ₹908 crore in Q3 FY26 despite 103% gross order value growth
  • 03/JPMorgan data shows Blinkit and Swiggy dialled down subsidies while Zepto raised median discounts by 9-10%

India's quick commerce giants are pulling back on discounts to prioritise profitability over reckless growth. Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto are recalibrating strategies as investor patience for cash burn wears thin.

Blinkit reported adjusted EBITDA of ₹4 crore in Q3 FY26. It swung from a ₹156 crore loss in Q2, marking a milestone for the sector.

Swiggy Instamart posted an adjusted EBITDA loss of ₹908 crore in the same quarter. Its gross order value doubled year-on-year to ₹7,938 crore, but losses widened on dark store and logistics costs.

Zepto remains privately held and continues leaning on discounts. JPMorgan noted Zepto's median discounts rose 9-10% between late October and early November, even as it slashed marketing spend by 60%.

Blinkit CEO Albinder Dhindsa said a strong quick commerce business cannot be built on heavy discounting. The company matched rival pricing only selectively where market share was at risk.

Swiggy's contribution margin improved to negative 2.5%, up 9 basis points quarter-on-quarter. The company has guided to contribution break-even by Q1 FY27.

The Competition Commission of India is monitoring predatory pricing and deep discounting. Over 2 lakh Kirana stores closed in 2025 due to quick commerce competition.

Bernstein projects the quick commerce market to reach $35 billion by 2030. The sector is expected to grow around 80% in 2026, but profitability timelines remain uncertain across players.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY ARE QUICK COMMERCE COMPANIES REDUCING DISCOUNTS?
BLINKIT, SWIGGY INSTAMART AND ZEPTO ARE CUTTING SUBSIDIES TO PRIORITISE PROFITABILITY OVER GROWTH-AT-ANY-COST. BLINKIT TURNED EBITDA POSITIVE AT ₹4 CRORE IN Q3 FY26, PROVING THAT SUSTAINABLE UNIT ECONOMICS ARE POSSIBLE WITHOUT HEAVY DISCOUNTING.
HOW DID BLINKIT PERFORM IN Q3 FY26?
BLINKIT REPORTED ADJUSTED EBITDA OF ₹4 CRORE IN Q3 FY26, SWINGING FROM A ₹156 CRORE LOSS IN Q2 FY26. ITS REVENUE SURGED 776% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO ₹12,256 CRORE, WITH MARKET SHARE RISING TO APPROXIMATELY 50%.
WHAT ARE SWIGGY INSTAMART'S FINANCIAL METRICS?
SWIGGY INSTAMART POSTED AN ADJUSTED EBITDA LOSS OF ₹908 CRORE IN Q3 FY26. ITS GROSS ORDER VALUE ROSE 103.2% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO ₹7,938 CRORE, PROCESSING 106.4 MILLION ORDERS FROM 12.8 MILLION USERS WITH AN AOV OF ₹746.
WHAT IS ZEPTO'S DISCOUNT STRATEGY?
JPMORGAN DATA SHOWS ZEPTO RAISED MEDIAN DISCOUNTS BY 9-10% BETWEEN LATE OCTOBER AND EARLY NOVEMBER 2025. MEANWHILE, ZEPTO CUT PERFORMANCE MARKETING SPEND BY AROUND 60%, WHICH HAS LED TO DECLINING WEEKLY ACTIVE USERS OVER THE PAST TWO MONTHS.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR INDIAN QUICK COMMERCE IN 2026?
BERNSTEIN PROJECTS THE SECTOR TO GROW AROUND 80% IN 2026 AND REACH $35 BILLION BY 2030. HOWEVER, PROFITABILITY TIMELINES VARY: BLINKIT IS ALREADY EBITDA POSITIVE, SWIGGY TARGETS CONTRIBUTION BREAK-EVEN BY Q1 FY27, AND ZEPTO IS PLANNING A ₹11,000 CRORE IPO.
