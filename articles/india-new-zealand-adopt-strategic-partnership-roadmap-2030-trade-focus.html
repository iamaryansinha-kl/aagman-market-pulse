India, New Zealand Adopt Strategic Partnership Roadmap to 2030 with Trade Focus

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India and New Zealand are deepening their ties.

The two countries have adopted a Strategic Partnership Roadmap to 2030 that focuses on expanding trade and economic cooperation over the next five years. The roadmap sets specific targets for bilateral trade and investment, covering areas including agriculture, education, technology, and climate cooperation.

Bilateral trade is currently modest. India and New Zealand trade around USD 1-2 billion annually, which is small compared to India's trade with other major economies. The roadmap aims to significantly expand this by identifying sectors where the two countries have complementary strengths and can build sustainable trade relationships.

Agriculture is a key area of cooperation. New Zealand is a world leader in dairy, meat, and horticulture production, with efficient farming systems and strong food safety standards. India, with its large and growing population, represents a significant market for high-quality agricultural products. The roadmap includes provisions for technical cooperation and investment in India's agricultural value chain.

Education and skills development feature prominently. New Zealand's education system is well-regarded, and the roadmap aims to increase the number of Indian students studying in New Zealand while facilitating partnerships between universities and technical institutions. This builds on the already strong people-to-people ties between the two countries.

Technology and innovation are identified as growth areas. The roadmap includes provisions for collaboration in areas like digital technology, biotechnology, and clean energy. New Zealand's expertise in sustainable agriculture and renewable energy could support India's goals in these sectors.

The climate cooperation component is significant. Both countries are committed to the Paris Agreement and have set net-zero targets. The roadmap includes joint initiatives on climate adaptation, renewable energy, and sustainable agriculture practices that could serve as a model for South-South cooperation.
