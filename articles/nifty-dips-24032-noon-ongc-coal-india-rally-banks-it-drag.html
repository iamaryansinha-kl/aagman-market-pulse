Nifty Dips to 24,032 at Noon; ONGC, Coal India Rally as Banks, IT Drag

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 slips 60.35 points or 0.25% to 24,032.35 at 12:50 PM, Sensex down 294.51 points
  • 02/ONGC leads gainers with 3.99% rise to ₹297.30; Eternal Ltd worst performer, down 3.32%
  • 03/Brent crude at $105-110 poses inflation risk; FII selling of ₹1,152 crore weighs on sentiment

Indian equity markets extended losses into Tuesday afternoon. The Nifty 50 slipped 60.35 points or 0.25 per cent to 24,032.35 by 12:50 PM. The Sensex fell 294.51 points or 0.38 per cent to 77,009.12.

Market breadth stayed positive on the BSE. 2,079 stocks advanced against 1,382 declines and 196 unchanged. 105 stocks hit 52-week highs while 17 touched 52-week lows.

Energy stocks led gains as crude oil prices remained elevated. ONGC surged 3.99 per cent to ₹297.30 on heavy volumes exceeding 2.5 crore shares. Coal India added 3.18 per cent to ₹466.90, touching an intraday high of ₹473.90.

On the losing side, Eternal Ltd fell 3.32 per cent to ₹247.01 on volumes over 4.12 crore shares. Axis Bank declined 2.35 per cent to ₹1,293.10 as banking stocks faced selling pressure. HCL Technologies slipped 2.12 per cent to ₹1,202.20.

Bank Nifty opened with a gap-down near 55,865. The 56,000–55,800 zone is now a key support band. A breach below could trigger weakness toward 55,500–55,200 levels.

"Crude oil prices continue to stay elevated, with Brent trading in the $105–110 per barrel range, posing a key risk to inflation," said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money. FII selling continued with foreign investors offloading ₹1,152 crore on Monday.

MCX Crude Oil traded near ₹9,200 while US Oil held near $97. The rupee remained under pressure with USD/INR near ₹94.20. Traders are watching the 23,900 support level on Nifty closely.
