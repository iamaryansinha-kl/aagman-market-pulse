Mahindra Finance Q4 Profit Surges 55% to ₹873 Crore, FY26 PAT Up 19%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mahindra Finance Q4 FY26 standalone PAT surged 55% YoY to ₹873 crore from ₹563 crore, driven by margin expansion
  • 02/FY26 full-year PAT rose 19% to ₹2,782 crore; board proposes final dividend of ₹7.50 per share, up from ₹6.50
  • 03/Non-vehicle portfolio grew 32% YoY; credit cost at 1.5% for Q4; watch FY26 credit costs at 1.7%

Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd (NSE: M&MFIN) reported standalone PAT of ₹873 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 55% jump from ₹563 crore in Q4 FY25.

Disbursements grew 11% to ₹17,184 crore during the March quarter. Assets under management rose 12% to ₹1.34 lakh crore.

Net interest margins expanded 101 basis points to 7.5%. The improvement reflects better yields and lower funding costs.

For the full year FY26, PAT increased 19% to ₹2,782 crore. On a consolidated basis, FY26 PAT rose 27% to ₹2,861 crore.

The board proposed a final dividend of ₹7.50 per share. This is up from ₹6.50 per share declared last year.

Asset quality remained within guidance. Gross stage 3 assets stood at 3.4%, while stage 2 and 3 assets were at 8.2%.

The company is diversifying beyond vehicle finance. Its non-vehicle portfolio grew 32% YoY, led by SME lending and loan-against-property products.

"This year's progress across growth, margins and risk was driven by disciplined execution," said Raul Rebello, MD & CEO, Mahindra Finance. He added that continued investments in the core vehicle financing franchise, new growth categories and technology will support sustainable growth.

Credit cost stood at 1.5% for the quarter. For FY26, credit costs rose to 1.7% following management overlays.

The company remains adequately capitalised. Its capital adequacy ratio is 18.8%, with a liquidity buffer of over ₹9,100 crore.
