Reliance Jio IPO: Price Band Set at ₹1,065-1,119 Per Share

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms has set an IPO price band of ₹1,065-1,119 per share, implying a total raise of ₹28,755-30,213 crore.
  • 02/The all-fresh issue has no offer-for-sale; proceeds will largely repay debt of telecom unit Reliance Jio Infocomm.
  • 03/Anchor allocations open October 19, the issue launches October 21, and listing is expected October 28.

Jio Platforms has set a price band of ₹1,065 to ₹1,119 per share for its initial public offering. ET Now reported the range citing agencies, with formal offer terms due in the red herring prospectus.

Based on the draft offer size of up to 27 crore fresh equity shares, the issue could raise about ₹28,755 crore at the lower end. At the upper end, the raise works out to roughly ₹30,213 crore. The final size will depend on the offer terms.

The timeline is tight. Anchor investor allocations are expected on October 19. The issue will likely open on October 21, and listing is expected on October 28, as Reuters reported earlier this week.

The issue is entirely fresh equity. There is no offer-for-sale, which means existing shareholders will not sell any shares in the IPO.

That structure matters for investors. Every rupee raised goes to the company rather than to selling shareholders, a signal that promoters and early backers are holding their positions.

Those existing shareholders are Reliance Industries, Meta's Jaadhu Holdings, and Google International. Their combined stake stays untouched through the listing.

At the upper end, the IPO implies a post-money valuation of at least ₹10.3 lakh crore. Against FY26 consolidated profit of ₹30,049 crore, that works out to a trailing earnings multiple of roughly 34x.

A multiple in the mid-30s prices Jio as a premium asset rather than a utility-like telecom operator. Whether the market accepts that tag will be clear when anchor books open on October 19.

A large part of the proceeds will repay debt linked to Reliance Jio Infocomm, the telecom operating subsidiary. The remainder is earmarked for general corporate purposes.

Jio reported revenue of about ₹1.46 lakh crore for FY26. Consolidated profit stood at ₹30,049 crore, and the company serves more than 524 million customers as of March 2026, per draft documents cited by media.

As of the draft filing, Reliance Industries held about 66.4% of Jio Platforms. Meta's Jaadhu Holdings held 9.98% and Google International held 7.73%. RIL remains the promoter after listing.

At ₹30,213 crore, the upper-end raise would surpass Hyundai Motor India and NSE among recent large issues. It would become the biggest listing in Indian stock market history.

India Inc has raised about $14 billion through IPOs so far this year, according to Bloomberg. That follows a record $22 billion raised in 2025, a pipeline Jio's issue will now dwarf.

A portion of the issue is expected to be reserved for eligible Reliance Industries shareholders. Sebi rules permit up to 10% of an issue for such a reservation; the exact size will appear only in the final offer document.

The reservation would give RIL's own investors a direct allocation in the IPO. For existing Reliance shareholders, it offers a route to Jio exposure at the offer price rather than the listing price.
