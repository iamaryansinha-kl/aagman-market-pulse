Stock to Buy Today: Bharti Airtel Eyes ₹2,120 Target on Technical Breakout

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bharti Airtel breaks above ₹1,870 resistance after forming a base at ₹1,770 in March; technical setup turns bullish
  • 02/Analyst consensus price target ranges from ₹1,900 to ₹2,350; Motilal Oswal has a Buy with target of ₹2,100
  • 03/A near-term pullback to ₹1,850 is possible before the stock resumes momentum toward the ₹2,120 target

Bharti Airtel Ltd (NSE: BHARTIARTL) is flashing a buy signal on technical charts. The stock has broken above the ₹1,870 resistance level after building a solid base around ₹1,770 in March.

The scrip was in a downtrend during January and February. Bears lost momentum in March, and the price has since climbed above both its 21-day and 50-day moving averages.

Technical analysts expect a short-term correction to around ₹1,850. After that dip, the stock is projected to regain bullish momentum and rally toward ₹2,120 in the coming weeks.

Bharti Airtel closed at ₹1,870 on May 3, 2026, after opening at ₹1,868. The day's trading range was ₹1,859.90 to ₹1,874.60.

Analyst consensus targets range from ₹1,900 to ₹2,350. Motilal Oswal has a Buy rating with a target of ₹2,100, while CLSA rates it Outperform with a target of ₹2,000.

The 52-week high stands at ₹2,174.50 and the low at ₹1,511. The stock trades at a market capitalisation of approximately ₹10.07 lakh crore.

The broader market is under pressure today. The Sensex fell 582.86 points to 76,913.50, and the Nifty declined 180.10 points to 23,997.55.
