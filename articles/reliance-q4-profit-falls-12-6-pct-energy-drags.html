Reliance Q4 Profit Falls 12.6% to ₹16,971 Crore, Energy Drags

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Reliance Industries Q4 consolidated net profit falls 12.6% YoY to ₹16,971 crore, below broader analyst estimates of ₹19,200-21,000 crore
  • 02/Revenue rises 12% to ₹2.94 lakh crore; EBITDA flat at ₹44,141 crore with margin down 180 bps to 15%
  • 03/Full-year PAT hits record ₹80,775 crore; Jio EBITDA surges 17.9%; board declares ₹6 per share dividend

Reliance Industries Ltd (NSE: RELIANCE) reported a 12.6 per cent drop in consolidated net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit after tax stood at ₹16,971 crore, down from ₹19,407 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations rose 12 per cent year-on-year to ₹2.94 lakh crore. EBITDA remained nearly flat at ₹44,141 crore against ₹43,832 crore in Q4 FY25.

The result missed broader analyst estimates. Consensus expectations pegged Q4 net profit at ₹19,200-21,000 crore, according to pre-result brokerage reports.

The energy business dragged overall performance. O2C segment EBITDA fell 12 per cent sequentially to ₹14,520 crore as crude premiums and logistics costs surged.

Jio Platforms was the standout performer. Quarterly EBITDA jumped 17.9 per cent year-on-year to ₹20,060 crore, while net profit rose 13 per cent to ₹7,935 crore.

Reliance Retail Ventures crossed 20,160 stores in Q4. Segment EBITDA rose 3.1 per cent to ₹6,921 crore, though net profit was flat at ₹3,563 crore.

For the full year, Reliance delivered record numbers. Annual revenue rose 9.8 per cent to ₹10.76 lakh crore, while PAT surged 16 per cent to ₹80,775 crore.

The board declared a dividend of ₹6 per share for FY2026. "We are advancing steadily toward the listing of Jio Platforms," said Chairman Mukesh D Ambani.
