Arohan Financial Services IPO: SEBI Filing for Rs 600 Crore Fresh Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Arohan Financial Services files DRHP with Sebi for a Rs 600 crore fresh issue, alongside a 4.04 crore share OFS by existing investors
  • 02/The NBFC-MFI has an AUM of Rs 6,308 crore and operates 1,073 branches across 17 states as of December 2025
  • 03/DAM Capital Advisors, Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors, and SBI Capital Markets are managing the maiden public offering

Arohan Financial Services has filed its draft red herring prospectus with Sebi for an initial public offering. The issue comprises a fresh issue of shares worth Rs 600 crore.

The IPO will also include an offer for sale of 4.04 crore shares by existing investors. Key sellers include US Teachers Insurance and Annuity Association, Michael & Susan Dell Foundation, and Aavishkaar Goodwell India Microfinance Development Company-II Ltd.

Tano Capital, TR Capital III Mauritius, and Danish Sustainable Development Goals Investment Fund are also participating in the OFS. The proceeds from the fresh issue will be used to bolster the company's capital base.

Arohan Financial Services is a technology-enabled NBFC-MFI offering income-generating loans to rural and semi-urban customers. It commenced operations in 2006 from a single location in Kolkata.

The company had an assets under management of Rs 6,308 crore as of December 2025. It operates through 1,073 branches spread across 17 states in India.

In January 2026, Managing Director Manoj Kumar Nambiar stated the company was targeting a Rs 1,500 crore maiden offering. The revised filing indicates a scaled-down fundraising plan.

The filing follows successful 2026 listings of Aye Finance and Kissht in the NBFC-MFI space. DAM Capital Advisors, Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors, and SBI Capital Markets are the book-running lead managers.
