Korea KOSPI Hits All-Time High at 7,822 on AI Chip Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KOSPI surges 4.32% to close at record 7,822.24 on May 11, driven by AI chip demand and retail investor buying
  • 02/Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix jump 6.3% and 11.5% to lifetime peaks, with chip exports up 150% in early May
  • 03/Authorities warn retail investors against excessive risk as borrowed investments near record 25 trillion won

The KOSPI surged 4.32% to close at a record 7,822.24 on Monday. It earlier jumped 5.35% to 7,899.32, triggering a trading curb. The rally was powered by AI chip demand and retail buying.

Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix led the charge. They rose 6.3% and 11.5%, respectively, both hitting lifetime highs. Samsung became only the second Asian company after TSMC to enter the trillion-dollar market cap club.

South Korea is the world's best-performing major market this year. The KOSPI has surged 86% in 2026, building on a 76% rally in 2025. Chip exports jumped 150% in the first ten days of May alone.

Retail investors, nicknamed "ants," net bought 2.9 trillion won ($1.97 billion) on Monday. Foreigners were net sellers of 3.5 trillion won. Retail borrowed investments stood at 24.9 trillion won, just shy of a record.

"There is profit-taking pressure among all investor groups, so note that short-term volatility can grow in semiconductor stocks as the KOSPI extends gains this week," said Han Ji-young, an analyst at Kiwoom Securities.

The Financial Supervisory Service warned retail investors against chasing short-term gains. It said it is monitoring leveraged investments and will take pre-emptive measures if needed to ensure market stability.

Year-to-date, Samsung Electronics has risen over 120% and SK Hynix over 160%. This outpaces a 15% gain in Nvidia and a 44% rise in TSMC. Despite the momentum, 738 of 897 stocks declined on Monday.
