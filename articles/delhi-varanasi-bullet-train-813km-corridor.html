Delhi-Varanasi Bullet Train: 813-km Corridor Cuts Travel to 3h 50m

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced the 813-km Delhi-Varanasi High-Speed Rail corridor will cut travel time to 3 hours 50 minutes
  • 02/The Union Budget 2026-27 proposed seven new bullet train corridors spanning ~4,000 km with ₹16 lakh crore expected investment
  • 03/The corridor connects 12 cities including Noida, Jewar, Agra, Lucknow, Prayagraj and Varanasi; DPR revision is underway

The Delhi-Varanasi High-Speed Rail corridor will span 813 kilometres. Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw detailed the route on June 29, 2026.

The project was announced in the Union Budget 2026-27. It is one of seven new high-speed rail corridors approved by the government.

Trains will run at a maximum speed of 350 kmph. The operational speed will be 320 kmph with an average of 250 kmph.

The Delhi-Varanasi journey will take approximately 3 hours 50 minutes. Delhi-Lucknow travel time will drop to 2 hours 10 minutes.

The Jewar to Lucknow segment will take 1 hour 40 minutes. The corridor connects 12 major cities including Noida, Agra, Prayagraj and Varanasi.

The Delhi station will be at Sarai Kale Khan. It will integrate with the Delhi Metro, Nizamuddin Railway Station and RRTS.

The seven corridors span nearly 4,000 km. They are expected to attract investments of about ₹16 lakh crore.

NHSRCL is updating the Detailed Project Report. The Mumbai-Ahmedabad corridor remains India's only sanctioned bullet train project at 508 km.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE DELHI-VARANASI HIGH-SPEED RAIL CORRIDOR?
THE DELHI-VARANASI HIGH-SPEED RAIL CORRIDOR IS A PROPOSED 813-KILOMETRE BULLET TRAIN ROUTE ANNOUNCED IN THE UNION BUDGET 2026-27. IT WILL CONNECT DELHI AND VARANASI WITH 12 INTERMEDIATE CITIES INCLUDING NOIDA, JEWAR, AGRA, LUCKNOW, PRAYAGRAJ AND BHADOHI.
HOW MUCH TIME WILL THE DELHI-VARANASI BULLET TRAIN SAVE?
THE DELHI-VARANASI JOURNEY WILL TAKE APPROXIMATELY 3 HOURS 50 MINUTES. THE DELHI-LUCKNOW SEGMENT WILL TAKE 2 HOURS 10 MINUTES. THE JEWAR TO LUCKNOW SEGMENT WILL TAKE 1 HOUR 40 MINUTES.
WHAT IS THE MAXIMUM SPEED OF THE PROPOSED BULLET TRAIN?
THE TRAINS WILL HAVE A MAXIMUM DESIGN SPEED OF 350 KMPH. THE OPERATIONAL SPEED WILL BE 320 KMPH WITH AN AVERAGE SPEED OF 250 KMPH.
WHO IS EXECUTING THE DELHI-VARANASI BULLET TRAIN PROJECT?
THE NATIONAL HIGH SPEED RAIL CORPORATION LIMITED (NHSRCL) WILL EXECUTE THE PROJECT. THE RAILWAY BOARD HAS DIRECTED NHSRCL TO REVISE THE DETAILED PROJECT REPORT BASED ON THE NEW ALIGNMENT AND GROUND CONDITIONS.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL INVESTMENT PLANNED FOR THE NEW BULLET TRAIN CORRIDORS?
THE SEVEN NEW HIGH-SPEED RAIL CORRIDORS ANNOUNCED IN THE UNION BUDGET 2026-27 SPAN NEARLY 4,000 KM. THEY ARE EXPECTED TO ATTRACT INVESTMENTS OF ABOUT ₹16 LAKH CRORE.
