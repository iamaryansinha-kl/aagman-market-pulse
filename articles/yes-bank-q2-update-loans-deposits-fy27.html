Yes Bank Q2 Update: Loans Up 23.8%, Deposits Up 19.5%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Yes Bank's provisional Q2 FY27 business update shows loans up 23.8% YoY to around ₹3.1 lakh crore, with deposits up 19.5% to ₹3.54 lakh crore
  • 02/The CASA ratio slipped to 30% from 33.7% a year earlier, while the credit-deposit ratio rose to 87.5% from 84.5%
  • 03/Shares gained 3% to ₹21.22 on Monday; the stock is down over 8% in a month amid a broader market decline

Yes Bank Ltd (NSE: YESBANK) released its provisional business update for the July-September quarter of FY27 on Sunday, October 4, 2026. The disclosure covers loan and deposit figures only.

Shares of the private lender gained 3% to ₹21.22 on the BSE on Monday, October 5, following the update.

Loans and advances stood at around ₹3.1 lakh crore at the end of Q2 FY27. That marks 23.8% year-on-year growth from the ₹2.5 lakh crore loan book in the same period of FY26.

On a sequential basis, the loan book rose 8.6% from ₹2.85 lakh crore reported in Q1 FY27. Lending momentum has stayed in high single digits on a quarterly basis.

Deposits rose 19.5% YoY to ₹3.54 lakh crore, from ₹2.96 lakh crore in the year-ago quarter. Sequentially, deposits grew 12.3% from ₹3.15 lakh crore in Q1 FY27.

The gap between loan growth and deposit growth explains the shift in the credit-deposit ratio over the year. Lending expanded faster than deposits on an annual basis.

Normalised for the FCNR(B) special RBI scheme, deposit growth stood at 6.5% YoY and 13.2% QoQ. The headline figures include the impact of that foreign-currency deposit scheme.

Current account and savings account deposits rose around 7% YoY to ₹1.06 lakh crore. However, the CASA ratio declined to 30% in Q2 FY27.

The CASA ratio stood at 33.7% in Q2 FY26 and 32.7% in Q1 FY27. The year-on-year slide of 3.7 percentage points points to a funding mix tilted toward term deposits.

Normalised for the special RBI scheme, the CASA ratio stood at 31.7% in Q2 FY27. A lower CASA mix typically means higher funding costs, since savings and current accounts are cheaper than fixed deposits.

The credit-to-deposit ratio rose to 87.5% during the quarter, from 84.5% a year earlier. It contracted from 90.4% in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

The sequential easing in the credit-deposit ratio reflects the strong 12.3% quarterly deposit growth. Deposit mobilisation ran ahead of lending for the quarter.

The update does not include profitability figures. Yes Bank has not disclosed net profit, asset quality, or margin numbers for the quarter.

This is a business update, not an earnings release. Detailed financial results for Q2 FY27 will be published separately.

On the market side, the stock has fallen over 2% in a week and 8% in a month amid a broader market decline. The October 5 bounce follows that stretch of weakness.

In 2026 so far, shares have dropped more than 3%. That still outperforms the Nifty Bank index, which has tumbled around 9% over the same period.

Over three years, the stock has returned around 21%. Over five years, the return is more than 58%.

The bank's market capitalisation stands at more than ₹65,130 crore. Yes Bank remains one of the most closely watched private banking stocks on the NSE.
