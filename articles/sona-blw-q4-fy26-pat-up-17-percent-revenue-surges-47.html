Sona BLW Q4 FY26: PAT Up 17% to ₹192 Cr, Revenue Surges 47%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sona BLW Precision Forgings reports Q4 FY26 PAT of ₹192 crore, a 17% YoY jump from ₹161.4 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue surged 47% to ₹1,272.30 crore, driven by EV traction motors and railway business consolidation
  • 03/Net order book stands at ₹23,700 crore; company won three European OEM orders and one hybrid programme during the quarter

Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd (NSE: SONACOMS) has reported a 17% rise in profit after tax to ₹192 crore for the quarter ended March 2026. The company had posted ₹161.4 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue for Q4 FY26 jumped 47% to ₹1,272.30 crore, up from ₹868.40 crore in Q4 FY25. The growth was led by EV traction and suspension motors, differential gears, and railway business consolidation.

For the full financial year, standalone PAT rose 11.52% to ₹646.41 crore against ₹579.68 crore in FY25. FY26 revenue climbed 26% to ₹4,475.10 crore, while EBITDA grew 13% to ₹1,106.90 crore.

"Q4 FY26 was our strongest quarter financially," said MD and Group CEO Vivek Vikram Singh. "We delivered our best-ever quarter, with the highest revenue, EBITDA, PAT, BEV revenue, and BEV revenue share."

Battery electric vehicle revenue formed 39% of automotive product revenue in Q4 FY26. The company won four driveline orders, three EV programmes, and one hybrid programme during the quarter.

For the first time in nearly four years, Sona BLW secured three orders from European OEMs. It also commercialised two new railway products — electric control panels and HVAC systems.

The company's net order book stood at ₹23,700 crore at March-end, with ₹5,700 crore added during FY26. This provides strong revenue visibility for the coming fiscal year.

Peers like Bharat Forge and Samvardhana Motherson International are also pivoting toward EV components. Sona BLW's 39% BEV revenue share puts it ahead in the electrification race among auto ancillaries.

The stock has risen over 24% in the past year, outperforming the broader Sensex. Investors will watch FY27 guidance and margin sustainability in the upcoming earnings call.
