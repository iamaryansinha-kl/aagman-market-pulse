Kaynes Technology Shares Crash 25% in 3 Days on Weak Q4; Elara Downgrades

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kaynes Technology India shares crashed nearly 25% in 3 days after weaker-than-expected Q4; stock hit intraday low of ₹3,132 on Monday
  • 02/Q4 net profit fell 22% YoY to ₹91 crore; revenue rose 26% to ₹1,243 crore but company missed FY26 guidance on revenue, cash flow, and working capital
  • 03/Elara downgraded rating to "Accumulate" from "Buy" and slashed target price to ₹3,530 from ₹5,700; FY27 growth guidance lowered to 30%

Kaynes Technology India Ltd (NSE: KAYNES) shares fell 4.27% on Monday to hit an intraday low of ₹3,132. The stock has now lost nearly 25% over three consecutive trading sessions.

The crash followed a weaker-than-expected Q4 FY26 earnings report released last week. The company reported a consolidated net profit of ₹91 crore, down 22% from ₹116 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations rose 26% YoY to ₹1,243 crore versus ₹984 crore a year earlier. However, the company missed its revised FY26 guidance on three key parameters tracked by Elara Securities.

FY26 revenue came in at ₹36 billion against the revised guidance of ₹40 billion. Operating cash flow remained sharply negative at ₹6 billion instead of turning positive as expected.

Net working capital days stayed elevated at 125 days. This missed the company's own target of bringing NWC below 100 days by the end of FY26.

Brokerage firm Elara Securities downgraded the stock to "Accumulate" from "Buy". It cut the target price to ₹3,530 from ₹5,700, valuing the company at 36x March FY28 estimated earnings versus 42x earlier.

Elara also reduced its FY27E and FY28E EPS estimates by 23% and 33% respectively. It cited lower Q4 sales, weaker guidance visibility, and persistent concerns around cash flow and working capital.

For FY27, management has guided for 30% revenue growth. This is below its earlier target of 40-50%, though still ahead of the broader EMS industry growth rate.

Despite the downgrade, Elara noted that the recent weakness was likely due to deferred execution rather than order cancellations. The upcoming OSAT plant is expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue from FY27 onward.

On the technical front, the stock's 14-day RSI stands at 31.9. This is just above the oversold threshold of 30. The stock is trading below all 8 simple moving averages, indicating a bearish setup.
