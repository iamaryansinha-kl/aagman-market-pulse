Adani Green FY27 Capex ₹45,000 Cr: ₹15,000 Cr For BESS, Rajasthan Hub

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Green Energy plans ₹45,000 crore capital expenditure for FY2027, with ₹15,000 crore earmarked for battery energy storage systems.
  • 02/The company added 5.1 GW of greenfield renewable capacity in FY26, taking total operational portfolio to 19.3 GW.
  • 03/Rajasthan is set to emerge as a key storage hub as the company scales up its BESS investments.

Adani Green Energy Ltd plans ₹45,000 crore in capital expenditure for FY2027. Of this, ₹15,000 crore is earmarked for battery energy storage systems.

The company added 5.1 GW of greenfield renewable energy capacity during FY26. This expansion took its total operational portfolio to 19.3 GW.

Rajasthan is set to emerge as a key storage hub. The state will play a central role in Adani Green's battery energy storage expansion plans.

For the March 2026 quarter, Adani Green reported a profit of ₹514 crore. Sri Lankan write-offs and refinancing costs impacted the full FY26 performance.

The BESS investment signals a strategic pivot toward energy storage. This complements the company's growing renewable energy generation base.

Large-scale battery storage will help Adani Green manage grid stability. It will also enable higher renewable energy penetration across its portfolio.

Investors should watch execution on the ₹45,000 crore capex plan. Timely commissioning of BESS projects in Rajasthan will be critical.
