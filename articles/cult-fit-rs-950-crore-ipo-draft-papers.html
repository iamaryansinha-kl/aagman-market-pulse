Cult.fit Files Draft IPO Papers to Raise Rs 950 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Cult.fit is going public.

The fitness and wellness startup has filed draft IPO papers with the Securities and Exchange Board of India. The company is targeting to raise Rs 950 crore through the initial public offering.

The issue will consist of both fresh equity and an offer for sale. Existing investors including Zomato, Temasek, and Kalaari Capital will partially exit through the OFS component.

Cult.fit was founded by Mukesh Bansal and Ankit Nagpal in 2016. The company started as a fitness chain under the Cult brand and has since expanded into mental wellness, primary healthcare, and healthy food delivery. It operates over 200 fitness centres across India.

The startup has raised multiple funding rounds from prominent investors. Zomato acquired a significant stake in Cult.fit in 2021 as part of its strategy to diversify beyond food delivery.

The fitness sector in India has grown rapidly post-pandemic. Consumers have become more health-conscious, driving demand for gym memberships, personal training, and wellness services. However, the sector remains fragmented with intense competition from both organised chains and local gyms.

Cult.fit's IPO will test whether fitness startups can command public market valuations similar to technology companies. The company will need to demonstrate a clear path to profitability to attract institutional investors.
