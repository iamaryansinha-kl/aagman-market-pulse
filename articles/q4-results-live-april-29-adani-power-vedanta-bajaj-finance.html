Q4 Results Live Apr 29: Adani Power Profit Up 64%, Vedanta Nearly Doubles

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Power Q4 net profit surges 64.3% to ₹4,271.40 crore, while Vedanta nearly doubles to ₹9,352 crore on April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Bajaj Finance, Federal Bank, and IOB report 22-43% profit jumps; Indian Bank, Granules India, and Bansal Wire post modest gains.
  • 03/Sensex closed up 0.79% at 77,496; investor focus shifts to Waaree Energies, KFin Tech, and Motilal Oswal results due later.

Adani Power Ltd (NSE: ADANIPOWER) reported a 64.3% jump in Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit to ₹4,271.40 crore. The figure stood at ₹2,599.23 crore in the year-ago period.

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) posted its highest-ever quarterly profit of ₹9,352 crore, nearly doubling from ₹4,961 crore in Q4 FY25. Revenue surged 29% to ₹51,524 crore.

"FY26 was a year of strong execution for Vedanta, with record operational performance across the portfolio," said Arun Misra, Executive Director. The company achieved lowest aluminium costs in five years.

Bajaj Finance (NSE: BAJFINANCE) reported a 22% rise in consolidated net profit to ₹5,465 crore. The result was largely in line with analyst estimates of ₹5,490 crore per LSEG data.

Indian Overseas Bank (NSE: IOB) saw Q4 profit jump 43% to ₹1,505.45 crore. Federal Bank's consolidated Q4 profit rose 22% to ₹1,341 crore on a one-time tax refund gain of ₹456 crore.

Indian Bank posted Q4 profit of ₹3,103.13 crore, up 5% YoY. FY26 PAT stood at ₹12,155.65 crore, with total business growing 12.79%.

Granules India (NSE: GRANULES) reported standalone Q4 profit of ₹98.9 crore versus ₹94 crore YoY. Bansal Wire Industries' profit rose 12% to ₹33.62 crore.

The Sensex closed at 77,496.36, up 0.79%. Nifty gained 0.76% to 24,177.65 as strong earnings lifted market sentiment.
