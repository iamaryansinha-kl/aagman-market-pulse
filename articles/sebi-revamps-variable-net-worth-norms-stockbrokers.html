SEBI Proposes Revamp of Variable Net Worth Rules for Stockbrokers

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI proposes overhaul of broker variable net worth calculation to align capital requirements with operational risk and client base
  • 02/Current 2022 framework based on client cash balances rendered ineffective by upstreaming of funds to clearing corporations
  • 03/Brokers with over 10,000 direct clients must maintain additional ₹50 lakh; public comments close May 15, 2026

SEBI has proposed a significant overhaul of variable net worth norms for stockbrokers. The draft paper was released on Friday, April 24, 2026.

The current framework was introduced in 2022. It requires brokers to maintain variable net worth equal to 10 per cent of average daily client cash balances over six months.

However, the upstreaming framework now transfers most client funds to clearing corporations. This leaves minimal balances with brokers, making the old calculation ineffective.

The revised methodology factors in multiple parameters. These include 10 per cent of average client credit balances over six months and fixed slabs based on active client counts.

Brokers serving more than 10,000 direct clients must maintain an additional ₹50 lakh. Incremental requirements apply for larger client bases.

Client accounts routed through authorised persons will attract separate capital requirements. These start from ₹5 lakh and scale up based on volume.

Net worth serves as the "second line of defence" after margins. SEBI says it must be robust enough to absorb risks not covered by margins.

Market participants have until May 15, 2026, to submit comments. The proposal follows recommendations from a working group of exchanges and broker associations.

CLIENT ACCOUNTS THROUGH AUTHORISED PERSONS WILL ATTRACT SEPARATE CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS STARTING FROM ₹5 LAKH, SCALING UP BASED ON TRADING VOLUME.
