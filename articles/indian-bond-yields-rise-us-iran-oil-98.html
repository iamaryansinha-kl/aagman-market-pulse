Indian Bond Yields Rise to 7.03% as US-Iran Tensions Push Oil to $98

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Benchmark 6.48% 2035 bond yield rose to 7.0343% at 10:00 a.m. IST on May 26 from 7.0270% prior close
  • 02/Brent crude climbed to $98 per barrel after US military strikes in southern Iran reversed earlier peace deal optimism
  • 03/Economists at Standard Chartered, ANZ, and MUFG expect RBI to hike rates as early as June 5 with another move in August

Indian government bond yields edged higher in choppy early trade on Tuesday, May 26. The benchmark 6.48% 2035 bond yield was at 7.0343% at 10:00 a.m. IST.

The yield had ended at 7.0270% on Monday. Bond prices move inversely to yields.

Renewed U.S.-Iran tensions reversed oil's direction. Brent crude climbed to around $98 per barrel after U.S. military strikes in southern Iran.

This reversed support from softer Treasury yields that had aided bulls. President Donald Trump said on Saturday that Washington and Iran had largely negotiated a peace deal memorandum.

Analysts cautioned that even if a deal is finalised, full energy flow recovery is unlikely to be immediate. Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz could take months to normalise.

Elevated oil prices impact India's inflation, current account deficit, and fiscal maths. They also add pressure on the Reserve Bank of India to hike interest rates.

India's overnight index swap rates moved higher tracking oil. The one-year swap was at 6.16%, while the two-year rate rose to 6.3850%. Economists at Standard Chartered, ANZ, and MUFG expect the RBI to hike rates as early as June 5.

"We could be in a narrow range of 7.01%-7.05% today and tomorrow, if oil prices remain around the current levels, and demand for the weekly auction could sway the market on Friday," a trader with a primary dealership said. New Delhi will raise ₹280 billion through bond sales on Friday.
