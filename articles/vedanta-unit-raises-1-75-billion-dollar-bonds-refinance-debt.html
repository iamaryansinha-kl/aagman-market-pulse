Vedanta Unit Raises $1.75 Billion in Dollar Bonds to Refinance Debt

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Resources Finance II accepts $1.75 billion in bids for three-tranche dollar bonds at coupons of 7.00%, 7.375%, and 7.75%
  • 02/Proceeds to refinance over $2 billion in high-yielding debt, including bonds with coupons ranging from 9.125% to 11.25%
  • 03/Pricing was 25 basis points below initial guidance; bonds guaranteed by parent Vedanta Resources and subsidiaries

Vedanta Resources Finance II has raised $1.75 billion through a three-tranche dollar bond issuance. The UK-based Vedanta Resources subsidiary accepted bids on Friday, three merchant bankers told Economic Times.

The offering comprises $500 million in six-year bonds at a 7.00% coupon. It also includes $700 million in eight-year bonds at 7.375% and $550 million in eleven-year bonds at 7.75%.

The final pricing was 25 basis points below initial guidance across all three tranches. Initial guidance stood at 7.25%, 7.625%, and 8.00% respectively.

Proceeds will primarily refinance over $2 billion of higher-yielding outstanding debt. The company plans to buy back four existing bonds with coupons between 9.125% and 11.25%.

The targeted repurchases include $550 million of the 9.475% 2030 bond, $500 million of the 11.25% 2031 paper, $500 million of the 9.125% 2032 bond, and $550 million of the 9.85% 2033 notes.

The bonds will be guaranteed by parent Vedanta Resources and subsidiaries Twin Star Holdings, Welter Trading, and Vedanta Holdings Mauritius II. The notes are expected to carry ratings of Ba3/BB-/BB.

In October, the same unit had raised $500 million via seven-year dollar bonds at 9.1250%. That issuance is among the bonds the company now intends to repurchase.

The bankers requested anonymity as they are not authorised to speak to the media. Vedanta did not respond to a Reuters email seeking comment on the transaction.
