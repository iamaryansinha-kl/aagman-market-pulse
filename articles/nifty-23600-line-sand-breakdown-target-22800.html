Nifty's 23,600 Line in the Sand: Why Missing This Level Could Pull the Market to 22,800

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Nifty's late-week profit booking has left the index vulnerable, with 23,600 emerging as the critical line in the sand. Anand James, Chief Market Strategist at Geojit Financial Services, warns that holding above this level keeps buying hopes alive. However, a breakdown would force traders to bring 22,800 back into the radar.

The index gave up gains made in the previous two weeks to end over 2% lower. The bounce off key supports found enough momentum to tip the scales from fear of collapse to hopes of seeing above 25,000. But the rapid ascent through Thursday and Friday rendered the trend vulnerable to profit booking.

The 23,850-23,900 region, from whose vicinity the turn lower unfolded, was indeed an obstacle that would have appeared too steep a wall to climb right away. James does not have an upside objective for now until 23,900 is conquered.

While the sharpness of Friday's turn is viewed with caution, the 23,600 region will serve as an important pivot. As long as Nifty trades above it, hopes of a renewed buying attempt could be seen. A sustained move below would shift the outlook bearish.

The Nifty IT index tumbled a further 6%, continuing its recent weakness. Derivative positioning reflects a mixed undertone, with puts seeing fresh short build-up and calls witnessing long additions in near out-of-the-money strikes.

Nearly 50% of IT stocks in the futures segment recorded short build-up both on Friday and on a week-on-week basis. Around 30% of stocks are showing an open interest put-call ratio below 0.5, indicating the potential for a near-term pullback.

Charts show oscillators in overbought conditions, with some showing positive divergence. Friday's rise was encouraging but not sufficient to signal a reversal. Directional indicators continue to indicate strong downside momentum.

Friday's close points to an inverted hammer formation, but James is inclined to weigh the inside bar close and see possibilities of an upside attempt in the coming week. The broader market sentiment remains cautious.

Among specific stocks, Kaynes was the top loser in the week and fell 27%. Despite a doji at the end of steep losses, momentum indicators suggest more pain is in store. The stock closed near the year's low seen on January 27.

HFCL shares have more than doubled in just 3 months. Oscillators now appear exhausted, and those looking to enter should be cautious with a stop loss near 145-142. Turbulence is expected early next week, but if this region holds firm, 167 could be expected.

James recommends KPRMILL as a buy with a target of 980 and stop loss at 895. The stock is showing signs of a constructive reversal on the daily chart, supported by a strong bullish engulfing candle formed near the key support zone around 900.

REFEX is another buy recommendation with a target of 310 and stop loss at 266. The stock has delivered a decisive breakout above horizontal resistance near the 270-275 zone, supported by rising volumes and a Supertrend breakout on the weekly timeframe.
