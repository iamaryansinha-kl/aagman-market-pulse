Pakistan Gen Z Drives 41% of New Stock Exchange Accounts in FY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Pakistan's Gen Z (aged 18-30) accounted for 41% of 180,148 new retail accounts at the Pakistan Stock Exchange in FY25-26
  • 02/Investor population remains under 0.2% vs India's 6% and Bangladesh's 1-2%; monthly account openings tripled to 15,000
  • 03/KSE 100-Index surged 43% YTD to 179,571; PSX targets 2.5 million new accounts in the next two years

Pakistan's Generation Z made up 41% of new retail accounts opened at the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) in fiscal year 2025-26. Of the 180,148 new investors who entered between August 2024 and May 2025, 74,629 were aged 18 to 30.

Aamir Mushtaq Kanju, Deputy General Manager at PSX, confirmed the figure. The average monthly account openings at the exchange tripled to 15,000 this year.

Despite this surge, Pakistan's investor population stands at less than 0.2% of its 260 million people. India reports 6% and Bangladesh 1-2%.

On Wednesday, the KSE 100-Index closed at 179,571.27 after a 1.1% gain. The year-to-date advance stands at 43% or 53,944 points.

"For many, the stock market now represents a powerful wealth multiplier," said Kanju. PSX targets 2.5 million new investor accounts in the next two years.

Owais Ashraf, analyst at AKD Investments, said the market delivered an annualised return of about 66% in dollar terms over the past three years. Macroeconomic stability measures and IMF support boosted confidence.

Pakistan secured a USD 37 billion IMF package after nearly defaulting in 2023. GDP grew 3.7% in FY2025-26, per the Economic Survey unveiled on June 11.

The federal budget for 2026-27 estimates total spending at PKR 18,771 billion with a GDP growth target of 4%. Defence spending was hiked 17.6% to PKR 3,000 billion.
