NTPC Q4 FY26 Results: PAT Jumps 34% to Rs 10,615 Cr; Rs 3.5 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NTPC Q4 FY26 consolidated PAT jumps 34% YoY to Rs 10,615 crore versus Rs 7,897 crore in the year-ago period
  • 02/Revenue for the quarter was nearly flat at Rs 49,688 crore, up just 0.29% YoY; PAT surged 90% sequentially from Q3
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of Rs 3.50 per share for FY26; total FY26 dividend now stands at Rs 6.25 per share including interim

NTPC Limited (NSE: NTPC) reported consolidated net profit of Rs 10,615 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 34% increase from Rs 7,897 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue for the March-ended quarter stood at Rs 49,688 crore. This is a marginal 0.29% rise compared to Rs 49,834 crore in the year-ago period.

On a sequential basis, PAT surged 90% from Rs 5,597 crore in Q3 FY26. Revenue grew 8% from Rs 45,846 crore in the December quarter.

The board has recommended a final dividend of Rs 3.50 per share for FY26. This is subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming Annual General Meeting.

The final dividend is in addition to the interim dividend of Rs 2.75 per share. Total payout for FY26 amounts to Rs 6.25 per equity share.

For the full financial year, NTPC posted PAT of Rs 27,546 crore. This is up 15% from Rs 23,953 crore reported in FY25.

Annual revenue was marginally lower at Rs 1,88,138 crore. This represents a 0.40% decline from the previous fiscal year.

Expenses for Q4 FY26 stood at Rs 43,238 crore. This was down 0.35% YoY from Rs 43,391 crore but up 9% QoQ.
