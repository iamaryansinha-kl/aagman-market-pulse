Amazon, Zomato, Zepto Deploy Heatwave Measures for Delivery Workers

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Amazon launches Project Ashray with 100 air-conditioned rest stops serving 150,000+ delivery partners monthly
  • 02/Zomato creates 5,000+ rest points with restaurant, TVS and Shell partners; Swiggy cooling vests cut body temp by 6-7°C
  • 03/Zepto equips dark stores with air coolers and heat-reflective coatings; insurance covers heatstroke hospitalisation

As brutal heatwaves push temperatures above 40 degrees Celsius across India, major food delivery and e-commerce platforms are rolling out mitigation measures for gig workers.

Amazon has introduced Project Ashray, a network of 100 air-conditioned rest stops across major cities. These offer seating, washrooms, drinking water, charging stations and first aid.

The facilities serve more than 150,000 delivery partners every month. They are accessible even to workers outside Amazon's own network.

Zomato has tied up with restaurant partners, TVS, and Shell to create over 5,000 rest points offering shade and water. These are open to the broader gig economy.

Swiggy has introduced low-cost antimicrobial cooling jackets using evaporative cooling technology. The company claims these can reduce body temperature by 6 to 7 degrees Celsius for a few hours when soaked in water.

Zepto is upgrading dark stores with air coolers and heat-reflective roof coatings. Every store has a dedicated indoor resting space with drinking water and washrooms.

Platforms are also distributing summer kits with ORS, glucose sachets, and electrolyte powders. Zomato and Blinkit delivery partners have health insurance covering OPD and hospitalisation.

Flipkart has activated a Doctor on Call service for its wishmasters and their families. Amazon has set up a dedicated no-cost ambulance support line for emergencies.
