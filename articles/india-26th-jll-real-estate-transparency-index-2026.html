India Jumps to 26th in JLL Global Real Estate Transparency Index 2026, Most Improved in Asia Pacific

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's real estate market is becoming easier for global capital to trust. The country climbed five places to rank 26th globally in JLL's 2026 Global Real Estate Transparency Index, making it the most-improved market in Asia Pacific and one of the top five improvers worldwide.

The jump, from 31st in 2024, moves India into the middle of the 'Transparent' tier, reflecting gains across regulatory, legal, transaction, listed-market and sustainability parameters. JLL attributed the improvement to a maturing regulatory environment, the expansion of India's REIT ecosystem and strong institutional investment. Private equity investment in Indian real estate reached $10.5 billion in 2025, up 17% year-on-year, with a further $4.3 billion invested in the first half of 2026, up 25%.

The strongest improvement came in the Regulatory & Legal parameter, where India jumped from 37th to 19th globally and from ninth to sixth in Asia Pacific. JLL credited the maturation of the RERA framework, FDI liberalisation and land-record digitisation under initiatives such as the National Urban Digital Mission, NAKSHA and the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme.

India also maintained a strong position in the Transaction Process parameter, ranking 10th globally and third in Asia Pacific, helped by better data availability on commercial real estate financing and institutional investment flows.

The listed market deepened. India's global ranking in the listed real estate parameter improved from 36th to 35th, while office REIT stock increased 58% from 2024 levels to 164 million sq ft in 2026. REIT-worthy assets now account for 46% of India's Grade A office inventory, and office REIT stock represents 18% of Grade A stock.

Sustainability improved as well. India's global sustainability ranking rose from 29th to 27th, supported by SEBI's BRSR reporting framework for the top 1,000 listed companies and the National Green Building Mission launched in 2025. Green-certified Grade A office stock rose from around 39% in 2020 to 66% by the first half of 2026, with certified buildings commanding a 10-15% rental premium.

Data centres are emerging as a major opportunity. The market had 1,637 MW of inventory by mid-2026 with just 2.8% vacancy, 4,317 MW under construction and a planned pipeline of 15,000 MW. JLL expects capacity to expand from 1.6 GW to 6 GW by 2029, requiring around $110 billion of investment, with global hyperscalers committing more than $50 billion to AI-ready facilities.

JLL flagged continuing gaps around Scope 3 reporting, property-level energy disclosures, climate-risk reporting and biodiversity reporting.

JLL India CEO Radha Dhir said India set the pace for Asia Pacific in this year's index and is positioned to move towards the 'Highly Transparent' tier. Lata Pillai, head of capital markets at JLL India, said record investment should be viewed as a foundation for further growth.
