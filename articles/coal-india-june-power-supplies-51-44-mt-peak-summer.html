Coal India June Power Supplies Rise 5.9% to 51.44 MT on Peak Summer Demand

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Coal India supplied 51.44 MT coal to power plants in June, up 5.9% from 48.57 MT last year
  • 02/Overall coal supplies rose 7.5% to 65.8 MT; non-regulated sector up 14.8% to 14.50 MT
  • 03/Q1 FY27 power supplies 154.75 MT up 1.8%; company targets 815 MT production for FY27

Coal India, the world's largest coal miner, announced strong supply numbers for June. The company supplied 51.44 million tonnes of coal to the country's power plants in the month. This represents a 5.9% year-on-year growth from 48.57 MT supplied in June last year.

The higher offtake by domestic power plants was driven by rising electricity demand. This comes amid the peak summer season. Power consumption increased 11.62% year-on-year to 166.46 billion units. Peak power demand rose to 264.76 GW from 242.77 GW in June 2025.

The company also recorded a 7.5% increase in its overall coal supplies. Total supplies rose to 65.8 MT in June from 61.2 MT in the year-ago period.

Coal supplies to the non-regulated sector registered robust growth. They increased 14.8% to 14.50 MT in June FY27 from 12.63 MT in June FY26.

During the first quarter of FY27, Coal India supplied 154.75 MT of coal to the power sector. This is up 1.8% from 151.93 MT supplied during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

Total coal supplies during Q1 FY27 stood at 197.7 MT. This marks a 3.5% increase from 191 MT supplied in the same period last year.

The higher dispatches helped liquidate 28.3 MT of pithead coal stock during the first quarter. The reduction in pithead inventory was a conscious business decision. It aims to improve inventory turnover, reduce carrying costs, and enhance supply chain efficiency.

The company said this forms part of its demand-synchronised mining and inventory optimisation strategy. This marks a shift from a purely volume-driven approach to a value-driven and demand-responsive operating model.

Coal India remains confident of achieving its coal production target of 815 MT and coal supply target of 850 MT for the ongoing financial year FY27.

On Monday, the company secured a Letter of Award from Bundelkhand Saur Urja Ltd. This is to develop a project at Jalaun Solar Park in Uttar Pradesh. The project comprises two 300 MW units and will supply power at a tariff of ₹2.73 per kWh.

The solar project is expected to be completed within 18 months of signing the power purchase agreement. The PPA is worth ₹2,831.11 crore according to the company's exchange filing.

For the March-ending quarter, Coal India reported a consolidated net profit of ₹10,839 crore. This compares with ₹9,751 crore in the same period last year, indicating an 11.15% year-on-year growth.

Consolidated revenue from operations rose 5.75% to ₹46,490 crore from ₹43,961 crore in the year-ago period. This also improved from ₹42,436 crore reported in the December-ended quarter.

The miner's average realisation from e-auction sales stood at ₹2,202 per tonne. This was lower than ₹2,363 per tonne in the year-ago period. The overall average price realisation of coal supplied fell by ₹23 year-on-year to ₹1,597.85 per tonne.
