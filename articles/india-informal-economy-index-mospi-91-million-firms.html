India to Launch Informal Economy Index Tracking 91.7 Million Firms

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoSPI is planning a high-frequency index to track India's informal economy of 91.7 million unincorporated enterprises employing 151.7 million workers
  • 02/The informal sector contributes nearly 50% to productivity and employs roughly 80% of India's workers, yet lacks any high-frequency tracking indicator
  • 03/Official timeline is 2031 but MoSPI aims to launch earlier; index could reshape RBI monetary policy, GDP estimates and corporate planning

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) is planning a high-frequency index for unincorporated enterprises. The index will periodically measure the health of India's informal economy.

India had 91.7 million unincorporated establishments in Q4 FY26. These enterprises employ 151.7 million people, up from 128.1 million in 2023-24.

The informal sector employs roughly 80% of India's workers. It contributes a little less than 50% to productivity but remains statistically underrepresented.

"The ministry aims to soon come out with the index for unincorporated sector enterprises," an official told ET. The official timeline is 2031, though MoSPI is aiming to launch earlier.

The index could reshape RBI monetary policy by revealing demand conditions in the informal economy. Currently, the central bank relies on formal sector indicators that paint an incomplete picture.

GDP estimates would improve by reducing dependence on organised sector proxies. Quarterly national accounts currently extrapolate informal activity from formal indicators.

FMCG firms like HUL, ITC, Dabur and Britannia, tractor makers Mahindra & Mahindra and Escorts Kubota, and two-wheeler companies Hero MotoCorp and TVS Motor would gain a new demand indicator. The index could track rural and informal income trends that drive their sales.

The proposal fits into MoSPI's broader data modernisation effort. The ministry has already replaced older surveys with ASUSE and begun publishing quarterly bulletins on unincorporated enterprises.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE PROPOSED INFORMAL ECONOMY INDEX?
THE MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION (MOSPI) IS PLANNING A HIGH-FREQUENCY INDEX TO TRACK UNINCORPORATED ENTERPRISES. IT WILL PERIODICALLY MEASURE THE HEALTH OF INDIA'S INFORMAL ECONOMY, WHICH CURRENTLY LACKS ANY REGULAR HIGH-FREQUENCY INDICATOR.
HOW LARGE IS INDIA'S INFORMAL ECONOMY?
ACCORDING TO MOSPI'S LATEST QUARTERLY ESTIMATES, INDIA HAD 91.7 MILLION UNINCORPORATED ESTABLISHMENTS IN Q4 FY26, EMPLOYING 151.7 MILLION PEOPLE. THE SECTOR EMPLOYS ROUGHLY 80% OF INDIA'S WORKERS AND CONTRIBUTES NEARLY 50% TO PRODUCTIVITY.
WHEN WILL THE INDEX BE LAUNCHED?
THE OFFICIAL TIMELINE IS 2031, BUT MOSPI IS AIMING TO LAUNCH IT EARLIER. "THE MINISTRY AIMS TO SOON COME OUT WITH THE INDEX FOR UNINCORPORATED SECTOR ENTERPRISES," AN OFFICIAL TOLD ET.
HOW WILL THE INDEX IMPACT RBI MONETARY POLICY?
THE INDEX WILL HELP THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA JUDGE WHETHER INFLATION IS DRIVEN BY STRONG DEMAND OR WEAKENING ACTIVITY IN THE INFORMAL SECTOR. THIS COULD PREVENT OVERLY AGGRESSIVE RATE TIGHTENING WHEN SMALL BUSINESSES ARE ALREADY UNDER STRESS.
WHICH COMPANIES AND SECTORS WILL BENEFIT FROM THIS INDEX?
FMCG COMPANIES LIKE HUL, ITC, DABUR AND BRITANNIA, TRACTOR MAKERS MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA AND ESCORTS KUBOTA, AND TWO-WHEELER FIRMS HERO MOTOCORP AND TVS MOTOR WOULD GAIN A NATIONALLY COMPARABLE INDICATOR OF RURAL AND INFORMAL DEMAND TRENDS.
