Online Instruments Files ₹750 Crore IPO via Fresh Issue with SEBI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Online Instruments (India) Ltd files DRHP with SEBI for ₹750 crore fresh issue; promoters offload up to 57.10 lakh shares via OFS
  • 02/Company may raise ₹150 crore via pre-IPO placement; FY25 revenue jumped to ₹547.4 crore from ₹335.9 crore in FY23
  • 03/Book-running lead managers Equirus Capital and Motilal Investment Advisors will manage the public issue

Online Instruments (India) Ltd has filed draft papers with SEBI for an IPO. The offering includes a ₹750 crore fresh issue.

Promoters Anita Mahesh Bellad and Rajeshwari Shivanand Mahashetti will sell up to 57.10 lakh equity shares through an Offer For Sale as part of the IPO.

The Bengaluru-based company may raise up to ₹150 crore through a pre-IPO placement. If completed, the fresh issue size will be reduced accordingly.

The company will use ₹160 crore to repay borrowings and ₹330 crore for working capital. Remaining funds will support acquisitions and general corporate purposes.

Incorporated in 2006, Online Instruments provides audiovisual systems integration solutions. It manufactures IFPDs and LED displays under the LOGIC brand.

Revenue from operations surged to ₹547.4 crore in FY25 from ₹335.9 crore in FY23. Net profit more than doubled to ₹35.3 crore from ₹15.5 crore during the same period.

As of December 31, 2025, it served 745 customers across sectors including healthcare, banking, and education. It also operates in Singapore, UAE, the US, and France.

Equirus Capital and Motilal Investment Advisors are the book-running lead managers to the issue. The draft papers were filed on Friday.
