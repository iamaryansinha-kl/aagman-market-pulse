AC Makers to Raise Prices: Intex Plans 4-5% Hike on LPG Cost Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Intex Technologies plans 4-5% AC price hike due to surging LPG-linked input costs, with industry-wide adjustments expected
  • 02/Mid-market brands like Voltas and Lloyd priced at ₹32,000-38,000 vs premium LG/Samsung at ₹44,000-55,000, gap of ₹12,000-17,000
  • 03/Intex targets ₹750-800 crore revenue by FY27, up from ~₹450 crore in FY26, as consumers downtrade to affordable options

Intex Technologies is evaluating a 4–5% increase in air conditioner prices. Director Keshav Bansal said sustained input cost pressures are now forcing some pass-through to consumers.

Commercial LPG prices are nearing ₹1,000 per cylinder. Suppliers of ceramics, glass, and metal coatings are passing on higher fuel costs across the AC supply chain.

Plastics and polymer prices have risen 10–15%. Copper and aluminium prices also remain elevated, adding further pressure on manufacturers.

Premium players LG, Samsung, and Panasonic are expected to adjust prices too. They had held headline prices steady during the peak summer season.

A 1.5-ton 3-star inverter AC from value brands costs ₹32,000–₹38,000. Premium equivalents are priced at ₹44,000–₹55,000, creating a gap of ₹12,000–₹17,000.

Intex reported revenue of around ₹450 crore in FY26, up 36% from ~₹330 crore in FY25. The company is targeting ₹750–800 crore by FY27.

The company operates over 25,000 dealers and reaches 18,000 pin codes. It remains debt-free and cash flow-positive, giving it flexibility to absorb part of the cost pressure.

"For a large section of consumers, price remains the primary decision driver," Bansal said. "Consumers are willing to shift to brands that offer similar features at a more accessible price point."

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY ARE AC MAKERS PLANNING TO RAISE PRICES?
INPUT COSTS HAVE SURGED DUE TO COMMERCIAL LPG PRICES NEARING ₹1,000 PER CYLINDER, PLASTICS AND POLYMER PRICES RISING 10-15%, AND ELEVATED COPPER AND ALUMINIUM COSTS. MANUFACTURERS HAVE ABSORBED MUCH OF THIS IMPACT BUT NOW NEED TO PASS SOME COSTS TO CONSUMERS.
HOW MUCH IS INTEX PLANNING TO HIKE AC PRICES?
INTEX TECHNOLOGIES IS EVALUATING A 4-5% PRICE INCREASE ON AIR CONDITIONERS. DIRECTOR KESHAV BANSAL INDICATED THE HIKE COULD BE IN THE RANGE OF 5-7% TO OFFSET SUSTAINED INPUT COST PRESSURES.
WHAT IS THE PRICE GAP BETWEEN PREMIUM AND MID-MARKET AC BRANDS?
IN THE 1.5-TON 3-STAR INVERTER AC SEGMENT, VALUE BRANDS LIKE INTEX, VOLTAS, AND LLOYD ARE PRICED AT ₹32,000-₹38,000. PREMIUM PLAYERS LIKE LG, SAMSUNG, AND PANASONIC RANGE FROM ₹44,000-₹55,000, A GAP OF ₹12,000-₹17,000 OR ROUGHLY 20-35%.
WHAT IS INTEX'S REVENUE AND GROWTH TARGET?
INTEX REPORTED REVENUE OF AROUND ₹450 CRORE IN FY26, UP ABOUT 36% FROM ~₹330 CRORE IN FY25. THE COMPANY IS TARGETING ₹750-₹800 CRORE BY FY27, SUPPORTED BY DEMAND SHIFTS IN AIR CONDITIONERS AND SMART TVS.
WHICH OTHER BRANDS ARE EXPECTED TO RAISE AC PRICES?
INDUSTRY SOURCES INDICATE BOTH PREMIUM PLAYERS SUCH AS LG ELECTRONICS AND SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS AND MID-MARKET BRANDS INCLUDING VOLTAS, LLOYD, GODREJ, AND KELVINATOR ARE EXPECTED TO TAKE CALIBRATED PRICE INCREASES IN THE COMING WEEKS.
