Sebi Lets Gautam Adani, Four Group Firms Settle Public Float Violations

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi allowed Gautam Adani and four Adani Group companies to settle lapses tied to the 25% minimum public shareholding rule for a total payment of ₹14.82 lakh
  • 02/The four companies are Adani Enterprises, Adani Power, Adani Ports & SEZ and Adani Energy Solutions (formerly Adani Transmission); 14 directors were named
  • 03/The settlement requires no corrective shareholding disclosures and contains no regulatory finding; probes into alleged stock manipulation remain pending

The Securities and Exchange Board of India has allowed Gautam Adani and four group companies to settle regulatory lapses. The case relates to the 25% minimum public shareholding requirement.

The settlement involves a total payment of ₹14.82 lakh ($154,403). Fourteen company directors, including Gautam Adani, were named in the order.

The four companies are Adani Enterprises, Adani Power, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone, and Adani Energy Solutions. Adani Energy Solutions was formerly known as Adani Transmission.

Sebi received complaints against the four companies in 2020. The complaints alleged non-compliance with minimum public float rules and related shareholding disclosure requirements.

The settlement order does not require the companies to make corrective shareholding disclosures. It also contains no regulatory finding on the alleged violations.

The case was part of 24 investigations Sebi opened after US short-seller Hindenburg Research alleged in 2023 that Adani group companies manipulated stock prices through tax havens. The group has denied the allegations.

In the last year, Sebi has allowed the group to settle several cases. However, probes into whether certain offshore investors were used to circumvent Indian rules remain pending.

Investigations into whether there was manipulation in the trading of Adani Group shares are also still ongoing.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH ADANI GROUP COMPANIES SETTLED WITH SEBI?
THE FOUR COMPANIES ARE ADANI ENTERPRISES, ADANI POWER, ADANI PORTS AND SPECIAL ECONOMIC ZONE, AND ADANI ENERGY SOLUTIONS (FORMERLY ADANI TRANSMISSION).
HOW MUCH DID THE ADANI GROUP PAY TO SETTLE?
THE COMPANIES AND 14 DIRECTORS, INCLUDING GAUTAM ADANI, SETTLED THE PROCEEDINGS BY PAYING A TOTAL OF ₹14.82 LAKH ($154,403).
WHAT WAS THE ALLEGED VIOLATION?
SEBI HAD ALLEGED VIOLATIONS OF MINIMUM PUBLIC SHAREHOLDING REQUIREMENTS AND RELATED SHAREHOLDING DISCLOSURE RULES, BASED ON COMPLAINTS RECEIVED IN 2020.
DOES THE SETTLEMENT REQUIRE CORRECTIVE DISCLOSURES?
NO. THE SETTLEMENT ORDER DOES NOT REQUIRE THE COMPANIES TO MAKE ANY CORRECTIVE SHAREHOLDING DISCLOSURES AND CONTAINS NO REGULATORY FINDING ON THE ALLEGED VIOLATIONS.
ARE OTHER ADANI-RELATED SEBI PROBES STILL PENDING?
YES. INVESTIGATIONS INTO WHETHER OFFSHORE INVESTORS WERE USED TO CIRCUMVENT INDIAN RULES AND WHETHER THERE WAS MANIPULATION IN ADANI GROUP SHARE TRADING REMAIN PENDING.
