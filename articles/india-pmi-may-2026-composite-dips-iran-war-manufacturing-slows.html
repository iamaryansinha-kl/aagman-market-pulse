India PMI May 2026: Composite Dips to 58.1 on Iran War, Manufacturing Slows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HSBC flash Composite PMI dipped to 58.1 in May from April's 58.2 as manufacturing output slowed on Middle East war impact
  • 02/Manufacturing PMI dropped to 54.3 while services PMI edged up to 58.9; new orders expanded at one of slowest rates in four years
  • 03/New export orders across the private economy grew at weakest pace in 19 months as global conflict dampened international demand

India's private sector growth eased in May 2026. The HSBC flash Composite PMI dipped to 58.1 from April's final reading of 58.2, data compiled by S&P Global showed.

The 50-mark separates expansion from contraction. The composite reading came in slightly above a Reuters poll median forecast of 58.0.

Manufacturing bore the brunt of the slowdown. The factory PMI dropped to 54.3 in May from April's 54.7, with new orders expanding at one of the slowest rates in nearly four years.

Production growth in manufacturing eased to its second-weakest level since mid-2022. Survey respondents cited the Middle East conflict and related travel disruptions as key dampeners.

Services activity offered a marginal counterweight. The services PMI business activity index rose to 58.9 from 58.8, with service providers hiring additional staff at the greatest extent in nearly a year.

New export orders across the private economy grew at their weakest pace in 19 months. Cooling international demand and geopolitical uncertainty weighed heavily on overseas bookings.

Input costs climbed across both sectors. Manufacturing outlays increased at the steepest rate since July 2022, driven by higher prices for energy, steel, and food items.

Firms limited the pass-through of higher overheads to customers. Output charges at the composite level rose at the weakest pace since January, indicating margin pressure.

Business optimism retreated to a three-month low in May. The latest figures suggest persistent cost burdens and cooling global demand are testing India's growth resilience.

WHAT IS THE INDIA FLASH COMPOSITE PMI FOR MAY 2026?
THE HSBC FLASH COMPOSITE PURCHASING MANAGERS' INDEX (PMI), COMPILED BY S&P GLOBAL, DIPPED TO 58.1 IN MAY 2026 FROM APRIL'S FINAL READING OF 58.2. THE 50-MARK SEPARATES EXPANSION FROM CONTRACTION.
HOW DID MANUFACTURING AND SERVICES PERFORM IN MAY 2026?
THE MANUFACTURING PMI DROPPED TO 54.3 IN MAY FROM APRIL'S 54.7, WITH NEW ORDERS AND PRODUCTION GROWTH SLOWING SHARPLY. THE SERVICES PMI ROSE MARGINALLY TO 58.9 FROM 58.8, OFFERING A SLIGHT COUNTERWEIGHT.
WHAT CAUSED THE SLOWDOWN IN INDIA'S PRIVATE SECTOR?
THE DECELERATION WAS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY THE MIDDLE EAST WAR AND COOLING INTERNATIONAL DEMAND. SURVEY RESPONDENTS NOTED THE IRAN CONFLICT AND TRAVEL DISRUPTIONS DAMPENED OVERSEAS ORDERS AND FACTORY OUTPUT.
WHAT IS THE STATUS OF EXPORT ORDERS AND INPUT COSTS?
NEW EXPORT ORDERS ACROSS THE PRIVATE ECONOMY GREW AT THEIR WEAKEST PACE IN 19 MONTHS. OVERALL INPUT COSTS WENT UP, WITH MANUFACTURING OUTLAYS RISING AT THE STEEPEST RATE SINCE JULY 2022 AMID HIGHER ENERGY, STEEL, AND FOOD PRICES.
WHAT DOES THE PMI DATA SIGNAL FOR INDIA'S ECONOMIC OUTLOOK?
BUSINESS OPTIMISM RETREATED TO A THREE-MONTH LOW, INDICATING PERSISTENT COST BURDENS AND COOLING GLOBAL DEMAND ARE TESTING THE RESILIENCE OF INDIA'S GROWTH ENGINE. FIRMS ABSORBED SOME COST PRESSURES AS OUTPUT CHARGES ROSE AT THE WEAKEST PACE SINCE JANUARY.
