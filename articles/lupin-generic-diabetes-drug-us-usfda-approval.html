Lupin Launches Generic Diabetes Drug in US After USFDA Approval

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Lupin gets USFDA approval to launch a generic diabetes drug in the United States
  • 02/The product strengthens Lupin's complex generics portfolio in the world's largest pharma market
  • 03/US sales contribute over 40% of Lupin's total revenue; every new launch adds to growth

Lupin Ltd has received final approval from the US Food and Drug Administration. The Mumbai-based pharmaceutical company will now launch its generic diabetes medication in the American market.

The company did not disclose the exact brand name or the reference drug in its exchange filing. It described the product as a complex generic that requires specialised manufacturing capabilities.

Complex generics typically command higher margins than standard tablets. They include injectables, inhalers, and sustained-release formulations that are harder to replicate.

Lupin is already among the top five generic drug suppliers in the US by prescription volume. Its American portfolio spans oral solids, injectables, and speciality products.

The US market accounts for roughly 40% of Lupin's consolidated revenue. New launches are critical to offsetting pricing pressure on older products.

India's pharmaceutical exports to the US crossed $8 billion in calendar year 2025. USFDA approvals for Indian facilities have accelerated after a pandemic-induced slowdown.

Lupin shares were trading at ₹1,847.30, up 0.8% on April 22. The stock has risen 35% over the past year on the back of strong US sales recovery.
