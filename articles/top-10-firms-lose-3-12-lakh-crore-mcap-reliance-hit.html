Top 10 Firms Lose Rs 3.12 Lakh Crore in M-Cap; Reliance Hardest Hit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nine of India's top 10 most valued firms lost a combined Rs 3.12 lakh crore in market capitalisation last week
  • 02/Reliance Industries took the biggest hit, shedding Rs 1,34,445.77 crore; only Bharti Airtel gained among the top 10
  • 03/BSE Sensex plunged 2,090 points (2.7%) and Nifty fell 532 points (2.2%) on geopolitical tensions and rising crude prices

Nine of India's top 10 most valued firms lost a combined Rs 3.12 lakh crore in market capitalisation last week. The BSE Sensex plunged 2,090.2 points or 2.7 per cent, while the NSE Nifty declined 532.65 points or 2.2 per cent.

Reliance Industries bore the heaviest losses. Its market valuation tumbled Rs 1,34,445.77 crore to Rs 18,08,420.81 crore, cementing its position as the most valued firm despite the drop.

"Markets ended the week lower, breaking out of their three-week consolidation phase amid lingering geopolitical tensions in West Asia, persistent weakness in the rupee, and rising inflationary concerns," Ajit Mishra, SVP, Research, Religare Broking Ltd, said.

The sharp rise in crude oil prices above the USD 105-per-barrel mark intensified worries over imported inflation, fiscal stress, and pressure on corporate margins, Mishra added.

State Bank of India's valuation eroded by Rs 52,245.3 crore to Rs 8,88,862.32 crore. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) tanked Rs 47,415.04 crore to Rs 8,19,062.65 crore.

Bajaj Finance dived Rs 27,892.28 crore to Rs 5,66,717.74 crore. HDFC Bank dropped Rs 20,630.01 crore to Rs 11,82,069.25 crore, while ICICI Bank lost Rs 14,290 crore to Rs 8,92,385.39 crore.

Larsen & Toubro's valuation fell Rs 9,078.87 crore to Rs 5,37,542.34 crore. Hindustan Unilever shed Rs 3,970.8 crore to Rs 5,33,592.18 crore, and LIC dipped Rs 2,182.12 crore to Rs 5,05,367.32 crore.

Bharti Airtel stood alone as the sole gainer among the top 10. Its market valuation jumped Rs 42,470.13 crore to Rs 11,60,525.16 crore, pushing it to the third spot in the rankings.

Reliance Industries remained the most valued firm. It was followed by HDFC Bank, Bharti Airtel, ICICI Bank, SBI, TCS, Bajaj Finance, Larsen & Toubro, Hindustan Unilever, and LIC.
