EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brickwork Ratings estimates India's demand-side push could save $37.8 billion in forex reserves this fiscal year
  • 02/Crude oil prices are likely to stay above $100 per barrel in 2026 with the rupee trading near ₹95 against the US dollar
  • 03/PM Modi's seven behavioural appeals target fuel, gold, fertilisers, foreign travel and edible oil consumption

Brickwork Ratings estimates India's demand-side push could save $37.8 billion, or roughly ₹3.59 lakh crore, in foreign exchange reserves this fiscal year. The report was published on May 17, 2026. Global crude oil prices are likely to stay above $100 per barrel for the rest of 2026. The Indian rupee remains under pressure near ₹95 per US dollar. Prime Minister Narendra Modi's seven behavioural appeals target voluntary demand reduction. These cover work from home, fuel conservation, avoiding foreign travel, postponing gold purchases, cutting edible oil use, promoting natural farming and adopting swadeshi products. A modest 10% reduction in crude imports could save $13.4 billion. A 50% cut in fertiliser imports may yield $7.3 billion in savings. Suspending non-essential foreign travel for one year could retain $7.9 billion domestically. A 10% drop in gold demand could save another $7.2 billion. Gold imports account for roughly $72 billion in FY26. Curbing purchases directly frees up foreign exchange to manage the higher oil import bill. India is near self-sufficient in key fertiliser nutrients but still imports 25-30% of total needs. Natural farming offers a triple dividend by cutting imports, reducing subsidy burden and improving soil health. "This strategic shift builds the structural resilience necessary to decouple domestic growth from global commodity volatility across energy, trade, and agriculture," Brickwork said in its report.
