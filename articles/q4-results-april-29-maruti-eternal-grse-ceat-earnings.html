Q4 Results April 29: Maruti Profit Dips 7%, Eternal Revenue Triples

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki Q4 standalone PAT falls 7% YoY to Rs 3,590 Cr despite record revenue of Rs 52,449 Cr up 28%
  • 02/Eternal Ltd Q4 revenue jumps 196.5% to Rs 17,292 Cr, PAT surges to Rs 174 Cr from Rs 39 Cr YoY
  • 03/GRSE shares rally 16% after Q4 profit rises 24.1% to Rs 303 Cr; CEAT jumps 12% on doubling of profit

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) reported a 7% year-on-year fall in standalone net profit to Rs 3,590 crore for Q4 FY2026. Revenue from operations rose 28% to Rs 52,449 crore, crossing the Rs 50,000 crore mark for the first time.

The company sold 676,209 vehicles in the quarter, its highest ever. Exports hit a record 0.45 million units for the full year FY2026. Total annual sales reached 2.42 million units.

The profit miss was driven by lower other income and higher tax outgo. Other income dropped to Rs 500 crore from Rs 1,528 crore in the year-ago period.

Maruti declared a final dividend of Rs 140 per share for FY2026. The stock rallied over 4% despite the profit miss, as brokerages stayed positive on volume growth.

Eternal Ltd (NSE: ETERNAL) posted Q4 revenue of Rs 17,292 crore, up 196.5% YoY. Adjusted PAT surged to Rs 174 crore from Rs 39 crore in Q4 FY2025. EBITDA jumped 575% to Rs 486 crore.

Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating on Eternal and raised its target price to Rs 347 from Rs 345. The stock surged 5% on the results.

CEAT reported a two-fold jump in consolidated net profit to Rs 243.8 crore. Shares rallied 12% to Rs 3,949.90. GRSE posted standalone net profit of Rs 303.19 crore, up 24.1% YoY, sending its stock up 16%.

Bandhan Bank's net profit rose to Rs 534 crore from Rs 318 crore YoY. Gross NPA improved to 3.27% from 3.33% QoQ. Dhanlaxmi Bank's adjusted PAT jumped 50.1% to Rs 43 crore.
