Aluminium Can Shortage Threatens ₹11,500 Crore Indian Beverage Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/A 12-13 crore aluminium can deficit threatens a ₹11,500 crore disruption across India's beer and beverage market
  • 02/Key suppliers Ball and CANPACK are operating at just 10-20% of normal domestic capacity
  • 03/Industry experts warn supply normalisation could take close to a year, with peak summer demand already hitting shelves

India's beverage industry faces a potential ₹11,500 crore disruption as a deficit of 12–13 crore aluminium cans builds up. Domestic supply from key manufacturers Ball and CANPACK has collapsed to just 10–20% of normal capacity.

The crunch follows a global aluminium price surge of 47–50% year-on-year to around $3,600 per tonne. India is import-dependent for can-grade aluminium, so the price spike has sharply raised input costs.

The impact is visible across bars, retail stores, and quick-commerce platforms. Diet Coke has begun disappearing in several markets. Monster Energy Zero Ultra, a top-selling zero-sugar variant, has also seen supply disruptions.

In several large beer markets, cans account for 75–80% of sales. Industry estimates suggest 60–70% of the ₹51,000 crore beer market now relies on aluminium cans.

Normal packaging inventory levels of 50–60 days are now running at just 20–30 days. Companies are draining pre-disruption stocks manufactured in January–February.

The shortfall translates into a combined hit of over ₹11,500 crore. This includes ₹6,250 crore in revenue losses and roughly ₹5,300 crore in margin pressure.

CRISIL estimates an additional ₹1,500 crore hit to brewer profitability. EBITDA margins are likely to compress by 250–300 basis points.

"At best, it will take close to a year for supply to normalise," said Anish Varshnei, co-founder and chief production officer at Goa-based Latambarcem Brewers. "Right now, we are just fighting for survival."

Glass bottles are also under pressure, with prices rising nearly 20%. Higher weight increases handling costs and breakage risks, making cans the preferred format across the supply chain.

United Breweries MD Vivek Gupta had earlier flagged the shortage as already impacting growth. He estimated a 1–2% hit to volumes with no immediate resolution in sight.

"The beer you are seeing today was produced two to three months earlier," Varshnei warned. "This summer is going to be very difficult."

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE SCALE OF THE ALUMINIUM CAN SHORTAGE IN INDIA?
THE INDUSTRY IS FACING A DEFICIT OF 12–13 CRORE ALUMINIUM CANS. DOMESTIC SUPPLY FROM KEY SUPPLIERS BALL AND CANPACK HAS FALLEN TO JUST 10–20% OF NORMAL CAPACITY, PUTTING THE ENTIRE ₹51,000 CRORE BEER MARKET AND NON-ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE SECTOR AT RISK.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL FINANCIAL IMPACT OF THIS SHORTAGE?
THE COMBINED HIT IS ESTIMATED AT OVER ₹11,500 CRORE. THIS INCLUDES ₹6,250 CRORE IN REVENUE LOSSES — ₹1,200–1,300 CRORE IN BEER AND NEARLY ₹5,000 CRORE IN NON-ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES — ALONG WITH MARGIN PRESSURE OF ROUGHLY ₹5,300 CRORE. CRISIL ESTIMATES AN ADDITIONAL ₹1,500 CRORE HIT TO BREWER PROFITABILITY.
WHY HAS THE ALUMINIUM CAN SUPPLY COLLAPSED?
THE CRISIS IS DRIVEN BY THREE SIMULTANEOUS SHOCKS: A 47–50% YEAR-ON-YEAR SURGE IN GLOBAL ALUMINIUM PRICES TO AROUND $3,600 PER TONNE, DISRUPTED IMPORTS DUE TO LOGISTICAL CHALLENGES, AND REGULATORY CONSTRAINTS. INDIA IS IMPORT-DEPENDENT FOR CAN-GRADE ALUMINIUM, LEAVING THE SUPPLY CHAIN EXPOSED.
HOW LONG WILL THE SHORTAGE LAST?
INDUSTRY EXPERTS WARN THAT SUPPLY NORMALISATION COULD TAKE CLOSE TO A YEAR. ANISH VARSHNEI, CO-FOUNDER OF LATAMBARCEM BREWERS, SAID: "AT BEST, IT WILL TAKE CLOSE TO A YEAR FOR SUPPLY TO NORMALISE. RIGHT NOW, WE ARE JUST FIGHTING FOR SURVIVAL."
WHAT ARE COMPANIES DOING TO MITIGATE THE SHORTAGE?
COMPANIES ARE EMERGENCY IMPORTING CANS FROM CHINA, SRI LANKA, VIETNAM, AND KOREA, THOUGH THIS REQUIRES UPFRONT CASH PAYMENTS. INVENTORY LEVELS HAVE DROPPED FROM 50–60 DAYS TO 20–30 DAYS. SOME FIRMS ARE PIVOTING TO PET PACKAGING AS A PARTIAL BUFFER, BUT SUBSTITUTION REMAINS LIMITED IN PREMIUM CATEGORIES WHERE CANS ARE THE ONLY FORMAT AVAILABLE.
