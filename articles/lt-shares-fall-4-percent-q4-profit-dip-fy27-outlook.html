L&T Shares Fall 4% After Q4 Profit Dips 3%; FY27 Outlook Softens

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/L&T shares fell nearly 4% to ₹3,900 after Q4 net profit dropped 3% YoY to ₹5,326 crore, missing Street estimates
  • 02/Consolidated revenue rose 11% to ₹82,762 crore, but EBITDA margins contracted to 10.4% from 11% last year
  • 03/Brokerages are divided: Jefferies sees 20% upside to ₹4,885, while Nomura cut to neutral with ₹3,940 target

Larsen & Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) shares fell nearly 4% in early trade on May 6, 2026. The stock dropped to an intraday low of ₹3,900 from the previous close of ₹4,054.50.

The decline came after the engineering giant reported mixed Q4 FY26 results on May 5. Revenue grew a solid 11% year-on-year to ₹82,762 crore, driven by execution across core segments.

However, consolidated net profit declined 3% YoY to ₹5,326 crore. The dip was largely due to a high base, as Q4 FY25 included an exceptional gain of ₹475 crore.

On a recurring basis, profit actually rose 5% to ₹5,289 crore. But EBITDA came in at ₹8,610 crore with margins at 10.4%, below estimates and down from 11% last year.

The order book surged to a record ₹7.4 lakh crore as of March 2026, up 28% YoY. International orders now account for 52% of the total backlog.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹38 per share for FY26, up from ₹34 last year. The record date will be fixed upon shareholder approval.

Management guided for 10-12% growth in orders and revenue for FY27. Execution in H1 FY27 is expected to remain soft due to supply chain disruptions.

Brokerage views are split. Jefferies raised its target to ₹4,885 with a buy rating, citing the Lakshya 2031 roadmap. Bernstein maintained outperform at ₹4,637.

Nomura downgraded to neutral and cut its target to ₹3,940, reducing FY27 and FY28 earnings estimates by 8%. HSBC maintained hold with a lower target of ₹3,800.

The company plans to invest $2 billion in data centres, electronics and semiconductors. Divestments of Hyderabad Metro and Nabha Power are expected by Q1 FY27.
