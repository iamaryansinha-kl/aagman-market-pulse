Sun Pharma to Buy Organon for $11.75 Billion in All-Cash Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sun Pharma will acquire all outstanding shares of US-listed Organon for $14 per share in an all-cash deal valued at $11.75 billion enterprise value
  • 02/The offer represents a 24% premium to Organon's closing price on April 24; Organon shares had doubled since April 8 when deal reports first surfaced
  • 03/Combined entity will post $12.4 billion revenue and rank among top 25 global pharmaceutical companies; deal closes early 2027 pending regulatory approvals

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (NSE: SUNPHARMA) has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Organon & Co (NYSE: OGN) for $14 per share. The all-cash transaction carries an enterprise valuation of $11.75 billion.

The deal was announced on April 27, 2026. It is the largest overseas acquisition by an Indian drugmaker.

Organon is a New Jersey-based global healthcare company spun off from Merck in 2021. It holds a portfolio of over 70 products in women's health and general medicines, sold across 140 countries.

For 2025, Organon reported revenue of $6.2 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $1.9 billion. The company carries debt of $8.6 billion and had cash of $574 million as of December 31, 2025.

The $14 per share offer marks a 24% premium to Organon's closing price on Friday, April 24. Organon shares had already risen roughly 100% since April 8 when media reports of the deal first emerged.

"Organon's portfolio, capabilities and global reach are highly complementary to our own," said Dilip Shanghvi, Executive Chairman of Sun Pharma. "Bringing the two organisations together can create a stronger and more diversified platform."

Post-deal, the combined company will generate $12.4 billion in revenue. Sun Pharma will rank among the top 25 global pharmaceutical firms and become the seventh largest biosimilar player globally.

Sun Pharma shares closed 3.7% lower at ₹1,618.5 on April 24. The stock has fallen 10% over the past month amid anticipation of the deal announcement.
