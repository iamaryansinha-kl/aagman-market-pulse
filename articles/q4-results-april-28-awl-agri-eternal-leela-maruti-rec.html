Q4 Results April 28: AWL Agri, Eternal, Leela Surge; Maruti, REC Fall

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/AWL Agri Business Q4 net profit surges 70.7% to ₹268.01 crore; Eternal rises 22% to ₹705 crore
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki Q4 profit falls 7% to ₹3,590.5 crore; REC Ltd drops 22% to ₹3,375 crore
  • 03/Over 25 companies declared results on April 28; Sensex closed 0.54% lower at 76,886.91

AWL Agri Business Ltd (NSE: AWL) reported standalone net profit of ₹268.01 crore for Q4 FY26. The figure marks a 70.7% jump from ₹157 crore in the year-ago quarter.

The company achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue of ₹21,465 crore. FY26 revenue stood at ₹74,731 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹1 per share.

Eternal Ltd posted standalone Q4 net profit of ₹705 crore, up 22% year-on-year from ₹575 crore. The company's FY26 PAT stood at ₹2,655 crore against ₹1,960 crore in FY25.

Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd (NSE: THELEELA) recorded standalone Q4 net profit of ₹91.2 crore. This compares to ₹36 crore in Q4 FY25, driven by 12% revenue growth to ₹484 crore.

"FY26 has been a landmark year for The Leela. We delivered a strong, broad-based performance led by double-digit RevPAR growth, driving a 19% EBITDA growth and our highest ever PAT of ₹4,030 million." - Anuraag Bhatnagar, Whole-time Director and CEO.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) saw standalone Q4 profit decline 7% to ₹3,590.5 crore from ₹3,857.3 crore. Consolidated net profit fell 6.45% to ₹3,659 crore on mark-to-market impact.

Despite the profit dip, Maruti achieved record quarterly sales of 676,209 units. Operating profit surged 30.4% to ₹4,409.2 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹140 per share.

REC Ltd reported a 22% fall in consolidated net profit to ₹3,375.08 crore from ₹4,309.98 crore. Total income dropped to ₹14,583.39 crore from ₹15,348.37 crore in Q4 FY25.

Dalmia Bharat Ltd posted standalone net profit of ₹73 crore, down from ₹77 crore in Q4 FY25. The cement maker recommended a final dividend of ₹5 per share.

Several other firms delivered strong numbers. City Union Bank profit rose 25% to ₹360 crore. AU Small Finance Bank surged 65% to ₹832 crore. UltraTech Cement gained 20% to ₹2,983 crore.

The Sensex closed 416.72 points lower at 76,886.91 on April 28. The Nifty 50 settled at 23,995.70, down 97 points or 0.4%.
