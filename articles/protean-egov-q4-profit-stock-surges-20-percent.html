Protean eGov Q4 Profit Jumps 53% to ₹31 Cr; Stock Surges 20%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Protean eGov Q4 net profit jumps 53% YoY to ₹31 crore, with revenue up 38% to ₹308 crore for the March 2026 quarter
  • 02/FY26 revenue hits record ₹998 crore up 19%; EBITDA climbs 27% to ₹188 crore with margins expanding to 18%
  • 03/Stock surges 20% to ₹654 on May 21 but remains down 48% over the past year; board recommends ₹10 final dividend

Protean eGov Technologies Ltd shares surged nearly 20% to ₹654.20 on May 21, 2026. The rally followed the company's strongest-ever quarterly and annual financial results.

For Q4 FY26, net profit jumped 53% year-on-year to ₹31 crore. This compares with ₹20 crore reported in the same quarter last year.

Revenue for the quarter expanded 38% to ₹308 crore. Growth was powered by sustained demand across its digital governance solutions portfolio.

Full-year FY26 revenue reached a record ₹998 crore. This marks a 19% increase over ₹841 crore reported in FY25.

Full-year net profit rose 14% to ₹105 crore. EBITDA climbed 27% to ₹188 crore, with margins improving 125 basis points to 18%.

The board recommended a 100% final dividend of ₹10 per share. The face value of each share is ₹10.

The company holds over ₹850 crore in cash and marketable securities. It remains completely debt-free as of March 31, 2026.

Ajay Rajan was appointed Additional Director and will take over as MD and CEO from June 1, 2026.

Despite the single-day rally, the stock has fallen nearly 48% over the past year. The market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹2,227 crore.
