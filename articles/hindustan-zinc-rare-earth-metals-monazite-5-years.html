Hindustan Zinc to Add 3 Rare Earth Metals in 5 Years via Monazite Block

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindustan Zinc CEO Arun Mishra announced plans to add 3 rare earth metals within 5 years, tapping a pipeline of 10 mineral blocks currently under exploration
  • 02/The company secured a monazite-rich rare earth block in Uttar Pradesh and aims to begin geological exploration by July or August 2026 pending regulatory clearances
  • 03/Shares closed at ₹588.50 on April 27, 2026, up 28% over 12 months, with a market cap of approximately ₹2,50,265 crore

Hindustan Zinc Limited (NSE: HINDZINC) plans to diversify beyond zinc and lead by adding three rare earth metals to its portfolio within five years. Chief Executive Officer Arun Mishra confirmed the target on April 27, 2026, citing a pipeline of 10 mineral blocks currently under exploration.

The company has secured a rare earth mineral block containing monazite in Uttar Pradesh. It is awaiting regulatory clearances and licence agreements before commencing geological exploration.

"We are waiting to get the licence, sign an agreement and then we will do the geological exploration," Mishra told The Hindu BusinessLine. "At the moment, we have to ensure there are no forest cover, then we can start exploration immediately. So, we are targeting exploration sometime by July or August."

Monazite is rich in neodymium, a critical rare earth element used in permanent magnets for electric vehicles, wind turbines and electronics. India held an estimated 12.73 million tonnes of monazite as of March 2021, according to the Indian Mineral Yearbook.

The mining of rare earth minerals has become a key focus for India as China cracked down on exports to protect reserves for domestic use. China controls approximately 76% of global rare earth production and over 90% of refining capacity.

Hindustan Zinc has already diversified by acquiring potash and tungsten blocks. It created a subsidiary, Hindmetal Exploration Services, to explore gold, platinum, lithium and graphite. The three new metals could include copper, gold or any other metal, Mishra added.

The National Critical Minerals Mission, launched in April 2025, targets 1,200 exploration projects by fiscal 2031. The Union Budget 2026-27 further announced dedicated rare earth corridors across Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.

Hindustan Zinc shares closed at ₹588.50 on April 27, 2026. The stock has gained approximately 28% over the past 12 months and trades between a 52-week low of ₹398 and a high of ₹733. The company reported Q4 FY26 net sales of ₹13,544 crore, up 49% year-on-year.

Despite high silver price volatility, Mishra expects industrial demand to remain strong from photovoltaic and cell manufacturing sectors. He noted that no other metal has better conductivity than silver apart from gold.
