Dividend Alert: 12 Stocks Go Ex-Dividend May 29 With Rs 250+ Payouts

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/12 companies will turn ex-dividend on Friday May 29 with cumulative payouts exceeding Rs 250 per share
  • 02/Bajaj Auto leads with Rs 150 final dividend; GSK Pharma follows at Rs 57 per share
  • 03/May 27 is the last buying date due to T+1 settlement and Bakri Id holiday on May 28

Twelve companies are set to turn ex-dividend on Friday, May 29, 2026. The combined dividend payouts exceed Rs 250 per share across the pack.

Investors must buy shares by Wednesday, May 27 to be eligible. Markets remain closed on May 28 for Bakri Id, making Thursday unavailable for trading.

SEBI's T+1 settlement rule requires one full trading day before the record date. Shares bought on May 27 will credit to demat accounts by May 29.

Bajaj Auto Ltd (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO) leads with a final dividend of Rs 150 per share. The company has declared 20 dividends since June 2008 and carries a 2% dividend yield.

GSK Pharma has fixed May 29 as the record date for its Rs 57 final dividend. The stock offers a dividend yield of nearly 2%, according to Trendlyne data.

ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company will trade ex-dividend for Rs 7 per share. Eris Lifesciences, JB Chemicals, and Torrent Pharma will go ex for Rs 7.21, Rs 9.3, and Rs 9 respectively.

Bank of India (Rs 4.65), Caplin Point Laboratories (Rs 4), S Chand and Company (Rs 4), Uno Minda (Rs 1.75), BCPL Railway Infrastructure (Re 1), and Phoenix Overseas (Rs 0.6) complete the list.

Bajaj Auto shareholders will receive payment on or around July 24, 2026. The dividend requires approval at the upcoming Annual General Meeting.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH 12 COMPANIES ARE GOING EX-DIVIDEND ON MAY 29, 2026?
THE 12 COMPANIES ARE BAJAJ AUTO (RS 150), GSK PHARMA (RS 57), ERIS LIFESCIENCES (RS 7.21), JB CHEMICALS (RS 9.3), TORRENT PHARMA (RS 9), ICICI LOMBARD (RS 7), BANK OF INDIA (RS 4.65), CAPLIN POINT LABORATORIES (RS 4), S CHAND AND COMPANY (RS 4), UNO MINDA (RS 1.75), BCPL RAILWAY INFRASTRUCTURE (RE 1), AND PHOENIX OVERSEAS (RS 0.6).
WHAT IS THE LAST DATE TO BUY SHARES TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR THESE DIVIDENDS?
MAY 27, 2026 (WEDNESDAY) IS THE LAST DATE TO BUY SHARES. MARKETS ARE CLOSED ON MAY 28 FOR BAKRI ID, AND SEBI'S T+1 SETTLEMENT RULE REQUIRES SHARES TO BE CREDITED TO DEMAT ACCOUNTS BY THE RECORD DATE OF MAY 29.
HOW MUCH IS THE TOTAL DIVIDEND PAYOUT ACROSS ALL 12 COMPANIES?
THE CUMULATIVE DIVIDEND PAYOUT ACROSS ALL 12 COMPANIES EXCEEDS RS 250 PER SHARE IF AN INVESTOR HOLDS ONE SHARE OF EACH COMPANY.
WHAT IS BAJAJ AUTO'S DIVIDEND HISTORY AND WHEN WILL PAYMENT BE MADE?
BAJAJ AUTO HAS DECLARED 20 DIVIDENDS SINCE JUNE 2008 WITH A DIVIDEND YIELD OF 2%. THE RS 150 PER SHARE FINAL DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON OR AROUND JULY 24, 2026, AFTER SHAREHOLDER APPROVAL AT THE AGM.
ARE THERE ANY OTHER CORPORATE ACTIONS ON MAY 29 BESIDES DIVIDENDS?
YES. LIC WILL TURN EX-RECORD DATE FOR ITS FIRST-EVER 1:1 BONUS ISSUE. LE MERITE EXPORTS HAS ALSO SET MAY 29 AS THE RECORD DATE FOR A STOCK SPLIT.
