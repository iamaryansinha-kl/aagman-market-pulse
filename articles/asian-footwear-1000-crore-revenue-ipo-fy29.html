Asian Footwear Targets ₹1,000 Crore Revenue, IPO by FY29; Plans 150 EBOs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Asian Footwear plans to double exclusive brand outlets to 150 and targets ₹1,000 crore revenue in FY27 ahead of an IPO
  • 02/Revenue has grown from ₹230 crore in FY22 to approximately ₹700 crore in FY26, a threefold increase in four years
  • 03/Company targets IPO by FY28–29 at ₹3,500–₹4,000 crore valuation, but faces intense competition in a price-sensitive market

Asian Footwear plans to double its exclusive brand outlets to 150 and target ₹1,000 crore revenue in FY27. The New Delhi-based company is preparing for an IPO by FY28–29.

Revenue has scaled from ₹230 crore in FY22 to around ₹700 crore in FY26. Growth was driven by wider distribution, stronger brand recall, and tighter manufacturing control.

Aayush Jindal, 34, an IIT Delhi graduate and former Bain consultant, leads the company. He is betting on value-driven athleisure for India's $7.7 billion organised footwear market.

The company received ₹225–234 crore from Motilal Oswal in 2021. It operates a facility in Bahadurgarh and is investing ₹100 crore to add two manufacturing units, expanding capacity to 10 million pairs annually.

Asian has entered the premium category with its limited-edition sneaker MoJo Clay. It offers memory foam, EVA soles, and air-cushion technology in the ₹800–₹2,500 price band.

The company competes with Campus Activewear in athleisure and Relaxo's Sparx in the mass market. MS Dhoni serves as brand ambassador to build aspiration in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

"Dhoni brings credibility and aspiration to the brand," Jindal said. "He resonates strongly with the consumers we are targeting in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets."

The IPO target is ₹1,500–₹2,000 crore revenue over the next two years. Sustaining growth while maintaining margins in a competitive, price-sensitive segment will be the real test.
