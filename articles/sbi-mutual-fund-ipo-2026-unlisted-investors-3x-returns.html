SBI Mutual Fund IPO 2026: Unlisted Investors Already Sitting On 3.8x Returns Ahead Of July Listing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI Mutual Fund unlisted shares have returned nearly 3.8x over three years, rising from Rs 900 to an effective Rs 3,434 post-bonus adjustment
  • 02/India's largest asset manager is set to launch its Rs 10,000 crore IPO in the first week of July 2026 after receiving Sebi approval
  • 03/Parent State Bank of India and partner Amundi will pare holdings via OFS; current unlisted price may already factor in much of the optimism

SBI Mutual Fund unlisted shares have delivered nearly four-fold returns for early investors over the past three years. The stock climbed from around Rs 900 to nearly Rs 2,800 before a 3:1 bonus issue adjusted the base price.

Following the bonus adjustment, shares currently trade at around Rs 858 in the unlisted market. This translates into an effective return of approximately 3.8 times for investors who entered three years ago, according to UnlistedZone data.

The wealth creation comes ahead of the company's much-awaited initial public offering. The IPO is expected to hit the market in the first week of July 2026, making it one of the biggest listings in India's asset management industry.

The company has already received Sebi's approval for the public issue. This clears one of the final regulatory hurdles before the IPO opens for subscription.

The proposed IPO is expected to raise around Rs 10,000 crore. Parent State Bank of India and joint venture partner Amundi are likely to pare part of their holdings through an offer for sale.

SBI Mutual Fund is India's largest fund house by assets under management. It has been a key beneficiary of sustained SIP inflows, growing retail participation, and the expanding financialisation of household savings.

Market participants caution that the sharp run-up in unlisted shares may have priced in much of the optimism. The current valuation leaves limited room for further upside at the IPO price, they note.
