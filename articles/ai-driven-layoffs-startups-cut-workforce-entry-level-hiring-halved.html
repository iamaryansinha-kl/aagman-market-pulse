AI-Driven Layoffs: Startups Cut 10% Workforce, Entry-Level Hiring Halved

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Pocket FM and Apna Mart each cut ~10% of workforce as startups adopt AI-driven automation to build leaner teams
  • 02/Entry-level hiring share dropped from 28% in 2024 to 15% in 2025; Gen AI hiring grew 178% YoY
  • 03/BFSI automation reduced manual work by 30%; conversational AI now handles ~60% of Tier-1 customer queries

Pocket FM and Apna Mart have each cut approximately 10 per cent of their workforce. The layoffs reflect a broader shift as Indian startups embrace AI-driven automation to build leaner organisations.

Founders face mounting investor pressure to improve profitability. At the same time, they are adopting AI tools that automate routine tasks and reduce dependence on large teams.

Industry executives say this wave differs from earlier funding-winter corrections. Companies are now fundamentally redesigning workflows around AI-enabled efficiency rather than just cutting costs.

"The current trend looks like a short-term correction driven by productivity gains rather than a permanent reduction in jobs," said Kapil Joshi, CEO of IT Staffing at Quess Corp.

Entry-level hiring dropped sharply from 28 per cent in 2024 to 15 per cent in 2025. Startups reduced campus recruitment and prioritised lateral talent for immediate impact.

Automation has reduced manual work in BFSI by nearly 30 per cent. Conversational AI now handles close to 60 per cent of Tier-1 customer queries, according to staffing executives.

Hiring for generative AI roles grew 178 per cent year-on-year. Demand for AIOps and MLOps talent has tripled since 2023, per Quess Corp estimates.

"Companies will continue hiring, but the focus will move toward multi-skilled, AI-enabled talent," said Neeti Sharma, CEO of TeamLease Digital. The shift reshapes employment rather than eliminating it.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY ARE STARTUPS CUTTING WORKFORCE IN 2026?
STARTUPS ARE RESTRUCTURING WORKFLOWS AROUND AI-DRIVEN AUTOMATION TO IMPROVE PROFITABILITY AMID MOUNTING INVESTOR PRESSURE. POCKET FM AND APNA MART EACH CUT APPROXIMATELY 10 PER CENT OF THEIR WORKFORCE AS PART OF THIS SHIFT TOWARD LEANER, PRODUCTIVITY-FOCUSED OPERATIONS.
HOW MUCH HAS ENTRY-LEVEL HIRING DROPPED?
THE SHARE OF ENTRY-LEVEL HIRING IN STARTUPS DROPPED SHARPLY FROM 28 PER CENT IN 2024 TO 15 PER CENT IN 2025, ACCORDING TO QUESS CORP DATA. COMPANIES REDUCED CAMPUS RECRUITMENT AND PRIORITISED LATERAL TALENT CAPABLE OF DELIVERING IMMEDIATE BUSINESS IMPACT.
WHAT AREAS ARE MOST AFFECTED BY AI AUTOMATION?
THE SHARPEST DISRUPTION IS IN CUSTOMER SUPPORT, MANUAL TESTING, BASIC CODING, DATA PROCESSING, REPORTING AND BACK-OFFICE OPERATIONS. IN BFSI, AUTOMATION HAS REDUCED MANUAL WORK BY NEARLY 30 PER CENT, WHILE CONVERSATIONAL AI HANDLES CLOSE TO 60 PER CENT OF TIER-1 CUSTOMER QUERIES.
WHICH ROLES ARE STILL IN HIGH DEMAND?
DEMAND FOR GENERATIVE AI ROLES GREW 178 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR, WHILE AIOPS AND MLOPS TALENT DEMAND HAS TRIPLED SINCE 2023. EMERGING TECHNOLOGY ROLES ACCOUNTED FOR OVER 46 PER CENT OF INCREMENTAL JOB CREATION BETWEEN 2024 AND 2025.
IS THIS A PERMANENT JOB LOSS TREND?
INDUSTRY EXECUTIVES DESCRIBE IT AS A WORKFLOW REDESIGN RATHER THAN PERMANENT ELIMINATION. "THE NATURE OF JOBS IS CHANGING FASTER THAN THE VOLUME OF JOBS DISAPPEARING," SAID NEETI SHARMA, CEO OF TEAMLEASE DIGITAL. COMPANIES WILL CONTINUE HIRING BUT FOCUS ON MULTI-SKILLED, AI-ENABLED TALENT.
