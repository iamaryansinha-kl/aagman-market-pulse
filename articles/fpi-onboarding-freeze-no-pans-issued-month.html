FPI Onboarding Freeze: No PANs Issued for a Month, 20 Investors Stuck

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/No PANs have been issued to newly registered FPIs for nearly a month as of May 10, 2026, blocking at least 20 investors from opening trading accounts
  • 02/The freeze stems from Income Tax Department changes to PAN forms within the Common Application Form framework, causing field-level validation failures
  • 03/SEBI is in discussions with CBDT and has proposed allowing "authorised signatories" instead of "authorised representatives" to ease the bottleneck

Foreign portfolio investors have not received any Permanent Account Numbers for nearly a month. The freeze follows changes to PAN application forms within the Common Application Form framework.

At least 20 newly registered FPIs are awaiting PAN allotment despite completing all registration formalities. Sources familiar with the matter confirmed the backlog.

"Normally, once the application is initiated through the custodian or designated depository participant, PAN is issued within two to three days. But for the past month, no PANs have been issued," a source said.

Without a PAN, FPIs cannot open trading or demat accounts. This effectively prevents them from investing in Indian securities markets altogether.

The disruption is linked to new PAN forms introduced by the Income Tax Department. Field-level validation issues around character limits and formatting are holding up applications.

Some FPIs are struggling to appoint "authorised representatives" under the updated framework. Domestic individuals are unwilling to take the role due to fears of tax liability exposure.

SEBI has taken up the matter and is in discussions with the government and CBDT. The regulator has proposed allowing "authorised signatories" as an alternative to ease onboarding.

A similar hold-up is being faced by foreign direct investors at the Reserve Bank of India. The PAN issuance flow runs from the Income Tax Department to Protean, then via APIs to depositories.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY ARE NO PANS BEING ISSUED TO NEWLY REGISTERED FPIS?
PAN ISSUANCE HAS STOPPED DUE TO CHANGES INTRODUCED BY THE INCOME TAX DEPARTMENT IN THE PAN APPLICATION FORMS INTEGRATED WITHIN THE COMMON APPLICATION FORM FRAMEWORK. FIELD-LEVEL VALIDATION ISSUES AND FORMATTING MISMATCHES ARE CAUSING APPLICATIONS TO GET HELD UP IN THE SYSTEM.
HOW MANY FPIS ARE AFFECTED BY THE PAN ISSUANCE FREEZE?
AT LEAST 20 NEWLY REGISTERED FOREIGN PORTFOLIO INVESTORS ARE CURRENTLY AWAITING PAN ALLOTMENT DESPITE HAVING COMPLETED ALL REGISTRATION FORMALITIES WITH CUSTODIANS AND DESIGNATED DEPOSITORY PARTICIPANTS.
WHAT IS THE NORMAL TIMELINE FOR PAN ISSUANCE TO FPIS?
UNDER NORMAL CIRCUMSTANCES, PAN IS ISSUED WITHIN TWO TO THREE DAYS AFTER THE APPLICATION IS INITIATED THROUGH THE CUSTODIAN OR DESIGNATED DEPOSITORY PARTICIPANT. THE CURRENT DELAY HAS NOW EXTENDED TO NEARLY ONE MONTH.
WHY ARE FPIS STRUGGLING TO APPOINT AUTHORISED REPRESENTATIVES?
DOMESTIC INDIVIDUALS ARE UNWILLING TO ACT AS AUTHORISED REPRESENTATIVES DUE TO CONCERNS AROUND POTENTIAL TAX LIABILITY EXPOSURE. SEBI HAS PROPOSED ALLOWING "AUTHORISED SIGNATORIES" INSTEAD TO EASE THE ONBOARDING PROCESS.
WHAT IS THE PAN ISSUANCE PROCESS FOR FPIS?
THE PAN ISSUANCE PROCESS FLOWS FROM THE INCOME TAX DEPARTMENT TO PROTEAN, AND THEN THROUGH APIs TO THE DEPOSITORIES. ONCE THE PAN IS ISSUED, NEW REGISTRANTS CAN OPEN DEMAT ACCOUNTS WITH STOCK EXCHANGES TO TRADE OR INVEST IN INDIAN SECURITIES.
