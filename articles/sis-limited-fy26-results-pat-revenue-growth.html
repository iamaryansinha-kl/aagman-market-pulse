SIS Limited FY26 Results: PAT Jumps 28%, Q4 Revenue Surges 31% to ₹4,489 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SIS Limited reports 28% profit growth in FY26, its strongest financial year on record with robust operational performance
  • 02/Q4 FY26 revenue surged 31% YoY to ₹4,489 crore from ₹3,428 crore; operating PAT jumped 27.9% to ₹105.5 crore
  • 03/Company returned ₹250 crore to shareholders via dividends and buybacks; all three business segments posted double-digit growth

SIS Limited, a security and facility management conglomerate, announced its Q4 and full-year FY26 results on April 30, 2026. The company delivered its strongest financial year on record.

Consolidated revenue from operations for Q4 FY26 stood at ₹4,489 crore. This marks a 31% year-on-year increase from ₹3,428 crore in Q4 FY25.

On a sequential basis, revenue grew 7.3% over Q3 FY26's ₹4,185 crore. The growth was broad-based across all three business segments.

Operating profit after tax for Q4 FY26 came in at ₹105.5 crore. This represents a 27.9% jump over ₹82.5 crore in the year-ago quarter.

The Q4 PAT figure includes a one-off adjustment of approximately ₹3 crore. This relates to costs from the APS acquisition.

Operating PAT margin held steady at 2.4% despite inflationary pressures. EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹207 crore, up 25.6% year-on-year.

However, EBITDA margin slipped marginally to 4.6% from 4.8% in Q4 FY25. The margin compression reflects continued investments in growth initiatives.

All three business segments recorded strong growth during the quarter. Security Solutions India revenue rose 34.2% YoY to ₹1,925 crore.

Security Solutions International grew 36.9% YoY to ₹1,950 crore. This was driven by e-commerce and government sector contracts.

The Singapore subsidiary Henderson turned operationally profitable during the quarter. This marks a significant milestone for the international business.

Facility Management Solutions revenue grew 8.1% to ₹635 crore. It posted its highest-ever quarterly EBITDA of ₹35 crore with margins expanding to 5.5% from 4.7% a year earlier.

The company's balance sheet improved significantly during the quarter. Net Debt to EBITDA fell to 0.99x from 1.25x in December 2025.

Cash conversion remained strong with OCF to EBITDA at 203.3% for the quarter. Debtor days stood at 63, the lowest since June 2023.

During FY26, SIS returned approximately ₹250 crore to shareholders. This was through a combination of dividends and buybacks.

The company employs over 300,000 people across India and Australia. It operates across security, facility management, and cash logistics segments.
