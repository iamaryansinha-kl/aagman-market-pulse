Kacholia, Kotak Life Buy Rs 120 Crore Fusion CX Stake Pre-IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ace investors Ashish Kacholia, Mukul Agrawal-backed Sanshi Fund and Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance bought a Rs 120 crore stake in Fusion CX days before its IPO.
  • 02/The 3.19% stake was picked up from promoters PNS Business and Rasish Consultants at Rs 289 apiece, the top of the IPO price band of Rs 275-289.
  • 03/The Rs 702 crore IPO opens on October 14 and closes on October 16; shares are scheduled to list on the BSE and NSE on October 22.

Ace investors Ashish Kacholia and Mukul Agrawal, along with Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance and others, have together invested Rs 120 crore in Fusion CX ahead of its initial public offering. The bets were placed through secondary transactions earlier this month.

Fusion CX is a Kolkata-headquartered provider of AI data infrastructure services. The category covers data-heavy backend work such as data annotation and AI training support services. The company is set to launch its Rs 702 crore IPO this week.

The stake purchases came from promoter selling shareholders PNS Business and Rasish Consultants. They transferred more than 41.52 lakh equity shares at Rs 289 apiece, totalling Rs 120 crore for a 3.19% stake in the company.

Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance acquired 17.30 lakh shares worth Rs 50 crore. Ashish Kacholia purchased 12.1 lakh shares worth Rs 35 crore in his personal capacity as well as through his firm Bengal Finance and Investment.

Mukul Agrawal-backed Sanshi Fund picked up 8.65 lakh shares worth Rs 25 crore. The entry price of Rs 289 matches the top of the IPO price band, signalling these investors see value at levels above the issue floor.

The IPO opens on October 14 and closes on October 16. It comprises a fresh issue of 1.73 crore shares worth Rs 500 crore and an offer for sale (OFS) of 69.90 lakh shares aggregating Rs 202 crore.

Share allotments are expected to be finalised on October 19. The company's shares are scheduled to list on the BSE and NSE on October 22. The IPO has a lot size of 51 shares, requiring a minimum investment of Rs 14,739 for retail investors applying at the upper end of the price band.

Of the proceeds, Rs 275 crore will be used to repay or prepay, in full or in part, certain outstanding borrowings of the company and its subsidiaries, including select step-down subsidiaries.

Additionally, Rs 61 crore will be invested in its step-down subsidiaries, Omind Technologies Inc. and Omind Technologies Private Limited, to upgrade IT tools, including Arya and MindVoice. The remaining proceeds will fund inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and general corporate purposes.

Fusion CX is among Rs 1,572 crore worth of IPOs opening this week. HD Fire Protect will open its Rs 712 crore IPO on October 13 and close on October 15; the issue is entirely an offer for sale of 2.63 crore shares at Rs 258-271, with listing expected on October 21.

The third issue, Sahajanand Medical Technologies, opens on October 15 and closes on October 19. It is entirely an OFS of 2.76 crore shares; the price band and final issue size are yet to be announced, with allotment expected on October 21 and listing scheduled for October 23.

The structure of the deal matters as much as its size. Because these were secondary purchases, the Rs 120 crore went to the selling promoters — PNS Business and Rasish Consultants — rather than into Fusion CX's own coffers. The buyers are effectively paying the upper end of the IPO price band, Rs 289 per share, for access before the listing. For retail investors watching from the sidelines, that is the signal: seasoned operators chose to pay full band value rather than wait three days and try their luck in the allotment lottery.

The IPO itself is a Rs 702 crore issue split between a Rs 500 crore fresh issue and a Rs 202 crore offer for sale. In a fresh issue, new shares are created and the company receives the money; in an OFS, existing shareholders sell their holdings and the company receives nothing. Fusion CX's mix leans toward growth capital — Rs 275 crore is earmarked for repaying or prepaying borrowings, and Rs 61 crore for upgrading IT tools at its Omind Technologies subsidiaries, including platforms named Arya and MindVoice. The remainder is reserved for acquisitions it has not yet identified, plus general corporate purposes.

For retail applicants, the arithmetic is simple: at a lot size of 51 shares and a maximum price of Rs 289, applying at the upper band costs Rs 14,739 per lot. Allotments are expected to be finalised on October 19, with listing on both the BSE and NSE on October 22.

Fusion CX arrives in a busy week for primary markets. Three mainboard IPOs together aim to raise about Rs 1,572 crore. HD Fire Protect opens on October 13 with a Rs 712 crore, entirely secondary sale priced between Rs 258 and Rs 271, implying a market capitalisation near Rs 4,749 crore at the top of the band, with listing expected on October 21. Sahajanand Medical Technologies follows on October 15, an entirely secondary issue whose price band is yet to be announced, with listing slated for October 23.

GLOSSARY: Secondary transaction: The sale of existing shares between investors, in which money changes hands between seller and buyer rather than flowing to the company. Offer for sale (OFS): The portion of an IPO in which existing shareholders sell their stakes; the company receives no proceeds. Fresh issue: Newly created shares sold in an IPO, with proceeds going to the company for its stated purposes. Price band: The lower and upper limits within which investors bid in a book-built IPO. Lot size: The minimum number of shares a retail investor must apply for in an IPO.
