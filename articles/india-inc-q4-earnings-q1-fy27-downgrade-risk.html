India Inc Q4 Earnings Strong But Q1 FY27 Downgrade Risk Looms

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India Inc delivered reassuring Q4 earnings but Q1 FY27 downgrade risk is rising sharply on crude and rupee pressure
  • 02/JP Morgan warns Nifty could fall to 20,500 in bear case; only 24% of companies have seen earnings cuts above 5%
  • 03/Strait of Hormuz normalization could take 3-4 months; aviation, auto, paints most at risk in Q1

India Inc delivered a broadly reassuring Q4 earnings season, reaffirming domestic economic strength. Markets now brace for Q1 FY27 as results trickle in.

A wave of earnings downgrades for Q1 FY27 now looks increasingly likely. Elevated crude prices, a weaker rupee, and rising inflation are pressuring corporate profits.

"Management commentary across most companies clearly flags macro, logistics, and pricing risks," JP Morgan's Rajiv Batra warned. He sees Q1 earnings facing pressure from higher input costs and currency depreciation.

JP Morgan's bear case Nifty target is 20,500, with base at 27,000 and bull at 30,000. MSCI India earnings growth is forecast at 11% for CY26 and 13% for CY27.

Goldman Sachs flagged that earnings revisions guide foreign flows in Indian equities. Low visibility around recovery will likely impede foreign re-buying near term.

Nifty EPS stands at Rs 1,231, down just 1.4% since March 31. Only 24% of companies have seen earnings cuts above 5%, and that share is declining.

Morgan Stanley argues earnings growth is turning after a six-quarter slowdown. It expects acceleration driven by RBI rate cuts, bank deregulation, and strong capex.

Geojit's Antu Eapen Thomas names aviation, auto, paints, fertilisers, and oil marketing as most vulnerable. Hormuz normalization could take three to four months.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE Q1 FY27 EARNINGS DOWNGRADE CONCERN FOR INDIA INC?
ELEVATED CRUDE PRICES DRIVEN BY THE IRAN CONFLICT, A WEAKER RUPEE, AND HIGHER-THAN-ANTICIPATED WHOLESALE PRICE INFLATION ARE PUSHING UP BORROWING AND IMPORT COSTS. THIS IS EXPECTED TO PRESSURE CORPORATE PROFITS IN Q1 FY27 AND POTENTIALLY Q2 IF THE OIL SHOCK PERSISTS.
WHAT ARE JP MORGAN'S NIFTY TARGETS FOR FY27?
JP MORGAN'S BEAR/BASE/BULL CASE NIFTY-50 TARGETS STAND AT 20,500, 27,000, AND 30,000 RESPECTIVELY. THE BANK FORECASTS MSCI INDIA EARNINGS GROWTH AT 11% FOR CY26 AND 13% FOR CY27.
HOW RESILIENT HAVE EARNINGS FORECASTS BEEN SO FAR?
NIFTY EPS IS STEADY AT RS 1,231, DOWN JUST 1.4% SINCE MARCH 31. ONLY 24% OF COMPANIES IN THE CONSENSUS UNIVERSE HAVE SEEN EARNINGS CUTS OF MORE THAN 5%, AND THAT SHARE IS DECLINING.
WHICH SECTORS ARE MOST AT RISK OF EARNINGS DOWNGRADES IN Q1?
AVIATION, CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY, AUTO, PAINTS, FERTILISERS, AND OIL MARKETING COMPANIES ARE MOST PRONE TO DOWNGRADES. ELEVATED CRUDE PRICES AND A WEAKER RUPEE DRIVE UP IMPORT COSTS, FUEL EXPENSES, AND RAW MATERIAL BILLS.
HOW LONG COULD THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ DISRUPTION IMPACT EARNINGS?
JP MORGAN ANALYSIS SUGGESTS THAT EVEN AFTER THE CONFLICT ENDS AND THE STRAIT REOPENS, NORMALIZATION OF ENERGY FLOWS COULD TAKE THREE TO FOUR MONTHS DUE TO VESSEL BACKLOGS AND ALTERNATIVE ROUTE REQUIREMENTS.
