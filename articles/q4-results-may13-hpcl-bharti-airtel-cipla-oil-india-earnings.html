Q4 Results May 13: HPCL Posts FY26 Net Profit; Bharti Airtel, Cipla, Oil India Lead Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindustan Petroleum Corporation posts net profit for FY26, reversing previous year losses as oil prices remain elevated
  • 02/Bharti Airtel, Cipla, Oil India, TVS Motor, DLF, Tata Motors and NLC India among major companies announcing Q4 FY26 results on May 13
  • 03/Cipla reported Q4 profit below expectations on weak US sales, with net profit crashing 55% to ₹555 crore

Dozens of companies announced their fourth quarter and full year financial results on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

Hindustan Petroleum Corporation posted a net profit for FY26, reversing losses from the previous fiscal year.

The strong performance came as elevated crude oil prices and refining margins supported earnings.

Bharti Airtel reported its quarterly results amid steady growth in its India mobile business.

The telecom major continued to see subscriber additions and average revenue per user improvements.

Cipla, however, missed profit estimates as weak sales in the United States dragged down performance.

The pharmaceutical major's net profit crashed 55 per cent to ₹555 crore in Q4 FY26.

Oil India announced its earnings as crude oil prices remained at elevated levels.

The state-run oil and gas explorer benefited from higher realisations on crude oil sales.

TVS Motor announced its quarterly results amid strong demand for two-wheelers.

The company has been gaining market share in both domestic and export markets.

DLF reported its earnings as the real estate sector showed signs of recovery.

Tata Motors announced results amid continued growth in its commercial vehicle and passenger vehicle segments.

NLC India reported earnings as the power sector navigated higher coal and fuel costs.
