GIFT Nifty Crashes 150+ Points: Global AI Sell-Off Rattles Indian Markets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/GIFT Nifty tumbled over 150 points on June 26, signaling a weak opening for Indian markets amid a global AI-led sell-off
  • 02/Japan's Nikkei 225 crashed 4.5%, South Korea's Kospi plunged 6.8%, and Taiwan's Taiex fell 3.6% as semiconductor stocks bled
  • 03/Nifty faces immediate support at 23,800 with upside target of 24,500; analysts warn of continued volatility in tech-heavy markets

GIFT Nifty dropped more than 150 points on Friday, June 26, 2026. It signals a weak opening for Indian equities after a global sell-off in AI and semiconductor stocks.

The panic spread across Asia. Japan's Nikkei 225 crashed 4.5%. South Korea's Kospi plunged as much as 6.8%.

Samsung Electronics, the world's largest memory chipmaker, plummeted 7%. South Korea's SK Hynix tumbled 6.6%. Both firms supply critical components to the global AI industry.

Japan's SoftBank Group, a major investor in AI startups, collapsed over 13%. Semiconductor equipment maker Advantest fell nearly 11%. Taiwan's Taiex shed 3.6%.

Hong Kong's Hang Seng declined 1.7%. China's Shanghai Composite lost 1.4%. Australia was the only major market to edge higher, bucking the regional trend.

The sell-off was driven by heavy profit booking after a months-long rally in AI-linked stocks. Weak tech sentiment and mixed Wall Street cues added pressure.

Overnight, Wall Street ended mixed. Chipmakers Qualcomm and Micron Technology reported better-than-expected earnings, but technology stocks still came under pressure. Apple shares declined sharply after announcing price increases across several products.

For Indian markets, the Nifty closed at 24,056 on Thursday, down 34 points. Analysts are watching key technical levels for direction.

"The Nifty remained volatile during the session amid the BSE F&O expiry as the index failed to break out above the falling trendline on the daily timeframe. However, the overall trend continues to remain positive, with the index sustaining above the 50-day exponential moving average. The RSI remains in a positive crossover, indicating strengthening momentum. The trend is likely to stay positive as long as the index holds above 23,800, while 24,500 remains the immediate upside target," said Rupak De, Senior Technical Analyst at LKP Securities.

Investors should brace for continued volatility. The sharp decline in richly valued technology stocks suggests caution ahead of the domestic market open.
