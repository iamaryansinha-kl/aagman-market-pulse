Sebi Rejects Anil Ambani's Settlement Bid Over Reliance Infrastructure

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI rejects Anil Ambani's settlement application over allegations of ₹65.26 billion fund misuse at Reliance Infrastructure Ltd
  • 02/This marks the second settlement rejection in 12 months; SEBI previously rejected his Yes Bank plea worth ₹1,828 crore in August 2025
  • 03/SEBI cited parallel investigations by ED and other agencies; Reliance Infrastructure is seeking to raise ₹30 billion from public markets

SEBI has rejected Anil Ambani's settlement request over allegations of fund misuse at Reliance Infrastructure Ltd. The regulator cited parallel investigations by other enforcement agencies.

The case involves ₹65.26 billion ($691 million) that SEBI alleges was improperly routed to entities linked to Ambani. SEBI said the transactions could be for "personal enrichment" rather than corporate purpose.

SEBI's investigation revealed a far larger diversion of ₹176.7 billion ($1.9 billion) to engineering contractor CLE Private Ltd. CLE then invested at least ₹112 billion in firms linked to the Ambani-led Reliance ADA Group over a decade through 2024.

SEBI determined that CLE "for all practical purposes" functioned as a Reliance ADA Group company. The regulator said it was "indirectly controlled" by Ambani and a few officials.

This is the second settlement rejection for Ambani in 12 months. SEBI rejected his Yes Bank plea in August 2025, which exposed him to a potential ₹1,828 crore penalty.

An Ambani group spokesperson said: "The allegations are categorically denied. The matters are sub judice, and the Group will continue to defend its position as legally advised." (NSE: RELINFRA)

Reliance Infrastructure shares settled at ₹81.76 on June 8, 2026, up 5%. The stock has fallen approximately 69% over the past year amid ongoing regulatory scrutiny and multiple executive arrests.

Under SEBI's settlement process, companies can pay a penalty without admitting wrongdoing. With the rejection, SEBI will now issue a detailed public order outlining alleged violations and penalties.

WHAT DID SEBI ALLEGE AGAINST ANIL AMBANI AND RELIANCE INFRASTRUCTURE?
SEBI ALLEGED THAT ANIL AMBANI AND RELIANCE INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROPERLY ROUTED ₹65.26 BILLION TO ENTITIES LINKED TO THE CONTROLLING SHAREHOLDER. THE REGULATOR SAID THE TRANSACTIONS COULD BE FOR "PERSONAL ENRICHMENT" RATHER THAN SERVING A CORPORATE PURPOSE FOR PUBLIC SHAREHOLDERS.
HOW MUCH MONEY DID SEBI SAY WAS DIVERTED TO CLE PRIVATE LTD?
SEBI ALLEGED THAT RELIANCE INFRASTRUCTURE DIVERTED ₹176.7 BILLION ($1.9 BILLION) TO CLE PRIVATE LTD OVER A DECADE THROUGH 2024. CLE THEN INVESTED AT LEAST ₹112 BILLION IN FIRMS LINKED TO THE AMBANI-LED RELIANCE ADA GROUP.
WHY DID SEBI REJECT THE SETTLEMENT APPLICATION?
SEBI CITED PARALLEL INVESTIGATIONS BY OTHER INDIAN ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES, INCLUDING THE ENFORCEMENT DIRECTORATE AND FINANCIAL CRIME INVESTIGATIVE AGENCIES. THE REGULATOR SAID THE MATTERS INVOLVED ALLEGED "MIS-UTILISATION OF COMPANY FUNDS."
IS THIS THE FIRST TIME SEBI HAS REJECTED ANIL AMBANI'S SETTLEMENT PLEA?
NO. THIS IS THE SECOND SETTLEMENT REJECTION FOR AMBANI IN 12 MONTHS. SEBI PREVIOUSLY REJECTED HIS PLEA IN THE YES BANK CASE IN AUGUST 2025, WHICH EXPOSED HIM TO A POTENTIAL PENALTY OF ₹1,828 CRORE.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT AFTER SEBI REJECTS A SETTLEMENT BID?
SEBI WILL TYPICALLY ISSUE A DETAILED PUBLIC ORDER OUTLINING THE ALLEGED VIOLATIONS. CONSEQUENCES CAN RANGE FROM MONETARY PENALTIES TO RESTRICTIONS ON ACCESSING CAPITAL MARKETS. COMPANIES AND ENTITIES MAY APPEAL AGAINST SUCH ORDERS IN COURT.
