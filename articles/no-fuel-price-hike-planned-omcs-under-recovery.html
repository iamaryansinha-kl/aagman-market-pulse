No Fuel Price Hike Planned as OMCs Bleed ₹2,400 Crore Daily: Govt

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Government rules out proposal to hike retail fuel prices despite OMCs facing ₹2,400 crore daily under-recoveries
  • 02/PSU oil firms are losing ₹20 per litre on petrol and ₹100 per litre on diesel at current crude prices
  • 03/India's crude import bill has risen by $190-210 million per day despite 13-15% decline in import volumes

The government has ruled out any proposal to raise retail petrol and diesel prices. Sujata Sharma, Joint Secretary at the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, stated there is "no such proposal under consideration."

PSU oil marketing companies are currently bearing under-recoveries of around ₹20 per litre on petrol. The under-recovery on diesel stands at approximately ₹100 per litre.

India's combined daily under-recovery stood at around ₹2,400 crore as of March 27, 2026. On April 2, under-recoveries were ₹24.40 per litre on petrol and ₹104.99 on diesel.

According to Kotak Institutional Equities, the Indian crude basket rose by $47 per barrel in March 2026. It further increased by $53 in the first half of April 2026.

Despite a 13-15 per cent decline in import volumes, India's crude import bill has increased by $190-210 million per day. The FY26 average crude price was $70 per barrel, but April 2026 has averaged above $113.

India imports over 88 per cent of its crude oil requirement. An increase of $1 per barrel leads to roughly ₹16,000 crore in additional annual import costs. FY25 import bill was approximately $137 billion.

The government reduced excise duty on petrol and diesel by ₹10 per litre. Petrol excise duty was cut to ₹3 per litre, while diesel duty was reduced to zero from ₹10 per litre.

Private refiners Nayara Energy and Shell have already raised fuel prices. The government also imposed a windfall tax on diesel and jet fuel exports to prioritise domestic supply.

NO. SUJATA SHARMA, JOINT SECRETARY AT THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS, HAS CONFIRMED THAT THERE IS "NO SUCH PROPOSAL UNDER CONSIDERATION" TO RAISE RETAIL FUEL PRICES.

PSU OMCs ARE CURRENTLY FACING UNDER-RECOVERIES OF AROUND ₹20 PER LITRE ON PETROL AND APPROXIMATELY ₹100 PER LITRE ON DIESEL.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL DAILY UNDER-RECOVERY FACED BY OIL MARKETING COMPANIES?
INDIA'S COMBINED DAILY UNDER-RECOVERY STOOD AT AROUND ₹2,400 CRORE AS OF MARCH 27, 2026.
HOW MUCH HAS THE CRUDE IMPORT BILL INCREASED?
DESPITE A 13-15 PER CENT DECLINE IN IMPORT VOLUMES, INDIA'S CRUDE IMPORT BILL HAS INCREASED BY $190-210 MILLION PER DAY, ACCORDING TO KOTAK INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES.

THE GOVERNMENT REDUCED EXCISE DUTY ON PETROL AND DIESEL BY ₹10 PER LITRE, CUTTING PETROL DUTY TO ₹3 AND DIESEL DUTY TO ZERO. IT ALSO IMPOSED A WINDFALL TAX ON DIESEL AND JET FUEL EXPORTS TO PRIORITISE DOMESTIC SUPPLY.
