Q4 Results May 4: BHEL Profit Soars 154%, Godrej PAT Up 70%, Ather Loss Narrows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BHEL standalone net profit surges 154% YoY to ₹1,282.68 crore in Q4 FY26, FY26 PAT jumps to ₹1,577.95 crore
  • 02/Godrej Properties Q4 consolidated PAT rises 70% to ₹645.44 crore; FY26 highest-ever profit at ₹1,840.66 crore
  • 03/Ather Energy narrows Q4 net loss to ₹100.23 crore from ₹234.36 crore; FY26 loss cut to ₹517 crore

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (NSE: BHEL) reported standalone net profit of ₹1,282.68 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a sharp 154% jump from ₹504.05 crore in the same quarter last year.

For the full fiscal year FY26, BHEL's PAT surged to ₹1,577.95 crore. This is more than triple the ₹512.97 crore posted in FY25.

The BHEL board has recommended a final dividend of ₹1.40 per share. Shares hit the upper circuit at ₹387.65, gaining 10% on the NSE.

Godrej Properties Ltd delivered its highest-ever quarterly net profit of ₹645.44 crore in Q4 FY26. This represents a 70% increase over ₹378.44 crore in Q4 FY25.

For FY26, Godrej Properties posted record annual net profit of ₹1,840.66 crore. This is up 32% from ₹1,389.23 crore in FY25.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹10 per share, or 200% on face value. FY26 booking value grew 16% YoY to ₹34,171 crore.

"FY26 was a record year across bookings, collections, cashflows and earnings," said Pirojsha Godrej, Executive Chairperson. He flagged global security-related disruptions as a risk to monitor.

Godrej Properties targets residential bookings exceeding ₹39,000 crore in FY27. The company added business development worth over ₹42,000 crore in FY26.

Ather Energy posted Q4 FY26 net loss of ₹100.23 crore. This is less than half the ₹234.36 crore loss reported in Q4 FY25.

For the full year, Ather's loss narrowed to ₹517.17 crore from ₹812.28 crore in FY25. The company ended FY26 with 700 Experience Centres, up from 351 in FY25.

Exide Industries reported standalone net profit of ₹213.44 crore in Q4 FY26. FY26 PAT stood at ₹1,111.33 crore against ₹1,076.93 crore in FY25. The board recommended a dividend of ₹2 per share.

Jindal Steel reported consolidated net profit of ₹1,041 crore in Q4 FY26. Revenue rose 23% YoY to ₹16,217.93 crore. The board declared a dividend of ₹2 per share.

Companies yet to announce Q4 results today include Ambuja Cements, Aditya Birla Capital, Sobha Ltd, Tata Technologies, and KEI Industries.
