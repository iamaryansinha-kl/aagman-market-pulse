Sensex Closes 0.46% Higher at 77,269; Nifty Gains 121 Points on May 4

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex closed at 77,269.40, gaining 355.90 points or 0.46% on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Nifty 50 ended at 24,119.30, up 121.75 points or 0.51%, with intraday high at 24,290
  • 03/Watch support at 24,000 and resistance at 24,300-24,400 for the next trading session

Indian equity markets closed higher on May 4, 2026, with the BSE Sensex settling at 77,269.40, up 355.90 points or 0.46%. The NSE Nifty 50 ended at 24,119.30, gaining 121.75 points or 0.51%.

Benchmark indices opened gap-up following positive election trends, with the BJP leading in Assam and West Bengal. However, profit booking at higher levels trimmed some of the early gains during the session.

The Sensex touched an intraday high of 77,910.75, while the Nifty reached 24,290 before retreating. Both indices managed to close in positive territory despite the late-session pullback.

The Nifty Realty index was the top sectoral performer, surging 2.45%. Metal, Infrastructure, and Pharma sectors also posted gains during the trading session.

The IT sector was the worst performer, declining 1%, while Media and PSU Bank indices also ended in the red. Bharti Airtel and Kotak Bank each dropped 2.5%, leading the Nifty losers list.

Adani Ports emerged as the top Nifty gainer, rising 2%, followed by Adani Enterprises, Eicher Motors, Reliance Industries, and Jio Financial Services. These heavyweights supported the benchmark indices.

The broader market also participated in the rally, with the Nifty Midcap and Smallcap indices each rising approximately 0.5%. More than 180 BSE stocks, including BHEL, Bajaj Auto, and Lloyds Metals, hit fresh 52-week highs.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, hovered around 18, indicating moderate investor sentiment. Analysts maintain key support levels at 24,000 for the Nifty and 77,000 for the Sensex, with resistance seen at 24,300-24,400 and 77,700-78,000 respectively.
