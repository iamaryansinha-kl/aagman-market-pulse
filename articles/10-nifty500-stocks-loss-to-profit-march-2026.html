10 Nifty500 Stocks Turn From Loss to Profit in March 2026 Quarter

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ten Nifty500 companies swung from net losses in March 2025 to net profits in March 2026, according to StockEdge profitability scan data
  • 02/Vodafone Idea posted the largest turnaround with Rs 51,970 crore profit versus a Rs 7,167 crore loss in the year-ago quarter
  • 03/The list spans telecom, steel, pharma, NBFC, banking, and real estate sectors, indicating broad-based earnings recovery

Ten Nifty500 companies have swung from net losses in the March 2025 quarter to net profits in the March 2026 quarter. The turnaround list spans telecom, steel, pharmaceuticals, NBFCs, banking, and real estate.

Vodafone Idea Ltd (NSE: IDEA) reported the sharpest turnaround. It posted a net profit of Rs 51,970 crore in Q4 FY26, compared to a net loss of Rs 7,167 crore in the same quarter last year.

IndusInd Bank Ltd (NSE: INDUSINDBK) turned a Rs 2,328.87 crore loss into a Rs 594.17 crore profit. The private lender had faced significant asset quality and provisioning pressures in the prior year.

Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd (NSE: TTML) swung to a Rs 580.93 crore profit from a Rs 306.42 crore loss. The telecom infrastructure player benefited from improved operational leverage.

Jindal Steel & Power Ltd (NSE: JINDALSTEL) reported a net profit of Rs 1,044.75 crore, reversing a Rs 339.4 crore loss in the year-ago period. Steel demand recovery and cost controls drove the swing.

Manappuram Finance Ltd (NSE: MANAPPURAM) turned a Rs 191.16 crore loss into a Rs 403.96 crore profit. The gold loan NBFC saw strong disbursement growth and stabilised asset quality.

Wockhardt Ltd (NSE: WOCKPHARMA) posted a Rs 166 crore profit versus a Rs 25 crore loss. The pharma company benefited from improved US generic filings and domestic formulation sales.

HFCL Ltd (NSE: HFCL) swung to a Rs 178.5 crore profit from a Rs 81.44 crore loss. The telecom gear maker gained from 5G rollout orders and export momentum.

One97 Communications Ltd (NSE: PAYTM) reported a Rs 184 crore profit, compared to a Rs 540 crore loss. The fintech firm cut costs and stabilised payments revenue after regulatory headwinds.

PVR Inox Ltd (NSE: PVRINOX) turned a Rs 125 crore loss into a Rs 186.7 crore profit. Box office recovery and higher ticket prices lifted the multiplex operator.

Aditya Birla Real Estate Ltd reported a Rs 10.84 crore profit, reversing a Rs 131.01 crore loss. The real estate developer saw improved project deliveries and sales velocity.
