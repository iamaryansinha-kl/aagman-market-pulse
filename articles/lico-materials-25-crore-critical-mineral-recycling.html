LICO Materials Gets ₹25 Crore Under Critical Mineral Recycling Scheme

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LICO Materials Private Limited will receive a ₹25 crore grant from the Ministry of Mines under the National Critical Mineral Mission
  • 02/The company is among 58 selected nationwide from hundreds of applicants for the ₹1,500-crore recycling incentive scheme
  • 03/LICO has committed a total investment of ₹240 crore and qualifies for a 20% CapEx subsidy through FY 2030-31

LICO Materials Private Limited has secured a ₹25 crore grant from the Ministry of Mines. The award comes under the Incentive Scheme for Promotion of Critical Mineral Recycling, a core pillar of the National Critical Mineral Mission.

The eligibility letter was issued by JNARDDC, the government's designated Project Management Agency. LICO is one of 58 companies selected nationwide to build India's domestic urban mining capability.

Against a total committed investment of ₹240 crore, LICO qualifies for a 20 per cent capital expenditure subsidy. The company also receives a multi-year operational expenditure subsidy linked to incremental commercial sales through FY 2030-31.

The ₹1,500-crore scheme aims to develop domestic recycling capacity for critical minerals from lithium-ion batteries, e-waste, and industrial scrap. The goal is to reduce India's import dependence and support the clean energy and advanced manufacturing sectors.

LICO was selected from among hundreds of applicants. The selection reflects the company's technical expertise in battery chemistry and hydrometallurgy, the company said in a statement.

"This recognition by the mines ministry and NCMM is the government's validation that what we are building in Karnataka is what India needs," Gaurav Dolwani, CEO of LICO Materials, said.

India's entire battery-critical mineral supply chain currently runs through East Asia. Domestic recycling-led production is an industrial security imperative as geopolitical tensions threaten global mineral supply chains.
