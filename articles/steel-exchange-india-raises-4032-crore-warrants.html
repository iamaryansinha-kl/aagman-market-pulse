Steel Exchange India Raises ₹40.32 Crore via Warrants; Stock Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Steel Exchange India raises ₹40.32 crore via fresh warrant allotment and conversion of existing warrants on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Funds part of a ₹350 crore board-approved raise; earlier ₹75 crore came from IMR Group in April 2026
  • 03/Stock trades at ₹10.01, up 32.58% in past month against the Nifty 50's 6.55%

Steel Exchange India Limited has raised ₹40.32 crore through fresh warrant allotment and conversion of existing warrants. The Visakhapatnam-based steel maker announced the capital strengthening on May 4, 2026.

The company allotted 4,40,00,000 fresh convertible equity warrants at ₹9.45 per warrant on a preferential basis. It received ₹10.40 crore, representing 25 per cent of the total subscription amount.

Simultaneously, 2,82,97,870 existing warrants issued earlier at ₹14.10 per warrant were converted into equity shares. The company received the balance subscription money of ₹29.92 crore on this conversion.

The board had on March 4, 2026 approved raising up to ₹350 crore through convertible warrants. This ₹40.32 crore tranche follows an earlier ₹75 crore infusion from IMR Group entities in April 2026.

The company intends to use the proceeds for operational expansion, working capital efficiency, and strategic growth. It manufactures TMT rebars under the SIMHADRI TMT brand from its integrated plant in Vizianagaram district.

The stock closed at ₹10.01 on the NSE on May 4, up 0.30 per cent from the previous close of ₹9.98. Total traded volume stood at 5.51 lakh shares valued at ₹0.55 crore.

The stock has returned 32.58 per cent over the past month, outperforming the Nifty 50 which gained 6.55 per cent. Market capitalisation stood at ₹1,248.47 crore as of mid-day trade.

The stock remains 13 per cent below its 52-week high of ₹11.55 and is down 38.02 per cent over three years. The 52-week low stands at ₹7.00.
