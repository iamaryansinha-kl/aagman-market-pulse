Tesla Begins Cybercab Robotaxi Production, Musk Says

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Elon Musk announced Tesla has begun producing its Cybercab Robotaxi vehicle.
  • 02/The two-door, two-seat sedan was unveiled two years ago without a steering wheel or pedals.
  • 03/Mass production will require specific exemptions from US regulators.

Tesla has begun production of its Cybercab Robotaxi. Elon Musk confirmed the development on April 24, 2026.

The Cybercab is a two-door, two-seat sedan. It was first unveiled two years ago.

The vehicle has no steering wheel or pedals. This design requires exemptions from US regulators for mass production.

Regulatory clearance remains a key hurdle. The company must secure specific exemptions before scaling output.

Tesla has not disclosed production volume targets. Pricing and launch timelines also remain unspecified.

The Cybercab marks Tesla's push into autonomous ride-hailing. It aims to compete in the robotaxi market.
