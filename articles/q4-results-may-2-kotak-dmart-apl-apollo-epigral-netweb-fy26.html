Q4 Results Watch May 2: Kotak Mahindra Bank, DMart, APL Apollo, Epigral, Netweb Tech to Report FY26 Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/17 companies including Kotak Mahindra Bank (NSE: KOTAKBANK), Avenue Supermarts (NSE: DMART), and Netweb Technologies (NSE: NETWEB) report Q4 FY26 results on May 2, 2026.
  • 02/Analysts estimate Kotak Mahindra Bank Q4 PAT at ₹3,700-4,000 Cr with NIM at 4.9-5.1%, while DMart revenue is pegged at ₹16,500-17,000 Cr.
  • 03/Investors should watch NIM trajectory for Kotak, same-store sales for DMart, volume growth for APL Apollo, and FY27 guidance from Netweb Tech management.

May 2, 2026 marks a busy earnings day with 17 companies scheduled to announce Q4 FY26 results. The headline names are Kotak Mahindra Bank, Avenue Supermarts (DMart), APL Apollo Tubes, Epigral, and Netweb Technologies India. Together these five stocks represent over ₹6.9 lakh crore in market capitalisation.

Kotak Mahindra Bank (NSE: KOTAKBANK) trades at ₹2,030 with a market cap of ₹4,04,000 Cr. The stock has returned -8% over the past year. Analysts at MOFSL, YES Securities, and JM Financial estimate Q4 FY26 revenue at ₹17,000-17,800 Cr and PAT at ₹3,700-4,000 Cr, up 6-8% year-on-year.

The key metric to watch is Net Interest Margin (NIM). In Q3 FY26, Kotak's NIM was 4.54%, down from 4.93% YoY. For Q4, consensus expects NIM between 4.9% and 5.1%. Loan growth is projected at 18-20% YoY, but margin compression remains a risk versus peers HDFC Bank (NIM 3.38%) and ICICI Bank (NIM 4.32%).

Avenue Supermarts (NSE: DMART) operates 389 DMart stores as of January 2026, up from 365 a year ago. The stock trades at ₹3,380, down 14.7% over 12 months, with a market cap of ₹2,19,674 Cr. Analysts expect Q4 revenue of ₹16,500-17,000 Cr and PAT of ₹800-850 Cr.

DMart's Q3 FY26 revenue was ₹15,735 Cr with net profit of ₹730 Cr. The retailer faces margin pressure from quick-commerce rivals Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart. Motilal Oswal maintains a "Buy" with a target of ₹4,800, while Jefferies has an "Underperform" rating with a target of ₹2,650.

APL Apollo Tubes (NSE: APLAPOLLO), the world's largest branded structural steel tube company, reported record Q3 FY26 sales volume of 916,976 tons, up 11% YoY. Revenue in Q3 was ₹5,508 Cr, and net profit for Q4 FY25 was ₹293 Cr. The stock trades at ₹1,489 with a market cap of ₹59,676 Cr.

Chairman Sanjay Gupta said in January 2026: "APL Apollo has delivered its best-ever quarterly performance in Q3FY26, with all-time high sales volume, EBITDA, and PAT." For 9MFY26, volume grew 11% YoY. The company recently achieved a record Q4 FY26 sales volume of 924,881 tons.

Epigral (NSE: EPIGRAL), formerly Meghmani Finechem, manufactures chlor-alkali derivatives and CPVC resins. The stock trades at ₹1,192 with a market cap of ₹5,139 Cr. In Q3 FY26, Epigral reported revenue of ₹597 Cr and net profit of ₹39 Cr. Analysts expect FY26 full-year revenue of around ₹2,475 Cr with 14-15% PAT growth YoY.

The company's TTM revenue stands at ₹2,419 Cr and net profit at ₹338 Cr. Debt-to-equity ratio has improved from 0.82 in FY23 to 0.31 in FY25. Epigral serves construction, water treatment, and agrochemical sectors across India and export markets.

Netweb Technologies India (NSE: NETWEB) is India's largest OEM in high-end computing solutions. The stock trades at ₹3,859 with a market cap of ₹21,957 Cr, up 130.9% over the past year. Q3 FY26 revenue hit a record ₹804.9 Cr, up 141% YoY, with PAT of ₹73.3 Cr, up 146.7% YoY.

For 9MFY26, Netweb reported operating income of ₹1,409.9 Cr, up 92% YoY, and PAT of ₹135.2 Cr, up 90.1% YoY. AI systems contributed 64% of Q3 revenue. Analysts estimate Q4 FY26 revenue at ₹380-420 Cr and PAT at ₹45-58 Cr, with EBITDA margin at 16-18%.

The company's order book stands at ₹5,258 Cr organic and ₹17,336 Cr strategic. MOFSL has a "Buy" with target ₹2,600, ICICI Direct at ₹2,550, and JM Financial at ₹2,400. However, margin pressure from strategic deals and soft organic growth of just 6% YoY remain concerns.

All five companies will also announce their FY27 guidance and dividend recommendations at their respective board meetings. For Kotak, a final dividend of ₹2.50 per share is expected. Netweb is in a reinvestment phase and unlikely to declare a dividend.
