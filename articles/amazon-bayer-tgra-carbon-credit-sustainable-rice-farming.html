Amazon Partners With Bayer's TGRA for Carbon Credit Offtake From Rice Farms

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Amazon has entered a pact with Bayer's TGRA for carbon credit offtake from sustainable rice farming
  • 02/The deal targets India and links agricultural practices to corporate carbon offsetting
  • 03/Watch for acreage covered and credit volume under the partnership

Amazon has signed a pact with Bayer's TGRA unit. The deal covers carbon credit offtake from sustainable rice farming.

The partnership is focused on India. It links smallholder agricultural practices to corporate carbon offsetting demand.

TGRA provides agritech and carbon measurement tools. Bayer brings crop science expertise to the table.

Amazon needs carbon credits to meet net-zero commitments. The company targets net-zero by 2040 across operations.

Rice farming is a major methane source. Sustainable practices like alternate wetting and drying can cut emissions by 30%.

India is the world's second-largest rice producer. Scaling climate-smart agriculture here has global carbon impact.

The deal reflects growing corporate interest in nature-based offsets. Agricultural carbon credits are gaining institutional traction.
