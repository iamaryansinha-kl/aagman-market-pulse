Rupee Plunges 32 Paise to 93.48 Against US Dollar on April 21

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The rupee declined 32 paise to settle at 93.48 (provisional) against the US dollar on Tuesday, April 21, 2026
  • 02/The fall came despite positive domestic equity markets, weighed down by a steady dollar and volatile crude oil prices amid West Asia uncertainty
  • 03/Analysts expect USD-INR spot to trade in a range of Rs 93.30 to Rs 93.90 in the near term

The rupee declined 32 paise to close at 93.48 (provisional) against the US dollar on Tuesday, April 21, 2026. The local currency opened at 93.25 and fell to an intra-day low of 93.63 before settling at the weaker level.

On Monday, the rupee had already lost 25 paise to close at 93.16. The currency had gained 47 paise in the preceding two sessions, making Tuesday's reversal sharper.

The decline was driven by a steady American currency and volatile crude oil prices. Uncertainties over the progress of West Asia peace negotiations also kept forex traders on edge.

The dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength against a basket of six currencies, rose 0.19 per cent to 98.09. Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading at $94.81 per barrel, down 0.70 per cent.

The Reserve Bank of India partially withdrew its April 1 directives on Monday that had curbed excessive speculation in non-deliverable forward (NDF) markets. Banks can now resume offering NDF contracts involving the rupee, but the $100-million cap on net open positions remains effective.

Positive domestic equity markets failed to lift the rupee. The 30-share Sensex rose 753.03 points (0.96 per cent) to 79,273.33, while the Nifty gained 211.75 points (0.87 per cent) to 24,576.60. Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth about ₹1,059.93 crore on Monday, according to exchange data.

Anuj Choudhary, Research Analyst at Mirae Asset ShareKhan, said the rupee fell on uncertainty over US-Iran talks and a surge in crude oil prices. "USD-INR spot price is expected to trade in a range of Rs 93.30 to Rs 93.90," he added.

India's forex reserves stood at $700.946 billion for the week ended April 10, up $3.825 billion. The rupee has weakened 9.66 per cent over the last 12 months, with the USD-INR pair hitting an all-time high of 99.82 in March 2026.
