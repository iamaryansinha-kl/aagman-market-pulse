Yes Bank to Raise ₹16,000 Crore via Equity and Debt Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Yes Bank board approves raising up to ₹16,000 crore through equity and debt instruments combined
  • 02/Equity portion capped at ₹7,500 crore and debt at ₹8,500 crore; total dilution not to exceed 10%
  • 03/The bank's capital adequacy ratio stood at 15.3% in March 2026, down from 15.6% a year ago

Yes Bank Ltd (NSE: YESBANK) will raise up to ₹16,000 crore ($1.69 billion) through a mix of equity and debt. The board approved the fundraising on Monday, June 29, 2026.

The equity component is capped at ₹7,500 crore. The debt issuance can go up to ₹8,500 crore.

Yes Bank stated the capital raise will not result in more than 10% dilution of existing shareholders' stake. The lender did not disclose specific instruments for the fundraise.

The bank's capital adequacy ratio stood at 15.3% at the end of March 2026. This compares with 15.6% a year earlier, though well above the regulatory minimum of 9%.

Yes Bank reported a 44.7% year-on-year jump in Q4 FY26 net profit to ₹1,068 crore. Full-year profit rose 44.5% to ₹3,476 crore.

Gross non-performing assets improved to 1.3% from 1.6% in Q4 FY25. Net NPA stands at 0.2%, among the lowest in the sector.

Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC) holds a 24.9% stake in Yes Bank, making it the largest shareholder. SBI continues as one of the largest shareholders with a board nominee.

The fundraise signals a shift from consolidation to growth. Yes Bank's CET-1 ratio was 13.8% as of March 2026, and the fresh capital will support accelerated loan book expansion.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH IS YES BANK RAISING AND THROUGH WHAT INSTRUMENTS?
YES BANK WILL RAISE UP TO ₹16,000 CRORE ($1.69 BILLION) THROUGH A COMBINATION OF EQUITY AND DEBT. THE EQUITY PORTION IS CAPPED AT ₹7,500 CRORE AND THE DEBT PORTION AT ₹8,500 CRORE.
WHAT IS THE MAXIMUM DILUTION EXISTING SHAREHOLDERS FACE?
YES BANK HAS STATED THE FUNDRAISE WILL NOT RESULT IN MORE THAN 10% DILUTION OF EXISTING SHAREHOLDERS' STAKE, INCLUDING ANY FUTURE CONVERSION OF DEBT INTO EQUITY.
WHAT IS YES BANK'S CURRENT CAPITAL ADEQUACY RATIO?
YES BANK'S CAPITAL ADEQUACY RATIO STOOD AT 15.3% AS OF MARCH 31, 2026, COMPARED TO 15.6% A YEAR EARLIER. ITS CET-1 RATIO WAS 13.8%. THE REGULATORY MINIMUM IS 9%.
WHO ARE YES BANK'S MAJOR SHAREHOLDERS?
SUMITOMO MITSUI BANKING CORPORATION (SMBC) IS THE LARGEST SHAREHOLDER WITH 24.9%. STATE BANK OF INDIA (SBI) REMAINS ONE OF THE LARGEST SHAREHOLDERS WITH A BOARD NOMINEE DIRECTOR.
WHAT WERE YES BANK'S Q4 FY26 FINANCIAL RESULTS?
Q4 FY26 NET PROFIT ROSE 44.7% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO ₹1,068 CRORE. FULL-YEAR PAT INCREASED 44.5% TO ₹3,476 CRORE. GROSS NPA IMPROVED TO 1.3% AND NET NPA STOOD AT 0.2%.
