Hindustan Laboratories, RK Steel Get SEBI Nod for IPOs: 3.41 Cr Shares

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI clears IPOs for Hindustan Laboratories (1.41 crore shares) and RK Steel Manufacturing (2 crore shares) on April 27, 2026
  • 02/Hindustan Labs will deploy ₹72.5 crore for working capital; RK Steel proceeds go to debt repayment and working capital
  • 03/Both companies filed DRHPs on January 3, 2026, and will list on the BSE and NSE

SEBI cleared IPOs for Hindustan Laboratories Limited and RK Steel Manufacturing Company on April 27, 2026. Both companies filed draft red herring prospectuses on January 3, 2026.

Hindustan Laboratories' IPO comprises a fresh issue of 50 lakh shares and an OFS of 91 lakh shares by promoter Rajesh Vasantray Doshi. The total offer size is 1.41 crore equity shares of face value ₹10 each.

The generic drug maker will deploy ₹72.5 crore from the fresh issue towards working capital requirements. The remaining portion will be used for general corporate purposes.

RK Steel Manufacturing's IPO is entirely a fresh issue of up to 2 crore equity shares with no OFS component. The Tamil Nadu-based company has a production capacity of 1,50,000 tons per annum.

RK Steel plans to allocate proceeds towards repayment of borrowings, working capital, and general corporate purposes. The company manufactures HR, GP, and CRCA steel tubes and pipes for construction and automotive sectors.

Hindustan Laboratories operates a WHO-GMP-certified facility in Palghar, Maharashtra. It supplies generic medicines including anti-diabetics and cardiac care drugs to government institutions across 27 states.

Choice Capital Advisors is the book-running lead manager for the Hindustan Laboratories IPO. Both companies will list their shares on the BSE and the NSE.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE IPO STRUCTURE OF HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES?
HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES LIMITED'S IPO COMPRISES A FRESH ISSUE OF 50 LAKH EQUITY SHARES AND AN OFFER FOR SALE OF 91 LAKH SHARES BY PROMOTER RAJESH VASANTRAY DOSHI. THE TOTAL OFFER SIZE IS 1.41 CRORE EQUITY SHARES OF FACE VALUE ₹10 EACH.
WHAT IS THE IPO STRUCTURE OF RK STEEL MANUFACTURING?
RK STEEL MANUFACTURING COMPANY'S IPO IS ENTIRELY A FRESH ISSUE OF UP TO 2 CRORE EQUITY SHARES OF FACE VALUE ₹10 EACH. THERE IS NO OFFER FOR SALE COMPONENT IN THE ISSUE.
HOW WILL HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES UTILISE THE IPO PROCEEDS?
HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES WILL DEPLOY NET PROCEEDS WORTH ₹72.5 CRORE FROM THE FRESH ISSUE TOWARDS FUNDING WORKING CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS. THE REMAINING AMOUNT WILL BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES.
HOW WILL RK STEEL MANUFACTURING UTILISE THE IPO PROCEEDS?
RK STEEL MANUFACTURING WILL ALLOCATE FUNDS TOWARDS REPAYMENT OR PREPAYMENT OF CERTAIN BORROWINGS, WORKING CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS, AND GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES. THE COMPANY HAS NO OFFER FOR SALE COMPONENT.
WHEN DID BOTH COMPANIES FILE THEIR DRHPS AND RECEIVE SEBI APPROVAL?
BOTH COMPANIES FILED THEIR DRAFT RED HERRING PROSPECTUSES WITH SEBI ON JANUARY 3, 2026. THEY RECEIVED SEBI'S OBSERVATIONS, WHICH IS EQUIVALENT TO IPO CLEARANCE, ON APRIL 27, 2026. BOTH COMPANIES WILL LIST THEIR SHARES ON THE BSE AND THE NSE.
