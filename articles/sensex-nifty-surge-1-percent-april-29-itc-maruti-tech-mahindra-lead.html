Sensex Nifty Surge 1% on April 29: ITC, Maruti, Tech Mahindra Lead Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex surged 661 points to close at 77,547.97 and Nifty gained 314 points to 24,310.15 on April 29, 2026
  • 02/ITC, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra, Coal India and M&M led Nifty 50 gains; IndiGo and Dr Reddy's lagged
  • 03/125 stocks hit 52-week highs and 93 were locked in upper circuit; rising crude prices capped further upside

Indian equity benchmarks rallied on April 29, 2026, as earnings-driven buying lifted sentiment across sectors. The BSE Sensex closed at 77,547.97, gaining 661 points or 0.86% from its previous close.

The Nifty 50 ended at 24,310.15, up 314 points or 1.31%. Gains were driven by stock-specific action following quarterly earnings announcements.

ITC, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra, Coal India and Mahindra & Mahindra led the Nifty gainers. IndiGo, Dr Reddy's Laboratories, JSW Steel and ICICI Bank were among the laggards.

Market breadth was firmly positive with 2,014 stocks advancing against 1,167 declines on the NSE. A total of 125 stocks touched their 52-week highs during the session.

Broader markets saw buying interest with smallcap stocks outperforming midcaps. Realty and FMCG sectors led sectoral gains, each advancing over 2%.

Rising crude oil prices capped further upside after reports of a possible US blockade of Iranian ports. Brent crude pressure remained a key monitorable for investors.

The rally was underpinned by earnings optimism and broad-based participation. However, global cues and oil price movements continue to influence market direction.
