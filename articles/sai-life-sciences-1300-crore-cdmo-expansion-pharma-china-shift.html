Sai Life Sciences Bets ₹1,300 Crore on CDMO Capacity as Pharma Shifts From China

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sai Life Sciences plans ₹1,100-1,300 crore capacity expansion by FY27, funded by internal accruals and debt
  • 02/Big pharma revenue contribution jumped from 28% to 49% over four years as global drugmakers diversify from China
  • 03/Phase III and pre-registration molecules rose from 6 to 11, signalling deeper commercial manufacturing partnerships

Sai Life Sciences Ltd (NSE: SAILIFE) is investing ₹1,100-1,300 crore in capacity expansion by FY27. The company currently carries virtually no debt on its books.

The capex will be funded through internal accruals and fresh borrowings. Management expects new production facilities to come on stream by end of FY27.

Sivaramakrishnan Chittor, CFO of Sai Life Sciences, told ET Now that India is now the best bet for global pharma. The supply chain realignment began two years ago but has accelerated after WuXi AppTec was placed on the US 1260H list.

"Today, India is their best bet, and Indian companies are scaling up, putting up capex, and making sure that they can cash in on the opportunity," Chittor said.

The company's revenue from global pharmaceutical companies has surged from 28% to 49% over the past four years. Sai Life works with 19 of the world's top 25 drugmakers.

Late-stage development projects have expanded sharply. Phase III and pre-registration molecules have grown from 6 to 11, reflecting a shift from clinical supply to commercial manufacturing partnerships.

Sai Life has maintained its revenue growth guidance of 15-20% CAGR. The company expects FY27 performance to be stronger in the second half as new capacity ramps up.

The transition of manufacturing contracts will take time due to regulatory approvals. However, management believes the structural shift creates a multi-year opportunity for India's CDMO sector.
