Tata Consumer Q4 Profit Up 22% to ₹424 Crore; FY26 Revenue Hits ₹20,290 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Consumer Products Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 22% to ₹424 crore from ₹349 crore in the year-ago period.
  • 02/India branded segment profit surged 47% to ₹1,504 crore in FY26, offsetting a 6% decline in international branded profit.
  • 03/Board recommends ₹10 per share dividend; 63rd AGM to approve. Watch volume growth sustainability and coffee cost headwinds.

Tata Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: TATACONSUM) posted a 22 per cent rise in consolidated net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit rose to ₹424 crore from ₹349 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations increased 18 per cent to ₹5,434 crore from ₹4,608 crore. Profit before exceptional items and tax (PBEIT) grew 32 per cent to ₹641 crore from ₹484 crore.

Standalone quarterly net profit rose 14 per cent to ₹315 crore from ₹277 crore. Standalone revenue climbed 16 per cent to ₹3,892 crore from ₹3,354 crore.

The quarter's revenue growth was powered by strong volume gains. India business volumes rose 13 per cent, international volumes 9 per cent, and the non-branded segment surged 41 per cent.

For the full year FY26, consolidated net profit rose 20 per cent to ₹1,547 crore. Consolidated revenue from operations increased 15 per cent to ₹20,290 crore from ₹17,618 crore.

The India branded business was the key earnings driver. Segment profit surged 47 per cent to ₹1,504 crore from ₹1,021 crore in FY25. Strong volume momentum and easing tea cost inflation supported the expansion.

The international branded business saw margin pressure despite revenue growth. Segment profit declined 6 per cent to ₹626 crore from ₹667 crore. US tariff headwinds and coffee cost inflation were the key drags.

The non-branded segment reported a 31 per cent decline in segment profit to ₹280 crore from ₹407 crore. The company attributed this to reversal of prior-year fair value gains rather than operational weakness.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹10 per share for FY26. This represents 1,000 per cent on the face value of Re 1 per share. The payout is subject to approval at the company's 63rd Annual General Meeting.

Shares of Tata Consumer Products closed 2.04 per cent higher at ₹1,175.95 on Thursday following the earnings announcement.
