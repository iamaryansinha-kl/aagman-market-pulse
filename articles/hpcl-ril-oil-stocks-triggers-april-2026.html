HPCL Refinery Fire, RIL CBM Bids, OIL Renewables: Oil Stocks in Focus

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HPCL's ₹79,450-crore Rajasthan refinery fire postpones PM Modi's scheduled April 21 inauguration
  • 02/Reliance Industries and Essar Group emerge as top bidders for 16 coal-bed methane blocks; Oil India bids for 3
  • 03/Oil India's renewable arm OGEL signs MoU with Numaligarh Refinery for green energy supply

Oil stocks are in focus this week on a cluster of sector-specific triggers. HPCL, Reliance Industries, and Oil India each face defining news flow that could sway near-term sentiment.

A major fire broke out near the crude distillation unit of HPCL's newly built Rajasthan refinery on April 20, 2026. The incident forced postponement of the inauguration scheduled for April 21 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

The Rajasthan refinery project cost has ballooned to ₹79,450 crore from an initial ₹43,129 crore. HPCL raised its equity contribution to ₹19,600 crore to retain its 74% stake in the project.

The refinery was targeting commercial operations from July 1, 2026. Trial runs were already underway when the fire erupted near the crude distillation unit, the heart of the facility.

HPCL shares trade around ₹370-374 with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹79,000 crore. The stock commands a P/E multiple of 5.15, reflecting investor caution on execution risks.

Reliance Industries Ltd and Essar Group have emerged as top bidders for 16 coal-bed methane blocks across two bidding rounds. The Directorate General of Hydrocarbons released the list of bidders for the CBM rounds.

State-owned Oil India Ltd bid for three CBM blocks. India's largest oil and gas producer ONGC stayed away from the bidding process entirely.

Reliance Industries shares closed at ₹1,363 on April 20, 2026. The stock trades at a 15.4% discount to its 52-week high of ₹1,611.80.

Global brokerage Morgan Stanley maintains an 'Overweight' rating on RIL with a target price of ₹1,803, implying 28% upside. The firm notes elevated refining margins and RIL's ability to process heavy sour crude as key advantages.

Oil Green Energy Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Oil India Ltd, signed an MoU with Numaligarh Refinery Ltd on April 20, 2026. The collaboration covers development, procurement, and supply of renewable energy with focus on group captive requirements.

Oil India shares trade around ₹470 with a market capitalisation of ₹76,467 crore. The stock trades at a P/E of 13.1 and has a 52-week range of ₹381-524.

Analysts caution that volatile crude oil prices and windfall tax risks remain overhangs for the sector. HPCL's project cost escalation and execution delays add stock-specific risk for investors.

RELIANCE INDUSTRIES AND ESSAR GROUP HAVE EMERGED AS TOP BIDDERS FOR 16 COAL-BED METHANE BLOCKS ACROSS TWO BIDDING ROUNDS. OIL INDIA LTD BID FOR THREE BLOCKS. ONGC, INDIA'S LARGEST OIL AND GAS PRODUCER, DID NOT PARTICIPATE IN THE BIDDING.
WHAT IS OIL INDIA'S RENEWABLE ENERGY DEVELOPMENT?
OIL GREEN ENERGY LTD, A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF OIL INDIA LTD, SIGNED AN MOU WITH NUMALIGARH REFINERY LTD ON APRIL 20, 2026. THE AGREEMENT COVERS COLLABORATION IN DEVELOPMENT, PROCUREMENT, AND SUPPLY OF RENEWABLE ENERGY WITH SPECIAL EMPHASIS ON GROUP CAPTIVE REQUIREMENTS.
