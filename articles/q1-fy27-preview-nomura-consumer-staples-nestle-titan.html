Q1 FY27 Preview: Nomura Sees Double-Digit Growth For Nestle, Titan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nomura expects consumer staples sales to grow 10.4% YoY in Q1 FY27, above the eight-quarter average of 7.8%
  • 02/Nestle India is forecast to lead with 21% sales growth and 23% EBITDA growth on 17% volume and mix expansion
  • 03/Titan jewellery sales are seen rising 38-40% YoY, though paint sector margins face pressure from 57% higher crude prices

Nomura expects consumer staples to deliver a strong start to FY27. The brokerage forecasts overall sector sales growth of 10.4% year-on-year for the June quarter, well above the eight-quarter average of 7.8%.

Volume growth is likely to remain stable, Nomura said. Low-to-mid single digit price hikes initiated after the West Asia war have not materially impacted demand for a third consecutive quarter.

Lower-priced input cost inventory and partial effects of price hikes will cushion gross margins. Raw material inflation after the conflict had raised some concerns, but margin pressure is expected to be limited.

Nestle India Ltd (NSE: NESTLEIND) is expected to post the strongest growth among peers. Under Managing Director Manish Tiwary, the company is forecast to deliver 17% volume and mix growth, driving 21% sales growth.

"We believe the impact on Nestle's margins will be limited, given its raw material basket is more heavily weighted toward agricultural commodities, where inflation has remained relatively subdued," Nomura said. The brokerage expects 23% EBITDA growth despite elevated advertising and promotion spending.

Titan Company Ltd (NSE: TITAN) is forecast to report jewellery sales growth of 38-40% YoY. April was boosted by strong Akshaya Tritiya sales and a low base from Adhik Maas last year. Gold prices have moderated 18% from their peak, which Nomura believes will support footfalls and volume growth.

Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) is expected to achieve 10% value growth with pricing returning at 4-5%. However, Nomura expects OPM at the lower end of guidance at 22.8%, leading to sub-double-digit EBITDA growth of approximately 9% YoY.

Colgate-Palmolive India Ltd (NSE: COLPAL) faces headwinds from an inverse duty structure of around 150 basis points. Nomura projects volume growth of 6% YoY and sales growth of 8.5%, with OPM at 30% and modest EBITDA growth of 3% YoY.

Dabur India Ltd (NSE: DABUR) is forecast to deliver 9% sales growth from its India business, led by 5% volume and 4% pricing growth. Home and Personal Care is seen growing 13% YoY, with overall EBITDA growth of 11%.

Britannia Industries Ltd (NSE: BRITANNIA) volume growth is expected to improve to 7.5% YoY. Lower-priced wheat inventory and palm oil hedges should keep margins stable, driving 12% EBITDA growth.

Marico Ltd (NSE: MARICO) is projected to deliver 8.5% sales growth. Value-Added Hair Oils are seen growing 25% YoY, while Parachute volumes may turn positive after five quarters of decline. EBITDA growth is forecast at approximately 19% YoY.

Tata Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: TATACONSUM) is expected to sustain strong volume-led growth with 15% YoY sales growth. Low-cost inventory should aid margins, supporting EBITDA growth of approximately 23% on a low base.

The paint sector is forecast to see 12% YoY sales growth, but margins will come under pressure. Crude prices are up 57% YoY, and Nomura expects gross margin contraction of around 115 basis points and EBITDA growth of only 7.5%.

"While the timing of the 13% price hikes means they will contribute only 7-8% in Q1, the full mitigation impact will be witnessed from Q2 onwards," Nomura said for the paints segment.
