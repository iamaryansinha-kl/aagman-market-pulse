IndiGo Issues Travel Advisory as Bad Weather Disrupts Jaipur Flight Operations

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IndiGo issued a travel advisory on May 2, 2026, after bad weather over Jaipur disrupted flight schedules and operations at the airport.
  • 02/The IMD forecasts above-normal rainfall across India in May 2026 at over 110% of the long-period average, indicating wetter-than-usual conditions.
  • 03/Passengers should check real-time flight status on the IndiGo website or mobile app as operations are adjusted based on weather conditions.

IndiGo (InterGlobe Aviation Ltd) issued a travel advisory on Saturday, May 2, 2026, after bad weather conditions over Jaipur impacted flight schedules. The airline stated it is closely monitoring the situation and working to minimise passenger inconvenience.

"Bad weather over Jaipur has impacted flight schedules," IndiGo said in its advisory. The airline assured passengers that all efforts are being made to ensure safe and smooth travel amid the disruption.

"We are closely monitoring the weather and doing our best to get you where you need to be, safely and smoothly," the airline stated. IndiGo requested passengers to stay updated on their flight status via the website or mobile app.

The airline added that its teams are assisting passengers at every step as operations are adjusted in response to weather conditions. "Please be assured that our teams are here to assist you at every step and provide full support," it said.

Earlier on Friday, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said India is likely to witness wetter-than-usual conditions in May 2026. Rainfall across the country is "most likely to be above normal" at over 110 per cent of the long period average (LPA).

Rainfall is expected to be normal to above-normal in most regions of the country. However, parts of east and northeast India and east-central India may receive below-normal rainfall during the month.

IndiGo operates over 2,000 daily flights across domestic and international routes. Weather-related disruptions during the pre-monsoon period often challenge Indian carriers as they navigate between maintaining schedules and ensuring passenger safety.

InterGlobe Aviation (NSE: INDIGO, BSE: 539448) is India's largest airline by market share and fleet size. The stock has traded in a 52-week range of ₹3,780 to ₹6,232, reflecting both operational growth and sector-wide margin pressures.
