Godrej Agrovet to Set Up Rs 300 Crore Oil Palm Complex in Telangana

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Godrej Agrovet is making a major push into palm oil.

The agribusiness company is investing Rs 300 crore to set up an oil palm complex in Telangana. The project will include processing facilities for crude palm oil and related products, strengthening the company's position in the edible oil value chain.

India is one of the world's largest importers of edible oils. The country imports nearly 60% of its edible oil requirements, with palm oil accounting for the largest share. Malaysia and Indonesia are the primary sources of imported palm oil.

The Telangana project is strategically located. The state has been promoting oil palm cultivation as part of its agricultural diversification strategy. Godrej Agrovet's processing complex will provide a ready market for local farmers growing oil palm.

The Rs 300 crore investment covers processing infrastructure, storage facilities, and logistics. The complex will have the capacity to process significant volumes of fresh fruit bunches into crude palm oil and palm kernel oil.

The investment aligns with the government's push for edible oil self-sufficiency. The National Mission on Edible Oils — Oil Palm has been encouraging domestic cultivation and processing to reduce import dependence.

Godrej Agrovet's existing portfolio includes animal feed, crop protection, dairy, and poultry. The palm oil expansion adds a new vertical to the company's integrated agribusiness model.
