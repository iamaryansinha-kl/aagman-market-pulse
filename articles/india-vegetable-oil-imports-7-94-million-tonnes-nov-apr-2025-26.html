India Vegetable Oil Imports Surge 13% to 7.94 Million Tonnes in Nov-Apr 2025-26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's vegetable oil imports rose 13% YoY to 7.94 million tonnes during November-April 2025-26, per Solvent Extractors Association of India data released May 13, 2026.
  • 02/Palm oil imports nearly doubled to 3.97 million tonnes, while soft oil shipments declined to 3.85 million tonnes from 4.13 million tonnes.
  • 03/Cooking oil prices and a weaker rupee pushed import value up 19% to Rs 87,000 crore; stocks have risen to 2.12 million tonnes.

India's vegetable oil imports rose 13% year-on-year to 7.94 million tonnes during November-April 2025-26. The Solvent Extractors Association of India (SEA) released the data on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

The world's largest cooking oil consumer had imported 7.04 million tonnes in the same period a year earlier. India's oil year runs from November to October.

Of the total imports, edible oils accounted for 7.82 million tonnes. Non-edible oils made up the remaining 121,000 tonnes.

Palm oil imports nearly doubled to 3.97 million tonnes from 2.74 million tonnes a year earlier. Indonesia and Malaysia remain the primary suppliers.

Soft oil shipments, which include soybean and sunflower oils, fell to 3.85 million tonnes from 4.13 million tonnes. Argentina leads soybean oil supply, followed by Brazil.

In value terms, imports for the November-April period climbed 19% to Rs 87,000 crore. This compares with Rs 73,000 crore in the year-ago period.

Cooking oil prices rose sharply over the past year. Palm oil prices are up 14-15% compared with April 2025 levels. Soybean oil and sunflower oil prices increased between 17% and 22%.

The Indian rupee depreciated over 9.2% against the dollar in the past year. SEA called this "a cause of concern" for importers and refiners.

Nepal exported approximately 2,17,000 tonnes of refined oils to India in the first half of the year. This comprised mainly refined soybean oil with smaller volumes of sunflower oil, RBD palmolein and rapeseed oil.

Total vegetable oil stocks rose to 2.12 million tonnes in May 2026 from 1.35 million tonnes in May 2025. Pipeline stocks have shown a steady upward trend since December 2025.
