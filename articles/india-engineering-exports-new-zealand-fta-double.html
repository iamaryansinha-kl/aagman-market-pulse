India Engineering Exports to New Zealand Set to Double After FTA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's engineering exports to New Zealand reached USD 140.5 million in 2025-26, up approximately 8% from USD 129.8 million in 2024-25
  • 02/New FTA grants zero-duty access to all Indian goods, with EEPC India targeting USD 280-300 million over the next five years
  • 03/Growth driven by automobiles, dairy machinery, and medical instruments; sector aims for USD 250 billion exports by 2030

India's engineering exports to New Zealand are poised to double over the next five years. The free trade agreement grants zero-duty market access to all Indian goods.

Engineering shipments to New Zealand rose 8% to USD 140.5 million in 2025-26. This compares to USD 129.8 million in the previous fiscal year.

The growth has been led by automobiles and dairy sector machinery. Medical and scientific instruments also contributed meaningfully to the increase.

Metal-based products such as iron, steel, aluminium, and zinc have shown steady traction. These categories are expected to benefit further from zero-duty access.

"With zero-duty access and predictable trade rules, there is a clear opportunity to scale up engineering exports to around USD 280-300 million over the next five years," said Pankaj Chadha, Chairman of EEPC India.

The pact will particularly benefit MSMEs by reducing trade barriers. It ensures stable market access in a relatively small but high-potential destination.

The development comes amid India's broader push to expand engineering exports through trade agreements. Existing pacts with Australia and the UAE have already opened new avenues.

The engineering sector is targeting exports of USD 250 billion by 2030. FTAs are seen as a key lever to drive scale and market diversification.
