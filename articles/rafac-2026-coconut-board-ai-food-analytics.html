RAFAC 2026: Coconut Development Board Pushes AI in Food Analytics

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Coconut Development Board opens RAFAC 2026 on April 29, a two-day national conference on food analytical chemistry with AI and Machine Learning as core themes
  • 02/India produced 21,373.62 million coconuts in FY 2023-24, contributing over 30% to global output; coconut product exports reached ₹3,555 crore in FY23
  • 03/Directors from NIIST, CFTRI, CIFT and IPC stressed food safety, traceability and value chain transformation for global competitiveness

The Coconut Development Board opened RAFAC 2026 on April 29, 2026. The two-day national conference focuses on Recent Advancements in Food Analytical Chemistry.

Prabhat Kumar, Chief Executive Officer of the Board, called for simplifying scientific knowledge. He stressed institutional collaboration to drive innovation through AI and Machine Learning integration.

C. Anandharamakrishnan, Director of National Institute for Interdisciplinary Science and Technology, highlighted advanced analytical technologies. He underlined value chain transformation to boost India's global competitiveness in the coconut sector.

India produced 21,373.62 million coconuts in FY 2023-24. The country contributes over 30% to global coconut production. Coconut product exports reached ₹3,555 crore in FY23.

Giridhar Parvathan, Director of Central Food Technological Research Institute, stressed modern analytical techniques. He cited value addition as critical for food safety and meeting global standards.

George Ninan, Director of Central Institute of Fisheries Technology, called for cost-effective rapid analytical methods. He urged integrated traceability systems across food supply chains.

T. B. Kalaiselvan, Secretary-cum-Scientific Director of the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission, emphasised quality and ethics. He said regulatory compliance is key to safeguarding public health.

Hanumanthe Gowda, Chief Coconut Development Officer, described RAFAC as a platform for research and innovation. A conference handbook on food analytical techniques was released during the inaugural session.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS RAFAC 2026 AND WHO IS ORGANISING IT?
RAFAC 2026 STANDS FOR RECENT ADVANCEMENTS IN FOOD ANALYTICAL CHEMISTRY. THE TWO-DAY NATIONAL CONFERENCE IS ORGANISED BY THE COCONUT DEVELOPMENT BOARD AND OPENED ON APRIL 29, 2026.
WHICH KEY OFFICIALS SPOKE AT THE RAFAC 2026 INAUGURAL SESSION?
SPEAKERS INCLUDED PRABHAT KUMAR (CEO, COCONUT DEVELOPMENT BOARD), C. ANANDHARAMAKRISHNAN (DIRECTOR, NATIONAL INSTITUTE FOR INTERDISCIPLINARY SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY), GIRIDHAR PARVATHAN (DIRECTOR, CENTRAL FOOD TECHNOLOGICAL RESEARCH INSTITUTE), GEORGE NINAN (DIRECTOR, CENTRAL INSTITUTE OF FISHERIES TECHNOLOGY), T. B. KALAISELVAN (SECRETARY-CUM-SCIENTIFIC DIRECTOR, INDIAN PHARMACOPOEIA COMMISSION) AND HANUMANTHE GOWDA (CHIEF COCONUT DEVELOPMENT OFFICER).
WHAT ROLE DOES AI PLAY IN RAFAC 2026?
PRABHAT KUMAR HIGHLIGHTED THE INTEGRATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING AS A KEY THEME. THE FOCUS IS ON USING EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES TO DRIVE INNOVATION IN FOOD ANALYTICAL CHEMISTRY AND SIMPLIFY SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE.
HOW SIGNIFICANT IS INDIA'S COCONUT PRODUCTION GLOBALLY?
INDIA PRODUCED 21,373.62 MILLION COCONUTS IN FY 2023-24, CONTRIBUTING OVER 30% TO GLOBAL OUTPUT. COCONUT PRODUCT EXPORTS REACHED ₹3,555 CRORE IN FY23.
WHAT WERE THE KEY RECOMMENDATIONS MADE BY SPEAKERS AT RAFAC 2026?
SPEAKERS CALLED FOR ADVANCED ANALYTICAL TECHNOLOGIES, VALUE CHAIN TRANSFORMATION, COST-EFFECTIVE RAPID TESTING METHODS, INTEGRATED TRACEABILITY SYSTEMS, AND STRONGER REGULATORY COMPLIANCE TO ENHANCE FOOD SAFETY AND GLOBAL COMPETITIVENESS.
