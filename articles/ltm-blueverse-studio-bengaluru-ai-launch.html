LTM Launches BlueVerse AI Studio in Bengaluru for Enterprise AI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LTM launches BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026, to accelerate enterprise agentic AI adoption with measurable business outcomes
  • 02/Studio joins existing locations in London and Mumbai; stock gains 0.66% to ₹4,375.20 with market cap at ₹136,562 crore
  • 03/Q3 FY2025-26 revenue at ₹10,781 crore up 11.59% YoY; watch Q4 results expected in coming weeks for AI revenue impact

LTM Ltd (formerly LTIMindtree) launched its BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026. The hub is designed to accelerate Enterprise Agentic AI adoption for clients.

The studio helps organisations move AI experiments into practical applications. It ensures trust, control, and clear governance frameworks throughout the scaling process.

BlueVerse Studio unites LTM AI capabilities under one ecosystem. This includes BlueVerse CraftStudio, physical AI showcases, and industry-centric AI offerings.

Clients get rapid prototyping, reusable components, and Responsible AI controls. Ready-to-use workflows demonstrate end-to-end transformation using client data and KPIs.

"The BlueVerse ecosystem is central to our vision to be our clients' gateway to the agentic enterprise era," said Venu Lambu, CEO and Managing Director, LTM.

LTM continues expanding its studio network globally. Studios already operate in London and Mumbai, with Bengaluru becoming the latest addition.

LTM shares closed at ₹4,375.20 on April 28, up 0.66% from previous close of ₹4,346.30. The stock is down 28.96% from its 52-week high of ₹6,429.50.

The company reported Q3 FY2025-26 revenue of ₹10,781 crore, up 11.59% YoY. Net profit declined 10.58% to ₹970.6 crore against ₹1,085.4 crore in the year-ago quarter.
