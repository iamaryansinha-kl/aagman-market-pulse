Electric Bus Sales Rise 19% in Jan–Apr 2026; JSW Greentech Enters With Pilot Rollout

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's electric bus market grew 19% YoY in January–April 2026, with registrations rising to 1,868 units from 1,571 in the same period last year.
  • 02/April dispatches fell 37% month-on-month to 347 units after a March spike of 553 units, reflecting contract execution cycles rather than weak demand.
  • 03/Watch JSW Greentech's ramp-up at its Aurangabad facility and the PM e-Bus Sewa tender outcomes in H2 2026 for a clearer demand signal.

India's electric bus market expanded roughly 19% year-on-year in the first four months of 2026. Vahan data shows total registrations climbed to 1,868 units between January and April, up from 1,571 units in the same period of 2025.

April dispatches stood at 347 units, a sharp 37% drop from March's 553 units. The decline reflects fiscal year-end delivery pull-forwards in March, not a demand slowdown. Electric bus deployments are tightly linked to depot readiness and state transport coordination.

PMI Electro Mobility led April with 111 units. JBM Auto and Switch Mobility followed at 62 units each. Pinnacle Mobility dispatched 52 units. Smaller volumes came from Aeroeagle at 28 units and JSW Greentech at 25 units.

JSW Greentech's 25-unit dispatch marks its formal entry into India's electric bus segment. The company secured CMVR Type Approval from ARAI in February 2026 for its 12-metre "JSW 12M 51+D AC SDX" model. These initial units are for pilot and internal fleet use, not large-scale public rollout.

Sanyam Gandhi, Whole-time Director at Ahmedabad-based Chartered Speed, said the market is shifting from winning tenders to charging and operating at scale. His firm holds an order book of about 3,000 electric buses. He noted that a three-to-six-month view is more meaningful than monthly figures.

JSW Greentech is setting up a greenfield manufacturing facility in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, Maharashtra. The plant targets annual capacity of 10,000 electric buses and 5,000 electric trucks. Industry analysts expect commercial dispatches to external customers to accelerate in the second half of 2026.

The company is actively bidding for the PM e-Bus Sewa and PM E-Drive public procurement programmes. These government schemes are the primary demand drivers for electric buses in India, with most states adopting the Gross Cost Contract model.

May 2026 data is still early, with only 43 electric buses registered so far — all from JBM Auto. Broader volumes are expected to build through the month as state transport undertakings resume procurement post the financial year close.

The electric bus segment remains dominated by battery-electric vehicles, which hold over 98% market share. The competitive landscape has shifted sharply after the December 2025 PM E-DRIVE tender, where PMI Electro Mobility and Eka Mobility secured roughly 80% of a 10,900-bus order.

Established players like Tata Motors and Olectra reported limited April dispatches, underscoring how tender wins and contract execution cycles dictate monthly registration patterns more than underlying consumer demand.
