Tata Motors Climbs to No.2 in April Passenger Vehicle Sales at 59,000 Units

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles sold 59,000 units in April, up 30.5% YoY, climbing to the No.2 position in monthly sales
  • 02/Mahindra & Mahindra followed with 56,331 units (up 8% YoY) and held the overall FY26 No.2 position
  • 03/Hyundai Motor India reported its highest-ever April domestic sales at 51,902 units, a 17% YoY increase

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles secured the number two position in monthly sales in April. Domestic wholesales stood at 59,000 units, up 30.5% year-on-year.

The company sold 45,199 units in April 2025. The sharp growth reflects sustained demand for Tata Motors' passenger vehicle portfolio.

Mahindra & Mahindra reported 56,331 units in April, an 8% increase YoY from 52,330 units. M&M held the overall number two position for FY26.

"The year 2027 has begun on a positive note in April by achieving SUV sales of 56,331 units, a growth of 8 per cent and total vehicle sales of 94,627 units, a 14 per cent growth over the same month last year," Nalinikanth Gollagunta, Chief Executive Officer, Automotive Division, M&M, said.

Hyundai Motor India sold 51,902 units, its highest-ever April domestic sales. The company posted 17% YoY growth compared with 44,374 units in April 2025.

"We have opened the new financial year on a strong note, carrying forward the momentum built in recent months into April 2026," Tarun Garg, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Hyundai Motor India Limited, said.

Toyota Kirloskar Motor sold 30,159 units, rising 21% YoY from 24,833 units. Kia India reported 27,286 units, marking 16% growth.

"Our highest-ever April performance builds on the steady growth we have seen over the past several months," Atul Sood, Senior Vice-President, Sales & Marketing, Kia India, said.

JSW MG Motor sold 6,018 units, up 3.2% YoY from 5,829 units. Renault India surged 108% to 5,413 units from 2,602 units in April 2025.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MANY UNITS DID TATA MOTORS SELL IN APRIL 2026?
TATA MOTORS PASSENGER VEHICLES SOLD 59,000 UNITS IN APRIL 2026 THROUGH DOMESTIC WHOLESALES (DISPATCHES TO DEALERS). THIS REPRESENTS A 30.5% YEAR-ON-YEAR GROWTH COMPARED WITH 45,199 UNITS IN APRIL 2025.
WHICH POSITION DID TATA MOTORS SECURE IN APRIL PASSENGER VEHICLE SALES?
TATA MOTORS SECURED THE NUMBER TWO POSITION IN MONTHLY PASSENGER VEHICLE SALES IN APRIL. MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA FOLLOWED AT NUMBER THREE WITH 56,331 UNITS, WHILE HYUNDAI MOTOR INDIA STOOD AT NUMBER FOUR WITH 51,902 UNITS.
HOW DID OTHER AUTOMAKERS PERFORM IN APRIL 2026?
MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA SOLD 56,331 UNITS (UP 8% YOY). HYUNDAI MOTOR INDIA SOLD 51,902 UNITS (UP 17% YOY). TOYOTA KIRLOSKAR MOTOR SOLD 30,159 UNITS (UP 21% YOY). KIA INDIA SOLD 27,286 UNITS (UP 16% YOY). JSW MG MOTOR SOLD 6,018 UNITS (UP 3.2% YOY). RENAULT INDIA SOLD 5,413 UNITS (UP 108% YOY).
WHAT WAS M&M'S POSITION IN FY26 OVERALL?
MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA HELD THE NUMBER TWO POSITION IN OVERALL DOMESTIC WHOLESALES FOR FINANCIAL YEAR 2025-26. TATA MOTORS' APRIL CLIMB TO NUMBER TWO IS ON A MONTHLY BASIS, NOT THE FULL FINANCIAL YEAR.
WHICH COMPANY REPORTED THE HIGHEST GROWTH IN APRIL 2026?
RENAULT INDIA REPORTED THE HIGHEST YEAR-ON-YEAR GROWTH AT 108%, WITH DOMESTIC WHOLESALES OF 5,413 UNITS AGAINST 2,602 UNITS IN APRIL 2025. TOYOTA KIRLOSKAR MOTOR FOLLOWED WITH 21% GROWTH.
