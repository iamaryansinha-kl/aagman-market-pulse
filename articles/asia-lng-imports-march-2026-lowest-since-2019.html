Asia LNG Imports March 2026: 21.12 MT Lowest Since 2019 On Middle East Conflict

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Asia LNG imports hit 21.12 mt in March 2026, the lowest March level in 8 years since 2019
  • 02/The 4.3% y-o-y decline was driven by reduced supply from Qatar and UAE amid the Middle East conflict
  • 03/US LNG exports reached a record 11.74 mt in March as non-GECF countries filled the supply gap

Asia's liquefied natural gas imports in March 2026 fell to 21.12 million tonnes. This marks the lowest March level in eight years since 2019.

The decline was 4.3 per cent year-on-year, a drop of 0.94 mt. The Gas Exporting Countries' Forum reported the figures in its April 2026 gas market report.

China, India and Pakistan led the import drop. Reduced LNG supply from Qatar and the UAE amid the Middle East conflict was the primary driver.

In China, a well-supplied domestic gas market also dampened spot LNG demand. India saw lower volumes from both Qatar and the UAE.

Taiwan and Thailand partially offset the decline. Taiwan boosted imports from Brunei, Canada and the US to meet stronger demand.

Thailand increased re-exported LNG from China. South Korea also offset lower Qatari supply with higher Canadian imports and Chinese re-exports.

For January to March 2026, Asia's LNG imports reached 69.25 mt. This was up 3.8 per cent year-on-year, an increase of 2.55 mt.

Global LNG exports fell 6.8 per cent y-o-y to 35.80 mt in March. This marked the largest decline since July 2020.

The Strait of Hormuz closure disrupted Qatar and UAE exports. Damage to two Qatar LNG trains totalling 12.8 mtpa could constrain supply further.

Non-GECF countries' share of global LNG exports jumped to 66.2 per cent. This was up sharply from 53.1 per cent a year earlier.

The US, Australia and Russia were the top LNG exporters in March. Qatar dropped out of the top three for the first time due to the conflict.

US LNG exports hit a record 11.74 mt in March. Corpus Christi Stage 3, Plaquemines LNG and Freeport LNG ramped up production.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WERE ASIA'S LNG IMPORTS IN MARCH 2026?
ASIA IMPORTED 21.12 MILLION TONNES OF LNG IN MARCH 2026, THE LOWEST MARCH LEVEL IN EIGHT YEARS SINCE 2019. THIS REPRESENTS A 4.3 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR DECLINE OF 0.94 MT.
WHAT CAUSED THE DROP IN ASIA LNG IMPORTS?
THE DECLINE WAS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY REDUCED LNG SUPPLY FROM QATAR AND THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES AMID THE ONGOING MIDDLE EAST CONFLICT. THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ CLOSURE DISRUPTED EXPORTS FROM BOTH COUNTRIES.
WHICH COUNTRIES LED THE IMPORT DROP AND WHICH OFFSET IT?
CHINA, INDIA AND PAKISTAN LED THE DROP IN LNG IMPORTS. TAIWAN AND THAILAND PARTIALLY OFFSET THE DECLINE WITH HIGHER IMPORTS FROM BRUNEI, CANADA, THE US AND RE-EXPORTED LNG FROM CHINA.
HOW DID GLOBAL LNG EXPORTS PERFORM IN MARCH 2026?
GLOBAL LNG EXPORTS FELL 6.8 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR TO 35.80 MT IN MARCH 2026, THE LARGEST DECLINE SINCE JULY 2020. NON-GECF COUNTRIES' EXPORT SHARE ROSE TO 66.2 PER CENT FROM 53.1 PER CENT A YEAR EARLIER.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR QATAR'S LNG SUPPLY?
DAMAGE TO TWO LNG TRAINS IN QATAR WITH A COMBINED CAPACITY OF 12.8 MTPA COULD REDUCE SUPPLY IN THE SHORT TO MEDIUM TERM. QATAR HAS DROPPED OUT OF THE TOP THREE GLOBAL EXPORTERS FOR THE FIRST TIME.
