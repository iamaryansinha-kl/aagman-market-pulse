Jyothy Labs Q4 Profit Down 12.3% to ₹67.5 Cr, FY26 Revenue Rises 3.9%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jyothy Labs Q4 FY26 net profit drops 12.3% to ₹67.5 crore from ₹77 crore in the year-ago quarter
  • 02/Revenue from operations rises 7.7% to ₹717.41 crore driven by 10.8% volume growth year-on-year
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of ₹3.5 per share for FY26; margins may remain under pressure near term

Jyothy Labs Ltd (NSE: JYOTHYLAB) reported a 12.3% decline in consolidated net profit to ₹67.5 crore for Q4 FY26. The FMCG firm had posted ₹77 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations grew 7.72% to ₹717.41 crore in the March quarter. Total revenue stood at ₹733.20 crore, up 7.6% year-on-year.

Volume growth came in at 10.8% during the quarter. However, the operating EBITDA margin contracted to 13.5% from about 16.9% a year ago.

Total expenses rose 11.8% to ₹637.7 crore. Lower sales realisation and input price inflation dented profitability.

For the full FY26, net profit fell 10.23% to ₹333.19 crore. Total consolidated income rose 3.87% to ₹3,011.85 crore.

"Margins may remain under pressure in the near term," said Chairperson and Managing Director M R Jyothy. She cited crude-linked inflation and West Asia developments as key cost drivers.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹3.5 per share for FY26. The record date is June 29, 2026, with payment on or after July 14.

Shares settled at ₹268.85 on BSE on May 4, up 1.22%. The stock trades well below its 52-week high of ₹383.
