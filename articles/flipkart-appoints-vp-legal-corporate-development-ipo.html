Flipkart Names Sharayu Jadhav and Varun Gupta as VPs Ahead of Planned IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Flipkart appoints Sharayu Jadhav as VP Legal and Varun Gupta as VP Corporate Development, announced April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Jadhav returns from Acko and Razorpay; Gupta joins from Reliance with nearly two decades of experience.
  • 03/These are the latest in at least six senior-level hires since January as Flipkart builds leadership bench for IPO.

E-commerce firm Flipkart announced on April 29, 2026, that it has appointed Sharayu Jadhav as Vice President of Legal and Varun Gupta as Vice President of Corporate Development.

Jadhav returns to Flipkart after serving in leadership roles at Acko and Razorpay. In her previous stint, she handled legal responsibilities for Flipkart's wholesale division and Myntra.

In her new role, Jadhav will oversee legal operations for the marketplace, supply chain, data privacy, technology law, and labour and employment.

Gupta joins from Reliance, where he managed corporate development and equity capital initiatives. He brings nearly two decades of experience in corporate development and investment banking.

At Flipkart, Gupta will direct strategic investments, acquisitions, partnerships, and Flipkart Ventures, the company's early-stage investment arm.

The Walmart-backed firm has been actively strengthening its leadership team ahead of its planned Initial Public Offering (IPO).

In March 2026, Flipkart appointed Somnath Das as Vice President of Supply Chain. In February, it named Vipin Kapooria as VP of Business Finance and Yogita Shanbhag as VP of Human Resources.

In January 2026, the company also added Jane Duke as Chief Ethics & Compliance Officer, Jason Chappel as VP Group Controller, and Amer Hussain as VP of Supply Chain for Grocery and Minutes.
