Fed to Hold Rates Through 2026 as Inflation Risks Outweigh Labour Weakness: Elara

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Elara Securities withdrew its call for three 75 bps rate cuts in 2026, now expects the Fed to hold rates through the year as inflation risks outweigh labour market weakness
  • 02/Core PCE forecast revised to 2.9% and headline PCE to 3.0-3.5% on tariff pass-through and surging energy and food prices
  • 03/A 20% chance of a 25 bps rate hike in December 2026 if the Strait of Hormuz remains closed; the 2026 FOMC voting rotation is seen as more hawkish

Elara Securities has withdrawn its earlier forecast of three 75 basis point rate cuts in 2026. The brokerage now expects the US Federal Reserve to hold rates steady through the year.

The shift follows an assessment that inflation risks decisively outweigh labour market concerns. Elara noted the Fed's 2% inflation target was no longer achievable in its view.

"With upside inflation risks set to outweigh downside risks to the labour market for a major part of the year, we withdraw our call of three rate cuts of 75bp in CY26E and now expect the Federal Reserve to hold rates," Elara stated in its report.

Elara revised its US core PCE forecast higher to 2.9% Q4/Q4, from 2.6% earlier. Headline PCE is seen at 3.0% to 3.5%.

"Tariffs, along with a surge in energy and food prices, would keep inflation elevated and sticky," the brokerage said. The report added that negative spillovers from the US-Iran conflict could be long-lasting.

On the labour market, Elara believes peak uncertainty has passed. Its Composite Index of Lead Indicators from Regional Fed Surveys pointed to the highest hiring optimism since February 2025.

ADP private payrolls turned positive at 21,000 on a three-month moving average basis, excluding education and health. Despite improved hiring momentum, Elara retained its unemployment rate projection at 4.6% for 2026.

The brokerage also sees growth risks as moderate and likely to materialise with a lag. It kept its 2026 GDP forecast at 2.2% Q4/Q4, with US energy exports from the Middle East conflict providing a 10-15 bps upside.

Elara assigned a 20% probability to a 25 bps hike in December 2026 if the Strait of Hormuz remains closed until September. It noted the 2026 FOMC voting rotation, with Hammack, Logan, Kashkari and Paulson as regional voters, leaves the committee "more hawkish or cautious."
