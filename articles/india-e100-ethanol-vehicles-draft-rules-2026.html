India Clears Legal Path for 100% Ethanol Vehicles: E100 Draft Rules

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoRTH issued a draft notification on April 27, 2026 to amend Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 for E100, E85 and Hydrogen-CNG fuels
  • 02/India has 2,000 crore litres of distillation capacity with excess capacity of around 10 billion litres currently unutilised
  • 03/Public comments close May 15, 2026; final rules expected Q3 2026 with E85 infrastructure targeted for 2027 rollout

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a draft notification on April 27, 2026. It amends the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 to formally recognise E100, E85 and Hydrogen-CNG as automotive fuels.

The draft creates a dedicated compliance annexure for E100, removing regulatory uncertainty. This paves the way for automobile manufacturers to develop and certify flex-fuel vehicles that can run entirely on ethanol or any blend with petrol.

The All India Distillers' Association called it the most significant policy shift since the Ethanol Blending Programme began. It transitions ethanol from a blending agent to a standalone primary fuel, giving distilleries long-term certainty to scale production.

India achieved its E20 target five years ahead of schedule in 2025. The country has built 2,000 crore litres of distillation capacity as of March 2026, with excess capacity of around 10 billion litres currently unutilised.

The Ethanol Blending Programme has saved approximately Rs.1.70 lakh crore in foreign exchange so far. India's fossil fuel import bill stands at an estimated Rs.22 lakh crore annually, making domestic biofuel adoption a critical priority.

The draft expands the gross vehicle weight compliance category from 3,000kg to 3,500kg, bringing light commercial vehicles into the ethanol fold. Petrol classifications have also been updated to reflect E10 and E20 categories.

Tata Motors, Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai and Toyota are already developing flex-fuel prototypes. Praful Vithalani of All India Sugar Trade Association urged a 15-year policy with subsidy guidelines for flex-fuel vehicles and supply infrastructure at fuel stations.

Public comments on the draft close on May 15, 2026. Final rules are expected in the third quarter of 2026, with E85 dispensing infrastructure targeted for rollout by 2027.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES THE MORTH DRAFT NOTIFICATION PROPOSE?
THE MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS ISSUED A DRAFT NOTIFICATION ON APRIL 27, 2026 TO AMEND THE CENTRAL MOTOR VEHICLES RULES, 1989. IT FORMALLY RECOGNISES E100 PURE ETHANOL, E85 AND HYDROGEN-CNG AS AUTOMOTIVE FUELS AND ALLOWS VEHICLE CERTIFICATION UP TO E100.
HOW DOES THIS BENEFIT THE ETHANOL INDUSTRY?
THE NOTIFICATION PROVIDES LONG-TERM REGULATORY CERTAINTY TO DISTILLERIES AND TRANSITIONS ETHANOL FROM A BLENDING AGENT TO A STANDALONE PRIMARY FUEL. INDIA HAS 2,000 CRORE LITRES OF DISTILLATION CAPACITY WITH EXCESS CAPACITY OF AROUND 10 BILLION LITRES CURRENTLY UNUTILISED.
WHAT ARE FLEX-FUEL VEHICLES AND WHICH COMPANIES ARE DEVELOPING THEM?
FLEX-FUEL VEHICLES CAN RUN ON PETROL, ETHANOL OR ANY BLEND OF THE TWO. TATA MOTORS, MAHINDRA, MARUTI SUZUKI, HYUNDAI AND TOYOTA ARE DEVELOPING PROTOTYPES. THE AUTOMOBILE INDUSTRY EXPECTS CERTIFICATION COSTS OF Rs.20,000 TO Rs.30,000 PER VEHICLE INITIALLY.
WHAT IS INDIA'S CURRENT ETHANOL BLENDING PROGRESS?
INDIA ACHIEVED THE E20 TARGET FIVE YEARS AHEAD OF SCHEDULE IN 2025. THE ETHANOL BLENDING PROGRAMME HAS SAVED APPROXIMATELY Rs.1.70 LAKH CRORE IN FOREIGN EXCHANGE. PETROL SALES IN INDIA REACHED 42.59 MILLION TONNES IN FY2025-26.
WHAT ARE THE NEXT STEPS AND TIMELINE?
PUBLIC COMMENTS ON THE DRAFT CLOSE ON MAY 15, 2026. FINAL RULES ARE EXPECTED IN Q3 2026. INDUSTRY STAKEHOLDERS HAVE URGED ROLLOUT IN SUGAR-SURPLUS STATES LIKE UTTAR PRADESH, MAHARASHTRA AND KARNATAKA IN THE FIRST PHASE.
