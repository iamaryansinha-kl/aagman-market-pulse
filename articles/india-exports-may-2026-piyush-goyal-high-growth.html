India Exports May 2026: Piyush Goyal Says High Growth Trajectory Continues

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal confirms India's exports are in a high growth trajectory during May 2026, continuing April's strong momentum
  • 02/Foreign direct investment into India has reached an all-time high, reinforcing global investor confidence
  • 03/India is increasingly viewed as a preferred investment destination amid shifting global supply chains

India's exports are maintaining a high growth trajectory in May 2026. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal confirmed the momentum is continuing from April's performance.

The minister highlighted that foreign direct investment has reached an all-time high. This signals strong global confidence in India's economic prospects.

India is now positioned as a preferred investment destination. Multinational corporations are actively diversifying supply chains away from China.

The government is pursuing policies to sustain export momentum. Trade facilitation measures and production-linked incentive schemes remain key levers.

Export growth in May builds on a recovering trade performance. India's merchandise exports had shown resilience despite global headwinds in early 2026.

FDI data at record levels points to capital inflows across sectors. Manufacturing, technology, and services continue to attract the largest share of investment.

Goyal's statement comes amid ongoing trade negotiations. India is working to expand market access with key trading partners.

WHAT DID PIYUSH GOYAL SAY ABOUT INDIA'S EXPORTS IN MAY 2026?
COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY MINISTER PIYUSH GOYAL STATED THAT INDIA'S EXPORTS ARE IN A HIGH GROWTH TRAJECTORY DURING MAY 2026, CONTINUING THE STRONG MOMENTUM SEEN IN APRIL.
WHAT IS THE STATUS OF FOREIGN DIRECT INVESTMENT IN INDIA?
ACCORDING TO PIYUSH GOYAL, FDI INTO INDIA HAS REACHED AN ALL-TIME HIGH, INDICATING STRONG GLOBAL INVESTOR CONFIDENCE IN THE COUNTRY'S ECONOMIC OUTLOOK.

INDIA IS INCREASINGLY VIEWED AS A PREFERRED DESTINATION DUE TO ITS LARGE DOMESTIC MARKET, GROWING MANUFACTURING BASE, AND GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN DIVERSIFICATION AWAY FROM CHINA.
WHAT GOVERNMENT POLICIES SUPPORT EXPORT GROWTH?
THE GOVERNMENT HAS PUSHED TRADE FACILITATION MEASURES AND PRODUCTION-LINKED INCENTIVE SCHEMES ACROSS SECTORS TO BOOST MANUFACTURING EXPORTS AND ATTRACT FDI.

INVESTORS SHOULD MONITOR ONGOING TRADE NEGOTIATIONS, FDI SECTOR-WISE DATA RELEASES, AND MONTHLY MERCHANDISE EXPORT FIGURES FOR APRIL AND MAY 2026 TO CONFIRM THE TREND.
