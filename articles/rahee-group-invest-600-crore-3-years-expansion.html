Rahee Group to Invest ₹600 Crore Over 3 Years in Manufacturing Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rahee Group plans total investment of up to ₹600 crore over the next three years to scale up manufacturing operations
  • 02/Rahee Track Technologies is setting up a ₹200-crore foundry in Odisha for cast manganese and steel castings production
  • 03/Rahee Infratech may consider IPO in next 2-3 years with standalone revenue target of ₹2,000 crore

Rahee Group plans to invest up to ₹600 crore over the next three years. The infrastructure firm will scale up operations across key verticals, CMD Pradeep Khaitan told PTI.

Rahee Track Technologies Ltd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Rahee Infratech Ltd, is setting up a ₹200-crore foundry. The facility in Odisha will manufacture cast manganese and steel castings.

Rahee Infratech Ltd will undertake annual capex of ₹60-70 crore over three years. This will enhance core infrastructure capabilities and expand manufacturing capacity.

The group will invest around ₹100 crore in Pandrol Rahee Technologies. This subsidiary, with a 40 per cent stake, will set up a line for modern fastening systems and plastic components.

"These investments are aligned with our long-term strategy to expand capacity," Khaitan said. The funds will support growing needs of railway and infrastructure sectors.

Rahee Group is focusing on sustainable rail solutions. This includes using recycled rubber from end-of-life tyres to reduce noise and vibration in metro systems.

Indian Railways is undertaking one of the world's largest modernisation programmes. Metro rail expansion across multiple cities will drive demand for advanced track solutions.

"India is becoming a competitive manufacturing hub for global rail markets," Khaitan stated. "Make in India is now evolving into Make for the World."

Pandrol Rahee Technologies is exploring export opportunities. Target markets include the US, UK, Japan, and emerging economies leveraging cost competitiveness.

Rahee Infratech aims for standalone revenue of ₹2,000 crore. The Rahee Group's combined turnover stands at approximately ₹1,800 crore currently.
