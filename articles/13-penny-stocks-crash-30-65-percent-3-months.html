13 Penny Stocks Crash 30-65% in 3 Months: Full List Revealed

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/13 penny stocks crashed between 30% and 65% over the past 3 months, screened by ACE Equity for market cap below Rs 1,000 crore
  • 02/Cropster Agro led the plunge with a 66% fall to Rs 5.15, while Padam Cotton Yarns and Leading Leasing Finance both dropped 62%
  • 03/All 13 stocks were filtered using criteria of share price under Rs 20 and minimum trading volume of 5 lakh shares

Thirteen penny stocks have crashed between 30% and 65% over the past three months. The data comes from an ACE Equity screen of stocks with market capitalisation below Rs 1,000 crore.

Cropster Agro led the declines with a 66% plunge. The stock closed at Rs 5.15, down from significantly higher levels three months ago.

Padam Cotton Yarns and Leading Leasing Finance And Investment Co both fell 62%. Padam closed at Rs 0.97, while Leading Leasing ended at Rs 1.34.

Silverline Technologies dropped 55% to Rs 6.97. Bluegod Entertainment fell 48% to Rs 2.16 per share.

Mangalam Industrial Finance declined 45% to Rs 0.61. Sylph Industries shed 43% and closed at just Rs 0.37.

Sellwin Traders fell 40% to Rs 4.88. Deep Health AI India lost 35% and ended at Rs 2.33.

Sampre Nutritions declined 34% to Rs 14.55. Biogen Pharmachem Industries fell 31% to Rs 0.41.

Garment Mantra Lifestyle and Pulsar International both dropped 27%. Garment Mantra closed at Rs 1.21, while Pulsar ended at Rs 0.78.

The screening criteria were strict: market cap below Rs 1,000 crore, share price under Rs 20, and minimum trading volume of 5 lakh shares. This approach captured only actively traded penny stocks with significant downside.

Penny stocks carry inherent risks due to low liquidity and high volatility. Limited transparency makes them prone to sudden price swings and potential manipulation.
