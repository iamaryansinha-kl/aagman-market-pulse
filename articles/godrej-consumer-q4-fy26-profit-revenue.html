Godrej Consumer Q4 Profit Up 10% to ₹452 Cr; FY26 Revenue Tops ₹15,000 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godrej Consumer Products Ltd Q4 FY26 net profit rose 9.7% YoY to ₹452 crore from ₹412 crore, driven by volume growth
  • 02/FY26 consolidated revenue crossed the ₹15,000 crore milestone with 9% full-year growth on 6% volume expansion
  • 03/Board declared ₹5 per share interim dividend for FY27 with record date May 12, 2026

Godrej Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: GODREJCP) reported a 9.7% year-on-year rise in Q4 FY26 net profit to ₹452 crore. Profit stood at ₹412 crore in the same quarter last year.

Consolidated revenue for the quarter grew 11% to ₹3,900 crore from ₹3,514 crore a year ago. EBITDA increased 11% to ₹841.4 crore, with margin holding flat at 21.6%.

For the full year FY26, consolidated sales rose 9% on 6% volume growth. Revenue crossed the ₹15,000 crore milestone for the first time in the company's history.

Q4 volume growth stood at 6%, with standalone business volumes up 8%. Standalone sales grew 10% during the quarter.

International markets showed mixed performance. Africa, the US and West Asia delivered 20% growth in Q4 and 23% for the full year. Indonesia sales rose 3% in Q4 but declined 2% across FY26.

Home care grew 12% in Q4, led by household insecticides, air fresheners and incense sticks. Personal care grew 3%, with soaps maintaining momentum aided by improved post-GST affordability.

The board declared an interim dividend of ₹5 per share for FY27, representing 500% on the face value of ₹1. The record date is May 12, 2026, with payment scheduled on or before June 4, 2026.

"The quarter ends a year in which consistent execution of our Goodness Manifesto, focus on category development and discipline on cost have come together to deliver healthy, profitable growth" — Sudhir Sitapati, Managing Director and CEO, GCPL.

Shares closed at ₹1,097.70 on the NSE on May 6, down 0.34%. The stock has gained nearly 10% over the past month but remains down over 12% on a one-year basis.
