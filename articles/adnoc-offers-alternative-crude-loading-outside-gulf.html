ADNOC Offers Alternative Crude Loading Outside Gulf as Hormuz Stays Shut

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ADNOC notifies clients they can load Das and Upper Zakum crude outside the Gulf next month as Hormuz stays closed
  • 02/Exports from Das Island and Zirku Island terminals have fallen drastically since the US-Iran war began in late February
  • 03/Alternative loading points include ship-to-ship transfers off Fujairah or Sohar; additional costs not specified

Abu Dhabi National Oil Company has notified customers they can load two crude grades outside the Gulf next month. The move comes as the Strait of Hormuz remains closed.

The two grades are Das and Upper Zakum. ADNOC will move them from Das Island and Zirku Island terminals inside the Gulf to outside locations.

Exports from these terminals have fallen drastically. Ship owners have been unwilling to transit the strait since the US-Iran war began in late February.

"To ensure that customers can continue to perform their contractual liftings, we are also making cargoes available for loading outside of the Arabian Gulf, on a case-by-case basis," ADNOC said in a client note.

The alternative delivery points include ship-to-ship transfers off Fujairah in the United Arab Emirates or off Sohar in Oman. ADNOC did not specify additional costs.

The notice did not state whether buyers or ADNOC would bear transfer costs. Two sources with knowledge of the matter confirmed the plan to Reuters.

ADNOC did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Clients can contact ADNOC staff to confirm plans for lifting term cargoes at alternative points.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH CRUDE GRADES CAN CUSTOMERS LOAD OUTSIDE THE GULF?
ADNOC IS OFFERING DAS AND UPPER ZAKUM CRUDE GRADES FOR LOADING OUTSIDE THE ARABIAN GULF. THESE ARE CURRENTLY EXPORTED FROM DAS ISLAND AND ZIRKU ISLAND TERMINALS.
WHY IS ADNOC OFFERING ALTERNATIVE LOADING LOCATIONS?
THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ HAS REMAINED CLOSED SINCE THE START OF THE US-IRAN WAR IN LATE FEBRUARY. SHIP OWNERS HAVE BEEN UNWILLING TO TRANSIT THE STRAIT, CAUSING DRASTIC FALLS IN EXPORTS FROM THE GULF TERMINALS.
WHAT ARE THE ALTERNATIVE DELIVERY POINTS?
THE ALTERNATIVE DELIVERY POINTS INCLUDE SHIP-TO-SHIP TRANSFERS OFF FUJAIRAH IN THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES OR OFF SOHAR IN OMAN. ADNOC DID NOT SPECIFY EXACT LOCATIONS IN THE CLIENT NOTICE.
WILL THERE BE ADDITIONAL COSTS FOR ALTERNATIVE LOADING?
ADNOC DID NOT STATE IF ADDITIONAL COSTS WILL BE INCURRED IN THE NOTICE. THE COMPANY ALSO DID NOT SPECIFY WHETHER ADNOC OR THE BUYER WOULD BEAR THESE COSTS.
HOW CAN CLIENTS ARRANGE ALTERNATIVE LOADING?
CLIENTS CAN REACH ADNOC STAFF TO CONFIRM THEIR PLANS TO LIFT TERM CARGOES USING THE ALTERNATIVE DELIVERY POINTS. ADNOC IS MAKING CARGOES AVAILABLE ON A CASE-BY-CASE BASIS.
