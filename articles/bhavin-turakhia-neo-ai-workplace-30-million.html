Bhavin Turakhia's Neo: $30M AI-Native Workplace to Rival Microsoft, Google

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bhavin Turakhia has invested $30 million of personal capital to build Neo, his fifth venture after Directi, Radix, Titan, and fintech unicorn Zeta
  • 02/Neo is an AI-native workplace platform with four integrated modules, targeting invite-only deployments by August 2026 and a public launch in January 2027
  • 03/The platform will compete with legacy productivity suites from Microsoft, Google, and domestic player Zoho by enabling humans and AI agents to work together

Serial entrepreneur Bhavin Turakhia has committed $30 million of his own capital to build Neo. The AI-native workplace platform aims to fundamentally reset how enterprises operate by letting humans and AI agents work side by side.

Turakhia told Mint in an interview on Wednesday that he began conceptualizing Neo last year. He assembled the founding team four months ago and formally started work in April 2026.

Neo includes four core modules. 'Friday' is an AI assistant connected to over 1,000 external applications. 'Tasket' handles project management. 'Studio' covers documents, spreadsheets, and diagrams. 'Drive' is a collaborative file-sharing workspace.

The platform is designed to centralize organizational knowledge so AI agents can operate without needing multiple integrations. Turakhia argues that simply adding AI features to existing tools will not fundamentally change enterprise productivity.

"Bolting AI onto existing tools is not the right approach, much like bolting the internet onto desktop products was never the right approach," Turakhia said. "Products had to be created as internet-native. Similarly, we believe products will have to be created as AI-native."

Neo will initially target mid-market knowledge-work companies in technology, IT services, and consulting. It will adopt a product-led growth strategy with a classic land-and-expand model.

The platform will remain model-agnostic, allowing enterprises to choose between proprietary and open-source large language models. Neo will not build its own foundation model.

Turakhia said the $30 million commitment should fund Neo for more than two years. The bulk of capital will be deployed into engineering and go-to-market functions.

India will remain the center of gravity for engineering and product development. Turakhia noted that India still trails Silicon Valley in the cultural integration of AI into everyday work, though the gap is closing quickly.

WHAT IS NEO AND WHO IS BUILDING IT?
NEO IS AN AI-NATIVE WORKPLACE PLATFORM BEING BUILT BY SERIAL ENTREPRENEUR BHAVIN TURAKHIA, FOUNDER OF FINTECH UNICORN ZETA. HE HAS INVESTED $30 MILLION OF HIS OWN CAPITAL INTO THE VENTURE, WHICH IS HIS FIFTH STARTUP AFTER DIRECTI, RADIX, TITAN, AND ZETA.
WHAT ARE THE KEY FEATURES OF THE NEO PLATFORM?
NEO INCLUDES FOUR CORE MODULES: 'FRIDAY' (AN AI ASSISTANT CONNECTED TO OVER 1,000 EXTERNAL APPLICATIONS), 'TASKET' (FOR PROJECT MANAGEMENT), 'STUDIO' (FOR DOCUMENTS, SPREADSHEETS, AND DIAGRAMS), AND 'DRIVE' (A COLLABORATIVE FILE-SHARING WORKSPACE). THE PLATFORM IS DESIGNED FOR HUMANS AND AI AGENTS TO WORK TOGETHER SEAMLESSLY.
WHEN WILL NEO BE AVAILABLE TO ENTERPRISE CUSTOMERS?
NEO PLANS TO BEGIN INVITE-ONLY EXTERNAL DEPLOYMENTS BY THE END OF AUGUST 2026. A BROADER PUBLIC LAUNCH IS SCHEDULED FOR JANUARY 2027. THE PLATFORM WILL INITIALLY TARGET MID-MARKET KNOWLEDGE-WORK COMPANIES IN TECHNOLOGY, IT SERVICES, AND CONSULTING.
WHO ARE NEO'S COMPETITORS AND HOW DOES IT DIFFERENTIATE?
NEO WILL COMPETE WITH LEGACY PRODUCTIVITY SUITES FROM MICROSOFT, GOOGLE, AND DOMESTIC PLAYER ZOHO. TURAKHIA'S KEY DIFFERENTIATION IS THAT NEO IS BUILT AS AI-NATIVE FROM THE GROUND UP, RATHER THAN HAVING AI FEATURES BOLTED ONTO EXISTING TOOLS. THE PLATFORM IS ALSO MODEL-AGNOSTIC, ALLOWING ENTERPRISES TO CHOOSE THEIR PREFERRED LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS.

BHAVIN TURAKHIA HAS SELF-FUNDED NEO WITH $30 MILLION OF HIS OWN CAPITAL. HE ESTIMATES THIS WILL PROVIDE MORE THAN TWO YEARS OF RUNWAY BEFORE THE COMPANY NEEDS TO CONSIDER EXTERNAL FUNDRAISING. THE CAPITAL WILL BE PRIMARILY ALLOCATED TO ENGINEERING AND GO-TO-MARKET FUNCTIONS. TURAKHIA HAS HISTORICALLY SELF-FUNDED HIS VENTURES, HAVING PREVIOUSLY SOLD HIS EARLY COMPANIES FOR $160 MILLION IN 2014.
