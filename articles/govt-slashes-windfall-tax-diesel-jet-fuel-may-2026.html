Govt Slashes Windfall Tax on Diesel, Jet Fuel From May 1, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The government has slashed the Special Additional Excise Duty on diesel exports to ₹23 per litre from ₹55.5 per litre, effective May 1, 2026.
  • 02/Aviation turbine fuel export duty was reduced to ₹33 per litre from ₹42 per litre while the road cess on diesel exports was set to nil.
  • 03/Domestic LPG rates were held unchanged but commercial LPG prices were hiked by ₹993 on average across major cities.

The government cut the windfall gains tax on diesel and aviation turbine fuel exports from May 1, 2026. The Special Additional Excise Duty on diesel was slashed by 58.6% to ₹23 per litre from ₹55.5 per litre.

The road and infrastructure cess on diesel exports was also set at nil for the fortnight. The duty on petrol exports will remain unchanged at nil.

Aviation turbine fuel export duty was cut by 21.4% to ₹33 per litre from ₹42 per litre. The rates are reviewed fortnightly based on average international crude and product prices.

Export levies on petrol, diesel and ATF were introduced on March 27, 2026. The Finance Ministry said the measures were designed to ensure domestic availability amid the West Asia crisis.

Domestic LPG rates have been kept unchanged despite pressure to raise prices. The government said approximately 80% of petroleum products saw no change in prices.

Commercial LPG cylinder prices were increased by ₹993 on average. In Delhi, the 19-kg cylinder now costs ₹3,071.50, up from ₹2,078.50 earlier.

This marks the third hike in commercial LPG since February 28 when the Iran war began. "Price revisions have been limited to select industrial segments," the Ministry said.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE NEW WINDFALL TAX ON DIESEL EXPORTS EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2026?
THE SPECIAL ADDITIONAL EXCISE DUTY ON EXPORT OF DIESEL HAS BEEN REDUCED TO ₹23 PER LITRE FROM ₹55.5 PER LITRE EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2026. THE ROAD AND INFRASTRUCTURE CESS ON DIESEL EXPORTS IS SET AT NIL.
HOW MUCH IS THE WINDFALL TAX ON AVIATION TURBINE FUEL EXPORTS AFTER THE REVISION?
THE SAED ON EXPORT OF AVIATION TURBINE FUEL HAS BEEN REDUCED TO ₹33 PER LITRE FROM ₹42 PER LITRE WITH EFFECT FROM MAY 1, 2026. THIS REPRESENTS A 21.4% REDUCTION.
WHAT IS THE STATUS OF DOMESTIC AND COMMERCIAL LPG PRICES?
DOMESTIC LPG RATES HAVE BEEN KEPT UNCHANGED. COMMERCIAL LPG PRICES HAVE BEEN INCREASED BY ₹993 ON AVERAGE, WITH DELHI NOW AT ₹3,071.50 AND MUMBAI AT ₹3,024.
WHEN WERE THE EXPORT LEVIES ON PETROLEUM PRODUCTS FIRST INTRODUCED?
EXPORT LEVIES ON PETROL, DIESEL AND AVIATION TURBINE FUEL WERE INTRODUCED ON MARCH 27, 2026 TO ENSURE DOMESTIC AVAILABILITY AMID THE WEST ASIA CRISIS.
HOW OFTEN ARE THESE WINDFALL TAX RATES REVIEWED?
THE RATES ARE REVIEWED ON A FORTNIGHTLY BASIS BASED ON AVERAGE INTERNATIONAL PRICES OF CRUDE OIL, PETROL, DIESEL AND ATF PREVAILING SINCE THE LAST REVIEW.
