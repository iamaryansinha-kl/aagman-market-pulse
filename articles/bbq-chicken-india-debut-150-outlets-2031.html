bb.q Chicken India Debut: Korean Chain Targets 150 Outlets by 2031

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Genesis bb.q group's bb.q Chicken will open its first two outlets in Bengaluru in June 2026, with a master franchise deal via SrikG Group
  • 02/The chain targets 30 stores by 2027 and 150 by 2031, with SrikG investing Rs 15 crore in year one and Rs 500-600 crore over five years
  • 03/India QSR market is worth $30.37 billion in 2026; Korean food orders grew 17-fold between 2020 and 2024 per Zomato data

Genesis BBQ Group's flagship brand bb.q Chicken is entering India with two outlets in Bengaluru in June 2026. The Vellore-based SrikG Group is the master franchisee.

A Hyderabad outlet follows in July. Stores in Chennai, Kochi, Vizag, and Vellore will push the count to 7-8 by year-end 2026.

The company plans 30 stores by 2027 and 150 by 2031. SrikG will invest Rs 15 crore in year one and Rs 500-600 crore across five years.

Globally, bb.q Chicken runs 3,500 outlets across 57 countries. It has over 2,300 stores in South Korea and close to 500 in North America.

"If there are three strongest countries in the world, it would be the US, China, and India," said Honggeun Yoon, Chairman of Genesis BBQ Group. He cited India's young population as the key driver.

Duhyeon Yun (Chris), Global CEO, said India could contribute 15-20% of the group's global business within five years. The chain's pricing will sit slightly above KFC but below Popeyes.

The India rollout will start with company-owned stores for year one before shifting to franchisees. Only 11 of bb.q Chicken's 2,300 South Korean stores are corporate-owned.

Batter and sauces will be imported from Genesis BBQ's Asia Pacific halal centre initially. Local production is planned within two to three years.

About 20% of the menu will be localised for Indian palates. The brand has also developed a plant-based chicken product for India's vegetarian consumers.

The timing aligns with a broader Korean food surge. Korean food orders on Zomato grew 17-fold between 2020 and 2024. FMCG players like Nestle, HUL, and ITC have launched Korean-inspired products.

The Indian QSR market is valued at $30.37 billion in 2026 and is projected to reach $47.28 billion by 2031 at a 9.26% CAGR. Competition is fierce from KFC, McDonald's, and local players.

bb.q Chicken fries its chicken in olive oil using recipes from its proprietary Chicken University R&D facility in Seoul. The brand gained visibility via Korean dramas on Netflix.
