HCL Tech Slumps 10% After Cutting FY27 Revenue Guidance to 1-4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HCL Tech shares crashed 10% after the company cut FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1-4% from an earlier 3-6% range
  • 02/Q4 FY26 revenue missed estimates at $3,764 million, down 0.76% QoQ on weak software seasonality
  • 03/Multiple brokerages including CLSA and Jefferies downgraded the stock following cautious management commentary

HCL Technologies Ltd reported its Q4 FY26 earnings on April 21, 2026. The results triggered a 10% sell-off in the stock on April 22, the sharpest single-day decline in over two years.

The company cut its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1-4% in constant currency terms. This is down from the 3-6% range that brokerages had expected.

Q4 FY26 revenue came in at $3,764 million, a 0.76% sequential decline. The miss was attributed to seasonality in the high-margin software business, which dropped 22-23% QoQ.

The IT services segment grew 1-1.5% sequentially, in line with expectations. ER&D services remained stable on steady deal execution in BFSI and high-tech verticals.

CLSA downgraded HCL Tech to "hold" from "buy" and cut its target price to ₹1,320. Jefferies maintained an "underperform" rating with a ₹1,260 target.

Management warned that artificial intelligence adoption could lead to 2-3% gross deflation in billing rates. They expect higher volumes to offset this impact over time.

HCL Tech shares closed at ₹1,293.40, down 10.2% on April 22. The stock has fallen 28% from its 52-week high and is trading near a two-year low.
