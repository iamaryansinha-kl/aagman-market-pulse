Meesho Shares Drop 4% After Rs 900 Crore Block Deal; UBS, Ventura, CLSA Diverge on Outlook

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Meesho shares fell nearly 4% on Wednesday, giving up part of Tuesday's sharp gains. The decline came as a large block deal worth around Rs 900 crore was reported on the BSE.

As many as 3.86 crore Meesho shares changed hands in a block deal at Rs 233 apiece, taking the total value of the transaction to Rs 899.71 crore.

The stock dropped to an intraday low of Rs 230.94 per share on the NSE in morning trade. This came a day after the stock surged nearly 10% following brokerage firm UBS raising its target price by 24% to Rs 260.

UBS Maintains Buy

UBS maintained its Buy rating on Meesho. The brokerage said its target price hike reflects higher medium-term growth estimates and a stronger margin trajectory.

UBS raised its FY29-31 Net Merchandise Value estimates by 7-18%, with a similar increase in contribution profit estimates and a 20-40% increase in EBITDA estimates.

The higher forecasts reflect continued growth in sellers, which rose 81% year-on-year to 1.04 million in Q1FY27. Buyers grew 29% YoY to 274 million. Rapid expansion in SKUs and logistics partners is also contributing.

UBS said the larger increase in EBITDA reflects a stronger medium-term margin trajectory, driven by improving ads monetisation and logistics economics.

Ventura Bullish, CLSA Bearish

Ventura Securities initiated coverage on Meesho last month with a Buy call and a target price of Rs 278. The brokerage said India's e-commerce market is entering a structural growth phase, helped by internet penetration, digital payments and online retail adoption across Tier II, Tier III and rural India.

CLSA, however, maintained its Underperform rating and target price of Rs 150. The brokerage said the stock already reflects overly optimistic expectations for advertising revenue, order growth and logistics savings.

The divergence in views reflects uncertainty about Meesho's path to profitability and the sustainability of its growth trajectory in India's competitive e-commerce market.
