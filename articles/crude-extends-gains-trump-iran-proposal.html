Crude Extends Gains as Trump Weighs Iran's Latest Peace Proposal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent crude climbs to $109.30 per barrel on April 28, 2026, extending weekly gains of approximately 17% as markets assess Iran peace prospects
  • 02/President Trump convened a high-level meeting to evaluate Tehran's proposal, though US officials remain skeptical of Iran's terms
  • 03/Goldman Sachs raised Q4 2026 Brent forecast to $90/bbl and warned prices could approach $120 if Hormuz blockade persists

Brent crude futures climbed to $109.30 per barrel on April 28, 2026. West Texas Intermediate rose to $97.28 per barrel. Both benchmarks extended sharp weekly gains.

The rally follows President Donald Trump convening a high-level meeting to review Iran's latest proposal. Tehran signalled via Pakistan that hostilities could cease under specific conditions.

Iran's terms include lifting the US naval blockade, a new Hormuz transit framework, and assurances against future military strikes. Washington remains skeptical of the offer.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio stated America will not accept Iran retaining control over Hormuz passage. The Strait remains largely blocked after nine weeks of conflict.

Goldman Sachs raised its Q4 2026 Brent forecast to $90 per barrel and WTI to $83. The bank warned oil could approach $120 if the stalemate continues.

"The economic risks are larger than our crude base case alone suggests because of the net upside risks to oil prices," said Goldman Sachs analyst Daan Struyven.

Brent has surged roughly 72% over the past year. The Strait of Hormuz typically carries about a fifth of global oil and LNG flows.

Investors are also monitoring central bank meetings and Wall Street earnings this week. Any escalation in the Hormuz standoff could trigger further supply-driven inflation.
