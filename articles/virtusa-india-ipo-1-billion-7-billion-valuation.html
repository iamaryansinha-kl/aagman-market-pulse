Virtusa Eyes $1 Billion India IPO, $7 Billion Valuation Target

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Virtusa Corp, owned by Swedish PE firm EQT, is planning an India IPO that could raise at least $1 billion and value the company at $7 billion or more
  • 02/The IT services firm employs 30,000 people across 32 countries; India already has delivery centres in five cities including Hyderabad and Bengaluru
  • 03/A road show in H2 2026 will determine the listing venue, deal size and timing; Citigroup, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley are advising

Virtusa Corp is planning an India IPO that could raise at least $1 billion. The EQT-owned IT firm could be valued at $7 billion or more.

Four sources with knowledge of the matter confirmed the plan. A road show in the second half of 2026 will decide the listing venue and timing.

Citigroup, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley have been appointed as advisors. More banks may join the syndicate as the deal progresses.

Founded in 1996 and headquartered in Massachusetts, Virtusa employs 30,000 people globally. India delivery centres operate in Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Gurugram.

At $1 billion, the listing would be India's biggest IPO so far in 2026. India has already raised $2.75 billion across 64 listings this year, per LSEG data.

EQT acquired control of Virtusa in 2022 through its purchase of Baring Private Equity Asia. Baring had taken Virtusa private from Nasdaq in 2021.

EQT manages around 270 billion euros ($316 billion) in assets. It recently raised $15.6 billion for its latest Asia private equity fund.

A Virtusa listing would give EQT an exit from a major Asia technology investment. Global PE firms face rising pressure from investors to return capital.
