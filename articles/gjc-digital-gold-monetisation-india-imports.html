GJC Urges Digital Gold Monetisation To Cut India's $69 Billion Import Bill

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gem and Jewellery Domestic Council proposes shifting gold monetisation scheme to digital ecosystem, converting physical gold into dematerialised bank balances
  • 02/India's gold imports surged 29% to $69 billion in Apr-Feb FY26, with gold accounting for over 5% of total imports and widening the trade deficit
  • 03/GJC Chairman Rajesh Rokde confirms ongoing engagement with RBI and Ministry of Finance to build a regulator-aligned, scalable framework

The Gem and Jewellery Domestic Council of India has urged the Centre to shift the Gold Monetisation Scheme onto a digital platform. The proposal would convert physical gold into dematerialised balances held within the banking system.

The revamped framework targets idle gold assets, including bullion, coins and jewellery. Investors would earn interest without selling their holdings.

India's gold imports jumped 29% to $69 billion during April-February FY26. Gold accounts for over 5% of total imports and widens the trade deficit.

The Current Account Deficit rose to $13.2 billion, or 1.3% of GDP, in the December quarter. Enhanced domestic gold mobilisation could reduce import dependence.

"The revamped GMS framework is designed to be practical, scalable, and fully aligned with regulatory expectations," said Avinash Gupta, Vice Chairman, GJC.

GJC Chairman Rajesh Rokde confirmed active engagement with RBI and Ministry of Finance. The Council aims to build a future-ready gold monetisation framework.

Past monetisation attempts have struggled due to fears over scrutiny of gold sources. The digital model seeks to address compliance concerns while unlocking productive value.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS GJC PROPOSING FOR THE GOLD MONETISATION SCHEME?
THE GEM AND JEWELLERY DOMESTIC COUNCIL OF INDIA HAS PROPOSED TRANSITIONING THE EXISTING GOLD MONETISATION SCHEME TO A DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM. PHYSICAL GOLD WOULD BE CONVERTED INTO DEMATERIALISED GOLD BALANCES HELD WITHIN THE BANKING SYSTEM THROUGH STRUCTURED ACCOUNT MECHANISMS.
HOW MUCH DID INDIA'S GOLD IMPORTS RISE IN FY26?
INDIA'S GOLD IMPORTS INCREASED BY 28.73% TO $69 BILLION DURING APRIL-FEBRUARY FY26, COMPARED WITH $53.52 BILLION IN THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR. GOLD ALONE ACCOUNTS FOR OVER 5% OF INDIA'S TOTAL IMPORTS.
WHO ARE THE KEY GJC OFFICIALS DRIVING THIS PROPOSAL?
RAJESH ROKDE, CHAIRMAN OF GJC, AND AVINASH GUPTA, VICE CHAIRMAN OF GJC, ARE LEADING THE ENGAGEMENTS WITH THE RBI AND MINISTRY OF FINANCE. ROKDE CONFIRMED ACTIVE DIALOGUE WITH REGULATORS TO BUILD A FUTURE-READY FRAMEWORK.
WHAT BENEFITS DOES THE DIGITAL GOLD MONETISATION SCHEME OFFER?
THE SCHEME ALLOWS INVESTORS TO EARN RETURNS ON IDLE GOLD ASSETS INCLUDING BULLION, COINS AND JEWELLERY. IT CONVERTS NON-YIELDING ASSETS INTO INTEREST-BEARING INSTRUMENTS AND CAN REDUCE IMPORT DEPENDENCE AND MODERATE THE CURRENT ACCOUNT DEFICIT.
WHAT CHALLENGES HAVE PREVIOUS GOLD MONETISATION SCHEMES FACED?
PAST ATTEMPTS AT GOLD MONETISATION HAVE YIELDED LIMITED SUCCESS DUE TO HOUSEHOLD APPREHENSION ABOUT SCRUTINY OF GOLD SOURCES AND POTENTIAL TAX OR ENFORCEMENT EXPOSURE. THE NEW DIGITAL FRAMEWORK AIMS TO STRENGTHEN FORMALISATION, IMPROVE COMPLIANCE AND ENHANCE MARKET EFFICIENCY.
