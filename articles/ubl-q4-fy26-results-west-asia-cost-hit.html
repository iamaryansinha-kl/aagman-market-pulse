UBL Shares Slump After Weak Q4; Company Flags ₹500 Cr West Asia Hit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/United Breweries Ltd Q4 FY26 net profit rose 4% to ₹102 crore but revenue declined 3.1% to ₹2,250 crore
  • 02/Management flagged ₹400-500 crore cost headwind from West Asia crisis over next 2-3 quarters
  • 03/UBL shares hit a fresh 52-week low of ₹1,382 on May 6, 2026, down 34.4% over the past year

United Breweries Ltd (NSE: UNITDSPR) reported Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹102 crore, up 4.3% year-on-year. Revenue from operations declined 3.1% to ₹2,250 crore against ₹2,323 crore in Q4 FY25.

The Kingfisher beer maker declared a final dividend of ₹10 per share for FY26. The payout will be made on or before September 10, 2026, subject to shareholder approval.

EBITDA for the quarter fell roughly 25% to ₹139 crore from ₹187 crore last year. The EBITDA margin contracted to 6.2% from 8% in the year-ago period.

The modest profit growth was flattered by a ₹74 crore exceptional gain from the sale of freehold land. Excluding this, profit before tax before exceptional items fell 68% year-on-year.

Management flagged an estimated cost headwind of ₹400-500 crore over the next two to three quarters. The impact stems from West Asia-linked supply disruptions, rising crude oil and gas prices, and rupee pressure.

CEO Vivek Gupta said the company has plans to mitigate at least ₹200-250 crore of this impact. Measures include productivity drives, selective pricing actions, and reducing trade spends in certain markets.

UBL shares touched a fresh 52-week low of ₹1,382 on May 6, 2026. The stock has shed nearly 34.4% over the past year, sharply underperforming the Nifty FMCG index which fell 9.9%.

Gross margins expanded 332 basis points to 45.4%, but this was offset by a 27% jump in ad spends and a 395% surge in finance costs. Beer category volume returned to 10% growth, with premium volumes up 16%.
