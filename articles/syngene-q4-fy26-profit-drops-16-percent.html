Syngene Q4 FY26 Profit Drops 16% to ₹153 Cr on Exceptional Charges

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Syngene International Q4 FY26 PAT declined 16% YoY to ₹153 crore, missing analyst estimates of ₹180-210 crore
  • 02/Company booked ₹25 crore exceptional expense for employee termination; revenue grew 2% to ₹1,037 crore
  • 03/Siddharth Mittal appointed MD and CEO from July 1, 2026; FY27 guidance and biologics client recovery key to watch

Syngene International Ltd (NSE: SYNGENE) reported a 16% year-on-year decline in profit after tax to ₹153 crore for Q4 FY26. The board announced results on April 29, 2026.

The decline was driven by ₹25 crore in exceptional expenses. These were net of tax and linked to employee termination benefits.

Quarterly revenue stood at ₹1,037 crore, up 2% year-on-year. Full-year revenue grew 3% with an EBITDA margin of 25%.

Managing Director Peter Bains said the results reflect specific impact from a single large-molecule biologics client. He noted underlying business momentum remained steady.

Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw transitioned to Executive Chairperson on April 1, 2026. Siddharth Mittal will succeed Bains as MD and CEO effective July 1, 2026.

Mittal joins from Biocon Ltd where he served as MD and CEO. The leadership change comes as Syngene invests in peptides and ADC capabilities.

Shaw said biotech funding remains discerning but Syngene's end-to-end model offers resilience. The company is building AI and digital capabilities for long-term growth.

Analysts had estimated Q4 PAT at ₹180-210 crore. The miss was largely due to the one-off exceptional charge and client-specific headwinds.
