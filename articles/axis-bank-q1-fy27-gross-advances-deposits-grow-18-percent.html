Axis Bank Q1 FY27: Gross Advances Surge 18.8% to ₹12.73 Lakh Crore, Deposits Up 18.2%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Axis Bank gross advances stood at ₹12.73 lakh crore as of 30 June 2026, up 18.8% YoY from ₹10.72 lakh crore in June 2025
  • 02/Total deposits increased 18.2% YoY to ₹13.73 lakh crore from ₹11.62 lakh crore; up 2.8% QoQ from ₹13.36 lakh crore
  • 03/CASA deposits grew 11.4% YoY to ₹5.22 lakh crore but declined 1.4% sequentially; term deposits at ₹8.51 lakh crore (+22.8% YoY)

Axis Bank has reported its Q1 FY27 business update. Gross advances stood at ₹12.73 lakh crore as of 30 June 2026. This marks an 18.8% year-on-year rise from ₹10.72 lakh crore in June 2025.

On a sequential basis, gross advances grew 2.3% from ₹12.44 lakh crore in the quarter ended March 2026.

Total deposits increased 18.2% year-on-year to ₹13.73 lakh crore from ₹11.62 lakh crore. Total deposits were up 2.8% quarter-on-quarter from ₹13.36 lakh crore in the March quarter.

Current account and savings account deposits grew 11.4% YoY to ₹5.22 lakh crore. However, CASA deposits declined 1.4% sequentially.

Term deposits in the June 2026 quarter stood at ₹8.51 lakh crore. This represents a 22.8% year-on-year increase and a 5.5% quarter-on-quarter rise.

Axis Bank is the third largest private sector lender in India. The bank's share price has risen 7% in one month and 12% in three months. The stock has climbed 6% in six months and 15% in one year.

Over the longer term, Axis Bank shares have surged 37% in three years and delivered 79% returns over the past five years.

On Friday, Axis Bank share price ended 1.56% lower at ₹1,342.50 apiece on the BSE.
