Crude Above $110 Rattles Dalal Street; Rupee Sinks to Record Low

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Crude oil topped $110/barrel after drone strikes near UAE nuclear plants, stoking supply fears and inflation concerns in oil-importing India
  • 02/The Indian rupee closed at a lifetime low of 96.35/$ and 10-year bond yields surged to 7.13%, highest in nearly seven weeks
  • 03/Nifty 50 ended flat at 23,649.95 as technology shares offset an early 1.4% plunge; rupee could weaken further to 98/$

Crude oil crossed $110 per barrel on Monday. Drone strikes near UAE nuclear plants stoked fears of further supply disruptions in the region.

The International Energy Agency warned that depleting oil inventories are shrinking at an accelerated pace. This could raise prices for food and fertilisers too.

India's rupee plunged to a lifetime low of 96.35 against the dollar. It touched an intraday low of 96.39 before recovering slightly at the close.

The 10-year benchmark bond yield jumped to 7.13%, the highest in nearly seven weeks. It was up from the previous close of 7.06%.

"The twin pressure of a higher oil import bill and foreign portfolio outflows continues to drive dollar demand," said Anindya Banerjee, head of currency research at Kotak Securities.

Banerjee added: "Every week we see an uptick in the rupee by 50-60 paise. As 96/$ is breached, the play is now at 98/$."

The NSE Nifty 50 rose 0.03% to close at 23,649.95 after plunging 1.4% in early trade. Technology shares prevented a broader equities rout.

Global bond yields hovered near multiyear highs. Japanese 30-year yields surged to their highest since the maturity was first sold in 1999.

WHAT HAPPENED TO CRUDE OIL PRICES ON MONDAY?
CRUDE OIL PRICES CROSSED $110 PER BARREL AFTER DRONE STRIKES NEAR NUCLEAR PLANTS IN THE UAE. THE INTERNATIONAL ENERGY AGENCY ALSO WARNED THAT DEPLETING COMMERCIAL OIL INVENTORIES ARE SHRINKING AT AN ACCELERATED PACE.
WHAT LEVEL DID THE RUPEE HIT AGAINST THE DOLLAR?
THE INDIAN RUPEE CLOSED AT A LIFETIME LOW OF 96.35 AGAINST THE US DOLLAR ON MONDAY. IT TOUCHED AN INTRADAY LOW OF 96.39 BEFORE RECOVERING SLIGHTLY.
HOW DID BOND YIELDS REACT TO THE MARKET VOLATILITY?
THE 10-YEAR BENCHMARK BOND YIELD SURGED TO 7.13%, THE HIGHEST LEVEL IN NEARLY SEVEN WEEKS. THIS WAS UP FROM THE PREVIOUS CLOSE OF 7.06%.
HOW DID THE EQUITY MARKETS PERFORM DESPITE THE RUPEE AND BOND SELLOFF?
THE NSE NIFTY 50 ROSE 0.03%, OR 6.45 POINTS, TO CLOSE AT 23,649.95. TECHNOLOGY SHARES PREVENTED A BROADER ROUT AFTER THE INDEX HAD PLUNGED 1.4% IN EARLY TRADE.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR THE RUPEE IN THE NEAR TERM?
ACCORDING TO ANINDYA BANERJEE OF KOTAK SECURITIES, THE RUPEE COULD WEAKEN FURTHER TO 98 PER DOLLAR. HE NOTED THAT EVERY WEEK THE RUPEE SEES AN UPTICK OF 50-60 PAISE AGAINST THE GREENBACK.
