Kotak Mahindra Bank Appoints Anup Kumar Saha as New MD & CEO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kotak Mahindra Bank appointed Anup Kumar Saha as its new MD & CEO on October 1, 2026, ending a closely watched succession search
  • 02/He succeeds Ashok Vaswani, who said in June 2026 he would not seek reappointment beyond his term ending December 31, 2026
  • 03/The appointment is subject to customary RBI clearance; Kotak shares rose over 2% on the announcement

Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd (NSE: KOTAKBANK) has appointed Anup Kumar Saha as its new Managing Director & CEO. The bank announced the decision on Wednesday, October 1, 2026, according to an ET Markets report.

The appointment ends a leadership search that began in June 2026. That is when incumbent Ashok Vaswani told the board he would not seek reappointment beyond his current term.

Vaswani's term as MD & CEO runs through December 31, 2026. He took charge on January 1, 2024, succeeding founder Uday Kotak after more than two decades of founder-led stewardship.

The appointment is subject to customary clearance from the Reserve Bank of India. The regulator has not yet issued a public approval letter setting an effective date for Saha to take charge.

Saha is an insider, but a relatively new one. He joined Kotak Mahindra Bank in January 2026 as a Whole-time Director, with RBI approving the role on March 6, 2026.

At the bank, he oversees consumer banking, marketing and data analytics. He was earlier designated President of the bank and sits at the centre of its customer-first and digital push.

Before Kotak, Saha spent about eight years at Bajaj Finance and roughly 14 years at ICICI Bank. His Bajaj Finance stint included a brief four-month tenure as MD & CEO in 2025.

He is an alumnus of IIT Kharagpur and IIM Lucknow. In total, he brings more than 32 years of experience, including 25 years in financial services.

The bank had recommended two internal candidates to the RBI in late August 2026. Executive directors Anup Saha and Paritosh Kashyap were both submitted, and Saha was ultimately picked.

Under RBI norms, banks must propose at least two candidates for the top job. The regulator can approve one name or ask for alternatives.

The choice of an internal candidate follows market expectations. "If Saha is the internal choice, the succession path is already substantially de-risked," brokerage Nomura said in a June 2026 note.

Kotak Mahindra Bank shares rose more than 2% following the October 1 announcement. The stock had fallen when Vaswani's exit first surfaced in June.

The lender is India's fourth-largest private bank. Under Vaswani, it had set a target of becoming the third-largest private lender by after-tax profit.

That growth ambition now passes to Saha. Investors will watch whether the leadership change alters the bank's credit growth, deposit strategy and margin trajectory.

The transition also caps a turbulent chapter for the franchise. It moved from two decades under Uday Kotak to a short three-year Vaswani term, and now to its third CEO in three years.
