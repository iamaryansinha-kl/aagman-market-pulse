Max Healthcare Q4 Results: PAT Rises 7% YoY to Rs 342 Crore, Revenue Up 12%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Max Healthcare Q4FY26 consolidated PAT rises 7% YoY to Rs 342 crore, up 14% sequentially from Q3FY26
  • 02/Revenue from operations grows 12% YoY to Rs 2,143 crore; network EBITDA up 8% to Rs 682 crore
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of Rs 2 per share; net debt reduced to Rs 1,908 crore from Rs 2,166 crore

Max Healthcare Institute Ltd (NSE: MAXHEALTH) reported consolidated net profit of Rs 342 crore for Q4FY26. This marks a 7% increase over Rs 319 crore in Q4FY25.

Revenue from operations rose 12% YoY to Rs 2,143 crore. The topline also grew nearly 4% sequentially from Rs 2,068 crore in Q3FY26.

The company's network PAT stood at Rs 387 crore, up 3% YoY and 12% QoQ. For the full year FY26, network PAT reached Rs 1,631 crore, a 22% jump over FY25.

Network gross revenue was Rs 2,664 crore, reflecting 10% YoY growth. International patient revenue contributed Rs 227 crore, accounting for 9% of hospital revenue.

Network operating EBITDA grew 8% YoY to Rs 682 crore. The EBITDA margin stood at 26.8%, compared to 27.2% in Q4FY25 and 26.1% in Q3FY26.

Clinician costs rose 230 bps YoY due to aggressive hiring of clinical talent. This investment supports future growth and upcoming capacity expansions across the network.

Max Lab revenue was Rs 52 crore, up 14% YoY, while Max@Home gross revenue reached Rs 73 crore, marking a strong 30% YoY increase.

Free cash from operations surged to Rs 581 crore and net debt declined to Rs 1,908 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of Rs 2 per equity share subject to AGM approval.
