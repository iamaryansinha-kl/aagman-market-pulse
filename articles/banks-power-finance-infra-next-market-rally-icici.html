Banks, Power Finance and Infra to Lead Next Market Rally: ICICI Securities

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICICI Securities' Pankaj Pandey says banks, power finance and infrastructure will lead the next leg of the Nifty rally
  • 02/HDFC Bank growth improved from 5.5% to 12% YoY; REC target price ₹520; PFC-REC combined AUM ₹11.5 lakh crore
  • 03/RBI Governor signals India close to global bond index inclusion; positive news possible in July 2026

India's banking sector is poised to lead the next phase of the equity market rally. Pankaj Pandey, Head of Research at ICICI Securities, told ET Now that credit growth is improving and deposit pressures are easing.

Concerns that weighed on the banking sector over the past year are fading. Lower 10-year bond yields, cooling foreign portfolio investor selling, and improving liquidity conditions are expected to support both private and public sector banks.

"The credit growth has been getting better. The challenges were on the deposit side, but with RBI relaxation and lower 10-year bond yields, the outlook has improved. We do not see much stress on the asset quality side, so banking is in a pretty good shape," Pandey said.

HDFC Bank has emerged as a key beneficiary. Growth improved from about 5.5% to nearly 12%, and Pandey expects the bank to grow at 16% to 17% this year. With a book value of around ₹444, removing the governance overhang could significantly improve valuations.

Non-banking financial companies are also likely to gain from a softer interest rate environment. Pandey noted that RBI Governor comments on India's progress towards meeting global bond index inclusion conditions could further support bond markets. "In the month of July, we could possibly hear some positive news on that front."

Power financiers remain attractive long-term bets. The PFC-REC combined entity could have an AUM book of around ₹11.5 lakh crore. Pandey's preference remains REC with a target price of ₹520. The 30% holding company discount assigned to PFC should eventually disappear.

Transmission infrastructure offers the biggest opportunity within the power sector. Pandey highlighted Siemens, Siemens Energy, CG Power and Kalpataru Power as key plays. Capacity constraints in renewable energy transmission should drive capex higher.

Astral remains the top pick in building materials with a target price of ₹1,900. The company aims to increase its adhesives market share from 11-12% to nearly 20%. The PVC business is also recovering with the Jal Jeevan Mission allocation outlook improving.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH SECTORS DOES PANKAJ PANDEY BELIEVE WILL LEAD THE NEXT MARKET RALLY?
PANKAJ PANDEY, HEAD OF RESEARCH AT ICICI SECURITIES, BELIEVES BANKING, POWER FINANCE AND INFRASTRUCTURE — SPECIFICALLY POWER TRANSMISSION — WILL LEAD THE NEXT LEG OF THE EQUITY MARKET RALLY. HE SAYS CREDIT GROWTH IS IMPROVING, DEPOSIT PRESSURES ARE EASING, AND ASSET QUALITY REMAINS STABLE.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR HDFC BANK ACCORDING TO PANDEY?
HDFC BANK'S GROWTH HAS IMPROVED FROM ABOUT 5.5% TO NEARLY 12% YEAR-ON-YEAR. PANDEY EXPECTS THE BANK TO GROW AT 16% TO 17% THIS YEAR, BROADLY IN LINE WITH THE INDUSTRY. WITH A BOOK VALUE OF AROUND ₹444, REMOVING THE GOVERNANCE OVERHANG COULD SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVE VALUATIONS AND PROVIDE MEANINGFUL SUPPORT TO THE NIFTY.
WHAT DOES PANDEY SAY ABOUT PFC AND REC?
THE PFC-REC COMBINED ENTITY COULD HAVE AN AUM BOOK OF AROUND ₹11.5 LAKH CRORE. PANDEY'S PREFERENCE REMAINS REC WITH A TARGET PRICE OF ₹520. THE 30% HOLDING COMPANY DISCOUNT ASSIGNED TO PFC SHOULD EVENTUALLY DISAPPEAR. BANKS WILL NOT HAVE THE CAPACITY TO FUND LARGE NUCLEAR PROJECTS IF CAPEX PICKS UP, SO PFC IS EXPECTED TO PLAY A CRUCIAL ROLE.
WHICH COMPANIES DOES PANDEY RECOMMEND IN POWER TRANSMISSION?
PANDEY IDENTIFIES POWER TRANSMISSION AS THE STRONGEST OPPORTUNITY IN THE POWER SECTOR DUE TO CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS. HE MENTIONS SIEMENS, SIEMENS ENERGY, CG POWER AND KALPATARU POWER AS KEY PLAYS. SWITCHGEAR OPPORTUNITIES ARE EXPECTED TO BE SIGNIFICANT, ESPECIALLY IN MEDIUM AND HIGH VOLTAGE EQUIPMENT.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR NBFCs AND WHAT COULD DRIVE BOND YIELDS LOWER?
NBFCs ARE LIKELY TO BENEFIT FROM A SOFTER INTEREST RATE ENVIRONMENT. PANDEY NOTED THAT THE RBI GOVERNOR MENTIONED MOST CONDITIONS FOR INCLUSION IN GLOBAL BOND INDICES HAVE BEEN MET. POSITIVE NEWS ON THIS FRONT COULD COME IN JULY 2026, WHICH WOULD BE BENEFICIAL FOR BOND YIELDS TO CORRECT FURTHER AND IS POSITIVE FOR THE ENTIRE NBFC SPACE.
