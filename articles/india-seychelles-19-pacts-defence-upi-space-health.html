India-Seychelles Sign 19 Pacts on Defence, UPI, Space, Health

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India and Seychelles unveil 19 outcomes on June 28, 2026, spanning defence, maritime security, UPI payments, space, and healthcare
  • 02/Bilateral trade reached $94 million in 2024; India is Seychelles' sixth-largest import partner with 6.3% share
  • 03/PM Modi is on a three-day state visit as Guest of Honour for National Day; 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations marked

India and Seychelles on Sunday unveiled 19 key outcomes following delegation-level talks. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and President Patrick Herminie signed the agreements in Victoria.

The pacts span defence, maritime security, digital payments, space, healthcare, agriculture, and education. They mark the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the two nations.

The outcomes include an extradition treaty, an agreement on peaceful uses of outer space, and an umbrella line of credit from the Export-Import Bank of India. An agreement was also signed for preliminary preparations for a new Seychelles National Hospital.

India will gift a fast patrol vessel to Seychelles. The Seychelles Defence Force will receive 10 utility vehicles and five Laser Radial class boats. The refit of PS Zoroaster for the Seychelles Coast Guard is complete.

A Dornier aircraft will be upgraded with a glass cockpit. Six ambulances, 500 metric tonnes of rice, and 8,500 metric tonnes of cement will also be handed over.

The National Payments Corporation of India signed a pact with the Central Bank of Seychelles to advance UPI-based digital payments. HLL Lifecare Ltd will supply affordable medicines under the Jan Aushadhi scheme.

Bilateral trade between India and Seychelles reached approximately $94 million in 2024. Seychelles imported goods worth $91.73 million from India. India ranks as the sixth-largest import partner for the archipelago.

The two countries also signed agreements on diplomatic training, agriculture research, and seafarer certification. Modi is on a three-day state visit and will participate in Seychelles' National Day celebrations as Guest of Honour.

WHAT ARE THE 19 OUTCOMES UNVEILED BY INDIA AND SEYCHELLES?
THE 19 OUTCOMES INCLUDE AN EXTRADITION TREATY, A SPACE COOPERATION AGREEMENT, A UPI-BASED DIGITAL PAYMENTS PACT, AN UMBRELLA LINE OF CREDIT FROM EXIM BANK, A FAST PATROL VESSEL GIFT, 10 UTILITY VEHICLES, 5 LASER RADIAL CLASS BOATS, REFIT OF PS ZOROASTER, DORNIER AIRCRAFT UPGRADE, 6 AMBULANCES, 500 METRIC TONNES OF RICE, 8,500 METRIC TONNES OF CEMENT, A NEW NATIONAL HOSPITAL AGREEMENT, DIPLOMATIC TRAINING PACT, AGRICULTURE RESEARCH DEAL, SEAFARER CERTIFICATION MOU, AND A PROFESSIONAL AND TECHNICAL EDUCATION CENTRE.
WHEN WERE THE 19 AGREEMENTS SIGNED BETWEEN INDIA AND SEYCHELLES?
THE 19 AGREEMENTS WERE UNVEILED ON SUNDAY, JUNE 28, 2026, FOLLOWING DELEGATION-LEVEL TALKS BETWEEN PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA MODI AND PRESIDENT PATRICK HERMINE IN VICTORIA, SEYCHELLES.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE UPI PAYMENTS AGREEMENT?
THE NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA INTERNATIONAL PAYMENTS LTD. SIGNED A PACT WITH THE CENTRAL BANK OF SEYCHELLES TO ADVANCE UPI-BASED DIGITAL PAYMENTS. THIS AIMS TO DEEPEN ECONOMIC AND TECHNOLOGICAL COOPERATION AND FACILITATE DIGITAL TRANSACTIONS IN THE ARCHIPELAGIC NATION.
HOW MUCH IS THE BILATERAL TRADE BETWEEN INDIA AND SEYCHELLES?
BILATERAL TRADE BETWEEN INDIA AND SEYCHELLES REACHED APPROXIMATELY $94 MILLION IN 2024. SEYCHELLES IMPORTED GOODS WORTH $91.73 MILLION FROM INDIA, WHILE EXPORTS TO INDIA TOTALED $2.46 MILLION.
