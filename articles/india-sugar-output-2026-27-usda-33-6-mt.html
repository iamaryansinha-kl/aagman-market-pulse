India Sugar Output to Jump 12% to 33.6 MT in 2026-27 on Better Monsoons: USDA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's sugar output for 2026-27 is forecast at 33.6 million tonnes, a 12% jump over current year's 30 million tonnes per USDA FAS New Delhi.
  • 02/Higher cane area (5.9 million hectares, +2%) and improved recovery rate (9.2% vs 8.3%) drive the increase after two years of favourable monsoons.
  • 03/For the first time in two years domestic production will exceed consumption (31 MT), ending the supply deficit and easing price pressures.

India's sugar production for the 2026-27 season starting October is projected to rise 12% to 33.6 million tonnes. The forecast comes from the Foreign Agricultural Service (FAS) New Delhi, the local office of the US Department of Agriculture (USDA).

The 33.6 million tonne figure on a raw value basis includes 31.4 million tonnes of crystal sugar and 62,000 tonnes of khandsari. Field surveys conducted in March 2026 across Maharashtra's cane-growing districts confirmed healthy crop stands.

The projected jump follows two consecutive years of favourable monsoons in 2024 and 2025. These restored groundwater levels and surface water reservoirs in Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Karnataka.

Sugarcane planted and harvested area for 2026-27 is projected at 5.9 million hectares, up 2% year-on-year. Cane production is likely to rise 2% to 463 million tonnes during the year.

The sugar recovery rate is forecast at 9.2%, up sharply from the current year's 8.3%. This reflects enhanced sucrose accumulation due to adequate soil moisture through critical growth stages.

For 2025-26, cane production has been revised downward to 455 million tonnes from an earlier 465 million tonnes. Excessive rainfall in late August and September 2025 disrupted crop development in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

Current year sugar production is revised to 30 million tonnes on a raw value basis, down 14% from the initial forecast of 35.2 million tonnes. Despite this, it still marks a 7% increase over 2024-25.

Trade body ISMA separately reported that 2025-26 sugar production registered a 7% increase to 27.52 million tonnes versus the previous year's 25.64 million tonnes.

Centrifugal sugar consumption for 2026-27 is forecast at 31 million tonnes on a raw value basis. This represents a 3% increase over the current year.

For the first time in two years domestic production will exceed consumption in 2026-27. This marks a significant shift in supply dynamics and should ensure adequate availability.

Government price support mechanisms including the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) and ethanol procurement guarantees under the National Biofuel Policy continue to incentivise cane cultivation over competing crops.

Khandsari and jaggery production is expected to rise 3% year-on-year. Growing consumer awareness of unrefined sweeteners is driving this shift toward traditional products.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE USDA FORECAST FOR INDIA'S SUGAR PRODUCTION IN 2026-27?
THE FOREIGN AGRICULTURAL SERVICE (FAS) NEW DELHI FORECASTS INDIA'S SUGAR PRODUCTION AT 33.6 MILLION TONNES ON A RAW VALUE BASIS FOR THE 2026-27 SEASON STARTING OCTOBER. THIS INCLUDES 31.4 MILLION TONNES OF CRYSTAL SUGAR AND 62,000 TONNES OF KHANDSARI.
HOW MUCH IS THE PRODUCTION INCREASE OVER THE CURRENT YEAR?
THE 33.6 MILLION TONNE FORECAST REPRESENTS A 12% INCREASE OVER THE CURRENT YEAR'S REVISED OUTPUT OF 30 MILLION TONNES. Cane production is projected at 463 MILLION TONNES, UP 2% FROM THE PREVIOUS YEAR.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE HIGHER SUGAR OUTPUT FORECAST?
TWO CONSECUTIVE YEARS OF FAVOURABLE MONSOONS (2024 AND 2025) RESTORED GROUNDWATER AND SURFACE WATER AVAILABILITY. THE SUGARCANE AREA IS PROJECTED AT 5.9 MILLION HECTARES (+2%) AND THE RECOVERY RATE IS EXPECTED TO IMPROVE TO 9.2% FROM 8.3%.
HOW DOES THE 2026-27 FORECAST COMPARE TO DOMESTIC CONSUMPTION?
DOMESTIC CONSUMPTION IS FORECAST AT 31 MILLION TONNES. FOR THE FIRST TIME IN TWO YEARS, PRODUCTION (33.6 MT) WILL EXCEED CONSUMPTION, ENSURING ADEQUATE SUPPLY AND EASING PRICE PRESSURES.
WHAT HAPPENED TO SUGAR PRODUCTION IN 2025-26?
THE 2025-26 ESTIMATE WAS REVISED DOWNWARD TO 30 MILLION TONNES FROM AN INITIAL FORECAST OF 35.2 MILLION TONNES. EXCESSIVE LATE-MONSOON RAIN IN AUGUST AND SEPTEMBER 2025 HURT CROP DEVELOPMENT IN MAHARASHTRA AND KARNATAKA, REDUCING YIELDS.
