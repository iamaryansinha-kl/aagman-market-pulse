RBI Launches 3 Key Surveys to Get Input for Monetary Policy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The Reserve Bank of India is gathering ground-level data.

The central bank has launched three key surveys to collect input for its upcoming monetary policy review. The surveys will gather data on inflation expectations, consumer confidence, and industrial outlook — all critical inputs for interest rate decisions.

The Inflation Expectations Survey of Households asks respondents about their views on current and future price levels. The data helps the RBI gauge whether the public expects inflation to rise, fall, or remain stable. Anchored inflation expectations are crucial for effective monetary policy.

The Consumer Confidence Survey measures how optimistic or pessimistic households are about their financial situation and the broader economy. Falling confidence can signal reduced spending, which affects growth and ultimately influences the central bank's policy stance.

The Industrial Outlook Survey collects views from manufacturing companies on production, orders, inventory, and employment. The data provides a forward-looking indicator of industrial activity, complementing lagging official statistics like the Index of Industrial Production.

The timing is significant. The RBI's next monetary policy meeting is scheduled for early August, and the June inflation data is expected to show price pressures above the 4% target. The survey data will help the Monetary Policy Committee assess whether the inflation spike is temporary or persistent.

The RBI has been conducting these surveys for years, but their importance has grown as the central bank shifted to an inflation-targeting framework. Survey data now plays a formal role in the MPC's deliberations.
