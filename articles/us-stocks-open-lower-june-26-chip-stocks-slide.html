US Stocks Open Lower: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq Fall as Chip Stocks Slide

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 116.9 points, or 0.23%, to 51,803.77 at the opening bell on June 26, 2026.
  • 02/The S&P 500 dropped 44.7 points (0.61%) to 7,312.74 and the Nasdaq Composite slid 253.2 points (1.00%) to 25,105.414.
  • 03/Chipmakers face renewed selling pressure after a stellar quarterly run, with investors questioning high valuations and massive AI spending.

Wall Street's main indexes opened lower on Friday, June 26, 2026. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 116.9 points, or 0.23%, to 51,803.77.

The S&P 500 declined 44.7 points, or 0.61%, to 7,312.74. The Nasdaq Composite dropped 253.2 points, or 1.00%, to 25,105.414.

Chipmakers led the decline after a stellar run this quarter. Investors are now questioning the high valuations of these companies.

Massive AI spending by companies is also under scrutiny. The selling pressure signals a cautious tone as the quarter concludes.

Technology stocks have rallied sharply in 2026 on AI-driven demand. The Nasdaq Composite has posted significant gains year-to-date.

The pullback reflects profit-taking amid elevated valuations. Chip stocks had surged on expectations of sustained AI investment.

The S&P 500 has gained approximately 8% so far in 2026. Analysts warn that future returns may slow after three strong years.

Investors are watching whether AI spending translates into revenue. The market remains sensitive to any signs of demand cooling.
