Venus Remedies Gets Argentina Nod for Ceftriaxone, Total Approvals Hit 39

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Venus Remedies secures marketing authorization from Argentina for its antibiotic Ceftriaxone, taking total global approvals to 39
  • 02/Argentina is Latin America's fourth-largest pharma market at USD 7.3 billion; India exported USD 17.5 million in pharma products there in 2024
  • 03/Latin American pharma market projected to grow from USD 91.85 billion in 2024 to USD 172.71 billion by 2034 at 6.52% CAGR

Venus Remedies has secured marketing authorization from Argentina's national regulatory authority for its antibiotic Ceftriaxone. The company announced the approval on Thursday.

The approval brings Venus Remedies' total marketing authorizations for Ceftriaxone to 39 globally. It marks a key milestone in the company's Latin America expansion strategy.

Argentina ranks as the fourth-largest pharmaceutical market in Latin America. The market was valued at approximately USD 7.3 billion in 2023.

Ceftriaxone is a cornerstone antibiotic for treating serious bacterial infections. It is used for meningitis, respiratory tract infections, bloodstream infections, and surgical infections.

"The approval of Ceftriaxone in Argentina represents an important step in strengthening our presence in the Latin American region," said Saransh Chaudhary, president, Global Critical Care, Venus Remedies Ltd.

India exported approximately USD 17.5 million worth of pharmaceutical products to Argentina in 2024. Latin America and the Caribbean accounted for 6.69 per cent of India's total pharma exports in FY 2024-25.

The broader Latin American pharmaceutical market is estimated at nearly USD 91.85 billion in 2024. It is projected to reach USD 172.71 billion by 2034, reflecting a CAGR of 6.52 per cent.

"Securing this marketing authorisation in Argentina is a strategically important milestone in our Latin America expansion journey," said Aditi K Chaudhary, president, International Business, Venus Remedies Ltd.
