13 BSE 500 Stocks Surge Up to 200% in 3 Months: 3 Turn Multibagger

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Thirteen BSE 500 stocks delivered 70-200% returns in three months, with HFCL leading at 194%
  • 02/BSE 500 index surged nearly 10% versus BSE Sensex's 4% gain, signalling broad market participation
  • 03/Three multibaggers — HFCL, Cemindia Projects, and Aditya Infotech — more than doubled investor wealth

Thirteen BSE 500 stocks delivered returns between 70% and 200% over the past three months. The rally was led by HFCL, which surged 194% from Rs 70 to Rs 205.

Three stocks turned multibaggers. Cemindia Projects gained 126%, climbing from Rs 538 to Rs 1,218. Aditya Infotech rallied 116%, advancing from Rs 1,673 to Rs 3,617.

The broader market outperformed headline indices by a wide margin. The BSE 500 surged nearly 10%, while the BSE Sensex managed only a 4% gain during the same period.

Around 32 stocks gained more than 50% in the three-month window. This indicates strong market breadth beneath the relatively modest index-level performance.

Kirloskar Oil Engines jumped 93% from Rs 1,329 to Rs 2,561. Ola Electric Mobility gained 85%, rising from Rs 23 to Rs 43, though it remains below its 52-week high of Rs 71.

Adani Green Energy advanced 82% from Rs 839 to Rs 1,525. RR Kabel rallied 81% to Rs 2,435, while Syrma SGS Technology rose 80% to Rs 1,426.

Aegis Logistics, Welspun Corp, Deepak Fertilisers, Wockhardt, and Apar Industries rounded out the top 13. Gains ranged from 69% to 77% across these five names.

Most of these stocks are trading near their 52-week highs. The momentum signals strong buying interest in midcap and selected large-cap segments of the market.
