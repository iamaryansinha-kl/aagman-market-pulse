HUL Q4 FY26 Profit Jumps 21% to ₹2,994 Cr; Revenue Up 8%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HUL Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 20.96% to ₹2,994 crore from ₹2,475 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue from product sales up 8.13% to ₹16,172 crore driven by 6% underlying volume growth, highest in 12 quarters
  • 03/Full-year FY26 profit at ₹15,059 crore; stock down 3% at ₹2,243.35 as CEO warns of geopolitical commodity volatility

Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) reported a 20.96% rise in consolidated net profit to ₹2,994 crore for Q4 FY26. The company had posted ₹2,475 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from sale of products grew 8.13% to ₹16,172 crore in the March quarter. This was driven by 6% underlying volume growth, which HUL called its highest in 12 quarters.

Total expenses in the quarter stood at ₹16,615 crore, up 7.2%. Total income, including other income, increased 5.01% to ₹16,580 crore.

For the full FY26, HUL reported a consolidated profit of ₹15,059 crore. Total income for the financial year rose 4.6% to ₹65,219 crore.

The FY26 profit was aided by the divestment of HUL's 19.8% stake in Nutritionalab for ₹307 crore. The company also completed the full acquisition of OZiva during the year.

CEO and Managing Director Priya Nair said FY26 saw improved demand driven by supportive macro-economic policies. She highlighted portfolio sharpening and frontline investments as key growth drivers.

Nair warned that heightened geopolitical tensions have led to commodity and currency volatility. HUL plans to navigate through disciplined savings, supply chain resilience, and calibrated pricing actions.

HUL shares closed at ₹2,243.35 on the BSE on April 30, down 3.01%. The stock has declined 2.39% over the past year despite the earnings beat.
