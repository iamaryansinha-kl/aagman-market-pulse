Ola Electric Registrations Jump 20% in April to 12,166 Units as Stock Rallies 60%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ola Electric records 20% month-on-month growth in April 2026 with 12,166 EV registrations, up from 10,133 units in March
  • 02/Ola was the only EV two-wheeler brand to grow in April as broader industry declined 22%; shares rallied 60% from all-time low of ₹21.21
  • 03/Company credits indigenous 4680 Bharat Cells for cost efficiency gains; Roadster X motorcycle gaining traction in UP and Bihar

Ola Electric recorded 12,166 EV registrations in April 2026. This is up 20% from 10,133 units in March. The company was the only EV two-wheeler brand to grow month-on-month.

The broader electric two-wheeler industry declined by over 22% in April. Ola Electric bucked the trend entirely. It gained market share while competitors lost ground.

Ola Electric shares closed at ₹36.57 at BSE on May 1, 2026. The stock has rallied over 60% from an all-time low of ₹21.21 hit earlier.

The company attributed the growth to its indigenously developed 4680 Bharat Cells. These cells have boosted cost efficiencies. Benefits were passed on to customers.

The Roadster X motorcycle is gaining traction in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Madhya Pradesh. The Roadster X+ 9.1 kWh variant offers up to 500 km IDC range.

"March marked a strong recovery and April has continued that momentum," an Ola Electric spokesperson said. "This reflects stronger execution, stabilising operations, and growing customer confidence."

The company expects the momentum to continue. It is focused on driving the next phase of EV penetration in India. The 4680 cell-powered products are seeing increasing adoption.
