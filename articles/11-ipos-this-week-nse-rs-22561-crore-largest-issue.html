11 IPOs Hit the Market This Week: NSE's Rs 22,561 Crore Issue Leads a Crowded Lineup

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eleven IPOs open this week across mainboard and SME segments, led by NSE's Rs 22,561 crore issue
  • 02/NSE opens September 17 with a GMP of Rs 210, signalling about 11.76% listing gains over the Rs 1,785 upper band
  • 03/SS Retail shows the strongest grey market interest at about 32% premium; Hero Motors signals roughly 28.57%

India's primary market is heading into a crowded week with 11 IPOs opening for subscription across the mainboard and SME segments. Grey market trends show investor interest concentrated in a few names rather than spread evenly.

The National Stock Exchange leads the pack with a Rs 22,561 crore IPO opening on September 17, the largest issue of the week. Its grey market premium of Rs 210 signals about 11.76% expected gains over the upper price band of Rs 1,785.

Hero Motors opens September 16 with a Rs 1,000 crore mainboard IPO. Its GMP of Rs 24 signals about 28.57% listing expectations at the upper price band of Rs 84.

SS Retail opens September 16 with a Rs 500 crore mainboard issue. Its GMP of Rs 135 is the strongest in the set, signalling about 31.84% expected gains.

Jindal Supreme opens September 16 with a Rs 125 crore mainboard IPO. Its GMP of Rs 27 points to about 29.03% expected gains over the upper price band.

Sonaselection India opens September 17 with a Rs 142 crore mainboard IPO. The grey market is showing no premium yet, suggesting limited early listing buzz.

Among SME issues, SpectraA Technology Solutions opens September 17 with a Rs 43 crore NSE SME IPO. Its GMP of Rs 27 signals about 22.88% expected gains.

Kheria Autocomp (Rs 46 crore) and Axiom Gas Engineering (Rs 50 crore) open September 17 and 18 respectively, with no active GMP yet. Quanto Agroworld (Rs 31 crore) and Shakti Polytarp (Rs 27 crore) open September 15 with no visible premium. Vama Wovenfab's Rs 50 crore BSE SME issue shows a modest 2.93% GMP.

The NSE IPO is the flagship listing of the week given the exchange's dominant position in Indian capital markets. Investor appetite for the issue will be closely watched as a gauge of primary market sentiment.
