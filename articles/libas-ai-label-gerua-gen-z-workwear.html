Libas Launches AI-Led Label Gerua for Gen Z Workwear

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Libas launches Gerua, an AI-led fashion label targeting the Gen Z workwear segment
  • 02/The new label focuses on tech-driven fashion solutions for young professionals
  • 03/Details on pricing, launch timeline, and market positioning remain undisclosed

Fashion brand Libas has launched Gerua, a new AI-led label. The label targets Gen Z professionals entering the workforce.

Gerua focuses on the emerging Gen Z workwear market. It combines technology with fashion design for younger consumers.

The label aims at young professionals seeking modern work attire. AI-driven processes are expected to shape product development.

Workwear for Gen Z represents a growing segment in Indian fashion. Brands are increasingly targeting first-job professionals with dedicated labels.

Libas has not disclosed specific pricing or launch timelines. Further details on product range and availability remain awaited.
