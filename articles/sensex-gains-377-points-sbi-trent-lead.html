Sensex Gains 377 Points as Markets Reverse Early Losses; SBI, Trent Lead

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex climbed 377 points by midday on April 20, 2026 after opening lower
  • 02/Banking stocks led gains with SBI, ICICI Bank among top performers
  • 03/Trent and select consumption stocks rallied despite global uncertainty

Indian equity markets reversed course on Monday, April 20, 2026. The Sensex gained 377 points by midday after opening in negative territory.

The turnaround came despite a fragile global backdrop. Rising US-Iran tensions and crude oil volatility had pressured early trade.

State Bank of India (SBI) and Trent were among the top gainers. ICICI Bank and Asian Paints also supported the index.

Banking stocks outperformed following ICICI Bank's quarterly results. The lender reported healthy asset growth though flagged West Asia conflict risks.

The Nifty traded above 24,300 by noon. This marked a recovery from opening lows below 24,300.

Earnings season provided some support to sentiment. Investors focused on corporate fundamentals amid global noise.

Crude oil remained elevated near $95 per barrel. This kept inflation concerns alive for import-dependent India.
