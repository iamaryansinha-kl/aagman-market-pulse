Bajaj Housing Finance Q4 FY26: PAT Up 14% to ₹669 Crore, Stock Dips

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Housing Finance Q4 FY26 PAT jumps 14% YoY to ₹669.19 crore on revenue of ₹2,902.61 crore
  • 02/FY26 full-year PAT climbs 18.3% to ₹2,560.34 crore; Morgan Stanley sees nearly double industry growth ahead
  • 03/NIM compresses 12 bps QoQ to 3.8% on competitive pricing; stock down 1% at ₹89.89 in morning trade

Bajaj Housing Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJAJHFL) reported a profit after tax of ₹669.19 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 14 per cent increase from ₹586.68 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Total revenue from operations rose to ₹2,902.61 crore. This compares with ₹2,504.05 crore in the same quarter last year.

For the full financial year FY26, PAT came in at ₹2,560.34 crore. This represents an 18.3 per cent jump from ₹2,162.90 crore in FY25.

Shares slipped around 1 per cent in late morning trade on Tuesday. The stock touched ₹89.89 at 11:24 am on the NSE, down from the previous close of ₹91.07.

Morgan Stanley said management commentary remained positive. The brokerage noted the company aims to grow at nearly twice the industry rate.

Reported net interest margin declined 12 basis points quarter-on-quarter to 3.8 per cent. Borrowing costs stood at around 7.3 per cent, showing a marginal decline.

Gross non-performing assets remained low at 27 basis points. Credit costs stood at 16 basis points, though competition from banks in home loans remains a concern.

In a separate filing, the company informed about the allotment of non-convertible debentures aggregating to ₹1,008.33 crore.
