Q4 Results: Gujarat Ambuja Net Up 22%; Birla Corp, Advanced Enzyme Rise

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gujarat Ambuja Exports FY26 net profit rises 21.6% to ₹304.99 crore; revenue jumps 24.2% to ₹5,728.60 crore
  • 02/Birla Corporation Q4 profit up 14.8% to ₹294.77 crore; FY26 revenue rises 5%; Advanced Enzyme FY26 net up 29.6% to ₹173.6 crore
  • 03/All three boards declared final dividends; GAEL ₹0.30, Advanced Enzyme ₹1.35; Birla dividend details awaited

Gujarat Ambuja Exports Limited (NSE: GAEL) reported standalone net profit of ₹304.99 crore for FY26. This marks a 21.6% rise from ₹250.82 crore in FY25. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹0.30 per share.

Revenue from operations surged 24.2% to ₹5,728.60 crore against ₹4,612.58 crore in the previous year. Profit before tax stood at ₹405.12 crore, up from ₹342.51 crore. EPS for the full year improved to ₹6.65 from ₹5.47.

The Maize Processing Division remained the largest revenue contributor at ₹3,565.95 crore. The company recorded an exceptional item of ₹3.97 crore related to India's new consolidated labour codes. The provision was revised down from an earlier estimate of ₹4.66 crore.

Birla Corporation Ltd (NSE: BIRLACORPN) posted Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit of ₹294.77 crore. This reflects a 14.8% increase from ₹256.6 crore in the year-ago quarter. FY26 revenue rose 5% on a consolidated basis.

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (NSE: ADVENZYMES) reported consolidated net profit of ₹173.608 crore for FY26. This marks a 29.6% jump from ₹133.974 crore in FY25. Consolidated revenue grew 17.1% to ₹745.757 crore from ₹636.91 crore.

The Advanced Enzyme board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.35 per share on face value of ₹2. The payout ratio stands at 67.5%. The board deferred any interim dividend to preserve capital for potential strategic actions.

All three companies announced their audited full-year and quarterly results on May 9, 2026. The boards approved the numbers at meetings held on Saturday. Shareholders will vote on the proposed dividends at upcoming AGMs.

Gujarat Ambuja Exports and Advanced Enzyme both demonstrated strong volume-led growth across their core divisions. Birla Corporation's cement-led revenue growth and margin improvement signal steady demand recovery. Investors should monitor commodity input costs and monsoon demand for FY27.
