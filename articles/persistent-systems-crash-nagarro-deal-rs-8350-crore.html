Persistent Systems Crash: Rs 8,350 Crore Wiped Out on Nagarro Deal Jitters

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Persistent Systems shares crashed nearly 11% to a 52-week low of Rs 4,312 on June 29, 2026
  • 02/The stock erased Rs 8,353 crore in market cap after announcing a €1.27 billion Nagarro acquisition
  • 03/Brokerages are split: Motilal Oswal sees value, but Equirus and ICICI warn of stretched valuations and execution risks

Persistent Systems shares plunged nearly 11% on Monday, June 29, 2026. The stock hit a fresh 52-week low of Rs 4,312 on the NSE, marking its worst single-day fall since October 2018.

The crash erased approximately Rs 8,353 crore from Persistent's market capitalisation. The trigger was the company's announcement to acquire Germany-based Nagarro at an enterprise value of €1.27 billion (around Rs 13,667 crore).

Persistent has already acquired a 21% stake in Nagarro through a share purchase agreement. It will now launch a voluntary public takeover offer for all remaining shareholders, followed by a delisting from the German exchange.

The deal price represents a 140% premium to Nagarro's June 25, 2026 closing price, according to Equirus Capital. This implies Nagarro's current investors are pessimistic about its standalone growth profile.

Nomura maintained its 'Neutral' rating with a target price of Rs 5,200. It noted the deal aligns with Persistent's $5 billion revenue target by FY31, but seamless execution is critical given the transaction is 67% of Persistent's existing size.

Equirus Capital has a 'Reduce' rating with a target price of Rs 5,010. It flagged Persistent's expensive valuations at 33.5x PE on FY27E earnings and warned of little margin for error amid volatile macro conditions.

Motilal Oswal remained more optimistic with a 'Buy' rating and target price of Rs 6,200. It noted the acquisition is priced at 9.1x EV/EBITDA, which it considers reasonable for a business of Nagarro's size.

ICICI Securities upgraded the stock to 'Hold' from 'Reduce' with a target price of Rs 4,930. It said turning around Nagarro's growth in a challenging demand environment remains a key monitorable for investors.
