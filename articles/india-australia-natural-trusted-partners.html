India, Australia Must Move Ahead as 'Natural and Trusted Partners'

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India and Australia are deepening their partnership.

The two countries are being urged to move ahead as natural and trusted partners amid shifting geopolitical dynamics in the Indo-Pacific. The call reflects growing convergence between the two democracies on trade, security, and strategic interests.

The partnership has multiple dimensions. Trade is expanding, with Australia supplying critical minerals and energy resources that India needs for its energy transition and manufacturing ambitions. Defence cooperation has strengthened through the Quad framework alongside the United States and Japan.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is in Melbourne for bilateral talks with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese. The meeting is expected to cover trade facilitation, migration, education, and security cooperation. Both leaders have emphasized the natural complementarity between their economies.

Critical minerals are a key focus. Australia is one of the world's largest producers of lithium, cobalt, and rare earth elements. India needs these materials for its electric vehicle and renewable energy manufacturing. A supply chain partnership would reduce both countries' dependence on Chinese mineral processing.

The strategic dimension is equally important. Both countries share concerns about Chinese assertiveness in the Indo-Pacific. The Quad has become the primary vehicle for coordinating their security policies, though both insist it is not an anti-China alliance.

Trade volumes have grown but remain below potential. A comprehensive economic cooperation agreement is under negotiation to reduce tariffs and expand market access in goods and services.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
What is the India-Australia partnership about?
Trade, defence, critical minerals, and strategic cooperation in the Indo-Pacific.
Why are they called natural partners?
Their economies are complementary — Australia has resources India needs, and both share democratic values and security concerns.
What are critical minerals?
Lithium, cobalt, rare earth elements needed for batteries and renewable energy.
What is the Quad?
A security dialogue between India, Australia, the US, and Japan.
Is this partnership aimed at China?
Both countries say the Quad is not anti-China, but they share concerns about Chinese assertiveness.
