Rupee Falls 11 Paise to 94.95 Against US Dollar on May 4, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee depreciates 11 paise to 94.95 against the US dollar in early trade on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Brent crude hovers near $108 per barrel; rupee hit all-time low of 95.33 on April 30
  • 03/RBI likely intervened after breaching 95; traders eye 95.20-95.30 as resistance zone

The rupee depreciated 11 paise to 94.95 against the US dollar in early trade on May 4, 2026. The domestic currency had settled at 94.84 in the previous session.

Forex traders said Brent crude oil is hovering near $108 per barrel. Elevated oil prices maintain pressure on oil-importing economies like India.

The rupee fell to an all-time low of 95.33 on April 30 after Brent briefly crossed $120 per barrel. Currency and fixed income markets were closed on May 1 for Maharashtra Day.

Unabated foreign capital outflows amid rising geopolitical uncertainties have dented investor sentiment. The rupee has declined nearly 2% over the past eight trading sessions.

West Asia tensions and uncertainty around the Strait of Hormuz keep markets on edge. US President Donald Trump said America could guide stranded ships through the strait, but no detailed plan was announced.

Market participants expect the Reserve Bank of India to continue monitoring currency movements. Traders noted the RBI likely intervened after the rupee fell below the 95 level.

"Right now, the rupee is standing at a critical zone — not just technically, but psychologically. The 95.20–95.30 range is expected to act as strong resistance in the near term," said Amit Pabari, managing director at CR Forex Advisors.

The rupee closed FY26 with a 9.88% drop, its worst decline in 14 years. Further movement depends on crude prices, FII flows, and West Asia developments.
