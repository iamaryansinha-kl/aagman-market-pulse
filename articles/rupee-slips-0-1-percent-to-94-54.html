Rupee slips 0.1% to 94.54 as Nifty 50 fall 0.4% countering Asian gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Indian rupee settled at 94.54 per dollar on Monday, down 0.1% from its previous close of 94.3950
  • 02/The Nifty 50 fell 0.4% (109.75 points), eroding gains from firmer Asian currencies after US-Iran de-escalation
  • 03/Traders are watching the US jobs report due Thursday for clues on potential Federal Reserve rate hikes this year

The Indian rupee settled at 94.54 per dollar on Monday, down 0.1% from its previous close of 94.3950. The decline came despite positive sentiment from a US-Iran de-escalation and gains in Asian currencies.

The Nifty 50 stock index fell 0.4%, shedding 109.75 points to close at 23,946.25. This weakness in domestic equities overshadowed the boost from regional currency gains.

Asian currencies rose between 0.1% to 0.4% versus the dollar. The gains followed a US-Iran agreement to halt recent hostilities and renew talks over the Strait of Hormuz.

Traders pointed to a pick-up in dollar demand from foreign banks in the latter half of the session. Most of this demand was likely on behalf of custodial clients.

Brent crude oil prices rose about 1% to $72.6 per barrel. This is well below a multi-year peak of $126 hit in late April.

The dollar index was slightly lower but remained near its highest level in over a year. Investors are now pricing in at least one Federal Reserve rate hike in 2026.

"With the pair not sticking to any one direction at the moment, short-tenor positions with tight stop-losses are preferable," a trader at a Mumbai-based bank said.

"Any signs of US growth and job creation accelerating — despite the negative impact of AI already seen on finance and IT sector jobs — would set the stage for a rate hike," said analysts at Standard Chartered. The key US jobs report is due Thursday.
