PC Jeweller Jumps 6% on Debt-Free Claim, Q1 Revenue Up 21% YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PC Jeweller shares jump 6% after claiming it will become debt-free in the current quarter
  • 02/Q1FY27 consolidated revenue grew approximately 21% year-on-year, reflecting turnaround progress
  • 03/Outstanding debt reduced by more than 90% since September 2024 settlement agreement with banks

PC Jeweller share price jumped over 6% in morning trade on Friday, 3 July. The stock opened at ₹9.96 against its previous close of ₹9.88 and hit an intraday high of ₹10.49, up 6.2%. The rally came after the company said it is set to become debt-free in the current quarter.

In an exchange filing after market hours on 2 July, PC Jeweller said its Q1FY27 consolidated revenue grew by approximately 21% year-on-year. This reflects significant progress in its ongoing turnaround journey. The company also claimed it continues to progress rapidly towards its goal of becoming debt-free.

PC Jeweller said it has successfully reduced its outstanding debt that was payable to the banks under the terms of the joint settlement agreement by approximately 24% during Q1FY27. With this reduction, the company has now reduced its outstanding debt by more than 90% as of the date, since the execution of the settlement agreement with banks on 30 September 2024.

The repayment of remaining outstanding debt and attaining a debt-free status in the ongoing quarter itself will significantly improve the company's financial position in the coming periods, the company said. The stock has been on a recovery path. It hit a 52-week low of ₹7.45 on 30 March this year and has jumped 24% in the past three months.

Over the last year, the jewellery stock has crashed 27%. However, it has seen recovery this year, as year-to-date in 2026, it is up 10%. The stock hit its 52-week high of ₹19.65 on 7 July last year. The company is one of India's largest jewellery retailers with a strong presence in the northern and western markets.

The debt reduction is a significant milestone for the company. It had faced severe financial stress in recent years due to promoter-related issues and debt pile-up. The settlement agreement with banks in September 2024 was a turning point, allowing the company to restructure its debt and focus on operations.

The Q1 revenue growth of 21% suggests the turnaround is gaining traction. The jewellery sector has seen robust demand in recent quarters, supported by strong gold prices and wedding season buying. PC Jeweller is well-positioned to benefit from this trend once it achieves debt-free status.

However, the stock remains volatile. It has a history of sharp price swings and remains significantly below its all-time highs. Investors should note that while the debt-free claim is positive, the company's long-term growth trajectory depends on sustaining revenue growth and rebuilding investor confidence.
