Eswari Global Metal Files DRHP for Rs 1,100-1,300 Crore IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eswari Global Metal Industries files DRHP with Sebi to raise Rs 1,100-1,300 crore through a mix of fresh issue and offer-for-sale
  • 02/The IPO comprises a fresh issue of up to Rs 500 crore and an OFS of 1.32 crore shares by promoters and selling shareholders
  • 03/Company reported Rs 1,401.5 crore revenue and Rs 83.9 crore net profit for the nine months ended December 2025

Eswari Global Metal Industries Ltd has filed draft papers with Sebi for an IPO targeting Rs 1,100-1,300 crore. The Coimbatore-based company submitted its DRHP on Sunday.

The offering includes a fresh issue of equity shares worth up to Rs 500 crore. It also features an offer-for-sale of up to 1.32 crore equity shares by promoters and another selling shareholder.

The company may consider a pre-IPO placement of up to Rs 100 crore. If completed, the fresh issue size will be reduced accordingly.

Promoters offloading shares include C Bharanikumar, Pradeep Chandrasekaran, Prasath Chandrasekaran, Sabarinathan Anbalagan, Hari Sudhan A, Nithin Arumugam, P Anbalagan and P Arumugam. Palaniappan Ramalingam is the other selling shareholder.

The fresh issue proceeds will fund Rs 150 crore for Phase-II expansion at its Mundra, Gujarat facility. Another Rs 250 crore will go toward debt repayment, with the balance for general corporate purposes.

Incorporated in 1987, Eswari Global Metal Industries recycles non-ferrous metals, plastics and e-waste. It manufactures pure lead, lead alloys, aluminium alloys, copper ingots, tin products and plastic granules.

The company operates nine manufacturing facilities across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu. Its total installed capacity stands at 165,106 metric tonnes per annum as of December 31, 2025.

For the nine months ended December 2025, revenue was Rs 1,401.5 crore. Net profit during the same period came in at Rs 83.9 crore.

DAM Capital Advisors, ICICI Securities and Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors are the book-running lead managers. KFin Technologies is the registrar to the issue.
