Blunav Raises $1 Million Seed Funding From Piper Serica for Airport Tech

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Airport tech startup Blunav raises $1 million in seed funding from venture capital firm Piper Serica
  • 02/First deployment at Chennai airport reduced runway occupancy time by 22% using Airside Management software
  • 03/Funds to be used for team expansion, product development, and scaling across India and international markets

Blunav, an airport operations technology company, has raised $1 million in seed funding from Piper Serica. The round was announced on May 7, 2026.

The company builds cloud-based airport operating systems. Its platform unifies flight operations, gate allocations, and passenger systems on a single connected platform.

Blunav aims to replace disconnected vendor stacks and manual processes. Most airports today run on fragmented systems that lack real-time integration.

The startup's first major deployment is at Chennai International Airport. After deployment, Blunav's Airside Management software cut runway occupancy time by 22 per cent.

"We are building a unified Airport Operating System to simplify and modernise airport operations globally," said Suseendar Marimuthu, Co-founder and CEO, Blunav.

Blunav plans to scale its platform across multiple airport environments over the next 12 to 24 months. The company targets underserved airports with scalable systems.

The fresh capital will fund team expansion and product development. Blunav intends to grow deployments across India and international markets.
