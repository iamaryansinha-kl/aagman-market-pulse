Sebi Seeks Clarification on Jio Platforms Rs 35,000 Crore IPO Filing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi has sought clarifications from Jio Platforms on its draft IPO papers, comprising an entirely fresh issue of up to 27 crore equity shares
  • 02/The IPO is expected to raise approximately Rs 35,000 crore, making it India's largest public issue; Rs 27,500 crore will go toward debt repayment
  • 03/Mukesh Ambani had announced the listing by June 2026 at RIL's AGM; the IPO will provide independent valuation for Jio's telecom and digital business

India's capital markets regulator Sebi has sought clarifications from Jio Platforms on its draft red herring prospectus (DRHP). The move signals the next step in the regulatory review process for what is expected to be India's largest initial public offering.

Jio Platforms filed draft papers for a proposed IPO comprising an entirely fresh issue of up to 27 crore equity shares. There is no offer-for-sale component, meaning all proceeds will flow directly into the company.

The company is expected to raise around Rs 35,000 crore through the public issue. This would make it the largest IPO in India's capital markets history.

According to the draft prospectus, Jio plans to deploy Rs 27,500 crore from the proceeds towards repayment or prepayment of debt. The remaining funds will be used for network expansion, artificial intelligence infrastructure, and digital services.

Reliance Industries Chairman Mukesh Ambani described the proposed listing as the group's most significant value-creation milestone this year. He announced the IPO plan at Reliance's annual general meeting in August 2025, targeting a listing by June 2026.

Jio Platforms counts several marquee global investors among its shareholders. These include Google, Meta, KKR, Silver Lake, Vista Equity Partners, General Atlantic, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, and Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund.

Reliance Industries currently owns approximately 67% of Jio Platforms. The IPO is also expected to provide the market with an independent valuation for Jio's telecom and digital business, which has remained embedded within Reliance's conglomerate structure.

The IPO structure was revised to an all-fresh issue after a valuation disagreement between Reliance and existing shareholders. The company appointed Akash Ambani as Managing Director for a five-year term in April 2026, reflecting focus on strategic continuity ahead of the listing.

THE IPO COMPRISES AN ENTIRELY FRESH ISSUE OF UP TO 27 CRORE EQUITY SHARES WITH NO OFFER-FOR-SALE (OFS) COMPONENT. THE COMPANY EXPECTS TO RAISE APPROXIMATELY RS 35,000 CRORE, WHICH WOULD MAKE IT THE LARGEST PUBLIC ISSUE IN INDIA'S CAPITAL MARKETS HISTORY.

JIO PLANS TO DEPLOY RS 27,500 CRORE TOWARD REPAYMENT OR PREPAYMENT OF DEBT. THE REMAINING FUNDS WILL BE USED FOR INVESTMENTS IN NETWORK EXPANSION, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE INFRASTRUCTURE, DIGITAL SERVICES, AND OTHER CORPORATE PURPOSES.

RELIANCE INDUSTRIES OWNS APPROXIMATELY 67% OF JIO PLATFORMS. OTHER SHAREHOLDERS INCLUDE GOOGLE, META, KKR, SILVER LAKE, VISTA EQUITY PARTNERS, GENERAL ATLANTIC, ABU DHABI INVESTMENT AUTHORITY, AND SAUDI ARABIA'S PUBLIC INVESTMENT FUND.

RELIANCE CHAIRMAN MUKESH AMBANI ANNOUNCED AT THE AUGUST 2025 AGM THAT JIO PLATFORMS WOULD LIST BY JUNE 2026. THE COMPANY FILED ITS DRAFT IPO PAPERS IN JUNE 2026, AND SEBI'S REVIEW PROCESS IS NOW UNDERWAY.
