HAL Appoints Ravi K as 22nd Chairman & MD; Succeeds D K Sunil

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ravi K assumes charge as 22nd Chairman & MD of HAL on May 1, 2026, succeeding D K Sunil who superannuated on April 30
  • 02/Ravi brings 30+ years of aerospace and defence experience; previously Director (Operations) and played key role in securing HAL's Maharatna status
  • 03/HAL shares closed at ₹4,338.80 on April 30 with market cap of ₹2.90 lakh crore; order book stands at ₹2.54 lakh crore

State-run aerospace major Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) (NSE: HAL) (BSE: 541154) on Friday announced that Ravi K has assumed charge as its 22nd Chairman and Managing Director. He took over on May 1, 2026, succeeding D K Sunil who superannuated on April 30.

Ravi K brings over 30 years of experience across aerospace and defence, manufacturing, and electronics sectors. The Bengaluru-headquartered company said this in an official exchange filing on May 1.

Prior to this appointment, Ravi served as Director (Operations) at HAL. In that role, he led strategic planning and played a key role in securing HAL's Maharatna status in October 2024.

"My vision is to transform HAL into a globally competitive aerospace and defence enterprise, driven by innovation, Artificial Intelligence (AI), operational excellence, and people," Ravi said in a statement.

During his tenure as Executive Director and General Manager of the LCA Tejas Division, he concluded major contracts for HAL. These include the contract to supply 180 LCA Tejas fighter jets to the Indian Air Force and 156 LCH Prachand helicopters to the Indian Army and Air Force.

He is credited with operationalising the LCA Tejas fleet in the IAF. He also strengthened indigenous capabilities by increasing local content and expanding production capacity in Nasik.

Ravi diversified HAL into civil manufacturing and MRO (Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul) operations. This created new revenue streams and expanded the company's market presence beyond defence contracts.

A Mechanical Engineering graduate from Malnad College of Engineering, Karnataka, Ravi is an alumnus of IIM Ahmedabad and IAS, Toulouse, France. He also serves as Nominee Director on the board of Multi-Role Transport Aircraft Ltd.

HAL reported revenue of ₹33,542.64 crore and profit of ₹8,323.4 crore for FY2025-26. The company holds an order book of ₹2.54 lakh crore, including the ₹62,370 crore contract for 97 LCA Mk1A aircraft.

The Maharatna status grants HAL enhanced financial autonomy. It can now invest up to ₹5,000 crore without government approval. HAL is the 14th company in India to achieve this status.
