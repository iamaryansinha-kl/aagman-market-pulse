Sedemac Mechatronics Block Deal: HDFC Life, A91 Among Sellers Offloading 10% Stake Worth Rs 1,329 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Investors in Sedemac Mechatronics are looking to offload up to 44.3 lakh shares, representing around 10% of the company's equity, through a block deal that could fetch approximately Rs 1,329 crore.

Sellers include A91 Emerging Fund, Xponentia Opportunities Fund and HDFC Life Insurance, among others, according to reports.

Deal Terms

The reported floor price for the block deal has been set at Rs 3,000 per share. This represents a discount of up to 1.2% to the prevailing market price, and roughly 1.7% below Wednesday's closing price. The shares are expected to be subject to a 60-day lock-in on further sale.

Sedemac Mechatronics shares finished Wednesday's session at Rs 3,052.50 apiece on the BSE, gaining 0.63% from the previous close of Rs 3,033.40. During the session, the stock traded in a range of Rs 2,970.05 to Rs 3,080.

Stock Has More Than Doubled Since IPO

Sedemac Mechatronics made its stock-market debut on March 11, 2026, after raising Rs 1,087.45 crore through its IPO at an issue price of Rs 1,352 per share.

The stock listed on the NSE at Rs 1,535, a 13.5% premium over the issue price. On the BSE, it opened at Rs 1,510, a gain of 11.7%. At Wednesday's closing price, the stock has gained around 126% from its IPO issue price.

The company has a market capitalisation of Rs 13,491.29 crore on the BSE.

About the Company

Sedemac Mechatronics is a supplier of control-intensive, application-critical electronic control units (ECUs) to leading original equipment manufacturers across the mobility and industrial markets in India, the United States and Europe.

The company says a majority of its revenue comes from products incorporating novel control technologies developed entirely in-house. These proprietary technologies are designed to provide differentiated solutions to OEM customers.
