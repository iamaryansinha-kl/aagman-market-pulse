PNC Infratech Surges 7% on ₹3,483 Crore NHAI Project Order Win

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PNC Infratech shares surged 7% after the company secured a ₹3,483 crore project order from NHAI
  • 02/The highway infrastructure order significantly boosts the company's order book and revenue visibility
  • 03/Investors reacted positively as the large-ticket contract strengthens PNC Infratech's execution pipeline

PNC Infratech shares surged 7% in trade on April 22, 2026. The rally followed the announcement of a ₹3,483 crore NHAI project order.

The National Highways Authority of India awarded the large infrastructure contract. This significantly expands PNC Infratech's order book.

The ₹3,483 crore contract provides multi-year revenue visibility. Highway construction projects typically span 24-36 months.

PNC Infratech specializes in road and highway infrastructure development. The NHAI order aligns with the company's core execution strength.

The stock's 7% jump reflects strong investor sentiment. Large order wins improve earnings predictability and balance sheet strength.

The company benefits from India's continued highway expansion push. Government capex on roads remains a key growth driver.
