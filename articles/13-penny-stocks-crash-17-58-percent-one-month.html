13 Penny Stocks Crash 17-58% in One Month: What Investors Should Know

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/13 penny stocks with market cap below Rs 1,000 crore plunged 17-58% over the past month, per ACE Equity data
  • 02/Gujarat Cotex led the decline with a 58% drop, followed by Laddu Gopal at 45% and Shalimar at 41%
  • 03/Low liquidity, high volatility, and limited transparency make these stocks prone to manipulation and sudden price drops

13 penny stocks with market cap below Rs 1,000 crore crashed 17-58% over the past month. The data was compiled by ACE Equity on June 28.

Gujarat Cotex led the decline with a 58% drop. Its shares closed at Rs 3.54 on June 27.

Laddu Gopal Online Services fell 45% to Rs 0.41. Shalimar Productions dropped 41% to Rs 0.29. Enbee Trade & Finance lost 40% to Rs 0.24.

Kapil Raj Finance declined 29% to Rs 2.07. Orient Tradelink fell 27% to Rs 6.22. A 1 and Ashnisha Industries both declined 24%.

The screening included stocks priced below Rs 20 with a minimum trading volume of 5 lakh shares. These are actively traded but highly speculative.

Penny stocks attract retail investors with low entry prices. However, they carry substantial risks due to low liquidity and limited transparency.

Price manipulation is a common concern in this segment. Sudden price drops can erase capital without warning.

Investors should set strict stop-losses and allocate only small capital to such stocks. Risk management is essential when dealing with illiquid counters.
