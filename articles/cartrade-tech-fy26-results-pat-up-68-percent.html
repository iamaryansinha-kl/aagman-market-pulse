CarTrade Tech FY26 Results: PAT Surges 68% to ₹244 Crore, Stock Jumps 15%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CarTrade Tech reports record FY26 PAT of ₹243.51 crore, up 68% from ₹145.27 crore in FY25
  • 02/Revenue from operations rose 22% to ₹869.77 crore while EBITDA more than doubled to ₹257 crore
  • 03/Shares surged over 15% to ₹2,081 on May 7 after the results, with market cap near ₹9,974 crore

CarTrade Tech Ltd (NSE: CARTRADE) reported its best-ever annual results for FY26 on May 7, 2026. Shares surged over 15 per cent to ₹2,081 on the NSE, touching an intraday high of ₹2,115.

Profit After Tax for the full year came in at ₹243.51 crore. This marks a 68 per cent jump from ₹145.27 crore recorded in FY25.

Revenue from operations grew 22 per cent year-on-year to ₹869.77 crore. EBITDA more than doubled, rising 70 per cent to ₹257 crore.

Operating margins expanded sharply to 33 per cent from 24 per cent in the previous year. The margin improvement reflects scale and cost discipline across the platform.

For Q4 FY26 alone, revenue stood at ₹220.75 crore, up 17 per cent year-on-year. PAT for the quarter was ₹70.85 crore, up 54 per cent, while EBITDA jumped 55 per cent to ₹71.65 crore.

All three business segments posted double-digit growth in Q4. The Consumer Group — comprising CarWale, BikeWale and CarTrade — grew revenue 25 per cent and EBITDA 72 per cent, with margins hitting 39 per cent.

The Remarketing segment, anchored by Shriram Automall, saw revenue rise 22 per cent and EBITDA climb 56 per cent. OLX India reported 16 per cent revenue growth and 34 per cent EBITDA growth.

The company attracted roughly 76 million average monthly unique visitors in Q4. Around 95 per cent of this traffic arrived organically, underscoring strong brand pull.

CarWale, BikeWale and OLX India each crossed 150 million annual unique users. The remarketing arm processed an annualised run-rate of 1.7 million auction listings.

CarTrade Tech now holds ₹1,244 crore in cash reserves and carries zero debt. Over the past three years, revenue CAGR stands at 29 per cent, EBITDA CAGR at 98 per cent, and PAT CAGR at 82 per cent.

Chairman and Founder Vinay Sanghi said the company's focus going forward would be on building AI-led products and technology platforms.

At ₹2,081, the stock trades at a trailing price-to-earnings ratio of approximately 39.6x. Total market capitalisation stands at roughly ₹9,974 crore.
