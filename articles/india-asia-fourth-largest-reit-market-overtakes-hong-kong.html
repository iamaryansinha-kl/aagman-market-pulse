India Overtakes Hong Kong as Asia's 4th-Largest REIT Market: Cushman & Wakefield

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's REIT market has reached a new milestone. The country has overtaken Hong Kong to become Asia's fourth-largest REIT market by market value, according to a report by Cushman & Wakefield.

The Asia REIT Market Insight 2025-2026 report shows India's REIT market value rose 62% from $11 billion at the end of 2024 to $17.7 billion by March 2026. The combined market value of 289 active REIT products across major Asian exchanges stood at $279.4 billion as of March 31, 2026, up 18% from $235.8 billion at the end of 2024.

India now has seven listed REITs, including two small and medium REITs (SM REITs).

What drove the growth? The report attributed the increase partly to new listings, including Knowledge Realty Trust and Bagmane Prime Office REIT. These two REITs added a combined 53.7 million square feet to the portfolios of India's listed REITs. They accounted for about three-quarters of the new space added to the six listed REITs between June 2025 and June 2026.

As of June 2026, six listed REITs in India collectively held around 178 million square feet, with another 36.7 million square feet under construction or planned.

Office assets continue to account for a significant part of India's REIT market. Occupancy levels remained high, supported by demand from multinational companies and Global Capability Centres (GCCs). Grade A office vacancy tightened.

Somy Thomas, executive managing director of Capital Markets India at Cushman & Wakefield, said the expansion of listed portfolios and development pipelines had increased the institutional depth of the market.

Asia REIT rankings Japan remains Asia's largest REIT market by value at $101.4 billion, followed by Singapore at $76.7 billion and the Chinese mainland at $32.1 billion. Hong Kong had a market value of $17.4 billion, marginally below India.

The Chinese mainland was the biggest source of new REIT listings. Of 27 new REIT listings across Asia between end-2024 and March 2026, 21 were in the Chinese mainland.

Japan's market value increased 12%, Singapore's rose 14% and Hong Kong's grew 8% between end-2024 and March 2026. Japan benefited from improving office fundamentals and hotel performance. Singapore saw increased acquisition activity. Hong Kong recorded a recovery in valuations alongside policy measures aimed at improving market liquidity.

Looking ahead, the report expects data-centre and hospitality REITs to remain areas of activity across Asia. Acquisitions and mergers could remain active as REIT managers seek to expand portfolios and diversify assets.

Commenting on the development, Chanakya Chakravarti, a global real estate investor and capital strategist, said: "India overtaking Hong Kong to become Asia's fourth-largest REIT market shows how commercial real estate is becoming a more established institutional asset class in India. The scale signifies that REITs are creating a more transparent route for institutional and global capital to access operating real estate, while giving developers and owners a mechanism to recycle capital from completed assets into new opportunities."
