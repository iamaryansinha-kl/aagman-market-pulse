Omaxe to Invest Rs 6,200 Cr in 19 Hotels Across 5 States Over 5 Yrs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Omaxe Ltd (NSE: OMAXE) announces Rs 6,200 crore investment to build 19 hotels across 5 states over 4-5 years, covering nearly 5 million sq ft
  • 02/12 of the 19 hotels will be in Uttar Pradesh, including two each in Ayodhya and Vrindavan, and three in Lucknow; flagship project is a 158-key Gateway Hotel by IHCL in Dwarka, New Delhi
  • 03/The investment is 4.5x Omaxe's current market cap of Rs 1,381 crore and comes amid five consecutive years of losses, raising execution and funding risks

Omaxe Ltd has announced a Rs 6,200 crore investment plan to develop 19 hotels across five states over the next 4-5 years. The company made the disclosure in a regulatory filing on June 29, 2026.

The 19 hotels will span nearly 5 million sq ft. They will be located in high-growth urban centres, pilgrimage destinations, and transit corridors.

Of the total, 12 hotels will be developed in Uttar Pradesh alone. This includes two hotels each in Ayodhya and Vrindavan, and three in Lucknow. The remaining properties will be spread across four other states.

A key project is a 158-key 'Gateway Hotel by IHCL' at Omaxe's 'The Omaxe State' in Dwarka, New Delhi. The company will rope in hospitality chains to manage these assets.

The hotels will be integrated with Omaxe's existing ecosystem of townships, mixed-use developments, and commercial projects. This leverages the company's land bank and existing infrastructure.

Omaxe was founded in 1987 by Rohtas Goel and got listed on the NSE and BSE in 2007. It has delivered 140.17 million sq ft of real estate across 31 cities in 8 states.

The Rs 6,200 crore bet is massive relative to Omaxe's size. Its current market capitalisation stands at Rs 1,381 crore. The investment is 4.5x its market value.

The company has posted losses for five straight years. FY2025 revenue was Rs 1,561 crore, down 3.3% YoY. Operating loss widened to Rs 608 crore from Rs 343 crore in FY2024.

Omaxe shares closed at Rs 75.5 on June 5, 2026. The stock is down 11.4% over the past year and trades well below its 52-week high of Rs 113.40.

Promoter shares worth 22.76% are pledged as of September 2025. This adds another layer of risk for investors tracking the execution of this ambitious plan.
