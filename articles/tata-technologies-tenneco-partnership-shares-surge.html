Tata Technologies Surges 9% on $100M Tenneco Partnership Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Technologies shares surged 9% to ₹729.20 after announcing expanded partnership with Tenneco LLC
  • 02/Tenneco will invest over $100 million across five years in engineering innovation and digital transformation
  • 03/Stock has rebounded 44% from March 2026 lows but remains 48% below its all-time high of ₹1,400

Tata Technologies Ltd (NSE: TATATECH) surged 9% to ₹729.20 in intraday trade on July 2, 2026. The rally followed an expanded strategic partnership with global automotive supplier Tenneco LLC.

Under the agreement, Tenneco will invest over $100 million over the next five years. The investment targets engineering innovation, digital transformation, and AI-led mobility solutions.

The partnership leverages India's engineering talent pool to accelerate product development. It aims to help Tenneco respond faster to evolving global automotive market demands.

The collaboration began in 2021 and has steadily expanded since. It is anchored by Tata Technologies' Global Engineering Center in Pune.

Warren Harris, CEO & Managing Director of Tata Technologies, said, "Tenneco has been a valued strategic partner, and this expanded engagement reflects the strength of our shared commitment."

Jon Bagrosky, Chief Administrative Officer of Tenneco, signed the agreement alongside Harris. Both executives reaffirmed their commitment to advancing technical excellence and long-term growth.

The stock has staged a strong recovery from its March 2026 low of ₹507. It has gained 44% from those levels but remains 48% below its November 2023 listing-day high of around ₹1,400.

Tata Technologies ended both 2024 and 2025 with negative returns. The stock is now up 10.4% year-to-date in 2026 as it attempts a turnaround.
