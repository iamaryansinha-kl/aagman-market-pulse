Victorinox Targets 39% Growth in India, Plans 5 Exclusive Stores by 2028

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Victorinox unveils India Vision 2030 with 5 exclusive stores by 2028 and dedicated e-commerce platform launch in 2026
  • 02/Recorded 41% growth in India during Jan-Jun 2026; targeting 39% growth for calendar 2026
  • 03/Swiss watch imports into India rose to ₹3,500 crore in 2025; EFTA deal cuts duties to zero over 7 years

Swiss brand Victorinox unveiled its India Vision 2030 roadmap on Friday. The plan includes five exclusive stores by 2028 and a dedicated e-commerce platform launching in 2026.

"India is one of Victorinox's most strategic growth markets globally," said CMO Veronika Elsener. The company will invest in products, retail, digital and brand building.

Victorinox recorded 41% growth in India during January-June 2026. The watches business, its largest category in India, drove this growth. The brand targets 39% growth for calendar 2026.

The company currently sells through 384 retail doors in India. It plans to expand to more than 400 by end-2026. Watch distribution partners include Ethos, Titan, Zimson, Golden Time and Rama.

Victorinox India reported revenue of ₹49.2 crore in FY25, per MCA filings. The company plans to increase marketing spending by 20% to support expansion.

The India strategy focuses on contemporary colours, local preferences, gifting occasions and accessible premium price points. This aims to broaden the customer base beyond traditional segments.

Victorinox's expansion aligns with rising Swiss watch imports into India. Imports reached about ₹3,500 crore in 2025, up from ₹3,244.6 crore in 2024.

The India-EFTA Trade Agreement reduces Swiss watch import duties from 22% to zero over seven years. The current duty stands at 15.71%, down from 22% pre-agreement.

TAG Heuer opened its first franchise boutique near New Delhi in April 2026. Rado has emerged as the brand's largest global market with dominant share in the ₹1-5 lakh segment.

Listed luxury watch retailer Ethos reported 28.8% revenue growth to ₹1,612.2 crore in FY26.
