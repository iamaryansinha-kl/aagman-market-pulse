General Insurers Clock 16.7% Premium Growth in Latest Reporting Period

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's general insurance industry has reported strong growth.

The sector clocked 16.7% premium growth in the latest reporting period. The double-digit expansion was broad-based, with motor, health, and commercial lines all contributing.

Motor insurance remained the largest segment by premium volume. Rising vehicle sales and mandatory third-party coverage requirements kept demand elevated. Health insurance also saw brisk growth as awareness increased post-pandemic.

Commercial lines benefited from the economic recovery. Fire, engineering, and liability covers saw renewed interest from businesses expanding capacity and investment.

The 16.7% growth rate outpaces overall economic expansion. It reflects the underpenetrated nature of the Indian general insurance market, where premium as a percentage of GDP remains well below global averages.

Industry executives expect the momentum to continue. Regulatory reforms, including the introduction of Bima Trinity and digital onboarding, are making insurance more accessible to retail and small-business customers.

Listed general insurers including ICICI Lombard, New India Assurance, and Star Health have been reporting steady premium growth. The sector has attracted significant investor interest, with several private equity firms backing insurance startups.
