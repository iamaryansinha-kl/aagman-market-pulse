NTPC Prepares First Feasibility Study for 2 GW Nuclear Project by 2032

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NTPC to submit first feasibility study for standalone nuclear project to DAE; aims for at least 2 GW capacity by 2032
  • 02/PSU plans 30 GW across 14 states with investment of lakhs of crores; two 700 MW units per state under exploration
  • 03/Bihar approved Banka district feasibility study; Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra among other exploration targets

NTPC Ltd will submit its first feasibility study for a standalone nuclear project to the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE). The power giant is seeking approval to proceed with its maiden independent nuclear venture in India.

The PSU has set a target of at least 2 GW of nuclear capacity by 2032. This ambition aligns with the government's broader goal of achieving 100 GW nuclear capacity by 2047.

NTPC plans to set up 30 GW of nuclear projects across at least 14 states. The total investment for this expansion is expected to run into lakhs of crores.

The company has completed feasibility study in one state and is nearing completion in two more. Bihar's Energy Department recently gave a go-ahead for a feasibility study in the state's Banka district.

NTPC is looking to install at least two units of 700 MW each in the states under exploration. Industry estimates suggest ₹15,000-20,000 crore per 1 GW of nuclear capacity with a minimum three-year timeline from concept to commissioning.

NTPC's current group-level installed capacity stands at 89,805.30 MW spanning coal, gas, hydro and solar. It holds a 49% stake in ASHVINI, a joint venture with NPCIL, for a 4x700 MW project in Rajasthan worth about ₹42,000 crore.

The DAE's Standing Site Selection Committee will review the submitted feasibility study. If cleared technically, the proposal moves to the Atomic Energy Commission for further processing.
