Kerala Budget 2026: ₹400 Cr Mission Samudra, NH-66 Six-Lane Upgrade

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kerala government allocates ₹400 crore for Mission Samudra to integrate 600-km coastline, 2 international seaports and 17 non-major ports
  • 02/NH-66 six-lane upgrade in Kerala has seen ₹16,500 crore spent so far with 450 km completed; most sections target completion by June 2026
  • 03/Southern Kerala Economic Corridor gets ₹50 crore, Rare Earth Corridor ₹100 crore; state debt stands at ₹5.07 lakh crore

Kerala Chief Minister V.D. Satheesan presented the revised Budget 2026-27 on June 19, 2026. The flagship announcement was Mission Samudra with a ₹400 crore allocation.

Mission Samudra aims to integrate Kerala's 600-km coastline, two international seaports, the Vizhinjam container transhipment terminal and 17 non-major ports. The five-year plan envisions Kerala as a unified "Port City."

The government will formulate a Kerala Maritime Policy to develop four non-major ports: Vizhinjam-Kovalam, Kollam, Beypore and Azhikkal. Vizhinjam is also being positioned as India's first green bunkering port.

The NH-66 six-lane upgrade spans approximately 643 km across Kerala. Union Minister Nitin Gadkari confirmed ₹16,500 crore has been spent, with around 450 km already completed.

Most NH-66 sections target completion between March and June 2026. The 40 km Azhiyur-Vengalam stretch has a revised deadline of August 2026 with 60% progress achieved.

The Budget earmarked ₹50 crore for the Southern Kerala Economic Corridor linking Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam and Alappuzha. Another ₹100 crore goes to the Rare Earth and Critical Minerals Corridor.

"Envisioning Kerala as a cohesive Port City, this initiative will seamlessly integrate our road, sea, rail and inland waterway networks," said CM V.D. Satheesan during his Budget speech.

The state faces fiscal strain with aggregate debt at ₹5.07 lakh crore and a revenue shortfall of ₹20,500 crore. The Plan Outlay was revised down from ₹35,750 crore to ₹30,370 crore.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS MISSION SAMUDRA AND HOW MUCH HAS BEEN ALLOCATED?
MISSION SAMUDRA IS A ₹400 CRORE PROGRAMME ANNOUNCED IN THE KERALA BUDGET 2026-27. IT AIMS TO INTEGRATE THE STATE'S 600-KM COASTLINE, TWO INTERNATIONAL SEAPORTS, ONE CONTAINER TRANSSHIPMENT TERMINAL AND 17 NON-MAJOR PORTS TO TRANSFORM KERALA INTO A MARITIME POWERHOUSE WITHIN FIVE YEARS.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF THE NH-66 SIX-LANE UPGRADE IN KERALA?
THE NH-66 UPGRADE COVERS APPROXIMATELY 643 KM ACROSS KERALA. UNION MINISTER NITIN GADKARI CONFIRMED ₹16,500 CRORE HAS BEEN SPENT SO FAR AND AROUND 450 KM HAS BEEN COMPLETED. MOST SECTIONS ARE TARGETED FOR COMPLETION BY JUNE 2026.
WHAT IS THE SOUTHERN KERALA ECONOMIC CORRIDOR?
THE SOUTHERN KERALA ECONOMIC CORRIDOR IS A NEW INITIATIVE WITH A ₹50 CRORE ALLOCATION. IT INTEGRATES THIRUVANANTHAPURAM, KOLLAM AND ALAPPUZHA INTO A UNIFIED ECONOMIC ZONE, WITH THIRUVANANTHAPURAM AS A KNOWLEDGE HUB, KOLLAM FOR MINERAL PROCESSING AND ALAPPUZHA AS INDIA'S BLUE ECONOMY CAPITAL.
WHAT OTHER INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS WERE ANNOUNCED IN THE KERALA BUDGET 2026-27?
KEY PROJECTS INCLUDE A ₹100 CRORE RARE EARTH AND CRITICAL MINERALS CORRIDOR, ₹20 CRORE FOR LIGHT METRO PROJECTS IN THIRUVANANTHAPURAM AND KOZHIKODE, ₹200 CRORE FOR AN AVIATION HUB, AND FAST-TRACKING OF THE BALARAMAPURAM-VIZHINJAM RAILWAY LINE.
WHAT IS KERALA'S FISCAL SITUATION AS PER THE 2026-27 BUDGET?
KERALA'S AGGREGATE DEBT STANDS AT ₹5.07 LAKH CRORE. THE STATE REPORTED A REVENUE SHORTFALL OF ₹20,500 CRORE AND REVISED THE PLAN OUTLAY DOWN FROM ₹35,750 CRORE TO ₹30,370 CRORE. SALARIES, PENSIONS AND INTEREST ACCOUNT FOR 77% OF REVENUE RECEIPTS.
