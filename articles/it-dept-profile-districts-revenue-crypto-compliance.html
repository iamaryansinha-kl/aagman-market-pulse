IT Dept to Profile Districts for Revenue Performance, Tighten Crypto Net

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IT Department will profile districts for net positive or negative revenue to identify compliance gaps and take corrective action
  • 02/FY26 direct tax collection fell ₹81,000 crore short; FY27 target of ₹26.97 lakh crore requires 15.25% growth versus budgeted 11.4%
  • 03/Department also plans three pilot projects to tighten crypto and VDA reporting through new specified financial transaction mechanisms

The Income Tax Department will profile districts for revenue performance and compliance. This is part of the Central Action Plan for FY27.

Direct tax collection in FY26 fell short by ₹81,000 crore. The required growth rate for FY27 has surged to 15.25 per cent.

The FY27 collection target stands at ₹26.97 lakh crore. This is significantly higher than the budget projection of 11.4 per cent growth.

Districts with negative revenue will be identified. Field formations will act with support from the Directorate of Tax Research and Analysis.

Maharashtra leads state-wise collections, followed by Karnataka and Delhi. The department will also analyse industrial sectors across five focus areas.

During FY26, total demand confirmed by IT Commissioner (Appeal) was ₹2.57 lakh crore. Efforts will focus on collecting these outstanding demands.

The plan includes doubling top PAN-wise demands to 10,000. At least three crypto pilot projects will explore new reporting entities and expanded transactions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE INCOME TAX DEPARTMENT'S NEW DISTRICT PROFILING INITIATIVE?
THE DEPARTMENT WILL PROFILE DISTRICTS FOR NET POSITIVE OR NEGATIVE REVENUES TO IDENTIFY COMPLIANCE GAPS. FIELD FORMATIONS WILL INVESTIGATE NEGATIVE REVENUE AREAS AND TAKE CORRECTIVE ACTION WITH SUPPORT FROM THE DIRECTORATE OF TAX RESEARCH AND ANALYSIS.
HOW MUCH DID DIRECT TAX COLLECTION FALL SHORT IN FY26?
DIRECT TAX COLLECTION IN FY26 FELL SHORT BY ₹81,000 CRORE. THIS SHORTFALL HAS PUSHED THE REQUIRED GROWTH RATE FOR FY27 TO 15.25 PER CENT.
WHAT IS THE FY27 DIRECT TAX COLLECTION TARGET?
THE TARGET FOR FY27 IS ₹26.97 LAKH CRORE. THIS REQUIRES A 15.25 PER CENT GROWTH RATE, COMPARED TO THE ORIGINAL BUDGET PROJECTION OF 11.4 PER CENT.
WHAT DEMAND COLLECTION EFFORTS ARE PLANNED?
DURING FY26, TOTAL DEMAND CONFIRMED BY INCOME TAX COMMISSIONER (APPEAL) WAS ₹2.57 LAKH CRORE. THE DEPARTMENT WILL STEP UP COLLECTION OF THESE CONFIRMED DEMANDS AND DOUBLE TOP PAN-WISE DEMANDS TO 10,000 FOR MONITORING.
WHAT NEW MEASURES ARE PLANNED FOR CRYPTO AND VIRTUAL DIGITAL ASSETS?
AT LEAST THREE SPECIAL PILOT PROJECTS WILL EXPLORE ONBOARDING NEW REPORTING ENTITIES AND EXPANDING SPECIFIED FINANCIAL TRANSACTION REPORTING FOR CRYPTO AND VDA TRANSACTIONS.
