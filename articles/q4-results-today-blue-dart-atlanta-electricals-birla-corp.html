Q4 Results Today: Blue Dart, Atlanta Electricals, Birla Corp

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Blue Dart Express Ltd, Atlanta Electricals Ltd, and Birla Corporation Ltd are scheduled to announce Q4 FY26 results on May 9, 2026.
  • 02/Analysts estimate Blue Dart Q4 revenue at ₹1,360-1,460 Cr and PAT at ₹100-118 Cr, while Birla Corp is expected to post ₹2,850 Cr revenue.
  • 03/Investors will track margin trends, cement pricing, and order book execution across all three companies.

Blue Dart Express Ltd (NSE: BLUEDART), Atlanta Electricals Ltd (NSE: ATLANTAELE), and Birla Corporation Ltd (NSE: BIRLACORP) are scheduled to announce their Q4 FY26 results on May 9, 2026. Board meetings for all three companies are fixed for Saturday.

Blue Dart's board meeting is set for 2:45 PM IST. Analysts estimate Q4 FY26 revenue between ₹1,360 crore and ₹1,460 crore. Profit after tax is projected at ₹100-118 crore, up from ₹53.2 crore in Q4 FY25.

The logistics major reported Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹1,616 crore and PAT of ₹70 crore. EBITDA stood at ₹291 crore with an 18% margin. Q4 estimates suggest margins could expand to 21-23%.

Atlanta Electricals will hold its board meeting on May 9 and an earnings call on May 11 at 10:00 AM IST. The transformer manufacturer posted Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹472 crore and PAT of ₹43 crore. Its order book reached an all-time high of ₹2,451 crore.

Birla Corporation's board meeting is scheduled for May 9, with a conference call on May 11 at 12:00 noon. The cement major reported Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹2,638 crore and PAT of ₹128 crore. Analysts expect Q4 revenue around ₹2,850 crore and PAT near ₹148 crore.

In Q4 FY25, Birla Corporation delivered revenue of ₹2,863 crore and net profit of ₹257 crore. Cement sales volume hit 5.2 million tonnes, up 8% year-on-year. The stock trades at ₹1,050, down 30% over the past year.

Blue Dart shares trade at ₹6,800, down 24% from their 52-week high of ₹9,200. Atlanta Electricals was listed on the NSE in September 2025 and has seen strong investor interest. All three companies face sector-specific demand challenges in the March quarter.

Investors will watch Blue Dart's margin expansion and Birla Corporation's cement pricing trends. Atlanta Electricals' order book execution and ESOS 2026 proposal are key monitorables. Management commentary on FY27 guidance will drive sentiment across all three stocks.
