SBI Warns Rupee Weakness Needs Structural BoP Fix to Anchor Inflation

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI's economic research department warns rupee depreciation of 6.39% since April 2025 is out of line with macro fundamentals
  • 02/BoP could turn negative for the third consecutive year; exchange rate becoming a pass-through for imported inflation
  • 03/SBI recommends diaspora funding scheme, G-Sec tax reform, restricting LRS outflows, and temporarily lowering the $250,000 annual remittance cap

State Bank of India has warned that persistent rupee weakness requires urgent balance of payments support measures. The bank's economic research department issued the note on April 29, 2026.

"The current rupee depreciation is not in line with India's macro fundamentals," said Soumya Ghosh, Group Chief Economic Advisor at SBI. He stressed the need for a structural solution to the BoP deficit.

The rupee declined 6.39 per cent between April 2, 2025 and February 27, 2026. Following the onset of the West Asia war, it fell further by 3.63 per cent.

SBI economists cautioned that the exchange rate cannot act as a shock absorber indefinitely. Increased volatility will turn it into a pass-through mechanism for imported inflation.

India's balance of payments could be negative for the third consecutive year. The report warned that inflationary expectations could become unanchored without timely intervention.

SBI recommended a diaspora funding scheme with calibrated corpus, yield, and tenor to attract patient capital. It also called for better tax treatment of Government Securities investments.

The bank suggested restricting remittances for deposits abroad, which have grown 25 per cent year-on-year. Investments in equity, debt, and immovable property have doubled on a YoY basis.

SBI proposed temporarily reducing the annual Liberalised Remittance Scheme limit of $250,000. Travel remittances account for roughly 55 per cent of LRS outflows and should be discouraged.

WHAT IS SBI'S MAIN WARNING ABOUT THE RUPEE?
SBI'S ECONOMIC RESEARCH DEPARTMENT HAS WARNED THAT THE CURRENT RUPEE DEPRECIATION IS NOT IN LINE WITH INDIA'S MACRO FUNDAMENTALS. WITHOUT TIMELY BALANCE OF PAYMENTS SUPPORT MEASURES, INFLATIONARY EXPECTATIONS COULD BECOME UNANCHORED AMID PERSISTENT RUPEE WEAKNESS AND VOLATILE CAPITAL FLOWS.
HOW MUCH HAS THE RUPEE DEPRECIATED?
THE RUPEE DECLINED 6.39 PER CENT BETWEEN APRIL 2, 2025 AND FEBRUARY 27, 2026. FOLLOWING THE ONSET OF THE WEST ASIA WAR, IT FELL FURTHER BY 3.63 PER CENT.
WHAT BOP MEASURES HAS SBI RECOMMENDED?
SBI RECOMMENDS A DIASPORA FUNDING SCHEME, BETTER TAX TREATMENT FOR GOVERNMENT SECURITIES INVESTMENTS, RESTRICTING REMITTANCES FOR DEPOSITS ABROAD, AND TEMPORARILY REDUCING THE ANNUAL LIBERALISED REMITTANCE SCHEME LIMIT OF $250,000.
WHY DOES SBI WANT TO RESTRICT LRS OUTFLOWS?
REMITTANCES FOR DEPOSITS ABROAD HAVE GROWN BY APPROXIMATELY 25 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR. TRAVEL REMITTANCES ACCOUNT FOR ABOUT 55 PER CENT OF LRS OUTFLOWS. SBI SAYS LRS SHOULD BE ALLOWED ONLY FOR EMERGENCY PURPOSES SUCH AS MEDICAL, EDUCATION, AND FAMILY MAINTENANCE.
WHAT IS THE RISK IF NO ACTION IS TAKEN?
SBI WARNS THAT INDIA'S BALANCE OF PAYMENTS COULD BE NEGATIVE FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. EXCHANGE RATE DEPRECIATION WOULD LEAD TO HIGHER IMPORTED INFLATION, AND INCREASED VOLATILITY WOULD TRANSFORM THE RUPEE INTO A PASS-THROUGH MECHANISM FOR IMPORTED INFLATION, DEFEATING THE PURPOSE OF PRUDENT MONETARY POLICY.
