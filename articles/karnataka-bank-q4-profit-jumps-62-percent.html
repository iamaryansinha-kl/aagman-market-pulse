Karnataka Bank Q4 Profit Jumps 62% to Rs 408 Crore, Dividend Declared

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Karnataka Bank Q4 FY26 net profit jumps 62% to Rs 408 crore from Rs 252 crore in the year-ago period
  • 02/Gross NPA declines to 2.78% from 3.08% and net NPA improves to 0.98% from 1.31%
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of Rs 5 per share; stock closed at Rs 248 on May 19

Karnataka Bank Ltd reported a 62% jump in fourth quarter net profit at Rs 408 crore. The figure stood at Rs 252 crore in the same period last year.

The bank's pre-provision operating profit rose 64% to Rs 615 crore. It was Rs 375 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Provisions for the quarter stood higher at Rs 90 crore against Rs 31 crore earlier. The spike in provisions indicates a conservative coverage approach.

For the full financial year FY26, net profit came in at Rs 1,311 crore. This compares with Rs 1,272 crore in FY25, marking a modest 3% annual rise.

Asset quality improved significantly during the quarter. Gross NPA declined to 2.78% from 3.08% a year prior.

Net NPA stood at 0.98% against 1.31% earlier. The decline in bad loans reflects healthier balance sheet trends.

The board recommended a final dividend of Rs 5 per equity share. This is 50% of the face value of Rs 10 per share for FY26.

Karnataka Bank shares closed at Rs 248 on May 19, up 2.02%. The stock trades on NSE under the ticker KTKBANK.
