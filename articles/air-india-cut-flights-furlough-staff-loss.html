Air India to Cut 20% Flights, Furlough Staff Amid ₹220 Billion Loss

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Air India board approves flight capacity cuts of over 20% for three months and furloughing nontechnical employees
  • 02/The airline closed FY2026 with a record loss exceeding ₹220 billion ($2.3 billion), compounding existing financial stress
  • 03/CEO Campbell Wilson resigned in April 2026; the Tata Group-owned carrier is actively searching for a replacement

Air India Ltd's board met on May 7, 2026, and approved sweeping cost-cutting measures. The airline plans to slash flight capacity by over 20% for the next three months.

The board also discussed furloughing nontechnical employees. Pay cuts for vice presidents and above, plus reduced bonuses for all staff, were on the table.

These moves mark the first major distress signal from an Indian airline since the Iran war began. Global aviation has been roiled by the conflict.

For Air India, the war compounds existing struggles. A fatal crash last year and Pakistan airspace closures had already disrupted operations severely.

The carrier closed FY2026 on March 31 with a record loss exceeding ₹220 billion ($2.3 billion). This erases much of the financial progress made under Tata Group ownership.

Singapore Airlines Ltd, which holds a 25.1% stake in Air India, has seen its earnings dragged down by these losses. It is now deepening its operational involvement at the carrier.

CEO Campbell Wilson resigned in April 2026, leaving a leadership vacuum. Rival IndiGo appointed aviation veteran Willie Walsh as CEO at the end of March.

Air India holds roughly 26.5% domestic market share, making it India's second-largest airline after IndiGo's 64%. The Vistara merger completed in November 2024 has not yet delivered the intended financial turnaround.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT COST-CUTTING MEASURES DID THE AIR INDIA BOARD APPROVE?
THE BOARD APPROVED CUTTING FLIGHT CAPACITY BY OVER 20% FOR THE NEXT THREE MONTHS. IT ALSO DISCUSSED FURLOUGHING NONTECHNICAL EMPLOYEES, REDUCING BONUSES FOR ALL STAFF, AND IMPLEMENTING PAY CUTS FOR VICE PRESIDENTS AND ABOVE.
WHAT IS AIR INDIA'S FINANCIAL LOSS FOR FY2026?
AIR INDIA CLOSED THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDING MARCH 31, 2026, WITH A RECORD LOSS EXCEEDING ₹220 BILLION ($2.3 BILLION). THIS IS THE LARGEST ANNUAL LOSS SINCE THE TATA GROUP TOOK CONTROL OF THE AIRLINE.
WHO OWNS AIR INDIA?
AIR INDIA IS OWNED BY THE TATA GROUP. SINGAPORE AIRLINES LTD HOLDS A 25.1% STAKE IN THE CARRIER AND IS DEEPENING ITS OPERATIONAL INVOLVEMENT AMID THE MOUNTING LOSSES.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT LEADERSHIP STATUS AT AIR INDIA?
CEO CAMPBELL WILSON RESIGNED IN APRIL 2026. THE AIRLINE IS ACTIVELY SEARCHING FOR A NEW CHIEF EXECUTIVE TO NAVIGATE THE CRISIS. AN AIR INDIA REPRESENTATIVE DID NOT IMMEDIATELY RESPOND TO REQUESTS FOR COMMENT.
HOW DOES AIR INDIA'S MARKET SHARE COMPARE TO INDIGO?
AIR INDIA HOLDS APPROXIMATELY 26.5% OF INDIA'S DOMESTIC MARKET, MAKING IT THE SECOND-LARGEST AIRLINE. INDIGO DOMINATES WITH A 64% SHARE. THE VISTARA MERGER COMPLETED IN NOVEMBER 2024 WAS INTENDED TO STRENGTHEN AIR INDIA'S COMPETITIVE POSITION.
