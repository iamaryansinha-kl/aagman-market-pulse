Ambit Capital Maintains Buy on IndiGo, Sets 1-Year Target at ₹5,300

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ambit Capital maintains Buy on InterGlobe Aviation with a one-year DCF-based target price of ₹5,300
  • 02/Brokerage expects 15% revenue CAGR and 28% EBITDA CAGR over FY26-29E despite 22% EPS cut for FY27
  • 03/Key catalysts include new CEO appointment, winter schedule clarity, and crude or forex normalisation in 2HFY27

Ambit Capital has maintained a Buy rating on InterGlobe Aviation Ltd, operator of IndiGo. The brokerage has set a one-year DCF-based target price of ₹5,300. The stock trades at ₹4,345.90.

The target implies 6x one-year forward EV/EBITDA and 5x one-year forward EV/EBITDAR. This reflects Ambit's confidence in IndiGo's long-term international expansion strategy.

IndiGo's international ASK increased from 23% in FY23 to 32%. The airline is adding routes every year and building a global network from its domestic base.

The brokerage expects a 40:60 owned-to-leased aircraft mix going forward. Currently, the ratio stands at 20:80. Owning aircraft reduces long-term finance costs despite higher upfront capital outlay.

Ambit forecasts a 15% revenue CAGR and 28% EBITDA CAGR over FY26-29E. The growth is underpinned by a favourable route mix towards Tier-2/3 cities and international destinations.

Near-term headwinds persist. Ambit cut its FY27 EPS estimate by 22% and FY28 EPS by 3%. It assumes crude at USD82 per barrel and USD/INR at 93 for FY27-28E.

IndiGo holds ₹51,600 crore in cash on its balance sheet. Investors are watching how the airline deploys this capital amid expansion and fleet acquisition plans.

Meaningful catalysts are expected in the second half of FY27. These include a new CEO appointment, clarity on the winter schedule, and normalisation in crude prices and the rupee.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT RATING AND TARGET PRICE HAS AMBIT CAPITAL ASSIGNED TO INDIGO?
AMBIT CAPITAL MAINTAINS A BUY RATING ON INTERGLOBE AVIATION LTD WITH A ONE-YEAR DCF-BASED TARGET PRICE OF ₹5,300. THE CURRENT MARKET PRICE IS ₹4,345.90.
WHAT GROWTH DOES AMBIT CAPITAL EXPECT FROM INDIGO OVER FY26-29E?
AMBIT EXPECTS A 15% REVENUE CAGR AND A 28% EBITDA CAGR FOR INDIGO OVER THE PERIOD FY26 TO FY29E. THE BROKERAGE ALSO PROJECTS A FAVOURABLE ROUTE MIX SHIFT TOWARDS TIER-2/3 CITIES AND INTERNATIONAL DESTINATIONS.
WHY DID AMBIT CAPITAL CUT ITS EPS ESTIMATES FOR INDIGO?
AMBIT CUT ITS FY27 EPS ESTIMATE BY 22% AND FY28 EPS BY 3% DUE TO NEAR-TERM HEADWINDS. THESE INCLUDE GEOPOLITICAL ISSUES, RISING CRUDE PRICES, AND INR DEPRECIATION. THE BROKERAGE BUILDS IN CRUDE AT USD82 PER BARREL AND USD/INR AT 93 FOR FY27-28E.
WHAT IS INDIGO'S CURRENT AND TARGETED OWNED-TO-LEASED AIRCRAFT MIX?
INDIGO'S CURRENT OWNED-TO-LEASED AIRCRAFT MIX IS 20:80. AMBIT EXPECTS THE AIRLINE TO MOVE TOWARDS A 40:60 MIX, AS OWNING AIRCRAFT IS LESS EXPENSIVE THAN LEASING OVER THE LONG TERM AND REDUCES FINANCE COSTS.
WHAT CATALYSTS DOES AMBIT CAPITAL EXPECT FOR INDIGO IN 2HFY27?
AMBIT IDENTIFIES THREE KEY CATALYSTS FOR THE SECOND HALF OF FY27. THESE ARE THE APPOINTMENT OF A NEW CEO, CLARITY ON THE WINTER SCHEDULE, AND NORMALISATION IN CRUDE OIL PRICES AND THE INDIAN RUPEE AGAINST THE US DOLLAR.
