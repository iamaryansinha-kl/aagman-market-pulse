Bajaj Finance Q2: AUM Up 26.5% to Nearly ₹5.85 Lakh Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Finance reported 26.5% YoY AUM growth to nearly ₹5.85 lakh crore in its provisional Q2 FY27 business update, its fastest pace in seven quarters.
  • 02/New loans booked rose 11% YoY to 1.345 crore, while the deposit base stood at ₹69,750 crore as of September 30, 2026.
  • 03/Shares jumped around 5% to ₹993 on Monday; Jefferies maintains Buy with a ₹1,280 target price, implying 35% upside, and keeps the stock among its top picks.

Bajaj Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJFINANCE) reported a 26.5% year-on-year jump in assets under management in its provisional business update for the second quarter of FY27. AUM stood at nearly ₹5.85 lakh crore as of September 30, 2026, compared with ₹4.6 lakh crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.

The company released the update on Saturday. During the July-September quarter alone, AUM increased by nearly ₹37,800 crore.

The stock responded immediately. Bajaj Finance shares jumped around 5% on Monday to ₹993 apiece, snapping a four-session losing streak.

The customer franchise reached 12.89 crore as of September 30, 2026. That compares with 11.1 crore a year earlier.

Customer acquisition stayed brisk. The company added more than 44 lakh customers during Q2 FY27 alone.

New loans booked grew 11% year-on-year to 1.345 crore in Q2 FY27. That compares with 1.217 crore new loans booked in Q2 FY26.

The company itself flagged a caveat on this number. It said the loan-booking figures are not directly comparable, owing to the early onset of the festive season in the previous year's base period.

The deposit base stood at ₹69,750 crore at the end of Q2 FY27. Deposits remain a key funding pillar for the non-banking financial company.

All of these numbers are provisional and subject to audit. The final Q2 results will be announced later, and they will include the full profit-and-loss detail.

Brokerage reaction was swift. Jefferies noted that the 26% YoY AUM growth marks Bajaj Finance's highest in seven quarters.

Jefferies also pointed out that new-loans growth slowed from 20% to 11% year-on-year. It attributed this partly to the early festive season in the base period, when loan bookings had grown 26% YoY.

Jefferies reaffirmed Bajaj Finance among its top picks and maintained a Buy rating. Its target price of ₹1,280 implies 35% upside from current levels.

The update also lands alongside a fresh capital raise. Last week, Bajaj Finance announced a ₹17,500 crore equity raise — ₹11,700 crore via a qualified institutions placement plus ₹5,800 crore via warrants to promoter Bajaj Finserv — its first equity capital raise since November 2023.

The stock's recent record has been mixed. Shares have fallen more than 6% over the past month, though they have gained over 2% in 2026 so far.

That still counts as outperformance. The Nifty 50 has fallen 14% this year, meaning Bajaj Finance has decisively beaten the benchmark.

The longer-term returns remain substantial. The stock has delivered over 26% returns in three years and 29% in five years. Its market capitalisation stands at more than ₹6.17 lakh crore.

What should investors watch from here? The final audited Q2 numbers, due later, will show how the AUM growth translated into profitability.
