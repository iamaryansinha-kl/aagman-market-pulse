Rolls Royce Opens £30M India Plant, Targets 10x Sourcing Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rolls-Royce inaugurates third IAMPL manufacturing unit in Hosur with £30 million investment to boost aero-engine component sourcing from India
  • 02/Company targets 10-fold increase in India supply chain sourcing and aims to double workforce to approximately 10,000 people
  • 03/Rolls-Royce has offered to co-develop a 120 kN combat jet engine for India's AMCA programme with full IP transfer to India

Rolls-Royce Holdings plc has inaugurated its third co-owned manufacturing unit in Hosur, Tamil Nadu. The facility represents an investment of approximately £30 million. Sashi Mukundan, executive vice president for transformation at Rolls-Royce India, confirmed the development.

The new unit expands the company's 50:50 joint venture with Hindustan Aeronautics Limited. The JV, International Aerospace Manufacturing Private Limited (IAMPL), already operates production lines in Bengaluru and Hosur.

The facility will manufacture high-precision components for civil and military aircraft engines. It strengthens Rolls-Royce's plan to at least double its supply chain sourcing from India over the next five years.

Rolls-Royce is also expanding its Global Capability and Innovation Centre in Bengaluru. The centre will become the company's largest capability hub globally, supporting engineering, HR, finance and procurement functions.

CEO Tufan Erginbilgic announced in February 2026 plans to double the India workforce to 10,000. He also set a 10-fold increase target for local supply chain sourcing. The India ecosystem currently employs over 4,000 people, including 2,800 engineers.

The UK-headquartered company reported a group operating profit of £3.5 billion for 2025. In April 2026, it signed a ₹3,800 crore seven-year supply agreement with Sigma Advanced Systems for aerospace components.

Rolls-Royce has offered to co-develop a 120 kN class combat jet engine core for India's Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft programme. The proposal includes full technology transfer with intellectual property ownership for India.

Over 1,400 Rolls-Royce engines currently power Indian defence platforms. These include Jaguar combat aircraft, Hawk trainers, and Arjun Main Battle Tanks. The company sources from over 100 Indian vendors including Tata, Bharat Forge and Godrej.

"We have deep ambitions to develop India as a home for Rolls-Royce building on our strong and successful partnership" — Tufan Erginbilgic, CEO, Rolls-Royce, during India visit in October 2025.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS ROLLS-ROYCE'S NEW INVESTMENT IN HOSUR?
ROLLS-ROYCE HAS INAUGURATED ITS THIRD CO-OWNED MANUFACTURING UNIT IN HOSUR, TAMIL NADU, WITH AN INVESTMENT OF APPROXIMATELY £30 MILLION (AROUND ₹330 CRORE). THE FACILITY WILL MANUFACTURE HIGH-PRECISION AERO-ENGINE COMPONENTS FOR CIVIL AND MILITARY AIRCRAFT.
WHAT IS IAMPL AND WHO OWNS IT?
INTERNATIONAL AEROSPACE MANUFACTURING PRIVATE LIMITED (IAMPL) IS A 50:50 JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN ROLLS-ROYCE AND HINDUSTAN AERONAUTICS LIMITED (HAL). IT OPERATES PRODUCTION LINES IN BENGALURU AND HOSUR.
HOW MUCH DOES ROLLS-ROYCE PLAN TO INCREASE SOURCING FROM INDIA?
ROLLS-ROYCE HAS SET A TARGET TO AT LEAST DOUBLE ITS SUPPLY CHAIN SOURCING FROM INDIA OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS. IN FEBRUARY 2026, CEO TUFAN ERGINBILGIC UNVEILED A MORE AMBITIOUS TARGET OF A 10-FOLD INCREASE IN LOCAL SOURCING AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S INDIA EXPANSION ROADMAP.
WHAT DEFENCE PROGRAMMES IS ROLLS-ROYCE TARGETING IN INDIA?
ROLLS-ROYCE HAS OFFERED TO CO-DEVELOP A 120 KN CLASS COMBAT JET ENGINE CORE FOR INDIA'S ADVANCED MEDIUM COMBAT AIRCRAFT (AMCA) PROGRAMME WITH FULL TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER AND IP OWNERSHIP FOR INDIA. THE COMPANY HAS ALSO EXPRESSED INTENT TO COLLABORATE WITH THE INDIAN NAVY ON AN ELECTRIC WARSHIP PROJECT.
HOW MANY PEOPLE DOES ROLLS-ROYCE CURRENTLY EMPLOY IN INDIA?
OVER 4,000 PEOPLE CURRENTLY WORK ACROSS THE ROLLS-ROYCE ECOSYSTEM IN INDIA, INCLUDING 2,800 ENGINEERS. THE COMPANY PLANS TO MORE THAN DOUBLE THIS WORKFORCE TO APPROXIMATELY 10,000 PEOPLE THROUGH ITS EXPANSION INITIATIVES.
