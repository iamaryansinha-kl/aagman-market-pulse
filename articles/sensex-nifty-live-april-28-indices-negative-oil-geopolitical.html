Sensex Nifty Live April 28: Indices Turn Negative on Oil, Geopolitical Jitters

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex falls ~150 pts to 77,155.39 and Nifty slips 15 pts to 24,077.10 as April F&O expiry meets oil-driven risk-off
  • 02/Crude oil surges with Brent near $102.59 and WTI at $97.45 as US-Iran Hormuz talks stall; rupee weakens 24 paise to 94.39
  • 03/Coal India leads Nifty gainers up 1.6% on Q4 beat; SBI, Sun Pharma, Axis Bank top losers; watch 24,000 support

Indian equity benchmarks turned negative on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. The Sensex fell around 150 points to 77,155.39, while the Nifty 50 dropped 15 points to 24,077.10.

The decline came as April derivative contracts expired. Unresolved geopolitical tensions in West Asia and elevated crude oil prices kept investors cautious.

Crude oil futures traded higher with Brent at $102.59 and WTI at $97.45. The rally followed reports that US President Donald Trump was unhappy with Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

The Indian rupee weakened 24 paise to 94.39 against the US dollar. Rising oil prices and month-end dollar demand weighed on the currency.

On the losing side, State Bank of India fell 1.16% to ₹1,098.90. Sun Pharmaceutical Industries declined 0.91% to ₹1,717.80, while Axis Bank dropped 0.85% to ₹1,312.90.

Coal India led Nifty 50 gainers, rising 1.57% to ₹459.60 after reporting a 12% jump in Q4 consolidated PAT to ₹10,908 crore. Grasim Industries and Adani Enterprises also traded higher.

The Nifty Bank index remained under pressure, falling over 250 points to trade near the 56,000 mark. PSU banks were particularly weak.

Ankit Jaiswal, Senior Research Analyst at Univest, noted that holding above 24,000 is the base case. A breakdown below this level could push Nifty toward the 23,800-23,600 zone.

FII net selling in April stands at approximately ₹56,363 crore month-to-date. DIIs have net bought ₹39,478 crore, partially offsetting foreign outflows.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, was elevated near 19.71. Global cues remained mixed with Asian markets trading near eight-week highs but oil keeping risk sentiment in check.
