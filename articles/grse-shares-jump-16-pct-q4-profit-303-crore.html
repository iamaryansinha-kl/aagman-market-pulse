GRSE Shares Jump 16% After Q4 Profit Surges 24% to ₹303 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/GRSE shares rally 16% after Q4 standalone net profit jumps 24.1% y-o-y to ₹303.19 crore
  • 02/Revenue from operations rises 29% to ₹2,119.21 crore; FY26 full-year PAT up 42% to ₹747.93 crore
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of ₹6.70 per share for FY26; record date to be announced

Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd (NSE: GRSE) shares surged 16% in early trade on April 29, 2026. The rally followed strong Q4 FY26 earnings and a dividend announcement.

The company reported a standalone net profit of ₹303.19 crore for the quarter ended March 2026. This marks a 24.1% increase from ₹244.24 crore in the year-ago period.

Revenue from operations rose 29% year-on-year to ₹2,119.21 crore. It stood at ₹1,642.03 crore in the corresponding quarter last fiscal.

For the full financial year FY26, profit after tax increased 42% to ₹747.93 crore. This compares with ₹527.40 crore in FY25.

The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹6.70 per share for FY26. The record date will be announced separately.

At 10:32 am, the stock traded 11% higher at ₹3,193.40. It touched an intraday high of ₹3,339 against the previous close of ₹2,875.30.

The sharp rally reflects robust earnings growth and improved operational performance. Investor sentiment was further supported by the dividend recommendation.

The company has delivered consistent growth across its shipbuilding and engineering operations. Strong execution and order momentum drove the annual performance.
