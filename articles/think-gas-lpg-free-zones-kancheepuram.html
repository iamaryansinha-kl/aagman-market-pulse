THINK Gas Launches 'LPG Free Zones' at Kancheepuram, Certifies 4,000+ Homes

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/THINK Gas certifies 7 residential communities as 'LPG Free Zones' at Kancheepuram, covering over 4,000+ households transitioned to PNG
  • 02/Initiative launched in partnership with PNGRB amid government push for 12 crore PNG connections by 2034 and mandatory PNG for apartments
  • 03/THINK Gas targets 30,000+ new PNG connections under National Drive 2.0; tariff reforms cut consumer prices by up to ₹5 per scm

THINK Gas has launched its 'LPG Free Zones' initiative at Kancheepuram in Tamil Nadu. The programme was announced on May 9, 2026, in partnership with the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB).

Seven large residential communities across the Kancheepuram Geographical Area have been certified as LPG Free Zones. These communities account for over 4,000 households that have transitioned from LPG cylinders to Piped Natural Gas.

THINK Gas is the merged brand of AG&P Pratham and THINK Gas. It holds 19 CGD licenses from PNGRB to operate across 49 districts in 10 states.

"Through the LPG Free Zone declaration, we aim to accelerate PNG adoption and build greater awareness around piped gas infrastructure. We remain committed to supporting more households in making a seamless transition to PNG," said Thirukkumaran N T, Regional Head - Kancheepuram, THINK Gas.

The initiative comes as the Centre mandates PNG compulsory for apartments where infrastructure exists. This regulatory push is expected to accelerate household migration from LPG to PNG.

India currently has approximately 1.56 crore domestic PNG connections as of September 2025. The government targets 12 crore connections by 2034 under its expanded CGD programme.

THINK Gas has committed to adding over 30,000 households under PNGRB's National Drive 2.0. The nationwide campaign runs from January to March 2026 across its 10 licensed states.

The company benefits from the PNGRB's unified tariff reform effective January 1, 2026. THINK Gas reduced PNG prices by up to ₹5 per standard cubic metre following the tariff reset.

THINK Gas has the largest foreign direct investment in India's greenfield CGD business. Its investors include I-Squared Capital and a Japanese consortium of OSAKA Gas, JOIN, Sumitomo Corporation and Konoike Transport.
