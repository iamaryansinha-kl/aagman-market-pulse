Piramal Finance Q4 Profit Surges 390% to ₹502 Crore, Stock Jumps 12%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Piramal Finance Q4 FY26 net profit surges 390% YoY to ₹502 crore, with FY26 PAT rising 210% to ₹1,506 crore
  • 02/AUM crosses ₹1 lakh crore with 25% YoY growth; retail book at 85%, legacy book shrinks to just 3%
  • 03/Nomura rates 'buy' at ₹2,150 target; Jefferies 'hold' at ₹1,940; stock has gained 56% in one year

Piramal Finance Ltd reported a consolidated net profit of ₹502 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 390 per cent year-on-year jump in quarterly earnings.

The company also posted FY26 profit after tax of ₹1,506 crore, up 210 per cent from the previous year. The sharp improvement reflects better margins and a stronger retail mix.

Assets under management crossed ₹1 lakh crore, growing 25 per cent year-on-year. The retail segment now accounts for 85 per cent of the total book.

The legacy book has shrunk to just 3 per cent of AUM. Gross NPAs declined to 2.3 per cent, reflecting improved asset quality across the portfolio.

Net interest margin expanded to 6.5 per cent during the quarter. The retail operating expense-to-AUM ratio fell to 3.6 per cent, indicating better operating leverage.

The stock surged nearly 12 per cent on April 28, hitting an intraday high of ₹2,073.20. It traded at ₹2,066.20 by 11.56 am against the previous close of ₹1,843.10.

Nomura maintained a 'buy' rating with a target price of ₹2,150. The brokerage said the company is "cleaning up and scaling up well," and expects RoAUM to hit 2.5 per cent by Q4 FY27.

Jefferies retained a 'hold' rating at ₹1,940, flagging valuation constraints. It noted that one-off gains were used to provision legacy assets, and return ratios may stay subdued in the near term.

Piramal Finance has gained 56 per cent over the past year. It sharply outperformed the Nifty 50, which declined 1.1 per cent in the same period.
