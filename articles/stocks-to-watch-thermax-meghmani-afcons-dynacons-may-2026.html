Stocks to Watch: Thermax, Meghmani, Afcons, Dynacons Lead Corporate Action

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Thermax partners with Ankur Scientific for India's first green methanol facility with 18,000 tonne capacity at Kandla
  • 02/Afcons Infrastructure secures ₹373 crore EPC order while Dynacons wins ₹750.82 crore RBI cloud infrastructure deal
  • 03/Meghmani Organics gets nano fertiliser approval; EMS, Envair Electrodyne and GRE Renew also report updates

Thermax Limited (NSE: THERMAX) has partnered with Ankur Scientific to build India's first Green Methanol Production Facility. The plant will come up at Deendayal Port Authority, Kandla, Gujarat with a production capacity of 18,000 tonnes.

India's shipping industry accounts for around 3% of national emissions. It will need 37,000 tonnes of methanol by 2030. The new facility will address nearly half of this demand.

Afcons Infrastructure Ltd (NSE: AFCONS) has secured a ₹373 crore order for civil infrastructure works. The project falls under its Urban Infrastructure and Metro Business Unit.

The Afcons order involves EPC contracts and tunnelling using TBM. Afcons is ranked in the Top 140 international contractors globally by ENR. It stands 12th in Bridges and 14th in Marine & Ports.

Dynacons Systems & Solutions Ltd has bagged a ₹750.82 crore order including GST from the Reserve Bank of India. The contract covers supply, installation and maintenance of Private Cloud Infrastructure.

The RBI is building a Next Generation Data Centre at Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Dynacons will support current and future workloads with provision for AI Cloud integration.

Meghmani Organics Ltd said its subsidiary received approval from the Ministry of Agriculture. The nod covers manufacturing of Nano DAP, Nano NPK and Nano Zinc fertiliser products.

EMS Ltd won a ₹143.80 crore sewer project in Varanasi. Envair Electrodyne got in-principle approval for a ₹136.87 crore CCL project. Asahi India Glass and Standard Surfactants reported operational incidents.

WHAT IS THERMAX'S GREEN METHANOL PROJECT?
THERMAX HAS PARTNERED WITH ANKUR SCIENTIFIC TO SET UP INDIA'S FIRST GREEN METHANOL PRODUCTION FACILITY AT DEENDAYAL PORT AUTHORITY, KANDLA, GUJARAT. THE FACILITY WILL HAVE A PRODUCTION CAPACITY OF 18,000 TONNES.
HOW MUCH IS THE AFCONS INFRASTRUCTURE ORDER WORTH?
AFCONS INFRASTRUCTURE LTD HAS SECURED AN ORDER VALUED AT ₹373 CRORE FOR CIVIL INFRASTRUCTURE WORKS INCLUDING EPC CONTRACTS AND TUNNELLING USING TBM UNDER ITS URBAN INFRASTRUCTURE AND METRO BUSINESS UNIT.
WHAT IS THE VALUE OF DYNACONS' RBI ORDER?
DYNACONS SYSTEMS & SOLUTIONS LTD HAS BEEN AWARDED AN ORDER WORTH ₹750.82 CRORES INCLUDING GST FOR PRIVATE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE FOR THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA'S DATA CENTRES.
WHAT APPROVAL DID MEGHMANI ORGANICS RECEIVE?
MEGHMANI ORGANICS' WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY MEGHMANI CROP NUTRITION LTD RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE FOR MANUFACTURING NANO DAP, NANO NPK AND NANO ZINC FERTILISER PRODUCTS.

EMS LTD WON A ₹143.80 CRORE SEWER PROJECT IN VARANASI. ENVAIR ELECTRODYNE GOT IN-PRINCIPLE APPROVAL FOR A ₹136.87 CRORE CCL PROJECT IN ODISHA. ASAHI INDIA GLASS REPORTED A CHIMNEY INCIDENT AND STANDARD SURFACTANTS REPORTED A FIRE ON MAY 3 WITH OPERATIONS RESUMING IN 2-3 MONTHS.
