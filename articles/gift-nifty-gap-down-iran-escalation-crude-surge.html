Gift Nifty Signals 150-Point Gap-Down on Iran Escalation, Crude Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gift Nifty at 24,035 signals a gap-down opening of over 150 points for Indian markets on May 5
  • 02/Iran-US war escalation drives crude above $100; Dow sees sharp selling pressure overnight
  • 03/Derivatives PCR at 0.61 signals bearish bias; support at 24,000-23,800, resistance capped at 24,300

Indian equity markets are set for a weak opening on Tuesday, May 5, 2026. Gift Nifty is trading at 24,035, indicating a gap-down of over 150 points.

The sell-off follows a fresh escalation in the Iran-US war. Crude oil has surged above $100 per barrel on renewed geopolitical concerns around the Strait of Hormuz.

The Nifty 50 closed at 24,119.30 on Monday, up 121.75 points. The Sensex ended at 77,269.40, gaining 355.90 points.

Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder of Livelong Wealth, said global cues remain fragile. "The US markets saw sharp selling pressure, with the Dow correcting significantly as crude oil surged on renewed geopolitical concerns around the Strait of Hormuz."

"Elevated oil prices, still holding above the $100 mark, continue to pose a macro risk for India, given its import dependence. Sustained strength in crude could weigh on inflation expectations and corporate margins, keeping risk appetite in check," Hariprasad added.

Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, noted that state assembly election outcomes and FPIs turning net buyers offer some support. However, geopolitical uncertainty and global risk aversion continue to dominate sentiment, limiting sustained upside.

Dhupesh Dhameja, Derivatives Research Analyst at SAMCO Securities, said PCR near 0.61 suggests a cautious-to-bearish undertone. Aggressive call writing at 24,200-24,300 caps upside, while put base at 24,000-23,800 reinforces support.

India VIX is sustaining near 18, indicating controlled volatility. This supports range-bound price action rather than trending moves.

"Structurally, until the index decisively breaks above the 24,300 resistance zone, rallies are likely to face selling pressure, making a sell-on-rise strategy more favorable," Dhameja said.

Taiwan, Vietnam and Australia markets are down in early trade on Tuesday. Major Asian-Pacific markets remain closed for holidays.
