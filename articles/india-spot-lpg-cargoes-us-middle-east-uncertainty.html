India Buys Spot LPG Cargoes From US as Middle East Gulf Supply Uncertain

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/State-run OMCs procure spot LPG cargoes from the US as Middle East Gulf supplies remain uncertain due to the ongoing West Asia conflict
  • 02/MEG LPG exports subdued at 150,000 b/d in April; India's weekly imports from West Asia fell to 89,000 tonnes, just 34% of total imports
  • 03/US Gulf Coast LPG exports hit near-record 2.4 mb/d; India has expanded sourcing base from 10 to 15 countries

State-run oil marketing companies are procuring spot LPG cargoes from the US. The move addresses supply shortfalls caused by the ongoing West Asia conflict.

Middle East Gulf LPG exports remain subdued at 150,000 barrels per day in April. Saudi Arabia's exports via Yanbu hold steady at 110,000 b/d month-on-month.

India has expanded its LPG sourcing base from 10 to 15 countries. Last month, the Oil Ministry secured 800,000 tonnes of inbound cargoes from the US, Russia, Australia, and others.

"We will buy LPG from wherever it is available." - Sujata Sharma, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. The statement signals aggressive supply diversification.

US Gulf Coast LPG exports are running at near-record levels of 2.4 million barrels per day. Global LPG exports recovered after falling over 1 mb/d since early March, per Vortexa data.

India's weekly LPG imports fell to 265,000 tonnes in the week to March 19 from 322,000 tonnes on March 5. West Asian inflows declined to just 89,000 tonnes, representing only 34 per cent of total imports.

S&P Global Energy CERA noted that India is increasingly turning to the US for LPG. If Middle Eastern tensions persist, North American supplies could gain a stronger foothold in India's import mix.

MIDDLE EAST GULF LPG EXPORTS HAVE REMAINED SUBDUED AT 150,000 BARRELS PER DAY IN APRIL (DAYS 1-21). SAUDI ARABIA'S LPG EXPORTS VIA YANBU ARE UNCHANGED MONTH-ON-MONTH AT AROUND 110,000 B/D.
HOW MANY SOURCES IS INDIA NOW IMPORTING LPG FROM?
INDIA HAS INCREASED ITS LPG SOURCING BASE FROM 10 TO 15 COUNTRIES. THE OIL MINISTRY HAS SECURED 800,000 TONNES OF ASSURED INBOUND LPG CARGOES FROM THE US, RUSSIA, AUSTRALIA, AND OTHER COUNTRIES.
WHAT DID THE JOINT SECRETARY AT MOPNG SAY ABOUT LPG PROCUREMENT?
SUJATA SHARMA, JOINT SECRETARY AT THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS, SAID, "WE WILL BUY LPG FROM WHEREVER IT IS AVAILABLE." THIS REFLECTS INDIA'S STRATEGY OF AGGRESSIVE SUPPLY DIVERSIFICATION.

S&P GLOBAL ENERGY CERA STATED THAT IF THE MIDDLE EASTERN CONFLICT CONTINUES FOR A LONG PERIOD, THERE IS A CHANCE FOR NORTH AMERICAN LPG TO GAIN A STRONGER FOOTHOLD IN INDIA'S IMPORT MIX. THE US GULF COAST IS ALREADY EXPORTING AT NEAR-RECORD LEVELS OF 2.4 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY.
