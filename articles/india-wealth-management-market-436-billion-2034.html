India's Wealth Management Market to Grow 2.5x to $436 Billion by 2034: Emkay Wealth

## A $265 billion growth runway

India's wealth-management market is poised to grow 2.5 times over the next decade, from approximately $171 billion in 2025 to $436 billion by 2034, according to Emkay Wealth Management. The implied compound annual growth rate is around 10.6%.

The drivers are structural: rising incomes, the financialisation of household savings and deeper participation in capital markets. Indian households are steadily shifting savings out of physical assets and plain deposits into mutual funds, equities and professionally managed products, with SIP-led investing pulling new investors in.

## The client base is widening

High-net-worth individuals remain the anchor segment at an estimated 62.8% of the market. But demand is spreading to mass-affluent, affluent, UHNI, family-office and institutional clients, and the product shelf is widening to cover portfolio management services, alternative investment funds, private credit, structured products, global assets and estate planning.

CEO Parag Morey framed the shift as one from wealth creation to wealth stewardship — clients asking for integrated approaches to accumulation, preservation and transmission rather than individual products.

Scale is already visible: quarterly average mutual fund industry AUM reached approximately Rs 77.98 lakh crore in FY26. Family offices, meanwhile, are expanding beyond investing into governance, succession and philanthropy.

## Where Emkay sees the growth

CIO Ashish Ranawade argued the next leg of growth sits outside the biggest companies. "The growth will be more, not in the large caps, or the top 250 companies, but in the small cap, micro cap, and even the SME segment," he said, while cautioning that global diversification remains necessary.

On fixed income, the firm is cautious on the long end of the curve, arguing limited scope for a big rally from current benchmark yields and flagging a move towards 7.10%. The preference is for accrual and performing-credit strategies over large duration bets.

Emkay also sees localisation and indigenisation sectors — electronics, defence, aerospace, specialty chemicals, precision engineering and space technology — as the structural opportunity, alongside infrastructure spending and financialisation.

## FAQ Q: How big will India's wealth market get? A/01/ Emkay Wealth projects $436 billion by 2034, up from about $171 billion in 2025 — a 10.6% CAGR.

Q: Who drives demand today? A/02/ HNIs at roughly 62.8% of the market, with mass-affluent and family-office segments growing fastest.

Q: What supports the growth? A/03/ Financialisation of household savings, SIP-led investing (MF average AUM of Rs 77.98 lakh crore in FY26) and demand for comprehensive advice.

Q: What does Emkay like in fixed income? A/04/ Accrual and performing-credit strategies rather than large duration exposure, with limited scope for a rally in long-dated yields.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS - A $436 billion wealth market by 2034 — 2.5x today's size. - Growth is broadening beyond HNIs into mass-affluent and family offices. - Small, micro and SME segments are flagged as the next growth engine. - Fixed income positioning favours credit over duration as yields face upward drift.
