Mindspace REIT Q4FY26: Revenue Rises 31% to ₹888 Cr on Record Leasing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mindspace REIT Q4FY26 revenue from operations surged 31% YoY to ₹888 crore, while NOI jumped 37.4% to ₹742 crore
  • 02/Gross leasing hit a record 3.5 million sq ft during the quarter, taking full-year tally to 7.1 msf with committed occupancy at 95.7%
  • 03/Distributions for the quarter stood at ₹431 crore with DPU of ₹6.64; FY26 NOI grew 29.2% to ₹2,664 crore

Mindspace Business Parks REIT (NSE: MINDSPACE) reported strong Q4FY26 results on April 29, 2026. Revenue from operations rose 31% on year to ₹888 crore.

Net operating income jumped 37.4% to ₹742 crore. Distributions stood at ₹431 crore, up 9.7% YoY, translating to a DPU of ₹6.64.

Excluding a one-off tax refund in the base quarter, distribution growth was significantly higher at 24.5%. This reflects strong underlying operational performance.

Gross leasing hit a record 3.5 million square feet during the quarter. Full-year leasing reached 7.1 msf.

Committed occupancy improved sequentially by 1.2 percentage points to 95.7%. This is the highest level since the REIT's listing.

Re-leasing spreads stood at 40.3% for the quarter. Portfolio in-place rents were ₹80.4 per square foot per month.

"FY26 was a very strong year across every metric. Large tenants are committing early, and that is the clearest signal of market confidence," said Ramesh Nair, MD & CEO.

For FY26, NOI grew 29.2% to ₹2,664 crore. Net asset value rose 9% to ₹527 per unit. The REIT is advancing a 5.4 msf pipeline and ₹5,541 crore Chennai acquisitions.
