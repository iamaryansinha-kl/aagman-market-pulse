Q4 Results Today, April 29, 2026: IOB, Granules India, Maruti Suzuki Report Earnings; Adani Power, Bajaj Finance, Vedanta Among Key Results Awaited

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

April 29, 2026, marked one of the busiest days on the Q4 FY26 earnings calendar, with more than a dozen companies across banking, auto, metals, NBFC, and energy sectors lined up to report their fourth-quarter numbers. By early afternoon, a clutch of mid-cap and large-cap names had already dropped their results, triggering sharp stock movements, while several index-heavy candidates were still awaited.

The benchmark indices traded listlessly through the session, reflecting the wait-and-see mood. The BSE Sensex was up 51.29 points at 76,937.82, and the NSE Nifty 50 added 32.15 points to 23,943.30. Gains in Maruti Suzuki, HCL Tech, and Infosys were offset by weakness in HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Reliance Industries.

---

BANKING: IOB NET PROFIT UP 43%, BANDHAN BANK SOARS 12%

Public-sector lender Indian Overseas Bank delivered one of the standout numbers of the morning. The Chennai-based bank reported a 43% year-on-year rise in standalone net profit to ₹1,505.45 crore for Q4 FY26, against ₹1,051.07 crore in the year-ago quarter. For the full year, IOB's PAT climbed to ₹5,208.03 crore from ₹3,334.71 crore in FY25. The stock responded with a 2% gain, trading at ₹35.54.

Private lender Bandhan Bank was the star performer in the banking pack. Its Q4 net profit surged to ₹534 crore from ₹318 crore a year earlier, while revenue from operations came in at ₹5,428 crore. Asset quality showed marginal improvement, with gross non-performing assets easing to 3.27% from 3.33% and net NPA sliding to 0.97% from 0.99%. The market cheered the numbers, sending Bandhan Bank shares up 12% to a fresh 52-week high.

Kolkata-based Dhanlaxmi Bank also reported improved profitability, with Q4 net profit rising to ₹43.49 crore from ₹28.98 crore. Net interest income expanded to ₹187 crore from ₹134 crore, and asset quality strengthened meaningfully, with gross NPA dropping to 1.89% from 2.36% and net NPA to 0.51% from 1.11%. Despite the healthy print, the stock slipped 3% to ₹30.76.

Federal Bank and Indian Bank, two other closely watched names, were scheduled to declare their Q4 results later in the day. Federal Bank had rescheduled its earnings call to 4:30 PM IST on April 29, while Indian Bank's numbers were also awaited. Analyst estimates pegged Federal Bank's Q4 PAT in the ₹1,050–1,150 crore range with NIM of 3.0–3.2%, and Indian Bank's Q4 PAT around ₹3,210 crore based on consensus from four analysts.

---

AUTO: MARUTI SUZUKI CLOCKS RECORD REVENUE, BUT MISSES PROFIT ESTIMATE

India's largest carmaker, Maruti Suzuki India, reported record quarterly revenue of ₹52,449.3 crore for Q4 FY26, a 29% jump from the year-ago period. EBITDA came in at ₹6,156.9 crore, up 44.4% year-on-year, with the operating margin expanding to 11.7%. The company sold 676,209 vehicles during the quarter, its highest-ever quarterly volume.

However, the adjusted net profit of ₹3,590.5 crore fell short of analyst expectations of ₹4,186.5 crore. Earnings per share stood at ₹114.2. The stock shrugged off the profit miss and rallied 4%, underlining investor confidence in the company's volume trajectory and margin recovery.

---

TYRES & CHEMICALS: CEAT RALLIES 12%, GRANULES INDIA PROFIT STEADY

CEAT Ltd delivered a blockbuster quarter. Consolidated net profit for Q4 FY26 more than doubled to ₹243.8 crore from ₹99.50 crore in the year-ago quarter. Revenue from operations surged to ₹4,218 crore from ₹3,420 crore. The scorching numbers sent the stock soaring 12% to ₹3,949.90.

Pharma intermediate manufacturer Granules India reported Q4 net profit of ₹98.9 crore against ₹94 crore a year ago. For the full year, PAT improved to ₹400.6 crore from ₹319.33 crore. Chairman and Managing Director Dr. Krishna Prasad Chigurupati said the company is prioritising operational excellence, sustainability, and global expansion to drive long-term value. The stock gained 2% to ₹716.50.

---

METALS & MINING: VEDANTA Q4 PAT NEARLY DOUBLES; STERLITE TECH SWINGS TO PROFIT

Natural resources major Vedanta Ltd was among the most anticipated results of the day. The company reported Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹9,352 crore, an 89% year-on-year surge from ₹4,961 crore in Q4 FY25. Full-year FY26 PAT stood at ₹25,096 crore, up 22% from ₹20,535 crore. The board declared a third interim dividend of ₹11 per share, taking the total dividend for FY26 to ₹34 per share.

Segmental performance was led by the aluminium business, which delivered EBITDA of ₹25,502 crore, while zinc, lead, and silver contributed ₹22,056 crore. Vedanta shares had gained 0.5% in early trade to ₹388 ahead of the numbers.

Sterlite Technologies swung to a standalone net profit of ₹33 crore in Q4 FY26 against a net loss of ₹8 crore in the year-ago quarter. For the full year, the company reported a PAT of ₹2 crore, a sharp turnaround from the ₹127 crore loss in FY25. The board, however, did not recommend any dividend for FY26 but approved raising up to ₹2,000 crore through securities issuance. The stock was flat at ₹287.55.

---

NBFC & FINANCIAL SERVICES: BAJAJ FINANCE, KFIN TECH, MOTILAL OSWAL AWAITED

Non-banking financial companies dominated the afternoon watchlist. Bajaj Finance, India's largest NBFC by market capitalisation, was scheduled to declare its Q4 FY26 results on April 29 with an investor call at 6:00 PM IST. Analyst consensus expected consolidated PAT in the ₹4,400–4,700 crore band on revenue of ₹17,000–17,800 crore, with AUM growth of 28–30% year-on-year. The stock was flat at ₹7,390 in morning trade.

KFin Technologies was also lined up for a Q4 announcement, with nine analysts estimating adjusted net income of ₹913 crore. Motilal Oswal Financial Services' Q4 numbers were also on the radar. Both stocks were yet to see any significant pre-result movement.

---

POWER & ENERGY: ADANI POWER, WAAREE ENERGIES RESULTS ON DECK

Adani Power, the country's largest private thermal power producer, was expected to report Q4 FY26 numbers later in the day, with two analysts estimating adjusted net profit at ₹3,060 crore. The stock was marginally lower at ₹578.65 in early trade.

Waaree Energies, one of India's leading renewable energy players, was also on the results calendar, with eight analysts pencilling in Q4 adjusted net income of ₹1,120 crore. The stock was up 3% at ₹3,424 ahead of the announcement.

State-run Power Finance Corporation (PFC) reported a 10.6% year-on-year decline in Q4 consolidated net profit to ₹5,731.81 crore from ₹6,414.43 crore. Revenue from operations dropped to ₹21,498.08 crore from ₹23,224.04 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹3 per share, taking the total FY26 dividend to ₹13.50 per share. The stock was up marginally at ₹388.30.

REC Ltd, another state-run power financier, saw Q4 net profit fall to ₹3,362.30 crore from ₹4,236.20 crore a year earlier. The stock shed 3% to ₹364.10.

---

OTHER NOTABLE RESULTS

AWL Agri Business (Adani Wilmar) reported consolidated Q4 net profit of ₹292 crore against ₹190 crore in Q4 FY25, while revenue rose to ₹21,465 crore from ₹18,200 crore. On a standalone basis, Q4 net profit jumped 70.7% to ₹268.01 crore. Despite the strong show, the stock reversed early gains and was down 2% at ₹200.45.

Star Health and Allied Insurance posted a massive turnaround, with Q4 net profit surging to ₹111 crore against a mere ₹50 lakh in the year-ago quarter. Revenue climbed to ₹4,327 crore from ₹3,798 crore. The stock rallied 4% to ₹538.80, approaching its 52-week high of ₹586.25.

Defence shipbuilder Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) reported a 24.1% jump in Q4 net profit to ₹303.19 crore from ₹244.24 crore, sending the stock up 16%.

Eternal Ltd (formerly JHS Svendgaard) stunned the market with Q4 net profit of ₹1.74 billion against ₹390 million a year ago. The stock rocketed 13% to ₹2,682. Morgan Stanley subsequently initiated coverage with an Overweight rating and a target price of ₹347.

Greenply Industries reported consolidated Q4 net profit of ₹31 crore against ₹16.60 crore, with revenue rising to ₹780 crore from ₹650 crore. The stock gained 13% to ₹288.47.

Emmvee Photovoltaic Systems swung to a Q4 standalone net profit of ₹29.40 crore against a negligible ₹22.62 lakh in the year-ago quarter. The board declared a dividend of Re 1 per share. The stock hit a 52-week high of ₹299.50.

Bansal Wire Industries reported a 12% rise in Q4 net profit to ₹33.62 crore from ₹30 crore. For FY26, PAT stood at ₹129.74 crore against ₹125.16 crore. The stock was up 3% at ₹312.50.

In the small-cap space, Surana Solar turned in a Q4 net profit of ₹19.80 lakh against a loss of ₹71.34 lakh a year earlier. For the full year, the company posted PAT of ₹123.97 lakh. The stock gained 4% to ₹29.32.

---

MARKET OUTLOOK

With several heavyweights still to report after market hours on April 29, traders and investors were treading cautiously. The Nifty 50's muted gain of 0.13% masked significant stock-specific volatility, with Bandhan Bank, CEAT, and GRSE surging double digits even as Dhanlaxmi Bank and AWL Agri Business sold off despite healthy earnings.

The afternoon and evening sessions promised more action as Bajaj Finance, Vedanta, Federal Bank, Indian Bank, Adani Power, and Waaree Energies were expected to drop their audited Q4 FY26 numbers. FY27 guidance, dividend announcements, and management commentary on margin trajectories will likely be the key triggers determining market direction in the near term.
