Gold Rises as Oil Drops After US Extends Ceasefire With Iran

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold prices rose as oil weakened after the US extended its ceasefire agreement with Iran
  • 02/Brent crude fell 13% on ceasefire day and now trades near $92 per barrel, down from $108
  • 03/The ceasefire remains fragile; Islamabad talks collapsed earlier in April with no nuclear deal

Gold prices gained ground as oil weakened following the extension of the US-Iran ceasefire. The move was reported on April 22, 2026.

Brent crude had fallen approximately 13 percent on April 7 when the initial ceasefire was announced. Prices dropped from around $108 to $92 per barrel in a single session.

Gold traded near $4,730 per ounce on April 22. The metal has fallen over 11 percent since the US-Israeli war on Iran began on February 28, 2026.

The ceasefire was brokered by Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Army Chief General Asim Munir. It initially covered a two-week suspension of hostilities.

Talks in Islamabad collapsed after 21 hours on April 12 with no agreement on the nuclear issue. The US subsequently announced a naval blockade of Iranian ports.

Every $10 fall in Brent crude saves India approximately ₹70,000 crore annually in import costs. The current decline provides relief to Indian fuel consumers and the government.

Goldman Sachs trimmed its Q2 2026 Brent forecast to $90 per barrel following the ceasefire. ANZ warned that permanent supply losses could still push prices above $100.

BRENT CRUDE FELL APPROXIMATELY 13% ON APRIL 7, DROPPING FROM AROUND $108 TO $92-95 PER BARREL.

THE CEASEFIRE WAS BROKERED BY PAKISTAN'S PRIME MINISTER SHEHBAZ SHARIF AND ARMY CHIEF GENERAL ASIM MUNIR.
WHAT HAPPENED AT THE ISLAMABAD PEACE TALKS?
TALKS COLLAPSED AFTER 21 HOURS ON APRIL 12 WITH NO AGREEMENT ON IRAN'S NUCLEAR PROGRAM, PROMPTING THE US TO ANNOUNCE A NAVAL BLOCKADE OF IRANIAN PORTS.
