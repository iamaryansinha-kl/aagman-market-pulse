BMW Group India Plans to Double MINI Sales in 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BMW Group India President Hardeep Singh Brar targets doubling MINI sales to over 1,400 units in calendar year 2026
  • 02/MINI sold 730 units in 2025 and 213 units in Q1 2026 (up 42% YoY); a three-pronged strategy has been adopted
  • 03/Local MINI Countryman launch planned for mid-2026; retail footprint to expand from 13 to 22 touchpoints across India

BMW Group India President and CEO Hardeep Singh Brar said MINI will target doubling its sales in 2026. The brand sold 730 units in 2025 and 213 units in the January-March quarter of 2026.

The Q1 2026 sales figure represents a 42 per cent year-on-year growth. Brar outlined a three-pronged strategy to hit the full-year target.

The strategy covers a wider product portfolio, localisation, and retail expansion. One new product and nine special editions are planned for the year.

The locally produced MINI Countryman will launch in mid-2026. Brar called it a "pivotal enabler to scale volumes and improve accessibility."

Petrol models contributed 61 per cent of MINI sales in 2025. With the local Countryman launch, this share is expected to rise to 70 per cent.

MINI will expand its retail footprint from 13 to 22 touchpoints by end of 2026. Entry into eight new cities is part of the plan.

Touchpoints in Jaipur and Jodhpur opened in Q1 2026. Six more cities — Lucknow, Ranchi, Guwahati, Surat, Vijayawada, and Cuttack — will follow this year.

Aftersales support is available at 52 BMW Group India touchpoints across 40 cities. MINI and BMW now share showroom space, enabling cross-selling between the two brands.
