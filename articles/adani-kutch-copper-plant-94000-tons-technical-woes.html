Adani Kutch Copper Plant: 94,000 Tons in Year One, Technical Woes

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Enterprises' (NSE: ADANIENT) Kutch copper plant produced 94,000 tons from April 2025 to February 2026 against 500,000 tonne annual capacity
  • 02/High impurities in copper concentrate feedstock including antimony, arsenic and uranium caused smelting instability; plant closed for repairs in late March
  • 03/Industry consultancies CRU, Wood Mackenzie and Benchmark expect 2026 output of 175,000-385,000 tons, well below the plant's full design capacity

Adani Enterprises Ltd.'s (NSE: ADANIENT) $1.2 billion Kutch copper plant produced 94,000 tons of refined copper in its first year. The 500,000 tonne-per-year facility has run at under 19% of capacity since commissioning.

Production data from India's Ministry of Mines covers April 2025 through February 2026. The plant was commissioned roughly ten months ago and has faced repeated engineering setbacks.

The facility closed for repair work in late March 2026. It remains unclear whether operations have restarted since the shutdown.

High levels of impurities in copper concentrate feedstock caused major operational problems. Contaminants included antimony, arsenic and uranium, four sources with direct knowledge told Bloomberg.

The plant needs 1.6 million tonnes of concentrate annually to run at full capacity. Import records show Kutch Copper bought just over 25% of that requirement between February 2024 and February 2026.

Satellite monitoring firm Earth-i detected no clear signs of sustained smelting at the Gujarat site since June 2025. The company tracks smoke, stockpiles and vehicle movements globally.

Industry consultancies CRU, Wood Mackenzie and Benchmark Mineral Intelligence project 2026 output between 175,000 and 385,000 tons. That range still falls short of the plant's 500,000 tonne design capacity.

An Adani spokesperson denied engineering challenges and said "execution and plant stabilization had been swift." The company claimed long-term concentrate supply contracts are fully meeting operational requirements.

HOW MUCH COPPER HAS ADANI'S KUTCH PLANT PRODUCED IN ITS FIRST YEAR?

WHAT TECHNICAL ISSUES HAVE HIT THE ADANI COPPER PLANT?
HIGH LEVELS OF IMPURITIES IN COPPER CONCENTRATE FEEDSTOCK, INCLUDING ANTIMONY, ARSENIC AND URANIUM, CAUSED OPERATIONAL INSTABILITY. THE PRESENCE OF THESE METALS AFFECTED THE PRIMARY SMELTING PROCESS AND THE PURITY OF OUTPUT.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF THE KUTCH COPPER PLANT?
THE FACILITY CLOSED FOR REPAIR WORK IN LATE MARCH 2026. IT WAS NOT CLEAR WHETHER OPERATIONS HAD RESTARTED AT THE TIME OF REPORTING, ACCORDING TO SOURCES FAMILIAR WITH THE MATTER.
HOW MUCH COPPER CONCENTRATE DOES THE PLANT NEED?
TO RUN AT FULL CAPACITY, THE PLANT NEEDS APPROXIMATELY 1.6 MILLION TONNES OF COPPER CONCENTRATE PER YEAR. IMPORT RECORDS SHOW KUTCH COPPER BOUGHT JUST OVER 25% OF THAT REQUIREMENT BETWEEN FEBRUARY 2024 AND FEBRUARY 2026.
WHAT ARE INDUSTRY ESTIMATES FOR 2026 PRODUCTION?
CONSULTANCIES CRU, WOOD MACKENZIE AND BENCHMARK MINERAL INTELLIGENCE EXPECT THE ADANI PLANT TO PRODUCE BETWEEN 175,000 AND 385,000 TONS OF COPPER IN 2026. THAT RANGE IS STILL WELL BELOW THE 500,000 TONNE DESIGN CAPACITY.
