Nayara Energy to Resume Vadinar Refinery by Mid-May After April Shutdown

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nayara Energy will resume operations at its 400,000-barrel-per-day Vadinar refinery by mid-May after an April 9 maintenance shutdown
  • 02/The turnaround was originally planned last year but delayed due to EU sanctions blocking procurement of key equipment
  • 03/Rosneft holds 49.13% in Nayara; the refinery is India's second-largest with 20 million tonnes annual capacity

Nayara Energy will restart its Vadinar refinery in Gujarat by mid-May. Sujata Sharma, joint secretary at the federal oil ministry, confirmed the timeline on April 28, 2026.

The 400,000-barrel-per-day facility was fully shut from April 9 for planned maintenance. It is India's second-largest refinery with 20 million tonnes per year capacity.

The maintenance turnaround was originally scheduled last year. Nayara had to postpone it due to European Union sanctions, which made procuring key equipment difficult.

Nayara Energy is majority-owned by Russian entities. Oil major Rosneft holds a 49.13% stake in the company, formerly known as Essar Oil Limited.

The Vadinar refinery has a Nelson complexity index of 11.8. This places it among the top ten refineries globally for processing heavy and ultra-heavy crude grades.

The shutdown removes a significant chunk of India's refining capacity. About 70% of Indian oil imports pass through Gujarat, where the Vadinar facility is strategically located.

The refinery operates over 6,000 retail fuel outlets across India. It also has a captive deep-water port and oil terminal with 58 million tonnes annual capacity.

The restart timeline remains subject to equipment availability and sanctions-related logistics. Any further delays could tighten domestic fuel supply ahead of the summer demand season.
