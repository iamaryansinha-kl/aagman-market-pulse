Bondada Engineering Bags ₹816 Cr NTPC Solar Order, Revenue Doubles

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bondada Engineering secures ₹816 crore order from NTPC Renewable Energy for 600 MW solar BOS works at Fatehgarh, Rajasthan with 16-month execution timeline
  • 02/FY26 revenue jumps 81% to ₹2,843 crore against ₹1,571 crore in FY25; net profit rises to ₹211 crore across renewables, telecom and railways verticals
  • 03/Total solar EPC order book reaches 5.3 GWp; cumulative NTPC group orders at ₹1,207 crore providing medium-term revenue visibility

Bondada Engineering Limited has secured a ₹816 crore order from NTPC Renewable Energy Limited. The Notification of Award covers Balance of System works on a 600 MW Solar PV Project at Fatehgarh, Rajasthan.

The scope includes engineering, procurement, construction and three years of operations and maintenance services. The project is to be executed within 16 months from the date of award.

This marks the second order from the NTPC Group, taking cumulative order inflow to approximately ₹1,207 crore. Combined capacity from NTPC projects now stands at nearly 1 GW.

With this addition, Bondada's total Solar EPC order book stands at approximately 5.3 GWp. The company reported FY26 revenue of ₹2,843 crore against ₹1,571 crore in FY25, an 81 per cent jump.

Net profit for FY26 came in at ₹211 crore, up from ₹115 crore in the previous year. Growth was driven by renewable energy, telecom infrastructure and railways segments.

Bondada Engineering shares traded at ₹345.10 on May 11, 2026, down 2.87 per cent or ₹10.20. The stock has traded in a 52-week range of ₹215 to ₹510.

Incorporated in 2012, Bondada operates manufacturing facilities in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Execution risks include working capital strain and rising input costs for solar modules on large-scale projects.
