Waaree Energies Stock Crashes 10% as Margin Woes Overshadow Q4 Profit Jump

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Waaree Energies shares plunged nearly 10% to ₹3,165 in early trade on April 30 despite Q4 FY26 profit surging 71% YoY to ₹1,061 crore
  • 02/Revenue jumped 112% to ₹8,480 crore but EBITDA missed estimates by 11%, as gross margins contracted sharply from Q3 levels
  • 03/Nomura maintains BUY with target of ₹3,750; investors eye margin recovery and FY27 guidance after semiconductor foray announcement

Waaree Energies Ltd (NSE: WAAREEENER) shares crashed nearly 10% in early trade on April 30, 2026. The stock fell to ₹3,165.20 from the previous close of ₹3,502.90. It was trading at ₹3,203.10 around 9:34 am on the NSE.

The selloff came despite robust Q4 FY26 earnings. Consolidated net profit surged 71% year-on-year to ₹1,061 crore. Revenue from operations jumped 112% to ₹8,480 crore on higher volumes.

Investor sentiment soured due to margin pressure. EBITDA missed Nomura's estimate by 11% and consensus by 8%. Gross margins contracted sharply from the previous quarter.

For context, Q3 FY26 EBITDA stood at ₹1,928 crore with a margin of 25.5%. Q4 EBITDA fell to approximately ₹1,756 crore, dragging the margin down to around 20.7%.

Brokerage firm Nomura maintained a BUY rating on the stock with a target price of ₹3,750. It noted revenue beat both its and consensus estimates by 12%, aided by strong volume growth.

The company also announced a strategic foray into the semiconductor space. The board approved a ₹10,000 crore fundraising plan to support expansion and diversification.

Nomura stated the stock is trading at 16x FY27F and 13x FY28F EBITDA. The valuation remains attractive if margins stabilise and FY27 guidance meets expectations.

The sharp stock reaction highlights how sensitive renewable energy stocks are to margin trends. Investors will watch Q1 FY27 execution closely for signs of recovery.
