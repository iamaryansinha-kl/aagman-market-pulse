Office Asset PE Inflow Rises 7% to $945 Million in H1 FY27: Anarock

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Private equity inflow into Indian office assets rose 7% to USD 945 million in April-September FY27, Anarock data showed
  • 02/Office inflow stood at USD 880 million in H1 FY26; investors are buying completed, leased Grade A assets for stable rental income
  • 03/Residential and industrial-logistics gained, retail drew zero, while data centre and hospitality PE inflows surged sharply

Private equity investment in Indian office assets rose 7% year-on-year to USD 945 million in the first half of FY27. The data covers April to September 2026 and was published by property consultant Anarock on October 6.

Office assets remained the largest real estate destination for PE capital in H1 FY27. In the same period last year, office inflows stood at USD 880 million.

The investment pattern is clear. Funds are buying completed, leased Grade A office properties that generate stable rental income.

This preference reflects a hunt for yield rather than development risk. Leased assets offer predictable cash flows at a time of global uncertainty.

The residential segment also saw higher allocations. PE inflow rose to USD 378 million from USD 330 million a year ago.

Industrial and logistics parks emerged as a new avenue. The segment attracted USD 162 million in H1 FY27, against nil inflow in the year-ago period.

Retail told the opposite story. The segment received zero PE investment in H1 FY27, compared with USD 374 million in the same period of FY26.

Beyond real estate, two segments surged. Data centres drew USD 783 million against USD 110 million a year ago, while hospitality attracted USD 324 million versus USD 88 million.

Anarock Capital CEO Shobhit Agarwal said the trend signals a deeper shift. "The first half of FY27 marks a clear turning point for private equity in Indian real estate. Investors are no longer just testing the waters; they are committing larger cheques, taking equity positions, and backing scalable platforms," he said.

He added that India is now a core allocation. "The fact that this has happened against an uncertain global backdrop shows that India is now seen as a core, long-term allocation rather than an opportunistic bet."

The numbers suggest PE firms are rotating toward income-producing assets. Completed offices, data centres and hospitality offer contracted cash flows, while speculative retail and development-stage bets are being avoided.

Office assets accounted for the largest share of real estate PE in H1 FY27. That ranking held even as the overall mix of sectors broadened, with industrial and logistics joining the table for the first time.

For the rest of FY27, the test will be whether this pace holds. Sustained inflows into leased offices would confirm that global funds now treat Indian commercial real estate as a yield anchor, not a trading bet.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH PRIVATE EQUITY DID INDIAN OFFICE ASSETS ATTRACT IN H1 FY27?
INDIAN OFFICE ASSETS ATTRACTED USD 945 MILLION IN PRIVATE EQUITY INFLOW IN APRIL-SEPTEMBER OF FY27, UP 7% FROM USD 880 MILLION IN THE SAME PERIOD OF FY26, ACCORDING TO ANAROCK DATA.
WHY ARE PE INVESTORS FAVOURING COMPLETED, LEASED GRADE A OFFICE ASSETS?
INVESTORS ARE BUYING COMPLETED, LEASED GRADE A ASSETS BECAUSE THEY GENERATE STABLE RENTAL INCOME. THIS OFFERS PREDICTABLE CASH FLOWS AND LOWER RISK COMPARED WITH DEVELOPMENT-STAGE PROJECTS.
WHICH REAL ESTATE SEGMENTS GAINED AND WHICH MISSED OUT IN H1 FY27?
RESIDENTIAL INFLOW ROSE TO USD 378 MILLION FROM USD 330 MILLION, AND INDUSTRIAL AND LOGISTICS PARKS ATTRACTED USD 162 MILLION VERSUS NIL A YEAR AGO. RETAIL RECEIVED ZERO PE INVESTMENT, DOWN FROM USD 374 MILLION IN H1 FY26.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE SURGE IN DATA CENTRE AND HOSPITALITY PE INFLOWS?
DATA CENTRE PE INFLOW JUMPED TO USD 783 MILLION FROM USD 110 MILLION, WHILE HOSPITALITY ROSE TO USD 324 MILLION FROM USD 88 MILLION. BOTH SEGMENTS OFFER CONTRACTED, INCOME-GENERATING ASSETS THAT ALIGN WITH THE CURRENT YIELD-FOCUSED APPROACH OF GLOBAL FUNDS.
WHAT DID ANAROCK'S CEO SAY ABOUT INDIA'S POSITION IN GLOBAL PORTFOLIOS?
SHOBHIT AGARWAL, CEO OF ANAROCK CAPITAL, SAID INDIA IS NOW SEEN AS A CORE, LONG-TERM ALLOCATION RATHER THAN AN OPPORTUNISTIC BET, WITH INVESTORS COMMITTING LARGER CHEQUES, TAKING EQUITY POSITIONS AND BACKING SCALABLE PLATFORMS. GLOSSARY: Private equity (PE): Investment funds that deploy capital into companies or assets, often with a medium-term horizon, in exchange for equity ownership. Grade A assets: Premium-quality properties with modern construction, strong locations, high-specification fittings and institutional-grade management. Rental yield: Annual rental income from a property expressed as a percentage of its value. Built-to-suit: A development built to a specific occupier's requirements, usually with a pre-committed lease. Capital inflow: The movement of investment money from external investors into a country, sector or asset class.
