Butterfly Gandhimathi Q4 PAT Up 27% to ₹11 Cr; FY26 Profit Surges 40%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Q4 FY26 net profit jumps 27% to ₹11 crore vs ₹9.03 crore YoY; revenue rises to ₹218 crore from ₹187 crore
  • 02/FY26 full-year PAT surges 40% to ₹45.64 crore (₹4,563.78 lakh) from ₹32.53 crore; revenue up 8% to ₹943.15 crore
  • 03/Company attributes growth to increased adoption of electric cooking appliances driven by LPG supply constraints; stock rallies 14%

Kitchen appliances maker Butterfly Gandhimathi Appliances reported strong Q4 and FY26 results on May 12, 2026. Net profit for the quarter ended March 2026 rose 27% to ₹11 crore from ₹9.03 crore in the year-ago period. Revenue for Q4 FY26 stood at ₹218 crore against ₹187 crore in Q4 FY25.

For the full year FY26, the company recorded a PAT of ₹47 crore, up 45% from ₹33 crore in FY25. Revenue rose to ₹943 crore from ₹865 crore in the previous year. On a standalone basis, net profit surged 40% to ₹45.64 crore. Revenue from operations reached ₹943.15 crore. Profit Before Tax was ₹61.26 crore.

The company said increased adoption of electric cooking appliances, driven by LPG supply constraints, contributed to growth in the quarter. This shift in consumer behavior has boosted demand for the company's core product portfolio.

Swetha Sagar, Manager & Chief Business Officer, said the company focused on building a stronger foundation for sustainable growth. This included organisational restructuring, capability enhancement, brand refresh, innovation roadmap development, and cost optimisation initiatives.

Butterfly Gandhimathi became part of Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd in 2022. The acquisition aimed to enhance Crompton's presence in the small appliances and kitchenware market.

The stock rallied 14% following the results announcement. Peer companies TTK Prestige and Stove Kraft also saw rub-off effects. The company trades at a face value of ₹10 per share with NSE symbol BUTTERFLY.

The board also approved the re-appointment of statutory, cost, internal, and tax auditors for FY27. The 39th Annual General Meeting has been scheduled for August 4, 2026.
