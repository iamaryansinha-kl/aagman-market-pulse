Air India Express Resumes Qatar, Bahrain Flights From 14 Indian Cities

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Air India Express reinstates operations to Qatar and Bahrain from April 30, 2026, connecting 14 Indian cities to the Gulf
  • 02/The airline operates over 500 daily flights with a fleet of 100+ Boeing 737 and Airbus A320 aircraft across 45 domestic and 17 international destinations
  • 03/Air India, Air India Express and IndiGo resume full-scale operations at Hamad International Airport from May 1, two months after Operation Epic Fury

Air India Express has announced the reinstatement of operations to Qatar and Bahrain from April 30, 2026. The Tata-owned airline is adding flights to the UAE, Oman and Saudi Arabia as well.

Flights will resume from 14 Indian cities including Amritsar, Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kochi, Kozhikode, Kannur, Lucknow, Mangaluru, Mumbai, Tiruchirappalli, Thiruvananthapuram and Varanasi.

The airline will continue operating to Al Ain, Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah, Sharjah, Jeddah, Riyadh and Muscat. Established routes connecting India to the Gulf region are being restored.

Air India Express operates over 500 daily flights across 45 domestic and 17 international destinations. Its fleet comprises more than 100 Boeing 737 and Airbus A320 aircraft.

The resumption comes exactly two months after Operation Epic Fury launched on February 28, 2026. The joint US-Israeli military campaign triggered a massive regional conflagration that disrupted air traffic across the Middle East.

Three Indian carriers will spearhead the return to Hamad International Airport in Doha from May 1, 2026. Air India, Air India Express and IndiGo are reconnecting Doha with major Indian metros and regional hubs.

"Indian carriers plan to resume operations at Hamad International Airport, Doha, as flight operations continue to expand," posted the Embassy of India Doha on X. The Embassy said this is a synchronised move between international aviation bodies.

Air India Express holds a 26.7% domestic market share as of November 2025, making it India's second-largest carrier behind IndiGo's 64.4%. The airline serves over 20 million loyalty programme members.

The Gulf route is critical for Indian aviation. Over 3.5 million Indian nationals reside in the UAE alone. Any disruption in air travel carries significant economic, humanitarian and diplomatic consequences.

Risks remain as the regional situation is still fluid. War risk insurance premiums for Gulf flights have increased dramatically since February. European carriers like British Airways and Qatar Airways have scheduled later resumption dates.
