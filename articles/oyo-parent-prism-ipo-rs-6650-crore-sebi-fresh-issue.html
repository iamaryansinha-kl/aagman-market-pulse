OYO Parent Prism Files Rs 6,650 Cr IPO Papers With SEBI, All Fresh Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/OYO parent Prism files updated DRHP with SEBI for Rs 6,650 crore IPO as entirely fresh issue with no offer for sale
  • 02/Rs 4,987.5 crore from net proceeds earmarked for repayment of borrowings; S&P upgraded outlook to Positive
  • 03/Company posted Rs 748 crore net profit in 9M FY26 on revenue of Rs 6,941 crore, up 11% over full FY25

Prism, formerly known as Oravel Stays and parent of OYO, has filed its Updated Draft Red Herring Prospectus-I with SEBI. The IPO is entirely a fresh issue worth up to Rs 6,650 crore, with no offer for sale component.

The company filed a confidential DRHP with SEBI at the end of December 2025. It received market regulator approval earlier in June 2026 before filing the updated papers.

Prism plans to use Rs 4,987.5 crore from net proceeds for repayment or prepayment of borrowings. The remaining funds will be used for general corporate purposes.

For the nine months ended December 31, 2025, revenue from operations stood at Rs 6,941 crore. This marks an 11% increase over the full FY25 revenue of Rs 6,259 crore.

Net profit for the 9M FY26 period was Rs 748 crore. The company now operates 43 brands across more than 35 countries with 144,583 total listings.

As of December 31, 2025, Prism had 24,303 hotels and 124,668 homes. Company-serviced hotel storefronts in India grew to 1,573 from 1,053 as of March 31, 2025.

The company derives 84% of revenue from outside India. The US contributed 27% and Europe 24% to 9M FY26 revenue, driven by the G6 Hospitality acquisition.

S&P Global Ratings revised Prism's outlook to Positive from Stable in June 2026. It affirmed the 'B' issuer credit rating on the company's senior secured term loan.

"Credit ratios could further strengthen if the company uses its IPO proceeds to pay down debt," S&P said. The company may also undertake a pre-IPO placement worth up to Rs 1,330 crore.
