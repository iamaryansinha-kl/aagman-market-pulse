Sensex Surges 609 Points on UAE OPEC Exit Hopes; Rupee Hits Record Low

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex gains 609.45 points (0.79%) to 77,496.36; Nifty rises 181.95 points (0.76%) to 24,177.65 on April 29, 2026
  • 02/UAE's reported exit from OPEC fuels hopes of softer crude prices; Brent crude remains elevated near $114 per barrel
  • 03/Rupee weakens 30 paise to record low close near 94.8 against dollar; FII outflows and crude surge keep pressure intact

Indian equity markets closed higher on April 29, 2026. The Sensex gained 609.45 points or 0.79 per cent to settle at 77,496.36.

The Nifty 50 advanced 181.95 points or 0.76 per cent to end at 24,177.65. Both indices touched intraday highs before profit-booking trimmed gains in the second half.

The primary catalyst was news of the UAE's exit from OPEC. This raised hopes of greater oil supply and softer crude prices ahead, a significant positive for oil-importing India.

Brent crude remained elevated near $114 per barrel. Domestic crude oil futures surged toward ₹9,800, reversing much of April's earlier correction.

The rupee weakened 30 paise to hit a record low close near 94.8 against the US dollar. Sustained FII outflows, elevated crude prices, and importer dollar demand pressured the currency.

Sectorally, FMCG led gains with a 1.7 per cent advance. Realty rose 1.3 per cent, while Auto and IT each posted roughly 1 per cent gains.

Heavyweights ITC, Reliance Industries, Maruti Suzuki, and Tech Mahindra drove the rally. IndiGo and Dr Reddy's ended as the session's top laggards.

"Markets will react to the outcome of the US Fed meeting in early trade on Thursday... elevated crude oil prices near the $110 mark continued to cap the upside," said Ajit Mishra of Religare Broking.
