Eternal Q4 FY26 Profit Surges 346% To ₹174 Cr; Revenue Doubles

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eternal's consolidated net profit surged 346% YoY to ₹174 crore in Q4 FY26, up from ₹39 crore a year ago
  • 02/Revenue from operations jumped 196% to ₹17,292 crore; Blinkit added 216 stores, taking total to 2,243
  • 03/Founder Deepinder Goyal expects $1 billion adjusted EBITDA by FY29 as annual NOV scales toward $20 billion

Eternal Limited (NSE: ZOMATO) has reported a 346% surge in consolidated net profit to ₹174 crore for Q4 FY26. The figure stood at ₹39 crore in the year-ago period.

Consolidated revenue from operations jumped to ₹17,292 crore, up 196% from ₹5,833 crore in Q4 FY25. The sharp growth reflects the combined momentum of Zomato and Blinkit.

Net order value for Zomato's food delivery business grew 18.8% year-on-year. This marks the third consecutive quarter of acceleration after bottoming out in Q1 FY26.

Blinkit's quick commerce NOV grew 95.4% year-on-year. The platform added 216 net new stores during the quarter, taking the total count to 2,243.

Deepinder Goyal, Founder of Eternal, said 109 million Indians transacted over $10 billion in FY26 across Blinkit, District, and Zomato. The first $10 billion in annual NOV took 18 years to achieve.

Goyal expects doubling to $20 billion annual NOV in under two years from now. He also projected $1 billion in adjusted EBITDA, hopefully by FY29.

Albinder Singh Dhindsa, Group CEO, said Blinkit's growth is moderating naturally off a larger base. He expects NOV growth CAGR north of 60% over the next three years.

The company is transferring District's technology stack to subsidiary Wasteland Entertainment for ₹24.19 crore. This includes identified employees moving to the unit.

Goyal dismissed concerns over AI disruption, noting most business happens offline. He added that Eternal is among India's largest deployers of AI across demand prediction and supply chain management.
