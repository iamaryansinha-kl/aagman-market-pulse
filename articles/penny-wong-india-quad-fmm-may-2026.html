Australian FM Penny Wong to Visit India for Quad Meeting on May 26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Australian Foreign Minister Penny Wong will visit New Delhi on May 26 for the Quad Foreign Ministers' Meeting
  • 02/US Secretary Marco Rubio, Japanese FM Toshimitsu Motegi, and Indian EAM S. Jaishankar will also attend
  • 03/Ministers will discuss maritime security, critical minerals, infrastructure, and disaster relief in the Indo-Pacific

Australian Foreign Minister Penny Wong will travel to New Delhi on May 26, 2026. She will attend the Quad Foreign Ministers' Meeting hosted by India.

The meeting will bring together the foreign ministers of all four Quad nations. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Japanese Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi will join.

India's External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar extended the invitations. The ministers will also hold bilateral talks and call on Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

The Quad leaders last met in Washington, D.C. on July 1, 2025. That meeting produced a joint statement on maritime security and critical minerals cooperation.

Wong will also hold the 17th Australia-India Foreign Ministers' Framework Dialogue. Australia and India are Comprehensive Strategic Partners.

The ministers will review progress on Quad initiatives. Priority areas include maritime security, critical minerals supply chains, and infrastructure development.

India is set to host the next Quad Leaders' Summit later this year. The May 26 meeting builds momentum ahead of that summit.

Wong said the Quad is vital in uncertain times. The four nations are working together for a peaceful, stable, and prosperous Indo-Pacific region.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN IS THE QUAD FOREIGN MINISTERS' MEETING IN INDIA?
THE QUAD FOREIGN MINISTERS' MEETING WILL TAKE PLACE IN NEW DELHI ON MAY 26, 2026.
WHICH FOREIGN MINISTERS WILL ATTEND THE QUAD MEETING IN INDIA?
AUSTRALIAN FOREIGN MINISTER PENNY WONG, US SECRETARY OF STATE MARCO RUBIO, JAPANESE FOREIGN MINISTER TOSHIMITSU MOTEGI, AND INDIAN EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MINISTER DR. S. JAISHANKAR WILL ATTEND.
WHAT WILL THE QUAD MINISTERS DISCUSS IN NEW DELHI?
THE MINISTERS WILL DISCUSS MARITIME SECURITY, CRITICAL MINERALS SUPPLY CHAINS, INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT, DISASTER RELIEF, AND RECENT DEVELOPMENTS IN THE INDO-PACIFIC REGION.
WILL THE MINISTERS MEET PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA MODI?
YES, THE FOREIGN MINISTERS OF AUSTRALIA, JAPAN, AND THE UNITED STATES ARE EXPECTED TO CALL ON PRIME MINISTER SHRI NARENDRA MODI DURING THEIR VISIT.
WHAT IS THE AUSTRALIA-INDIA FOREIGN MINISTERS' FRAMEWORK DIALOGUE?
IT IS THE 17TH EDITION OF THE BILATERAL DIALOGUE BETWEEN AUSTRALIA AND INDIA. THE TWO NATIONS ARE COMPREHENSIVE STRATEGIC PARTNERS COOPERATING ON TRADE, DEFENCE, CLIMATE, AND TECHNOLOGY.
