Sensex, Nifty Slip at Open as Crude Above $100 Weighs on Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex opened 275 points lower at 76,993 and NSE Nifty slipped 89 points to 24,029 at 9:17 a.m. on May 5, 2026
  • 02/Crude oil stayed above $100 per barrel amid Strait of Hormuz tensions, while the Indian rupee hit a record low of 95.39 per dollar
  • 03/Analysts expect range-bound trade with a sell-on-rise strategy until Nifty breaks above 24,300 or below 23,800

Indian equity markets opened lower on Tuesday, May 5, 2026. The BSE Sensex was down 275 points at 76,993, while the NSE Nifty slipped 89 points to 24,029 at 9:17 a.m.

Gift Nifty had signalled a gap-down opening of over 150 points. The weak start tracked elevated crude oil prices and rising geopolitical tensions around the Strait of Hormuz.

Brent crude stayed above the $100 per barrel mark. Persistently high oil prices threaten to fuel inflation and squeeze corporate margins in import-dependent India.

The Indian rupee hit a record low of 95.39 against the US dollar. Capital outflows and the oil shock amplified concerns over the current account deficit and growth.

On Monday, the Nifty 50 had managed to advance 0.5% but closed below the 24,120 mark. Nifty Realty led gains with a 2.5% jump, while Nifty IT declined 0.9%.

Derivatives data points to a cautious-to-bearish undertone. The put-call ratio stood at 0.61, while strong call writing at 24,200–24,300 capped near-term upside.

Analysts say markets are likely to stay range-bound in the near term. Traders are expected to prefer a sell-on-rise strategy until a clear breakout emerges.

A sustained move above 24,300 or below 23,800 could trigger the next directional trend. Until then, volatility remains relatively contained with the India VIX near 18.
