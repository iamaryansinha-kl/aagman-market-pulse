Zee Entertainment Sues JioStar for $3M Over Music Copyright Breach

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Zee Entertainment filed a 1,800-page lawsuit on April 14 seeking $3 million in damages from JioStar for alleged music copyright infringement
  • 02/Zee alleges JioStar used its copyrighted music at least 50 times after licensing agreements expired in 2024 and 2025
  • 03/The next court hearing is scheduled for July 23; JioStar denies infringement and calls damages demands coercive

Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd has sued JioStar, the Reliance-Disney joint venture, for $3 million in damages. The 1,800-page lawsuit was filed in New Delhi on April 14, 2026.

Zee alleges JioStar used its copyrighted music at least 50 times after certain licensing agreements expired in 2024 and 2025. The licenses were not renewed due to disagreements over commercial terms.

The lawsuit marks the latest legal clash between Zee and the Ambani-led group. Reliance and Disney merged their media assets in an $8.5 billion deal in 2024 to form JioStar.

Zee claims its music was used without authorization across TV shows and the JioHotstar streaming platform. JioHotstar is India's largest streaming app with approximately 500 million monthly users.

Zee owns a catalogue of more than 19,450 songs across 17 languages. The company called the unauthorized use "illegal exploitation" that amounted to copyright infringement in its court filing.

JioStar has denied the allegations. In a March 16 letter, it "categorically rejected" what it called "coercive demands" for damages and said residual archival hosting did not constitute infringement.

The case was briefly heard on May 5, when the judge directed JioStar to ensure no ongoing infringement and to comply within 15 days. The next hearing is scheduled for July 23, 2026.

The dispute adds to broader tensions between the two companies. Zee and Reliance are also locked in London arbitration, where Reliance is seeking $1 billion in damages over a terminated cricket licensing deal.
