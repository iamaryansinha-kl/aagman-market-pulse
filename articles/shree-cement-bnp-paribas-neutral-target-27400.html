Shree Cement Broker Call: BNP Paribas Neutral, Target ₹27,400

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BNP Paribas maintains Neutral rating on Shree Cement with target price of ₹27,400, raised 2% after Q4FY26 results
  • 02/Q4FY26 standalone EBITDA declined 9% YoY to ₹1,161/tonne; CLC volumes rose 9% to 10.8 mt but margins missed estimates
  • 03/FY27 volume guidance at 40 mt; watch Q1 cost inflation of ₹150-200/tonne and 80-mtpa capacity target by FY29

BNP Paribas has maintained a Neutral rating on Shree Cement Ltd (NSE: SHREECEM). The brokerage raised its target price to ₹27,400 after Q4FY26 results.

The stock closed at ₹25,554.10 on May 7, 2026. The target implies an upside of approximately 7.2% from current levels.

Q4FY26 standalone EBITDA declined 9% year on year. The result was largely in line with BNP Paribas estimates and consensus.

Cement volumes rose around 9% to 10.8 million tonne. Blended realisation was up 3% quarter on quarter but fell 2% year on year.

EBITDA per tonne stood at ₹1,161, 5% below BNP Paribas estimates. Operating cost per tonne remained flat sequentially despite Q4 operating leverage.

The company commissioned its Kodla integrated unit of 3.5 mtpa in Q4. Total domestic cement capacity reached 69 mtpa.

FY27 capital expenditure guidance is lower at ₹1,500 crore. Focus areas include RMC, rail sidings, and the 1 mtpa Meghalaya unit.

Green power share was 61% in Q4FY26 and premium products accounted for 22% of trade sales. The company expects ₹150-200 per tonne cost increase in Q1FY27. It targets 80 mtpa capacity by FY29.

WHAT IS BNP PARIBAS' RATING AND TARGET PRICE FOR SHREE CEMENT?
BNP PARIBAS HAS MAINTAINED A NEUTRAL RATING ON SHREE CEMENT LTD WITH A TARGET PRICE OF ₹27,400. THE TARGET WAS RAISED BY 2% AFTER FACTORING IN Q4FY26 RESULTS.

SHREE CEMENT'S CLC VOLUMES ROSE APPROXIMATELY 9% TO 10.8 MILLION TONNE IN Q4FY26. BLENDED REALISATION WAS UP 3% QUARTER ON QUARTER BUT DOWN 2% YEAR ON YEAR. EBITDA PER TONNE STOOD AT ₹1,161, WHICH WAS 5% BELOW BNP PARIBAS ESTIMATES.
WHAT IS SHREE CEMENT'S CURRENT CAPACITY AND EXPANSION PLAN?
SHREE CEMENT COMMISSIONED ITS 3.5 MTPA KODLA INTEGRATED UNIT IN Q4FY26, TAKING TOTAL DOMESTIC CAPACITY TO 69 MTPA. THE COMPANY RETAINS AN 80-MTPA CAPACITY TARGET BY FY29, CONTINGENT ON DEMAND OUTLOOK AND UTILISATION. FY27 CAPEX GUIDANCE IS ₹1,500 CRORE.
WHAT COST PRESSURES DOES SHREE CEMENT FACE?
THE COMPANY EXPECTS OPERATING COSTS TO RISE BY ₹150-200 PER TONNE IN Q1FY27 COMPARED TO Q4FY26. DESPITE Q4 OPERATING LEVERAGE, COSTS WERE FLAT SEQUENTIALLY IN Q4. THE GREEN POWER SHARE WAS 61% IN Q4FY26.
WHAT IS THE VOLUME GUIDANCE FOR FY27?
SHREE CEMENT HAS GUIDED FY27 VOLUMES AT 40 MILLION TONNE. THE COMPANY AIMS TO CORRECT ITS VOLUME TRAJECTORY WHILE HOLDING PRICES STEADY.
