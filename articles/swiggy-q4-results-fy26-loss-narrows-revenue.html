Swiggy Q4 Results FY26: Loss Narrows to ₹800 Cr, Revenue Up 45%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Swiggy narrows Q4 FY26 consolidated net loss to ₹800 crore from ₹1,081 crore in the year-ago quarter, a 26% reduction
  • 02/Revenue surges nearly 45% YoY to ₹6,383 crore as total expenses climb to ₹7,448 crore from ₹5,610 crore
  • 03/Full-year FY26 loss widens to ₹4,154 crore; investors watch Instamart unit economics and path to breakeven

Swiggy Ltd reported a consolidated net loss of ₹800 crore for Q4 FY26 ended March 31, 2026. The food delivery and quick commerce firm announced results on May 8, 2026.

The loss narrowed 26% from ₹1,081 crore in Q4 FY25. It marks modest progress in Swiggy's uphill path to profitability.

Revenue for the quarter jumped 44.7% year-on-year to ₹6,383 crore. This compares with ₹4,410 crore in the same quarter last year.

Total expenses rose sharply to ₹7,448 crore during January-March FY26. A year ago, expenses stood at ₹5,610 crore.

For the full fiscal year FY26, Swiggy's net loss widened to ₹4,154 crore. This compares with ₹3,117 crore in FY25, showing the cost of scaling Instamart.

Swiggy shares closed around ₹280 on May 8, 2026, on the NSE. The stock trades well below its November 2024 IPO price of ₹390 per share.

Rival Zomato reported a Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹174 crore on revenue of ₹17,292 crore. Zomato's quick commerce arm Blinkit has reduced losses even as Swiggy's Instamart bleeds cash.

The key question ahead is whether Swiggy can improve take-rates and contribution margins in quick commerce. Dark store expansion costs and customer acquisition spending remain the biggest drags on profitability.
