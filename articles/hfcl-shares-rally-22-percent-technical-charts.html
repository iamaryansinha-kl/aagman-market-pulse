HFCL Shares Rally 22% in 5 Sessions: Technical Charts Signal More Upside

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HFCL shares gain over 22% in five consecutive sessions, reaching a day's high of Rs 158 on May 26
  • 02/Q4 FY2026 net profit swings to Rs 178.5 crore from Rs 81.4 crore loss; revenue surges 128% YoY
  • 03/Technical analysts flag Rs 150-155 breakout band as key support; RSI near 78 indicates strong momentum

HFCL Ltd shares rallied 3% to a day's high of Rs 158 on the BSE on Tuesday, May 26, 2026. The stock extended its winning streak to a fifth consecutive session, gaining over 22% during this period.

The sharp rally follows strong Q4 FY2026 results announced in April. The company reported a consolidated net profit of Rs 178.50 crore.

This marks a significant turnaround from a net loss of Rs 81.44 crore in Q4 FY2025. Revenue surged 128% YoY to Rs 1,824 crore.

On a sequential basis, profit jumped 82% from Rs 98 crore in Q3 FY2026. Revenue climbed 51% quarter-on-quarter from Rs 1,211 crore.

Technical analyst Ruchit Jain, Vice President at Motilal Oswal, noted renewed buying interest over the past two months. He said prices have rallied with strong volumes, indicating an ongoing uptrend.

"HFCL has moved from a base breakout into a momentum extension," said Harshal Dasani, Business Head at INVasset PMS. He noted the stock crossed its 52-week high zone with a sharp one-week move.

Kunal Kamble, Senior Technical Research Analyst at Bonanza, said the RSI is placed near 78. The stock trades above key EMAs with higher volumes, confirming aggressive accumulation.

Institutional activity shows mixed signals. Mutual fund holdings rose from 6.68% to 6.92% in Q4 FY2026, while foreign portfolio investors trimmed their stake from 7.48% to 7.08%.
