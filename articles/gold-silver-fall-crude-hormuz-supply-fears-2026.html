Gold, Silver Fall as Crude Surges on Hormuz Supply Fears

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MCX Gold fell 0.64% to ₹1,51,721 and silver dropped 1.15% to ₹2,41,824 on April 28 as stalled US-Iran talks weighed on safe havens
  • 02/Crude oil bucked the trend with MCX Crude surging 3.28% to ₹9,106 as the Strait of Hormuz blockade entered its ninth week
  • 03/US Federal Reserve policy decision due this week; gold support at ₹1,51,130 and resistance at ₹1,52,050 per Kotak Neo

Gold and silver prices declined on April 28, 2026. MCX Gold fell 0.64% to ₹1,51,721 per 10 grams. COMEX spot gold dropped 1% to $4,693.7 per troy ounce.

MCX Silver shed 1.15% to ₹2,41,824 per kg. COMEX silver was down 1.78% to $75.57 per ounce. The sell-off was driven by stalled US-Iran peace talks.

The US 10-year Treasury yield held at 4.342%. This added pressure on non-yielding assets like gold. Safe-haven demand weakened as diplomatic hopes faded.

Crude oil surged in the opposite direction. MCX Crude Oil jumped 3.28% to ₹9,106 per barrel. Brent climbed 2.75% to $108.2 and WTI rose 2.09% to $96.4.

Axis Direct cited supply tightness as the Strait of Hormuz remains almost shut. The conflict has now entered its ninth week. Iran proposed a phased reopening via Pakistani mediators.

"Oil prices continued to push higher, sustaining concerns over an energy-driven inflation shock that could delay interest rate cuts" - Manav Modi, Motilal Oswal analyst.

Kotak Neo sees gold's near-term bias as sideways. MCX support is at ₹1,51,130 and resistance at ₹1,52,050. Axis Direct placed critical levels at ₹1,54,000 and ₹1,50,000.

Markets await the US Federal Reserve policy decision this week. Jerome Powell chairs his final meeting before Kevin Warsh takes over. US GDP, consumer confidence and ADP employment data are also due.
