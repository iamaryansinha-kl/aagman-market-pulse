KFin Tech Shares Drop 4% After Q4 Net Profit Falls to ₹81 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KFin Technologies shares dropped 4% in early trade on April 30 after Q4 net profit fell 4.5% y-o-y to ₹81.14 crore.
  • 02/Revenue grew 23% to ₹347.33 crore; FY26 full-year PAT rose to ₹343.71 crore from ₹332.62 crore.
  • 03/Jefferies maintains buy with ₹1,200 target; investors await management commentary for margin outlook.

KFin Technologies Ltd (NSE: KFINTECH) shares dropped around 4% in early trade on April 30, 2026. The stock touched a low of ₹936.25, down from the previous close of ₹977.30.

The company reported a 4.5% year-on-year decline in consolidated net profit to ₹81.14 crore for Q4 FY26. This compares to ₹85.05 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations surged 23% y-o-y to ₹347.33 crore during the quarter. The growth was driven by strong momentum in core segments.

On a full-year basis, profit after tax increased to ₹343.71 crore in FY26 from ₹332.62 crore in FY25. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹12 per share.

Global brokerage Jefferies maintained a buy rating with a target price of ₹1,200. It noted that Q4 EBITDA came in 5% ahead of estimates, supported by the domestic mutual fund segment.

"Adjusted profit was 11% above estimates, aided by higher other income," Jefferies said. Mutual fund RTA yields stood at 3.25 basis points during the quarter.

Domestic brokerage Motilal Oswal highlighted structural tailwinds in the mutual fund industry. It noted that scaling up the Ascent platform and its profitability trajectory will remain key monitorables.

KFin Technologies shares were trading at ₹952.75 on the NSE around 10:10 am. The stock has gained over 48% in the past year despite Thursday's dip.
