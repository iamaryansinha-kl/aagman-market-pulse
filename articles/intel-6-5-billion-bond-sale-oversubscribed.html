Intel $6.5 Billion Bond Sale: 7.7x Oversubscribed, $50B Demand

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Intel attracted $50 billion in orders for a $6.5 billion bond sale, 7.7 times oversubscribed versus 4x average this year
  • 02/Proceeds will fund the $14.2 billion buyback of a 49% stake in Irish Fab 34 chip plant from Apollo Global Management
  • 03/Credit default swap spreads tightened to 0.6pp from 0.85pp in late March as investors bet on the turnaround

Intel Corp priced a $6.5 billion bond sale on April 27, 2026. The offering drew approximately $50 billion in orders from global investors.

The deal was 7.7 times oversubscribed, nearly double the 4x average for deals this year. Bonds priced at just 0 to 0.05 percentage points wider than existing Intel debt, signaling strong demand.

Proceeds will fund Intel's $14.2 billion buyback of a 49% stake in its Fab 34 chip plant in Ireland. Apollo Global Management had paid $11.2 billion for that stake in 2024.

Intel's credit ratings had slipped from A tier to BBB as profits deteriorated over recent years. The bond sale signals investors are buying into the company's turnaround story.

Credit default swap spreads dropped to 0.6 percentage points for five-year protection. This compares with 0.85 percentage points in late March 2026, reflecting growing confidence.

The five-part offering included a 2066 maturity note yielding 1.3pp over Treasuries. That was 0.35pp tighter than initial price talk, according to a person with knowledge of the matter.

Citigroup, JPMorgan, Barclays, Bank of America, and Deutsche Bank managed the sale. Intel last sold dollar notes in February 2024.

The company recently delivered a sales forecast that shattered Wall Street expectations. It is trying to win back its technological edge after years of market share losses in computer chips.
