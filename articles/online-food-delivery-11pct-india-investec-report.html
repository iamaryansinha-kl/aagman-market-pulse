Online Food Delivery 11% of India Market, $27B Potential: Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Online food delivery accounts for just 11% of India's total food services market, up from 3% in 2018, per an Investec Equities report dated May 4, 2026
  • 02/The market grew from $1.4 billion in 2018 to $9.1 billion in 2024 at a 37% CAGR, and is projected to reach $27 billion by 2030
  • 03/Zomato (NSE: ETERNAL) leads with 55-58% share, Swiggy (NSE: SWIGGY) holds 42-45%; Flipkart may enter with a Bengaluru pilot by mid-2026

Online food delivery in India accounts for roughly 11% of the total food services market, according to a report by Investec Equities published on May 4, 2026. The segment has grown from about 3% in 2018.

The market expanded from $1.4 billion in 2018 to $9.1 billion in 2024. This represents a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of around 37%.

Investec projects the segment to reach approximately $27 billion by 2030. This implies a projected CAGR of about 19% over the next six years.

In rupee terms, the market grew from ₹270 billion in FY20 to ₹790 billion in FY25. It is projected to reach ₹1.7-2.2 trillion by FY30, implying a CAGR of 17-23%.

Rising urbanisation, time constraints, and growth of nuclear households are driving online ordering. Smartphone penetration and digital payments have made ordering more accessible.

Expansion of cloud kitchens, improved restaurant ecosystems, and stronger logistics have reduced delivery times. This has boosted customer experience and repeat usage.

Zomato (NSE: ETERNAL) holds 55-58% market share, while Swiggy (NSE: SWIGGY) holds 42-45%. Together they handle over 95% of restaurant delivery orders.

Flipkart is reportedly evaluating entry into food delivery, with a pilot expected in Bengaluru by mid-2026. Analysts note intense competition and execution risks remain.
