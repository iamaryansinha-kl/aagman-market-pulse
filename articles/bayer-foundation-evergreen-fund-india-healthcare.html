Bayer Foundation Anchors Evergreen Fund for Healthcare and Climate in India

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bayer Foundation has anchored the AWE Evergreen Philanthropic Fund to drive social impact across healthcare, climate resilience, and sustainable agriculture in India
  • 02/The fund targets improving 1 million lives, empowering 1.5 million women, and enhancing livelihoods for 0.5 million people in rural and small-town India
  • 03/The EPF addresses the "missing middle" financing gap for impact-focused enterprises that remain overlooked by traditional capital providers

Bayer Foundation has anchored the AWE Evergreen Philanthropic Fund (EPF). The fund will deploy capital through an evergreen pooling vehicle created by US-based AWE Foundation.

The EPF is designed to address the persistent "missing middle" financing gap. Impact-focused enterprises often remain overlooked due to structural biases and limited access to capital and networks.

The fund will back enterprises building gender-equitable, scalable solutions. These solutions aim to improve livelihood and health outcomes for women, smallholder farmers, and healthcare workers across smaller towns and villages in India.

The collaboration aligns Bayer Foundation's mission of accelerating transformative social innovations in health and agriculture with AWE Foundation's focus on women's economic empowerment and climate action.

Through the EPF, AWE Foundation will focus on measurable impact outcomes. These include improving healthcare access, strengthening community health workers through digital tools, increasing farmer income and productivity, reducing food waste, and supporting climate-smart agricultural practices.

The EPF has set ambitious targets with Bayer Foundation's support. It aims to improve health outcomes for 1 million lives, help 1.5 million women gain agency, and enhance livelihoods for 0.5 million rural and small-town populations.

"Women entrepreneurs are powerful catalysts for change - strengthening communities, accelerating inclusive innovation, and shaping more resilient futures," said Chitkala Kalidas, Executive Director, Bayer Foundation.

Seema Chaturvedi, Chair of AWE Foundation, said the EPF structure can unlock catalytic capital, crowd in private investment, and accelerate the scale-up of solutions that transform livelihoods at the last mile.

All investments made through the EPF will be subject to independent governance oversight and conflict-of-interest protocols. Impact will be tracked using clearly defined key performance indicators.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE AWE EVERGREEN PHILANTHROPIC FUND?
THE AWE EVERGREEN PHILANTHROPIC FUND (EPF) IS A POOLING VEHICLE CREATED BY US-BASED AWE FOUNDATION AND ANCHORED BY BAYER FOUNDATION. IT PROVIDES LONG-TERM CATALYTIC CAPITAL TO SUPPORT SOCIAL IMPACT-RELATED INVESTMENTS IN HEALTHCARE, CLIMATE RESILIENCE, AND SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE ACROSS INDIA.
WHAT ARE THE IMPACT TARGETS OF THE EPF?
THE FUND HAS SET THREE MEASURABLE TARGETS: IMPROVING HEALTH OUTCOMES FOR 1 MILLION LIVES, PROMOTING GENDER EQUITY BY HELPING 1.5 MILLION WOMEN GAIN AGENCY, AND ENHANCING LIVELIHOODS FOR 0.5 MILLION RURAL AND SMALL-TOWN POPULATIONS INCLUDING FARMERS.
WHAT IS THE "MISSING MIDDLE" FINANCING GAP?
THE "MISSING MIDDLE" REFERS TO IMPACT-FOCUSED ENTERPRISES THAT ARE TOO LARGE FOR GRANTS AND TOO SMALL OR RISKY FOR TRADITIONAL VENTURE CAPITAL. THESE ENTERPRISES REMAIN OVERLOOKED DUE TO STRUCTURAL BIASES AND LIMITED ACCESS TO CAPITAL AND NETWORKS.
WHO ARE THE KEY LEADERS BEHIND THIS INITIATIVE?
CHITKALA KALIDAS, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF BAYER FOUNDATION, AND SEEMA CHATURVEDI, CHAIR OF AWE FOUNDATION AND MANAGING PARTNER AT AWE FUNDS, ARE THE KEY LEADERS DRIVING THIS PARTNERSHIP. BOTH HAVE SPOKEN ABOUT THE ROLE OF WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS AND BLENDED FINANCE IN DRIVING SOCIAL CHANGE.
HOW WILL THE FUND ENSURE ACCOUNTABILITY AND MEASURE IMPACT?
ALL INVESTMENTS MADE THROUGH THE EPF ARE SUBJECT TO INDEPENDENT GOVERNANCE OVERSIGHT AND CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST PROTOCOLS. IMPACT WILL BE TRACKED USING CLEARLY DEFINED KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS ACROSS HEALTHCARE ACCESS, DIGITAL TOOLS FOR HEALTH WORKERS, FARMER PRODUCTIVITY, FOOD WASTE REDUCTION, AND CLIMATE-SMART AGRICULTURE.
