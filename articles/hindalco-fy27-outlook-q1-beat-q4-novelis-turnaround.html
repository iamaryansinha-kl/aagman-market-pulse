Hindalco FY27 Outlook: Q1 to Beat Q4, Novelis EBITDA at $500/Tonne

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindalco's Q1 FY27 will outperform Q4 FY26, with aluminium prices at $3,400-$3,500/tonne through end-2026
  • 02/Novelis turnaround begins in FY27 as Oswego restarts and Bay Minette commissions; EBITDA to stabilize at $500/tonne
  • 03/Deleveraging at Novelis to start from FY28; no IPO planned until operations normalize

Hindalco Industries Ltd (NSE: HINDALCO) expects a stronger FY27. Managing director Satish Pai told ET that Q1 FY27 will beat Q4 FY26.

Aluminium prices will stay at $3,400-$3,500 per tonne until late 2026. About 2.5-3.0 million tonnes of supply is offline due to the West Asia crisis.

Novelis, Hindalco's US arm, will turn around in FY27. The Oswego plant restarts next week and the Bay Minette hot mill is commissioning.

Once both facilities are operational, deleveraging will begin from FY28. Novelis EBITDA should stabilize at around $500 per tonne consistently.

The India business is performing well across upstream and downstream. Copper profits were boosted by sulphuric acid and downstream demand.

Hindalco shares closed at ₹1,099.30 on May 21, 2026. The stock has rallied 29% from its March low of ₹854.65.

Analysts are bullish with HSBC at a target of ₹1,430 and CLSA at ₹1,240. Citi noted Q4 consol EBITDA rose 28% QoQ to ₹101.8 billion.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID HINDALCO MD SATISH PAI SAY ABOUT FY27 PERFORMANCE?
SATISH PAI SAID HINDALCO'S Q1 FY27 WILL BE BETTER THAN Q4 FY26. HE EXPECTS ALUMINIUM PRICES TO REMAIN AT $3,400-$3,500 PER TONNE UNTIL THE END OF 2026. FY27 WILL BE A MUCH STRONGER YEAR FOR THE COMPANY.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR NOVELIS IN FY27?
FY27 IS THE TURNAROUND YEAR FOR NOVELIS. THE OSWEGO PLANT WILL RESTART NEXT WEEK AND THE BAY MINETTE HOT MILL IS BEING COMMISSIONED. ONCE BOTH ARE OPERATIONAL, NOVELIS EBITDA WILL STABILIZE AT AROUND $500 PER TONNE.
WHY ARE ALUMINIUM PRICES EXPECTED TO STAY ELEVATED?
ABOUT 2.5-3.0 MILLION TONNES OF ALUMINIUM HAVE GONE OFF THE MARKET DUE TO THE WEST ASIA CRISIS. IT WILL TAKE SIX MONTHS TO BRING THIS CAPACITY BACK ONLINE, KEEPING SUPPLY TIGHT AND PRICES HIGH.
WHEN WILL NOVELIS START DELEVERAGING?
DELEVERAGING AT NOVELIS WILL BEGIN FROM FY28 AFTER THE OSWEGO RESTART AND BAY MINETTE COMMISSIONING ARE COMPLETE. MANAGEMENT HAS RULED OUT AN IPO FOR NOVELIS BY FY28, FOCUSING ONLY ON OPERATIONAL NORMALIZATION.
HOW HAS THE HINDALCO STOCK PERFORMED RECENTLY?
HINDALCO SHARES CLOSED AT ₹1,099.30 ON MAY 21, 2026. THE STOCK HAS GAINED ABOUT 29% FROM ITS MARCH LOW OF ₹854.65, SUPPORTED BY STRONG LME ALUMINIUM PRICES AND IMPROVING NOVELIS OUTLOOK.
