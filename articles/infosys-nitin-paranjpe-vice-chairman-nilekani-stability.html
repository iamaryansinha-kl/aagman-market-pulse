Infosys Names Nitin Paranjpe Vice Chairman; Nilekani Says No Chair Change

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Infosys Board appoints Nitin Paranjpe as non-executive Vice Chairman with immediate effect, recognising his contribution since joining in January 2024
  • 02/Chairman Nandan Nilekani confirms he will continue in his role with no transition planned; appointment signals governance depth, not succession
  • 03/Infosys shares closed at ₹1,181.50 on May 1, up 1.20%; stock is 31.6% below its 52-week high of ₹1,728

Infosys Ltd (NSE: INFY) has appointed Nitin Paranjpe as non-executive Vice Chairman of the Board. The appointment takes effect immediately as per the company’s May 1, 2026 regulatory filing.

Paranjpe joined the Infosys Board as an independent director on January 1, 2024. His current term runs through December 31, 2028. The promotion recognises his contributions during his 16-month tenure.

“We are delighted to have Nitin in this position,” said Nandan Nilekani, Chairman of the Board. “His expertise has already made a significant impact on the Board, and we look forward to his continued leadership in this expanded role.”

Nilekani explicitly addressed succession speculation. He stated he will continue as Chairman “as long as necessary” and confirmed there is “no plan for a chairman transition.”

Paranjpe brings nearly four decades of leadership experience from Unilever. He joined Hindustan Lever in 1987 and rose to become Unilever’s Chief Operating Officer.

He currently serves as non-executive Chairman of Hindustan Unilever Ltd and non-executive Vice Chairman of Heineken NV. He was Unilever’s Chief People & Transformation Officer until his retirement in June 2024.

Infosys shares closed at ₹1,181.50 on May 1, gaining 1.20%. The stock has declined 21% over the past six months and trades 31.6% below its 52-week high of ₹1,728.

The company’s market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹4.79 lakh crore. It remains India’s second-largest IT services firm by market value.

In a separate move, the Board approved Shreyas Shibulal’s request to reclassify from the Promoter category to Public. Shreyas is the son of Infosys co-founder S. D. Shibulal.
