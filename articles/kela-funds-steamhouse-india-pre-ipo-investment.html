Kela Funds Invest Rs 50 Crore in Steamhouse India Pre-IPO Placement

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Madhusudan Kela-backed Singularity funds and Niveshaay Sambhav Fund invest Rs 49.99 crore in Steamhouse India via pre-IPO placement at Rs 73 per share
  • 02/Steamhouse allotted 68,49,315 equity shares; the three investors now hold approximately 2.94% of pre-offer equity
  • 03/Surat-based Steamhouse India has filed its Updated Draft Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI and is preparing for its initial public offering

Ace investor Madhusudan Kela-backed funds have invested Rs 49.99 crore in Steamhouse India through a pre-IPO placement. Singularity Large Value Fund III, Singularity Equity Fund I, and Niveshaay Sambhav Fund participated in the transaction.

The shares were allotted at an issue price of Rs 73 per equity share. Steamhouse India issued a total of 68,49,315 equity shares through the private placement.

Singularity Large Value Fund III received 47,94,520 shares worth Rs 34.99 crore. Singularity Equity Fund I was allotted 6,84,932 shares aggregating Rs 5 crore. Niveshaay Sambhav Fund got 13,69,863 shares worth Rs 9.99 crore.

Following the allotment, the three investors together hold around 2.94% of Steamhouse India's pre-offer equity share capital. Per SEBI regulations, the proposed IPO fresh issue size will be reduced by the pre-IPO amount raised.

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Surat, Steamhouse India operates a centralized steam supply model for industrial clients. The company serves over 167 clients across chemicals, textiles, pharmaceuticals, food processing, paper, and manufacturing sectors.

Steamhouse delivers industrial steam at approximately 190 degrees Celsius through a network spanning over 45 kilometres. Its pipeline infrastructure covers industrial clusters including Sachin, Vapi, Ankleshwar, Sarigam, Panoli, and Nandesari.

The company uses IoT and AI-enabled systems across procurement, generation, and distribution processes. It is also expanding into nitrogen compression, waste-to-energy solutions, and aviation logistics as adjacent businesses.

Steamhouse has commissioned a waste-to-steam boiler at Vapi that converts non-recyclable plastic waste into industrial steam. It has also secured a 5 MW waste-to-steam project from Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation under a public-private partnership model.

The Surat-based firm has filed its Updated Draft Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI for the proposed public issue. Equirus Capital is the sole book-running lead manager to the IPO.
