Passenger Vehicle Sales Fall 10% in April as EVs Hit Record 5.7% Market Share

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Passenger vehicle registrations fell 10.2% month-on-month to 3,98,146 units in April 2026 after March year-end surge.
  • 02/EV volumes declined just 1.8% to 22,677 units, lifting electric vehicle share of total PV sales to a record 5.7%.
  • 03/Maruti Suzuki led with 1,58,223 units (40% share); Tata Motors topped EVs with 8,507 units (37.5% EV share).

Passenger vehicle registrations fell 10.2% month-on-month in April 2026 to 3,98,146 units. The decline follows a year-end surge that had pushed March volumes to 4,43,201 units.

In contrast, electric vehicle registrations showed far greater resilience. EV volumes dipped just 1.8% to 22,677 units in April, down from 23,097 units in March.

With overall passenger vehicle sales falling faster, EV penetration rose to nearly 5.7% of total PV sales. This marks a new high, up from about 5.2% the previous month.

Maruti Suzuki continued to dominate the broader market with 1,58,223 units in April. The company holds nearly 40% of total passenger vehicle retail volumes.

Tata Motors secured second place with 57,472 units. It also led the EV segment with 8,507 units, capturing approximately 37.5% of electric vehicle sales.

Mahindra & Mahindra held third overall with 54,897 units. In EVs, it ranked second with 5,394 units, good for a 23.8% share.

Hyundai remained fourth in total PV sales with 47,345 units. The decline was broad-based, with Tata and Mahindra seeing drops over 14% while Hyundai fell a more modest 5%.

"India's passenger EV market is witnessing a healthy transformation," said Gaurav Vangaal of S&P Global Mobility. He cited recent launches like the Maruti e-Vitara, Mahindra XEV 9s, and Tata Punch EV as evidence of manufacturer commitment.

Poonam Upadhyay, Director at Crisil Ratings, noted that E4W registrations in April 2026 came in at about 22,405 units, up roughly 72% year-on-year. She said this signals momentum built at the tail end of FY26 is carrying into the new fiscal year.

VinFast emerged as the standout performer, with registrations surging approximately 67% month-on-month to 1,231 units. This made it the fastest-growing EV player and pushed it into fourth position in the segment.

The top three EV manufacturers together accounted for over 83% of EV retail volumes. MG Motor ranked third with 4,978 units, representing a 21.9% share.

Industry observers say E4W penetration currently stands at roughly 4-5% of total passenger vehicle sales. If current trends hold, penetration could reach 7-8% by the end of FY27.
