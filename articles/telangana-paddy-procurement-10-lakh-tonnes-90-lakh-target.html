Telangana Paddy Procurement Crosses 10 Lakh Tonnes, Aims for 90 Lakh

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Telangana breaches 10-lakh tonne paddy procurement mark for 2025-26 rabi season against a target of 90 lakh tonnes
  • 02/State releases ₹500.20 crore under MSP; 4.50 lakh tonnes of fine variety procured so far with coarse variety making up the rest
  • 03/8,575 Paddy Purchase Centres operate at full capacity as state expects record production of 1.52 crore tonnes from 22.25 lakh hectares

Telangana has crossed the 10-lakh tonne mark in paddy procurement for the 2025-26 rabi season. The state has set a target to procure 90 lakh tonnes from the rabi output.

The procured quantity includes 4.50 lakh tonnes of fine variety. The remaining quantity comprises coarse variety so far.

The state government has released ₹500.20 crore towards Minimum Support Price payments. These funds cover farmer payments under the ongoing procurement operations.

Telangana grew paddy across 22.25 lakh hectares this season. It expects a record production of 1.52 crore tonnes from the rabi crop.

The state targets 30 lakh tonnes of fine variety and 60 lakh tonnes of coarse paddy. Procurement is well on course to reach the overall target.

The Civil Supplies Department is operating 8,575 Paddy Purchase Centres at full capacity. These include 4,350 PACS centres, 3,563 IKP centres, and 662 centres run by other agencies.

"We are lifting the procured paddy to designated rice mills to avoid stock pile-ups at the villages," an official of the Telangana Civil Supplies Department said. The state has provisioned 32 lakh tonnes of total godown space for storing the procured paddy.

Civil Supplies Minister N Uttam Kumar Reddy said the check-posts along the state borders were checking paddy trucks. This is to prevent grain from outside from sneaking into the state's procurement centres.
