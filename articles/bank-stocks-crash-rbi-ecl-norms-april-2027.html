Bank Stocks Crash as RBI Finalises ECL Norms From April 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI rejected banks' pleas for more time and confirmed ECL framework implementation from April 1, 2027, replacing the incurred loss model.
  • 02/BSE PSU Bank index crashed 2.20% to 4,854.92, with SBI and Bank of Baroda among the top losers on April 28, 2026.
  • 03/Analysts warn higher provisioning requirements will weigh on margins near term, especially for PSU banks and lenders with unsecured and MSME exposure.

Bank stocks faced heavy selling on April 28, 2026, after the Reserve Bank of India confirmed the Expected Credit Loss (ECL) framework will take effect from April 1, 2027.

The RBI on April 27 declined pleas from banks seeking more time to transition. The central bank said lenders had already been given a one-year timeline to prepare internal systems.

The BSE PSU Bank index plunged 2.20% to close at 4,854.92. The broader BSE Bankex fell 1.61% to end at 62,360.06.

Union Bank of India dropped 3.07%. Bank of Maharashtra fell 2.53%, Canara Bank 2.42%, Bank of India 2.27%, Bank of Baroda 2.26%, and State Bank of India 1.88%.

Among private lenders, Axis Bank lost 2.65%, IDFC First Bank fell 1.92%, ICICI Bank dropped 1.77%, and HDFC Bank slipped 0.96%.

"Sectoral trends were mixed, with financials among the key laggards. The shift to a forward-looking provisioning model is expected to increase capital requirements and impact profitability," said Siddhartha Khemka, Head of Research at Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd.

The ECL framework replaces the current incurred loss model with a forward-looking system. Banks will now need to recognise credit risks earlier and maintain continuous monitoring.

"Banking stocks led the decline after the RBI confirmed its expected credit loss framework, raising concerns over higher provisioning," said Vinod Nair, Head of Research at Geojit Investments Limited.

The central bank has provided transitional arrangements including a three-year timeline for applying Effective Interest Rate on legacy loan accounts.

"Sentiment in public sector banks deteriorated sharply. PSU banks with higher exposure to unsecured and MSME segments are particularly vulnerable," said Hariprasad K, Research Analyst and Founder at Livelong Wealth.

The broader 30-share BSE Sensex declined 416.72 points or 0.54% to settle at 76,886.91. The NSE Nifty dropped 97 points or 0.40% to end at 23,995.70.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID THE RBI ANNOUNCE ON APRIL 27, 2026?
THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA REJECTED PLEAS FROM COMMERCIAL BANKS SEEKING MORE TIME TO TRANSITION TO THE EXPECTED CREDIT LOSS FRAMEWORK. THE RBI CONFIRMED THE NEW SYSTEM WILL BE IMPLEMENTED FROM APRIL 1, 2027, REPLACING THE CURRENT INCURRED LOSS MODEL.
HOW MUCH DID BANK STOCKS FALL ON APRIL 28, 2026?
THE BSE PSU BANK INDEX FELL 2.20% TO CLOSE AT 4,854.92. THE BSE BANKEX DECLINED 1.61% TO 62,360.06. UNION BANK OF INDIA DROPPED 3.07%, BANK OF MAHARASHTRA FELL 2.53%, AND STATE BANK OF INDIA SLIPPED 1.88%.
WHAT IS THE ECL FRAMEWORK AND WHY DOES IT MATTER?
THE EXPECTED CREDIT LOSS (ECL) FRAMEWORK IS A FORWARD-LOOKING PROVISIONING MODEL THAT REQUIRES BANKS TO RECOGNISE POTENTIAL CREDIT RISKS EARLIER THAN UNDER THE CURRENT INCURRED LOSS MODEL. IT WILL LIKELY INCREASE PROVISIONING REQUIREMENTS AND REDUCE NEAR-TERM PROFITABILITY.
WHICH BANKS ARE MOST AT RISK FROM THE ECL TRANSITION?
ANALYSTS AT MOTILAL OSWAL AND LIVELONG WEALTH WARN THAT PSU BANKS AND LENDERS WITH HIGHER EXPOSURE TO UNSECURED LOANS AND MSME SEGMENTS FACE THE GREATEST MARGIN PRESSURE. LARGE PRIVATE BANKS ARE EXPECTED TO NAVIGATE THE TRANSITION MORE SMOOTHLY.
WHAT TRANSITIONAL RELIEF HAS THE RBI PROVIDED?
THE RBI HAS OFFERED A CALIBRATED TRANSITION FRAMEWORK INCLUDING TRANSITIONAL ARRANGEMENTS FOR ONE-TIME CAPITAL IMPACT AND A THREE-YEAR TIMELINE FOR APPLYING EFFECTIVE INTEREST RATE ON LEGACY LOAN ACCOUNTS.
