Advent International to Invest Rs 3,150 Crore for 24.9% Stake in Yatharth Hospitals; Stock Hits Record High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Advent International is betting Rs 3,150 crore on India's hospital boom. The US-based private equity firm announced on Thursday that it has entered into a definitive agreement to invest the amount as primary capital to acquire a 24.9% stake in Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services, subject to customary closing conditions.

The market response was immediate. Shares of Yatharth rallied more than 10% on Friday to a fresh all-time high of Rs 1,184 on the NSE, breaking a record set just the previous day. The stock has gained 20% in two sessions since the deal was announced, and is up more than 70% in 2026. It has doubled from its 52-week low of Rs 538.25 in less than eight months, taking the company's market capitalisation to around Rs 11,163 crore.

Under the transaction, the promoter Tyagi family will remain the largest shareholder and continue to guide the company's long-term vision alongside its leadership team.

Yatharth has built one of North India's leading healthcare networks, operating nine multi-speciality hospitals with around 2,800 beds and an overall announced capacity of nearly 3,250 beds. Its growth has been sharp: consolidated revenue rose 51% year-on-year to Rs 392.70 crore in the June quarter, with average revenue per occupied bed up 7% at Rs 34,758. Whole-time director Yatharth Tyagi recently told analysts the chain grew 37% in FY26 and is set to easily surpass that growth this year.

For Advent, the investment extends a long India playbook. The firm has been a major backer of Indian pharma companies, and its earlier hospital bet, a 2012 investment in Care Hospitals, now sits inside Aster DM Quality Care, the platform created by merging Aster DM Healthcare and Quality Care India, where TPG and Blackstone teamed up to build the country's second-largest healthcare chain.

Advent MD Pankaj Patwari said the investment underscores the firm's deep and long-standing commitment to India's healthcare sector, which it believes is entering a decade of structural growth as access expands, quality improves and consolidation advances.

Yatharth Tyagi said the transaction validates the platform's strength and that Advent brings healthcare expertise, global insights and a value-creation mindset beyond capital.

The deal is the latest in a rush of private equity money into Indian hospitals, as financial investors position for a sector where demand is growing faster than supply and scale players can consolidate fragmented markets.
