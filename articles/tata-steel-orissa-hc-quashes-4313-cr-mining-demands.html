Tata Steel Wins Court Relief: HC Quashes ₹4,313 Cr Mining Demands

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Orissa High Court quashes two demand notices totalling over ₹4,313 crore against Tata Steel's Sukinda chromite mine
  • 02/Stock touched 52-week high of ₹218.24 on NSE, up 1.47% to ₹216.40; shares have gained 52% over the past year
  • 03/Court ruled 2021 penal provisions cannot be applied retrospectively; Mining Plan prevails over MDPA

Tata Steel Ltd (NSE: TATASTEEL) won a major legal victory on April 28, 2026. The Orissa High Court quashed demand notices totalling over ₹4,313 crore.

The notices were issued by the Deputy Director of Mines, Jajpur. They alleged shortfalls in chromite ore dispatch from Tata Steel's Sukinda mine in Odisha.

The first demand, dated July 3, 2025, sought ₹1,902.72 crore for the fourth year of the Mine Development and Production Agreement. The second, dated October 3, 2025, demanded ₹2,410.89 crore for the fifth year.

Tata Steel challenged both notices through separate writ petitions before the High Court, citing Rule 12A of the Minerals Concession Rules, 2016. The court pronounced its judgment on April 20, 2026. Tata Steel received the order on April 27.

The court held that penal provisions under sub-rules 1A, 1B, and 1C of Rule 12A, effective July 1, 2021, cannot be applied retrospectively. It also ruled that the Mining Plan takes precedence over the MDPA in cases of inconsistency.

Tata Steel stated it believes both demand letters now stand quashed to the extent they conflict with the court's directions. The company has not indicated whether authorities plan to appeal the ruling.

Tata Steel shares touched a 52-week high of ₹218.24 on the NSE during the April 28 session. The stock closed up 1.47 per cent at ₹216.40.

The stock has gained nearly 52 per cent over the past year. It significantly outperformed the Nifty 50, which declined 0.77 per cent over the same period.
