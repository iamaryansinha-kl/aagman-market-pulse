Swiggy Names Swapnil Bajpai CEO of Dineout and Scenes to Scale Going-Out Business

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Swiggy appointed internal veteran Swapnil Bajpai as CEO of Dineout and Scenes on May 2, 2026, consolidating its going-out vertical under single leadership.
  • 02/Swiggy acquired Dineout in May 2022 for approximately $200 million in an all-equity deal, adding 50,000+ restaurant partners to its ecosystem.
  • 03/The move signals Swiggy's push into higher-margin dining-out and experiences as it trails Zomato's 55-58% food delivery market share with its own 42-45%.

Swiggy Limited has appointed Swapnil Bajpai as Chief Executive Officer of its Dineout and Scenes businesses, effective May 2, 2026. The elevation consolidates the company's dining-out and experiences verticals under one leader as competition with Zomato intensifies beyond core food delivery.

Bajpai is a Swiggy veteran who most recently served as Senior Vice President and business head for Swiggy Dineout, Assure and Lynk. His prior roles spanned advertising, monetisation, supply and sales across both food delivery and dine-out divisions.

Swiggy entered the dining-out segment in May 2022 through its acquisition of Dineout from Times Internet in a deal valued at approximately $200 million. The all-equity transaction added over 50,000 restaurant partners and brought founders including Ankit Mehrotra into the Swiggy fold.

The Dineout business had continued to operate under its founders since the acquisition, maintaining merchant relationships independently. Bajpai's appointment marks the transition from a founder-led unit to a fully integrated Swiggy-run vertical.

Scenes, Swiggy's events and nightlife platform, will now sit alongside Dineout under Bajpai's leadership. The combined vertical targets commissions, prepaid deals, advertising and ticketing — a model with lower logistics costs than food delivery.

Swiggy's push comes as its core food delivery business generates an annualised adjusted EBITDA of roughly ₹960 crore, according to Q2 FY26 disclosures. However, the company reported a consolidated net loss of ₹1,065 crore in Q3 FY26 as Instamart expansion drained cash.

Rival Zomato commands a 55-58% share of India's food delivery market against Swiggy's 42-45%, per 2026 estimates. Zomato's market capitalisation of roughly ₹1,85,000 crore is 2.2 times Swiggy's approximately ₹85,873 crore valuation.

Before joining Swiggy, Bajpai spent time at Reckitt as regional sales manager and held multiple roles at Procter & Gamble managing trade programmes and go-to-market strategies. That FMCG background aligns with Swiggy's need to scale merchant adoption and monetisation.
