Apollo Hospitals FY26 Profit Jumps 34% to ₹1,942 Crore on Strong Revenue Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Apollo Hospitals reports FY26 consolidated net profit of ₹1,942 crore, up 34% YoY, on revenue of ₹25,229 crore
  • 02/Q4 FY26 net profit jumped 36% to ₹529 crore while revenue rose 18% to ₹6,605 crore
  • 03/Company plans to add 1,500 beds in next 12-18 months and demerge pharmacy business for listing by Q4 FY27

Apollo Hospitals Enterprises Ltd (NSE: APOLLOHOSP) reported a 34% jump in consolidated net profit to ₹1,942 crore for FY26. Revenue for the full year rose 16% to ₹25,229 crore.

For Q4 FY26, net profit surged 36% to ₹529 crore. Revenue grew 18% to ₹6,605 crore compared to the same quarter last year.

The robust performance was driven by growth across core businesses. Healthcare services revenue stood at ₹12,555 crore, up 13% YoY. Healthcare net profit rose 14% to ₹1,628 crore.

As of March 31, 2026, Apollo had 8,131 operating beds across its network. Overall hospital occupancy improved to 68% in Q4 FY26 from 67% in Q4 FY25.

The company aims to add another 1,500 operating beds in the next 12-18 months. This expansion across key metros will take total operating beds to 10,000.

"Our expansion strategy continues to be guided by India's long-term healthcare needs," said Prathap C Reddy, Chairman. Four hospitals were launched over the past six months.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹10 per equity share for FY26. The proposed demerger of pharmacy and digital health businesses is progressing toward a potential listing by Q4 FY27.

"Growth was supported by improved performance in specialties and high-complexity procedures," said Krishnan Akhileswaran, Group CFO. No adverse impact was seen from the West Asia crisis on foreign patient inflows.
