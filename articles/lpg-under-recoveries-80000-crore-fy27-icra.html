LPG Under-Recoveries Seen at ₹80,000 Crore in FY27: ICRA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICRA projects LPG under-recoveries at ₹80,000 crore for FY27 if current pricing gaps persist through the full fiscal year.
  • 02/International LPG prices surged after West Asia supply disruption forced India to procure costly cargoes from the US and Australia.
  • 03/Fertiliser subsidy requirement is estimated at ₹2.05-2.25 lakh crore for FY27, up from the budgeted ₹1.71 lakh crore.

Ratings agency ICRA estimates domestic LPG under-recoveries at ₹80,000 crore for FY27. The projection assumes current pricing gaps persist for the full fiscal year.

The West Asia conflict severely disrupted India's LPG supply routes. The world's third-largest energy consumer was forced to procure costly alternative cargoes from the US and Australia.

Prashant Vasisht, Senior VP and Co-Group Head at ICRA, said international LPG prices have surged sharply. Domestic LPG production was increased by refining companies, but under-recoveries on sale of subsidised cylinders remain high for PSU oil marketing companies.

On marketing losses for petrol and diesel, Vasisht noted that stable pump prices amid elevated crude oil prices are hurting OMC profitability. At crude prices of $120-125 per barrel, marketing margins are estimated at negative ₹14 per litre for petrol and negative ₹18 per litre for diesel.

The fertiliser subsidy is another pain point. ICRA projects the requirement at ₹2.05-2.25 lakh crore for FY27, with an upward bias. The budgeted allocation stands at ₹1.71 lakh crore.

Vasisht expects the government to enhance fertiliser subsidy allocation during FY27. This is necessary to maintain a stable credit profile for the sector amid sharp raw material price inflation.

The expected impact of El-Nino on the upcoming Kharif monsoon may limit farmers' ability to absorb price increases. This adds pressure on both fertiliser demand and rural consumption.

ICRA's outlook on the fuel retailing segment remains negative. The outlook on crude oil refining remains stable, supported by adequate refining margins from healthy product cracks.

The City Gas Distribution sector faces rising cost pressures from currency depreciation and higher gas prices. CNG margins are expected to face headwinds as increased costs may not be fully passed to consumers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE ESTIMATED LPG UNDER-RECOVERY FOR FY27?
ICRA ESTIMATES DOMESTIC LPG UNDER-RECOVERIES AT ₹80,000 CRORE FOR FY27, ASSUMING CURRENT PRICING GAPS CONTINUE FOR THE FULL FISCAL YEAR.
WHY HAVE LPG UNDER-RECOVERIES RISEN SHARPLY?
THE WEST ASIA CONFLICT DISRUPTED INDIA'S LPG SUPPLY ROUTES, FORCING THE COUNTRY TO PROCURE COSTLY CARGOES FROM THE US AND AUSTRALIA, WHICH SURGED INTERNATIONAL PRICES.
WHAT ARE THE MARKETING LOSSES ON PETROL AND DIESEL?
AT CRUDE OIL PRICES OF $120-125 PER BARREL, ICRA ESTIMATES MARKETING MARGINS AT NEGATIVE ₹14 PER LITRE FOR PETROL AND NEGATIVE ₹18 PER LITRE FOR DIESEL.
WHAT IS THE FERTILISER SUBSIDY PROJECTION FOR FY27?
ICRA PROJECTS THE FERTILISER SUBSIDY REQUIREMENT AT ₹2.05-2.25 LAKH CRORE FOR FY27, COMPARED TO THE BUDGETED ALLOCATION OF ₹1.71 LAKH CRORE.
WHAT IS ICRA'S OUTLOOK ON THE ENERGY SECTOR?
ICRA'S OUTLOOK ON CRUDE OIL REFINING IS STABLE DUE TO HEALTHY PRODUCT CRACKS, WHILE THE OUTLOOK ON FUEL RETAILING REMAINS NEGATIVE DRIVEN BY STEEPLY NEGATIVE MARKETING MARGINS ON AUTO FUELS.
