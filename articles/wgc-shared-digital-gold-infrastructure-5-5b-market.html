WGC Proposes Shared Digital Gold Infrastructure for $5.5B Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/World Gold Council proposes "Gold as a Service" — a shared physically-backed infrastructure platform for digital gold products
  • 02/Tokenized gold market surged 177% to $4.4 billion in 2025 and now exceeds $5.5 billion, but XAUT and PAXG control 97% of supply
  • 03/WGC Chief Strategy Officer Terry Heymann says the initiative targets fragmented custody standards and aims to engage governments and market participants globally

The World Gold Council has unveiled a white paper proposing "Gold as a Service." It is a shared infrastructure platform designed to standardise digital gold issuance and custody.

WGC Chief Strategy Officer Terry Heymann told PTI that current digital gold products operate across fragmented infrastructure. Inconsistent custody standards and governance frameworks limit scalability.

The platform will be owned by the WGC. It lets market participants build digital gold products without developing their own end-to-end systems.

The tokenized gold market has ballooned to roughly $5.5 billion as of March 2026. It surged from $1.6 billion in early 2025 — a 177% year-on-year jump.

Trading volume for tokenized gold hit $178 billion in 2025. Yet Tether Gold (XAUT) and Paxos Gold (PAXG) control about 97% of total supply.

Gold prices have risen 42.59% over the past year. Spot gold traded at $4,726.18 per ounce on April 24, 2026, down from a 52-week high of $5,477.79.

Global gold ETFs attracted record net inflows of $88.55 billion in 2025. Total assets under management doubled to $558.9 billion. India ranked third globally with $4.37 billion in inflows.

"The idea is to provide shared infrastructure to help gold play a greater role in the digital economy," Heymann said. The white paper was co-authored with Boston Consulting Group. The timeline for rollout remains unspecified.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE WGC'S "GOLD AS A SERVICE" INITIATIVE?
THE WORLD GOLD COUNCIL HAS PROPOSED A SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE PLATFORM CALLED "GOLD AS A SERVICE" TO STANDARDISE THE ISSUANCE, CUSTODY, AND REDEMPTION OF DIGITAL GOLD PRODUCTS. THE PLATFORM WILL BE OWNED AND OPERATED BY THE WGC AND BUILT AS OPEN MIDDLEWARE THAT ANY MARKET PARTICIPANT CAN ACCESS.
WHY DOES DIGITAL GOLD NEED SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE?
CURRENT DIGITAL GOLD PRODUCTS OPERATE ACROSS FRAGMENTED INFRASTRUCTURE WITH INCONSISTENT CUSTODY STANDARDS, REDEMPTION TERMS, AND GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS. THESE STRUCTURAL FRICTIONS CONSTRAIN FUNGIBILITY, FRAGMENT LIQUIDITY, AND LIMIT GOLD'S BROADER FINANCIAL UTILITY.

THE TOKENIZED GOLD MARKET REACHED APPROXIMATELY $5.5 BILLION IN MARCH 2026, UP FROM $1.6 BILLION IN EARLY 2025 — A 177% YEAR-ON-YEAR INCREASE. TRADING VOLUME SURGED TO $178 BILLION IN 2025.
WHO DOMINATES THE TOKENIZED GOLD MARKET?
TETHER GOLD (XAUT) AND PAXOS GOLD (PAXG) TOGETHER CONTROL APPROXIMATELY 97% OF TOTAL TOKENIZED GOLD SUPPLY. THIS EXTREME CONCENTRATION REFLECTS HIGH BARRIERS TO ENTRY FOR NEW ISSUERS.
