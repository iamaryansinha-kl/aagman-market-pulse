Tata Consumer Q4 FY26: Profit ₹419 Cr, Revenue Crosses ₹20K Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Consumer Products reports Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹419 crore, up 21% YoY, with revenue rising 18% to ₹5,434 crore
  • 02/FY26 revenue crosses ₹20,000 crore mark for first time; digital and quick commerce channels now contribute 36% of India business
  • 03/CEO Sunil D'Souza guides for double-digit topline growth and 50-75 bps EBITDA margin expansion in FY27

Tata Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: TATACONSUM) reported Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit of ₹419 crore. The figure marks a 21% year-on-year increase.

Revenue from operations rose 18% to ₹5,434 crore during the quarter. EBITDA grew 27% to ₹796 crore. Margins expanded 150 basis points to 14.6%.

For the full year FY26, revenue crossed ₹20,000 crore for the first time. The company delivered 15% annual growth. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹10 per share.

Digital channels have become a core growth engine for the company. Quick commerce and e-commerce contribute 21% of India business, growing 62% year-on-year.

Modern trade adds another 15%, taking newer retail channels to 36% of domestic revenues. "We will be where the consumer is," said CEO Sunil D'Souza. He noted legacy tracking systems fail to capture quick commerce sales.

Growth businesses including Sampann, Tata Copper+, Capital Foods and Organic India contribute 31% of India business. These segments grew 33% in Q4. Sampann crossed ₹1,600 crore in annual revenue during FY26.

The company launched 80 new products during FY26. Innovation-led revenue has grown sevenfold in recent years. It now contributes 4.5% of overall sales.

Tata Consumer ended FY26 with net cash of nearly ₹3,000 crore. D'Souza guided for double-digit topline growth and 50 to 75 basis points of margin expansion in FY27. International revenue grew 11% in constant currency despite shipping disruptions.
