J B Ecotex Files DRHP for Rs 400 Crore IPO With 1.29 Crore Share OFS

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/J B Ecotex Ltd filed its DRHP with SEBI on September 29, 2026 for a Rs 400 crore fresh issue plus an OFS of up to 1.29 crore (12,950,000) promoter shares.
  • 02/The Surat company is India's second-largest PET recycler by installed capacity at 180,360 tonnes; FY26 revenue rose to Rs 827.6 crore from Rs 527.3 crore in FY24.
  • 03/SEBI observations typically take 30-75 business days; the company then has 12 months to launch, and may first raise up to Rs 80 crore in a pre-IPO placement.

J B Ecotex Ltd filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI on September 29, 2026. The Surat-based company is planning a Rs 400 crore fresh issue alongside an Offer for Sale of up to 1.29 crore equity shares.

The OFS consists of up to 12,950,000 equity shares being sold by the promoters. The equity shares carry a face value of Rs 5 each.

CONTEXT: J B Ecotex is a leading Indian PET recycling and sustainable manufacturing company. Per a 1Lattice report cited in the DRHP, it is the second-largest PET recycling company in India by installed capacity of 180,360 tonnes as of August 31, 2026.

The company recycles PET bottles and other PET waste into rPET flakes, recycled polyester staple fibre, and food-grade rPET resins. It operates two manufacturing facilities at Dhamdod in Surat.

Its products feed the textiles, food-grade packaging, and general packaging markets. The Surat base places it inside Gujarat's dense textile and recycling cluster.

The company is the only one in India with both mechanical and chemical PET waste recycling capabilities. It is also one of only two companies in the country with commercial-scale chemical recycling capability.

Revenue from operations rose to Rs 827.6 crore in FY26, from Rs 527.3 crore in FY24. Net profit increased to Rs 22.4 crore from Rs 21.1 crore over the same period.

Margins remain thin. Net profit of Rs 22.4 crore on Rs 827.6 crore of revenue implies a net margin of roughly 2.7%, a risk factor investors will weigh against the growth story.

The company recycled over 3,528.44 million plastic bottles in fiscal 2026. It runs captive wind power of 7.20 MW across three windmills and captive solar capacity of 6.60 MW.

In FY26, 63.52% of its total power consumption came from captive generation and contracted renewable sources. That supports its zero-water-discharge positioning and lowers energy cost volatility.

Net proceeds from the fresh issue are earmarked for loan repayment and growth. Per the DRHP, Rs 320 crore will go toward repayment or pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings of the company and its material subsidiary, plus general corporate purposes.

The company may consider a pre-IPO placement of up to Rs 80 crore with book-running lead managers. If completed, that amount will be reduced from the fresh issue.

The filing lands in the middle of an IPO filing flood. In the September 21-26 week alone, 14 companies filed preliminary IPO papers with SEBI, including Anmol Industries, J Infratech, Deon Energy, Arete 22, Maharashtra Oil Extractions, and Mount Everest Breweries.

J B Ecotex filed on the standard DRHP route rather than any expedited path. Prior to the IPO, the company had raised about $4.78 million across two private funding rounds, per Tracxn, with the first round on March 20, 2025 and an earlier seed round.

The offer will be made through the book-building process. Not more than 50% of the net offer is reserved for qualified institutional buyers, with not less than 15% for non-institutional bidders and 35% for retail individual bidders.

SEBI typically issues its observations within 30-75 business days for mainboard IPOs. Once observations are received, the company has a 12-month window to launch the issue.

IMPACT: A listing would give public-market investors a rare pure-play exposure to India's recycled-PET value chain. Demand tailwinds include plastic-waste management rules, EPR mandates, and brand sustainability commitments that force packaged-goods makers to buy recycled content.

The flip side is execution risk. The business depends on PET waste collection economics, crude-linked input price swings, and thin margins, all of which can pressure earnings between pricing cycles.

Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors and JM Financial are the book-running lead managers. MUFG Intime India is the registrar, and the shares are proposed to be listed on both the NSE and BSE.
