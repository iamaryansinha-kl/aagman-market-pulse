Adani Total Gas Q4 Profit Rises 8.4% to ₹168 Cr, ₹0.25 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ATGL Q4 FY26 net profit rises 8.4% to ₹168 crore; revenue up 17% to ₹1,695 crore
  • 02/Board declares ₹0.25 per share dividend for FY26; record date fixed at June 12, 2026
  • 03/Full-year profit flat at ₹656 crore despite volume growth; stock trades near ₹635

Adani Total Gas Ltd (NSE: ATGL) reported an 8.4% rise in Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit. Profit came in at ₹168 crore against the year-ago quarter. The board declared a dividend of ₹0.25 per share.

Revenue from operations grew 17% year-on-year to ₹1,695 crore in the March quarter. The company maintained gas supply despite elevated procurement costs and currency volatility.

For the full year FY26, net profit rose marginally by 0.3% to ₹656 crore. Revenue increased 18% to ₹6,408 crore. Volume growth was offset by higher input costs.

The ₹0.25 per share dividend is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM. The record date is June 12, 2026. Payout will occur on or after June 26 if approved.

"Despite geopolitical disruptions from West Asia, elevated LNG prices, and currency volatility, our nimble sourcing strategy ensured uninterrupted gas supply," said Suresh P Manglani, CEO & ED, ATGL.

Combined CNG and PNG volumes grew 15% to 433 mmscm in FY26. The company expanded to 1,169 CNG stations and added 49 new outlets during the year.

PNG home connections crossed 13.1 lakh, serving over 5 million consumers daily. Industrial and commercial connections rose to 11,529. Pipeline infrastructure reached 28,005 inch-km.

ATGL shares closed near ₹635 on April 27, 2026. The stock trades 20% below its 52-week high of ₹798. The company commands a market capitalisation of roughly ₹64,500 crore.
