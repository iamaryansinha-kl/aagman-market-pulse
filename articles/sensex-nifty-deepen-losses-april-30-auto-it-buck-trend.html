Sensex, Nifty Deepen Losses; Auto and IT Buck the Trend on April 30

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex closed 583 pts lower at 76,914 and Nifty 50 dropped 180 pts to 23,998 on April 30, 2026
  • 02/Only the Nifty IT index gained 0.37% while all other sectoral indices ended in the red
  • 03/Crude oil near $109/barrel and rupee at ₹94.85 weighed on sentiment; markets closed Friday for Maharashtra Day

Indian benchmark indices ended lower on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The BSE Sensex fell 582.86 points or 0.75% to settle at 76,913.50. The NSE Nifty 50 declined 180.10 points or 0.74% to close at 23,997.55.

The selloff deepened through the afternoon session. At 1 PM, the Sensex was down 729.85 points at 76,766.51. The Nifty had shed 237.25 points to 23,940.40.

Market breadth turned sharply negative. Of 3,369 stocks traded on the NSE, only 1,295 advanced while 1,976 declined. Just 82 stocks hit 52-week highs against 30 at fresh lows.

Crude oil prices and rupee weakness drove the bearish mood. US crude is trading near $109 per barrel, well above the $105 mark. The rupee is hovering near ₹94.85 against the dollar, approaching the critical ₹95 level.

Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, warned of continued pressure. "Volatility expected to stay elevated and downside risks prevailing unless geopolitical tensions ease," he said. Supply disruption fears tied to the Strait of Hormuz blockade remain a key overhang.

Auto and IT stocks provided the only bright spots. Bajaj Auto led Nifty gainers, rising 3.22% to ₹9,851.00 from ₹9,543.50 on volumes of over 7.48 lakh shares. Bajaj Finance added 1.99% and Infosys climbed 1.33%.

The losers were led by Tata Motors, which fell 3.96% to ₹338.75. Hindustan Unilever slid 2.64% to ₹2,253.20 despite reporting quarterly numbers. Shriram Finance and Axis Bank also declined over 2% each.

Technically, the Nifty's breach of the 24,000 psychological level signals near-term fragility. Analysts place immediate support at 23,800. With markets closed Friday for Maharashtra Day, traders are unlikely to take aggressive positions.
