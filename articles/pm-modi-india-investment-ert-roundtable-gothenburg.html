PM Modi Pitches India Investment Vision to Europe CEOs at ERT Roundtable

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PM Narendra Modi addressed the European Round Table for Industry in Gothenburg, Sweden, urging top European CEOs to deepen investments across 10 strategic sectors
  • 02/The India-EU Free Trade Agreement concluded in January 2026 was described by Ursula von der Leyen as the "mother of all deals"
  • 03/Modi proposed an annual India-Europe CEOs Round-table and a dedicated India Desk at ERT to institutionalise engagement

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the European Round Table for Industry (ERT) in Gothenburg, Sweden on Sunday. The roundtable was hosted by the Volvo Group.

Modi urged Swedish and European companies to enhance presence in manufacturing, green hydrogen, clean energy, telecom, and digital infrastructure. He highlighted five broad collaboration areas for European investors.

Electronics, deep tech manufacturing, artificial intelligence, infrastructure, mobility, urban transformation, healthcare, and life sciences were among the sectors pitched. Modi stressed co-creating the next wave of technology innovation in India.

"In the last 12 years, India has been working on the basic mantra of reforms, performance, and transformation. And with the government's political will, this Reform Express is moving forward at full speed," Modi said at the event.

He welcomed the India-EU Free Trade Agreement concluded in January 2026. He called it a transformative partnership for trade, technology, manufacturing, services, and resilient supply chains.

Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission, had described the FTA as the "mother of all deals." Sweden PM Ulf Kristersson and Crown Princess Victoria also attended the roundtable.

Modi proposed holding the India-Europe CEOs Round-table annually. He also suggested creating a dedicated India Desk at ERT to strengthen institutional engagement.

He reiterated India's vision of "Design for India, Make in India and Export from India." India represents one of the world's most attractive destinations for investment, innovation, and manufacturing, he underlined.
