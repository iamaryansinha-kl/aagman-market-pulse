SIS Group Targets ₹20,000 Crore Revenue in FY27, Eyes 15% Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SIS Group aims for ₹20,000 crore revenue in FY27 after closing FY26 at an all-time high of ₹16,000 crore
  • 02/Company clocks roughly ₹1,500 crore monthly revenue with ₹850 crore from India and the rest from international operations
  • 03/Group plans SIS Prosegur IPO in FY27; DRHP already filed and under consideration

SIS Ltd has set a revenue target of ₹20,000 crore for the financial year ending March 2027. Group Managing Director Rituraj Sinha confirmed the guidance in an interview on May 3, 2026.

The company closed FY26 with an all-time high revenue of approximately ₹16,000 crore. SIS clocked roughly ₹1,500 crore in monthly revenue as of March 2026.

The group is focused on two key metrics: 15 per cent growth and 15 per cent return on capital. Sinha said the company has maintained this pace since its listing in 2018.

Out of the monthly ₹1,500 crore, approximately ₹850 crore comes from India. The remaining comes from international operations. The Indian business is growing at over 15 per cent annually.

SIS is nearly double the size of its nearest competitor in India’s security solutions market. It is also the largest player in the Indian facility management segment.

The company is prioritising a shift from pure manpower services to technology-led solutions. This includes AI-powered CCTV monitoring, robot dogs, drones, and robotic cleaning devices.

Sinha described this approach as "ManTech" — combining artificial intelligence with human intelligence. He stressed that technology is value-additive, not a replacement for the existing 3.5 lakh workforce.

The international business has grown at an average of 10 to 11 per cent annually over the past eight years. Sinha expects this trajectory to continue in FY27.

SIS is also planning the IPO of its cash logistics joint venture SIS Prosegur in FY27. The DRHP has been filed and is currently under consideration. The listing was delayed due to unfavourable market conditions linked to the Gulf situation.
