Hindustan Zinc Secures Tungsten, Potash, Rare Earth Mineral Blocks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindustan Zinc Ltd has secured four critical mineral blocks covering tungsten, potash, rare earth elements and halite through government auctions
  • 02/The company won a 1,841-hectare potash and halite block in Rajasthan's Hanumangarh district with a 3.05% bid under Tranche V auction
  • 03/Chairperson Priya Agarwal Hebbar says HZL is evolving from a zinc-silver company into a "multi-metal, future-ready enterprise" aligned with India's resource security goals

Hindustan Zinc Ltd (NSE: HINDZINC) is pivoting aggressively toward critical mineral production. The Vedanta Group company has secured mineral auction blocks for tungsten, potash, rare earth elements and halite.

The company was declared the successful bidder for the Jhandawali-Satipura amalgamated potash and halite block in Rajasthan. The block spans 1,841.22 hectares in Hanumangarh district and is classified at the G3 exploration level.

Hindustan Zinc secured the Rajasthan block with a highest final price offer of 3.05%. The tender was issued on January 20, 2025, under the Ministry of Mines' Tranche V critical mineral auction.

This follows earlier wins including a tungsten and associated minerals block in Andhra Pradesh and a rare earth elements block in Uttar Pradesh. Together, these four blocks signal a deliberate diversification beyond zinc, lead and silver.

"Hindustan Zinc is moving beyond its zinc and silver legacy to become a multi-metal, future-ready enterprise," said Chairperson Priya Agarwal Hebbar. She added that as demand for critical minerals grows across clean energy and digital infrastructure, the company is building capabilities to "responsibly power India's growth."

The push aligns with India's national goal of reducing heavy import dependence for critical minerals. Hebbar noted in her letter to shareholders that "resource security will define the next phase of industrial and economic growth" as global supply chains shift from efficiency to resilience.

Hindustan Zinc reported its best-ever quarterly and annual mined metal production for FY26. Q4 mined metal output hit 315 kt, up 2% year-on-year and 14% quarter-on-quarter. Refined metal production reached a record 282 kt, rising 5% on both yearly and sequential bases.

The company is also investing ₹3,823 crore to build India's first 10 MTPA tailings reprocessing plant. It has set a target to double production capacity to 2 million tonnes, which could lift annual revenue to ₹62,000-65,000 crore.

Shares of Hindustan Zinc closed at ₹562.00 on April 13, 2026, down 0.37% on the BSE. The stock has faced pressure from broader commodity volatility and concerns over West Asia geopolitical tensions.
