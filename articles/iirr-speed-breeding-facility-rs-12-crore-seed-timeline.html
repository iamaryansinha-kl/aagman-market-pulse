IIRR Speed Breeding Facility: Rs 12 Crore to Cut Seed Timeline to 2 Years

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IIRR builds Rs 12 crore Speed Breeding facility with 12 chambers at its Rajendranagar campus near Hyderabad
  • 02/New facility cuts seed variety breeding time from 4-5 years to just 2 years by enabling 4-5 crop cycles per year
  • 03/Foundation stone laid by ICAR DG M.L. Jat; facility to begin operations in 4-5 months and serve as largest in southern India

The Indian Institute of Rice Research has begun work on a Speed Breeding facility at its Rajendranagar campus. The Rs 12-crore project includes 12 advanced breeding chambers.

M.L. Jat, Secretary of DARE and Director General of ICAR, laid the foundation stone on Wednesday. The facility is expected to begin operations in 4-5 months.

Traditional breeding methods require 4-5 years to stabilise lines across 8-10 crop cycles. The new facility will complete the same work in just 2 years.

It can run 4-5 crop cycles in a single calendar year. This is achieved by controlling photoperiod, light spectrum, intensity, temperature, and carbon dioxide levels.

"With seed breeding, we can attain the same number of crop cycles in just 2 years, substantially reducing breeding timelines," said Satendra Mangrauthia, Principal Scientist at IIRR.

Once operational, it will be the largest Speed Breeding facility in southern India. It will cater to researchers from government and private organisations working on rice and other crops.

The facility will strengthen genome editing research in rice. It will also aid rapid development of mapping populations and genetic studies.
