Sensex Surges 897 Points, Nifty Up 1.13% as Earnings Lift Market Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex rallied 897.85 points or 1.17% to 77,784.76, while Nifty 50 jumped 272.30 points or 1.13% to 24,268 by mid-session on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Gains were driven by stock-specific action post Q4 earnings, with 2,014 stocks advancing and 125 hitting 52-week highs on the NSE
  • 03/Rising crude oil prices and US-Iran tensions capped further upside; ITC, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra led the top gainers

Benchmark indices rallied sharply on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The BSE Sensex surged 897.85 points or 1.17% to 77,784.76 by 1:45 PM.

The Nifty 50 gained 272.30 points or 1.13% to 24,268. Both indices opened strong and sustained momentum through the session.

Gains were primarily driven by stock-specific action following quarterly earnings announcements. Investors reacted positively to corporate results across multiple sectors.

However, the upside remained capped amid concerns over rising crude oil prices. Reports suggested the US may extend its blockade of Iranian ports.

ITC, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra, Coal India, and Mahindra & Mahindra led the Nifty 50 gainers. Maruti Suzuki shares rose over 4% following strong Q4 earnings.

IndiGo, Dr Reddy's Laboratories, JSW Steel, and ICICI Bank were among the laggards. The market breadth remained firmly positive overall.

As many as 2,014 stocks advanced out of 3,288 traded on the NSE. Meanwhile, 1,167 stocks declined and 107 remained unchanged.

A total of 125 stocks touched their 52-week highs, while 13 hit 52-week lows. Additionally, 93 stocks were locked in upper circuit limits.

Broader markets also saw buying interest, with smallcap stocks outperforming midcaps. Smallcaps rose around 1%, indicating broad-based participation.

On the sectoral front, realty and FMCG indices led the gains. Both sectors advanced over 2%, while media and consumer durables traded in negative territory.
