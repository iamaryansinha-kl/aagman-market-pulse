Ambuja Cements Q4 Profit Jumps 79% to ₹1,830 Crore; Warns on Costs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ambuja Cements Q4 FY26 net profit surges 78.5% YoY to ₹1,830 crore on record volume of 19.9 million tonnes
  • 02/Revenue rose 10% to ₹10,892 crore but EBITDA margin contracted to 13.4% on fuel and input cost pressures
  • 03/Company flags cost headwinds into H1FY27; expects muted 5% cement demand growth in FY27

Ambuja Cements Ltd (NSE: AMBUJACEM) reported consolidated net profit of ₹1,830 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 78.5% year-on-year jump from ₹1,025 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations rose 10% to ₹10,892 crore, up from ₹9,894 crore last year. The company posted its highest-ever quarterly sales volume of 19.9 million tonnes.

Operating EBITDA stood at ₹1,464 crore with margins at 13.4%. EBITDA per tonne came in at ₹735, reflecting cost pressures from fuel, diesel, and packaging materials.

For the full year FY26, net profit rose 10% to ₹4,728 crore. Revenue grew 19% to ₹40,446 crore, with annual EBITDA of ₹6,539 crore.

"FY26 has been a year of resilience for the cement sector... Ambuja delivered a resilient performance with highest-ever annual volumes," said Vinod Bahety, Whole-time Director and CEO.

The company flagged persistent cost pressures continuing into H1FY27. It expects cement demand growth of around 5% in FY27, citing geopolitical risks and a below-normal monsoon forecast.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹2 per share for FY26, with record date June 12, 2026. Capacity expansion projects will push total capacity to around 119 MTPA by H1FY27.

Ambuja shares traded at ₹450.40, up 1.4% post-results. The stock has fallen 20% over the past year, underperforming broader cement sector indices.
