Rupee Falls 14 Paise to Close at 94.82, Nears All-Time Low Against US Dollar

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The rupee depreciated 14 paise to close at 94.82 against the US dollar on April 29, just 3 paise shy of its all-time low of 94.85
  • 02/Brent crude surged 3.13% to $114.74 per barrel while FIIs sold Rs 2,103.74 crore on Tuesday, keeping sustained pressure on the currency
  • 03/Analysts watch the upcoming US Federal Reserve policy decision and West Asia crisis escalation for near-term rupee direction

The Indian rupee depreciated 14 paise to close at 94.82 (provisional) against the US dollar on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The currency finished just 3 paise above its all-time low closing of 94.85 recorded on March 27.

At the interbank foreign exchange market, the rupee opened at 94.79 against the greenback. It weakened to an intraday low of 94.86 before settling at 94.82 for the session.

The previous day, the rupee had already dropped 53 paise to close at 94.68. The back-to-back declines reflect persistent pressure from multiple fronts.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, surged 3.13% to $114.74 per barrel in futures trade. Forex traders said elevated crude prices near $115 will sharply raise India's import bill, worsening the trade deficit.

The ongoing West Asia crisis and fears of a wider conflict are fuelling investor anxiety. Safe-haven demand is keeping the dollar firm, adding to the rupee's struggles.

Foreign Institutional Investors sold equities worth Rs 2,103.74 crore on Tuesday, according to exchange data. Sustained FII outflows have been a major drag on the rupee throughout 2026.

"The trend remains weak, with the currency consistently facing selling pressure on rebounds, indicating a lack of strong support at higher levels. In the near term, 94.40 is likely to act as resistance, while 95.25 remains the next key support, with the rupee expected to stay volatile and driven by crude and capital flows," said Jateen Trivedi, VP Research Analyst – Commodity and Currency, LKP Securities.

Meanwhile, the dollar index, which gauges the greenback against six major currencies, rose 0.08% to 98.72. Analysts said investors were also awaiting cues from the upcoming US Federal Reserve policy decision.

On the domestic equity front, markets closed higher despite the currency weakness. The Sensex gained 609.45 points to 77,496.36, and the Nifty rose 181.95 points to 24,177.65.

In a parallel development, the United Arab Emirates announced it will leave OPEC effective May 1, adding another layer of uncertainty to global oil markets.
