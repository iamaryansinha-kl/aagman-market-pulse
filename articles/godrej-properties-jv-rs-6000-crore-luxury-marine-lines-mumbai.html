Godrej Properties Signs JV for ₹6,000 Crore Luxury Project at Marine Lines

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godrej Properties will jointly develop a 2.5-acre luxury housing project at Marine Lines, South Mumbai, with an estimated revenue potential of ₹6,000 crore
  • 02/Development rights jointly held by Man Infraconstruction and Shreepati Group have been transferred to Godrej Properties; MICL has a revenue-sharing arrangement
  • 03/Mumbai luxury home sales rose 12% to a record ₹18,512 crore in H1 2026, as Godrej targets ₹39,000 crore in bookings for FY27

Godrej Properties Ltd has entered into a development agreement for a prime 2.5-acre land parcel in Marine Lines, South Mumbai. The company disclosed the deal in a regulatory filing on Monday.

The project is a luxury housing development with an estimated overall revenue potential of around ₹6,000 crore. It marks one of the largest additions to Godrej Properties' Mumbai portfolio this year.

Man Infraconstruction Ltd (MICL) informed exchanges separately that it has partnered with Godrej Properties for the project. The development rights jointly held by MICL and Shreepati Group have now been transferred to Godrej Properties.

MICL has a revenue-sharing arrangement with Godrej Properties. The project will be jointly branded as part of the collaboration.

Manan Shah, Managing Director of MICL Group, said the transaction unlocks the project's full potential. He said early realisation of cash flows will give MICL greater financial flexibility to pursue new opportunities in Mumbai.

The MICL Group will recoup its existing investment in the project to the tune of ₹300 crore, Shah said.

Gaurav Pandey, MD & CEO of Godrej Properties, said Mumbai continues to be a key market. He noted that the company's premium and luxury projects in the city have seen a consistently strong response.

The backdrop is a booming South Mumbai luxury market. Sales of luxury homes priced at ₹10 crore and above rose 12% to a record ₹18,512 crore in the first half of 2026, according to India Sotheby's International Realty and CRE Matrix.

Godrej Properties is targeting sales bookings of ₹39,000 crore for the current fiscal. It sold properties worth ₹34,171 crore in FY2025-26, making it the largest listed realty firm by sales bookings.
