LIC Hikes Stake in Central Bank of India to 6.06% via Market Purchase

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Life Insurance Corporation of India has raised its stake in Central Bank of India to 6.06%
  • 02/The additional acquisition was made through open market purchases, crossing the 5% threshold
  • 03/LIC has been steadily increasing holdings in public sector banks as part of its equity strategy

Life Insurance Corporation of India has increased its shareholding in Central Bank of India to 6.06%. The acquisition was executed through open market transactions.

The additional purchase takes LIC's holding in the public sector bank past the 6% mark. This represents a significant position in the lender's equity base.

Central Bank of India is a Mumbai-headquartered public sector bank. It is listed on both the NSE (CENTRALBK) and the BSE.

LIC holds substantial stakes across India's banking sector. Its portfolio includes major positions in several public and private sector lenders.

The insurer's investment approach focuses on long-term value creation. It typically accumulates shares in financially sound companies with stable earnings profiles.

Central Bank of India has been working on improving asset quality and profitability. The lender's stock performance reflects ongoing operational turnaround efforts.

LIC's increased stake signals institutional confidence in the bank's direction. Large insurer participation often attracts attention from other institutional investors.
