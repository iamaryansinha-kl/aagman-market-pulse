UPI April 2026: 22.35 Bn Txns Worth ₹29.03 Lakh Cr; Daily Volumes Hit Record

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/UPI processed 22.35 billion transactions worth ₹29.03 lakh crore in April 2026, down marginally from March's all-time high
  • 02/Daily average volumes rose to a record 745 million per day, up from 730 million in March, signalling sustained underlying demand
  • 03/PhonePe and Google Pay dominate with ~45% and ~33% market share respectively; industry eyes fraud detection and cross-border UPI as next growth frontier

UPI processed 22.35 billion transactions worth ₹29.03 lakh crore in April 2026. The numbers dipped marginally from March's record peak of 22.64 billion transactions worth ₹29.53 lakh crore.

The month-on-month decline was expected. April has 30 days versus 31 in March, and follows the typical financial year-end surge seen in March.

Year-on-year growth stayed robust at 25% in volume and 21% in value. This signals continued expansion of India's digital payments ecosystem.

Daily run rates improved meaningfully. UPI averaged 745 million transactions per day in April, up from 730 million in March. Average daily transaction value stood at ₹96,766 crore.

"The slight dip is more a function of March's surge. What stands out is the increase in daily volumes, which shows underlying demand is still rising. We're also seeing higher-value transactions grow faster, indicating that users are increasingly relying on UPI for more meaningful spends," said Aakash Sinha, Co-founder and CEO of Cashfree Payments.

The market remains dominated by two players. PhonePe crossed 10 billion transactions in March with roughly 45% market share. Google Pay processed about 7.5 billion transactions, holding around one-third of the market. Paytm trailed with approximately 1.7 billion transactions.

February 2026 had seen 20.39 billion transactions worth ₹26.84 lakh crore. The March quarter showed a sharp jump before the slight April moderation.

Industry executives view the April numbers as normalisation, not slowdown. Updated authentication frameworks and clearer recurring payment guidelines are expected to improve success rates and drive adoption in smaller towns.

WHAT WAS THE DAILY AVERAGE VOLUME OF UPI TRANSACTIONS IN APRIL 2026?
UPI RECORDED AN AVERAGE OF 745 MILLION TRANSACTIONS PER DAY IN APRIL 2026, UP FROM 730 MILLION IN MARCH 2026. THIS IS A RECORD HIGH FOR DAILY RUN RATES.

ON A YEAR-ON-YEAR BASIS, UPI TRANSACTION VOLUMES ROSE 25 PER CENT AND TRANSACTION VALUE INCREASED 21 PER CENT IN APRIL 2026.

PHONEPE AND GOOGLE PAY DOMINATE THE UPI ECOSYSTEM. PHONEPE CROSSED 10 BILLION TRANSACTIONS IN MARCH 2026 WITH AROUND 45 PER CENT MARKET SHARE, WHILE GOOGLE PAY PROCESSED ABOUT 7.5 BILLION TRANSACTIONS ACCOUNTING FOR ROUGHLY ONE-THIRD OF THE MARKET. PAYTM TRAILED WITH ABOUT 1.7 BILLION TRANSACTIONS.
WHY DID UPI TRANSACTIONS DIP IN APRIL 2026 COMPARED TO MARCH?
THE SEQUENTIAL DECLINE FROM MARCH'S ALL-TIME HIGH WAS EXPECTED DUE TO TWO FACTORS: APRIL HAS 30 DAYS COMPARED TO 31 IN MARCH, AND MARCH TYPICALLY SEES A FINANCIAL YEAR-END SURGE. INDUSTRY EXECUTIVES DESCRIBE THE APRIL DIP AS NORMALISATION RATHER THAN A SLOWDOWN IN ADOPTION, NOTING THAT RECORD DAILY VOLUMES INDICATE UNDERLYING DEMAND CONTINUES TO RISE.
