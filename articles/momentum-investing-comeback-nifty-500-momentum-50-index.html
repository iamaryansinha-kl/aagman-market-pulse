Momentum Investing Surges 17.29% in Q1 FY2027, Beats Value and Quality

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 500 Momentum 50 index surged 17.29% in Q1 FY2027, outperforming all other factor investing styles
  • 02/The comeback follows 15 months of weak returns and a 7.5% decline in 2025 driven by tariff shocks
  • 03/Fund managers warn momentum can see sharp drawdowns during trend reversals and is best suited for experienced investors

Momentum investing has staged a sharp comeback on the Indian stock market. The Nifty 500 Momentum 50 index surged 17.29% in the April-June quarter, beating all other factor styles.

The strategy had delivered tepid returns for 15 months before this rebound. In 2025, the index had declined 7.5%, hurt by tariff shocks and geopolitical conflicts.

In comparison, the Nifty 500 Quality index rose 16.07% in the same quarter. The Value index gained 7.89%, while Low Volatility returned 5.52%.

"Momentum is a factor that typically performs well during bull markets or when markets exhibit a strong and persistent trend," said Siddharth Srivastava, head of ETF products at Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India). "Apart from large caps and certain segments like IT and FMCG, others have done relatively better and that is what momentum has been able to capture."

The strategy had delivered stellar returns of 47.7% in 2023 and 27.2% in 2024. However, sudden volatility spikes in 2025 hurt performance, said Rohit Tandon, senior fund manager at Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund.

"Momentum does well when market volatility increases gradually from a low base. It also outperforms during periods where fear is high, leading to high volatility," Tandon said. "During 2025, momentum underperformed as markets were affected by tariffs and geopolitical conflicts. This led to sudden and sharp volatility jumps rather than a gradual increase."

Over a three-year rolling period, momentum has outpaced the benchmark by 7.8%. This was the highest among all factors, according to Viraj Gandhi, chief executive at Samco Mutual Fund.

Value outperformed by 5.96% over three years, while quality and low volatility delivered 3.65% and 3.09% respectively. The outperformance persisted over five years as well, with momentum beating the benchmark by 7.72%.

The strategy is not without risks. On June 4, 2024, when Lok Sabha election results triggered a market crash, the Nifty 50 fell 5.93%. The Momentum 50 index fell a steeper 10.82%.

"Momentum funds can see sharp drawdowns when trends reverse or markets turn sideways," said Abhishek Kumar, founder of SahajMoney. "These products are best suited for experienced investors with high risk tolerance and should ideally be part of a satellite portfolio rather than the core portfolio."

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS MOMENTUM INVESTING AND HOW DID IT PERFORM IN Q1 FY2027?
MOMENTUM INVESTING IS A STRATEGY THAT FOCUSES ON STOCKS SHOWING STRONG UPWARD PRICE TRENDS. IN THE APRIL-JUNE QUARTER, THE NIFTY 500 MOMENTUM 50 INDEX SURGED 17.29%, OUTPACING QUALITY (16.07%), VALUE (7.89%), AND LOW VOLATILITY (5.52%).
WHY DID MOMENTUM UNDERPERFORM IN 2025?
MOMENTUM UNDERPERFORMED IN 2025 WITH THE INDEX DECLINING 7.5%. ACCORDING TO ROHIT TANDON OF KOTAK MAHINDRA MUTUAL FUND, THE STRATEGY WAS HURT BY SUDDEN AND SHARP VOLATILITY JUMPS DRIVEN BY TARIFFS AND GEOPOLITICAL CONFLICTS, RATHER THAN A GRADUAL INCREASE IN VOLATILITY FROM A LOW BASE.
HOW DOES MOMENTUM COMPARE TO OTHER FACTOR STRATEGIES OVER THE LONG TERM?
OVER A THREE-YEAR ROLLING PERIOD, MOMENTUM OUTPACED THE BENCHMARK BY 7.8% — THE HIGHEST AMONG ALL FACTORS. VALUE OUTPERFORMED BY 5.96%, QUALITY BY 3.65%, AND LOW VOLATILITY BY 3.09%. OVER FIVE YEARS, MOMENTUM BEAT THE BENCHMARK BY 7.72%.
WHAT ARE THE RISKS OF MOMENTUM INVESTING?
MOMENTUM FUNDS CAN SEE SHARP DRAWDOWNS WHEN TRENDS REVERSE OR MARKETS TURN SIDEWAYS. FOR EXAMPLE, ON JUNE 4, 2024, WHEN THE NIFTY 50 FELL 5.93%, THE MOMENTUM 50 INDEX FELL 10.82%. INVESTORS NEED HIGH RISK TOLERANCE AND A LONG-TERM HORIZON.
SHOULD MOMENTUM INVESTING BE PART OF A CORE OR SATELLITE PORTFOLIO?
ACCORDING TO SEBI-REGISTERED INVESTMENT ADVISER ABHISHEK KUMAR, MOMENTUM PRODUCTS ARE BEST SUITED FOR EXPERIENCED INVESTORS WITH HIGH RISK TOLERANCE. THEY SHOULD IDEALLY BE PART OF A SATELLITE PORTFOLIO RATHER THAN THE CORE PORTFOLIO.
