Tech Mahindra Q4 Preview: Margins To Expand 50-70 Bps Despite Flat Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tech Mahindra's Q4 FY26 EBIT margin is expected to expand 50-70 bps sequentially to around 13.6%, driven by Project Fortius cost savings
  • 02/Revenue is seen as flattish QoQ, though Axis Securities projects 3.5% sequential growth from BFSI and manufacturing verticals
  • 03/The company announces Q4 FY26 results on April 22, 2026, with investor focus on FY27 margin guidance and deal pipeline momentum

Tech Mahindra Ltd (NSE: TECHM) will announce its Q4 FY26 results on April 22, 2026. The board meets on Wednesday to approve audited financials for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Brokerages expect EBIT margin expansion of 50-70 basis points sequentially. Motilal Oswal Financial Services estimates margin at ~13.6%, up 50 bps from the previous quarter.

Choice Institutional Equities projects a 70 bps margin increase led by the Project Fortius cost efficiency programme. Kotak Institutional Equities estimates 60 bps expansion from operating efficiencies and rupee depreciation.

"Management remains confident of achieving its FY27 EBIT margin target of 15 per cent, supported mainly by gross margin expansion," Choice Institutional Equities said in its research note.

Revenue growth is expected to remain muted. Axis Securities estimates 3.5 per cent sequential revenue growth, driven by BFSI and manufacturing verticals.

Telecom and communication verticals are also expected to grow. Hi-tech and auto segments remain volatile, according to analyst estimates.

Kotak Institutional Equities forecasts $1.1 billion in net new deal wins for Q4. This is flat sequentially but up 38 per cent annually. The Orange deal signed is noted as substantial.

However, Kotak expects muted net profit growth due to a $25 million forex loss. Nuvama Research estimates a lower 30 bps margin expansion but still supports the 15 per cent FY27 margin guidance.

Tech Mahindra shares trade around ₹1,493-₹1,512. The stock has a 52-week high of ₹1,807 and a low of ₹1,161. One-year returns remain negative at approximately -18%.

Analysts will watch management commentary on FY27 revenue guidance. Reinvestment plans to accelerate growth and the company's agentic AI approach are key focus areas.

WHAT IS THE EXPECTED EBIT MARGIN EXPANSION FOR TECH MAHINDRA IN Q4 FY26?
BROKERAGES EXPECT EBIT MARGIN TO EXPAND 50-70 BASIS POINTS SEQUENTIALLY. MOTILAL OSWAL ESTIMATES ~13.6% (UP 50 BPS), CHOICE INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES PROJECTS 70 BPS, AND KOTAK INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES FORECASTS 60 BPS EXPANSION.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE MARGIN IMPROVEMENT AT TECH MAHINDRA?
THE MARGIN IMPROVEMENT IS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY PROJECT FORTIUS, THE COST EFFICIENCY PROGRAMME INITIATED UNDER CEO MOHIT JOSHI. COST OPTIMISATION, FIXED-PRICE PROJECT OPTIMISATION, AND RUPEE DEPRECIATION TAILWINDS ARE ALSO CONTRIBUTING FACTORS.
WHAT IS THE FY27 MARGIN GUIDANCE FOR TECH MAHINDRA?
MANAGEMENT HAS GUIDED FOR A FY27 EBIT MARGIN TARGET OF 15%. ANALYSTS AT CHOICE INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES AND NUAMA RESEARCH HAVE BOTH NOTED THAT THIS TARGET APPEARS ACHIEVABLE BASED ON CURRENT GROSS MARGIN EXPANSION TRENDS.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS FOR TECH MAHINDRA IN Q4 FY26?
KOTAK INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES EXPECTS A $25 MILLION FOREX LOSS THAT COULD MUTE NET PROFIT GROWTH DESPITE MARGIN EXPANSION. REVENUE GROWTH REMAINS FLATTISH, AND HI-TECH AND AUTO VERTICALS CONTINUE TO SHOW VOLATILITY.
