Amber Enterprises Shares Crash 18% After Q4 Results Despite Profit Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Amber Enterprises shares plunged nearly 18% on May 18 following Q4 results
  • 02/Net profit grew 15% YoY to ₹134 crore; revenue rose over 10% to ₹4,148 crore
  • 03/The market sold off sharply despite positive headline numbers, signaling deeper concerns

Amber Enterprises shares crashed almost 18% on Monday, May 18, 2026. The sell-off followed the company's January-March quarter results announcement.

Net profit for Q4 FY2026 rose 15% year-on-year to ₹134 crore. Revenue grew over 10% to ₹4,148 crore.

The market reaction was sharply negative despite the headline growth numbers. The 18% single-day fall signals deeper investor concerns.

Profit growth and revenue expansion typically support positive sentiment. However, the sell-off suggests expectations may have been higher.

Amber Enterprises is a key player in the room air conditioner OEM space. It supplies components to major consumer durable brands.

The sharp decline wiped out significant market value in a single session. Investors will watch for management commentary.

The disconnect between results and stock performance raises questions. Analysts may revise targets after reviewing detailed financials.
