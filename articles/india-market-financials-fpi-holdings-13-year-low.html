India Market Shift: Financials Lead as FPI Holdings Fall to 13-Year Low

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE Market Pulse report shows Indian markets shifting from commodity-led to financials-dominated, with financials now at 25% of market cap
  • 02/FPI holdings fell to 16.60% by December 2025, a 13-year low, while domestic institutional investors hit a record 18.72%
  • 03/Market concentration is declining in India unlike the US, signaling broader dispersion and structural maturity

The National Stock Exchange's April 2026 Market Pulse report benchmarks global markets and reveals structural shifts in Indian equity markets. India's market has moved from commodity-led to financials-dominated over three decades.

In 1995, materials commanded 25% of total market capitalisation, followed by energy at 18%. By 2025, financials emerged as the leading sector with 25% share.

Industrials and consumer discretionary now hold 13% and 12% respectively. Information technology has risen to 8% from just 1% in 1995, though it peaked at 16% in 2000.

The NSE study notes rising concentration in US markets, while India has moved in the opposite direction. Declining concentration indicates broader dispersion across firms and sectors.

Foreign portfolio investor holdings peaked at 25.72% in March 2023. They fell sharply to a 13-year low of 16.60% as of December 31, 2025, according to Prime Database.

Domestic mutual fund holdings touched a record 11.10%. Domestic institutional investors including discretionary mutual funds hit a new peak of 18.72%.

FPI share fell to 15.9% in consumer discretionary, a 14-quarter low. Financials witnessed a near 21-year low of 23.4% foreign ownership, though FPIs remain the largest non-promoter shareholders.

Communication services recorded a 25-quarter high of 23.7% FPI share. Energy saw a five-quarter high of 16.7%. The trend signals a maturing, more balanced market structure.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW HAS THE SECTORAL COMPOSITION OF INDIAN MARKETS CHANGED OVER THREE DECADES?
INDIAN MARKETS HAVE SHIFTED FROM A COMMODITY-LED STRUCTURE TO ONE DOMINATED BY FINANCIALS. IN 1995, MATERIALS HELD 25% AND ENERGY 18% OF MARKET CAPITALISATION. BY 2025, FINANCIALS ACCOUNTED FOR 25%, FOLLOWED BY INDUSTRIALS AT 13% AND CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY AT 12%.
WHAT HAPPENED TO FPI HOLDINGS IN INDIAN MARKETS?
FOREIGN PORTFOLIO INVESTOR HOLDINGS PEAKED AT 25.72% IN MARCH 2023 AND FELL TO A 13-YEAR LOW OF 16.60% AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2025, ACCORDING TO PRIME DATABASE.
HOW HAVE DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL INVESTOR HOLDINGS TRENDED?
DOMESTIC MUTUAL FUND HOLDINGS TOUCHED A RECORD 11.10%. DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS, INCLUDING DISCRETIONARY MUTUAL FUNDS, HIT A NEW PEAK OF 18.72% AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2025.
WHICH SECTORS SAW THE SHARPEST DECLINE IN FPI OWNERSHIP?
CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY AND FINANCIALS WITNESSED THE SHARPEST DECLINES. FPI SHARE IN CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY FELL TO 15.9%, A 14-QUARTER LOW. FINANCIALS FELL TO 23.4%, NEAR A 21-YEAR LOW.
WHAT DOES DECLINING MARKET CONCENTRATION MEAN FOR INDIAN EQUITY MARKETS?
DECLINING CONCENTRATION INDICATES BROADER DISPERSION ACROSS FIRMS AND SECTORS, SIGNALING MARKET MATURITY. A MORE BALANCED PRESENCE OF FPIS AND DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS IS LIKELY TO HELP CUSHION VOLATILITY.
