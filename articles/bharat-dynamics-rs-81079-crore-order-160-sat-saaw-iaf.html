Bharat Dynamics Bags Rs 810.79 Crore Defence Ministry Order for 160 SAT-SAAW Weapons

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Shares of Bharat Dynamics gained 2% to Rs 1,181 on the BSE on Thursday after the Ministry of Defence awarded the company an Rs 810.79 crore contract for 160 Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapons (SAT-SAAW) along with associated equipment for the Indian Air Force.

The gains came even as the broader market declined up to 1%.

What Is SAT-SAAW?

SAT-SAAW is an air-to-ground precision-guided glide bomb that can be launched from platforms including the Jaguar, Hawk and Su-30 MKI fighter jets. The weapon is designed to strike enemy airfields from a long stand-off range.

It has been designed and developed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and will have 60% indigenous content. The programme also includes the indigenisation of key sub-systems such as the Inertial Measurement Unit, Long Impact Delay Fuse and Twin Store Carrier.

Deliveries are scheduled for 2027-28 and 2028-29. The Defence Ministry said the induction of SAT-SAAW will strengthen the IAF's operational capabilities and bolster India's defence preparedness.

Order Comes Amid Defence Spending Push

The order comes just days after the Defence Acquisition Council, chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, approved acquisition proposals worth around Rs 1.10 lakh crore, with around 98% set to be sourced from Indian industry.

India's defence budget has surged from Rs 2.53 trillion in FY14 to Rs 6.81 trillion in FY26. India's domestic defence opportunity could exceed $60 billion over the next four years, with domestic defence capital spending expected to grow at a 16% CAGR between FY26 and FY30 — significantly ahead of the 10% growth expected in overall defence capex.

More than $120 billion of acquisition proposals cleared during FY25-26 provide visibility for medium-term order flows, international brokerage Jefferies said. Private companies are making inroads too — the revenue share of private players among key listed defence companies rose from 9% in fiscal 2023 to 16% in fiscal 2026.
