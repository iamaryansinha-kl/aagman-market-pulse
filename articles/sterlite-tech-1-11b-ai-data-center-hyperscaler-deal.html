Sterlite Tech Secures $1.11B AI Data Center Deal From Hyperscaler

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sterlite Technologies subsidiary receives $1.11 billion Product Award Letter from global hyperscaler for AI data center optical connectivity products
  • 02/Multi-year contract spans FY27 to FY29 with execution through March 2029; includes reciprocal risk-sharing framework with capped liabilities
  • 03/Stock surged 5% to ₹463.20 on May 25; up 175% in past 3 months with RSI at 81.2 indicating overbought conditions

Sterlite Technologies Ltd (NSE: STLTECH) announced its subsidiary has received a Product Award Letter from a global hyperscaler. The multi-year contract is valued at approximately $1.11 billion.

The agreement covers supply of optical connectivity products for AI-ready data center infrastructure in the United States. Execution runs from FY27 through March 2029.

Purchase orders will be released periodically during the contract period. Both parties operate under a reciprocal risk-sharing framework that caps financial liabilities for supply shortages or demand shortfalls.

The company clarified that the deal involves no related-party transactions. Neither the promoter group nor associated companies have any interest in the awarding entity.

"Under this agreement, STL, through its optical solutions, will support building AI data center infrastructure in the US for this hyperscaler," said Ankit Agarwal, Managing Director of STL. "We are enabling the connectivity backbone for AI data centers."

STL recently launched its Neuralis AI Data Center portfolio. The offering includes ultra-high-density pre-terminated fiber cables for GPU clusters and the Celesta IBR cable series supporting up to 6,912 fibers.

Shares of Sterlite Technologies closed at ₹463.20 on May 25, up nearly 5%. The stock has rallied over 175% in the past three months and commands a market capitalization of approximately ₹20,522 crore.

The 14-day RSI stands at 81.2, indicating strongly overbought conditions. The stock trades above all eight key simple moving averages, signaling sustained bullish momentum despite the possibility of a near-term pullback.
