NSE Files for IPO: ₹30,000 Crore Issue to List on BSE, Targets January 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE filed listing papers with SEBI on June 17, targeting a ₹30,000 crore IPO on BSE before January 30, 2027
  • 02/NSE is valued at ₹5 lakh crore unlisted but trades at 38x-43x FY26 earnings, a discount to BSE's 69x multiple
  • 03/NSE FY26 profit fell 16% to ₹10,302 crore on regulatory tightening in derivatives, creating an unusual valuation setup

NSE filed its listing papers with SEBI on June 17, 2026. The exchange targets a listing before January 30, 2027. Early estimates place the issue size at approximately ₹30,000 crore.

NSE shares will list exclusively on BSE. Indian securities law bars an exchange from listing on its own platform. This gives BSE one of its most consequential listings.

NSE commands 88% of India's cash-market turnover and 91% of equity futures and options. Globally, it ranks first in equity derivatives with a 51% market share.

NSE's total operational revenue declined 3% year-on-year to ₹16,601 crore in FY26. Profit fell 16% to ₹10,302 crore from ₹12,188 crore in FY25. The exchange attributed the decline to lower transaction charges and Sebi's tightening of the futures and options segment.

NSE is cited at 38x to 43x FY26 price-to-earnings on a price band of ₹1,600 to ₹1,800 per share. BSE currently trades at 69x FY26 earnings. The dominant exchange may come to market at a discount to its smaller rival.

"Listing of any eligible institution is a positive development for India's capital markets," said BSE managing director Sundararaman Ramamurthy. "Valuations and stock prices are outcomes of a company's growth prospects and execution, and are best left to the market."

Pankaj Murarka, CIO at Renaissance Investment Managers, said both exchanges can sustain strong multiples due to their oligopolistic structure. "Markets can go up or down, but in casino parlance, the house never loses. Exchanges are the house," he noted.

BSE's revenue grew from ₹928 crore in FY2022 to ₹5,226 crore in FY26, a 41% CAGR. Its market cap surged from ₹5,742 crore to ₹1.58 lakh crore over the same period.
