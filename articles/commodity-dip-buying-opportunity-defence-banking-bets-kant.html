Commodity Dip a Buying Opportunity; Defence, Banking Top Bets: Kant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dharmesh Kant of Cholamandalam Securities says commodity corrections are buying opportunities, with 1 to 1.5 years of upcycle left
  • 02/He recommends Hindalco, Vedanta, JSW Steel in commodities; BEL, HAL, Mazagon Dock in defence; prefers banking over auto
  • 03/Silver demand to compound at 15-17% CAGR; credit growth at 17-18%; lower crude to boost corporate margins in Q2-Q3

Dharmesh Kant of Cholamandalam Securities believes the recent commodity correction is a buying opportunity. Speaking to ET Now on June 25, he said the broader commodity cycle remains intact.

Copper, aluminium, crude oil and silver have all fallen sharply in recent sessions. Kant sees such pullbacks as normal within long-term commodity cycles.

"We have already seen a major part of the upcycle, and normally it corrects and consolidates," Kant said. He expects at least one to one-and-a-half years of upcycle remaining.

Kant recommends Hindalco, Vedanta and JSW Steel for commodity exposure. He noted silver demand is structurally supported by electric vehicles, electronics and solar panels.

Silver demand is likely to compound at 15-17% CAGR going forward, Kant said. He expects lower crude prices to boost corporate profitability in Q2 and Q3.

On defence, Kant favours Bharat Electronics (NSE: BEL), Hindustan Aeronautics (NSE: HAL) and Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders. He called them "no-brainer" long-term plays over three years.

Kant remains cautious on auto and ancillaries due to a high base effect. He prefers banking as the strongest indirect beneficiary of lower energy prices.

India's credit growth will be around 17-18%, Kant said. Ground checks show no let-up in consumption, credit demand or collections.
