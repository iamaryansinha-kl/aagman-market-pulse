Q4 Results Today Live: HCL Tech, Nestle, Persistent, Tata Elxsi

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HCL Tech, Nestle India, Persistent Systems and Tata Elxsi announce Q4 FY26 results on April 21, 2026
  • 02/HCL Tech PAT estimated at Rs 4,648 crore; Nestle at Rs 926 crore; Persistent at Rs 521 crore; Tata Elxsi at Rs 188 crore
  • 03/Combined revenue estimate crosses Rs 41,000 crore; margins under scrutiny amid wage hike and input cost pressures

HCL Technologies Ltd (NSE: HCLTECH), Nestle India Ltd (NSE: NESTLEIND), Persistent Systems Ltd (NSE: PERSISTENT) and Tata Elxsi Ltd (NSE: TATAELXSI) announce Q4 FY26 results on April 21, 2026.

Analysts estimate HCL Tech revenue at Rs 34,024 crore with EBIT of Rs 5,977 crore. PAT is expected at Rs 4,648 crore, up 15% year-on-year.

Nestle India revenue is seen at Rs 6,186 crore, a 12% rise from the year-ago period. EBITDA is estimated at Rs 1,498 crore with a margin of 24.21%.

Persistent Systems is expected to report revenue of Rs 3,980 crore. EBIT is seen at Rs 661 crore with a margin of 16.60%, translating to PAT of Rs 521 crore.

Tata Elxsi revenue is estimated at Rs 991.96 crore with EBIT of Rs 213 crore. Net profit is projected at Rs 187.73 crore with a margin of 21.47%.

Combined revenue estimate for all four companies crosses Rs 41,000 crore. Margins remain under scrutiny amid wage hike and input cost pressures across IT and FMCG sectors.

HCL Tech and Tata Elxsi results come post-market close on April 21. Nestle and Persistent Systems also disclose their quarterly numbers today through stock exchange filings.

The results will set the tone for FY27 guidance. Investors watch HCL Tech's software segment, Nestle's volume growth, Persistent's deal pipeline, and Tata Elxsi's margin recovery.
