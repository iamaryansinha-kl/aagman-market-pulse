Uber Adds Hotel Bookings to App Through New Expedia Partnership

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Uber partners with Expedia to add hotel bookings across 700,000 properties directly in its app, announced at the GO-GET event on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Uber One members get 20% off 10,000 hotels and 10% back in credits; rival DoorDash recently added restaurant reservations via SevenRooms
  • 03/Uber stock trades near $74, down 11% YTD, with market cap at $152 billion; integration with Expedia app begins in June

Uber Technologies Inc (NYSE: UBER) has partnered with Expedia Group to add hotel bookings directly in its app. The feature was unveiled at Uber's annual GO-GET product event on April 29, 2026.

The partnership gives Uber users access to 700,000 hotels and properties globally through Expedia's inventory. More than 1 million Vrbo vacation rentals will be added later this year.

"Uber is becoming an app for everything—helping people go, get, and now travel all in one place," said CEO Dara Khosrowshahi. The company handles over 100 million airport trips annually and 1.5 billion trips outside riders' home cities each year.

Uber One members, who pay $9.99 per month, receive a 20% discount on a rotating list of 10,000 hotels. They also earn 10% back in Uber credits that can be used for ride bookings.

Expedia Group CEO Ariane Gorin called the tie-up "mutually beneficial." Uber rides will be integrated into the Expedia app starting in June, with push notifications for discounted airport transfers.

The hotel push pits Uber against super-app rivals. DoorDash recently added restaurant reservations after acquiring SevenRooms. Uber also launched Travel Mode with OpenTable bookings and "Eats for the Way" in select US cities.

Uber shares closed near $74 on April 29, down 11% year-to-date from a 52-week high of $101.99. The company carries a market capitalization of approximately $152 billion and trades at a P/E ratio of 15.5.

The company reported Q4 2025 revenue of $14.3 billion, up 20.1% year-over-year. Free cash flow hit a record $2.808 billion, rising 64.6% YoY, giving Uber financial muscle to fund its expansion.
