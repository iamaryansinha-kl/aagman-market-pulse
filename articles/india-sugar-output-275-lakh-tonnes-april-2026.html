India Sugar Output Rises 7% to 275.28 Lakh Tonnes by April End

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's sugar production hits 275.28 lakh tonnes by April 30, 2026, up 7% from 256.49 lakh tonnes in the same period last year.
  • 02/Only 5 mills remain operational versus 19 last year; Maharashtra leads with 99.2 lakh tonnes while Uttar Pradesh trails at 89.65 lakh tonnes.
  • 03/ISMA demands early MSP revision as Maharashtra cane arrears surge to ₹2,130 crore from ₹752 crore last season.

India's sugar production for the 2025-26 season reached 275.28 lakh tonnes as of April 30, 2026. This marks a 7 per cent increase over the 256.49 lakh tonnes produced in the same period last year.

Only 5 sugar mills are currently operational across the country. At the same time last year, 19 mills were still running.

Maharashtra has emerged as the top producer with 99.2 lakh tonnes, sharply higher than 80.93 lakh tonnes last year. Karnataka follows with 48.01 lakh tonnes against 40.40 lakh tonnes in the prior season.

Uttar Pradesh output declined to 89.65 lakh tonnes from 92.40 lakh tonnes last year. All factories in the state have closed operations for the season.

The Indian Sugar and Bioenergy Manufacturers Association (ISMA) has renewed its demand for an early revision of the Minimum Selling Price (MSP). Escalating production costs and subdued ex-mill realizations are squeezing mill cash flows.

Cane payment arrears in Maharashtra stood at ₹2,130 crore as of mid-April. This is nearly triple the ₹752 crore recorded on the corresponding date last season.

ISMA also called for advancing ethanol blending beyond E20 towards higher blends such as E25 and E85. The industry urged expedited rollout of flex-fuel vehicles and GST rationalisation to boost adoption.

A few mills in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu will operate during the special season from June and July 2026. Historically, these operations contribute around 5 lakh tonnes of additional sugar.
