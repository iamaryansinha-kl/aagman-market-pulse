Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers Targets ₹5,000 Crore Revenue by FY28

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers targets ₹5,000 crore revenue by FY27-28, nearly doubling from FY26's ₹2,700 crore
  • 02/Company is investing ₹500 crore internally to expand stores in Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli, Hyderabad and a second US outlet
  • 03/Digital gold mobile app launching within two weeks; FY26 growth was 17-20% despite lower volumes from high gold prices

Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers (VBJ) has set a revenue target of ₹5,000 crore by financial year 2027-28. The 125-year-old Chennai-based retailer closed FY26 with revenue of approximately ₹2,700 crore.

The company posted 17-20% growth in FY26 despite lower sales volumes. High gold and silver prices drove value growth even as volume declined.

VBJ is investing ₹500 crore of internal funds to expand its retail footprint. New stores are planned in Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Hyderabad over the coming years.

The company currently operates two stores in Chennai and one in the US. It plans to open a second US outlet targeting the Indian diaspora.

Amarendran Vummidi, Managing Partner, spoke at the launch of a 14,000 sqft flagship store in Chennai. The multi-level showroom has dedicated floors for gold, diamonds, platinum and sapphire.

"The format of the stores depends upon the size of the market," Amarendran said. Chennai and Coimbatore can support large formats while tier 2/3 cities need more flexible sizing.

VBJ is launching a mobile app for digital gold investing within the next two weeks. Jithendra Vummidi, Managing Partner, announced the upcoming digital initiative.

The retailer's business mix is approximately 65% gold, 30% diamonds, and 5% platinum. "Demand in India remains largely resilient due to the strong cultural and investment linkage with gold," Amarendran added.
