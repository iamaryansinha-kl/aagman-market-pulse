Nifty Indices Rejig Live Today: MCX to See $80M Inflow, Bharti Airtel $88M Outflow

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE quarterly index rebalancing takes effect today, June 29, 2026, triggering significant passive fund flows across Indian equities
  • 02/MCX leads inflows at $80M; Bharti Airtel faces biggest outflow at $88M per Nuvama Institutional Equities estimates
  • 03/HDFC Bank and SBI gain weight on Nifty Bank; Axis Bank, Canara Bank see outflows as index weights shift

The National Stock Exchange of India's quarterly index rebalancing goes live today, June 29, 2026. Changes to free-float weights across multiple indices will trigger substantial passive fund flows.

Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) will likely see the largest inflows at $80 million. NTPC follows at $77 million, with Laurus Labs at $73 million and Adani Power at $72 million.

Bharti Airtel faces the biggest cumulative outflow at $88 million. Eicher Motors and Asian Paints follow closely at $69 million each, according to Nuvama Institutional Equities.

In the Nifty 50, Coal India, ICICI Bank, Eternal, HDFC Bank, and Bajaj Finance will see weight increases. These changes could drive inflows worth up to $26 million into the benchmark index.

Bharti Airtel, SBI, L&T, Grasim Industries, and Maruti Suzuki will see their Nifty 50 weights reduced. Maruti Suzuki alone could face outflows of $61 million.

On the Nifty Bank index, HDFC Bank is the key gainer with expected inflows of $37 million. SBI will also receive $29 million as its weight increases.

Axis Bank will likely see outflows worth $19 million on Nifty Bank. Canara Bank and Bank of Baroda face $10 million outflows each due to weight reductions.

In broader markets, DMart shares will see the highest inflows on Nifty Next 50 at $11 million. Adani Energy Solutions faces $6 million outflows on the same index.

RBL Bank will likely see outflows exceeding $7 million across smallcap indices. Meesho will gain nearly $3 million from weight increases on Nifty Smallcap indices.

The rejig reflects changes in free-float market capitalisation over the past quarter. Passive funds tracking these indices will adjust their portfolios accordingly by market close today.
