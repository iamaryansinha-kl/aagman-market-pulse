VA Tech Wabag Incorporates New Subsidiary North Chennai Tru Water

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/VA Tech Wabag incorporates new wholly-owned subsidiary North Chennai Tru Water Private Limited effective April 1, 2026
  • 02/The subsidiary was registered with the Registrar of Companies, Chennai as part of Wabag's expansion strategy
  • 03/This follows Wabag's February 2026 joint venture with Peak Sustainability for a bioenergy subsidiary in Ghaziabad

VA Tech Wabag Limited has incorporated a new wholly-owned subsidiary. The entity, North Chennai Tru Water Private Limited, became effective on April 1, 2026.

The subsidiary was registered with the Registrar of Companies in Chennai. Wabag made the disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR.

This move adds to Wabag's growing portfolio of special purpose vehicles. The company has been actively expanding beyond its core water treatment business.

In February 2026, Wabag formed Ghaziabad Bioenergy Private Limited. That subsidiary was a 51:49 joint venture with Peak Sustainability Partners LLP for a compressed bio-gas project.

The bioenergy subsidiary was established with a paid-up capital of ₹1 lakh. Wabag contributed ₹51,000 for its 51% stake.

Wabag had announced plans in 2024 to develop 100 CBG plants. The agreement with Peak Sustainability covers sewage treatment facilities across India.

The company reported consolidated revenues of ₹3,686 crore on a TTM basis. Its debt-to-equity ratio stands at a conservative 0.13.

Wabag shares have declined 11.71% over the past year. The stock is trading below its 52-week high of ₹1,944.
