Jio IPO Sees Over $20 Bn in Bids From Fidelity, Allianz

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- Jio Platforms has received more than $20 billion in bids ahead of its proposed IPO, Bloomberg reported.
  • •- Global investors including Fidelity, Allianz and Capital Group, along with other overseas and local institutions, have indicated interest.
  • •- The IPO could raise about Rs 30,200 crore at a valuation of up to Rs 10.3 lakh crore, making it India's largest-ever IPO.
  • •- The issue is expected to open next week and will be entirely a fresh issue, with no offer for sale by existing investors.
  • •- Grey market premiums of Rs 170-180 per share imply roughly 15-16% over the likely upper end of the reported price band.

Reliance Industries' digital unit Jio Platforms has received more than $20 billion in bids for what could become India's biggest-ever IPO, Bloomberg reported on Tuesday, as global and domestic investors line up for Mukesh Ambani's telecom and digital-services business ahead of its expected launch next week.

Global investors including Fidelity, Allianz and Capital Group, along with other overseas and local institutions, have indicated interest in the offering, according to the report. The final list of anchor investors is likely to be decided in the coming days, though the process is still ongoing and no final decisions have been made.

The level of interest is striking given how sharply Jio's valuation has been reset from earlier, more aggressive expectations. After initial estimates had gone as high as $170 billion, Jio Platforms is now aiming for a valuation of up to Rs 10.3 lakh crore, or about $106 billion, Reuters reported earlier.

At the upper end of the expected price band, the IPO could raise about Rs 30,200 crore. That would put it ahead of Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,900 crore issue in 2024 and make it the largest IPO in Indian market history.

The issue is expected to open next week, before October 30, though the formal price band is yet to be announced. Reports indicate Jio could price the issue at Rs 1,065 to Rs 1,119 per share, implying a valuation of about Rs 10.3 lakh crore.

The structure of the offering matters as much as its size. The IPO is expected to be entirely a fresh issue, which means existing investors including Meta Platforms, Alphabet's Google, Mubadala and Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund are not expected to sell shares. Proceeds will go to Jio Platforms rather than to existing shareholders — a design that lets the company fund its expansion directly from public markets.

Akash Ambani, managing director of Jio Platforms and son of Reliance Industries chairman Mukesh Ambani, has been leading investor meetings across Hong Kong, London and New York in recent weeks, Bloomberg reported. Jio is being pitched as more than a telecom operator, with Reliance seeking to position it as a broader technology and digital infrastructure platform spanning mobile services, fixed broadband, cloud, entertainment, enterprise technology and artificial intelligence offerings.

The market, however, is likely to weigh the pitch against telecom fundamentals: cash flows, average revenue per user, 5G monetisation, broadband growth and the ability to build profitable digital services beyond connectivity.

Grey market activity has also picked up ahead of the launch. Unofficial trades have been quoting a premium of about Rs 170 to Rs 180 per share, implying roughly 15-16% over the likely upper end of the reported price band. Grey market prices are unofficial and can change quickly once the price band, anchor book and final issue details are announced.

The valuation marks a sharp jump from Jio's 2020 private fundraising round, when most financial and sovereign investors entered at an equity valuation of about Rs 4.91 lakh crore and Google invested at about Rs 4.36 lakh crore. The proposed IPO valuation is more than double those levels, though below the most bullish expectations built up earlier.

Brokerages have broadly placed Jio's value in the Rs 10 lakh crore to Rs 11 lakh crore range, with Motilal Oswal estimating around Rs 11 lakh crore and Nomura placing the IPO equity value between Rs 9.8 lakh crore and Rs 10.3 lakh crore.

For Reliance Industries, the listing could unlock value from its digital business and give public investors direct exposure to Jio's telecom and technology platform. With over $20 billion of bids already lined up, demand looks more than sufficient to absorb the largest primary issuance in Indian market history — the remaining question is where the price band lands, and how much of the grey market's optimism survives contact with the formal anchor book.

The scale of the book-building also says something about the primary market Jio is entering. India has already seen 96 IPOs raise roughly Rs 1.1 lakh crore in 2026, a record pace for the market, and the year's marquee listings have consistently drawn institutional demand several times their offer size. A $20 billion bid tally for a roughly Rs 30,200 crore raise implies demand multiples that would make allocation intensely competitive, particularly if the final price band comes in at the reported Rs 1,065-1,119 range.

Reliance Industries shares, which carry the value of the soon-to-be-listed digital unit, traded higher through Tuesday's session, closing near Rs 1,167 as the broader Sensex rallied over 1,100 points — an afternoon when even a stock as large as RIL moved with the market's tide rather than against it. The listing itself, expected before October 30, will be the next checkpoint; after that, the performance of the stock will determine whether Jio's reset valuation was conservative or still ambitious.

GLOSSARY: - Anchor investor: A qualified institutional investor allotted shares ahead of an IPO's public subscription to anchor demand. - Fresh issue: An IPO component where new shares are created and proceeds go to the company, rather than to selling shareholders. - Grey market premium: The unofficial premium at which unlisted IPO shares trade before listing; informal and indicative only. - ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): A telecom metric tracking revenue generated per subscriber, central to valuing connectivity businesses. - Accredited investor: An investor meeting regulatory thresholds for income or net worth, eligible for certain higher-risk offerings.
