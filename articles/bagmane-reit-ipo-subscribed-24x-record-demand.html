Bagmane REIT IPO Subscribed 24x: ₹3,405 Cr Issue Gets Record Demand

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bagmane Prime Office REIT IPO closes at 23.71 times subscription, with bids for 350.77 crore units against 14.79 crore on offer
  • 02/The issue received over 2 lakh applications, the highest ever for a REIT IPO in India
  • 03/Proceeds from the ₹3,405 crore issue will fund acquisitions of Luxor and Bagmane Rio assets in Bengaluru

Bagmane Prime Office REIT's ₹3,405 crore IPO closed with a subscription of 23.71 times. The issue received bids for 350.77 crore units against 14.79 crore units on offer.

The IPO price band was set at ₹95-100 per unit. It comprised a fresh issue of ₹2,390 crore and an offer for sale of ₹1,015 crore by Blackstone.

The other investors category fetched 21.87 times subscription. Institutional investors subscribed 25.25 times their allotted portion.

The company received over 2 lakh applications. This is the highest ever for a REIT issue in India, according to sources.

Ahead of the IPO, Bagmane secured ₹1,150 crore from anchor investors. The Blackstone-backed REIT owns six Grade A+ business parks in Bengaluru.

The portfolio spans 20.3 million square feet across key micro-markets. Locations include Outer Ring Road and the Secondary Business District.

Proceeds will acquire Luxor at Bagmane Capital Tech Park spanning one million sq ft. It will also part-fund a 93 per cent stake in Bagmane Rio.

The 1.1 million sq ft Bagmane Rio Business Park is part of the acquisition plan. Book running lead managers include JM Financial, Kotak Mahindra Capital, and Axis Capital.
