Q4 Results Today: UltraTech Cement Profit Rises 20% to ₹3,000 Crore, SBI Cards Asset Quality Improves as 48 Companies Report Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The April 27, 2026 earnings calendar was one of the busiest days of the Q4 FY26 results season, with 48 companies across cement, financial services, consumer goods, and industrials announcing their audited financials for the quarter ended March 31, 2026. The domestic equity markets responded positively, with the BSE Sensex closing 639.42 points or 0.83% higher at 77,303.63, and the NSE Nifty 50 rising 194.75 points or 0.81% to settle at 24,092.70, according to exchange closing data reported by The Hindu BusinessLine.

UltraTech Cement emerged as the headline act, reporting consolidated net profit of ₹3,000.02 crore for Q4 FY26, compared to ₹2,474.79 crore in the same quarter last year, marking a 20% year-on-year increase. Revenue for the quarter stood at ₹25,500 crore, up 10.4% YoY, while EBITDA surged 21.2% to ₹5,600 crore, translating to an EBITDA margin of 21.71% versus 20.02% a year ago. The company's board recommended a final dividend of ₹240 per share. UltraTech also crossed the 200 million tonnes per annum domestic capacity milestone during the quarter and reported a 50% jump in operating cash flows, while announcing its strategic pivot into the wires and cables business.

SBI Cards and Payment Services met market estimates with Q4 net profit of ₹609.30 crore, up from ₹556.64 crore in Q4 FY25, representing 14.2% year-on-year growth. For the full financial year, profit stood at ₹2,166.71 crore compared to ₹1,916.41 crore in FY25. The more significant takeaway for investors was the sharp improvement in asset quality: gross stage 3 assets dropped to 2.41% of gross advances as of March 31, 2026, down from 3.08% a year ago and 2.86% sequentially, while net stage 3 assets fell to 1.04% from 1.46% YoY and 1.28% QoQ. This sequential improvement suggests stress in the unsecured retail credit cycle may be peaking or beginning to ease, as noted in The Hindu BusinessLine's live coverage.

Tamilnad Mercantile Bank delivered a standout quarter with net profit jumping 28% year-on-year to ₹373.65 crore. For the full year FY26, the bank's profit rose 13.3% to ₹1,337.55 crore from ₹1,182.61 crore in FY25, while total income grew 9% to ₹6,696.49 crore. The board recommended a higher final dividend of ₹12.50 per share, up from ₹11.00 last year. The bank's advance book expanded 20.32% YoY to ₹53,380 crore, deposits grew 14.94% to ₹61,712 crore, and low-cost CASA deposits surged 22.35% to ₹17,365 crore as of March 31, 2026.

Supreme Industries reported a powerful 46.3% surge in net profit to ₹430 crore, with some sources citing ₹433.6 crore, driven by significant margin expansion. Revenue grew 17.4% YoY to ₹3,530 crore, while EBITDA jumped 49.4% to ₹620 crore, pushing the EBITDA margin to 17.66% from 13.73% a year earlier — a nearly 400 basis points expansion. The company attributed the strong bottom-line performance to favorable polymer prices and declared a final dividend of ₹25 per share.

Nippon Life India Asset Management posted standalone net profit of ₹382.64 crore for Q4 FY26, compared to ₹295.78 crore in the year-ago quarter, reflecting a 29% year-on-year increase. Full-year FY26 PAT stood at ₹1,497.75 crore versus ₹1,252.23 crore in FY25. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹12.50 per share. Kirloskar Pneumatic Company also delivered exceptional numbers, with net profit surging 73.7% YoY to ₹140 crore on revenue of ₹710 crore, EBITDA soaring 69.1% to ₹186 crore, and margins expanding to 26.34% from 18.87%. The company announced a 1:2 stock split and a final dividend of ₹8.50 per share.

Several other notable results hit the wires on April 27. Varun Beverages reported a 20.1% year-on-year rise in net profit to ₹872.4 crore, up from ₹726.5 crore in Q4 FY25, and declared a dividend of ₹0.50 per share. IndusInd Bank swung to a net profit of ₹533 crore from a loss of ₹2,329 crore in the year-ago quarter, with net interest income at ₹4,371.5 crore and asset quality showing sequential improvement. Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India, however, bucked the positive trend, reporting a standalone net loss of ₹178.26 crore compared to a profit of ₹57.49 crore in Q4 FY25, while its board approved the acquisition of 100% stake in Aditatva Estates Private Limited.

Looking ahead, the earnings pipeline remains dense. Tuesday, April 28, 2026 will see 45 more companies report, including Maruti Suzuki, Star Health Insurance, Bandhan Bank, and Dalmia Bharat. The full week from April 27 to May 2 has 208 BSE- and NSE-listed companies scheduled to declare results, as tracked by ET Now News, keeping investors and analysts occupied through the peak of the FY26 reporting season.
