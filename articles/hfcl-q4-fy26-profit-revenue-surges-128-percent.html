HFCL Q4 FY26 Profit Jumps to ₹184 Cr as Revenue Surges 128% YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HFCL swung to a consolidated net profit of ₹184.45 crore in Q4 FY26 from a loss of ₹83.30 crore in Q4 FY25, driven by a 128% revenue surge
  • 02/FY26 revenue hit ₹4,949.27 crore with 21% growth; full-year profit rose 90% to ₹329.44 crore as the order book doubled to ₹21,206 crore
  • 03/The board declared a dividend of ₹0.20 per share; investors should watch preform facility capex execution and defence export orders

HFCL Limited reported a consolidated net profit of ₹184.45 crore for the quarter ending March 31, 2026. The company had posted a loss of ₹83.30 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue for Q4 FY26 surged to ₹1,824 crore. This marks a 128% jump from the ₹800.72 crore recorded in Q4 FY25.

On a sequential basis, revenue grew 51% while profit increased 80%. The EBITDA margin expanded by 2,126 basis points to 18.47%.

For the full year FY26, revenue reached ₹4,949.27 crore. This reflects 21% growth over FY25 when revenue stood at ₹4,064.52 crore.

Full-year profit after tax jumped 90% to ₹329.44 crore. The company described FY26 as its strongest-ever financial performance.

The order book more than doubled to ₹21,206 crore. In FY25, the order book was ₹9,967 crore.

The optical fiber cable order book hit ₹13,483 crore, the highest on record. HFCL cited rising demand from the United States, Europe and Asia.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹0.20 per equity share. This equals 20% on the face value of Re 1 per share.

"We strongly believe that HFCL is entering a structurally stronger and more predictable growth phase," said Mahendra Nahata, Managing Director, HFCL. He pointed to expansion in exports, long-term contracts and high-margin products.

HFCL will invest approximately ₹580 crore in a preform manufacturing facility. This backward integration step aims to reduce import dependence and improve margins.

The company entered a MoU for defence aerospace opportunities. The confirmed export-oriented defence order book stands at approximately ₹1,930 crore.

HFCL shares gained 3.5% on April 30, 2026, closing at ₹110.85 on the NSE. The stock has risen sharply this year as order inflows accelerated.
