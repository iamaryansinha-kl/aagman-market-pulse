Rupee REER at 92.72, Decade Low on Iran War, Portfolio Outflows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's 40-currency real effective exchange rate (REER) fell to 92.72, marking the lowest valuation in over a decade
  • 02/The rupee dropped 24 paise to 94.25 against the US dollar; crude Brent oil surged to $105.99 on US-Iran tensions
  • 03/Analysts see limited near-term recovery scope as portfolio outflows and oil prices keep pressure on the currency

India's rupee has hit its most undervalued level in over ten years. The currency's 40-currency real effective exchange rate fell to 92.72.

The REER accounts for inflation differentials across trading partner economies. A reading below 100 signals the rupee is undervalued relative to its fair value.

The rupee dropped 24 paise to 94.25 against the US dollar in early trade on April 24, 2026. It opened flat in interbank forex markets before weakening.

The dollar index traded 0.12 per cent higher at 98.71. A stronger greenback adds pressure on emerging market currencies including the rupee.

Crude oil prices have surged on escalating US-Iran tensions. June Brent futures rose to $105.99 per barrel, up 0.88 per cent, while WTI crude climbed to $96.45.

Higher oil prices widen India's trade deficit and increase demand for dollars. The country imports over 80 per cent of its crude requirements.

Foreign portfolio investors have pulled capital from Indian equities amid global risk aversion. This makes the rupee Asia's worst-performing currency in 2026 so far.

Despite the extent of undervaluation, analysts see little scope for a near-term recovery. Geopolitical tensions and oil supply risks continue to cloud the outlook.

WHAT IS THE CURRENT REAL EFFECTIVE EXCHANGE RATE (REER) OF THE INDIAN RUPEE?
THE INDIAN RUPEE'S 40-CURRENCY REAL EFFECTIVE EXCHANGE RATE (REER) FELL TO 92.72, WHICH IS THE LOWEST VALUATION IN OVER A DECADE. A REER BELOW 100 INDICATES THE CURRENCY IS UNDERVALUED RELATIVE TO ITS TRADING PARTNERS.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT EXCHANGE RATE OF THE RUPEE AGAINST THE US DOLLAR?
THE RUPEE DROPPED 24 PAISE TO 94.25 AGAINST THE US DOLLAR IN EARLY TRADE ON APRIL 24, 2026. IT OPENED AT 94.25 IN THE INTERBANK FOREX MARKET, DOWN FROM THE PREVIOUS CLOSE.
WHAT FACTORS ARE DRIVING THE RUPEE'S DECLINE?
THE RUPEE IS UNDER PRESSURE FROM ESCALATING US-IRAN TENSIONS, SURGING CRUDE OIL PRICES WITH BRENT AT $105.99 AND WTI AT $96.45, STRONG FOREIGN PORTFOLIO OUTFLOWS FROM INDIAN EQUITIES, AND A STRONGER US DOLLAR INDEX AT 98.71.
WHY DO CRUDE OIL PRICES IMPACT THE RUPEE?
INDIA IMPORTS OVER 80 PER CENT OF ITS CRUDE OIL REQUIREMENTS. HIGHER OIL PRICES WIDEN THE TRADE DEFICIT AND INCREASE DEMAND FOR US DOLLARS TO PAY FOR IMPORTS, PUTTING DOWNWARD PRESSURE ON THE RUPEE.
IS THERE ANY NEAR-TERM RECOVERY EXPECTED FOR THE RUPEE?
DESPITE THE RUPEE BEING FUNDAMENTALLY UNDERVALUED, ANALYSTS SEE LITTLE NEAR-TERM SCOPE FOR A RECOVERY. GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS IN WEST ASIA AND ONGOING PORTFOLIO OUTFLOWS CONTINUE TO CREATE AN UNFAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT FOR THE CURRENCY.
