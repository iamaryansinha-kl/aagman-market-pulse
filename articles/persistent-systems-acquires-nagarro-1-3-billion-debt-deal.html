Persistent Systems Acquires Nagarro for $1.3 Billion in Debt Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Persistent Systems will acquire Nagarro SE for $1.3 billion (₹13,540 crore), funded by a $1.4 billion Barclays loan facility
  • 02/Nagarro's revenue growth has slowed to 5% over three years from 18% over five years, with EBITDA margins of 14-15% vs Persistent's 17-18%
  • 03/The combined entity will have $2.9 billion revenue and 46,000 employees, with Persistent's European share rising from 9% to 22%

Persistent Systems Ltd (NSE: PERSISTENT) has agreed to acquire Munich-based Nagarro SE for an enterprise value of $1.3 billion (₹13,540 crore). The deal will be funded through fresh debt.

The acquisition price of €81 per share is nearly double Nagarro's three-month average. Nagarro's stock has already halved from its December 2025 peak of €80.4.

Nagarro's annual revenue growth has decelerated sharply to 5% over three years from 18% over five years. The company operates at an EBITDA margin of 14-15%, below Persistent's 17-18%.

Persistent has secured a $1.4 billion loan facility from Barclays at an effective interest rate of 4.1-4.8%. The net debt-EBITDA of the combined entity is expected at 1.9-2.5x.

Sandeep Kalra, Persistent's CEO, said the valuation reflects a control premium as Nagarro will be delisted. He hinted at possible private equity funding at the asset level.

The combined entity will post $2.9 billion (₹27,000 crore) in revenue with 46,000 employees. It will become India's seventh largest IT company by revenue.

Persistent's European revenue share will expand to 22% from 9%. Nagarro brings clients including Siemens, Lufthansa, and SAP, with negligible customer overlap.

The deal is expected to conclude by the March 2027 quarter. Persistent's stock has declined 23% year-to-date amid AI disruption fears.

THE DEAL WILL BE FUNDED THROUGH A $1.4 BILLION LOAN FACILITY FROM BARCLAYS AT AN EFFECTIVE INTEREST RATE OF 4.1-4.8%. INTERNAL ACCRUALS AND OPERATING CASH FLOWS ARE INSUFFICIENT TO REPAY THE LOAN WITHIN TWO YEARS.
WHAT ARE THE FINANCIAL METRICS OF THE COMBINED ENTITY?
THE COMBINED ENTITY IS EXPECTED TO GENERATE $2.9 BILLION (₹27,000 CRORE) IN REVENUE WITH 46,000 EMPLOYEES. THE NET DEBT-TO-EBITDA RATIO IS PROJECTED AT 1.9-2.5X, FALLING BELOW 1X BY FY30.
WHY IS NAGARRO'S GROWTH A CONCERN FOR THE DEAL?
NAGARRO'S ANNUAL REVENUE GROWTH HAS SLOWED TO 5% OVER A THREE-YEAR PERIOD FROM 18% OVER A FIVE-YEAR PERIOD. ITS EBITDA MARGIN OF 14-15% TRAILS PERSISTENT'S 17-18%.
WHAT STRATEGIC BENEFITS DOES PERSISTENT EXPECT FROM THE DEAL?
PERSISTENT'S EUROPEAN REVENUE SHARE WILL RISE FROM 9% TO 22%, REDUCING US MARKET DEPENDENCE. THE COMBINED ENTITY WILL HAVE OVER 350 CLIENTS WITH $1 MILLION-PLUS BILLING AND NEGLIGIBLE CUSTOMER OVERLAP.
