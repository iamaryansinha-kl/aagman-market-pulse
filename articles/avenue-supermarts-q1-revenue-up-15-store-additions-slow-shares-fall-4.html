Avenue Supermarts Q1 Revenue Up 15% But Store Additions Slow, Shares Fall 4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Avenue Supermarts Q1FY27 revenue rises 15.13% to ₹18,343.49 crore, but store additions slow to just 3 net stores
  • 02/Annualized revenue per store declines 4% YoY to ₹146.3 crore; total stores at 503 as of June 30
  • 03/Antique Stock Broking maintains 'Hold', cuts target to ₹4,437; profitability seen subdued near term

Avenue Supermarts Ltd, the operator of the D-Mart retail chain, reported a 15.13% rise in its standalone revenue from operations to ₹18,343.49 crore for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026. The company had posted revenue of ₹15,932.12 crore in the same quarter a year ago.

On a sequential basis, D-Mart's revenue in Q1FY27 increased 3.7% from ₹17,683.86 crore in the March quarter (Q4FY26). However, the total number of stores stood at 503 as of June 30, 2026, with only 3 net stores added during the quarter. This compares with 9 stores added in Q1FY26 and 85 stores added during FY26.

Annualized revenue per store declined around 4% year-on-year to ₹146.3 crore. Analysts believe the moderation in revenue growth is attributable to slower store additions. The company has been expanding aggressively but the pace has slowed in the current quarter.

"Profitability is expected to remain subdued due to elevated operating costs and weaker GM&A performance. Aggressive upfront investments in new stores, coupled with elevated real estate costs, are also expected to weigh on ROCE," said Antique Stock Broking.

The brokerage firm cut its estimates by 3% and 2% for FY27 and FY28 to account for weaker-than-expected Q1FY27 performance. It expects sales and EBITDA CAGR of 17% each over FY26-FY28. However, it maintained a 'Hold' rating and cut the target price to ₹4,437 from ₹4,524, based on 40x FY28 EV/EBITDA.

"Near-term challenges such as subdued performance in the margin of general merchandise and apparel, weak recovery in mature stores/SSSG, and increasing competition intensity from online grocery format mainly in metros or Tier-1 cities is likely to keep near-term performance under check," Antique added.

Avenue Supermarts will announce its financial results for Q1FY27 on July 11. The board will also consider raising funds by issue of debt securities by way of private placement. The stock fell 4.13% to ₹4,014.85 on the BSE in morning trade on 3 July.

The stock has fallen 4% in one month and declined 8% in three months. It has gained 7% in six months but dropped 8% in one year. DMart share price has slipped 16% in two years but has rallied 21% over the past five years.

The retail sector is facing intense competition from online grocery formats, particularly in metro and Tier-1 cities. DMart's value-focused model has helped it gain market share, but margin pressure from competition and store expansion costs remains a concern.
