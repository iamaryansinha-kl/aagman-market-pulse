Nalco Q4 Profit Drops 16.6% as West Asia Alumina Exports Hit by Tensions

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nalco CMD Brijendra Pratap Singh says West Asia, accounting for 40-50% of alumina exports, has been affected by geopolitical tensions
  • 02/Spot alumina prices have fallen to USD 305-310 per tonne due to oversupply as West Asian smelters operate at reduced capacity
  • 03/Nalco Q4 FY26 net profit dropped 16.6% to Rs 1,722.44 crore; revenue fell to Rs 5,012.82 crore from Rs 5,267.83 crore YoY

National Aluminium Company Ltd (NSE: NATIONALUM) said its alumina exports to West Asia have been disrupted by geopolitical tensions. CMD Brijendra Pratap Singh confirmed the impact during the Q4 earnings call on May 10, 2026.

"Our alumina export to the Middle East... around 40-50 per cent of our export was going there, which has been affected," said Singh.

West Asian smelters are operating at reduced capacity. They will not return to full output immediately, keeping spot alumina prices under pressure.

Global spot alumina prices have declined to USD 305-310 per tonne. The shift in export destinations due to West Asia supply disruptions has contributed to the oversupply.

Nalco produced 23 lakh tonnes of alumina in FY26 and exported 13.08 lakh tonnes. The company is now securing orders from Indonesia and other alternative markets.

The aluminium value chain has been affected by the conflict. Refined aluminium prices have surged due to production shutdowns, even as alumina prices have fallen.

Nalco reported a 16.6 per cent drop in consolidated net profit to Rs 1,722.44 crore for Q4 FY26. Revenue declined to Rs 5,012.82 crore from Rs 5,267.83 crore in the year-ago period.

The company had posted a profit of Rs 2,067.23 crore in Q4 FY25. Nalco is one of India's leading integrated aluminium producers.

HOW HAVE GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS AFFECTED NALCO'S ALUMINA EXPORTS?
NALCO'S ALUMINA EXPORTS TO WEST ASIA HAVE BEEN DISRUPTED DUE TO ONGOING GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS. WEST ASIA ACCOUNTS FOR 40-50 PER CENT OF THE COMPANY'S TOTAL ALUMINA SHIPMENTS.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT SPOT PRICE OF ALUMINA?
GLOBAL SPOT ALUMINA PRICES HAVE FALLEN TO USD 305-310 PER TONNE. THE DECLINE IS DRIVEN BY OVERSUPPLY AS WEST ASIAN SMELTERS OPERATE AT REDUCED CAPACITY.
WHAT WERE NALCO'S ALUMINA PRODUCTION AND EXPORT NUMBERS IN FY26?
NALCO PRODUCED 23 LAKH TONNES OF ALUMINA IN 2025-26, OF WHICH 13.08 LAKH TONNES WERE EXPORTED. THE COMPANY IS DIVERSIFYING TO INDONESIA AND OTHER MARKETS.
HOW DID NALCO PERFORM FINANCIALLY IN Q4 FY26?
NALCO REPORTED A 16.6 PER CENT DROP IN CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT TO RS 1,722.44 CRORE. REVENUE DECLINED TO RS 5,012.82 CRORE FROM RS 5,267.83 CRORE IN Q4 FY25.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR ALUMINA PRICES?
SPOT ALUMINA PRICES WILL REMAIN UNDER PRESSURE UNTIL WEST ASIAN SMELTERS RETURN TO FULL CAPACITY. THE PRODUCTION CURTAILMENT IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE IN THE NEAR TERM.
