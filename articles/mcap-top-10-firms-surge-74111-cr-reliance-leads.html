Mcap Of Top 10 Firms: 6 Surge Rs 74,111 Cr, Reliance Leads Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The combined market valuation of 6 of India's top-10 most valued firms surged by Rs 74,111.57 crore during the past week
  • 02/Reliance Industries emerged as the biggest gainer, adding Rs 24,696.89 crore to reach Rs 18,33,117.70 crore in market cap
  • 03/Markets ended with marginal gains amid volatile, range-bound trading driven by rupee weakness and mixed global cues

The combined market valuation of 6 of India's top-10 most valued firms surged by Rs 74,111.57 crore last week. The BSE benchmark climbed 177.36 points, or 0.23 per cent, during the period.

Reliance Industries Ltd (NSE: RELIANCE) emerged as the biggest gainer. Its market capitalisation jumped by Rs 24,696.89 crore to reach Rs 18,33,117.70 crore.

Tata Consultancy Services Ltd (NSE: TCS) added Rs 19,338.68 crore, taking its valuation to Rs 8,38,401.33 crore. ICICI Bank Ltd (NSE: ICICIBANK) surged Rs 14,515.93 crore to Rs 9,06,901.32 crore.

Life Insurance Corporation of India (NSE: LIC) gained Rs 9,076.37 crore, reaching Rs 5,14,443.69 crore. Bajaj Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJFINANCE) added Rs 3,797.83 crore to Rs 5,70,515.57 crore. Larsen & Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) rose Rs 2,685.87 crore to Rs 5,40,228.21 crore.

On the losing side, Bharti Airtel Ltd (NSE: BHARTIARTL) shed Rs 20,229.67 crore, falling to Rs 11,40,295.49 crore. Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) eroded Rs 16,212.18 crore to Rs 5,17,380 crore.

State Bank of India (NSE: SBIN) lost Rs 12,784.4 crore, declining to Rs 8,76,077.92 crore. HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK) dipped Rs 2,094.35 crore to Rs 11,79,974.90 crore.

"Markets ended the week with marginal gains amid a highly volatile and range-bound trading environment," said Ajit Mishra, SVP, Research, Religare Broking Ltd. He cited rupee weakness, mixed global cues, and uncertainty around inflation and interest rates as key drivers.

Reliance Industries retained its position as India's most valued firm. The top-10 ranking remained unchanged, with HDFC Bank, Bharti Airtel, ICICI Bank, SBI, TCS, Bajaj Finance, L&T, HUL, and LIC rounding out the list.
