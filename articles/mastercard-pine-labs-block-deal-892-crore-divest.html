Mastercard Asia Pacific May Sell Entire 4.3% Pine Labs Stake via ₹892 Crore Block Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Mastercard Asia Pacific may sell up to 49.7 million shares of fintech company Pine Labs. The sale represents approximately 4.3% of the company and is valued at up to ₹892.5 crore.

The transaction is structured as a block deal with a floor price of ₹179.50 per share. This represents a discount of about 7.3% to Pine Labs' previous closing price of ₹192.25 on the NSE.

The sale covers Mastercard's entire stake As of June 30, 2026, Mastercard Asia Pacific held 49,724,182 shares, representing a 4.31% stake in Pine Labs. The proposed sale of up to 49.7 million shares is broadly equivalent to the entire stake disclosed by Mastercard as of that date.

The transaction is a 100% secondary sale. Proceeds will accrue to Mastercard Asia Pacific as the selling shareholder, not to Pine Labs.

Citigroup Global Markets India is the sole placement agent for the transaction.

Deal timeline The books opened on September 21 and are scheduled to close on September 22 at around 7:30 AM IST. There is an option for earlier closure.

The expected trade date is September 22. Settlement is scheduled for September 23.

No pricing guidance will be provided until the shares are crossed on the exchanges on September 22.

Pine Labs stock performance Pine Labs shares ended Monday's trading session at ₹193.55 apiece on the NSE, up 0.68% from the previous close of ₹192.25. The stock traded between ₹191.15 and ₹195.70 during the session.

Pine Labs made its stock market debut on November 14, 2025. The IPO raised approximately ₹3,900 crore. Shares listed at ₹242 apiece on the NSE, representing a 9.5% premium to the IPO issue price of ₹221.

The stock has declined since listing. At the proposed floor price of ₹179.50, the block deal would value the sale below the IPO listing price.

About Pine Labs Pine Labs is a fintech company focused on digitising commerce for merchants, consumer brands, enterprises and financial institutions. Its technology platforms support digital payments, card issuance and value-added financial services.

The company operates across India and international markets, including Malaysia, UAE, Singapore, Australia, the US and Africa.

Mastercard's exit comes less than a year after Pine Labs' IPO. The payments giant was an early investor in the company.
