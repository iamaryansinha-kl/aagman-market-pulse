Awfis Q4 Profit Doubles to Rs 23.24 Crore on Higher Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Awfis Space Solutions Q4 FY2026 net profit surges 107% to Rs 23.24 crore on total income of Rs 429.16 crore
  • 02/Full-year FY2026 profit rises only 4.4% to Rs 70.85 crore despite 25.8% revenue growth, highlighting margin pressure
  • 03/Stock trades at Rs 341, down 48% from 52-week high; analysts hold average target of Rs 685 with FY2027 revenue estimates at Rs 1,880 crore

Awfis Space Solutions Ltd reported a consolidated net profit of Rs 23.24 crore for Q4 FY2026. This marks a 107% jump from Rs 11.23 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Total income rose to Rs 429.16 crore in Q4 FY2026. This compares with Rs 359.45 crore in Q4 FY2025, showing a 19.4% year-on-year increase.

The board approved the results on May 25, 2026. The strong performance was driven by sustained demand for managed workspaces across the company's network.

For the full fiscal year 2025-26, net profit stood at Rs 70.85 crore. This is up from Rs 67.87 crore in FY2024-25, a modest 4.4% annual growth.

Full-year total income climbed to Rs 1,586.12 crore from Rs 1,260.74 crore. The 25.8% annual revenue growth signals strong operational expansion.

Awfis operates over 250 co-working centres across 18 cities. The company follows an asset-light managed aggregation model for rapid scaling.

The stock trades at Rs 341.15, down 48% from its 52-week high of Rs 718.95. Seven analysts cover the stock with an average price target of Rs 685.

Analysts estimate FY2027 revenue at Rs 1,880 crore and EPS at Rs 16.94. The company's debt-to-equity ratio stands elevated at 3.03, a key risk to monitor.
