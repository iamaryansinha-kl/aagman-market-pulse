5 Listed Indian REITs Distribute Rs 2,566 Crore in Q4 FY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's 5 listed REITs distributed over Rs 2,566 crore to 4.25 lakh unitholders in Q4 FY26
  • 02/Full FY26 cumulative distribution exceeded Rs 8,900 crore, marking over 50% YoY growth
  • 03/Total gross asset value crossed Rs 2.72 lakh crore; sixth REIT Bagmane Prime listed this month

India's five listed Real Estate Investment Trusts distributed over Rs 2,566 crore in the March quarter. The payout reached more than 4.25 lakh unitholders.

For the full financial year 2025-26, cumulative distributions exceeded Rs 8,900 crore. This marks a year-on-year growth of over 50 per cent.

The five REITs are Brookfield India Real Estate Trust, Embassy Office Parks REIT, Knowledge Realty Trust, Mindspace Business Parks REIT, and Nexus Select Trust. Together they manage 187 million sq ft of Grade A assets.

The total gross asset value of the Indian REIT market stood at over Rs 2.72 lakh crore as of March 31, 2026. Combined market capitalisation crossed Rs 1.7 lakh crore by May 22.

Alok Aggarwal, Managing Director and CEO of Brookfield India Real Estate Trust and Chairperson of the Indian REITs Association, said the sector delivered strong growth in distributions and portfolio expansion.

"The cumulative distribution of over Rs 8,900 crore in FY26 represents robust year-on-year growth of over 50 per cent," Aggarwal said. He noted the sector's ability to deliver stable cash flows.

A sixth REIT, Bagmane Prime Office REIT, was listed on stock exchanges earlier this month. This expands the listed REIT universe beyond the existing five.

REITs were introduced in India in 2019 under SEBI regulations. They allow investors to access rent-yielding commercial real estate through a regulated, liquid structure.
