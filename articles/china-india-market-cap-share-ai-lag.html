China, India Top Firms Lose Market Cap Share as AI Race Widens Gap

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/China's and India's top 10 firms each now hold ~19% of total market cap, down from 26% and 22% a year ago
  • 02/Taiwan's benchmark rose 54% on TSMC, Korea's Kospi roughly doubled on SK Hynix and Samsung AI demand
  • 03/India's Nifty 50 is down ~8% YTD, led by legacy giants vulnerable to AI disruption

China and India's top ten companies now each account for about 19% of total market capitalization. That is down from 26% and 22% respectively a year ago, according to Bloomberg data.

Hong Kong also saw concentration slip to 9.8% from 10%. The city remains the least top-heavy major market in Asia.

Taiwan's benchmark surged 54% this year on Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. Korea's Kospi roughly doubled on SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics.

In South Korea, the top 10 companies now account for about 65% of the market. That is roughly double their share a year ago.

Taiwan's top-10 concentration grew to 56% from 49%. It was already Asia's most top-heavy market.

India's Nifty 50 is down about 8% this year. The index is dominated by legacy giants like Reliance Industries and HDFC Bank.

Even India's leading tech firms, Tata Consultancy Services and Infosys Ltd., are rooted in traditional software services. These are seen as vulnerable to AI disruption.

"In the tech-heavy markets, AI and memory winners are driving index concentration higher. But in India, China and Hong Kong, concentration is falling because there is no single dominant AI winner," said Charu Chanana, chief investment strategist at Saxo Markets.
