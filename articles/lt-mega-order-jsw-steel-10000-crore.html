L&T Bags ₹10,000-15,000-Crore Mega Order From JSW Steel

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Larsen and Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) bags a mega order valued at ₹10,000-15,000 crore from JSW Steel Ltd, its largest ever in the domestic metals sector
  • 02/JSW Steel is expanding crude steel capacity from 35 MTPA to over 50 MTPA by 2031 across brownfield and greenfield sites in Karnataka and Odisha
  • 03/L&T shares fell 3.3% to ₹3,919.2 on May 6 as the broader market reacted to the company's muted FY27 margin guidance of 8.3%

Larsen and Toubro Ltd announced on May 6, 2026, that its minerals and metals vertical has secured a "mega" order from JSW Steel Ltd. The contract is valued in the ₹10,000 crore to ₹15,000 crore range.

L&T classifies orders between ₹10,000 crore and ₹15,000 crore as "mega" in its internal framework. This marks the single largest order the company has ever won in the domestic metals segment.

JSW Steel is executing a strategic expansion to raise its crude steel processing capacity. The target is to grow from 35 million tonnes per annum to over 50 MTPA by 2031.

The contract covers engineering, procurement, and installation of critical steelmaking facilities. This includes blast furnaces and steel melt shops across multiple brownfield and greenfield projects.

The work will be carried out mainly at JSW Steel's sites in Ballari, Karnataka and Paradip, Odisha. Both locations are key hubs in JSW's integrated steel operations.

"Securing the largest-ever order in the metals segment marks a defining moment for L&T's minerals and metals business. Our long-standing partnership with JSW Steel reflects a shared commitment to scale, innovation and nation building," said DK Sen, Advisor to CMD at L&T.

L&T reported its fourth quarter earnings on May 5, 2026. It has guided for 10-12% revenue growth and order inflow growth for FY27, with margins expected to hold steady at 8.3%.

The company cautioned that the first and second quarters of FY27 could see disruptions. Delayed shipments are expected to impact execution timelines during the first half.

L&T shares were trading at ₹3,919.2 at 10:05 am on May 6, down 3.3% for the session. The stock has gained 5% over the past month but is down 5.3% year-to-date in 2026.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE VALUE OF THE ORDER L&T WON FROM JSW STEEL?
L&T CLASSIFIES THE ORDER AS "MEGA," WHICH CORRESPONDS TO A VALUE RANGE OF ₹10,000 CRORE TO ₹15,000 CRORE. THIS IS THE LARGEST ORDER THE COMPANY HAS EVER SECURED IN THE DOMESTIC METALS SECTOR.
WHAT FACILITIES WILL L&T BUILD FOR JSW STEEL?
L&T'S MINERALS AND METALS VERTICAL WILL INSTALL CRITICAL PROCESS FACILITIES INCLUDING BLAST FURNACES AND STEEL MELT SHOPS. THE WORK COVERS ENGINEERING, PROCUREMENT, AND INSTALLATION ACROSS MULTIPLE BROWNFIELD AND GREENFIELD EXPANSIONS.
WHERE WILL THE PROJECTS BE LOCATED?
THE PRIMARY PROJECT SITES ARE JSW STEEL'S FACILITIES AT BALLARI IN KARNATAKA AND PARADIP IN ODISHA. BOTH ARE STRATEGIC HUBS IN JSW'S INTEGRATED STEEL OPERATIONS.
WHY IS JSW STEEL EXPANDING ITS CAPACITY?
JSW STEEL IS EXPANDING ITS CRUDE STEEL PROCESSING CAPACITY FROM 35 MTPA TO OVER 50 MTPA BY 2031. THIS IS PART OF THE COMPANY'S LONG-TERM STRATEGIC GROWTH ROADMAP TO MEET RISING DOMESTIC AND EXPORT DEMAND.
HOW DID L&T SHARES REACT TO THE NEWS?
L&T SHARES FELL 3.3% TO ₹3,919.2 ON MAY 6, 2026, TRADING LOWER ALONGSIDE THE BROADER MARKET. THE DECLINE FOLLOWED THE COMPANY'S Q4 EARNINGS REPORT AND MUTED FY27 MARGIN GUIDANCE OF 8.3%.
