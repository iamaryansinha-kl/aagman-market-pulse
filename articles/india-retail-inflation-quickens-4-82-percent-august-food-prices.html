India's Retail Inflation Quickens to 4.82% in August from 4.45% in July as Food Prices Bite

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Retail inflation accelerated to 4.82% in August 2026 from 4.45% in July, the highest reading since India's revised CPI series launched
  • 02/Food inflation rose to 5.95% from 5.52%; onion inflation more than doubled to 48.27% from 22.54%
  • 03/RBI cut its FY27 inflation forecast to 5% and expects headline inflation to peak in Q3 on food and fuel pressures

India's retail inflation accelerated to 4.82% in August 2026 from 4.45% in July, government data released on Monday showed. The reading came in close to a Reuters poll projection of 4.80%, with economist estimates ranging between 4.40% and 5.05%.

The August print is the highest since India adopted its revised CPI series with a new base year earlier this year. Inflation breached the Reserve Bank of India's medium-term target of 4% in June, rising to 4.38% from 3.93% in May.

Rural inflation stood at 5.23% in August, significantly higher than urban inflation of 4.31%. Food inflation climbed to 5.95% from 5.52% in July, with rural food inflation at 5.64% and urban food inflation at 6.13%.

Onion inflation more than doubled to 48.27% in August from 22.54% in July. Garlic inflation rose to 43.60% from 35.36%, while ginger inflation moderated to 73.82% from 83.57%.

Tomato prices fell 31.09% and potato inflation stood at -13.14%, cushioning the broader rise in food prices. Sugar prices hit a record high in August; the export ban in place since May will continue until end-September.

Jewellery remained a major source of pressure. Silver jewellery inflation hit 107.11%, the highest in the CPI basket, though down from 109.89% in July. Gold, diamond and platinum jewellery inflation rose to 35.53% from 32.98%.

Services inflation stayed elevated. Restaurants and accommodation services recorded 8.38% inflation, food and beverage serving 8.41%, and transport services for goods 14.64%.

The RBI cut its FY27 inflation forecast to 5% from 5.1% at its August 5 meeting. Quarterly projections: Q1 at 4.1%, Q2 at 4.7%, Q3 at 5.9%, Q4 at 5.5%. Core inflation forecast was lowered to 4.3% from 4.7%.

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra said headline inflation is expected to rise further in the near term and peak in Q3, driven primarily by food and fuel prices. He added that core inflation excluding precious metals remains benign.

The RBI is mandated to keep headline retail inflation at 5% for FY27. Malhotra reiterated that containing inflation and maintaining price stability is the central bank's foremost priority.
