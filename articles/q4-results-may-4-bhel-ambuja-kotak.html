Q4 Results May 4 Live: BHEL, Ambuja Cements, Kotak Bank Earnings Today

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BHEL and Ambuja Cements lead a packed Q4 FY26 results calendar on May 4, with both boards meeting to approve earnings and consider dividends
  • 02/Kotak Mahindra Bank PAT rises 13% YoY to Rs 4,027 crore but shares fall 5%; Zen Technologies profit crashes 83%
  • 03/Jindal Steel posts a massive beat with Q4 PAT at Rs 1,861.6 crore versus Rs 886.4 crore analyst estimate

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (NSE: BHEL) and Ambuja Cements (NSE: AMBUJACEM) headline a packed Q4 FY26 results calendar on May 4, 2026. Both boards meet today to approve quarterly earnings and consider dividend recommendations.

Analyst estimates for BHEL Q4 FY26 are wide. JM Financial projects revenue of Rs 10,028 crore (up 12% YoY) with EBITDA of Rs 1,423 crore and PAT of Rs 1,005.9 crore (up 158% YoY). Nuvama estimates revenue at Rs 11,136 crore with PAT of Rs 637 crore. The range reflects uncertainty around an industrial gas shortage that may have impacted execution by Rs 2,500-3,000 crore.

Ambuja Cements is expected to report revenue of Rs 9,500-10,200 crore versus Rs 7,900 crore in Q3 FY26. PAT estimates range from Rs 850-1,000 crore. The board will also consider a fund raise of up to Rs 15,000 crore. Brokerage targets range from Rs 530 to Rs 660.

Kotak Mahindra Bank has already reported Q4 standalone PAT of Rs 4,027 crore, up 13% YoY from Rs 3,552 crore. Net interest income grew 8.1% to Rs 7,875 crore. Provisions dropped 43% to Rs 516 crore. The stock still fell 5% to Rs 363 in early trade.

Zen Technologies Ltd crashed over 12% after posting standalone PAT of Rs 14.75 crore, down 83% YoY. Revenue from operations fell 75.5% to Rs 71.90 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of Re 1 per share.

Jindal Steel and Power Ltd delivered the biggest beat of the morning. Consolidated PAT came in at Rs 1,861.6 crore versus analyst expectation of Rs 886.4 crore. Revenue rose 23% YoY to Rs 16,217.9 crore. EBITDA jumped to Rs 2,939 crore with margin at 18.1%. The board declared a dividend of Rs 2 per share.

ACC Ltd, part of the same Adani Group as Ambuja, missed estimates. PAT fell to Rs 242.6 crore versus expectation of Rs 421.9 crore. EBITDA per tonne dropped 30% YoY to Rs 527. Margin contracted to 8.8% from 13.7% a year ago.

CDSL declared a dividend of Rs 12.5 per share. FY26 net profit stood at Rs 45,507 lakh with EPS of Rs 21.82. The quarter was softer at Rs 7,979 lakh due to lower market activity.
