Markets to Enter "Drag Phase," Not Deep Correction: Vikas Khemani

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vikas Khemani predicts a "prolonged drag phase" for Indian equities rather than a sharp correction or breakout rally
  • 02/Energy prices remain the single biggest overhang; Q1 earnings will reflect war-driven supply chain disruptions
  • 03/India's domestic growth stays intact; manufacturing, IT, and select NBFCs offer bottom-up opportunities

Vikas Khemani of Carnelian Asset Management predicts Indian markets will enter a prolonged "drag phase." He does not expect a deep correction or sharp breakout in the near term.

In a conversation with ET Now, Khemani identified sticky energy prices as the single biggest risk factor. Inflation, interest rates, and currency movement are all linked to oil trajectory.

Khemani noted that Q1 earnings will reflect the true impact of war-driven disruptions. Supply chain pressures and elevated energy costs were not fully visible in recent quarterly results.

"I do not expect much fall to be honest unless something again worsens in the oil... it will just hang in there." - Vikas Khemani, Founder, Carnelian Asset Management

India's macro resilience remains intact despite global headwinds. Volume growth has been strong across consumer, automobiles, insurance, and credit segments. The investment cycle has held firm.

Manufacturing and capex themes remain structurally strong. Currency depreciation benefits exporters and strengthens demand drivers for select domestic plays.

Khemani sees IT as a contrarian opportunity with reasonable valuations. He highlighted bottom-up opportunities across manufacturing, pharma, CDMO, BFSI, and select IT names.

On NBFCs, Khemani advocates a stock-specific approach. His fund's holding in Aditya Birla Capital has risen 3.5x over three years from an entry near Rs 100.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT MARKET OUTLOOK DOES VIKAS KHEMANI PREDICT?
VIKAS KHEMANI OF CARNELIAN ASSET MANAGEMENT PREDICTS A PROLONGED "DRAG PHASE" FOR INDIAN EQUITIES. HE DOES NOT EXPECT A SHARP CORRECTION OR A STRAIGHT-LINE RALLY, BUT RATHER A CONSOLIDATION PHASE UNTIL ENERGY PRICES STABILISE.
WHAT IS THE SINGLE BIGGEST RISK FACTOR FOR MARKETS ACCORDING TO KHEMANI?
ENERGY PRICES AND STICKY OIL COSTS ARE THE SINGLE BIGGEST OVERHANG. INFLATION, INTEREST RATES, CURRENCY MOVEMENT, AND US YIELDS ARE ALL LINKED TO THE TRAJECTORY OF ENERGY PRICES.
HOW WILL Q1 EARNINGS BE IMPACTED?
Q1 EARNINGS WILL REFLECT THE TRUE IMPACT OF WAR-DRIVEN SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTIONS AND ELEVATED ENERGY COSTS. THE RECENT QUARTERLY RESULTS LARGELY PREDATED THE FULL EFFECT OF THESE GLOBAL DISRUPTIONS.
WHICH SECTORS DOES KHEMANI FIND ATTRACTIVE?
HE SEES OPPORTUNITIES IN MANUFACTURING, PHARMA, CDMO, BFSI, AND SELECT IT NAMES. HE VIEWS IT AS A CONTRARIAN OPPORTUNITY WITH REASONABLE VALUATIONS AND MID-TEENS EARNINGS GROWTH POTENTIAL.
WHAT IS KHEMANI'S VIEW ON MID AND SMALLCAPS?
KHEMANI BELIEVES THE REAL ALPHA LIES IN BOTTOM-UP STOCK PICKING RATHER THAN INDEX-LEVEL ASSUMPTIONS. HIS FUND IS FULLY INVESTED AND TARGETS COMPANIES THAT CAN DOUBLE EARNINGS IN THREE TO FOUR YEARS.
