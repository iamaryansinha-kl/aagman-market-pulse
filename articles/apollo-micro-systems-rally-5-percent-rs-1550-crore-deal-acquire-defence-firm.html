Apollo Micro Systems Shares Rally 5% on Rs 1,550 Crore Deal to Acquire Defence Firm

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Apollo Micro Systems is expanding its defence footprint.

Shares of the Hyderabad-based company rallied 5% after it announced a Rs 1,550 crore deal to acquire a defence firm. The acquisition strengthens Apollo Micro Systems' position in the defence electronics sector and expands its capabilities in areas like avionics, radars, and communication systems.

The target company is a defence electronics manufacturer with established relationships with the Indian armed forces and defence public sector undertakings. The acquisition gives Apollo Micro Systems access to new product lines, technical capabilities, and a broader customer base within the defence ecosystem.

The deal is strategically timed. India's defence budget has been growing steadily, and the government is pushing aggressively for indigenisation under the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative. Domestic defence companies are benefiting from policies that restrict imports and encourage local manufacturing.

Apollo Micro Systems has been building its defence capabilities through both organic growth and acquisitions. The company supplies electronic systems to the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force, including missile guidance systems, electronic warfare suites, and communication equipment.

The Rs 1,550 crore deal will be funded through a combination of internal accruals and debt. The acquisition is expected to be earnings accretive from the first year, with cost synergies from combined operations and cross-selling opportunities.

The defence sector is seeing consolidation. Several mid-sized defence companies are acquiring smaller firms to build comprehensive capabilities and compete for larger contracts. The government prefers vendors with end-to-end capabilities, driving a wave of mergers and acquisitions.
