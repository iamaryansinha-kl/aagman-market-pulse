Iran Rial Hits Record Low of 1.8 Million to $1 Amid Shaky Ceasefire

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Iran's rial plunged to a record low of 1.8 million per US dollar on April 29, 2026, after holding steady for weeks during the war
  • 02/The currency began sliding two days ago as a shaky ceasefire with the US and Israel continues; a US blockade persists on oil shipments
  • 03/Experts warn the rial's fall will fuel inflation on imported goods including food, medicine, electronics, and raw materials

Iran's national rial currency crashed to a record low of 1.8 million to $1 on Wednesday. The slide began two days prior as trading resumed.

The rial had remained stable for weeks during the war with the US and Israel. The conflict began on February 28, 2026.

A shaky ceasefire is now in place but a US blockade continues. The blockade stops and intercepts Iranian oil shipments.

Oil exports are a key source of government revenue and hard currency for Iran. The blockade is squeezing the battered economy further.

Experts warn the rial's collapse will fuel inflation across imported goods. Food, medicine, electronics, and raw materials are all dollar-linked.

Iran relies heavily on imports for essential commodities. The falling rial raises prices for ordinary citizens already facing economic hardship.

The record low comes despite weeks of relative currency stability during active conflict. Markets now appear more fearful of the blockade than the war itself.

WHEN DID THE IRANIAN RIAL BEGIN TO FALL?
THE RIAL BEGAN TO SLIDE TWO DAYS BEFORE APRIL 29, 2026. IT HAD REMAINED STABLE FOR WEEKS DURING THE WAR WITH THE US AND ISRAEL THAT BEGAN ON FEBRUARY 28, 2026.
WHY DID THE RIAL REMAIN STABLE DURING THE WAR BUT FALL NOW?
THE RIAL HELD STEADY DURING ACTIVE COMBAT BECAUSE THERE WAS LITTLE TRADING OR IMPORTS COMING INTO IRAN. AS ACTIVITY RESUMED UNDER THE SHAKY CEASEFIRE, MARKET PRESSURES RETURNED AND THE CURRENCY COLLAPSED.
WHAT IS THE IMPACT OF THE US BLOCKADE ON IRAN'S ECONOMY?
THE US BLOCKADE CONTINUES TO STOP AND INTERCEPT IRANIAN OIL SHIPMENTS. THIS CUTS INTO A KEY SOURCE OF GOVERNMENT REVENUE AND HARD CURRENCY, INCREASING PRESSURE ON THE ALREADY-BATTERED ECONOMY.
HOW WILL THE FALLING RIAL AFFECT IRANIAN CITIZENS?
EXPERTS WARN THE FALL OF THE RIAL WILL FURTHER FUEL INFLATION. MANY IMPORTED GOODS INCLUDING FOOD, MEDICINE, ELECTRONICS, AND RAW MATERIALS ARE PRICED ACCORDING TO THE DOLLAR RATE, MAKING ESSENTIAL ITEMS MORE EXPENSIVE.
