Sensex Jumps 609 Points to 77,496; Nifty Hits 24,177 on Earnings Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex rallied 609.45 points (0.79%) to settle at 77,496.36 on April 29, 2026, reversing previous day's losses
  • 02/Nifty 50 gained 181.95 points (0.76%) to 24,177.65, led by Maruti Suzuki (+4%) and ITC (+2.5%) on Q4 earnings
  • 03/Rupee hit record closing low of 94.8450 amid oil surge; US Fed decision and UAE OPEC exit weigh on near-term sentiment

Sensex gained 609.45 points or 0.79% to close at 77,496.36 on April 29, 2026. The Nifty 50 index rose 181.95 points or 0.76% to settle at 24,177.65.

The rally reversed the previous session's losses when Sensex had dropped 416 points. Gains were driven by stock-specific action after quarterly earnings announcements.

Maruti Suzuki surged over 4% to ₹13,319 after reporting Q4 revenue of ₹52,449 crore, up 29% year-on-year. ITC, Tech Mahindra, Coal India and M&M were among other top Nifty gainers.

On the flip side, IndiGo, Dr Reddy's Laboratories, JSW Steel and ICICI Bank lagged the index. Broader markets joined the rally with small-caps rising around 1%.

Sectorally, realty and FMCG stocks led gains with each advancing over 2%. Media and consumer durables were the only indices in negative territory.

The rupee closed at a record low of 94.8450 per US dollar, down 0.3%. Brent crude eased to $104.02 after the UAE announced its OPEC exit effective May 1.

"Nifty continues to trade near a crucial support zone, with 23,900–24,000 as immediate support and 24,200–24,300 as key resistance," said Gaurav Udani, Founder of ThinCredBlu Securities.

Markets remain watchful of the US Federal Reserve's interest rate decision due Wednesday. Foreign investors sold ₹2,104 crore on April 28 while domestic buyers invested ₹1,712 crore.
