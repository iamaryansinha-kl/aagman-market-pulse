Bajel Projects Secures ₹400 Crore+ 500 kV Transmission Orders in MENA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajel Projects secures two international orders cumulatively exceeding ₹400 crore for 500 kV overhead transmission lines in the MENA region.
  • 02/The contracts are part of a flagship national grid reinforcement programme aimed at strengthening high-voltage infrastructure across West Asia and North Africa.
  • 03/The win builds on the company's MENA presence including its 50:50 joint venture with the Al Sharif Group in Saudi Arabia.

Bajel Projects has secured two international orders cumulatively exceeding ₹400 crore. The contracts cover construction of two contiguous sections of 500 kV overhead transmission lines.

The company announced the wins on April 29, 2026. Both orders fall in the ultra-mega band.

The project forms part of a flagship national grid reinforcement programme. It aims to strengthen the high-voltage backbone across the MENA region.

The infrastructure will enable reliable power evacuation. This supports growing economic and industrial demand across West Asia and North Africa.

Rajesh Ganesh, Managing Director & CEO, said the wins reflect global confidence in Bajel's engineering and execution capabilities.

"Winning these orders is a defining moment for Bajel Projects," Ganesh said. "We remain committed to delivering to the highest standards of quality and safety."

The order strengthens Bajel's footprint in MENA. It complements the company's strong domestic book and recent international wins.

Bajel operates a 50:50 joint venture with the Al Sharif Group in Saudi Arabia. This consolidates its position as a trusted international EPC partner.
