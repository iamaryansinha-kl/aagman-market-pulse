NSDL Q4 PAT Up 8.4% to ₹90.3 Crore; Board Recommends ₹4 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSDL Q4 consolidated PAT rises 8.4% y-o-y to ₹90.3 crore; total income jumps 23.6% to ₹486.8 crore
  • 02/Board recommends final dividend of ₹4 per share for FY26; standalone FY26 PAT climbs 12.1% to ₹360.6 crore
  • 03/Payments bank deposits cross ₹521 crore with 43.5 lakh accounts; UPI volumes surge nearly six-fold

National Securities Depository Limited (BSE: 544467) reported consolidated net profit of ₹90.3 crore for Q4 FY26. The figure marks an 8.4% increase over the year-ago period.

Consolidated total income for the March quarter rose 23.6% to ₹486.8 crore from ₹393.8 crore a year ago. The growth was driven by traction in its payments bank and database management businesses.

For the full year FY26, NSDL posted a 10.8% rise in net profit to ₹380 crore. Total income grew 8.1% to ₹1,660.2 crore.

The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹4 per equity share for FY26. Shareholders registered on the record date will be eligible for the payout.

During the year, NSDL added 21 new depository participants and 49.4 lakh net beneficiary owner accounts. Its net BO market share climbed to 14% for the year from around 9% earlier.

The company's payments bank subsidiary reported deposit balances crossing ₹521 crore with over 43.5 lakh accounts. UPI volumes grew nearly six-fold year-on-year.

On a standalone basis, Q4 net profit rose 5.2% to ₹79.7 crore on income of ₹195.4 crore. For FY26, standalone profit climbed 12.1% to ₹360.6 crore on income of ₹835.1 crore.

NSDL shares are listed only on the BSE. The stock closed at ₹882.60 on April 9, 2026, and trades below its 52-week high of ₹1,425. The company listed in August 2025 at ₹880.
