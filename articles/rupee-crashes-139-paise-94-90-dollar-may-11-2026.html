Rupee Crashes 139 Paise to 94.90 Against U.S. Dollar on May 11

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee crashes 139 paise to 94.90 against the U.S. dollar in early trade on May 11, 2026, after Trump rejects Iran's ceasefire response
  • 02/The local unit opened at 94.97 before inching up to 94.90; previous close was 93.51 on May 8
  • 03/Brent crude surged 4.17% to $105.5 per barrel; dollar index at 98.20; FPIs offloaded ₹4,110.60 crore on Friday

The rupee crashed 139 paise to 94.90 against the U.S. dollar in early trade on Monday. The local unit opened at 94.97 before recovering slightly to 94.90.

On Friday, the rupee had gained 71 paise to close at 93.51. The sharp reversal came after U.S. President Donald Trump rejected Iran's response to the American peace proposal.

Trump called Iran's proposal to end the months-long war "totally unacceptable". Crude oil prices surged rapidly following the rejection, pressuring the rupee lower.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, rose sharply by 4.17% to $105.5 per barrel. The dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength, was trading at 98.20, up 0.20%.

On the domestic equity market front, the Sensex plummeted 810.35 points, or 1.05%, to 76,517.84. The Nifty tanked 225.40 points, or 0.93%, to 23,950.75.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹4,110.60 crore on Friday, according to exchange data. India's forex reserves dropped by $7.794 billion to $690.693 billion during the week ended May 1.

"After U.S. President Donald Trump rejected Iran's response, Brent oil rose higher, dollar index also higher and risky assets lower," Anil Kumar Bhansali, Head of Treasury and Executive Director, Finrex Treasury Advisors LLP, said.

"Thus the war continues keeping oil prices higher and accordingly rupee opened lower than Friday's close," Bhansali added. The RBI was present near 94.70 levels on Friday, taking the closing higher to 94.48.
