Piramal Finance Surges 12% as Q4 Profit Jumps to ₹502 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Piramal Finance stock surges 12.5% to ₹2,066 after Q4 FY26 profit jumps 390% YoY to ₹502 crore on improved margins and retail mix.
  • 02/AUM crosses ₹1 lakh crore with retail book at 85%; legacy assets shrink to 3% as brokerages raise price targets.
  • 03/Nomura sees RoAUM hitting 2.5% by Q4 FY27; Jefferies flags valuation limits with a hold rating despite improving profitability.

Piramal Finance Ltd (NSE: PIRAMAL) rallied nearly 12.5% on April 28, 2026. The stock hit an intraday high of ₹2,073.20 and traded at ₹2,066.20 by 11:56 am, up from the previous close of ₹1,843.10.

The surge followed Q4 FY26 earnings released on April 27. Consolidated net profit jumped 390% year-on-year to ₹502 crore. The growth was driven by improved margins, a stronger retail mix, and one-off gains from asset sales.

Full-year FY26 profit after tax rose 210% to ₹1,506 crore. Assets under management crossed ₹1 lakh crore, growing 25% year-on-year. The retail segment now contributes 85% of the total book.

Net interest margin expanded to 6.5%, up 23 basis points from the prior year. The cost of funds fell 29 bps to 8.84%, while the retail operating expense-to-AUM ratio declined to 3.6%.

Asset quality improved. Gross NPAs fell to 2.3% and net NPAs to 1.6%, both down 30 basis points quarter-on-quarter. The growth book now accounts for about 97% of AUM.

"The company is cleaning up and scaling up well," said Nomura, which maintained a buy rating with a target price of ₹2,150. It expects return on assets under management to reach 2.5% by Q4 FY27 and exceed 3% by FY28.

Jefferies retained a hold rating with a target of ₹1,940, noting that valuations may limit upside given subdued return ratios. Emkay Global raised its target to ₹2,000 with an add rating, citing branch expansion and scaling of gold loans and microfinance. HDFC Securities also maintained an add rating with a revised target of ₹1,765.

Management has guided for around 25% AUM growth and roughly 50% profit growth in FY27. The company holds ₹8,640 crore in cash and liquid investments, with capital adequacy at a strong 19.8%.
