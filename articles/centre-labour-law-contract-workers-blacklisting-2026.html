Centre Tightens Labour Law for Contract Workers, Violations Can Trigger Blacklisting

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Department of Expenditure issued fresh instructions on May 8, 2026 mandating strict wage and PF compliance for all government contractors
  • 02/Defaulting contractors now face debarment for up to three years under amended Rule 151 of the General Financial Rules, 2017
  • 03/Drawing and Disbursing Officers must verify contractor wage compliance every month starting with immediate effect

The Centre has introduced a new procurement-linked compliance framework for contract workers through fresh instructions by the Department of Expenditure on May 8, 2026. The reforms apply across Central Ministries, Departments, autonomous bodies, and Central Public Sector Enterprises.

The Ministry of Labour and Employment said the move targets delayed wages, non-payment of provident fund, and weak contractor accountability. It operationalises safeguards under the four Labour Codes.

Under Section 55(3) of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, principal employers must ensure timely wage payments by contractors. Daily wage workers must be paid by the end of their shift.

Weekly wage earners must receive payment before the weekly holiday, fortnightly workers within two days of the period end, and monthly employees within seven days of the succeeding month. All payments must be made via bank transfer or electronic mode.

Contractors must now electronically inform principal employers after salary disbursement. Government departments must include penalty clauses in contracts for any delayed wage payments.

Contractors must submit bills by the 10th of every month, while Drawing and Disbursing Officers must clear payments by the 15th. Ministries must also earmark funds specifically for outsourced manpower payments on GeM platforms.

The Centre has amended Rule 151 of the General Financial Rules, 2017, expanding debarment grounds. Firms can now be excluded from government procurement for up to three years for failing to pay wages or deposit statutory social security contributions.

Principal employers can directly pay contract workers if contractors delay payments repeatedly. Such repeat violations will attract blacklisting and wider debarment action across all government procurement.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAS THE CENTRE MANDATED FOR GOVERNMENT CONTRACT WORKERS?
THE DEPARTMENT OF EXPENDITURE ISSUED FRESH INSTRUCTIONS ON MAY 8, 2026 REQUIRING ALL CONTRACTORS ENGAGED BY CENTRAL MINISTRIES, DEPARTMENTS, AUTONOMOUS BODIES AND CPSES TO PAY WAGES AND PROVIDENT FUND CONTRIBUTIONS ON STRICT TIMELINES. VIOLATIONS CAN LEAD TO BLACKLISTING AND UP TO THREE YEARS OF PROCUREMENT DEBARMENT.
WHAT ARE THE WAGE PAYMENT DEADLINES UNDER THE NEW RULES?
DAILY WAGE WORKERS MUST BE PAID BY THE END OF THE SHIFT, WEEKLY WAGE EARNERS BEFORE THE WEEKLY HOLIDAY, FORTNIGHTLY WORKERS WITHIN TWO DAYS OF THE PERIOD END, AND MONTHLY EMPLOYEES WITHIN SEVEN DAYS OF THE SUCCEEDING MONTH. ALL PAYMENTS MUST BE MADE THROUGH BANK TRANSFER OR ELECTRONIC MODE.
WHAT HAPPENS IF A CONTRACTOR REPEATEDLY DELAYS WAGE PAYMENTS?
PRINCIPAL EMPLOYERS CAN DIRECTLY PAY CONTRACT WORKERS IN CASES OF PROLONGED DELAY. REPEAT VIOLATIONS WILL ATTRACT BLACKLISTING AND WIDER DEBARMENT ACTION. THE AMENDED RULE 151 OF THE GENERAL FINANCIAL RULES, 2017 ALLOWS EXCLUSION FROM GOVERNMENT PROCUREMENT FOR UP TO THREE YEARS.
WHAT COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION IS REQUIRED FROM GOVERNMENT OFFICERS?
DRAWING AND DISBURSING OFFICERS (DDOS) MUST VERIFY CONTRACTOR WAGE PAYMENT COMPLIANCE EVERY MONTH. CONTRACTORS MUST SUBMIT BILLS BY THE 10TH OF EVERY MONTH AFTER WAGE DISBURSAL, WHILE DDOS MUST CLEAR PAYMENTS BY THE 15TH.
HOW DOES THIS FRAMEWORK PROTECT CONTRACT WORKERS?
THE FRAMEWORK OPERATIONALISES WORKER SAFEGUARDS UNDER ALL FOUR LABOUR CODES. IT MANDATES ELECTRONIC WAGE PAYMENTS, REQUIRES CONTRACTORS TO INFORM PRINCIPAL EMPLOYERS AFTER DISBURSEMENT, AND EMPOWERS GOVERNMENT DEPARTMENTS TO PENALISE DELAYS AND DIRECTLY PAY WORKERS WHEN CONTRACTORS DEFAULT.
