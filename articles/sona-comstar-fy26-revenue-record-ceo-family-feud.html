Sona Comstar FY26: Revenue Hits ₹4,475 Cr, CEO Dismisses Family Feud Impact

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sona Comstar reports Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹1,272 crore, up 47% YoY, with record BEV contribution at 39%
  • 02/FY26 full-year revenue rises 26% to ₹4,475 crore; net order book stands at ₹23,700 crore
  • 03/CEO Vivek Vikram Singh says promoter family feud has "zero impact" on operations, strategy or governance

Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd (Sona Comstar) (NSE: SONACOMS) posted record results for FY26 and Q4. Revenue for the full year rose 26% to ₹4,475 crore.

In the March quarter, revenue jumped 47% year-on-year to ₹1,272 crore. EBITDA grew 32% and net profit rose 17% during the same period.

Battery electric vehicle revenue touched a record 39% of total sales in Q4. All key operating metrics, including revenue, EBITDA, net profit and EV share, hit their highest-ever quarterly levels.

The company ended FY26 with a net order book of about ₹23,700 crore. CEO Vivek Vikram Singh described this as a 10-year pipeline of expected revenue across 67 EV programmes and 35 customers.

Capital expenditure for FY26 stood at approximately ₹350–400 crore. Singh said capacity has been built ahead of demand, particularly in traction motors, with no immediate need for large-scale capex.

A high-profile family dispute over control of the Sona Group, often pegged at ₹30,000 crore, has escalated into a legal battle. The Delhi High Court ordered status quo and the Supreme Court nudged mediation.

"There is zero impact on operations, on strategy, on governance, on how the company is run," Singh said. He called the ₹30,000 crore dispute estimate misleading, saying "I don't know how anybody came to that number."

Sona Comstar shares gained 3.5% following the court developments. The company operates with around 72% public shareholding and a majority-independent board, providing insulation from promoter-level disputes.
