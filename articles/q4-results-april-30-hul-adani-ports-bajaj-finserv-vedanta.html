Q4 Results April 30 Live: HUL, Adani Ports, Bajaj Finserv, Vedanta in Focus

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Over 50 BSE and NSE companies announce Q4 FY26 results on April 30, 2026, led by HUL, Adani Ports, Adani Enterprises, and Bajaj Finserv
  • 02/Bajaj Finance Q4 PAT rises 22% YoY to Rs 54.6 billion; Vedanta net profit surges 89% to Rs 9,352 crore on higher commodity prices
  • 03/Brokerages upgrade Bajaj Finance targets; Morgan Stanley and Citi see 22-24% AUM growth in FY27; watch HUL volume recovery and Adani Ports cargo guidance

Over 50 BSE and NSE listed companies report Q4 FY26 earnings on April 30, 2026. Marquee names include Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR), Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd (NSE: ADANIPORTS), and Bajaj Finserv Ltd (NSE: BAJAJFINSV).

Bajaj Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJFINANCE) reported Q4 net profit of Rs 54.6 billion, up 22% year-on-year. Revenue rose 18% to Rs 216.1 billion, while assets under management grew 22% to Rs 5.1 trillion.

Brokerages reacted positively to Bajaj Finance results. Morgan Stanley maintained Overweight with a target price of Rs 1,120, while Citi upgraded to Buy. CLSA set a target of Rs 1,200.

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) recorded an 89% jump in March quarter net profit to Rs 9,352 crore. Revenue from operations surged 30.6% YoY to Rs 52,851 crore, while EBITDA grew 60.9% to Rs 18,447 crore.

Waaree Energies reported Q4 consolidated net profit of Rs 10.6 billion versus Rs 6.19 billion a year ago. Revenue more than doubled to Rs 84.8 billion, though EBITDA margin compressed to 18.58% from 23.04% YoY.

Navin Fluorine International Ltd posted Q4 revenue of Rs 937.71 crore, up 33.78% YoY. EBITDA jumped 79.7% to Rs 321.15 crore, and PAT soared 123.9% to Rs 212.62 crore.

Street estimates expect Hindustan Unilever to deliver mid single-digit revenue growth on softer input costs. Investors will watch underlying volume growth and the final dividend recommendation. Adani Ports has guided for 7-9% cargo volume growth in FY26.

Benchmark indices opened lower on April 30. The Sensex fell 788 points to 76,707.80, while the Nifty 50 dropped 248 points to 23,929.50. All sectoral indices traded in negative territory.
