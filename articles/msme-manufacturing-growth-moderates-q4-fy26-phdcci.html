MSME Manufacturing Growth Moderates to 56.5 in Q4 FY26 on West Asia Crisis: PHDCCI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The SME Business Activity Index stood at 56.5 in Q4 FY26, down from 58.9 in the previous quarter, signaling slower expansion
  • 02/The SME Business Outlook Index for Q1 FY27 dipped to 58.7 from 60.7, reflecting cautious optimism amid West Asia crisis
  • 03/Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz disruptions have added 15-20 days to transit times, raising freight costs and insurance premiums for MSMEs

India's MSME manufacturing sector stayed in expansion territory during January-March 2026, but growth momentum weakened. The SME Business Activity Index fell to 56.5 in Q4 FY26 from 58.9 in Q3 FY26, according to the PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry's SME Market Sentiment Index (SMESI) Round 4.

The survey covered 3,000 manufacturing MSMEs across the country. It tracks two key metrics: the SME Business Activity Index (SME-BAI) and the SME Business Outlook Index (SME-BOI).

New orders and production drove the expansion, though at a slower clip. Around 37 per cent of surveyed firms reported higher new orders during the quarter.

Employment trends remained flat. Nearly 60 per cent of firms saw no change in hiring levels or supplier delivery timelines. Inventory levels improved moderately, pointing to gradual demand recovery.

Looking ahead, the SME Business Outlook Index for Q1 FY27 (April-June 2026) stood at 58.7. This is down from 60.7 in the prior quarter. About 37 per cent of firms expect business activity to expand in the coming quarter.

Capital expenditure plans look brighter. Around 47 per cent of firms expect to increase capex in Q1 FY27. Hiring sentiment is split: 27 per cent anticipate more hiring while 23 per cent expect a decline.

The West Asia crisis is taking a direct toll on operations. "Red Sea / Strait of Hormuz disruptions re-routing has added 15-20 days' transit time has caused trouble in inventory management," the report noted. Freight costs and marine insurance premiums have climbed, pushing up input and export costs for smaller manufacturers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE SME BUSINESS ACTIVITY INDEX FOR Q4 FY26?
THE SME BUSINESS ACTIVITY INDEX STOOD AT 56.5 IN Q4 FY26, INDICATING CONTINUED EXPANSION IN THE MSME MANUFACTURING SECTOR. HOWEVER, THIS IS DOWN FROM 58.9 RECORDED IN THE PREVIOUS QUARTER (Q3 FY26).
WHICH ORGANIZATION CONDUCTED THIS SURVEY AND HOW MANY FIRMS WERE COVERED?
THE SURVEY WAS CONDUCTED BY THE PHD CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY (PHDCCI) UNDER ITS SME MARKET SENTIMENT INDEX (SMESI) ROUND 4. IT COVERED 3,000 MANUFACTURING MSMEs ACROSS INDIA.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR Q1 FY27 (APRIL-JUNE 2026)?
THE SME BUSINESS OUTLOOK INDEX FOR Q1 FY27 REGISTERED 58.7, DOWN FROM 60.7 IN THE PREVIOUS QUARTER. ABOUT 37 PER CENT OF FIRMS EXPECT EXPANSION IN BUSINESS ACTIVITY, WHILE NEARLY HALF ANTICIPATE NO CHANGE.
HOW IS THE WEST ASIA CRISIS IMPACTING MSME MANUFACTURERS?
LOGISTICS DISRUPTIONS DUE TO REROUTING THROUGH THE RED SEA AND STRAIT OF HORMUZ HAVE INCREASED TRANSIT TIME BY 15-20 DAYS. FREIGHT COSTS AND MARINE INSURANCE PREMIUMS HAVE RISEN, ADDING TO INPUT AND EXPORT COSTS FOR MSMEs.
WHAT ARE THE KEY TRENDS IN EMPLOYMENT AND CAPEX?
EMPLOYMENT REMAINED MOSTLY UNCHANGED, WITH NEARLY 60 PER CENT OF FIRMS REPORTING NO CHANGE IN HIRING. HOWEVER, ABOUT 47 PER CENT OF FIRMS EXPECT AN INCREASE IN CAPITAL EXPENDITURE IN Q1 FY27, SHOWING CAUTIOUS INVESTMENT INTENT.
