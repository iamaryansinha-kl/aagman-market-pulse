UPL Q4 Profit Jumps 20% to ₹1,294 Cr on Strong Sales

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/UPL Q4 FY26 net profit rises 20% to ₹1,294 crore against ₹1,079 crore in the year-earlier quarter on robust sales growth
  • 02/Total income climbs 18% to ₹18,335 crore; full-year FY26 profit surges over two-fold to ₹2,220 crore from ₹820 crore
  • 03/Company repaid $500 million debt in March; management guidance exceeded across revenue, EBITDA and gearing metrics

UPL Ltd (NSE: UPL) reported consolidated net profit of ₹1,294 crore for Q4 FY26. Profit jumped 20% from ₹1,079 crore in the year-earlier quarter.

Total income rose 18% to ₹18,335 crore in the March quarter. The figure stood at ₹15,573 crore a year ago.

Expenses for the quarter climbed to ₹16,528 crore against ₹14,001 crore in the corresponding period last year.

For the full FY26, net profit surged more than two-fold to ₹2,220 crore. The previous year profit was ₹820 crore.

Full-year total income grew 11.15% to ₹51,839 crore from ₹46,637 crore in FY25. The company termed it a record year.

"Despite unprecedented macroeconomic headwinds testing the global agricultural sector, our resilient market leadership has proven to be our greatest strength," said Chairman and Group CEO Jai Shroff.

Group CFO Bikash Prasad said the company outperformed guidance on revenue, EBITDA and gearing. He noted PBT is four times versus the previous year.

The company repaid $500 million of debt in March. It also proactively refinanced short-term obligations due in September to enhance liquidity.
