EQT Closes Record $15.6B Asia Buyout Fund Amid Fundraising Slump

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EQT closed BPEA Private Equity Fund IX at $15.6 billion in total commitments, making it the largest Asia-Pacific-dedicated PE fund ever raised
  • 02/The fund was oversubscribed and hit its hard cap despite Asian PE fundraising falling to a 12-year low in 2025 after four straight years of decline
  • 03/BPEA IX is already 5-10% deployed and will focus on control investments in technology, healthcare, and industrial technology across Asia

Swedish private equity firm EQT has closed BPEA Private Equity Fund IX at $15.6 billion in total commitments. The fund reached its hard cap and was oversubscribed, with $14.9 billion in fee-generating assets under management.

The close was announced on April 21, 2026. It makes BPEA IX the largest Asia-Pacific-dedicated private equity fund raised to date, according to Preqin data.

The milestone stands out sharply against a bleak fundraising climate. Capital raised for Asian PE funds fell to a 12-year low in 2025, marking four consecutive years of decline, per Bain & Company.

Investor appetite remained robust despite the slowdown. The fund attracted over 75 new investors, including more than 45 from EQT's broader investment platform.

Commitments were globally diversified and broadly balanced across the Americas, Europe and the Middle East, and Asia Pacific. Pension funds and sovereign wealth funds were the leading contributors.

BPEA IX will target control investments in technology, healthcare, industrial technology, services, and technology services. It seeks businesses with resilient fundamentals where EQT can drive operational improvement.

The fund is already 5-10% deployed, including closed, signed, and announced transactions. EQT Private Capital Asia has deployed roughly $30 billion across more than 160 transactions since its founding in 1997.

EQT's Asia platform currently holds stakes in approximately 65 companies across 10 countries, employing over 270,000 people. In India alone, the firm manages over $8 billion across assets including HDFC Credila, Indira IVF, Sagility, and CitiusTech.

The fundraise validates EQT's 2022 merger with Baring Private Equity Asia, which deepened its regional footprint. EQT has since monetised investments including the $1.5 billion sale of renewable energy platform O2 Power to JSW Neo Energy.

"In a highly competitive and selective fundraising market, our ability to deliver consistent realizations was a differentiator for our investors," said Jean Eric Salata, chairperson of EQT Asia.
