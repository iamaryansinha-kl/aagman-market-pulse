Sebi Proposes IPO Pre-Open Session Changes for Better Price Discovery

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI has proposed a review of the pre-open call auction session for IPOs and relisted stocks to fix price suppression issues
  • 02/Current dummy price bands and base price calculations are causing artificial price distortions and frequent upper circuits
  • 03/The regulator has invited public comments on proposed changes; feedback window is open now

SEBI has proposed changes to the pre-open call auction session for IPOs and relisted stocks. The consultation paper was released on Tuesday, May 21, 2026.

The regulator wants to fix the current dummy price band and base price mechanism. Market participants say these rules are artificially suppressing price discovery.

Under existing rules, IPO and relisted stocks go through a one-hour pre-open session from 9 am to 10 am on listing day. Only limit orders are permitted; market orders are not allowed.

The current dummy price bands vary by category. IPO stocks trade within minus 50% to plus 100% of the base price. Relisted stocks use minus 85% to plus 50%, while SME IPOs use minus 90% to plus 90%.

SEBI cited one case where 90% of buy orders in a relisted scrip were rejected during the call auction session. They fell outside the dummy price bands, blocking participation.

The distortions cause persistent buying pressure once normal trading begins. This frequently leads to upper circuits and triggers additional surveillance measures.

SEBI now seeks public feedback on whether these mechanisms require changes. The goal is to improve efficiency and reduce price distortions amid evolving market conditions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAS SEBI PROPOSED REGARDING IPO PRE-OPEN SESSIONS?
SEBI HAS PROPOSED A REVIEW OF THE PRE-OPEN CALL AUCTION SESSION FRAMEWORK FOR IPOS AND RELISTED STOCKS. THE AIM IS TO FIX THE DUMMY PRICE BAND AND BASE PRICE MECHANISM THAT IS ARTIFICIALLY SUPPRESSING PRICE DISCOVERY.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT DUMMY PRICE BAND FOR IPO STOCKS?
FOR MAIN BOARD IPOS, THE DUMMY PRICE BAND RANGES FROM MINUS 50% TO PLUS 100% OF THE BASE PRICE. FOR RELISTED STOCKS, IT IS MINUS 85% TO PLUS 50%. SME IPOS USE A RANGE OF MINUS 90% TO PLUS 90%.
WHAT PROBLEM DID SEBI IDENTIFY WITH THE CURRENT RULES?
SEBI NOTED THAT THE DUMMY PRICE BAND AND BASE PRICE MECHANISM FOR RELISTED SCRIPS IS LEADING TO ARTIFICIALLY SUPPRESSED PRICE DISCOVERY. IN ONE INSTANCE, 90% OF BUY ORDERS WERE REJECTED FOR BEING OUTSIDE THE PRICE BANDS.
WHEN DOES THE PRE-OPEN CALL AUCTION SESSION TAKE PLACE?
THE PRE-OPEN CALL AUCTION SESSION RUNS FOR ONE HOUR FROM 9 AM TO 10 AM ON THE LISTING OR RELISTING DAY. ONLY LIMIT ORDERS ARE ALLOWED; MARKET ORDERS ARE NOT PERMITTED DURING THIS SESSION.
WHAT HAPPENS IF NO EQUILIBRIUM PRICE IS DISCOVERED DURING THE SESSION?
FOR IPOS, THE STOCK MOVES INTO NORMAL TRADING USING THE ISSUE PRICE AS THE BASE PRICE. FOR RELISTED STOCKS, ALL ORDERS ARE CANCELLED AND THE CALL AUCTION CONTINUES ON SUBSEQUENT TRADING DAYS UNTIL A PRICE IS DISCOVERED.
