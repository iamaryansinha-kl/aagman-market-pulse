Amazon's Rapid Delivery Push Wipes Out $15 Billion for Eternal, Swiggy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eternal and Swiggy have shed over $15 billion (Rs 1.4 lakh crore) as Amazon and Flipkart muscle into India's rapid delivery market
  • 02/Blinkit shares are down 28% from their October peak while Swiggy's Instamart has plunged 47% from September highs
  • 03/Amazon Now plans to expand to 300+ cities while Flipkart targets 1,500 dark stores, intensifying competition for years ahead

Eternal Ltd and Swiggy Ltd have lost a combined $15 billion (Rs 1.4 lakh crore) in market value. The rout follows an aggressive push by Amazon and Flipkart into India's booming quick commerce segment.

Blinkit, owned by Eternal, has slipped 28% from its October all-time high. Swiggy's Instamart has plunged about 47% from its September peak.

Amazon Now plans to expand from 15 cities to over 300 in India. The company also pledged to invest $13 billion in AI and cloud infrastructure in the country.

Flipkart Minutes has scaled to 1,000 dark stores across 130 cities. The Walmart-backed firm aims to reach 1,500 stores in 180-plus cities in the coming months.

Blinkit operated 2,243 dark stores as of March 31, per Macquarie. Swiggy trails with 1,143 dark stores.

"The challenge right now is that the competition is really high, so near-term profitability is depressed," said Franklin Templeton fund manager Yi Ping Liao. "The risk is the duration of the competitive intensity."

Macquarie analysts downgraded Swiggy to Underperform and slashed target prices for both firms. They expect "rising and persistent competitive intensity for years, not quarters."

Zepto, the third major player, plans a $1 billion IPO next month. Its unlisted shares have already fallen 32% since February, from 58 rupees to 39 rupees.
