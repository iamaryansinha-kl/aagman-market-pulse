India Food Delivery to Reach $27 Billion by 2030 on Tier-2 City Boom

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's online food delivery market is projected to triple from $9.1 billion in 2024 to $27 billion by 2030 at a 19% CAGR, per an Investec Equities report dated May 2, 2026.
  • 02/Tier-2+ cities now contribute 20-25% of gross order value, while Indian consumers average only 3-5 monthly orders versus 8-10 in the US.
  • 03/Watch Zomato and Swiggy as average order values climb from ₹282-₹407 in FY22 toward ₹461-₹463 in FY26E, with Redseer projecting ₹542 by 2028.

India's online food delivery market is entering a new growth phase driven by rising order frequency and deeper Tier-2+ city penetration, according to an Investec Equities report dated May 2, 2026.

The sector is projected to expand from $9.1 billion in 2024 to nearly $27 billion by 2030, clocking a 19 per cent compound annual growth rate.

The broader food services market has grown from ₹4 trillion in FY20 to ₹7 trillion in FY25, and is expected to hit ₹11-12 trillion by FY30.

Online food delivery specifically has scaled from ₹270 billion in FY20 to ₹790 billion in FY25, targeting ₹1.7-2.2 trillion by FY30 at 17-23 per cent CAGR.

Penetration remains low at 13 per cent of total food consumption, far below 58-62 per cent in the US and 42-46 per cent in China, leaving significant runway.

The ecosystem counted 85 million annual transacting users in 2024, with 23 million converting to monthly users. That share is forecast to climb to 32 per cent by 2029 as the user base reaches 137 million.

Indian consumers currently place just 3-5 orders per month, well below the 8-10 seen in the US and 5-10 in China. Average order values are also climbing, with Zomato's AOV rising from ₹282 in FY22 to ₹461 in FY26E, and Swiggy's from ₹407 to ₹463.

Redseer projects industry-wide AOV will reach ₹542 by 2028 at 6-8 per cent CAGR. With organised market share expanding from 36-41 per cent in FY20 to 47-52 per cent in FY25, Investec sees a long runway for delivery-led players.
