S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 Tumble as OpenAI Sales Miss Fuels Tech Jitters

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/S&P 500 fell 0.7% and Nasdaq 100 dropped 1.3% after a report said OpenAI missed user acquisition and sales targets
  • 02/OpenAI rival Anthropic PBC gained ground in coding and enterprise markets, according to the Wall Street Journal
  • 03/Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon and Meta report earnings on Wednesday; Apple follows Thursday with AI demand in focus

The S&P 500 fell 0.7% and the Nasdaq 100 lost 1.3% on April 28. A report that OpenAI missed sales and user targets dragged technology stocks lower.

The Wall Street Journal reported that OpenAI missed several internal sales targets. Rival Anthropic PBC gained ground in the coding and enterprise markets.

OpenAI pushed back against the report. The ChatGPT maker said its consumer and enterprise businesses are "firing on all cylinders."

Oracle Corp and CoreWeave Inc slumped alongside the broader tech sell-off. Big tech companies represent about a quarter of the S&P 500's total value.

Alphabet Inc, Microsoft Corp, Amazon.com Inc and Meta Platforms report earnings on Wednesday. Apple Inc is scheduled to follow on Thursday.

"The most important question for investors is whether the AI train can keep driving the market forward," said Dennis Follmer at Montis Financial.

Tech sector earnings are expected to have grown 41% in the first quarter. The results have been largely shielded from Iran war disruptions.

Brent crude rose to around $111 amid Middle East tensions. West Texas Intermediate crude gained 3.1% to $99.31 a barrel.
