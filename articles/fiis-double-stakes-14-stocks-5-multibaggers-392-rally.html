FIIs Double Stakes in 14 Stocks; 5 Multibaggers, Rally Up To 392%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FIIs doubled holdings in 14 BSE 1000 stocks between March 2025 and March 2026, with MTAR Technologies surging 392%
  • 02/Five stocks turned multibaggers including Ather Energy up 193% and Precision Wires India up 125%
  • 03/Around 51 stocks saw FII holdings more than double; 22 delivered over 25% gains in the past year

Foreign Institutional Investors doubled their stakes in 14 stocks within the BSE 1000 universe between March 2025 and March 2026. The data from ACE Equity reveals a clear accumulation pattern by FIIs.

MTAR Technologies led the rally with a 392% surge. FII holding in the stock jumped from 6.74% in March 2025 to 17.31% in March 2026. The share price rose from Rs 1,613 to Rs 7,939.

Ather Energy saw its FII stake climb from 7.00% to 17.21%. The stock gained 193%, moving from Rs 341 to Rs 998. Precision Wires India rose 125% as FII holding went from 0.47% to 1.65%.

Five stocks delivered multibagger returns. Jayaswal Neco Industries climbed 114% and Timex Group India advanced 103%. Both saw FII holdings rise sharply from near-zero levels.

Among other notable performers, Hitachi Energy India gained 73% with FII stake rising to 11.68%. Bank of Maharashtra rose 63% as foreign holding increased to 5.55%.

The broader trend shows 51 stocks in the BSE 1000 saw FII holdings more than double. Around 22 of these delivered gains exceeding 25% in the past year.

The stakes moved up and down through the year. But the March 2025 to March 2026 comparison shows strong conviction-based buying by foreign investors.
