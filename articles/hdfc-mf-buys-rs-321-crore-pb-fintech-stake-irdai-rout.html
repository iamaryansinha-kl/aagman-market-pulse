HDFC Mutual Fund Buys Rs 321 Crore PB Fintech Stake as Irdai Reform Rout Deepens

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

HDFC Mutual Fund bought 25 lakh shares of PB Fintech at Rs 1,282.30 per share on September 24, according to NSE bulk deal data, in a transaction worth about Rs 321 crore.

The purchase came on a day when shares of PB Fintech, the parent company of Policybazaar and Paisabazaar, plunged 36% following the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India's proposed overhaul of insurance distribution rules.

The Irdai Shock

Irdai's consultation paper proposes changes to the way insurers pay commissions to distributors. Under the proposed framework, commission limits would be linked to factors including the type of insurance product, distribution channel, size of the policy and the effort involved in selling it.

The regulator has also proposed bringing back commission caps across life, health and motor insurance — caps that were removed in 2023.

PB Fintech shares breached multiple circuit limits during Thursday's trading session before closing at Rs 1,207 apiece on the NSE, a 52-week low. The stock recorded its worst single-day fall since its listing in November 2021. The decline wiped out more than Rs 31,426 crore from PB Fintech's market capitalisation, which stood at Rs 55,863.51 crore at the end of Thursday's session.

Revenue Impact Warning

For PB Fintech, the immediate concern is the potential impact on its general insurance business. Co-founder and group CEO Yashish Dahiya told analysts on September 24 that the proposed commission structure could reduce the company's revenue from general insurance to between one-third and 40% of its current level.

Dahiya said the proposed commission caps could have a severe impact on PB Fintech's non-life insurance business, potentially prompting tighter spending and slower hiring. The regulatory changes could force the company to rethink the pace and shape of its expansion.

Buying the Dip

HDFC MF's bulk purchase signals institutional confidence in the stock even amid the regulatory uncertainty. PB Fintech operates as a digital aggregator, connecting consumers with financial partners rather than retaining credit or insurance risk. Founded in 2008 and based in Gurugram, it runs Policybazaar and Paisabazaar.
