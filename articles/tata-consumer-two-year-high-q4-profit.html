Tata Consumer Hits Two-Year High After Q4 Profit Jumps 22%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Consumer Products stock surged 6.6% to a two-year high of ₹1,254 on May 11 after Q4 profit jumped 22%
  • 02/Q4 revenue grew 18% to ₹5,434 crore; FY26 crossed ₹20,000 crore with 20% profit growth
  • 03/26 analysts rate it "buy" with median target of ₹1,315; BoB Capital sees 18% earnings CAGR through FY29

Tata Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: TATACONSUM) shares surged as much as 6.6% on Monday, May 11, 2026. The stock touched ₹1,254.2, its highest level in over two years.

As of 11:00 a.m. IST, the stock traded at ₹1,221.9, up 3.9%. It was the top gainer on the Nifty FMCG index, which slipped 0.1%.

The rally followed Q4 FY26 results announced on May 8. Consolidated revenue rose 18% year-on-year to ₹5,433.62 crore. Net profit climbed 22% to ₹424.02 crore.

FY26 revenue crossed ₹20,000 crore for the first time, reaching ₹20,280.43 crore. Full-year net profit grew 20% to ₹1,546.80 crore. The board recommended a ₹10 per share dividend.

BoB Capital analysts expect sales, EBITDA and earnings CAGR of 10%, 14% and 18% through FY29. CLSA backed the growth trajectory, citing e-commerce and quick commerce channels.

Systematix analysts noted a broad-based volume-led beat. Easing tea costs and improving portfolio mix helped expand Q4 EBITDA margin by 100 basis points to 14.57%.

26 analysts rate the stock "buy" on average, with a median price target of ₹1,315 per LSEG data. The 52-week range is ₹1,007.20 to ₹1,220.90.

"We delivered another quarter of consistent double-digit topline growth," said Sunil D'Souza, Managing Director and CEO. The company's growth businesses now account for 31% of India revenue.
