Chennai Metro Ridership Crosses 90 Lakh in April 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Chennai Metro Rail Ltd recorded 90,18,069 passengers during April 2026
  • 02/Peak single-day ridership touched 3,65,807 passengers during the month
  • 03/NCMC Singara Chennai Card accounted for nearly half of all ticketing modes

Chennai Metro Rail Ltd (CMRL) recorded 90,18,069 passengers during April 2026. The ridership figures cover the period from April 1 to April 30.

The metro system logged its highest single-day ridership for the month at 3,65,807 passengers. The exact date of the peak was not disclosed by CMRL.

The NCMC Singara Chennai Card remained the most preferred ticketing option. It accounted for nearly half of all modes of ticket purchase during the month.

Other ticketing options included CMRL Travel Card, single-journey Paper QR/Token, and Static QR at stations. CMRL did not provide a detailed split across these remaining modes.

The April 2026 figure follows strong ridership in 2025. Chennai Metro first crossed the one-crore mark in July 2025 with 1.03 crore passengers.

Ridership crossed the one-crore mark again in September 2025. The system recorded 1.01 crore passengers during that month.

The metro network currently spans operational corridors across Chennai. It serves key commercial and residential zones in the city.
