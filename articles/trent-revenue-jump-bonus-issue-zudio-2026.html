Trent Revenue Jumps 20%, Declares Bonus Issue As Zudio Powers Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Trent Ltd posts 20% revenue jump for FY2026 as budget fashion chain Zudio continues aggressive pan-India expansion
  • 02/Board approves bonus issue; details on ratio and record date expected to be announced shortly
  • 03/Zudio's value-format stores remain the primary growth engine, offsetting softer trends in Westside premium retail

Trent Ltd has reported a 20% jump in revenue for the financial year ended March 2026. The Tata Group retail company declared a bonus issue alongside the results.

The board approved the bonus share issue on April 22, 2026. The record date and ratio will be communicated to stock exchanges in a separate filing.

Zudio, Trent's mass-market fashion format, continues to drive the bulk of topline growth. The chain added over 100 stores during the fiscal year, crossing the 500-store mark nationwide.

Average revenue per store at Zudio remains above ₹2 crore per month. The format targets tier-2 and tier-3 cities where organised retail penetration is still low.

Westside, Trent's premium lifestyle brand, reported mid-single-digit same-store sales growth. The segment faced margin pressure due to higher marketing spends and inventory liquidation ahead of season change.

Trent shares have risen 35% over the past twelve months. The stock trades at a premium valuation compared to peers Aditya Birla Fashion and Reliance Retail.

Analysts at Morgan Stanley maintain an "overweight" rating. They expect Zudio to contribute over 60% of Trent's revenue by FY2028.
