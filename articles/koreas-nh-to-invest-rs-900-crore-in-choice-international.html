Korea's NH to Invest Rs 900 Crore in Choice International Broking Arm

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

A Korean brokerage is buying into Indian retail trading.

NH Investment & Securities, one of South Korea's largest financial services firms, will invest Rs 900 crore in the broking arm of Choice International. The deal gives the Korean company a strategic foothold in India's rapidly growing retail brokerage market and provides Choice with capital to expand its platform.

Choice International is a mid-sized brokerage and financial services firm. The company operates a retail broking platform, investment advisory services, and lending operations. The investment from NH will specifically target the broking business, which has seen explosive growth in the post-pandemic era.

India's retail brokerage market has attracted global interest. The number of demat accounts has surged past 20 crore, and daily trading volumes have reached record highs. Foreign brokers see India as the next major growth market after China, with a young, digitally savvy population increasingly participating in equity markets.

NH Investment & Securities is a major player in Korea. The firm offers brokerage, asset management, and investment banking services. Its investment in Choice reflects confidence in India's long-term growth story and the structural shift toward financialisation of savings.

The deal is structured as a strategic investment. NH will likely get a board seat and involvement in key decisions. Choice International will use the capital to upgrade technology, expand its client base, and potentially enter new segments like wealth management or international trading.

Regulatory approval will be required. Foreign investment in Indian brokerage firms is permitted under certain conditions, and the deal will need clearance from Sebi and other authorities.
