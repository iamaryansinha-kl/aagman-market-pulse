Sensex Nifty Slide at Open: Crude at $120, FII Sell ₹2,468 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex opened 679 points lower at 76,816 and Nifty fell 224 points to 23,954 as Brent crude surged past $120 per barrel.
  • 02/FIIs sold ₹2,468 crore on April 29 while DIIs bought ₹2,262 crore; India VIX held near 17.43 reflecting sustained uncertainty.
  • 03/Markets are closed on Friday for Maharashtra Day; Nifty technical support sits at 23,900–23,800 with resistance at 24,200–24,300.

Indian equity markets opened sharply lower on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The BSE Sensex dropped 679 points or 0.88% to open at 76,816, while the NSE Nifty 50 fell 224 points or 0.92% to 23,954.

Brent crude oil briefly breached $120 per barrel in early trade. Supply disruptions linked to the US naval blockade of Iranian ports and the effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz drove the spike.

Foreign Institutional Investors sold equities worth ₹2,468 crore on April 29, extending sustained selling pressure. Domestic Institutional Investors provided partial support with net purchases of ₹2,262 crore, though the outflow gap remains a concern.

The US Federal Reserve held its benchmark interest rate steady at 3.50%–3.75% for the third consecutive meeting. Four FOMC members dissented, the highest dissent count since October 1992, underscoring internal debate on inflation risks.

Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments Limited, warned on the macro impact. "Brent crude at $120 threatens to worsen India's macros; the downside risk to growth and upside risk to inflation will rise if crude price remains elevated at this level," he said.

India VIX, the market's fear gauge, held near 17.43, indicating continued nervousness. Markets are closed on Friday for Maharashtra Day, adding overnight positioning risk ahead of a long weekend.

Aakash Shah, Technical Research Analyst at Choice Equity Broking, tied the decline to global cues. "The weakness comes amid rising crude oil prices due to Iran-related tensions and cautious global sentiment after the US Federal Reserve maintained its interest rate stance," he said.

On the sectoral front, Shriram Finance fell 2.42% to ₹933.65, while IndiGo dropped 2.31% to ₹4,245. Bajaj Finance bucked the trend, rising 2.53% to ₹953.55 after Q4 earnings, with ONGC up 1.99% on higher oil prices.

Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, highlighted the dependency on external factors. "Market sentiment remains fragile and highly news-driven, with crude oil volatility, geopolitical developments, currency pressures, and institutional flows acting as key drivers," he said.

Technically, Nifty finds immediate support at 23,900–23,800, with resistance at 24,200–24,300. A sustained move above 24,500 would be needed to confirm bullish continuation, according to analysts tracking the session.
