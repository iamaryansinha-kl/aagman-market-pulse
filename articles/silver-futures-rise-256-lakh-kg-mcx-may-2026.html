Silver Futures Rise to ₹2.56 Lakh Per kg on MCX, Up 1.21%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Silver futures for July delivery rose 1.21% to ₹2,56,333 per kg on the Multi Commodity Exchange on May 7, 2026
  • 02/Fresh positions built by traders drove the rally amid ongoing uncertainty over a West Asia peace deal
  • 03/MCX silver turnover stood at 1,053 lots as investors tracked geopolitical developments

Silver futures for July delivery rose 1.21 per cent to ₹2.56 lakh per kg on May 7, 2026. The contract settled at ₹2,56,333 on the Multi Commodity Exchange.

The white metal gained ₹3,068 per kilogram during the session. Total business turnover stood at 1,053 lots.

Fresh positions built by market participants led the rally. Uncertainty over a West Asia peace deal supported buying interest.

Silver often draws safe-haven demand during geopolitical stress. The metal has seen sharp swings in recent months.

Gold futures also edged higher on May 7 on spot demand. Precious metals typically move together during global uncertainty.

Traders are watching West Asia developments for further price direction. Any peace deal breakthrough could trigger profit-booking.

MCX silver remains sensitive to global cues and domestic positioning. Volume at 1,053 lots reflects cautious participation.
