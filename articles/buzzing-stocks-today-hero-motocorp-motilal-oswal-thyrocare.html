Buzzing Stocks Today: Hero MotoCorp, Motilal Oswal, Thyrocare in Focus

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hero MotoCorp opens 2 new PREMIA stores in Tamil Nadu, taking state total to 11 outlets
  • 02/Motilal Oswal gets PFRDA approval as NPS pension fund sponsor; Clean Max restructures Apple India deal at ₹4,377/share
  • 03/Brigade announces 1:3 bonus issue; Wadhwa Group targets ₹650 crore inventory monetisation at Panvel township

Shares of multiple companies are in focus today following fresh deals, capacity expansions, regulatory approvals and bonus issue announcements. Key names include Hero MotoCorp, Motilal Oswal, Clean Max, Brigade, Thyrocare, Wadhwa Group, Motherson, Gandhar Oil, Zaggle and Shringar House of Mangalsutra.

Hero MotoCorp has opened two new Hero PREMIA stores in Dindigul and Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. The double launch brings the total number of Premia stores in the state to 11, reflecting the brand's aggressive expansion in the premium two-wheeler segment.

Motilal Oswal Asset Management Company Ltd has received approval from the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). The company will act as a Sponsor of Pension Fund under the National Pension System (NPS) and will set up a separate pension fund entity as investment manager.

Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions executed an amended investment agreement with Apple India Pvt Ltd and Clean Max Taurus Pvt Ltd. Clean Max will hold about 51% and Apple India 49% of Taurus, with equity priced at ₹4,377 per share. Share transfers under the amended agreement should be completed by May 12.

Brigade Enterprises Ltd has recommended a bonus issue of equity shares in the proportion of 1:3. The record date for reckoning eligible shareholders will be communicated separately after member approval.

The Wadhwa Group has partnered with ArisUnitern RE Solutions, a subsidiary of Arisinfra Solutions Ltd, for its Wadhwa Wise City township in Panvel, Maharashtra. The alliance will focus on monetising approximately ₹650 crore in inventory, with plans to release an additional ₹400-450 crore.

Thyrocare Technologies Ltd has launched a genomics laboratory in Navi Mumbai. The facility uses next-generation sequencing technologies and is supported by a specialised on-ground team for seamless delivery of advanced genomic testing.

Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd has secured support from the Abu Dhabi Investment Office (ADIO) for its manufacturing hub in Abu Dhabi. Gandhar Oil Refinery has purchased a land parcel measuring 18,353 square metres at Silvassa adjoining its present factory. Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services signed a three-year agreement with Supreme Industries Ltd for its Propel reward platform.
