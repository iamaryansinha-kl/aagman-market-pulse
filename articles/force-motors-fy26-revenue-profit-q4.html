Force Motors FY26 Revenue Up 12.2% to ₹9,056 Crore; Q4 Profit Falls 36%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Force Motors Ltd (NSE: FORCEMOT) reports FY26 revenue of ₹9,056.5 crore, up 12.2% YoY, with record net profit at ₹1,211 crore, a 51% jump
  • 02/Q4 standalone profit drops 36% to ₹273.7 crore from ₹429.6 crore due to absence of last year's exceptional gains
  • 03/Board recommends ₹50 per share dividend; Urbania and Trax platforms drive volume growth above 70%

Pune-based Force Motors Ltd reported its financial results for FY2026 on April 29, 2026. The company posted full-year revenue of ₹9,056.5 crore, up 12.2% from ₹8,071.2 crore in FY25.

Net profit for the full year surged 51% to a record ₹1,211 crore. This compares with ₹800 crore in the previous fiscal year.

However, standalone net profit for the March quarter fell 36% year-on-year to ₹273.7 crore. The drop was largely due to the absence of exceptional gains recorded in the year-ago period of ₹429.6 crore.

Revenue from operations for Q4 rose to ₹2,549.7 crore from ₹2,355.9 crore a year earlier. Profit before tax before exceptional items improved to ₹373.3 crore from ₹268.1 crore.

The annual performance included exceptional income of ₹288.6 crore from government incentives. This was partly offset by ₹77.4 crore in employee costs linked to new labour codes.

Operationally, domestic wholesales rose 20% during the year. The Traveller platform maintained over 70% market share in the van segment.

The Urbania platform recorded over 100% growth in volumes. The Trax range also saw more than 70% growth, supported by rural and semi-urban expansion.

"We have been a segment creator since our inception," said Prasan Firodia, Managing Director. The board recommended a dividend of ₹50 per share, subject to approval.
