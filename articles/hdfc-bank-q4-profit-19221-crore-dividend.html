HDFC Bank Q4 Profit Rises 9% to ₹19,221 Crore; Declares ₹13 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HDFC Bank Q4 FY26 net profit rose 9% to ₹19,221 crore versus ₹17,616 crore last year
  • 02/Net interest income grew 12% to ₹32,000 crore; NIM stable at 4.1%
  • 03/Board declared final dividend of ₹13 per share for FY26

HDFC Bank reported Q4 FY26 results on April 18, 2026. Standalone net profit rose 9% year-on-year to ₹19,221 crore from ₹17,616 crore in Q4 FY25.

Net interest income grew 12% to approximately ₹32,000 crore. Net interest margin remained stable at 4.1%.

The bank maintained strong credit growth across retail and corporate segments. Deposit growth also remained healthy at around 15% YoY.

Asset quality remained stable. Gross NPA ratio was largely unchanged while net NPA improved marginally.

The Board of Directors declared a final dividend of ₹13 per share. The total dividend for FY26 stands at ₹15.50 per share.

HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector lender. The results came broadly in line with analyst expectations.

The bank continues to invest in digital infrastructure. Technology spends are expected to remain elevated in the coming quarters.
