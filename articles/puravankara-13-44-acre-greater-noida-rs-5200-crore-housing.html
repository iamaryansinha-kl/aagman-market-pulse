Puravankara Buys 13.44-Acre Land in Greater Noida, Eyes Rs 5,200 Crore Revenue from Housing Project

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Realty firm Puravankara has acquired a 13.44-acre land parcel in Greater Noida to develop a housing project with an estimated revenue of Rs 5,200 crore.

The company disclosed in a regulatory filing on Tuesday that it has entered the Delhi-NCR real estate market with the allotment of the land parcel. The company did not disclose the land cost.

Project Details

The plot has a saleable potential of 4.57 million sq ft. The project is expected to generate Rs 5,200 crore in revenue.

Ashish Puravankara, the company's Managing Director, said NCR is one of India's largest and most dynamic real estate markets. He described the land acquisition as a meaningful first step towards building a strong presence in the region.

Company Background

Bengaluru-based Puravankara Ltd is one of the country's leading real estate developers. As of June 30, 2026, the company has completed 97 projects totalling approximately 59 million sq ft of area across nine cities.

Its existing footprint spans Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune, and Goa. The Greater Noida acquisition adds Delhi-NCR as its tenth market.
