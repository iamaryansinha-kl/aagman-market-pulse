Services Exports Hit $418.3 Bn, Offset 64% of India's FY26 Trade Deficit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's services exports crossed $418.3 billion in FY26, a 7.9% YoY increase, crossing the $400 billion mark for the first time
  • 02/Services exports now account for 48.6% of total exports, up from 47% in FY25, showing structural shift
  • 03/Net services surplus of $213.9 billion offset 64.2% of the merchandise trade deficit of $119.3 billion

India's services exports reached $418.3 billion in FY26, according to the Finance Ministry's April Monthly Economic Review. This marks the first time the sector has crossed the $400 billion threshold.

The growth rate stands at 7.9% year-on-year. Services now account for 48.6% of India's total exports, up from 47% in FY25.

This shift signals a structural change in India's export composition. The economy is gradually pivoting from merchandise-led trade to digitally delivered and professional services.

The Finance Ministry highlighted the sector's stabilising role. Services exports generated a net surplus of $213.9 billion during the fiscal year.

This surplus offset 64.2% of the merchandise trade deficit. The total trade deficit widened to $119.3 billion in FY26, up from $94.7 billion in FY25.

The review comes amid a volatile global trade environment. Geopolitical tensions and supply chain disruptions have kept merchandise exports subdued.

IT, business services, and financial activities continue to drive services growth. The sector is acting as a crucial shock absorber for the economy.

India's growing services export base is supporting macroeconomic stability. It helps mitigate external vulnerabilities and sustain external balance.
