CEEW CRAVIS Platform Predicts 15-40 Extra Hot Days Annually Till 2045

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CEEW launches CRAVIS platform forecasting 15-40 additional hot days yearly in India over next two decades versus 1981-2010 baseline
  • 02/Heavy rainfall events projected to rise 10-30 days annually; Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka among hardest-hit states
  • 03/Platform projects data through 2051-70 with district-level granularity for policymakers and infrastructure planners

CEEW launched CRAVIS on April 29, 2026. The AI platform predicts 15-40 extra unusually hot days each year in India through 2045.

The forecast compares against the 1981-2010 climatic baseline. Warm nights could increase by 20-40 annually. This compounds heat stress on populations and crops.

Heavy rainfall events may rise 10-30 days per year in many districts. Central and southern states face the sharpest increases.

Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu are flagged as high-risk zones. Both temperature and precipitation spikes will hit these states hardest.

CRAVIS draws on 40 years of historical climate data. It projects outcomes for 2030-50 and 2051-70 periods.

The platform covers 279 indicators at district, grid and state levels. Over 20 metrics span temperature, precipitation and humidity.

"CRAVIS is designed to move climate intelligence from models to the hands of decision-makers at all levels of governance." - Arunabha Ghosh, CEO, CEEW

The tool targets policymakers, farmers and infrastructure planners. District-level granularity allows localized adaptation strategies.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS CRAVIS AND WHO DEVELOPED IT?
CRAVIS (CLIMATE RESILIENCE ANALYTICS AND VISUALISATION INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM) IS AN AI-POWERED CLIMATE INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM DEVELOPED BY DELHI-BASED THINK TANK COUNCIL ON ENERGY, ENVIRONMENT AND WATER (CEEW). IT WAS LAUNCHED ON APRIL 29, 2026.
HOW MANY EXTRA HOT DAYS DOES CRAVIS PREDICT FOR INDIA?
CRAVIS PREDICTS INDIA WILL SEE AN ADDITIONAL 15 TO 40 UNUSUALLY HOT DAYS EACH YEAR OVER THE NEXT TWO DECADES, COMPARED TO THE 1981-2010 CLIMATIC BASELINE.
WHICH STATES WILL SEE THE STRONGEST CLIMATE IMPACTS?
CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN STATES INCLUDING MAHARASHTRA, TELANGANA, ANDHRA PRADESH, KARNATAKA AND TAMIL NADU ARE EXPECTED TO WITNESS THE STRONGEST INCREASE IN BOTH HOT DAYS AND HEAVY RAINFALL EVENTS.
HOW MANY ADDITIONAL HEAVY RAINFALL DAYS ARE PROJECTED?
MANY DISTRICTS COULD SEE 10 TO 30 ADDITIONAL HEAVY RAINFALL DAYS ANNUALLY OVER THE NEXT TWO DECADES, ACCORDING TO THE CRAVIS PLATFORM.
WHAT DATA SOURCES AND COVERAGE DOES CRAVIS PROVIDE?
CRAVIS COMBINES 40 YEARS OF HISTORICAL CLIMATE DATA AND PROVIDES PROJECTIONS FOR 2030-50 AND 2051-70. IT COVERS 279 INDICATORS AT DISTRICT, GRID AND STATE LEVELS, DISTILLED INTO OVER 20 ANALYSIS-FRIENDLY METRICS SPANNING TEMPERATURE, PRECIPITATION AND HUMIDITY.
