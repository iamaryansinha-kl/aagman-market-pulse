LTM BlueVerse Studio Bengaluru Launch: Q4 Profit Up 23.4% YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LTM Ltd launches BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026, to scale enterprise agentic AI from pilots to production
  • 02/Q4 FY26 net profit jumped 23.4% YoY to ₹1,392.3 crore on revenue of ₹11,291.7 crore, up 15.6% YoY
  • 03/LTM shares closed at ₹4,380.60 on BSE, up 0.80%; stock trades 31.9% below its 52-week high of ₹6,429.50

LTM Ltd (formerly LTIMindtree) launched BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026. The facility is designed to help enterprise clients scale AI pilots into full production systems.

The studio unites LTM's AI capabilities under one roof. It includes BlueVerse CraftStudio, physical AI showcases, and industry-specific use cases with built-in governance.

Clients can run separate agentic AI pilots with clear scaling plans. The framework covers rapid prototyping, reusable components, and Responsible AI controls.

The launch comes on the heels of strong Q4 FY26 numbers. Revenue hit ₹11,291.7 crore, up 15.6% YoY. Net profit surged 23.4% to ₹1,392.3 crore.

EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹1,973 crore, up 23.6% YoY. The EBITDA margin expanded to 17.5% from 16.3% in Q4 FY25.

Order inflow in Q4 rose 5.62% YoY to $1.69 billion. The company added 13 new clients, taking the active base to 751.

The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹53 per share. The record date and AGM schedule will be announced shortly.

"The BlueVerse ecosystem is central to our vision to be our clients' gateway to the agentic enterprise era," said Venu Lambu, CEO and Managing Director, LTM.

LTM shares closed at ₹4,380.60 on BSE on April 28, up 0.80%. The stock has declined 31.9% from its 52-week high of ₹6,429.50.

Analysts remain divided on near-term upside. Motilal Oswal has a Buy rating with a target of ₹5,400, while ICICI Securities maintains a Hold at ₹4,380.

WHAT IS BLUEVERSE STUDIO AND WHY DID LTM LAUNCH IT IN BENGALURU?
BLUEVERSE STUDIO IS A DEDICATED FACILITY FOR ENTERPRISE CLIENTS TO SCALE AI PILOTS INTO PRODUCTION SYSTEMS. IT COMBINES BLUEVERSE CRAFTSTUDIO, PHYSICAL AI SHOWCASES, AND INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC USE CASES WITH GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS. BENGALURU JOINS EXISTING STUDIOS IN LONDON AND MUMBAI.
WHAT WERE LTM'S Q4 FY26 FINANCIAL RESULTS?
LTM REPORTED CONSOLIDATED REVENUE OF ₹11,291.7 CRORE IN Q4 FY26, UP 15.6% YOY. NET PROFIT ROSE 23.4% TO ₹1,392.3 CRORE. EBITDA STOOD AT ₹1,973 CRORE WITH A MARGIN OF 17.5%, UP FROM 16.3% IN Q4 FY25.
WHAT IS THE LATEST DIVIDEND DECLARED BY LTM?
THE BOARD RECOMMENDED A FINAL DIVIDEND OF ₹53 PER EQUITY SHARE OF FACE VALUE RE 1. THE RECORD DATE AND AGM SCHEDULE WILL BE COMMUNICATED IN DUE COURSE. UPON APPROVAL, PAYMENT WILL BE MADE WITHIN 10 DAYS OF THE AGM.

LTM SHARES CLOSED AT ₹4,380.60 ON THE BSE, UP 0.80% FROM THE PREVIOUS CLOSE OF ₹4,346.00. THE INTRADAY HIGH WAS ₹4,435.00. THE STOCK TRADES 31.9% BELOW ITS 52-WEEK HIGH OF ₹6,429.50.

KEY RISKS INCLUDE US RECIPROCAL TARIFFS IMPACTING INDIAN IT SERVICES, FII OUTFLOWS FROM INDIAN EQUITIES, MARGIN COMPRESSION IF AI INVESTMENTS DO NOT YIELD EXPECTED RETURNS, AND EXECUTION RISK AROUND THE BLUEVERSE STUDIO CLIENT ADOPTION RATE.
