Godrej Properties FY26 PAT Hits Record ₹1,850 Crore, Up 32%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FY26 PAT reached record ₹1,850 crore, up 32% from ₹1,400 crore in FY25, with Q4 PAT surging 70% YoY to ₹650 crore
  • 02/Annual bookings of ₹34,171 crore across 17,513 units marked third consecutive year as India's largest residential developer
  • 03/Net debt rose to ₹6,414 crore on ₹8,653 crore land buys; watch FY27 guidance of ₹39,000 crore bookings and debt levels

Godrej Properties delivered its strongest fiscal year yet, reporting record annual profit after tax of ₹1,850 crore for FY26, marking a 32% jump from ₹1,400 crore in FY25. The fourth quarter alone contributed ₹650 crore in PAT, up 70% year-on-year and 233% sequentially.

Revenue momentum accelerated sharply in Q4 FY26, climbing 64% YoY to ₹3,458 crore. Total income for the quarter stood at ₹3,806.65 crore, up 42%, while full-year total income reached ₹8,410.88 crore compared to ₹6,967.05 crore in the previous year.

The company cemented its position as India's largest residential developer for the third straight year, clocking annual bookings of ₹34,171 crore with 16% growth. It sold 17,513 units spanning 27 million square feet across key markets.

Regional performance remained broad-based, with the Mumbai Metropolitan Region leading at ₹10,312 crore, followed by Bengaluru at ₹8,801 crore and the National Capital Region at ₹7,412 crore. The quarterly collections of ₹7,947 crore set a new record for any Indian real estate developer in a single quarter.

Executive Chairperson Pirojsha Godrej highlighted that the company achieved its highest ever bookings, collections, operating cash flows, earnings, and business development during the year. Operating cash flows for FY26 stood at ₹7,830 crore.

The company pursued an aggressive land acquisition strategy, spending ₹8,653 crore in FY26 to fuel future growth. This pushed net debt higher to ₹6,414 crore from ₹3,269 crore, though the net debt-to-equity ratio remained comfortable at 0.33.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹10 per share, representing a 200% payout, marking the company's first dividend distribution since 2015. The record date for the dividend has been fixed at July 28, 2026.

For FY27, Godrej Properties has guided for bookings of ₹39,000 crore, customer collections of ₹24,000 crore, and deliveries covering 13.5 million square feet. The stock closed at ₹1,886, up 2.77%, with a market capitalization of ₹56,732 crore and a trailing price-to-earnings multiple of 33.08.
