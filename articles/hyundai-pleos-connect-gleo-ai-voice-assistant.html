Hyundai Unveils Pleos Connect With Gleo AI Voice Assistant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hyundai Motor Co. unveils Pleos Connect infotainment system with Gleo AI voice assistant on April 30, 2026
  • 02/System debuts May 2026 on Grandeur sedan in South Korea; 20 million Hyundai and Kia vehicles by 2030
  • 03/Hyundai Motor India (NSE: HYUNDAI) trades at ₹1,706, down 40.97% from 52-week high; parent at KRW 556,000

Hyundai Motor Co. unveiled Pleos Connect, its next-generation infotainment system, on April 30, 2026. The system is anchored by Gleo AI, a voice assistant capable of natural, multi-step conversations.

Pleos Connect draws on Tesla's minimalist aesthetic with a wide central touchscreen. Hyundai retained physical controls on the steering wheel and below the screen for safety and usability.

Gleo AI understands regular conversations instead of forcing robotic voice commands. It can handle multiple requests at once and identify who in the car is speaking.

The system debuts in May 2026 on the new Grandeur sedan in South Korea. A phased global rollout will follow, with continuous over-the-air updates enabling performance improvements after purchase.

Hyundai expects Pleos Connect to be installed in 20 million vehicles made by the company and affiliate Kia Corp. through 2030. This covers the automaker's broader push toward software-defined vehicles.

"Our ultimate goal is to achieve Cloud Mobility, where all forms of mobility are connected through software on the cloud, and continuously evolve over time." - Chang Song, president of Hyundai and Kia's AVP Division.

Hyundai Motor India Ltd (NSE: HYUNDAI) traded at ₹1,706 on April 7, 2026, down 40.97% from its 52-week high. The parent company trades at KRW 556,000 in Seoul, up 170.66% over the past year.

The launch intensifies competition in the in-car software market. Rivals include Mercedes-Benz's MBUX, BMW's iDrive, and Tesla's vertically integrated infotainment stack.
