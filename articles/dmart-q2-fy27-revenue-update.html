DMart Q2 FY27: Revenue Up 18.4% YoY to Rs 19,206 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Avenue Supermarts reported an 18.4% year-on-year rise in Q2 FY27 standalone revenue from operations to Rs 19,206.18 crore
  • 02/Despite the growth, DMart shares fell 4.07% to Rs 3,657.70 on the NSE on Monday
  • 03/The update carried no profit or margin figures; the Q2 revenue number is subject to limited review by statutory auditors

Avenue Supermarts Ltd (NSE: DMART), the operator of the DMart chain of supermarkets, reported standalone revenue from operations of Rs 19,206.18 crore for the second quarter of FY27.

The figure marks an 18.4% rise over the same quarter last year. Revenue from operations stood at Rs 16,218.79 crore in Q2 FY26.

The quarter covers the July-September period of the 2026-27 financial year. Business updates are typically released ahead of full quarterly results.

Avenue Supermarts runs its stores under the DMart brand. The company, headquartered in Mumbai, is among India's largest food and grocery retailers.

The company disclosed the number in its Q2 FY27 business update. The update was revenue-only in nature.

The Q2 revenue figure is subject to limited review by the company's statutory auditors. No profit, EBITDA, or margin details were provided in the update.

A limited review is narrower in scope than a full audit. It offers a moderate level of assurance on the figures presented.

The business update format gives investors an early read on the top line. The complete earnings picture, including costs and margins, will come with the full results.

Despite the double-digit revenue growth, DMart shares fell 4.07% to Rs 3,657.70 on the NSE on Monday. No reason for the decline was disclosed or reported.

The longer revenue trend remains upward. Quarterly revenue has risen from Rs 12,307.72 crore in Q2 FY24 to Rs 19,206.18 crore in Q2 FY27.

DMart operated 518 stores as of September 30, 2026. The count includes one store at Sanpada, Navi Mumbai, which is currently closed to customers for reconstruction.

On the price chart, the stock has had a weak run. Shares fell 3.81% over the past week and 2.95% over the past month.

The declines extend further back. The stock is down 1.55% in 2026 so far and has fallen 17.20% over the past year.

Over three years, DMart shares are down 5.25%. Over five years, the stock has declined 14.06%.

Avenue Supermarts commands a market capitalisation of around Rs 2.38 lakh crore. It remains one of India's largest listed retailers by value.

Separately from the business update, DMart allotted commercial paper worth Rs 500 crore on September 30. The CP is proposed for listing on the BSE.

The instrument carries a 90-day tenure. It matures on December 29, 2026.

The CP carries a coupon of 6.12% and is unsecured in nature. It has been rated ICRA A1+, which is ICRA's highest category for short-term debt instruments.

Commercial paper is a short-term funding instrument. The 90-day tenure places this issuance at the short end of the money market.

For investors, the business update answers the revenue question but leaves the earnings picture open. Profitability and margin trends will only become clear when the company releases its full quarterly results.

The full results will also show how the 518-store network performed through the quarter. Until then, the 18.4% revenue growth is the only hard number investors have from the company.
