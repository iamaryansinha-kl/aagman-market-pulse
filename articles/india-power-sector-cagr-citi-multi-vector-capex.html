India Power Sector 5-6% CAGR Forecast: Citi Sees Multi-Vector Capex Upcycle

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Citi Research forecasts 5-6% CAGR for India's power sector driven by electrification, data centres, and cooling demand
  • 02/Multi-vector capex upcycle spans thermal, renewables, transmission, and grid storage for the first time
  • 03/GENCO capacity additions over FY26-FY32 seen at 2.3-2.5x FY19-FY25 levels; El Nino tailwinds expected in 2026

India's power sector is poised for sustained medium-term growth. Citi Research forecasts a 5-6 per cent CAGR on the back of multiple demand drivers.

This marks India's first-ever multi-vector capital expenditure upcycle. It spans thermal power, renewables, transmission infrastructure, and grid storage simultaneously.

The growth is driven by electrification, data centres, cooling loads, and manufacturing. These multiple load vectors provide greater visibility and sustainability than past cycles.

"Our medium-term forecast is set at a 5-6 per cent CAGR on the strength of electrification, data centres, cooling load, and policy-supported manufacturing sectors," Citi Research stated.

GENCO capacity additions between FY26 and FY32 are expected at 2.3-2.5 times the FY19-FY25 levels. This surge addresses the time-of-day sensitivity of demand and supply dynamics.

"Regulators' focus has pivoted from capacity to reliability/flexibility, and favorable policies are providing greater visibility," the Citi report noted. The Central Electricity Authority's resource adequacy guidelines and long-term transmission plans anchor this phase.

Power demand growth has decelerated over the past two years. Headline deficits mask specific strains during non-solar hours, a risk flagged by the CEA for the next two years.

Citi believes power-sector stocks still hold upside potential despite recent re-rating. El Nino-linked tailwinds for agricultural pump and cooling demand are expected in 2026.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE GROWTH FORECAST FOR INDIA'S POWER SECTOR?
CITI RESEARCH FORECASTS A 5-6 PER CENT CAGR FOR INDIA'S POWER SECTOR OVER THE MEDIUM TERM, DRIVEN BY ELECTRIFICATION, DATA CENTRES, COOLING LOADS, AND POLICY-SUPPORTED MANUFACTURING SECTORS.
WHAT IS A MULTI-VECTOR CAPEX UPCYCLE?
THIS IS INDIA'S FIRST-EVER SIMULTANEOUS CAPITAL EXPENDITURE EXPANSION ACROSS THERMAL POWER, RENEWABLES, TRANSMISSION INFRASTRUCTURE, AND GRID STORAGE — UNLIKE PREVIOUS CYCLES DRIVEN BY SINGLE FACTORS.
HOW MUCH WILL GENERATION CAPACITY GROW?
GENCO CAPACITY ADDITIONS BETWEEN FY26 AND FY32 ARE EXPECTED TO BE 2.3 TO 2.5 TIMES HIGHER THAN THE LEVELS SEEN BETWEEN FY19 AND FY25, ACCORDING TO CITI ESTIMATES.
WHAT REGULATORY CHANGES ARE SUPPORTING THIS GROWTH?
REGULATORS HAVE SHIFTED FOCUS FROM MERE CAPACITY ADDITION TO SYSTEM RELIABILITY AND FLEXIBILITY. THE CEA RESOURCE ADEQUACY GUIDELINES, LONG-TERM TRANSMISSION PLANS, AND THE DRAFT NATIONAL ELECTRICITY POLICY 2026 PROVIDE STRUCTURAL ANCHORS.
WHAT RISKS AND TAILWINDS SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH?
HEADLINE POWER DEFICITS MASK NON-SOLAR-HOUR STRAINS, A RISK FLAGGED BY THE CEA FOR THE NEXT TWO YEARS. IN 2026, EL NINO-LINKED TAILWINDS ARE EXPECTED TO BOOST AGRICULTURAL PUMP AND COOLING DEMAND.
