AWL Flags 20% Rise in Oil-Linked Costs Amid West Asia Conflict

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/AWL Agri Business (NSE: AWL) faces roughly 20% surge in crude-linked input costs as the West Asia conflict drives up oil, chemicals and packaging prices
  • 02/Brent crude has climbed from the low $70s to above $110 per barrel; the cost impact on AWL is estimated at 25-50 basis points
  • 03/Company forecasts 8-9% sales volume growth in FY2027 and expects per-tonne margins to remain broadly stable despite the pressure

AWL Agri Business (NSE: AWL), maker of Fortune cooking oil and Kohinoor rice, is grappling with a roughly 20 per cent surge in crude-linked input costs. The West Asia conflict has driven up prices for fuel, chemicals and packaging materials.

"Costs have gone up for us in terms of chemicals, packing material and coal," said Shrikant Kanhere, managing director and CEO. He told Reuters the issue remains a cause of concern.

Brent crude has surged from the low $70s per barrel before the conflict to above $110. Global oil markets are pricing in fears of supply disruptions from the region.

The cost impact translates to roughly 25 to 50 basis points for AWL. The company is adjusting prices in line with market movements while absorbing part of the increase.

AWL is cutting packaging and fuel use at its plants to limit the profit hit. It expects per-tonne margins to be broadly stable in fiscal 2027.

Kanhere forecast sales volume growth of 8 per cent to 9 per cent in fiscal 2027. This is nearly double last year's pace, with edible oils growing at a mid-single-digit rate and foods posting double-digit growth.

The company's online channels and large-format grocers posted nearly 50 per cent growth last year. AWL is investing heavily in these channels to scale up volumes.

Peer companies face similar pressures. Bisleri and Hindustan Unilever have also raised prices to counter higher conflict-linked input costs.

AWL HAS SEEN A ROUGHLY 20 PER CENT SURGE IN SOME CRUDE-LINKED INPUT COSTS SINCE THE WEST ASIA CONFLICT BEGAN, INCLUDING CHEMICALS, PACKAGING MATERIALS AND FUEL.
WHAT IS THE ESTIMATED COST IMPACT ON AWL'S OPERATIONS?
THE 20 PER CENT RISE IN INPUT COSTS TRANSLATES INTO A COST IMPACT OF ROUGHLY 25 TO 50 BASIS POINTS FOR THE COMPANY.
HOW IS AWL RESPONDING TO THE COST PRESSURES?
AWL IS ADJUSTING PRICES IN LINE WITH MARKET MOVEMENTS, ABSORBING PART OF THE INCREASE WHILE PASSING THE REST TO CONSUMERS. THE COMPANY IS ALSO CUTTING PACKAGING AND FUEL USE AT ITS PLANTS.
WHAT IS AWL'S VOLUME GROWTH FORECAST FOR FISCAL 2027?
CEO SHRIKANT KANHERE FORECASTS SALES VOLUME GROWTH OF 8 PER CENT TO 9 PER CENT IN FISCAL 2027, NEARLY DOUBLE LAST YEAR'S PACE.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR AWL'S MARGINS DESPITE COST PRESSURES?
AWL EXPECTS PER-TONNE MARGINS TO BE BROADLY STABLE IN FISCAL 2027, SUPPORTED BY PRICE ADJUSTMENTS AND COST CONTROL MEASURES AT ITS PLANTS.
