SBI Report Calls for BoP Support Measures Amid Rupee Weakness

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI economists warn BoP could be negative for third consecutive year, calling for urgent structural support measures
  • 02/Rupee fell 6.39% between April 2025 and February 2026, then another 3.63% after West Asia war onset
  • 03/SBI proposes diaspora bonds, G-Sec tax reforms, and temporary LRS limit cuts to control capital outflows

SBI's economic research department has warned that inflationary expectations could become unanchored without timely balance of payments support measures. Soumya Ghosh, Group Chief Economic Advisor at SBI, said the current rupee depreciation is not in line with India's macro fundamentals.

"The current rupee depreciation is not in line with India's macro fundamentals. It is imperative to control the second-round effects. Exchange Rate Depreciation leads to higher imported inflation." - Soumya Ghosh, Group Chief Economic Advisor, SBI

The rupee declined 6.39 per cent between April 2, 2025 and February 27, 2026. It fell a further 3.63 per cent following the onset of the West Asia war.

SBI economists said the exchange rate cannot act as a shock absorber indefinitely. Increased volatility transforms it into a pass-through mechanism for imported inflation, defeating monetary policy objectives.

The bank suggested soliciting funds from the diaspora through a calibrated scheme. It should feature optimal yield, longer tenor, and tax-friendly treatment to avoid hot money inflows.

SBI also recommended rationalising tax treatment for G-Sec investments. A stable lower TDS rate could allow foreign funds to invest directly without complex treaty-jurisdiction structures.

Investments in equity, debt, and immovable property under LRS have doubled year-on-year. Remittances for deposits abroad have grown by approximately 25 per cent YoY.

SBI proposed temporarily reducing the annual LRS limit of $2,50,000 for select categories. Travel remittances, which account for around 55 per cent of LRS outflows, should be discouraged.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE MAIN WARNING IN THE SBI REPORT?
SBI'S ECONOMIC RESEARCH DEPARTMENT HAS WARNED THAT WITHOUT TIMELY BALANCE OF PAYMENTS SUPPORT MEASURES, INFLATIONARY EXPECTATIONS COULD BECOME UNANCHORED AMID PERSISTENT RUPEE WEAKNESS AND VOLATILE CAPITAL FLOWS.
HOW MUCH HAS THE RUPEE DEPRECIATED?
THE RUPEE DECLINED 6.39 PER CENT BETWEEN APRIL 2, 2025 AND FEBRUARY 27, 2026. FOLLOWING THE ONSET OF THE WEST ASIA WAR, IT FELL A FURTHER 3.63 PER CENT.
WHAT BOP MEASURES HAS SBI PROPOSED?
SBI HAS PROPOSED SOLICITING FUNDS FROM THE DIASPORA, BETTER TAX TREATMENT FOR G-SEC INVESTMENTS, RESTRICTING REMITTANCES FOR DEPOSITS ABROAD, AND TEMPORARILY REDUCING THE ANNUAL LRS LIMIT OF $2,50,000 IN FY27.
WHY DOES SBI WANT TO RESTRICT LRS OUTFLOWS?
INVESTMENTS IN EQUITY, DEBT AND IMMOVABLE PROPERTY UNDER LRS HAVE DOUBLED YEAR-ON-YEAR. REMITTANCES FOR DEPOSITS ABROAD HAVE GROWN BY APPROXIMATELY 25 PER CENT YOY. TRAVEL REMITTANCES ACCOUNT FOR AROUND 55 PER CENT OF LRS OUTFLOWS.
WHAT IS SBI'S VIEW ON THE RUPEE DEPRECIATION?
SOUMYA GHOSH, GROUP CHIEF ECONOMIC ADVISOR AT SBI, SAID THE CURRENT RUPEE DEPRECIATION IS NOT IN LINE WITH INDIA'S MACRO FUNDAMENTALS. SBI ECONOMISTS ADDED THAT THE EXCHANGE RATE CANNOT ACT AS A SHOCK ABSORBER INDEFINITELY.
