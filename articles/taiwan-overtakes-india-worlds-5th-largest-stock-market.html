Taiwan Overtakes India as World's 5th Largest Stock Market at $4.95T

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Taiwan's stock market capitalization reached $4.95 trillion, overtaking India's $4.92 trillion to become the world's 5th largest
  • 02/TSMC alone accounts for roughly 42% of Taiwan's benchmark index and has rallied 49% this year on AI semiconductor demand
  • 03/Global funds have sold nearly $24 billion of Indian equities in 2026 while chasing the AI boom in Taiwan and South Korea

Taiwan's stock market has overtaken India in total value. The island's market capitalization reached $4.95 trillion as of Monday, Bloomberg data shows.

India's market value has slipped to $4.92 trillion. Taiwan is now ranked fifth globally, behind only the US, China, Japan, and Hong Kong.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co powers the surge. TSMC accounts for roughly 42% of the benchmark index, and its shares have rallied 49% in 2026.

The chipmaker dominates the semiconductor supply chain for artificial intelligence infrastructure. AI optimism is reshaping global capital flows toward tech hardware hubs.

India faces record foreign outflows and a weakening rupee. The Nifty is down 8% this year, heading for its first annual decline after a decade of gains.

Global funds have sold nearly $24 billion of Indian equities in 2026. India's MSCI emerging markets weight has dropped to about 12% from 19% last year.

"Taiwan's rising market cap reflects its heavy concentration in tech hardware," said Yi Ping Liao of Franklin Templeton. Taiwan's regulator last month raised the single-stock fund limit to 25% from 10%.

JPMorgan estimates the rule change could attract over $6 billion in inflows. While Taiwan leads in market value, India's $4.15 trillion economy dwarfs the island's $977 billion GDP.
