IMO Drafts Hormuz Evacuation Plan for Hundreds of Stranded Ships

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IMO Secretary-General Arsenio Dominguez announced an evacuation plan for hundreds of merchant ships stranded in the Persian Gulf for over seven weeks
  • 02/Ships have been stuck since US and Israeli strikes on Iran began; 20,000 seafarers are affected and at least 10 have died in documented incidents
  • 03/The plan uses the eastbound traffic lane of the 1968 Traffic Separation Scheme and can only activate when all parties agree to halt attacks on maritime assets

The International Maritime Organization is drafting an evacuation plan for hundreds of merchant ships trapped in the Persian Gulf. Secretary-General Arsenio Dominguez confirmed the framework on April 21, 2026, during Singapore Maritime Week.

The ships have been stranded for more than seven weeks since US and Israeli strikes on Iran began in late February 2026. Iran responded by effectively blocking the Strait of Hormuz to Western-allied shipping.

Around 20,000 seafarers are impacted in the region, according to IMO data. Documented incidents since March 1 have killed at least 10 seafarers and left several others severely injured or missing.

The proposed evacuation framework is voluntary and maintains UNCLOS navigation rights. It uses the eastbound lane of the Traffic Separation Scheme adopted by IMO in 1968.

Iran has developed an alternative route close to its coast over recent weeks. In some cases, vessels must pay a transit fee.

The IMO Council called for the safe-passage framework on March 18-19. All parties must agree to refrain from attacking maritime assets before the plan can proceed.

The Strait of Hormuz normally handles about 20 million barrels of oil per day. That represents roughly 25% of global seaborne oil trade and 20% of world LNG trade.

Roughly 130 container ships and 280 bulk carriers are trapped inside the Persian Gulf. Major lines including MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM, and Hapag-Lloyd have suspended all Hormuz transits.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE IMO HORMUZ EVACUATION PLAN?
THE IMO HAS PROPOSED A VOLUNTARY EVACUATION FRAMEWORK TO FACILITATE THE SAFE DEPARTURE OF HUNDREDS OF MERCHANT SHIPS CURRENTLY CONFINED IN THE PERSIAN GULF. THE PLAN USES THE EASTBOUND TRAFFIC LANE OF THE EXISTING TRAFFIC SEPARATION SCHEME ADOPTED BY THE IMO IN 1968. IT CAN ONLY BE ACTIVATED ONCE ALL PARTIES TO THE CONFLICT AGREE TO REFRAIN FROM ATTACKING MARITIME ASSETS IN THE REGION.
HOW MANY SHIPS AND SEAFARERS ARE STRANDED?
HUNDREDS OF SHIPS ARE TRAPPED IN THE PERSIAN GULF, INCLUDING OVER 130 CONTAINER SHIPS AND 280 BULK CARRIERS. AN ESTIMATED 500 TANKER VESSELS HAVE BEEN IMPACTED. AROUND 20,000 SEAFARERS ARE AFFECTED IN THE REGION, INCLUDING 611 ON 22 INDIAN-FLAGGED VESSELS ALONE.
WHY ARE THE SHIPS STUCK?
THE SHIPS HAVE BEEN STRANDED FOR MORE THAN SEVEN WEEKS SINCE US AND ISRAELI STRIKES ON IRAN BEGAN IN LATE FEBRUARY 2026. IRAN RESPONDED BY EFFECTIVELY BLOCKING THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ TO WESTERN-ALLIED SHIPPING. ON APRIL 13, THE US NAVY LAUNCHED A FULL-SCALE NAVAL BLOCKADE OF IRANIAN PORTS.
WHAT IS THE MARKET IMPACT OF THE STRAIT CLOSURE?
THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ NORMALLY HANDLES ABOUT 20 MILLION BARRELS OF OIL PER DAY, REPRESENTING 25% OF GLOBAL SEABORNE OIL TRADE AND 20% OF WORLD LNG TRADE. THE IEA CALLED THIS THE LARGEST SUPPLY DISRUPTION IN THE HISTORY OF THE GLOBAL OIL MARKET. ABOUT 14 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY HAVE ZERO PIPELINE BYPASS OPTIONS.
WHEN WILL THE EVACUATION PLAN TAKE EFFECT?
THE PLAN CAN ONLY BE ACTIVATED WHEN THERE ARE CLEAR SIGNS OF DE-ESCALATION. IMO SECRETARY-GENERAL ARSENIO DOMINGUEZ STATED ON APRIL 21 THAT VESSEL DEPARTURES WOULD BE PRIORITISED BASED ON HOW LONG CREWS HAVE BEEN STRANDED. THE IMO IS WORKING WITH LITTORAL STATES INCLUDING IRAN AND OMAN TO FINALISE THE BLUEPRINT.
