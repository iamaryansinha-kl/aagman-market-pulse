Naidu Announces ₹2,800 Cr Varanasi Airport Expansion at Hub-Spoke Flight Launch

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Civil Aviation Minister K Ram Mohan Naidu announces ₹2,800 crore expansion plan for Varanasi airport at the launch of India's first hub-and-spoke flight
  • 02/Air India Easy Connect flight AI1111 departs Varanasi at 9:30 am on June 25, 2026, connecting passengers to 17 international destinations via Delhi
  • 03/Six more cities including Amritsar, Ahmedabad, Kochi, Goa, Hyderabad, and Chennai will join the hub-and-spoke model within six weeks

Civil Aviation Minister K Ram Mohan Naidu announced a ₹2,800 crore expansion plan for Varanasi airport. He made the announcement during the inauguration of India's first operational hub-and-spoke international flight on June 25, 2026.

Air India launched its Easy Connect service flight AI1111 from Varanasi to Delhi. The flight departs at 9:30 am and connects passengers to 17 international destinations.

Passengers can complete immigration and baggage formalities at Varanasi itself. They transit through Delhi as international travellers without re-checking luggage.

The 17 destinations include London Heathrow, Frankfurt, Milan, Rome, Zurich, Singapore, Dubai, Riyadh, Phuket, and Kuala Lumpur. Connections are timed within four hours of arrival at Delhi.

Naidu said six more cities will be converted to hub-and-spoke models in the next six weeks. Air India CEO Campbell Wilson named Amritsar, Ahmedabad, Kochi, Goa, Hyderabad, and Chennai as upcoming cities.

Civil Aviation Secretary Samir Kumar Sinha said the model benefits Tier-II and Tier-III cities. It will create jobs and attract investment beyond major metro hubs.

India now operates 164 airports, up from 74 in 2014. The country recorded nearly 250 million air passengers in 2025 with 10% annual growth.

P. Balaji, Group Head at Air India, said the model requires close coordination across airlines, airports, and government agencies. He added that Air India is committed to expanding this network across more cities.
