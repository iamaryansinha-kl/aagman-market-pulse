AI Slowdown Trade Hits Nvidia, SoftBank, SK Hynix as Global Tech Stocks Fall Up to 10%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/AI-linked stocks fell across global markets after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called for slowing the pace of AI development
  • 02/SoftBank, one of OpenAI's biggest investors, plunged 10% in Japan; SK Hynix fell more than 6% and Samsung Electronics dropped over 4% in South Korea
  • 03/European chip stocks tumbled: ASML -5%+, Nokia -8%, Infineon -7%; data centre names Siemens Energy and Schneider Electric also slid
  • 04/US premarket: Micron -5%, Intel nearly -6%, Nvidia almost -3%; the Nasdaq opened 1% lower
  • 05/Anthropic safety researcher Evan Hubinger said he sees a more than 10% chance AI "kills all humans" within a decade

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
What is the AI slowdown trade?
A/01/ It refers to investors selling AI-linked stocks — chipmakers, cloud providers and data centre operators — on fears that AI development will be deliberately slowed for safety reasons, undermining growth expectations.
Which stocks fell the most?
A/02/ SoftBank dropped 10% in Japan, Nokia fell about 8%, Infineon lost over 7% and SK Hynix fell more than 6%. In US premarket, Intel dropped nearly 6% and Micron fell about 5%.
What did Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei say?
A/03/ Amodei published an essay on Saturday saying "we must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models," citing safety concerns.
Could this affect Indian markets?
A/04/ Yes. Indian IT services companies and the Nifty IT index could face pressure if the global AI trade unwinds, since much of the recent rally has been driven by AI spending expectations.
