Zerodha Will Keep Direct Mutual Fund Plans for Free, Says Nithin Kamath

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Zerodha is sticking to its free direct mutual fund model.

Founder Nithin Kamath has confirmed that the company will continue to offer direct mutual fund plans at no cost to investors. The announcement ends months of speculation that India's largest stockbroker might start charging fees for the service.

Direct mutual fund plans allow investors to buy funds without paying distributor commissions. The expense ratios on direct plans are lower than regular plans, giving investors higher returns over time. Zerodha's Coin platform has become one of the most popular venues for buying direct plans.

The decision to keep the service free is a strategic one. While Zerodha forgoes revenue from mutual fund distribution, the platform drives customer acquisition and engagement. Investors who come for mutual funds often expand into stocks, derivatives, and other products where Zerodha does charge fees.

The mutual fund industry has been debating distribution fees. Sebi has been pushing for greater transparency in commissions, and some platforms have started charging nominal fees for direct plans. Zerodha's decision to stay free puts pressure on competitors to follow suit or risk losing customers.

Kamath has been vocal about the importance of investor education. He has argued that keeping direct plans free aligns with Zerodha's mission of democratising investing and reducing costs for retail participants.

The move is likely to accelerate the shift toward direct plans. India's mutual fund industry has seen record inflows, and a growing share is coming through direct channels as investors become more cost-conscious.
