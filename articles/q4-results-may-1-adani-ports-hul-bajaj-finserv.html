Q4 Results May 1: Adani Ports Profit Up 9%, HUL Revenue Grows 8%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Ports Q4 FY26 net profit rises 9% to ₹3,308 crore on 26% revenue growth to ₹10,738 crore
  • 02/HUL revenue grows 8% to ₹16,207 crore, fastest in 12 quarters; Bajaj Finserv declares ₹1.50 dividend
  • 03/Jindal Steel, ZEN Technologies, Punjab Chemicals scheduled to declare Q4 FY26 results on May 1, 2026

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd (NSE: ADANIPORTS) reported a 9% rise in Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit to ₹3,308 crore. Revenue surged 26% year-on-year to ₹10,738 crore. The board recommended a dividend of ₹7.50 per share.

Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) posted an 8% increase in revenue to ₹16,207 crore for the March quarter. This marks its fastest revenue growth in 12 quarters. Underlying performance came in ahead of Street estimates.

Bajaj Finserv Ltd (NSE: BAJAJFINSV) declared a dividend of ₹1.50 per share. Consolidated profit after tax rose 5% to ₹2,538.6 crore. Net interest income jumped 18% to ₹13,728 crore while AUM grew 22% to ₹5.10 lakh crore.

Adani Enterprises Ltd (NSE: ADANIENT) reported a standalone net profit of ₹343.56 crore, plunging 92% year-on-year. Higher depreciation from the Navi Mumbai airport and copper plant dragged the bottom line into a consolidated net loss of ₹221 crore.

Railtel Corporation of India Ltd reported a 24.9% jump in Q4 profit to ₹141.7 crore. Revenue rose 27.6% to ₹1,669 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.25 per share.

Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd (NSE: SONACOMS) posted a 17% increase in PAT to ₹192 crore. Revenue surged 47% year-on-year to ₹1,272.30 crore, reflecting strong demand in the mobility technology segment.

Edelweiss Financial Services Ltd swung to a standalone profit of ₹162.32 crore against a ₹1.65 crore loss in the year-ago quarter. The board declared a dividend of ₹1.50 per share. The stock traded 6% lower at ₹116.19.

Jindal Steel & Power Ltd (NSE: JINDALSTEL), ZEN Technologies Ltd (NSE: ZENTEC), and Punjab Chemicals & Crop Protection Ltd are scheduled to declare Q4 FY26 results on May 1, 2026. Analysts expect Jindal Steel revenue at ₹12,500-13,500 crore and PAT at ₹1,200-1,500 crore.
