KPIT Technologies Q4 Earnings: Shares Slide 4.5% as Profit Drops 33%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KPIT Technologies shares fell 4.51% to ₹714.85 after Q4 FY26 results missed street expectations on May 7, 2026
  • 02/Q4 revenue rose 12% YoY to ₹1,711 crore but PAT plunged 33% to ₹163 crore, with FY26 PAT down to ₹637.3 crore
  • 03/FY27 outlook hinges on commercial vehicle growth, connected services, and the planned $60-120M Cymotive acquisition

Shares of KPIT Technologies Ltd (NSE: KPITTECH) fell 4.51% to ₹714.85 on Thursday morning. The stock opened at ₹748.55 before sliding to a session low of ₹709.20.

The decline followed Q4 FY26 results announced on Wednesday, May 6, 2026. Revenue grew 12% YoY to ₹1,711 crore in rupee terms.

Q4 EBITDA stood at ₹353.2 crore, up 9.4% over the same period last year. However, quarterly PAT dropped 33% to ₹163 crore, missing analyst estimates.

For the full year, revenue in dollar terms rose 4.8% to $724.8 million. Annual PAT fell sharply to ₹637.3 crore from ₹839.6 crore in FY25.

The profit decline was driven by a one-time statutory impact from new labour codes, higher finance costs, and equity investee losses. These headwinds offset the top-line growth.

The stock is down 38.51% year-to-date and 44.25% over the past year. It significantly underperformed the Nifty 200, which gained 3.73% in the same period.

KPIT's 52-week high of ₹1,434.50, hit in June 2025, now looks distant. The company's market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹19,603 crore.

Management guided for a stronger FY27, citing growth in commercial vehicles, connected services, and new geographies. The board also declared a ₹5.25 per share final dividend and disclosed plans to acquire cybersecurity firm Cymotive for $60-120 million.
