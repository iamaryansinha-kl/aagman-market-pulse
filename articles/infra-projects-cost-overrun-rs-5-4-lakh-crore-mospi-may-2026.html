Infra Projects Cost Overrun Rs 5.4 Lakh Crore: MoSPI May 2026 Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's 1,987 ongoing infrastructure projects each worth over Rs 150 crore have recorded a cumulative cost overrun of approximately Rs 5.4 lakh crore as per MoSPI's May 2026 report
  • 02/Total revised cost stands at Rs 42.50 lakh crore against original cost of Rs 37.09 lakh crore; cumulative expenditure incurred is Rs 21.82 lakh crore or 51.34% of revised cost
  • 03/Ministry of Road Transport & Highways leads with 1,149 projects (58% of total), while 817 projects (41%) have achieved over 80% physical progress

India's major infrastructure projects have recorded a cumulative cost overrun of around Rs 5.4 lakh crore. This is according to the monthly government report for May 2026 released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).

A total of 1,987 ongoing projects across 17 Central Ministries were monitored. The total revised cost stands at Rs 42.50 lakh crore against the original cost of Rs 37.09 lakh crore.

The cumulative expenditure incurred on these projects is Rs 21.82 lakh crore. This accounts for approximately 51.34 per cent of the total revised project cost.

The transport and logistics sector accounts for the highest number of ongoing projects at 1,476. Its revised cost estimates stand at Rs 23.50 lakh crore.

Of the 1,987 projects, 813 are mega projects with an original cost of Rs 31.58 lakh crore. The remaining 1,174 are major projects amounting to Rs 5.52 lakh crore.

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways leads with 1,149 projects or 58 per cent of the total. Its share of the total revised project cost is Rs 10.95 lakh crore or 26 per cent.

The Ministry of Railways is implementing 261 projects with a revised cost of Rs 8.79 lakh crore. The Ministry of Coal is implementing 121 projects at Rs 2.23 lakh crore revised cost.

A significant number of projects are at advanced stages. About 817 projects or 41 per cent have achieved over 80 per cent physical progress. Meanwhile, 280 projects or 14 per cent have crossed 80 per cent financial completion.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TOTAL COST OVERRUN REPORTED BY MOSPI FOR INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS?
THE MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION (MOSPI) REPORTED A CUMULATIVE COST OVERRUN OF APPROXIMATELY RS 5.4 LAKH CRORE ACROSS 1,987 ONGOING INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS IN ITS MAY 2026 REPORT.
WHAT IS THE ORIGINAL COST VERSUS THE REVISED COST OF THESE INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS?
THE TOTAL ORIGINAL COST OF THE 1,987 PROJECTS WAS RS 37.09 LAKH CRORE, WHILE THE TOTAL REVISED COST STANDS AT RS 42.50 LAKH CRORE. THE CUMULATIVE EXPENDITURE INCURRED IS RS 21.82 LAKH CRORE.
WHICH MINISTRY ACCOUNTS FOR THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF ONGOING INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS?
THE MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS ACCOUNTS FOR THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF PROJECTS WITH 1,149 PROJECTS, REPRESENTING 58 PER CENT OF THE TOTAL. ITS SHARE OF TOTAL REVISED PROJECT COST IS RS 10.95 LAKH CRORE OR 26 PER CENT.
HOW MANY PROJECTS HAVE ACHIEVED OVER 80 PER CENT PHYSICAL PROGRESS?
A TOTAL OF 817 PROJECTS, WHICH IS 41 PER CENT OF ALL ONGOING PROJECTS, HAVE ACHIEVED OVER 80 PER CENT PHYSICAL PROGRESS. ADDITIONALLY, 280 PROJECTS OR 14 PER CENT HAVE CROSSED 80 PER CENT FINANCIAL COMPLETION.
WHAT IS THE BREAKDOWN BETWEEN MEGA PROJECTS AND MAJOR PROJECTS?
OF THE TOTAL 1,987 PROJECTS, 813 ARE MEGA PROJECTS WITH A PROJECT COST OF RS 1,000 CRORE AND ABOVE WITH AN ORIGINAL COST OF RS 31.58 LAKH CRORE. THE REMAINING 1,174 ARE MAJOR PROJECTS (RS 150 CRORE TO RS 1,000 CRORE) AMOUNTING TO RS 5.52 LAKH CRORE.
