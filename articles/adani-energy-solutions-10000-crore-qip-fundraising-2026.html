Adani Energy Solutions Board Approves ₹10,000 Crore QIP Fundraising Plan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Energy Solutions board approves raising up to ₹10,000 crore via QIP or other permissible modes at July 1 meeting
  • 02/EGM scheduled for July 25, 2026 to seek shareholder approval; Q1 FY2027 results to be approved on July 21
  • 03/Stock trades at ₹1,538.75, up 72% over past year; trading window closed till July 24 or 48 hours post-results

Adani Energy Solutions Ltd has approved a proposal to raise up to ₹10,000 crore. The board cleared the plan at its meeting on July 1, 2026, through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) or other permissible modes.

The fundraising will involve issuing equity shares of face value ₹10 each and/or other eligible securities. The company may carry this out in one or more tranches, with pricing determined as per applicable laws.

The proposal requires approval from shareholders and regulatory authorities. The board has called an Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) on July 25, 2026, to seek shareholder consent.

Adani Energy Solutions also scheduled its Q1 FY2027 board meeting for July 21, 2026. The meeting will consider and approve unaudited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

The company's trading window was closed from July 1, 2026. It will reopen on July 24, 2026, or 48 hours after the unaudited financial results are made public.

The stock gained 1.1% to hit a day's high of ₹1,538.75 on BSE on July 2. It has risen 72% over the past year and 60% in the last three months.

The scrip hit its 52-week high of ₹1,614 in June 2026. Its 52-week low was ₹745.45, recorded in September 2025.
