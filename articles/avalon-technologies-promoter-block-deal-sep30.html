Avalon Technologies Block Deal: Promoters Offload 6% for Rs 871 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Three promoter entities of Avalon Technologies sold a 6% stake worth Rs 871 crore via bulk deals on September 30, 2026
  • 02/About 4.1 million shares changed hands, and Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund emerged as the buyer of the entire block
  • 03/The stock dipped as much as 5% intraday but recovered to close at Rs 2,340 on the BSE, up 1.37%

Promoter entities of Avalon Technologies Ltd divested a 6% stake worth Rs 871 crore on Wednesday, September 30, 2026. Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund acquired the stake through bulk deals.

Three entities sold the shares: promoter Bhaskar Srinivasan, the BBS Family Trust and the KBS Family Trust. Together they offloaded about 4.1 million shares, equal to 6% of the company, according to data from Kotak Neo.

The transaction was executed as bulk deals on the exchanges. Media reports ahead of the deal had estimated the block at up to Rs 862 crore. The final transaction value came in at Rs 871 crore.

No fresh shares were issued in the transaction. It was entirely a secondary sale between existing shareholders.

Bulk deals allow large parcels of shares to change hands in a single negotiated transaction rather than through the regular order book.

This route gives promoters a way to monetise holdings efficiently. It also gives institutional buyers a meaningful entry point without pushing the open-market price around.

The market reaction on deal day was sharp but brief. Avalon shares fell as much as 5% intraday during the session as the block went through.

The stock then recovered to close at Rs 2,340 on the BSE, up 1.37% on the day. That recovery points to absorption of the supply by the institutional buyer.

Promoter selling is often read as a negative signal by the market. Here, the identity of the buyer complicates that read.

A domestic mutual fund stepping in as counterparty suggests conviction rather than distress. Kotak Mahindra MF's purchase indicates institutional comfort with the company's fundamentals and the EMS theme.

The transaction is a partial exit, not a change of control. Only 6% of the company changed hands, and the remaining promoter holding is unaffected by this deal.

Avalon Technologies Ltd is an electronic manufacturing services (EMS) company. Its end markets include industrials, aerospace, defence and electric vehicle electronics, according to its public profile.

EMS companies in India have drawn steady institutional interest. Global manufacturers diversifying supply chains have lifted demand for local electronics manufacturing capacity.

Block deals have become a standard liquidity mechanism in Indian midcaps. They let promoters raise capital while giving institutions a negotiated entry into names that rarely trade in size.

Domestic mutual funds have increasingly been the buyer of choice in such promoter stake sales. That trend reflects the growing weight of domestic institutional money in midcap price discovery.

What happens next depends on follow-up disclosures. Investors will watch for any further promoter filings and updates to the company's shareholding pattern.

The key question for the market was whether supply of this size could clear in a single session. Deal-day price action suggests it largely did.

The stock's behaviour in the sessions ahead will also be tracked closely. Sustained strength after a 6% block would reinforce the absorption narrative seen on deal day.

WHAT IS A BULK DEAL OR BLOCK DEAL?
A BULK DEAL IS A TRANSACTION IN WHICH A LARGE NUMBER OF SHARES CHANGE HANDS IN A SINGLE TRADE, TYPICALLY AT A NEGOTIATED PRICE OUTSIDE THE REGULAR ORDER BOOK. IT ALLOWS LARGE HOLDERS TO BUY OR SELL MEANINGFUL STAKES WITHOUT MOVING THE OPEN-MARKET PRICE SHARPLY.
