India Plans to Boost Export Scheme as Middle East War Hits Trade

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India plans to raise spending on the RoDTEP export refund scheme and extend its tenure by five years amid Middle East conflict
  • 02/March exports fell 7.4% YoY with 24 of 30 major categories declining as freight rates surged on Gulf routes
  • 03/Incentive rates were halved in February but restored in March; final budget decision between finance and commerce ministries is pending

India is planning to bolster its RoDTEP export refund scheme. Two government officials said the move aims to counter trade headwinds from the Middle East conflict.

The scheme reimburses exporters for taxes not refunded under other programmes. It currently runs until September 30 with a budget exceeding $1 billion.

India's merchandise exports fell 7.4% year-on-year in March. Twenty-four of thirty major export categories declined during the month.

Shipments to Gulf partners including the UAE and Saudi Arabia dropped sharply. Exporters face cash-flow strains from prohibitively high freight rates.

The February budget had nearly halved the scheme's allocation for FY 2026/27. Incentive rates were cut but restored in March as the conflict escalated.

The scheme covers over 10,000 products from agriculture to engineering goods. It offers incentives worth 1% to 4% of product value.

"The present incentive rates do not adequately represent the cost challenges that exporters suffer," said Pankaj Chadha, Chairman of Engineering Exports Promotion Council.

New Delhi also approved a $1.9 billion emergency credit guarantee programme this week. In March, it offered insurance cover for shipments through affected corridors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE RODTEP SCHEME?
THE REMISSION OF DUTIES AND TAXES ON EXPORT PRODUCTS (RODTEP) IS A SCHEME THAT REIMBURSES EXPORTERS FOR TAXES AND LEVIES PAID TO FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL AUTHORITIES THAT ARE NOT REFUNDED UNDER OTHER PROGRAMMES. IT COVERS OVER 10,000 PRODUCTS AND OFFERS INCENTIVES WORTH 1% TO 4% OF PRODUCT VALUE.
HOW MUCH IS THE CURRENT BUDGET FOR THE RODTEP SCHEME?
THE CURRENT BUDGET FOR THE SCHEME IS A LITTLE OVER $1 BILLION. IT IS VALID UNTIL SEPTEMBER 30.
WHAT HAPPENED TO INDIA'S EXPORTS IN MARCH 2026?
INDIA'S MERCHANDISE EXPORTS FELL 7.4% YEAR-ON-YEAR IN MARCH. 24 OF 30 MAJOR EXPORT CATEGORIES DECLINED, AND SHIPMENTS TO KEY GULF TRADING PARTNERS INCLUDING THE UAE AND SAUDI ARABIA FELL SHARPLY.
WHY IS THE GOVERNMENT PLANNING TO BOOST THE SCHEME?
THE GOVERNMENT IS PLANNING TO RAISE SPENDING AND EXTEND THE SCHEME BY FIVE YEARS TO HELP EXPORTERS FACING CASH-FLOW STRAINS. HIGH FREIGHT RATES ON RISK-FRAUGHT GULF ROUTES AND THE MIDDLE EAST CONFLICT HAVE CLOUDED THE TRADE OUTLOOK.
WHAT OTHER MEASURES HAS THE GOVERNMENT TAKEN?
EARLIER THIS WEEK, INDIA APPROVED A NEW EMERGENCY CREDIT GUARANTEE PROGRAMME WORTH $1.9 BILLION. IN MARCH, THE GOVERNMENT ALSO OFFERED INSURANCE COVER FOR SHIPMENTS MOVING THROUGH AFFECTED CORRIDORS TO STABILISE COSTS.
