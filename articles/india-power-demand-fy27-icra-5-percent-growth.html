India Power Demand to Rise 5-5.5% in FY27: ICRA Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICRA forecasts India's power demand to grow 5.0-5.5% in FY27, up sharply from the tepid 1% growth recorded in FY26
  • 02/Rising industrial activity, EV adoption, data centres and sub-par rainfall expectations are expected to drive electricity consumption
  • 03/Thermal plant load factor to stay around 65% amid renewable growth and 6-GW of thermal capacity addition

Rating agency ICRA forecasts India's power demand will grow 5.0-5.5 per cent in financial year 2026-27. This marks a sharp rebound from the tepid one per cent growth recorded in 2025-26.

The demand uptick will be supported by continued momentum in industrial and commercial activity. Agricultural and household consumption is also expected to rise given weather conditions.

Emerging demand sources include electric vehicles and data centres. These sectors are becoming significant new consumers of electricity in the Indian grid.

Sub-par rainfall amid a potential El Nino will boost power use for irrigation and cooling. The weather factor adds to base demand from rural and residential users.

The all-India thermal plant load factor fell to 65-66 per cent in 2025-26. It is likely to remain around 65 per cent in FY27 amid healthy renewable generation growth.

About 6 gigawatts of new thermal capacity is expected to come online during the year. Coal stock levels for domestic power plants stood at a comfortable 19 days as on April 8, 2026.

"Thermal power acts as a reliable base-load supply, aiding grid stability, amid expectations of power demand growth," said Ankit Jain, Vice President and Co-Group Head - Corporate Ratings, ICRA.

ICRA's outlook for the power distribution segment remains Negative. The cash gap per unit for discoms could stay high at 30-33 paise per unit in FY27 amid limited tariff hikes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS ICRA'S POWER DEMAND GROWTH FORECAST FOR INDIA IN FY27?
ICRA FORECASTS INDIA'S POWER DEMAND TO GROW BY 5.0-5.5 PER CENT IN FINANCIAL YEAR 2026-27, UP SHARPLY FROM THE ONE PER CENT GROWTH RECORDED IN 2025-26.
WHAT ARE THE KEY DRIVERS OF POWER DEMAND GROWTH IN FY27?
THE KEY DRIVERS INCLUDE CONTINUED MOMENTUM IN INDUSTRIAL AND COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY, ELECTRIC VEHICLE ADOPTION, DATA CENTRES, AND INCREASED AGRICULTURAL AND HOUSEHOLD DEMAND DUE TO EXPECTATIONS OF SUB-PAR RAINFALL AMIDST A POTENTIAL EL NINO.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR THERMAL POWER PLANT UTILISATION IN FY27?
THE ALL-INDIA THERMAL PLANT LOAD FACTOR FELL TO 65-66 PER CENT IN 2025-26 AND IS LIKELY TO REMAIN AROUND 65 PER CENT IN 2026-27, GIVEN HEALTHY RENEWABLE GENERATION GROWTH AND ABOUT 6-GW OF THERMAL CAPACITY ADDITION.
WHAT DID ICRA SAY ABOUT COAL STOCK LEVELS AND GRID STABILITY?
COAL STOCK LEVELS FOR DOMESTIC POWER PLANTS WERE COMFORTABLE AT AROUND 19 DAYS AS ON APRIL 8, 2026. ANKIT JAIN, VICE PRESIDENT AT ICRA, SAID THERMAL POWER ACTS AS A RELIABLE BASE-LOAD SUPPLY, AIDING GRID STABILITY AMID EXPECTATIONS OF POWER DEMAND GROWTH.
WHAT IS ICRA'S OUTLOOK FOR THE POWER DISTRIBUTION SEGMENT?
ICRA'S OUTLOOK FOR THE POWER DISTRIBUTION SEGMENT REMAINS NEGATIVE. THE CASH GAP PER UNIT FOR DISCOMS COULD REMAIN HIGH AT 30-33 PAISE PER UNIT IN FY27 AMID LIMITED TARIFF HIKES AND INCREASED POWER PURCHASE COSTS.
