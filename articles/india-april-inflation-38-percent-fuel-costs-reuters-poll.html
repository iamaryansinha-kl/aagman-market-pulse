India's April Inflation Likely Rose to 3.8% on Higher Fuel Costs, Poll Shows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's April consumer inflation likely rose to 3.80% from 3.40% in March, per a Reuters poll of 46 economists.
  • 02/Higher fuel costs and LPG price hikes following the US-Iran war are feeding into consumer prices after 12 months of subdued inflation.
  • 03/CPI data releases on May 12; economists expect core inflation at 3.55% and warn of potential RBI rate pressure if monsoon disappoints.

India's annual consumer inflation likely rose to 3.80% in April from 3.40% in March. A Reuters poll of 46 economists conducted May 4-8 showed the uptick.

Forecasts in the poll ranged from 2.80% to 4.20%. The figure moves inflation closer to the Reserve Bank of India's 4% medium-term target.

Higher fuel costs following the US-Iran war are now feeding into retail prices. Crude oil remains about 40% above pre-war levels.

India is the world's third-largest oil importer. It has cut taxes on petrol and diesel to shield consumers from rising prices.

"We expect April headline inflation to tick up on higher food and some pass-through of higher global energy prices," said Rahul Bajoria of BofA Securities.

Indian companies raised LPG prices in March after the Middle East conflict disrupted supplies. That impact likely carried into April, said HDFC Bank's Sakshi Gupta.

Core inflation, excluding volatile food and fuel, is expected at 3.55% in April. The official CPI data releases on May 12.

Wholesale price inflation likely rose to 4.40% in April from 3.88% in March. A below-normal monsoon warning could further pressure food prices.
