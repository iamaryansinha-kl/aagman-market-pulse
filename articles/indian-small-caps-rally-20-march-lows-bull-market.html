Indian Small-Caps Rally 20% From March Lows to Near Bull Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty Smallcap 250 Index rebounds 20% from March lows, nearing bull market territory as first broader segment to reach milestone
  • 02/Retail investors return to small-cap space after 10% March selloff triggered by Middle East conflict and valuation concerns
  • 03/Brokerages lift earnings estimates for smaller companies; fund managers favor mining, industrials, power and capital markets sectors

India's small-cap stocks are staging a sharp comeback. The Nifty Smallcap 250 Index advanced 1% in early trading on Wednesday.

The index has now extended its rebound from March lows to 20%. This puts it on the cusp of a bull market milestone.

The 250-member gauge had fallen 10% in March amid a bruising correction. Valuations came under pressure amid worries over earnings improvement pace.

A recovery in broader markets last month pushed the index 17% higher. This marked its best-ever monthly rally on record.

Retail investors are driving the renewed interest in small-caps. They serve as key drivers and a barometer of risk sentiment in this segment.

Brokerages have begun lifting earnings estimates for smaller companies. They cite stronger growth visibility relative to larger peers.

"Growth in small- and mid-cap companies is structurally stronger than in large caps," said Alok Agarwal, Fund Manager, Alchemy Capital Management. Investors are building positions selectively, he added.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi's party delivered a strong showing in recent state elections. This reinforced expectations that reforms will continue, supporting the domestic backdrop.
