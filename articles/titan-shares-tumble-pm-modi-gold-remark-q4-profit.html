Titan Shares Tumble 8% After PM Modi's Gold Remark, Q4 Profit Up 35%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Titan Company shares crashed 8% on May 11 after PM Modi urged citizens to avoid non-essential gold purchases for one year
  • 02/Q4FY26 net profit surged 35% YoY to ₹1,179 crore, with domestic jewellery EBIT margin at 11.1%
  • 03/Brokerages remain divided: Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan target ₹5,400, while Citi stays neutral at ₹5,075

Titan Company Ltd (NSE: TITAN) shares plunged 8% in early trade on Monday, May 11, 2026. The stock hit an intraday low of ₹4,150.10, down 7.9% from the previous close of ₹4,509.

The sell-off came after Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged citizens to avoid non-essential gold purchases for one year. The measure aims to reduce pressure on forex outflows amid the West Asia war.

The sharp drop erased gains from the previous session, when Titan hit a 52-week high of ₹4,605. The stock led Nifty 50 losers on Monday despite strong quarterly results.

Titan posted a 35% year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit to ₹1,179 crore for Q4FY26. This compares with ₹871 crore in the year-ago period.

The domestic jewellery business delivered healthy profitability, with EBIT margin at 11.1%. This was ahead of investor expectations of 10.5-11%.

Tanishq, Mia and Zoya reported a combined EBIT margin of 11.3%. CaratLane margin stood at 8.3% in the quarter.

Domestic jewellery EBIT rose 41% year-on-year, driven by strong buyer growth and higher gold prices. However, consolidated jewellery EBIT margin declined 100 basis points to 10% due to international losses.

Management said the international jewellery business could return to positive margins within two to three quarters. Middle East disruptions and Damas consolidation weighed on overseas operations.

CLSA maintained an outperform rating with a target price of ₹5,249. It said standalone sales growth of 78% was led largely by higher average transaction values.

Citi retained a neutral rating with a target of ₹5,075. It cautioned that sustained high gold prices may have led to preponement of consumer demand, making FY27 growth trends critical.

Goldman Sachs reiterated its buy rating with a target of ₹5,400. It highlighted that domestic jewellery EBIT margin exceeded investor expectations despite elevated gold prices.

JP Morgan upgraded the stock from neutral to overweight, setting a target price of ₹5,400. BofA Securities maintained a buy with a target of ₹4,830, citing multiple levers to sustain 15-20% earnings CAGR.
