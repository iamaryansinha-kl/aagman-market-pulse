Zen Technologies Q4 Profit Crashes 83% to ₹14.75 Cr, Stock Tanks 12%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Zen Technologies Q4 standalone net profit crashes 83% YoY to ₹14.75 crore from ₹84.92 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue from operations plunges 75.5% to ₹71.90 crore; stock tanks over 12% to ₹1,466 intraday on May 4
  • 03/Board declares ₹1 per share final dividend for FY26; full-year standalone PAT drops to ₹145.85 crore from ₹262.95 crore

Zen Technologies Ltd (NSE: ZENTEC) reported a standalone net profit of ₹14.75 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks an 83% year-on-year decline from ₹84.92 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations collapsed 75.5% to ₹71.90 crore. The figure stood at ₹293.49 crore in Q4 FY25.

The dismal numbers triggered a sharp sell-off in early trade on May 4, 2026. The stock plunged over 12%, hitting an intraday low of ₹1,466.10 from the previous close of ₹1,671.10.

On a consolidated basis, Q4 revenue came in at ₹178.08 crore and net profit at ₹47.24 crore. Both figures also reflected steep YoY contractions of over 45% and 68% respectively.

Full-year standalone PAT for FY26 stood at ₹145.85 crore. This is down sharply from ₹262.95 crore reported in FY25.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹1 per share for FY26. The face value of each share is ₹1, making it a 100% dividend on face value.

The company also approved the appointment of Dr. Sreenivas Rao Yellamanchali as Chief Technology Officer. He takes charge effective May 06, 2026.

Zen Technologies ended FY26 with an order book exceeding ₹1,300 crore. Brokerages remained cautious on the stock following the earnings update.
