JSW Energy Sells ₹3,150 Cr JSW Steel Stake to GQG, SBI Mutual Fund

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JSW Energy sells 2.5 crore JSW Steel shares for ₹3,150 crore via NSE block deal on May 18, 2026
  • 02/GQG Partners buys 1.5 crore shares (0.61%) for ₹1,890 crore; SBI Mutual Fund adds 1 crore shares (0.4%) for ₹1,260 crore
  • 03/JSW Energy held 2.86% stake in JSW Steel as of March 2026; proceeds to fund 30 GW capacity target by 2030

JSW Energy Ltd has divested a portion of its stake in JSW Steel Ltd via a block deal on the National Stock Exchange. The transaction fetched gross proceeds of ₹3,150 crore.

GQG Partners Emerging Markets Equity Fund purchased 1.5 crore shares, representing a 0.61% stake, for ₹1,890 crore. SBI Mutual Fund acquired an additional 1 crore shares, or 0.4% stake, for ₹1,260 crore.

The seller, JSW Energy, was a promoter entity holding 2.86% in JSW Steel as of March 2026. The deal price was fixed at ₹1,260 per share.

JSW Steel shares closed at ₹1,292.80 on May 18, 2026, up 1.09% on the NSE. The stock traded above all key moving averages following the block deal.

SBI Mutual Fund, through its SBI Arbitrage Opportunities Fund, already held a 1.16% stake in JSW Steel prior to this purchase. The fresh acquisition strengthens its position further.

JSW Energy is aggressively expanding its power generation footprint. It aims to reach 30 GW capacity by 2030, up from 13.4 GW currently operational.

The company reported a 2.5-fold jump in net profit to ₹420 crore in Q3 FY26. EBITDA grew 98% year-on-year, while free cash reserves stood above ₹7,000 crore.

All new capacity additions are backed by pre-existing Power Purchase Agreements. This provides revenue visibility and reduces merchant power exposure for investors.
