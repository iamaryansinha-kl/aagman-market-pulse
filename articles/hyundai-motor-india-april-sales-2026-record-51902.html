Hyundai Motor India April Sales Hit Record 51,902 Units, Up 17% YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hyundai Motor India sold 51,902 domestic units in April 2026, marking a 17% year-on-year jump and its highest-ever April.
  • 02/The Venue SUV drove the record with 12,420 units after scoring a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating, while exports fell 16% to 13,708 units.
  • 03/Total sales reached 65,610 units, up 8% y-o-y; the company is banking on new launches including Exter, Verna and Ioniq 5 for sustained growth.

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) reported domestic wholesales of 51,902 units in April 2026. The figure marks a 17% year-on-year increase and the company's highest-ever April sales.

The company sold 44,374 units in April 2025. The record performance reflects sustained demand across the product portfolio.

The Hyundai Venue SUV emerged as the top performer with 12,420 units sold. This is its highest-ever monthly domestic tally, aided by a recent 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating.

Exports declined to 13,708 units from 16,400 units in April last year. The 16% drop reflects shifting international demand patterns.

Combined domestic and export sales stood at 65,610 units for the month. This is an 8% rise over the 60,774 units recorded in April 2025.

Recent launches including the new Exter, Verna, Ioniq 5, and Creta Summer Edition have strengthened the line-up. The Grand i10 Nios Vibe Edition and Venue Knight Edition also contributed.

"We have opened the new financial year on a strong note, carrying forward the momentum built in recent months into April 2026," said Tarun Garg, Managing Director and CEO, HMIL.

Garg noted that future-ready innovation and benchmark safety remain key priorities. The company is positioning its refreshed portfolio to sustain growth through FY2027.
