Radisson Targets 160 Hotels, 19,500 Keys in India by December 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Radisson Hotel Group targets 157-160 operational hotels and 19,000-19,500 keys in India by December 2026
  • 02/Currently operates 142 hotels with 17,000 keys; 84 hotels under development; 18 new openings planned this year
  • 03/West Asia crisis caused 27-28% RevPAR decline in select metros in March; tier-2 and tier-3 cities remain resilient

Radisson Hotel Group expects to operate 157-160 hotels in India by December 2026. The portfolio will span 19,000-19,500 keys across the country.

The group currently operates 142 hotels with 17,000 keys. It has 84 hotels under development, reflecting a strong expansion pipeline.

Nikhil Sharma, Managing Director and COO for South Asia, confirmed the target. He said the group remains "very much on track" for its 2030 goal.

"We are at 17,000 keys in operation currently. With 18 more hotels set to open this year, we are adding between 2,000 and 2,500 keys," Sharma told PTI.

The 2030 target stands at 500 hotels in India. This represents more than triple the current operational footprint.

On demand trends, Sharma noted a mixed impact from the West Asia crisis. Some metro cities saw demand slowdown while tier-2 and tier-3 markets held steady.

March saw a 27-28 per cent decline in RevPAR across select metro cities. The impact was not uniform across all markets.

April trends will be critical in assessing recovery momentum. Domestic demand from smaller cities continues to provide a cushion to the sector.
