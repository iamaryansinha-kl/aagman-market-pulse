FMC Sells India Business to Crystal Crop for $252 Million in All-Cash Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FMC Corporation signs definitive agreement to sell FMC India Private Ltd to Crystal Crop Protection for $252 million in an all-cash transaction
  • 02/Deal value dropped sharply from earlier $450 million reported price tag, reflecting tough conditions in India's crop protection market
  • 03/Transaction expected to close by year-end 2026, subject to regulatory approvals; all proceeds earmarked for FMC debt reduction

FMC Corporation has signed a definitive agreement to sell its India commercial business. Crystal Crop Protection Ltd will acquire FMC India Private Ltd for $252 million.

The deal was announced on May 7, 2026. It covers FMC India's entire commercial crop protection operations and brand licences in the country.

Crystal Crop will gain preferred access to FMC's active ingredients and future product pipeline. The transaction is expected to close by December 2026, subject to regulatory approvals.

FMC intends to use all sale proceeds for debt reduction. It will continue receiving cash generated by the India business until closing.

The deal value dropped sharply from the $450 million price reported earlier. CNBC-TV18 first reported Crystal Crop's interest on February 6, 2026.

FMC had announced its intent to exit the India commercial business in July 2025. It aimed to focus resources on higher-growth global opportunities.

"Crystal Crop is well-positioned to serve Indian farmers with FMC's portfolio," said Pierre Brondeau, Chairman and CEO of FMC Corporation.

Crystal Crop Chairman Ankur Aggarwal said the deal accelerates innovation across chemical and biological domains. Founded in 1994, Crystal Crop has made about 15 acquisitions in recent years. FMC shares rose 2.97% to $15.23 in pre-market trading.
