Kacholia, Khemani Backs Venus Pipes' Rs 372 Crore Preferential Issue to Cut Debt

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ The preferential issue is worth up to Rs 372 crore at Rs 1,670 per share.
  • •- 02/ Ashish Kacholia is proposed 1.79 lakh shares; his holding would reach 0.78% post-issue.
  • •- 03/ Vikas Khemani-linked Carnelian funds are proposed 2.12 lakh and 86,826 shares.
  • •- 04/ Rs 344 crore of proceeds go to debt repayment within six months.
  • •- 05/ Shareholders vote at an EGM on October 8; the stock jumped 9%.

## Marquee names on the list

Venus Pipes and Tubes has proposed a Rs 372 crore preferential issue to a clutch of well-known investors, sending the stock up 9% on Thursday.

The company plans to issue up to 22.27 lakh shares at Rs 1,670 apiece, including a premium of Rs 1,660 per share. Ashish Kacholia has been proposed an allotment of 1.79 lakh shares, which would take his holding to 0.78% of the post-issue capital.

Vikas Khemani-linked Carnelian Bharat Amritkaal Fund is proposed 2.12 lakh shares, with Carnelian Bharat Amritkaal Fund-2 set to receive 86,826 shares. Khemani and Swati Vikas Khemani are the ultimate beneficial owners of the two funds.

The broader list includes WhiteOak Capital Equity Fund, WhiteOak Capital Equity Trust, WhiteOak Capital ELSS Tax Saver Fund, Tata Business Cycle Fund, Tata Multicap Fund and Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance. All allottees are non-promoter entities, and the issue will not change management or control.

## The real purpose: debt reduction

Most of the money is earmarked for the balance sheet. Venus Pipes plans to use Rs 344 crore from the proceeds to repay or pre-pay certain borrowings — including estimated prepayment charges and accrued interest — within six months. Another Rs 28 crore is for general corporate purposes.

Because the issue exceeds Rs 100 crore, CARE Ratings has been appointed as the monitoring agency for the use of proceeds and will file quarterly reports until the money is fully deployed.

Shareholders will vote on the allotment at an extraordinary general meeting on October 8, 2026.

## Why it matters

Preferential placements to marquee investors at a stated price often act as a valuation anchor, particularly for a mid-cap manufacturer raising money to deleverage. Here the signal is amplified: the issue price of Rs 1,670 implies the market is being asked to underwrite growth at a premium to prevailing levels, and the named buyers are among the most followed stock-pickers in the small- and mid-cap universe.

For Venus Pipes, cutting debt lowers interest costs ahead of any rate-hike cycle — a live concern after the Fed's overnight move and rising Indian bond yields.
