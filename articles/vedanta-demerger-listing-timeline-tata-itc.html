Vedanta Demerger: What Tata Motors and ITC Teach Us About Listing Timelines

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta's mega demerger has created four distinct entities, with investors now eagerly awaiting their stock exchange listings
  • 02/Recent large demergers like Tata Motors and ITC Hotels provide useful benchmarks for expected listing timelines
  • 03/The listing timeline remains the most watched event for Vedanta shareholders tracking value unlocking across the four businesses

Vedanta Ltd has completed its mega demerger, creating four distinct business entities. The split separates aluminium, oil and gas, iron and steel, and power businesses into independent companies.

Investors are now tracking the timeline for these entities to list on stock exchanges. The listing will unlock the underlying value of each standalone business.

Recent large demergers offer useful parallels. Tata Motors completed its demerger of commercial vehicles and passenger vehicles. ITC Hotels also underwent a similar restructuring.

These precedents suggest the listing process typically takes several months post-demerger. Regulatory approvals and compliance requirements are key factors.

Tata Motors' demerger showed how separate listings can unlock shareholder value. Each business trades at valuations more reflective of its specific sector.

ITC Hotels' split demonstrated similar dynamics. Pure-play entities often attract different investor bases and valuation multiples.

Vedanta shareholders are watching for exchange approvals and record date announcements. The four entities will trade independently once listed.
