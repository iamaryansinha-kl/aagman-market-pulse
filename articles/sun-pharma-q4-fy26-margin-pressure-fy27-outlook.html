Sun Pharma Q4 FY26: EBITDA Margin Dips 160 Bps, FY27 Growth to Slow

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sun Pharma Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin contracted 160 bps YoY to 27.1%, an eight-quarter low
  • 02/FY26 revenue reached ₹58,220.1 crore up 11.9%; FY27 growth expected to moderate to high single-digit
  • 03/Stock trades at 38.6x P/E, above 3-year and 5-year averages; upside may remain capped

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (NSE: SUNPHARMA) reported double-digit revenue and net profit growth for the March quarter of FY26. EBITDA margin came under pressure from increased investments and lower milestone income.

EBITDA margin contracted 160 basis points year-on-year to 27.1% in the March quarter. This marks an eight-quarter low for the company.

R&D expenses as a proportion of revenue hit an eight-quarter high of 6.7% in Q4. The company expects R&D spending to remain elevated at 6-7% of sales for FY27.

Revenue for FY26 stood at ₹58,220.1 crore, registering 11.9% growth. The company expects revenue growth to moderate to high single-digit in FY27.

The domestic formulation segment remained strong, outperforming the broader industry for two consecutive years. This was driven by new launches and market share gains in existing products.

The US formulations segment declined marginally in the March quarter. Pricing pressure and base portfolio erosion could keep it subdued in the near term.

The stock currently trades at a P/E of 38.6, above its three-year average of 35 and five-year average of 32. This premium valuation may limit upside in the medium term.

The proposed Organon acquisition and a pipeline across dermatology, oncology and metabolic therapies remain key growth drivers. Recovery in US formulations will depend on new product launches and pipeline approvals.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS SUN PHARMA'S EBITDA MARGIN IN Q4 FY26 AND HOW DID IT CHANGE?
SUN PHARMA'S EBITDA MARGIN CONTRACTED 160 BASIS POINTS YEAR-ON-YEAR TO 27.1% IN THE MARCH QUARTER OF FY26. THIS REPRESENTS AN EIGHT-QUARTER LOW FOR THE COMPANY.
WHAT DROVE THE MARGIN PRESSURE IN Q4 FY26?
THE MARGIN PRESSURE WAS DRIVEN BY HIGHER INVESTMENTS, LOWER MILESTONE INCOME FROM PRODUCTS, AND ELEVATED R&D SPENDING. R&D EXPENSES AS A PROPORTION OF REVENUE HIT AN EIGHT-QUARTER HIGH OF 6.7% IN THE MARCH QUARTER.
WHAT IS SUN PHARMA'S REVENUE AND GROWTH OUTLOOK FOR FY27?
SUN PHARMA REPORTED FY26 REVENUE OF ₹58,220.1 CRORE, UP 11.9%. FOR FY27, THE COMPANY EXPECTS REVENUE GROWTH TO MODERATE TO HIGH SINGLE-DIGIT LEVELS.
HOW IS THE DOMESTIC FORMULATION SEGMENT PERFORMING?
THE DOMESTIC FORMULATION SEGMENT REMAINED STRONG, OUTPERFORMING THE BROADER INDUSTRY FOR TWO CONSECUTIVE YEARS. THE MOMENTUM IS EXPECTED TO SUSTAIN, SUPPORTED BY NEW LAUNCHES AND EXPANSION INTO HIGH-GROWTH SEGMENTS SUCH AS GLP-1 THERAPIES.
WHAT IS THE VALUATION OF SUN PHARMA AND WHAT COULD LIMIT THE STOCK UPSIDE?
SUN PHARMA'S STOCK TRADES AT A P/E OF 38.6, WHICH IS ABOVE ITS THREE-YEAR AVERAGE OF 35 AND FIVE-YEAR AVERAGE OF 32. THIS PREMIUM VALUATION, COMBINED WITH PROFITABILITY PRESSURE AND SLOWER REVENUE GROWTH, MAY LIMIT UPSIDE IN THE MEDIUM TERM.
