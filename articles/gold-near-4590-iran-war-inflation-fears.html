Gold Near $4,590 After 2.4% Drop as Iran War Fuels Inflation Fears

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold steadied near $4,589 an ounce after falling 2.4% over two sessions to the lowest level in almost four weeks
  • 02/The indefinite closure of the Strait of Hormuz amid US-Iran war talks continues to heighten inflation risks and pressure bullion
  • 03/Traders await rate decisions from the US Fed, ECB, BoE and BoC this week; technical support broke at $4,650

Gold steadied near $4,590 an ounce on April 29 after a sharp two-day decline. The metal fell 2.4% to its lowest level in almost four weeks.

Spot gold dipped 0.2% to $4,589.04 in early Singapore trade. Silver rose 0.1% to $73.13 an ounce, while platinum and palladium were little changed.

The Strait of Hormuz remains closed indefinitely as US-Iran war negotiations drag on. President Trump said Iran has asked the US to lift a naval blockade of the waterway.

Inflation risks are rising from the energy supply shock. This raises the likelihood that central banks will hold interest rates steady for longer, which hurts non-yielding gold.

The Federal Reserve is widely expected to hold rates at its meeting ending Wednesday. The Bank of Japan left its benchmark rate unchanged at 0.75% on Tuesday.

Gold has lost about 13% since the Iran conflict began at the end of February. Crude oil prices have soared in the same period, worsening the inflation outlook.

"A break below recent support around $4,650" triggered some technical selling, said Ole Hansen, head of commodity strategy at Saxo Bank, in a note.

"The market's immediate focus remains on mediation efforts," Hansen added. A reopening of the strait and a subsequent drop in oil would be the biggest short-term upside catalyst for metals.

WHAT IS DRIVING THE RECENT VOLATILITY IN GOLD PRICES?
THE INDEFINITE CLOSURE OF THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ AMID THE ONGOING US-IRAN WAR IS HEIGHTENING GLOBAL INFLATION RISKS. RISING ENERGY COSTS ARE PUSHING CENTRAL BANKS TOWARD STEADY OR HIGHER INTEREST RATES, WHICH INCREASES THE OPPORTUNITY COST OF HOLDING NON-YIELDING BULLION.
HOW MUCH HAS GOLD FALLEN SINCE THE IRAN CONFLICT BEGAN?
GOLD HAS LOST ABOUT 13% SINCE THE CONFLICT BEGAN AT THE END OF FEBRUARY 2026. CRUDE OIL PRICES HAVE SOARED DURING THE SAME PERIOD, DEEPENING THE ENERGY SUPPLY SHOCK.
WHAT TECHNICAL LEVELS ARE TRADERS WATCHING?
TECHNICAL SELLING EMERGED AFTER GOLD BROKE BELOW RECENT SUPPORT AROUND $4,650, ACCORDING TO OLE HANSEN, HEAD OF COMMODITY STRATEGY AT SAXO BANK. THE MARKET'S FOCUS IS ON MEDIATION EFFORTS AND A POTENTIAL REOPENING OF THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ.
WHAT CENTRAL BANK DECISIONS ARE EXPECTED THIS WEEK?
THE FEDERAL RESERVE IS WIDELY EXPECTED TO HOLD RATES STEADY AT ITS MEETING ENDING WEDNESDAY. TRADERS ARE ALSO WATCHING DECISIONS FROM THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK, THE BANK OF ENGLAND AND THE BANK OF CANADA IN COMING DAYS.
