Nifty Holds 24,000 on April Expiry; Oil, Iran Tensions Weigh on Markets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 holds above 24,000 on April series expiry, closing at 24,086.70, down 6 points amid volatile global cues
  • 02/Brent crude trades at $102.39 per barrel as Iran conflict disrupts Strait of Hormuz, keeping markets cautious
  • 03/FIIs offloaded ₹1,152 crore on Monday while DIIs bought ₹4,124 crore; India VIX at 18.37 signals uncertainty

The Nifty 50 closed at 24,086.70 on April 28, down 6 points from Monday's close of 24,092.70. The April derivative contracts expired amid mixed global cues and geopolitical tension.

The Sensex ended at 77,328.64, gaining 25.01 points. It had opened lower at 77,094.79 and was down 129.64 points or 0.17 per cent in early morning trade.

Brent crude was trading at $102.39 a barrel. The ongoing Iran conflict has disrupted the Strait of Hormuz, creating supply uncertainty for oil markets globally.

Coal India led Nifty 50 gainers with a 1.57 per cent rise to ₹459.60. State Bank of India fell the most, down 1.16 per cent to ₹1,098.90 from its previous close of ₹1,111.85.

Foreign Institutional Investors remained net sellers, offloading equities worth approximately ₹1,152 crore on Monday. Domestic Institutional Investors provided an offset with net buying of around ₹4,124 crore.

"...Nifty is expected to open slightly lower around 24,000...traders should avoid aggressive positions at the open," said Gaurav Udani, Founder of ThinCredBlu Securities.

"...elevated crude oil prices continue to be a key concern, with Brent trading in the $106–110 per barrel range, adding to inflationary pressures and weighing on sentiment," said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money.

"...FIIs are likely to continue selling...when that happens, fairly-valued largecaps will outperform," said Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments.
