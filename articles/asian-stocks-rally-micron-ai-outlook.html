Asian Stocks Rally With US Futures on Micron's Blowout AI Outlook

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Asian stocks and US futures rallied after Micron Technology's blowout sales forecast reignited confidence in the AI trade
  • 02/Nasdaq 100 futures rose almost 2%, South Korea's Kospi surged nearly 5%, and Micron shares soared about 15% after the market close
  • 03/Investors now await Thursday's US PCE inflation data for further cues on the Federal Reserve's interest-rate path

Asian stocks rallied alongside US equity futures after Micron Technology's blowout sales outlook reignited confidence in the artificial-intelligence trade. The chipmaker's quarterly forecast crushed Wall Street estimates.

Contracts for the Nasdaq 100 rose almost 2% while those for the S&P 500 gained 0.5%. South Korea's Kospi benchmark surged nearly 5% in early trading.

The broader MSCI Asia Pacific Index jumped more than 1%. Micron, the largest US maker of computer memory chips, soared about 15% after the market close.

Brent crude extended losses to trade below $74 a barrel. Oil prices tumbled more than 4% in the previous session on signs of swelling supply and progress on a US-Iran peace deal.

"Micron far exceeded analyst expectations and forecast robust future chip demand," said Kyle Rodda, analyst at Capital.com. "US futures have more than recovered the losses sustained in cash trade."

SK Hynix announced plans for a US stock listing worth about $29 billion. Its shares surged as high as 11% in early Asian trading on the news.

The 10-year Treasury yield plunged 11 basis points while the 30-year touched 4.85%. The two-year yield slipped to about 4.15%.

Forecasters expect Thursday's personal consumption expenditures price index to show acceleration in May. This data will shape the Federal Reserve's interest-rate outlook.
