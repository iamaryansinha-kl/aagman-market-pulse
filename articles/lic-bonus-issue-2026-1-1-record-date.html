LIC Bonus Issue 2026: 1:1 Bonus Share Allotment, Record Date May 29

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LIC announces 1:1 bonus issue; shareholders to receive one free share for every share held
  • 02/Record date fixed at May 29, 2026; only shareholders on record will be eligible for bonus shares
  • 03/Announcement follows Q4 results showing 23% YoY profit growth to ₹23,467 crore; stock movement ahead of record date to watch

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) has announced a 1:1 bonus issue. The board approved the issue on May 21, 2026.

Shareholders will receive one additional equity share for every share held. The record date to determine eligibility is set at May 29, 2026.

The bonus issue announcement follows LIC's Q4 FY2026 earnings. The company reported a 23% year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit.

The Q4 net profit stood at ₹23,467 crore. This reflects strong operational performance in the insurance and investment segments.

LIC, India's largest insurer, is listed on both NSE and BSE. The stock trades under the ticker symbol LIC.

The bonus issue is subject to shareholder approval and regulatory clearances. Once approved, the bonus shares will be credited to eligible demat accounts.

Investors holding LIC shares in their demat accounts as of May 29, 2026 will qualify. The bonus shares typically get credited within 15-30 days of the record date.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE LIC BONUS ISSUE ANNOUNCED IN MAY 2026?
LIC HAS ANNOUNCED A 1:1 BONUS ISSUE, WHICH MEANS SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE ONE ADDITIONAL EQUITY SHARE FOR EVERY SHARE THEY CURRENTLY HOLD.
WHAT IS THE RECORD DATE FOR THE LIC BONUS ISSUE?
THE RECORD DATE TO DETERMINE ELIGIBILITY FOR THE BONUS ISSUE IS FRIDAY, MAY 29, 2026. ONLY SHAREHOLDERS HOLDING LIC SHARES IN THEIR DEMAT ACCOUNTS ON THIS DATE WILL QUALIFY.
WHAT WERE LIC'S Q4 FY2026 EARNINGS?
LIC REPORTED A 23% YEAR-ON-YEAR RISE IN CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT FOR Q4 FY2026, REACHING ₹23,467 CRORE.
WHAT DOES A 1:1 BONUS ISSUE MEAN FOR SHAREHOLDERS?
A 1:1 BONUS ISSUE MEANS THE NUMBER OF SHARES HELD BY EACH SHAREHOLDER WILL DOUBLE. THE SHARE PRICE WILL ADJUST PROPORTIONATELY, SO THE TOTAL VALUE OF HOLDINGS REMAINS THE SAME INITIALLY.
WHEN WILL THE BONUS SHARES BE CREDITED TO SHAREHOLDERS?
AFTER THE RECORD DATE OF MAY 29, 2026, THE BONUS SHARES ARE TYPICALLY CREDITED TO ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS' DEMAT ACCOUNTS WITHIN 15 TO 30 DAYS, PENDING REGULATORY AND SHAREHOLDER APPROVALS.
