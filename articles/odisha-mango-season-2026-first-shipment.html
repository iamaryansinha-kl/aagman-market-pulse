Odisha Mango Season 2026: First Domestic Shipment With ₹45/Kg Prices

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Odisha dispatches first 3-tonne domestic mango consignment from Dhenkanal to Siliguri, kicking off the 2026 marketing season
  • 02/Farmer price realisation jumps to ₹45/kg from ₹25/kg in 2025, an 80% YoY gain at season start
  • 03/State targets over 500 tonnes of total mango trade this season, scaling up from 120 tonnes exported to 11 countries last year

Odisha's 2026 mango marketing season began with the first major domestic linkage. Madanmohan Farmer Producer Company dispatched 3 tonnes of premium mangoes from Dhenkanal to Siliguri, West Bengal. The shipment was facilitated under the National Cooperative Development Corporation.

Farmers are seeing a sharp jump in earnings this year. Price realisation has climbed to ₹45 per kg from ₹25 per kg in 2025. This marks an 80 per cent year-on-year increase at the start of the season.

The initiative falls under the PSFPO programme led by the Directorate of Horticulture. It is implemented by Palladium Consulting India Pvt. Ltd. with support from the Gates Foundation. On-ground assistance comes from Harsha Trust.

Officials noted that the quality of the first consignment met market expectations. This helped strengthen buyer confidence early in the season. Strong quality signals bode well for larger shipments ahead.

Odisha exported over 120 tonnes of mangoes to 11 countries last year. The state is now targeting over 500 tonnes of total mango trade for the 2026 season. Both domestic and overseas channels are being scaled up.

The programme aims to build a more structured and market-linked ecosystem for farmers. It links small producers directly to buyers, cutting out intermediaries. Higher price discovery is the immediate outcome.

Early momentum suggests the season could bring significant income gains for mango growers. Sustained buyer demand and quality standards will determine the final trade volumes. Weather and logistics remain key watch points.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH DID THE FIRST ODISHA MANGO SHIPMENT WEIGH?
THE FIRST MAJOR DOMESTIC SHIPMENT FROM ODISHA WEIGHED 3 TONNES OF PREMIUM MANGOES. IT WAS DISPATCHED FROM DHENKANAL TO SILIGURI, WEST BENGAL BY MADANMOHAN FARMER PRODUCER COMPANY UNDER THE NATIONAL COOPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT MANGO PRICE IN ODISHA FOR THE 2026 SEASON?
FARMERS ARE REALISING ₹45 PER KG FOR MANGOES AT THE START OF THE 2026 SEASON. THIS IS UP 80% FROM LAST YEAR'S ₹25 PER KG PRICE AT THE SAME STAGE.
WHICH PROGRAMME IS FACILITATING THE ODISHA MANGO SHIPMENTS?
THE SHIPMENTS ARE PART OF THE PROMOTION AND STABILIZATION OF FARMER PRODUCER ORGANIZATIONS (PSFPO) PROGRAMME. IT IS LED BY THE DIRECTORATE OF HORTICULTURE UNDER THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS EMPOWERMENT, AND IMPLEMENTED BY PALLADIUM CONSULTING INDIA PVT. LTD. WITH GATES FOUNDATION SUPPORT AND ON-GROUND ASSISTANCE FROM HARSHA TRUST.
WHAT IS ODISHA'S MANGO TRADE TARGET FOR THE 2026 SEASON?
ODISHA IS TARGETING OVER 500 TONNES OF TOTAL MANGO TRADE FOR THE 2026 SEASON. LAST YEAR, THE STATE EXPORTED OVER 120 TONNES TO MORE THAN 11 COUNTRIES.
WHY DOES THIS MANGO SEASON MATTER FOR ODISHA FARMERS?
THE SEASON SIGNALS A MORE STRUCTURED, MARKET-LINKED ECOSYSTEM THAT CONNECTS SMALL FARMERS DIRECTLY TO BUYERS. THE 80% PRICE JUMP BOOSTS FARMER CONFIDENCE AND INCOME AT THE START OF THE SEASON, WITH OFFICIALS NOTING THE FIRST CONSIGNMENT QUALITY MET MARKET EXPECTATIONS.
