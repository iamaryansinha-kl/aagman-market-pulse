FMCG Majors Stay Upbeat Despite El Niño Risks; Dabur, Godrej See Strong Q1 Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dabur expects double-digit revenue growth in Q1 FY27; Godrej Consumer sees high-teens growth
  • 02/Both companies cite resilient consumer sentiment despite El Niño risks, geopolitical tensions and rising input costs
  • 03/Dabur PAT expected to grow double-digit; GCPL margins improving as commodity costs ease in closing weeks of Q1

Consumer goods makers Dabur India and Godrej Consumer Products maintained upbeat Q1 FY27 guidance on Friday. Both cited resilient consumer sentiment despite macroeconomic headwinds.

Dabur expects double-digit consolidated revenue growth for the June quarter. The company said consumer sentiment remained resilient despite challenging geopolitical and hyperinflationary pressures.

Godrej Consumer Products expects high-teens revenue growth in Q1 FY27. This is ahead of its full-year double-digit guidance. Underlying volume growth is expected at strong high single-digit levels.

The India Meteorological Department warned that El Niño conditions are strengthening over the equatorial Pacific. Below-normal monsoon rainfall could hurt farm output and rural consumption.

"We remain mindful that El Niño conditions can heighten weather volatility," Godrej said. It added that geographically diversified sourcing provides meaningful resilience.

Input costs remained elevated through most of Q1. Crude, plastic, surfactants and vegetable oil prices rose after the Iran-US conflict in February 2026.

However, GCPL noted commodity costs began easing in the closing weeks of the quarter. Calibrated pricing actions and cost-savings programmes are supporting margins.

CPI inflation hit a 16-month high of 3.9% in May. Food prices drove the increase, according to MoSPI data.

Dabur reported FY26 revenue of ₹13,193 crore with 5% growth. Annual net profit rose 7.4% to ₹1,869 crore.

Godrej Consumer reported FY26 consolidated revenue of ₹15,177.9 crore, up 8.4%. Shares of Dabur closed at ₹444 and GCPL at ₹1,078 on Friday.
