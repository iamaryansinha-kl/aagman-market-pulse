Nifty to Hit 24,500 Next Week, Says Dharmesh Shah; M&M, L&T Top Picks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICICI Direct's Dharmesh Shah expects Nifty to reach 24,500 next week, with strong support at 23,800
  • 02/Bank Nifty broke above 58,000; Shah sets near-term target at 59,300 with support at 57,500
  • 03/Top picks are M&M (target ₹3,400, stop loss ₹2,900) and L&T (target ₹4,500, stop loss ₹4,050)

ICICI Direct's Dharmesh Shah expects the Nifty 50 to hit 24,500 in the coming week. The index has been testing resistance near its 100-day exponential moving average around 24,200.

Shah advises investors to treat any dip toward 23,800 as a buying opportunity. Strong support at that level limits downside risk for the week ahead.

Crude oil trending toward $68-69 per barrel is the key catalyst driving Shah's bullish view. Rupee stability and falling commodity prices add further tailwinds for Indian equities.

Bank Nifty closed above the critical 58,000 mark and now eyes 59,300. The banking index carries a 35% weightage in the Nifty 50, making its momentum significant for the broader market.

Scanning Nifty 50 constituents across banking, capital goods, and auto, the majority are indicating 5% to 10% upside from current levels. This breadth signals a genuine rally rather than a narrow one.

Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd (NSE: M&M) is Shah's top largecap auto pick. The stock retraced approximately 61% of its rally from ₹2,600 to ₹3,800, forming a strong base near the 52-week EMA.

Larsen & Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) is his highest-conviction capital goods call. The stock built a base in the ₹4,150-₹4,250 range and closed above ₹4,250, a technically significant breakout after the Middle East conflict selloff.

"Crude oil is the main trigger point for moving the Nifty above 24,200 — and things look positive from both the current and medium-term perspective," Shah said.
