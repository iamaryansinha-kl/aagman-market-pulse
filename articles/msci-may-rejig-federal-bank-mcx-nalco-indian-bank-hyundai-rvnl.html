MSCI May Rejig: Federal Bank, MCX, NALCO, Indian Bank Added; Hyundai, RVNL Exit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MSCI added Federal Bank, Multi Commodity Exchange, National Aluminium and Indian Bank to its Global Standard Index in the May periodic review
  • 02/Hyundai Motor India, Jubilant Foodworks, Kalyan Jewellers and Rail Vikas Nigam were excluded from the index, effective May 29, 2026
  • 03/Nuvama expects passive inflows of $491 million into Federal Bank, $373 million into MCX, $308 million into NALCO and $209 million into Indian Bank

Global index provider MSCI announced changes to its widely tracked Global Standard Index on Wednesday.

Federal Bank, Multi Commodity Exchange of India, National Aluminium Company and Indian Bank will enter the index.

Hyundai Motor India, Jubilant Foodworks, Kalyan Jewellers and Rail Vikas Nigam will be excluded.

The changes will take effect on May 29, 2026.

India's weight in the MSCI Global Standard Index remains broadly steady at 12.3 per cent.

This compares with 12.4 per cent after the February review.

The number of Indian constituents remains unchanged at 165.

Adani Energy Solutions was initially seen as a provisional addition.

It was left out after being placed under the NSE's additional surveillance mechanism framework.

MSCI indexes are key global benchmarks tracked by large passive funds.

Index changes drive significant stock-specific flows as funds rebalance portfolios.

Inclusions typically attract fresh passive capital.

Deletions often trigger outflows as index-tracking funds sell the excluded stocks.

Nuvama Quantitative and Alternative Research expects passive inflows of $491 million into Federal Bank.

MCX could see $373 million, National Aluminium $308 million and Indian Bank $209 million.
