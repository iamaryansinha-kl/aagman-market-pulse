India-Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement Comes Into Force From 4 July 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The India-Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement (BIA) came into force on 4 July 2026, marking a new phase in economic ties
  • 02/The BIA was signed on 8 September 2025 and creates balance between investor protection and sovereign policy space
  • 03/Expected to boost cross-border investment in defence, water, agriculture, cybersecurity, pharma and innovation sectors

The India-Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement (BIA) came into force on Saturday, 4 July 2026. This marks a new chapter in economic ties between the two nations.

The agreement was signed on 8 September 2025. It is expected to provide investors with greater certainty while deepening bilateral economic engagement.

The finance ministry described the BIA as a "landmark step towards strengthening bilateral economic relations." It ensures a secure and predictable investment climate.

The agreement creates a balance between protecting investments and retaining sovereign policy space. It reflects modern principles and evolving jurisprudence of international investment law.

India and Israel already share strong trade and technology partnerships. Key sectors include defence, water, agriculture, cybersecurity, pharma and innovation.

The BIA is expected to add momentum to investment flows in these sectors. It addresses investor concerns around protection, dispute resolution and regulatory clarity.

"The BIA is expected to contribute to increased cross-border investment activity and further deepen the economic partnership between India and Israel," the finance ministry said.

The agreement is flexible enough to retain sovereign policy space. This is in line with legitimate public policy objectives for both nations.
