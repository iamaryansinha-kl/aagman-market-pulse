SEBI Proposal to Raise MII CEO Age Cap to 70 Faces Industry Resistance

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI is discussing a proposal to raise the CEO age cap for market infrastructure institutions from 65 to 70 years.
  • 02/Industry participants have largely opposed the move, citing governance and succession planning risks.
  • 03/BSE CEO Sundararaman Ramamurthy is among those approaching the current age threshold of 65.

SEBI is discussing a proposal to raise the upper age limit for managing directors and CEOs of market infrastructure institutions. The change would increase the age cap from 65 to 70 years.

The proposal would affect stock exchanges including the National Stock Exchange (NSE: NSE), BSE Ltd (BSE: BSE), and Metropolitan Stock Exchange (MSE). It would also cover commodity exchanges like NCDEX and MCX, along with depositories and clearing houses.

Most industry peers have opposed the move. "The current limit ensures timely transition and avoids overdependence on individuals. This discipline should not be diluted," a source familiar with the discussions said.

"Extending the age limit could delay leadership transitions and affect the pipeline for next-generation executives," a senior exchange official said. "Fresh perspectives are increasingly valuable as markets become more technology-driven."

Divisions have also emerged within SEBI itself. Two distinct views exist among officials. The proposal remains at a discussion stage, with SEBI seeking industry feedback before any public consultation.

BSE Managing Director and Chief Executive Sundararaman Ramamurthy is approaching the current age threshold of 65. The tenure of Arun Raste, MD and CEO of NCDEX, ends this June.

Current SEBI regulations prescribe tenure limits and an age cap of 65 for MDs and CEOs at MIIs. Executives can serve fixed terms up to five years, subject to approval. The age limit acts as a hard stop.

MIIs are treated as public utilities given their role in price discovery and market stability. Any revision to the age cap would require SEBI board approval and public consultation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE CURRENT AGE CAP FOR MII CEOs AND MDs IN INDIA?
THE CURRENT UPPER AGE CAP FOR MANAGING DIRECTORS AND CHIEF EXECUTIVES OF MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE INSTITUTIONS IS 65 YEARS, AS PRESCRIBED BY SEBI REGULATIONS.
WHAT AGE CAP IS SEBI PROPOSING FOR MII CEOs?
SEBI IS DISCUSSING A PROPOSAL TO RAISE THE UPPER AGE LIMIT FROM 65 TO 70 YEARS FOR MDs AND CEOs OF MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE INSTITUTIONS.
WHICH INSTITUTIONS WOULD BE AFFECTED BY THIS PROPOSED CHANGE?
THE PROPOSAL WOULD AFFECT STOCK EXCHANGES INCLUDING NSE, BSE, AND MSE; COMMODITY EXCHANGES LIKE NCDEX AND MCX; AS WELL AS DEPOSITORIES AND CLEARING HOUSES.
WHY IS THE INDUSTRY OPPOSING THE AGE CAP INCREASE?
INDUSTRY PARTICIPANTS ARGUE THAT RAISING THE AGE CAP COULD WEAKEN SUCCESSION PLANNING, DELAY LEADERSHIP TRANSITIONS, AND REDUCE THE PIPELINE FOR NEXT-GENERATION EXECUTIVES AT SYSTEMICALLY IMPORTANT INSTITUTIONS.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF THIS PROPOSAL?
THE PROPOSAL IS CURRENTLY AT A DISCUSSION STAGE. SEBI IS SEEKING INDUSTRY FEEDBACK BEFORE MOVING FORWARD WITH PUBLIC CONSULTATION. ANY REVISION WOULD REQUIRE SEBI BOARD APPROVAL.
