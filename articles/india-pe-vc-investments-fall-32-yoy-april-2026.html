India PE-VC Investments Fall 32% YoY In April 2026 To $12 Billion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PE-VC investments in India fell 32% year-on-year in April 2026 on a value basis
  • 02/January-April 2026 saw $12 billion across 435 deals versus $14.6 billion across 467 deals in the same period last year
  • 03/Deal volume dropped 7% while average deal size shrank, signalling tighter capital deployment across stages

Private equity and venture capital investments in India declined 32% year-on-year in April 2026. The slowdown reflects a broader pullback in capital deployment across growth and late-stage deals.

Total PE-VC investments for the first four months of 2026 stood at approximately $12 billion. This compares with $14.6 billion deployed during the same period in 2025.

Deal count also fell, with 435 transactions completed against 467 in the year-ago period. The 7% decline in volume suggests fewer new commitments beyond just smaller cheque sizes.

The contraction comes amid elevated interest rates and tighter global liquidity conditions. Institutional investors have turned more selective on valuation multiples and path to profitability.

Early-stage deal flow has proven relatively more resilient than growth and late-stage rounds. Founders report longer fundraising cycles and more structured term sheets across the board.

Sectors such as financial services, consumer technology, and healthcare continued to attract the bulk of capital. Infrastructure and manufacturing saw comparatively muted activity.

Analysts expect the investment pace to remain subdued through the June quarter. A meaningful recovery is likely contingent on rate cuts and improved exit conditions via IPOs and secondary sales.
