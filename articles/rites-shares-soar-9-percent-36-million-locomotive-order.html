RITES Shares Soar 9% After Bagging $36 Million Locomotive Supply Order

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

RITES has landed a major export order.

The public sector engineering consultancy has secured a $36 million contract for locomotive supply. The announcement sent RITES shares soaring 9% in Tuesday's trading session.

The order is for the supply of locomotives to an overseas client. RITES did not name the specific country or rail operator in its disclosure to the stock exchanges.

The contract adds to RITES' growing project execution portfolio. Traditionally known as a consultancy firm for Indian Railways, the company has been aggressively diversifying into turnkey project implementation, exports, and leasing.

RITES has supplied rolling stock to several countries including Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, and African nations. The locomotive order reinforces its position as a key exporter of Indian rail equipment.

The PSU reported strong financial performance in recent quarters. Its order book has been bolstered by both domestic and international contracts, reducing dependence on consultancy fees.

The $36 million order is significant relative to RITES' quarterly revenue. Analysts say the contract demonstrates the company's ability to compete in the global rolling stock market.
