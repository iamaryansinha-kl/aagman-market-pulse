Rupee Falls 13 Paise to 94.81 Against US Dollar on Oil Price Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Rising crude oil prices and month-end dollar demand weighed on the currency amid safe-haven flows
  • 03/US Federal Reserve policy decision due today; rates seen on hold but tone will drive direction

The rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The currency opened at 94.79 in the interbank foreign exchange market.

Forex traders said rising global crude oil prices kept the rupee under sustained pressure. Month-end dollar demand added to the weakness.

The focus has shifted to the upcoming US Federal Reserve policy decision. While rates are expected to remain unchanged, the tone will carry more weight than the action.

Elevated crude prices have increased India's import bill concerns. A cautious global risk environment has pushed investors toward safe-haven assets.

The rupee had closed at 94.68 in the previous session. The 13-paise decline reflects continued external headwinds facing emerging market currencies.

Dollar strength and global uncertainty continue to influence short-term rupee movement. Market participants are tracking the Fed commentary for future cues.
