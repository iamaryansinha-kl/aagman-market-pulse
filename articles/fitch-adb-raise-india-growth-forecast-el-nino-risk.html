Fitch, ADB Raise India Growth Forecasts as Agency Upgrades Pile Up; El Nino Risk Looms

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Fitch Ratings has raised India's GDP growth forecast for the current fiscal year to 6.9% from 6.4%, citing stronger-than-expected expansion in the June quarter and the economy's resilience despite external shocks.

The agency, however, expects momentum to moderate during the rest of the fiscal year. Below-normal monsoon rainfall, elevated inflation and signs of slowing manufacturing and services activity could weigh on growth.

Fitch Expects October Rate Hike

Fitch expects the Reserve Bank of India to raise its policy rate by 25 basis points in October to 5.5%. It then sees a further increase to 5.75% in early 2027, before rates ease back to 5.5% in 2028.

The rate-hike expectation reflects a combination of strong demand, price rises and adverse supply developments. The US-Iran war has created terms-of-trade deterioration in the first half of 2026, but India's economy has shown resilience.

Private investment prospects look more buoyant. Fitch expects investment to rise by more than 10%, with non-food credit growth reaching 19% year-on-year in July.

ADB Also Upgrades

The Asian Development Bank raised its 2026 growth forecast for India to 7% from 6.6%. The ADB cited resilient private consumption, strong gross fixed capital formation, accelerating public infrastructure spending and a revival in private capital expenditure.

However, the ADB flagged a strengthening El Nino as a significant risk. India's monsoon is running 15% below the long-term average, raising concerns over Kharif crops including rice, sugarcane and corn.

A weaker harvest could add to food-price pressures and squeeze household budgets. Lower hydropower generation could compound the energy shock.

El Nino and Inflation Risk

Retail inflation rose to 4.8% in August, its third straight month above the RBI's 4% target. The ADB expects India's retail inflation to average 5.2% in 2026, leaving policymakers with less room to absorb another surge in food and energy prices.

Four Agencies Now at 6.9-7%

With the Fitch and ADB upgrades, four major institutions now forecast India growth between 6.9% and 7% for FY27. S&P Global Ratings raised its forecast to 7% from 6.6% earlier on Wednesday. Moody's Ratings had raised its forecast to 7% from 6% on September 18.

The World Economic Forum has identified India as having the strongest growth outlook among economies covered in its latest survey. Jefferies sees real GDP growth of 6.5-7% in the current fiscal year and expects corporate earnings growth to accelerate to 17% next fiscal from 14%.
