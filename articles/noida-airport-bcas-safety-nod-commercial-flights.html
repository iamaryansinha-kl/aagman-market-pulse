Noida International Airport Gets BCAS Safety Nod, Commercial Flights Near

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Noida International Airport secured BCAS approval for its Aerodrome Safety Plan on April 28, 2026, clearing a key regulatory hurdle toward flight operations
  • 02/The ₹29,560 crore greenfield project at Jewar spans 5,000+ hectares with Phase 1 capacity of 12 million passengers per annum
  • 03/Airlines and airport operators will now finalise a mutually aligned date for commencement of commercial operations

Noida International Airport received approval for its Aerodrome Safety Plan from the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS) on April 28, 2026. This approval marks a critical milestone toward operational readiness.

The airport is working closely with airlines and partners to finalise a date for commencement of commercial flight operations. A formal launch date announcement is expected shortly.

The Aerodrome Safety Plan defines standard operating procedures for aircraft movement, emergency response, and ground handling. It ensures coordination between aviation authorities, airlines, and airport operators.

The greenfield project at Jewar in Gautam Buddh Nagar district spans over 5,000 hectares. It is being developed under a public-private partnership model by Yamuna International Airport Private Limited.

Yamuna International Airport is a subsidiary of Zurich Airport International AG. The concession agreement covers a 40-year period for development and operations.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the airport on March 28, 2026. Phase 1 features a 3,900-meter runway and terminal capacity of 12 million passengers per annum.

The total estimated project cost across all four phases is ₹29,560 crore. Phase 1 alone carries an investment of approximately ₹10,000 crore.

Once fully operational, the airport will feature six runways and handle up to 70 million passengers annually. It is expected to ease congestion at Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE BCAS APPROVAL RECEIVED BY NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT?
NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT RECEIVED APPROVAL FOR ITS AERODROME SAFETY PLAN FROM THE BUREAU OF CIVIL AVIATION SECURITY (BCAS) ON APRIL 28, 2026. THIS CLEARANCE CONFIRMS THAT THE SECURITY FRAMEWORK AND PROCEDURES ARE IN PLACE IN LINE WITH REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS.
WHERE IS NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT LOCATED AND WHO IS DEVELOPING IT?
THE AIRPORT IS LOCATED AT JEWAR IN GAUTAM BUDDH NAGAR DISTRICT, UTTAR PRADESH, SPANNING OVER 5,000 HECTARES. IT IS BEING DEVELOPED BY YAMUNA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT PRIVATE LIMITED (YIAPL), A SUBSIDIARY OF ZURICH AIRPORT INTERNATIONAL AG, UNDER A 40-YEAR PPP CONCESSION.
WHAT IS THE CAPACITY AND COST OF THE NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT PROJECT?
PHASE 1 OF THE PROJECT HAS A TERMINAL CAPACITY OF 12 MILLION PASSENGERS PER ANNUM AND AN ESTIMATED INVESTMENT OF APPROXIMATELY ₹10,000 CRORE. AT FULL BUILD-OUT WITH SIX RUNWAYS, THE AIRPORT WILL HANDLE UP TO 70 MILLION PASSENGERS ANNUALLY WITH A TOTAL PROJECT COST OF ₹29,560 CRORE.
WHEN WAS THE NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT INAUGURATED AND WHEN WILL FLIGHTS START?
PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA MODI INAUGURATED THE AIRPORT ON MARCH 28, 2026. COMMERCIAL FLIGHT OPERATIONS WILL BEGIN AFTER FINALISING A DATE WITH AIRLINES AND PARTNERS FOLLOWING THE BCAS CLEARANCE.
WHICH AIRLINES ARE EXPECTED TO OPERATE FROM NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT?
INDIGO, AIR INDIA EXPRESS, AND AKASA AIR HAVE INDICATED READINESS TO START SERVICES FROM THE AIRPORT IN THE FIRST PHASE. INITIAL OPERATIONS WILL FOCUS ON DOMESTIC ROUTES TO CITIES INCLUDING MUMBAI, BENGALURU, AND HYDERABAD.
