SEBI Tightens Open Market Buyback Rules: 66 Days, Promoter Freeze

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI proposes completing open market buybacks within 66 working days, rejecting PMAC's six-month recommendation
  • 02/Promoter holdings to be frozen at ISIN level during buyback period; 40% utilization rule retained in first half
  • 03/Merchant banker appointment made optional; public comments invited till May 29, 2026

SEBI has proposed a fresh framework for reintroducing open market buybacks through stock exchanges. The consultation paper was released on May 8, 2026. Public comments are invited till May 29, 2026.

Buybacks must be completed within 66 working days from opening. This is shorter than PMAC's recommended six months. SEBI said longer durations make buybacks less relevant amid changing markets.

At least 40 per cent of the buyback size must be utilized in the first half. This existing requirement is being retained. PMAC had suggested raising it to 50 per cent.

Promoter shares and specified securities will be frozen at the ISIN level during buybacks. This prevents insiders from benefiting while the company repurchases shares. The freeze does not apply to tender offer buybacks.

SEBI proposed making merchant banker appointments optional. Responsibilities will shift to companies, stock exchanges, and secretarial auditors. This aims to reduce compliance costs for listed companies.

The separate trading window requirement will be removed. Buybacks will go through the normal trading mechanism instead. Companies will no longer need to display their identity as purchasers on screens.

Companies must electronically intimate shareholders within one working day of public announcement. An explicit provision will prevent buybacks that breach minimum public shareholding norms.

The proposals follow an April 2026 consultation paper and PMAC recommendations. Changes under the Finance Act, 2026 on gaps between buybacks also influenced the balanced approach.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE PROPOSED TIMELINE FOR OPEN MARKET BUYBACKS UNDER SEBI'S NEW FRAMEWORK?
SEBI HAS PROPOSED THAT OPEN MARKET BUYBACKS BE COMPLETED WITHIN 66 WORKING DAYS FROM THE DATE OF OPENING OF THE OFFER.
WHAT HAPPENS TO PROMOTER HOLDINGS DURING THE BUYBACK PERIOD?
SHARES AND SPECIFIED SECURITIES HELD BY PROMOTERS AND THEIR ASSOCIATES WILL BE FROZEN AT THE ISIN LEVEL DURING THE BUYBACK PERIOD. THIS DOES NOT APPLY TO TENDER OFFER BUYBACKS.
IS THE APPOINTMENT OF A MERCHANT BANKER STILL MANDATORY FOR BUYBACKS?
NO. SEBI HAS PROPOSED MAKING THE APPOINTMENT OF A MERCHANT BANKER OPTIONAL. RESPONSIBILITIES WILL BE REASSIGNED TO COMPANIES, STOCK EXCHANGES, AND SECRETARIAL AUDITORS.
WHAT IS THE UTILIZATION REQUIREMENT FOR BUYBACKS?
COMPANIES MUST UTILIZE AT LEAST 40 PER CENT OF THE BUYBACK SIZE WITHIN THE FIRST HALF OF THE OFFER PERIOD. THIS EXISTING REQUIREMENT IS BEING RETAINED.
WHEN IS THE DEADLINE FOR PUBLIC COMMENTS ON THESE PROPOSALS?
SEBI HAS INVITED PUBLIC COMMENTS ON THE PROPOSALS TILL MAY 29, 2026.
