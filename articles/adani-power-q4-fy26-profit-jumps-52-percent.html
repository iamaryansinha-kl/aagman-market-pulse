Adani Power Q4 FY26 Profit Jumps 52% to ₹4,017 Crore, Beats Estimates

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Power Ltd reports Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹4,017 crore, up 52% YoY, beating analyst estimates of ₹3,063 crore.
  • 02/Revenue remained flat at ₹14,223 crore while power sale volume rose to 27.2 BU; lower tax expense drove bottom-line growth.
  • 03/Company targets 23.7 GW capacity by 2032; debt rose to ₹53,556 crore; shares fell 2% to ₹219 on April 29.

Adani Power Ltd reported a 52% year-on-year jump in consolidated net profit to ₹4,017 crore for Q4 FY26. The result beat analyst estimates of ₹3,063 crore by a wide margin.

Revenue from operations stood at ₹14,223 crore, marginally lower by 0.09% compared to the same quarter last year. Subdued short-term demand and unseasonal rains weighed on topline growth.

The sharp profit rise was driven by lower tax expenses and improved realisations in contracted capacities. EBITDA declined 1.7% to ₹4,732 crore, missing estimates of ₹5,074 crore.

Power sale volume increased to 27.2 billion units from 26.4 billion units in Q4 FY25. Demand improved in March after weaker offtake during unseasonally cold weather.

On a full-year basis, FY26 revenue fell 3.5% to ₹54,240 crore. Net profit edged down 0.8% to ₹12,834 crore as muted merchant demand persisted through the year.

"As the world goes through another energy price shock, the security and sovereignty of India's energy supply assume critical importance," said SB Khyalia, Chief Executive Officer of Adani Power Ltd.

The company is advancing a 23.7 GW capacity expansion plan. Mahan Phase II has reached 86% completion. Total debt rose to ₹53,556 crore as of March 31, 2026.

Adani Power shares fell nearly 2% to ₹219 on the BSE on April 29. The stock has faced pressure amid concerns over rising debt levels and subdued power demand.
