India IIP Growth May 2026: Output Up 5.1% on Manufacturing, Electricity

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Index of Industrial Production grew 5.1% YoY in May 2026, accelerating from 4.9% in April as manufacturing and electricity drove expansion
  • 02/Manufacturing output rose 5.5% and electricity surged 9.9%, while mining contracted 1.6%; capital goods output jumped 12.9%
  • 03/MoSPI will release June IIP data on July 28; the new series uses Output PPI as deflator with base year 2022-23

India's industrial production grew 5.1% year-on-year in May 2026. The Index of Industrial Production quickened from 4.9% in April, according to MoSPI data released on June 29.

The Quick Estimate of IIP stood at 122.7 in May 2026. This compares with 116.7 in the same month last year.

Manufacturing output expanded 5.5%, powering the overall growth. Electricity and gas supply surged 9.9%. Water supply and waste management also rose 5.5%.

Mining and quarrying contracted 1.6%, dragging the overall index. Within manufacturing, 16 of 23 industry groups posted positive growth in May.

Capital goods output registered the fastest expansion at 12.9%. Consumer durables rose 7.2%, while infrastructure goods and intermediate goods gained 5.9% and 5.8%.

Electrical equipment manufacturing jumped 20.8%. Motor vehicles and trailers grew 14.5%. Basic metals expanded 4.6%. These three were the top contributors.

MoSPI has adopted Output Producer Price Index as the deflator for the new IIP series. The base year is now 2022-23, replacing WPI.

The change affects 234 of 463 item groups. This accounts for 36.02% of the index weight. June data will be released on July 28.
