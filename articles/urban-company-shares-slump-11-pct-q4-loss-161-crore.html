Urban Company Shares Slump 11% as Q4 Loss Balloons to ₹161 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Urban Company Ltd (NSE: URBANCO) shares slump 11% to ₹125.97 as Q4 FY26 net loss balloons to ₹161 crore from ₹2.84 crore a year ago
  • 02/Operating revenue rose 42.6% YoY to ₹425.6 crore, but InstaHelp adjusted EBITDA loss widened sharply to ₹119 crore
  • 03/Goldman Sachs neutral at ₹140, Morgan Stanley underweight at ₹128; company targets adjusted EBITDA breakeven by Q3 FY28

Urban Company Ltd (NSE: URBANCO) shares tumbled 11 per cent on Monday, May 11, 2026. The stock hit a low of ₹124.40 and traded at ₹125.97 by noon on the NSE, down from Friday's close of ₹139.67.

The sell-off followed Q4 FY26 results announced on May 8. The company posted a consolidated net loss of ₹161 crore, compared with ₹2.84 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations grew 42.6% year-on-year to ₹425.6 crore. The India consumer services segment contributed ₹288.5 crore, accounting for 68% of operating revenue.

The widening loss was driven entirely by InstaHelp, the quick-service housekeeping vertical. InstaHelp posted an adjusted EBITDA loss of ₹119 crore on revenue of ₹8.94 crore and 2.7 million orders.

"We are happy to have found InstaHelp. A business that did not exist until a year ago, fulfilled 2.7 million orders last quarter," said Abhiraj Singh Bhal, Founder and CEO, Urban Company.

Brokerages remained divided on the stock. Goldman Sachs maintained a neutral rating with a target price of ₹140. Morgan Stanley kept an underweight rating and raised its target to ₹128 from ₹120.

Motilal Oswal initiated coverage with a neutral rating and a ₹125 target. JM Financial maintained a reduce rating with a March 2027 target price of ₹130.

The company ended FY26 with ₹2,021 crore in cash. It reiterated its target of achieving consolidated adjusted EBITDA breakeven by Q3 FY28.
