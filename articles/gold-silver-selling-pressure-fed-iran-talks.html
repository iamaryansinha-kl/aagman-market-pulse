Gold, Silver Face Selling Pressure as Fed Meeting, US-Iran Talks Loom

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold and silver may face selling pressure this week as traders track US-Iran peace talks and the Federal Reserve's April 29 policy decision
  • 02/MCX gold futures dropped ₹1,910 (1.23%) to ₹1.54 lakh per 10 grams, while silver plunged ₹12,506 (4.9%) to ₹2.44 lakh per kilogram last week
  • 03/Domestic commodity markets will remain closed on Friday, May 1, for Maharashtra Day, shortening the trading week

Gold and silver prices may face further selling pressure this week. Traders are tracking US-Iran peace talks, crude oil rates, and the Federal Reserve's policy decision.

On the Multi Commodity Exchange, gold futures dropped ₹1,910, or 1.23 per cent, to close at ₹1.54 lakh per 10 grams. Silver plunged ₹12,506, or 4.9 per cent, to settle at ₹2.44 lakh per kilogram.

In international markets, Comex gold fell $138.7, or 2.8 per cent, to $4,740.9 per ounce. Silver declined $5.4, or 6.6 per cent, to $76.41 per ounce.

"Focus in the coming week will remain on the progress in peace talks between the US and Iran," said Pranav Mer, Vice President at JM Financial Services Ltd.

The April 29 FOMC meeting will be the last chaired by Jerome Powell. The policy statement and press conference will be significant for precious metals prices.

Gold's downside was limited by a weaker rupee, which declined around 1.4 per cent last week. A softer rupee makes dollar-denominated gold more expensive domestically.

Gold failed to breach $5,000 per ounce in the international market. Profit-booking set in after gains of 10-12 per cent in the previous four weeks. The US-Iran blockade of the Strait of Hormuz has also pushed crude oil prices above $100 per barrel.

Analysts expect gold to find support at lower levels but remain vulnerable. Silver may stay more volatile due to its dual role as a precious and industrial metal.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT ARE THE CURRENT GOLD AND SILVER PRICES ON THE MCX?
ON THE MULTI COMMODITY EXCHANGE, GOLD FUTURES CLOSED THE WEEK AT ₹1.54 LAKH PER 10 GRAMS AFTER DROPPING ₹1,910 OR 1.23 PER CENT. SILVER FUTURES SETTLED AT ₹2.44 LAKH PER KILOGRAM AFTER PLUNGING ₹12,506 OR 4.9 PER CENT.
WHAT ARE THE KEY EVENTS AFFECTING GOLD AND SILVER THIS WEEK?
THE APRIL 29 FEDERAL OPEN MARKET COMMITTEE MEETING, US-IRAN PEACE TALKS, AND MONETARY POLICY DECISIONS FROM THE BANK OF JAPAN, BANK OF ENGLAND, AND EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK ARE THE KEY EVENTS. US DATA ON HOUSING, PCE INFLATION, AND CONSUMER CONFIDENCE WILL ALSO GUIDE SENTIMENT.
WHY DID GOLD AND SILVER DECLINE LAST WEEK?
GOLD FAILED TO BREACH $5,000 PER OUNCE INTERNATIONALLY, TRIGGERING PROFIT-BOOKING AFTER 10-12 PER CENT GAINS IN THE PREVIOUS FOUR WEEKS. STRONGER US RETAIL SALES, JOBLESS CLAIMS, AND CONSUMER SENTIMENT DATA BOOSTED THE DOLLAR AND TREASURY YIELDS, WEIGHING ON PRECIOUS METALS.
HOW DID THE RUPEE IMPACT DOMESTIC GOLD PRICES?
THE RUPEE DECLINED AROUND 1.4 PER CENT DURING THE PAST WEEK. THIS WEAKER RUPEE LIMITED GOLD'S DOWNSIDE IN THE DOMESTIC MARKET BY MAKING DOLLAR-DENOMINATED GOLD MORE EXPENSIVE FOR INDIAN BUYERS.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR GOLD AND SILVER IN THE NEAR TERM?
ANALYSTS EXPECT GOLD TO FIND SUPPORT NEAR LOWER LEVELS BUT REMAIN VULNERABLE IF THE DOLLAR STAYS FIRM AND GEOPOLITICAL RISKS EASE. SILVER MAY REMAIN MORE VOLATILE DUE TO ITS DUAL ROLE AS BOTH A PRECIOUS AND AN INDUSTRIAL METAL. ESCALATING WEST ASIA TENSIONS OR DOVISH CENTRAL BANK SIGNALS COULD REVIVE BUYING INTEREST.
