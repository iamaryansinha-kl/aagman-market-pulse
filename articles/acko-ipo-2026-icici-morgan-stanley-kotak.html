ACKO Appoints ICICI, Morgan Stanley, Kotak for $2.5 Billion IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ACKO appoints ICICI Securities, Morgan Stanley and Kotak Securities as book-running lead managers for its IPO targeting $2-2.5 billion valuation
  • 02/FY25 revenue surged 35% to Rs 2,837 crore while net losses fell 37% to Rs 424 crore, outpacing the sub-10% industry growth
  • 03/DRHP filing with SEBI expected in coming months; IPO will include fresh issue plus offer-for-sale by existing investors

ACKO has appointed ICICI Securities, Morgan Stanley and Kotak Securities as book-running lead managers for its IPO. The Bengaluru-based digital insurer is targeting a valuation of $2-2.5 billion.

The IPO will comprise a fresh issue of shares alongside an offer-for-sale by existing investors. ACKO plans to file its draft red herring prospectus with SEBI in the coming months.

Founded by Varun Dua in 2016, ACKO received its insurance licence in late 2017 and began operations in 2018. The company operates a direct-to-consumer model, bypassing agents and intermediaries entirely.

In FY25, ACKO reported revenue of Rs 2,837 crore, up 35% from Rs 2,106 crore in FY24. This growth far outpaced the sub-10% expansion recorded by the broader insurance sector.

The company trimmed its net loss by 37% to Rs 424 crore in FY25 from Rs 673 crore in FY24. Gross premium income rose 31% to Rs 2,085 crore, accounting for 73.5% of total income.

Advertising spend fell 12% to Rs 497 crore while employee costs dropped 6% to Rs 334 crore. ACKO has raised over $583 million from investors including General Atlantic, Accel and Elevation Capital.

The company faces regulatory headwinds. IRDAI imposed a Rs 1 crore penalty on ACKO for payments to Ola Financial Services and has rejected requests for relaxation from expense management limits.

ACKO's IPO will test investor appetite for Indian insurtech at scale. Competitor Digit Insurance reported Rs 2,088 crore in operating revenue and Rs 116.5 crore profit after tax in Q2 FY26.
