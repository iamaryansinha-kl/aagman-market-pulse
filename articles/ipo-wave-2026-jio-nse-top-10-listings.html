Jio Rs 35,000 Cr IPO, NSE Rs 25,000 Cr Among 10 Issues in H2 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms files Rs 35,000 crore IPO with Sebi, the largest fresh issue in Indian history
  • 02/NSE revives decade-delayed IPO plan targeting Rs 25,000 crore; SBI MF eyes Rs 10,000 crore in July
  • 03/Zepto, Acko, Milky Mist and FabIndia among new-age firms lining up for H2 2026 listings

Jio Platforms has filed draft papers with Sebi for a Rs 35,000 crore IPO. The fresh issue of up to 27 crore shares would be the largest public offering in India's history.

The company will deploy Rs 27,500 crore toward debt repayment. The balance will fund network expansion, artificial intelligence infrastructure and digital services.

The National Stock Exchange has revived its IPO plan after a decade-long delay. It filed its DRHP with Sebi and aims to raise around Rs 25,000 crore through an offer for sale.

SBI Mutual Fund is targeting the first week of July for its listing. The asset manager expects to raise around Rs 10,000 crore, with parent SBI and partner Amundi partially diluting their stakes.

Quick commerce platform Zepto is widely expected to launch its IPO in the coming months. The company is rapidly expanding its dark store network and competes with Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Flipkart Minutes.

Tamil Nadu-based dairy firm Milky Mist has secured Sebi approval for a Rs 2,035 crore IPO. It plans to use proceeds for expansion, debt reduction and strengthening its premium dairy portfolio.

Digital insurer Acko and lifestyle retailer FabIndia are also reviving their public market plans. Acko is backed by General Atlantic and Amazon, while FabIndia is improving profitability before its listing.

The pipeline signals a strong rebound after a muted first half of 2026. The mix of large corporates and new-age firms could revive retail investor participation in primary markets.
