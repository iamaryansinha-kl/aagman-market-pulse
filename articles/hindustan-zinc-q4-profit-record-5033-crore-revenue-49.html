Hindustan Zinc Q4 Profit Hits Record ₹5,033 Crore, Revenue Surges 49%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindustan Zinc Q4 net profit jumps 68% YoY to record ₹5,033 crore for quarter ended March 31, 2026
  • 02/Full-year FY26 revenue hits all-time high of ₹40,844 crore, up 20%, with net profit at ₹13,832 crore
  • 03/FY27 production guidance set at 1,150 KT mined metal; stock closed at ₹588.50 on NSE, down 0.61%

Hindustan Zinc Ltd, the Vedanta Group company and world's largest integrated zinc producer, reported record quarterly results on April 24, 2026. Net profit for Q4 FY26 surged 68 per cent year-on-year to ₹5,033 crore.

Revenue for the quarter stood at ₹13,544 crore, a 49 per cent jump over the same period last year. The growth was driven by higher zinc and silver prices, record production volumes, and a stronger dollar.

Quarterly EBITDA came in at ₹7,747 crore, up 61 per cent YoY. The EBITDA margin stood at an industry-leading 57 per cent, reflecting strong operational efficiency.

For the full year FY26, the company posted its highest-ever annual revenue of ₹40,844 crore, up 20 per cent. Net profit rose 34 per cent to ₹13,832 crore, also an all-time high.

Annual EBITDA was ₹22,162 crore, up 27 per cent, with a full-year margin of 54 per cent. Earnings per share for FY26 stood at ₹32.74. Free cash flow before growth capex was ₹13,337 crore.

On the operational front, mined metal production hit a quarterly record of 315 kilotonnes. Refined metal output also touched a record 282 kilotonnes. Zinc cost of production fell 9 per cent YoY to $903 per tonne, the lowest since the company's underground transition.

Silver production rose 11 per cent sequentially to 176 tonnes in the quarter. Silver contributed approximately 45 per cent to overall profitability in FY26, aided by silver prices surging 165 per cent YoY to an average of $84.3 per troy ounce.

The company guided FY27 mined metal production at 1,150 (±10) kilotonnes and refined metal at 1,100 (±10) kilotonnes. Zinc cost of production is projected at $975–$1,000 per tonne. Growth capital expenditure is budgeted at $500–600 million.

As of March 31, 2026, cash and cash equivalents stood at ₹13,846 crore, against total borrowings of ₹8,252 crore. The company holds a CRISIL AAA rating. The stock closed at ₹588.50 on the NSE, down 0.61 per cent, with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹2.49 lakh crore.
