City Union Bank Q4FY26: ICICI Securities Retains Buy, Target ₹325

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICICI Securities retains BUY on City Union Bank with target price of ₹325, CMP at ₹275.70
  • 02/Q4FY26 loan growth hit 26% YoY, highest in 13 years, driven by gold loans and MSME focus
  • 03/Bank guides for stable NIM and RoA improvement to 1.65-1.7% by Q4FY27; SMA 2 at 0.7%

ICICI Securities has retained its BUY rating on City Union Bank. The brokerage maintains a target price of ₹325 against the current market price of ₹275.70.

City Union Bank reported strong Q4FY26 results. Loan growth jumped to 26 per cent year on year, marking the highest level in 13 years.

Gold loans led the acceleration in credit growth. The bank's portfolio mix stands at MSME 55-60 per cent and gold loans at 29 per cent.

The management guided for mid-teens growth ahead of system credit. It aims to limit gold loan exposure to about 30 per cent over time.

The bank expects stable net interest margins. RoA is guided to improve 10-15 basis points to 1.65-1.7 per cent by Q4FY27 from 1.56 per cent.

Asset quality improved to multi-year lows. SMA 2 stands at 0.7 per cent and SMA 0+1+2 are at their lowest levels in several years.

During the outgoing CEO's tenure from FY11 to FY26, deposits and loans grew 6-7 times each. Net worth and market cap surged 10-11 times.

ICICI Securities values the stock at about 1.8 times FY28E adjusted book value. Key risks include execution challenges and volatility in gold prices.
