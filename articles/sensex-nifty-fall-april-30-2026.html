Sensex Falls 583 Pts to 76,913; Nifty Down 180 to 23,998 on Apr 30

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex fell 582.86 points (0.75%) to close at 76,913.50, while NSE Nifty 50 declined 180.10 points (0.74%) to 23,997.55 on April 30, 2026.
  • 02/Market breadth turned sharply negative with 1,976 stocks declining versus 1,295 advancing on the NSE; only the Nifty IT index managed gains at 0.37%.
  • 03/The rupee breached 95 per US dollar mark while gold prices surged past ₹1.5 lakh per 10 grams amid a hawkish Federal Reserve pause and geopolitical worries.

Indian benchmark indices ended sharply lower on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The BSE Sensex fell 582.86 points or 0.75% to settle at 76,913.50, while the NSE Nifty 50 declined 180.10 points or 0.74% to close at 23,997.55.

Of the 3,369 stocks traded on the NSE, only 1,295 advanced while 1,976 declined and 98 remained unchanged. A total of 82 stocks touched their 52-week highs, while 30 hit 52-week lows.

Except for the Nifty IT index, which gained 0.37%, all other sectoral indices ended in the red. The selling pressure was broad-based across most sectors.

The indices opened with a sharp gap-down, with Sensex falling 788.56 points to 76,707.80 at the open. By mid-day, losses deepened further to over 1,033 points on the Sensex before a partial recovery in the afternoon session.

The weakness followed the US Federal Reserve's hawkish pause, where it kept interest rates unchanged but shifted its language to describe inflation as "elevated" rather than "modestly high." The Fed offered no signal on the timing of future rate cuts.

The rupee breached the 95 per US dollar mark for the first time since March 30, 2026. Gold prices surged over 1% to ₹1,50,000 per 10 grams. Crude oil futures rose ₹70 to ₹10,196 amid reports of US military options regarding Iran.

Top Nifty gainers included Bajaj Finance (+3.77%), Coal India (+1.65%), and ONGC (+0.85%). Top losers included Eternal (-2.61%), Shriram Finance (-2.40%), and Axis Bank (-2.34%).
