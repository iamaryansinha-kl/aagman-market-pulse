Crude Shock, Hormuz Tensions Drag Sensex Down 274 Pts; ONGC Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex fell 273.89 pts (0.35%) to 76,995.51 and Nifty dropped 101.40 pts (0.42%) to 24,017.90 at 9:16 am on May 5, driven by surging crude and Hormuz fears
  • 02/Brent crude spiked to $115 intraday before settling at $113.24; Strait of Hormuz handles roughly 20% of global oil supply and is at centre of geopolitical storm
  • 03/Key support at 23,900–24,000 for Nifty and 77,000 for Sensex; resistance at 24,300–24,400; today is F&O expiry adding to intraday volatility

Indian equity benchmarks opened lower on May 5 as surging crude oil prices and Strait of Hormuz tensions dented sentiment. The Sensex fell 273.89 points to 76,995.51, down 0.35 per cent from its previous close of 77,269.40.

The Nifty 50 dropped 101.40 points to 24,017.90, down 0.42 per cent. It had closed at 24,119.30 on Monday and opened at 24,052.60 before sliding further.

Brent crude futures spiked as high as $115 intraday before settling at $113.24. WTI crude traded at $104.41. Reports of missile and drone strikes near the UAE and a US warship being targeted triggered the spike.

The Strait of Hormuz handles roughly 20 per cent of global oil supply and is at the centre of the storm. The rupee depreciated to a record low near 95.23, while the US 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.44 per cent.

On Wall Street overnight, the Dow Jones fell over 550 points. The S&P 500 slipped 0.4 per cent and the Nasdaq dropped 0.2 per cent. FIIs were net buyers of approximately ₹2,835 crore on Monday, while DIIs bought ₹4,764 crore.

"The resumption of hostilities in the Hormuz region and Brent crude again spiking to around $113 are headwinds for the market. Also the US 10-year bond yield rising to 4.44 per cent and the rupee sliding to 95.23 are unfavourable from the FPI flows perspective." - Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist, Geojit Investments Limited.

Among Nifty50 gainers, ONGC led at ₹294.30, up 0.48 per cent. Adani Ports rose 0.42 per cent to ₹1,749.90, while TCS, ITC and Reliance Industries gained up to 0.24 per cent.

Shriram Finance fell the most, down 1.59 per cent to ₹945.20. Bajaj Finance dropped 1.42 per cent, Maruti Suzuki shed 1.22 per cent, and HDFC Bank fell 1.08 per cent. The IT sector remained under pressure.

Gaurav Udani, Founder of Thincredblu Securities, said 23,900–24,000 remains immediate Nifty support. Shrikant Chouhan of Kotak Securities flagged 24,000/77,000 as pivotal. Today marks F&O expiry, with India VIX elevated at around 18.3.
