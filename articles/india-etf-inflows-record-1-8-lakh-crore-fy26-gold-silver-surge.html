India ETF Inflows Record ₹1.8 Lakh Crore in FY26 as Gold, Silver Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian ETFs recorded record net inflows of over ₹1.8 lakh crore in FY26, more than double the previous peak of ₹83,390 crore seen in FY22
  • 02/Commodity ETFs led the surge with gold and silver attracting ₹99,280 crore (55%), while equity ETFs received ₹77,780 crore (43%)
  • 03/Gold ETF AUM jumped from ₹59,000 crore to over ₹1.71 lakh crore in FY26; watch if the trend sustains as prices cool

Indian Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) recorded their highest-ever annual net inflows of over ₹1.8 lakh crore in FY26. The figure more than doubled the previous peak of ₹83,390 crore set in FY22, according to a study by Zerodha Fund House published on April 30, 2026.

Over the past five years, from FY21 to FY25, annual ETF inflows had stayed in a tight band of ₹46,000 crore to ₹83,000 crore. FY26 broke that range decisively.

Commodity ETFs were the primary driver. Gold and silver ETFs together pulled in ₹99,280 crore, accounting for 55% of total inflows. Equity ETFs received ₹77,780 crore, or 43% of the total.

Gold ETFs alone saw net inflows exceeding ₹68,000 crore. That exceeds the combined inflows of approximately ₹30,200 crore recorded across the previous five financial years.

Silver ETFs, which were introduced only in 2022, attracted over ₹30,000 crore in net inflows during FY26. This exceeds their total assets under management at the start of the fiscal year.

Assets under management for gold ETFs expanded sharply. AUM rose from about ₹59,000 crore in March 2025 to over ₹1.71 lakh crore in March 2026.

January 2026 was the strongest single month, with inflows exceeding ₹39,000 crore. The surge came amid global market uncertainty and a flight to safe-haven assets.

Gold prices gained 47% in FY26, closing near ₹1.5 lakh per 10 grams. Silver more than doubled to ₹2.3 lakh per kilogram.

The Zerodha Fund House report noted that rising bullion prices, combined with the tax efficiency of ETFs versus physical metals, likely drove the preference shift.

Average daily ETF turnover also climbed sharply. It rose from ₹237 crore in FY21 to over ₹4,200 crore during April 2025 to February 2026. This signals improved liquidity and broader participation.

The findings point to a shift in Indian investor behaviour. Portfolios are tilting toward diversified, commodity-linked instruments through the ETF route.

HOW DID EQUITY ETFS PERFORM COMPARED TO COMMODITY ETFS?
EQUITY ETFS RECEIVED ₹77,780 CRORE, ACCOUNTING FOR 43% OF TOTAL FY26 INFLOWS. FOR THE FIRST TIME, COMMODITY ETFS SURPASSED EQUITY ETFS IN ANNUAL INFLOWS IN INDIA.

GOLD ETF AUM ROSE FROM ABOUT ₹59,000 CRORE IN MARCH 2025 TO OVER ₹1.71 LAKH CRORE IN MARCH 2026. THIS NEAR-TRIPLING REFLECTS BOTH INFLOW DRIVEN GROWTH AND THE 47% RISE IN DOMESTIC GOLD PRICES DURING THE YEAR.
WHAT DOES THIS MEAN FOR INDIAN INVESTORS GOING FORWARD?
THE DATA SIGNALS A BROADER SHIFT TOWARD ETF-BASED DIVERSIFICATION INTO COMMODITIES, PARTICULARLY GOLD AND SILVER. HOWEVER, WITH GOLD UP 47% AND SILVER MORE THAN DOUBLING IN FY26, FUTURE INFLOWS MAY COOL IF BULLION PRICES STABILISE OR CORRECT.
