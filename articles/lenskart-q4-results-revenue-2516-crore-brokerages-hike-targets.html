Lenskart Q4: Revenue Surges 46% to ₹2,516 Cr, Brokers Hike Targets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Lenskart Q4FY26 revenue surges 46% YoY to ₹2,516 crore; FY26 revenue up 32% to ₹9,002 crore
  • 02/Adjusted PAT jumps 148% YoY to ₹530 crore for FY26; EBITDA climbs 55.3% to ₹1,789 crore
  • 03/Jefferies, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Emkay, and Motilal Oswal raise target prices to ₹576-₹675 range

Lenskart Solutions Ltd (NSE: LENSKART) reported Q4FY26 revenue of ₹2,516 crore. This marks a 46% year-on-year jump from ₹1,727 crore in Q4FY25.

Net profit for the quarter declined 9% YoY to ₹200 crore. The company had posted ₹219 crore in the year-ago period.

For the full financial year ended March 31, 2026, revenue stood at ₹9,002 crore. This represents a 32% increase over FY25.

FY26 EBITDA climbed 55.3% YoY to ₹1,789 crore. Adjusted PAT surged 148% to ₹530 crore for the full year.

The strong results triggered a 6% jump in Lenskart shares. The stock hit a near one-month high of ₹517 on NSE during Thursday trade. Market capitalisation rose above ₹5,235 crore to nearly ₹90,000 crore.

Jefferies maintained a Buy rating and raised its base-case target to ₹600. It sees upside potential of 23% from the previous close of ₹486.85. In an upside scenario, Jefferies expects the stock to rally to ₹675.

Goldman Sachs also maintained a Buy call with a target of ₹625. It noted Q4 revenue beat estimates by 5% and highlighted improving pre-Ind AS 116 EBITDA margins at 12.8%.

Morgan Stanley maintained an Overweight rating with a target of ₹576. It noted Lenskart reiterated a steady-state EBITDA margin target of 25% and expects similar store additions in FY27.

Emkay raised its target to ₹625 while maintaining a Buy. It highlighted that free eye tests drove ~25% volume growth in FY26 and the company funded all expansion via internal accruals. Net cash stood at approximately ₹40 billion.

Motilal Oswal raised its target to ₹650 while maintaining a Buy. It raised FY27 and FY28 revenue estimates by 5% and 6% respectively, and expects 25%/42%/44% CAGR in revenue/pre-Ind AS EBITDA/PAT over FY26-28E.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS LENSKART'S Q4FY26 REVENUE?
LENSKART REPORTED REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS OF ₹2,516 CRORE IN Q4FY26, REPRESENTING A 46% YEAR-ON-YEAR INCREASE FROM ₹1,727 CRORE IN Q4FY25.
WHAT WAS LENSKART'S FULL-YEAR FY26 REVENUE AND PROFIT?
FOR FY26 ENDED MARCH 31, 2026, LENSKART REPORTED REVENUE OF ₹9,002 CRORE (UP 32% YOY), EBITDA OF ₹1,789 CRORE (UP 55.3% YOY), AND ADJUSTED PAT OF ₹530 CRORE (UP 148% YOY).
WHY DID LENSKART'S Q4 NET PROFIT DECLINE DESPITE STRONG REVENUE?
Q4FY26 NET PROFIT DECLINED 9% YOY TO ₹200 CRORE FROM ₹219 CRORE IN Q4FY25, EVEN AS REVENUE SURGED 46%. THE COMPANY DID NOT DISCLOSE SPECIFIC REASONS IN THE SUMMARY REPORTED.
WHICH BROKERAGES RAISED LENSKART TARGET PRICES AND WHAT ARE THE NEW TARGETS?
JEFFERIES RAISED ITS BASE-CASE TARGET TO ₹600 (UPSIDE ₹675); GOLDMAN SACHS TO ₹625; EMKAY TO ₹625; MOTILAL OSWAL TO ₹650; AND MORGAN STANLEY TO ₹576. ALL MAINTAINED BUY OR OVERWEIGHT RATINGS.
WHAT IS LENSKART'S LONG-TERM MARGIN TARGET AND GROWTH OUTLOOK?
LENSKART HAS REITERATED A STEADY-STATE EBITDA MARGIN TARGET OF NEARLY 25%. JEFFERIES EXPECTS 45%+ EBITDA CAGR OVER FY26-29E. MOTILAL OSWAL MODELS 25% REVENUE CAGR AND 42-44% EBITDA/PAT CAGR OVER FY26-28E. THE COMPANY TARGETS ANNUAL VOLUME GROWTH OF 25% AND PLANS SIMILAR STORE ADDITIONS IN FY27.
