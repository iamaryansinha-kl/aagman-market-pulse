US Yield Surge Piles Fresh Pressure on India Bonds Bruised by Oil

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's benchmark 6.48% 2035 bond yield is expected to trade between 6.98% and 7.05% on Thursday after closing at 6.9928% on Wednesday.
  • 02/The 10-year US Treasury yield jumped to 4.43%, its highest in five weeks, after the Federal Reserve left rates unchanged in its most divided decision since 1992.
  • 03/Brent crude has climbed 70% since the war began on February 28, keeping inflation and fiscal worries alive for Indian policymakers.

Indian government bonds are set to extend their bearish run on Thursday. A spike in US Treasury yields has added fresh challenges for a market already battling a relentless oil price surge.

The benchmark Indian 6.48% 2035 bond yield is expected to drift between 6.98% and 7.05%. It closed at 6.9928% on Wednesday. Bond yields move inversely to prices.

US Treasuries witnessed a sharp selloff overnight. Yields rose further in Asian hours after the Federal Reserve left rates unchanged in its most divided decision since 1992. Three of 12 policymakers dissented.

The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to 4.43%, its highest level in five weeks. It stayed around those levels through Thursday's Asian session.

"Sentiment is clearly defensive as the move in Treasuries is another headwind for a market that was already struggling to digest the oil rally," a private-bank trader said. The oil surge is keeping inflation and fiscal worries alive.

The benchmark Brent crude contract has climbed 70% since the war began on February 28. It touched its highest level in nearly four years. Concerns persist that Middle East supply will remain bottled up.

US President Donald Trump spoke with oil companies about mitigating the impact of a possible months-long blockade of Iran's ports. Though a ceasefire has held since early April, Iran has kept shipping through the Strait of Hormuz largely shut. The conduit handles about 20% of global oil supplies.

India's overnight index swap rates are set to further encourage paid positions. The one-year OIS rate ended at 5.99%, the two-year at 6.24%, and the liquid five-year OIS at 6.6150%.
