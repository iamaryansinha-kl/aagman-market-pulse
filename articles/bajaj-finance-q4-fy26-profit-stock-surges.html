Bajaj Finance Q4 FY26: Profit Up 22% to ₹5,465 Crore, Stock Surges 5%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Finance Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 22% y-o-y to ₹5,465 crore, matching analyst estimates of ₹5,490 crore
  • 02/AUM grows 21% to ₹3.73 lakh crore; NII up 20% to ₹10,716 crore; gross NPA improves marginally to 0.27%
  • 03/Stock surged 5% to ₹975 in early trade on April 30 despite weak market; brokerages maintain buy ratings with targets up to ₹1,210

Bajaj Finance Ltd reported a 22% year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit to ₹5,465 crore for Q4 FY2026. Revenue grew 18% to ₹21,606 crore during the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

The board declared a dividend of ₹6 per share for FY2026. The record date is fixed at June 30, 2026.

Assets under management rose 21% to ₹3,72,986 crore as of March 31, 2026. Net interest income increased 20% to ₹10,716 crore from ₹8,911 crore in Q4 FY2025.

Asset quality improved with gross NPA at 0.27% and net NPA at 0.11%. Loan losses and provisions declined to ₹2,008 crore from ₹2,167 crore in the year-ago period.

The company recorded an additional expected credit loss provision of ₹142 crore towards management overlay. This reflects a cautious approach amid macro-economic uncertainty.

Bajaj Finance shares surged 5% to ₹975 in early trade on April 30, 2026. The stock had closed at ₹932.8 on April 29, gaining 1% ahead of the results.

Multiple global brokerages maintained bullish ratings after the earnings. CLSA set a target of ₹1,200, while Jefferies and Nomura pegged targets at ₹1,210 and ₹1,140 respectively.

However, Macquarie remained cautious with an underperform rating and ₹860 target. Motilal Oswal also stayed neutral at ₹1,000, citing limited near-term upside despite strong fundamentals.
