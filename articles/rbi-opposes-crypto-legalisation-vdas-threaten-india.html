RBI Opposes Crypto Legalisation, Tells Parliament Panel VDAs Threaten India

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI opposes legalisation of virtual digital assets including cryptocurrencies, tells Parliamentary panel they threaten emerging economies like India
  • 02/Cites risks of terror financing, narcotics smuggling; notes difficulty tracking offshore crypto entities
  • 03/China and Qatar have banned similar activities; RBI strengthens CBDC pilot as alternative

The Reserve Bank of India has opposed the legalisation of virtual digital assets (VDAs) including cryptocurrencies. It told a Parliamentary Standing Committee on Finance on Thursday that these assets pose a threat to emerging economies like India and should remain prohibited.

The RBI cited examples of China and Qatar. Both countries have banned similar financial activities. It noted that Europe has permitted VDAs only in a very stringent and regulated manner. The Parliamentary Committee is headed by BJP member Bhartruhari Mahtab.

The central bank submitted that digital money could be used to carry out illegal activities. These include terror funding and narcotics smuggling. It also noted that it is extremely difficult to keep a tab on offshore entities involved in crypto trade.

The Parliamentary Standing Committee held a meeting with RBI representatives on 'A Study on Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) and Way Forward'. The committee also discussed the same with representatives of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

ICAI informed the committee that it backs a comprehensive legal framework governing VDAs. It said it can help by providing a principle-based approach and guidance to improve clarity for stakeholders. ICAI can also undertake comprehensive research on the various forms of VDAs and analyse their economic characteristics.

Following the meeting, Mahtab told reporters that the RBI is against legalising VDAs in the country. He said the committee had been auditing part of VDAs as per the income tax law. The panel also examined the need for further policy interventions to support the growth of the country's digital financial ecosystem while protecting consumers and financial institutions.

The RBI's stance comes as it strengthens its pilot initiatives for sovereign digital currency, the digital rupee. The committee noted that the digital rupee's adoption has been slower compared with privately issued digital assets. RBI's CBDC pilots are ongoing in the wholesale and retail segments.

The committee will look at what India should do to find a way out. The RBI is pushing for a complete ban while the industry and some regulators advocate for regulated frameworks. The final decision will rest with the government and Parliament.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE RBI'S STANCE ON CRYPTOCURRENCY LEGALISATION?
THE RBI OPPOSES THE LEGALISATION OF VIRTUAL DIGITAL ASSETS INCLUDING CRYPTOCURRENCIES. IT TOLD A PARLIAMENTARY PANEL THAT THESE ASSETS THREATEN EMERGING ECONOMIES LIKE INDIA AND SHOULD REMAIN PROHIBITED.
WHAT RISKS DID THE RBI CITE?
THE RBI CITED RISKS OF TERROR FUNDING AND NARCOTICS SMUGGLING. IT ALSO NOTED THAT OFFSHORE ENTITIES INVOLVED IN CRYPTO TRADE POSE A SIGNIFICANT CHALLENGE FOR REGULATORY AUTHORITIES.
WHICH COUNTRIES HAVE BANNED SIMILAR ACTIVITIES?
CHINA AND QATAR HAVE BANNED SIMILAR FINANCIAL ACTIVITIES. EUROPE HAS PERMITTED VDAS ONLY IN A VERY STRINGENT AND REGULATED MANNER.
WHAT IS THE RBI'S ALTERNATIVE TO CRYPTOCURRENCY?
THE RBI IS STRENGTHENING ITS PILOT INITIATIVES FOR SOVEREIGN DIGITAL CURRENCY, THE DIGITAL RUPEE. CBDC PILOTS ARE ONGOING IN BOTH WHOLESALE AND RETAIL SEGMENTS.
WHAT DID THE PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEE DISCUSS WITH ICAI?
ICAI INFORMED THE COMMITTEE THAT IT BACKS A COMPREHENSIVE LEGAL FRAMEWORK FOR VDAS. IT CAN PROVIDE PRINCIPLE-BASED GUIDANCE AND UNDERTAKE RESEARCH ON VDAS' ECONOMIC CHARACTERISTICS FOR FINANCIAL REPORTING.
