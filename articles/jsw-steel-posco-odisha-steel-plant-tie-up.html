JSW Steel and POSCO Tie Up for ₹35,000 Crore Odisha Steel Plant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JSW Steel and POSCO have formed a partnership for a steel plant in Odisha
  • 02/The project investment is estimated at ₹35,000 crore
  • 03/The announcement coincides with South Korean President Lee Jae-myung's visit to India

JSW Steel has entered into a strategic partnership with POSCO. The South Korean steel giant will collaborate on a new steel plant in Odisha.

The project represents an estimated investment of ₹35,000 crore. It aligns with JSW Steel's capacity expansion plans.

The announcement coincides with high-level diplomatic engagements. South Korean President Lee Jae-myung is visiting India for bilateral talks.

POSCO is one of the world's largest steel producers. The company brings advanced technology and global expertise.

The Odisha plant will add significant steel-making capacity. It supports India's goal of expanding domestic steel production.

The partnership reflects strengthening India-South Korea economic ties. Both countries are looking to deepen trade and investment relations.

JSW Steel has been actively expanding its capacity. The company aims to increase output to meet growing domestic demand.
