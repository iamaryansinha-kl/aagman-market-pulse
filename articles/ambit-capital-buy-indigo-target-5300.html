Ambit Capital Reiterates Buy on IndiGo, Sets DCF Target at ₹5,300

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ambit Capital maintains Buy on Interglobe Aviation (IndiGo) with a one-year DCF-based target price of ₹5,300 against CMP of ₹4,345.90
  • 02/The brokerage expects 15% revenue and 28% EBITDA CAGR through FY26-29E despite 22/3% EPS cuts to FY27/28E
  • 03/Key catalysts expected in 2HFY27 include new CEO appointment, winter schedule clarity, and crude/FX normalisation

Ambit Capital has maintained a Buy rating on Interglobe Aviation Ltd (IndiGo) (NSE: INDIGO). The one-year DCF-based target price is ₹5,300 against a CMP of ₹4,345.90. The research note was published on April 29, 2026.

The brokerage expects 15% revenue and 28% EBITDA CAGR for IndiGo over FY26-29E. The valuation implies 6x one-year forward EV/EBITDA and 5x one-year forward EV/EBITDAR.

IndiGo holds ₹51,600 crore in cash on its balance sheet. This provides ample capital allocation flexibility for fleet expansion and international route additions.

International ASK increased from 23% in FY23 to 32% currently. The airline plans to shift its owned-to-leased aircraft mix from 20:80 to 40:60 over time.

Owning aircraft is less expensive than leasing over the long term. It reduces finance costs despite higher upfront capital requirements, a model adopted by global carriers.

Ambit Capital builds in crude at USD 82 per barrel and USD/INR at 93 for FY27-28E. Near-term headwinds persist due to geopolitical issues and rupee depreciation.

The brokerage cut FY27E EPS by 22% and FY28E EPS by 3%. Consensus estimates still face downside risk from fuel cost inflation, the report noted.

"Key investor pushbacks focus on three key aspects: Can IndiGo replicate domestic success globally? Is owning an aircraft better than leasing? And plausible capital allocation opportunities given ₹51,600 crore cash on B/S." - Ambit Capital research report by KS Badri Narayanan, April 29, 2026
