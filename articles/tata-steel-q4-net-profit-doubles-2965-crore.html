Tata Steel Q4 Net Profit More Than Doubles to ₹2,965 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Steel Q4 FY2026 consolidated net profit more than doubles to ₹2,965 crore from ₹1,201 crore a year ago
  • 02/Full-year profit triples to ₹10,886 crore; EBITDA up 35% to ₹34,848 crore with 320 bps margin expansion
  • 03/Company declares ₹4 per share dividend but flags West Asia cost pressures and European regulatory risks

Tata Steel Ltd reported consolidated net profit of ₹2,965 crore for Q4 FY2026. This is more than double the ₹1,201 crore posted in the same quarter last year.

Revenue for the March quarter rose to ₹63,270.13 crore from ₹56,218.11 crore in Q4 FY2025. EBITDA climbed to ₹9,953 crore from ₹6,762 crore.

For the full fiscal year, net profit more than tripled to ₹10,886 crore. Revenue reached ₹232,140 crore, up from ₹218,543 crore in FY2025.

Annual EBITDA surged 35% to ₹34,848 crore. The EBITDA margin expanded by 320 basis points year-on-year.

The performance was driven by higher volumes and improved product mix in India. Benefits of approximately ₹10,868 crore from the cost transformation programme also contributed.

"Tata Steel delivered a markedly improved performance for the second year in a row, despite subdued steel prices across key markets," CFO Koushik Chatterjee said.

European operations showed improvement too. Profitability in the Netherlands rose, while losses in the UK narrowed.

The company reduced net debt by ₹2,285 crore to ₹80,144 crore. The net debt-to-EBITDA ratio now stands at 2.3 times.

Tata Steel has announced a dividend of ₹4 per share. It spent ₹14,026 crore on capital expenditure during FY2026.

However, the company warned that West Asia tensions are pressuring supply chains and input costs. Regulatory challenges in Europe also remain a concern.

Tata Steel shares closed at ₹216.80 on the BSE on Friday, down 1.97% from the previous close.
