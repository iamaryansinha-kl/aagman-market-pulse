Puravankara Acquires 15 Acre Bengaluru Land for Rs 2,300 Cr Project

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Puravankara Ltd acquires 14.57-acre land in Bengaluru's Mandur, Budigere with potential GDV of Rs 2,300 crore
  • 02/7.92 acre via joint development agreement and 6.65 acre purchased directly by the company
  • 03/Total Bengaluru land bank now stands at 25.61 million sq ft; launch timeline not yet disclosed

Puravankara Ltd (NSE: PURVA) has acquired a 14.57-acre land parcel in Bengaluru. The company disclosed the deal in a regulatory filing on Monday, May 25, 2026.

The land is located in Mandur, Budigere, a strategic micro-market in the city. Out of the total, 7.92 acre is under a joint development agreement.

The remaining 6.65 acre has been purchased directly by Puravankara. The company did not disclose the land cost or revenue-sharing terms.

The total saleable area for the entire project will be about 1.8 million sq ft. The potential gross development value is around Rs 2,300 crore.

"This deal is part of our ongoing efforts to systematically add high-quality projects to our launch pipeline across strategic micro-markets. This reflects our disciplined approach to growth, a long-term view for the organisation, and confidence in the fundamentals of these markets." - Ashish Puravankara, Managing Director, Puravankara Ltd

With this acquisition, the company's total land bank in Bengaluru stands at 25.61 million sq ft of developable area. The city remains a core market for the developer.

Puravankara has completed 95 projects totalling 57 million sq ft across nine cities. These include Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune and Goa.

The company did not specify a launch timeline for the new project. Investors should watch for pre-launch activity in the Budigere micro-market.
