Global Gold Demand Hits Record $193 Billion in Q1 2026, Up 74%: WGC

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global gold demand rose 2% YoY to 1,231 tonnes in Q1 2026, with total value surging 74% to a record $193 billion
  • 02/Bar and coin investment jumped 42% to 474 tonnes as Asian investors led buying, while jewellery demand fell 23%
  • 03/Central banks added 244 tonnes (+3% YoY); geopolitical tensions and high prices to shape demand ahead

Global gold demand reached 1,231 tonnes in Q1 2026, up 2% from the year-ago quarter. The total value hit a record $193 billion, surging 74% year-on-year.

Bar and coin investment drove the bulk of growth, jumping 42% to 474 tonnes. This was the second-highest quarter on record for physical investment demand.

Chinese investors bought a record 207 tonnes of bars and coins, smashing the previous high of 155 tonnes set in Q2 2013. India's bar and coin purchases hit their highest first quarter since 2013, nearly matching jewellery demand.

Central banks purchased 244 tonnes on a net basis, up 3% year-on-year. Poland added 20 tonnes in February, taking total holdings to 570 tonnes, while Turkey offloaded roughly 10% of its reserves.

Jewellery demand fell 23% to 335 tonnes as record prices deterred buyers. However, spending on jewellery still rose 31%, signalling resilient consumer sentiment towards gold.

The LBMA gold price averaged $4,873 per ounce during the quarter, a record quarterly average. ETFs saw inflows of 62 tonnes, down sharply from 230 tonnes in Q1 2025 due to US fund outflows in March.

The World Gold Council expects geopolitical risks and inflation to support investment demand through 2026. Jewellery demand will likely remain under pressure due to persistent high prices, though spending levels may stay resilient.

Total gold supply rose 2% to 1,231 tonnes. Mine production edged up 2% to 885 tonnes, while recycled gold increased 5% to 366 tonnes.

THE VALUE OF QUARTERLY GOLD DEMAND SURGED 74% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO A RECORD $193 BILLION, DRIVEN BY BOTH HIGHER VOLUMES AND EXCEPTIONAL PRICE PERFORMANCE.
