Crisil Stress Test: West Asia Conflict To Dent India Inc Profits 200 Bps

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Crisil stress test shows prolonged West Asia conflict could reduce India Inc operating margins by nearly 200 basis points in FY2027
  • 02/Crude oil surged past $100/barrel; Strait of Hormuz disruption impacts 90% of India's LPG and 70% of LNG imports
  • 03/Strong balance sheets with median gearing of 0.5x and interest coverage of 5x may cushion the blow for most sectors

Crisil Ratings has warned that a prolonged West Asia conflict could dent India Inc profitability. The stress test flags a potential 200 basis points hit to operating margins in fiscal 2027.

The Strait of Hormuz shutdown has sent crude oil prices surging past $100 per barrel. India relies on the region for 90% of its LPG, 70% of LNG, and 50% of its crude oil needs.

The ratings agency assessed 30 sectors accounting for 65% of its rated debt portfolio. Median revenue growth is projected at 8% for FY2027, with EBITDA margin holding at 12%.

However, prolonged disruptions could compress operating margins by 150 basis points. Elevated raw material and logistics costs are the primary drivers of this pressure.

The ceramics sector faces the worst impact. Production halts could slash revenue by one-third and profitability by half due to heavy natural gas dependence.

Airlines face a triple whammy from travel restrictions, fuel cost spikes, and rupee depreciation. Crude-linked sectors like specialty chemicals and polyester textiles also face margin pressure.

Auto component makers could see margins dip up to 100 basis points. Limited pass-through ability in the aftermarket segment and freight cost escalations are key concerns.

India Inc enters this crisis with strong balance sheets. Median gearing stands at 0.5 times, while interest coverage is a healthy 5 times for fiscal 2026.

CRISIL ASSESSED 30 SECTORS ACCOUNTING FOR 65% OF ITS RATED DEBT. CERAMICS, AIRLINES, SPECIALTY CHEMICALS, POLYESTER TEXTILES, AND AUTO COMPONENTS FACE THE HIGHEST RISK.
WHY IS THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ DISRUPTION SIGNIFICANT FOR INDIA?
INDIA DEPENDS ON THE REGION FOR 90% OF LPG, 70% OF LNG, AND 50% OF CRUDE OIL. THE STRAIT HANDLES 20% OF GLOBAL LNG AND ONE-THIRD OF GLOBAL FERTILISER TRADE.
WHAT ARE THE KEY ASSUMPTIONS IN CRISIL'S STRESS TEST?
CRISIL ASSUMED A 4-5 MONTH CONFLICT DURATION, CRUDE AT $100 PER BARREL IN Q1 FY2027, AVERAGING $85-90 FOR THE FULL YEAR, AND EXCHANGE RATE AT RS 92-94 PER US DOLLAR.
WILL STRONG BALANCE SHEETS HELP COMPANIES WEATHER THE CRISIS?
YES. INDIA INC'S MEDIAN GEARING IS 0.5 TIMES AND INTEREST COVERAGE IS 5 TIMES. STRONG BALANCE SHEETS PROVIDE CUSHION TO ABSORB SUPPLY CHAIN SHOCKS WITHOUT WEAKENING CREDIT PROFILES.
