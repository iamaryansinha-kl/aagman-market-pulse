Kent RO IPO Delayed: Mideast War Dents Market, Chairman Says No Rush

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kent RO Systems shelves IPO plans citing Iran conflict and volatile global markets, chairman rules out listing for at least one year
  • 02/Revenue rose to ₹1,400 crore in FY2026 from ₹1,260 crore prior year; company expects 15% growth in FY2027
  • 03/PhonePe and Reliance Jio also paused IPOs; Kent imports 15% raw materials and raised prices 2% since conflict began

Kent RO Systems has shelved its IPO plans. Chairman Mahesh Gupta cited the Iran conflict and global market volatility as key reasons.

"We are not in a hurry," Gupta told Reuters on Monday. He ruled out a public listing for at least a year.

The company secured regulatory clearance for an IPO last June. The planned listing was an offer for sale by existing shareholders.

Kent's revenue rose to roughly ₹1,400 crore in FY2026. This compares to about ₹1,260 crore in the previous fiscal year.

Gupta expects revenue growth of around 15% in FY2027. Low water purifier adoption in India drives this outlook.

The company imports about 15% of its raw materials. The Middle East conflict has pushed up transportation, metals, and plastics costs.

Kent has raised product prices by about 2% since the conflict began. Further hikes depend on input cost trends.

Profits could come under pressure from rising global costs. India has low water purifier penetration despite 70% groundwater contamination.

PhonePe and Reliance Jio Platforms have also paused IPO plans. The Iran conflict has slowed listings from Hong Kong to London.
