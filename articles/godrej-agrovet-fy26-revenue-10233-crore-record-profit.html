Godrej Agrovet FY26 Revenue Crosses ₹10,233 Crore, Record Profit at ₹440 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godrej Agrovet (NSE: GODREJAGRO) reports record FY26 revenue of ₹10,233 crore and underlying PAT of ₹440 crore, up 13.9% year-on-year
  • 02/Oil Palm segment profit surged 68% to ₹384 crore, while Animal Nutrition grew 19.3% to ₹347 crore; Crop Care and Dairy dragged
  • 03/Net working capital dropped from 39 days to 25 days, lifting ROCE to 20% from 16%; watch segmental recovery and palm oil prices

Godrej Agrovet Limited (NSE: GODREJAGRO) has crossed the ₹10,000 crore revenue milestone for the first time. The company posted consolidated revenue of ₹10,233 crore for FY26, a 9.1% increase over the previous year.

Profit after tax on an underlying basis rose 13.9% to ₹440 crore. As-reported PAT came in at ₹445 crore, up 10.4% year-on-year.

Profit before tax excluding non-recurring items grew 17.2% to ₹569 crore. EBITDA on the same basis rose 10.8% to ₹936 crore, with margins holding steady at 9.1%.

The Oil Palm business was the standout performer. Segment profit jumped nearly 68% year-on-year to ₹384 crore, driven by double-digit volume growth and improved extraction ratios.

Palm kernel oil prices surged 46.7% to ₹2,14,754 per metric tonne. This tailwind significantly boosted the segment's profitability.

Animal Nutrition, the largest revenue contributor at 46% of total sales, delivered 12% volume growth. Cattle feed volumes jumped 18%, and segment profit rose 19.3% to ₹347 crore.

Not all segments fared well. Crop Care profit fell 27.2% to ₹224 crore amid adverse weather and regulatory disruptions. Creamline Dairy's EBITDA dropped 33% to ₹53 crore due to elevated milk procurement costs.

Subsidiary Astec LifeSciences turned around meaningfully. It achieved near-EBITDA break-even in FY26 after posting a ₹61 crore loss at the EBITDA level in FY25. Revenue grew 17.5% to ₹448 crore.

Working capital efficiency improved sharply. Net working capital fell from 39 days in FY25 to 25 days in FY26. Return on capital employed improved to 20% from 16% a year earlier.

In Q4 FY26, as-reported PAT surged 54.7% to ₹102 crore. This was partly aided by a ₹34 crore one-time gain on land sale. Investors should watch palm oil price trends and the Crop Care recovery.
