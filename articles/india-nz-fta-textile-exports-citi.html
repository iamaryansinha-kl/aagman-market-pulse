India-NZ FTA to Boost Textile Exports, Aid $350 Billion Sector Goal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CITI expects the India-New Zealand FTA to boost textile exports and support the sector's $350 billion target by 2030
  • 02/New Zealand imported NZ$80.22 million of Indian made-up textile articles in the year ended December 2025, making it the fourth-largest import category
  • 03/Segments such as sustainable textiles, home textiles and technical textiles offer strong growth potential in the New Zealand market

CITI said on April 27, 2026, that the India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement will boost textile exports. The industry body expects the deal to support the sector's $350 billion target by 2030.

The FTA will provide duty-free access to Indian textiles in the New Zealand market. This will help exporters reduce dependence on select markets and move up the value chain, CITI said.

Made-up textile articles were the fourth-largest category of Indian imports into New Zealand in the year ended December 2025. New Zealand's imports of these articles stood at NZ$80.22 million during the period.

"Amid the ongoing geopolitical tension in West Asia, the signing of the FTA between India and New Zealand comes as a breath of fresh air for India's textile and apparel exporters" - Ashwin Chandran, Chairman, CITI.

Chandran added that New Zealand is a high-income and quality-conscious market. This validation can support Indian exporters in scaling up the value chain and creating more jobs.

Segments such as sustainable textiles, home textiles and technical textiles offer strong growth potential in New Zealand. New Zealand's high-quality wool exports could help Indian firms manufacture high-end garments.

India's textile and apparel sector is the country's second-largest employer. It targets $100 billion in exports by 2030 as part of the broader $350 billion industry goal.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES THE INDIA-NEW ZEALAND FTA MEAN FOR TEXTILE EXPORTERS?
THE FTA WILL PROVIDE DUTY-FREE ACCESS TO INDIAN TEXTILES IN THE NEW ZEALAND MARKET. IT WILL HELP EXPORTERS REDUCE DEPENDENCE ON SELECT MARKETS AND MOVE UP THE VALUE CHAIN, ACCORDING TO CITI.
WHAT IS THE SIZE OF NEW ZEALAND'S IMPORTS OF INDIAN TEXTILE ARTICLES?
NEW ZEALAND'S IMPORTS OF INDIAN MADE-UP TEXTILE ARTICLES STOOD AT NZ$80.22 MILLION IN THE YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 2025. THIS MADE IT THE FOURTH-LARGEST CATEGORY OF INDIAN IMPORTS INTO NEW ZEALAND.
WHAT IS INDIA'S TEXTILE SECTOR TARGET BY 2030?
INDIA'S TEXTILE AND APPAREL SECTOR TARGETS $350 BILLION IN OVERALL SIZE BY 2030. IT ALSO AIMS FOR $100 BILLION IN EXPORTS BY THE SAME YEAR, ACCORDING TO CITI.
WHICH TEXTILE SEGMENTS OFFER GROWTH POTENTIAL IN NEW ZEALAND?
SEGMENTS SUCH AS SUSTAINABLE TEXTILES, HOME TEXTILES AND TECHNICAL TEXTILES OFFER STRONG GROWTH POTENTIAL IN THE NEW ZEALAND MARKET, CITI SAID. NEW ZEALAND'S HIGH-QUALITY WOOL EXPORTS COULD ALSO HELP INDIAN FIRMS MANUFACTURE HIGH-END GARMENTS.
WHO IS ASHWIN CHANDRAN AND WHAT DID HE SAY?
ASHWIN CHANDRAN IS CHAIRMAN OF THE CONFEDERATION OF INDIAN TEXTILE INDUSTRY (CITI). HE SAID THE FTA COMES AS A BREATH OF FRESH AIR FOR INDIA'S TEXTILE AND APPAREL EXPORTERS AMID ONGOING GEOPOLITICAL TENSION IN WEST ASIA.
