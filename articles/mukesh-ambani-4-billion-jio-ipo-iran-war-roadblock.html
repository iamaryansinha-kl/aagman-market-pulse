Mukesh Ambani's $4 Billion Jio IPO Hits Iran War Roadblock

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Reliance Industries Ltd (NSE: RELIANCE) has slowed Jio Platforms IPO preparations as Iran war triggers market volatility and capital flight from India
  • 02/The $4 billion offering would be India's largest ever, surpassing Hyundai Motor India's $3.3 billion record
  • 03/Ambani's H1 2026 timeline is at risk; company now pivots to fresh issue, scrapping existing investor selldowns

Reliance Industries Ltd (NSE: RELIANCE) has slowed preparations for Jio Platforms' IPO. The $4 billion offering faces delays due to the Iran war.

The Middle East conflict has worsened a downturn in Indian stocks. It has also accelerated capital flight and slowed decisions by key stakeholders.

At the heart of the issue is a valuation concern. The deepening slump in India's equity markets makes it harder to price the deal fairly.

The weakness risks valuing Jio below rival Bharti Airtel Ltd. That would be a blow to existing investors seeking returns on their stakes.

Jio's listing would be the first public offering by a major Reliance unit in nearly two decades. It would also mark India's largest-ever IPO.

The plan got a boost in March when the government approved changes to listing requirements. But Ambani's pledge to complete the deal in H1 2026 is now at risk.

The company has pivoted to offering entirely new shares. It scrapped earlier plans for selldowns by existing investors including Meta and Google.

Jio's investor roster includes Saudi PIF, Mubadala, ADIA, Silver Lake, KKR, Vista, and General Atlantic. Some Middle East investors face delays in board approvals.
