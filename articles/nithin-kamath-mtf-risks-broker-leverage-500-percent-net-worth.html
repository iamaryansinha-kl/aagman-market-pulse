Nithin Kamath Warns MTF Risks Surge as Broker Leverage Hits 500% of Net Worth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Zerodha co-founder Nithin Kamath warned that surging MTF books pose systemic risk despite flat Indian equity markets
  • 02/Nearly 50% of industry MTF exposure lies in non-F&O stocks, making brokers vulnerable to illiquidity during sharp corrections
  • 03/Some brokers carry MTF exposure at 500% of net worth, the regulatory maximum; Zerodha maintains around 25%

Nithin Kamath has raised a red flag on rapidly expanding margin trading facility (MTF) books across Indian brokerages. The Zerodha co-founder warned that aggressive leveraged bets could trigger systemic risks if markets correct sharply.

MTF allows investors to buy stocks by borrowing funds from brokers against pledged shares or margin collateral. The product has grown rapidly over the past two years as retail participation surged.

Kamath noted that MTF books are expanding aggressively even though Indian equity markets have moved largely sideways in recent months. This makes the situation more dangerous than markets like South Korea, where heavy borrowing accompanied a 150% rally.

Nearly 50% of the industry's MTF exposure currently sits in non-futures-and-options stocks. These counters are significantly less liquid than large-cap F&O names, amplifying exit risk during downturns.

The biggest danger, Kamath said, arises when brokers cannot liquidate pledged stocks quickly enough during sudden declines. "If a stock moves more than the margin provided, say 20%, the bad debit is on the broker," he explained.

A customer may pledge Stock A, receive 80% margin, and use it to build leveraged positions worth 400% in the same stock. If that stock is a mid or small-cap, circuit filters can prevent any exit when markets turn.

Kamath revealed that some brokers carry MTF exposure as high as 500% of net worth, the current regulatory ceiling. Zerodha's own MTF book stands at around 25% of its net worth, though it has risen significantly over the past 16 months.

Zerodha currently does not allow collateral margin for MTF purchases. Kamath admitted competitive pressure may eventually force a policy change despite the risks.

THE ZERODHA CO-FOUNDER WARNED THAT RAPIDLY RISING MARGIN TRADING FACILITY (MTF) EXPOSURE ACROSS BROKERAGES POSES A MAJOR SYSTEMIC RISK FOR INDIA'S STOCK MARKET IF MARKETS WITNESS A SHARP CORRECTION.
HOW MUCH OF INDUSTRY MTF EXPOSURE IS IN NON-F&O STOCKS?
NEARLY 50% OF THE INDUSTRY'S MTF EXPOSURE CURRENTLY LIES IN NON-FUTURES-AND-OPTIONS STOCKS, A SEGMENT GENERALLY CONSIDERED LESS LIQUID COMPARED WITH LARGE-CAP F&O COUNTERS.
WHAT IS THE MAXIMUM MTF EXPOSURE ALLOWED FOR BROKERS?
REGULATORS CURRENTLY ALLOW BROKERS TO CARRY MTF EXPOSURE UP TO 500% OF THEIR NET WORTH, WHICH IS THE MAXIMUM LIMIT. SOME BROKERS ARE OPERATING AT THIS CEILING.
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A PLEDGED STOCK FALLS BEYOND THE MARGIN PROVIDED?
IF A STOCK PRICE FALLS MORE THAN THE MARGIN PROVIDED BY CUSTOMERS, SAY 20%, THE BROKER BEARS THE BAD DEBT. RECOVERY FROM CUSTOMERS IN SUCH SITUATIONS IS OFTEN DIFFICULT.
WHAT IS ZERODHA'S CURRENT MTF POLICY?
ZERODHA CURRENTLY DOES NOT ALLOW CUSTOMERS TO USE COLLATERAL MARGIN FOR MTF PURCHASES, THOUGH COMPETITIVE PRESSURE IN THE INDUSTRY MAY EVENTUALLY FORCE CHANGES. ITS OWN MTF BOOK IS AROUND 25% OF NET WORTH.
