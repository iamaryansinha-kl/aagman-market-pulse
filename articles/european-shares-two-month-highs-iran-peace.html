European Shares Climb to Two-Month Highs on Iran Peace Hopes

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The pan-European STOXX 600 rose 0.67% to 629.28 points at 0818 GMT on May 25, reaching its highest level in over two months
  • 02/Brent crude slid 4.7% to $98 per barrel as Iran and the U.S. moved toward negotiating an end to their conflict
  • 03/Euro zone inflation data is due later this week; markets expect two ECB rate increases of 25 basis points each by year-end

European shares opened at their highest levels in more than two months on Monday, May 25, 2026. The pan-European STOXX 600 index rose 0.67% to 629.28 points at 0818 GMT.

The index stood just shy of the record high touched in late February, before the Middle East war erupted on February 28. Most sectors gained ground in early trading.

Banking stocks led the rally with a sharp 1.9% jump. Airlines also advanced strongly, with Lufthansa up 3.7% and Air France KLM surging 7.5%.

The gains were driven by signs that Iran and the U.S. were negotiating an end to their conflict. U.S. President Donald Trump said on Saturday that a framework to reopen the Strait of Hormuz had been "largely negotiated."

About one-fifth of the world's crude oil and natural gas typically passes through the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude slid 4.7% to $98 a barrel on easing supply fears.

The STOXX 600 has lagged global peers since the war began, as higher crude prices threatened to stoke inflation in energy-dependent Europe. Monday's rally marked a tentative reversal of that underperformance.

"Contentious issues like Iran's nuclear plans, its uranium enrichment and the control of the Strait of Hormuz remain potential obstacles," said Kyle Rodda, senior financial market analyst at Capital.com.

Trading volumes are expected to be light, with U.S. and UK markets closed for public holidays. Investors will also watch euro zone inflation data due later this week for clues on price pressures in May.
