Agrizy Launches Phytochemistry Lab for $650B Nutraceutical Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Agrizy launches phytochemistry R&D lab targeting the $650 billion global nutraceutical market
  • 02/Facility uses USP-standard validation and sources from farmers across four Indian states
  • 03/Company develops advanced delivery systems including phytosomes and nanoformulations

Agrizy, a contract research, development and manufacturing organisation (CRDMO), has launched a phytochemistry R&D laboratory. The facility was announced on April 29, 2026.

The lab targets the $650 billion global nutraceutical market. It focuses on producing scientifically validated, regulatory-compliant botanical ingredients.

India's herbal farmers have historically supplied raw materials globally. Value realisation remained limited due to fragmented procurement and inconsistent quality standards.

The facility extracts, isolates and purifies phyto-ingredients from medicinal plants. Each ingredient undergoes multi-stage fingerprinting per United States Pharmacopoeia standards.

"This investment enhances our capability to develop scientifically validated herbal ingredients that comply with global standards and deliver consistent performance," said S Vijaya Kumar, CEO, Wellness, Agrizy.

The lab develops phytosomes, nanoformulations and water-soluble botanical formats. These improve absorption and stability across capsules, tablets and functional beverages.

Agrizy sources from farmers, FPOs and VLAs across Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal. Key crops include turmeric, coleus, bacopa and ashwagandha.

The sourcing model sets phytochemical and marker-based specifications at the farm level. It integrates Ayurvedic pharmacognosy with modern analytical validation.
