KFin Tech Shares Fall 4% as Q4 Net Profit Drops 4.5% to ₹81 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KFin Technologies shares dropped around 4% on April 30 as Q4 FY26 net profit fell 4.5% YoY to ₹81.14 crore
  • 02/Revenue from operations grew 23% to ₹347.33 crore; FY26 full-year PAT rose to ₹343.71 crore from ₹332.62 crore
  • 03/Jefferies maintains buy with ₹1,200 target; Motilal Oswal flags Ascent platform scaling as key monitorable

KFin Technologies Ltd (NSE: KFINTECH) shares slipped around 4% in early trade on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The stock touched a low of ₹936.25 against the previous close of ₹977.30, and was trading at ₹952.75 around 10:10 am.

The decline followed the company's Q4 FY26 earnings, where consolidated net profit fell 4.5% year-on-year to ₹81.14 crore. This compares to ₹85.05 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue from operations, however, rose a strong 23% YoY to ₹347.33 crore. The growth was driven by core segments, even as profitability took a quarterly hit.

On a full-year basis, profit after tax improved to ₹343.71 crore in FY26 from ₹332.62 crore in FY25. The board also recommended a final dividend of ₹12 per share.

Global brokerage Jefferies maintained a buy rating with a target price of ₹1,200. It noted Q4 EBITDA came in 5% ahead of estimates, supported by the domestic mutual fund segment.

Jefferies added that mutual fund RTA yields stood at 3.25 basis points, ahead of estimates though marginally lower quarter-on-quarter. The Ascent platform saw 27% YoY revenue growth with 127 new fund wins in the quarter.

Domestic brokerage Motilal Oswal said structural tailwinds in the mutual fund industry will drive long-term growth. It flagged scaling the Ascent business and its profitability trajectory as key monitorables ahead.
