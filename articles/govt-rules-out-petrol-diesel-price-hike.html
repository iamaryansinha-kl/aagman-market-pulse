Govt Rules Out Petrol, Diesel Price Hike Amid Crude Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ministry of Petroleum says no proposal to raise petrol, diesel prices; rates frozen since April 6, 2022
  • 02/PSU OMCs bear under-recoveries of ₹20/litre on petrol and ₹100/litre on diesel as of last week
  • 03/Indian crude basket surged from $70/bbl FY26 average to over $113/bbl in April 2026

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas said on April 28, 2026, that there is no proposal to raise petrol and diesel prices. Retail prices have remained frozen since April 6, 2022.

Sujata Sharma, Joint Secretary in the MoPNG, stated, "There is no proposal to increase petrol and diesel prices." The statement comes amid rising global crude costs and supply concerns.

India's combined daily under-recovery stood at around ₹2,400 crore till March 27, 2026. PSU oil marketing companies are absorbing these losses to keep retail rates unchanged.

Around April 2, under-recoveries were ₹24.40 per litre on petrol and ₹104.99 per litre on diesel. Last week, the Ministry cited ₹20 and ₹100 per litre respectively.

The Indian crude basket rose from an FY26 average of $70 per barrel to over $113 in April 2026. Kotak Institutional Equities noted a $47 jump in March and $53 in early April.

Despite a 13-15 per cent decline in crude imports, India's import bill has increased by $190-210 million per day. The surge reflects higher global oil prices.

Sharma said all refineries are operating at high capacity with adequate crude inventories. She urged citizens to avoid panic buying triggered by rumours.

"Panic buying is observed at certain retail outlets due to rumours. Regular retail prices for petrol and diesel are unchanged," Sharma added.
