Sensex, Nifty Slide at Open on Crude Shock, FII Outflows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex drops 647.03 points (0.83%) to 76,849.33 and Nifty falls 212.55 points (0.88%) to 23,965.10 at the open on April 30, 2026.
  • 02/Brent crude briefly breached $120 per barrel on Strait of Hormuz tensions; FIIs sold ₹2,468 crore on April 29 while DIIs bought ₹2,262 crore.
  • 03/Markets are closed Friday for Maharashtra Day; traders face overnight positioning risk amid geopolitical uncertainty and state election outcomes.

Indian equity benchmarks opened sharply lower on Thursday morning. The Sensex dropped 647.03 points or 0.83 per cent to 76,849.33. The Nifty 50 fell 212.55 points or 0.88 per cent to 23,965.10 as of 9:16 AM.

The previous session had ended on a positive note. The Sensex closed Wednesday at 77,496.36 and the Nifty at 24,177.65. Those gains evaporated within minutes of the opening bell.

Brent crude briefly breached $120 per barrel before easing to the $107-110 range. Supply disruptions linked to the blockade and closure of the Strait of Hormuz drove the spike. India imports over 80 per cent of its crude, making the surge a direct macro risk.

Foreign Institutional Investors sold equities worth approximately ₹2,468 crore on April 29. Domestic Institutional Investors provided partial support with net purchases of around ₹2,262 crore. The rupee remained under pressure from elevated oil prices and foreign outflows.

Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments Limited, warned: "Brent crude at $120 threatens to worsen India's macros; the downside risk to growth and upside risk to inflation will rise if crude price remains elevated at this level."

Markets are closed on Friday for Maharashtra Day. The long weekend raises overnight positioning risk. State election outcomes and geopolitical developments are adding to trader caution through the session.

Among Nifty 50 constituents, Shriram Finance fell the most at 2.42 per cent to ₹933.65. IndiGo declined 2.31 per cent and Axis Bank shed 2.15 per cent. ONGC and Coal India bucked the trend, rising 1.99 per cent and 1.50 per cent respectively.

Technically, Nifty finds immediate support at 23,900-23,800 and resistance at 24,200-24,300. A sustained move above 24,500 would be needed to confirm any bullish continuation, according to analysts.
