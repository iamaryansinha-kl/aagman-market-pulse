India Stock Market Loses $924 Billion as AI Rally Shifts to Taiwan, Korea

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India is on the verge of dropping out of the world's top five stock markets for the first time in three years as $924 billion in market value evaporates.
  • 02/Foreign investors have withdrawn a net $42 billion since end-2024, pushing their ownership to a 14-year low and below domestic institutions for the first time in 20 years.
  • 03/MSCI EM weight fell to 12% from 19%; roughly two-thirds of the reallocation reflects AI positioning moving to Taiwan (up 42%) and Korea (up 78%) this year.

India's stock market is set to drop out of the world's top five for the first time in three years. $924 billion in market value has vanished since the September 2024 peak of $5.73 trillion.

Foreign investors have pulled a net $42 billion since the end of 2024. Their ownership has fallen to a 14-year low, Goldman Sachs data shows.

Foreign investors now hold less than domestic institutions for the first time in over 20 years. The rotation has accelerated sharply this year.

India's MSCI emerging markets weight collapsed to about 12% from 19% last year. M&G Investments says roughly two-thirds of outflows reflect AI-driven reallocation.

Taiwan's equity benchmark has surged 42% this year, while Korea's benchmark is up 78%. India's gauge has fallen more than 9% in the same period.

"This isn't a dip you buy," said Gary Dugan, chief executive of Global CIO Office. "The assumptions about where these businesses are in 10 years have to change."

The NSE Nifty IT Index has dropped more than 26% this year. India's $315 billion IT services sector faces disruption as generative AI automates coding and back-office work.

GDP growth is forecast at 6.5% for 2027 and 2028, down from an average of 8.3% over the past four years, per IMF estimates.
