15 Stocks In Focus May 4: Infosys, Tech Mahindra, HCLTech Lead Deal Flow

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/15 stocks including Infosys, Tech Mahindra, HCLTech, and Reliance are in focus on May 4 following dealmaking and corporate expansion
  • 02/NCC secured orders worth ₹1,703.27 crore, Siemens bagged a ₹1,825 crore contract, and Vodafone Idea got AGR dues relief
  • 03/Key sectors driving activity: IT services, telecom, infrastructure, energy, pharma, and hospitality

Fifteen stocks are in focus on Monday, May 4, 2026, following a wave of corporate developments. India Inc. witnessed brisk dealmaking across IT, telecom, infrastructure, and consumer sectors.

HCLTech (NSE: HCLTECH) has expanded its Cornerstone Partnership with MetLife Stadium. It is now the Official AI Partner of the stadium, the New York Giants, and the New York Jets. This extends its digital transformation role first established in September 2022.

Tech Mahindra (NSE: TECHM) has acquired Avant Tech Solutions, a Canada-based payments modernisation and wealth platforms specialist. The deal adds capabilities in real-time payments, ISO 20022 migration, wire modernisation, and open banking across North America.

Reliance Retail, a subsidiary of Reliance Industries (NSE: RELIANCE), has acquired Anomaly. Anomaly is the haircare brand founded by Priyanka Chopra Jonas. The company took ownership of the brand's trademarks, assets, and digital properties. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Infosys (NSE: INFY) has appointed Nitin Paranjpe as non-executive Vice Chairman of the Board, effective immediately. He is currently the non-executive Chairman of Hindustan Unilever Ltd and brings extensive governance experience.

Vodafone Idea Ltd (NSE: IDEA) received significant relief on its adjusted gross revenue (AGR) dues. The Department of Telecommunications cut the payable amount to ₹64,046 crore from an earlier estimate of ₹87,695 crore as of December 31, 2025.

NCC Ltd (NSE: NCC) received four orders in April totalling ₹1,703.27 crore excluding GST. The Buildings Division accounted for ₹929.96 crore, the Electrical Division for ₹603.41 crore, and the Transportation Division for ₹169.90 crore.

Siemens secured a new order worth over ₹1,825 crore. The internal work allocation covers manufacturing and supply of bogies, traction motors, and gearboxes for a group company.

Bharat Forge Ltd (NSE: BHARATFORG) acquired a 30% stake in Fortuna Engineering Private Limited at an equity value of ₹129.60 crores. Fortuna is a leading manufacturer of precision-machined engine components for automotive and industrial applications.

Coal India Limited (NSE: COALINDIA) received a Letter of Award from GRIDCO Limited, Odisha, for a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project. This marks a strategic diversification for the coal major into renewable energy infrastructure.

Aurobindo Pharma subsidiary CuraTeQ Biologics received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for Bevqolva, its bevacizumab biosimilar. The company has two additional biosimilar applications under review by Health Canada.
