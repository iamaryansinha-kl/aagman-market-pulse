AMC Q4 Results: Profits Fall on Volatility, SIP Inflows Stay Strong

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Listed fund houses reported sequential profit declines in Q4 FY26 as equity market turbulence and rupee depreciation hit earnings
  • 02/UTI AMC swung to a ₹67 crore loss from ₹121 crore profit; Nippon AMC fell 5% to ₹384 crore; HDFC AMC bucked trend with 19% y-o-y profit growth
  • 03/SIP inflows crossed ₹65 lakh crore in FY26; TER cut expected to hit margins by 3-4 bps; smaller AMCs face bigger challenge than large ones

The net profit of listed asset management companies fell sequentially in the March quarter. Volatile equity markets and geopolitical uncertainty weighed on revenues and profitability.

UTI AMC swung to a loss of ₹67 crore against a profit of ₹121 crore in the December quarter. The company also reported a 25 per cent fall in revenue on a quarter-on-quarter basis.

Nippon AMC saw the smallest sequential decline with profit falling 5 per cent to ₹384 crore. ICICI AMC revenue remained flat at ₹1,517 crore during the same period.

On a year-on-year basis, HDFC AMC bucked the trend with a 19 per cent increase in profit. However, except for Nippon MF and ICICI MF, all fund houses reported a drop in March quarter net profit.

All listed fund houses reported revenue growth of 7 to 30 per cent year-on-year. Nippon MF revenue rose 5 per cent sequentially to ₹739 crore while Canara Robeco gained 4 per cent to ₹114 crore.

Shweta Rajani, Head of Mutual Funds at Anand Rathi Wealth, said top AMCs delivered strong FY26 results with revenues growing faster than costs. She expects the TER cut to impact smaller AMCs more than larger ones.

Rajesh Singla, CEO and Fund Manager at Alpha AMC, said structural SIP inflows crossing ₹65 lakh crore in FY26 provide a durable revenue base. AMC revenues are leveraged to AUM levels and SIP continuity.

Analysts expect the cut in total expense ratio to impact margins by 3-4 basis points. Fund houses are currently negotiating with intermediaries to pass on the incremental cost.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW DID LISTED AMCs PERFORM IN Q4 FY26 ON A SEQUENTIAL BASIS?
ALL LISTED FUND HOUSES REPORTED A DECLINE IN NET PROFIT QUARTER-ON-QUARTER. UTI AMC SWUNG TO A LOSS OF ₹67 CRORE FROM A PROFIT OF ₹121 CRORE IN THE DECEMBER QUARTER. NIPPON AMC SAW THE SMALLEST FALL AT 5 PER CENT TO ₹384 CRORE.
WHICH AMCs BUCKED THE TREND ON A YEAR-ON-YEAR BASIS?
HDFC AMC REPORTED A 19 PER CENT INCREASE IN NET PROFIT YEAR-ON-YEAR. NIPPON MF AND ICICI MF ALSO POSTED BETTER NUMBERS Y-O-Y. HOWEVER, ALL OTHER FUND HOUSES REPORTED A DROP IN MARCH QUARTER NET PROFIT.
WHAT WERE THE REVENUE TRENDS FOR MAJOR AMCs IN Q4?
ON A SEQUENTIAL BASIS, NIPPON MF REVENUE ROSE 5 PER CENT TO ₹739 CRORE AND CANARA ROBECO GAINED 4 PER CENT TO ₹114 CRORE. ICICI AMC REVENUE WAS FLAT AT ₹1,517 CRORE. YEAR-ON-YEAR, ALL FUND HOUSES SAW REVENUE RISE BETWEEN 7 AND 30 PER CENT.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR AMC MARGINS GOING FORWARD?
ANALYSTS EXPECT THE CUT IN TOTAL EXPENSE RATIO TO IMPACT MARGINS BY 3-4 BASIS POINTS. LARGER AMCs MAY FACE MARGIN PRESSURE BUT NOT A THREAT TO OVERALL PROFITABILITY, WHILE SMALLER AMCs WITHOUT SCALE ADVANTAGE FACE A BIGGER CHALLENGE.
WHAT ROLE ARE SIP INFLOWS PLAYING IN AMC STABILITY?
MONTHLY SIP INFLOWS REMAIN ABOVE ₹30,000 CRORE AND STRUCTURAL SIP INFLOWS CROSSED ₹65 LAKH CRORE IN FY26. THIS PROVIDES AMCs WITH A DURABLE REVENUE BASE THAT DOES NOT RELY ENTIRELY ON MARKET RETURNS.
