Ather Energy Q4 FY26: Loss Halves YoY, Revenue Surges 74% to ₹1,175 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ather Energy Q4 FY26 net loss drops 57% y-o-y to ₹100.23 crore, while revenue surges 74% to ₹1,174.66 crore
  • 02/FY26 full-year loss narrows to ₹517.17 crore from ₹812.28 crore; revenue nearly doubles to ₹3,671.76 crore
  • 03/BHEL, Ambuja Cements, Aditya Birla Capital, Godrej Properties among others set to announce Q4 results today

Ather Energy Ltd (NSE: ATHERENERG) reported its audited Q4 FY26 results on May 4, 2026. The EV maker's net loss narrowed sharply to ₹100.23 crore from ₹234.36 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations surged 73.7% to ₹1,174.66 crore compared to ₹676.08 crore in the year-ago quarter. Total income for Q4 stood at ₹1,213.77 crore.

For the full year FY26, Ather's net loss reduced to ₹517.17 crore from ₹812.28 crore in FY25. Revenue jumped 62.8% to ₹3,671.76 crore from ₹2,255.01 crore.

The company turned operating cash flow positive in FY26, generating ₹31.89 crore against a cash burn of ₹720.70 crore in FY25. Total assets expanded to ₹4,721.51 crore, boosted by its ₹2,626 crore IPO.

Ather completed its IPO in FY26, issuing 9.28 crore shares at ₹321 each. Of the proceeds, ₹1,008.93 crore has been utilised, with ₹1,617.07 crore still unutilised.

The board approved re-appointment of Deloitte Haskins & Sells as statutory auditors for FY27-FY31. It also approved incorporation of two wholly-owned subsidiaries.

Meanwhile, Kotak Mahindra Bank shares fell 5% to ₹363 despite a 13% y-o-y rise in Q4 net profit to ₹4,027 crore. Zen Technologies crashed over 12% after Q4 profit plunged 83% to ₹14.75 crore.

BHEL, Ambuja Cements, Aditya Birla Capital, Godrej Properties, Sobha, Tata Technologies, and KEI Industries are among companies announcing Q4 results on May 4.
