Functional & Innovative Foods Files DRHP With SEBI for IPO: 85 Lakh Shares, Two New Plants

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Functional & Innovative Foods files DRHP with SEBI for an IPO of up to 85 lakh equity shares
  • 02/Fresh issue of 60 lakh shares and OFS of 25 lakh shares; face value ₹10 per share
  • 03/Proceeds to fund two new manufacturing facilities and repay outstanding borrowings

Functional & Innovative Foods Limited has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI. The company is planning an initial public offering of up to 85 lakh equity shares.

The IPO comprises a fresh issue of up to 60 lakh equity shares and an offer for sale of up to 25 lakh shares. Each share has a face value of ₹10.

The company plans to use the net proceeds to fund capital expenditure. Two new manufacturing facilities are planned: Unit V at Namakkal, Tamil Nadu and Unit VI at Dhar, Madhya Pradesh.

A portion of the funds will be used for prepayment or repayment of certain outstanding borrowings. The company will also invest in its wholly owned subsidiary, Christy Quality Foods India Private Limited.

The investment in the subsidiary will be through debt or equity. It will enable repayment of a portion of the subsidiary's outstanding borrowings.

Remaining funds will meet long-term working capital requirements and general corporate purposes.

For the financial year ended March 31, 2025, the company reported revenue of ₹260.12 crore. EBITDA was ₹33.83 crore and profit after tax was ₹23.22 crore.

For the nine-month period ended December 31, 2025, revenue was ₹232.44 crore. EBITDA was ₹36.97 crore and profit after tax was ₹25.61 crore.

Beeline Capital Advisors is the book-running lead manager. KFin Technologies has been appointed as the registrar.

The company is an integrated food contract manufacturer. It offers a diversified product portfolio ranging from ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook items to staples, sugar alternatives, and spices.

It operates four manufacturing units with nine production facilities in Tamil Nadu. The facilities are equipped with automated systems for material handling, processing, and packaging.

Through its subsidiary Christy Quality Foods, the company manufactures branded food products under the "Nallas" brand. These are distributed across Tamil Nadu.
