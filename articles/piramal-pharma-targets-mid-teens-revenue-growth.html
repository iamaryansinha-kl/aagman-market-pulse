Piramal Pharma Targets Early-to-Mid-Teens Revenue Growth Over 2-3 Years

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Piramal Pharma chairperson Nandini Piramal projects early-to-mid-teens revenue growth over the next 2-3 years, up from a 3% decline in FY2026
  • 02/CDMO division, which contributes 55% of revenue, saw a 10% slump in FY2026, dragging margins down to 13% from 17%
  • 03/Global biotech funding improved 80% in H2 versus prior year, boosting order inflows; company is prioritizing antibody-drug conjugates over weight-loss APIs

Piramal Pharma Ltd (NSE: PPLPHARMA) expects to accelerate earnings growth over the next two to three years. Chairperson Nandini Piramal outlined the target on April 29, 2026.

The company projects early-to-mid-teens revenue growth annually. This follows a difficult FY2026 when overall revenue declined 3% year-on-year.

Contract drug manufacturing, which accounts for 55% of total revenue, slumped 10% in FY2026. The revenue drop compressed margins to 13% from 17%.

Global biotech funding is recovering strongly. Funding in the second half was approximately 80% higher than the same period last year.

"We've added a significant number of customers, both big pharma and biotech, as this sector has grown," Piramal said.

The company is prioritizing antibody-drug conjugates, a targeted cancer therapy segment. It is avoiding weight-loss drug APIs due to intense competition and falling prices.

"These are high fixed-cost businesses, and as revenues grow, operating leverage flows through to the bottom line," Nandini Piramal told Reuters.

Indian drugmakers also stand to gain from U.S. curbs on biotech funding for rival Chinese firms. Piramal Pharma is positioned to capture this shift.

PIRAMAL PHARMA REPORTED A 3% DECLINE IN FISCAL 2026 REVENUE. ITS CONTRACT DRUG MANUFACTURING DIVISION, WHICH CONSTITUTES 55% OF REVENUE, SLUMPED 10%. MARGINS SHRANK TO 13% FROM 17%.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE IMPROVED OUTLOOK FOR PIRAMAL PHARMA?
GLOBAL BIOTECH FUNDING IN THE SECOND HALF WAS ABOUT 80% HIGHER THAN THE PRIOR YEAR, TRANSLATING INTO HIGHER REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS AND ORDER INFLOWS. LOWER INTEREST RATES AND REDUCED UNCERTAINTY IN THE U.S. PHARMA SECTOR ARE KEY FACTORS.
WHICH SEGMENTS IS PIRAMAL PHARMA PRIORITIZING?
THE COMPANY IS FOCUSING ON ANTIBODY-DRUG CONJUGATES (ADCS), WHICH ARE TARGETED CANCER THERAPIES. IT IS STEERING CLEAR OF WEIGHT-LOSS DRUG APIs LIKE SEMAGLUTIDE DUE TO INTENSE COMPETITION AND FALLING PRICES.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH?
INVESTORS SHOULD TRACK ORDER INFLOWS IN THE CDMO DIVISION, MARGIN RECOVERY FROM THE CURRENT 13% LEVEL, AND THE COMPANY'S ABILITY TO ADD NEW BIG PHARMA AND BIOTECH CUSTOMERS IN THE ADC SEGMENT.
