RBI Considers Rate Hike as Rupee Hits Record Low Near 97 Per Dollar

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI is considering all options including rate hikes and currency swaps after the rupee hit a fresh low of almost 97 to the dollar this week
  • 02/Governor Sanjay Malhotra held internal meetings; next MPC decision scheduled for June 3-5 with benchmark rate currently at 5.25%
  • 03/RBI announced a $5 billion swap auction on Wednesday and may raise dollars via NRI deposits that could draw up to $50 billion

The Reserve Bank of India is weighing an interest rate hike to stabilize the rupee. The currency plunged to a fresh low of nearly 97 per dollar this week.

Governor Sanjay Malhotra and top officials held a series of internal meetings to discuss available options. The discussions remain private at this stage.

The next scheduled monetary policy decision is on June 5. The six-member MPC will meet on June 3-5 to deliberate on the stance.

The benchmark repo rate stands at 5.25% so far this year. Most economists now predict a hike as inflation pressures build up.

On Wednesday, the RBI announced a $5 billion swap auction to infuse liquidity and boost dollar reserves. More such auctions may follow.

Other options include raising dollars through non-resident Indian deposit schemes. These could attract up to $50 billion, according to sources.

Foreign fund outflows from Indian stocks in 2026 have already surpassed last year's record of $19 billion. Investors are dumping local assets.

Policymakers believe India's economic fundamentals remain strong. However, that strength is not reflecting in the exchange rate at present.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY IS THE RBI CONSIDERING A RATE HIKE?
THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA IS CONSIDERING A RATE HIKE TO STABILIZE THE RUPEE AFTER IT PLUNGED TO A FRESH LOW OF NEARLY 97 PER DOLLAR. RAISING BORROWING COSTS WOULD HELP SPUR FOREIGN BOND INFLOWS BY WIDENING THE INTEREST RATE DIFFERENTIAL BETWEEN THE US AND INDIA.
WHEN IS THE NEXT RBI MONETARY POLICY DECISION?
THE NEXT SCHEDULED MONETARY POLICY DECISION IS ON JUNE 5, 2026. THE SIX-MEMBER MONETARY POLICY COMMITTEE WILL MEET ON JUNE 3-5 TO DELIBERATE ON THE POLICY STANCE AND POSSIBLE RATE ACTION.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT RBI BENCHMARK RATE?
THE RBI'S BENCHMARK REPO RATE IS CURRENTLY AT 5.25%. THE RATE HAS BEEN KEPT UNCHANGED SO FAR THIS YEAR, ALTHOUGH MOST ECONOMISTS NOW PREDICT A HIKE IN THE COMING MONTHS AS INFLATION ACCELERATES.
WHAT OTHER MEASURES IS THE RBI CONSIDERING BESIDES A RATE HIKE?
THE RBI IS ALSO CONSIDERING CURRENCY SWAPS, RAISING DOLLARS THROUGH NON-RESIDENT INDIAN DEPOSIT SCHEMES, AND SELLING SOVEREIGN DOLLAR BONDS. THE RBI ANNOUNCED A $5 BILLION SWAP AUCTION ON WEDNESDAY AND MAY CONDUCT MORE.
HOW MUCH COULD NRI DEPOSIT SCHEMES RAISE?
THE RBI ESTIMATES THAT NON-RESIDENT INDIAN DEPOSIT SCHEMES COULD DRAW AS MUCH AS $50 BILLION THIS TIME, COMPARED WITH ABOUT $30 BILLION DURING THE 2013 TAPER TANTRUM PERIOD.
