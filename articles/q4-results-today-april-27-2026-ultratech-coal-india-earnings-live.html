Q4 Results Today April 27, 2026: UltraTech, Coal India, AU SFB, SBI Cards Among 48 Companies Announcing Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The earnings season reaches its peak on April 27, 2026, with 48 BSE and NSE-listed companies scheduled to announce their fourth-quarter results for FY26. The list includes two Nifty 50 constituents, UltraTech Cement and Coal India, alongside major financial services firms such as AU Small Finance Bank, Bajaj Housing Finance, SBI Cards and Payment Services, and City Union Bank. Investors are also watching Adani Total Gas, Varun Beverages, Nippon Life India Asset Management, and The Phoenix Mills for fresh guidance on consumer demand, infrastructure spending, and credit growth.

Brokerage estimates compiled by Bloomberg and tracked by The Hindu Businessline show significant expectations for the day's headline results. UltraTech Cement faces a consensus Q4 net profit estimate of ₹28.2 billion from 20 analysts, while Coal India carries an estimated Q4 PAT of ₹91.2 billion from 8 analysts. SBI Cards and Payment Services is expected to report Q4 net profit of ₹6.06 billion according to 11 analyst estimates, and Bajaj Housing Finance faces a consensus estimate of ₹6.56 billion from 10 analysts.

Financial sector results announced in the preceding days have set a mixed tone. Shriram Finance reported Q4 net profit of ₹3,014 crore, up 41% year-on-year from ₹2,139 crore, with net interest income rising 21% to ₹6,751 crore. IndusInd Bank swung to a Q4 profit of ₹533 crore against a loss of ₹2,329 crore in the year-ago period, beating the CNBC-TV18 poll estimate of ₹420.4 crore. NII came in at ₹4,371.5 crore versus the poll estimate of ₹4,359.4 crore.

Axis Bank, however, delivered a weaker-than-expected Q4 with net profit falling 1% year-on-year to ₹7,071 crore. The bank's operating profit dropped 8% quarter-on-quarter to ₹10,013 crore, while provisions surged 57% QoQ to ₹3,522 crore. The board declared a dividend of ₹1 per share. Axis Bank shares fell 4.61% in early trade on April 27, making it the worst performer among Nifty 50 constituents.

Reliance Industries, which announced its Q4 results on April 24, reported a 12.6% drop in consolidated net profit to ₹16,971 crore for the March quarter. The board declared a dividend of ₹6 per share. Reliance shares traded at ₹1,326.80 on the NSE at 9:30 am on April 27, down 0.20% from the previous close. Hindustan Zinc posted a record quarterly profit of ₹5,033 crore for Q4, up from ₹2,976 crore a year ago.

The broader market opened in positive territory on April 27, with the Sensex climbing 360 points to 77,024.49 and the Nifty 50 rising 131 points to 24,029.40 at 9:17 am. By 10:00 am, the Sensex was up 413 points at 77,077.49 and the Nifty had gained 111 points to 24,008.85. Sun Pharma led Nifty gainers with a 4.60% rise, followed by Adani Ports at 2.52% and JSW Steel at 2.21%. Top losers included Axis Bank at -4.61% and Shriram Finance at -2.77%.

Analysts at Univest and other brokerages have flagged Coal India's Q4 results as a critical domestic trigger, given that Q3FY26 saw pressure from e-auction volume declines. Any revenue growth and dividend announcement for FY26 could boost PSU sentiment and DII buying flows. UltraTech Cement's results will signal cement sector volume growth trends and realisation per tonne data, with the company targeting 200 MTPA capacity expansion.

Other companies on the April 27 results calendar include Jindal Saw, Kirloskar Pneumatic, Mahindra Holidays & Resorts, Piramal Finance, Punjab & Sind Bank, Rallis India, Rossari Biotech, Supreme Industries, Tamilnad Mercantile Bank, and Unicommerce Esolutions. Investors will also watch for potential dividend declarations from several of these firms, as boards typically recommend final dividends alongside Q4 results.
