Maharashtra Day Holiday: Markets Shut After Sensex Drops 583 Points, Trade Resumes May 4

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex declined 582.86 points (0.75%) to 76,913.50 and Nifty 50 fell 180.10 points (0.74%) to 23,997.55 on April 30
  • 02/Markets are shut on May 1 for Maharashtra Day; equity, currency and debt trading will resume on Monday, May 4
  • 03/Global pressures and geopolitical concerns weighed on sentiment, with Brent crude surging above $120 before retreating to $116.59

Indian equity, currency and debt markets will remain closed today, May 1, on account of Maharashtra Day. Trading activity across all segments will resume on Monday, May 4.

The market holiday follows a volatile session on April 30, when benchmark indices posted sharp losses. BSE Sensex declined 582.86 points, or 0.75 per cent, to settle at 76,913.50. The NSE Nifty 50 dropped 180.10 points, or 0.74 per cent, to close at 23,997.55.

Domestic markets opened significantly lower on Thursday. The Nifty 50 began at 23,899.20, down 278.45 points or 1.15 per cent. The Sensex opened at 76,582.24, declining 914.12 points or 1.18 per cent.

Selling pressure was driven by surging crude oil prices and continued uncertainty in West Asia. Brent crude futures fell 1.22 per cent to $116.59, after earlier surging above $120 per barrel during the session.

Asian markets traded with a cautious tone on Thursday. US markets showed mixed signals with a negative bias. Dow Jones Futures slipped 0.18 per cent and the S&P 500 dipped 0.04 per cent, while the Nasdaq posted a marginal gain of 0.04 per cent.

Market participants attributed the domestic weakness to global headwinds and geopolitical tensions. The sharp intra-day swings reflected heightened risk aversion among investors.

With crude volatility and West Asia uncertainty persisting, market participants are expected to remain cautious when trading resumes next week. Investors will closely monitor global cues and oil price movements.
