Go Digit Insurance Q4 PBT Jumps 49% to ₹173 Crore, Stock Edges Up

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Go Digit General Insurance Q4 FY26 profit before tax jumps 49% to ₹173 crore under IGAAP, up from ₹116 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Full-year FY26 PBT climbs to ₹632 crore from ₹425 crore; GDPI rises 21.3% to ₹2,402 crore in Q4
  • 03/Stock trades marginally higher at ₹317.30 but remains down 8.39% year-to-date; Ind AS transition from April 2026 is a key overhang

Go Digit General Insurance Ltd (NSE: GODIGIT) reported a 49% jump in Q4 FY26 profit before tax. PBT under IGAAP rose to ₹173 crore from ₹116 crore in the year-ago quarter.

For the full financial year, PBT climbed to ₹632 crore from ₹425 crore in FY25. The board released the results on Tuesday, April 28, 2026.

Gross Direct Premium Income for Q4 grew 21.3% year-on-year to ₹2,402 crore. Assets under management rose 16.3% to ₹22,922 crore as of March 31, 2026.

The solvency ratio improved to 2.42x from 2.24x a year ago. This signals stronger capital adequacy and financial resilience for the insurer.

Despite the earnings beat, the stock has underperformed in 2026. It is down 8.39% year-to-date against a 3.83% decline in the Nifty 500 index.

Shares were trading at ₹317.30 on Wednesday morning, up just 0.16% from the previous close of ₹316.80. Sell orders dominated with 65.23% of the order book on the sell side.

The stock trades at a price-to-earnings multiple of 53.80. Its 52-week high of ₹381.40 was recorded in September 2025.

Investors are watching the company's transition to Ind AS accounting standards. IRDAI has mandated the shift from April 2026, adding near-term uncertainty.
