Jio Platforms IPO in Final Stages After Global Roadshows, RHP Next

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms is giving final touches to an estimated $3.8 billion public offer, touted as India's largest, after concluding worldwide investor roadshows.
  • 02/The roadshows covered the US, UK, Dubai, Singapore and Hong Kong; the company has now begun preparing its red herring prospectus after Sebi's final observations on August 28.
  • 03/The offer of up to 27 crore fresh shares is about 2.9% of the post-issue equity base and could value the company near $137 billion; no launch date or price band has been confirmed.

Jio Platforms is in the final stages of its much-anticipated public offer. The company is giving finishing touches to offer documents estimated at $3.8 billion, touted as India's largest IPO, after concluding worldwide roadshows, a source aware of the development told PTI.

The overseas roadshows were led by Jio Platforms Managing Director Akash Ambani. He was joined by Isha Ambani, Executive Director of Reliance Retail Ventures.

According to the source, the first leg of the IPO process is now complete. "Jio Platforms has completed the first leg of the IPO after roadshows with potential investors globally. The roadshows were held in the US, UK, Dubai, Singapore and Hong Kong. Now, the company has started preparations for filing a red herring prospectus," the source said.

Regulatory clearance is already in hand. Sebi issued its final observations on August 28, after Jio Platforms filed its draft IPO papers in June.

The scale of the offer is significant but measured. Per the draft prospectus, the company plans to issue up to 27 crore fresh equity shares, equivalent to roughly 2.9% of its post-issue equity base.

The offering could value Jio Platforms at about $137 billion, people familiar with the matter had earlier indicated. The final valuation and price band will only be known once the red herring prospectus is filed.

The use of proceeds is largely defined. The funds will primarily repay or prepay, in whole or in part, about ₹27,500 crore of outstanding borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm Ltd, the material subsidiary. The remainder will go toward general corporate purposes.

Jio Platforms houses Reliance's digital businesses, including telecommunications. Its telecom arm, Reliance Jio Infocomm, held a 39.29% share in mobile connections, serving 506 million customers, and a 32.89% share in fixed-line connections, serving 157.9 million customers.

The 5G numbers are striking. Jio operates the largest 5G Standalone network outside China, with 26.85 crore 5G customers as of June 2026. The company claims its 5G network carries close to 60% of India's wireless data traffic.

Jio also leads the Fixed Wireless Access segment globally with about 1.5 crore subscribers. That is roughly 1.5 times the second-largest player, US-based T-Mobile.

Financially, the business has been growing at a steady clip. In FY26, profit after tax rose 15% to ₹30,053 crore, while annual revenue climbed 14.5% to ₹1,46,885 crore.

The shareholder base already includes some of the world's biggest technology investors. In 2020, Meta invested ₹43,574 crore for a 9.99% stake, and Google invested ₹33,737 crore for 7.73%. Silver Lake, Vista Equity Partners, General Atlantic, KKR, Mubadala, ADIA, TPG, L Catterton, Intel Capital and Qualcomm Ventures collectively put in about ₹74,745 crore for roughly 15.2%.

Reliance Industries owns about 66.4% of Jio Platforms. Meta and Google together hold about 17.7%, per the draft prospectus.

The listing carries historic weight for the conglomerate. It would be the first public offering from the Reliance group since 2008, and the first IPO of a consumer-focused business within the group. Jio Platforms is chaired by Mukesh Ambani, with eldest son Akash as managing director.

The timing also lands in a hot primary market. More than two dozen IPOs have been announced or launched since July 1, nearly matching the 28 recorded in H1 2026.

Investors will now watch for the red herring prospectus filing for the first official look at the price band and timeline. Until then, the $3.8 billion size and $137 billion valuation remain estimates, not confirmations.
