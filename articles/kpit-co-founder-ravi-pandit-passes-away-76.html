KPIT Technologies Co-Founder Ravi Pandit Passes Away at 76 in Pune

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KPIT Technologies co-founder and Chairman Ravi Pandit passed away at age 76 in Pune on May 8, 2026 following a brief illness
  • 02/Pandit built KPIT into a global automotive software leader with operations across 15 countries and FY26 revenue of ₹6,009 crore
  • 03/KPIT stock trades near ₹715, down 49% from its 52-week high of ₹1,434; investors will watch leadership transition closely

S B Ravi Pandit, co-founder and Chairman of KPIT Technologies Ltd, passed away on Friday morning, May 8, 2026. He was 76 years old.

The company confirmed the news in a statement. Pandit died in Pune following a brief illness.

He is survived by his wife Nirmala and children Chinmay and Prachi.

Pandit founded KPIT Technologies in 1990 alongside Kishor Patil. The Pune-headquartered company grew into a global leader in software-defined mobility.

KPIT now operates across 15 countries. Its engineering solutions power millions of vehicles worldwide in autonomous driving, electrification, and clean energy.

For FY2025-26, KPIT reported revenue of ₹6,009.67 crore and profit of ₹836.8 crore. The company's market capitalisation stands at ₹20,522 crore.

Pandit was also a gold-medallist Chartered Accountant and an alumnus of MIT Sloan School of Management.

He co-founded the K&P Group and chaired Kirtane & Pandit Chartered Accountants, employing over 1,200 professionals globally.

Beyond business, Pandit served on the board of Thermax Ltd. He also represented industry as President of MCCIA and contributed to the National Green Hydrogen Mission.

KPIT shares closed near ₹715 on May 7, 2026. The stock has declined 49% from its 52-week high of ₹1,434.50 hit earlier in the year.

The company recently announced plans to acquire Israeli auto cybersecurity firm Cymotive for up to $120 million.
