Fidelity Investments Acquires 2% Lodha Stake for ₹1,864 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Fidelity Investments acquires 1.99% stake in Lodha Developers for ₹1,864.56 crore via eight block deals on the NSE on June 25, 2026
  • 02/Promoter entities Hightown Constructions and Homecraft Developers sold 1.98 crore shares at ₹937.85 per share
  • 03/Lodha Developers Q4 FY2026 net profit rose 9% to ₹1,008.1 crore; shares closed at ₹949.90, up 1.28%

Fidelity Investments has acquired a 2 per cent stake in Lodha Developers for ₹1,864.56 crore. The US-based asset manager purchased the shares through open market block deals on the NSE on June 25, 2026.

The acquisition involved 1,98,81,296 shares at an average price of ₹937.85 apiece. Fidelity executed the purchase through eight affiliates in eight separate tranches.

Two promoter entities, Hightown Constructions and Homecraft Developers, collectively sold the stake. Their combined holding dropped to 70.28 per cent from 72.27 per cent.

Lodha Developers reported a 9 per cent rise in consolidated net profit to ₹1,008.1 crore for Q4 FY2026. Total income grew to ₹4,840 crore from ₹4,420 crore in the year-ago period.

The Mumbai-based realty firm has shown consistent operational growth. Profit in the year-ago quarter stood at ₹922.8 crore, indicating steady margin improvement.

Lodha Developers shares closed at ₹949.90 on June 25, up 1.28 per cent. The stock gained ahead of the block deal announcement.

Fidelity has been active in Indian markets. Earlier in June, it sold a 1.3 per cent stake in Meesho for ₹988 crore.

In January 2026, Fidelity also divested 1.94 per cent in Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands for ₹261 crore. The Lodha deal signals renewed real estate interest.
