Bharti Airtel Claims No.2 Spot, Overtakes HDFC Bank in Market Cap

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bharti Airtel's market cap touched ₹11.78 lakh crore on May 18, surpassing HDFC Bank's ₹11.74 lakh crore.
  • 02/Airtel shares gained over 2% while HDFC Bank declined, reflecting divergent sector momentum.
  • 03/Reliance Industries remains India's most valuable firm at ₹19.6 lakh crore; Airtel now ranks second.

Bharti Airtel Ltd (NSE: BHARTIARTL) has overtaken HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK) to become India's second most valuable listed company. On May 18, 2026, Airtel's market capitalisation touched ₹11.78 lakh crore, edging past HDFC Bank's ₹11.74 lakh crore.

Airtel shares gained over 2% during the session, briefly pushing its market value to nearly ₹11.9 lakh crore. The stock has delivered a 13.46% return over the past year, outperforming the Sensex which rose just 1.6% in the same period.

HDFC Bank shares declined on the day, with the stock trading around ₹1,544 on the NSE. The bank had held the No.2 position for an extended period, ranking behind only Reliance Industries Ltd (NSE: RELIANCE).

Reliance Industries continues to dominate the top spot with a market cap of approximately ₹19.6 lakh crore. The reshuffle marks a significant shift in India's market cap hierarchy, with a telecom operator displacing a financial giant for the first time in recent years.

Airtel operates across 18 countries including India and 14 African markets. It serves 463.38 million wireless subscribers in India, holding a 37.24% market share according to TRAI data published in February 2026.

The company's average revenue per user (ARPU) stood at ₹256 as of September 2025, well ahead of Reliance Jio's estimated ₹195 and Vodafone Idea's ₹163. Its 5G services now cover all 7,900 towns in India with average download speeds of 240 Mbps.

HDFC Bank remains India's largest private sector lender by assets. The bank's weight in the Nifty 50 index was 6.24% as of March 2026, while Airtel stood at 6.02%. The swap in rankings could trigger passive fund rebalancing.
