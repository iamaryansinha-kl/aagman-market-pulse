South Korea AI ETFs: First Leveraged Single-Stock Funds Launch This Week

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/South Korea will debut its first single-stock leveraged ETFs this week, linked to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, offering 2x daily returns
  • 02/Korean retail investors have already poured $1.3 billion YTD into a Hong Kong-listed 2x Samsung product, exceeding Tesla and Microsoft trackers
  • 03/Analysts warn the funds will intensify concentration risk and volatility in a market where 5% intraday Kospi swings are now common

South Korea is set to launch its first single-stock leveraged ETFs this week. The products will track Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix with 2x daily leverage.

Both companies sit at the heart of the global artificial intelligence supply chain. Samsung and SK Hynix dominate memory chip production worldwide.

The funds aim to deliver twice the daily price movement of each stock. A 3% gain in Samsung would produce a 6% ETF return on the same day.

Regulators previously barred single-stock leveraged products due to their high-risk nature. They reversed course to lure domestic retail flows back from offshore markets.

Korean investors have already shown massive appetite for such exposure. Year-to-date inflows of $1.3 billion into a Hong Kong-listed 2x Samsung ETF exceeded similar Tesla and Microsoft trackers.

The nation has over 14 million retail investors. Many are day traders seeking to capitalize on the AI-driven rally that has tripled the Kospi since late 2024.

"The ETFs will intensify the existing problem - the concentration risk," said Jung In Yun, CEO at Fibonacci Asset Management Global in Singapore. "This poses a structural problem for longer-term investors as the volatility of the index will remain elevated."

Five percent intraday swings in the Kospi have become increasingly common. Leveraged products could amplify these moves as issuers rebalance positions to maintain target ratios.
