India Exports Rise in Early April Despite West Asia Crisis, Says Piyush Goyal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said exports rose during first three weeks of April compared to same period last year
  • 02/Shipments are being rerouted due to challenges in the Strait of Hormuz amid ongoing West Asia conflict
  • 03/India has finalised trade pacts with UAE, UK, EU, EFTA, Australia, Mauritius and Oman; 12 more on the anvil

India's exports have registered a healthy increase during the first three weeks of April. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal confirmed the uptick on Wednesday, April 29, 2026.

Despite the ongoing war in West Asia, domestic exporters have shown tremendous enthusiasm. Goyal told reporters in New Delhi that shipments continue to flow.

Consignments to West Asia are being rerouted through alternate routes. The Strait of Hormuz remains a challenge for maritime trade.

Goyal said free trade agreements finalised by India would give a huge boost to domestic industry. India has completed trade pacts with UAE, UK, EU, EFTA, Australia, Mauritius and Oman.

Another 12 trade agreements are on the anvil. The government is also negotiating with Israel, Gulf Cooperation Council and Canada.

On US trade talks, Goyal said discussions held about a week ago went very well. The Indian team has returned and engagement continues.
