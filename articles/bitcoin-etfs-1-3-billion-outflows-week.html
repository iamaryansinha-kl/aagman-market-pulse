Bitcoin ETFs Bleed $1.3 Billion in Week as Crypto Slump Deepens

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $1.3 billion in outflows over the past week, the longest streak of institutional redemptions since funds launched in 2024
  • 02/BlackRock's IBIT led departures at $860 million, on pace for its seventh straight week of outflows; typical investor now sits on losses of about 40%
  • 03/Total crypto market cap has halved to $2 trillion from October's $4 trillion peak; Glassnode analysts warn of sustained capital withdrawal

US spot Bitcoin ETFs suffered more than $1.3 billion in withdrawals over the past week. The outflows mark the longest streak of institutional redemptions since the funds launched in 2024.

BlackRock's IBIT led the departures with $860 million in net outflows. It is on pace for its seventh straight week of outflows, the longest streak on record.

The year-to-date exodus now totals $4.5 billion, according to Bloomberg data. This is the most persistent period of capital withdrawal since the ETFs began trading.

Glassnode analysts wrote that "sustained redemptions indicate many investors are choosing to reduce exposure rather than accumulate." This is a sharp break from the previous pattern where ETF investors bought every dip.

The total crypto market cap has shrunk to $2 trillion from over $4 trillion in early October. Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies have failed to mount a meaningful recovery since the October shock selloff.

Analysts at Bespoke Investment Group said the typical IBIT investor now sits on losses of about 40%. The average dollar invested was at a 30% gain by mid-2025.

"Bitcoin ETFs have been an absolute disaster for investors," Bespoke analysts wrote. They noted a fresh crypto rally could change the story, but for now capital is fleeing.

Timothy Enneking, managing partner at Psalion, said the original crypto "old guard" remains "quite sanguine." He added that many see this drop as a reduction in volatility compared to past cycles.
