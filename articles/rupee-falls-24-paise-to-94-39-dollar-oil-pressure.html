Rupee Falls 24 Paise to 94.39 Against US Dollar, Oil Pressure

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The rupee depreciated 24 paise to 94.39 against the US dollar in early trade on April 28, 2026, weighed by oil prices
  • 02/Brent crude traded at $109.30 per barrel, up 0.99%, while FIIs offloaded ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday
  • 03/The dollar index was at 98.49; month-end dollar demand and geopolitical risks could keep the rupee under pressure

The rupee depreciated 24 paise to 94.39 against the US dollar in early trade on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. It opened at 94.35 before losing ground to touch 94.39.

On Monday, the rupee had settled at 94.15 against the dollar. The fall comes amid elevated crude oil prices and month-end dollar demand.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading at $109.30 per barrel, up 0.99 per cent. Forex traders said sustained oil prices keep India's import position vulnerable.

The dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength against a basket of six currencies, was trading at 98.49. A stronger dollar adds pressure on emerging market currencies.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday, according to exchange data. Unabated capital outflows amid geopolitical uncertainties dented sentiment further.

"The Indian rupee seems to be standing at a crossroads, not weak enough to fall, not strong enough to rise," said CR Forex Advisors MD Amit Pabari. He noted that softer growth expectations reduce the cushion the currency usually enjoys.

Anil Kumar Bhansali, Head of Treasury at Finrex Treasury Advisors, said month-end dollar demand could keep the USD higher for the next two days. Rising crude prices remain a key headwind for the rupee.

On the domestic equity market front, the 30-share Sensex was trading 68.28 points lower at 77,235.35. The broader Nifty was up 14.25 points at 24,029.25.
