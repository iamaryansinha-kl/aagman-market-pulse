Noel Tata Votes Against Srinivasan and Singh at Tata Education Trust Board

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Trusts chairman Noel Tata voted against reappointing Venu Srinivasan and Vijay Singh to the Tata Education and Development Trust board on May 10, 2026.
  • 02/Mehli Mistry and JN Mistry also voted against, while Srinivasan and Singh voted for each other, deepening the trustee rift.
  • 03/The clash centres on Tata Sons listing: Srinivasan and Singh back an IPO, while Noel Tata prefers the holding company stays unlisted.

Tata Trusts chairman Noel Tata voted against the reappointment of Venu Srinivasan and Vijay Singh. The vote was cast at the Tata Education and Development Trust (TEDT) board meeting last week.

The trustee terms of both Srinivasan and Singh expired on Sunday, May 10, 2026. Sources say former trustee Mehli Mistry, JN Mistry and Noel Tata all voted against renewal.

Srinivasan and Singh voted in favour of each other's reappointment. Noel Tata cast his vote after the negative votes were already recorded.

Tata Education is a non-shareholding trust allied to the Sir Ratan Tata Trust. It does not hold equity in Tata Sons, unlike the main trusts.

The split highlights a wider rift among trustees. Srinivasan and Singh have publicly backed listing Tata Sons, where Tata Trusts holds around 66 per cent.

Noel Tata prefers Tata Sons stays unlisted. Both Tata Sons and Tata Trust boards had earlier passed unanimous resolutions to keep it that way.

Last Friday's trust meeting was aborted. One agenda item was removing Srinivasan as Tata Trusts' nominee on the Tata Sons board, with Bhaskar Bhat as a possible replacement.

Mehli Mistry's objections are tied to governance complaints filed before the Maharashtra Charity Commissioner. He alleged Singh took commissions from Tata Sons in violation of the Trust Act.

Mistry also alleged Srinivasan misused his nominee director role. He secured free design work from Gerry McGovern, then COO of Jaguar Land Rover, for Norton Motorcycles, a TVS Motor subsidiary.
