Nifty Midcap 100 Hits Record 62,094 as Crude Prices Cool Sharply

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Nifty Midcap 100 scaled a record 62,094 on May 7, closing 1.10% higher while benchmarks stayed virtually flat.
  • 02/Brent crude fell ~3% toward $98/barrel on US-Iran peace hopes, nearly $28 below last week's peak.
  • 03/Investors await Friday's US jobs report for Federal Reserve rate-cut cues and fresh risk sentiment.

The Sensex declined 114 points, or 0.15%, to settle at 77,844.52 on May 7, 2026. The Nifty slipped just 4.30 points, or 0.02%, to close at 24,326.65.

The Nifty touched an intraday high of 24,482.10 before profit booking dragged it back. Weekly expiry-driven positioning added to the choppy session.

The Nifty Midcap 100 breached 62,000 for the first time, hitting 62,094 intraday. It closed 1.10% higher, while the Smallcap index gained 0.87%.

"The positive undertone was primarily driven by a sharp cooling in crude oil prices amid optimism surrounding a potential US–Iran peace agreement," said Ajit Mishra, SVP Research at Religare Broking.

Sectorally, the Defence index topped the charts with a 3.30% gain. Auto, Realty and Metals drew buying interest. FMCG, IT and PSU Banks faced selling pressure.

The RBI approved Kotak Mahindra Bank raising its stake to 9.99% each in AU Small Finance Bank and Federal Bank. Bajaj Auto announced a share buyback of up to ₹5,632 crore at ₹12,000 per share.

Brent crude extended losses to trade near $98 per barrel, down roughly 3%. COMEX gold pushed above $4,760 per ounce and silver crossed $80.

Markets await Friday's official US jobs report for labour market cues. Softer ADP payroll data has already reinforced rate-cut hopes.
