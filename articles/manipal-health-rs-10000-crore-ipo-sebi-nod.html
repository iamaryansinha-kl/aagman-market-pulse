Manipal Health Moves Closer to Rs 10,000 Crore IPO After Sebi Approval

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Manipal Health Enterprises has cleared a key regulatory hurdle.

The hospital chain has received approval from the Securities and Exchange Board of India for its Rs 10,000 crore initial public offering. The nod allows the company to proceed with investor roadshows and book-building.

Manipal operates 28 hospitals across 15 cities in India. The chain is known for its clinical excellence and medical education institutions, including the Manipal Academy of Higher Education.

The company's backers include TPG Capital and Singapore's Temasek Holdings. Both investors acquired stakes in Manipal Health in recent years and will partially exit through the offer for sale component.

The IPO structure includes both fresh equity and an offer for sale. The fresh issue will fund hospital expansions, medical equipment purchases, and debt repayment. The OFS will allow existing shareholders to monetise part of their holdings.

At Rs 10,000 crore, the Manipal Health IPO would be among the largest healthcare listings in India. The offering will test investor appetite for hospital chains at a time when the sector is consolidating and expanding into tier-2 cities.
