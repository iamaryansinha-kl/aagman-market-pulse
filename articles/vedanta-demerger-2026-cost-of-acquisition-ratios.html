Vedanta Demerger 2026: Cost of Acquisition Ratios Revealed for All Five Entities

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta announces cost of acquisition ratios: parent company 52.34%, Malco Energy 21.49%, Talwandi Sabo Power 12.23%, Vedanta Aluminium 7.15%, Vedanta Iron & Steel 6.79%
  • 02/For every ₹10,000 invested pre-demerger, ₹5,234 attributed to Vedanta Ltd, ₹2,149 to Malco Energy, ₹1,223 to Talwandi Sabo Power, ₹715 to Vedanta Aluminium, and ₹679 to Vedanta Iron & Steel
  • 03/Nuvama values demerged entities at ₹336 (Vedanta), ₹477 (Aluminium), ₹47 (Oil & Gas), ₹30 (Steel), ₹47 (Power); listings expected mid-June 2026

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) has shared the cost of acquisition apportionment ratios for its five post-demerger entities. The company filed the ratios with stock exchanges on Saturday, clearing a key step for investors tracking tax implications.

Vedanta Ltd will retain 52.34% of the total cost of acquisition. This is the residual entity housing zinc, copper, and ferrochrome operations.

Malco Energy — to be renamed Vedanta Oil & Gas — accounts for 21.49% of the cost. This is the second-largest allocation after the parent company.

Talwandi Sabo Power, set to become Vedanta Power, carries 12.23% of the acquisition cost. The entity operates thermal plants with 4,780 MW combined capacity.

Vedanta Aluminium Metal (VAML) accounts for 7.15% of the cost. Analysts see this as the most valuable demerged business despite the lower cost allocation.

Vedanta Iron & Steel accounts for 6.79% of the total cost. This covers iron ore operations in Karnataka, Goa, Odisha, and the ESL steel business.

For an investor who bought Vedanta shares worth ₹10,000 before the demerger, the cost splits to ₹5,234 for Vedanta Ltd, ₹2,149 for Malco Energy, ₹1,223 for Talwandi Sabo Power, ₹715 for Vedanta Aluminium, and ₹679 for Vedanta Iron & Steel. The total remains ₹10,000.

Nuvama Institutional Equities has published target prices for all five entities. It values Vedanta (zinc and copper) at ₹336 per share, aluminium at ₹477, oil & gas at ₹47, steel and iron ore at ₹30, and power at ₹47 per share.

Vedanta opened at ₹289.50 on NSE after the special pre-open session on April 30. The stock has since gained around 14%, touching an intraday high of ₹329.35 on May 19.

The four new companies are expected to list on BSE and NSE by mid-June 2026. Share transfers began on May 8 and were expected to complete by May 11.

VEDANTA LTD ACCOUNTS FOR 52.34% OF THE TOTAL COST. MALCO ENERGY (VEDANTA OIL & GAS) ACCOUNTS FOR 21.49%, TALWANDI SABO POWER (VEDANTA POWER) FOR 12.23%, VEDANTA ALUMINIUM METAL FOR 7.15%, AND VEDANTA IRON & STEEL FOR 6.79%.

THESE RATIOS HELP INVESTORS CALCULATE CAPITAL GAINS TAX WHEN SELLING EACH ENTITY.

NUVAMA VALUES VEDANTA LTD (ZINC AND COPPER) AT ₹336 PER SHARE, VEDANTA ALUMINIUM AT ₹477 PER SHARE, VEDANTA OIL & GAS AT ₹47 PER SHARE, VEDANTA STEEL & IRON ORE AT ₹30 PER SHARE, AND VEDANTA POWER AT ₹47 PER SHARE.

WHAT IS THE DEMERGER RATIO AND HOW MANY SHARES WILL INVESTORS RECEIVE?
FOR EVERY ONE SHARE OF VEDANTA LTD HELD THROUGH THE RECORD DATE OF MAY 1, 2026, SHAREHOLDERS RECEIVE ONE SHARE EACH IN THE FOUR NEW COMPANIES. THE 1:1 RATIO APPLIES TO ALL FOUR ENTITIES: VEDANTA ALUMINIUM METAL, TALWANDI SABO POWER, MALCO ENERGY, AND VEDANTA IRON & STEEL.
