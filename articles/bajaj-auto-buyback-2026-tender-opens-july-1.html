Bajaj Auto Buyback 2026: ₹5,633 Cr Tender Opens July 1

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Auto's ₹5,633 crore share buyback opens for tender on July 1 and closes on July 7, 2026
  • 02/Buyback price fixed at ₹12,000 per share, a 15-19% premium to market; 46.94 lakh shares (1.68% equity) on offer
  • 03/Record date was June 24, 2026; 15% of offer reserved for small shareholders holding up to ₹2 lakh

Bajaj Auto Ltd (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO) has announced that its ₹5,633 crore share buyback will open for tender on July 1, 2026. The offer will close on July 7, 2026.

The board-approved buyback price is fixed at ₹12,000 per share. This represents a premium of approximately 15-19% over the prevailing market price.

The company will buy back up to 46.94 lakh equity shares with a face value of ₹10 each. This amounts to 1.68% of the company's total paid-up equity share capital.

The buyback is being conducted through the tender offer route. Shareholders holding Bajaj Auto shares in their demat accounts as of the June 24, 2026 record date are eligible to participate.

This marks Bajaj Auto's largest-ever buyback and its second in two years. In 2024, the company bought back shares at ₹10,000 apiece through the same tender route.

As of March 2026, Bajaj Auto had consolidated paid-up equity capital and free reserves of ₹36,128 crore. The board has confirmed the buyback will not adversely affect the company's financial position.

The company also declared a dividend of ₹150 per share (1,500% on face value) for FY26. The total payout, including buyback and dividend, underscores strong cash flow generation.

For Q4 FY26, Bajaj Auto reported a standalone net profit of ₹2,746 crore, up 34% YoY. Revenue from operations rose 32% YoY to ₹16,006 crore.

Brokerage views are mixed. Jefferies retained a "Hold" rating with a target of ₹10,500, while Morgan Stanley maintained "Underweight" with a target of ₹9,259. HSBC raised its target to ₹11,300.

Eligible shareholders can tender shares through their stockbrokers in demat form. Physical shareholders can also participate per the letter of offer procedures. KFin Technologies is the registrar.

15% of the buyback is reserved for small shareholders holding shares worth ₹2 lakh or less as of the record date. Historically, this category sees higher acceptance ratios.

Investors should note that capital gains tax at 12.5% (long-term) or 20% (short-term) will apply on gains from accepted shares. The buyback itself does not change the company's core business outlook.

WHAT IS THE BUYBACK PRICE AND SIZE OF THE BAJAJ AUTO BUYBACK 2026?
THE BUYBACK PRICE IS FIXED AT ₹12,000 PER EQUITY SHARE. THE TOTAL BUYBACK SIZE IS ₹5,633 CRORE, COVERING UP TO 46.94 LAKH SHARES REPRESENTING 1.68% OF THE TOTAL PAID-UP EQUITY CAPITAL.
WHEN DOES THE BAJAJ AUTO BUYBACK OPEN AND CLOSE FOR TENDER?
THE TENDER OFFER OPENS ON JULY 1, 2026, AND CLOSES ON JULY 7, 2026. ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS MUST TENDER THEIR SHARES THROUGH THEIR STOCKBROKERS DURING THIS WINDOW.
WHAT IS THE RECORD DATE FOR THE BAJAJ AUTO BUYBACK?
THE RECORD DATE IS FIXED AT JUNE 24, 2026. ONLY SHAREHOLDERS HOLDING BAJAJ AUTO SHARES IN THEIR DEMAT OR PHYSICAL FORM AS OF THIS DATE ARE ELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE IN THE BUYBACK.
HOW CAN SHAREHOLDERS PARTICIPATE IN THE BAJAJ AUTO BUYBACK?
ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS CAN TENDER SHARES THROUGH THEIR STOCKBROKERS IN DEMAT FORM. PHYSICAL SHAREHOLDERS MUST SUBMIT ORIGINAL SHARE CERTIFICATES AND SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS TO THEIR BROKER. KFIN TECHNOLOGIES IS THE REGISTRAR FOR THE BUYBACK.
WHAT ARE THE TAX IMPLICATIONS FOR INVESTORS PARTICIPATING IN THE BUYBACK?
CAPITAL GAINS TAX AT 12.5% FOR LONG-TERM HOLDINGS AND 20% FOR SHORT-TERM HOLDINGS WILL APPLY ON THE GAINS FROM ACCEPTED SHARES. INVESTORS SHOULD CONSULT A TAX ADVISOR TO UNDERSTAND THEIR SPECIFIC LIABILITY.
