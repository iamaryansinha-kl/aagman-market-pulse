NSE Chief Chauhan Urges Firms to Prioritize Growth Over Share Prices

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE MD and CEO Ashish Chauhan urges companies to build sustainable businesses and focus on profitability rather than chasing share prices
  • 02/A company earning Rs 2 crore annual profit could command Rs 40-50 crore market capitalisation once listed on public markets
  • 03/Chauhan cites Infosys ESOP strategy as example of how listing unlocks capital and talent acquisition opportunities

National Stock Exchange of India Ltd (NSE) MD and CEO Ashish Chauhan has urged companies to prioritize building sustainable businesses. He said firms should focus on improving profitability and creating long-term value rather than merely chasing higher share prices.

Speaking at the 9th JITO Incubation & Innovation Foundation (JIIF) Day event on June 27, 2026, Chauhan addressed entrepreneurs and startup founders. He emphasized that a company's market valuation should reflect genuine business performance and growth.

"If the company's profit increases, the share value should increase. You cannot keep increasing value without creating actual business growth," Chauhan said. He advised entrepreneurs to remain focused on their core business rather than getting influenced by market trends or peer pressure.

Companies that consistently grow and deliver results eventually get recognised by the market, he added. The public markets reward profitable businesses with a valuation that private balance sheets cannot match.

A company earning an annual profit of Rs 2 crore could command a market capitalisation of Rs 40 to 50 crore once listed, Chauhan said. This gives promoters room to raise capital, bring in partners and expand operations.

Listing also gives a company its own currency, he noted. A listed promoter can use stock to acquire other businesses, draw in partners and reward staff through stock options. He cited Infosys founders NR Narayana Murthy and Nandan Nilekani's early use of employee stock options to attract talent.

Chauhan highlighted that innovation is not limited to major technological breakthroughs. "Whatever you do, if you do it differently and in a better way, that is also innovation," he said.

On the listing journey, Chauhan said getting listed is not as difficult as often perceived. But companies need to focus on compliance, governance and transparency after entering public markets.

CHAUHAN ADVISED THAT COMPANIES NEED TO FOCUS ON COMPLIANCE, GOVERNANCE AND TRANSPARENCY AFTER ENTERING PUBLIC MARKETS. GETTING LISTED IS NOT AS DIFFICULT AS OFTEN PERCEIVED, BUT MAINTAINING DISCIPLINE POST-LISTING IS CRITICAL.
