ITC Q4 Results: Cigarette Margin Squeeze Drags Overall Profitability

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ITC standalone EBITDA margin fell to 50% from 61% YoY as cigarette business faced tax-driven margin pressure
  • 02/FMCG margin improved to 8.3% from 6.3% but could not offset cigarette weakness; agri margin dropped to 5.8%
  • 03/Stock down 24% in 2026 trading at 18x P/E below 23-24x historical average; near-term outlook tied to tax impact

ITC Ltd reported margin pressure in the March quarter (Q4 FY2026). The standalone EBITDA margin on a gross revenue basis dropped to 50% from 61% in the year-ago period.

The cigarette business, which accounts for nearly half of revenue, bore the brunt of the damage. Sharp tax hikes increased costs and squeezed profitability in the core segment.

Other divisions showed improvement but could not fully compensate. FMCG segment margin expanded to 8.3% from 6.3% in the same quarter last year.

The agri business margin contracted to 5.8% from 7% during the period. US tariffs, climate-related supply issues, and West Asia-led logistical disruptions weighed on the segment.

The company is implementing pricing and portfolio tactics to manage the tax impact. It has taken multiple steps in recent months to strengthen its portfolio and protect market share.

Revenue growth and profitability in the near term remain exposed to the risk of higher illicit cigarette trade. Illicit trade already makes up nearly one-third of the legal market in India.

ITC shares have dropped nearly 24% so far in 2026. The stock trades at a price-to-earnings multiple of around 18x, below its three- and five-year average of 23-24x.

While valuations appear attractive, the stock is likely to remain under pressure until there is more clarity on how the company navigates these challenges and whether its strategies deliver results.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAPPENED TO ITC'S MARGINS IN THE MARCH QUARTER?
ITC'S STANDALONE EBITDA MARGIN ON GROSS REVENUE DROPPED TO 50% IN Q4 FY2026 FROM 61% IN THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER. THE CIGARETTE BUSINESS, WHICH CONTRIBUTES NEARLY HALF OF REVENUE, FACED SIGNIFICANT MARGIN PRESSURE DUE TO RECENT TAX INCREASES.
WHY DID ITC'S CIGARETTE BUSINESS SEE WEAKER PROFITS?
SHARP TAX HIKES ON CIGARETTES INCREASED COSTS AND SQUEEZED MARGINS IN THE CORE CIGARETTE SEGMENT. THE COMPANY STATED THAT STEEP TAX INCREASES HAVE HISTORICALLY FUELLED ILLICIT TRADE, WHICH ALREADY ACCOUNTS FOR NEARLY ONE-THIRD OF THE LEGAL MARKET IN INDIA.
DID ANY OTHER ITC SEGMENTS PERFORM WELL?
YES. THE FMCG SEGMENT MARGIN EXPANDED TO 8.3% FROM 6.3% YEAR-ON-YEAR. PAPERBOARDS ALSO SHOWED IMPROVED PROFITABILITY. HOWEVER, THESE SEGMENTS ARE SMALLER IN SIZE AND COULD NOT FULLY OFFSET THE DECLINE IN CIGARETTE MARGINS.
WHAT HAPPENED TO ITC'S AGRI BUSINESS?
THE AGRI SEGMENT MARGIN CONTRACTED TO 5.8% FROM 7% DURING THE PERIOD. THIS WAS DUE TO US TARIFFS, CLIMATE-RELATED SUPPLY ISSUES, WEST ASIA-LED LOGISTICAL DISRUPTIONS, AND DOMESTIC STOCK LIMITS AND EXPORT CURBS ON KEY COMMODITIES.
HOW HAS ITC'S STOCK PERFORMED AND WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK?
ITC SHARES HAVE FALLEN NEARLY 24% SO FAR IN 2026. THE STOCK TRADES AT A P/E OF AROUND 18X, BELOW ITS THREE- AND FIVE-YEAR AVERAGE OF 23-24X. WHILE VALUATIONS APPEAR ATTRACTIVE, THE STOCK IS LIKELY TO REMAIN UNDER PRESSURE UNTIL THERE IS MORE CLARITY ON HOW THE COMPANY NAVIGATES TAX-RELATED CHALLENGES AND WHETHER ITS PRICING AND PORTFOLIO STRATEGIES DELIVER RESULTS.
