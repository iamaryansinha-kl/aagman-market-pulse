Quick Commerce India to Hit $65-70 Billion by 2030: Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's quick commerce sector has doubled annually over the last two years, according to a new report
  • 02/The market is projected to reach $65-70 billion by 2030, driven by structural advantages
  • 03/Key growth drivers include high population density, low labor costs, and low online grocery penetration

India's quick commerce market has doubled annually over the past two years. A new report projects the sector will touch $65-70 billion by 2030.

The growth is supported by structural factors unique to the Indian market. High population density creates favorable conditions for quick delivery models.

Low labor and real estate costs further strengthen the unit economics. These factors allow platforms to operate with lower overheads compared to global peers.

Low online grocery penetration represents a significant headroom for expansion. Most Indian households still rely on traditional kirana stores for daily needs.

The quick commerce model has gained traction in major metro cities. Platforms now compete on delivery speed and product assortment.

The $65-70 billion projection signals strong investor interest in the sector. However, profitability remains a key challenge for most players.
