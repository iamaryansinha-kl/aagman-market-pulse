Varun Beverages Q1CY26 Profit Jumps 20% to ₹878.7 Crore; Revenue Up 18%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Varun Beverages Q1CY26 net profit climbs 20.1% YoY to ₹878.7 crore on revenue of ₹6,574.2 crore, up 18.1%
  • 02/Consolidated volumes rose 16.3% to 36.34 crore cases; gross margins expanded 62 bps to 55.2% on early raw material procurement
  • 03/Board declared interim dividend of ₹0.50 per share with record date May 1, 2026; stock trades at ₹501.40, up 2.23%

Varun Beverages Limited (NSE: VBL) reported strong first-quarter results for January–March 2026. Net revenue rose 18.1% year-on-year to ₹6,574.2 crore. Profit after tax climbed 20.1% to ₹878.7 crore, up from ₹731.4 crore in Q1CY25.

EBITDA grew 21% to ₹1,528.9 crore, with margins improving 55 basis points to 23.3%. Gross margins expanded 62 basis points to 55.2%, aided by early procurement of raw materials despite an inflationary environment. India EBITDA margins improved 112 basis points, driven by volume efficiencies.

Consolidated sales volumes rose 16.3% to 36.34 crore cases. India volumes grew 14.4%, while international territories expanded faster at 21.4%. Per-case realization in India dipped 1.5% as the company pursued volume-led initiatives including pack upsizing and targeted price-point launches.

At the consolidated level, realization per case improved 1.6%, boosted by favourable currency movements in international markets. Low-sugar and no-sugar products now account for roughly 63% of consolidated sales volumes, reflecting a continued portfolio shift toward healthier variants.

The quarter's most significant strategic development was the completion of the Twizza acquisition in South Africa through subsidiary BevCo, at an enterprise value of ZAR 205.3 crore, effective March 18, 2026. VBL also signed an agreement to acquire Crickley Dairy in South Africa for approximately ZAR 23.8 crore, pending regulatory approvals.

Depreciation rose 30.9% due to new plants commissioned last year in Buxar, Prayagraj, Damtal, and Meghalaya. Finance costs increased 18% on account of the Twizza acquisition financing.

The board approved an interim dividend of ₹0.50 per share, representing 25% of face value. The total cash outflow amounts to approximately ₹169.1 crore. The record date is fixed at May 1, 2026.

The stock responded positively to the results, trading at ₹501.40 on April 27, up 2.23%, with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹1.69 lakh crore. Motilal Oswal has a Buy rating with a target price of ₹550, while the consensus 12-month target stands at ₹537.85 based on 26 analyst estimates.
