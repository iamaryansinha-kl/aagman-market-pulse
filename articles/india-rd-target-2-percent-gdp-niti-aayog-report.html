India R&D Target 2% of GDP: Niti Aayog Report Calls for Major Boost

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Niti Aayog recommends increasing India's R&D investment from 0.64% to 2% of GDP within four to five years
  • 02/Report suggests restoring 5% GST slab for R&D procurement and fiscal incentives to boost private sector spending
  • 03/Current R&D funding is heavily dependent on public sources with limited private sector and philanthropic participation

Niti Aayog has urged India to increase R&D investment from 0.64% to 2% of GDP. The target should be achieved within four to five years.

The recommendation comes from a report titled "Ease of Doing Research & Development in India - Removing Obstacles, Promoting Enablers". It was released on Monday.

The report suggests restoring a 5% GST slab for R&D procurement. It also calls for time-bound fiscal incentives to encourage private sector R&D spending.

Adding R&D expenditure reporting under Schedule III of the Companies Act 2013 is recommended. This would improve data on private sector R&D investment.

The report recommends strengthening CSR provisions under the Companies Act. It also suggests at least 125% tax deduction for individual R&D donations under the Income Tax Act.

India's R&D funding remains heavily dependent on public sources. Private sector and philanthropic participation is limited.

Researchers face complex application processes and long delays in fund release. Rigid financial rules and cumbersome procurement constrain effective use of resources.

India's researcher density remains low despite a large talent pool. The postdoctoral ecosystem is underdeveloped and many institutional positions remain unfilled.
