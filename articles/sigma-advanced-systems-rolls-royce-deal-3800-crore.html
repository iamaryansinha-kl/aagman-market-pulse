Sigma Advanced Systems Rolls-Royce: ₹3,800 Cr, 7-Yr Aerospace Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sigma Advanced Systems signed a seven-year agreement with Rolls-Royce worth ₹3,800 crore (~£300 million) for aerospace components on April 27, 2026.
  • 02/The stock hit its upper circuit limit of ₹248.55 on BSE, up 4.98% from the previous close of ₹236.75, marking a new 52-week high.
  • 03/The deal provides multi-year revenue visibility and deepens Sigma's role in Rolls-Royce's global aerospace supply chain via its India-UK dual-source model.

Sigma Advanced Systems has signed a seven-year agreement with Rolls-Royce valued at ₹3,800 crore. The pact was announced on April 27, 2026, through an official company release.

Under the deal, Sigma will supply high-precision-engineered, safety-critical components and assemblies for Rolls-Royce's aerospace programs. Deliveries will flow through the company's manufacturing network across India and the UK.

The agreement is a program-level partnership, not a simple component order. It reflects Sigma's shift from a location-specific supplier to an integrated global manufacturing platform.

"This partnership with Rolls-Royce reflects how our strategy is taking shape. It validates the investments we have made in building a connected India–UK platform," Sunil Kumar Kalidindi, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director at Sigma Advanced Systems, said in the release.

Sigma shares hit the upper circuit on BSE at ₹248.55 on April 27. The stock gained 4.98% from its previous close of ₹236.75, touching a fresh 52-week high.

The company's market capitalisation stood at ₹4,380.44 crore after the announcement. Promoters hold 71.22% of the equity.

The stock has surged over 310% from its 52-week low of ₹60.53, recorded on May 9, 2025. The one-week high and low stood at ₹248.55 and ₹145.40 respectively.
