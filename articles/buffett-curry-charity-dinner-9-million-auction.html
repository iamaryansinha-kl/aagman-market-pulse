Warren Buffett-Stephen Curry Charity Dinner Auction Hits $9 Million Record Bid

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/A charity dinner with Warren Buffett and Stephen Curry fetched a record $9,000,100 winning bid on eBay
  • 02/Buffett will match the winning bid for both non-profits, pushing total charitable donations near $27 million
  • 03/The private dinner takes place June 24 in Omaha, supporting Glide Foundation and Eat. Learn. Play.

A charity auction offering dinner with Warren Buffett and Stephen Curry closed at $9,000,100 on eBay. The winning bidder remains unidentified.

The winner and up to seven guests will dine privately on June 24 in Omaha, Nebraska. Buffett, Stephen Curry, and Ayesha Curry will attend the event.

Proceeds will be split between Glide Foundation and Eat. Learn. Play. Both organizations focus on vulnerable communities and childhood development programmes.

Buffett agreed to match the winning bid for each non-profit. This pushes the total charitable contribution linked to the event to nearly $27 million.

The 95-year-old Oracle of Omaha built Berkshire Hathaway into a global conglomerate. Forbes estimates his current fortune at approximately $143.5 billion.

Curry is a four-time NBA champion and two-time league MVP. He founded Eat. Learn. Play. with wife Ayesha to support nutrition, literacy, and physical activity.

Buffett previously raised about $53.2 million for Glide through 21 charity lunch auctions from 2000 to 2022. The previous record bid was $19 million in 2022.

This marks one of the largest celebrity-driven fundraising events globally. It reflects Buffett's long-standing belief that businesses and charities can create wider social impact.
