Rupee Falls 11 Paise to 94.27 Against US Dollar in Fifth Straight Session

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The rupee fell 11 paise to 94.27 against the US dollar on April 27, 2026, marking its fifth straight session of decline.
  • 02/The fall is driven by persistent dollar demand, rising Brent crude at $106.55 per barrel, and FII selling of ₹8,827.87 crore on Friday.
  • 03/India's forex reserves stand at $703 billion as of April 17, providing a cushion against further volatility.

The Indian rupee depreciated 11 paise to 94.27 against the US dollar in early trade on Monday, April 27, 2026. The currency opened at 94.25 and touched 94.27, down from Friday's close of 94.16.

This marks the fifth consecutive session of rupee decline. Forex traders attribute the weakness to persistent dollar demand and a broader shift toward safe-haven assets amid geopolitical uncertainty.

The Reserve Bank of India has loosened its grip on currency management, allowing market forces greater influence. This policy shift has contributed to the recent volatility in the rupee's movement.

Brent crude, India's primary import, was trading 1.16 per cent higher at $106.55 per barrel. Rising oil prices widen India's trade deficit and increase dollar demand for energy payments.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded Indian equities worth ₹8,827.87 crore on Friday, according to exchange data. The renewed outflows follow a brief period of buying that had offered temporary relief to the currency.

"For now, the rupee continues to lean toward gradual weakness. Uncertainty remains the dominant force," said Amit Pabari, Managing Director at CR Forex Advisors. He noted that the 92.80–93.20 zone acts as strong support, while 93.50 to 94.50 defines the near-term range.

India's forex reserves crossed $703 billion as of April 17, reflecting consistent buffer build-up. This provides the RBI with adequate firepower to intervene if depreciation becomes disorderly.

Meanwhile, the dollar index was down 0.09 per cent at 98.44, offering slight relief. The 30-share Sensex was trading 0.68 per cent higher at 77,183.17, while the Nifty gained 0.55 per cent to 24,029.25.
