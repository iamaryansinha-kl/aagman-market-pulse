Kissht Founders Invest Rs 40 Crore at Rs 201/Share Before Rs 926-Crore IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kissht founders Ranvir Singh and Krishnan Vishwanathan invest nearly Rs 40 crore at Rs 201 per share, above the IPO price band of Rs 162-171
  • 02/The Rs 926-crore IPO opens on April 30 and closes on May 5; listing expected on BSE and NSE on May 8
  • 03/Promoters hold 32.30% aggregate stake; AUM surged from Rs 1,267 crore in FY23 to Rs 5,955 crore in 9M FY26

Promoters of OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd, which operates digital lending platform Kissht, have infused nearly Rs 40 crore into the company ahead of its initial public offering. Founders Ranvir Singh and Krishnan Vishwanathan invested at Rs 201 per share.

The investment price of Rs 201 per share sits above the IPO price band of Rs 162-171. This signals strong confidence in the business from the founders themselves.

According to the Red Herring Prospectus, the promoters hold an aggregate 32.30 per cent stake in the company. This pre-IPO infusion further reinforces their commitment ahead of the public listing.

The Rs 926-crore IPO comprises a fresh issue of Rs 850 crore and an offer-for-sale of 44,39,788 equity shares worth Rs 76 crore at the upper end. The issue opens on April 30 and closes on May 5.

Kissht has scaled up sharply in recent years. Assets under management rose from about Rs 1,267 crore in FY23 to Rs 5,955 crore in the first nine months of FY26.

Revenue from operations increased from Rs 984 crore to Rs 1,675 crore during the same period. Profit after tax grew from Rs 27.7 crore in FY23 to Rs 197 crore in FY24.

Proceeds from the fresh issue will augment the capital base of subsidiary Si Creva. The remaining funds will be used for general corporate purposes.

The shares are expected to list on the BSE and NSE on May 8. Market participants said founder participation at a premium reflects confidence in the company's intrinsic value.
