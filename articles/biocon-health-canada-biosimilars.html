Biocon Gets Health Canada NOC for Bosaya and Vevzuo Biosimilars

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Biocon Ltd secures Health Canada NOC for two denosumab biosimilars — Bosaya (Prolia) and Vevzuo (Xgeva) — on April 3, 2026
  • 02/The approvals could benefit over 2 million adults with osteoporosis and hundreds annually with cancer-related bone metastasis
  • 03/Biocon has now commercialized 12 biosimilar products globally with 20+ assets in its pipeline; watch for revenue contribution from Canada

Biocon Limited (NSE: BIOCON; BSE: 532523) has received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for two denosumab biosimilars. The approvals were granted on April 3, 2026, the company disclosed in a regulatory filing on April 21, 2026.

Bosaya™ (denosumab) is a biosimilar to Prolia®, approved as a 60 mg/mL injection in a prefilled syringe. It treats osteoporosis in postmenopausal women and men at high fracture risk, and increases bone mass in cancer patients on hormone therapy.

Vevzuo™ (denosumab) is a biosimilar to Xgeva®, approved as a 120 mg/1.7 mL injection in a single-dose vial. It reduces skeletal-related events in patients with multiple myeloma and bone metastases from solid tumors.

The approval rests on analytical, nonclinical, and clinical data showing no clinically meaningful differences from the reference products. Biocon said the two biosimilars are highly similar to Prolia and Xgeva in quality, safety, and efficacy.

Over 2 million adults in Canada live with osteoporosis. Hundreds more face bone metastasis annually, a common complication of advanced cancer. Denosumab products increase bone mass by blocking RANKL, the protein driving bone resorption.

Shreehas Tambe, CEO & Managing Director of Biocon Ltd, said the approval reflects strong scientific and regulatory capabilities. He added that it reinforces Biocon's commitment to affordable biologic therapies across immunology and oncology.

Biocon has commercialized 12 biosimilar products globally to date. Its pipeline holds 20+ biosimilar assets along with GLP-1 peptides and complex generics. The company operates in over 120 countries with 7 manufacturing sites.

Biocon shares traded at ₹362.45 on the NSE on April 21, 2026, up 0.95%. The stock has declined approximately 15% from its 52-week high of ₹423.05 in November 2025. Revenue contribution from the Canadian launches will be tracked in upcoming quarterly filings.
