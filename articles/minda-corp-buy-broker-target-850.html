Broker's Call: Minda Corp (Buy) — Target Price ₹850

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Leading brokerage firm initiates coverage on Minda Corp with a Buy rating and target price of ₹850 per share
  • 02/Target implies 18% upside from current market price of approximately ₹720
  • 03/Key investment triggers include EV component orders, margin expansion, and export growth

A leading domestic brokerage has initiated coverage on Minda Corporation Ltd with a Buy rating. The firm has set a target price of ₹850 per share.

The target price represents an 18% upside from the stock's current market price of around ₹720. The brokerage sees multiple growth levers over the next 12-18 months.

Minda Corp is a key supplier of auto components to original equipment manufacturers. Its product portfolio includes switches, controllers, and lighting systems for two-wheelers and passenger vehicles.

The brokerage highlighted Minda's expanding electric vehicle component orders as a key trigger. EV-related revenue is expected to grow at 35% annually through FY2028.

Operating margin expansion is another catalyst. The brokerage expects EBITDA margins to improve from 12.5% in FY2025 to 14.2% by FY2027.

Export revenues currently contribute 18% of total sales. The company is targeting a 25% export share by FY2027 through new customer additions in Europe and North America.

The stock has gained 34% over the past year. It trades at a P/E multiple of 22x FY2026 estimated earnings.

"Minda Corp is well-positioned to benefit from the domestic auto upcycle and the global shift toward electrification," the brokerage note stated.
