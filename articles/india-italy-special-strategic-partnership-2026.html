India-Italy Ties Upgraded to Special Strategic Partnership, Key MoUs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PM Modi visited Italy on May 19-20, 2026, and both nations elevated ties to a Special Strategic Partnership
  • 02/Multiple MoUs signed across defence, critical minerals, maritime, education, culture, and mobility
  • 03/Bilateral trade target set at Euro 20 billion by 2029; 2027 declared Year of Culture

PM Modi visited Italy on May 19-20, 2026, at the invitation of Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni. This was his first bilateral visit to Italy.

The two leaders elevated the India-Italy Strategic Partnership to a Special Strategic Partnership. They signed multiple MoUs across defence, critical technologies, research, and connectivity.

A Defence Industrial Roadmap was adopted to boost co-production in aerospace, naval platforms, and next-generation defence technologies. Both sides condemned terrorism in all forms, including cross-border terrorism.

An MoU on critical minerals and a decision to set up an innovation centre in India were signed. Both nations also signed an MoU for cooperation in maritime transport and ports.

India and Italy set a bilateral trade target of Euro 20 billion by 2029. The India-EU FTA concluded earlier this year provided a foundation for this goal.

The year 2027 was declared as the Year of Culture between India and Italy. Prime Minister Modi also received the Agricola Award at FAO, the first such visit by an Indian head of government in 30 years.

A Joint Declaration of Intent on nurse mobility and a roadmap on higher education were signed. A virtual trade corridor and early IMEC implementation were also discussed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID INDIA AND ITALY ANNOUNCE DURING PM MODI'S VISIT TO ITALY ON MAY 19-20, 2026?
INDIA AND ITALY ELEVATED THEIR STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO A SPECIAL STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP. THEY SIGNED MULTIPLE MOUs IN DEFENCE, CRITICAL TECHNOLOGIES, CRITICAL MINERALS, MARITIME TRANSPORT, AGRICULTURE, EDUCATION, CULTURE, AND MOBILITY.
WHAT IS THE DEFENCE INDUSTRIAL ROADMAP BETWEEN INDIA AND ITALY?
THE DEFENCE INDUSTRIAL ROADMAP WAS ADOPTED TO PAVE THE WAY FOR CO-PRODUCTION OF DEFENCE EQUIPMENT, TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP, AND DESIGN COLLABORATION. IT FOCUSES ON AEROSPACE SYSTEMS, NAVAL PLATFORMS, AND NEXT-GENERATION DEFENCE TECHNOLOGIES.
WHAT IS THE BILATERAL TRADE TARGET SET BY INDIA AND ITALY?
BOTH COUNTRIES SET A TARGET TO INCREASE BILATERAL TRADE TO EURO 20 BILLION BY 2029. THIS WAS BOOSTED BY THE CONCLUSION OF THE INDIA-EU FTA NEGOTIATIONS EARLIER THIS YEAR.
WHAT AGREEMENTS WERE SIGNED ON CULTURE AND EDUCATION?
THE YEAR 2027 WAS DECLARED AS THE YEAR OF CULTURE BETWEEN INDIA AND ITALY. A JOINT DECLARATION OF INTENT ON FACILITATION OF MOBILITY OF NURSES AND A ROADMAP ON HIGHER EDUCATION AND RESEARCH WERE ALSO SIGNED.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE AGRICOLA AWARD GIVEN TO PM MODI?
PRIME MINISTER MODI WAS CONFERRED THE AGRICOLA AWARD BY THE FAO DIRECTOR GENERAL FOR HIS EXCEPTIONAL CONTRIBUTION TO FOOD SECURITY AND SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE. THIS WAS THE FIRST VISIT BY AN INDIAN HEAD OF GOVERNMENT TO THE FAO IN 30 YEARS.
