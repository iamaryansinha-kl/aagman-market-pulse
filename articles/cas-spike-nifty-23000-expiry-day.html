Nifty CAS Spike: 300-Point Surge in a Minute Crosses 23,000 on Expiry

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty jumped more than 300 points in under a minute at the start of the Closing Auction Session on October 6, briefly crossing 23,000 on the weekly expiry day.
  • 02/The index surged from 22,717.70 at 3:19:59 pm to 23,026 at 3:20:30 pm, then pared gains to close at 22,776, the day's high excluding CAS levels.
  • 03/Jefferies data shows September index options premium ADTO recovered to Rs 68,100 crore, broadly in line with July, while Sebi's CAS review decision is expected by month-end.

The Nifty 50 spiked more than 300 points in under a minute on Tuesday, October 6. The surge came within the first seconds of the 20-minute Closing Auction Session (CAS) on the weekly expiry day.

At 3:19:59 pm, the Nifty stood at 22,717.70. Roughly 30 seconds later, at 3:20:30 pm, it had jumped about 2% to 23,026, briefly crossing the psychologically important 23,000 mark.

The move did not hold. The index pared most of the spike and settled at 22,776, still the day's high once CAS levels are excluded. The Sensex traced the same pattern, hitting 73,259 before closing at 73,068, 125 points above its pre-CAS level.

Sebi introduced the CAS on August 3 for stocks in the futures and options segment: during the 20-minute window, buy and sell orders are collected and matched at a single equilibrium price. Built to improve price discovery, it has instead produced repeated expiry-day swings.

That persistent volatility is now pushing the regulator toward a partial reversal. According to a Reuters report, Sebi is likely to stop using closing auctions for derivatives settlement prices for at least one year.

Settlement would instead revert to the volume-weighted average price (VWAP) of the last 30 minutes of trading. The auction would still set end-of-day prices for underlying stocks in the less liquid cash market. Changes are likely to be announced by the end of October.

Options volumes, meanwhile, are recovering. Jefferies data shows industry-wide September index options premium ADTO, or average daily turnover, at Rs 68,100 crore, broadly in line with July levels. Sebi separately said it received 20,000 comments on its consultation covering CAS, market timings and settlement methodology.

Jefferies said issues related to CAS may finally be easing. The brokerage named Groww, MCX, KFIN and Nuvama as its top capital market picks, flagging Groww as a potential beneficiary of order-volume recovery, MTF book pick-up and a rise in September commodity trading.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT EXACTLY HAPPENED TO THE NIFTY IN THE CLOSING AUCTION ON OCTOBER 6?
AT 3:19:59 PM, THE NIFTY STOOD AT 22,717.70. WITHIN 30 SECONDS OF THE CLOSING AUCTION SESSION OPENING AT 3:20 PM, THE INDEX SPIKED ABOUT 2% TO 23,026, BRIEFLY CROSSING THE 23,000 MARK ON THE WEEKLY EXPIRY DAY. IT PARED MOST OF THE MOVE AND CLOSED AT 22,776, STILL THE DAY'S HIGH EXCLUDING CAS LEVELS. THE SENSEX ROSE TO 73,259 IN THE SAME WINDOW BEFORE CLOSING AT 73,068, 125 POINTS ABOVE ITS PRE-CAS LEVEL.
WHY DOES THE CAS CREATE SUCH LARGE EXPIRY-DAY SWINGS?
DURING THE 20-MINUTE CLOSING AUCTION SESSION, ALL BUY AND SELL ORDERS ARE COLLECTED AND MATCHED AT A SINGLE EQUILIBRIUM PRICE. ON WEEKLY EXPIRY DAYS, LARGE DERIVATIVES POSITIONS UNWIND INTO THIS ORDER-DRIVEN WINDOW, WHICH AMPLIFIES MOVES. SEBI INTRODUCED THE MECHANISM ON AUGUST 3 TO IMPROVE PRICE DISCOVERY IN F&O STOCKS, BUT TUESDAY'S 300-POINT JUMP IN UNDER A MINUTE SHOWS THE EXPIRY-DAY VOLATILITY IS PERSISTING DESPITE THAT INTENT.
WHAT CHANGE TO DERIVATIVES SETTLEMENT IS SEBI CONSIDERING?
ACCORDING TO A REUTERS REPORT, SEBI IS LIKELY TO STOP USING CLOSING AUCTIONS FOR DERIVATIVES SETTLEMENT PRICES FOR AT LEAST ONE YEAR. SETTLEMENT WOULD INSTEAD REVERT TO THE VWAP, OR VOLUME-WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE, OF THE LAST 30 MINUTES OF TRADING. THE CLOSING AUCTION WOULD STILL SET END-OF-DAY PRICES FOR UNDERLYING STOCKS IN THE LESS LIQUID CASH MARKET. THE CHANGES ARE LIKELY TO BE ANNOUNCED BY THE END OF OCTOBER.
ARE OPTIONS VOLUMES RECOVERING AFTER THE AUGUST CAS DISRUPTION?
YES. JEFFERIES DATA SHOWS SEPTEMBER INDEX OPTIONS PREMIUM ADTO, OR AVERAGE DAILY TURNOVER, AT RS 68,100 CRORE. THAT IS BROADLY IN LINE WITH JULY LEVELS FROM BEFORE THE CAS WAS INTRODUCED, INDICATING THE POST-AUGUST DROP IN ACTIVITY HAS REVERSED. JEFFERIES SAID ISSUES RELATED TO THE CAS MAY FINALLY BE EASING.
WHAT IS JEFFERIES' VIEW ON CAPITAL MARKET STOCKS?
JEFFERIES NAMED GROWW, MCX, KFIN AND NUVAMA AS ITS TOP CAPITAL MARKET PICKS. THE BROKERAGE FLAGGED GROWW AS A POTENTIAL BENEFICIARY OF AN ORDER-VOLUME RECOVERY, MTF BOOK PICK-UP AND A RISE IN COMMODITY TRADING IN SEPTEMBER, ALL TIED TO THE NORMALISATION OF TRADING ACTIVITY AFTER THE CAS DISRUPTION. GLOSSARY: CLOSING AUCTION SESSION (CAS): The 20-minute end-of-day window for F&O stocks in which orders are collected and matched at a single equilibrium price. ADTO (AVERAGE DAILY TURNOVER): The average value of trades per day over a period, used to measure market activity levels. VWAP (VOLUME-WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE): The average price of a security weighted by traded volume; Sebi is weighing a return to a 30-minute VWAP for derivatives settlement. WEEKLY EXPIRY: The day each week when short-term derivative contracts settle, typically associated with heavier trading and sharper price moves. EQUILIBRIUM PRICE: The single price at which the maximum number of buy and sell orders match in an auction.
