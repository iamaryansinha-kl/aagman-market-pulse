Q4 Results Today Live: IOB Profit Jumps 43%, GRSE Rallies 16% On Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian Overseas Bank Q4 net profit surges 43% y-o-y to ₹1,505.45 crore for FY26 fourth quarter
  • 02/GRSE shares jump 16% after Q4 profit rises 24.1% to ₹303.19 crore; Sterlite Tech swings to ₹33 crore profit
  • 03/Adani Power, Bajaj Finance, Waaree Energies, Vedanta and Federal Bank results expected later today

Multiple companies announced Q4 FY2026 earnings on April 29, 2026. Indian Overseas Bank led PSU banks with a sharp profit jump.

Indian Overseas Bank reported standalone net profit of ₹1,505.45 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 43% increase from ₹1,051.07 crore in Q4 FY25.

For the full year, IOB's PAT stood at ₹5,208.03 crore against ₹3,334.71 crore in FY25. The stock rose 2% to ₹35.54 on the NSE.

Granules India posted Q4 standalone net profit of ₹98.9 crore. This compares to ₹94 crore in the same quarter last year.

The pharmaceutical company's full-year PAT increased to ₹400.6 crore from ₹319.33 crore in FY25. Shares traded up 2% at ₹716.50.

"We delivered a strong performance in Q4FY26, driven by continued portfolio expansion and disciplined execution," said Dr. Krishna Prasad Chigurupati, Chairman & MD of Granules India.

Bansal Wire Industries reported a 12% rise in Q4 standalone net profit to ₹33.62 crore. The company's FY26 PAT stood at ₹129.74 crore versus ₹125.16 crore in FY25.

Sterlite Technologies swung to a standalone net profit of ₹33 crore in Q4 FY26. It had posted an ₹8 crore loss in the year-ago quarter.

The company's full-year PAT was ₹2 crore compared to a ₹127 crore loss in FY25. The board approved raising up to ₹2,000 crore via private placement.

CEAT shares rallied 12% after posting a two-fold jump in Q4 consolidated net profit to ₹243.8 crore. GRSE shares surged 16% as Q4 profit rose 24.1% to ₹303.19 crore.

Adani Power, Bajaj Finance, Waaree Energies, Vedanta, Federal Bank, Indian Bank, KFin Tech, and Motilal Oswal are scheduled to announce results later in the day.
