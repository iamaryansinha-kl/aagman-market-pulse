Rupee Falls 13 Paise to 94.81 Against US Dollar in Early Trade

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee depreciates 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on April 29, 2026, after falling 53 paise the previous session
  • 02/Elevated Brent crude at $111.03 per barrel and month-end dollar demand weigh on the currency amid safe-haven flows
  • 03/Focus shifts to US Federal Reserve policy decision; resistance seen at 94.50-94.80 and support at 93.50-93.80

The rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on Wednesday. It opened at 94.79 and touched 94.81 in initial deals.

On Tuesday, the rupee had depreciated 53 paise to settle at 94.68 against the dollar. The currency remains under sustained pressure from elevated global crude oil prices.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading at $111.03 per barrel in futures trade. Persistently high crude prices are keeping the rupee on the defensive.

The dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength against six currencies, was trading 0.04 per cent higher at 98.67. Month-end dollar demand and a broader shift toward safe-haven assets are denting sentiment.

"The oil is above $111 with no resolution to the US-Iran crisis, and the non-passage of ships from the Strait of Hormuz is a matter of concern, keeping oil and the dollar up," said Anil Kumar Bhansali, Head of Treasury and Executive Director at Finrex Treasury Advisors LLP.

"The rupee is navigating through a phase where global uncertainty, oil prices, and capital flows are all pulling at once," said CR Forex Advisors MD Amit Pabari. He sees 93.50-93.80 as a strong demand zone and 94.50-94.80 as resistance.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded Indian equities worth ₹2,103.74 crore on Tuesday, exchange data showed. Persistent FII selling adds further pressure on the domestic currency.

Market attention has now shifted to the upcoming US Federal Reserve policy decision. While rates are expected to remain unchanged, the tone of the statement will carry more weight than the action itself.
