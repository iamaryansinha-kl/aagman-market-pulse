Punjab Basmati Exporters Seek Centre Intervention for Industry Restructuring

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Punjab Rice Millers Exporters Association has written to Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal seeking immediate restructuring of Basmati Export Development Foundation
  • 02/The association flagged structural gaps, lack of long-term vision, and bureaucratic leadership within BEDF which was set up over two decades ago under APEDA
  • 03/Exporters also raised concerns about utilisation of ₹70 per tonne GI protection fund collected from them, calling for a high-powered task committee

The Punjab Rice Millers Exporters Association has written to the Centre seeking restructuring of the Basmati Export Development Foundation. The letter addressed to Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal highlights what the body calls "structural gaps" within BEDF.

BEDF operates under the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority. It was established over two decades ago to protect and promote basmati rice under Geographical Indication norms globally.

The association noted that BEDF has struggled to maintain effectiveness in key international markets. It pointed to the absence of a clear long-term roadmap for exports and domestic markets.

The body also flagged inadequate representation of experienced professionals in leadership roles. It called for appointing qualified agricultural experts with hands-on experience in production, processing, and global marketing.

The association termed the current functioning of BEDF as overly bureaucratic. It noted that leadership is often composed of short-term or retired personnel, limiting effectiveness in addressing fast-changing global trade scenarios.

Exporters contribute ₹70 per tonne towards safeguarding the GI status of basmati rice. The association raised concerns about the impact of this expenditure, especially in countries where basmati registration is still pending.

India exported 6.07 million tonnes of basmati rice valued at $5.94 billion in 2024-25. Punjab alone contributes 40% of the country's total basmati exports.

During April-January 2025-26, basmati exports reached 5.39 million tonnes, up 11% year-on-year. However, value realisations declined to $4.68 billion from $4.87 billion in the same period last year.
