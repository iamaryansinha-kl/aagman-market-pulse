Q4 Results April 20: Groww PAT Jumps 122%, ICICI Bank Beats Estimates

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Billionbrains Garage Ventures (Groww) reports Q4FY26 PAT of ₹686 crore, up 122% YoY from ₹309 crore
  • 02/ICICI Bank Q4 net profit at ₹13,702 crore beats poll estimates of ₹12,677 crore; NII up 8.4% YoY
  • 03/HDFC Bank Q4 profit rises 9% to ₹19,221 crore; Yes Bank profit jumps 45% to ₹1,068 crore

Billionbrains Garage Ventures (Groww) reported consolidated PAT of ₹686 crore for Q4FY26. This marks a 122% jump from ₹309 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Total income rose 80.73% YoY to ₹1,536 crore from ₹850 crore. EBITDA surged 141.81% to ₹939 crore.

Groww's platform EBITDA stood at 66.93%. Active users grew 19.86% despite a declining market.

Customer assets in stocks and mutual funds rose 34.7% YoY. MF SIP inflows grew 34.85% and MF AUM expanded 38.91%.

ICICI Bank Q4 net profit came in at ₹13,702 crore. This beat poll estimates of ₹12,677 crore.

Net interest income was ₹22,979 crore, up 8.4% YoY. Gross NPA improved to 1.40% from 1.53% QoQ.

The bank's board recommended a final dividend of ₹12 per share. Shares traded up 1% at ₹1,363.60.

HDFC Bank reported Q4 net profit of ₹19,221 crore, up 9% YoY. NII was ₹33,082 crore, slightly below poll estimates.

Asset quality improved with gross NPA at 1.15% and net NPA at 0.38%. The board declared a final dividend of ₹13 per share.

Yes Bank Q4 standalone net profit jumped 45% to ₹1,068 crore from ₹738 crore. The stock was flat at ₹20.30.

Jio Financial reported a 14% decline in consolidated net profit to ₹272 crore. Its shares fell 2% to ₹239.34.

Companies due to announce Q4 results today include Bank of Maharashtra, PNB Housing Finance, SML Mahindra, and Navkar Corporation.
