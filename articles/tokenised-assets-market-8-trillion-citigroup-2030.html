Tokenised Assets Market to Hit $8.2 Trillion by 2030: Citigroup

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Citigroup Inc. estimates tokenised assets could reach $2.7 trillion to $8.2 trillion by 2030
  • 02/The current market is a tiny $32 billion industry, with US Treasuries and equities leading adoption
  • 03/Mirae Asset ($377 billion) partners with Ondo Finance to bring Global X ETFs onto blockchain

Tokenised assets, digital representations of real-world securities, are gaining momentum. Bitcoin is down 50% since October, shifting investor attention to utility-driven tokens.

The global tokenised assets market is currently valued at $32 billion. Citigroup Inc. estimates this could balloon to $2.7 trillion to $8.2 trillion by 2030.

US Treasuries and equities are expected to lead adoption. Younger retail investors are driving the shift toward blockchain-based financial products.

South Korea's Mirae Asset Global Investments, managing $377 billion, has partnered with Ondo Finance. The five-year-old platform, founded by two ex-Goldman Sachs employees, will bring Mirae's Global X ETF lineup onto the blockchain.

This allows investors to access space innovation, robotics, and AI exposure 24/7. Even when US markets are closed, tokens can be traded peer-to-peer or on exchanges.

Japanese government bonds are also making their blockchain debut. JGBs are being tokenised so global trading desks can use them as collateral at any hour.

Tokenised assets settle instantly. A 10-cent gas fee beats the multi-day settlement limbo and locked capital of traditional brokers.

Ondo's OUSG and BlackRock's BUIDL, both tokenised money-market funds, sparked the trend. These tokens can trade outside their issuing platforms and be posted as margin in leveraged trades.
