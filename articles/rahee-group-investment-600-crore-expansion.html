Rahee Group to Invest ₹600 Crore Over 3 Years for Capacity Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rahee Group announces ₹600 crore investment plan spread over 3 years to expand operations
  • 02/Subsidiary Rahee Track Technologies is establishing a ₹200 crore foundry in Odisha
  • 03/The facility will manufacture cast manganese and steel castings for railway sector

Rahee Infratech Ltd has announced plans to invest up to ₹600 crore over the next three years. The investment will fund capacity expansion across its operations.

Rahee Track Technologies Ltd, a subsidiary of Rahee Infratech, is setting up a foundry in Odisha. The facility represents an investment of ₹200 crore.

The foundry will manufacture cast manganese and steel castings. These products serve the railway and infrastructure sectors.

Rahee Group operates in railway infrastructure and track solutions. The expansion aligns with growing demand from Indian Railways and metro projects.

The Odisha facility will enhance the company's manufacturing capabilities. It reduces dependence on external suppliers for critical components.

The three-year investment timeline allows phased execution. This approach manages cash flow while scaling operations.

Railway infrastructure spending has increased significantly. Government focus on modernisation creates demand for domestic manufacturing.

THE ₹200 CRORE FACILITY IN ODISHA IS A FOUNDRY THAT WILL MANUFACTURE CAST MANGANESE AND STEEL CASTINGS FOR THE RAILWAY AND INFRASTRUCTURE SECTORS.

RAHEE TRACK TECHNOLOGIES LTD, A SUBSIDIARY OF RAHEE INFRATECH LTD, IS ESTABLISHING THE ODISHA FOUNDRY.

THE FACILITY WILL PRODUCE CAST MANGANESE AND STEEL CASTINGS USED IN RAILWAY TRACKS, POINTS, AND CROSSINGS.
WHY IS THIS EXPANSION SIGNIFICANT?
THE EXPANSION ALIGNS WITH INCREASED RAILWAY INFRASTRUCTURE SPENDING. IT WILL REDUCE IMPORT DEPENDENCE FOR CRITICAL COMPONENTS AND SUPPORT THE MAKE IN INDIA INITIATIVE.
