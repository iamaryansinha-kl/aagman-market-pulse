Uber Adds Hotel Bookings via Expedia, Pushes 'Everything App' Strategy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Uber adds hotel bookings powered by Expedia's 700,000-property inventory, announced at its annual product event on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Uber One members pay $9.99/month and get 20% off 10,000 hotels plus 10% back in Uber credits for ride bookings
  • 03/Over 100 million people use Uber for airport trips yearly; 1.5 billion trips occurred outside riders' home cities last year

Uber Technologies Inc (NYSE: UBER) has added hotel bookings to its app. The feature launched on April 29, 2026, powered by Expedia Group's inventory of 700,000 hotels and properties worldwide.

Uber Chief Product Officer Sachin Kansal called this a major step toward becoming an "everything app." The company wants users to handle rides, deliveries, and travel in one place.

Uber One members, who pay $9.99 monthly, receive a 20% discount on a rotating list of 10,000 hotels. They also earn 10% back in Uber credits that can be used for ride bookings.

Any Uber user can book hotels through the app without a paid membership. Over 1 million vacation rentals from Expedia's Vrbo platform will be added later this year.

Over 100 million people use Uber for airport transportation each year. Last year, 1.5 billion Uber trips took place outside a rider's home city.

Uber also announced OpenTable restaurant reservations within its app. A premium Uber Black snack service will launch soon in Atlanta, Philadelphia, and Los Angeles.

The company launched Uber Eats in 2015 and grocery delivery in 2020. Each expansion has brought new revenue streams beyond core ride-hailing.

Uber shares trade near $72, down roughly 12% year-to-date. Wall Street analysts maintain a consensus Buy rating with a median price target near $107.

UBER ONE MEMBERS, WHO PAY $9.99 PER MONTH, RECEIVE A 20% DISCOUNT ON A ROLLING LIST OF 10,000 HOTELS. THEY ALSO EARN 10% BACK IN UBER CREDITS THAT CAN BE USED TO BOOK RIDES.

UBER PARTNERED WITH EXPEDIA GROUP, WHICH PROVIDES ACCESS TO 700,000 HOTELS AND OTHER PROPERTIES GLOBALLY. THE PARTNERSHIP TOOK MONTHS OF TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION, ACCORDING TO CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER SACHIN KANSAL.
WHY IS UBER EXPANDING INTO HOTEL BOOKINGS?
UBER AIMS TO BECOME AN "EVERYTHING APP" THAT COMBINES RIDES, DELIVERIES, AND TRAVEL SERVICES IN ONE PLACE. OVER 100 MILLION PEOPLE USE UBER FOR AIRPORT TRIPS EACH YEAR, AND 1.5 BILLION TRIPS TOOK PLACE OUTSIDE RIDERS' HOME CITIES LAST YEAR.

UBER ANNOUNCED OPENTABLE RESTAURANT RESERVATIONS WITHIN ITS APP AND AN UPGRADED TRAVEL MODE FOR FINDING POINTS OF INTEREST. A PREMIUM UBER BLACK SNACK SERVICE WILL LAUNCH IN ATLANTA, PHILADELPHIA, AND LOS ANGELES IN THE COMING WEEKS.
