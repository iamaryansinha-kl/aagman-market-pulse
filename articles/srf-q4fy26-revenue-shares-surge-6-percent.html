SRF Q4FY26 Revenue Hits ₹4,615 Cr, Shares Jump 6.5% On Estimates Beat

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SRF Ltd Q4FY26 consolidated revenue rises to ₹4,615 crore, up 7% YoY, beating analyst estimates
  • 02/Shares surged 6.5% following the results; refrigerant capex revised upward on stronger growth outlook
  • 03/Investors to watch refrigerant segment execution and margin trajectory in upcoming quarters

SRF Ltd (NSE: SRF) reported consolidated revenue of ₹4,615 crore for Q4FY26. The figure marks a 7% increase compared to the same quarter last year.

SRF shares surged 6.5% following the earnings announcement. Investors responded positively as the quarterly results exceeded market estimates.

The chemical and refrigerant manufacturer revised its refrigerant capital expenditure plans upward. This signals stronger growth expectations in the refrigerant business segment.

SRF operates across multiple segments including fluorochemicals, specialty chemicals, and packaging films. The company's diversified portfolio supports revenue resilience across market cycles.

The upward revision in refrigerant capex indicates management confidence in sustained demand trends. This could support earnings growth in the coming quarters if execution remains on track.

Investors should note that chemical sector margins remain sensitive to input cost fluctuations. Global demand conditions will influence the company's near-term performance trajectory.
