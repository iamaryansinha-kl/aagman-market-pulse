Emmvee Photovoltaic Hits 52-Week High of ₹299.50 After FY26 Results Double Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited surged 8.27% to a 52-week high of ₹299.50 after reporting FY26 revenue that more than doubled to ₹5,050 crore
  • 02/Profit after tax jumped 193% YoY to ₹1,082 crore with EBITDA margins expanding to 34%, while the company ended FY26 virtually debt-free
  • 03/The stock has returned 55.89% YTD versus NIFTY 500's negative 3.85%; order book more than doubled to 9.4 GW, signaling strong forward visibility

Shares of Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited (NSE: EMMVEE) surged to a 52-week high of ₹299.50 in early trade on April 29, 2026. The stock gained 8.27% or ₹22.51 over Tuesday's close of ₹272.28.

The rally followed strong Q4 and full-year FY26 results reported after market close on Tuesday. The Bengaluru-based solar module and cell manufacturer posted revenue from operations of ₹50,499 million for FY26.

Revenue more than doubled from ₹23,356 million in FY25. EBITDA rose to ₹17,344 million with margins expanding to 34 per cent.

Profit after tax jumped to ₹10,816 million, a 193 per cent year-on-year increase. The company ended the year virtually debt-free with a net debt-to-equity ratio of negative 0.06x.

Operationally, Emmvee commissioned two additional 2.5 GW solar module lines during the year. Total installed module capacity now stands at 10.3 GW.

Cell production utilisation improved to 70 per cent for FY26, up sharply from 43.3 per cent in FY25. The order book more than doubled to 9.4 GW from 4.9 GW a year ago.

By 11:21 am on Wednesday, the stock was trading at ₹294.79 on the NSE. Heavy volumes of 176.41 lakh shares changed hands, translating to a traded value of ₹519.70 crore.

The stock has hit its upper circuit band of ₹299.50. Sell-side pressure outweighed buyers, with 56.16 per cent of order quantity on the sell side against 43.84 per cent on the buy side.

The stock was listed in November 2025 at a 52-week low of ₹171.51. It has now returned 55.89 per cent year-to-date against NIFTY 500's negative 3.85 per cent over the same period.

Total market capitalisation stood at ₹20,361 crore. Total installed capacity of 10.3 GW and an order book of 9.4 GW provide strong revenue visibility for FY27.
