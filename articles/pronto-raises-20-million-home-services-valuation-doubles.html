Pronto Raises $20 Million: Home Services Startup Doubles Valuation Since March

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Pronto raises $20 million in a fresh funding round, doubling its valuation from March 2026
  • 02/General Catalyst backs the startup offering laundry, kitchen prep, and cleaning from ₹125 with 15-minute worker availability
  • 03/The funding signals investor confidence in India's instant home services segment amid rising urban demand

Pronto has raised $20 million in a fresh funding round. The startup's valuation has doubled since March 2026.

The company is backed by venture capital firm General Catalyst. It operates in India's growing instant home services market.

Pronto offers laundry, kitchen preparation, and home cleaning services. Prices start at ₹125 per service.

Workers are available within 15 minutes of booking. This speed is a key differentiator in the home services space.

The $20 million infusion will support expansion and technology investments. It follows a broader trend of capital flowing into quick-commerce and instant services.

The doubling of valuation within two months reflects strong investor appetite. India's urban consumers increasingly demand convenience and speed in daily chores.
