Ceigall India JV Bags ₹918-Cr Jaipur Metro Order for Phase-II Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ceigall India Ltd's joint venture CIL-SAM secures ₹918.04-crore contract from Jaipur Metro Rail Corporation Ltd for Phase-II infrastructure
  • 02/The order covers design and construction of an elevated viaduct and ten elevated metro stations across a 10.8-km stretch
  • 03/Project execution timeline is 34 months; Ceigall India holds 74% stake in CIL-SAM with SAM India Builtwell owning 26%

Ceigall India Ltd (NSE: CEIGALL) on Sunday said its joint venture CIL-SAM India has bagged a ₹918.04-crore project from Jaipur Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.

The contract involves the design and construction of an elevated viaduct and ten elevated metro stations. Architectural finishing is excluded from the scope.

The work falls under Jaipur Metro's Phase-II expansion covering 10.8 km. The company announced the win in a regulatory filing on May 3, 2026.

The total bid cost stands at ₹918.04 crore inclusive of 18% GST. The project must be executed within 34 months from the award date.

The ten stations to be built are Prahladpura, Manpura, Bilwa Kalan, Bilwa, Goner Mod, Sitapura, JECC, Kumbha Marg, Haldighati Gate, and Pinjrapole Gaushala.

Ceigall India holds a 74 per cent stake in the joint venture. SAM India Builtwell Pvt Ltd owns the remaining 26 per cent.

Ramneek Sehgal, Chairman and Managing Director of Ceigall India, said, "Winning this Jaipur Metro Phase-II package marks an important milestone for Ceigall as we continue to diversify our infrastructure portfolio beyond roads and highways into urban mobility projects."

Ceigall India is a Ludhiana-based infrastructure EPC company. It builds highways, expressways, bridges, flyovers, railway overbridges, tunnels, and runways.

The company listed on the exchanges in August 2024 through an IPO that raised ₹1,253 crore. It has since expanded into hybrid annuity model projects and operation and maintenance contracts.

Ceigall India shares closed at ₹334.10 on April 30, 2026. The stock has a 52-week high of ₹424.80 and a low of ₹241.85, with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹4,500 crore.
