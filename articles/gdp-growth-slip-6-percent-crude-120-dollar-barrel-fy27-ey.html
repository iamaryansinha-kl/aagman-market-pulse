GDP Growth May Slip to 6% if Crude Hits $120/Barrel in FY27: EY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EY India warns GDP growth may slip to around 6% if the Indian crude basket averages $120/barrel in FY27
  • 02/RBI projected 6.9% GDP growth and 4.6% inflation for FY27; IMF forecasts 6.5% growth
  • 03/EIA sees Brent crude peaking at $115/barrel in Q2 2026 as West Asian crisis persists

India's GDP growth could fall to approximately 6% in FY27 if the Indian crude basket averages $120 per barrel. EY India issued the warning on Wednesday amid escalating West Asian tensions.

Retail inflation may surge to the RBI's upper tolerance limit of 6% under the same scenario. The central bank had projected inflation at 4.6% for FY27 in its April policy review.

DK Srivastava, Chief Policy Advisor at EY India, said policy options are limited. He recommended an upward repo rate revision and faster diversification of crude supply sources.

The US Energy Information Administration expects Brent crude to rise from $81 per barrel in Q1 2026 to a peak of $115 in Q2 2026. Prices may ease later depending on how the crisis evolves.

The IMF projects India's GDP growth at 6.5% for FY27. The ADB and World Bank forecast 6.9% and 6.6% respectively.

The RBI projected FY27 growth at 6.9%, down from 7.6% estimated for FY26. Srivastava said the West Asian crisis may last longer than initially expected.

Even under an adverse scenario, India's FY27 growth is expected to be more than double the global growth rate. The ICB price is likely to exceed $95 per barrel on average.

THE RBI PROJECTS 6.9 PER CENT, THE IMF 6.5 PER CENT, THE ASIAN DEVELOPMENT BANK 6.9 PER CENT, AND THE WORLD BANK 6.6 PER CENT.

THE EIA'S APRIL 2026 SHORT-TERM ENERGY OUTLOOK PROJECTS BRENT CRUDE RISING FROM USD 81 PER BARREL IN Q1 2026 TO A PEAK OF USD 115 IN Q2 2026.

EY INDIA CHIEF POLICY ADVISOR DK SRIVASTAVA RECOMMENDED AN UPWARD REVISION IN THE REPO RATE AND ACCELERATED DIVERSIFICATION OF SOURCES OF CRUDE SUPPLY TO MINIMISE ADVERSE IMPACTS.
