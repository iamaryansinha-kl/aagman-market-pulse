Godrej Consumer Sees High-Teens Q1 Growth, Indonesia Back on Track

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godrej Consumer Products expects high-teens revenue growth in Q1FY27, ahead of full-year double-digit guidance
  • 02/Indonesia business reports mid-teens revenue growth with double-digit volume growth; GAUM business registers strong double-digit growth
  • 03/Margins likely lower in Q1 due to crude volatility but expected to recover progressively through FY27

Godrej Consumer Products Ltd on Friday said it expects to deliver high-teens revenue growth in Q1FY27. The growth will be backed by strong high-single-digit underlying volume growth across all categories. This is ahead of the company's full-year guidance of double-digit revenue growth.

The standalone business is expected to deliver double-digit revenue growth. This will be supported by high-single-digit volume growth with broad-based expansion across categories. The FMCG industry witnessed an acceleration in value growth during the quarter while the demand environment remained stable.

In terms of international business, the Indonesia business delivered a meaningful step-up in performance. It reported mid-teens revenue growth driven by double-digit underlying volume growth. The company said competitive pressures are now easing and market share gains are sustained across categories.

The GAUM (Godrej Africa, USA, and Middle East) business also delivered an exceptionally strong quarter. It registered strong double-digit sales growth supported by underlying volume growth in the teens. This marks continued momentum in the company's key international markets.

The company said the June quarter witnessed significant volatility in crude oil and other raw material costs. Sourcing challenges led to lower fill rates across markets. However, the business successfully navigated these headwinds through agile planning, sourcing, and calibrated pricing actions.

Consolidated EBITDA is expected to come in ahead of double-digit guidance. However, margins are likely to be lower due to exceptional cost pressures. The company expects to recover progressively through fiscal 2027 as commodity prices begin to ease.

"With revenue growth tracking ahead of our original expectations and input costs beginning to ease, we enter the remainder of FY27 with increased confidence. We remain firmly on track to deliver our guidance for the full year with the strong likelihood to exceed the same in select metrics," the company said.

The company remains mindful that El Niño conditions could increase weather-related volatility across key markets. This could potentially disrupt agricultural output and rural demand. However, its geographically diversified sourcing network provides meaningful resilience against such risks.
