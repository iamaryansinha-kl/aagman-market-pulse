Jio Platforms IPO Switches to Pure Fundraise, Drops Investor Exit Plans

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms pivots planned Mumbai IPO to pure fundraise, dropping earlier plans for investor exits amid West Asia uncertainty
  • 02/Company plans to sell 2.5% stake; estimated valuation $133-180 billion; could raise $4-4.5 billion
  • 03/Strategic investors Meta, Google to stay invested; IPO awaits government notification on revised SEBI norms

Reliance Jio Platforms Ltd has pivoted its planned Mumbai IPO to a pure fundraise. The company dropped earlier plans that would have allowed some shareholders to sell their stakes, two sources familiar with the matter said.

The decision comes amid uncertainty in global markets due to the West Asia situation. "Given the uncertainty in markets due to the situation in West Asia, investors have chosen to stay invested in the company rather than use the IPO as an exit opportunity," one source said.

Jio Platforms, which houses the world's second-largest telecom company by users after China Mobile, counts Meta, Google, and Vista Equity Partners among its investors. The strategic investors are expected to retain their stakes in the listing.

The IPO is planned for the first half of 2026 and targets a 2.5% stake sale. At an estimated valuation of $133-180 billion, the offering could raise $4-4.5 billion, making it India's largest-ever IPO.

The planned offering surpasses Hyundai Motor India's $3.3 billion IPO from 2025. Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs have been shortlisted as lead bankers for the public offering.

The company is awaiting a final government notification on revised SEBI norms. The new rules allow companies valued above ₹5 lakh crore to list with a 2.5% public float instead of the mandatory 5%.

Jio serves over 500 million subscribers across India. The company's revenue remains concentrated in telecom operations, making it sensitive to ARPU trends and competitive pressure from Airtel and Vodafone Idea.

Mukesh Ambani first announced a five-year listing plan for Jio in 2019. The IPO timeline depends on regulatory clarity, with the company currently preparing its Draft Red Herring Prospectus.
