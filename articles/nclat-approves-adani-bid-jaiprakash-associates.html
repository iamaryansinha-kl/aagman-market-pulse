NCLAT Approves Adani Bid for Jaiprakash Associates, Rejects Vedanta Plea

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NCLAT bench rejects Vedanta plea, upholds CoC approval of Adani's ₹14,535 crore resolution plan for Jaiprakash Associates
  • 02/Vedanta had offered ₹17,926 crore — ₹3,400 crore higher than Adani — but CoC preferred higher upfront cash payment
  • 03/JAL was admitted to insolvency in June 2024 with claims exceeding ₹57,000 crore; Vedanta may appeal to Supreme Court

The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) on May 4, 2026, dismissed Vedanta Ltd's plea against Adani Enterprises' successful bid for Jaiprakash Associates Ltd (JAL). The bench comprising Chairperson Justice (retired) Ashok Bhushan and Technical Member Barun Mitra upheld the Committee of Creditors' decision.

Adani Enterprises Ltd's resolution plan is valued at ₹14,535 crore. The CoC had approved it based on higher upfront payment terms. Vedanta had subsequently increased its upfront offer but was rejected.

Vedanta emerged as the highest bidder with an offer of ₹17,926 crore. This was ₹3,400 crore higher than Adani's plan on total value. Vedanta's net present value stood at ₹12,506 crore.

The company alleged procedural unfairness. It claimed lenders violated value maximisation principles under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. Vedanta was not given reasons or an opportunity to clarify.

JAL was admitted to corporate insolvency in June 2024. The Allahabad bench of NCLT acted on a petition filed by ICICI Bank. Admitted claims exceed ₹57,000 crore.

National Asset Reconstruction Company holds over 85 per cent voting share in the CoC. It is the largest financial creditor in the insolvency process.

Solicitor General Tushar Mehta defended the CoC's decision before NCLAT. He argued bidders lack a vested right to selection on highest financial offer alone. Vedanta is likely to move the Supreme Court.

Adani Enterprises Ltd (NSE: ADANIENT) closed at ₹2,394.90 on April 30, 2026. Jaiprakash Associates Ltd (NSE: JPASSOCIAT) traded at ₹2.42 on the NSE.

WHAT DID NCLAT RULE ON ADANI'S BID FOR JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES?
THE NATIONAL COMPANY LAW APPELLATE TRIBUNAL ON MAY 4, 2026, DISMISSED VEDANTA'S PLEA AND UPHELD THE COMMITTEE OF CREDITORS' APPROVAL OF ADANI ENTERPRISES' ₹14,535 CRORE RESOLUTION PLAN FOR JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES LTD.
HOW MUCH DID VEDANTA OFFER FOR JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES?
VEDANTA LTD OFFERED ₹17,926 CRORE FOR JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES, WHICH WAS ₹3,400 CRORE HIGHER THAN ADANI'S PLAN ON TOTAL VALUE. ITS NET PRESENT VALUE OFFER STOOD AT ₹12,506 CRORE.
WHY DID THE COMMITTEE OF CREDITORS CHOOSE ADANI'S LOWER BID?
THE COC APPROVED ADANI'S BID BASED ON HIGHER UPFRONT PAYMENT TERMS. THE NCLAT HELD THAT THE COC EXERCISED ITS "COMMERCIAL WISDOM" AND THAT THE DECISION WAS NOT LEGALLY PERVERSE.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL DEBT OF JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES?
JAIPRAKASH ASSOCIATES HAD ADMITTED CLAIMS EXCEEDING ₹57,000 CRORE UNDER THE INSOLVENCY PROCESS. AS OF JANUARY 2026, THE COMPANY DISCLOSED OUTSTANDING BORROWINGS OF ₹55,357 CRORE.
CAN VEDANTA STILL CHALLENGE THE NCLAT DECISION?
YES. LEGAL CIRCLES EXPECT VEDANTA TO MOVE THE SUPREME COURT AGAINST THE NCLAT ORDER. VEDANTA'S COUNSEL HAD ARGUED THAT THE BID GAP VIOLATED THE INSOLVENCY AND BANKRUPTCY CODE'S VALUE MAXIMISATION PRINCIPLE.
