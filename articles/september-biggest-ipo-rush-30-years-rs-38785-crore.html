September Sees Biggest IPO Rush in Nearly 30 Years as 30 Mainboard Issues Raise Rs 38,785 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

September has emerged as the busiest month in nearly three decades for mainboard initial public offerings, with 30 issues launched so far, the highest monthly count since December 1996 when 33 issues were launched in a single month.

The month's fundraising has also been significant. Mainboard IPOs raised approximately Rs 38,785 crore, the biggest monthly total since October 2025, when 10 issues raised Rs 45,188 crore.

NSE's Rs 22,561 crore IPO, the largest so far this year, is the top contributor to September's tally.

SME Segment Also Active

The small and medium enterprise segment has seen a sharp increase in activity. Thirty-seven SME issues were launched in September, raising around Rs 1,486 crore. This is the highest monthly count since the same month last year.

IPO Characteristics

Of the 30 mainboard IPOs launched during the month, only six had an issue size exceeding Rs 1,000 crore. The remaining issues were relatively smaller offerings, with an average size of around Rs 400 crore.

More than half the money raised through mainboard IPOs this year has come through offer-for-sale transactions. Of the Rs 1.12 lakh crore raised through mainboard IPOs so far this year, Rs 66,630 crore came through the OFS route.

Driving Factors

The surge in primary market activity comes despite volatility in the secondary market, with continued foreign investor selling, hardening bond yields, tighter global monetary conditions and geopolitical tensions pushing crude oil prices higher.

Aamar Deo Singh, head of research at Angel One, said the exuberance in the primary market cannot be read in isolation. He noted that volatility has been the theme for a few quarters, and money has grown jumpy, chasing whatever window opens.

Strong domestic liquidity has been an important supporting factor. SIP flows continue to provide a steady source of capital for equities. Strong demand for several new issues has encouraged companies to tap the primary market despite broader market volatility.

Rajesh Singla, CEO and fund manager at Alpha AMC and Planify, said the September SME IPO surge is about a pipeline that has been building for months finally hitting the market before financials age out. He expects the pace of SME IPOs to continue through the rest of the year.
