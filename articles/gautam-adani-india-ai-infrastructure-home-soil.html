Gautam Adani Urges India to Build Sovereign AI Infrastructure

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gautam Adani told the CII Annual Business Summit 2026 that India must build, power, and own its AI infrastructure on home soil
  • 02/Adani Group has committed USD 100 billion to energy transition and another USD 100 billion to data centre business
  • 03/India's installed power capacity crossed 500 GW in March 2026, with AI data centre capacity projected to hit 75 GW by 2047

Gautam Adani called for India to build sovereign artificial intelligence infrastructure across the entire value chain. The Adani Group Chairman delivered the message on Monday at the Confederation of Indian Industry's Annual Business Summit 2026 in New Delhi.

"The world that is emerging is not flat. It is fractured and contested," Adani said at the summit. He warned that semiconductors, data, clouds, and AI are being weaponised amid rising geopolitical fragmentation.

"India must not rent the infrastructure of its intelligence future. India must build it, power it and own it on its own soil," Adani told the audience. He said AI should be treated as strategic infrastructure.

India's installed power capacity crossed 500 gigawatts as of March 2026. The country is on track to quadruple this to 2,000 GW by 2047. India's AI-driven data centre capacity stands at 5 GW by 2030.

That data centre capacity could surge to nearly 75 GW by 2047. Adani warned the country must prepare immediately for the surge in compute demand.

The Adani Group has committed USD 100 billion towards energy transition. This includes a 30-GW renewable project at Khavda in Gujarat where 35 per cent is already commissioned. Adani described it as the world's largest single-site renewable plant.

The group has also committed a further USD 100 billion to data centres. This includes a partnership with Google for a gigawatt-scale campus in Visakhapatnam. Microsoft, Flipkart, and Uber are also partnering on data infrastructure.

Adani outlined a three-layer AI framework spanning power generation, compute infrastructure, and AI applications. "If our data is processed on distant shores, it means our future is being written on foreign shores," he said.

"The real measure of AI will not be how many jobs it replaces. The real measure will be how many Indians it empowers," Adani said. He added India's domestic demand uniquely positions it to build large-scale AI infrastructure.
