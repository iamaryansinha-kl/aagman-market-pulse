Tiger Global-Backed Moneyview Sets IPO Price Band at Rs 32-34 for Rs 1,092 Crore Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Another fintech is heading to the bourses. Digital lending platform Moneyview, backed by Accel, Tiger Global and Ribbit Capital, has set the price band for its initial public offering at Rs 32-34 per equity share, with the Rs 1,091.61 crore issue opening for subscription on September 24.

The structure splits between growth and exits. The IPO comprises a fresh issue of Rs 750 crore and an offer-for-sale of up to 10.04 crore equity shares by promoters and existing investors. Founders Puneet Agarwal and Sanjay Aggarwal will sell shares through the OFS, alongside Accel, Tiger Global, Ribbit Capital, DI Investment and Crimson Winter. At the upper end of the band, Moneyview commands an implied market capitalisation of Rs 5,984.79 crore.

The allocation is retail-heavy. Of the total issue, 50% is earmarked for qualified institutional buyers, 15% for non-institutional investors and 35% for retail investors. The anchor portion opens September 23, the public issue closes September 28, allotment is expected on September 29 and trading is scheduled to commence on October 1.

The company connects users with banks, NBFCs, insurers and other financial institutions. As of June 2026, it had 14.03 crore registered users and 48 financial partners. It began personal loan distribution in FY17 and moved into on-balance-sheet lending through material subsidiary Whizdm Finance in FY20, later adding insurance, credit cards, digital gold, payments and earned wage access.

The proceeds have a defined job. Moneyview plans to allocate Rs 325 crore of the fresh issue to increasing loan disbursals under default loss guarantee arrangements and Rs 250 crore to strengthening Whizdm Finance's capital base, with the balance for general corporate purposes.

The financials show acceleration. Profit jumped 159% year-on-year to Rs 174 crore in the June 2026 quarter from Rs 67.15 crore a year earlier, with revenue from operations up 50.2% to Rs 1,041.1 crore. For the full FY26, profit was nearly flat at Rs 242.7 crore against Rs 240.3 crore, while revenue climbed 43.3% to Rs 3,351.2 crore.

Axis Capital, BofA Securities India, IIFL Capital Services and Kotak Mahindra Capital Company are the merchant bankers. The listing joins a packed September window alongside NSE, NTPC Green Energy and Lenskart, testing whether primary-market appetite, which has stayed strong even as foreign investors sell listed stocks, extends to a mid-cap digital lender.
