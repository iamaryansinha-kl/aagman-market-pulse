Sunil Singhania-Led Abakkus Asset Manager Files DRHP With Sebi for IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Abakkus Asset Manager Ltd, led by veteran investor Sunil Singhania, has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with the Securities and Exchange Board of India on September 22, 2026 for going public.

The IPO Structure

The proposed initial public offering will comprise an Offer for Sale (OFS) of 15 million equity shares, each having a face value of Rs 2. Since the issue is entirely an OFS, the proceeds from the share sale will go to existing selling shareholders.

The IPO is expected to have a 50% allocation for Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs), 15% for Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs), and 35% for retail investors. An employee reservation portion is also proposed.

Financial Performance

Abakkus has reported strong financial growth over the past three fiscal years. For FY26, the company reported revenue of Rs 796 crore, up from Rs 707 crore in FY25 and Rs 479 crore in FY24.

Profit after tax rose to Rs 327 crore in FY26, compared with Rs 258 crore in FY25 and Rs 205 crore in FY24.

Business Verticals

The company offers investment solutions across four major business verticals: Alternates (Category III AIFs and PMS), Offshore and Other Products (RIA services and the Aryabhata UCITS fund), Private Equity (Category I and II AIFs), and Mutual Funds.

Abakkus has emerged as one of the fastest-growing India-focused asset management platforms in terms of non-mutual fund quarterly average AUM. Its QAAUM recorded a CAGR of 18.39% between FY24 and FY26.

As of March 31, 2026, Abakkus ranked fourth in the total equity strategy discretionary PMS segment, with AUM of Rs 18,538.14 crore and a 5.25% market share, according to the ICRA Report.

Promoters and Book-Running Managers

The promoters include Sunil Banwarilal Singhania, Kanchan Sunil Singhania, Abakkus Expert Professionals LLP, SUKK Family Trust and Ecosmart Climate Solutions.

The IPO's book-running lead managers are Axis Capital, ICICI Securities, IIFL Capital and JM Financial. KFin Technologies will act as the registrar to the issue.
