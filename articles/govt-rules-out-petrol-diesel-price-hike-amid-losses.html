Govt Rules Out Hike in Petrol, Diesel Prices Amid ₹2,400 Cr Daily Loss

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Govt rules out petrol, diesel price hike; retail prices frozen since April 6, 2022
  • 02/PSU OMCs face ₹2,400 crore combined daily under-recovery; diesel loss at ₹82-105 per litre
  • 03/Indian crude basket jumped from $70 to $113 per barrel; all refineries running at high capacity

The government has ruled out any proposal to raise retail petrol and diesel prices. Petroleum Ministry Joint Secretary Sujata Sharma confirmed this on April 28, 2026.

Auto fuel prices have remained unchanged since April 6, 2022. This freeze spans nearly four years despite a sharp surge in global crude oil costs.

India's combined daily under-recovery stands at around ₹2,400 crore as of March 27, 2026. PSU oil marketing companies are absorbing massive losses on every litre sold.

Under-recoveries were around ₹26 per litre on petrol and ₹81.90 on diesel around March 27. By April 2, diesel under-recoveries spiked to ₹104.99 per litre while petrol stood at ₹24.40.

The Indian crude basket averaged $70 per barrel in FY26. It jumped to over $113 per barrel in April 2026. Kotak Institutional Equities noted the basket rose $47 in March and $53 in early April.

Despite a 13-15 per cent decline in crude imports, India's import bill has risen by $190-210 million per day. The gap between global costs and domestic retail prices keeps widening.

All refineries are operating at high capacity with adequate crude inventories. Sharma also addressed panic buying at certain outlets, calling it unfounded and driven by rumours.

"There is no proposal to increase petrol and diesel prices," Sujata Sharma said. She added that regular retail prices remain unchanged at PSU OMC retail outlets.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
IS THE GOVERNMENT PLANNING TO RAISE PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES?
NO. THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS HAS CONFIRMED THERE IS NO PROPOSAL TO RAISE RETAIL PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES. JOINT SECRETARY SUJATA SHARMA MADE THIS STATEMENT ON APRIL 28, 2026.
HOW MUCH ARE PSU OIL COMPANIES LOSING ON FUEL SALES?
INDIA'S COMBINED DAILY UNDER-RECOVERY IS AROUND ₹2,400 CRORE AS OF MARCH 27, 2026. AROUND APRIL 2, PSU OIL MARKETING COMPANIES WERE BEARING LOSSES OF ₹24.40 PER LITRE ON PETROL AND ₹104.99 PER LITRE ON DIESEL.
WHEN WERE PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES LAST INCREASED?
RETAIL PRICES OF PETROL AND DIESEL WERE LAST RAISED ON APRIL 6, 2022. THEY HAVE REMAINED FROZEN FOR NEARLY FOUR YEARS DESPITE A SHARP RISE IN GLOBAL CRUDE OIL PRICES.
HOW MUCH HAS THE INDIAN CRUDE BASKET PRICE RISEN?
THE INDIAN CRUDE BASKET AVERAGED $70 PER BARREL IN FY26. IT JUMPED TO OVER $113 PER BARREL IN APRIL 2026. KOTAK INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES NOTED THE BASKET ROSE BY $47 PER BARREL IN MARCH 2026 AND $53 IN THE FIRST HALF OF APRIL.
WHY IS THERE PANIC BUYING AT SOME PETROL PUMPS?
PANIC BUYING IS DRIVEN BY RUMOURS OF AN IMMINENT PRICE HIKE. THE GOVERNMENT HAS CLARIFIED THAT ALL REFINERIES ARE OPERATING AT HIGH CAPACITY WITH ADEQUATE CRUDE INVENTORIES, AND SUFFICIENT STOCKS OF PETROL AND DIESEL ARE AVAILABLE AT ALL PSU RETAIL OUTLETS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
