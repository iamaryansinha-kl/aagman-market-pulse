NSE Launches Electronic Gold Receipts Trading on May 4, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE launched Electronic Gold Receipts trading on May 4, 2026, after receiving SEBI approval, with the first 1,000-gram gold bar successfully dematerialised into an EGR
  • 02/EGRs are dematerialised securities representing ownership of physical gold stored in SEBI-accredited vaults, fully backed and tradable on the exchange like other demat instruments
  • 03/India traded approximately 12 tonnes of digital gold worth ₹16,000 crore between January and November 2025; EGRs aim to bring this fragmented market under formal regulation

The National Stock Exchange of India Ltd (NSE) launched Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) on May 4, 2026. The SEBI-approved segment allows investors to trade gold in dematerialised form.

NSE successfully dematerialised a 1,000-gram gold bar into the first EGR. The move symbolises seamless conversion of physical gold into a regulated, tradable electronic instrument.

EGRs represent ownership of physical gold stored in SEBI-accredited vaults. Each receipt is fully backed by the underlying metal and held electronically through depositories.

Investors can trade EGRs on the exchange in smaller denominations. This improves liquidity and flexibility comparable to equity shares held in demat form.

NSE MD & CEO Ashishkumar Chauhan compared the launch to the dematerialisation of equity shares in the 1990s. "By enabling gold to be held in dematerialized form as EGR and traded seamlessly on a regulated platform, many of the challenges associated with physical handling, storage and parity assurance can be addressed," Chauhan said.

Sriram Krishnan, Chief Business Development Officer at NSE, said the exchange is positioning gold as a modern asset class. "We are democratizing access to gold, enabling investors across the nation to trade with unprecedented transparency and confidence," Krishnan said.

The launch targets jewellers, refiners, traders, and institutional investors. NSE aims to reduce dependence on fragmented local benchmarks and foster deeper financial inclusion.

India remains the world's largest market for digital gold. Indians purchased roughly 12 tonnes worth approximately ₹16,000 crore via digital platforms between January and November 2025.

However, much of this volume flowed through unregulated fintech apps. SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey had acknowledged in December 2025 that the EGR framework had not gained desired traction so far.

Budget 2026 clarifies taxation: long-term capital gains on digital gold held over 24 months attract a flat 12.5% tax. Short-term gains are taxed at the investor's income slab, and every purchase attracts 3% GST.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT ARE ELECTRONIC GOLD RECEIPTS (EGRS) LAUNCHED BY NSE?
ELECTRONIC GOLD RECEIPTS ARE DEMATERIALISED SECURITIES THAT REPRESENT OWNERSHIP OF PHYSICAL GOLD. EACH EGR IS FULLY BACKED BY PHYSICAL GOLD STORED IN SEBI-ACCREDITED VAULTS AND HELD ELECTRONICALLY THROUGH DEPOSITORIES, MAKING GOLD TRADABLE ON THE EXCHANGE LIKE EQUITY SHARES.
WHEN DID NSE LAUNCH EGR TRADING AND WHAT WAS THE FIRST TRANSACTION?
NSE LAUNCHED ELECTRONIC GOLD RECEIPTS TRADING ON MAY 4, 2026. THE EXCHANGE SUCCESSFULLY DEMATERIALISED A 1,000-GRAM GOLD BAR INTO THE FIRST EGR, DEMONSTRATING OPERATIONAL READINESS FOR DELIVERY-BACKED GOLD TRADING.
WHO CAN BENEFIT FROM TRADING EGRS ON NSE?
EGRS ARE DESIGNED TO BENEFIT JEWELLERS, REFINERS, TRADERS, AND INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS. RETAIL INVESTORS CAN ALSO PARTICIPATE IN SMALLER DENOMINATIONS, GAINING IMPROVED LIQUIDITY AND FLEXIBILITY COMPARABLE TO OTHER DEMAT FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS.
HOW DOES THE EGR LAUNCH IMPACT INDIA'S EXISTING DIGITAL GOLD MARKET?
INDIA IS THE WORLD'S LARGEST DIGITAL GOLD MARKET, WITH INDIANS PURCHASING ABOUT 12 TONNES WORTH APPROXIMATELY ₹16,000 CRORE VIA UNREGULATED FINTECH PLATFORMS BETWEEN JANUARY AND NOVEMBER 2025. EGRS BRING GOLD TRADING UNDER FORMAL SEBI REGULATION, OFFERING TRANSPARENCY AND INVESTOR PROTECTION THAT UNREGULATED PLATFORMS DO NOT PROVIDE.
WHAT ARE THE TAX IMPLICATIONS FOR INVESTORS IN EGRS OR DIGITAL GOLD?
AS PER BUDGET 2026, LONG-TERM CAPITAL GAINS ON DIGITAL GOLD HELD FOR MORE THAN 24 MONTHS ARE TAXED AT A FLAT 12.5%. SHORT-TERM GAINS ARE TAXED AT THE INVESTOR'S INCOME TAX SLAB. EVERY PURCHASE OF DIGITAL GOLD ATTRACTS A 3% GST AT ENTRY.
