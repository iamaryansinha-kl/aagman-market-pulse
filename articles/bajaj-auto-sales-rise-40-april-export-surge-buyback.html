Bajaj Auto Sales Rise 40% in April on Export Surge; Buyback Vote on May 6

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Auto reported 40% YoY sales growth in April at 5.13 lakh units, with exports surging 83% to 2.65 lakh
  • 02/Commercial vehicle exports jumped 125% and two-wheeler exports rose 78%, as overseas markets overtake domestic volumes
  • 03/Board meets May 6 to consider Q4 results and potential share buyback; stock up 4.5% at ₹10,445

Bajaj Auto Ltd reported a 40% year-on-year rise in total sales for April 2026 at 5.13 lakh units. The surge was driven by strong export demand.

Exports jumped 83% to 2.65 lakh units, overtaking domestic volumes for the first time in recent months. Domestic sales grew 13% to 2.48 lakh units.

Commercial vehicle exports, primarily three-wheelers, surged 125%. Two-wheeler exports rose 78%, signalling a broad-based recovery in overseas markets.

Analysts at Elara Capital and Motilal Oswal attribute the export surge to improving macro conditions. Currency stabilisation in Africa, particularly Nigeria, and a recovery in Sri Lanka are key drivers.

Nearly 80% of the incremental volume growth in April came from exports. This underlines how Bajaj Auto's performance is once again being powered by its global business.

The three-wheeler segment, Bajaj's most profitable export franchise, is rebounding sharply after a prolonged slowdown. Analysts see this as a restoration of the company's core earnings engine.

Brokerages including CLSA and JM Financial see FY27 as a "triple-engine" phase for Bajaj Auto. The three growth levers are exports, clean mobility, and premiumisation.

The board will meet on May 6, 2026 to consider March quarter results and a potential share buyback. Surplus cash is estimated at ₹15,000–₹16,000 crore.

Shares of Bajaj Auto (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO) were trading 4.5% higher at ₹10,445 on May 4. The stock is near its highest level since October 2024.
