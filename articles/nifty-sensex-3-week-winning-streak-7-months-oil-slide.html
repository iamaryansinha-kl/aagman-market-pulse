Nifty, Sensex Log 3-Week Winning Streak, Longest in 7 Months on Oil Slide

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex logged their third straight weekly gain, the longest winning streak in seven months
  • 02/Brent crude fell 1.4% to $72.7 a barrel as U.S.-Iran peace deal eased supply concerns for the world's third-largest oil importer
  • 03/RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra ruled out near-term rate hikes; pharma and financials led sectoral gains

India's Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex closed higher on Thursday, logging their third straight weekly gain. This marks the longest weekly winning streak for Indian benchmarks in seven months.

The Nifty 50 rose 0.14% to 24,056, while the BSE Sensex added 0.14% to 77,100.47. For the week, the Nifty advanced 0.2% and the Sensex 0.4%.

Brent crude fell 1.4% to $72.7 a barrel as more tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz. The U.S.-Iran peace deal eased growth and inflation concerns for India, the world's third-largest oil importer.

Reserve Bank of India Governor Sanjay Malhotra ruled out a near-term rate hike. Lower borrowing costs support earnings, consumption and valuations for Indian companies.

The pharma index gained 2.1% this week after reports that the U.S. FDA reached out to Indian drugmakers. Financials rose 1.3%, banks 0.9% and private lenders 1.5% after RBI allowed loans against foreign-currency deposits.

Interglobe Aviation climbed 8.5% as lower oil prices eased cost concerns for the airline. Metals logged a weekly loss of 4.4% on weaker global prices.

"As markets will be shut on Friday, investors trimmed positions in late trades," said Ankur Punj, managing director at Equirus Wealth. Profit-taking capped intraday gains of over 1%.

"Below-normal monsoon is a near-term risk, but RBI's foreign-currency deposit move and bond tax removal for foreign investors should support inflows," said Bajaj Finserv AMC.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT TRIGGERED THE RALLY IN INDIAN STOCK MARKETS THIS WEEK?
FALLING BRENT CRUDE PRICES TO $72.7 PER BARREL, THE U.S.-IRAN PEACE DEAL EASING SUPPLY CONCERNS, AND RBI GOVERNOR SANJAY MALHOTRA RULING OUT A NEAR-TERM RATE HIKE BOOSTED SENTIMENT. RECENT MEASURES TO SUPPORT THE RUPEE AND ATTRACT OVERSEAS INFLOWS ALSO HELPED.
WHAT WERE THE CLOSING LEVELS OF NIFTY 50 AND SENSEX ON JUNE 25, 2026?
THE NIFTY 50 ROSE 0.14% TO 24,056 AND THE BSE SENSEX ADDED 0.14% TO 77,100.47. BOTH BENCHMARKS HAD RISEN OVER 1% DURING THE SESSION BEFORE PROFIT-TAKING IN THE FINAL HOUR.
WHICH SECTORS LED THE GAINS THIS WEEK?
THE PHARMA INDEX GAINED 2.1% AFTER REPORTS OF U.S. FDA OUTREACH TO INDIAN DRUGMAKERS. FINANCIALS ROSE 1.3%, BANKS 0.9%, AND PRIVATE LENDERS 1.5% AFTER RBI ALLOWED LOANS AGAINST FOREIGN-CURRENCY DEPOSITS.
WHY DID METALS AND MID-CAPS UNDERPERFORM?
METALS LOGGED A WEEKLY LOSS OF 4.4% TRACKING WEAKER GLOBAL PRICES ON RISING U.S. RATE HIKE EXPECTATIONS IN 2026. MID-CAPS FELL 1.2% WHILE SMALL-CAPS WERE LITTLE CHANGED FOR THE WEEK.
WHAT RISKS SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH GOING FORWARD?
BELOW-NORMAL MONSOON IS A NEAR-TERM RISK FOR INDIA. HOWEVER, RBI'S MOVE TO MOBILISE FOREIGN-CURRENCY DEPOSITS AND THE GOVERNMENT'S REMOVAL OF BOND TAXES FOR FOREIGN INVESTORS SHOULD SUPPORT INFLOWS AND MARKETS.
