Sebi Introduces Auto-Pledge for Unpaid Securities, Strengthens Safeguards

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi introduces auto-pledge framework where unpaid securities are automatically pledged to trading members' CUSPA account
  • 02/Trading members must notify clients and liquidate if dues remain unpaid beyond 5 trading days from pay-out date
  • 03/Depositories will auto-release pledges on 6th trading day if neither invoked nor released

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) on Friday introduced a comprehensive regulatory framework for unpaid client securities. The new rules bring in an auto-pledge mechanism while strengthening safeguards to protect investors.

Under the new framework, securities purchased outside the Margin Trading Facility (MTF) that remain unpaid will continue to be credited to the client's demat account. However, an automatic pledge will be created in favour of a dedicated account maintained by the trading member, known as the Client Unpaid Securities Pledgee Account (CUSPA). This will happen without requiring any separate consent from the client.

The pledge will be distinctly tagged as "unpaid" in accordance with Sebi's regulations. Following the creation of the pledge, trading members must promptly notify clients through email or SMS regarding their outstanding payment obligation. They must also inform clients of the TM's right to liquidate the pledged securities if dues remain unpaid.

Sebi has instructed trading members to establish a formal policy for dealing with unpaid securities. This policy must clearly define the circumstances, process and timelines for invoking or releasing pledges. It must also specify the maximum period allowed for clients to fulfil payment obligations, which cannot exceed five trading days from the pay-out date.

Although securities pledged in the TM's CUSPA account can be counted for reporting client margin collection to the Clearing Corporation, Sebi clarified that trading members cannot extend any additional trading exposure against these pledged securities.

Trading members must calculate on a daily basis the maximum value of securities that can remain under pledge. If the value exceeds the permissible threshold, the trading member must release the excess pledged quantity by the following trading day. If the client does not clear dues within the prescribed period, the TM will invoke the pledge and liquidate the securities.

Sebi said depositories will verify blocked securities against the client's obligations before selling them in the market using the client's Unique Client Code (UCC). Any surplus remaining after settling dues will be credited back to the client's ledger.

If a pledge is neither invoked nor released within five trading days from the pay-out date, depositories will automatically release it at the close of the sixth trading day. The securities will then become freely available in the client's demat account.

The regulator has also barred trading members from using securities held in the CUSPA account as collateral with banks or NBFCs to raise funds. Sebi has directed stock exchanges to issue detailed operational guidelines within 30 days from the date of the circular.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE NEW AUTO-PLEDGE FRAMEWORK INTRODUCED BY SEBI?
SEBI HAS INTRODUCED AN AUTO-PLEDGE FRAMEWORK WHERE UNPAID SECURITIES PURCHASED OUTSIDE MTF ARE AUTOMATICALLY PLEDGED TO THE TRADING MEMBER'S CLIENT UNPAID SECURITIES PLEDGEE ACCOUNT (CUSPA) WITHOUT REQUIRING SEPARATE CLIENT CONSENT.
HOW LONG DO CLIENTS HAVE TO PAY THEIR OUTSTANDING DUES?
CLIENTS HAVE A MAXIMUM OF FIVE TRADING DAYS FROM THE PAY-OUT DATE TO FULFIL THEIR PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS. BEYOND THIS, THE TRADING MEMBER CAN INVOKE THE PLEDGE AND LIQUIDATE SECURITIES.
CAN TRADING MEMBERS USE PLEDGED SECURITIES FOR ADDITIONAL EXPOSURE?
NO. SEBI HAS CLARIFIED THAT TRADING MEMBERS CANNOT EXTEND ANY ADDITIONAL TRADING EXPOSURE AGAINST PLEDGED SECURITIES, ALTHOUGH THEY CAN COUNT THEM FOR REPORTING CLIENT MARGIN COLLECTION.
WHAT HAPPENS IF THE PLEDGE IS NOT INVOKED OR RELEASED WITHIN 5 DAYS?
DEPOSITORIES WILL AUTOMATICALLY RELEASE THE PLEDGE AT THE CLOSE OF THE SIXTH TRADING DAY. THE SECURITIES WILL THEN BECOME FREELY AVAILABLE IN THE CLIENT'S DEMAT ACCOUNT WITHOUT ANY ENCUMBRANCE.
CAN TRADING MEMBERS USE CUSPA SECURITIES AS COLLATERAL?
NO. SEBI HAS BARRED TRADING MEMBERS FROM USING SECURITIES HELD IN THE CUSPA ACCOUNT AS COLLATERAL WITH BANKS OR NBFCs TO RAISE FUNDS.
