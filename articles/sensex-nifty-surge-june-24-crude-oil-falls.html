Sensex Jumps 791 Points, Nifty Reclaims 24,000 as Crude Oil Tumbles 4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex surged 790.54 points (1.04%) to 76,991.22 and Nifty jumped 197.55 points (0.8%) to 24,021.65 on June 24
  • 02/Brent crude oil crashed 4% to $74.02 per barrel, its lowest since February 28, easing inflation fears
  • 03/FIIs sold ₹1,843 crore while DIIs bought ₹3,637 crore; VIX fell 4% to 13.4 as fear eased

Indian equity markets rebounded sharply on Wednesday, June 24, 2026, erasing the previous session's losses. The BSE Sensex surged 790.54 points, or 1.04%, to close at 76,991.22.

The NSE Nifty 50 gained 197.55 points, or 0.8%, to end at 24,021.65. The recovery was broad-based, led by banking, IT, and oil marketing stocks.

Brent crude oil extended losses, falling 4% to $74.02 per barrel. This marked its lowest level since February 28, when the US launched strikes on Iran.

"Sentiment was also supported by expectations that discounted Iranian crude could be sold to India, which would benefit the economy," said Rajesh Palviya, head of research at Axis Direct.

The Nifty IT index rose 2.05%, while the Bank Nifty climbed 1.7%. RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra told ET Now that the central bank is yet to consider interest rate hikes.

"Tuesday's fall was because of the global sell-off in technology stocks and weakness in the Korean market. Today, the reversal has helped sentiment," said Siddhartha Khemka, head of research at Motilal Oswal Financial Services.

Foreign portfolio investors remained net sellers, offloading shares worth ₹1,843 crore. Domestic institutional investors offset this with purchases worth ₹3,637 crore.

Broader markets saw modest gains. The Nifty Midcap 150 rose 0.1% and the Smallcap 250 gained 0.2%. On the BSE, 2,168 stocks advanced against 2,101 declines out of 4,430 traded.

Technically, the Nifty bounced from support around 23,800. Palviya cautioned that resistance may emerge in the 24,200-24,300 range without fresh triggers.

Across Asia, South Korea surged 3.3%, China rose 0.1%, and Hong Kong gained 0.3%. Taiwan fell 2.2% and Japan declined 0.9%. The India Volatility Index (VIX) dropped 4% to 13.4.
