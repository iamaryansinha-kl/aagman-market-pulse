Honasa Consumer Q4 FY26: Profit Doubles to Rs 69 Cr, Revenue Hits Record Rs 682 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Honasa Consumer Q4 FY26 PAT jumps to Rs 69 crore, more than doubling from the year-ago period
  • 02/Revenue hits record Rs 682 crore with 28% YoY growth; FY26 marks third straight quarter above 20%
  • 03/Board approves maiden final dividend of Rs 3 per share; FY26 full-year PAT reaches Rs 200 crore

Honasa Consumer Ltd (NSE: HONASA) reported Q4 FY26 profit after tax of Rs 69 crore. The figure more than doubled from the corresponding quarter last year.

Revenue for the quarter reached a record Rs 682 crore, up 28% year-on-year. This marked the company's highest-ever quarterly sales.

EBITDA stood at Rs 77 crore, also the highest quarterly operating profit on record. The margin improvement reflects stronger execution and cost control.

FY26 marked the third consecutive quarter of more than 20% revenue growth. Full-year profit after tax came in at Rs 200 crore.

The board approved a maiden final dividend of Rs 3 per equity share. The payout represents 51.2% of FY26 standalone PAT, subject to shareholder approval.

Chairman and CEO Varun Alagh credited improved execution across focus categories and offline distribution expansion. The company billed over 1.2 lakh outlets directly through distributors in FY26.

Younger brands grew over 40% YoY during FY26. The Derma Co brand maintained double-digit EBITDA while gaining market share.

Recently acquired men's grooming brand Reginald Men crossed an annual revenue run-rate exceeding Rs 100 crore in its first consolidated quarter. The acquisition doubled Reginald Men's revenue year-on-year.
