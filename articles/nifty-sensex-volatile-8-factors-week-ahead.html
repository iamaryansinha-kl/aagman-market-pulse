Nifty at 24,056: Will Sensex Extend Gains or Turn Volatile? 8 Key Factors to Watch This Week

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty closed at 24,056, facing a strong resistance hurdle at 24,200; breakout could open rally to 24,450-24,600 zone
  • 02/Eight factors including June F&O expiry on Tuesday, Q1 business updates, auto sales on July 1, and crude below $73 will steer market direction
  • 03/FPI selling has tapered in the second half of June, but sustained buying may take time; Bank Nifty faces resistance at 59,000-59,300

Indian markets enter the week at a crucial juncture after modest gains amid range-bound trading. The Nifty closed at 24,056, up 34.35 points, while broader markets saw profit booking. Eight key factors will dictate Dalal Street's next move.

The June F&O expiry falls on Tuesday, accompanied by quarterly portfolio rebalancing by institutional investors. Derivatives positioning has improved marginally, but expiry-related adjustments could trigger heightened volatility.

Q1 business updates will shift attention from macro factors to corporate earnings. Mayuresh Joshi, Head of Equity at Marketsmith India, expects a washout quarter. Supply chain issues, input cost inflation, and some demand softening will weigh on results.

Auto sales data for June, scheduled for July 1, is another major domestic trigger. Investors will track dispatch trends across passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, and tractors to gauge demand momentum after monsoon onset.

Foreign institutional investor flows have shown signs of easing. Dr VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments, said the relentless FPI selling appears to be over. The rupee's appreciation and India's relative attractiveness support this view.

Crude oil crashing below $73 per barrel is a huge positive for India. It eases inflationary pressures, improves the current account, and reduces input costs for multiple sectors. However, geopolitical developments could quickly reverse this.

On the technical front, Nifty faces a strong hurdle at 24,200. A sustained breakout above that level could open a rally towards 24,450-24,600. On the downside, 24,000 and 23,770 are immediate support levels.

Bank Nifty continues to outperform and trades above its key moving averages. It faces resistance in the 59,000-59,300 zone, while 57,500 and 57,000 provide strong support.
