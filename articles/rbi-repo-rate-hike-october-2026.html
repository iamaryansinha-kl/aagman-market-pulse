RBI Hikes Repo Rate 25 bps to 5.50%, First Hike Since Feb 2023

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% on October 7, 2026 — the first hike since February 2023, ending a near four-year easing era
  • 02/FY27 GDP forecast was raised to 7.1% from 6.7%, even as the FY27 CPI inflation projection climbed to 5.2% with Q3 seen at 6%
  • 03/Governor Sanjay Malhotra said rate cuts are off the table; policy action ahead can only be a rate hike or a pause

The Reserve Bank of India raised the policy repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The move ends a near four-year easing era and marks the central bank's first rate increase since February 2023.

The six-member Monetary Policy Committee, headed by Governor Sanjay Malhotra, voted unanimously for the hike. The change in policy stance to "calibrated tightening" was adopted by a 4-2 majority.

The Standing Deposit Facility rate now stands at 5.25%, with the Marginal Standing Facility rate and the bank rate at 5.75%. System liquidity remains ample, averaging a ₹5.9 lakh crore daily surplus since the previous MPC meeting.

In a notable move, the RBI raised its FY27 real GDP forecast to 7.1% from 6.7% earlier — a 40 basis point upgrade. Quarterly growth is projected at 7.2% in Q2, 6.9% in Q3, 6.8% in Q4, and 7.1% in Q1FY28.

The FY27 CPI inflation projection was raised to 5.2% from 5.1%. Retail inflation climbed to 4.8% in August from 4.5% in July, and the central bank sees Q3 inflation at 6%, Q4 at 5.7%, and Q1FY28 at 5.6%. FY27 core inflation is pegged at 4.4%.

"Rate cuts are off the table in the near term, and policy action ahead can only be a rate hike or a pause," Governor Malhotra said. The drivers: rising crude oil prices, higher food prices, an uneven and deficient monsoon, El Niño risk to the rabi crop, and renewed geopolitical tensions in West Asia.

For borrowers, calibrated tightening means EMIs on floating-rate home and corporate loans will drift higher as banks reset lending rates. Savers stand to benefit, with deposit rates likely to rise in step with the repo rate.

The RBI also announced two account aggregator measures by end-2026: interoperability among NBFC account aggregators, and bank deposit information in consolidated account statements from SEBI-regulated depositories. A technical consultative committee for financial markets will be constituted.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID THE RBI ANNOUNCE IN ITS OCTOBER 2026 MONETARY POLICY?
THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA RAISED THE POLICY REPO RATE BY 25 BASIS POINTS TO 5.50% ON OCTOBER 7, 2026. THIS IS THE FIRST RATE INCREASE SINCE FEBRUARY 2023. THE MONETARY POLICY COMMITTEE VOTED UNANIMOUSLY FOR THE HIKE, AND THE POLICY STANCE CHANGED TO "CALIBRATED TIGHTENING" BY A 4-2 MAJORITY.
WHY DID THE RBI HIKE RATES DESPITE RAISING ITS GROWTH FORECAST?
THE RBI CHOSE INFLATION INSURANCE OVER GROWTH SUPPORT. FY27 CPI INFLATION IS NOW PROJECTED AT 5.2%, UP FROM 5.1% EARLIER, WITH Q3 INFLATION SEEN AT 6%. RETAIL INFLATION ROSE TO 4.8% IN AUGUST FROM 4.5% IN JULY. RISING CRUDE OIL PRICES, HIGHER FOOD PRICES, A DEFICIENT MONSOON, EL NINO RISK TO THE RABI CROP, AND WEST ASIA TENSIONS DROVE THE DECISION.
WHAT DOES "CALIBRATED TIGHTENING" MEAN FOR BORROWERS AND SAVERS?
CALIBRATED TIGHTENING MEANS THE RBI'S POLICY ACTION AHEAD CAN ONLY BE A RATE HIKE OR A PAUSE, WITH RATE CUTS OFF THE TABLE IN THE NEAR TERM. EMIS ON FLOATING-RATE HOME AND CORPORATE LOANS ARE LIKELY TO RISE AS BANKS RESET LENDING RATES HIGHER. SAVERS STAND TO BENEFIT, AS DEPOSIT RATES ARE EXPECTED TO CLIMB IN LINE WITH THE 5.50% REPO RATE.
WHAT IS THE RBI'S FY27 GROWTH AND INFLATION OUTLOOK?
FY27 REAL GDP GROWTH IS FORECAST AT 7.1%, RAISED FROM 6.7% EARLIER — A 40 BASIS POINT UPGRADE. QUARTERLY GDP IS SEEN AT 7.2% IN Q2, 6.9% IN Q3, 6.8% IN Q4, AND 7.1% IN Q1FY28. FY27 CPI INFLATION IS PROJECTED AT 5.2%, WITH QUARTERLY READINGS OF 4.9% IN Q2, 6% IN Q3, 5.7% IN Q4, AND 5.6% IN Q1FY28. FY27 CORE INFLATION IS PEGGED AT 4.4%.
WHAT OTHER MEASURES DID THE RBI ANNOUNCE ALONGSIDE THE RATE HIKE?
BY END-2026, NBFC ACCOUNT AGGREGATORS WILL BECOME INTEROPERABLE, ALLOWING USERS TO AGGREGATE FINANCIAL INFORMATION ACROSS ACCOUNT AGGREGATORS THROUGH A SINGLE ONBOARDING. SEBI-REGULATED DEPOSITORIES WILL ALSO BE ABLE TO INCLUDE BANK DEPOSIT INFORMATION IN CONSOLIDATED ACCOUNT STATEMENTS. THE RBI WILL ALSO CONSTITUTE A TECHNICAL CONSULTATIVE COMMITTEE FOR FINANCIAL MARKETS. SYSTEM LIQUIDITY REMAINS AMPLE, WITH AN AVERAGE DAILY SURPLUS OF ₹5.9 LAKH CRORE SINCE THE PREVIOUS MPC. GLOSSARY: Repo Rate: The interest rate at which the RBI lends to commercial banks, and the anchor for all lending and deposit rates in the economy. A hike makes loans costlier and deposits more rewarding. Monetary Policy Committee (MPC): The six-member RBI body that sets the policy rate, headed by the Governor. A unanimous vote means all members backed the decision. Calibrated Tightening: An official policy stance signalling that the central bank's bias is toward raising rates further or holding them high, rather than cutting — here adopted by a 4-2 majority. Standing Deposit Facility (SDF): The RBI's tool for absorbing surplus liquidity from banks without collateral, set 25 basis points below the repo rate at 5.25% after this decision. Marginal Standing Facility (MSF): The emergency overnight borrowing window for banks, set 25 basis points above the repo rate at 5.75% after this decision. Core Inflation: Consumer price inflation excluding food and fuel — the stickier, demand-driven component the RBI watches when setting rates. Projected at 4.4% for FY27. Account Aggregator (AA): A regulated framework that lets individuals share financial data across institutions with consent. Interoperability means one AA login can fetch data held through another.
