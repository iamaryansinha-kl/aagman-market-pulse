Sensex Nifty Slide 0.8% on Crude Shock; FIIs Net Sell ₹2,468 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex fell 647 points (0.83%) and Nifty dropped 213 points (0.88%) at the open on April 30 amid crude oil surge and FII selling
  • 02/Brent crude briefly breached $120 per barrel on Strait of Hormuz blockade fears, while FIIs sold ₹2,468 crore on April 29
  • 03/Friday May 1 is a market holiday for Maharashtra Day; traders face a long weekend amid geopolitical uncertainty

Benchmark indices opened sharply lower on Thursday morning. The Sensex dropped 647.03 points or 0.83% to 76,849.33. The Nifty 50 fell 212.55 points or 0.88% to 23,965.10.

Gift Nifty at 24,145 had already signalled a gap-down of 80-90 points. Asian markets opened largely in the red, mirroring overnight weakness on Wall Street.

Brent crude oil briefly breached $120 per barrel before easing to the $107-110 range. The spike was driven by supply disruption fears linked to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz by Iran.

The US Federal Reserve held its benchmark interest rate steady at 3.50% to 3.75% for the third straight meeting. The decision drew strong dissents among policymakers and sent a hawkish signal to markets.

Foreign Institutional Investors sold Indian equities worth ₹2,468 crore on April 29. Domestic Institutional Investors partially offset this with net purchases of ₹2,262 crore.

"Brent crude at $120 threatens to worsen India's macros; the downside risk to growth and upside risk to inflation will rise if crude price remains elevated at this level," said Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments Limited.

The Indian rupee opened weaker by 17 paise at 95.02 per dollar versus the previous close of 94.85. India VIX held near 17.43, reflecting continued market uncertainty.

Markets will remain closed on Friday, May 1, for Maharashtra Day. With geopolitical tensions and election outcomes pending, traders are unlikely to carry overnight positions through the long weekend.

On the Nifty, Shriram Finance fell the most at 2.42% to ₹933.65. IndiGo declined 2.31% and Axis Bank shed 2.15%. Bajaj Finance led gainers with a 2.53% rise to ₹953.55.

"Market sentiment remains fragile and highly news-driven, with crude oil volatility, geopolitical developments, and institutional flows acting as key drivers," said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money.
