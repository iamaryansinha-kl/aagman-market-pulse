India Gross FDI FY26: CEA Nageswaran Sees Over $90 Billion, 2% GDP

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Chief Economic Adviser V Anantha Nageswaran estimates gross FDI in FY26 to exceed $90 billion, breaking the $70-80 billion range of the past four years
  • 02/Gross FDI inflows reached $88.3 billion during April-February FY26, rising 18.1% YoY; net FDI improved to $6.3 billion from $1.5 billion a year ago
  • 03/If achieved, FY26 would be the first full fiscal where FDI hits 2% of GDP; manufacturing and services led sectoral inflows

Chief Economic Adviser V Anantha Nageswaran expects India to close FY26 with gross FDI exceeding $90 billion. FDI inflows for April-February already crossed $88.3 billion.

This would mark the first time FDI for a full fiscal reaches 2% of GDP. India has been stuck in the $70-80 billion range for the past four years.

"FDI in April-February exceeded $88 billion and there is one more month in the year. Going by this trend, FY26 is likely to close with over $90 billion with northward bias," Nageswaran told The Hindu BusinessLine.

Gross FDI during April-February FY26 rose 18.1% year-on-year to $88.3 billion. Net FDI improved sharply to $6.3 billion from $1.5 billion a year ago.

Net FDI turned positive in February after six straight months in negative territory. Higher gross inflows and lower repatriations drove the turnaround.

Manufacturing, computer services, financial services, business services, and communication services attracted over two-thirds of total equity inflows. Singapore, the US, Mauritius, Japan, and the Netherlands contributed around three-fourths.

Greenfield project announcements reached $65 billion during April-January FY26. Top investors included Amazon, Microsoft, Google, General Catalyst, and MUFG Bank.

India moved to 15th position among global FDI recipients in 2024 from 16th in 2023, per UNCTAD's World Investment Report 2025.

WHAT WAS THE YEAR-ON-YEAR GROWTH IN GROSS FDI DURING APRIL-FEBRUARY FY26?
GROSS FDI INCREASED BY 18.1% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO $88.3 BILLION DURING APRIL-FEBRUARY FY26. NET FDI ROSE TO $6.3 BILLION FROM $1.5 BILLION A YEAR AGO.
WHICH SECTORS AND COUNTRIES WERE THE TOP CONTRIBUTORS TO FDI INFLOWS?
MANUFACTURING, COMPUTER SERVICES, FINANCIAL SERVICES, BUSINESS SERVICES, AND COMMUNICATION SERVICES ACCOUNTED FOR MORE THAN TWO-THIRDS OF TOTAL EQUITY INFLOWS. SINGAPORE, THE US, MAURITIUS, JAPAN, AND THE NETHERLANDS WERE THE MAJOR SOURCE COUNTRIES, CONTRIBUTING AROUND THREE-FOURTHS OF TOTAL INFLOWS.
WHAT POLICY MEASURES HAS THE GOVERNMENT TAKEN TO BOOST FDI?
THE REGULATORY COMPLIANCE BURDEN INITIATIVE HAS CUT OVER 42,000 COMPLIANCES UNDER 670 ACTS NATIONWIDE. THE JAN VISHWAS ACT OF 2023 DECRIMINALISED 183 PROVISIONS ACROSS 42 CENTRAL ACTS, AND A SIMILAR 2026 ACT REPLACES IMPRISONMENT WITH CIVIL PENALTIES FOR OVER 780 PROVISIONS UNDER 79 CENTRAL ACTS.
