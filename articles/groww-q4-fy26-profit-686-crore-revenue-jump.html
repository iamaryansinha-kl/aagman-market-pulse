Groww Q4 Profit Jumps 95% to ₹686.4 Crore on 87% Revenue Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Groww Q4 FY26 net profit jumped 95% to ₹686.4 crore versus ₹351.7 crore last year
  • 02/Revenue surged 87% to ₹1,058 crore from ₹565 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 03/The fintech platform saw strong growth in user base and trading activity

Groww, the Bengaluru-based fintech platform, reported strong Q4 FY26 results. Net profit jumped 95% year-on-year to ₹686.4 crore compared to ₹351.7 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue surged 87% to ₹1,058 crore from ₹565 crore in the year-ago quarter. The growth was driven by increased trading volumes and expansion in user base.

The company operates a discount brokerage platform targeting retail investors. It has emerged as one of India's largest stockbroking platforms by active client base.

User acquisition remained robust during the quarter. Higher market volatility contributed to increased trading activity across equity and derivatives segments.

Groww competes with Zerodha, Upstox, and traditional brokerages. The company has diversified into mutual funds distribution and digital gold.

The strong profit growth indicates improving unit economics. Revenue per user has increased alongside operational leverage benefits.

The results come ahead of the company's potential IPO plans. Groww has been reportedly exploring a public listing in the coming years.
