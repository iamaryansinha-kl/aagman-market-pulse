India Gold Investment Tops Jewellery For First Time Ever in Q1 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's gold investment demand surged 52% YoY to 82 metric tons in Q1 2026, surpassing jewellery consumption for the first time on record
  • 02/Jewellery demand fell 19.5% to 66 tons as prices rallied; total gold consumption still rose 10.2% to 151 tons
  • 03/Gold ETF inflows jumped 186% to a record 20 tons; WGC expects investment demand to stay strong in coming quarters

India's gold investment demand surpassed jewellery consumption for the first time on record in the March 2026 quarter. The World Gold Council reported the historic shift on April 29, 2026.

Investment demand surged 52% year-on-year to 82 metric tons. Jewellery demand fell 19.5% to 66 tons during the same period.

Total gold consumption in India rose 10.2% to 151 metric tons. Investment demand accounted for 54.3% of total consumption, a historic high.

Gold ETF inflows jumped 186% to a record 20 tons. Investors shifted away from equities amid subdued stock market returns.

Domestic gold prices have nearly doubled since January 2025. India's Nifty 50 index gained only 2.4% in the same period.

"For the first time investment demand surpassed jewellery demand," said Sachin Jain, chief executive of WGC's Indian operations.

"Investment demand will become increasingly prominent in the coming quarters, with both financial and retail investors showing more interest in gold." - Sachin Jain, CEO, WGC India.

Investment demand typically accounts for about a quarter of India's gold consumption. The March quarter marks a historic shift in buyer behavior.

Weak equity performance has driven investors toward gold. The trend is likely to continue through coming quarters, Jain said.
