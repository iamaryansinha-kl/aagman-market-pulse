Dixon Tech Shares Jump 4% on Government Nod to Form JV With Vivo

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Dixon Technologies is getting a major partner.

Shares of the electronics manufacturing services company jumped 4% after the government approved its joint venture with Chinese smartphone maker Vivo. The JV will manufacture smartphones in India under the production-linked incentive scheme, marking a significant step in Vivo's localisation efforts.

Vivo has been under regulatory scrutiny in India. The Chinese smartphone maker faced investigations over alleged fund remittance violations and ownership structures. The government has been pushing Chinese companies to localise manufacturing and reduce dependence on imports from China.

The JV structure addresses regulatory concerns. By partnering with Dixon, an Indian company, Vivo can demonstrate compliance with India's push for domestic manufacturing. The arrangement also aligns with the government's broader strategy of encouraging Chinese companies to operate through joint ventures with Indian partners.

Dixon will benefit from access to Vivo's manufacturing technology and order volumes. The company is already one of India's largest EMS players, with contracts from brands like Samsung, Xiaomi, and Motorola. Adding Vivo significantly expands its smartphone manufacturing capacity and revenue potential.

The PLI scheme will provide additional incentives. The government offers cashbacks based on incremental sales of manufactured goods. For smartphones, the PLI has been a major driver of India's emergence as the world's second-largest mobile phone manufacturer.

The deal comes amid broader India-China tensions. While political relations remain strained, economic cooperation in manufacturing continues. The government has allowed Chinese companies to participate in PLI schemes provided they meet localisation requirements and partner with Indian firms.
