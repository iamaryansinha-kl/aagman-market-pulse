Meesho Q4 Results: Loss Narrows 88% to ₹166 Crore, Revenue Jumps 47%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Meesho reports Q4FY26 net loss narrows 88% to ₹166 crore from ₹1,368 crore in the same period last year
  • 02/Revenue surges 47% driven by higher user engagement and improved monetisation on the platform
  • 03/The sharp reduction in losses signals a strengthening path toward profitability for the e-commerce platform

Meesho has reported its financial results for the fourth quarter of FY26. The e-commerce platform narrowed its net loss by 88% to ₹166 crore. Last year, the company posted a loss of ₹1,368 crore in the same quarter.

Revenue for the quarter jumped 47% compared to the year-ago period. The growth came on the back of higher user engagement across the platform.

The Bengaluru-based company has been aggressively scaling its operations while cutting down on cash burn. The latest numbers reflect improved unit economics and better cost management.

Higher user engagement translated into increased order volumes and better monetisation. The platform connects sellers with buyers across tier-2 and tier-3 cities in India.

Meesho operates a zero-commission model for sellers, earning through logistics and advertising services. The revenue jump suggests these monetisation streams are gaining traction at scale.

The e-commerce sector in India has seen intense competition from Amazon India and Flipkart. Meesho has differentiated itself by targeting price-sensitive consumers in smaller towns.

Investors and analysts will watch the trajectory of losses in coming quarters. A sustained reduction in burn rate could position Meesho for eventual profitability and potential public market interest.
