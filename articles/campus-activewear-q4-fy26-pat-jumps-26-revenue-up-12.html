Campus Activewear Q4 FY26: PAT Jumps 26% to ₹44.1 Cr, Revenue Up 12%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Campus Activewear Q4 FY26 PAT jumps 25.8% YoY to ₹44.1 crore on revenue of ₹455.6 crore
  • 02/FY26 revenue crosses ₹1,770 crore mark at ₹1,774.1 crore; EBITDA margin expands 145 bps to 17.5%
  • 03/Stock surged 6.69% to ₹251.85 on May 26; watch raw material inflation and margin trajectory

Campus Activewear Ltd (NSE: CAMPUS) reported Q4 FY26 PAT of ₹44.1 crore, up 25.8% from ₹35.1 crore in Q4 FY25. The board announced results on May 25, 2026.

Revenue from operations rose 12.3% YoY to ₹455.6 crore in Q4 FY26. The figure stood at ₹405.7 crore in the year-ago quarter.

EBITDA grew 15.4% YoY to ₹88.5 crore. EBITDA margin expanded to 19.2% from 18.7% in Q4 FY25. PAT margin improved to 9.6% from 8.5%.

For the full year FY26, revenue crossed ₹1,774.1 crore, up 11.4% YoY. Sales volumes rose 4.2% to 26 million pairs. Average selling price climbed 6.9% to ₹683 from ₹639 in FY25.

FY26 EBITDA stood at ₹314.7 crore with margin at 17.5%. PAT for the full year came in at ₹150.1 crore. PAT margin improved to 8.4%.

CEO Nikhil Agarwal said growth was driven by deeper distribution, higher online sales, and a stronger product mix. Sneaker demand and traction in women’s and kids’ categories supported positioning as a family footwear brand.

The management flagged inflationary pressure in certain raw materials amid global geopolitical developments. Price hikes across selected SKUs are being used to manage the impact.

Campus Activewear shares closed at ₹251.85 on May 26, up 6.69%. The stock trades at a P/E of 51.17 and P/B of 9.53. RSI (14) is at 43.3, suggesting neutral momentum.
