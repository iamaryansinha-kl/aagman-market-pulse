TCS Q1 Results: Profit Rises 5% YoY to Rs 13,349 Crore; Company Declares Rs 12 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Tata Consultancy Services has kicked off the IT earnings season.

The country's largest software exporter reported a 5% year-on-year rise in June quarter profit to Rs 13,349 crore. Revenue growth was modest, reflecting the ongoing macroeconomic headwinds that have slowed technology spending by global clients.

The results were largely in line with analyst expectations. TCS had guided for cautious growth this year, and the Q1 numbers confirm that the IT sector is still navigating a period of subdued demand from key markets in North America and Europe.

The company declared an interim dividend of Rs 12 per share, maintaining its track record of consistent shareholder payouts. TCS is known for its generous dividend policy, and the declaration will provide income to millions of retail and institutional investors.

Operating margins came under pressure. The company reported a margin of 24.2%, down slightly from the previous quarter. Wage inflation, subcontractor costs, and investments in generative AI capabilities have been compressing margins across the industry.

The deal pipeline remains healthy but conversion is slower. TCS reported strong total contract value in recent quarters, but clients are taking longer to convert these wins into active projects. The lag between signing and revenue recognition has extended.

The stock market had already priced in modest results. TCS shares had dipped ahead of the earnings announcement, reflecting investor caution. The post-results movement will depend on management commentary about the outlook for the remainder of the year.
