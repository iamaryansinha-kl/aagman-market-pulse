Tata Motors Files Record 144 Patents in FY26, Total Grants Top 650

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Motors Ltd (NSE: TATAMOTORS) filed 144 patent applications in FY26, its highest ever in a single year
  • 02/The company secured 15 patent grants during the year, pushing cumulative granted patents past the 650 mark
  • 03/FY26 filings also include 21 design applications and 35 copyright applications, spanning EV and hydrogen ICE tech

Tata Motors Ltd filed a record 144 patent applications in FY26. This marks the company's highest number of filings in a single financial year.

The filings align with Tata Motors' strategic priorities. These include enhanced vehicle safety, improved reliability, optimised total cost of ownership, and superior occupant comfort.

"The record number of patent applications filed in FY26 is a testament to the passion, creativity, and technical excellence of our engineering teams...As we look ahead, we remain committed to harnessing our innovation capabilities to serve the long-term interests of our customers, communities, and the nation," said Aniruddha Kulkarni, Vice President and Head, Engineering at TML.

The company also strengthened its broader IP portfolio. It filed 21 design applications and 35 copyright applications during the year.

TML secured 15 patent grants in FY26. This pushed the cumulative number of granted patents to over 650.

The patent push reflects Tata Motors' focus on future-ready mobility. This spans emerging technologies such as electric vehicles and hydrogen-based internal combustion engines.

Tata Motors shares closed at ₹825.65 on April 28, 2026. The stock has gained approximately 18% over the past year, outperforming the Nifty Auto index.

The strong R&D output signals sustained investment in product development. It also highlights the company's intent to protect its technological edge across commercial and passenger vehicle segments.
