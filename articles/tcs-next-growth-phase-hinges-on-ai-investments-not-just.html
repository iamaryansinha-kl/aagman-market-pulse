TCS' Next Growth Phase Hinges on AI Investments, Not Just Deal Momentum

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

TCS is looking beyond deal momentum for its next growth phase.

The country's largest IT services company is betting that artificial intelligence investments, not just traditional deal wins, will drive its next phase of expansion. The company is spending heavily on generative AI capabilities, training its workforce, and building solutions that embed AI into client operations.

The shift is necessary because deal momentum alone is not enough. TCS reported modest Q1 revenue growth, and the pipeline, while healthy, is converting slower than in the past. Clients are cautious about large transformational projects, and pricing pressure remains intense.

AI offers a different growth model. Instead of selling hours and headcount, TCS can sell AI-powered outcomes — automated processes, predictive analytics, and intelligent customer interfaces. These solutions command higher margins and create stickier client relationships.

TCS has launched several AI initiatives. The company has trained over 5 lakh employees on AI fundamentals, built proprietary large language models for enterprise use, and created AI accelerators for specific industries like banking, healthcare, and retail.

The challenge is monetisation. While AI investments are substantial, converting them into revenue is taking time. Clients are still experimenting with AI, and many are not yet ready to pay premium prices for AI-driven solutions. The transition from pilot to production is slower than hoped.

Competitors are making similar bets. Infosys, Wipro, and HCLTech are all investing in AI, and global consultancies like Accenture and Deloitte are pushing hard into the space. TCS needs to differentiate through scale, domain expertise, and execution speed.
