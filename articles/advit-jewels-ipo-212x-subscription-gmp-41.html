Advit Jewels IPO 212x Subscribed: Hottest Issue of 2026; GMP at 41%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Advit Jewels IPO closes at 212.62 times subscription, making it the most subscribed issue of 2026 so far
  • 02/NII portion subscribed 536.38x, QIB 174.98x, retail 95.29x; grey market premium at 41%
  • 03/Issue price fixed at Rs 138 per share; listing scheduled for July 1 on NSE and BSE SME platforms

Advit Jewels Ltd's IPO closed with a massive 212.62 times subscription. The issue, which opened on June 23, 2026, became the most sought-after public offering of the year so far.

The NII portion was subscribed 536.38 times. The QIB quota received bids for 174.98 times the shares reserved. Retail investors subscribed their portion 95.29 times.

The IPO was priced at a fixed rate of Rs 138 per share. It comprised a fresh issue of 1.19 crore equity shares with no offer for sale component.

Proceeds are earmarked for working capital requirements, repayment of borrowings, and general corporate purposes. The company has no plans for an offer for sale by existing shareholders.

Advit Jewels manufactures handcrafted Kundan, Polki, diamond, and studded jewellery under the Rambhajo brand. It serves B2B customers including dealers and retailers, as well as made-to-order buyers.

Revenue nearly doubled to Rs 124.9 crore in FY25 from Rs 69.4 crore in FY24. Net profit rose to Rs 25.4 crore from Rs 14.7 crore in the same period.

For the nine months ended December FY26, the company posted revenue of Rs 123.8 crore. Profit after tax stood at Rs 25.4 crore, indicating sustained momentum.

Brokerage firm Equivision assigned a Subscribe rating to the IPO. It cited strong earnings growth, improving profitability, and the company's position in the organised jewellery market.

The grey market premium stood at around 41% ahead of the issue. The shares are scheduled to list on July 1, 2026, on the NSE and BSE SME platforms.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS THE SUBSCRIPTION STATUS OF THE ADVIT JEWELS IPO?
ADVIT JEWELS' IPO CLOSED WITH A TOTAL SUBSCRIPTION OF 212.62 TIMES. THE NII PORTION WAS SUBSCRIBED 536.38 TIMES, THE QIB PORTION 174.98 TIMES, AND THE RETAIL PORTION 95.29 TIMES.
WHAT IS THE ISSUE PRICE AND LISTING DATE OF ADVIT JEWELS?
THE IPO WAS PRICED AT A FIXED ISSUE PRICE OF RS 138 PER SHARE. THE SHARES ARE SCHEDULED TO LIST ON JULY 1, 2026, ON THE NSE AND BSE SME PLATFORMS.
WHAT IS THE GREY MARKET PREMIUM (GMP) OF ADVIT JEWELS?
AHEAD OF THE ISSUE, ADVIT JEWELS SHARES WERE COMMANDING A GREY MARKET PREMIUM OF APPROXIMATELY 41% OVER THE ISSUE PRICE OF RS 138. HOWEVER, GMP IS AN UNOFFICIAL INDICATOR AND MAY NOT REFLECT ACTUAL LISTING PERFORMANCE.
WHAT IS THE FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE OF ADVIT JEWELS?
REVENUE ROSE TO RS 124.9 CRORE IN FY25 FROM RS 69.4 CRORE IN FY24. NET PROFIT INCREASED TO RS 25.4 CRORE FROM RS 14.7 CRORE. FOR 9M FY26, REVENUE WAS RS 123.8 CRORE WITH PAT OF RS 25.4 CRORE.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS FOR INVESTORS IN ADVIT JEWELS?
KEY RISKS INCLUDE FLUCTUATIONS IN GOLD AND DIAMOND PRICES, CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION, AND DEPENDENCE ON THE DISCRETIONARY SPENDING PATTERNS OF JEWELLERY BUYERS. BROKERAGE EQUIVISION HAS ALSO FLAGGED THESE FACTORS IN ITS SUBSCRIBE RATING.
