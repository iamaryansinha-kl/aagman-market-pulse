El Nino May Last Until January 2027, Threaten Kharif Crops: IMD

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IMD predicts El Nino may last until January 2027, threatening both kharif and rabi crop seasons with deficient rainfall
  • 02/Monsoon forecast at 92% of long-period average of 87 cm; south-west monsoon onset over Andamans expected around May 14-16
  • 03/Positive Indian Ocean Dipole may develop late in monsoon season but El Nino impact could overshadow even a near-positive IOD

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) on May 1, 2026 said most global models forecast El Nino emergence during India's monsoon season. The weather event may persist until at least January 2027, potentially lowering rainfall during both kharif and rabi seasons.

Mrutyunjay Mohapatra, Director General of IMD, stated that ENSO-neutral conditions over the equatorial Pacific are evolving towards El Nino. The impact of El Nino could overshadow even a near-positive Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD), he added.

Currently, neutral IOD conditions prevail over the Indian Ocean. Climate models indicate positive IOD may develop towards the end of the monsoon season. India's kharif sowing begins around June 1, while rabi planting starts in October.

IMD has predicted below-normal monsoon at 92% of the long-period average of 87 cm for 2026. Last year, the south-west monsoon hit Kerala on May 24, the earliest since 2009. The Andaman onset was also a week earlier on May 13 against the normal May 20.

Kalyan Goswami, Director General of Agrochem Federation of India, said good rainfall in May may help boost kharif sowing by ensuring adequate soil moisture. "We expect higher area coverage for pulses and oilseeds and lowering irrigation costs for farmers," he said.

Ankur Aggarwal, Chairman of CropLife India, warned the kharif season faces two parallel risks. The 92% monsoon projection due to El Nino could lead to delayed sowing, uneven crop establishment, and heightened pest pressure including blast in rice and fall armyworm in maize.

Above-normal heatwave days are likely in parts of the Himalayas foothills, east coast states, Gujarat and Maharashtra in May. Average rainfall over the country during May is most likely to be above normal, IMD said.

The West Asia conflict is compounding risks by disrupting upstream agrochemical supply chains. Key inputs like methanol, ammonia, LPG and petrochemical derivatives face cost pressures and potential tightness.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW LONG IS EL NINO EXPECTED TO LAST ACCORDING TO IMD?
MOST GLOBAL MODELS FORECAST EL NINO WILL EMERGE DURING INDIA'S MONSOON SEASON AND MAY PERSIST UNTIL AT LEAST JANUARY 2027, POTENTIALLY IMPACTING BOTH KHARIF AND RABI CROP SEASONS.
WHAT IS THE MONSOON FORECAST FOR 2026?
IMD HAS PREDICTED BELOW-NORMAL MONSOON RAINFALL AT 92% OF THE LONG-PERIOD AVERAGE OF 87 CM FOR THE YEAR 2026.
WHEN IS THE SOUTH-WEST MONSOON EXPECTED TO ARRIVE?
THE SOUTH-WEST MONSOON MAY SET IN OVER THE ANDAMAN AND NICOBAR ISLANDS AROUND MAY 14-16, 2026, COMPARED TO THE NORMAL DATE OF MAY 20.
WHO ARE THE KEY EXPERTS QUOTED ON THIS FORECAST?
MRUTYUNJAY MOHAPATRA, DIRECTOR GENERAL OF IMD, MADE THE PRIMARY FORECAST. KALYAN GOSWAMI OF AGROCHEM FEDERATION OF INDIA AND ANKUR AGGARWAL OF CROPLIFE INDIA COMMENTED ON AGRICULTURAL IMPLICATIONS.
WHAT CROPS ARE MOST AT RISK FROM THE EL NINO FORECAST?
KHARIF CROPS INCLUDING PADDY, PULSES, OILSEEDS, COTTON, MAIZE AND SOYBEAN FACE RISKS OF DELAYED SOWING, UNEVEN ESTABLISHMENT, AND HEIGHTENED PEST AND DISEASE PRESSURE. RABI CROPS LIKE WHEAT AND MUSTARD MAY ALSO BE AFFECTED DUE TO REDUCED SOIL MOISTURE.
