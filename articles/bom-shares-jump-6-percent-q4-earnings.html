Bank of Maharashtra Q4: Profit Jumps 35% to ₹2,014 Cr, Stock Up 6%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bank of Maharashtra Q4 FY26 net profit surges 34.9% YoY to ₹2,014 crore, up from ₹1,493 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/NII grows 18.8% to ₹3,702 crore; gross NPA improves to 1.45% and net NPA hits record low of 0.13%
  • 03/Board proposes final dividend of ₹1.20 per share; total FY26 dividend at ₹2.20 including interim payout of ₹1

Bank of Maharashtra Ltd (NSE: MAHABANK) reported Q4 FY2026 net profit of ₹2,014 crore on April 20, 2026. The figure marks a 34.9% jump from ₹1,493 crore in Q4 FY2025.

Net interest income for the quarter rose 18.8% year-on-year to ₹3,702 crore from ₹3,116 crore. Operating profit grew 16.9% to ₹2,946 crore.

Asset quality improved sharply during the quarter. Gross NPA declined to 1.45% from 1.74% a year ago, while net NPA hit a record low of 0.13%.

Total business grew 17.5% to ₹6.43 lakh crore as of March 31, 2026. Total deposits rose 14.1% to ₹3.51 lakh crore and gross advances climbed 21.7% to ₹2.92 lakh crore.

For the full year FY2026, net profit reached a record ₹7,019 crore, up 27.2% from ₹5,520 crore in FY2025. Net interest income for the year stood at ₹13,664 crore, a 17.1% increase.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.20 per share for FY2026, taking the total dividend to ₹2.20 including the interim payout of ₹1. The bank also approved raising up to ₹7,500 crore through equity.

Bank of Maharashtra shares surged over 6% on April 20, hitting an intraday high of ₹77 on the NSE. The stock has gained nearly 20% so far in 2026.

The bank's provision coverage ratio stood at 98.59%, among the highest in the industry. However, slippages increased sequentially, warranting close monitoring in coming quarters.
