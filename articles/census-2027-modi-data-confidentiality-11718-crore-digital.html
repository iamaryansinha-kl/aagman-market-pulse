Census 2027: Modi Assures Data Confidentiality, ₹11,718 Cr Budget

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PM Narendra Modi assured citizens that Census 2027 data will remain fully confidential and protected by robust digital security in his Mann Ki Baat address on April 26, 2026
  • 02/The Union Cabinet approved ₹11,718.24 crore for Census 2027 on December 12, 2025 — over 5x the ₹2,200 crore spent in 2011
  • 03/House-listing for 12 million families is already complete; population enumeration begins February 2027 covering an estimated 140 crore people

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has assured citizens that all information shared for Census 2027 will remain completely secure and confidential. In his monthly Mann Ki Baat radio address on April 26, 2026, he urged every Indian to participate and make the exercise a success.

The Census 2027 is the largest administrative and statistical exercise in the world. The Union Cabinet approved a budget of ₹11,718.24 crore on December 12, 2025, more than five times the ₹2,200 crore spent on the 2011 Census.

This marks India's first fully digital census after a 16-year gap. The last decadal census was conducted in 2011 when India's population stood at 121.09 crore. The 2021 cycle was postponed due to COVID-19.

The exercise is being conducted in two phases. Phase 1 — Houselisting and Housing Census — runs from April to September 2026. Phase 2 — Population Enumeration — is scheduled for February 2027.

So far, house-listing for approximately 12 million families has been completed. About 30 lakh field functionaries including enumerators, supervisors, and trainers will be deployed across the country.

Census 2027 will generate an estimated 1.02 crore man-days of employment. Around 18,600 technical personnel will be engaged for roughly 550 days to support digital data handling and monitoring.

For the first time, citizens can self-enumerate via the portal se.census.gov.in available in 16 regional languages. President Droupadi Murmu, PM Modi, and Home Minister Amit Shah have already completed their self-enumeration.

Snow-bound regions including Ladakh, Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand will conduct population enumeration in September 2026 instead of February 2027.

The census will include caste enumeration for the first time since 1931, approved by the Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs on April 30, 2025. Data will be captured electronically during Phase 2.

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw called Census 2027 a "historic leap in India's data governance." The digital design includes mobile apps for Android and iOS, a Census Management & Monitoring System (CMMS) portal, and Census-as-a-Service (CaaS) for ministries.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE BUDGET FOR CENSUS 2027?
THE UNION CABINET APPROVED ₹11,718.24 CRORE FOR CENSUS 2027 ON DECEMBER 12, 2025. THIS IS OVER FIVE TIMES THE ₹2,200 CRORE BUDGET OF THE 2011 CENSUS.
WHEN WILL CENSUS 2027 BE CONDUCTED?
PHASE 1 — HOUSELISTING AND HOUSING CENSUS — RUNS FROM APRIL TO SEPTEMBER 2026. PHASE 2 — POPULATION ENUMERATION — IS SCHEDULED FOR FEBRUARY 2027, WITH SNOW-BOUND AREAS COMPLETING IT IN SEPTEMBER 2026.
HOW MANY PEOPLE WILL CENSUS 2027 COVER?
THE CENSUS IS EXPECTED TO COVER OVER 140 CRORE PEOPLE ACROSS INDIA, MAKING IT THE WORLD'S LARGEST ADMINISTRATIVE AND STATISTICAL EXERCISE.
WHAT MAKES CENSUS 2027 DIFFERENT FROM PREVIOUS CENSUSES?
IT IS INDIA'S FIRST FULLY DIGITAL CENSUS USING MOBILE APPS, SELF-ENUMERATION VIA SE.CENSUS.GOV.IN, REAL-TIME MONITORING THROUGH CMMS, AND ELECTRONIC CASTE ENUMERATION FOR THE FIRST TIME SINCE 1931.
IS THE DATA COLLECTED DURING CENSUS 2027 CONFIDENTIAL?
YES. ALL DATA IS COLLECTED UNDER THE CENSUS ACT, 1948 AND IS STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL. PM MODI ASSURED CITIZENS THAT INFORMATION WILL BE PROTECTED BY ROBUST DIGITAL SECURITY AND ENCRYPTION MEASURES.
