FM Asks Lenders to Design Credit Repayments Around Cash Flows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Finance Minister asks lenders to design credit products with repayments linked to borrower cash flows instead of fixed EMIs
  • 02/Move targets MSMEs and seasonal businesses that face irregular income cycles and struggle with rigid repayment schedules
  • 03/Account Aggregator framework and TReDS platform expected to play key role in enabling cash-flow-based lending

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has asked lenders to move beyond standard loan products. She wants credit repayments designed around actual business cash flows and cycles.

The directive targets MSMEs, farmers, and seasonal businesses. These borrowers often face irregular income patterns that clash with fixed monthly EMIs.

Traditional term loans with rigid schedules force borrowers to hold back cash. This misalignment between repayments and revenue cycles raises default risk.

India has 5.7 crore MSMEs employing 7.5 crore people. Many lack formal income documentation but generate steady cash flows through seasonal trade.

The Account Aggregator framework now allows lenders to access real-time bank transaction data. This enables underwriting based on actual cash flows rather than collateral or credit history alone.

TReDS has already facilitated over ₹7 lakh crore in receivables financing for MSMEs. The platform proves that cash-flow-linked credit can work at scale.

Experts say cash-flow-based lending can cut defaults by matching repayments to harvest cycles, festive seasons, and project timelines. Some lenders already offer bullet repayments for agricultural loans.

The push aligns with global best practices. In Germany, banks align repayments with Mittelstand cash flows, giving firms room to invest instead of firefighting liquidity.

However, execution risks remain. Lenders need better data infrastructure and risk models that account for income volatility in informal sectors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID THE FINANCE MINISTER ASK LENDERS TO DO?
THE FINANCE MINISTER URGED LENDERS TO MOVE BEYOND STANDARD LOAN PRODUCTS AND DESIGN CREDIT WITH REPAYMENTS LINKED TO ACTUAL BORROWER CASH FLOWS AND BUSINESS CYCLES.
WHY IS CASH-FLOW-BASED LENDING IMPORTANT FOR MSMES?
INDIA HAS 5.7 CRORE MSMES WITH IRREGULAR INCOME PATTERNS. RIGID EMI SCHEDULES OFTEN CLASH WITH SEASONAL REVENUE CYCLES, RAISING DEFAULT RISK AND FORCING BORROWERS TO HOLD BACK OPERATING CASH.
WHAT TECHNOLOGY ENABLES CASH-FLOW-BASED LENDING?
THE ACCOUNT AGGREGATOR FRAMEWORK ALLOWS LENDERS TO ACCESS REAL-TIME BANK TRANSACTION DATA. TReDS HAS ALREADY FACILITATED OVER ₹7 LAKH CRORE IN RECEIVABLES FINANCING FOR MSMEs.
WHICH BORROWERS WILL BENEFIT MOST?
SEASONAL BUSINESSES, FARMERS, TRADERS, AND MSMES WITH UNEVEN CASH FLOWS WILL BENEFIT MOST. THESE BORROWERS OFTEN LACK FORMAL INCOME DOCUMENTATION BUT GENERATE STEADY REVENUE THROUGH TRADE CYCLES.
WHAT ARE THE CHALLENGES IN IMPLEMENTING THIS?
LENDERS NEED BETTER DATA INFRASTRUCTURE, RISK MODELS FOR INFORMAL SECTOR VOLATILITY, AND PRODUCT DESIGN CAPABILITY. MANY CURRENT SYSTEMS ARE BUILT FOR COLLATERAL-BASED OR FIXED-SCHEDULE LENDING.
