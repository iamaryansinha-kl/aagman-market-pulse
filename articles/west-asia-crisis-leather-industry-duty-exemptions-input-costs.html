West Asia Crisis Pushes Leather Industry Input Costs Up 60%, Seeks Duty Relief

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Leather and footwear industry faces 40-60% spike in input costs due to the ongoing West Asia crisis and Strait of Hormuz closure
  • 02/Industry has urged the commerce ministry for duty-free import of crust and finished leathers and exemptions on synthetic leather, machinery, and chemicals
  • 03/Leather exports fell 2.36% YoY to $4.26 billion in FY2026; total sector exports including non-leather goods expected at $5.6 billion

India's leather and footwear industry has sought urgent duty exemptions on critical inputs. Input costs have surged 40-60% due to the ongoing West Asia crisis.

The industry recently flagged the issue with the commerce and industry ministry. It has urged duty-free import of crust and finished leathers to boost domestic manufacturing.

Exporters have also pushed for early implementation of the proposed FLOAT scheme. FLOAT stands for Footwear and Leather Oriented Transformation.

The requested duty exemptions cover synthetic leather (PU-coated fabrics), footwear components, metal accessories, machinery, threads, moulds, toe puffs, eyelets, leather chemicals, and packaging materials.

Certain rubber chemicals, PU leather, adhesives, plastics, and shoe soles are petroleum-derived. The West Asia crisis has disrupted oil and gas shipments through the Strait of Hormuz, which Iran closed.

Other key inputs are imported from China, Korea, Indonesia, and Japan. The country's leather sector imports dipped 4.49% year-on-year to $938 million.

Leather and leather product exports fell 2.36% year-on-year to $4.26 billion in 2025-26. Including non-leather goods, total sector exports are expected to reach $5.6 billion.

Total leather sector exports stood at $5.57 billion in 2024-25. This compares to $5.38 billion in 2023-24 and $6 billion in 2022-23.

"The industry is facing a sharp increase in raw material and input costs — rising by 40-60 per cent — due to the West Asia crisis," an industry official said.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DUTY EXEMPTIONS IS THE LEATHER INDUSTRY SEEKING?
THE INDUSTRY HAS SOUGHT DUTY-FREE IMPORT OF CRUST AND FINISHED LEATHERS, PLUS EXEMPTIONS ON SYNTHETIC LEATHER (PU-COATED FABRICS), FOOTWEAR COMPONENTS, METAL ACCESSORIES, LEATHER AND FOOTWEAR MACHINERY, THREADS, MOULDS, TOE PUFFS, EYELETS, LEATHER CHEMICALS, AND PACKAGING MATERIALS.
WHY ARE INPUT COSTS RISING FOR THE LEATHER INDUSTRY?
THE ONGOING WEST ASIA CRISIS HAS PUSHED INPUT COSTS UP BY 40-60 PER CENT. THE CLOSURE OF THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ BY IRAN HAS DISRUPTED OIL AND GAS SHIPMENTS, DIRECTLY IMPACTING PETROLEUM-DERIVED INPUTS LIKE RUBBER CHEMICALS, PU LEATHER, ADHESIVES, PLASTICS, AND SHOE SOLES.
WHAT IS THE FLOAT SCHEME?
FLOAT STANDS FOR FOOTWEAR AND LEATHER ORIENTED TRANSFORMATION. IT IS A PROPOSED GOVERNMENT SCHEME COVERING THE ENTIRE PRODUCT RANGE, RAW MATERIALS, MACHINERY, AND INPUTS. THE INDUSTRY HAS URGED EARLY IMPLEMENTATION OF THIS SCHEME.
HOW HAVE LEATHER EXPORTS PERFORMED IN FY2026?
LEATHER AND LEATHER PRODUCT EXPORTS DIPPED BY 2.36 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR TO $4.26 BILLION IN 2025-26. INCLUDING NON-LEATHER GOODS, TOTAL SECTOR EXPORTS ARE EXPECTED TO REACH $5.6 BILLION. TOTAL SECTOR EXPORTS WERE $5.57 BILLION IN 2024-25.
WHICH COUNTRIES DOES INDIA IMPORT LEATHER INPUTS FROM?
CERTAIN KEY INPUTS ARE IMPORTED FROM CHINA, KOREA, INDONESIA, AND JAPAN. THE COUNTRY'S IMPORTS IN THE LEATHER SECTOR DIPPED 4.49 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR TO $938 MILLION.
