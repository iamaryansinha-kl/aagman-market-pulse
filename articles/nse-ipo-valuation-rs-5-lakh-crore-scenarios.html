NSE IPO Valuation at Rs 5 Lakh Cr: 2 Key Scenarios for Investors

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE IPO to offer 148.9 million shares via OFS, valuing exchange at roughly Rs 5 lakh crore
  • 02/Centrum pegs FY28E EPS at 36x base case for Rs 5T cap; revenue grew 24% CAGR over FY21-26
  • 03/STT hikes and RBI norms effective July 1 pose risks to derivatives volume and valuation

National Stock Exchange of India Ltd has filed its DRHP for an IPO structured entirely as an Offer for Sale. Up to 148.9 million equity shares, around 6% of outstanding equity, will be offloaded by existing shareholders.

Key sellers include State Bank of India, MS Strategic (Mauritius), Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, Aranda Investments (Mauritius) and Bank of Baroda. This signals broad-based monetisation by marquee financial institutions.

Centrum Institutional Research characterises NSE as a behemoth with about 93% market share in cash equities. It holds nearly 100% in equity futures and roughly 72% in equity index options turnover in FY26.

Assuming a market capitalisation of Rs 5 trillion, the implied valuation works out to around 36x FY28E EPS under the base case. At Rs 5.5 trillion, multiples rise to nearly 40x FY28E EPS.

Revenue from operations grew at a strong 24% CAGR over FY21-26, with EBITDA and net profit rising at 23% CAGR. Margins stayed consistently above 70% and ROE above 30% even after a softer FY26.

"We expect earnings momentum to recover from FY27 onwards, with net profit projected to grow at ~16% CAGR over FY26-FY28E," Centrum's report states. Structural drivers remain intact despite cyclical and regulatory headwinds.

Nearly 80% of NSE's revenues are linked to trading-related activities, with options alone contributing around 60% of operating revenue. This makes the franchise acutely sensitive to derivatives volumes and regulatory changes.

The Union Budget FY27 raised STT on equity futures sales from 0.02% to 0.05%. STT on options selling rose from 0.10% to 0.15%, explicitly aimed at curbing excessive short-term speculation.

Revised RBI norms effective July 1, 2026, are expected to increase capital consumption for banks and intermediaries. This could dampen leverage and trading volumes, especially in derivatives.

NSE currently services over 129 million unique registered investors and enables trading across 3,228 securities. Its Core Settlement Guarantee Fund stands at about Rs 130.8 billion, anchoring its risk architecture.

WHAT IS THE EXPECTED VALUATION OF NSE IN THE IPO?
NSE'S PROPOSED IPO COULD VALUE THE EXCHANGE AT AROUND RS 5 LAKH CRORE (RS 5 TRILLION). AT THIS MARKET CAPITALISATION, CENTRUM INSTITUTIONAL RESEARCH ESTIMATES THE IMPLIED VALUATION AT AROUND 36X FY28E EPS UNDER THE BASE CASE.
HOW MANY SHARES ARE BEING OFFERED IN THE NSE IPO?
THE IPO WILL OFFER UP TO 148.9 MILLION EQUITY SHARES VIA AN OFFER FOR SALE (OFS). THIS REPRESENTS AROUND 6% OF THE OUTSTANDING EQUITY. KEY SELLERS INCLUDE STATE BANK OF INDIA, MS STRATEGIC (MAURITIUS), CANADA PENSION PLAN INVESTMENT BOARD, ARANDA INVESTMENTS (MAURITIUS) AND BANK OF BARODA.
WHAT ARE NSE'S FINANCIAL METRICS OVER THE PAST FIVE YEARS?
REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS GREW AT A 24% CAGR OVER FY21-26, WITH EBITDA AND NET PROFIT RISING AT 23% CAGR. MARGINS CONSISTENTLY STAYED ABOVE 70% AND ROE ABOVE 30%, EVEN AFTER A SOFTER FY26.
WHAT REGULATORY RISKS COULD IMPACT NSE'S VALUATION?
THE UNION BUDGET FY27 RAISED STT ON EQUITY FUTURES FROM 0.02% TO 0.05% AND ON OPTIONS SELLING FROM 0.10% TO 0.15%. REVISED RBI NORMS EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026, ARE EXPECTED TO INCREASE CAPITAL CONSUMPTION AND POTENTIALLY DAMPEN TRADING VOLUMES.
WHAT IS THE REVENUE BREAKDOWN OF NSE?
NEARLY 80% OF NSE'S REVENUES ARE LINKED TO TRADING-RELATED ACTIVITIES. OPTIONS ALONE CONTRIBUTE AROUND 60% OF OPERATING REVENUE AND FUTURES ANOTHER 9%, MAKING THE FRANCHISE ACUTELY SENSITIVE TO DERIVATIVES VOLUMES AND REGULATORY CHANGES.
