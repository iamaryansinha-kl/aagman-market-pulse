Kalyan Jewellers Q4 Profit Jumps 118% to ₹409.5 Crore; FY26 Net Up 89%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kalyan Jewellers Q4 FY26 net profit more than doubles to ₹409.5 crore from ₹187.6 crore year-ago
  • 02/Total income surges 66% to ₹10,321.1 crore; full-year income up 43% to ₹35,950.9 crore
  • 03/Company sees strong Akshaya Tritiya demand; 507 showrooms across India, UK, US and West Asia

Kalyan Jewellers India Ltd (NSE: KALYANKJIL) reported consolidated net profit of ₹409.50 crore for Q4 FY2025-26. The figure represents a 118% jump from ₹187.6 crore in the year-ago period.

Total income for the January-March quarter surged 66% to ₹10,321.1 crore. This compares with ₹6,222.3 crore in the corresponding quarter last year.

Expenses for the quarter stood at ₹9,782.28 crore, against ₹5,971.75 crore a year earlier. The company maintained profitability despite higher operating costs.

For the full fiscal year 2025-26, net profit rose 89% to ₹1,350.39 crore. The previous year saw profit of ₹714.17 crore.

Total income for FY2025-26 increased 43% to ₹35,950.88 crore. This was up from ₹25,189.66 crore in the prior fiscal.

"We ended the previous financial year on a very strong note and have carried the momentum into the ongoing financial year," said Ramesh Kalyanaraman, Executive Director.

The company witnessed strong growth during Akshaya Tritiya sales this year. Consumer demand remains encouraging, especially around wedding purchases.

Headquartered in Thrissur, Kerala, Kalyan Jewellers operates 507 showrooms. Its retail area exceeds 12,00,000 sq ft across India, the UK, the US and West Asia.
