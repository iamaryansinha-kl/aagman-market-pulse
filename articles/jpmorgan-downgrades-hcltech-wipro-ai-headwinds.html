JPMorgan Downgrades HCLTech, Wipro on AI Headwinds: Growth Risks Ahead

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JPMorgan downgrades HCLTech, Wipro, and Tata Technologies to Underweight from Neutral, citing a two-year growth slowdown ahead
  • 02/HCLTech target price slashed 27% to ₹1,000; Wipro and Tata Tech also cut as current valuations fail to reflect demand risks
  • 03/Top picks remain TCS, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Coforge, Persistent, and Sagility; brokerage warns FY27 revenue growth estimates face further cuts

JPMorgan has downgraded HCL Technologies Ltd (NSE: HCLTECH), Wipro Ltd (NSE: WIPRO), and Tata Technologies Ltd to Underweight from Neutral. The brokerage said current stock prices have yet to capture the demand risks ahead.

The HCLTech target price was cut 27% to ₹1,000 from ₹1,370. Wipro and Tata Technologies also saw their price targets reduced.

JPMorgan warned that Indian IT firms face an "unprecedented confluence" of headwinds over the next two years. These include generative AI-led deflation, geopolitical uncertainty, and a weak business cycle.

The brokerage has lowered April-June revenue growth assumptions for all companies in its coverage. This follows delays in deal closures and slower revenue conversion.

"Accenture's print and guidance confirms that weakness is not only in 1Q27, but also likely to bleed into 2QFY27," JPMorgan analysts said in a client note.

JPMorgan expects further cuts to FY27 revenue growth forecasts. The usual first-half strength is unlikely to play out this year due to a softer start.

"Until we see AI inflation becoming a tailwind, we would prefer to be cautious on the pace of growth recovery, as well as structural growth for the industry," the note added.

Growth acceleration is unlikely even for mid-cap firms over the medium term. JPMorgan said the structural impact of AI on traditional IT services is a longer-term concern.

JPMorgan's top picks in the sector remain TCS (NSE: TCS), Infosys (NSE: INFY), Tech Mahindra, Coforge, Persistent Systems, and Sagility.

HCLTech shares ended the previous session 2% lower at ₹1,108. The stock has declined 5% in the past month and 32.2% so far in 2026.
