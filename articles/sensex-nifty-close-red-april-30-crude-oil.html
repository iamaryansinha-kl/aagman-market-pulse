Sensex, Nifty Close in Red on April 30; Crude Oil Surge Spooks Investors

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex crashed 582.86 points (0.75%) to close at 76,913.50; Nifty 50 fell 180.10 points (0.74%) to 23,997.55 on April 30, 2026
  • 02/Brent crude briefly surged above USD 120 per barrel, sending the rupee to a record low near 95.33 per dollar and spooking investors
  • 03/Only IT stocks managed gains; market breadth turned sharply negative with broad-based selling across most sectors

Indian benchmark indices ended sharply lower on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The BSE Sensex fell 582.86 points to close at 76,913.50. The NSE Nifty 50 slipped 180.10 points to end at 23,997.55.

Markets opened weak and sustained selling pressure through the entire session. Brent crude briefly surged above USD 120 per barrel, intensifying concerns over inflation and currency weakness.

The rupee weakened to a record low near 95.33 per US dollar. Bond yields rose amid risk aversion as foreign institutional investors continued to pull money out of Indian equities.

Only IT stocks managed gains on Thursday. Top Nifty gainers included Bajaj Finance, Coal India, and ONGC. Eternal, Shriram Finance, and Axis Bank led the decline.

Asian and US markets reflected cautious sentiment, with most indices trading in the red. The US Federal Reserve held rates steady but signaled a hawkish stance on inflation.

"Chair Jerome Powell acknowledged heightened uncertainty but reaffirmed the Fed's unwavering commitment to the 2% inflation target," said Umesh Sharma, CIO-Debt, The Wealth Company Mutual Fund.

Analysts flagged expiry-related volatility, sustained FII outflows, and geopolitical risks around the Strait of Hormuz as key pressures dictating near-term market direction.

Gold prices rallied over 1% to approximately ₹1.5 lakh per 10 grams. Investors sought safe-haven assets amid the broad risk-off sentiment across global markets.
