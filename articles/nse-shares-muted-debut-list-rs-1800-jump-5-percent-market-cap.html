NSE Shares List at 0.8% Premium in Muted Debut, Then Jump 5% to Rs 4.64 Lakh Crore Market Cap

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Shares of the National Stock Exchange made a muted Dalal Street debut on Thursday, listing at Rs 1,800 apiece on the BSE — a premium of just 0.8% over the IPO price of Rs 1,785. The listing missed grey market expectations, with unlisted shares trading at around a 2% premium ahead of the debut.

Muted Debut, Then a Sharp Jump

In less than an hour since listing, NSE shares jumped more than 5% from the IPO price to hit a high of Rs 1,878 apiece. The sharp gains added around Rs 19,290 crore to the exchange's market capitalisation, taking it to about Rs 4.64 lakh crore. At debut, the market cap stood at around Rs 4.45 lakh crore — sharply higher than the Rs 1.33 lakh crore that rival BSE currently commands.

The listing marks the end of a long-running process that faced several regulatory hurdles.

IPO Recap

NSE's IPO, which closed on September 21, raised Rs 22,562 crore entirely through an offer for sale of 12.64 crore shares by existing shareholders. None of the proceeds go to the exchange itself.

The issue was priced in a band of Rs 1,700 to Rs 1,785 per share, with a lot size of 8 shares and a minimum retail application of Rs 14,280. A day before the IPO opened, NSE raised Rs 6,746 crore from more than 150 anchor investors.

The maiden offer was subscribed nearly 6 times. QIBs led demand at around 13 times their reserved portion, while retail and non-institutional portions were booked 1.4 times and 6.6 times respectively.

Brokerages Turn Bullish on Day One

Macquarie initiated coverage with an Outperform rating and a target price of Rs 1,965, implying 10% upside. The brokerage called NSE "The Dominator," citing its leading market share, technology and deep liquidity, and described it as the "lynchpin" of India's financialisation. It expects platform expansion to drive revenue growth, while noting near-term pressure from the proposed CAS framework.

Emkay Research initiated with a Buy call and a target of Rs 2,050, implying 15% upside. The brokerage pointed to India's long capital-market growth runway, NSE's resilient leadership across business segments, and the strong profitability and cash generation that MIIs command globally.

PL Capital initiated with an Accumulate rating and a Rs 1,950 target. It noted NSE holds over 93% share in the cash market and 100% in stock and index futures, though index options share has declined to 65% in FY27 due to regulatory hurdles. It builds in an 11% revenue CAGR over FY26-29 and expects EBITDA margin to recover to 76% by FY29 from 71% in FY26.

Swastika Investmart noted the IPO was valued at 40.9-42.9 times FY26 diluted EPS, a discount to BSE's 54.28 times, offering valuation support. Around 79% of revenue is linked to trading activity, it added.
