Maruti, Eternal Lead Nifty Higher; Crude at $110, Fed Meet Weigh on Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki and Eternal led Nifty 50 higher by 122 points to 24,118, with Sensex up 358 points at 77,245 in early trade on April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Brent crude hovered around $110–113 per barrel and the Federal Reserve's policy meeting conclusion kept traders cautious about near-term upside.
  • 03/Foreign investors sold ₹2,100 crore on Tuesday while domestic buyers partially offset with ₹1,712 crore; rupee hit one-month low at 94.54.

Indian markets opened higher on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The Sensex gained 358.78 points (0.47%) to 77,245.69, while the Nifty 50 added 122.55 points (0.51%) to 24,118.25.

Maruti Suzuki led the Nifty gainers, surging 3.31% to ₹13,319 from its previous close of ₹12,892. Trading volumes reached 47,606 shares worth ₹6,291.33 lakh.

Eternal, Zomato's parent company, was the second biggest gainer. It rose 3.11% to ₹260.94 from ₹253.07 on heavy volumes of over 1.80 crore shares valued at ₹47,667.09 lakh.

Gaurav Udani, Founder of ThinCredBlu Securities, described the market as range-bound. "23,900–24,000 remains immediate support, while 24,200–24,300 continues to act as the key resistance zone," he said.

Dr. V.K. Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments, warned about crude's impact. "Brent crude at $110 is negative for India," he said. "The downside risk to India's growth and upside risk to inflation will remain high."

Devarsh Vakil of HDFC Securities flagged that the UAE will exit OPEC on May 1. The move could have medium-term supply implications but is unlikely to ease oil prices immediately.

Foreign institutional investors remained net sellers on Tuesday, dumping equities worth ₹2,100 crore. Domestic institutional investors bought ₹1,712 crore, partially offsetting the outflows.

The rupee depreciated 35 paise to close at a one-month low of 94.54 against the dollar. Elevated crude, currency pressure, and Fed uncertainty kept sentiment fragile.
