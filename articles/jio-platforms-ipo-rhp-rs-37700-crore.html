Jio Platforms Files Rs 37,700 Crore RHP, Biggest in India

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms has filed its Red Herring Prospectus with SEBI for a Rs 37,700 crore IPO, the largest ever in India at roughly $4 billion
  • 02/The price band is set at Rs 1,150 to Rs 1,220 per share; the issue opens October 21 and lists October 28, timed around Dussehra
  • 03/The IPO is a 100% fresh issue of up to 270 million new shares with no offer for sale, meaning no exits for Meta, Google or private equity backers

Jio Platforms has filed its Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) with market regulator SEBI. The offering sizes the IPO at Rs 37,700 crore, or roughly $4 billion.

This will be India's largest-ever initial public offering. It surpasses Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,859 crore IPO in 2024, which currently holds the record.

The RHP was filed after October 12. It discloses a price band of Rs 1,150 to Rs 1,220 per share for the public issue.

The timeline is tightly scheduled. The anchor book opens on October 19.

The IPO opens for subscription on October 21, around the festival of Dussehra. It closes on October 23.

Share allotment is scheduled for October 26. The listing is targeted for October 28.

The company plans to wrap up the entire IPO process before October 30. That leaves a narrow window between listing and month-end.

The structure of the deal is notable. It is a 100% fresh issue of up to 270 million new shares.

There is no offer for sale. No existing investor is selling shares in this IPO.

That means Meta, Google, Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake, Vista, General Atlantic, KKR and ADIA are all staying put. None of the roughly 30.9% institutional stakeholding is being cashed out.

For investors, a pure fresh issue has a specific meaning. Every rupee raised goes into the company rather than to selling shareholders.

The flip side is dilution. The new shares equal about 2.9% of the company's post-issue equity.

The use of proceeds is clearly laid out. The company plans to prepay up to Rs 27,500 crore of borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm (RJIL), its telecom unit.

The remainder of the proceeds will go towards general corporate purposes. The RHP does not earmark any portion for paying out existing investors.

This is the first IPO from the Reliance group in about 20 years. The last was Reliance Petroleum in 2006.

The road to this filing has been long. Jio Platforms filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) in June.

SEBI approval came on August 28. The RHP filing after October 12 converts that approval into a dated, priced event.

The investor lineup behind Jio Platforms is heavyweight. Meta holds 9.98% and Google holds 7.73%.

Saudi Arabia's PIF, Silver Lake, Vista, General Atlantic, KKR and ADIA round out the institutional register. Together these investors account for roughly 30.9% of the company.

The Dussehra timing carries symbolic weight for the group. Listing around the festival positions the debut as an auspicious milestone for India's largest private company.

For the market, the scale of the offering sets a new benchmark. At Rs 37,700 crore, the issue eclipses the previous record by nearly Rs 10,000 crore.

Investors weighing the issue will focus on the Rs 1,150 to Rs 1,220 price band. The final price within the band will be decided through the book-building process across the October 21 to October 23 subscription window.

The anchor book on October 19 will offer an early signal. Anchor investors typically include institutional players allocating capital ahead of the public window.

The full IPO process, from RHP to listing, is designed to complete inside about two weeks. That is an aggressive schedule by Indian primary market standards.

If the timeline holds, Jio Platforms will list on October 28. The company intends to have everything wrapped up before October 30.

WHAT IS THE SIZE OF THE JIO PLATFORMS IPO?
JIO PLATFORMS HAS FILED ITS RED HERRING PROSPECTUS WITH SEBI FOR AN INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING OF RS 37,700 CRORE, OR ROUGHLY $4 BILLION. THIS MAKES IT THE LARGEST IPO EVER IN INDIA.
WHAT IS THE PRICE BAND FOR THE JIO PLATFORMS IPO?
THE DISCLOSED PRICE BAND IS RS 1,150 TO RS 1,220 PER SHARE. THE FINAL PRICE WITHIN THE BAND WILL BE DETERMINED THROUGH THE BOOK-BUILDING PROCESS DURING THE ISSUE.
WHEN DOES THE JIO PLATFORMS IPO OPEN AND WHEN IS THE LISTING?
THE ANCHOR BOOK OPENS ON OCTOBER 19. THE IPO OPENS ON OCTOBER 21, AROUND DUSSEHRA, CLOSES ON OCTOBER 23, ALLOTMENT IS ON OCTOBER 26, AND LISTING IS TARGETED FOR OCTOBER 28.
IS THE JIO PLATFORMS IPO A FRESH ISSUE OR DOES IT INCLUDE AN OFFER FOR SALE?
IT IS A 100% FRESH ISSUE OF UP TO 270 MILLION NEW SHARES. THERE IS NO OFFER FOR SALE, SO EXISTING INVESTORS SUCH AS META, GOOGLE, PIF, SILVER LAKE, VISTA, GENERAL ATLANTIC, KKR AND ADIA ARE NOT SELLING ANY SHARES.
HOW WILL JIO PLATFORMS USE THE IPO PROCEEDS?
THE COMPANY PLANS TO PREPAY UP TO RS 27,500 CRORE OF BORROWINGS OF RELIANCE JIO INFOCOMM, ITS TELECOM UNIT. THE REMAINDER WILL BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES. GLOSSARY: RED HERRING PROSPECTUS (RHP): The preliminary prospectus filed with SEBI before an IPO. It discloses the offer size, price band, share structure and use of proceeds but does not include the final issue price. FRESH ISSUE: New shares created and sold by the company itself. The money raised goes to the company, unlike a secondary sale where it goes to existing shareholders. OFFER FOR SALE (OFS): A mechanism where existing shareholders sell their shares in an IPO. Jio Platforms' offering has no OFS component. ANCHOR BOOK: The portion of an IPO allocated to institutional investors a day before the public subscription opens. The anchor book here opens on October 19. PRICE BAND: The range within which investors can bid for shares in a book-built IPO. Jio Platforms' band is Rs 1,150 to Rs 1,220 per share. BOOK BUILDING: The price discovery process where investor bids across the price band determine the final issue price.
