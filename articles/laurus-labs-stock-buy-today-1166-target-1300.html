Stock to Buy Today: Laurus Labs at ₹1,166.50 With ₹1,300 Target

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Hindu Business Line issues a BUY call on Laurus Labs at ₹1,166.50 with a short-term target of ₹1,300
  • 02/The stock surged 6% on May 4, confirming a bull flag pattern; immediate support placed at ₹1,120
  • 03/Q4 FY26 net sales rose 5.31% YoY to ₹1,811.57 crore; Motilal Oswal has a Buy with target ₹1,370

Laurus Labs Ltd (NSE: LAURUSLABS) gets a BUY recommendation from The Hindu Business Line. The stock trades at ₹1,166.50 on May 5, 2026.

The 6 per cent surge on May 4 confirms a bull flag formation on the daily chart. This signals that the upmove could be gaining fresh momentum.

Immediate support is at ₹1,120. Below that, ₹1,090 acts as the next strong support to limit downside if the price breaks lower.

Traders can buy at the current market price of ₹1,166.50. Accumulate on dips near ₹1,130 for better entry levels.

The short-term price target is ₹1,300 in the coming weeks. Exit long positions at ₹1,290 to book profits near the resistance zone.

Keep a strict stop-loss at ₹1,075. Trail it higher to ₹1,190 once the stock crosses ₹1,210, and revise to ₹1,220 at ₹1,240.

Laurus Labs reported Q4 FY26 consolidated net sales of ₹1,811.57 crore, up 5.31% year-on-year. Motilal Oswal maintains a Buy rating with a target of ₹1,370.

The company runs 110 active CDMO projects with 15 in commercial supply. This segment has driven the recent growth surge, but the stock trades at a P/E around 66x. Any earnings disappointment could trigger sharp multiple compression.
