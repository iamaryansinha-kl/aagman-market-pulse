Paytm Crashes 10% on UPI MDR Deferral Report, Rs 10,970 Crore Lost

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/One97 Communications (NSE: PAYTM) crashed 10% on October 8, wiping off more than Rs 10,970 crore in market value after reports the UPI MDR rollout may be deferred
  • 02/NPCI had announced a 0.40% MDR on select UPI transactions above Rs 2,000 from October 15; analysts estimated FY28 MDR revenue of up to Rs 1,160 crore for Paytm
  • 03/The deferral is a proposal under government consideration, not a confirmed decision; festive-season economics and retail trader pushback are the factors to watch

One97 Communications Ltd (NSE: PAYTM), the parent of fintech major Paytm, saw its shares crash 10% in early trade on Thursday, October 8. The slide wiped off more than Rs 10,970 crore from the company's market capitalisation within minutes of the opening bell.

The trigger was a report in Business Standard, citing people familiar with the matter, that the government is considering a proposal to defer the new UPI Merchant Discount Rate. The fee was scheduled to kick in on October 15.

Under the reported proposal, the MDR rollout would shift to January 1. The delay would keep UPI payments free for merchants through the festive season and until Christmas.

The stock dropped to Rs 1,558.80 apiece on the NSE, its lowest level since mid-August. At the lows, Paytm's market capitalisation slipped below the Rs 1 lakh crore mark.

The stock staged a partial recovery as the morning progressed. By around 11 am, shares traded about 5% lower, and market capitalisation climbed back above Rs 1.05 lakh crore.

Even so, the fall snapped a three-session gaining streak for the counter. It showed how tightly investors had tied Paytm's earnings story to the start of the MDR regime.

NPCI had last month announced an MDR of 0.40% on select Person-to-Merchant UPI transactions above Rs 2,000, effective October 15. The fee was meant to end zero-cost processing for larger merchant payments on the network.

Paytm was widely seen as one of the biggest beneficiaries of the new regime. Analysts estimated FY28 UPI MDR revenue of as much as Rs 1,160 crore for the company.

After the NPCI announcement, brokerages hiked target prices and earnings estimates for the stock. Paytm itself had said the MDR would generate additional revenue from its merchant business on transactions that were free earlier.

The deferral report dampened sentiment among investors hoping for bumper gains from the MDR introduction. It also underlined the gap between an announced fee and an implemented one.

On October 7, RBI Governor Malhotra defended the 0.4% MDR before the Supreme Court, saying a small fee will not hit transaction volumes. The court declined to grant an interim stay.

Other payment stocks fell in tandem with Paytm. Mobikwik and Pine Labs also crashed, with the payments pack falling as much as 10% in the session.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY DID PAYTM SHARES CRASH ON OCTOBER 8?
ONE97 COMMUNICATIONS, THE PARENT OF PAYTM, CRASHED 10% IN EARLY TRADE AFTER MEDIA REPORTS SAID THE ROLLOUT OF THE NEW UPI MERCHANT DISCOUNT RATE MAY BE DEFERRED TO JANUARY 1 FROM OCTOBER 15. THE STOCK WIPED OFF MORE THAN RS 10,970 CRORE IN MARKET VALUE, AND MARKET CAPITALISATION DIPPED BELOW RS 1 LAKH CRORE AT THE LOWS.
WHAT IS THE UPI MDR ANNOUNCED BY NPCI?
NPCI LAST MONTH ANNOUNCED A MERCHANT DISCOUNT RATE OF 0.40% ON SELECT PERSON-TO-MERCHANT UPI TRANSACTIONS ABOVE RS 2,000. THE FEE WAS SCHEDULED TO TAKE EFFECT ON OCTOBER 15, 2026, REPLACING THE CURRENT ZERO-FEE STRUCTURE FOR LARGER MERCHANT PAYMENTS ON UPI.
HOW MUCH REVENUE WAS PAYTM EXPECTED TO EARN FROM THE UPI MDR?
ANALYSTS ESTIMATED FY28 UPI MDR REVENUE OF AS MUCH AS RS 1,160 CRORE FOR PAYTM, WHICH WAS SEEN AS ONE OF THE BIGGEST BENEFICIARIES OF THE NEW REGIME. BROKERAGES HIKED TARGET PRICES AND EARNINGS ESTIMATES AFTER THE NPCI ANNOUNCEMENT, AND PAYTM SAID THE FEE WOULD ADD REVENUE FROM TRANSACTIONS THAT WERE EARLIER FREE.
IS THE DEFERRAL OF THE UPI MDR TO JANUARY 1 CONFIRMED?
NO. THE DEFERRAL IS A PROPOSAL UNDER CONSIDERATION BY THE GOVERNMENT, ACCORDING TO MEDIA REPORTS CITING PEOPLE FAMILIAR WITH THE MATTER. THE OFFICIAL START DATE REMAINS OCTOBER 15 UNLESS THE GOVERNMENT FORMALLY ANNOUNCES A CHANGE. A DEFERRAL WOULD KEEP UPI FREE FOR MERCHANTS THROUGH THE FESTIVE SEASON UNTIL CHRISTMAS, AND RETAIL TRADERS' ASSOCIATIONS HAVE PUSHED BACK AGAINST THE FEE.
HOW DID PAYTM'S STOCK MOVE DURING THE SESSION?
THE STOCK FELL TO RS 1,558.80 ON THE NSE, ITS LOWEST LEVEL SINCE MID-AUGUST, AND LATER PARTIALLY RECOVERED TO TRADE ABOUT 5% LOWER AROUND 11 AM. MARKET CAPITALISATION CLIMBED BACK ABOVE RS 1.05 LAKH CRORE, THOUGH THE STOCK STILL SNAPPED ITS THREE-SESSION GAINING STREAK. GLOSSARY: MDR (Merchant Discount Rate): The fee merchants pay on digital payments, split among the payment company, issuing bank and network. NPCI's new UPI MDR is 0.40% on select merchant transactions above Rs 2,000. Market capitalisation: The total value of a company's shares — share price multiplied by shares outstanding. Paytm's briefly dipped below Rs 1 lakh crore on Thursday before recovering. Person-to-Merchant (P2M) transaction: A UPI payment from an individual to a business. The MDR applies only to select P2M payments above Rs 2,000, not to personal transfers.
