RBI FEMA Rules: No Reporting for Individuals, Rs 10 Lakh Export Relief

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI has clarified that individuals doing transactions of a personal nature are exempt from reporting under the new FEMA trade rules that took effect October 1, 2026
  • 02/Exporters with transactions up to Rs 10 lakh per bill can file a self-declaration instead of uploading invoices and supporting documents
  • 03/RBI will shortly issue FAQs to address what deputy governor Rohit Jain called a "misunderstanding" over reporting obligations

The Reserve Bank of India has clarified that individuals are not required to report transactions of a personal nature under the new foreign exchange trade regulations. The revised rules took effect on October 1, 2026.

Governor Sanjay Malhotra said routine individual transactions need no reporting. These include payments for subscriptions to TV channels, apps, journals and newspapers.

Individuals receiving money from overseas are also outside the reporting net. This covers payments for services such as tutoring or small software-related assignments done as an individual.

"So if you are subscribing to a TV channel or an app, or to some journals or newspapers, or you are providing, on the other hand, services as an individual outside — could be tutoring services, could be some small software, etc — and getting paid for it, those as individuals are not required to be reported" - Sanjay Malhotra, RBI Governor

The clarification follows widespread confusion after the revised framework took effect this month. The rules had spooked freelancers, app subscribers and small exporters who feared every foreign payment would need fresh paperwork.

The RBI has also eased documentation for smaller export transactions. Exporters with transactions of up to Rs 10 lakh per bill can submit a self-declaration instead of uploading invoices and other supporting documents.

"Up to Rs 10 lakh per bill — not annually, per bill — a self-declaration will suffice," Malhotra said. The per-bill threshold offers meaningful relief to small exporters dealing in lower-value consignments.

Deputy governor Rohit Jain said the regulations were issued in January ahead of the October 1 implementation. The aim is to liberalise the handling of trade transactions by authorised dealer banks and improve ease of doing business.

"Individuals are not included with respect to the reporting requirements for the contracts of a personal nature. So there seems to be some misunderstanding on the reporting obligations, which we will clarify shortly through an FAQ" - Rohit Jain, Deputy Governor, RBI

The revised framework has also brought services exports and imports within the reporting system for authorised dealers. That shift had triggered concern among freelancers receiving foreign income that they would face fresh compliance burdens.

Jain said the regulations are intended to reduce the compliance burden on authorised dealers, exporters and importers. They were not designed to introduce additional reporting requirements for individuals.

For now, the central bank's message is that the obligations fall on authorised dealers and trade counterparties, not on individuals. Exporters and small businesses should watch for the upcoming FAQ document for final clarity on the scope of the reporting requirements.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
ARE INDIVIDUALS REQUIRED TO REPORT TRANSACTIONS UNDER THE NEW FEMA TRADE RULES?
NO. THE RBI HAS CLARIFIED THAT INDIVIDUALS UNDERTAKING TRANSACTIONS OF A PERSONAL NATURE ARE NOT REQUIRED TO COMPLY WITH REPORTING REQUIREMENTS UNDER THE NEW FOREIGN EXCHANGE TRADE REGULATIONS THAT TOOK EFFECT ON OCTOBER 1, 2026. THE CLARIFICATION APPLIES TO BOTH OUTWARD PAYMENTS, SUCH AS SUBSCRIPTIONS, AND INWARD PAYMENTS FOR PERSONAL SERVICES.
WHAT KINDS OF INDIVIDUAL TRANSACTIONS ARE EXEMPT FROM REPORTING?
ROUTINE TRANSACTIONS SUCH AS PAYMENTS FOR SUBSCRIPTIONS TO TV CHANNELS, APPS, JOURNALS AND NEWSPAPERS ARE EXEMPT. INDIVIDUALS RECEIVING PAYMENTS FROM OVERSEAS FOR SERVICES LIKE TUTORING OR SMALL SOFTWARE-RELATED ASSIGNMENTS ARE ALSO NOT REQUIRED TO REPORT, RBI GOVERNOR SANJAY MALHOTRA SAID.
WHAT IS THE RS 10 LAKH SELF-DECLARATION FACILITY FOR EXPORTERS?
EXPORTERS WITH TRANSACTIONS OF UP TO RS 10 LAKH PER BILL CAN FURNISH A SELF-DECLARATION INSTEAD OF UPLOADING INVOICES AND OTHER SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS. THE THRESHOLD APPLIES PER BILL AND NOT ANNUALLY, AS CLARIFIED BY RBI GOVERNOR SANJAY MALHOTRA.
WHY WERE THE NEW FEMA TRADE REGULATIONS INTRODUCED?
THE REGULATIONS, ISSUED IN JANUARY AHEAD OF THEIR OCTOBER 1 IMPLEMENTATION, AIM TO LIBERALISE THE HANDLING OF TRADE TRANSACTIONS BY AUTHORISED DEALER BANKS, SIMPLIFY PROCESSES AND IMPROVE EASE OF DOING BUSINESS, ACCORDING TO RBI DEPUTY GOVERNOR ROHIT JAIN.
WILL THE RBI ISSUE FURTHER CLARIFICATION ON THE RULES?
YES. THE CENTRAL BANK WILL SHORTLY ISSUE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS TO CLARIFY THE SCOPE OF THE REGULATIONS AND SETTLE THE REPORTING QUESTION DEFINITIVELY. JAIN DESCRIBED CONCERNS OVER REPORTING OBLIGATIONS AS A MISUNDERSTANDING THAT THE UPCOMING FAQ WILL ADDRESS. GLOSSARY: FEMA: The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 — the law governing foreign exchange transactions in India, administered by the RBI. Authorised Dealer (AD) Bank: A bank authorised by the RBI to deal in foreign exchange and handle trade documentation on behalf of customers. Self-Declaration: A signed statement by an exporter confirming transaction details, accepted by the RBI in place of uploaded invoices for bills up to Rs 10 lakh. Reporting Requirement: The obligation to file details of a foreign exchange transaction with the RBI. The new trade rules extended this system to services trade; individuals doing personal transactions are exempt. IFSCA: The International Financial Services Centres Authority — the unified regulator for GIFT City financial services entities.
