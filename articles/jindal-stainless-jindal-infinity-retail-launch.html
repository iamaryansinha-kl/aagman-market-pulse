Jindal Stainless Launches Jindal Infinity Rebar, Enters Retail Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jindal Stainless Ltd launched stainless steel rebar Jindal Infinity on April 20, 2026, marking its entry into retail
  • 02/The move shifts the company beyond B2B to directly reach end-consumers, builders and fabricators in construction
  • 03/Jindal Stainless targets 4.2 million tonnes annual melt capacity by FY27; FY25 turnover stood at ₹40,182 crore

Jindal Stainless Ltd launched Jindal Infinity on April 20, 2026. The stainless steel rebar marks the company's first retail foray.

The product targets end-consumers, builders and fabricators. It moves the company beyond its traditional B2B business model.

Stainless steel rebars offer higher corrosion resistance than conventional rebars. They have a higher critical chloride threshold for aggressive environments.

"Our entry into retail with Jindal Infinity marks a strategic extension into the construction value chain," said Managing Director Abhyuday Jindal.

"We are creating the foundation for scale. The initial response underscores the need for high-performance materials," said Head of Sales Rajeev Garg.

Jindal Stainless reported an annual turnover of ₹40,182 crore in FY25. It operates 16 manufacturing and processing facilities globally.

The company aims to reach 4.2 million tonnes of annual melt capacity by FY27. It has a network across 12 countries.

In India, Jindal Stainless runs 10 sales offices and six service centres as of March 2025. Facilities span India, Spain and Indonesia.
