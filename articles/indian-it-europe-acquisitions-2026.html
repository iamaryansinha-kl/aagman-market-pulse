Indian IT Firms Go Shopping in Europe: Why Acquisitions Are Accelerating

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Persistent Systems' Nagarro buy and LTM's Randstad deal signal a sharp pivot by Indian IT firms toward Europe as US growth slows.
  • 02/Europe's IT services market stands at $490 billion and is projected to reach $675 billion by 2031, offering a massive runway.
  • 03/Key risk: cultural integration, strict EU labour laws and higher local costs could squeeze margins if execution falters.

Indian IT services companies are stepping up acquisitions across Europe. Persistent Systems' purchase of Germany-based Nagarro and LTM Ltd's buyout of Randstad's technology arm in May 2026 headline the trend.

HCL Technologies, Wipro, and Infosys have also snapped up niche firms in continental Europe recently. These deals mark a clear shift from the industry's historical reluctance to buy European assets.

For Persistent Systems, the Nagarro deal is transformative. Europe's share of its revenue will jump to 22% from roughly 9% previously. The acquisition delivers instant scale in a market where local presence matters.

The US still accounts for about 70% of Indian IT industry revenue, according to Nasscom. Slower spending, tighter immigration policies and geopolitical uncertainty are now pushing firms to diversify.

Europe's digital transformation agenda is creating fresh demand. Regulations around AI, cybersecurity and data governance are forcing enterprises to modernise legacy systems. This opens a significant services opportunity.

European IT firms also trade at lower valuations than comparable US peers. Acquisitions provide faster access to local talent, client relationships and regulatory expertise than organic growth.

However, the strategy carries risks. Europe's stringent labour laws, complex regulations and cultural differences make integration challenging. Higher-cost local operations could squeeze margins if deals underperform.

The market opportunity is substantial. Europe's IT services market, estimated at about $490 billion, is expected to grow to around $675 billion by 2031.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH INDIAN IT FIRMS HAVE RECENTLY ACQUIRED EUROPEAN COMPANIES?
PERSISTENT SYSTEMS ACQUIRED GERMANY-BASED NAGARRO. LTM LTD PURCHASED RANDSTAD'S TECHNOLOGY AND CONSULTING BUSINESS IN MAY 2026. HCLTECH, WIPRO AND INFOSYS HAVE ALSO BOUGHT NICHE FIRMS ACROSS CONTINENTAL EUROPE IN RECENT YEARS.
WHY ARE INDIAN IT COMPANIES TARGETING EUROPE NOW?
SLOWER SPENDING, TIGHTER US IMMIGRATION POLICIES AND GEOPOLITICAL UNCERTAINTY ARE PUSHING DIVERSIFICATION. EUROPE'S $490 BILLION IT SERVICES MARKET, BACKED BY DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION REGULATIONS, OFFERS STRONG GROWTH POTENTIAL. EUROPEAN FIRMS ALSO TRADE AT LOWER VALUATIONS THAN US PEERS.
HOW BIG IS THE EUROPEAN IT SERVICES MARKET?
THE EUROPEAN IT SERVICES MARKET IS ESTIMATED AT ABOUT $490 BILLION AND IS PROJECTED TO GROW TO AROUND $675 BILLION BY 2031. IT REMAINS FRAGMENTED ACROSS COUNTRIES, LANGUAGES AND INDUSTRY VERTICALS.
WHAT IS THE BIGGEST GAIN FOR INDIAN IT FIRMS FROM THESE ACQUISITIONS?
THE BIGGEST GAIN IS MARKET ACCESS AND CREDIBILITY. ACQUIRING ESTABLISHED EUROPEAN FIRMS PROVIDES LOCAL CLIENTS, DELIVERY CENTRES AND REGULATORY EXPERTISE. THIS HELPS INDIAN FIRMS WIN ENTERPRISE AND GOVERNMENT CONTRACTS THAT REQUIRE A LOCAL PRESENCE.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS OF THIS EUROPEAN EXPANSION STRATEGY?
INTEGRATION RISKS INCLUDE CULTURAL DIFFERENCES, STRINGENT EU LABOUR LAWS AND COMPLEX REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS. EUROPE'S STRICT DATA PROTECTION RULES RAISE COMPLIANCE COSTS. EXPANDING HIGHER-COST LOCAL OPERATIONS COULD SQUEEZE PROFITABILITY IF INTEGRATION FALLS SHORT.
