D-Street Ends Week in Green: Nifty, Sensex Gain as US-Iran Talks Loom

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian stock markets ended the week with gains as Nifty 50 and Sensex advanced on Friday
  • 02/Investors remained cautious due to ongoing US-Iran diplomatic negotiations
  • 03/Market sentiment balanced between weekly optimism and geopolitical watchfulness

Indian stock markets closed the week on a positive note. The Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex both posted gains on Friday.

Trading desks dubbed the mood "war and peace." Weekly gains were visible, but geopolitical tension kept participants alert.

US-Iran talks dominated global headlines. Diplomatic developments between Washington and Tehran added a layer of uncertainty.

Domestic benchmarks still managed to stay in the green. Buyers stepped in despite the overhang of international risk.

The Nifty 50 held key support levels through the week. Institutional flows remained steady, aiding the recovery.

Broader markets mirrored the headline indices. Midcap and smallcap stocks also participated in the Friday uptick.

Analysts advised a stock-specific approach ahead. Global cues and crude oil trends are likely to guide near-term direction.
