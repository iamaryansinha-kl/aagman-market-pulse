UltraTech Cement Q4 Preview: Net Profit Seen Up 14% to ₹2,818 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/UltraTech Cement is expected to report a 14% rise in Q4 FY26 net profit at ₹2,818 crore against ₹2,482 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue seen up 12% at ₹25,901 crore and EBITDA up 14% at ₹5,277 crore, driven by higher realisation and lower costs
  • 03/Brokerages expect dispatch growth; investors will watch volume momentum, pricing discipline, and EBITDA per tonne closely

UltraTech Cement Ltd is expected to report a 14% rise in March quarter net profit at ₹2,818 crore. The same quarter last year saw a profit of ₹2,482 crore. (NSE: ULTRACEMCO)

Revenue is seen increasing 12% to ₹25,901 crore against ₹23,063 crore in Q4 FY25. EBITDA is expected to jump 14% to ₹5,277 crore from ₹4,618 crore.

Brokerages expect dispatch growth across the consolidated business. Views differ on whether realisations improved or weakened on a sequential basis.

The company, an Aditya Birla Group firm, is India's largest cement producer by capacity. Grey cement capacity stands at 188.8 mtpa with expansion plans in progress.

Nuvama Research expects consolidated volumes to rise about 12% year-on-year. It sees blended EBITDA per tonne declining roughly 2.4% YoY to ₹1,099.

Nomura Research estimates operating cost per tonne rising 2% year-on-year on higher raw material and power costs. It expects volumes up 10% YoY and EBITDA per tonne at ₹1,085.

For the full FY26, revenue was up 21.8% year-on-year in the first nine months. Management expects utilisation levels to exceed 90% in Q4 on strong demand.

The company's ability to maintain volume momentum, pricing discipline, and EBITDA per tonne will be watched keenly by investors when results are declared.
