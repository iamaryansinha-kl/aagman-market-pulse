WeWork India Opens 17th NCR Centre at Aerocity, Adds 1.1 Lakh Sq Ft

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/WeWork India opens its 17th centre in the National Capital Region at Worldmark 6, Aerocity, adding ~1.1 lakh sq ft and over 1,400 desks
  • 02/India’s flexible workspace inventory has tripled between 2020 and 2025, crossing 100 million sq ft as per CBRE India data
  • 03/WeWork India is the largest flexible workspace operator by revenue for three consecutive years; watch enterprise demand and GCC traction

WeWork India Management Limited opened its 17th centre in the National Capital Region on Monday. The new facility at Worldmark 6, Aerocity, adds approximately 1.1 lakh square feet across the third floor.

The centre houses over 1,400 desks and is designed for enterprises, global capability centres, and high-growth companies. Facilities include collaboration zones, meeting rooms, an indoor event space, and wellness areas.

Members have direct metro access via Aerocity Metro Station on the Airport Express Line. The location targets premium occupiers seeking connectivity and Grade-A infrastructure.

Arnav S. Gusain, Chief of Supply at WeWork India and CEO of Rivet by WeWork India, said the launch responds to growing enterprise demand for well-located assets in Aerocity. He noted that large occupiers and GCCs are treating flexible workspaces as a core real estate strategy, not an ancillary option.

India’s flexible workspace sector crossed 100 million square feet in total inventory, having tripled between 2020 and 2025 according to CBRE India. Delhi-NCR is the second-largest market after Bengaluru, with 21–23 million square feet of inventory.

WeWork India operates across eight cities with 73 centres spanning 8.2 million square feet as of December 2025. The company has over 1.21 lakh desks in total across Chennai, New Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, and Hyderabad.

The company is listed on the NSE under WEWORK and on the BSE under scrip code 544570. It has been India’s largest flexible workspace operator by total revenue for the past three fiscal years, as per CBRE data.

Shares of WeWork India traded at ₹544.25 on the NSE on Monday, down 0.47 per cent from the previous close. The market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹7,368 crore.
