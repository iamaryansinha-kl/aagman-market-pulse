PMEGP Generates 36.33 Lakh Jobs, Sets Up 4 Lakh Micro-Enterprises in Five Years

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PMEGP fully utilised its ₹13,554.42 crore budget and established 4,03,706 micro-enterprises against a target of 4,02,000 during FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26
  • 02/The scheme generated employment for approximately 36.33 lakh individuals, with 80% of units located in rural areas
  • 03/Women accounted for nearly 40% of beneficiaries, while SC/ST/OBC entrepreneurs represented 54% of total assisted units

The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) has fully utilised its approved budget of ₹13,554.42 crore over the five-year period from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26. The scheme facilitated the establishment of 4,03,706 micro-enterprises, exceeding its target of 4,02,000 units.

PMEGP is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises through the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC). It provides Margin Money subsidy on bank loans to first-generation entrepreneurs in the non-farm sector.

The programme generated sustainable employment for approximately 36.33 lakh individuals during the 15th Finance Commission cycle. This reflects sustained demand for credit-linked entrepreneurship schemes at the grassroots level.

Nearly 80% of the enterprises established under PMEGP are located in rural areas. This supports rural industrialisation and balanced regional development across states.

Women beneficiaries account for nearly 40% of the total micro-enterprises assisted under the scheme. Around 45% of the total Margin Money subsidy disbursed has been provided to women entrepreneurs.

Approximately 54% of the beneficiaries belong to SC, ST or OBC categories. This highlights the scheme's focus on socially disadvantaged communities and inclusive growth.

The Ministry of MSME has indicated it will strengthen PMEGP in the forthcoming cycle. The focus will be on innovation, scalability, and wider outreach to sustain momentum.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS PMEGP AND WHICH MINISTRY IMPLEMENTS IT?
THE PRIME MINISTER'S EMPLOYMENT GENERATION PROGRAMME (PMEGP) IS A CREDIT-LINKED SUBSIDY SCHEME IMPLEMENTED BY THE MINISTRY OF MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISES (MSME) THROUGH THE KHADI AND VILLAGE INDUSTRIES COMMISSION (KVIC). IT PROVIDES MARGIN MONEY SUBSIDY ON BANK LOANS TO FIRST-GENERATION ENTREPRENEURS FOR SETTING UP MICRO-ENTERPRISES IN THE NON-FARM SECTOR.
HOW MANY MICRO-ENTERPRISES DID PMEGP SET UP IN THE LAST FIVE YEARS?
PMEGP FACILITATED THE ESTABLISHMENT OF 4,03,706 MICRO-ENTERPRISES DURING FY 2021-22 TO FY 2025-26. THIS EXCEEDED THE TARGET OF 4,02,000 ENTERPRISES SET FOR THE 15TH FINANCE COMMISSION CYCLE.
WHAT WAS THE TOTAL BUDGET UTILISED BY PMEGP AND HOW MANY JOBS WERE CREATED?
THE SCHEME FULLY UTILISED ITS APPROVED BUDGETARY OUTLAY OF ₹13,554.42 CRORE. IT GENERATED SUSTAINABLE EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR APPROXIMATELY 36.33 LAKH INDIVIDUALS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
WHAT SHARE OF PMEGP BENEFICIARIES ARE WOMEN AND FROM RURAL AREAS?
WOMEN ACCOUNT FOR NEARLY 40% OF THE TOTAL MICRO-ENTERPRISES ASSISTED UNDER PMEGP, AND AROUND 45% OF THE TOTAL MARGIN MONEY SUBSIDY WAS DISBURSED TO WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS. NEARLY 80% OF ALL ENTERPRISES ESTABLISHED ARE LOCATED IN RURAL AREAS.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR PMEGP IN THE NEXT FINANCE COMMISSION CYCLE?
THE MINISTRY OF MSME HAS COMMITTED TO STRENGTHENING PMEGP IN THE FORTHCOMING CYCLE WITH AN ENHANCED FOCUS ON INNOVATION, SCALABILITY, AND WIDER OUTREACH TO CONTINUE DRIVING MICRO-ENTERPRISE DEVELOPMENT AND EMPLOYMENT GENERATION.
