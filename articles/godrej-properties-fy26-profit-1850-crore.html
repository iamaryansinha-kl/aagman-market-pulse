Godrej Properties FY26 Profit Rises 32% to ₹1,850 Cr, Stock Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godrej Properties Limited (NSE: GODREJPROP) reports record annual PAT of ₹1,850 crore for FY26, a 32% jump from ₹1,400 crore in FY25
  • 02/Q4 FY26 collections hit ₹7,947 crore with net profit up 70% to ₹650 crore; company is India's largest residential developer for third straight year
  • 03/FY27 guidance set at ₹39,000 crore bookings and ₹24,000 crore collections; net debt rose to ₹6,414 crore on aggressive land acquisitions

Godrej Properties Limited (NSE: GODREJPROP) reported its highest-ever annual profit after tax of ₹1,850 crore for FY26. This marks a 32% jump from ₹1,400 crore in FY25. Results were announced on Monday, May 4, 2026.

The stock responded positively, rising 2.77% to ₹1,886 on the NSE. However, the shares remain down 16.48% over the past year amid broader mid-cap weakness.

Q4 FY26 was the strongest quarter on record for collections. Customer collections reached ₹7,947 crore, up 14% year-on-year. This is the highest quarterly collection by any Indian real estate developer.

Quarterly net profit stood at ₹650 crore, surging 70% year-on-year. Total income for Q4 came in at ₹3,895 crore, a 47% increase over Q4 FY25.

Full-year booking value grew 16% to ₹34,171 crore, beating the company's own guidance of ₹32,500 crore. It sold 17,513 units covering 27 million square feet. This is the third consecutive year as India's largest residential developer by bookings.

Regional sales were led by MMR at ₹10,312 crore, followed by Bengaluru at ₹8,801 crore and NCR at ₹7,412 crore. Full-year total income was ₹8,374 crore against bookings of ₹34,171 crore, reflecting the gap between sales and accounting recognition.

Net debt rose to ₹6,414 crore as of March 31, 2026, from ₹3,269 crore a year earlier. The company spent ₹8,653 crore on land and approvals. The net debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.33.

For FY27, Godrej Properties guided for booking value of ₹39,000 crore and collections of ₹24,000 crore. The company plans deliveries of 13.5 million square feet. The stock trades at a trailing P/E of 33.08 with a market cap of approximately ₹56,732 crore.
