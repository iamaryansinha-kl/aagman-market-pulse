CBDT Plans Lower Value Accounts, Pre-Filling Alerts on E-Portal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CBDT Nudge 2.0 campaign prompted disclosure of ₹1 lakh crore in foreign assets and ₹6,500 crore income via 1.57 lakh revised ITRs
  • 02/Tax department collected over ₹800 crore; Bengaluru topped revised returns among 18 major cities
  • 03/Board now plans to include lower-value CRS/FATCA accounts and introduce pre-filling alerts on the e-portal

The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) plans to expand its foreign asset disclosure framework. The board will include lower-value CRS and FATCA accounts and introduce pre-filling alerts on the income tax e-portal.

The move follows the Nudge 2.0 campaign run during November-December 2025. Taxpayers with unreported foreign assets for assessment year 2025-26 were sent SMS and email alerts.

The campaign led to cumulative foreign asset declarations of approximately ₹1 lakh crore. Total foreign income declared exceeded ₹6,500 crore through over 1.57 lakh revised and belated ITRs.

The Income Tax Department collected over ₹800 crore in tax from these disclosures. Bengaluru topped the list of 18 major cities for revised returns declaring foreign assets.

Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi and Mumbai followed Bengaluru in asset disclosure volumes. Most declarations came from professionals rather than businessmen or politicians, sources told BusinessLine.

CBDT now proposes a continuous nudging model with predictive risk analysis. The plan includes automatic field pre-filling across ITR schedules and expanded global coordination.

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman announced a foreign asset disclosure scheme for smaller taxpayers in the 2026 Budget. This builds on the Saksham Nudge programme success.

Over the last two years, more than 1.11 crore revised and updated returns were filed under Saksham Nudge campaigns. These yielded ₹8,800 crore in tax collections.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT MEASURES IS CBDT PLANNING FOR FOREIGN ASSET DISCLOSURE?
CBDT PLANS TO INCLUDE LOWER-VALUE CRS AND FATCA ACCOUNTS IN ITS TARGET BASE. IT ALSO PROPOSES PRE-FILLING ALERTS ON THE E-PORTAL, PREDICTIVE RISK ANALYSIS, AND AUTOMATIC FIELD FILLING ACROSS ITR SCHEDULES.
WHAT WERE THE RESULTS OF THE NUDGE 2.0 CAMPAIGN?
THE NUDGE 2.0 CAMPAIGN RESULTED IN FOREIGN ASSET DECLARATIONS OF APPROXIMATELY ₹1 LAKH CRORE. TAXPAYERS DECLARED OVER ₹6,500 CRORE IN FOREIGN INCOME THROUGH MORE THAN 1.57 LAKH REVISED AND BELATED ITRs.
HOW MUCH TAX DID THE INCOME TAX DEPARTMENT COLLECT?
THE DEPARTMENT COLLECTED OVER ₹800 CRORE IN TAX FROM THE FOREIGN ASSET AND INCOME DISCLOSURES. OVER THE LAST TWO YEARS, SAKSHAM NUDGE CAMPAIGNS HAVE YIELDED ₹8,800 CRORE FROM 1.11 CRORE REVISED RETURNS.
WHICH CITIES LED IN FOREIGN ASSET DISCLOSURES?
BENGALURU TOPPED THE LIST AMONG 18 MAJOR CITIES. HYDERABAD, CHENNAI, DELHI AND MUMBAI COLLECTIVELY ACCOUNTED FOR THE LARGEST VOLUMES OF REVISED RETURNS AND ASSET DISCLOSURES.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE 2026 BUDGET ANNOUNCEMENT?
FINANCE MINISTER NIRMALA SITHARAMAN ANNOUNCED A FOREIGN ASSETS DISCLOSURE SCHEME FOR SMALLER TAXPAYERS IN THE 2026 BUDGET. THIS BUILDS ON THE SUCCESS OF THE SAKSHAM NUDGE PROGRAMME AND TARGETS BEHAVIOURAL NON-COMPLIANCE.
