Bajaj Housing Finance Q4 FY26: PAT Up 14% to ₹669 Crore, NIM Dips

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Housing Finance Q4 FY26 PAT rises 14% YoY to ₹669.19 crore; full-year FY26 PAT up 18.3% to ₹2,560.34 crore
  • 02/AUM grows 23% to ₹1.41 lakh crore but NIM compresses 12 bps q-o-q to 3.8% on competitive pricing and loan mix shift
  • 03/Stock fell ~1% to ₹89.89 despite earnings beat; Morgan Stanley flags near-term margin pressure but stable asset quality

Bajaj Housing Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJAJHFL) reported a 14% rise in profit after tax for Q4 FY26. PAT came in at ₹669.19 crore, up from ₹586.68 crore in Q4 FY25.

Total revenue from operations rose to ₹2,902.61 crore in Q4 FY26. This compares with ₹2,504.05 crore in the same quarter last year.

For the full financial year FY26, PAT stood at ₹2,560.34 crore. This marks an 18.3% increase from ₹2,162.90 crore in FY25.

Assets under management grew 23% year-on-year to ₹1.41 lakh crore. Loan disbursements also rose 23% to ₹17,506 crore for the quarter.

Net interest margin declined 12 basis points sequentially to 3.8%. The compression was driven by competitive pricing and a shift in loan mix.

Gross non-performing assets remained low at 27 basis points. Credit costs stood at 16 basis points, with no visible stress across portfolios.

Shares fell around 1% to ₹89.89 on the NSE at 11:24 am on April 28. The stock had opened at ₹91.70 and touched an intraday high of ₹93.

Morgan Stanley said management aims to grow at nearly twice the industry rate. The brokerage expects NIM to remain stable in Q1 FY27, with mild compression ahead.
