SRK Chairman Govind Dholakia Calls Diamond Crisis 'Worst in 60 Years'

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SRK Chairman and BJP MP Govind Dholakia describes India's diamond sector downturn as the worst in 60 years
  • 02/The crisis is driven by the rapid rise of lab-grown diamonds disrupting natural diamond demand globally
  • 03/Dholakia sees signs of recovery ahead despite current severe industry challenges

Govind Dholakia, Chairman of SRK Diamonds and BJP MP, has issued a stark warning. He described the current downturn in India's diamond sector as the worst in 60 years.

The crisis is primarily driven by lab-grown diamonds. These synthetic alternatives have disrupted demand for natural diamonds globally.

India processes nearly 90% of the world's diamonds. The Surat-based industry employs millions of workers across the value chain.

Dholakia was recently appointed Chairman of Surat Diamond Bourse. He took over after the resignation of previous chairman Vallabhbhai Patel.

The Surat Diamond Bourse is the world's largest office building. It serves as the nerve center for India's diamond trade.

Lab-grown diamonds now account for a growing share of global sales. They cost 70-90% less than natural diamonds while appearing identical.

Major retailers have increasingly embraced lab-grown alternatives. This shift has pressured prices and margins for natural diamond processors.

Despite the grim assessment, Dholakia sees recovery signs ahead. Industry veterans remain cautiously optimistic about a demand rebound.
