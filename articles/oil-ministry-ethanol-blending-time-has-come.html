Oil Ministry Backs Higher Ethanol Blending, Calls It 'Idea Whose Time Has Come'

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Oil Ministry has endorsed higher ethanol blending, describing it as an idea whose time has come for India's energy transition
  • 02/India is moving toward 20% ethanol blending in petrol, up from the current 10% mandate, to reduce crude oil imports
  • 03/The policy is expected to boost sugarcane farmer incomes while cutting the country's $100 billion plus annual oil import bill

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas has strongly backed higher ethanol blending in petrol. An official described the move as an idea whose time has come.

India currently mandates 10% ethanol blending in petrol. The government has set a target of 20% blending by 2025-26.

The policy push comes as India imports over 85% of its crude oil requirements. The annual import bill exceeds $100 billion.

Ethanol is produced primarily from sugarcane molasses in India. It provides a crucial revenue stream for sugarcane farmers.

Higher blending levels will require expanding ethanol production capacity. Distilleries need upgrades to meet the 20% target timeline.

The Oil Ministry's endorsement signals strong government commitment. It removes uncertainty around the ethanol blending program's future direction.

The 20% target aligns with India's broader energy security goals. It also supports the agriculture sector through additional offtake.

INDIA CURRENTLY MANDATES 10% ETHANOL BLENDING IN PETROL UNDER THE ETHANOL BLENDED PETROL PROGRAM.
WHAT IS THE TARGET ETHANOL BLENDING RATE?
THE GOVERNMENT HAS SET A TARGET OF 20% ETHANOL BLENDING IN PETROL BY THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2025-26.
WHY IS THE OIL MINISTRY PUSHING FOR HIGHER ETHANOL BLENDING?
THE OIL MINISTRY VIEWS HIGHER ETHANOL BLENDING AS CRITICAL TO REDUCING INDIA'S DEPENDENCE ON CRUDE OIL IMPORTS, WHICH EXCEED $100 BILLION ANNUALLY.
HOW DOES ETHANOL BLENDING BENEFIT FARMERS?
ETHANOL IS PRODUCED FROM SUGARCANE MOLASSES, PROVIDING AN ADDITIONAL REVENUE STREAM AND STABLE OFTAKE FOR SUGARCANE FARMERS ACROSS INDIA.
WHAT CHALLENGES EXIST IN REACHING THE 20% TARGET?
EXPANDING FROM 10% TO 20% BLENDING REQUIRES SIGNIFICANT CAPACITY EXPANSION AT DISTILLERIES AND ADEQUATE FEEDSTOCK SUPPLY CHAINS TO MEET THE TIMELINE.
