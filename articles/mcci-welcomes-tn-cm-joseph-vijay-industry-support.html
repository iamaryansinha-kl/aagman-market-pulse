MCCI Welcomes New Tamil Nadu CM Joseph Vijay, Pledges Industry Support

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Madras Chamber of Commerce & Industry welcomes new TN CM Joseph Vijay and his elected Government
  • 02/MCCI President Ramkumar Shankar expressed confidence in the leadership to steer the State toward economic prosperity
  • 03/Chamber commits to working closely with the Government on policy formation, investment, and employment

The Madras Chamber of Commerce & Industry (MCCI) has formally welcomed Joseph Vijay as the new Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. The industry body issued a statement on Sunday, May 10, 2026, congratulating the newly elected Government on securing the people's mandate.

Ramkumar Shankar, President of MCCI, conveyed his best wishes to the new administration. He expressed confidence in its ability to guide the State toward greater economic prosperity and inclusive growth.

"We are confident that the new Government will continue to foster a progressive, stable and business-friendly ecosystem that promotes trade, industry, investment, innovation and entrepreneurship," Shankar said.

Shankar emphasized the importance of a strong partnership between Government and the business community. He said such collaboration would be crucial in accelerating economic development, enhancing competitiveness, and attracting investments.

The Chamber also committed to working closely with the Government on policy formation. It pledged to promote sustainable and inclusive economic initiatives across Tamil Nadu.

MCCI said it aims to contribute meaningfully to the State's long-term socio-economic development agenda. The body highlighted employment generation as a key priority in its engagement with the new administration.
