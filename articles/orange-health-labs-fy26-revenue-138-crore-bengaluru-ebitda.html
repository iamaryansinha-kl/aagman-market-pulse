Orange Health Labs FY26 Revenue Hits ₹138 Cr; Bengaluru at 20% EBITDA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Orange Health Labs reports ₹138 crore revenue for FY26, up 65% from ₹85 crore in FY25
  • 02/Bengaluru operations deliver 20% EBITDA at ₹85-90 crore run rate, contributing nearly half of total revenue
  • 03/Company plans expansion to 10-12 cities and entry into radiology over next 2-3 years

Orange Health Labs has reported ₹138 crore in revenue for FY26. The Bengaluru-based diagnostics startup grew 65% from ₹85 crore in FY25.

The company's annual revenue run rate stands at ₹180 crore. Co-founder Tarun Bhambra expects 60-70% growth in FY27.

Bengaluru contributes nearly half of total revenue. The city operates at ₹85-90 crore run rate with 20% EBITDA profitability.

The company runs a hub-based reverse logistics network. It has cut sample collection time from 60 minutes to 30 minutes.

At-home diagnostics now make up 20-25% of the industry. Five years ago, it was under 10%.

Preventive testing comprises 35-40% of Orange Health's business. The market-wide share has grown from 8-12% to 25-30%.

The company expanded from 15 to 75 collection centres. It targets 200 centres by fiscal end to tap the offline market.

Orange Health plans to enter 10-12 cities in 2-3 years. It will expand into radiology and scans beyond pathology.
