Dr Reddy's Gets Health Canada Approval for Generic Semaglutide Injection

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Reddy's Laboratories receives Health Canada NOC for generic Semaglutide injection, becoming first authorized company in Canada
  • 02/Approval covers 2mg and 4mg pens; Canada has 3.9 million diagnosed diabetes cases representing 9.7% of population
  • 03/Launch preparations are underway; CEO Erez Israeli calls Canada a priority market for GLP-1 expansion

Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (NSE: DRREDDY) has received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for its generic Semaglutide injection. The approval was announced on April 29, 2026.

The company becomes the first to receive market authorization for generic Semaglutide Injection in Canada. The approval came ahead of Health Canada's review target date.

The market authorization covers the 2 mg per pen and 4 mg per pen variants. Both formulations contain 1.34 mg per mL concentration.

Around 3.9 million people in Canada live with diagnosed diabetes. This represents 9.7% of the population over one year old.

Over 6% of Canadian adults live with prediabetes. The number of diabetes cases is expected to rise as the population ages.

Erez Israeli, Chief Executive Officer of Dr. Reddy's, said the approval marks a milestone in the company's GLP-1 journey. He highlighted expertise in peptide science and complex product development.

"Canada remains a priority market for us," Israeli said. The company is dedicated to expanding access to affordable GLP-1 treatments for patients.

Launch preparations are currently underway. Dr. Reddy's said it is well-positioned to bring the treatment option to Canadian patients.
