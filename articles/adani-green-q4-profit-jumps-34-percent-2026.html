Adani Green Q4 Profit Jumps 34% to ₹514 Cr on Strong Power Sales

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Green Energy Ltd reports Q4 FY26 consolidated profit of ₹514 crore, up 34.3% YoY from ₹383 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Power supply revenue rose 16% to ₹3,094 crore while EBITDA from power supply climbed 20% to ₹2,944 crore with margins steady at 91%
  • 03/Operational capacity reached 19,294 MW after adding record 5,051 MW in FY26; watch FY27 capacity addition and Khavda project progress

Adani Green Energy Ltd (NSE: ADANIGREEN) reported a 34.3% jump in consolidated profit for Q4 FY26. Net profit came in at ₹514 crore against ₹383 crore in the year-ago quarter.

The board approved the audited results on April 24, 2026. The strong bottom-line growth was driven by higher power sales and increased capacity utilisation across its renewable portfolio.

Revenue from power supply rose 16% year-on-year to ₹3,094 crore in Q4 FY26. Overall consolidated revenue climbed 13.7% to ₹37.27 crore for the quarter.

EBITDA from the core power supply unit grew 20% to ₹2,944 crore. The EBITDA margin held steady at an industry-leading 91%, unchanged from the year-ago period.

For the full fiscal year FY26, profit attributable to parent shareholders stood at ₹1,652 crore. This marks a 14% increase over the ₹1,444 crore reported in FY25.

The company achieved its highest-ever annual greenfield capacity addition of 5,051 MW in FY26. Total operational capacity now stands at 19,294 MW, up 35% from 14,243 MW at the end of FY25.

Energy sales volumes surged 34% YoY to 37,567 million units during FY26. The company achieved 106% of its annual commitment under existing Power Purchase Agreements.

"FY26 was a landmark year in Adani Green's history. The company commissioned 5.1 GW of greenfield capacity, the highest globally by any company outside China," said Sagar Adani, Executive Director at Adani Green Energy.

The flagship 30 GW Khavda renewable energy plant in Gujarat now has 9.4 GW operational. The company also installed 1,376 MWh of battery storage capacity at Khavda during the fiscal year.

Adani Green shares traded around ₹940 on the NSE ahead of the results. The stock has fallen sharply from its 52-week high of ₹2,100, reflecting broader Adani group volatility and sector headwinds.
