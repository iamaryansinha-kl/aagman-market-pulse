India's Factory Output Growth Slows to 4.1% in March IIP Data

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Index of Industrial Production grew 4.1% YoY in March 2026, down from 5.2% in February per the Statistics Ministry
  • 02/Manufacturing expanded 4.3% and mining rose 5.5%, but electricity output grew only 0.8%
  • 03/Capital goods surged 14.6% and auto manufacturing jumped 18.1%; watch electricity weakness ahead

India's factory output growth slowed to 4.1% year-on-year in March 2026. The Index of Industrial Production fell from 5.2% growth in February, data from the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation showed.

The slowdown was driven by weaker electricity output. Electricity generation grew just 0.8% in March, a sharp deceleration from previous months.

Manufacturing remained the backbone of growth at 4.3%. Mining also held up well with 5.5% growth during the month.

Auto manufacturing was a standout performer. The "manufacture of motor vehicles, trailers and semi-trailers" category surged 18.1%. Auto components, commercial vehicles, and axles drove this jump.

Capital goods output surged 14.6% in March. Infrastructure and construction goods grew 6.7%, while consumer durables rose 5.3%.

The three top use-based contributors were infrastructure goods, capital goods, and primary goods. Basic metals output expanded 8.6%, while machinery and equipment grew 11.2%.

Consumer non-durables growth was muted at 1.1%. Intermediate goods grew 3.3%, while primary goods expanded just 2.2%.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS INDIA'S FACTORY OUTPUT GROWTH IN MARCH 2026?
INDIA'S INDEX OF INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTION (IIP) GREW 4.1% YEAR-ON-YEAR IN MARCH 2026. THIS IS DOWN FROM 5.2% GROWTH RECORDED IN FEBRUARY 2026, ACCORDING TO DATA RELEASED BY THE MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION.
WHICH SECTORS DROVE GROWTH AND WHICH LAGGED?
MANUFACTURING GREW 4.3% AND MINING EXPANDED 5.5% IN MARCH 2026. HOWEVER, ELECTRICITY OUTPUT ROSE ONLY 0.8%, PULLING DOWN THE OVERALL IIP GROWTH. AUTO MANUFACTURING SURGED 18.1%, CAPITAL GOODS JUMPED 14.6%, AND INFRASTRUCTURE GOODS GREW 6.7%.
WHAT WERE THE TOP CONTRIBUTORS TO IIP GROWTH IN MARCH?
THE TOP THREE POSITIVE CONTRIBUTORS WERE "MANUFACTURE OF BASIC METALS" AT 8.6%, "MANUFACTURE OF MOTOR VEHICLES, TRAILERS AND SEMI-TRAILERS" AT 18.1%, AND "MANUFACTURE OF MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT" AT 11.2%. AUTO COMPONENTS, COMMERCIAL VEHICLES, AND AXLES DROVE THE AUTO SEGMENT.
HOW DID DIFFERENT USE-BASED CATEGORIES PERFORM?
CAPITAL GOODS SURGED 14.6%, INFRASTRUCTURE AND CONSTRUCTION GOODS ROSE 6.7%, AND CONSUMER DURABLES GREW 5.3%. CONSUMER NON-DURABLES GREW ONLY 1.1%, INTERMEDIATE GOODS EXPANDED 3.3%, AND PRIMARY GOODS ROSE JUST 2.2%.
WHY DOES THE ELECTRICITY SECTOR WEAKNESS MATTER?
ELECTRICITY OUTPUT GREW ONLY 0.8% IN MARCH 2026, A SHARP SLOWDOWN COMPARED TO PREVIOUS MONTHS. THIS DRAGGED THE OVERALL IIP GROWTH DOWN FROM 5.2% IN FEBRUARY TO 4.1% IN MARCH. SUSTAINED WEAKNESS IN ELECTRICITY COULD SIGNAL BROADER INFRASTRUCTURE OR DEMAND CONSTRAINTS AHEAD.
