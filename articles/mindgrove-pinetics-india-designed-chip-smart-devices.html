Mindgrove-Pinetics Partnership Brings India-Designed Chip Into Smart Devices

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mindgrove Technologies and Pinetics sign two-year commercial partnership for India-designed chips in smart devices
  • 02/This marks the first time an India-designed chip integrates into commercial biometric access control, smart locks and camera applications
  • 03/The collaboration addresses a critical gap in India's electronics value chain from silicon to finished products

Mindgrove Technologies has entered a commercial partnership with Pinetics. The two-year agreement marks the first integration of an India-designed chip into commercial smart devices.

Mindgrove is a fabless semiconductor company headquartered at IIT Madras Research Park. Pinetics is a Pune-based electronic product design and development firm.

The partnership brings indigenous semiconductor technology to biometric access control systems, smart locks and camera applications. Expansion to additional product categories is planned.

India has established capabilities in PCB design and assembly. However, nearly all semiconductor components have historically been imported, leaving a persistent gap in the value chain.

Pinetics will build systems on module around Mindgrove's silicon. This extends domestic chip design downstream to the product layer for OEMs and ODMs.

Mindgrove's Secure IoT SoC is already taped out at 28nm and runs at 700 MHz. It is production-ready for biometric systems, smart locks, meters and PoS machines.

Its Vision SoC is currently in development under India's Design Linked Incentive scheme. This chip targets CCTV cameras, dashcams, ADAS and smart TV applications.

The partnership creates a viable alternative to imported solutions in a sector dominated by foreign modules. It represents the first opportunity for India's electronics manufacturing base to source domestically designed silicon.

"When domestic product companies choose to build on Indian silicon, it accelerates the entire value chain," said Shashwath TR, Co-founder and CEO of Mindgrove Technologies.

"We can now design modules around silicon that is developed in India, supported in India, and priced for the Indian market," said Navin Goyal, Co-Founder and CEO of Pinetics.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES THE MINDGROVE-PINETICS PARTNERSHIP DO?
THE TWO-YEAR AGREEMENT INTEGRATES INDIA-DESIGNED CHIPS FROM MINDGROVE INTO COMMERCIAL SMART DEVICES BUILT BY PINETICS. THIS IS THE FIRST SUCH DOMESTIC CHIP-TO-PRODUCT PARTNERSHIP FOR BIOMETRIC SYSTEMS, SMART LOCKS AND CAMERAS.
WHAT CHIPS IS MINDGROVE PROVIDING?
MINDGROVE'S SECURE IOT SOC IS ALREADY PRODUCTION-READY AT 28NM, 700 MHZ FOR BIOMETRIC SYSTEMS AND SMART LOCKS. ITS VISION SOC IS IN DEVELOPMENT FOR CCTV, DASHCAMS, ADAS AND SMART TV APPLICATIONS.
WHY IS THIS PARTNERSHIP SIGNIFICANT FOR INDIA?
INDIA HAS PCB DESIGN AND ASSEMBLY CAPABILITIES BUT IMPORTS NEARLY ALL SEMICONDUCTOR COMPONENTS. THIS COLLABORATION CLOSES THE GAP BY CREATING A DOMESTIC PATH FROM CHIP DESIGN TO FINISHED PRODUCTS.
WHAT PRODUCTS WILL USE THESE INDIGENOUS CHIPS?
INITIAL APPLICATIONS INCLUDE BIOMETRIC ACCESS CONTROL SYSTEMS, SMART LOCKS, AND CAMERA APPLICATIONS. EXPANSION TO ADDITIONAL PRODUCT CATEGORIES IS PLANNED OVER THE TWO-YEAR AGREEMENT PERIOD.
WHAT IS THE DESIGN LINKED INCENTIVE SCHEME?
THE GOVERNMENT'S DLI SCHEME OFFERS FINANCIAL INCENTIVES AND DESIGN INFRASTRUCTURE SUPPORT FOR 100 DOMESTIC COMPANIES, STARTUPS AND MSMES DEVELOPING SEMICONDUCTOR DESIGNS OVER FIVE YEARS. MINDGROVE'S VISION SOC IS SUPPORTED UNDER THIS SCHEME.
