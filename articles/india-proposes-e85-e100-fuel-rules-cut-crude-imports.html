India Proposes E85 and E100 Fuel Rules to Cut Crude Oil Imports

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a draft notification on April 28, 2026 to amend Central Motor Vehicles Rules for E85 and E100 fuels
  • 02/E85 blends 85% ethanol with petrol; E100 allows vehicles to run on nearly pure ethanol as India pushes beyond its achieved E20 target
  • 03/Draft rules are open for public comments; E85 is expected to debut as a separate fuel grade to avoid misfuelling in existing vehicles

India's Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a draft notification late on April 28, 2026. The proposal amends the Central Motor Vehicles Rules to formally incorporate higher ethanol-blended fuels.

The draft includes provisions for E85 fuel, a blend of 85% ethanol with petrol. It also covers E100, which would allow vehicles to run on nearly pure ethanol.

The draft rules have been opened for public comments. The government will take a final decision after reviewing stakeholder feedback.

India achieved its target of 20% ethanol blending (E20) in 2025. The nationwide rollout of E20 petrol with a minimum RON of 95 was mandated from April 1, 2026.

The move to higher blends aims to reduce costly imports of petroleum products. India currently imports about 85% of its crude oil requirements.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi said ethanol blending has saved India approximately ₹1.5 lakh crore in foreign exchange. It has also cut crude imports by about 4.5 crore barrels annually.

India's ethanol sector now has a cumulative production capacity of around 2,000 crore litres. About 1,000 crore litres are currently used for blending with petrol.

E85 is expected to debut as a separate fuel grade rather than replacing existing blends. This would protect the large installed base of vehicles engineered for E10 and E20.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAS INDIA'S MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT PROPOSED REGARDING ETHANOL FUELS?
THE MINISTRY ISSUED A DRAFT NOTIFICATION ON APRIL 28, 2026 PROPOSING AMENDMENTS TO THE CENTRAL MOTOR VEHICLES RULES. THE DRAFT INCLUDES PROVISIONS FOR E85 (85% ETHANOL WITH PETROL) AND E100 (NEARLY PURE ETHANOL) FUELS.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF ETHANOL BLENDING IN INDIA?
INDIA ACHIEVED ITS 20% ETHANOL BLENDING TARGET (E20) IN 2025. THE NATIONWIDE MANDATORY ROLLOUT OF E20 PETROL WITH MINIMUM RON 95 BEGAN ON APRIL 1, 2026.
HOW MUCH FOREIGN EXCHANGE HAS ETHANOL BLENDING SAVED FOR INDIA?
PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA MODI STATED THAT ETHANOL BLENDING HAS SAVED INDIA APPROXIMATELY ₹1.5 LAKH CRORE IN FOREIGN EXCHANGE. IT HAS ALSO REDUCED CRUDE OIL IMPORTS BY ABOUT 4.5 CRORE BARRELS ANNUALLY.
WHY IS E85 EXPECTED TO BE A SEPARATE FUEL GRADE?
E85 IS EXPECTED TO BE OFFERED AS A SEPARATE FUEL GRADE TO AVOID MISFUELLING. USING E85 IN NON-COMPATIBLE VEHICLES CAN CAUSE CORROSION, ENGINE DAMAGE, AND OTHER MECHANICAL ISSUES.
WHAT IS INDIA'S ETHANOL PRODUCTION CAPACITY?
INDIA'S ETHANOL SECTOR HAS A CUMULATIVE PRODUCTION CAPACITY OF AROUND 2,000 CRORE LITRES. OF THIS, ABOUT 1,000 CRORE LITRES ARE CURRENTLY USED FOR BLENDING WITH PETROL.
