India's WPI Inflation Edges Higher to 9.9% in August, Fuel and Power Lead the Rise

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/WPI inflation rose to 9.9% YoY in August from 9.8% in July; WPI was nil in August 2025
  • 02/Fuel and power inflation jumped to 22.9% from 20.1%; mineral oil inflation climbed to 38.5%
  • 03/Food inflation hit a 20-month high of 7.1%; Ind-Ra projects WPI at 10.2% in September

India's wholesale price inflation edged higher to 9.9% year-on-year in August from 9.8% in July, official data released Monday showed. WPI inflation was nil in August 2025, underscoring how sharply price pressures have built over the past year.

Fuel and power remained the largest contributor, rising to 22.9% in August from 20.1% in July. Mineral oil inflation climbed to 38.5% from 32.4%, while crude petroleum and natural gas inflation rose to 34.4% from 27%.

Manufactured products inflation increased marginally to 8.4% from 8.3%. Within manufacturing, chemical products recorded 14.3% inflation, tobacco 13.2%, and textiles 12.6%.

Primary articles inflation eased to 7.8% from 8.5%. Food inflation, accounting for around a quarter of the WPI basket, rose to a 20-month high of 7.1% in August from 6.7% in July.

"WPI food inflation was largely led by fruits and vegetables, milk, spices, and sugar segments," said Rahul Agrawal, principal economist at ICRA. He expects food inflation to harden further in September-October on an unfavourable base and stronger sequential price increases.

Producer Price Index output inflation grew to 9.8% year-on-year in August from 9.6% in July. The government's trial Input PPI for manufacturing fell to 104.2 in August from 105.9 in July, a 1.6% decline.

Devendra Pant, chief economist at India Ratings and Research, said global uncertainty from the unresolved West Asia crisis will keep inflation elevated. Deficient rain and a weaker currency add further pressure.

Ind-Ra expects wholesale inflation to increase to 10.2% in September and remain elevated through the rest of 2026. ICRA projects WPI to exceed 9.5-10% in September-October before cooling on a favourable base.

Electricity prices remained in deflation, but renewed strength in crude petroleum due to the West Asia crisis pushed fuel inflation higher. Economists expect elevated fuel inflation until a permanent resolution to the crisis is reached.
