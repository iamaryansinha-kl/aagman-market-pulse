Markets Open Higher: Sensex Up 0.73%, Maruti Surges 4% as Kotak Falls 4.2%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex opens at 77,257.27 and surges 563.98 points (0.73%) to 77,477.48 shortly after market open on May 4, 2026.
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki rallies 4.17% to ₹13,869 and Bajaj Auto climbs 3.90% to ₹10,383.50, while Kotak Mahindra Bank drops 4.21% to ₹367.15.
  • 03/FIIs extended selling streak to nine sessions, offloading over ₹8,000 crore on April 30; DII buying continues for six straight sessions.

Benchmark indices opened sharply higher on May 4, buoyed by global cues and election sentiment. The BSE Sensex gained 563.98 points or 0.73 per cent to 77,477.48 shortly after 9:16 am. The NSE Nifty 50 rose 179.45 points or 0.75 per cent to 24,177.00.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) surged 4.17 per cent to ₹13,869, after opening at ₹13,559 against a previous close of ₹13,314. Bajaj Auto Ltd (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO) climbed 3.90 per cent to ₹10,383.50 from a previous close of ₹9,994. The auto sector gained on robust monthly sales data showing 8-10 per cent year-on-year growth.

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd (NSE: ADANIPORTS) rose 3.74 per cent to ₹1,719.30. Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) gained 1.95 per cent to ₹2,294.70, while Tata Consumer Products Ltd (NSE: TATACONSUM) added 2.11 per cent to ₹1,168.70.

Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd (NSE: KOTAKBANK) was the biggest drag on the Nifty, falling 4.21 per cent to ₹367.15 from a previous close of ₹383.30. Over 50.49 lakh shares worth ₹18,647.95 lakhs changed hands, the highest volume among Nifty movers. Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (NSE: DRREDDY) dropped 2.33 per cent to ₹1,292.10.

"Today's market action may be unduly influenced by the state election results with focus on West Bengal," said Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments. "The real market trend will be guided by crude oil prices, which will be decided by news and happenings in West Asia."

Crude oil prices stayed elevated with July Brent futures at $108.60 and WTI June futures at $102.12. The Indian rupee slipped past the 95-per-dollar mark on April 30, pressuring import-heavy sectors. India's GST collections hit a record ₹2,42,702 crore in April 2026, up 8.7 per cent year-on-year.

Foreign institutional investors extended their selling streak to nine consecutive sessions on April 30, offloading equities worth over ₹8,000 crore. Domestic institutional investors invested ₹3,487 crore in equities for six straight sessions. India VIX remained elevated at around 18.4, signalling continued market caution.

Nifty faces immediate resistance at 24,300-24,400, while 23,800 remains a key support level. "Despite the broader trend holding positive, the current phase reflects consolidation, and a clear breakout above the resistance band is needed to extend the upmove," said Hitesh Tailor, Research Analyst at Choice Equity Broking.
