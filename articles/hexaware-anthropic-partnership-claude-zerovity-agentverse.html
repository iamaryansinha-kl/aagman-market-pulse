Hexaware Signs Multi-Year Anthropic Pact; Stock Jumps 11% to Rs 566

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hexaware Technologies shares surged 11% to Rs 566 on Friday after announcing a multi-year partnership with Anthropic.
  • 02/Claude AI models will be integrated into Hexaware's Zerovity engineering platform and AgentVerse, its platform for building and governing AI agents with over 600 pre-built agents.
  • 03/Hexaware joins Anthropic's Claude Partner Network as a Preferred Partner, with more than 1,100 Claude-certified professionals already on staff.

Hexaware Technologies has signed a multi-year partnership with Anthropic. The IT services firm will bring Anthropic's Claude AI models into its platforms, and its shares surged 11% to Rs 566 on Friday on the news.

The deal makes Hexaware a Preferred Partner in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network. Both companies will work jointly on AI solutions and enterprise deployments, focusing on IT operations and software engineering.

The integration targets two Hexaware platforms. Zerovity is the company's AI-native engineering platform; AgentVerse is its platform for building and governing AI agents, with more than 600 pre-built agents that enterprises can deploy.

The pitch is about converting AI interest into outcomes. IT services firms are under pressure to show that enterprise AI enthusiasm can translate into billable work, and Hexaware is positioning this partnership as exactly that bridge.

"Enterprises are focused on driving business outcomes. We are putting Claude at the heart of Zerovity and AgentVerse, so our clients reach production results faster, at a cost they can plan around," said Siddharth Dhar, President and Global Head - AI at Hexaware.

The company has already built a trained bench. More than 1,100 professionals are certified on Claude, and Hexaware plans to expand training across its delivery teams.

The platforms target familiar enterprise pain points. Zerovity's Zero Friction Enterprise offerings address technical debt, security vulnerabilities, backlogs, support tickets, process bottlenecks and software licence costs.

For development teams, Zerovity offers token optimisation and built-in software delivery agents — a direct answer to the cost anxiety that has stalled many corporate AI budgets.

Clients can engage in two ways: either by starting with a specific business outcome, or by connecting their existing development tools directly to the Zerovity platform.

This is not a cold-start relationship. Hexaware is already authorised to resell Claude through Amazon Bedrock, so the Preferred Partner badge formalises an existing commercial tie rather than opening a new one.

Anthropic is leaning in because services firms are its route to enterprise scale. "Hexaware's customers already trust them with IT operations and software engineering. That is where much of this work gets done and where Claude can have an outsized impact," said Era Sahni, Head of Partnerships - International at Anthropic.

"Our teams have already certified more than 1,100 people on Claude. As a Preferred Partner, we can take that capability to clients together with Anthropic, from the first proof of value to production at scale," said Vinod Chandran, Chief Operating Officer of Hexaware.

For investors, the caveat is disclosure. The company has not quantified the revenue potential of the partnership, and alliance announcements in Indian IT have a mixed record of converting into material earnings. The 11% stock jump prices in optimism that delivery numbers will eventually have to justify.
