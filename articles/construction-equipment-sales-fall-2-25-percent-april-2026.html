Construction Equipment Sales Fall 2.25% in April 2026 on High Base Year

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Retail sales of construction equipment fell 2.25% YoY to 6,348 units in April 2026 from 6,494 units in April 2025
  • 02/JCB India expanded market share to 45.02% from 41.18% YoY, while Action Construction Equipment slipped to 11.55% from 14.29%
  • 03/ICRA forecasts 3-5% FY27 industry growth, supported by the Centre's ₹12.2 lakh crore capex outlay in the Budget

Retail sales of construction equipment fell 2.25% year-on-year to 6,348 units in April 2026. The figure was down from 6,494 units in the same month last year, according to data released by the Federation of Automobile Dealers Associations.

Sales also declined 8% month-on-month from 6,906 units in March 2026. The category includes excavators, backhoe loaders, wheel loaders and bulldozers.

JCB India retained its leadership position and expanded market share to 45.02% in April 2026 from 41.18% a year earlier. Action Construction Equipment, ranked second, saw its share drop to 11.55% from 14.29%.

However, Ajax Engineering, Escorts Kubota and Bull Machines each improved their market share on a year-on-year basis. The FADA data showed a shift in the competitive landscape beyond the top two.

"The 2.25% decline in April 2026 was largely optical, as the base period benefited from accelerated inventory clearance ahead of the CEV-V emission norms transition" - Ritu Goswami, Vice-President and Sector Head, Corporate Sector Ratings, ICRA Ltd.

ICRA has maintained its FY27 construction equipment industry volume growth forecast at 3-5%. The outlook is supported by the Union government's ₹12.2 lakh crore capital expenditure outlay in the FY27 Budget.

Higher allocations for highways, railways, the Jal Jeevan Mission and PMGSY extension till 2028 are expected to drive demand. The infrastructure pipeline remains robust despite the April blip.
