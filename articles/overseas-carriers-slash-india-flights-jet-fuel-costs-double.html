Overseas Carriers Slash India Flights as Jet Fuel Costs Double Since March

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Qantas temporarily suspends Sydney-Bengaluru flights from August through October-end due to soaring jet fuel costs
  • 02/Thai AirAsia cuts capacity by 30% and suspends Phuket-Chennai and Phuket-Kochi routes as fuel prices double since March
  • 03/Air India reduces Europe and North America flights by 10-12% with CEO warning further trims for June and July schedules

International airlines are scaling back India operations as jet fuel costs surge. Qantas will suspend Sydney-Bengaluru flights from August through October-end.

Thai AirAsia has cut overall capacity by 30 per cent. The low-cost carrier is suspending Phuket-Chennai and Phuket-Kochi services.

Jet fuel prices for international flights have doubled since March. Domestic carriers face a capped 25 per cent surcharge hike limit.

Air India is reducing Europe and North America flight frequency by 10-12 per cent. The airline is also trimming its upcoming Mumbai-Tokyo route in July.

Air India CEO Campbell Wilson said rising fuel costs made routes unprofitable. The airline cut flights in April and May and will trim June and July schedules.

Anil Kalsi, Vice-President of Travel Agents Federation of India, said international leisure demand has slowed. Customers are now thinking twice before booking trips.

Air India Express has reduced flights to Thailand in May. Popular South-East Asian routes have not seen demand pickup amid elevated prices.

QANTAS IS TEMPORARILY SUSPENDING FLIGHTS BETWEEN SYDNEY AND BENGALURU FROM AUGUST THROUGH OCTOBER-END. THE AUSTRALIAN CARRIER HAS ALSO REDUCED FREQUENCY ON DOMESTIC AND NEW ZEALAND ROUTES.

THAI AIRASIA HAS CUT OVERALL CAPACITY BY 30 PER CENT. THE AIRLINE IS SUSPENDING FLIGHTS FROM PHUKET TO CHENNAI AND KOCHI, AND REDUCING SERVICES TO MULTIPLE OTHER INDIAN CITIES.

AIR INDIA IS REDUCING EUROPE AND NORTH AMERICA FLIGHT FREQUENCY BY 10-12 PER CENT. THE AIRLINE IS ALSO TRIMMING ITS UPCOMING MUMBAI-TOKYO ROUTE IN JULY AND CUTTING FLIGHTS IN APRIL, MAY, JUNE AND JULY.
