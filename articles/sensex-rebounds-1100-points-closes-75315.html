Sensex Rebounds 1,100 Points From Day's Low to Close at 75,315

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex recovered 1,100 points from day's low to close at 75,315 on May 18, 2026
  • 02/Nifty 50 gained 333 points from intraday low to end the session at 23,650
  • 03/Benchmark indices showed resilience amid volatile global cues; banking and IT stocks led recovery

Indian equity markets staged a sharp rebound on Monday, May 18, 2026. The BSE Sensex recovered 1,100 points from its day's low to settle at 75,315.

The Nifty 50 index gained 333 points from its intraday low. It closed the session at 23,650.

At the final bell, the Sensex was up 77 points from its previous close. The Nifty registered a modest gain of 6 points.

Banking and IT sectors led the recovery from lower levels. Select large-cap stocks attracted buying interest during the afternoon session.

The sharp rebound suggests underlying market resilience. Investors stepped in after the initial sell-off.

Global cues remained mixed during the trading session. Domestic flows likely provided support at lower levels.

Traders will watch for follow-through buying in the coming sessions. The 23,500 level on Nifty now acts as immediate support.
