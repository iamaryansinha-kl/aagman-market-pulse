Johnson Lifts Raises Toshiba JV Stake to 80% from 49%, Becomes Majority Owner

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Johnson Lifts Pvt. Ltd. increased its stake in Toshiba Johnson Elevators (India) Pvt. Ltd. from 49% to over 80%, becoming the majority shareholder.
  • 02/The combined group now commands 20% of India's elevator market and 30% of the escalator market, per company statements.
  • 03/Wilfred Martin Clarence continues as Managing Director; TJEI remains the exclusive Indian partner for Toshiba-branded products.

Johnson Lifts Pvt. Ltd., India's largest elevator and escalator manufacturer with a turnover of ₹4,000 crore, has increased its stake in Toshiba Johnson Elevators (India) Pvt. Ltd. to over 80%. The move was announced on April 27, 2026, and lifts Johnson Lifts from its previous 49% holding.

Toshiba Johnson is a joint venture between Johnson Lifts and Toshiba Elevator and Building Systems Corporation. The partnership was formed in 2012 and operates under the TJEI brand in India.

Wilfred Martin Clarence will continue as Managing Director of Toshiba Johnson. He also joins the Board of Directors following the stake increase, the company confirmed at a press briefing in Chennai.

Yohan K John, Director at Johnson Lifts, told reporters that the combined presence of JLPL and TJEI represents 20% of the elevator market and 30% of the escalator market in India. This strengthens the group's position in the premium and high-speed mobility segment.

"The company now targets deeper market penetration, strengthening sales and service capabilities and enhance customer engagement aiming to establish itself as the leading Japanese elevator brand in India," said Wilfred Martin Clarence, Managing Director of Toshiba Johnson.

TJEI will continue as the sole and exclusive business partner in India for Toshiba-branded elevators, escalators, and travelators. It retains responsibility for sales, installation, maintenance, customer service, and all ongoing projects.

The Indian elevator and escalator market has been growing steadily amid rapid urbanization and infrastructure development. The Asia Pacific region held 41.5% of the global elevator market in 2026, according to Coherent Market Insights.
