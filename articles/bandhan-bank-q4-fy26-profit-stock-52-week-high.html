Bandhan Bank Q4: Profit Up 68% to ₹530 Cr, Stock Hits 52-Week High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bandhan Bank Q4 FY26 net profit rises 68% YoY to ₹530 crore, beating street estimates by over 100%
  • 02/Stock surges 10% to 52-week high of ₹199.90; up 34% in past month, sharply outperforming Nifty Private Bank
  • 03/JM Financial and Nirmal Bang turn constructive; watch FY27 ROA guidance of 1.6-1.8% and asset quality trajectory

Bandhan Bank shares surged over 10% to hit a 52-week high of ₹199.90 on the NSE on Wednesday, April 29. The stock traded at ₹196.76 around 11:08 am with over 6.38 crore shares changing hands and a traded value of ₹1,249 crore.

The rally follows Q4 FY26 results declared on April 28, which came in significantly ahead of street expectations. Net profit rose 68% year-on-year and 159% sequentially to ₹530 crore.

Net interest income grew modestly to ₹2,795 crore. The net interest margin recovered to 6.2% from 5.9% in the previous quarter, signaling improved profitability.

Asset quality showed clear improvement. Gross NPA ratio eased to 3.3% and credit cost fell sharply to 2.0% from 3.9% a year ago, aided by lower slippages in the Emerging Entrepreneurs Business portfolio.

Provisions dropped to ₹677 crore against an expected ₹1,008 crore. The sharp decline in credit cost and slippages has boosted investor confidence in the bank's turnaround story.

JM Financial upgraded its target price to ₹200 from ₹160, maintaining an ADD rating. The brokerage noted credit cost fell to 1.9% versus its estimate of 3.1%, while PAT beat estimates by 115%.

Nirmal Bang's retail research desk called the results "above expectations." Adjusted PAT of ₹534 crore beat their estimate of ₹410 crore, and the stock trades at a trailing P/ABV of 1.2x.

The stock has gained nearly 34% over the past month and 38% year-to-date. Management has maintained its FY27 exit ROA guidance of 1.6–1.8%, a target the market is increasingly willing to credit.
