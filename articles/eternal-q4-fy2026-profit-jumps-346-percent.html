Eternal Q4 FY2026: Net Profit Jumps 346% to ₹174 Crore, Brokerages Raise Targets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eternal Q4 FY26 net profit surged 346% YoY to ₹174 crore; revenue jumped 196% to ₹17,292 crore
  • 02/Morgan Stanley raised target price to ₹347; Investec and UBS maintained buy ratings with targets of ₹375 and ₹310
  • 03/Macquarie flagged risks with underperform rating; competitive intensity and moderating quick commerce growth are near-term concerns

Eternal Limited (NSE: ETERNAL) reported consolidated net profit of ₹174 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 346% jump from ₹39 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Consolidated revenue from operations surged 196% year-on-year to ₹17,292 crore. The corresponding quarter last year saw revenue of ₹5,833 crore.

Shares jumped as much as 5% in early trade on April 29, 2026, hitting an intraday high of ₹265.40. The stock closed at ₹253.07 on the previous day.

Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating and raised its target price to ₹347 from ₹345. The brokerage highlighted that the focus remains on profitable growth.

Investec maintained a buy rating with a target of ₹375, citing improving visibility on future growth. UBS also held a buy rating with a target of ₹310, calling the Q4 performance steady.

Macquarie maintained an underperform rating with a target of ₹200. It flagged concerns around moderating sequential growth and warned that elevated competitive intensity could pressure unit economics.

Food delivery net order value grew 18.8% YoY to ₹97.6 billion. Margins held steady at 5.5% quarter-on-quarter. Blinkit net order value surged over 95% YoY despite seasonal moderation.

Management expects quick commerce net order value to grow at over 60% CAGR. The company has guided for $1 billion adjusted EBITDA by FY29, supported by improving unit economics.
