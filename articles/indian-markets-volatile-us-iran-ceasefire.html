Indian Markets Volatile as US-Iran Ceasefire Expiry Hits Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian equity markets are expected to open cautiously on April 21 as the two-week US-Iran ceasefire expires, with Gift Nifty signaling an 80-point gap-up at 24,410
  • 02/India VIX trades at 18.79, up 9.18% from previous close, reflecting elevated uncertainty around geopolitical tensions and weekly derivatives expiry
  • 03/Q4 earnings from HCL Technologies, Nestle India and Tata Elxsi alongside crude oil at ₹8,329/barrel on MCX will drive stock-specific action through the session

Indian markets are poised for a volatile trading session on Tuesday, April 21, 2026. The two-week ceasefire between the United States and Iran is set to expire today, keeping traders on edge.

The S&P BSE Sensex closed at 78,520.30 on Monday, up 26.76 points. The Nifty 50 settled at 24,364.85, gaining 11.30 points. Gift Nifty at 24,410 signals an opening gap-up of roughly 80 points.

"With the truce period nearing expiry, uncertainty has heightened around the next phase of negotiations," said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money. Market participants are focused on whether the ceasefire will be extended or tensions could resurface.

Crude oil futures on MCX surged ₹562 to ₹8,329 per barrel. Gold fell ₹668 to ₹153,941 per 10 grams, while silver dropped ₹4,675 to ₹252,467 per kg. Oil prices have climbed 31% since the Iran conflict began.

Foreign portfolio investors sold shares worth ₹39,907 crore in April through April 16. This follows cash sales of ₹122,540 crore in March and ₹41,435 crore in January. Total FPI outflows since the war began stand at ₹1.61 lakh crore.

The Indian rupee weakened 19 paise to close at 93.10 against the US dollar. The 10-year bond yield opened at 6.95% versus the prior close of 7.05%, reflecting some stabilization.

India VIX, the volatility gauge, trades at 18.79, up 9.18% from its previous close of 17.21. "Derivatives activity is expected to drive heightened volatility given the weekly expiry," said Hariprasad K, Founder of Livelong Wealth.

Key companies reporting Q4 FY2026 results today include HCL Technologies, Nestle India and Tata Elxsi. Management commentary on demand visibility and margin outlook will be closely tracked.

Global cues remain mixed. US markets closed lower amid renewed geopolitical tensions over the weekend. Japan's Nikkei is trading higher by over 400 points, while South Korea's Kospi hit fresh record highs on earnings optimism.

Analysts expect a relief rally if diplomatic talks show progress. A breakdown in negotiations could trigger renewed selling pressure across Indian equities, particularly in oil marketing and aviation stocks.
