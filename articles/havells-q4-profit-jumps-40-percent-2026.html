Havells Q4 Net Profit Surges 40% To ₹723 Crore On Strong Consumer Demand

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Havells India Q4 FY26 net profit jumps 40% year-on-year to ₹723.39 crore, beating analyst estimates
  • 02/Revenue growth driven by strong demand in electrical consumer durables and switchgear segments
  • 03/FY27 outlook positive on rural recovery and new product launches in kitchen appliances

Havells India Ltd has reported a 40% jump in net profit for the fourth quarter of FY2026. Profit after tax stood at ₹723.39 crore compared to ₹516 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations grew 22% year-on-year to ₹5,847 crore. The electrical consumer durables segment led the growth, followed by cables and switchgear.

EBITDA margins expanded 140 basis points to 14.8%. Raw material cost stabilisation and premium product mix drove the margin improvement.

The Lloyd consumer durables segment reported a 28% revenue increase. Air conditioner sales were strong ahead of the summer season.

Management guided for 15-18% revenue growth in FY2027. Rural demand recovery and government infrastructure spending are key tailwinds.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹6 per share. Combined with the interim dividend, total payout for FY26 reaches ₹12 per share.

Havells shares closed 3.2% higher on April 22. The stock has gained 18% year-to-date, outperforming the Nifty FMCG index.
