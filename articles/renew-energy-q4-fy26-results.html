ReNew Energy Q4 Net Drops 75% to ₹77.7 Cr; FY26 PAT Doubles

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ReNew Energy Global Q4FY26 net profit drops 75% year-on-year to ₹77.7 crore
  • 02/Despite the weak quarter, full year FY26 profit after tax jumped twofold from FY25
  • 03/Results reported on May 19, 2026; investors await FY27 outlook and commissioning pipeline

ReNew Energy Global reported a 75% year-on-year decline in net profit for Q4FY26. The quarterly profit stood at ₹77.7 crore.

The sharp drop comes despite the renewable power producer delivering strong full-year numbers. FY26 profit after tax doubled compared to the previous fiscal year.

This divergence suggests uneven earnings distribution across the four quarters. Q4 typically sees project commissioning delays and revenue recognition volatility for renewable firms.

ReNew Energy is among India's largest renewable power developers. It builds and operates utility-scale wind and solar projects across multiple states.

The company's shares are widely held by global and domestic institutional investors. Renewable energy stocks remain sensitive to policy changes, tariff trends, and interest rate cycles.

The annual doubling of PAT indicates stronger operational performance in the first three quarters of FY26. Investors will now look for management commentary on the factors behind the Q4 compression.

Going forward, the FY27 project pipeline and commissioning schedule will be critical monitorables. Order book health and capacity additions will drive the next phase of earnings growth.
