ECCO Plans 50 Stores in India by 2028 as Premium Footwear Demand Surges

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ECCO currently operates 9 stores in India and plans to expand to 20 by year-end and 50 by 2028
  • 02/The company entered India in 2019 with just two stores; nearly 80 multi-brand points of sale also exist
  • 03/80% of revenue expected from top 8 metros; women's segment share to rise from current 25%

Danish premium footwear brand ECCO is accelerating its India expansion. The company plans to grow from 9 stores to 20 by year-end, targeting 50 stores by 2028.

ECCO entered India in 2019 with just two stores. It now operates nine outlets alongside nearly 80 points of sale across multi-brand channels.

Footwear prices start above ₹10,000 per pair. The brand competes in the premium segment where consumers seek quality over discounts.

"India was ignored for a very long time, but today it is one of the most high-potential markets for us," said Sumeet Lohia, Country Manager, ECCO India.

The company expects nearly 80% of business to come from the top eight Indian cities. It has no plans to chase lower price points despite rising competition.

"We are not playing the price-conscious game," said Lohia. "Our focus is on building a premium walking brand."

Globally, women account for 55% of ECCO's sales. In India, men dominate at 75% as women's premium consumption is still emerging.

The company remains offline-heavy. Consumers spending ₹20,000 on shoes want to touch, feel, and try before buying.

Quick commerce is not part of the immediate strategy. ECCO may evaluate it for shoe-care products later.
