India Wheat Procurement Tops 30 MT, Nears Revised 34.5 MT Target

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Centre procured 30.15 MT wheat until May 12, 2026, surpassing year-ago levels for the first time this season after trailing for 40 days
  • 02/Haryana bought 8.46 MT, up 20% YoY and exceeding its 7.2 MT target; Punjab reached 12.16 MT under relaxed quality norms
  • 03/Procurement window runs until June 30; Centre raised targets in MP, UP, Rajasthan, and Bihar to offset weather-related shortfalls

India's wheat procurement has crossed the 30 million tonne mark. The Centre purchased 30.15 MT until May 12, 2026. This is up 3 per cent from 29.17 MT in the year-ago period.

For the first time this season, overall purchases have overtaken last year's volumes. Procurement had trailed year-ago levels for 40 days since the April 1 season start.

Punjab leads with 12.16 MT procured, up 3 per cent from 11.79 MT year-ago. The state's entire purchase was under relaxed quality specifications after unseasonal rains caused widespread lustre loss.

Haryana has exceeded its target, purchasing 8.46 MT against a goal of 7.2 MT. This marks a 20 per cent jump from 7.05 MT procured in the year-ago period.

Madhya Pradesh has narrowed its shortfall from 59 per cent to just 9 per cent. The state has procured 7.04 MT versus 7.77 MT year-ago. The Centre raised its target from 7.8 MT to 10 MT in late April.

Uttar Pradesh has reached 1.17 MT against 0.98 MT year-ago. Rajasthan procured 1.61 MT, up from 1.55 MT. Bihar touched 29,249 tonnes, up 76 per cent from 16,655 tonnes last year.

The Centre has fixed the minimum support price at ₹2,585 per quintal for 2026-27. Wheat production is estimated at 110-120 million tonnes after weather damage in nine states between February and April.

"While the federation estimated wheat production at 110 million tonnes, the agriculture ministry's pre-rainfall figure was 120 million tonnes. The reality will be somewhere between 110 and 120 million tonnes," Food Secretary Sanjeev Chopra said.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH WHEAT HAS THE CENTRE PROCURED SO FAR IN 2026?
AS OF MAY 12, 2026, THE CENTRE HAS PROCURED 30.15 MILLION TONNES OF WHEAT, UP 3 PER CENT FROM 29.17 MILLION TONNES IN THE CORRESPONDING YEAR-AGO PERIOD.
WHAT IS THE GOVERNMENT'S WHEAT PROCUREMENT TARGET FOR THE 2026-27 SEASON?
THE GOVERNMENT HAS RAISED THE PROCUREMENT TARGET TO 34.5 MILLION TONNES, UP FROM AN INITIAL TARGET OF 30.3 MILLION TONNES SET AT THE BEGINNING OF THE SEASON.
WHICH STATES ARE LEADING WHEAT PROCUREMENT THIS SEASON?
PUNJAB LEADS WITH 12.16 MT, FOLLOWED BY HARYANA WITH 8.46 MT. HARYANA HAS ALREADY EXCEEDED ITS TARGET OF 7.2 MT, WHILE PUNJAB IS NEAR ITS 12.2 MT GOAL.
WHAT IS THE MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE FOR WHEAT IN 2026-27?
THE MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE FOR WHEAT HAS BEEN FIXED AT ₹2,585 PER QUINTAL FOR THE 2026-27 RABI MARKETING SEASON, UP FROM ₹2,425 IN THE PREVIOUS YEAR.
WHAT IMPACT DID UNSASONAL RAINS HAVE ON WHEAT PROCUREMENT?
UNSASONAL RAINS AND HAILSTORMS BETWEEN FEBRUARY AND APRIL DAMAGED CROPS IN NINE STATES. PUNJAB'S ENTIRE PROCUREMENT WAS UNDER RELAXED QUALITY NORMS. MADHYA PRADESH'S PROCUREMENT INITIALLY FELL 59 PER CENT BEFORE RECOVERING TO A 9 PER CENT SHORTFALL.
