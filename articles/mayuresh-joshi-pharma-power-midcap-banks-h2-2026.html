Mayuresh Joshi Bets on Pharma, Power & Midcap Banks for H2 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Marketsmith's Mayuresh Joshi favors pharma CDMOs, power utilities and midcap private banks for second half of 2026
  • 02/He is trimming PSU bank positions after hitting targets; sees Karur Vysya Bank as standout pick among midcap lenders
  • 03/Q1 earnings will be messy due to supply chain issues and inflation; investors should look through noise and position for H2

Mayuresh Joshi, Head-Equity at Marketsmith India, sees a cleaner earnings runway for the second half of 2026. He told ET Now that the earnings outlook has improved as West Asian geopolitical tensions ease.

Joshi favors pharma, domestic engineering, and power and utilities for the second half. FMCG and consumer discretionary sectors are expected to consolidate rather than lead.

Within pharma, his clearest conviction is in the CDMO and CSM segment. He flags Sai Life Sciences as a key holding with ongoing capacity additions and structurally strong growth.

Aether Industries also earns a mention for its customised specialty chemicals segment. EBITDA margins are expected to hold firm as new product launches ramp up.

On hospitals, Joshi holds Max Healthcare and Apollo Hospitals. He praises their asset-light expansion into tier II and tier III cities while keeping leverage off balance sheets.

In banking, Joshi has been trimming PSU bank positions after target hits. He is watching large private banks with caution due to deposit accretion and NIM compression concerns.

Karur Vysya Bank stands out as his top pick among midcap private banks. Karnataka Bank and City Union Bank are also on his watchlist for selective entry.

With the RBI unlikely to hike rates meaningfully and global central banks turning cautious, Joshi sees gradual market improvement ahead. The Q1 pain may be the setup investors need.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH SECTORS IS MAYURESH JOSHI BACKING FOR H2 2026?
JOSHI FAVORS PHARMA (ESPECIALLY CDMO AND CSM), DOMESTIC ENGINEERING, AND POWER AND UTILITIES. HE EXPECTS FMCG AND CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY TO CONSOLIDATE RATHER THAN OUTPERFORM.
WHICH PHARMA STOCKS DOES JOSHI LIKE?
HE FLAGS SAI LIFE SCIENCES AS A KEY HOLDING IN THE CDMO SEGMENT, CITING ONGOING CAPACITY ADDITIONS AND STRUCTURALLY STRONG GROWTH. AETHER INDUSTRIES ALSO EARNS A MENTION FOR ITS CUSTOMISED SPECIALTY CHEMICALS BUSINESS.
WHAT IS JOSHI'S VIEW ON BANKS?
HE HAS BEEN TRIMMING PSU BANK POSITIONS AFTER HITTING TARGETS. AMONG MIDCAP PRIVATE BANKS, KARUR VYSYA BANK IS HIS TOP PICK. KARNATAKA BANK AND CITY UNION BANK ARE ON HIS WATCHLIST.
WHY DOES JOSHI THINK Q1 WILL BE MESSY?
HE ATTRIBUTES WEAK Q1 EARNINGS TO SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTIONS, INPUT COST INFLATION, AND SOFTENING DEMAND. CONSUMER BUSINESSES FACE AN ADDITIONAL EL NINO WILDCARD THAT COULD HIT RURAL SPENDING IN Q2.
WHAT IS JOSHI'S BROADER MARKET OUTLOOK?
WITH WEST ASIAN RISKS FADING AND DOMESTIC MACRO HOLDING, HIS BASE CASE IS A GRADUALLY IMPROVING MARKET LED BY QUALITY BUSINESSES IN PHARMA, HEALTHCARE, ENGINEERING, AND SELECT FINANCIAL NAMES.
