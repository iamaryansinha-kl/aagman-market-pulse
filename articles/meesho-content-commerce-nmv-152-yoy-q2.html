Meesho Content Commerce NMV Up 152% YoY, 1.6 Lakh Creators

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Meesho's Content Commerce NMV grew 152% year-on-year in the period August 1, 2025 to August 31, 2026
  • 02/1.6 lakh active creators powered the growth, and 90% of them are nano creators with fewer than 10,000 followers
  • 03/Meesho shares rallied as much as 6% to a day's high of Rs 240 on BSE on Tuesday

Meesho's Content Commerce engine scaled sharply in its latest business update. Net merchandise value (NMV) from the segment grew 152% year-on-year, measured over August 1, 2025 to August 31, 2026.

The growth was powered by 1.6 lakh active creators. At Meesho, content commerce means selling products through content created by people, rather than relying solely on traditional advertising.

That distinction matters for how distribution works. Discovery runs through individuals — homemakers, graduates, and first-time creators — who showcase products inside their own networks.

The structure of the creator base is the real story here. Nano creators, those with fewer than 10,000 followers, accounted for 90% of the active creator base.

That long tail extends beyond reach into who these creators are. Homemakers made up 40% of active creators, and 78% of active homemaker creators were from non-metro India.

Young graduates formed the next-largest group at 30% of active creators, or around 50,000 people. Altogether, about 81% of active creators came from non-metro India.

Geography shows up in orders too, not just creator sign-ups. Tier-3 and Tier-4 markets contributed 66% of Content Commerce orders in the period.

A quarter of active creators were first-time creators. The model is pulling in people who were never professional influencers, expanding the pool well beyond established names.

The mix also widens who can earn on the platform. With 40% homemakers and 30% young graduates, content creation works as a side income rather than a full-time profession.

"We are seeing this translate into meaningful scale, with Content Commerce NMV growing 152% year-on-year, supported by 1.6 lakh active creators over the last 12 months." - Nikita Dawda, General Manager, Content Commerce, Meesho, in a regulatory filing.

For a value-commerce platform, this is a structural shift in how products get sold. Value commerce — retail built on low prices and high volumes — is increasingly being re-sold through individuals rather than ads.

Each nano creator effectively functions as a small distribution point. With 90% of creators in the nano band, growth is not concentrated in a handful of big-name influencer accounts.

That spread lowers dependence on any single creator or region. Two-thirds of orders from Tier-3 and Tier-4 markets show the model reaching buyers that traditional advertising spend often misses.

Markets noticed the update. Meesho shares rallied as much as 6% to a day's high of Rs 240 on BSE on Tuesday.

The stock move is one data point. The more durable question is whether a creator base this long and this new can keep converting into compounding NMV growth.
