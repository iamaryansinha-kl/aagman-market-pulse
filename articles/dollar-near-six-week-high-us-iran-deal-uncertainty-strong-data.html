Dollar Near Six-Week High on US-Iran Deal Uncertainty and Strong US Data

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dollar index at 99.24, near six-week peak of 99.515 on US-Iran deal uncertainty and strong US economic data
  • 02/US manufacturing hit four-year high in May; weekly jobless claims fell; euro dipped to $1.1613
  • 03/Yen weakened past 159 per dollar; Indonesia forced exporters to store 100% revenue in state banks

The dollar held near a six-week high on Friday, trading at 99.24 against a basket of currencies. It approached its peak of 99.515 from the previous session, the highest level since April 7.

The greenback found support from conflicting signals over a U.S.-Iran peace deal. Washington and Tehran remain at odds over uranium stockpiles and control of the Strait of Hormuz.

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio noted "some good signs" in the ongoing talks. Despite mixed messages, investors latched on to hopes of some progress.

Strong U.S. economic data provided additional backing. Weekly jobless claims fell last week, while manufacturing activity rose to a four-year high in May.

The euro dipped 0.03% to $1.1613, while sterling was flat at $1.3431. The pound is set to gain 0.8% for the week after falling over 2% last week.

The yen weakened 0.1% to 159.09 per dollar, struggling past the key 159 threshold. It has given up more than half of its post-intervention gains from weeks ago.

Emerging Asian currencies faced pressure from high oil prices and dollar strength. Indonesia ordered all natural resource exporters to store 100% of export revenues in state-owned banks from June 1.

WHAT IS DRIVING THE DOLLAR'S STRENGTH?
THE DOLLAR IS BEING SUPPORTED BY MIXED SIGNALS OVER A U.S.-IRAN PEACE DEAL AND STRONG U.S. ECONOMIC DATA, INCLUDING FALLING WEEKLY JOBLESS CLAIMS AND MANUFACTURING ACTIVITY THAT ROSE TO A FOUR-YEAR HIGH IN MAY.
WHERE ARE THE EURO AND POUND TRADING?
THE EURO DIPPED 0.03% TO $1.1613, WHILE THE POUND WAS FLAT AT $1.3431. STERLING IS SET TO GAIN 0.8% FOR THE WEEK AFTER FALLING MORE THAN 2% LAST WEEK DUE TO POLITICAL TURMOIL IN BRITAIN.
WHY IS THE YEN WEAKENING?
THE YEN FELL 0.1% TO 159.09 PER DOLLAR, STRUGGLING PAST THE KEY 159 THRESHOLD. IT HAS GIVEN UP MORE THAN HALF OF ITS POST-INTERVENTION GAINS DESPITE LIKELY INTERVENTION FROM TOKYO WEEKS AGO.
WHAT MEASURES IS INDONESIA TAKING TO SUPPORT ITS CURRENCY?
INDONESIA ANNOUNCED THAT ALL NATURAL RESOURCE EXPORTERS MUST STORE 100% OF THEIR EXPORT REVENUES IN STATE-OWNED BANKS FROM JUNE 1. THIS MOVE AIMS TO INCREASE ONSHORE U.S. DOLLAR SUPPLY AND STABILIZE THE PLUMMETING RUPIAH.
