Prestige Estates Q2 Sales Bookings Rise 20% to Rs 7,225 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- Prestige Estates reported 20% year-on-year growth in Q2 sales bookings to Rs 7,225 crore.
  • •- The company sold 3,910 units across 5.84 million square feet during the September quarter.
  • •- First-half pre-sales reached Rs 13,804 crore with 11.88 million sq ft of sales volume and 7,247 units sold.
  • •- Chairman and MD Irfan Razack said new launches introduced late in the quarter saw good sales traction.
  • •- As of June 2026, the group has delivered 319 projects spanning 216 million sq ft, with a pipeline of 137 projects across 229 million sq ft.

Bengaluru-based realty firm Prestige Estates Projects Ltd on Tuesday reported a 20 per cent growth in sales bookings to Rs 7,225 crore for the quarter ended September 2026, lifted by strong housing demand, especially for large branded developers.

In a regulatory filing, the company said its residential business recorded pre-sales of Rs 72,250 million during the second quarter of the current fiscal, up 20 per cent year-on-year, with sales volume of 5.84 million square feet and 3,910 units sold.

The first half of the fiscal was even stronger in absolute terms: Prestige recorded pre-sales of Rs 13,804 crore with sales volume of 11.88 million square feet and 7,247 units sold across the six months — putting the company firmly on pace for another record year.

Irfan Razack, Chairman and Managing Director of Prestige Group, said the residential business continues to see healthy demand, and pointed to the response to new launches as the standout detail. "What is particularly encouraging is the response to our new launches, a couple of which were introduced towards the end of the quarter but nevertheless saw good sales traction," he said. Sustenance sales in ongoing projects also remained strong, reinforcing the company's view that underlying demand for housing continues to be healthy.

The numbers extend a striking day for Indian real estate earnings. Hours earlier, Puravankara had reported a 62 per cent jump in September-quarter bookings to Rs 2,136 crore, with average price realisation up 32 per cent. Two of the country's prominent southern developers posting double-digit to near-triple-digit growth on the same evening underlines how broad the current housing upcycle has become.

Prestige's model gives it particular leverage to this demand. Unlike pure-play residential developers, the group builds across residential, commercial, retail, hospitality and integrated township formats, which lets it capture buyers at multiple price points and monetise land banks through mixed-use development. Its scale is formidable: as of June 2026, the group has delivered 319 projects spanning 216 million square feet, and currently holds a pipeline of 137 projects across 229 million square feet.

The Bengaluru anchor remains the core of the story. The city has been India's strongest housing market for three consecutive years, driven by technology-sector employment, returning office culture and a structural shortage of quality supply in prime micro-markets. Prestige's brand in its home market is arguably the strongest of any developer, and that shows up in the sustenance sales Razack highlighted — existing projects continuing to sell even without the novelty of a fresh launch.

For investors, the quarterly update offers two readings. On the positive side, a 20 per cent bookings growth at Prestige's scale — Rs 7,225 crore in a single quarter — demonstrates that demand has not yet been priced out of the market, and late-quarter launch traction suggests the momentum carried into October. On the cautious side, the comparison with Puravankara's 62 per cent shows growth rates are dispersing across developers, with execution and city mix increasingly determining who outperforms.

With the festive season now underway, the December quarter will be the real test. Developers typically time their biggest launches for the October-November window, and Prestige's 137-project pipeline gives it ample ammunition. If the launch response Razack described repeats through the festive weeks, the company's full-year pre-sales could set a new benchmark for the Indian residential sector.

The quarter also carries a signal about launch cadence. Two of the launches Razack referenced arrived late in the September quarter, meaning their full sales contribution lands in the December quarter — a built-in tailwind for the next update. In a sector where quarterly numbers are often launch-timed rather than demand-timed, that scheduling detail matters as much as the headline growth rate.

Prestige's diversified portfolio sets it apart from the pure-play residential names it is often compared with. The group's commercial and retail formats generate annuity-style income that smooths the cyclicality of home sales, and its integrated townships — large mixed-use developments that combine housing, offices and amenities — let it monetise the same land bank multiple ways. That structure has made Prestige a favourite among institutional investors who want real estate exposure without full dependence on the residential cycle.

Tuesday's twin results — Puravankara's 62 per cent and Prestige's 20 per cent — also map the sector's dispersion. Smaller, price-aggressive developers are growing faster off lower bases, while large branded players are compounding at steady double digits on enormous scales. Both patterns point the same direction: India's housing demand cycle, now in its fourth strong year, still has legs, and the organised sector is consolidating share from unorganised builders as buyers gravitate toward trusted brands.

For the stock, the quarter's message is continuity rather than surprise. Twenty per cent bookings growth at this scale, with late-quarter launches promising more, is roughly what the market has come to expect from Prestige — and in a year when many sectors have disappointed, dependable execution has its own premium.

GLOSSARY: - Pre-sales (sales bookings): The value of homes sold during a period, usually before construction completion. - Sustenance sales: Ongoing sales in previously launched projects, as distinct from sales driven by new launches. - Integrated township: A large mixed-use development combining housing, offices, retail and amenities in one master-planned project. - H1: The first six months of the fiscal year, April through September for Indian companies. - Sales volume: The total floor area sold in a period, measured in square feet or million square feet.
