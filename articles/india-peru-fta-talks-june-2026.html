India-Peru Free Trade Pact Talks to Resume in June 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India and Peru will resume free trade pact negotiations in June 2026, with a potential deal signed by year-end, says Peru's ambassador Javier Paulinich
  • 02/Peru, the world's third-largest copper producer, produced 2.7 million tonnes in 2024 and attracted $4.96 billion in foreign investment in the sector
  • 03/India may source 91% to 97% of copper concentrate from overseas by 2047, with demand expected to climb to 3.3 million tonnes by 2030

India and Peru will hold the next round of free trade pact talks in June 2026. A deal could be signed by the end of the year, Peru's ambassador to India Javier Paulinich told Reuters on May 11.

"In principle, in June we are going to resume the negotiations," Paulinich said. India's Ministry of Commerce and Industry did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Peru is the world's third-largest producer of copper. The country produced about 2.7 million tonnes in 2024 and attracted $4.96 billion in foreign investment in the sector.

Peru is also negotiating a chapter on critical minerals with India. Hindalco Industries is looking to buy copper from Peru, Paulinich said. The company did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

India is the world's second-biggest importer of refined copper. The country's copper imports rose 4 per cent to 1.2 million tonnes in the fiscal year ending March 2025.

India's copper demand is expected to climb to 3 to 3.3 million tonnes by 2030. By 2047, demand could reach 8.9 to 9.8 million tonnes, the government has said.

India may have to source 91 per cent to 97 per cent of its copper concentrate requirements from overseas by 2047. The government has urged domestic mining companies to invest abroad to secure supply chains.
