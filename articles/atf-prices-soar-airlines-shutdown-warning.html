ATF Prices Soar to $260/Barrel: Airlines Warn of Shutdown Risk

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FIA warns India's airlines are "on the verge of stopping operations" as ATF prices hit $260/barrel
  • 02/Fuel costs have ballooned from 30-40% to 55-60% of operating costs amid West Asia conflict
  • 03/FIA demands crack band pricing, suspension of 11% excise duty, and VAT reduction on ATF

The Federation of Indian Airlines has sent an urgent letter to the Union Government dated April 26, 2026. The industry body warned that airlines are "on the verge of closing down or stopping operations" due to a record surge in Aviation Turbine Fuel prices.

FIA members include IndiGo, Air India, and SpiceJet. The letter was addressed to the Ministry of Civil Aviation and reviewed by businessline.

Brent crude prices have surged from $72 per barrel to $118 amid the ongoing West Asia conflict. ATF prices have risen even more steeply from $87.24 per barrel to a peak of $260.24.

The government capped domestic ATF price increases at ₹15 per litre. International ATF prices rose by ₹73, creating a severe imbalance between domestic and international operations.

Crack differentials between Brent crude and ATF have widened from $11-18 per barrel to over $132 per barrel. Fuel costs have jumped from the traditional 30-40% of airline expenditure to 55-60% of operating costs.

The FIA has urged the government to reinstate the 2022 crack band pricing mechanism. It also asked for a temporary suspension of the 11% excise duty on ATF and a reduction in VAT on aviation fuel.

"Today, airlines are in a very difficult, precarious and challenging situation," the FIA said. The association warned that failure to act could force capacity cuts and network reductions.

Other pressures include rupee depreciation, airspace closures, and higher supplier costs. The FIA cautioned that irrational ATF pricing could lead to aircraft being grounded and increased flight cancellations.

WHY HAVE INDIAN AIRLINES ISSUED A "MAYDAY" WARNING TO THE GOVERNMENT?
THE FEDERATION OF INDIAN AIRLINES HAS WARNED THE UNION GOVERNMENT THAT AIRLINES ARE "ON THE VERGE OF CLOSING DOWN OR STOPPING OPERATIONS" DUE TO AN UNPRECEDENTED SURGE IN AVIATION TURBINE FUEL PRICES.
HOW MUCH HAVE ATF PRICES INCREASED?
ATF PRICES HAVE SURGED FROM $87.24 PER BARREL TO A PEAK OF $260.24 PER BARREL AND ARE CURRENTLY TRADING ABOVE $235 PER BARREL. BRENT CRUDE HAS RISEN FROM $72 TO $118 PER BARREL.
WHAT PERCENTAGE OF OPERATING COSTS DO FUEL COSTS NOW REPRESENT?
FUEL COSTS HAVE BALLOONED FROM THE TRADITIONAL 30-40% OF AIRLINE EXPENDITURE TO NEARLY 55-60% OF OPERATING COSTS.
WHAT SPECIFIC MEASURES HAS THE FIA DEMANDED FROM THE GOVERNMENT?
THE FIA HAS URGED THE GOVERNMENT TO REINSTATE THE CRACK BAND PRICING MECHANISM FROM 2022, TEMPORARILY SUSPEND THE 11% EXCISE DUTY ON ATF, AND REDUCE VAT ON AVIATION FUEL IN MAJOR STATES.
WHAT ARE THE POTENTIAL CONSEQUENCES IF THE GOVERNMENT DOES NOT ACT?
THE FIA WARNED THAT FAILURE TO ADDRESS FUEL PRICING COULD FORCE AIRLINES TO RATIONALISE CAPACITY, REDUCE NETWORK DEPLOYMENT, CURTAIL CONNECTIVITY, GROUND AIRCRAFT, AND INCREASE FLIGHT CANCELLATIONS.
