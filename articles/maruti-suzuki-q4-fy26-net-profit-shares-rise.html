Maruti Suzuki Q4 FY26: Net Profit Drops 7% to ₹3,590 Cr, Shares Jump 4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki India reports Q4 FY26 standalone net profit of ₹3,590.5 crore, down 7% YoY from ₹3,857.3 crore
  • 02/Shares surge over 4% to ₹13,443 despite earnings miss; Morgan Stanley, JM Financial and Motilal Oswal maintain buy ratings
  • 03/Management guides for ~10% domestic volume growth in FY27; brokerages see margins bottoming out in Q1 before recovery

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd shares jumped over 4 per cent in early trade on Wednesday. The stock touched ₹13,443 on the NSE at 9:32 am after the company announced its March quarter results.

The company reported standalone net profit of ₹3,590.5 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 7 per cent decline from ₹3,857.3 crore in the year-ago period.

EBITDA margin came in at 11.7 per cent while EBIT margin stood at 8.4 per cent. Lower employee costs and reduced discounts provided partial support.

Dealer inventory stands at just 12 days while the order backlog remains robust at around 1,90,000 vehicles. Strong domestic demand, especially in entry-level cars, underpinned sentiment.

Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating with a target price of ₹17,895. The brokerage noted exports were a key driver of FY26 outperformance.

JM Financial maintained a buy rating with a target of ₹16,570, while Motilal Oswal reiterated buy at ₹15,529. Both expect 10 per cent domestic volume growth in FY27.

"Management guidance of around 10 per cent domestic volume growth in FY27 is about 200 basis points ahead of estimates," Morgan Stanley said.

Near-term margin pressures persist due to higher commodity costs and new model launch expenses. Brokerages expect margins to bottom out in Q1 before improving.
