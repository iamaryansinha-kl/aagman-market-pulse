US GDP Q1 2026 Revised Up to 2.1% as Import Drop Boosts Growth Data

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/US GDP growth for Q1 2026 revised upward to 2.1% annual rate from the previous 1.6% estimate
  • 02/Downward revision to imports was the key driver, partly offset by weaker consumer spending data
  • 03/Information services, including the AI sector, was among the biggest contributors to the expansion

The US economy grew at a 2.1% annual rate in the first quarter of 2026. The Commerce Department released its third estimate on Thursday, June 26.

The figure was revised up from the previous estimate of 1.6%. It now aligns more closely with the government's initial estimate of 2.0%.

The upward revision was driven by a decrease in imports. Imports are subtracted in GDP calculations, so lower imports boosted the overall figure.

Consumer spending was revised downward, partly offsetting the import gain. The Commerce Department confirmed this in its official release.

Key contributors to the expansion included investment, exports, government spending, and consumer spending. All four major components added to real GDP growth.

Information services emerged as a major growth driver. This sector includes segments of the booming Artificial Intelligence industry that has powered recent US growth.

The revision signals underlying resilience in the world's largest economy. Markets will watch the second-quarter data for confirmation of sustained momentum.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE REVISED US GDP GROWTH RATE FOR Q1 2026?
THE US ECONOMY GREW AT AN ANNUAL RATE OF 2.1% IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2026, ACCORDING TO THE COMMERCE DEPARTMENT'S THIRD ESTIMATE RELEASED ON JUNE 26, 2026.
WHAT WAS THE PREVIOUS GDP ESTIMATE BEFORE THIS REVISION?
THE PREVIOUS ESTIMATE PUT Q1 2026 GDP GROWTH AT 1.6%. THE REVISED 2.1% FIGURE IS MORE IN LINE WITH THE GOVERNMENT'S FIRST ESTIMATE OF 2.0%.
WHAT CAUSED THE UPWARD REVISION TO US GDP?
THE REVISION WAS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY A DOWNWARD REVISION TO IMPORTS, WHICH ARE SUBTRACTED IN THE CALCULATION OF GDP. THIS WAS PARTLY OFFSET BY A DOWNWARD REVISION TO CONSUMER SPENDING.
WHICH SECTORS CONTRIBUTED MOST TO THE Q1 2026 GROWTH?
THE MAIN CONTRIBUTORS WERE INVESTMENT, EXPORTS, GOVERNMENT SPENDING, AND CONSUMER SPENDING. INFORMATION SERVICES, INCLUDING THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE INDUSTRY, WAS AMONG THE BIGGEST CONTRIBUTORS.
WHAT DOES THIS REVISION MEAN FOR MARKETS AND INVESTORS?
THE REVISION SIGNALS RESILIENCE IN THE US ECONOMY AND MAY INFLUENCE FEDERAL RESERVE POLICY EXPECTATIONS. INVESTORS WILL WATCH THE SECOND-QUARTER DATA TO CONFIRM WHETHER THE MOMENTUM IS SUSTAINED.
