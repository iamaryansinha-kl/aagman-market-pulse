Arvind Panagariya to RBI: Let Rupee Depreciate or Reserves Will Bleed Out

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/16th Finance Commission Chairman Arvind Panagariya urges RBI to let the rupee depreciate rather than defend it with foreign exchange reserves
  • 02/The rupee touched the 100-per-dollar mark in the one-year forward market, but Panagariya says "100 is just a number, like 99 and 101"
  • 03/He warns that defending the rupee is a "losing proposition" that will bleed reserves until they are exhausted

Arvind Panagariya has urged the Reserve Bank of India to allow the rupee to weaken. The Chairman of the 16th Finance Commission made the appeal in a post on X.

The rupee touched the key 100-per-dollar mark in the one-year forward market on Wednesday. Currency markets are pricing in a weakening bias for the USD/INR pair over the next 12 months.

"Do not let the psychology of Rs 100 per dollar determine your policy response," Panagariya wrote. "100 is just a number, like 99 and 101."

He warned that defending the rupee would be a losing strategy. "Trying to defend the rupee will continue to bleed the reserves until they are exhausted," he said.

Panagariya also cautioned against dollar-denominated sovereign bonds and high-interest NRI deposits. He called these temporary and expensive measures to support the currency.

Drawing comparisons with the 2013 currency crisis, he said India's macroeconomic situation is much stronger today. Inflation was in double digits then, but remains relatively controlled now.

The rupee rebounded 50 paise from its all-time closing low to settle at 96.36 against the dollar on Thursday. The recovery came as crude oil prices retreated from elevated levels.

WHAT DID ARVIND PANAGARIYA ADVISE THE RBI REGARDING THE RUPEE?
ARVIND PANAGARIYA, CHAIRMAN OF THE 16TH FINANCE COMMISSION, URGED THE RBI TO ALLOW THE RUPEE TO DEPRECIATE RATHER THAN AGGRESSIVELY DEFEND IT WITH FOREIGN EXCHANGE RESERVES.
WHY DOES PANAGARIYA SAY THE RBI SHOULD NOT FOCUS ON THE RS 100 PER DOLLAR LEVEL?
PANAGARIYA SAID "100 IS JUST A NUMBER, LIKE 99 AND 101." HE BELIEVES THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE RS 100-PER-DOLLAR MARK SHOULD NOT DICTATE MONETARY POLICY DECISIONS.
WHAT DID PANAGARIYA WARN ABOUT DEFENDING THE RUPEE?
HE WARNED THAT "TRYING TO DEFEND THE RUPEE WILL CONTINUE TO BLEED THE RESERVES UNTIL THEY ARE EXHAUSTED." HE CALLED ANY STRATEGY OTHER THAN DEPRECIATION A "LOSING PROPOSITION."
WHAT MEASURES DID PANAGARIYA CAUTION AGAINST USING TO SUPPORT THE RUPEE?
HE CAUTIONED AGAINST RELYING ON DOLLAR-DENOMINATED SOVEREIGN BONDS AND HIGH-INTEREST NRI DOLLAR DEPOSITS. HE CALLED THESE TEMPORARY AND EXPENSIVE MEASURES.
HOW DOES INDIA'S CURRENT MACROECONOMIC SITUATION COMPARE TO THE 2013 CURRENCY CRISIS?
PANAGARIYA SAID INDIA'S MACROECONOMIC SITUATION IS MUCH STRONGER TODAY. INFLATION WAS IN DOUBLE DIGITS IN 2013, WHEREAS CURRENT INFLATION LEVELS REMAIN CONTROLLED DUE TO PRUDENT MONETARY MANAGEMENT BY THE RBI.
