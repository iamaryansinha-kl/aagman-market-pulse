SBI Funds IPO: India's Largest MF House Eyes ₹13,000 Cr July Launch

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI Funds Management, India's largest MF house with over ₹12.5 lakh crore AUM, plans a ₹13,000 crore IPO launch as early as July 2026
  • 02/The SBI-Amundi joint venture filed its DRHP with SEBI in mid-March and has concluded roadshows with the top 20 asset managers
  • 03/Unlisted shares trade at ₹760-770 apiece, valuing the firm at ~₹1.55 lakh crore, just below ICICI Prudential AMC's ₹1.58 lakh crore market cap

SBI Funds Management has kicked off roadshows for its initial public offering. The country's largest mutual fund house aims to launch the ₹13,000 crore issue as early as July 2026.

Senior executives including deputy managing director D.P. Singh and chief investment officer R. Srinivasan have met investment teams of the top 20 asset managers. The meetings seek commitments for the IPO and the anchor book.

The asset management joint venture is between State Bank of India and Amundi. SBI currently holds 61.9% and Amundi holds 36.4% in SBI Funds Management.

SBI Funds filed its draft red herring prospectus with SEBI in mid-March 2026. The fund house manages assets under management of over ₹12.5 lakh crore.

"The aim is to launch the IPO in the first month of the second quarter of FY27 once regulatory approvals come through," a person aware of the discussions said.

SBI Funds shares traded at around ₹760-770 apiece in the unlisted market last week. This values the fund house at approximately ₹1.55 lakh crore.

ICICI Prudential Asset Management, the second-largest mutual fund house, has a market cap of ₹1.58 lakh crore. HDFC Asset Management Company, the third largest, is valued at about ₹1.16 lakh crore.

Interactions with most large institutional investors have now been concluded. The IPO timing depends on receiving final regulatory approvals from SEBI.
