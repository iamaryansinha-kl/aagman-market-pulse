TABP Snacks and Beverages Targets ₹350 Crore in FY27, Mulls IPO by FY29

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/TABP Snacks and Beverages expects FY27 revenue of ₹300-₹350 crore, up 44-68% from FY26's ₹208 crore topline
  • 02/The Coimbatore-based company aims to hit ₹800 crore in three years before initiating plans for a public offering
  • 03/TABP will invest ₹70-₹80 crore to add 4-6 owned manufacturing plants and expand into Gujarat, MP, and West Bengal

TABP Snacks and Beverages expects revenues of ₹300-₹350 crore in FY27. The Coimbatore-based beverage maker closed FY26 with a topline of ₹208 crore.

The company targets ₹800 crore in topline over the next three years. It plans to initiate an IPO only after crossing that threshold.

TABP sells beverages under its Plunge brand at the ₹10 price point. Its portfolio includes lemon salt, jeera, pineapple, paneer, and grape soda flavours.

"We target bottom of the pyramid consumers who often depend on roadside Sarbatwala, Nimbu Paniwala shops where the quality of the product is always a big question mark," said Prabhu Gandhikumar, co-founder and CEO of TABP.

Tamil Nadu contributes about 42 per cent of the company's sales. TABP also operates in Maharashtra and Odisha.

The near-term focus is on expanding into Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and West Bengal. Gandhikumar said the firm is balancing 70 per cent of effort on core markets and 30 per cent on new geographies.

TABP currently operates 16 manufacturing units with a combined capacity of about 3,000 bottles per minute. Only one unit is fully owned.

The firm plans to move to about 25 per cent in-house manufacturing by adding 4 to 6 owned plants in the next three years. The expansion will require an investment of ₹70-₹80 crore.

TABP is a 100 per cent offline channel with no online or quick commerce presence. Gandhikumar said the economics of selling a ₹10 product online do not work due to logistics costs.

Beyond beverages, TABP sells packaged water and low-cost snacks at ₹5. These contribute to less than 10 per cent of the company's sales.
