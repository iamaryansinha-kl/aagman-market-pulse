Andhra Panchayats Collect 79% of ₹1,285 Cr OSR Demand in 2025-26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Over 13,000 Gram Panchayats in Andhra Pradesh collected 78.91% of total OSR demand in 2025-26
  • 02/Total demand stood at ₹1,285 crore; actual collection crossed ₹1,000 crore for the first time
  • 03/Digital tools, IIM Ahmedabad training module, and Samarth Portal drove higher compliance

Gram Panchayats across Andhra Pradesh collected over ₹1,000 crore in Own Source Revenue during 2025-26. The collection represents 78.91% of total demand across 26 districts.

Total tax and non-tax demand stood at approximately ₹1,285 crore. Over 13,000 panchayats participated in the statewide collection drive.

"This will boost financial self reliance and lower dependency on grants," said Vivek Bharadwaj, Secretary in Panchayti Raj Ministry.

OSR includes property tax, library cess, sports cess, fire cess, water tax, drainage tax and lightening tax. Non-tax revenue covers private water tap fees, cell tower renewal charges, auction amounts and trade licences.

The Ministry collaborated with IIM Ahmedabad on a training module for OSR. It also launched the Atma Nirbhar Panchayat Special Award (ANPSA) to incentivize higher collections.

The Samarth Portal enables online tax collection through payment gateways. It supports demand notices, online payments, and monitoring by central and state governments.

The 16th Finance Commission recommended ₹4.35 lakh crore for Rural Local Bodies for 2026-31. This includes ₹3.48 lakh crore as basic grant and ₹87,000 crore as performance-linked component.

The Samarth Portal soft-launched in Chhattisgarh and Himachal Pradesh. Wider state adoption is expected to drive further improvement in OSR collection ratios.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH OWN SOURCE REVENUE DID ANDHRA PRADESH GRAM PANCHAYATS COLLECT IN 2025-26?
OVER 13,000 GRAM PANCHAYATS IN ANDHRA PRADESH COLLECTED OVER ₹1,000 CRORE IN OWN SOURCE REVENUE. THIS REPRESENTS 78.91% OF THE TOTAL DEMAND OF APPROXIMATELY ₹1,285 CRORE ACROSS 26 DISTRICTS.
WHAT TAXES AND NON-TAXES ARE INCLUDED IN OWN SOURCE REVENUE?
OSR INCLUDES PROPERTY TAX, LIBRARY CESS, SPORTS CESS, FIRE CESS, WATER TAX, DRAINAGE TAX AND LIGHTENING TAX. NON-TAX REVENUE COVERS PRIVATE WATER TAP FEES, CELL TOWER RENEWAL CHARGES, AUCTION AMOUNTS AND TRADE LICENCES.
WHAT DIGITAL TOOLS WERE USED TO IMPROVE COLLECTION?
THE PANCHAYATI RAJ MINISTRY LAUNCHED THE SAMARTH PORTAL FOR ONLINE TAX COLLECTION VIA PAYMENT GATEWAYS. IT SUPPORTS DEMAND NOTICES, ONLINE PAYMENTS, AND REAL-TIME MONITORING BY CENTRAL AND STATE GOVERNMENTS.
WHAT INCENTIVES AND TRAINING WERE PROVIDED TO GRAM PANCHAYATS?
THE MINISTRY COLLABORATED WITH IIM AHMEDABAD TO DEVELOP AN OSR TRAINING MODULE. IT ALSO INTRODUCED THE ATMA NIRBHAR PANCHAYAT SPECIAL AWARD (ANPSA) TO PROMOTE HIGHER OWN SOURCE REVENUE COLLECTION.
WHAT DID THE 16TH FINANCE COMMISSION RECOMMEND FOR RURAL LOCAL BODIES?
THE 16TH FINANCE COMMISSION RECOMMENDED TOTAL GRANTS OF OVER ₹4.35 LAKH CRORE FOR RURAL LOCAL BODIES FOR 2026-31. THIS INCLUDES ₹3.48 LAKH CRORE AS BASIC GRANT AND OVER ₹87,000 CRORE AS PERFORMANCE-LINKED COMPONENT.
