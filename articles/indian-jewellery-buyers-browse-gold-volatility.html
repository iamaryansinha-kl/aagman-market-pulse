Indian Jewellery Buyers Browse More, Buy Later as Gold Volatility Bites

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian jewellery buyers are browsing 10 to 30 times more than they purchase as gold price volatility stretches the buying cycle
  • 02/Consumers are increasingly research-driven, comparing designs across platforms and tracking prices before making purchases
  • 03/Shift visible toward lighter, lower-ticket jewellery like 9K and 14K daily wear pieces as budgets tighten

India's jewellery buyers are behaving less like impulse shoppers and more like market watchers. The shift is driven by sharp gold price volatility and elevated prices.

Consumers are browsing 10 to 30 times more than they buy. They track prices, compare designs, and shortlist options across platforms before committing.

"You cannot walk into a jewellery store 10 times a month and not buy anything," said Gaurav Singh Kushwaha, Founder and CEO of Bluestone. "Online works beautifully for browsing."

Suvankar Sen, MD and CEO of Senco Gold and Diamonds, noted the change. "Customers are comparing designs, tracking prices, and planning purchases more consciously."

Intent remains strong but the journey has become research-oriented. Many buyers delay purchases or wait for price corrections before committing.

This has stretched the purchase cycle. While footfalls and digital traffic remain healthy, conversion rates have slowed across the industry.

Consumers are blending channels. They discover and research online, but complete purchases in-store where trust and tactile experience still matter.

Tanishq CEO Arun Narayan said online platforms are becoming critical for immersion. "Traffic to our website is growing strongly. It reflects high consumer interest."

A visible shift is emerging toward lighter jewellery. 9K and 14K daily wear pieces, coins, and smaller investments are gaining preference.

Even weddings and festive occasions are seeing tactical behaviour. Buyers delay decisions until closer to the event, timing purchases more carefully.

WHILE FOOTFALLS AND DIGITAL TRAFFIC REMAIN HEALTHY, CONVERSION RATES HAVE SLOWED. THE PURCHASE CYCLE HAS STRETCHED AS CONSUMERS BECOME MORE RESEARCH-ORIENTED.

LIGHTER, LOWER-TICKET JEWELLERY LIKE 9K AND 14K DAILY WEAR PIECES, COINS, AND SMALLER INVESTMENTS ARE GAINING PREFERENCE AS BUDGETS TIGHTEN.
