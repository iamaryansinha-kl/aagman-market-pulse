Fusion CX IPO: Price Band Fixed at Rs 275-289 for Rs 702 Crore Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Fusion CX has fixed a price band of Rs 275-289 per share for its Rs 702 crore IPO, which opens on October 14, 2026
  • 02/The issue is a mix of a Rs 500 crore fresh issue and a Rs 202 crore offer for sale, with listing scheduled on October 22
  • 03/The stock is priced at 21.55x diluted FY26 earnings, but 85.26% of revenue comes from the US and Canada and the top five clients pay 39.55%

Fusion CX Ltd has fixed its IPO price band at Rs 275-289 per share. The Rs 702 crore issue opens on October 14, 2026, and closes on October 16, 2026.

The floor price of Rs 275 and the cap of Rs 289 represent 275 times and 289 times the face value. Retail investors must bid for a minimum lot of 51 shares, which works out to Rs 14,739 at the upper band. The pricing values the company at 20.51x diluted FY26 EPS at the lower band and 21.55x at the upper band.

The issue is a book-built offering combining a fresh issue of 1.73 crore shares worth Rs 500 crore with an offer for sale of 69.90 lakh shares worth Rs 202 crore. Of the fresh proceeds, Rs 29.19 crore will repay or prepay borrowings of the company and its subsidiaries, including select step-down subsidiaries. Another Rs 7.47 crore flows into step-down units Omind Technologies Inc and Omind Technologies Pvt Ltd to upgrade IT tools, including the Arya and MindVoice AI platforms.

Allotment is expected to be finalised on October 19, 2026. The listing is scheduled on both BSE and NSE on October 22, 2026. Nuvama Wealth Management is the book-running lead manager, and KFin Technologies is the registrar.

The growth numbers behind the offer are sharp. Total income rose 37% to Rs 1,852 crore in FY26 from Rs 1,352 crore in FY25, while profit after tax more than doubled, climbing 128% to Rs 170 crore from Rs 74 crore. The three-year weighted average return on net worth stands at 24.59%, which supports the premium pricing relative to slower-growing peers.

Fusion CX is not a traditional call-centre play. Incorporated in 2004, it runs a technology-driven customer experience business spanning voice, chat, email, social media, and messaging, with 13,357 employees in CX operations as of March 31, 2026. Its technology subsidiary, Omind Technologies, builds AI-powered tools for customer engagement, marketing, quality automation, and workforce management.

Concentration is the risk investors must price in. Its client list spans telecom, BFSI, healthcare, retail, and technology, yet the US and Canada contribute 85.26% of revenue, with India at just 11.29%. The top five clients account for 39.55% of revenue, and the top ten contribute 49.66%.

For retail investors, the math is a trade-off: a mid-cap CX player with 128% profit growth and a 21.55x multiple, set against heavy geographic dependence on North America and a client base where two-fifths of revenue sits with five names. The Rs 275-289 band leaves little room for error if either concentration wobbles.

GLOSSARY: Book-built issue: An IPO where investors bid within a price band and the final price is discovered from demand. Fusion CX's issue will build a book between Rs 275 and Rs 289 per share. Offer for sale (OFS): The part of an IPO where existing shareholders sell their stakes rather than the company issuing new shares. Fusion CX's OFS is Rs 202 crore of the Rs 702 crore issue.
