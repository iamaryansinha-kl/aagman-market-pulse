Disaster Resilience Must Be Built Into All Infrastructure Projects

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/A senior government official says disaster resilience must be integrated into all new infrastructure projects
  • 02/India's NDMA is adopting drone technology and AI-driven models for faster disaster response
  • 03/The CDRI coalition, launched by India in 2019, now leads global climate-resilient infrastructure efforts

A senior government official has said disaster resilience must be integrated into every infrastructure project across India. The statement was made on April 22, 2026 amid rising climate threats.

India ranks 9th in the long-term Global Climate Risk Index. Extreme events in 2024-25 alone caused over 3,000 deaths and widespread agricultural damage.

The National Disaster Management Authority has adopted drone swarm technology for cyclone damage assessment. It covers 500 square kilometers within two hours of deployment.

Prime Minister Modi's 10-Point Agenda for Disaster Risk Reduction guides national policy. The agenda emphasizes investments at the local level and leveraging technology for early warning systems.

The India Cooling Action Plan has already been implemented across over 250 cities. Rising temperatures are linked to both communicable and non-communicable diseases, making climate response a public health priority.

India launched the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure in 2019 at the UN Climate Summit. CDRI now partners with over 30 nations to promote resilient infrastructure globally.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY DOES THE GOVERNMENT WANT DISASTER RESILIENCE IN INFRASTRUCTURE?
WITH INDIA RANKING 9TH IN THE GLOBAL CLIMATE RISK INDEX AND OVER 3,000 DEATHS FROM EXTREME EVENTS IN 2024-25, BUILDING RESILIENCE INTO INFRASTRUCTURE IS CRITICAL TO REDUCING FUTURE LOSSES.
WHAT TECHNOLOGY IS NDMA USING FOR DISASTER RESPONSE?
NDMA HAS ADOPTED DRONE SWARM TECHNOLOGY FOR CYCLONE DAMAGE ASSESSMENT, COVERING 500 SQUARE KILOMETERS WITHIN TWO HOURS OF DEPLOYMENT.
WHAT IS THE COALITION FOR DISASTER RESILIENT INFRASTRUCTURE?
CDRI IS A GLOBAL INITIATIVE LAUNCHED BY INDIA IN 2019 AT THE UN CLIMATE SUMMIT TO PROMOTE CLIMATE-RESILIENT INFRASTRUCTURE ACROSS MEMBER NATIONS.
HOW DOES CLIMATE CHANGE AFFECT PUBLIC HEALTH IN INDIA?
RISING TEMPERATURES AGGRAVATE BOTH TROPICAL DISEASES AND LIFESTYLE-RELATED NON-COMMUNICABLE DISEASES, MAKING CLIMATE RESPONSE A PUBLIC HEALTH PRIORITY.
WHAT IS THE INDIA COOLING ACTION PLAN?
THE INDIA COOLING ACTION PLAN IS A NATIONAL INITIATIVE ALREADY IMPLEMENTED IN OVER 250 CITIES TO ADDRESS RISING TEMPERATURES AND REDUCE ENERGY BURDEN FROM COOLING DEMAND.
