Indian Railways Freight Up 4% in June, Passenger Traffic Rises 2.3%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian Railways freight loading grew 4% to 142.21 million tonnes in June, with earnings adding ₹430 crore
  • 02/Passenger traffic reached 638.1 million, up 2.3% YoY; non-suburban segment led growth at 3.9%
  • 03/Q1 FY27 freight loading hit 419.08 million tonnes, building on sustained operational momentum

Indian Railways carried 142.21 million tonnes of freight in June 2026. This marks a 4% increase over 136.71 million tonnes in June 2025.

Passenger traffic rose to 638.1 million in June, up from 623.7 million a year earlier. The non-suburban segment grew 3.9%, while suburban traffic rose 0.9%.

Freight earnings generated additional revenue of ₹430 crore during June. This represents a 3% increase over the same month last year.

Fertilizer loading surged 19.1%, while balance other goods grew 17.3%. Iron ore and domestic coal rose 9.4% and 4.9% respectively.

The national transporter supplied 7% more domestic coal to thermal power stations in June. This supported uninterrupted power generation during the extended summer.

Q1 FY27 freight loading reached 419.08 million tonnes, up from 413.05 million tonnes in Q1 FY26. Iron ore and clinker loading rose 7.44% and 6.54% respectively.

Vande Bharat services expanded to 164 trains, including the new Sleeper service. Amrit Bharat services reached 72 with four new launches in June.
