Elevate Campuses Raises Rs 945 Crore From Anchor Investors Including SBI MF, Norges Bank

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Hillhouse-backed Elevate Campuses has raised Rs 945 crore from anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering. The anchor book drew participation from approximately 20 domestic and international investors.

Anchor Investors

The anchor investor lineup includes State Bank of India Mutual Fund, Mirae Asset Management, Bandhan Mutual Fund, 360 One Asset Management and White Oak Capital Mutual Fund.

Norway's Norges Bank Government Pension Fund Global, one of the world's largest sovereign wealth funds, also participated in the anchor book. The company confirmed that the anchor investor portion was fully subscribed.

Background

Elevate Campuses is a Mumbai-based company in the education sector. The company is backed by Hillhouse, a global investment firm.

The strong anchor response signals growing institutional enthusiasm for the education sector among both domestic and international investors. The participation of Norges Bank is particularly notable as sovereign wealth funds typically conduct extensive due diligence before committing to anchor books.

The IPO forms part of the broader surge in primary market activity in September 2026, which has seen 30 mainboard issues raise approximately Rs 38,785 crore, the biggest monthly fundraise in nearly a year.
