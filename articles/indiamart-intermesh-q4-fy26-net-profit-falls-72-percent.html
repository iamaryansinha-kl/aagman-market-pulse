Indiamart Intermesh Q4 FY26 Net Profit Falls 72% to ₹50.2 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indiamart Intermesh Q4 FY26 net profit falls 72.2% to ₹50.2 crore from ₹180.6 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue from operations grows 13.85% to ₹404.3 crore but total income declines 20.15% to ₹370.4 crore
  • 03/FY26 full-year profit down 13.8% to ₹474.7 crore; CEO Dinesh Agarwal cites AI-driven platform investments

Indiamart Intermesh Ltd (NSE: INDIAMART) reported a 72.2% crash in consolidated net profit to ₹50.2 crore for Q4 FY26. The B2B e-commerce firm had posted ₹180.6 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations rose 13.85% to ₹404.3 crore in the March quarter. It stood at ₹355.1 crore in Q4 FY25.

However, total income including other income fell 20.15% to ₹370.4 crore. The decline signals pressure on non-operational revenue streams.

Total expenses climbed 19% to ₹279.3 crore during the quarter. Web and related services contributed ₹368.3 crore while accounting software added ₹36.1 crore.

For the full FY26, net profit declined 13.8% to ₹474.7 crore. Total consolidated income rose 6.8% to ₹1,773.1 crore.

The Q4 profit of ₹50.2 crore fell well below analyst estimates of ₹148-170 crore. The miss reflects margin compression and rising cost pressures.

CEO Dinesh Agarwal said, "We remain focused on driving sustained growth by continuously enhancing platform quality, deepening buyer-seller engagement, and building a more trusted marketplace experience. Our rapid adoption of AI is making the experience more seamless and efficient."

The results were announced on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The stock has declined 16% over the past year amid sector headwinds and FII selling pressure.
