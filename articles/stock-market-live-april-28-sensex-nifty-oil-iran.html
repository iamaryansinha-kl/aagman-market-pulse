Stock Market Live April 28: Sensex Drops 169 Pts, Nifty Down 48 As Oil Spikes

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian markets opened lower on April 28 with Sensex down 169 pts to 77,208.44 and Nifty 48 pts lower near 24,044 as Brent crude spiked to $109
  • 02/FPIs offloaded ₹1,151 crore for a sixth straight session while DIIs countered with ₹4,124 crore in buying for the third consecutive day
  • 03/Nifty faces resistance at 24,300 with support at 23,800; April F&O expiry and Iran war tensions remain key triggers to watch

Indian stock markets opened lower on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. The BSE Sensex fell 169 points to 77,208.44 while the Nifty 50 dropped 48 points to trade near 24,044.

The weakness was driven by a spike in Brent crude oil to around $109 per barrel. Ongoing Iran war tensions have kept supply risks elevated, particularly around the Strait of Hormuz which handles about one-fifth of global oil flows.

GIFT Nifty futures had signaled a soft start, trading at 24,002 ahead of the opening bell. The benchmark had closed Monday at 24,092.70 after snapping a three-session losing streak.

Foreign portfolio investors remained in sell mode, offloading domestic equities worth ₹1,151 crore on Monday. This marked their sixth consecutive session of net selling in the cash market.

Domestic institutional investors continued to absorb the selling pressure, buying shares worth ₹4,124 crore. They have now been net buyers for three straight sessions.

"Corporate earnings may not yet show the full impact of the Iran conflict. Forward guidance is where companies will start pricing in higher future costs," said Vikram Kasat, Head Advisory at PL Capital.

Elevated crude prices pose a direct risk to India, the world’s third-largest crude importer. Higher oil widens the import bill, stokes inflation, and squeezes corporate margins across sectors.

Nifty technical support is placed at 23,800 while the index is testing the 24,300 resistance zone for the umpteenth time. Tuesday also marks the expiry of April derivative contracts.

BRENT CRUDE OIL IS TRADING NEAR $109 PER BARREL DUE TO ESCALATING IRAN WAR TENSIONS. THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ, WHICH HANDLES ABOUT ONE-FIFTH OF GLOBAL OIL FLOWS, REMAINS A KEY SUPPLY RISK.

FOREIGN PORTFOLIO INVESTORS SOLD ₹1,151 CRORE WORTH OF INDIAN EQUITIES ON MONDAY, EXTENDING THEIR SELLING STREAK TO SIX SESSIONS. DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS BOUGHT ₹4,124 CRORE, REMAINING NET BUYERS FOR A THIRD STRAIGHT SESSION.
WHAT ARE THE KEY TECHNICAL LEVELS FOR NIFTY?
NIFTY IMMEDIATE SUPPORT IS AT 23,800 AND RESISTANCE IS NEAR 24,300. THE INDEX HAS TESTED THE 24,300 ZONE MULTIPLE TIMES WITHOUT A SUSTAINED BREAKOUT.

APRIL F&O CONTRACTS EXPIRE ON TUESDAY, WHICH CAN ADD VOLATILITY. INVESTORS SHOULD ALSO TRACK CORPORATE EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR SIGNS OF OIL-RELATED COST PRESSURES, AND ANY DEVELOPMENTS IN THE IRAN CONFLICT.
