Welspun Enterprises Bags ₹7,300 Crore Pune-Shirur Highway Contract

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Welspun Enterprises receives ₹7,300 crore Letter of Award from MSIDC for the Pune-Shirur highway corridor on NH-753F
  • 02/Outstanding order book rises to ₹18,755 crore from ₹13,341 crore as of December 31, 2025, after Q4 FY26 execution adjustments
  • 03/The DBFOT toll project spans 53.40 km with a 29-year concession including four years of construction

Welspun Enterprises Ltd has received a Letter of Award dated April 29, 2026, from Maharashtra State Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd (MSIDC). The contract covers construction of a six-lane partially elevated highway corridor from Km 10+600 to Km 64+000 on NH-753F in Maharashtra.

The project spans a minimum design length of 53.40 km along the Pune-Shirur section. It will be executed on a DBFOT (Design, Build, Finance, Operate and Transfer) toll basis.

The total project cost is approximately ₹7,300 crore. The sub-concession period runs for 29 years, including a four-year construction window starting from the Appointed Date.

MSIDC is acting as the implementing and tendering agency under a government-to-government arrangement. The arrangement is between the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) and the Maharashtra Public Works Department.

The award lifts Welspun Enterprises' outstanding order book to approximately ₹18,755 crore. This is up from ₹13,341 crore as of December 31, 2025, after adjusting for Q4 FY26 execution.

The order book is now spread across three verticals. Water accounts for ₹10,813 crore, tunnelling for ₹1,791 crore, and transportation for ₹6,152 crore inclusive of this project.

Managing Director Sandeep Garg said the addition rebalances the order book across verticals. It also improves revenue visibility for the company.

Welspun Enterprises shares were trading at ₹530.50 on the NSE at 11:52 am on April 30. The stock was down ₹9.60 or 1.78 percent on the day.
