Sensex Live May 4: Benchmarks Up 325 Points, Nifty Gains 100

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex traded up 325 points (0.42%) at 77,239 and Nifty 50 rose 100 points (0.42%) to 24,098 at 1:02 p.m. on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Early rally saw Sensex jump 820 points (1.07%) to 77,733.93 at 9:55 a.m. before gains narrowed amid profit-booking
  • 03/Analysts expect a volatile, news-driven week driven by state election outcomes, Brent crude near $108, and key Q4 earnings from BHEL, Ambuja Cements, and auto majors

Benchmark indices traded higher in afternoon deals on May 4, 2026, after opening with gains of more than 1%. At 1:02 p.m., the Sensex was up 325 points (0.42%) at 77,239, while the Nifty 50 rose 100 points (0.42%) to 24,098.

The market opened strong after a holiday break. Sensex gained 534.84 points (0.70%) to 77,448.34 at 9:16 a.m. By 9:55 a.m., it surged 820.43 points (1.07%) to 77,733.93. The Nifty 50 gained 240.85 points (1%) to 24,238.40 at the morning high.

Gains narrowed by noon as profit-booking set in. Kotak Mahindra Bank and Bharti Airtel dragged the broader indices, while Hindustan Unilever and Bajaj Auto led the gainers. Kotak Bank fell 5% despite a Q4 profit beat, making it the top Nifty 50 loser.

Analysts expect a volatile, news-driven week ahead. "The most immediate catalyst will be the outcome of key state elections," said Hariprasad K, Research Analyst and Founder, Livelong Wealth. Investors are tracking results from West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Assam, and Puducherry.

Crude oil remains the single most critical macro variable. Brent crude futures traded at $108.60, up 0.40%, while WTI was at $102.12, up 0.17%. Elevated oil prices amid West Asia tensions pose risks to inflation, the rupee, and corporate margins.

The rupee fell 11 paise to 94.95 against the US dollar in early trade. India VIX remains elevated at around 18.4, signaling underlying caution in the market. A sustained upside move could help compress volatility and ease option premiums.

Stock-specific action was driven by Q4 earnings. BHEL shares hit the upper circuit with a 10% jump after Q4 profit surged to ₹1,283 crore from ₹504 crore a year ago. Godrej Properties rose 4% after posting a record ₹1,850 crore FY26 profit.

Bajaj Auto surged 4% after April sales rose 40% year-on-year to 5.13 lakh units, driven by strong export demand. Adani Ports gained 2% after reporting 15% YoY cargo volume growth in April at 43.1 MMT.
