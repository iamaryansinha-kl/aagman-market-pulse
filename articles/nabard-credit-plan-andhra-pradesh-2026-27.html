NABARD Projects ₹5.11 Lakh Crore Credit Plan for Andhra Pradesh 2026-27

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NABARD projects ₹5.11 lakh crore credit plan for Andhra Pradesh in fiscal year 2026-27
  • 02/Chief Minister Naidu urged NABARD to support the state's horticulture sector growth
  • 03/The plan targets agriculture, rural infrastructure, and allied sector financing

NABARD has projected a ₹5.11 lakh crore credit plan for Andhra Pradesh for 2026-27. The plan covers agriculture, allied activities, and rural development.

Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu met with NABARD officials recently. He requested stronger support for the state's horticulture sector.

Andhra Pradesh's agriculture sector remains a key economic driver. The state produces significant quantities of rice, cotton, and horticulture crops.

The credit plan allocation reflects growing institutional focus on rural financing. It aims to boost farm productivity and allied sector investments.

Naidu's push for horticulture support aligns with AP's climate advantages. The sector offers higher value addition than traditional food grains.

NABARD's state credit plans typically fund irrigation, warehousing, and rural infrastructure. They also support farmer producer organizations.

The ₹5.11 lakh crore target represents a substantial commitment to AP's rural economy. Implementation across districts will be closely watched.

ANDHRA PRADESH HAS CLIMATE ADVANTAGES FOR GROWING FRUITS AND VEGETABLES. HORTICULTURE OFFERS HIGHER VALUE ADDITION COMPARED TO TRADITIONAL FOOD GRAINS AND DIVERSIFIES FARM INCOME.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT WITH THIS CREDIT PLAN?
NABARD WILL WORK WITH STATE GOVERNMENT AND BANKS TO DISBURSE CREDIT ACROSS DISTRICTS. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS AND SECTOR-WISE ALLOCATION WILL BE FINALIZED IN COMING MONTHS.
