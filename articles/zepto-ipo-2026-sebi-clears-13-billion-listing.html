Zepto IPO 2026: SEBI Clears $1.3 Billion Quick Commerce Listing Plan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI clears Zepto's $1.3 billion (~Rs 11,000-12,000 crore) IPO, targeting July-September 2026 listing
  • 02/Four-year-old quick commerce startup reports Rs 9,669 crore revenue in FY25, up 129% YoY
  • 03/IPO includes Rs 11,000 crore fresh issue plus offer-for-sale by early investors

Zepto Limited has received SEBI approval for its initial public offering. The quick commerce platform is targeting a listing in the July-September quarter of 2026.

The IPO size is expected at around $1.3 billion, or roughly Rs 11,000-12,000 crore. Of this, approximately Rs 11,000 crore will be raised as fresh capital.

The remaining amount will come through an offer-for-sale by early investors. The confidential filing route allows Zepto to revise issue size before launch.

At just four years old, Zepto will be the youngest venture capital-backed Indian startup to go public. The company was founded by Aadit Palicha.

For the financial year ended March 2025, Zepto reported revenue of Rs 9,669 crore. This marks a 129% increase over the previous year.

However, net losses nearly tripled to Rs 3,367 crore from Rs 1,214 crore. The cash burn reflects aggressive expansion in a competitive market.

Zepto appointed Morgan Stanley, Axis Capital, HSBC, Goldman Sachs, JM Financial, IIFL Securities and Motilal Oswal as bankers. Morgan Stanley leads the issue.

The company had raised $450 million in October 2025 at a $7 billion valuation. As of late November, it held approximately Rs 7,000 crore in cash reserves.

It competes against Eternal-owned Blinkit and Swiggy's Instamart. BofA Securities data shows Blinkit controls over 50% of the quick commerce market.

Bernstein analysts estimate the quick commerce market could grow to $35 billion by 2030. The IPO will intensify competition for capital and market share.
