Sterling Tools Surges 6% on ARAS Deal With China's Nanjing Haohang

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sterling Tools Limited surged 5.98% to ₹260 on April 27 after announcing a Technology Collaboration and Supply Agreement with Nanjing Haohang Technology Co. of China for ARAS development.
  • 02/The Faridabad-based auto components maker will localise engineering, manufacturing and sales of Advanced Rider Assistance Systems for India's two-wheeler market.
  • 03/The stock remains down nearly 20% over the past year despite Monday's rally, with long-term execution on ARAS and EV tech as key monitorables.

Sterling Tools Limited (NSE: STERTOOLS) surged nearly 6% on April 27, 2026. The stock climbed to ₹260 by 2:32 PM, up ₹14.66 from its previous close of ₹245.34.

The rally followed the company's disclosure of a Technology Collaboration Agreement and Supply Agreement with Nanjing Haohang Technology Co., Ltd. of China.

The partnership aims to develop Advanced Rider Assistance Systems (ARAS) for India's two-wheeler market. Sterling Tools will handle local engineering, system adaptation, manufacturing and sales.

Nanjing Haohang is a joint venture between Haobo Electronic Technology and Nanjing Chuhang Technology. It brings an existing global ARAS platform for motorcycles and electric two-wheelers.

ARAS functions like ADAS in passenger cars. It uses sensors, software and control systems to warn riders of hazards. The companies have tested Front Collision Warning, Rear Collision Warning, Blind Spot Detection, Lane Change Warning and Wrong-Side Alert on Indian roads.

"Two-wheeler accidents are a significant contributor to road fatalities in India," said Anish Agarwal, Director at Sterling Tools. The tie-up targets this gap with proven technology adapted for local conditions.

Total traded volume stood at approximately 1.03 lakh shares. Buy orders accounted for 62% of activity. The company's market capitalisation stood at roughly ₹945 crore.

The deal adds to Sterling Tools' pivot toward next-generation auto tech. Its subsidiaries already operate in EV power electronics, high-voltage DC contactors and rare-earth-magnet-free motor solutions.

Despite Monday's gain, the stock is down nearly 20% over the past year. It has fallen about 37% over three years, trailing the Nifty 50 which returned roughly flat and 34% over the same periods.
