Titan Shares Fall 4% After Q2 Update; Nomura Keeps Buy, ₹5,425 Target

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Titan shares fell 4% to ₹4,370 on the BSE on October 7, 2026, despite 25% YoY growth across consumer businesses in Q2FY27
  • 02/Jewellery grew 21%, watches 30% and EyeCare 28%; demand moderated late in the quarter as the festive calendar shifted to Q3FY27
  • 03/Nomura retains Buy with a ₹5,425 target and calls any correction from the high base an accumulation opportunity

Titan Company Ltd (NSE: TITAN) shares fell 4% to ₹4,370 on the BSE on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The drop came a day after the Tata group company reported 25% year-on-year growth across its consumer businesses in Q2FY27.

The stock had already reacted to the business update on Tuesday, closing at ₹4,550, down 0.66%. Wednesday's slide shows the market looking past a strong headline quarter to a demanding base in the quarters ahead.

Jewellery grew 21% YoY in Q2FY27, watches 30%, and EyeCare 28%. Domestic businesses rose 22% while international revenue surged 97%, powered by strong double-digit momentum in North America and a resilient GCC business.

Consumer demand stayed healthy through most of the quarter but moderated towards the end. The festive calendar shifted into Q3FY27, pulling some purchases out of the September quarter.

Within jewellery, studded pieces grew in the early thirties, aided by the 'Festival of Diamonds' campaign. Plain gold rose around 20% while coin demand fell in high single digits from a high base.

Jewellery buyer growth was in the mid-single digits while average ticket sizes rose in double digits. Tanishq, Mia, Zoya and beYon together grew 20%; CaratLane grew 32%. Watches saw analogue growth in the early thirties and a high single-digit smartwatch recovery.

Titan added 78 net stores in the quarter, taking the total consumer store count to 3,758 as of September 2026. Jewellery closed with 1,269 stores, watches 1,379, EyeCare 847 and international 164.

Nomura has retained its Buy rating with a target price of ₹5,425, valuing Titan at 60x Jun-28F EPS. At ₹4,370, the stock trades at 53x Mar-28F EPS of ₹86.1.

The brokerage flags a high base: Titan cycles jewellery growth of 40% in Q3, 45% in Q4 and 38% in Q1. It expects optical moderation in reported growth from Q3 onwards as the benefit of elevated gold prices fades.

Despite this, Nomura forecasts FY27F consolidated sales growth of 19% and EBIT growth of 23%, with jewellery sales and EBIT CAGR of 19% and 18% over FY26-30E and an EPS CAGR of 22% over FY26-29F.

Nomura keeps Titan among its top sector picks. Its view: any correction driven by optical moderation is an accumulation opportunity. The key risks are a sales slowdown from high gold prices and competition.

GLOSSARY: High Base Effect: When year-on-year growth looks weaker simply because the year-ago quarter was unusually strong — Titan faces comparisons of 40-45% jewellery growth in coming quarters. EPS (Earnings Per Share): A company's profit divided by its shares outstanding. Nomura values Titan at 60 times its June 2028 forecast EPS of Rs 86.1. EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Taxes): Operating profit, used to measure core business performance. Nomura forecasts 23% consolidated EBIT growth in FY27. CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate): The smoothed annual growth rate over a multi-year period. Nomura models 19% jewellery sales CAGR over FY26-30E. Studded Jewellery: Pieces combining gold with diamonds or gemstones, typically carrying higher margins than plain gold jewellery. Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG): Revenue growth from stores open at least a year, excluding new openings — a measure of underlying demand.
