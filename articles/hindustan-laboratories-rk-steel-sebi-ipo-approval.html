Hindustan Laboratories, RK Steel Get SEBI Nod for IPOs: 3.41 Crore Shares on Offer

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI clears Hindustan Laboratories and RK Steel Manufacturing IPOs on April 27, with observations equivalent to final approval
  • 02/Hindustan Laboratories offers 1.41 crore shares (50 lakh fresh + 91 lakh OFS); RK Steel floats 2 crore fresh shares
  • 03/Both companies filed DRHPs on January 3, 2026; shares to list on BSE and NSE, dates yet to be announced

Hindustan Laboratories Limited and RK Steel Manufacturing Company have received SEBI approval to launch their initial public offerings. The market regulator issued its observations on April 27, 2026, clearing both companies to proceed.

The two firms filed their draft red herring prospectus papers on January 3, 2026. In SEBI's regulatory framework, obtaining observations is equivalent to securing final approval for a public offering.

Hindustan Laboratories' maiden IPO comprises a book-built issue of 1.41 crore equity shares of face value ₹10 each. The offer includes a fresh issue of 50 lakh shares and an offer for sale of 91 lakh shares by promoter Rajesh Vasantray Doshi.

The generic drug maker will deploy ₹72.5 crore from fresh proceeds toward working capital requirements. The balance will be used for general corporate purposes.

Hindustan Laboratories manufactures generic medicines primarily for government institutions under a business-to-government framework. The company is exposed to risks including heavy reliance on government tenders and dependence on top clients for revenue.

RK Steel Manufacturing Company's IPO is a pure fresh issue of up to 2 crore equity shares with no OFS component. The steel tubes and pipes manufacturer had pre-issue equity of ₹49.4 crore across 4.94 crore shares.

The company plans to allocate up to ₹76 lakh for working capital and approximately ₹43.23 lakh for repayment of borrowings. The remaining funds will cover general corporate purposes and issue-related expenses.

RK Steel operates from a single manufacturing facility in Tamil Nadu. The company derives over 98% of its revenue from Southern India, creating geographic concentration risk.

Choice Capital Advisor is the book-running lead manager for Hindustan Laboratories, while GYR Capital Advisors handles RK Steel's issue. MUFG Intime India is the registrar for both IPOs. Shares of both companies will list on the BSE and NSE.

WHAT IS THE TOTAL ISSUE SIZE OF THE HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES IPO?
HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES' IPO COMPRISES A TOTAL OF 1.41 CRORE EQUITY SHARES OF FACE VALUE ₹10 EACH. THIS INCLUDES A FRESH ISSUE OF 50 LAKH SHARES AND AN OFFER FOR SALE OF 91 LAKH SHARES BY PROMOTER RAJESH VASANTRAY DOSHI.
HOW WILL RK STEEL MANUFACTURING COMPANY UTILIZE ITS IPO PROCEEDS?
RK STEEL WILL DEPLOY UPTO ₹76 LAKH FOR WORKING CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS AND APPROXIMATELY ₹43.23 LAKH FOR REPAYMENT OR PREPAYMENT OF BORROWINGS. THE REMAINING FUNDS WILL BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES AND ISSUE-RELATED EXPENSES.
WHEN DID BOTH COMPANIES FILE THEIR DRAFT PAPERS AND RECEIVE SEBI CLEARANCE?
BOTH HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES AND RK STEEL MANUFACTURING FILED THEIR DRAFT RED HERRING PROSPECTUS ON JANUARY 3, 2026. THEY RECEIVED SEBI'S OBSERVATIONS — WHICH AMOUNTS TO FINAL APPROVAL — ON APRIL 27, 2026.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS FOR INVESTORS IN THESE IPOs?
HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES FACES RISKS FROM HEAVY RELIANCE ON GOVERNMENT TENDERS AND TOP-CLIENT CONCENTRATION. RK STEEL DERIVES OVER 98% OF REVENUE FROM SOUTHERN INDIA AND OPERATES FROM A SINGLE FACILITY IN TAMIL NADU, CREATING GEOGRAPHIC AND OPERATIONAL CONCENTRATION RISKS.
WHERE WILL THE SHARES OF BOTH COMPANIES BE LISTED?
SHARES OF BOTH HINDUSTAN LABORATORIES LIMITED AND RK STEEL MANUFACTURING COMPANY WILL BE LISTED ON THE BOMBAY STOCK EXCHANGE (BSE) AND THE NATIONAL STOCK EXCHANGE (NSE). THE EXACT LISTING DATES AND PRICE BANDS HAVE NOT YET BEEN ANNOUNCED.
