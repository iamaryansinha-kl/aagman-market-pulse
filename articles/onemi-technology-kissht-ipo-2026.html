OnEMI Technology (Kissht) IPO Opens at ₹162-171, Aims to Raise ₹926 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/OnEMI Technology (Kissht) opens ₹926-crore IPO today at ₹162-171 price band; 87-share lot, ₹14,877 minimum
  • 02/Fresh issue ₹850 crore plus OFS ₹75.92 crore; anchor investors already committed ₹277.7 crore at ₹171 upper band
  • 03/Allotment on May 6, tentative listing May 8; SBI Securities values issue at 1.6x post-issue P/ABV

OnEMI Technology Solutions (Kissht) opened its ₹926-crore initial public offering on April 30, 2026. The price band is fixed at ₹162 to ₹171 per equity share.

The issue comprises a fresh issue of ₹850 crore and an offer-for-sale of 4,439,788 shares worth ₹75.92 crore. The IPO closes on May 5, 2026.

The company raised ₹277.7 crore from anchor investors ahead of the public issue. It allocated 1,62,44,216 shares at ₹171 to marquee names including Citigroup, BNP Paribas, Goldman Sachs, HDFC Mutual Fund, and ICICI Prudential.

Investors can bid for a minimum lot of 87 shares, requiring ₹14,877 at the upper band. The book-building process reserves 50% for QIBs, 15% for non-institutional investors, and 35% for retail.

Net proceeds of ₹637.50 crore will augment the capital base of subsidiary Si Creva. The company had 63.73 million registered users and 11.17 million customers as of December 31, 2025.

SBI Securities notes NII/PPOP/NPAT CAGR of 15.8%/29.6%/140.9% over FY23-FY25. NIM stood at 23.8% in FY25, with GNPA/NNPA at 2.9%/0.4% as of December 2025.

At the upper price band of ₹171, the issue is valued at a post-issue P/ABV of 1.6x. The grey market premium was ₹4 ahead of opening, suggesting a modest listing gain of approximately 2%.

The stock will list on BSE and NSE, with allotment expected on May 6 and tentative listing on May 8, 2026. KFin Technologies is the registrar; JM Financial, HSBC, Nuvama, SBI Capital Markets, and Centrum Broking are the lead managers.
