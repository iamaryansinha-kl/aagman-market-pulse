Nagpur Airport to GMR: Gadkari Wants Dubai, Singapore Flights

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur formally handed over to GMR Group under the PPP model on June 25, 2026
  • 02/Union Minister Nitin Gadkari urged Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu to start direct Nagpur-Dubai and Nagpur-Singapore flights immediately
  • 03/Gadkari highlighted nearly ₹7.5 lakh crore in investments across Vidarbha and called the airport transfer a "historic moment"

Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur was formally handed over to GMR Group under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model on June 25, 2026. Union Minister Nitin Gadkari described the transfer as a "historic moment" after years of legal and administrative processes.

Speaking at the handover ceremony, Gadkari urged Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu to start direct international flights from Nagpur to Dubai and Singapore. He said such connectivity would allow passengers from the region to seamlessly reach Southeast Asia, China, Europe and other global markets.

The ceremony was attended by Union Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu, Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, Ramtek MP Shyamkumar Barve, Rajya Sabha MP Praful Patel and Smt. Maya Ivnate. Gadkari, Naidu and Fadnavis jointly unveiled the airport's new logo.

Gadkari highlighted that Nagpur's central location gives it strong strategic advantage. The airport serves large parts of Madhya Pradesh, Telangana and Chhattisgarh in addition to Maharashtra.

The Union Minister pointed to investments worth nearly ₹7.5 lakh crore across sectors in the Vidarbha region. He said the upgraded airport would accelerate growth across tourism, agriculture, mining and export-oriented industries.

GMR Group will undertake phased redevelopment of the airport. The plan includes modern cargo facilities, Maintenance Repair and Overhaul (MRO) infrastructure, and upgraded passenger amenities.

Gadkari also suggested showcasing Nagpur's renowned Orange Barfi and other regional specialties at the airport. This would provide visibility to local entrepreneurs and strengthen Vidarbha's cultural identity.

The PPP handover marks the beginning of a major expansion plan. The goal is to substantially increase passenger and cargo handling capacities and strengthen regional connectivity across Central India.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE NAGPUR AIRPORT ON JUNE 25, 2026?
DR BABASAHEB AMBEDKAR INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT, NAGPUR WAS FORMALLY HANDED OVER TO GMR GROUP UNDER THE PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP (PPP) MODEL. THE HANDOVER CEREMONY WAS ATTENDED BY UNION MINISTER NITIN GADKARI, CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER K. RAMMOHAN NAIDU, AND MAHARASHTRA CHIEF MINISTER DEVENDRA FADNAVIS.
WHAT DIRECT FLIGHTS DID GADKARI REQUEST FROM NAGPUR?
NITIN GADKARI URGED THE MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION TO START DIRECT INTERNATIONAL FLIGHTS FROM NAGPUR TO DUBAI AND SINGAPORE. HE STATED THAT SUCH CONNECTIVITY WOULD BOOST TRADE, TOURISM, INVESTMENT, AND BUSINESS EXCHANGES WHILE ENHANCING NAGPUR'S INTEGRATION WITH GLOBAL MARKETS.
WHAT REDEVELOPMENT PLANS DOES GMR GROUP HAVE FOR NAGPUR AIRPORT?
GMR GROUP WILL UNDERTAKE PHASED REDEVELOPMENT INCLUDING MODERN CARGO FACILITIES, MAINTENANCE REPAIR AND OVERHAUL (MRO) INFRASTRUCTURE, AND UPGRADED PASSENGER AMENITIES. THE PLAN AIMS TO SUBSTANTIALLY INCREASE PASSENGER AND CARGO HANDLING CAPACITIES.
HOW MUCH INVESTMENT IS FLOWING INTO VIDARBHA REGION?
NITIN GADKARI HIGHLIGHTED INVESTMENTS WORTH NEARLY ₹7.5 LAKH CRORE ACROSS SECTORS IN VIDARBHA. THIS INCLUDES MAJOR COMMITMENTS IN STEEL, AUTOMOBILES, SOLAR ENERGY, AND HEALTHCARE FROM COMPANIES LIKE MAHINDRA, SUPER SMELTERS, AND GALAXY SOLAR ENERGY.
WHY IS NAGPUR'S LOCATION STRATEGIC FOR AVIATION GROWTH?
NAGPUR IS LOCATED AT THE GEOGRAPHICAL CENTRE OF INDIA. THE AIRPORT SERVES LARGE PARTS OF MADHYA PRADESH, TELANGANA, AND CHHATTISGARH IN ADDITION TO MAHARASHTRA, GIVING IT A NATURAL ADVANTAGE AS A LOGISTICS, CARGO, AND AVIATION HUB FOR CENTRAL INDIA.
