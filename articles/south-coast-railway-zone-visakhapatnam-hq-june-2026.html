South Coast Railway Zone Notified: Visakhapatnam HQ, Effective June 1, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Centre issues gazette notification for South Coast Railway Zone, effective June 1, 2026, with Visakhapatnam as headquarters
  • 02/Zone covers Guntakal, Guntur, Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam divisions carved from East Coast and South Central Railways
  • 03/Rs 184 crore sanctioned for HQ setup; Rs 1,06,000 crore in railway projects currently underway in Andhra Pradesh

The Centre has issued a gazette notification establishing the South Coast Railway Zone. Visakhapatnam will serve as its headquarters.

The new zone comes into effect from June 1, 2026. It becomes the 18th railway zone of Indian Railways.

Four divisions will fall under the zone. Guntakal, Guntur, Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam are included.

The zone was carved out from two existing zones. Parts came from East Coast Railway and South Central Railway.

The demand dates back to the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act of 2014. It gained momentum after the N Chandrababu Naidu-led NDA government took office in 2024.

On February 7, 2025, the Union Cabinet approved the zone creation. Prime Minister Narendra Modi laid the foundation stone for the HQ in January 2025.

A 52.2-acre plot at Mudasarlova has been allocated. The state government handed over the land in August 2024.

Rs 184 crore has been sanctioned for the headquarters setup. The region connects mineral-rich areas to key eastern ports including Visakhapatnam.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN DOES THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY ZONE COME INTO EFFECT?
THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY ZONE WILL COME INTO EFFECT FROM JUNE 1, 2026, AS PER THE GAZETTE NOTIFICATION ISSUED BY THE CENTRE.
WHERE WILL THE HEADQUARTERS OF THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY ZONE BE LOCATED?
THE HEADQUARTERS WILL BE LOCATED IN VISAKHAPATNAM, ANDHRA PRADESH. A 52.2-ACRE PLOT AT MUDASARLOVA HAS BEEN ALLOCATED FOR THE HQ.
WHICH DIVISIONS WILL COME UNDER THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY ZONE?
THE ZONE WILL INCLUDE FOUR DIVISIONS: GUNTAKAL, GUNTUR, VIJAYAWADA, AND VISAKHAPATNAM (FORMERLY WALTAIR DIVISION OF EAST COAST RAILWAY).
HOW MUCH FUNDING HAS BEEN ALLOCATED FOR THE NEW RAILWAY ZONE?
RS 184 CRORE HAS BEEN SANCTIONED FOR SETTING UP THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY HEADQUARTERS. ADDITIONALLY, APPROXIMATELY RS 1,06,000 CRORE WORTH OF RAILWAY PROJECTS ARE CURRENTLY UNDERWAY IN ANDHRA PRADESH.
WHY WAS THE SOUTH COAST RAILWAY ZONE CREATED?
THE ZONE FULFILS A LONG-PENDING DEMAND DATING BACK TO THE ANDHRA PRADESH REORGANISATION ACT OF 2014. IT AIMS TO IMPROVE RAILWAY ADMINISTRATION AND CONNECTIVITY IN THE STATE, LINKING MINERAL-RICH AREAS TO KEY PORTS ON THE EASTERN COAST.
