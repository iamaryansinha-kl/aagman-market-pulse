MARKETS PUBLISHED: APR 21, 2026

Closing Bell: Sensex ended at 79,273.33; Nifty 50 at 24,577

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex surged 753.03 points to close at 79,273.33 on April 21, 2026, gaining 0.96% from its previous close of 78,520.30
  • 02/NSE Nifty 50 climbed 212 points to settle at 24,577, reclaiming the 24,500 mark for the first time since early March
  • 03/All sectoral indices ended in the green, with FMCG and realty leading at around 2% each; market advance-decline ratio was 2:1

The BSE Sensex closed at 79,273.33 on April 21, 2026. The index gained 753.03 points, or 0.96%, from its previous close of 78,520.30.

The NSE Nifty 50 settled at 24,577. It advanced 212 points, marking its first close above 24,500 since early March.

FMCG and realty stocks led the rally, rising around 2% each. Banking and telecom sectors added approximately 1%.

The Nifty Bank index gained 789 points to close at 57,371. Financials remained the key driver of the benchmark advance.

Nestle India surged 7% after reporting strong quarterly results. It emerged as the top gainer on the Nifty 50.

Trent advanced 3% ahead of its earnings announcement. Bajaj Finance and Hindustan Unilever also supported the upmove.

SBI Life Insurance fell 4% ahead of its quarterly results. Bharat Electronics and Jio Financial Services were among the laggards.

Broader markets joined the rally. The midcap index rose 0.4%, while smallcaps gained nearly 1%.

Market breadth remained positive with an advance-decline ratio of 2:1. The Indian rupee weakened 37 paise to close at 93.49 against the US dollar.
