Axis Bank Q2 FY27: Advances Up 23% to Rs 13.85 Lakh Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Axis Bank reports gross advances of Rs 13.846 lakh crore as of September 30, 2026, up 22.7% YoY and 8.8% QoQ
  • 02/Deposits rise 20.7% YoY to Rs 14.521 lakh crore; FCNR(B) mobilisation of $10.62 billion lifts reported balances
  • 03/Ex-FCNR, advances grew 18.8% YoY and deposits 17%; Sanjeev Moghe resigns, relieved October 30

Axis Bank Ltd (NSE: AXISBANK) disclosed its provisional Q2 FY27 business update. Gross advances stood at Rs 13.846 lakh crore as of September 30, 2026.

That is a 22.7% increase from Rs 11.284 lakh crore a year earlier. Sequentially, advances grew 8.8% from Rs 12.729 lakh crore at June-end.

Deposits came in at Rs 14.521 lakh crore, up 20.7% YoY from Rs 12.035 lakh crore. Sequentially, deposits grew 5.8% from Rs 13.729 lakh crore at June-end.

CASA (current account and savings account) deposits stood at Rs 5.296 lakh crore, up 10.6% YoY and 1.5% QoQ. Term deposits rose 27.3% YoY and 8.4% QoQ to Rs 9.225 lakh crore.

The headline numbers carry an FCNR(B) effect. FCNR(B) refers to foreign currency non-resident (bank) deposits. The bank mobilised $10.62 billion, roughly Rs 1.018 lakh crore, under the RBI's swap facility.

A portion of those flows was used to reduce non-retail term deposits, which declined 8.6% sequentially. The bank also disclosed ex-FCNR numbers that strip out this layer.

Excluding $4.57 billion (about Rs 43,800 crore) of leveraging loans extended by international branches to FCNR(B) deposit placers, gross advances grew 18.8% YoY and 5.3% QoQ. Total deposits grew 17% YoY and 2.6% QoQ, while term deposits rose 21.3% YoY and 3.2% QoQ.

Separately, the bank disclosed standby letters of credit (SBLCs) issued in favour of other banks for loans against FCNR(B) deposits. These stood at $4.69 billion, or roughly Rs 44,900 crore.

On the management front, Sanjeev Moghe, Senior Management Personnel and Group Head: Branch Banking – South, tendered his resignation on October 4, 2026. He moves on to pursue an opportunity outside the bank and will be relieved at close of business on October 30, 2026.

In market action, Axis Bank shares ended Monday 0.42% higher at Rs 1,222.20 on the NSE, trading in a range of Rs 1,216.40 to Rs 1,237.40. The stock is down 4% year-to-date, with a market capitalisation of Rs 3,80,555.16 crore.

All figures are provisional and subject to limited review by the bank's statutory auditors.
