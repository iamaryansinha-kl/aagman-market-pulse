Vi Shares Jump 2.7% as DoT Cuts AGR Dues by ₹23,649 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/DoT cuts Vodafone Idea's AGR dues by ₹23,649 crore to ₹64,046 crore, a 27% reduction from the earlier ₹87,695 crore provisional figure
  • 02/Vi shares open at ₹10.50, up 2.74% on May 4, with intraday high of ₹11.00 and traded volume crossing 72 crore shares
  • 03/Revised payment schedule stretches to March 2041 with six annual instalments of approximately ₹10,608 crore each

Vodafone Idea Ltd (NSE: IDEA) shares opened higher on May 4, 2026, trading at ₹10.50 — up ₹0.28 or 2.74 per cent. The stock touched an intraday high of ₹11.00 at open.

The rally follows a regulatory development disclosed on April 30. The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) finalized Vi's Adjusted Gross Revenue (AGR) dues at ₹64,046 crore as of December 31, 2025.

This represents a 27 per cent reduction from the earlier provisional figure of ₹87,695 crore. A DoT-constituted committee conducted the reassessment.

Under the revised schedule, Vi will pay ₹124 crore annually from March 2026 through March 2031. This covers dues pertaining to FY18 and FY19 that were outside the scope of reassessment.

A minimum of ₹100 crore annually will then be paid over four years ending March 2035. The remaining dues will be split into six equal instalments of approximately ₹10,608 crore each from March 2036 through March 2041.

The stock's traded volume stood at over 72 crore shares by mid-morning. The traded value was ₹770 crore, reflecting heavy investor interest.

At the current price, Vi's market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹1.13 lakh crore. The stock has gained over 51 per cent in the past year.

However, Vi shares remain about 18 per cent below their 52-week high of ₹12.80 recorded on December 31, 2025. Sector analysts noted the finality provides banks and investors greater clarity on Vi's financial position.
