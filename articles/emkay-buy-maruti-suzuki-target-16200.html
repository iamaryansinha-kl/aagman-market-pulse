Emkay Global Maintains Buy On Maruti Suzuki With ₹16,200 Target Price

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Emkay Global reiterates Buy rating on Maruti Suzuki with target price of ₹16,200, valuing it at 28x FY28E core PER
  • 02/Q4 revenue rose 28% YoY on 12% volume growth and 4% ASP increase; EBITDAM fell 60bps QoQ to 11.7% on commodity pressures
  • 03/MSIL targets 10% FY27 domestic PV volume growth vs 5-7% industry; watch new SUV and EV pipeline through FY30

Emkay Global has maintained its Buy rating on Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. The brokerage has set a target price of ₹16,200, valuing the stock at 28x FY28E core PER.

The stock closed at ₹13,260.20 on April 29, 2026. This implies an upside of around 22% from current levels.

Maruti Suzuki reported a steady Q4 performance. Revenue grew 28% year-on-year, driven by 12% volume growth and average selling prices rising 4% quarter-on-quarter.

EBITDAM stood at 11.7% in Q4, down 60 basis points QoQ. Adjusted Q3 FY26 margin was 12.4% after accounting for a one-off labour core impact of ₹590 crore.

Gross margin contracted by 70 basis points during the quarter. Commodity pressure sustained, causing an 80bps hit to EBIT margins in Q4 alone.

Profit after tax missed estimates due to lower other income. The company recorded a mark-to-market loss of ₹750 crore during the quarter.

Management is confident about FY27. Maruti targets 10% domestic passenger vehicle volume growth. This is well above SIAM's industry forecast of 5-7% for the same period.

The company holds a strong orderbook of 190,000 units. Small cars account for 130,000 of these orders, reflecting resilient demand despite West Asia conflict concerns.

Channel inventory remains lean at just 12 days as of FY26-end. Two new plants with 250,000 units per annum capacity each are ramping up faster than planned.

Management sees headroom for ASP improvement from Q4 FY26 levels. This will be driven by higher-end models and a rising electric vehicle mix.

The company plans 7 new SUVs and multiple EVs by FY30. This pipeline is expected to support market share gains in the domestic PV segment.
