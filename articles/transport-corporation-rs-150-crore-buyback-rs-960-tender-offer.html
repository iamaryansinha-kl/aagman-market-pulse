TCI Buyback 2026: Rs 150 Crore Tender Offer at Rs 960 Per Share

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Transport Corporation of India's board approved a Rs 150 crore share buyback at Rs 960 per share on September 29, 2026, an 11% premium to the previous close
  • 02/The tender offer covers up to 15.62 lakh shares with a record date of October 9, 2026, and follows a Rs 160 crore buyback at Rs 1,200 per share approved in August 2024
  • 03/Promoters holding roughly 68.66% will not participate, so the entire offer is open to public shareholders ahead of the October 9 record date

The board of Transport Corporation of India Ltd (NSE: TCI) approved a share buyback worth Rs 150 crore on September 29, 2026. The company disclosed the decision after its board meeting the same day.

DATA: The buyback price is fixed at Rs 960 per equity share. That represents an 11% premium to the stock's previous close.

DATA: The offer covers up to 15.62 lakh fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs 2 each. The record date for determining shareholder eligibility is October 9, 2026.

DATA: The buyback will follow the tender offer route under SEBI's Buy-Back of Securities Regulations, 2018 and the Companies Act, 2013. Accepted shares will be bought on a proportionate basis.

CONTEXT: The promoter group will not take part. Promoters informed the company through a letter dated September 29, 2026 that they do not intend to participate in the buyback. The offer excludes promoters and the promoter group entirely.

DATA: The buyback size works out to 6.76% of TCI's aggregate fully paid-up equity capital and free reserves on a standalone basis, using audited figures as of March 31, 2026. On a consolidated basis, it is 6.15%. Transaction costs are excluded from the stated size.

DATA: The board has kept room to fine-tune pricing. The board or the buyback committee may increase the buyback price and correspondingly reduce the number of shares up to one working day before the record date. The overall size stays unchanged at Rs 150 crore.

CONTEXT: This is TCI's second buyback in just over two years. On August 24, 2024, the board approved a Rs 160 crore tender-offer buyback at Rs 1,200 per share, with a record date of September 4, 2024. The current price of Rs 960 sits below that level, a fact that reflects the stock's de-rating since the earlier offer.

MARKET REACTION: The stock had surged 13-14% on September 25, when the board meeting was first announced. It zoomed 13.12% to Rs 942.95 intraday that day. On September 29, the session the buyback was approved, shares traded almost flat and ended up 0.22% at Rs 866.75.

CONTEXT: Promoters held roughly 68.66% of TCI as of June 2026. Foreign institutional investors held about 3%, while mutual funds held around 11.3% of the company.

IMPACT: With promoters sitting out, the buyback reduces the share count for remaining holders and can lift earnings per share. Promoter stake will also rise proportionately if public shareholders tender into the offer.

CONTEXT: The same board meeting approved the incorporation of a wholly-owned subsidiary in the People's Republic of China. The move is part of TCI's strategy to expand its international logistics network across key global trade corridors.

CONTEXT: TCI is India's largest integrated logistics player, operating across freight, supply chain solutions, and seaways and coastal shipping segments. Standalone figures for FY26 show revenue of about Rs 4,353 crore, up 7.2% year on year, and net profit of about Rs 444 crore, up 12.1%, according to Trendlyne data.

OUTLOOK: The tender-offer route gives every eligible public shareholder a chance to exit part of their holding at Rs 960. The final acceptance ratio will depend on how much stock is tendered into the offer. Investors will now watch the October 9 record date and any price revision the board chooses to make up to one working day before it.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAS THE TCI BOARD APPROVED AND AT WHAT PRICE?
THE BOARD OF TRANSPORT CORPORATION OF INDIA APPROVED A RS 150 CRORE BUYBACK OF EQUITY SHARES ON SEPTEMBER 29, 2026. THE BUYBACK PRICE IS RS 960 PER EQUITY SHARE, AN 11% PREMIUM TO THE PREVIOUS CLOSE, COVERING UP TO 15.62 LAKH FULLY PAID-UP SHARES OF FACE VALUE RS 2 EACH.
WHAT IS THE RECORD DATE FOR THE TCI BUYBACK?
THE RECORD DATE IS OCTOBER 9, 2026. ONLY SHAREHOLDERS HOLDING TCI SHARES ON THAT DATE WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE IN THE TENDER OFFER.
WILL TCI PROMOTERS PARTICIPATE IN THE BUYBACK?
NO. THE PROMOTER GROUP INFORMED THE COMPANY THROUGH A LETTER DATED SEPTEMBER 29, 2026 THAT IT DOES NOT INTEND TO PARTICIPATE. THE BUYBACK EXCLUDES PROMOTERS AND THE PROMOTER GROUP, LEAVING THE FULL RS 150 CRORE OFFER FOR PUBLIC SHAREHOLDERS.
HOW DOES THIS BUYBACK COMPARE WITH TCI'S PREVIOUS ONE?
IN AUGUST 2024, TCI'S BOARD APPROVED A RS 160 CRORE TENDER-OFFER BUYBACK AT RS 1,200 PER SHARE WITH A RECORD DATE OF SEPTEMBER 4, 2024. THE CURRENT OFFER IS SMALLER AT RS 150 CRORE AND PRICED AT RS 960 PER SHARE, BELOW THE 2024 LEVEL.
WHAT ELSE DID THE TCI BOARD APPROVE ON SEPTEMBER 29, 2026?
THE SAME MEETING APPROVED THE INCORPORATION OF A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY IN THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA. THE MOVE IS PART OF TCI'S STRATEGY TO EXPAND ITS INTERNATIONAL LOGISTICS NETWORK ACROSS KEY GLOBAL TRADE CORRIDORS.
