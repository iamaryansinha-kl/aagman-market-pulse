Syngene Q4 FY26: Revenue Up 2% to ₹1,037 Cr, PAT Dips 16%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Syngene International Q4 FY26 revenue rises 2% YoY to ₹1,037 crore, up 13% sequentially from Q3
  • 02/PAT before exceptional items falls 16% to ₹153 crore; EBITDA margin drops to 29% from 34% a year ago
  • 03/FY27 seen as flat transition year due to client headwinds; growth expected to accelerate from FY28 on biologics pipeline

Syngene International Ltd (NSE: SYNGENE) reported Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹1,037 crore, up 2% year-on-year. Revenue grew 13% sequentially from the previous quarter.

Profit after tax before exceptional items declined 16% year-on-year to ₹153 crore. Reported PAT after exceptional items stood at ₹148 crore.

EBITDA for the quarter came in at ₹303 crore, down 12% year-on-year. The EBITDA margin contracted to 29% from 34% in the same quarter last year.

For the full year FY26, revenue from operations grew 3% to ₹3,739 crore. Operating EBITDA fell 12% to ₹918 crore, with the full-year margin at 25%.

The decline was driven by destocking from a key biologics client linked to Librela, a monoclonal antibody for osteoarthritis in dogs. Higher operating costs and facility investments also weighed on margins.

Managing Director and CEO Peter Bains said the core business remains resilient. "While the overall numbers reflect the continuing impact from a single large molecule biologics client, our underlying business continued to show steady momentum," he said.

The company commissioned a new antibody-drug conjugate discovery lab and expanded its Bengaluru biologics facility. Its Bayview facility in the US is nearing operationalisation with active customer discussions underway.

Management indicated that FY27 will be a transition year with broadly flat performance. Growth is likely to be weighted towards the second half as new contracts begin contributing from FY28 onwards.
