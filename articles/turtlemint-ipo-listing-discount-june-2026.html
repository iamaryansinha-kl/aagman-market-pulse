Turtlemint IPO Debuts At 11% Discount To Issue Price On NSE, BSE

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Turtlemint Fintech Solutions lists at Rs 134.90 on NSE, an 11.25% discount to the Rs 152 issue price
  • 02/The Rs 883 crore IPO was subscribed 1.24x overall, with QIB portion at 1.63x and NII segment undersubscribed at 0.55x
  • 03/The company reported a net loss of Rs 187.4 crore on revenue of Rs 748.9 crore for the nine months ended December 2025

Turtlemint Fintech Solutions made a muted debut on Dalal Street on Monday, June 29, 2026. The stock opened at Rs 134.90 on the NSE, reflecting an 11.25% discount to the IPO price of Rs 152.

On the BSE, shares listed at Rs 136.20, a 10.39% discount. The weak listing aligned with negative grey market premium signals ahead of the debut.

The Rs 883 crore public issue comprised a fresh issue of Rs 661 crore and an offer for sale worth Rs 222 crore. Anchor investors contributed Rs 397.2 crore before the IPO opened.

The issue received an overall subscription of 1.24 times. The QIB portion was booked 1.63 times, while the retail category saw 1.11 times subscription. The NII segment remained undersubscribed at 0.55 times.

Founded in 2015, Turtlemint operates a technology-enabled insurance distribution platform. It connects customers, advisors, and insurers through a phygital model.

The company has built over 6.32 lakh digital partners, including more than 5.07 lakh certified PoSPs. It works with 45 insurance companies across life, health, motor, and other categories.

The platform facilitated over 21.8 million insurance policy sales between April 2022 and December 2025. Platform premiums exceeded Rs 10,000 crore, covering nearly 98% of India's pin codes.

For the nine months ended December 2025, Turtlemint reported total income of Rs 748.9 crore. It posted a net loss of Rs 187.4 crore, slightly narrower than the Rs 194.1 crore loss in FY25.

The company plans to use IPO proceeds for technology infrastructure, product development, marketing, and strategic acquisitions. Working capital for its subsidiary and general corporate purposes are also planned.
