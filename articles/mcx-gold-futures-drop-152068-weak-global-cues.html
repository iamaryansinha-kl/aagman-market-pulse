MCX Gold Futures Drop to ₹1,52,068/10g on Weak Global Cues

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MCX gold June futures drop ₹462 or 0.3% to ₹1,52,068 per 10g in a business turnover of 1,337 lots on May 11, 2026
  • 02/Weak global cues drag prices lower; COMEX gold futures decline 0.84% to $4,675.66 per ounce in New York
  • 03/Silver futures diverge and rise; traders eye US macro data, Federal Reserve policy signals, and geopolitical risks

MCX gold futures for the June 2026 delivery fell ₹462 to ₹1,52,068 per 10 grams on May 11, 2026. The contracts traded lower by 0.3 per cent in a business turnover of 1,337 lots.

Analysts attributed the decline in gold prices to weak global cues and reduced spot demand. Domestic buying remained subdued as elevated prices discouraged retail purchases.

Globally, gold futures declined by 0.84 per cent to $4,675.66 per ounce in New York. Since the West Asia conflict began in late February, gold has dropped more than 10 per cent.

Rising oil prices have fueled inflation worries and clouded the outlook for interest rates. This has pressured non-yielding assets like gold.

Silver futures bucked the trend, rising to ₹2,62,370 per kg on the MCX. The metal's industrial demand component supports its divergence from gold.

India's gold imports are expected to fall to a near 30-year low of around 15 metric tons in April 2026. Dealers offered discounts of up to $15 per ounce over official domestic prices.

Retail gold prices in Mumbai stood at ₹15,234 per gram for 24-carat on May 11. Prices vary across cities due to local taxes and jewellers' margins.

Traders remain cautious ahead of US macroeconomic data and Federal Reserve policy signals. Immediate support for MCX gold is seen near ₹1,50,000 per 10 grams.
