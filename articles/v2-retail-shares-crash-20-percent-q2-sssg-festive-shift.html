V2 Retail Shares Crash 20% on Festive Shift, Q2 SSSG -14.9%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/V2 Retail shares crashed as much as 20% to a day's low of Rs 162.55 on the BSE on Monday, October 5, 2026
  • 02/The plunge came even as the value-fashion retailer reported a 28.4% YoY rise in Q2 FY27 standalone revenue to Rs 905 crore
  • 03/Calendar-basis same-store sales growth fell to -14.9% because Navratri and Durga Puja shifted out of the quarter; on a festival-normalised basis SSSG was +0.5%

V2 Retail shares crashed as much as 20% on Monday, October 5, 2026. The stock hit a day's low of Rs 162.55 on the BSE during the session.

The sharp fall came on the same day the company reported its quarterly business update. That update showed revenue growing strongly, which makes the scale of the sell-off the central story.

Standalone revenue stood at Rs 905 crore in Q2 FY27. That compares with Rs 705 crore in Q2 FY26, a year-on-year increase of 28.4%.

The company attributed the revenue growth to the continued expansion of its store network. Growth in this business has been driven by adding stores, and this quarter was no different.

The problem for the stock was same-store sales growth, or SSSG. On a calendar basis, SSSG came in at -14.9% for Q2 FY27.

That negative number is a calendar distortion rather than a demand collapse, according to the company. The base quarter, Q2 FY26, included the Navratri and Durga Puja period.

This year, those festivals fall in October 2026. That places them inside Q3 FY27, outside the July-September quarter just reported.

On a festival-normalised basis, the picture changes completely. Same-store sales were broadly stable, with growth of 0.5% in Q2 FY27 on that adjusted basis.

The gap between -14.9% on a calendar basis and +0.5% on a normalised basis is entirely a timing effect. The festive demand did not disappear; it moved into the next quarter.

Store-level productivity held at a steady level through the quarter. Monthly sales per square foot stood at Rs 700 in Q2 FY27.

V2 Retail's store count is a key part of the growth story. The company operated 427 stores as of the quarter.

A large share of that network is new. Of the 427 stores, 106 were opened during the first half of FY27 alone.

New stores take time to reach full productivity. The company said its new stores are still in the ramp-up phase, which also weighed on the quarter's same-store metrics.

The revenue line tells the expansion story clearly: Rs 905 crore versus Rs 705 crore a year earlier. That is a Rs 200 crore increase in quarterly revenue, delivered in a quarter without its biggest festive weeks.

The tension in the update is what moved the market. Reported revenue rose 28.4% while the headline SSSG print was negative, and the stock fell up to 20% to Rs 162.55.

The company's own framing is that the number needs to be read through the seasonal distortion. It said the timing shift underlines why seasonal effects must be stripped out to judge underlying stability.

By that logic, underlying demand was flat to slightly positive in the quarter. A +0.5% festival-normalised SSSG with Rs 700 of monthly sales per square foot is a stable, not collapsing, base.

The next quarter will test that argument directly. With Navratri and Durga Puja falling in October 2026, the festive demand lands in Q3 FY27.

The company said it is positioned to capture that demand uplift in Q3. It also said it expects to sustain long-term growth momentum.

For now, the market has priced the quarter harshly. A 20% intraday crash to Rs 162.55 against 28.4% revenue growth is a violent reaction to a calendar quirk.

Whether that reaction proves overdone depends on Q3. The festive numbers, and how much of the shifted demand returns, are the next checkpoint for V2 Retail.
