KPI Green Energy Bags ₹621-Cr NTPC Solar Order in Rajasthan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/KPI Green Energy Ltd (NSE: KPIGREEN) secures ₹621 crore order from NTPC Renewable Energy for 500 MW solar PV projects at Bikaner, Rajasthan
  • 02/Contract split into two blocks: ₹367 crore for 300 MW and ₹254 crore for 200 MW, excluding GST
  • 03/Scope includes 3-year O&M and 10-year AMC; marks KPI's entry into Rajasthan solar market

KPI Green Energy Ltd (NSE: KPIGREEN) has bagged a ₹621 crore order from NTPC Renewable Energy Ltd. The contract is for a balance of system package for 500 MW solar PV projects at Bikaner, Rajasthan.

The company announced the order on May 8, 2026, through a regulatory filing. Notifications of award came from NTPC Renewable Energy, a subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Ltd (NSE: NTPCGREEN).

The contract is split into two blocks. The 300 MW block is valued at ₹367 crore, while the 200 MW block is worth ₹254 crore. Both figures exclude GST.

The scope covers supply of plant and equipment, inland transportation, insurance, installation, and testing. It also includes civil works, three years of comprehensive O&M, and a ten-year annual maintenance contract from commercial operation date.

This project marks KPI Green Energy's entry into Rajasthan, one of India's largest renewable energy hubs. The company has historically focused on Gujarat and Maharashtra for its solar EPC business.

KPI Green Energy shares closed at ₹477.50 on May 8, down 2.20% from the previous close. The stock has risen 16.55% over the past year, with a 52-week high of ₹563 and low of ₹335.65.

The company reported revenue of ₹2,696 crore in FY2026, with profit growing at an 84.8% CAGR over the past five years. However, promoters have pledged 44.7% of their holdings, a risk factor investors should monitor.

Analysts remain constructive on the stock with buy ratings and price targets near ₹560. Execution of long-term O&M contracts and order book conversion will be key to sustaining the company's valuation at current P/E of around 20x.
