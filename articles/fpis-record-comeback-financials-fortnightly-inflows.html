FPIs Stage Record Comeback in Financial Stocks with Fortnightly Inflows

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Foreign portfolio investors have returned to India's financial sector in force.

Banking and financial stocks attracted record fortnightly inflows from foreign investors. The two-week period saw the highest FPI buying in financials in recent memory.

The surge marks a sharp reversal from earlier months when foreign investors were net sellers in the sector. Rising interest rates, asset quality concerns, and global risk aversion had driven FPIs away from Indian lenders.

The comeback is being led by large private sector banks. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank have all seen renewed buying interest from foreign funds.

Analysts attribute the reversal to several factors. India's banking sector has shown improving asset quality, with gross non-performing assets declining across major lenders. The Reserve Bank of India's stable rate stance has also reduced uncertainty for financial stocks.

The financial sector has underperformed the broader market this year. The recent FPI buying suggests foreign investors see value in banking stocks at current valuations.

Record fortnightly inflows into India's financial sector.

Large private sector banks like HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak.

Improving asset quality, stable RBI rates, and attractive valuations.
