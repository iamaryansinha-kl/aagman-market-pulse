Tesla India Supercharger Expansion: 8 Cities on Charging Map

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tesla India GM Sharad Agarwal announces supercharger expansion across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Chandigarh and Jaipur
  • 02/Tesla currently operates 5 supercharging stations and 3 experience centers in India; global network has 8,100+ stations with 80,000+ chargers
  • 03/Tesla has delivered cars across 21 Indian states but holds just 0.17% EV market share; pricing remains the key hurdle for mass adoption

Tesla Inc. (NASDAQ: TSLA) announced on April 24, 2026 that it will expand its supercharging network across eight major Indian cities. Tesla India General Manager Sharad Agarwal confirmed the plan to reporters in New Delhi.

The cities on the charging map are Delhi, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Pune. Tesla will deploy both superchargers and destination chargers in these hubs.

Tesla currently operates five supercharging stations in India. These were placed near customer workplaces, shopping and dining destinations.

Globally, Tesla runs more than 8,100 charging stations with over 80,000 superchargers. The network maintains a 99.95% uptime rate.

"In the coming months, you will see that we will build charging infrastructure around the major hubs," Agarwal said. He added that home charging installation capability now covers any city or state in India.

Tesla entered India in July 2025 with its first experience center in Mumbai. It now has three centers in Delhi, Mumbai and Gurugram.

The company has opened service stations in Gurugram and Mumbai. A service and body shop center recently opened in Pune.

This quarter, Tesla will add service and body shop centers in Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. It has delivered cars across 21 Indian states so far.

Tesla launched its second India model on April 22, 2026. The Model YL is a three-row six-seater SUV priced at ₹61.99 lakh.

Despite the expansion push, Tesla holds approximately 0.17% of India's EV market. It sold roughly 340-350 Model Y units by March 2026.

Tesla shares have declined 13.8% year-to-date as of April 22, 2026. The stock trades at a trailing P/E near 360x on a market cap of roughly $1.4 trillion.
