Mitsu Chem Plast Q4 FY26 Net Profit Doubles to Rs 14.2 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Q4 FY26 net profit doubled to Rs 14.2 crore from Rs 7.1 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Revenue for the quarter jumped 28% to Rs 102.3 crore against Rs 79.8 crore last year
  • 03/FY26 full-year profit surged 69% to Rs 48.5 crore, watch debt reduction and margin trends

Mitsu Chem Plast Ltd has reported strong Q4 FY26 results with net profit doubling to Rs 14.2 crore compared to Rs 7.1 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue for the quarter came in at Rs 102.3 crore, marking a solid 28% growth over Rs 79.8 crore reported in Q4 FY25.

The company saw its EBITDA margin expand to 14.2% from 11.8%, reflecting improved operational efficiency and better pricing power during the quarter.

For the full financial year FY26, Mitsu Chem Plast delivered a net profit of Rs 48.5 crore against Rs 28.7 crore in FY25, registering a robust 69% jump.

Full-year revenue stood at Rs 385.4 crore compared to Rs 311.2 crore in the previous year, underpinned by sustained demand across core business verticals.

The company manufactures blow-molded and injection-molded plastic products primarily serving industrial containers and automotive component markets.

Management has highlighted a positive outlook driven by strong demand from both packaging and automotive sectors, which continue to support order flows.

The stock currently trades in the Rs 95-100 range with a face value of Rs 10 per share, while debt levels have been reduced significantly during FY26.
