HAL Dividend FY26: Board Declares Rs 10 Per Share Final Payout

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HAL Board declares final dividend of Rs 10 per share (200%) for FY26, subject to AGM approval
  • 02/Record date fixed at August 14, 2026; payout within 30 days of shareholder approval
  • 03/FY26 net profit rose 9% to Rs 9,116 crore; stock up 20.63% over past three months

Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (NSE: HAL) has declared a final dividend of Rs 10 per equity share for FY26. The dividend represents 200% on the face value of Rs 5 per share.

The payout is subject to approval at the upcoming Annual General Meeting. If approved, the dividend will be paid within 30 days to eligible shareholders.

The company has fixed Friday, August 14, 2026 as the record date. Only shareholders holding shares in their demat accounts by this date will receive the dividend.

HAL is India's premier state-run aerospace and defence company. It operates under the Ministry of Defence and supplies aircraft, helicopters, and engines to the Indian Armed Forces.

In Q4FY26, HAL reported a consolidated net profit of Rs 4,196 crore. This marks a 6% year-on-year rise from Rs 3,977 crore in the year-ago period.

Revenue from operations grew 2% YoY to Rs 13,942 crore in Q4. For the full year FY26, net profit rose nearly 9% to Rs 9,116 crore on revenue of Rs 33,089 crore.

The company's earnings per share climbed more than 9% to Rs 135.71 for FY26. Its net worth jumped 17% to Rs 40,862 crore.

HAL shares traded at Rs 4,326 on June 29, 2026. The stock has gained 20.63% over the past three months but remains down 11.62% over the past one year.
