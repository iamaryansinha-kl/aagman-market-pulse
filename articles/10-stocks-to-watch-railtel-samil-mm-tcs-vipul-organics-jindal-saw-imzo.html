10 Stocks to Watch: RailTel ₹145 Cr ECL Deal, SAMIL Nissin Buy, M&M Carnot

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RailTel Corporation of India Ltd (NSE: RAILTEL) secures ₹145.47 crore telecom contract from Eastern Coalfields Ltd with a five-year execution timeline ending May 2031
  • 02/Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd (NSE: SAMIL) acquires 51% of Nissin India for ₹9.23 crore; Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd (NSE: M&M) lifts Carnot Technologies holding to 80.72%
  • 03/Tata Consultancy Services Ltd (NSE: TCS) signs dual MoUs with Siemens Energy; Vipul Organics' AdiMem unit begins commercial membrane sales from Gujarat

RailTel Corporation of India Ltd (NSE: RAILTEL) has bagged a ₹145.47 crore telecom and digital connectivity contract from Eastern Coalfields Ltd. The company received the Letter of Acceptance on April 27, 2026. Execution spans five years, with completion targeted for May 2, 2031.

Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd (NSE: SAMIL) will acquire a 51% equity stake in Nissin Advanced Coating Indo Co. Pvt Ltd for ₹9.23 crore. Post-deal, SAMIL and its wholly owned subsidiary will hold 100% of the thin film coating and PVD systems provider.

Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd (NSE: M&M) has executed a share purchase agreement for an additional 28.03% stake in Carnot Technologies. Its holding rises to 80.72% of paid-up equity and 89% of voting rights, deepening its precision farming and telematics push.

Tata Consultancy Services Ltd (NSE: TCS) signed two MoUs with Siemens Energy AG and Siemens Energy India Ltd. The partnership covers IT services, industrial AI, data centres and digital energy technology initiatives.

Vipul Organics' unit AdiMem Technologies has commenced commercial sales of membranes. The company will manufacture Reverse Osmosis and Ultrafiltration membranes at its Sayakha, Gujarat facility using proprietary Reverse Phase Transition technology.

Jindal Saw Ltd (NSE: JINDALSAW) has received board approval to set up an LSAW pipe plant in Saudi Arabia via its joint venture. A new HSAW pipe project will also be incorporated in the Kingdom to capture rising regional demand.

Marine Electricals (India) Ltd has secured orders worth ₹70.86 crore. SK Minerals & Additives Ltd carries a consolidated order book of ₹63.10 crore. Izmo Microsystems, a unit of Izmo Ltd, has partnered with IIT Madras' CPPICS as its official Photonic IC Packaging Partner.

Huwel Lifesciences and Avio Smart Market Stack Ltd have onboarded a Singapore-based regional partner. The collaboration targets six Southeast Asian markets including Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines for healthcare diagnostics expansion.
