Asian Monsoon 2026: ITCZ Crosses Equator as Australian Wet Season Ends

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Australian Bureau of Meteorology formally declared the 2025-26 northern Australian wet season over on May 7, clearing the path for Asian monsoon organisation
  • 02/The Inter Tropical Convergence Zone has crossed into the Northern Hemisphere, stretching north of Maldives toward Sri Lanka and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands
  • 03/IMD forecasts isolated heavy rainfall over Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Puducherry for the next seven days, with the south-west monsoon expected over Kerala around June 1

The Australian Bureau of Meteorology declared the 2025-26 northern Australian wet season over on Thursday, May 7, 2026. This seasonal turning point signals the shift of atmospheric focus toward India and South Asia.

The Southern Hemisphere tropics have steadily shut down. Monsoonal activity is retreating from Australia, removing a key hurdle to the timely onset of the Indian monsoon.

The Inter Tropical Convergence Zone has crossed into the Northern Hemisphere. It now stretches north of Malé in the Maldives toward Sri Lanka and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

These regions serve as the monsoon's early staging posts. The ITCZ will advance toward the Kerala coast in the coming weeks, with onset expected around June 1.

India Meteorological Department announced isolated heavy rainfall over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, and Karaikal for the next seven days. Kerala and Mahe will also see heavy pre-monsoon showers during this period.

South Interior Karnataka is forecast to receive heavy rain on Thursday and Friday, May 7 and 8. Pre-monsoon thunderstorms are gathering momentum as May progresses.

Isolated to scattered thunderstorms, lightning, and gusty winds of 30-50 km/hr are likely over multiple southern states during the next five days. The monsoon over the Andaman and Nicobar Islands is expected within a fortnight.

North-West and East India will see a volatile pattern. Severe convective activity and intense heat will alternate, with thunderstorms, lightning, and isolated hailstorms interrupting rising temperatures.

THE USUAL ONSET OF THE SOUTH-WEST MONSOON OVER THE KERALA COAST IS EXPECTED AROUND JUNE 1, 2026. THE MONSOON OVER THE ANDAMAN AND NICOBAR ISLANDS IS EXPECTED WITHIN A FORTNIGHT.
