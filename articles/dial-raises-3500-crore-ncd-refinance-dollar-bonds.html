DIAL Raises ₹3,500 Crore NCDs to Refinance Dollar Bonds

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/DIAL has raised Rs 3,500 crore through 15-year rupee-denominated non-convertible debentures to refinance $522.6 million of 6.125% dollar notes maturing in October 2026, according to people familiar with the development
  • 02/The NCDs carry an initial coupon of 8.71% a year payable quarterly, linked to the RBI repo rate at an initial spread of 375 basis points, with resets after five and 10 years
  • 03/IIFCL led the investor book with a Rs 1,426 crore subscription, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank arranged the issue, and ICRA flags refinancing risk with $500 million of dollar bonds still due in June 2029

Delhi International Airport Ltd has raised Rs 3,500 crore through 15-year rupee-denominated non-convertible debentures, according to people familiar with the development. The funds will refinance its $522.6 million of dollar notes maturing in October 2026.

The NCDs carry an initial coupon of 8.71% a year, payable quarterly. The coupon is linked to the RBI repo rate, set at an initial spread of 375 basis points, and will reset after five and 10 years over the 15-year tenure of the debentures.

The proceeds are earmarked for the full redemption, repurchase or refinancing of the $522.6 million 6.125% notes due in October 2026. They will also be used to meet issue-related costs.

India Infrastructure Finance Company Ltd (IIFCL) was the largest investor, subscribing Rs 1,426 crore. NIIF Infrastructure Finance invested Rs 550 crore, while HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and IDFC First Bank subscribed Rs 481 crore, Rs 433 crore and Rs 409 crore, respectively. SBI Capital Markets picked up Rs 200 crore.

HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank were the arrangers of the issue. The key information document names HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, IDFC First Bank and NIIF Infrastructure Finance as anchor investors, with a combined allocation of Rs 1,050 crore.

After refinancing the October 2026 maturity, DIAL will have $500 million of dollar bonds due in June 2029. Ratings agency ICRA has said the company could partly repay the 2029 bonds through internal cash accruals and land monetisation, and refinance the balance.

"DIAL has a moderately leveraged capital structure, with the presence of bullet repayments in the medium term, exposing the company to refinancing risk. It has major debt maturities due in October 2026 and June 2029" - ICRA.

DIAL is a joint venture between GMR Airports Ltd and Airports Authority of India. GMR Airports holds 74% and AAI 26%. The company operates Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport under a 30-year concession, extendable by another 30 years.
