Fractal Analytics Q4 Revenue Up 17% to ₹886 Cr; FY26 PAT Jumps 30%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Q4 FY26 revenue rises 17% YoY to ₹886 crore; gross margin improves to 48.2%; PAT more than doubles to ₹116 crore
  • 02/FY26 full-year revenue climbs 19% to ₹3,300 crore; net income jumps 30% to ₹287 crore; EBITDA margin expansion continues
  • 03/Healthcare & Life Sciences segment grew 78% YoY; company crosses 6 clients with $20M+ revenue; net revenue retention at 114%

Enterprise AI firm Fractal Analytics reported strong financial performance for Q4 and full year FY26 ended March 31, 2026. The company listed on BSE and NSE in February 2026.

For Q4 FY26, consolidated revenue rose 17% year-on-year to ₹886 crore. Gross margin improved to 48.2%. Net income more than doubled to ₹116 crore. This was supported by high net revenue retention and an industry-leading net promoter score.

For the full financial year FY26, revenue climbed 19% to ₹3,300 crore. Net income increased 30% to ₹287 crore. Margin expansion and deeper relationships with large clients underpinned profitability.

Segment performance was mixed. Strong gains came in healthcare, BFSI, and consumer-packaged goods. This was offset by weakness in telecom, media, and technology. Geographically, Europe and the Americas led growth.

The Healthcare & Life Sciences segment was the standout performer. It grew 78% year-on-year and accounted for 20% of revenue in Q3 FY26. The Banking and Financial Services segment grew 26% YoY and made up 12% of revenue.

The company deepened client relationships. Net Revenue Retention stood at 114% in Q3 FY26. The number of clients generating over $20 million in revenue increased by 2 YoY to 6. Clients generating over $1 million increased by 8 YoY to 58.

Fractal's consumer-targeted AI health assistant, Vaidya.ai 2.0, became the first AI model to achieve a 50+ score on OpenAI's HealthBench (Hard). This outperformed ChatGPT-5, GPT-5.2, and Gemini Pro 3.

PiEvolve, an evolutionary agentic engine, was ranked among top-performing agents on OpenAI's MLE-Bench. It outperformed agents from Google, Microsoft, and Meta.

Srikanth Velamakanni, Group CEO & Executive Vice-Chairman, said the company delivered a great quarter improving across nearly every metric. He said best-in-class organic growth, gross margins, and high client retention reflect the strength of enterprise AI capabilities.

The company invests over 5% of annual revenue in AI R&D. It was selected by the Government of India under the IndiaAI Mission to build the country's first Large Reasoning Model spanning up to 70 billion parameters.
