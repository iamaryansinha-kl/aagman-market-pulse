Vedanta Q4 Profit Nearly Doubles to ₹6,698 Crore on Metal Price Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit nearly doubles to ₹6,698 crore from ₹3,484 crore a year ago
  • 02/Higher LME aluminium, zinc and silver realisations drive the surge; 9-month FY26 revenue exceeds ₹120,000 crore
  • 03/Investors watch FY27 guidance, demerger timeline effective May 1, and cost control commentary

Vedanta Limited (NSE: VEDL) reported consolidated net profit of ₹6,698 crore for Q4 FY26. The quarter ended March 31, 2026. Profit nearly doubled from ₹3,484 crore in the year-ago period.

The board approved the results on April 29, 2026. The strong showing was driven by higher base metal prices across aluminium, zinc and silver.

Revenue for the nine-month period ended December 2025 exceeded ₹120,000 crore. Nine-month EBITDA stood at ₹37,529 crore.

In Q3 FY26, Vedanta posted record EBITDA of ₹15,171 crore. Revenue was ₹45,899 crore and PAT was ₹7,807 crore with a 41% margin.

Higher LME realisations for aluminium and zinc lifted segment profitability. Aluminium cost of production has fallen 37% across 14 consecutive quarters.

Alumina production at the Lanjigarh refinery jumped 104% year-on-year. This reduces dependence on volatile spot market procurement.

Brokerages had projected Q4 PAT between ₹7,935 crore and ₹9,550 crore. The actual ₹6,698 crore print came below consensus but still marks a sharp operational recovery.

The demerger scheme for Vedanta's businesses is set to be effective May 1, 2026. Management aims to grow aluminium EBITDA from ~USD 2 billion to ~USD 4 billion.

Vedanta shares have gained over 76% in the past year. The stock trades at a P/E of roughly 17.5x.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS VEDANTA'S Q4 FY26 CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT?
VEDANTA LIMITED REPORTED CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT OF ₹6,698 CRORE FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2026. THIS COMPARES WITH ₹3,484 CRORE IN Q4 FY25, REPRESENTING A NEAR-DOUBLING OF PROFIT YEAR-ON-YEAR.
WHAT DROVE THE SURGE IN VEDANTA'S Q4 PROFIT?
HIGHER BASE METAL PRICES, PARTICULARLY LME-LINKED REALISATIONS FOR ALUMINIUM, ZINC AND SILVER, DROVE THE PROFIT SURGE. OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY GAINS INCLUDING A 37% REDUCTION IN ALUMINIUM COST OF PRODUCTION OVER 14 QUARTERS ALSO SUPPORTED MARGINS.
HOW DOES Q4 FY26 COMPARE TO BROKERAGE ESTIMATES?
BROKERAGES INCLUDING MOTILAL OSWAL AND KOTAK INSTITUTIONAL EQUITIES HAD PROJECTED Q4 PAT BETWEEN ₹7,935 CRORE AND ₹9,550 CRORE. THE ACTUAL ₹6,698 CRORE CAME BELOW CONSENSUS BUT STILL MARKS A 92% YEAR-ON-YEAR IMPROVEMENT.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE VEDANTA DEMERGER?
THE DEMERGER SCHEME FOR VEDANTA'S BUSINESSES IS SET TO BECOME EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2026. A SEPARATELY LISTED ALUMINIUM ENTITY COULD ATTRACT PURE-PLAY METALS INVESTORS AND POTENTIALLY COMMAND A HIGHER VALUATION MULTIPLE.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH NEXT?
INVESTORS SHOULD MONITOR FY27 VOLUME GUIDANCE, MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY ON COST OF PRODUCTION TRENDS, NET DEBT LEVELS, AND THE ALUMINIUM EBITDA GROWTH TRAJECTORY TOWARD MANAGEMENT'S USD 4 BILLION TARGET.
