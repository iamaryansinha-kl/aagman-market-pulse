Emirates Restores 96% Global Network with 137 Destinations, 1,300 Flights

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Emirates restores 96% of global network, operating 1,300 weekly flights to 137 destinations across 72 countries
  • 02/Operations suspended February 28 after US-Israeli strikes on Iran; UAE airspace reopened May 2
  • 03/Capacity at 75% of pre-disruption levels; 4.7 million passengers carried between March 1 and April 30

Emirates announced on May 4 that 96% of its global network is restored. The Dubai-based carrier now operates over 1,300 weekly flights.

The airline flies to 137 destinations across 72 countries. This represents approximately 75% of its pre-disruption capacity.

Emirates suspended all operations on February 28, 2026. The shutdown followed US-Israeli military strikes against Iran.

The UAE aviation authority lifted airspace restrictions on May 2. Dubai Airports has entered what it calls the next phase of recovery.

Between March 1 and April 30, Emirates carried 4.7 million passengers. The figure demonstrates sustained demand despite the disruption.

Dubai International Airport handled 18.6 million passengers in Q1 2026. That was down 20% year-on-year due to the February shutdown.

March traffic at DXB collapsed to 2.5 million passengers. The figure represents a 65.7% decline compared to March 2025.

Emirates offers complimentary date changes and 24-hour fare holds. European rivals remain cautious, with Air France and Lufthansa suspending Dubai flights until May 10 and May 31.
