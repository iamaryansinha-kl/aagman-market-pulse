UP RERA Approves 12 Realty Projects Worth Rs 1,664 Crore Across Six Districts, Lucknow Leads

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority (UP RERA) has approved 12 new real estate projects across six districts, involving an estimated investment of Rs 1,663.85 crore.

The projects were approved at UP RERA's 214th authority meeting chaired by chairman Sanjay Bhoosreddy. They will comprise 3,090 residential and commercial units.

Lucknow Leads

Lucknow accounts for the highest investment and the largest number of units. The city has four projects: two residential, one commercial and one mixed-use. These involve an estimated investment of Rs 609.35 crore and 1,972 units.

Ghaziabad follows with three residential projects involving Rs 605.06 crore and 574 residential units.

Hapur has two residential projects with an estimated investment of Rs 123.45 crore and 192 units.

Three More Districts

Ayodhya, Prayagraj and Kanpur have one project each. The Ayodhya project involves Rs 72.61 crore and 85 residential units. The Prayagraj project is worth Rs 51.66 crore with 109 units. The Kanpur project involves Rs 201.72 crore and 158 units.

Boost for Construction Activity

UP RERA said the projects would boost construction activity and create opportunities in allied sectors such as construction materials, engineering and transport.

The authority said it would focus on ensuring compliance with the RERA Act, timely completion and protection of homebuyers' interests.

Bhoosreddy said transparent and systematic development of the real estate sector, along with timely approval and monitoring of projects, remained a priority for the authority.
