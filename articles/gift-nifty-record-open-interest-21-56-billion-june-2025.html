GIFT Nifty Hits Record $21.56 Billion Open Interest on June 25

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/GIFT Nifty recorded an all-time high open interest of 446,150 contracts valued at $21.56 billion on June 25, 2026
  • 02/The milestone surpassed the previous record of 410,100 contracts worth $21.23 billion set on October 24, 2025
  • 03/Cumulative turnover on NSE International Exchange has exceeded $3.21 trillion since operations began in July 2023

GIFT Nifty hit a record open interest of $21.56 billion on June 25, 2026. The milestone reflects rising global investor participation in India's offshore derivatives market.

The contract count reached an all-time high of 446,150. This surpassed the previous record of 410,100 contracts worth $21.23 billion set on October 24, 2025.

The record open interest translates to approximately Rs 2.04 lakh crore. It signals sustained confidence in GIFT Nifty as a preferred offshore trading platform.

Trading activity on NSE International Exchange (NSE IX) has grown rapidly. The platform commenced full-scale operations on July 3, 2023.

Since then, cumulative trading volume has crossed 69.56 million contracts. Total cumulative turnover has exceeded $3.21 trillion as of June 25, 2026.

NSE IX currently commands over 99.6% market share within the International Financial Services Centre (IFSC). The exchange is based at GIFT City, Gujarat.

"We are glad to witness the success of GIFT Nifty and express our sincere gratitude to all the participants for their overwhelming support," the exchange said in a statement.

The exchange has secured regulatory exemptions from the U.S. CFTC and relief from the U.S. SEC. This enables eligible U.S. investors to participate in derivatives listed on NSE IX.
