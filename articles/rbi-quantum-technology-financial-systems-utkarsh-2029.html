RBI Utkarsh 2029: Quantum Technology Framework for Financial Systems

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI's Utkarsh 2029 strategy framework includes developing frameworks for AI and quantum technology in the financial sector
  • 02/Quantum computing poses threat to existing cryptographic methods securing financial transactions, says RBI Deputy Governor
  • 03/Framework runs April 2026 to March 2029 across six pillars including effective technology and future-ready organisation

The Reserve Bank of India has unveiled its medium-term strategy framework 'Utkarsh 2029'. The framework covers the period from April 2026 to March 2029.

RBI seeks to develop frameworks for use of emerging technologies such as AI and quantum in the financial sector. This falls under the 'effective technology' pillar.

The central bank will examine how quantum technology can strengthen financial systems. It also addresses risks from quantum computing to existing cryptography.

Deputy Governor Michael Debabrata Patra highlighted the threat at a SAARCFINANCE seminar in January 2024. He noted quantum computers can rapidly perform code-breaking calculations.

Current cryptographic methods that secure financial transactions face growing vulnerability. Quantum computing could compromise encryption protecting banking data.

The framework also covers expanding CBDC for cross-border payments. It will scale up the Unified Lending Interface to improve credit access.

Under Utkarsh 2029, RBI aims to promote responsible innovation. It will build cyber risk capacity and set up Digital and AI sandboxes for regulated entities.

The bank also plans to advance Project Sa-Mudra to modernise currency management. All internal processes will be end-to-end digitalised under the framework.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE RBI'S UTKARSH 2029 FRAMEWORK?
UTKARSH 2029 IS THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA'S MEDIUM-TERM STRATEGY FRAMEWORK COVERING APRIL 2026 TO MARCH 2029. IT IS BASED ON SIX PILLARS INCLUDING ROBUST REGULATIONS, CUSTOMER CENTRICITY, COMPETITIVE MARKETS, EFFECTIVE TECHNOLOGY, FUTURE-READY ORGANISATION AND GLOBAL INDIA.
HOW DOES QUANTUM TECHNOLOGY FEATURE IN RBI'S UTKARSH 2029?
RBI SEEKS TO DEVELOP FRAMEWORKS FOR USE OF EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES SUCH AS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND QUANTUM COMPUTING IN THE FINANCIAL SECTOR. THE CENTRAL BANK WILL EXAMINE HOW QUANTUM TECHNOLOGY CAN STRENGTHEN FINANCIAL SYSTEMS WHILE ADDRESSING ASSOCIATED RISKS.
WHAT CYBERSECURITY RISK DOES QUANTUM COMPUTING POSE TO FINANCIAL SYSTEMS?
QUANTUM COMPUTING CAN RAPIDLY PERFORM CODE-BREAKING CALCULATIONS. THIS CREATES GROWING CONCERN ABOUT THE VULNERABILITY OF EXISTING CRYPTOGRAPHIC METHODS THAT SECURE FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS AND BANKING DATA.
WHAT OTHER INITIATIVES ARE INCLUDED IN UTKARSH 2029?
THE FRAMEWORK PRIORITISES EXPANDING CENTRAL BANK DIGITAL CURRENCY FOR CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS, SCALING THE UNIFIED LENDING INTERFACE, AND ADVANCING PROJECT SA-MUDRA TO MODERNISE CURRENCY MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS.
WHAT IS THE TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF UTKARSH 2029?
THE UTKARSH 2029 FRAMEWORK COVERS THE PERIOD FROM APRIL 2026 TO MARCH 2029. RBI WILL PROGRESSIVELY IMPLEMENT INITIATIVES ACROSS ALL SIX PILLARS DURING THIS THREE-YEAR PERIOD.
