Rupee Falls 41 Paise to Close at 94.56 Against US Dollar

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee falls 41 paise to close at 94.56 against the US dollar on April 28, hitting an intraday low of 94.58
  • 02/Brent crude surged past $111 per barrel and FIIs have pulled out over $19 billion from Indian equities this year
  • 03/Rupee may weaken further if West Asia conflict escalates, though FY27 baseline sees 92-93 range if crude averages $85-90

The rupee depreciated 41 paise to close at 94.56 against the US dollar on April 28. The currency opened at 94.35 and touched an intraday low of 94.58 during the session.

On Monday, the rupee had gained just one paisa to settle at 94.15. Tuesday's decline erased those marginal gains completely.

Forex traders said the rupee has weakened sharply, factoring in a likely widening of the Current Account Deficit. Volatile capital flows continue to pressure the currency.

Foreign institutional investors have pulled out over $19 billion from Indian equities so far this year. They offloaded ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday alone.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, surged 2.97 per cent to $111.4 per barrel. Elevated oil prices inflate India's import bill and trade deficit.

"The primary pressure on the rupee stems from weak capital inflows. Net FDI inflows have been subdued, while FPI flows have been affected by elevated global uncertainty," said Rajani Sinha, Chief Economist, CareEdge Ratings.

The dollar index rose 0.24 per cent to 98.73, adding to the rupee's woes. Meanwhile, the Sensex dropped 416.72 points to 76,886.91.

"In a downside scenario where the conflict escalates and crude oil prices rise further, there would be further weakening pressure on the rupee," Sinha added.
