India Power Transmission to Get ₹9 Lakh Crore Capex Infusion by 2032

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's power transmission sector is set to receive ₹9 lakh crore in capital expenditure by 2032 per the National Electricity Plan
  • 02/The plan targets 168 GW inter-regional capacity, 1,91,474 ckm transmission lines, and 1,274 GVA transformation capacity
  • 03/Execution risk remains the key monitorable given past delays in transmission projects tied to land acquisition and approvals

India's power transmission sector is set for a massive overhaul. The National Electricity Plan targets ₹9 lakh crore in capital expenditure by 2032.

The plan envisions adding 1,91,474 circuit kilometers of transmission lines. This will strengthen the national grid backbone significantly.

An additional 1,274 GVA of transformation capacity will be installed. This supports the evacuation of renewable energy from resource-rich states.

The inter-regional transmission capacity target is set at 168 GW by 2032. This enables power flow from surplus regions to deficit centers.

Renewable integration is the primary driver behind this expansion. Solar and wind projects in Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu need evacuation infrastructure.

Central and state transmission utilities will share the execution burden. Power Grid Corporation of India is expected to lead the central transmission push.

Land acquisition and right-of-way clearance remain chronic bottlenecks. Past projects have faced multi-year delays due to local opposition and forest clearances.

Private sector participation has been limited in interstate transmission. The government may need to revisit tariff incentives to attract private capital.

The ₹9 lakh crore outlay includes greenfield lines, substations, and HVDC corridors. It is among the largest infrastructure commitments in India's power sector history.

THE NATIONAL ELECTRICITY PLAN TARGETS ₹9 LAKH CRORE IN CAPITAL EXPENDITURE FOR THE POWER TRANSMISSION SECTOR BY THE YEAR 2032.
WHAT IS THE TARGET FOR INTER-REGIONAL TRANSMISSION CAPACITY BY 2032?
THE PLAN TARGETS 168 GW OF INTER-REGIONAL TRANSMISSION CAPACITY BY 2032 TO ENABLE POWER FLOW BETWEEN SURPLUS AND DEFICIT REGIONS.
HOW MUCH TRANSMISSION LINE LENGTH AND TRANSFORMATION CAPACITY IS PLANNED?
THE PLAN ENVISIONS ADDING 1,91,474 CIRCUIT KILOMETERS OF TRANSMISSION LINES AND 1,274 GVA OF TRANSFORMATION CAPACITY BY 2032.
WHY IS THIS TRANSMISSION EXPANSION NEEDED?
THE PRIMARY DRIVER IS RENEWABLE ENERGY INTEGRATION. SOLAR AND WIND PROJECTS IN STATES LIKE RAJASTHAN AND GUJARAT NEED ROBUST EVACUATION INFRASTRUCTURE TO SUPPLY POWER TO DEMAND CENTERS.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS TO THIS PLAN?
LAND ACQUISITION, RIGHT-OF-WAY CLEARANCE, AND FOREST APPROVALS HAVE CAUSED MULTI-YEAR DELAYS IN PAST TRANSMISSION PROJECTS. PRIVATE SECTOR PARTICIPATION ALSO REMAINS LIMITED.
