Balmer Lawrie Shares Crash 6% as Board Rejects Bonus, Buyback, Split Plans

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Balmer Lawrie & Company board rejects bonus issue, stock split and share buyback at May 17 meeting, citing DIPAM guidelines
  • 02/Shares tumble over 6% to Rs 182.64; company cancelled identical proposals in May 2025 for the same reason
  • 03/Board recommends Rs 4.25 per share dividend for FY26; Q4 net profit rises 12% YoY to Rs 84.5 crore

Balmer Lawrie & Company Ltd (NSE: BALMLAWRIE) board rejected plans for a bonus issue, stock split and share buyback. The decision was taken at a board meeting held on May 17, 2026.

The Kolkata-headquartered Mini Ratna PSU cited compliance with Ministry of Finance DIPAM guidelines. These rules govern capital restructuring for Central Public Sector Enterprises.

This marks the second consecutive year the company has shelved such proposals. Balmer Lawrie cancelled identical plans in May 2025 for similar reasons.

Balmer Lawrie shares crashed over 6% to trade at Rs 182.64 on Monday. The stock had hit a 52-week low of Rs 148.36 in March 2026.

The board recommended a final dividend of Rs 4.25 per share for FY26. The record date will be announced later. Shareholders must approve it at the AGM.

For Q4 FY26, consolidated net profit rose 12% YoY to Rs 84.5 crore. Revenue from operations jumped 22% to Rs 744 crore.

Full year FY26 net income reached Rs 2,784.60 crore, up 8.03%. PAT increased 5.53% to Rs 245.68 crore for the financial year.

The stock remains down over 23% from its September 2025 peak of Rs 238. It has gained 49% over the past three years.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY DID BALMER LAWRIE'S BOARD REJECT THE BONUS ISSUE, STOCK SPLIT AND BUYBACK?
THE BOARD CITED COMPLIANCE WITH THE CONSOLIDATED GUIDELINES ON CAPITAL RESTRUCTURING OF CPSES ISSUED BY THE MINISTRY OF FINANCE, DEPARTMENT OF INVESTMENT AND PUBLIC ASSET MANAGEMENT (DIPAM).
WHAT DIVIDEND HAS BALMER LAWRIE RECOMMENDED FOR FY2026?
THE BOARD HAS RECOMMENDED A FINAL DIVIDEND OF RS 4.25 PER SHARE FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDING MARCH 31, 2026, SUBJECT TO SHAREHOLDERS' APPROVAL AT THE AGM.
WHAT WAS BALMER LAWRIE'S Q4 FY2026 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE?
CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT FOR Q4 FY2026 ROSE 12% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO RS 84.5 CRORE. REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS GREW 22% TO RS 744 CRORE.
HOW DID BALMER LAWRIE SHARES REACT TO THE BOARD DECISION?
SHARES TUMBLED OVER 6% TO RS 182.64 ON MAY 18, 2026. THE STOCK IS DOWN OVER 23% FROM ITS 52-WEEK HIGH OF RS 238 HIT IN SEPTEMBER 2025.
HAS BALMER LAWRIE REJECTED THESE PROPOSALS BEFORE?
YES. THE COMPANY CANCELLED IDENTICAL PROPOSALS FOR A BONUS ISSUE, STOCK SPLIT AND SHARE BUYBACK IN MAY 2025, CITING THE SAME DIPAM GUIDELINES.
