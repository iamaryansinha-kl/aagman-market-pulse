Crude Oil Prices Crash 42% From $126 Peak: Brent Below $73

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent crude plunges 42% from its April 30 peak of $126 per barrel to $72.40 as tanker traffic resumes through the Strait of Hormuz
  • 02/Macquarie Group slashes 2026 Brent forecast to $77 from $89 and 2027 outlook to $64 from $74 per barrel
  • 03/Near-term volatility likely as shipowners remain cautious; Saudi Aramco CEO warns stability may not return until 2027

Brent crude has crashed 42% from its April 30 peak of $126 per barrel. On Thursday, the global benchmark fell below $73 for the first time since February 27, 2026.

Brent futures for August delivery settled at $72.40 per barrel, down $1.40 or 2%. U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude slipped to $69 per barrel, falling $1.12 or 1.6%.

The sharp decline follows an interim peace agreement between the United States and Iran last week. Tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz is gradually returning to pre-war levels.

The Strait of Hormuz carries roughly 20% of global crude supplies. Its closure in February triggered months of supply fears and extreme price volatility.

Macquarie Group has slashed its Brent forecast for 2026 to $77 per barrel from $89 previously. Its 2027 outlook was cut to $64 from $74.

"The market is substantially underestimating the pace of recovery and the ability of the oil market to heal itself," Macquarie strategists Peter Taylor and Vikas Dwivedi wrote in a Tuesday note.

However, Saudi Aramco CEO Amin Nasser cautioned last month that disruptions could delay a return to stability until 2027. Some shipowners also remain wary of operating conditions in the Persian Gulf.

Indian oil-importing companies are benefiting from the crash. Shares of IndiGo and SpiceJet rallied up to 4% as crude prices fell below pre-war levels.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH HAVE CRUDE OIL PRICES FALLEN FROM THEIR APRIL PEAK?
BRENT CRUDE HAS PLUNGED 42% FROM ITS APRIL 30 PEAK OF $126 PER BARREL TO $72.40 PER BARREL AS OF JUNE 25, 2026. WTI CRUDE HAS FALLEN TO $69 PER BARREL.
WHAT CAUSED THE SHARP DECLINE IN OIL PRICES?
THE DECLINE FOLLOWS AN INTERIM PEACE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND IRAN, WHICH HAS ALLOWED TANKER TRAFFIC TO RESUME THROUGH THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ. THE STRAIT CARRIES APPROXIMATELY 20% OF GLOBAL CRUDE SUPPLIES.
WHAT ARE MACQUARIE GROUP'S LATEST OIL PRICE FORECASTS?
MACQUARIE HAS CUT ITS 2026 BRENT FORECAST TO $77 PER BARREL FROM $89 PREVIOUSLY. ITS 2027 OUTLOOK WAS REDUCED TO $64 PER BARREL FROM $74.
WHY DOES SAUDI ARAMCO WARN THAT STABILITY MAY NOT RETURN UNTIL 2027?
SAUDI ARAMCO CEO AMIN NASSER CAUTIONED THAT REOPENING THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ FULLY REQUIRES COORDINATING VESSEL MOVEMENTS, RESTARTING OIL WELLS, REPAIRING INFRASTRUCTURE, AND DE-MINING OPERATIONS. SOME SHIPOWNERS REMAIN WARY OF THE REGION.
HOW DOES THE OIL PRICE CRASH IMPACT INDIA?
AS A MAJOR OIL-IMPORTING NATION, INDIA BENEFITS FROM LOWER ENERGY IMPORT BILLS. AVIATION STOCKS HAVE ALREADY REACTED POSITIVELY, WITH INDIGO AND SPICEJET SHARES RALLYING UP TO 4% FOLLOWING THE CRUDE PRICE DECLINE.
