PFC-REC Merger Approved: 88:100 Share Swap Ratio For Mega PSU Merger

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PFC and REC boards approved merger with 88 PFC shares for every 100 REC shares on June 28, 2026
  • 02/Combined entity will hold over ₹11 lakh crore in loans, creating India's largest power-sector financier
  • 03/Record date yet to be fixed; scheme requires SEBI, NCLT, and shareholder approvals before becoming effective

Power Finance Corporation Ltd (NSE: PFC) and Rural Electrification Corporation Ltd (NSE: REC) boards approved their merger scheme on Sunday, June 28, 2026. The share-swap ratio is fixed at 88 PFC shares for every 100 REC shares held.

The companies announced this in an exchange filing on June 29. Both are public-sector undertakings (PSUs) under the Ministry of Power.

An REC shareholder holding 100 shares as of the record date will receive 88 PFC shares. Her existing 100 REC shares will be cancelled upon completion.

The record date to determine shareholder eligibility is yet to be ascertained. The scheme now requires approvals from shareholders, stock exchanges, SEBI, the NCLT, and other statutory authorities.

The merged entity will command a combined loan book exceeding ₹11 lakh crore. This makes it India's largest power-sector financing institution by a significant margin.

PFC currently owns a 52.63% stake in REC. The Government of India holds 55.99% in PFC but does not directly own a stake in REC.

The merger plan was first signalled in February 2026. Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said the government would restructure PFC and REC to streamline operations. Both stocks had jumped sharply on that announcement.

REC shares closed at ₹364.65 on June 25, 2026, up 0.28%. The merger has been in the works since PFC acquired the government's stake in REC.

"For fresh money, the prudent approach is to wait for the swap ratio or accumulate only in tranches," said Harshal Dasani, Business Head at INVasset PMS. He noted that PFC looks better suited for conservative exposure, while REC is more of a merger-arbitrage call with higher event risk.

The core business remains structurally supported by power capex, transmission financing, and renewable energy projects. However, analysts caution that valuations have already priced in a fair amount of that cycle.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE SHARE-SWAP RATIO FOR THE PFC-REC MERGER?
THE APPROVED SHARE-SWAP RATIO IS 88 EQUITY SHARES OF PFC OF ₹10 EACH FULLY PAID UP FOR EVERY 100 EQUITY SHARES OF REC OF ₹10 EACH. AN REC SHAREHOLDER WITH 100 SHARES WILL RECEIVE 88 PFC SHARES.
WHAT IS THE RECORD DATE FOR THE PFC-REC MERGER?
THE RECORD DATE TO DETERMINE ELIGIBILITY OF SHAREHOLDERS FOR THE MERGER SCHEME IS YET TO BE FIXED. IT WILL BE ANNOUNCED AFTER REGULATORY APPROVALS ARE SECURED.
WHAT WILL BE THE SIZE OF THE MERGED PFC-REC ENTITY?
THE MERGED ENTITY WILL HAVE A COMBINED LOAN BOOK EXCEEDING ₹11 LAKH CRORE, MAKING IT INDIA'S LARGEST POWER-SECTOR FINANCING INSTITUTION.
WHAT APPROVALS ARE STILL NEEDED FOR THE MERGER TO COMPLETE?
THE SCHEME REQUIRES APPROVALS FROM SHAREHOLDERS, STOCK EXCHANGES, SEBI, THE NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL (NCLT), AND OTHER STATUTORY AUTHORITIES BEFORE BECOMING EFFECTIVE.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS IN PFC AND REC DO NOW?
ANALYSTS ADVISE STAGGERED INVESTMENT RATHER THAN CHASING HEADLINES. PFC IS VIEWED AS BETTER SUITED FOR CONSERVATIVE EXPOSURE, WHILE REC CARRIES HIGHER EVENT RISK AS A MERGER-ARBITRAGE PLAY. WAIT FOR REGULATORY CLARITY AND THE RECORD DATE BEFORE TAKING FRESH POSITIONS.
