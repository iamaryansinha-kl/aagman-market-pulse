Waaree Energies Stock Drops 10% Despite Q4 Profit Surging 71%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Waaree Energies shares plunged nearly 10% to Rs 3,165.20 on April 30, despite Q4 FY26 net profit surging 71% YoY to Rs 1,061 crore
  • 02/Revenue from operations more than doubled to Rs 8,480 crore, but EBITDA margin contracted sharply to 18.6% from around 23% previously
  • 03/Nomura maintains BUY with a target of Rs 3,750, noting revenue beat estimates by 12% but EBITDA missed by 11%

Waaree Energies Ltd (NSE: WAAREEENER) shares crashed nearly 10% in early trade on April 30, 2026. The stock opened at a gap-down of 6.14% and slid to Rs 3,165.20 from the previous close of Rs 3,502.90.

The sharp fall came just hours after the company reported robust Q4 FY26 earnings. Consolidated net profit jumped 71% year-on-year to Rs 1,061 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Revenue from operations surged 112% YoY to Rs 8,480 crore. The top-line growth was driven by higher module volumes across India, the US, and Europe.

However, investor sentiment turned negative on margin pressure. EBITDA rose 80% YoY to Rs 1,577 crore, but the EBITDA margin contracted to 18.6% from approximately 23% in the prior quarter.

Brokerage firm Nomura maintained a BUY rating on the stock with a target price of Rs 3,750. Nomura noted that revenue beat both its and consensus estimates by 12%, aided by strong volume growth.

On the profitability front, EBITDA missed Nomura's estimate by 11% and consensus by 8%. The miss was attributed to a sharp contraction in gross margins during the quarter.

The company also announced a dividend of Rs 2 per share and a strategic foray into the semiconductor space. The board approved a Rs 10,000 crore fundraising plan to support expansion and diversification.

Waaree Energies shares are currently trading at 16x FY27F and 13x FY28F EBITDA. The stock remains above its 50-day, 100-day, and 200-day moving averages despite the near-term correction.
