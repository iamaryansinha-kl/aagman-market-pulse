5 Brokerage Stock Picks for 2026: Ambuja, SRF, Dixon Target 15-23% Upside

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Five stocks across cement, electronics, chemicals, cables and B2B e-commerce get fresh brokerage buy ratings
  • 02/Target prices range from Rs 494 to Rs 14,200 with upside potential between 15% and 23%
  • 03/Elara Securities, JM Financial, Motilal Oswal and HDFC Securities issued the recommendations on June 26, 2026

Top brokerage firms have issued fresh buy recommendations on five stocks across multiple sectors. The picks span cement, electronics manufacturing, specialty chemicals, cables and B2B e-commerce.

Elara Securities has maintained an 'Accumulate' rating on Ambuja Cements Ltd (NSE: AMBUJACEM). The brokerage has set a target price of Rs 494 against the current market price of Rs 426. This implies a potential upside of 15%.

JM Financial has reiterated its 'Buy' rating on Dixon Technologies (NSE: DIXON). The target price stands at Rs 14,200 compared to the last traded price of Rs 12,090. The expected upside is 17%.

Motilal Oswal has maintained a 'Buy' recommendation on SRF Ltd (NSE: SRF). The brokerage sees the stock reaching Rs 3,350 from its current price of Rs 2,766. This represents a potential upside of 21%.

Motilal Oswal has also reiterated a 'Buy' on Polycab India Ltd (NSE: POLYCAB). The target price is Rs 11,950 versus the current market price of Rs 9,689. The upside potential here is 23%, the highest among the five picks.

HDFC Securities has maintained a 'Buy' rating on IndiaMART InterMESH (NSE: INDIAMART). The target price is Rs 2,400 against the last traded price of Rs 1,971. The brokerage expects a 21% upside.

The recommendations come at a time when brokerages remain constructive across sectors. However, target prices are based on current market conditions and assumptions about earnings growth.

Investors should note that these are analyst estimates and not guarantees of returns. Market conditions, sector headwinds and company-specific risks can impact actual performance.
