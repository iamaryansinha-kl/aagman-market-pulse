Axis Bank Q4 Results: Stock Drops 5%, Net Profit Slips to ₹7,071 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Axis Bank standalone net profit declined 0.6% YoY to ₹7,071 crore in Q4 FY26, beating the CNBC-TV18 poll estimate of ₹6,989 crore but missing margin expectations.
  • 02/The stock crashed nearly 5% to an intraday low of ₹1,299.90 on April 27, 2026, leading Nifty 50 losers from the previous close of ₹1,365.90.
  • 03/Brokerages issued mixed ratings: Elara Capital and JM Financial maintain Buy with targets of ₹1,629 and ₹1,575, while Motilal Oswal stays Neutral at ₹1,475.

Axis Bank Ltd (NSE: AXISBANK) reported standalone net profit of ₹7,071 crore for Q4 FY26 on April 25, 2026. The figure was marginally below ₹7,117.50 crore in the year-ago quarter, a decline of 0.6%.

The stock plunged nearly 5% on April 27, 2026, hitting an intraday low of ₹1,299.90. It had closed at ₹1,365.90 on April 25, 2026, and was the biggest loser in the Nifty 50 index.

Net interest income rose 5% year-on-year to ₹14,457 crore. Net interest margin stood at 3.62% for the quarter, though brokerages noted sequential compression.

Total provisions surged to ₹3,522 crore from ₹1,359 crore a year ago. The bank created an additional one-time provision of ₹2,001 crore for macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties.

Asset quality improved sequentially. Gross NPA fell to 1.23% from 1.40% in Q3 FY26, while net NPA declined to 0.37% from 0.42%.

Advances grew 19% year-on-year to ₹12.34 lakh crore. Deposits rose 14% YoY, with the CASA ratio holding steady at 40%.

"While we enter the new financial year with confidence and optimism, we are conscious of the global macro and geopolitical situation," said Amitabh Chaudhry, Managing Director and CEO.

Elara Capital maintained a Buy rating with a target price of ₹1,629. JM Financial also kept Buy, raising its target to ₹1,575, while Motilal Oswal retained Neutral at ₹1,475.
