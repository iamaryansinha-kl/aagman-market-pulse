Sebi Resolves 5,548 Investor Complaints in May via SCORES 2.0 Platform

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi resolved 5,548 investor complaints in May 2026 through its upgraded SCORES 2.0 platform, exceeding fresh inflows
  • 02/Pending complaints dropped from 6,167 to 5,537; only 10 cases remain unresolved beyond three months
  • 03/Entities took an average of 5 days to submit action reports in April; first-level reviews averaged 8 days

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) disposed of 5,548 investor complaints in May 2026. The resolution came through its online grievance redressal platform SCORES 2.0.

The regulator received 4,918 fresh complaints during the month. Despite the inflow, Sebi managed to reduce the overall backlog.

Pending complaints declined from 6,167 to 5,537 as of May 31, 2026. This marks a net reduction of 630 cases in a single month.

Only 10 complaints remained pending for over three months. These involved entities including KFin Technologies Pvt Ltd, Secur Credentials Ltd, and Eastern Investments Ltd.

Entities took an average of five days to submit action taken reports in April. The average resolution time for first-level review complaints stood at eight days.

Under SCORES 2.0, complaints are auto-forwarded to entities concerned. They get 21 days to submit action taken reports to investors.

Investors can seek a first-level review within 15 days if dissatisfied. A second-level review can be filed within another 15 days, after which Sebi directly examines the matter.

Sebi clarified that pending figures include cases where entities submitted action reports within time. These remain open to allow investors to seek review if unsatisfied with responses.
