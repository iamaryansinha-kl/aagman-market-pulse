Sensex Nifty Close Higher on April 29: Sensex Up 609 Pts to 77,496, Nifty at 24,178

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex surged 609.45 points or 0.79% to settle at 77,496.36 while NSE Nifty 50 gained 181.95 points or 0.76% to close at 24,177.65 on April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Stock-specific gains after quarterly earnings offset concerns over higher Brent crude prices and the US Fed policy decision due later in the day.
  • 03/The rupee hit a record closing low of 94.8450 per US dollar, down 0.3%, as oil prices surged amid Iran conflict and foreign outflows continued.

Indian equity benchmarks closed firmly higher on April 29, 2026. The BSE Sensex settled at 77,496.36, up 609.45 points or 0.79% from its previous close of 76,886.91.

The broader NSE Nifty 50 index advanced 181.95 points or 0.76% to end at 24,177.65. Gains were driven by stock-specific action following quarterly earnings announcements.

Among Nifty 50 constituents, ITC, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra, Coal India and M&M led the gainers. IndiGo, Dr Reddy's Laboratories, JSW Steel and ICICI Bank were the top laggards.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd shares rose over 4% after reporting Q4 results. Revenue from operations came in at ₹52,449.3 crore, up 29% year-on-year. EBITDA stood at ₹6,156.9 crore, up 44.4% YoY.

Brokerages maintained positive outlook on Maruti. Morgan Stanley maintained Overweight with a target price of ₹17,895. Goldman Sachs kept its Buy rating with a target of ₹15,800. Motilal Oswal maintained Buy with target ₹15,529.

Broader markets also saw buying interest, with small-cap stocks outperforming midcaps and rising around 1%. On the sectoral front, realty and FMCG stocks led the gains, each advancing over 2%.

The rupee closed at a record low of 94.8450 per US dollar, down 0.3%. The currency came under pressure from surging oil prices amid the Iran conflict and continued foreign portfolio outflows.

Brent crude oil futures were at $104.02, down 0.36%, while WTI futures traded at $99.32, down 0.61%. Crude prices slipped slightly after the UAE announced its exit from OPEC effective May 1.

Foreign institutional investors sold ₹2,104 crore in equities on April 28, while domestic institutional investors bought ₹1,712 crore. The US Federal Reserve is widely expected to hold rates steady at 3.5%-3.75% later today.
