India-UAE Trade Crosses $100 Billion on CEPA Pact: Goyal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India-UAE bilateral trade reached $101.25 billion in 2025-26, crossing the $100 billion mark for the first time.
  • 02/The CEPA pact, which came into force in May 2022, has driven growth in gems, engineering goods, electronics, and agriculture.
  • 03/India's exports to the UAE rose 2% to $37.36 billion, but the trade deficit widened to $26.53 billion.

India's bilateral trade with the United Arab Emirates has crossed $100 billion. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal announced the milestone on May 1, 2026.

The Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) came into force in May 2022. It has acted as a gateway to Africa, GCC nations, the Middle East, CIS countries, and parts of Europe.

Bilateral merchandise trade stood at $101.25 billion in 2025-26. This compares to $100.03 billion in 2024-25.

Services trade is also rising steadily, Goyal noted in a social media post. The pact is opening new markets and creating greater value for Indian exporters.

During 2025-26, India's exports to the UAE rose about 2% to $37.36 billion. Imports from the UAE were up 0.77% to $63.89 billion.

The trade deficit stands at $26.53 billion. Key growth sectors include gems and jewellery, engineering goods, electronics, and agriculture.

Foreign direct investment from the UAE into India stood at $2.45 billion during April-December 2025-26. This compares to $4.34 billion in the full year 2024-25.

"Bilateral merchandise trade has grown strongly, crossing $100 billion, with services trade also rising steadily, opening new markets and greater value," Goyal said.
