Marcellus, VanEck Launch Global Multi-Asset Strategy Via GIFT City

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Marcellus Investment Managers has tied up with US asset manager VanEck to offer global multi-asset strategies to Indian investors
  • 02/The strategy spans global equity ETFs, bond ETFs, commodities and real asset ETFs, structured via Marcellus' GIFT City IFSC arm
  • 03/Marcellus GIFT City already manages over $75 million across more than 1,000 investors through global investing structures

Saurabh Mukherjea-led Marcellus Investment Managers has tied up with US-based investment manager VanEck. The partnership widens global investment offerings for Indian investors, taking Marcellus beyond its existing global equities product.

The new multi-asset strategy includes global equity ETFs, bond ETFs, commodities and real asset ETFs. VanEck provides model portfolio recommendations, while the strategy itself is structured and managed by Marcellus.

For an HNI portfolio, a global multi-asset sleeve does a specific job. It diversifies across asset classes and currencies, reducing dependence on Indian equities, Indian bonds and the rupee at the same time.

Access for Indian investors runs through Marcellus' GIFT City IFSC branch. The branch is regulated by the International Financial Services Centres Authority, or IFSCA.

The structure uses a Category III AIF feeder format into the master fund. This detail matters, because the feeder route keeps the entire investment inside a regulated, tax-efficient onshore framework rather than an offshore account.

This feeder architecture is the core of the GIFT City pitch. The investor deals with a domestic, IFSCA-regulated entity, while the capital is deployed into VanEck-recommended global exposures through the master fund.

Marcellus' GIFT City platform is not a new experiment. It has built more than $75 million in assets across over 1,000 investors over nearly four years, through various structures for global investing.

Thematic ETF exposures available under the partnership include nuclear energy and uranium, gold, and semiconductors. These give Indian investors dollar-denominated exposure to global themes without opening foreign brokerage accounts.

Marcellus notes that Indian investors have so far experienced multi-asset investing primarily through Indian equities, bonds and gold. The new strategy spans global asset classes, adding dollar diversification aimed squarely at HNI and UHNI investors.

"Working with managers of VanEck's calibre is a natural extension of what we've built at Marcellus GIFT City," Mukherjea said. "Indian UHNIs have long had to navigate inefficient, indirect routes to access the best of global assets — that changes now."

He added that the collaboration offers clients a well-structured, regulated and tax-efficient route to world-class global investing, right from home.

Kristen Capuano, Chief Marketing Officer at VanEck, said the firm is bringing its Wealth Builder model to Marcellus clients. The models combine equities, fixed income, and real assets within a systematic framework, going beyond traditional core allocations.

The competitive field includes direct offshore accounts and international mutual funds. Against offshore accounts, the GIFT City route offers regulated custody and lower operational friction. Against international mutual funds, it offers a wider multi-asset canvas and direct dollar exposure.

There are considerations on the other side. Category III AIF structures typically carry higher minimums and fees than mutual funds, and multi-asset global strategies introduce currency and overseas market risk that domestic-only investors may be less familiar with.

The tie-up also signals a broader shift. US asset managers are plugging into Indian distribution through GIFT City, using IFSC structures as the regulated onshore gateway for global investing.

For HNI investors weighing global allocation, the practical question is structure, not access. GIFT City now offers a regulated route where indirect, expensive workarounds were once the only option.

WHO IS THE TARGET INVESTOR FOR THIS OFFERING?
THE OFFERING IS AIMED AT INDIAN HIGH NET WORTH AND ULTRA HIGH NET WORTH INDIVIDUALS SEEKING DOLLAR DIVERSIFICATION. IT COMPETES WITH DIRECT OFFSHORE ACCOUNTS AND INTERNATIONAL MUTUAL FUNDS BY OFFERING A REGULATED ONSHORE ROUTE WITH REGULATED CUSTODY AND TAX CLARITY. GLOSSARY: GIFT City: Gujarat International Finance Tec-City — India's offshore financial centre in Gandhinagar, where IFSCA-regulated entities offer global investment products to Indian and foreign investors under a separate regulatory regime. Category III AIF: An alternative investment fund permitted to use leverage and complex strategies. The feeder structure pools Indian investor money into a master fund that holds the underlying global assets. ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund): A fund that trades on an exchange like a stock and tracks an index, sector, or commodity — the vehicle Marcellus will use for global equity, bond, commodity and real-asset exposure. Multi-Asset Strategy: A portfolio spread across asset classes — equities, bonds, commodities, real assets — rather than a single one, built to reduce dependence on any one market's cycle. Dollar Diversification: Holding assets denominated in US dollars so that portfolio value is not tied entirely to the rupee's fortunes.
