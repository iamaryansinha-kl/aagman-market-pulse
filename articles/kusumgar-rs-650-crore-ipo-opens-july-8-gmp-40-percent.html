Kusumgar IPO Opens July 8 at Rs 650 Crore; Grey Market Premium Signals 40% Listing Pop

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Kusumgar is hitting the primary market this week.

The speciality chemicals company's Rs 650 crore initial public offering opens for subscription on July 8. The issue will close on July 10, giving investors a three-day window to bid.

The company's shares are commanding a grey market premium of nearly 40%. This signals strong demand and the possibility of a healthy listing pop when the stock debuts on the exchanges.

Kusumgar manufactures textile auxiliaries, speciality chemicals, and dyes. The company serves textile mills, garment manufacturers, and industrial clients across India and export markets.

The IPO consists of a fresh issue of shares. The proceeds will be used to fund capital expenditure, repay debt, and meet working capital requirements. The company plans to expand manufacturing capacity at its existing facilities.

The grey market premium suggests retail and institutional investors are optimistic about the company's growth prospects. Speciality chemicals has been a favoured sector in recent IPOs, with several listings delivering strong returns.

Kusumgar will list on both the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange. Allotment is expected to be finalised by July 14, with listing likely on July 16.
