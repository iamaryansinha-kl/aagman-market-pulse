India's FY27 Nominal GDP Growth at 12.5% to Support Fiscal Deficit Target: EY India

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EY India projects nominal GDP growth at 12.5% for FY27, combining 6.7% real growth with 4.5% inflation
  • 02/Fiscal deficit expected to remain at 4.3-4.4% of GDP; Centre met revised FY26 target of 4.4% at Rs 15.2 lakh crore
  • 03/Capital expenditure growth slowed sharply to 1.6% in FY26 from 10.8%; restoring public investment key for sustaining growth

EY India projects India's nominal GDP growth at 12.5% for FY27. This combines real GDP growth of 6.7% with higher inflation of 4.5%.

The "Economy Watch" report said higher nominal growth will bolster tax revenues. This helps the Centre manage fiscal pressures despite global uncertainties.

Real economic growth may moderate from FY26 levels amid global headwinds. However, inflation is expected to lift nominal GDP growth, supporting tax collections.

The Centre met its revised FY26 fiscal deficit target of 4.4% of GDP. The deficit fell to Rs 15.2 lakh crore from Rs 15.8 lakh crore in FY25.

EY expects the FY27 budgeted fiscal deficit at 4.3% of GDP to be either realised or marginally exceeded. Subsidies may exceed Budget estimates.

Growth in government capital expenditure slowed sharply to 1.6% in FY26. This is down from 10.8% growth recorded a year earlier.

"It is desirable to restore capital expenditure growth and at least achieve the FY27 budgeted growth of 11.5%," the EY report said.

Under EY's base scenario, the current account deficit is projected at 1.5% of GDP. This assumes normalised shipments through the Strait of Hormuz.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS EY INDIA'S PROJECTION FOR NOMINAL GDP GROWTH IN FY27?
EY INDIA'S "ECONOMY WATCH" REPORT PROJECTS NOMINAL GDP GROWTH AT 12.5% FOR FY27. THIS IS BASED ON 6.7% REAL GDP GROWTH AND 4.5% CPI INFLATION.
WHAT IS THE EXPECTED FISCAL DEFICIT FOR FY27?
EY INDIA EXPECTS THE FY27 BUDGETED FISCAL DEFICIT AT 4.3% OF GDP TO BE EITHER REALISED OR MARGINALLY EXCEEDED AT 4.4%. SUBSIDIES MAY EXCEED BUDGET ESTIMATES.
HOW DID INDIA'S FISCAL DEFICIT PERFORM IN FY26?
THE CENTRE MET ITS REVISED FY26 FISCAL DEFICIT TARGET OF 4.4% OF GDP. THE DEFICIT FELL TO RS 15.2 LAKH CRORE FROM RS 15.8 LAKH CRORE IN FY25.
WHAT IS THE CONCERN AROUND GOVERNMENT CAPITAL EXPENDITURE?
GOVERNMENT CAPITAL EXPENDITURE GROWTH SLOWED SHARPLY TO 1.6% IN FY26 FROM 10.8% A YEAR EARLIER. EY RECOMMENDS RESTORING CAPEX GROWTH TO AT LEAST THE FY27 BUDGETED TARGET OF 11.5%.
WHAT ASSUMPTIONS UNDERLIE EY'S GROWTH PROJECTIONS FOR FY27?
EY ASSUMES GLOBAL CRUDE OIL PRICES REMAIN LOWER AND SHIPMENTS THROUGH THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ NORMALISE. UNDER THIS SCENARIO, REAL GDP GROWTH IS PROJECTED AT 6.6-6.8%.
