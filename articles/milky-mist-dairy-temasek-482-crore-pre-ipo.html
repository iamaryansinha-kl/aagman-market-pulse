Milky Mist Dairy raises ₹482 crore from Temasek in pre-IPO round

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Milky Mist Dairy raises around ₹482 crore from Temasek affiliate Jongsong Investments in a pre-IPO round at ₹139.76 per share
  • 02/The fundraise includes ₹350 crore primary capital and ₹125 crore secondary sale by promoters; FY26 revenue hit ₹3,275 crore with 34% growth
  • 03/SEBI approved the ₹2,035 crore IPO in October 2025; CEO K Rathnam says the listing is on track with approval valid until October 2026

Milky Mist Dairy Food Ltd has raised around ₹482 crore in a pre-IPO round. Jongsong Investments Pte. Ltd., a Temasek affiliate, led the entire investment.

The fundraise includes a primary capital raise of ₹350 crore and a secondary share sale of roughly ₹125 crore. Promoters Sathishkumar T and Anitha S sold 89,43,903 shares.

As part of the primary issuance, Milky Mist allotted 5,43,789 equity shares at ₹139.76 per share. It also issued 25,00,000 CCPS at the same price, convertible 1:1 before listing.

The company clocked revenue of around ₹3,275 crore in FY26. It achieved 34% top-line growth, beating its own estimate of 33%.

Milky Mist obtained SEBI approval for its ₹2,035 crore IPO in October 2025. The issue comprises a ₹1,785 crore fresh issue and a ₹250 crore OFS by promoters.

"The IPO is definitely on track," CEO K Rathnam told BusinessLine last month. SEBI approval expires in October 2026, and the company is evaluating market conditions.

The dairy player competes with Amul, Nestlé India, Britannia, and Hatsun Agro. It relies on one facility in Perundurai, Tamil Nadu, with 71% revenue from the South.

Quick commerce now contributes 10% of revenue. Protein offerings are growing 100% year-on-year, driven by demand for Greek yogurt and high-protein paneer.
