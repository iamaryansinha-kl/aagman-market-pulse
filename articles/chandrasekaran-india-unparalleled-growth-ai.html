India to See 'Unparalleled' Economic Growth for Decades Amid AI-Led Changes: N Chandrasekaran

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Tata Sons Chairman N Chandrasekaran has painted an emphatically bullish picture of India's economic trajectory. Speaking at the convocation of Sai University in Chennai on Saturday, he said the country is entering a period of growth that would be unparalleled in its own history and unmatched by any part of the world, and that this run could last several decades.

The driver, in his telling, is a convergence of forces. Chandrasekaran described the current moment as one of rapid change spanning the global economy, technology, regulation and AI, and argued that the disruption cuts both ways. While there is growing discussion about job losses due to AI, he said the technology is opening new areas of opportunity at the same time.

His boldest claim was about what does not yet exist. Five years from now, he told the graduates, there will be industries that do not exist today, and people may not even know the terminology for them yet. The world, he said, is full to the brim with possibilities.

The advice that followed was practical. Chandrasekaran urged graduates to run dual races, working toward long-term goals while pursuing shorter-term achievements to maintain momentum. He listed six qualities for navigating the changing economy: continuous learning, curiosity, collective work, creativity, self-reflection and ambitious goals.

On creativity he was pointed. "Creativity in business is premium," he said, and people who do not keep pace will get outdated. He also quoted marathon runner Yuma Ikanga, that the will to win means nothing without the will to prepare.

The speech comes at a delicate moment for Chandrasekaran personally. The Tata Trusts, which control Tata Sons, recently opposed a third term for him as chairman, a dispute that has put the group's leadership question in public view. That the chairman chose a student convocation to make a sweeping case for India's economic exceptionalism, days after that news broke, is itself a statement of continuity.

Whether India's growth delivers on the unparalleled framing will depend on execution, from manufacturing scale-up to energy availability. But from one of the country's most prominent corporate leaders, the message to a graduating cohort was unambiguous: aim high, think big, and prepare relentlessly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID CHANDRASEKARAN SAY ABOUT INDIA'S GROWTH?
That India will see economic growth unparalleled in its own history and compared with any part of the world, continuing for several decades.
WHAT IS DRIVING THIS, ACCORDING TO HIM?
AI-led changes, technology and regulatory shifts that will create entirely new industries within five years.
WHERE DID HE SPEAK?
At the convocation of Sai University in Chennai, where he was the chief guest.
