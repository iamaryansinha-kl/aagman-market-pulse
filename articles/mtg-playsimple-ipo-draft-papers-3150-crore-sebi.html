MTG's PlaySimple Files ₹3,150 Crore IPO Draft Papers With SEBI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PlaySimple Games files draft papers for ₹3,150 crore IPO with market regulator; issue entirely an Offer For Sale by Swedish parent MTG
  • 02/Founded in 2014, PlaySimple operates 30+ live casual puzzle titles across 110+ countries including hit games Word Trip and Crossword Jam
  • 03/MTG intends to retain majority ownership post-IPO; timing to be decided closer to opening of the offer

Swedish gaming firm Modern Times Group MTG AB has filed draft papers for a ₹3,150 crore IPO. Its wholly-owned Indian subsidiary PlaySimple Games Ltd submitted the papers to SEBI on Thursday.

The IPO will be entirely an Offer For Sale. MTG will act as the promoter and selling shareholder.

The proceeds from the issue will accrue to MTG. No fresh capital will flow into PlaySimple itself.

The offer size is expected to be up to ₹3,150 crore. This translates to roughly $350 million based on current exchange rates.

MTG stated it intends to retain majority ownership in PlaySimple. The final timing will be decided closer to the offer opening.

PlaySimple was founded in 2014. It has grown into a leading player in the word games segment.

The company runs over 30 live casual puzzle titles. Its games reach players across more than 110 countries.

Popular titles include Word Search Explorer, Daily Themed Crossword, Crossword Jam, Word Trip and Word Jam. These cater to users across age groups and skill levels.
