Corporate Bonds Surge as FD Rates Fall: Retail Investors Pour ₹4,389 Cr in April

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Retail investors invested ₹4,389 Cr in corporate bonds in April 2026, nearly 2.5x the volume from the same month last year
  • 02/Corporate bond issuances reached $128 billion in CY 2025, nearly double compared to 2020 levels
  • 03/India's bond market is valued at $2.8 trillion; Grip Invest spans 8,000-plus pin codes as first-time bond buyers emerge from tier-2 and tier-3 cities

Retail investors poured ₹4,389 crore into corporate bonds in April 2026. This is nearly 2.5 times the volume recorded in the same month last year.

The shift is accelerating as fixed deposit rates moderate below the 6.5-7% range. Post-tax real returns on FDs are now looking thin for many investors.

Corporate bonds in the AA to A segment are offering spreads that compensate for incremental credit risk. A BBB-rated issuer offers 12-13% while an AA issuer sits at 9-10%.

India's corporate bond issuances reached $128 billion in calendar year 2025. This is nearly double the levels seen in 2020, signaling structural demand meeting supply.

The bond market itself is valued at $2.8 trillion. The retail slice is still small, but participation is compounding quarter by quarter.

First-time bond investors from tier-2 and tier-3 cities are entering the space. Many had never looked beyond a bank deposit until now.

"We're roughly at the 2012-13 moment of equities, if the analogy holds. The regulatory scaffolding is in place, and now the behavioral shift is happening." - Nikhil Aggarwal, Founder & Group CEO, Grip Invest.

SEBI's reforms on online bond platforms, reduction in face value minimums, and better disclosure norms have paved the way. The OBPP Association recently launched an education campaign titled 'Bonds - Ek Sashakt Bandhan'.

HOW MUCH DID RETAIL INVESTORS POUR INTO CORPORATE BONDS IN APRIL 2026?
RETAIL INVESTORS WITH TICKET SIZES BELOW ₹50 LAKH INVESTED ₹4,389 CRORE IN CORPORATE BONDS IN APRIL 2026. THIS IS NEARLY 2.5 TIMES THE VOLUME RECORDED IN THE SAME MONTH LAST YEAR.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE SHIFT FROM FIXED DEPOSITS TO CORPORATE BONDS?
FIXED DEPOSIT RATES HAVE MODERATED BELOW THE 6.5-7% RANGE. POST-TAX REAL RETURNS ONCE ADJUSTED FOR INFLATION ARE LOOKING THIN, PUSHING INVESTORS UP THE RISK-RETURN CURVE.
WHAT ARE THE YIELD SPREADS BETWEEN DIFFERENT CREDIT RATINGS?
A BBB-RATED ISSUER OFFERS 12-13% WHILE AN AA-RATED ISSUER OFFERS 9-10%. THE 300 BPS SPREAD REPRESENTS THE MARKET'S ESTIMATE OF DEFAULT PROBABILITY AND LIQUIDITY RISK.
HOW BIG IS INDIA'S CORPORATE BOND MARKET?
INDIA'S BOND MARKET IS VALUED AT $2.8 TRILLION. CORPORATE BOND ISSUANCES IN CALENDAR YEAR 2025 REACHED $128 BILLION, NEARLY DOUBLE THE LEVELS FROM 2020.
WHAT SHOULD RETAIL INVESTORS CHECK BEFORE INVESTING IN CORPORATE BONDS?
INVESTORS SHOULD START WITH THE CREDIT RATING, CHECK THE ISSUER'S INTEREST COVERAGE RATIO, EVALUATE CONCENTRATION RISK, AND ASSESS LIQUIDITY FOR EXIT. RED FLAGS INCLUDE YIELDS 500+ BPS ABOVE GOVERNMENT SECURITIES, SHORT TRACK RECORDS, THIN CREDIT RATINGS, AND LOW SECONDARY MARKET LIQUIDITY.
