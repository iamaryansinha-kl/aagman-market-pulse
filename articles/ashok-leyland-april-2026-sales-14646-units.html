Ashok Leyland April Sales Up 9% at 14,646 Units on M&HCV Strength

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Total April 2026 sales hit 14,646 units, up 9% from 13,426 units in April 2025
  • 02/M&HCV segment drove growth with 9,847 units sold, up 12% YoY; LCV up 3% at 4,799 units
  • 03/Watch FY27 momentum as Switch Mobility electric bus orders under PM E-Bus Sewa scheme ramp up

Ashok Leyland reported a 9% year-on-year increase in total sales for April 2026, selling 14,646 units compared to 13,426 units in the same month last year. The strong performance reflects continued demand across both domestic and export markets.

The Medium and Heavy Commercial Vehicles segment was the key growth driver, with sales rising 12% to 9,847 units. This solidifies the company's position as a leader in the M&HCV space where it commands approximately 30% market share.

Light Commercial Vehicles posted a modest 3% gain, reaching 4,799 units in April 2026. While slower than the heavy segment, this still indicates stable demand in the lighter truck category.

Domestic sales grew 8% to 13,452 units, showing healthy traction within India. Exports outpaced overall growth with a 17% jump to 1,194 units, highlighting momentum in overseas markets.

For the full fiscal year FY26, Ashok Leyland recorded total sales of 1,71,233 units, marking a 12% increase over FY25's 1,52,591 units. The M&HCV segment contributed 1,12,847 units, up 14%, while LCV sales grew 8% to 58,386 units.

The company's key export markets include the GCC region, specifically Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Kuwait, as well as Africa. These geographies continue to drive international revenue alongside the strong domestic base.

Switch Mobility, the electric bus joint venture, is gaining traction through government orders under the PM E-Bus Sewa scheme. This positions Ashok Leyland for a meaningful footprint in India's electric public transport transition.

The stock closed at ₹162.29 on May 4, 2026. Investors should monitor order inflows from the electric bus segment and export trajectory for cues on FY27 performance.
