Sensex Jumps 380 Points at Open on May 7 as Hormuz Tensions Ease

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex opened 380 points higher at 78,339 on May 7, but pared gains through the session to trade near 78,011 by 2 pm
  • 02/Easing US tensions around the Strait of Hormuz reduced Middle East conflict concerns, pulling crude oil prices lower
  • 03/Derivatives indicators suggest caution with aggressive call writing and low put-call ratio pointing to range-bound trade

Indian equity markets opened sharply higher on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The Sensex jumped 380.72 points to open at 78,339.24 from the previous close of 77,958.52.

By 9:17 am, the index had pared most gains to trade at 78,049.34, up 90.82 points or 0.12%. The Nifty 50 rose 46.85 points to 24,377.80.

The market swung into negative territory by 11:11 am. The Sensex dropped 127.75 points or 0.16% to 77,830.77, while the Nifty fell 14.65 points to 24,316.30.

By 2 pm, benchmark indices recovered modestly. The Sensex traded at 78,010.75, up 52.23 points or 0.07%. The Nifty was at 24,365.75, up 34.80 points or 0.14%.

Global sentiment improved after signs that the US may have paused aggressive actions around the Strait of Hormuz. Declining crude oil prices supported domestic equities.

Brent crude futures traded at $102.02, up 0.74%, while WTI crude was at $95.77, up 0.73%. On MCX, May crude futures were at ₹9,076.

Japan's Nikkei 225 surged 4.19% to 62,009.59, breaking the 62,000 mark. The Hang Seng gained 1.2% to 26,531.35, and Taiwan's Taiex added 1.7%.

Derivatives data signaled underlying caution. Aggressive call writing at higher strikes and a relatively low put-call ratio pointed to limited near-term upside.
