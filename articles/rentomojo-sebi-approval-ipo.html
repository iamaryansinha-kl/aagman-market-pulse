Furniture Rental Startup Rentomojo Gets Sebi Nod for IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Rentomojo has received the green light from the Securities and Exchange Board of India to launch its initial public offering.

The Sebi approval clears the way for the furniture and appliance rental startup to proceed with its listing plans. The company had filed draft red herring prospectus documents earlier this year.

Rentomojo operates a subscription-based rental model for furniture, appliances, and electronics. The platform targets urban professionals and millennials who prefer flexibility over ownership.

The startup competes in India's growing sharing economy segment. It has built a presence across major cities including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, and Hyderabad.

The IPO will test investor appetite for new-age consumer internet companies. Several such firms have listed in recent years with mixed performance, making the market selective about valuations.

Rentomojo's public issue will include a fresh issue of shares. The company intends to use the proceeds to expand its inventory, strengthen technology infrastructure, and grow its customer base.
