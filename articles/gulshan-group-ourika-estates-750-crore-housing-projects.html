Gulshan Group, Ourika Estates to Invest ₹750 Crore on Two Housing Projects in Ghaziabad, Lucknow

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Noida-based realty firm Gulshan Group has partnered with Ourika Estates to develop two housing projects in Ghaziabad and Lucknow. The total investment will exceed ₹750 crore.

The two companies announced the joint venture on Monday. In the first phase, they have acquired land parcels in Ghaziabad and Lucknow through auctions conducted by the respective development authorities.

Project details The land in Ghaziabad spans 8,750 square metres. The Lucknow plot is located in Gomti Nagar Extension and covers 10,328 square metres.

Over the development lifecycle, the platform plans to invest over ₹750 crore. The two projects will create about 1.50 million square feet of built-up area across premium residential developments.

The two partners are also actively evaluating opportunities across North India's emerging growth corridors.

Leadership comments Gulshan Nagpal, Promoter of Gulshan Group, described the association with Ourika Estates as a step towards creating a larger portfolio of premium, future-ready residential projects.

Deepak Kapoor, also a Promoter of Gulshan Group, said housing demand is strong across major cities in North India, especially for credible players.

Prasoon Chauhan, Director of Ourika Estates, said: "With an initial investment outlay exceeding Rs 750 crore, this vehicle is built to perform."

Market context Gulshan Group has developed multiple housing projects in Delhi-NCR. Ourika Estates has a presence across key urban centres in Uttar Pradesh, including Bareilly, Ayodhya, Ghaziabad and Lucknow.

Housing sales across eight major cities fell 6% year-on-year to 91,729 units in the April-June quarter, according to PropTiger. The decline was attributed to low demand, with buyers being cautious amid global uncertainties. In Delhi-NCR specifically, sales fell 7% annually to 9,352 units from 10,051 units.

Despite the near-term softness, developers are continuing to invest in premium residential projects in high-growth corridors. The NCR and Lucknow markets have seen sustained interest from both established and new developers.
