L&T Industrial Electronics Scale-Up: 400 Staff, 20,000 Sq M Coimbatore Plant by 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/L&T Electronic Products & Systems (LTEPS) targets "meaningful scale" by early 2027 from its 40-acre Coimbatore campus
  • 02/A new 20,000 sq metre G+3 building is planned; workforce will expand from 400 to 800 employees
  • 03/First products include EV motor control units (60kW-300kW); two PCB assembly lines are already operational

Larsen & Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) has launched a new business vertical called L&T Electronic Products & Systems (LTEPS). The division marks L&T's formal entry into the B2B industrial electronics market.

LTEPS will anchor manufacturing at L&T's existing 40-acre campus in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Two PCB assembly lines are already commissioned and producing DC-DC and AC-DC converters for chargers.

The company is now planning a ground-plus-three building of approximately 20,000 sq metres on the same campus. The new facility is in the planning and permit stage.

"The aim is to get the G+3 complex up by the end of the year," said Prashant Chiranjive Jain, Head, Corporate Centre, L&T. He anticipates the division will reach "meaningful scale" by early next year.

The vertical currently employs about 400 people. The workforce is expected to double to around 800 as the business ramps up, Jain added.

LTEPS will focus on power electronics, automotive electronics, industrial robotics, and ESDM. It also covers defence systems including radar, fire control, and missile systems.

One of the first products is a motor-control-unit platform for EV motors. It will launch in three variants ranging from 60 kilowatt to 300 kilowatt, suitable for three-wheelers and commercial four-wheelers.

"We have got an anchor customer. And we have got a very positive response for this product," Jain said. The division will take about two years to qualify and test products across all standards.

L&T Chairman S N Subrahmanyan tied the move to the company's Lakshya 2031 vision. The foray aims to deepen technology leadership and boost India's self-reliance in critical electronics manufacturing.

The division will leverage synergies with L&T Technology Services and L&T Semiconductor Technologies. L&T Semiconductor can design system-on-chip solutions for LTEPS products.

L&T shares closed around ₹4,037 on April 28, 2026. The stock trades 7.75% below its 52-week high of ₹4,440.

Jefferies maintains a Buy rating with a ₹4,500 target price. Investec has a Buy rating with a target of ₹4,860.

THE DIVISION CURRENTLY HAS ABOUT 400 EMPLOYEES ON THE GROUND. THE WORKFORCE IS EXPECTED TO GROW TO AROUND 800 EMPLOYEES AS THE BUSINESS RAMPS UP TO MEANINGFUL SCALE BY EARLY 2027.
WHAT ARE THE FIRST PRODUCTS FROM LTEPS?
ONE OF THE FIRST PRODUCTS IS A MOTOR-CONTROL-UNIT PLATFORM FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES. IT WILL LAUNCH IN THREE VARIANTS FROM 60 KILOWATT TO 300 KILOWATT, TARGETING THREE-WHEELERS AND COMMERCIAL FOUR-WHEELERS.
