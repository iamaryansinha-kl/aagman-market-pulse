NSE IPO DRHP Filing: Exchange Targets June 5-15 Window With Sebi

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE is preparing to file its draft red herring prospectus with Sebi between June 5 and June 15, 2026
  • 02/The exchange and its lead bankers recently held discussions and agreed to proceed with the early June filing timeline
  • 03/NSE declined to comment on the matter; the final IPO timeline remains subject to Sebi approval and market conditions

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) is preparing to submit its draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) for its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The exchange is targeting a filing window between June 5 and June 15, 2026.

The exchange and its lead bankers to the issue recently held discussions. They agreed to proceed with the Sebi filing in early June, according to people familiar with the development.

"Work has actively started for the DRHP filing, and NSE may look to file papers between June 5th and 15th," said one person familiar with the process. NSE did not comment on the matter when approached.

The NSE IPO has been among the most anticipated and long-pending public offerings in Indian capital markets. The exchange, incorporated in 1992, is the largest stock exchange in India by trading volumes.

BSE, NSE's rival and the country's oldest stock exchange, is already listed. BSE shares closed at ₹4,291.20 on May 25, 2026, up 2.33% on the day.

The DRHP filing with Sebi is the first formal step in the IPO process. Once filed, Sebi reviews the document before the exchange can proceed with investor roadshows and price discovery.

The listing would mark a milestone for Indian market infrastructure. It would give investors direct exposure to the country's dominant equity trading platform.

WHAT IS A DRHP AND WHY DOES IT MATTER?
A DRAFT RED HERRING PROSPECTUS (DRHP) IS THE FIRST FORMAL DOCUMENT FILED WITH SEBI FOR AN IPO. IT CONTAINS DETAILS ABOUT THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS, FINANCIALS, AND OFFERING. SEBI REVIEWS IT BEFORE THE COMPANY CAN PROCEED WITH ROADSHOWS AND PRICE DISCOVERY.
