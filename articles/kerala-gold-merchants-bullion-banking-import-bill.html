Kerala Gold Merchants Seek Bullion Banking System to Cut Import Bill

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kerala Gold and Silver Merchants Association urges bullion banking system to cut ₹12 lakh crore annual gold import bill
  • 02/India holds 25,000-30,000 tonnes of gold domestically, representing 12-15% of global mined gold reserves
  • 03/Gold import duty doubled from 6% to 15%, raising prices by ₹10,200 per sovereign and spurring smuggling risks

The Kerala Gold and Silver Merchants Association has called for a policy shift toward gold recycling. State General Secretary S. Abdul Nazar made the appeal on May 13, 2026.

Nazar urged the government to establish a transparent bullion banking system. This would integrate domestically held gold into the formal financial framework.

India currently imports around 800 tonnes of gold annually. The estimated cost is ₹12 lakh crore, draining foreign exchange reserves.

According to World Gold Council estimates, Indian households hold 25,000-30,000 tonnes. This represents nearly 12 to 15 per cent of all gold mined globally.

The association criticised the recent hike in gold import duty from 6 per cent to 15 per cent. Nazar warned this could fuel smuggling activity.

When the duty stood at 15 per cent earlier, India imported nearly 1,000 tonnes. Imports declined to below 800 tonnes when the rate was cut to 6 per cent.

The 9 per cent duty increase has pushed gold prices higher by around ₹10,200 per sovereign. Smuggling one kilogram could now yield profits exceeding ₹20 lakh.

Adding 3 per cent GST in the parallel market raises illegal profits further. The association argues policy should tap domestic gold reserves rather than restrict purchases.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT HAS THE KERALA GOLD AND SILVER MERCHANTS ASSOCIATION DEMANDED?
THE ASSOCIATION HAS URGED THE GOVERNMENT TO ADOPT A POLICY FRAMEWORK FOCUSED ON RECYCLING DOMESTICALLY HELD GOLD AND ESTABLISHING A TRANSPARENT BULLION BANKING SYSTEM TO REDUCE DEPENDENCE ON IMPORTS.
HOW MUCH GOLD DOES INDIA IMPORT ANNUALLY?
INDIA IMPORTS AROUND 800 TONNES OF GOLD ANNUALLY AT AN ESTIMATED COST OF ₹12 LAKH CRORE.
HOW MUCH GOLD IS HELD DOMESTICALLY IN INDIA?
ACCORDING TO WORLD GOLD COUNCIL ESTIMATES, INDIAN HOUSEHOLDS, TRUSTS AND INSTITUTIONS HOLD APPROXIMATELY 25,000 TO 30,000 TONNES OF GOLD. THIS REPRESENTS NEARLY 12 TO 15 PER CENT OF THE WORLD'S TOTAL MINED GOLD RESERVES.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT GOLD IMPORT DUTY?
THE GOLD IMPORT DUTY WAS RECENTLY INCREASED FROM 6 PER CENT TO 15 PER CENT. THE 9 PER CENT HIKE HAS PUSHED GOLD PRICES UP BY AROUND ₹10,200 PER SOVEREIGN.
WHY DOES THE ASSOCIATION OPPOSE HIGHER IMPORT DUTIES?
NAZAR WARNED THAT HIGHER DUTIES COULD ENCOURAGE SMUGGLING. ONE KG OF SMUGGLED GOLD CAN GENERATE PROFITS EXCEEDING ₹20 LAKH, AND WITH 3 PER CENT GST IN THE PARALLEL MARKET, ILLEGAL PROFITS COULD REACH NEARLY ₹24 LAKH.
