Nifty Rangebound: 23,800-24,000 Hurdle, 23,250 Support Key

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty expected to trade rangebound with a mild bullish undertone amid mixed domestic and global cues
  • 02/Immediate resistance zone sits at 23,800-24,000; a sustained breakout above this range is needed for further upside
  • 03/Support placed at 23,150-23,250; breach below could trigger deeper correction toward 22,800

The Nifty 50 is expected to trade in a range this week. Analysts see a mild bullish undertone as the index consolidates amid mixed cues.

Immediate resistance is pegged at 23,800-24,000. A decisive close above this band would open room for a move toward 24,200-24,500.

On the downside, support is seen at 23,150-23,250. A breakdown below this zone could drag the index toward the 22,800 level.

Global markets remain a key watch factor. Wall Street volatility and crude oil movement will likely influence sentiment.

Domestic flows have been steady. FII activity and pre-budget positioning by institutional investors remain near-term drivers.

Traders are advised to watch the 23,800 level closely. A range breakout on either side should set the near-term trend.

Sector rotation is likely to continue. IT, banking, and auto stocks may see selective buying within the broader range.
