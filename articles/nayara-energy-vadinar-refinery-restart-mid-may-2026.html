Nayara Energy Vadinar Refinery to Restart by Mid-May After April 9 Shutdown

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nayara Energy's 400,000 bpd Vadinar refinery will resume operations by mid-May after a full maintenance shutdown starting April 9
  • 02/The shutdown was originally planned last year but postponed due to EU sanctions blocking key equipment procurement
  • 03/Nayara accounts for 8% of India's refining capacity; prolonged outage could tighten domestic fuel supply

Nayara Energy will restart its Vadinar refinery in Gujarat by mid-May. Sujata Sharma, joint secretary in India's federal oil ministry, confirmed the timeline.

The 400,000-barrel-per-day facility was fully shut from April 9 for scheduled maintenance. It is India's second-largest refinery.

Nayara had originally planned a month-long turnaround last year. It postponed the shutdown because European Union sanctions made it difficult to secure key equipment.

The refiner is majority-owned by Russian entities including oil major Rosneft. The EU included Nayara in its 18th sanctions package in July 2025.

The Vadinar plant accounts for approximately 8% of India's total refining capacity of 5.2 million barrels per day. A prolonged outage would tighten domestic fuel supply.

Before the full shutdown, the refinery was operating at 70-80% capacity due to sanctions-related disruptions. Saudi Aramco and Iraq's SOMO had stopped selling crude to Nayara.

Nayara has turned to Russian Urals crude and sanctioned tankers to maintain supply. It operates over 6,500 fuel stations across India.

India does not recognize unilateral EU sanctions. The government has allowed Nayara to continue sourcing Russian crude despite Western pressure.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN WILL NAYARA ENERGY RESUME OPERATIONS AT THE VADINAR REFINERY?
NAYARA ENERGY IS EXPECTED TO RESUME OPERATIONS AT ITS 400,000-BARREL-PER-DAY VADINAR REFINERY BY MID-MAY 2026, ACCORDING TO SUJATA SHARMA, JOINT SECRETARY IN INDIA'S FEDERAL OIL MINISTRY.
WHY WAS THE REFINERY SHUT DOWN?
THE VADINAR REFINERY WAS FULLY SHUT FROM APRIL 9, 2026 FOR SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE AND TURNAROUND ACTIVITIES. THE SHUTDOWN WAS ORIGINALLY PLANNED LAST YEAR BUT POSTPONED DUE TO EU SANCTIONS BLOCKING KEY EQUIPMENT PROCUREMENT.
HOW MUCH OF INDIA'S REFINING CAPACITY DOES NAYARA ENERGY ACCOUNT FOR?
NAYARA ENERGY ACCOUNTS FOR APPROXIMATELY 8% OF INDIA'S TOTAL REFINING CAPACITY OF 5.2 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY. THE VADINAR FACILITY IS THE SECOND-LARGEST REFINERY IN THE COUNTRY.
WHAT IMPACT HAVE EU SANCTIONS HAD ON NAYARA ENERGY?
THE EU'S 18TH SANCTIONS PACKAGE IN JULY 2025 DISRUPTED NAYARA'S OPERATIONS. SAUDI ARAMCO AND IRAQ'S SOMO STOPPED SELLING CRUDE TO THE REFINERY. NAYARA HAS RESPONDED BY SOURCING RUSSIAN URALS CRUDE AND USING SANCTIONED TANKERS FOR TRANSPORT.
WHO OWNS NAYARA ENERGY?
NAYARA ENERGY IS MAJORITY-OWNED BY RUSSIAN ENTITIES INCLUDING ROSNEFT, WHICH HOLDS A 49% STAKE IN THE VADINAR REFINERY. THE COMPANY OPERATES OVER 6,500 FUEL STATIONS ACROSS INDIA AND SUPPLIES BOTH DOMESTIC AND EXPORT MARKETS.
