OFSS Shares Tumble 5% as Parent Oracle Sends Force Majeure Notice for AI Data Centre Project

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Shares of Oracle Financial Services Software (OFSS) tumbled more than 5% on Friday after US-based parent Oracle issued a 'force majeure' notice to developer Blue Owl, triggering worries across the trillion-dollar market for AI infrastructure financing.

OFSS shares fell to Rs 10,332 apiece on the NSE, the lowest level in more than nine weeks since July 23, making it the top loser on the Nifty IT index, which itself was down around 1%.

The notice concerns Jupiter, a data centre campus that Blue Owl unit STACK Infrastructure is building in New Mexico to support ChatGPT-maker OpenAI.

Blue Owl said Oracle's notice did not alter the parties' commitment to the project.

Oracle is aiming to delay payments should the data centre fail to come online in 2028 as planned, rather than exiting as the main tenant, Bloomberg News reported. Companies typically invoke force majeure to free themselves from contractual obligations when problems arise beyond their control.

The project is already running about a year behind schedule, Reuters quoted a person familiar with the matter as saying. Blue Owl has about $3 billion of equity invested in the project.

Earlier this month, OFSS shares came under pressure after the Financial Times reported that around $18 billion in loans tied to an Oracle-leased data centre in New Mexico had come under pressure, with the loans quoted at 89 to 91 cents on the dollar by syndicate banks including Santander and Jefferies.

Concerns centre on escalating local opposition to the 1,400-acre Project Jupiter campus in Dona Ana County over fears it would impact water supply and air quality.

Oracle shares dropped around 3.5% on Wall Street, while Blue Owl fell around 4%.

OFSS is the Indian subsidiary of Oracle. The stock has fallen more than 12% in one week, underperforming the Nifty IT index, which has fallen nearly 3% in the same period. Despite the slide, OFSS is up 36% in 2026 so far, even as the Nifty IT index has fallen more than 26%. The company has a market capitalisation of nearly Rs 90,900 crore.
