Mangalam Worldwide 1:10 Stock Split Approved: Smallcap Up 120% in a Year

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mangalam Worldwide board approves 1:10 stock split on May 15, 2026; each Rs 10 share to become 10 shares of Re 1 each
  • 02/Stock hit a fresh 52-week high of Rs 369.70 and has gained over 120% in the past year
  • 03/Split subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals; company also recommended Rs 0.30 per share final dividend for FY26

Mangalam Worldwide Ltd has approved a 1:10 stock split. Each equity share with a face value of Rs 10 will be subdivided into 10 shares of Re 1 each.

The board took the decision in a meeting held on Friday, May 15, 2026. The proposal is subject to shareholders' and regulatory approvals.

The stock hit a fresh 52-week high of Rs 369.70 on May 18, 2026. It gained 4% in the day's trade.

The multibagger counter has delivered returns of over 120% in the past year. Its market capitalisation stands at Rs 1,080 crore on the NSE.

Mangalam Worldwide is a fully integrated stainless-steel manufacturer. Its operations span scrap melting to seamless pipes and tubes production.

The proposed split aims to enhance liquidity and encourage broader retail participation. The board also approved an alteration to the Capital Clause of the Memorandum of Association.

For FY26, the company reported revenue of Rs 1,214.98 crore and PAT of Rs 50.14 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of Rs 0.30 per share.

The company also approved a direct listing proposal on the Main Board of BSE. This is subject to approval by BSE or relevant authorities.

"The proposed stock split reflects the company's continued focus on creating long-term value for shareholders and improving accessibility of the stock for a wider investor base" - Chandragupt Prakash Mangal, Managing Director, Mangalam Worldwide Limited.

The company has an annual manufacturing capacity of 190,000 MT. Its portfolio includes stainless steel billets, ingots, bars, and seamless pipes for critical applications.
