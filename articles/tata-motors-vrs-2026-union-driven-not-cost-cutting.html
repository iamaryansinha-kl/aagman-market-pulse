Tata Motors VRS 2026: Union-Driven Scheme, Not Cost-Cutting

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Motors says its April 2026 VRS was employee and union-driven, not a cost-cutting or headcount reduction exercise
  • 02/Only 275-300 of roughly 750 eligible permanent employees opted in, representing less than 0.5% of the 58,442 total workforce
  • 03/This was the company's third VRS in four years and the first since its October 2025 demerger into separately listed PV and CV businesses

Tata Motors Ltd (NSE: TATAMOTORS) has clarified that its recently concluded voluntary retirement scheme was driven by employee and union requests. The company explicitly denied that the exercise was aimed at cost-cutting or workforce reduction.

"The scheme was offered in response to requests received from employees and unions across locations. It was not introduced as a cost-cutting, headcount reduction or workforce rationalisation exercise," a company official said.

The scheme ran from April 10 to April 30, 2026. It was open to permanent employees aged above 40 years and those with at least 10 years of service.

Tata Motors had a total workforce of 58,442 employees at the end of FY25. Around 275 to 300 employees opted in from a pool of roughly 750 eligible workers.

The compensation package offered up to 36 months of salary along with gratuity and encashment benefits. The payout was tiered by age, ranging from 80 percent for employees aged 40 to 45, up to 100 percent for those aged 50 to 55.

Additional benefits included medical coverage for up to 10 years, provisions of ₹1 to 1.5 lakh in medical support, and a group incentive of ₹50,000 to 1.48 lakh. Pension benefits will follow EPFO norms.

"The trade-off for companies is that they take a one-time payout hit on the balance sheet, but in return secure permanent savings on salaries, provident fund obligations, insurance and other employee costs over the long term," said an industry executive tracking restructuring trends in the auto sector.

This was Tata Motors' third VRS exercise in roughly four years. It was also the first since the company's October 2025 demerger into separately listed passenger vehicle and commercial vehicle businesses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS THE TATA MOTORS VRS SCHEME AND WHEN DID IT RUN?
THE VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT SCHEME RAN FROM APRIL 10 TO APRIL 30, 2026. IT WAS OPEN TO PERMANENT EMPLOYEES AGED ABOVE 40 YEARS AND/OR WITH AT LEAST 10 YEARS OF SERVICE.
HOW MANY EMPLOYEES OPTED FOR THE TATA MOTORS VRS?
AROUND 275 TO 300 EMPLOYEES OPTED IN FROM A POOL OF ROUGHLY 750 ELIGIBLE WORKERS. THIS REPRESENTS LESS THAN 0.5 PERCENT OF TATA MOTORS' TOTAL WORKFORCE OF 58,442 AT THE END OF FY25.
WHAT COMPENSATION DID THE TATA MOTORS VRS OFFER?
THE SCHEME OFFERED COMPENSATION OF UP TO 36 MONTHS OF SALARY ALONG WITH GRATUITY, LEAVE AND NOTICE PERIOD ENCASHMENT, AND CONTINUED HEALTH INSURANCE. PAYOUTS WERE TIERED BY AGE, RANGING FROM 80 PERCENT TO 100 PERCENT OF LAST DRAWN SALARY.
WHY DOES TATA MOTORS SAY THE VRS WAS NOT AIMED AT COST-CUTTING?
A COMPANY OFFICIAL STATED THE SCHEME WAS OFFERED IN RESPONSE TO REQUESTS FROM EMPLOYEES AND UNIONS ACROSS LOCATIONS. THE COMPANY MAINTAINED IT WAS NOT INTRODUCED AS A COST-CUTTING, HEADCOUNT REDUCTION OR WORKFORCE RATIONALISATION EXERCISE.
WHAT ADDITIONAL BENEFITS WERE INCLUDED IN THE TATA MOTORS VRS?
BENEFITS INCLUDED MEDICAL COVERAGE FOR UP TO 10 YEARS, PROVISIONS OF ₹1 TO 1.5 LAKH IN MEDICAL SUPPORT, A GROUP INCENTIVE OF ₹50,000 TO ₹1.48 LAKH, AND PENSION BENEFITS AS PER EPFO NORMS. EMPLOYEES COULD CHOOSE MONTHLY PAYMENTS UNTIL AGE 60, LUMP-SUM, OR STAGGERED TRANCHES.
