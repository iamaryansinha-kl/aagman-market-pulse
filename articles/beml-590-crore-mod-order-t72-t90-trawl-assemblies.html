BEML Bags ₹590 Crore MoD Order for T-72/T-90 Tank Trawl Assemblies

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BEML awarded ₹590 crore contract by Ministry of Defence for indigenously developed trawl assemblies for T-72 and T-90 tanks
  • 02/Total MoD procurement is ₹975 crore; balance ₹385 crore awarded to Electro Pneumatics and Hydraulics under Aatmanirbhar push
  • 03/Trawl systems enhance Indian Army's minefield breaching capability; contract signed April 21 in presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh

BEML Limited (NSE: BEML) has secured a ₹590 crore contract from the Ministry of Defence. The order covers supply of trawl assemblies for the Indian Army's T-72 and T-90 tanks.

The contract was formally signed and exchanged on April 21, 2026, in New Delhi. Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh was present at the signing ceremony.

The total Ministry of Defence procurement value is ₹975 crore. BEML's share is ₹590 crore; the balance ₹385 crore went to Electro Pneumatics and Hydraulics (India) Private Limited.

The advanced mine trawls have been indigenously designed and developed by DRDO. BEML collaborated with DRDO's R&D Engineers unit based in Pune for development and production.

"Our collaboration with DRDO is a testament to the strength of India's defence R&D ecosystem" — Shantanu Roy, Chairman & Managing Director, BEML Limited.

The trawl systems use multi-layered mine-clearing mechanisms with rollers. They create safe passage for mechanised forces through minefields with anti-tank mines and proximity magnetic fuses.

This is a Buy (Indian-Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured) case. It reduces dependence on foreign imports and supports the Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative in defence manufacturing.

The project will generate direct and indirect employment through MSME component manufacturing. BEML shares closed at ₹1,770.50 on April 21, 2026, with a market capitalisation of ₹14,746 crore.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE VALUE OF THE DEFENCE CONTRACT WON BY BEML?
BEML LIMITED HAS WON A ₹590 CRORE CONTRACT FROM THE MINISTRY OF DEFENCE, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA, FOR THE SUPPLY OF TRAWL ASSEMBLIES FOR T-72 AND T-90 TANKS.
WHAT ARE TRAWL ASSEMBLIES AND WHY DO THEY MATTER?
TRAWL ASSEMBLIES ARE VEHICLE-MOUNTED MINE-CLEARING DEVICES FITTED TO THE FRONT OF TANKS. THEY USE ROLLERS TO PHYSICALLY DETONATE LANDMINES AND CREATE SAFE LANES FOR MECHANISED FORCES THROUGH MINEFIELDS WITH ANTI-TANK MINES AND PROXIMITY MAGNETIC FUSES.
WHO DEVELOPED THE TRAWL ASSEMBLY TECHNOLOGY?
THE TRAWL ASSEMBLIES WERE INDIGENOUSLY DESIGNED AND DEVELOPED BY DRDO IN COLLABORATION WITH BEML LIMITED. DRDO'S R&D ENGINEERS UNIT BASED IN PUNE LED THE DEVELOPMENT WORK.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL VALUE OF THE MINISTRY OF DEFENCE'S TRAWL PROCUREMENT?
THE MINISTRY OF DEFENCE SIGNED CONTRACTS WORTH ₹975 CRORE IN TOTAL. BEML LIMITED RECEIVED ₹590 CRORE AND ELECTRO PNEUMATICS AND HYDRAULICS (INDIA) PRIVATE LIMITED RECEIVED THE BALANCE ₹385 CRORE.
HOW DOES THIS ORDER ALIGN WITH INDIA'S DEFENCE POLICY?
THIS IS A BUY (INDIAN-INDIGENOUSLY DESIGNED, DEVELOPED AND MANUFACTURED) CASE. IT MARKS A PIVOTAL STEP TOWARDS MODERNISING INDIA'S DEFENCE INFRASTRUCTURE, REDUCING DEPENDENCE ON FOREIGN IMPORTS, AND EMPOWERING INDIGENOUS INDUSTRIES UNDER THE AATMANIRBHAR BHARAT INITIATIVE.
