CleanMax Bulk Deal: Augment India Sells ₹1,096 Crore Stake; Goldman, HSBC Buy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Augment India I Holdings LLC divested 85.03 lakh Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions shares worth about ₹1,096.07 crore through two NSE bulk deals on Monday
  • 02/HSBC India Infrastructure Equity Mother Fund, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance and Goldman Sachs Investments Mauritius were among the buyers, picking up 26.22 lakh shares for ₹337.86 crore
  • 03/Augment India Holdings held a 9.50% stake in CleanMax as of June 30, 2026; the stock closed at ₹1,378.35, down 1.02%, with a market cap of ₹16,202 crore

Augment India I Holdings LLC on Monday divested shares worth around ₹1,096.07 crore in Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions. The sale was executed through two bulk deals on the National Stock Exchange.

According to NSE bulk-deal data, Augment India I Holdings sold 8,69,152 shares at ₹1,288.40 apiece. It sold another 76,33,385 shares at ₹1,289.01 apiece.

The two transactions together involved 85,02,537 shares. Goldman Sachs Investments Mauritius I Limited and HSBC India Infrastructure Equity Mother Fund were among the buyers.

On the buying side, HSBC India Infrastructure Equity Mother Fund bought 7,88,000 shares at ₹1,288.40 apiece. ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company bought 11,15,384 shares at the same price.

Goldman Sachs Investments Mauritius I Limited purchased 7,18,539 shares at ₹1,289.04 apiece. Together, the three buyers acquired 26,21,923 shares, valued at around ₹337.86 crore.

According to BSE shareholding data, Augment India Holdings LLC held 1,11,40,172 shares as of June 30, 2026. That represented a 9.50% stake in Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions.

CleanMax shares ended Monday's session at ₹1,378.35 apiece, down 1.02% from the previous close of ₹1,392.55 on the BSE. The stock traded in a range of ₹1,473.90 to ₹1,301.00 during the session.

The power generation company had a market capitalisation of ₹16,202.42 crore on the BSE.
