ICICI Bank Q4 Profit Jumps 8.5% to ₹13,702 Crore; Declares ₹12 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICICI Bank Q4 FY26 net profit rose 8.5% to ₹13,702 crore versus ₹12,625 crore last year
  • 02/Net interest income grew 13% to ₹22,979 crore; NIM stood at 4.32%
  • 03/Board declared final dividend of ₹12 per share for FY26

ICICI Bank reported strong Q4 FY26 results on April 20, 2026. Net profit rose 8.5% year-on-year to ₹13,702 crore compared to ₹12,625 crore in Q4 FY25.

Net interest income grew 13% to ₹22,979 crore from ₹20,340 crore in the year-ago quarter. Net interest margin remained stable at 4.32% versus 4.30% sequentially.

Asset quality showed continued improvement. Gross NPA ratio declined to 1.40% from 1.53% quarter-on-quarter. Net NPA ratio improved to 0.33% from 0.37%.

Provisions stood at ₹96 crore compared to ₹891 crore in Q4 FY25. The sharp decline reflects improved asset quality and lower credit costs.

The Board of Directors declared a final dividend of ₹12 per equity share. This adds to the interim dividend already paid for FY26.

ICICI Bank is India's second-largest private sector lender. The results came in ahead of analyst estimates, driving the stock up over 2%.

The bank maintained its guidance for credit growth and NIM stability. Management remains confident despite global economic uncertainties.
