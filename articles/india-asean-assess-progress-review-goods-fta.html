India and ASEAN Assess Progress of Talks for Review of Goods FTA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India and ASEAN are taking stock of their trade relationship.

The two sides are assessing the progress of talks for a review of their goods free trade agreement. The existing pact, implemented over a decade ago, is being updated to reflect current trade realities and economic structures.

The review covers several key areas. Tariff liberalisation schedules are being examined to ensure both sides are gaining meaningful market access. Rules of origin provisions are being tightened to prevent third-party goods from routing through ASEAN to enter India at preferential rates.

Trade facilitation measures are also under discussion. Simplifying customs procedures, harmonising standards, and reducing non-tariff barriers could boost bilateral trade flows significantly.

India-ASEAN trade has grown substantially since the FTA was signed. However, India has consistently run a trade deficit with the bloc, importing more than it exports. The review aims to address this imbalance through improved market access for Indian goods and services.

The review process has been ongoing for several years. Multiple rounds of negotiations have been held, but disagreements over tariff schedules and sensitive product lists have slowed progress.

Both sides recognise the strategic importance of the relationship. ASEAN is India's fourth-largest trading partner, and India is ASEAN's seventh-largest. The review is seen as essential for keeping the economic partnership relevant in a rapidly changing global trade environment.
