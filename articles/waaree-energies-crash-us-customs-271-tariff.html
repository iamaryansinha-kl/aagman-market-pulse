Waaree Energies Crashes 5% After US Customs Slaps 271% Tariff on Solar Imports

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Waaree Energies shares fell nearly 5% after US Customs found the company evaded anti-dumping duties on solar cells from Vietnam and Malaysia
  • 02/US CBP imposed tariffs up to 271.28% on Waaree's imported modules; the ruling covers imports between 2021 and June 23, 2026
  • 03/JM Financial retains Add rating with Rs 3,185 target, but warns reputational damage may impact 65-70% of Waaree's Rs 53,000 crore order book

Waaree Energies Ltd (NSE: WAAREE) crashed nearly 5% on Monday. The stock hit Rs 2,860.40 on NSE, its lowest level since March 17, 2026.

The sell-off followed a US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) ruling on June 23, 2026. CBP found that Waaree had evaded anti-dumping and countervailing duties on solar cells imported from Vietnam and Malaysia.

The agency imposed anti-dumping tariffs of up to 271.28% on Waaree's imported solar modules. The ruling covers imports spanning from 2021 through June 23, 2026.

Waaree Energies issued a clarification on Sunday. The company said the investigation did not find Chinese solar cells in its panels. It added that the probe was limited to a narrow set of historical import entries with no impact on ongoing US operations.

The investigation stemmed from a 2025 petition by the American Alliance for Solar Manufacturing Trade Committee. The petition flagged data showing Waaree's sharply rising imports of Chinese solar cells into India and then onward to the US.

JM Financial maintained its Add rating on Waaree Energies. The brokerage set a target price of Rs 3,185 apiece, implying nearly 6% upside from the previous close.

However, JM Financial noted that the CBP finding carries reputational weight. The brokerage warned it could impact a significant part of 65-70% of Waaree's total order book of Rs 53,000 crore.

"The downside looks limited as CBP confirmed Waaree had enough non-Chinese-cell production to cover its US shipments," JM Financial said. It added that the agency chose not to apply a blanket adverse-inference ruling and restricted the finding to certain historical entries linked to Vietnam and Malaysia.

Waaree Energies shares have fallen over 5% in one week and nearly 8% in one month. The stock is down around 2% in 2026 so far. The company currently has a market capitalisation of less than Rs 83,300 crore.

Key monitorables include the quantum of retroactive duties, the outcome of any de novo administrative or judicial appeal by Waaree, and any spillover effect on the pending 123.04% preliminary AD/CVD determination covering India-origin solar imports.
