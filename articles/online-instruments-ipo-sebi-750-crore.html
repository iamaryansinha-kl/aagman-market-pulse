Online Instruments IPO: SEBI Filing for ₹750 Crore Fresh Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Online Instruments files DRHP with SEBI for ₹750 crore fresh issue plus OFS of 57.10 lakh shares by promoters
  • 02/FY25 revenue jumped to ₹547.4 crore from ₹335.9 crore in FY23; net profit rose to ₹35.3 crore from ₹15.5 crore
  • 03/Proceeds earmarked: ₹160 crore for debt repayment, ₹330 crore for working capital; pre-IPO placement of ₹150 crore possible

Online Instruments (India) Ltd has filed draft papers with SEBI for an IPO. The offering comprises a ₹750 crore fresh issue. The Bengaluru-based company may also do a pre-IPO placement of up to ₹150 crore.

Promoters Anita Mahesh Bellad and Rajeshwari Shivanand Mahashetti will sell up to 57.10 lakh shares via an Offer For Sale. The DRHP was filed on Friday.

The company plans to use ₹160 crore from the fresh issue to repay borrowings. Another ₹330 crore will fund working capital requirements. Remaining proceeds will support acquisitions and general corporate purposes.

Incorporated in 2006, Online Instruments provides audiovisual systems integration solutions. It manufactures interactive flat panels, LED displays, and accessories under the LOGIC brand. It also makes white-labelled products for OEMs.

The company operates three manufacturing facilities in Bengaluru. It has executed AVSI projects across India and overseas markets including Singapore, Malaysia, UAE, the US, and France.

As of December 31, 2025, it had served 745 customers in India. These span sectors such as enterprise, healthcare, banking, manufacturing, retail, education, and airports.

Revenue from operations rose to ₹547.4 crore in FY25 from ₹335.9 crore in FY23. Net profit doubled to ₹35.3 crore from ₹15.5 crore during the same period.

Equirus Capital and Motilal Investment Advisors are the book-running lead managers to the issue. The company has not yet disclosed the price band or listing timeline.
