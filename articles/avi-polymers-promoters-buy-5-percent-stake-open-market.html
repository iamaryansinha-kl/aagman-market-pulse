AVI Polymers Promoters to Buy 5% Stake via Open Market After FY26 Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Promoter group of AVI Polymers plans to acquire up to 5% of total equity through open market transactions, disclosed on April 30, 2026
  • 02/FY26 revenue surged to ₹312.11 crore with PAT at ₹20.33 crore; company achieved debt-free status and net worth of ₹115.99 crore
  • 03/Acquisition will follow SEBI creeping acquisition rules in phases; watch execution timeline and impact on thin promoter holding of 1.10%

The promoter group of AVI Polymers Ltd (BSE: 539288) plans to acquire up to 5 per cent of the company's total paid-up equity share capital through open market purchases. The company disclosed the plan on April 30, 2026.

The acquisition will be carried out in a phased manner over the coming months. It will comply with SEBI's creeping acquisition regulations and all applicable insider trading requirements.

The planned stake hike follows a sharp turnaround in FY26. AVI Polymers reported revenue of ₹312.11 crore and net profit of ₹20.33 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.

Net profit grew 25 times year-on-year. The balance sheet turned fully debt-free, and net worth expanded to ₹115.99 crore.

The company also rolled out two wholly-owned AI-led subsidiaries. KrishiBuddy and AVI Health AI are now operational and aim to unlock new revenue streams beyond the core polymer trading business.

Chintan Patel, Managing Director of AVI Polymers, said the promoter move reflects conviction in the company's long-term growth potential. "As we continue to build a technology-driven business with new-age platforms such as KrishiBuddy and AVI Health AI, we believe the current valuation presents a compelling opportunity," he said.

AVI Polymers shares closed at ₹19.02 on April 29, up 4.97 per cent. The stock has gained roughly 50 per cent over the past year from its 52-week low of ₹10.61.

Promoter holding stood at just 1.10 per cent as of March 2026. The planned 5 per cent acquisition would raise it to approximately 6.10 per cent, a meaningful shift in ownership structure.
