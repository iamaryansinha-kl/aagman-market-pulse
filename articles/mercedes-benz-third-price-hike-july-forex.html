Mercedes-Benz Plans Third Price Hike in July on Forex Surge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mercedes-Benz India plans third price hike in Q3 FY2026 due to significant exchange rate deterioration, after two increases already
  • 02/Company launches CLA BEV starting at ₹55 lakh; 400+ bookings received with 60% first-time luxury buyers
  • 03/Santosh Iyer warns demand could soften if prices keep rising; deliveries of CLA to commence soon

Mercedes-Benz India is planning a third price hike in the third quarter starting July 2026. Managing Director Santosh Iyer cited significant exchange rate deterioration as the primary driver.

The company has already implemented two price increases to offset forex volatility. Supply chain delays are also contributing to cost pressures.

Iyer said buffer stocks have kept supply-side challenges manageable. However, rising exchange rates remain the bigger worry for the German luxury carmaker.

"We have to be careful on the demand side as well – that we cannot continue to increase prices and expect demand to be the same," Iyer said.

The company launched the CLA battery electric vehicle with introductory prices starting at ₹55 lakh. Three variants are on offer.

The CLA 200 'Standard Range' is priced at ₹55 lakh. The CLA 250+ 'Long Range' costs ₹59 lakh. The CLA BEV 'Launch Edition' is at ₹64 lakh.

The CLA 250+ offers 792 km range, 0-100 kmph in 6.7 seconds, and 85.5 kWh battery. Mercedes has received over 400 bookings.

About 60 per cent of CLA buyers are first-time luxury car customers. The majority are Gen Z and millennials, Iyer disclosed.
