Torrent Power Plans ₹4,000 Crore Bond Issue to Fund Nabha Power Buy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Torrent Power plans to raise ₹4,000 crore ($421.8 million) via its largest-ever corporate bond issue, with market entry expected in May 2026
  • 02/Proceeds will bankroll the ₹6,900 crore acquisition of Nabha Power from Larsen & Toubro, approved by India's competition regulator earlier this month
  • 03/The AA+ rated bonds may feature staggered redemptions and a three-year tranche to attract mutual funds, alongside longer-tenor notes

Torrent Power Ltd plans to raise as much as ₹4,000 crore through its largest-ever corporate bond issue. The power producer expects to tap the market in May, with deal discussions likely to conclude within the next week.

Three merchant bankers told Reuters that this marks the first major acquisition-financing bond sale of the current financial year. Proceeds will fully fund Torrent Power's acquisition of Nabha Power.

In February, Torrent Power agreed to buy Nabha Power from Larsen & Toubro for approximately ₹6,900 crore including debt. India's competition regulator approved the deal earlier this month, clearing the path for closure.

Nabha Power operates a 1,400-megawatt coal-fired plant in Punjab. It supplies all output to the state power corporation under a 25-year power purchase agreement.

The planned bond issue is expected to feature staggered redemptions. Principal will be repaid in multiple installments rather than as a lump sum at maturity.

The company may also issue a three-year tranche to attract large mutual funds. This would lower the weighted average cost of borrowing, one banker said.

"The company has also tied up with some mutual funds, so it is highly possible that they end up splitting the quantum in shorter as well as longer tenors," one of the bankers told Reuters.

The bonds are rated AA+ by Crisil and India Ratings. In March, Torrent Power raised ₹2,000 crore via staggered redemption bonds maturing in eight, nine and 10 years at an annual coupon of 7.97%.

The acquisition adds to Torrent Power's push to scale its thermal portfolio. Indian utilities are turning to coal-based capacity to meet the country's surging electricity demand.

Bond issuance to fund acquisitions gathered pace in 2025 as companies diversified away from bank loans. Torrent Power's deal signals the trend is continuing into the new financial year.
