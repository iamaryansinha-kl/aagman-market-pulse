Tata AMC Launches Titanium Equity Long-Short SIF, NFO Closes May 11

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Asset Management launches its second Specialised Investment Fund, Titanium Equity Long-Short SIF, with NFO closing on May 11, 2026
  • 02/Minimum investment is ₹10 lakh; net equity exposure can shift between -25% and 100% based on market conditions
  • 03/Fund maintains minimum 80% gross equity allocation and can take unhedged short positions up to 25% of portfolio value

Tata Asset Management has launched the Titanium Equity Long-Short Fund. It is the company's second Specialised Investment Fund offering.

The New Fund Offer closes on May 11, 2026. The minimum investment required is ₹10 lakh per investor.

The fund targets medium-to-long-term capital appreciation. It actively manages both long and short exposures in equities and equity derivatives.

Net equity exposure can vary between -25% and 100%. The fund adjusts positioning dynamically based on prevailing market conditions.

It maintains a minimum 80% gross allocation to listed equity. Derivatives hedging is permitted alongside unhedged short positions up to 25% of portfolio value.

Anand Vardarajan, Chief Business Officer at Tata Asset Management, said the SIF framework bridges traditional mutual funds and AIF or PMS structures. It allows sophisticated strategies in a tax-efficient wrapper.

Suraj Nanda, Fund Manager of Titanium SIF, noted the fund can morph into aggressive long, short, or arbitrage-like positioning. This flexibility aims to convert volatility into opportunity across all market phases.

Traditional equity products keep net equity high at all valuations. The Titanium fund dynamically allocates based on market valuations, seeking returns less dependent on market direction.
