Adani Power Enters Nuclear Energy with New Subsidiary CMAEL

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Power has created a new subsidiary, CMAEL, to enter nuclear energy
  • 02/The subsidiary will explore opportunities in nuclear power generation
  • 03/This aligns with government policy to allow private sector participation in nuclear energy

Adani Power Ltd has taken steps to enter the nuclear energy sector. The company has incorporated a new wholly-owned subsidiary, CMAEL.

The subsidiary will explore opportunities in nuclear power generation. This marks Adani Power's diversification beyond conventional thermal power.

The move aligns with recent government policy changes. The centre has opened nuclear power to private sector participation.

However, safety and fuel management remain under government oversight. Private players can operate plants while the state retains control of uranium.

Adani Power is one of India's largest private power producers. The company has been diversifying into renewable energy sources.

Nuclear power offers baseload electricity without carbon emissions. It complements intermittent renewable sources like solar and wind.

The government's nuclear policy aims to increase clean energy capacity. Private participation is expected to accelerate capacity addition.
