SB Energy to Sell Up to $500 Million in Shares to Japanese Investors Ahead of US IPO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SB Energy will sell up to $500 million worth of shares to Japanese investors as it prepares for a US listing, a regulatory filing showed
  • 02/Proceeds will fund operating expenses for data centres, power generation facilities and associated infrastructure
  • 03/Nvidia has committed to invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy through a private placement at the IPO price
  • 04/OpenAI holds warrants valued at roughly $5.5 billion in the company
  • 05/SoftBank could seek a valuation of about $50 billion for the data centre developer, Reuters has reported
