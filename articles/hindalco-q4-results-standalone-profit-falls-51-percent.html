Hindalco Q4 Results: Standalone Profit Plunges 51% to Rs 2,597 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindalco Industries' standalone net profit for Q4 FY26 fell 51% year-on-year to Rs 2,597 crore.
  • 02/The company declared a dividend for FY26, signalling confidence in cash flows despite quarterly volatility.
  • 03/Consolidated revenue stood at Rs 64,890 crore, up 16% YoY, with net profit at Rs 5,283 crore.

Hindalco Industries reported its fourth-quarter results for FY26 on May 22, 2026. The standalone net profit dropped sharply by 51% year-on-year to Rs 2,597 crore.

The decline reflects margin pressure in the aluminium and copper segments. Input costs and global commodity price volatility weighed on profitability during the quarter.

On a consolidated basis, the picture was stronger. Revenue grew 16% YoY to Rs 64,890 crore. Consolidated net profit surged 66.4% to Rs 5,283 crore.

The divergence between standalone and consolidated numbers is driven by Novelis, Hindalco's US-based subsidiary. Novelis contributed significantly to the consolidated profit.

India's aluminium upstream business continued to outperform with industry-best EBITDA margins of 45%. The downstream segment also saw record volumes during the quarter.

Novelis faced headwinds from the Oswego disruption and tariffs but managed sequential improvement. The company is on track to deliver $150 million in cost savings by FY26-end.

Hindalco's board recommended a final dividend of Rs 5 per share for FY26. The payout reflects management confidence in cash flow generation despite quarterly earnings volatility.

The company has gained about 70% over the past year. Analysts maintain target prices near Rs 1,125, suggesting further upside potential.
