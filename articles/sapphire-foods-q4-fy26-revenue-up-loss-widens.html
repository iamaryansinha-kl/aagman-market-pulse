Sapphire Foods Q4 FY26: Revenue Up 11% But Loss Widens to ₹12.6 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sapphire Foods India Ltd (NSE: SAPPHIRE) reports Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹789.8 crore, up 11% YoY, its best quarterly performance in 12 quarters
  • 02/Consolidated net loss widens to ₹12.6 crore vs ₹2 crore profit in Q4 FY25; FY26 net loss at ₹32 crore vs ₹16.7 crore profit in FY25
  • 03/KFC India revenue surges 15% to ₹549.5 crore but Pizza Hut India revenue falls 6% to ₹117.4 crore; 1,052 restaurants as of March 31, 2026

Sapphire Foods India Ltd reported its strongest quarter in 12 quarters. Q4 FY26 consolidated revenue rose 11% YoY to ₹789.8 crore.

The growth was driven by a sharp recovery at KFC India. The brand posted its highest quarterly revenue growth in eight quarters.

Despite the top-line uptick, the company remained in the red. It posted a consolidated net loss of ₹12.6 crore against a profit of ₹2 crore in Q4 FY25.

KFC India revenue jumped 15% to ₹549.5 crore. Same-store sales grew 4%, or 6% excluding the Chaitra Navratri impact, the best in 14 quarters.

Restaurant EBITDA margin at KFC improved 110 bps to 16.8%. Gross margin gains and aggressive value offers such as the ₹99 Chicken Krisper Burger Meal drove footfall.

Pizza Hut India remained a drag. Quarterly revenue fell 6% to ₹117.4 crore and SSSG declined 7%, with restaurant EBITDA margin worsening to -6.0%.

For FY26, consolidated revenue stood at ₹3,115.9 crore, up 8% YoY. Adjusted EBITDA declined 9% to ₹238.2 crore, with margins compressing 150 bps to 7.6%.

Exceptional items of ₹12.8 crore in Q4 — relating to labour code changes and ESOP modification costs tied to the announced merger with Devyani International — weighed on the bottom line.

The Sri Lanka business delivered its sixth consecutive quarter of double-digit SSSG at 11%. Restaurant sales rose 15% to LKR 422.1 crore in local currency terms.

Total restaurant count stood at 1,052 as of March 31, 2026. The stock closed at ₹174.50, down 0.81%, and has fallen nearly 46% over the past year.
