Sensex Surges 609 Pts to 77,496; Nifty Tops 24,170 on Earnings Boost

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The BSE Sensex closed at 77,496.36, up 609.45 points or 0.79%, while the NSE Nifty 50 settled at 24,177.65, up 181.95 points or 0.76% on April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki surged 4.6%, ITC gained 3.8%, and Tech Mahindra rose 3.6% as quarterly earnings drove stock-specific action across sectors.
  • 03/The rupee hit a record closing low of 94.8450 per dollar; Brent crude stayed near $104 amid the UAE's announced OPEC exit effective May 1.

Indian equity benchmarks closed higher on April 29, 2026. The BSE Sensex settled at 77,496.36, gaining 609.45 points or 0.79%.

The NSE Nifty 50 index ended at 24,177.65, up 181.95 points or 0.76%. Gains were driven by stock-specific action following quarterly earnings.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) surged 4.6% to lead Nifty gainers. ITC Ltd rose 3.8%, while Tech Mahindra Ltd climbed 3.6%.

Among Nifty laggards, InterGlobe Aviation Ltd slipped 1.8% and Dr Reddy's Laboratories Ltd fell 1%. Realty and FMCG sectors led sectoral gains, each advancing over 2%.

"Profit booking in the final hours trimmed early gains," said Ajit Mishra, SVP of Research at Religare Broking. The Nifty had surged over 1% at mid-session.

The rupee hit a record closing low of 94.8450 per US dollar, down 0.3%. Brent crude futures traded near $104 after the UAE announced its OPEC exit from May 1.

Foreign institutional investors sold Rs 2,104 crore on April 28, while domestic institutional investors bought Rs 1,712 crore. FII outflows have remained a persistent headwind for Indian equities.

Gaurav Udani of ThinCredBlu Securities noted that 23,900–24,000 is immediate Nifty support, while 24,200–24,300 acts as key resistance. Traders await the US Federal Reserve policy decision due later today.
