Aditya Birla Fashion Q4 FY26: Net Loss Widens to Rs 163.81 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Aditya Birla Fashion reports Q4 FY26 net loss of Rs 163.81 crore against net profit of Rs 23.55 crore in the year-ago period
  • 02/Revenue grew 15.74% to Rs 1,990.13 crore, the highest organic growth in 12 quarters, but expenses rose 16% to Rs 2,287.58 crore
  • 03/FY26 full-year loss widened to Rs 829.89 crore on revenue of Rs 8,486.53 crore; shares closed at Rs 67.46 on May 25

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Ltd (NSE: ABFRL) reported a consolidated net loss of Rs 163.81 crore for Q4 FY26. The company had posted a net profit of Rs 23.55 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue from operations grew 15.74% to Rs 1,990.13 crore in the March quarter. It stood at Rs 1,719.48 crore in the year-ago period.

Total expenses increased 16% to Rs 2,287.58 crore during the quarter. The faster rise in costs compared to revenue growth squeezed margins further.

The company described this as its "highest organic growth in the last 12 quarters". Consumption trends remain stable with demand in line with the previous quarter.

The quarter saw fewer wedding dates compared to the corresponding period last year. However, value retail continues to grow, driven by small-town expansions.

ABFRL added 70 new stores in Q4 across its Pantaloons, Style Up, OWND, TMRW, TASVA, and TCNS portfolio. It also owns ethnic brands including Sabyasachi and Jaypore.

For the full FY26, the consolidated loss widened to Rs 829.89 crore. Total revenue climbed 12.29% to Rs 8,486.53 crore.

Shares of Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Ltd settled at Rs 67.46 on May 25, 2026, down 0.07% from the previous close.

REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS GREW 15.74% TO RS 1,990.13 CRORE IN Q4 FY26, COMPARED TO RS 1,719.48 CRORE IN THE SAME QUARTER LAST YEAR. THE COMPANY DESCRIBED THIS AS ITS HIGHEST ORGANIC GROWTH IN THE LAST 12 QUARTERS.
WHAT WERE THE TOTAL EXPENSES IN Q4 FY26?
TOTAL EXPENSES INCREASED 16% TO RS 2,287.58 CRORE IN THE MARCH QUARTER. THE FASTER RISE IN COSTS COMPARED TO REVENUE CONTRIBUTED TO THE WIDER NET LOSS.
WHAT IS THE FULL-YEAR FY26 PERFORMANCE OF ABFRL?
FOR THE ENTIRE FINANCIAL YEAR 2025-26, ABFRL'S CONSOLIDATED LOSS WIDENED TO RS 829.89 CRORE. TOTAL CONSOLIDATED REVENUE ROSE 12.29% TO RS 8,486.53 CRORE.
