Pristine Logistics Files DRHP With Sebi for IPO Under Confidential Route

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Pristine Logistics & Infraprojects confidentially files DRHP with Sebi under the regulator's pre-filing route for a proposed IPO
  • 02/Company operates 12 terminals, 5,000+ domestic rail containers, and ~1.2 million sq ft warehousing as of December 2025
  • 03/Recently secured a ₹3,422 crore overburden excavation contract from South Eastern Coalfields Limited, adding long-term revenue visibility

Pristine Logistics and Infraprojects Ltd has confidentially filed its draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) with capital markets regulator Sebi. The filing was made under the regulator's pre-filing route, marking a key step toward a proposed initial public offering.

Founded in 2008, the company operates an integrated rail-focused multimodal logistics platform. It offers transportation and infrastructure solutions for both containerised and non-containerised cargo across domestic and export-import corridors.

The company has emerged as one of India's fastest-growing rail-focused multimodal logistics players in terms of revenue and EBITDA growth between FY23 and FY25. Its terminal network expanded from eight to twelve terminals during this period.

Container volumes grew from 402,049 TEUs in FY23 to 506,447 TEUs in FY25. Non-containerised cargo volumes rose from 1.92 million metric tonnes to 2.51 million metric tonnes in the same period.

As of December 31, 2025, Pristine operated more than 5,000 domestic rail containers and around 455 specialised 40-foot dwarf containers. It manages a warehousing network of approximately 1.2 million square feet across India.

The company is expanding with new terminals at Bhurkunda in Jharkhand, Haldia in West Bengal, and Bengaluru in Karnataka. The Haldia facility will mark its entry into port-based liquid cargo logistics.

Pristine recently secured a long-term overburden excavation and removal contract worth ₹3,422 crore from South Eastern Coalfields Limited. This provides significant long-term revenue visibility for the mining logistics vertical.

The company offers a diversified portfolio including EXIM and domestic container logistics, rail transportation, bulk rail logistics, warehousing, and container maintenance. It also acquired Sical Logistics to strengthen its presence in south India.
