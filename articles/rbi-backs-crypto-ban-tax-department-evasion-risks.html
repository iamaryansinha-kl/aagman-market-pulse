RBI Backs Crypto Ban as Tax Department Warns of Evasion Risks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's regulatory stance on cryptocurrencies is hardening.

The Reserve Bank of India has reiterated its support for a ban on private cryptocurrencies. The central bank has consistently argued that digital assets pose risks to financial stability, monetary sovereignty, and investor protection.

Separately, the tax department has warned about tax evasion risks associated with cryptocurrency transactions. The anonymity and cross-border nature of digital asset trades make them attractive to those seeking to hide income from tax authorities.

The RBI's position has been clear for years. The central bank has repeatedly called for an outright ban on private cryptocurrencies, preferring instead to develop a central bank digital currency. The digital rupee pilot is already underway in select cities.

The tax department's concerns add another layer of regulatory pressure. India currently taxes cryptocurrency gains at 30% and imposes a 1% tax deducted at source on transactions. However, enforcement remains challenging given the decentralised nature of crypto markets.

Global regulatory approaches vary. Some countries have embraced cryptocurrencies with clear frameworks, while others have imposed strict restrictions. India's position aligns more closely with China's outright ban than with the United States' regulated approach.

The combined stance from the RBI and tax department makes it unlikely that India will soften its position on private cryptocurrencies in the near term. Investors and exchanges operating in India face an uncertain regulatory environment.
