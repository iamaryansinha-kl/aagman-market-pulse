Nifty Crashes Below 24,000 as Crude Surges Past $105 on Iran Standoff

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 plunged 360.30 points (1.49%) to close at 23,815.85, its lowest level in three weeks
  • 02/Brent crude surged roughly 4% to around $105.7/barrel after Trump rejected Iran's peace proposal
  • 03/Rupee hit a fresh record low of 95.32 against the dollar, while India VIX jumped over 10% to 18.5

The Nifty 50 crashed below the 24,000 mark on Monday, May 11, 2026. The index closed at 23,815.85, down 360.30 points or 1.49 per cent.

The Sensex shed 1,312.91 points, or 1.70 per cent, to settle at 76,015.28. Selling was broad-based, with 382 stocks in the Nifty 500 universe ending in the red.

The immediate trigger was the collapse of US-Iran peace talks. US President Donald Trump rejected Iran's proposal, dismissing demands around the Strait of Hormuz blockade as "totally unacceptable."

Brent crude surged roughly 4 per cent to around $105.7 a barrel, and domestic futures climbed above ₹9,200. The rupee hit a fresh record low of 95.32 against the dollar, with the USDINR pair facing resistance at 95.45–95.80.

"Selling pressure was broad-based, with most sectoral indices closing lower... the sharp correction was primarily triggered by a spike in crude oil prices," said Ajit Mishra, SVP Research at Religare Broking.

Consumer Durables fell 3.7 per cent, and Realty declined sharply. Titan Company was among the top Nifty losers, while Kalyan Jewellers and Senco Gold also slid steeply.

Pharma and Healthcare were the only sectors to eke out marginal gains. They benefited from their defensive character and the rupee-depreciation tailwind on export earnings.

AMFI data showed domestic mutual fund investors remained steady with net equity inflows of ₹38,440 crore in April. Analysts warn that a decisive Nifty break below 23,800 could accelerate a move toward the 23,500–23,150 zone.

BRENT CRUDE SURGED ROUGHLY 4 PER CENT TO AROUND $105.7 PER BARREL. DOMESTIC CRUDE FUTURES CLIMBED ABOVE ₹9,200 AFTER TRUMP REJECTED IRAN'S PEACE PROPOSAL AND THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ REMAINED SHUT.
WHAT HAPPENED TO THE INDIAN RUPEE?
THE RUPEE HIT A FRESH RECORD LOW, PLUNGING NEARLY 1 PER CENT TO 95.32 AGAINST THE DOLLAR IN EARLY TRADE. THE USDINR PAIR NOW FACES RESISTANCE AT 95.45–95.80.
