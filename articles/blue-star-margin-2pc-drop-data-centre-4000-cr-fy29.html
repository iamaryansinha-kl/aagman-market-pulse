Blue Star Margin to Fall 2% This Summer, Bets on ₹4,000 Cr Data Centre Push

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Blue Star MD Thiagarajan sees 2-2.5% operating margin drop this summer despite 35% AC volume growth
  • 02/Input costs surged 13% but only 8% passed on to consumers; FY27 margin guidance cut to 7-7.5%
  • 03/Data centre revenue target ₹4,000 crore by FY29 with MEP order book of ₹2,000 crore; festive season recovery uncertain

Blue Star Managing Director B. Thiagarajan confirmed the room AC industry grew 35% by volume and 30% by value this summer. He said the company faces a 2-2.5% operating margin drop despite strong demand.

Input costs for copper, steel, aluminium, and plastics climbed 13% through May. Blue Star passed on roughly 8% to consumers. The remaining 5% landed directly on margins.

The company had built inventory earlier at lower prices. But it imported materials at higher costs as the summer peaked, pushing the weighted average cost up at the worst possible time.

Thiagarajan revised the full-year operating margin guidance to 7-7.5% for FY27. He had earlier guided for 8-8.5%. A meaningful recovery hinges on the festive season, which he called uncertain.

On the data centre front, Blue Star is making a three-pronged push. It leads the MEP and EPC cooling market with an order book of ₹2,000 crore in a ₹3,000-4,000 crore market.

In conventional data centre chillers, Blue Star holds a 15-20% share of a ₹1,250-1,500 crore market. The company is now entering liquid cooling and CDU partnerships for the next generation.

Thiagarajan expects data centres to account for 20% of total revenue by FY29. Blue Star targets ₹20,000 crore overall revenue, implying roughly ₹4,000 crore from the data centre vertical alone.

"We are keeping our fingers crossed," Thiagarajan said about the festive season. "The lost margins to regain will always be very difficult." He expects cost relief in three to four months.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH IS BLUE STAR'S OPERATING MARGIN EXPECTED TO DROP THIS SUMMER?
BLUE STAR MANAGING DIRECTOR B. THIAGARAJAN EXPECTS THE OPERATING MARGIN TO DROP BY 2% TO 2.5% THIS SUMMER. NORMALLY, IN A STRONG SUMMER SEASON, THE OPERATING MARGIN IS AROUND 10%.
WHAT CAUSED THE MARGIN PRESSURE FOR BLUE STAR?
INPUT COSTS FOR COPPER, STEEL, ALUMINIUM, PLASTICS, AND PETROLEUM-BASED MATERIALS ROSE APPROXIMATELY 13% THROUGH MAY. BLUE STAR WAS ABLE TO PASS ON ONLY AROUND 8% TO CONSUMERS, LEAVING THE REMAINING 5% TO HIT MARGINS DIRECTLY.
WHAT IS BLUE STAR'S REVISED MARGIN GUIDANCE FOR FY27?
THIAGARAJAN REVISED THE FULL-YEAR OPERATING MARGIN GUIDANCE TO 7-7.5% FOR FY27. HE HAD EARLIER GUIDED FOR 8-8.5% AT THE START OF THE SUMMER SEASON.
WHAT IS BLUE STAR'S DATA CENTRE REVENUE TARGET?
BLUE STAR EXPECTS DATA CENTRES TO CONTRIBUTE APPROXIMATELY 20% OF TOTAL REVENUE BY FY29. WITH AN OVERALL REVENUE TARGET OF ₹20,000 CRORE, THIS IMPLIES ROUGHLY ₹4,000 CRORE FROM THE DATA CENTRE VERTICAL ALONE.
WHEN DOES BLUE STAR EXPECT COST RELIEF?
THIAGARAJAN ESTIMATES IT WILL TAKE ANOTHER THREE TO FOUR MONTHS BEFORE ANY MEANINGFUL CORRECTION IN RAW MATERIAL COSTS SETS IN. FURTHER PRICE HIKES TO CONSUMERS ARE NOT RULED OUT.
