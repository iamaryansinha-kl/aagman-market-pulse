Vedanta Ex-Demerger, BHEL-DRDO Pact: 9 Stocks to Watch on April 29

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Ltd goes ex-demerger on April 30; investors must buy by April 29 to receive 1:1 shares in four new listed entities
  • 02/BHEL signs DRDO technology transfer pact for naval infrared systems; Enviro Infra's EIE Renewables acquires Suyog Urja for ₹311 crore
  • 03/Peak XV exits MobiKwik stake for ₹133 crore at ₹214.01/share; RVNL bags ₹39.21 crore NMDC order in Hyderabad

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) goes ex-demerger on April 30, 2026. Investors must buy shares by April 29 to qualify for the corporate action.

Eligible shareholders will receive one share each in four new entities. These are Vedanta Aluminium Metal, Vedanta Power, Vedanta Oil & Gas, and Vedanta Iron and Steel.

The record date is May 1, 2026, which falls on Maharashtra Day holiday. Vedanta shares hit an all-time high of ₹794.90 on April 21 ahead of the event.

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (NSE: BHEL) signed a technology transfer agreement with DRDO's NSTL. The pact covers Gas Turbine Infrared Suppression Systems for naval vessels.

EIE Renewables, a unit of Enviro Infra Engineers, will acquire Suyog Urja for ₹311 crore. It will pay for 51% equity upfront and the remaining 49% by July 31, 2028.

Peak XV Partners sold its entire 7.89% stake in MobiKwik for ₹133.01 crore. The bulk deal priced shares at ₹214.01 each.

Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd (NSE: RVNL) bagged a ₹39.21 crore order from NMDC for a residential campus in Hyderabad. Adroit Infotech won a ₹23 crore SAP contract from an Indonesian firm.

OneSource Specialty Pharma received EU GMP certification for its Bengaluru sterile facility. Wanbury cleared Korea FDA inspection with zero observations. Solara Active Pharma got US FDA VAI status, though approval timelines remain uncertain.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE VEDANTA DEMERGER EX-DATE AND RECORD DATE?
VEDANTA GOES EX-DEMERGER ON APRIL 30, 2026. THE RECORD DATE IS MAY 1, 2026, WHICH IS A MARKET HOLIDAY DUE TO MAHARASHTRA DAY. INVESTORS MUST BUY SHARES BY APRIL 29 TO BE ELIGIBLE UNDER THE T+1 SETTLEMENT CYCLE.
WHAT WILL VEDANTA SHAREHOLDERS RECEIVE IN THE DEMERGER?
ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE ONE SHARE EACH IN FOUR NEW ENTITIES FOR EVERY VEDANTA SHARE HELD: VEDANTA ALUMINIUM METAL LTD, VEDANTA POWER LTD (FORMERLY TALWANDI SABO POWER), VEDANTA OIL & GAS LTD (FORMERLY MALCO ENERGY), AND VEDANTA IRON AND STEEL LTD.
WHAT IS THE BHEL-DRDO TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER AGREEMENT ABOUT?
BHEL SIGNED A LICENSING AGREEMENT FOR TRANSFER OF TECHNOLOGY WITH DRDO'S NAVAL SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGICAL LABORATORY (NSTL) FOR GAS TURBINE INFRARED SUPPRESSION SYSTEMS (GT-IRSS) MEANT FOR NAVAL VESSELS.
HOW MUCH DID EIE RENEWABLES PAY TO ACQUIRE SUYOG URJA?
EIE RENEWABLES, A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF ENVIRO INFRA ENGINEERS, ENTERED INTO A SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE SUYOG URJA LIMITED FOR ₹311 CRORE. IT WILL BUY 51% IMMEDIATELY AND THE REMAINING 49% BY JULY 31, 2028. SUYOG URJA HAD A TURNOVER OF ₹171.99 CRORE AND NET WORTH OF ₹30.34 CRORE AS OF MARCH 31, 2025.
WHO EXITED THE MOBIKWIK STAKE AND FOR HOW MUCH?
PEAK XV PARTNERS SOLD 62.15 LAKH SHARES OR 7.89% STAKE IN MOBIKWIK FOR ₹133.01 CRORE AT ₹214.01 PER SHARE. BUYERS INCLUDED VIRIDIAN ASIA OPPORTUNITIES MASTER FUND (13.2 LAKH SHARES), SOCIETE GENERALE (6.6 LAKH SHARES), AND ELIMATH ADVISORS (30 LAKH SHARES).
