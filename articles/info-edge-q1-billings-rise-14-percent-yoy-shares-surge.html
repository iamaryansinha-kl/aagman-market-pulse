Info Edge Q1 Billings Rise 14% YoY; Shares Surge 11%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Info Edge has delivered a strong start to FY27.

The online classifieds company reported a 14% year-on-year increase in billings for the first quarter. The growth was driven by its recruitment platform Naukri.com and real estate portal 99acres.

Shares of Info Edge surged 11% following the quarterly update. Investors responded positively to the double-digit billing growth, which signals healthy demand from employers and property advertisers.

Naukri.com remains India's largest job portal. The platform has benefited from sustained hiring activity across IT, financial services, and manufacturing sectors. The recruitment market has remained resilient despite broader economic concerns.

99acres, the real estate listings platform, also contributed to growth. India's property market has shown signs of recovery in major metros, with both residential and commercial segments seeing increased transaction volumes.

Info Edge holds significant stakes in several unlisted companies including Zomato and Policybazaar. The value of these investments has grown substantially, adding to the company's overall market capitalisation.

The company has been investing in technology and artificial intelligence to improve its platforms. AI-powered matching and recommendation tools are being rolled out across Naukri and 99acres to enhance user experience.
