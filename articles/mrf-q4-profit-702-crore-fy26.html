MRF Q4 Profit Jumps 38% to ₹702 Crore on Strong Sales Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MRF Ltd reports 38% jump in Q4 consolidated net profit to ₹702.25 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026
  • 02/Consolidated revenue from operations grew to ₹8,044.22 crore against ₹7,074.82 crore in the year-ago period
  • 03/Board recommends final dividend of ₹229 per share; FY26 full-year profit up to ₹2,426.1 crore from ₹1,873.29 crore

MRF Ltd reported consolidated net profit of ₹702.25 crore in Q4 FY26. This marks a 38% rise from ₹510.5 crore in the same quarter last year. The tyre maker announced results on Thursday.

Consolidated revenue from operations stood at ₹8,044.22 crore in the quarter. This compares to ₹7,074.82 crore in Q4 FY25. The growth came on the back of strong sales momentum.

Total expenses during the quarter increased to ₹7,267.51 crore. In the year-ago period, expenses were ₹6,531.04 crore. The company filed the numbers in a regulatory disclosure.

The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹229 per share. The face value of each share is ₹10. The payout is subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming annual general meeting.

For the full financial year FY26, consolidated net profit rose to ₹2,426.1 crore. This is up from ₹1,873.29 crore recorded in FY25. The annual profit growth stands at approximately 29.5%.

Consolidated revenue for FY26 stood at ₹31,149.01 crore. The preceding financial year saw revenue of ₹28,153 crore. The top line expanded by roughly 10.6% year-on-year.

MRF shares trade on the NSE under the ticker MRF. The stock commands a premium valuation among domestic tyre manufacturers. Investors will track the dividend timeline and demand trends ahead.
