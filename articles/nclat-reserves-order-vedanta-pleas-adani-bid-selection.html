NCLAT Reserves Order on Vedanta Pleas Against Adani Bid Selection

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal has reserved its order on Vedanta's pleas
  • 02/Vedanta had challenged the selection of an Adani Group bid in an insolvency matter
  • 03/The final verdict will have significant implications for creditor recoveries and the bidding process

The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) has reserved its order on Vedanta's pleas. The appeals challenge the selection of an Adani Group bid in a corporate insolvency case.

Vedanta had filed legal challenges contesting the bidding outcome. The company argued against the process that led to Adani's selection as the successful resolution applicant.

The NCLAT heard arguments from all parties involved. It has now reserved judgment, meaning the final order will be pronounced at a later date.

The case carries significant weight for India's insolvency framework. It tests the integrity of the resolution process under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

Creditor recoveries depend heavily on the final adjudication. A ruling in Vedanta's favor could reopen the bidding process entirely.

The Adani Group remains the selected resolution applicant pending the NCLAT verdict. Both corporate groups are locked in a high-stakes legal battle.
