Aimtron FY26 Revenue Nearly Doubles to ₹301 Crore, PAT Up 79%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Aimtron Electronics reported FY26 consolidated revenue of ₹301.2 crore, an 89.2% jump from FY25, with PAT up 79.4% to ₹46 crore
  • 02/H2 FY26 revenue grew 45.7% over H1 to ₹178.6 crore, showing sharp acceleration in business momentum through the year
  • 03/Full-year EBITDA margin compressed to 22.6% due to early-stage investments in new international and manufacturing subsidiaries

Aimtron Electronics Limited reported FY26 consolidated revenue of ₹301.2 crore. This marks an 89.2% jump from ₹159.2 crore in FY25.

Profit after tax rose 79.4% to ₹46 crore. The company had posted ₹25.6 crore in PAT during the previous fiscal year.

The Vadodara-based EMS provider attributed growth to stronger order execution and an improved product mix. High-value design-led manufacturing across Network Security, Green Energy, and AI sectors drove volumes.

In H2 FY26 alone, revenue reached ₹178.6 crore with EBITDA of ₹36.9 crore. This represents 45.7% revenue growth over H1, signaling sharp acceleration.

Full-year EBITDA margin stood at 22.6%. The modest compression reflects early-stage investments in newly established international and manufacturing subsidiaries.

PAT margin for FY26 came in at 15.3%, down from 16.1% in FY25. Higher tax provisions and subsidiary consolidation caused the slight dip.

Chairman Mukesh Jeram Vasani said FY26 marked a significant milestone. He reaffirmed the company's focus on scaling design-led manufacturing across domestic and international markets.

The stock trades at ₹1,017 on the NSE (NSE: AIMTRON). It has more than doubled from its 52-week low of ₹477. Peers like Kaynes Technology operate in the same EMS space.
