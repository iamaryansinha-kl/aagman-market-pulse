Sapphire Foods Q4 FY26: Revenue Up 11% to ₹790 Cr, Loss at ₹12.6 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sapphire Foods Q4 FY26 revenue rises 11% YoY to ₹789.8 crore, the strongest quarterly performance in 12 quarters
  • 02/Company posts net loss of ₹12.6 crore vs ₹2 crore profit in Q4 FY25; FY26 net loss widens to ₹32 crore
  • 03/KFC India revenue up 15% to ₹549.5 crore but Pizza Hut drags with 6% revenue decline; stock down 46% over past year

Sapphire Foods India Limited (NSE: SAPPHIRE) reported Q4 FY26 consolidated revenue of ₹789.8 crore, up 11% year-on-year. This marks the company's strongest quarterly performance in 12 quarters.

Despite the revenue growth, the company posted a consolidated net loss of ₹12.6 crore for the quarter. This compares against a profit of ₹2 crore in Q4 FY25.

For the full year FY26, revenue stood at ₹3,115.9 crore, up 8% YoY. The company swung to a net loss of ₹32 crore against a profit of ₹16.7 crore in FY25.

Adjusted EBITDA for FY26 declined 9% to ₹238.2 crore. Margins compressed 150 basis points to 7.6%, reflecting cost pressures across the business.

KFC India was the standout performer. Quarterly revenue grew 15% to ₹549.5 crore, the highest in eight quarters. Same-store sales growth came in at 4%, or 6% excluding the Chaitra Navratri impact, the best in 14 quarters.

KFC's restaurant EBITDA margin improved 110 basis points to 16.8%. The brand deployed value offers like the ₹99 Chicken Krisper Burger Meal in northern and western markets to drive footfalls.

Pizza Hut India remained a weak spot. Quarterly revenue fell 6% to ₹117.4 crore and same-store sales growth declined 7%. The brand's restaurant EBITDA margin worsened to -6.0%, down 140 basis points.

The Sri Lanka business delivered its sixth consecutive quarter of double-digit same-store sales growth at 11%. Restaurant sales rose 15% to LKR 422.1 crore in local currency terms.

Exceptional items of ₹12.8 crore in Q4 related to labour code changes and ESOP modification costs tied to the announced merger with Devyani International. These one-time costs further weighed on the bottom line.

Total restaurant count stood at 1,052 as of March 31, 2026. The stock closed at ₹174.50 on the NSE, down 0.81% on April 28, and has declined nearly 46% over the past year.
