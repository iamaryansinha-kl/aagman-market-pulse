Bondada Engineering Q4 Profit Rises 13% to ₹62.88 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bondada Engineering Q4 FY26 consolidated PAT rises 13% to ₹62.88 crore from ₹55.5 crore in the year-ago quarter
  • 02/Revenue jumps 27.7% to ₹916.20 crore vs ₹717.68 crore; company cites execution discipline and order book conversion
  • 03/CMD Bondada Raghavendra Rao reaffirms commitment to sustaining growth momentum and margin improvement in FY27

Bondada Engineering Ltd reported consolidated profit after tax of ₹62.88 crore for Q4 FY2025-26. This marks a 13 per cent increase over the year-ago quarter.

The company had posted a consolidated PAT of ₹55.5 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal. The rise underscores sustained margin improvement.

Consolidated income rose to ₹916.20 crore from ₹717.68 crore in the year-ago period. Revenue growth stands at approximately 27.7 per cent on a year-on-year basis.

The Hyderabad-based infrastructure firm said the strong financial performance reflects its ability to scale efficiently. It also highlights the company's sharp focus on profitability.

"The financial performance for FY 2025-26 reflects our disciplined approach to execution, strong order book conversion, and prudent financial management," said Chairman and Managing Director Bondada Raghavendra Rao.

Rao added that the company remains committed to sustaining growth momentum. It will maintain financial discipline while creating long-term value for stakeholders.

The firm is positioning itself as a key contributor to the country's infrastructure build-out and energy transition. Order book execution drove the quarterly outperformance.

Investors will watch FY27 guidance and order inflows. The company must sustain margin expansion amid rising competition in the infrastructure sector.
