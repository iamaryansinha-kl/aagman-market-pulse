Godavari Biorefineries ₹130 Cr Grain Ethanol Unit to Commission This Quarter

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Godavari Biorefineries will commission its ₹130 crore 200 KLPD grain ethanol unit in North Karnataka this quarter.
  • 02/Annual ethanol output will rise from around 10 crore litres to 16 crore litres, giving the company dual-feedstock flexibility.
  • 03/India's E20 ethanol blending mandate is now in effect; commissioning execution will be key to capturing demand.

Godavari Biorefineries Ltd (NSE: GODAVARIB) is set to commission a 200 KLPD grain-based ethanol distillery in North Karnataka this quarter. The company invested approximately ₹130 crore in the facility.

On an annualised basis, total ethanol output will increase from around 10 crore litres to approximately 16 crore litres. The new unit adds dual-feedstock capability alongside existing sugarcane-based operations.

The investment was funded through internal accruals and debt. The grain distillery will use corn and maize as alternative feedstocks to mitigate climate and policy risks.

The expansion aligns with India's E20 ethanol blending mandate, which took effect from April 1, 2026. Ethanol demand is rising as the country targets 20% blending nationwide.

In Q3 FY26, the company's bio-based chemical EBITDA margins expanded to 7.7% from 4.5% a year ago. The Jivana brand portfolio crossed ₹100 crore in revenue during the first nine months of FY26.

Godavari Biorefineries posted FY25 revenue of ₹1,870.3 crore, up from ₹1,686.7 crore in FY24. However, FY25 PAT turned negative at ₹23 crore against ₹12 crore in the prior year.

The company has reaffirmed a 3x EBITDA growth target by FY29. Risks include rising sugarcane costs and potential delays in equipment delivery.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE CAPACITY OF THE NEW GRAIN ETHANOL UNIT BEING COMMISSIONED BY GODAVARI BIOREFINERIES?
THE NEW GRAIN-BASED ETHANOL DISTILLERY HAS A CAPACITY OF 200 KILOLITRES PER DAY (KLPD), WHICH IS EQUIVALENT TO 200,000 LITRES PER DAY. ON AN ANNUALISED BASIS, THIS WILL INCREASE THE COMPANY'S TOTAL ETHANOL OUTPUT FROM AROUND 10 CRORE LITRES TO APPROXIMATELY 16 CRORE LITRES.
HOW MUCH HAS GODAVARI BIOREFINERIES INVESTED IN THE NEW GRAIN ETHANOL FACILITY?
GODAVARI BIOREFINERIES HAS INVESTED APPROXIMATELY ₹130 CRORE IN THE NEW 200 KLPD CORN/GRAIN-BASED DISTILLERY. THE FUNDING WAS RAISED THROUGH A COMBINATION OF INTERNAL ACCRUALS AND DEBT.
WHERE IS THE NEW GRAIN ETHANOL UNIT LOCATED?
THE NEW GRAIN-BASED ETHANOL DISTILLERY IS LOCATED IN NORTH KARNATAKA. THE COMPANY ALREADY OPERATES EXISTING SUGARCANE-BASED FACILITIES AT SAMEERWADI, KARNATAKA AND SAKARWADI, MAHARASHTRA.
WHY IS GODAVARI BIOREFINERIES ADDING GRAIN-BASED ETHANOL CAPACITY?
THE ADDITION OF GRAIN-BASED ETHANOL PROVIDES DUAL-FEEDSTOCK FLEXIBILITY ALONGSIDE THE COMPANY'S EXISTING SUGARCANE-BASED OPERATIONS. THIS HELPS MITIGATE RISKS FROM POOR MONSOONS, CLIMATE DISRUPTIONS, AND POLICY CHANGES, WHILE POSITIONING THE COMPANY TO MEET GROWING DEMAND UNDER INDIA'S ETHANOL BLENDING PROGRAM.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH FOR NEXT?
INVESTORS SHOULD TRACK THE EXACT COMMISSIONING TIMELINE OF THE GRAIN DISTILLERY THIS QUARTER. THEY SHOULD ALSO MONITOR THE COMPANY'S EXECUTION ON ITS 3X EBITDA GROWTH TARGET BY FY29, BIO-CHEMICAL MARGIN TRENDS, AND RISKS FROM RISING SUGARCANE COSTS AND ETHANOL PRICING POLICIES.
