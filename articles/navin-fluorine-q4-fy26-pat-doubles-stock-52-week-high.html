Navin Fluorine Q4 FY26: PAT Surges 124% to ₹213 Cr, Hits 52-Week High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Navin Fluorine Q4 FY26 consolidated PAT surges 124% YoY to ₹213 crore on revenue of ₹938 crore, up 34%
  • 02/Full-year FY26 PAT more than doubles to ₹664 crore from ₹289 crore; board declares ₹8.60 per share final dividend
  • 03/Brokerages split: Jefferies and Elara Capital maintain buy with targets of ₹8,385 and ₹8,681; Citi stays sell at ₹6,500

Navin Fluorine International shares hit a 52-week high of ₹7,200 on April 30. The stock surged 6.5% in early trade before paring gains to trade near ₹6,752.50 on the NSE.

The company reported standalone Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹135.03 crore. This marks a 76.2% jump from ₹76.61 crore in the year-ago quarter. Revenue grew 29% to ₹626.51 crore.

On a consolidated basis, Q4 PAT surged 124% to ₹213 crore. Revenue rose 34% to ₹938 crore. Operating EBITDA jumped 80% to ₹321 crore with margins at 34.2%.

Full-year FY26 PAT more than doubled to ₹664 crore from ₹289 crore in FY25. Net operating revenue rose 41% to ₹3,313.9 crore. EBITDA more than doubled to ₹1,082 crore at 32.6% margins.

The board recommended a final dividend of ₹8.60 per share. This is 430% on the face value of ₹2. The payout signals confidence in sustained cash generation.

This marks the sixth consecutive quarter of revenue and profit growth. The CDMO segment grew 61% in Q4 while specialty chemicals rose 39%. HPP revenue increased 20% year-on-year.

Brokerages remain divided on valuation. Jefferies maintains buy with a target of ₹8,385 and expects 24% EPS CAGR through FY28. Citi stays sell at ₹6,500, flagging refrigerant pricing risks.

DAM Capital and Elara Capital also have buy ratings. Elara raised its target to ₹8,681, citing a multi-year compounding phase. The management targets sustaining EBITDA margins around 30% for FY27.
