Jeremy Grantham Investing Wisdom: 5 Rules for Volatile Markets in 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jeremy Grantham, who predicted the dot-com crash and 2008 housing crisis, urges investors to prioritize discipline over excitement in 2026
  • 02/AI-linked stocks and Indian defence/railway/smallcap sectors show stretched valuations while IT and private banks face neglect despite improving fundamentals
  • 03/Grantham's five rules: buy cheaply, stay patient, avoid leverage, diversify, ignore the crowd — core principles unchanged despite market noise

Jeremy Grantham believes successful investing is less about excitement and more about discipline. The legendary investor, who identified the Japan bubble, dot-com mania, and 2008 housing crisis, says markets always return to fair value.

Current global markets are swinging between optimism and anxiety. Sticky US inflation, West Asia geopolitical tensions, and elevated crude oil prices are creating uncertainty for investors worldwide.

Grantham warns that investors are rewarded for buying assets cheaply, not for taking the biggest risks. AI-linked stocks continue to command premium valuations, and he has previously warned that excessive AI enthusiasm could resemble a "bubble within a bubble."

In India, select defence, railway, and momentum-driven smallcaps have seen valuations stretch far beyond historical averages. Meanwhile, sectors like IT, private banks, and some export-oriented businesses have faced neglect despite improving fundamentals.

Grantham's philosophy centers on "mean reversion" — markets eventually move back toward fair valuations after periods of excess. Investors should be cautious when everybody is euphoric and attentive when fear dominates headlines.

Retail participation in equities has surged dramatically over the past few years. Yet many retail investors acknowledge that long-term investing creates greater wealth than rapid-fire trading, with patience and discipline as the real drivers of sustainable returns.

Individual investors have a major advantage over professional fund managers. They are not forced to deliver quarterly performance or constantly justify temporary underperformance, making patience their biggest edge.

Grantham's key warning is against borrowing aggressively to invest. Derivatives participation is surging among retail traders in India, but leverage can quickly destroy capital when volatility rises. Preserving capital remains the foundation of long-term compounding.

Diversification matters more than ever. Leadership has shifted multiple times between IT, financials, consumption, manufacturing, commodities, and energy over the past decade alone. A diversified portfolio improves survival during difficult periods.

Grantham's core message is not about panic — it is about preparation. Buy with discipline, focus on value, stay patient, avoid leverage, and think in years rather than weeks. History suggests that disciplined investing often looks boring right before it proves effective.

WHO IS JEREMY GRANTHAM AND WHY SHOULD INVESTORS LISTEN TO HIM?
JEREMY GRANTHAM IS A LEGENDARY INVESTOR WHO CORRECTLY IDENTIFIED MAJOR MARKET BUBBLES INCLUDING JAPAN IN THE LATE 1980S, THE DOT-COM MANIA, AND THE 2008 HOUSING CRISIS. HE BELIEVES MARKETS EVENTUALLY RETURN TO FAIR VALUE NO MATTER HOW EUPHORIC OR FEARFUL THEY BECOME.
WHAT IS GRANTHAM'S WARNING ABOUT AI STOCKS IN 2026?
GRANTHAM HAS PREVIOUSLY WARNED THAT EXCESSIVE ENTHUSIASM AROUND AI COULD RESEMBLE A "BUBBLE WITHIN A BUBBLE." AI-LINKED STOCKS CONTINUE TO COMMAND PREMIUM VALUATIONS ACROSS GLOBAL MARKETS WHILE INVESTORS CHASE MOMENTUM THEMES WITHOUT EVALUATING FUNDAMENTALS.
WHICH INDIAN SECTORS DOES GRANTHAM'S PHILOSOPHY SUGGEST ARE OVERVALUED?
IN INDIA, POCKETS OF THE MARKET — ESPECIALLY SELECT DEFENCE, RAILWAY, AND MOMENTUM-DRIVEN SMALLCAPS — HAVE SEEN VALUATIONS STRETCH FAR BEYOND HISTORICAL AVERAGES. AT THE SAME TIME, SECTORS LIKE IT, PRIVATE BANKS, AND SOME EXPORT-ORIENTED BUSINESSES HAVE WITNESSED PHASES OF NEGLECT DESPITE IMPROVING FUNDAMENTALS.
WHAT ARE GRANTHAM'S FIVE CORE INVESTING RULES FOR VOLATILE MARKETS?
GRANTHAM'S FIVE CORE PRINCIPLES ARE: (1) BUY WITH DISCIPLINE, NOT EXCITEMENT, (2) FOCUS ON VALUE INSTEAD OF NARRATIVES, (3) STAY PATIENT DURING VOLATILITY, (4) AVOID EXCESSIVE LEVERAGE, AND (5) THINK IN YEARS, NOT WEEKS. THESE RULES REMAIN REMARKABLY UNCHANGED DESPITE EVOLVING MARKET CONDITIONS.
WHY DOES GRANTHAM BELIEVE RETAIL INVESTORS HAVE AN ADVANTAGE OVER PROFESSIONAL FUND MANAGERS?
GRANTHAM ARGUES THAT INDIVIDUAL INVESTORS POSSESS A MAJOR ADVANTAGE BECAUSE THEY ARE NOT FORCED TO DELIVER QUARTERLY PERFORMANCE OR CONSTANTLY JUSTIFY TEMPORARY UNDERPERFORMANCE. THIS ALLOWS THEM TO STAY PATIENT THROUGH CORRECTIONS AND PROFIT-BOOKING PHASES, WHICH IS OFTEN THE BIGGEST EDGE IN VOLATILE MARKETS.
