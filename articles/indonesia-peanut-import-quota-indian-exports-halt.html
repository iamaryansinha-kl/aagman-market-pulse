Indonesia Peanut Import Quota Halts Indian Exports for 3-6 Months

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indonesia's new Ministerial Regulation mandates global GAP certification for peanut imports, giving exporters only 14 days to comply
  • 02/India exported 2.77 lakh tonnes of peanuts worth $280 million to Indonesia in 2024-25, representing one-third of total shipments
  • 03/Export halt expected for 3-6 months; domestic prices in Indonesia could spike 60-80% or even double

Indonesia has introduced a quota system for peanut imports through a new Ministerial Regulation. The rule was announced during the weekend of April 25-26, 2026.

Exporters can load non-quota peanuts until May 7, 2026. Trade sources say this gives just 14 days to prepare, against the usual 90-day window.

The regulation requires global Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) certification for all peanut exporters. Obtaining this certification takes three to six months.

India is the largest peanut exporter to Indonesia. In 2024-25, Jakarta imported 2.77 lakh tonnes worth $280 million from India.

This represents nearly a third of India's total peanut exports of 7.46 lakh tonnes valued at $795 million during the same period.

A Singapore-based trading source said no peanut exports will be possible for the next three to six months. Prices could rise 60-80% or even double.

Indonesia had suspended groundnut imports from India on September 2, 2025 over aflatoxin concerns. Imports resumed in November after inspections and tighter norms.

The new quota applies to peanut kernels and in-shell peanuts. Roasted peanuts are exempt from the requirement.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS INDONESIA'S NEW PEANUT IMPORT REGULATION?
INDONESIA INTRODUCED A MINISTERIAL REGULATION DURING THE WEEKEND OF APRIL 25-26, 2026, REQUIRING GLOBAL GOOD AGRICULTURAL PRACTICES (GAP) CERTIFICATION FOR PEANUT IMPORTS. NON-QUOTA PEANUTS CAN BE LOADED UNTIL MAY 7, 2026.
HOW LONG WILL INDIAN PEANUT EXPORTS TO INDONESIA BE HALTED?
EXPORTS ARE EXPECTED TO STALL FOR THREE TO SIX MONTHS. OBTAINING THE REQUIRED GLOBAL GAP CERTIFICATION TAKES THREE TO SIX MONTHS, AND TRADE SOURCES SAY NO COUNTRY CAN MEET THIS REQUIREMENT IMMEDIATELY.
HOW MUCH DOES INDIA EXPORT PEANUTS TO INDONESIA?
IN 2024-25, INDONESIA IMPORTED 2.77 LAKH TONNES OF PEANUTS VALUED AT $280 MILLION FROM INDIA. THIS ACCOUNTS FOR ABOUT ONE-THIRD OF INDIA'S TOTAL GROUNDNUT EXPORTS OF 7.46 LAKH TONNES WORTH $795 MILLION.
WHAT HAPPENED TO INDONESIA'S PREVIOUS BAN ON INDIAN PEANUTS?
INDONESIA SUSPENDED GROUNDNUT IMPORTS FROM INDIA ON SEPTEMBER 2, 2025, DUE TO ALLEGED HIGH AFLATOXIN LEVELS. THE BAN WAS LIFTED IN NOVEMBER 2025 AFTER INDONESIAN INSPECTIONS, BUT THE APPROVED EXPORTER LIST WAS REDUCED TO ABOUT 75.
WHAT IMPACT WILL THE NEW QUOTA HAVE ON PRICES?
A SINGAPORE-BASED TRADING SOURCE SAID PRICES COULD INCREASE BY 60-80% OR EVEN DOUBLE. NO COUNTRY WILL BE ABLE TO MEET THE NEW CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENT IN THE SHORT TERM, CREATING A SUPPLY CRUNCH.
