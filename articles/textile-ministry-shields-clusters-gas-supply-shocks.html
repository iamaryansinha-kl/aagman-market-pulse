Textile Ministry Shields 1,200 Clusters From West Asia Gas Crisis

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Textile Ministry launches assessment of natural gas supplies across 500+ textile and 700+ handicraft clusters
  • 02/47.4 MMSCMD or 25% of India's gas supplies affected by force majeure from Gulf producers amid Hormuz blockade
  • 03/GAIL confirms meeting 80% of cluster demand, ready to source from spot market though at higher cost to units

The Textile Ministry has launched a nationwide assessment of natural gas supplies across all textile and handicraft clusters. The move aims to prevent shutdowns as West Asia supply disruptions deepen.

Over 500 textiles and 700 handicrafts clusters operate in India. Many units rely on natural gas for dyeing, furnaces, and machinery.

India's total natural gas consumption is approximately 189 MMSCMD. About 97.5 MMSCMD is produced domestically while the rest is imported.

Roughly 47.4 MMSCMD, or 25% of total supplies, has been hit by force majeure. Gulf-based producers suspended long-term contracts due to the ongoing conflict.

Clusters in Surat and Sanganer face severe shortages. Several units have reported partial or full shutdowns with workers migrating home.

The glass industry in Ferozepur has been hit particularly hard. The Ministry's internal monitoring group aims to prevent more such crises.

GAIL currently meets at least 80% of cluster gas demand. The company has assured the Ministry it can tap the volatile spot market if needed.

The government issued a Natural Gas Control Order on March 9, 2026. It was enacted under the Essential Commodities Act to protect priority sectors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY IS THE TEXTILE MINISTRY ASSESSING GAS SUPPLIES ACROSS CLUSTERS?
THE MINISTRY IS CONDUCTING A NATIONWIDE ASSESSMENT TO IDENTIFY POTENTIAL SHORTAGES AND PREVENT SHUTDOWNS IN OVER 500 TEXTILE AND 700 HANDICRAFTS CLUSTERS AMID SUPPLY DISRUPTIONS FROM THE WEST ASIA CONFLICT.
HOW MUCH OF INDIA'S GAS SUPPLY HAS BEEN AFFECTED?
APPROXIMATELY 47.4 MMSCMD, OR 25% OF INDIA'S TOTAL NATURAL GAS SUPPLIES, HAS BEEN DISRUPTED DUE TO FORCE MAJEURE CONDITIONS INVOKED BY MAJOR GULF-BASED PRODUCERS.
WHAT PERCENTAGE OF CLUSTER DEMAND IS GAIL CURRENTLY MEETING?
GAIL HAS CONFIRMED IT IS MEETING AT LEAST 80% OF THE NATURAL GAS REQUIREMENTS FOR TEXTILE AND HANDICRAFT CLUSTERS, WHICH ARE CLASSIFIED AS A PRIORITY SECTOR.
WHAT MEASURE HAS THE GOVERNMENT TAKEN TO MANAGE GAS SUPPLIES?
THE GOVERNMENT ISSUED A NATURAL GAS CONTROL ORDER ON MARCH 9, 2026, UNDER THE ESSENTIAL COMMODITIES ACT TO MANAGE SUPPLIES AND PROTECT PRIORITY SECTORS INCLUDING TEXTILES.
WHAT WILL HAPPEN IF SPOT MARKET GAS IS SOURCED FOR CLUSTERS?
IF FURTHER DISRUPTIONS OCCUR, GAIL IS PREPARED TO PROCURE GAS FROM THE VOLATILE SPOT MARKET. HOWEVER, THE HIGHER PRICES WOULD LIKELY BE PASSED ON TO THE INDUSTRIAL UNITS.
