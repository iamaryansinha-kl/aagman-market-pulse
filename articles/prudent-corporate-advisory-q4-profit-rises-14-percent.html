Prudent Corporate Advisory Q4 Profit Rises 14% to ₹59 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Ltd reports Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹59 crore, up 14% from ₹52 crore in the year-ago quarter
  • 02/Revenue grows 27% to ₹361 crore driven by strong mutual fund distribution and insurance segments; operating profit up 35% to ₹93 crore
  • 03/Board declares dividend of ₹3.50 per share; equity AUM reaches ₹1,15,479 crore with record net sales of ₹13,911 crore

Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Ltd (NSE: PRUDENT) has reported a 14% rise in net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit rose to ₹59 crore from ₹52 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue surged 27% to ₹361 crore against ₹283 crore in the year-ago period. Growth was driven by a 26% increase in quarterly average AUM in the mutual fund segment.

The insurance segment also contributed strongly with 41% growth. The company declared a dividend of ₹3.50 per share for the quarter.

Equity-oriented AUM grew 16% year-on-year to ₹1,15,479 crore despite the Nifty 50 falling 5% during the full year. Record net sales of ₹13,911 crore and the Indus Capital acquisition drove this outperformance.

The monthly SIP book expanded 21% year-on-year to ₹1,188 crore. Strong revenue growth and controlled costs pushed operating profit up 35% to ₹93 crore.

Operating margin expanded by 150 basis points year-on-year to 26%. This reflects improving operational efficiency across the business.

For the full year FY26, net profit rose 13% to ₹222 crore from ₹196 crore. Revenue increased 19% to ₹1,317 crore from ₹1,104 crore.

Sanjay Shah, Managing Director of Prudent Group, said equity mutual fund net sales hit a record ₹13,911 crore last fiscal with 5,100 new partners added. He noted the health insurance vertical delivered highest-ever fresh premium growth at 35%.

Shares of the company closed up one per cent at ₹2,931 on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The stock has remained resilient amid volatile broader market conditions.
