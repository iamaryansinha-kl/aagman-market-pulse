IndiGo, Hotel Stocks Fall 5% After PM Modi's Overseas Travel Appeal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/InterGlobe Aviation, parent of IndiGo, fell 5% to ₹4,299.40 on May 11 after PM Modi's appeal to curb overseas travel
  • 02/Hotel stocks ITC Hotels, Mahindra Holidays, Indian Hotels, Leela Palaces and online platforms Yatra, Le Travenues also declined 1-5%
  • 03/Analyst Khushi Mistry at Bonanza says outbound tourism and aviation are most vulnerable; domestic hospitality may benefit from shift

InterGlobe Aviation, parent of IndiGo, settled 5 per cent lower at ₹4,299.40 on the NSE on May 11, 2026. The stock had opened at ₹4,522.70 and fell to an intraday low of ₹4,287.10.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged citizens to reduce discretionary foreign travel, overseas vacations and destination weddings. The appeal comes amid concerns over West Asia war impact on India's foreign exchange outflows.

Modi also asked people to reduce petrol and diesel usage by using metros and public transport. He requested deferring non-essential gold purchases for one year.

Hotel and hospitality stocks faced selling pressure across the board. ITC Hotels fell over 3 per cent to ₹159.37, while Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India slipped 2 per cent to ₹234.73.

Indian Hotels Company declined close to 2 per cent to ₹661.30. Chalet Hotels dipped 1 per cent to ₹781.10 and Leela Palaces fell over 2 per cent to ₹413.95.

Online travel platforms also came under pressure. Le Travenues Technology fell 3 per cent to ₹163.10, while Yatra Online tanked over 5 per cent to ₹102.31.

"The outbound tourism and aviation sector appears more vulnerable as overseas vacations and destination weddings are discretionary expenses that consumers can easily postpone" - Khushi Mistry, Research Analyst at Bonanza

Mistry added that domestic tourism, hospitality and resort operators may benefit if travellers opt for local vacations instead. The shift from international to domestic travel could support Indian hotel chains.
