GST Council 57th Meet: Arrest Powers Scrapped, Prosecution Limit Rs 5 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The GST Council at its 57th meeting on Thursday removed tax officers' powers to arrest individuals and raised the prosecution threshold from Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore
  • 02/No GST rate changes were announced; rate matters will now be taken up once a year at a dedicated rate meeting
  • 03/Most reforms take effect from April 1, 2027; refunds get faster with 90% of eligible claims sanctioned automatically after risk assessment

The GST Council on Thursday approved the biggest overhaul of GST enforcement since 2017. Tax officers' powers to arrest individuals under GST law will be removed.

The prosecution threshold has been raised fivefold, from Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore. The general penalty, where no specific penalty is prescribed, was cut from Rs 25,000 to Rs 10,000.

The minimum punishment provision stands deleted. Fines and imprisonment will now rest at judicial discretion, not statute.

Late filers and businesses that make mistakes will face tax recovery, interest and proportionate penalties. No additional punitive action applies to such defaults.

There were no rate changes. The Finance Ministry said the rate structure is settled, and GST rate matters will be considered once a year at a dedicated rate meeting.

Refunds get faster. The acknowledgement timeline drops from 15 days to 10, deemed acknowledged if officers do not respond. After risk assessment, 90% of eligible refund claims will be sanctioned automatically, with orders in 3 working days instead of 7.

Excess electronic cash ledger balance refunds become fully automatic. Small businesses with turnover up to Rs 5 crore get an optional annual-return scheme — annual returns, quarterly payments — and show-cause notices now carry a Rs 10,000 minimum threshold.

Small e-commerce sellers can use a platform operator's warehouse in another state as their principal place of business. Registration approvals will be automated.

Input tax credit has been approved on health and life insurance bought by businesses for employees — a long-standing industry ask. Credit also extends to telecom towers, certain pipelines, free samples and specified expired stock.

Inverted duty structure refunds are extended to input services for credit availed on or after November 1, 2026. The plant and machinery exclusion from refunds is removed for exporters and eligible businesses, effective April 1, 2027.

A committee of officers will be formed to protect genuine buyers from input tax credit reversals caused by supplier defaults. Findings are due within 3 months for the next council meeting.

Export and transit relief is part of the package. Overseas branch services and work on foreign clients' goods in India qualify for export benefits. Physical transit checks now require specific intelligence with prior Joint Commissioner-level authorisation, and only originating and destination states may inspect goods in transit.

"The reforms will be implemented from April 1, 2027," Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said. Ministry data shows taxable supplies rose 25.8% to Rs 50.58 lakh crore a month after the two-rate structure took effect. The effective tax rate on domestic supplies fell from 14.55% to 13.13%, and GST revenue is up 11% in FY2026-27.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID THE GST COUNCIL DECIDE ON ARREST POWERS AT ITS 57TH MEETING?
THE GST COUNCIL APPROVED THE REMOVAL OF GST TAX OFFICERS' POWERS TO ARREST INDIVIDUALS UNDER GST LAW. IT ALSO RAISED THE PROSECUTION THRESHOLD FIVEFOLD FROM RS 1 CRORE TO RS 5 CRORE. THE CHANGES WILL BE IMPLEMENTED FROM APRIL 1, 2027.
WILL GST RATES CHANGE AFTER THE 57TH COUNCIL MEETING?
NO. THE COUNCIL ANNOUNCED NO RATE CHANGES. THE FINANCE MINISTRY SAID THE RATE STRUCTURE IS SETTLED, AND GST RATE MATTERS WILL BE CONSIDERED ONCE A YEAR AT A DEDICATED RATE MEETING.
WHAT ARE THE NEW GST REFUND TIMELINES?
THE ACKNOWLEDGEMENT TIMELINE IS CUT FROM 15 DAYS TO 10 DAYS, DEEMED ACKNOWLEDGED IF THERE IS NO RESPONSE. AFTER RISK ASSESSMENT, 90% OF ELIGIBLE REFUND CLAIMS WILL BE SANCTIONED AUTOMATICALLY, WITH ORDERS ISSUED IN 3 WORKING DAYS INSTEAD OF 7. EXCESS ELECTRONIC CASH LEDGER BALANCE REFUNDS BECOME FULLY AUTOMATIC.
WHAT INPUT TAX CREDIT CHANGES WERE APPROVED?
BUSINESSES CAN NOW CLAIM INPUT TAX CREDIT ON HEALTH AND LIFE INSURANCE BOUGHT FOR EMPLOYEES, AS WELL AS ON TELECOM TOWERS, CERTAIN PIPELINES, FREE SAMPLES AND SPECIFIED EXPIRED STOCK. INVERTED DUTY STRUCTURE REFUNDS EXTEND TO INPUT SERVICES FOR CREDIT AVAILED ON OR AFTER NOVEMBER 1, 2026, AND THE PLANT AND MACHINERY EXCLUSION IS REMOVED FOR EXPORTERS FROM APRIL 1, 2027.
WHAT RELIEF DID THE COUNCIL ANNOUNCE FOR SMALL BUSINESSES?
AN OPTIONAL ANNUAL-RETURN SCHEME WAS APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE FOR BUSINESSES WITH TURNOVER UP TO RS 5 CRORE SUPPLYING EXCLUSIVELY TO CONSUMERS, WITH ANNUAL RETURNS AND QUARTERLY PAYMENTS. SHOW-CAUSE NOTICES NOW REQUIRE A RS 10,000 MINIMUM THRESHOLD, SMALL E-COMMERCE SELLERS CAN USE A PLATFORM OPERATOR'S WAREHOUSE IN ANOTHER STATE AS THEIR PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS, AND REGISTRATION APPROVALS WILL BE AUTOMATED. THE GENERAL PENALTY WAS ALSO CUT FROM RS 25,000 TO RS 10,000. GLOSSARY: Input tax credit (ITC): GST paid on a business's purchases (inputs, services, capital goods) that can be offset against the GST it collects on sales. Extending ITC to more categories lowers effective business cost. Prosecution threshold: The minimum tax amount at which criminal prosecution can be initiated. Raised from Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore. Show-cause notice: A formal written demand from tax authorities asking a business to explain why action should not be taken. Now requires a Rs 10,000 minimum threshold. Inverted duty structure: When tax on inputs exceeds tax on the final product, leaving unused credits. Refund extension to input services and plant & machinery unblocks this cash.
