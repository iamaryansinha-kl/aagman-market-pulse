Bagmane REIT IPO Opens Today at ₹95-100 Price Band, ₹1,360 Cr Anchored

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bagmane Prime Office REIT opens ₹3,405 crore IPO for public subscription on May 5, 2026 at ₹95-100 price band
  • 02/Anchor investors subscribed ₹1,360 crore on May 4; marquee names include SBI Pension, Quant MF, Kotak MF, UTI MF, Edelweiss MF
  • 03/IPO closes May 7; allotment May 12; listing on NSE/BSE May 15; grey market premium at ₹4.25 indicating 4.25% premium

Bagmane Prime Office REIT opened for public subscription on May 5, 2026. The IPO is priced at ₹95 to ₹100 per unit.

The total issue size is ₹3,405 crore. It comprises a fresh issue of ₹2,390 crore and an offer-for-sale of ₹1,015 crore by Blackstone-backed selling unitholders.

The REIT raised ₹1,360 crore from anchor investors on May 4, 2026. It allotted 11,49,74,850 units at ₹100 each to marquee institutions.

Anchor investors include SBI Pension, SBI Life, White Oak, Quant MF, UTI MF, Kotak MF, Edelweiss MF, Tata AIG, Kotak Life, and Max Life.

The REIT owns six Grade A+ business parks in Bengaluru spanning 20.3 million sq ft. Leasable area stands at 19.6 million sq ft.

Committed occupancy is 98.8% as of December 2025. The weighted average lease expiry is 7.4 years. Tenants include Google, Amazon, Nvidia, and Samsung.

Revenue for 2025 stood at ₹2,370.75 crore with PAT of ₹897.10 crore. The top 10 tenants contribute 63% of gross contracted rentals.

The IPO closes on May 7. Allotment is on May 12 and listing on May 15. Grey market premium is ₹4.25, a 4.25% premium over the upper price band.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE PRICE BAND FOR THE BAGMANE REIT IPO?
THE PRICE BAND FOR THE BAGMANE PRIME OFFICE REIT IPO IS FIXED AT ₹95 TO ₹100 PER UNIT. ANCHOR INVESTORS WERE ALLOTTED UNITS AT THE UPPER END OF ₹100 PER UNIT.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL SIZE OF THE BAGMANE REIT IPO?
THE TOTAL ISSUE SIZE IS ₹3,405 CRORE, COMPRISING A FRESH ISSUE OF ₹2,390 CRORE AND AN OFFER-FOR-SALE OF ₹1,015 CRORE BY BLACKSTONE-BACKED SELLING UNITHOLDERS.
HOW MUCH DID BAGMANE REIT RAISE FROM ANCHOR INVESTORS?
BAGMANE REIT RAISED ₹1,360 CRORE FROM ANCHOR INVESTORS ON MAY 4, 2026. IT ALLOTTED 11,49,74,850 UNITS AT ₹100 EACH TO INSTITUTIONS INCLUDING SBI PENSION, SBI LIFE, QUANT MF, KOTAK MF, UTI MF, EDELWEISS MF, TATA AIG, KOTAK LIFE, AND MAX LIFE.
WHEN WILL THE BAGMANE REIT SHARES LIST?
THE IPO CLOSES ON MAY 7, 2026. BASIS OF ALLOTMENT IS EXPECTED ON MAY 12, AND LISTING ON THE NSE AND BSE IS SCHEDULED FOR MAY 15, 2026.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS FOR BAGMANE REIT INVESTORS?
THE ENTIRE PORTFOLIO IS CONCENTRATED IN BENGALURU, EXPOSING IT TO SINGLE-CITY RISK. THE TOP 10 TENANTS CONTRIBUTE 63% OF GROSS CONTRACTED RENTALS, AND 98.7% OF TENANTS ARE FOREIGN MNCS WITH 88.5% BEING GCCS, MAKING IT VULNERABLE TO ANY DOWNSIZING BY GLOBAL TECH FIRMS.
