Housing Sales Fall 6% in Q2 Across Top 7 Cities; Launches Rise 7%: ANAROCK

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Housing sales drop 6% YoY to 90,715 units in Q2 2026 across India's top 7 cities, per ANAROCK Research
  • 02/West Asia conflict, IT sector uncertainty dent buyer sentiment; new launches still rise 7% to 1.06 lakh units
  • 03/MMR and Bengaluru account for 48% of sales; premium housing dominates; inventory up 10% to 6.16 lakh units

Residential sales across India's top seven cities declined 6% year-on-year in Q2 2026. Approximately 90,715 units were sold compared to 96,285 units in the year-ago period.

Sequentially, sales dropped 11% from about 1.02 lakh units in Q1 2026. Buyer sentiment weakened due to the West Asia conflict and IT sector uncertainty.

New housing supply rose 7% year-on-year to around 1.06 lakh units. Launches were down 16% quarter-on-quarter as developers turned cautious.

The Mumbai Metropolitan Region and Bengaluru led all markets. Together they accounted for nearly 48% of total sales and 53% of new launches.

Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru were the only cities to post annual sales growth. Pune saw the sharpest decline with sales falling 15% year-on-year.

Average prices across the top seven cities rose 7% year-on-year. Quarterly growth moderated to just 1%, indicating slowing price momentum.

Available housing inventory increased 10% year-on-year to over 6.16 lakh units. Bengaluru registered the sharpest rise in unsold stock at 34%.

"The Middle East war's impacts on the entire sector were all too obvious," said Anuj Puri, Chairman, ANAROCK Group. He noted the market is now more balanced as supply catches up with absorption.
