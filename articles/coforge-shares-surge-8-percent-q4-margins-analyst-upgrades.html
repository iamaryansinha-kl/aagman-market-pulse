Coforge Jumps 8% On Strong Q4 Margins, Brokerages Upgrade Target Price

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Coforge shares rallied 8% after Q4 results showed strong margin improvement, beating Street estimates
  • 02/Multiple brokerages including top firms reiterated Buy ratings, raising target prices post-earnings
  • 03/Investors await management guidance on FY27 growth outlook and deal pipeline momentum

Coforge Ltd (NSE: COFORGE) shares surged 8% on Wednesday following robust Q4 FY25 earnings. The stock hit an intraday high of ₹7,850 before closing at ₹7,720.

The IT services firm reported strong margin expansion in the March quarter. Operating margins improved significantly compared to the year-ago period.

Revenue growth remained steady despite global macro headwinds. The company maintained its growth trajectory across key verticals.

Multiple brokerages reiterated Buy ratings on the stock post-results. Several firms raised their target prices citing improved profitability metrics.

The margin beat signals better operational execution. Cost optimization measures and utilization improvements contributed to the outperformance.

Coforge has outperformed the Nifty IT index over the past year. The stock reflects investor confidence in its mid-tier IT positioning.

Deal wins and pipeline remain healthy heading into FY26. Management commentary on large deal traction will be closely watched.

The company continues to focus on digital transformation services. BFSI and travel verticals remain key revenue drivers.
