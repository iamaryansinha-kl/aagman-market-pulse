India's Farm Data Platform Market to Hit ₹29,000 Cr by 2035 on 29% AI Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's AI-in-agriculture market is projected to surge from $205.68M in 2024 to $3.48B by 2035, representing a 29.32% CAGR.
  • 02/DeHaat reported India's highest agritech revenue of ₹3,010 crore in FY25 with ₹369 crore net profit, while the sector attracted over $2B in funding since 2020.
  • 03/The government's ₹2,817 crore Digital Agriculture Mission has generated 84.8 million farmer IDs as of February 2026, creating the backbone for platform adoption.

The Indian agricultural sector is undergoing a data-driven transformation. The AI-in-agriculture market is set to expand from $205.68 million in 2024 to $3.48 billion by 2035. This represents a compound annual growth rate of 29.32%, according to Market Research Future.

Farm data platforms are evolving from simple digitization tools to decision intelligence systems. Early agri-tech focused on sensors and drones. The current shift targets real-time decision-making across entire value chains with built-in accountability.

DeHaat leads the domestic market with ₹3,010 crore gross revenue in FY25. The Gurugram-based startup achieved an 11% year-on-year increase and reported ₹369 crore net profit. It serves farmers across 12 states with end-to-end agri services.

The government's Digital Agriculture Mission, approved in September 2024, allocates ₹2,817 crore. As of February 2026, over 84.8 million farmer IDs have been created under the AgriStack initiative. This digital public infrastructure provides the backbone for platform scalability.

CropIn's partnership with Syngenta and state governments demonstrates measurable impact. Their satellite-based AI analytics covering 30,000+ farm plots increased average profits by over 30% per acre in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.

Fasal's IoT sensor network delivered a 20% reduction in water and pesticide use for farmers in Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Telangana. AgroStar's AI advisory platform boosted yields by 27% while cutting input costs by 17% for 5 million+ farmers.

Vinay Nair, Founder & CEO of Khetibuddy, notes that platforms winning the next decade will connect farm activity to supply chain compliance and carbon accounting. The shift from data collection to decision intelligence separates leaders from laggards.

The global agricultural data management platform market was valued at $1.19 billion in 2024. It is projected to reach $3.49 billion by 2032 at 16.9% CAGR. India represents the fastest-growing regional opportunity amid rapid digitalization.
