Jio Platforms IPO May Open Oct 21; Listing Targeted Oct 28

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Platforms' IPO could open for subscription on October 21 and close on October 23, according to a CNBC TV-18 report
  • 02/The anchor book is likely to open on October 19, with shares targeted to list on the exchanges on October 28
  • 03/The entirely fresh-issue offering is estimated at $3.8 billion and is being touted as India's largest IPO, with the timeline subject to market conditions

Jio Platforms' much-awaited IPO could open for subscription on October 21. The issue is expected to remain open until October 23, according to a report by CNBC TV-18.

The anchor book is likely to open on October 19. Shares are targeted to list on the stock exchanges on October 28.

These dates are not confirmed. The timeline remains subject to market conditions and could change depending on developments in financial markets, the report said.

The proposed IPO is being touted as India's largest. The issue is estimated at $3.8 billion.

The issue will consist entirely of fresh shares. That means the proceeds will go to the company rather than existing shareholders selling their holdings.

A significant portion of the funds is expected to be used to reduce debt. That makes the offering's structure unusual among large Indian listings, where secondary sales often dominate.

Jio Platforms plans to use the funds primarily to repay or prepay, either fully or partly, around Rs 27,500 crore of outstanding borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm, its telecom subsidiary. The balance will be used for general corporate purposes.

The company has received encouraging feedback from foreign institutional investors (FIIs) and domestic investors during recent meetings, PTI reported.

Jio Platforms has wrapped up worldwide roadshows and is giving final touches to its public offer documents, according to a source aware of the development.

The roadshows were led by Jio Platforms Managing Director and Reliance Jio Chairman Akash Ambani. Reliance Retail Ventures Limited Executive Director Isha Ambani also fronted the investor outreach.

Jio Platforms Ltd is the digital holding company of Reliance Industries. It houses the group's digital businesses, including telecommunications.

Its telecom subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm, has a 39.29 per cent share of the mobile connections market. It serves 506 million mobile customers.

On the fixed-line side, Reliance Jio Infocomm holds a 32.89 per cent share of connections. It serves 157.9 million fixed-line customers.

Jio Platforms filed its draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) with SEBI in June. The regulator issued its final observations on August 28, allowing the company to move ahead with the offering.

Last month, Sebi's approval cleared the way for what is expected to be the country's largest stock-market listing. The regulatory sign-off has now set up the final sequencing of dates.

If the reported schedule holds, the anchor book opening on October 19 would give institutional investors a first look two days ahead of the retail and non-institutional window. The October 21-23 subscription window would then be followed by an October 28 listing.

For investors, the fresh-issue structure means any allocation directly funds the company's balance sheet. There is no offer-for-sale component in the reported structure, so existing shareholders are not cashing out through this issue.

The debt-repayment plan centres on roughly Rs 27,500 crore of borrowings at the telecom operating company. Prepaying or repaying those obligations could reduce interest costs at the subsidiary level and simplify the capital structure.

The investor feedback reported by PTI suggests demand-side comfort from both foreign and domestic institutions. However, the company's own reported timeline keeps the caveat front and centre: market conditions could still shift the dates.

For now, October 21 is the date to watch. Whether the window opens on schedule depends on how financial markets behave in the weeks leading up to the offer.
