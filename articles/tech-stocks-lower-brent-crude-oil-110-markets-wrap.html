Tech Stocks Drag Markets Lower as Brent Crude Oil Surges Past $110

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nasdaq 100 futures drop 0.6% on AI investment concerns after report that OpenAI missed user and sales targets
  • 02/Brent crude surges 3% to $111.53, marking seventh straight gain as Strait of Hormuz remains shut
  • 03/Fed, ECB and Bank of England face policy decisions this week with oil-driven inflation risks rising

Nasdaq 100 futures fell 0.6 per cent as technology stocks led equity markets lower on April 28. The Wall Street Journal reported that OpenAI recently missed goals for new users and sales.

SoftBank Group Corp., a major OpenAI backer, slumped 9.9 per cent in Tokyo trading. Shares of Oracle Corp., AMD and CoreWeave Inc. also fell in US premarket trading.

Brent crude advanced for a seventh straight day, rising 3 per cent to $111.53 a barrel. The Strait of Hormuz remains shut, keeping supply risks elevated.

S&P 500 futures fell 0.2 per cent while Dow Jones futures gained 0.3 per cent. The MSCI Asia Pacific Index dropped 0.4 per cent and the MSCI Emerging Markets Index fell 0.7 per cent.

Tech stocks had rallied nearly 10 per cent in April on AI optimism. Wednesday's earnings from four hyper-scalers will test whether that momentum holds.

"The single most important line item isn't revenue or margins; it's capex," said Amanda Lyons, IT-sector lead at Energy Group Capital. Any hint of slowing AI spend could weigh on the sector.

Euro-area consumers expect inflation at 4 per cent over 12 months, up from 2.5 per cent in February. Traders now see the ECB and Bank of England hiking as soon as June.

Bitcoin fell 0.5 per cent to $76,559.1 while Ether dropped 0.5 per cent to $2,280.17. Gold slid 1.5 per cent to $4,611.21 an ounce.

THE S&P 500 FUTURES FELL 0.2%, THE DOW JONES FUTURES ROSE 0.3%, THE MSCI ASIA PACIFIC INDEX FELL 0.4% AND THE MSCI EMERGING MARKETS INDEX DROPPED 0.7%. BITCOIN AND ETHER BOTH FELL 0.5%.
