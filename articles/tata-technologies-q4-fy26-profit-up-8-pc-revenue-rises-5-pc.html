Tata Technologies Q4 FY26: Profit Up 8% to Rs 204 Cr, Revenue Rises 5%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Q4 standalone net profit rises 8% YoY to Rs 204 crore on revenue of Rs 1,395 crore, up 5%
  • 02/FY26 full-year profit jumps to Rs 732 crore from Rs 676.95 crore; revenue at Rs 5,420 crore
  • 03/Board declares Rs 12 dividend per share; stock trades near Rs 574 after Q3 one-off charge normalized

Tata Technologies reported a standalone net profit of Rs 204 crore for the quarter ended March 2026, marking an 8% increase compared to the same period last year. Revenue for the quarter stood at Rs 1,395 crore, up 5% year-on-year.

For the full financial year 2025-26, the company posted a profit of Rs 732 crore against Rs 676.95 crore in FY25. Annual revenue rose to Rs 5,420 crore from Rs 5,168.5 crore in the previous year.

The board has declared a dividend of Rs 12 per share. The face value of each share is Rs 2.

The company provides engineering and digital services primarily to automotive and aerospace clients. Its client roster includes Jaguar Land Rover, Tata Motors, and BMW.

On May 4, 2026, the stock closed at around Rs 574 on the exchanges. The company had listed on the bourses in November 2023 through an IPO that raised Rs 3,042 crore.

The third quarter of FY26 had seen a one-time charge of Rs 139.87 crore related to labour codes, which weighed on earnings then. The fourth quarter results reflect normalized operations without such extraordinary items.

The full-year profit growth of roughly 8% signals steady operational execution despite a modest single-digit revenue expansion. The engineering services segment continues to drive the bulk of the topline.

Investors will watch how Tata Technologies scales its digital services portfolio and whether margin improvement sustains into FY27. The dividend payout remains a positive signal for minority shareholders.
