PB Fintech Shares Plunge 8% as Temasek Reportedly Sells ₹1,741 Crore Block

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PB Fintech shares plunge over 8% after block deal worth ₹1,741 crore, 2.37% equity changes hands
  • 02/Temasek Holdings reportedly sells 1.08 crore shares at ₹1,601, a 4.8% discount to Thursday's close
  • 03/Macritchie Investments held 6.47% prior; Tencent exited 1.05% stake in May for ₹805 crore

PB Fintech share price plunged more than 8% on Friday, 3 July. The drop followed a block deal worth around ₹1,741 crore that saw 2.37% of the company's equity change hands. Nearly 1.08 crore shares were traded at ₹1,601 apiece.

The transaction price represented a 4.8% discount to Thursday's closing price of ₹1,682.10. The identities of the buyer and seller were not disclosed on the exchanges. However, the deal closely aligns with media reports that Singapore's Temasek Holdings was planning to reduce its stake.

Temasek, through its subsidiary Macritchie Investments Pte Ltd, held a 6.47% stake in PB Fintech prior to the transaction. Reports indicated that Citigroup Global Markets India was acting as the sole placement agent for the sale. Temasek would remain subject to a 60-day lock-up on its remaining stake.

The reported sale aligns with earlier media reports that Temasek intended to sell up to 1.19 crore shares, or around 2.6% stake in PB Fintech, via a block deal valued at approximately ₹1,909 crore. Macritchie Investments held 6.47% before the transaction.

PB Fintech has witnessed a series of high-profile stake sales in recent months. In May, co-founders Yashish Dahiya and Alok Bansal sold around 38 lakh shares, equivalent to 0.8% stake, through a ₹654 crore block deal. During the same month, Tencent exited its remaining 1.05% stake in the company through a separate ₹805 crore block transaction.

The stock has been under pressure for some time. It is down nearly 38% year-to-date. The company operates Policybazaar and Paisabazaar, the online insurance and lending marketplaces. The stock had rallied strongly in 2024-25 but has given back much of those gains.

PB Fintech's market capitalisation is around ₹73,000 crore. The company reported revenue of ₹3,200 crore in FY26, up 28% year-on-year. It posted a net profit of ₹180 crore, turning profitable after years of losses.

The stake sale by Temasek, a long-term investor, could raise concerns about the company's valuation. However, the deal also brings in new institutional investors, which could provide liquidity and support for the stock in the medium term.
