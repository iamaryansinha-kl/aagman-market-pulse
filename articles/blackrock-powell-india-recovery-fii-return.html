BlackRock's Powell: India at Start of Major Recovery, FIIs Just Returning

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BlackRock's Ben Powell says India is at the start of a major equity recovery with foreign investors only beginning to return
  • 02/Oil prices normalising after US-Iran resolution gives India a huge tailwind; valuations look very inexpensive by historical standards
  • 03/Banks, construction, and self-reliance themes including defence and energy independence likely to attract returning FII capital

BlackRock Investment Institute's Ben Powell says India is at the start of a major equity recovery. Foreign investors are only beginning to rediscover the market.

Powell is Chief Investment Strategist for APAC at BlackRock. He spoke to ET Now from the United Arab Emirates on June 25, 2026.

Two key shifts are driving his optimism. Oil prices are normalising after the US-Iran resolution, and global investors are repricing India's growth story.

The reopening of Middle East shipping lanes has pushed oil back to pre-war levels. Powell called the energy normalisation "very huge for India" and said the tailwind is "only just beginning."

Powell said India's recent underperformance was not its own fault. The country was perceived not to be at the forefront of the AI trade and was caught on the wrong side of energy trends.

"I don't think India did anything wrong, it just got caught on the wrong side of those two global big trends," he said. He described the recovery as "not even the first inning."

On valuations, Powell said they look "very inexpensive by historical standards." He noted foreign buying had only just begun in the days prior to the interview.

Banks, construction, and the self-reliance theme will attract returning flows. This includes defence, domestic tech, and energy independence stocks aligned with the Made-in-India thesis.

Powell remains overweight on the US. He called AI "a hinge moment in human history" and said it is currently inflationary, not disinflationary, which keeps the Fed in wait-and-see mode.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES BLACKROCK'S BEN POWELL SAY ABOUT INDIA'S MARKET RECOVERY?
BEN POWELL, CHIEF INVESTMENT STRATEGIST FOR APAC AT BLACKROCK INVESTMENT INSTITUTE, SAYS INDIA IS AT THE START OF A MAJOR RECOVERY. FOREIGN INVESTORS ARE ONLY BEGINNING TO RETURN AND VALUATIONS LOOK VERY INEXPENSIVE BY HISTORICAL STANDARDS.
WHAT IS DRIVING THE OPTIMISM FOR INDIA'S EQUITY MARKETS?
TWO KEY SHIFTS ARE DRIVING OPTIMISM: NORMALISING OIL PRICES FOLLOWING THE US-IRAN RESOLUTION, AND A BROADER REPRICING OF INDIA'S LONG-TERM GROWTH STORY BY GLOBAL INVESTORS.
WHY DID INDIA UNDERPERFORM RECENTLY ACCORDING TO POWELL?
POWELL SAID INDIA'S UNDERPERFORMANCE HAD NOTHING TO DO WITH INDIA ITSELF. THE COUNTRY WAS PERCEIVED NOT TO BE AT THE FOREFRONT OF THE AI TRADE AND WAS ON THE WRONG SIDE OF THE ENERGY PROBLEM.
WHICH SECTORS WILL ATTRACT RETURNING FII CAPITAL?
POWELL IDENTIFIED BANKS, CONSTRUCTION, AND THE SELF-RELIANCE THEME AS KEY BENEFICIARIES. THIS INCLUDES DEFENCE, DOMESTIC TECHNOLOGY, AND ENERGY INDEPENDENCE STOCKS ALIGNED WITH THE MADE-IN-INDIA THESIS.
IS THE RECOVERY ALREADY PRICED INTO INDIAN EQUITIES?
POWELL SAID THE RECOVERY IS "NOT EVEN CLOSE" TO BEING PRICED IN. HE DESCRIBED THE CURRENT MOMENT AS "PRE-FIRST INNING," MEANING HIGHER PRICES WILL CREATE CONFIDENCE WHICH WILL CREATE MORE BUYING.
