Nifty, Sensex to Open Lower as Fed Holds Rates at 3.5%-3.75%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gift Nifty at 24,145 signals an 80-90 point gap-down opening for domestic indices on April 30, 2026
  • 02/US Federal Reserve holds benchmark rate at 3.5%-3.75% for third consecutive meeting with hawkish dissent among policymakers
  • 03/Friday May 1 is Maharashtra Day holiday; traders may avoid carrying positions amid global volatility and geopolitical risks

Domestic markets are set for a weak opening on Thursday. Gift Nifty at 24,145 signals an 80-90 point gap-down for the Nifty 50.

The Nifty closed at 24,177.65 on Wednesday, up 181.95 points. The Sensex finished at 77,496.36, gaining 609.45 points.

The US Federal Reserve held the federal funds rate at 3.5% to 3.75%. This marks the third consecutive meeting without a rate change.

The Federal Reserve characterised inflation as "elevated," shifting from "somewhat elevated" previously. Strong dissents emerged among policymakers on the rate outlook.

"While domestic indices are holding near key levels, the broader setup remains fragile," said Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder, Livelong Wealth.

Ajitabh Bharti, Executive Director and Co-founder, CapitalXB, said the lack of a rate cut means FPIs may remain hesitant. "Foreign Institutional Investors might stay away from Indian equities until US yields soften," he said.

Asian markets opened largely in the red on Thursday. Geopolitical tensions around the Iran conflict continue to keep risk appetite subdued.

Friday May 1 is a market holiday for Maharashtra Day. Traders are unlikely to carry positions into the long weekend amid global uncertainty.
