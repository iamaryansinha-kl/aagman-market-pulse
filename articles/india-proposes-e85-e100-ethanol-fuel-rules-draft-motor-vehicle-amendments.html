India Proposes E85, E100 Ethanol Fuel Rules in Draft Motor Vehicle Amendments

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoRTH issues draft notification to amend Central Motor Vehicles Rules for E85 and E100 fuels on April 28, 2026
  • 02/India has achieved E20 nationwide rollout from April 1, with ethanol production capacity at 20 billion litres
  • 03/30-day public comment period open; Toyota, Maruti Suzuki and Honda have showcased flex-fuel prototypes

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a draft notification late on April 28, 2026. It proposes amendments to the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989.

The draft formally incorporates E85 fuel containing 85 per cent ethanol and E100 for near-pure ethanol operation. Biodiesel definitions are expanded from B10 to B100.

The proposal is open for public comments for 30 days. A final decision will follow the consultation period.

India completed its nationwide E20 rollout on April 1, 2026. The 20 per cent ethanol blending target was achieved ahead of the original 2030 schedule.

Union Minister Nitin Gadkari said last week, "In the near future, India should aspire to achieve 100 per cent ethanol blending." He cited the energy crisis from the West Asia conflict as the reason for self-reliance.

India's ethanol production capacity stands at approximately 20 billion litres. Current domestic demand is around 10 billion litres, leaving significant surplus capacity.

Ethanol has 27-30 per cent lower energy density than petrol. Existing E20 vehicles cannot run on E85 due to corrosion risks and require flex-fuel engines.

Toyota, Maruti Suzuki and Honda have showcased flex-fuel prototypes. Two-wheeler makers are also preparing compatible models for higher ethanol blends.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES THE DRAFT NOTIFICATION PROPOSED BY MORTH INCLUDE?
THE DRAFT PROPOSES AMENDMENTS TO THE CENTRAL MOTOR VEHICLES RULES TO FORMALLY INCORPORATE E85 (85% ETHANOL) AND E100 (NEAR-PURE ETHANOL) FUELS. IT ALSO EXPANDS BIODIESEL DEFINITIONS FROM B10 TO B100.
WHEN DID INDIA ACHIEVE THE E20 ETHANOL BLENDING TARGET?
INDIA COMPLETED THE NATIONWIDE ROLLOUT OF E20 PETROL ON APRIL 1, 2026. THE 20 PER CENT ETHANOL BLENDING TARGET WAS ACHIEVED AHEAD OF THE ORIGINAL 2030 TIMELINE UNDER THE NATIONAL BIOFUEL POLICY.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT ETHANOL PRODUCTION CAPACITY IN INDIA?
INDIA HAS AN ESTIMATED ETHANOL PRODUCTION CAPACITY OF AROUND 20 BILLION LITRES, INCLUDING GRAIN-BASED DISTILLERIES. CURRENT DOMESTIC DEMAND IS APPROXIMATELY 10 BILLION LITRES.
WHY CAN EXISTING E20 VEHICLES NOT USE E85 FUEL?
E85 CANNOT BE USED IN EXISTING E20 VEHICLES BECAUSE ETHANOL IS CORROSIVE TO CONVENTIONAL ENGINE COMPONENTS. FLEX-FUEL VEHICLES WITH SPECIALISED SENSORS AND CORROSION-RESISTANT PARTS ARE REQUIRED FOR E85 AND E100 OPERATION.
HOW LONG IS THE PUBLIC COMMENT PERIOD FOR THE DRAFT RULES?
THE DRAFT NOTIFICATION HAS BEEN OPENED FOR PUBLIC COMMENTS FOR 30 DAYS. THE MINISTRY WILL FINALISE THE RULES AFTER REVIEWING STAKEHOLDER FEEDBACK.
