Demat Account Additions Pick Up on D-Street Rebound

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Retail investors are returning to the market.

Demat account additions have picked up as Indian stock markets rebound from recent corrections. The acceleration in new account openings signals renewed retail interest in equities after a period of subdued participation.

India has one of the world's largest retail investor bases. The total number of demat accounts has crossed 15 crore, with millions of new accounts opened in the past year alone. The pace of additions had slowed during the market correction but is now recovering.

The rebound in account openings coincides with improving market sentiment. The Nifty and Sensex have recovered from their lows, and investors who had stayed on the sidelines are now re-entering. Systematic investment plans have also seen increased registrations.

Young investors continue to drive growth. A significant portion of new demat accounts are opened by investors under 30, who prefer digital platforms and app-based trading. The democratisation of investing through zero-brokerage platforms has lowered entry barriers.

The trend is positive for market liquidity and depth. A broader retail base provides stability during foreign investor outflows. Domestic investors have increasingly become the counterbalance to FPI selling in recent years.

However, the quality of new investors remains a concern. Many first-time investors lack experience and may be entering near market highs. Market regulator Sebi has been running investor education campaigns to promote informed participation.
