Rupee Falls 41 Paise to Close at 94.56 Against US Dollar

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee depreciated 41 paise to close at 94.56 (provisional) against the US dollar on Tuesday, April 28, 2026
  • 02/Brent crude surged 2.62% to $111.07 per barrel; FIIs have pulled out over $19 billion from Indian equities this year
  • 03/Rupee likely to average 92-93 range in FY27 if crude stays at $85-90, says CareEdge economist Rajani Sinha

The rupee depreciated 41 paise to close at 94.56 (provisional) against the US dollar on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. It opened at 94.35 and touched an intraday low of 94.58 during the session.

On Monday, the rupee had settled with gains of just one paisa at 94.15. The sharp reversal reflects renewed selling pressure in currency markets.

Elevated crude oil prices and unabated foreign capital outflows dented investor sentiment. Foreign Institutional Investors have pulled out over $19 billion from Indian equities so far this year.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading higher by 2.62% at $111.07 per barrel. The dollar index rose 0.24% to 98.73, adding pressure on emerging market currencies.

On the domestic equity market front, the Sensex dropped 416.72 points to settle at 76,886.91. The Nifty declined 97 points to close at 23,995.70.

"The primary pressure on the rupee stems from weak capital inflows. Net FDI inflows have been subdued, while FPI flows have been affected by elevated global uncertainty," said Rajani Sinha, Chief Economist, CareEdge Ratings.

Sinha expects the rupee to average 92-93 in FY27 if crude prices stay at $85-90 per barrel. However, further escalation in the West Asia conflict could trigger additional weakening.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday, according to exchange data. Persistent foreign fund outflows continue to weigh heavily on the rupee.
