Sensex, Nifty Open Weak on April 30: Gift Nifty Signals 80-90 Point Gap Down

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gift Nifty at 24,145 signals an 80-90 point gap down opening for the Nifty on April 30 amid mixed global cues
  • 02/US Federal Reserve holds rates steady at 3.5%-3.75% for a third straight meeting but characterizes inflation as "elevated"
  • 03/Markets face added uncertainty with Friday's Maharashtra Day holiday keeping traders cautious about carrying positions

Domestic equity markets opened on a weak note on April 30, 2026. Gift Nifty at 24,145 indicated a gap down of approximately 80-90 points from the previous close of 24,177.65.

On April 29, the Sensex had closed at 77,496.36, up 609.45 points or 0.79%. The Nifty 50 finished at 24,177.65, gaining 181.95 points or 0.76%.

The US Federal Reserve held its benchmark rate steady at 3.5%-3.75% for the third consecutive meeting. It shifted its inflation description from "somewhat elevated" to "elevated," citing global energy prices.

"While domestic indices are holding near key levels, the broader setup remains fragile, shaped by elevated oil prices, global uncertainty, and a high-impact event calendar," said Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder, Livelong Wealth.

Asian markets opened largely in the red, mirroring overnight weakness on Wall Street. Brent crude near USD 115 per barrel continues to pressure risk appetite and imported inflation.

With Friday being a closed holiday for Maharashtra Day, traders are unlikely to carry positions through the session. This typically amplifies volatility and compresses trading volumes on the preceding day.

Immediate Nifty support is placed at the 24,000 level, with resistance near 24,300-24,350. A sustained breakdown below 24,000 could trigger selling pressure toward 23,957.

FII net selling of Rs 595.78 crore on April 29 was offset by DII buying of Rs 2,103.74 crore. This domestic-institutional tug-of-war remains a key flow dynamic to watch.
