Sensex Rebounds 359 Points to 77,246 as Asian Markets Rally on April 29

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex climbs 358.92 points to 77,245.83 and NSE Nifty rises 101.2 points to 24,096.90 in opening trade on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Buying by domestic institutional investors and a rally in Asian markets helped cushion Tuesday's decline of 416 points on the Sensex
  • 03/All eyes on state election results and Middle East developments for market direction in the coming sessions

Equity benchmarks Sensex and Nifty rebounded in early trade on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The 30-share BSE Sensex climbed 358.92 points to 77,245.83 at the open.

The 50-share NSE Nifty gained 101.2 points to 24,096.90. Both indices had ended lower on Tuesday amid geopolitical uncertainty.

Buying by domestic institutional investors offered support to the markets. Analysts noted that DII flows helped cushion the downside from sustained foreign institutional investor outflows.

A rally in Asian markets also boosted sentiment. Hong Kong's Hang Seng was up 1%, while the Shanghai Composite gained 0.1%.

The rupee remained under pressure, opening 0.2% lower at 94.74 per US dollar. It had closed at a one-month low of 94.54 on Tuesday.

Maruti Suzuki surged 4.44% to ₹13,465, while Coal India climbed 3.16% to ₹481.75. Tech Mahindra and Reliance Industries also featured among the early gainers.

The Nifty Oil and Gas index was a top performer, with ONGC hitting a fresh 52-week high. Nifty Metal was the only major index in the red, down 0.77%.

State election results due Wednesday could add volatility. The market is also watching Middle East developments and the US Federal Reserve's two-day policy meeting.
