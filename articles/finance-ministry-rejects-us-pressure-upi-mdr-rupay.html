Finance Ministry Rejects US Pressure Claims Over New UPI MDR, Says Only RuPay Credit Runs on UPI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ The Finance Ministry denied that US pressure influenced the new 0.4% UPI MDR.
  • •- 02/ The NPCI circular of September 15 allows only RuPay credit cards for credit transactions on UPI.
  • •- 03/ The 0.4% MDR applies from October 15 to merchant payments above Rs 2,000.
  • •- 04/ Consumers still pay zero; small merchants and rural QR payments stay free.
  • •- 05/ UPI processed 24.51 billion transactions worth Rs 29.82 lakh crore in August.

## Ministry pushes back

The Department of Financial Services on Thursday rejected claims that American pressure shaped India's decision to impose a Merchant Discount Rate on select UPI payments. The clarification responded to observations in the US Trade Representative's 2026 report about the inability of US electronic payment providers to participate in UPI on a level playing field.

"The allegation that MDR has been introduced under any external influence is patently false and misleading," the ministry said in a post on X. The NPCI circular of September 15 does not allow credit transactions on UPI through any card other than RuPay, it noted — a policy designed to make RuPay the preferred credit card in India.

Opposition parties, including the Congress, had alleged the government succumbed to US pressure while deciding to introduce the MDR.

## What the new MDR does

From October 15, a 0.4% MDR applies to person-to-merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000. The charge is levied on merchants, not consumers, and is capped at Rs 300 for transactions of Rs 75,000 or more.

Person-to-person transfers and transactions below Rs 2,000 remain free.

For essential services — railways, telecom, fuel and insurance — a flat Rs 5 fee applies above Rs 2,000. Capital-market payments such as mutual fund and stockbroking transactions attract a lower 0.02% MDR, also capped at Rs 300.

Small merchants receiving up to Rs 1 lakh a month through UPI QR codes are exempt, and QR payments in rural and semi-urban areas remain free. Five per cent of MDR collections will fund expanded UPI acceptance among small merchants.

## The competition argument

The ministry said NPCI's 30% market-share cap for third-party app providers, mandated in November 2020, could not be implemented because smaller companies had no revenue model to compete with market leaders.

"Introduction of MDR on select high-value transactions will provide a self-sustaining revenue model to smaller companies to compete for a higher share in the UPI ecosystem," it said, calling the move a step in protecting India's sovereignty in electronic payments.

UPI processed 24.51 billion transactions worth nearly Rs 29.82 lakh crore in August 2026 — scale that, until now, generated no direct revenue for the ecosystem.

## FAQ Q: Did US pressure cause the UPI MDR? A/01/ The Finance Ministry says no, calling the allegation "patently false and misleading" and pointing to the September 15 NPCI circular as an existing-policy decision.

Q: What changes on October 15? A/02/ A 0.4% MDR applies to merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300. Consumers continue to pay nothing.

Q: Which transactions stay free? A/03/ Person-to-person transfers, payments below Rs 2,000, small merchants up to Rs 1 lakh monthly via QR, and rural or semi-urban QR payments.

Q: Can international credit cards run on UPI? A/04/ No. The NPCI circular permits only RuPay credit cards for credit transactions on UPI.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS - The government framed the UPI MDR as sovereignty protection, not a concession. - Only RuPay credit cards can process credit transactions on UPI. - Merchants above Rs 2,000 pay 0.4%, capped at Rs 300; consumers pay nothing. - August UPI volume: 24.51 billion transactions, Rs 29.82 lakh crore.
