Sandeep Bagla: Largecap Valuations Ripe for Comeback as Nifty Stagnates

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/TRUST Mutual Fund CEO Sandeep Bagla says largecap valuations in banking and IT have corrected to comfortable levels, offering a prime entry point for 2026
  • 02/The new TRUSTMF Large & Midcap Fund mandates minimum 35% allocation each to large-cap and mid-cap stocks, with residual exposure to small caps
  • 03/Key themes: financialization of savings, premium consumption, infrastructure, defence, and technological innovation

Sandeep Bagla, CEO of TRUST Mutual Fund, sees corrected largecap valuations as a strategic entry point for investors in 2026. The stagnation in Nifty, driven by sluggish banking and IT heavyweights, has created an opportunity.

The newly launched TRUSTMF Large & Midcap Fund mandates a minimum 35% allocation each to large-cap and mid-cap companies. The residual portion allows up to 30% in small-cap stocks, with around 15% expected exposure.

Bagla notes that heavyweight sectors like banking and IT have seen valuations compress to comfortable levels. Earnings outlook is improving as investor concerns subside, making largecaps attractive for medium-term investors.

The fund aims to capture both the resilience of large caps and the growth potential of mid caps. Sector weightages will drive alpha in large caps, while stock selection will matter more in mid and small caps.

Between 2020 and 2025, three stocks moved from small caps to large caps generating 108% CAGR. Forty-three stocks moved from small caps to mid caps, delivering 58% CAGR. This demonstrates the growth trajectory in smaller segments.

Bagla is bullish on five key megatrends: financialization of savings, premium consumption, infrastructure creation, defence, and technological innovation. He believes these themes have a long runway as India's per capita GDP rises.

India is estimated to add 20% of the global working age population in the coming years. As incomes rise, consumption and investment will grow, benefiting companies serving these needs.

"We believe that growth investing is likely to be rewarding and we are currently favourably inclined towards themes like financialization of savings, premium consumption, infrastructure creation, defence, and technological innovation." - Sandeep Bagla, CEO, TRUST Mutual Fund.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TRUSTMF LARGE & MIDCAP FUND?
THE TRUSTMF LARGE & MIDCAP FUND IS A NEWLY LAUNCHED SCHEME FROM TRUST MUTUAL FUND THAT MANDATES A MINIMUM 35% ALLOCATION EACH TO LARGE-CAP AND MID-CAP COMPANIES. THE RESIDUAL PORTION ALLOWS UP TO 30% IN SMALL-CAP STOCKS, WITH AN EXPECTED ALLOCATION OF AROUND 15%.
WHY DOES SANDEEP BAGLA SEE LARGE CAPS AS ATTRACTIVE NOW?
BAGLA BELIEVES LARGE CAP VALUATIONS, ESPECIALLY IN BANKING AND IT, HAVE CORRECTED TO COMFORTABLE LEVELS AFTER UNDERPERFORMING. HE SEES THIS AS A PRIME ENTRY POINT FOR INVESTORS WITH A MEDIUM-TERM HORIZON AS EARNINGS OUTLOOK IMPROVES.
WHAT ARE THE KEY MEGATRENDS BAGLA IS TRACKING?
THE FIVE KEY MEGATRENDS ARE: FINANCIALIZATION OF SAVINGS, PREMIUM CONSUMPTION, INFRASTRUCTURE CREATION, DEFENCE, AND TECHNOLOGICAL INNOVATION. THESE ARE EXPECTED TO BENEFIT FROM RISING PER CAPITA GDP AND STEADY ECONOMIC GROWTH.
WHAT IS THE SMALL CAP ALLOCATION EXPECTED IN THE FUND?
THE FUND CAN ALLOCATE UP TO 30% TO SMALL CAPS, BUT THE EXPECTED ALLOCATION IS AROUND 15%. BAGLA NOTES THAT SMALL CAPS REMAIN AN OCEAN OF OPPORTUNITIES IN INDIA.
WHAT IS BAGLA'S VIEW ON BANKING AND IT SECTORS?
BAGLA HAS BEEN UNDERWEIGHT ON LARGE IT COMPANIES AND BANKS DUE TO LOW GROWTH AND HIGH VALUATIONS. HOWEVER, HE NOW BELIEVES THE BANKING SYSTEM WILL SEE IMPROVED LIQUIDITY AND BETTER MARGINS DUE TO RECENT MEASURES ON FOREIGN DEPOSITS AND ECB CHANGES.
