ED Attaches ₹3,034 Cr Anil Ambani Assets in RCom Money Laundering Case

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ED attaches fresh assets worth ₹3,034.90 crore belonging to Anil Ambani's Reliance Group under PMLA Section 5 on April 28, 2026
  • 02/Total seizure in Reliance Anil Ambani Group cases now stands at ₹19,344 crore across multiple attachment orders
  • 03/Attached assets include Mumbai flat, Khandala farmhouse, Ahmedabad land, and 7.71 crore R-Infra shares held via family trust

The Enforcement Directorate has attached assets worth ₹3,034.90 crore belonging to Anil Ambani's Reliance Group. The provisional order was issued on April 28, 2026 under Section 5 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).

The attached assets include a flat in Usha Kiran Building, Mumbai. They also include a farmhouse in Khandala and land parcels in Sanand, Ahmedabad.

The order also covers 7.71 crore shares of Reliance Infrastructure Ltd. These shares are held by Risee Infinity Pvt Ltd under the RiseE Trust umbrella.

The RiseE Trust is a private family trust of Anil Ambani's family members. ED says it was set up to shield assets from personal liabilities arising from personal guarantees to lender banks.

The fresh attachment takes the total seizure in Reliance Anil Ambani Group cases to ₹19,344 crore. This includes previous attachments of the Pali Hill residence and 42 other properties.

RCOM and its group companies owe ₹40,185 crore to domestic and foreign lenders. Nine banks have declared the loan accounts as fraud based on CBI FIRs.

The ED probe is based on CBI FIRs registered on complaints from State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, and LIC of India.

A Special Investigation Team constituted on Supreme Court directions is probing the case. ED said confiscated property will be restored to victim banks after legal process.
