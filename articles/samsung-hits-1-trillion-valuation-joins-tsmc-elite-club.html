Samsung Hits $1 Trillion Valuation, Joins TSMC In Elite Asia Tech Club

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Samsung Electronics reached a $1 trillion market valuation after shares quadrupled over the past year
  • 02/The milestone makes it only the second Asian firm after TSMC to hit the mark, driven by AI chip demand
  • 03/SK Hynix and TSMC also hit record highs as Asia becomes cornerstone of global AI ecosystem

Samsung Electronics reached a $1 trillion market valuation on Wednesday. Shares in the world's largest memory maker have more than quadrupled over the past year.

The milestone came as Samsung shares rallied as much as 13% in Seoul trading. The gains pushed South Korea's Kospi benchmark above 7,000 for the first time ever.

Samsung, SK Hynix and TSMC sit at the heart of Asia's AI transformation. The trio's chipmaking dominance has made the region a cornerstone of the global AI ecosystem.

Samsung's semiconductor arm reported historic March quarter profits. The division delivered a 48-fold jump as AI data centre orders drove hefty margins.

Analysts expect the memory supercycle to continue through 2027. Samsung itself warned that supply will tighten further next year, keeping NAND and DRAM prices elevated.

The stock trades at just 5.9 times forward earnings, down from 14.4 times in October. Sell-side estimates suggest roughly 25% upside over the next 12 months.

Foreign investors are driving the rally, with a near-record 2.9 trillion won of Kospi shares bought on Monday. The won climbed as much as 1.3% versus the dollar.
