Hindustan Zinc Shares Fall 7%: Silver Crash Drags Stock Lower

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hindustan Zinc shares crashed 7% over two consecutive trading sessions amid a sharp correction in silver prices
  • 02/MCX silver plunged over Rs 5,000 per kg in a single day and has corrected nearly 13% from recent peaks
  • 03/The stock's movement remains tightly linked to silver price trends; investors should watch MCX silver levels closely

Hindustan Zinc Ltd shares have fallen 7% over the past two trading sessions. The sharp decline follows a crash in silver prices on the Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX).

MCX silver plunged over Rs 5,000 per kg in a single trading day. The white metal has now corrected nearly 13% from its recent highs.

The selloff in silver has directly impacted Hindustan Zinc's stock. The company is one of India's largest integrated zinc and silver producers.

Silver forms a significant portion of Hindustan Zinc's revenue mix. Any volatility in precious metal prices tends to reflect immediately in the company's stock performance.

The broader metals complex has witnessed heightened volatility in recent weeks. Global cues and demand outlook concerns have triggered sharp moves across commodities.

Hindustan Zinc is a subsidiary of Vedanta Ltd and operates one of the world's largest zinc mines. Its stock has historically shown high correlation with zinc and silver price movements.

Investors holding the counter should track MCX silver prices for near-term direction. A sustained recovery in silver may help stabilise the stock at current levels.
