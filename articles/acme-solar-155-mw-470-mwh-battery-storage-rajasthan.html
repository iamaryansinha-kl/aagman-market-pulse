ACME Solar Commissions 155 MW/470 MWh Battery Storage in Rajasthan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ACME Solar Holdings commissions 155 MW/470 MWh of battery storage projects in Rajasthan through its subsidiaries
  • 02/Projects are connected to the Inter-State Transmission System and will operate on a merchant basis
  • 03/Company owns 2,540 MW operational solar capacity; BESS adds grid-scale storage to its renewable portfolio

ACME Solar Holdings Ltd (NSE: ACMESOLAR) has commissioned 155 MW/470 MWh of battery energy storage projects in Rajasthan. The projects were developed through the company's subsidiaries.

The newly commissioned storage assets are connected to the Inter-State Transmission System (ISTS). They will operate on a merchant basis, selling power into the open market.

This marks ACME Solar's entry into grid-scale battery storage at the commercial level. The company has historically focused on solar IPP development and operations.

ACME Solar owns and operates 2,540 MW of solar capacity across India. It has another 3,780 MW under development, spanning solar, wind, and hybrid projects.

The company recently secured ₹1,100 crore in refinancing from SBI for a 300 MW renewable project in Rajasthan. This signals active capital management across its portfolio.

Shares of ACME Solar closed at approximately ₹275 on the BSE. The stock has gained around 20% from its IPO listing price of ₹253.50.
