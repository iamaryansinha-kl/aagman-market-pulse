8 Stocks in Focus: Paytm RBI Action, Wipro AI Deal, Mastek Sale Lead Monday

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI cancelled Paytm Payments Bank licence on April 24, 2026; Paytm says core app, UPI, QR services remain fully operational with zero financial impact
  • 02/Wipro partners with Kongsberg Digital for AI Digital Twin solutions; Mastek signs ₹60 crore building sale MoU for 1,57,233 sq. ft. Chennai asset
  • 03/NLC India receives composite mining licence for two Chhattisgarh blocks; Pine Labs acquires Shopflo serving 1,000+ e-commerce brands

Wipro Ltd (NSE: WIPRO) has partnered with Kongsberg Digital to deploy AI-powered Digital Twin solutions for the Energy & Utilities sector. The collaboration was announced on April 23, 2026.

Mastek (NSE: MASTEK) signed a Memorandum of Understanding to sell a commercial building with 1,57,233 sq. ft. built-up area at Mahindra World City SEZ, Chennai. The ₹60 crore deal will be received in two tranches.

One 97 Communications Ltd (NSE: PAYTM) clarified that RBI cancelled the banking licence of associate Paytm Payments Bank Ltd on April 24, 2026. The company stated its core services continue without disruption and it has zero financial exposure to PPBL.

NLC India Ltd (NSE: NLCINDIA) received a composite licence from the Chhattisgarh government for Semhardih and Raipura Phosphorite & Limestone blocks in Balod district. The company was the Preferred Bidder in the Tranche-V Critical and Strategic Minerals auction.

Pine Labs acquired Shopflo, a checkout optimisation platform used by over 1,000 e-commerce brands and 60 million consumers. The deal expands Pine Labs into a full-stack offline and online commerce platform.

Ampvolts Ltd will build 17 high-capacity EV charging hubs in Western India with Cholamandalam Leasing Ltd. The infrastructure will support Montra Electric RHINO 5538 trucks for long-haul freight.

Innovision secured a labour supply order from MP East Zone Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd in Jabalpur. Quality Power Electrical Equipments Ltd won a reactor supply order for a US data centre project.

The BSE Sensex closed at 76,664.21, down 999.79 points, and the NSE Nifty settled at 23,897.95, down 275.10 points on April 27, 2026. Investors are tracking these eight stocks for corporate action impact.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY IS PAYTM IN FOCUS ON APRIL 27, 2026?
THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA CANCELLED THE BANKING LICENCE OF PAYTM PAYMENTS BANK LIMITED ON APRIL 24, 2026, EFFECTIVE FROM THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS THAT DAY. ONE 97 COMMUNICATIONS, PAYTM'S PARENT, CONFIRMED IT HAS NO FINANCIAL EXPOSURE TO PPBL AND THAT ALL CORE SERVICES INCLUDING THE PAYTM APP, UPI, QR, SOUNDBOX, AND PAYMENT GATEWAY CONTINUE WITHOUT INTERRUPTION.
WHAT IS THE WIPRO-KONGSBERG DIGITAL PARTNERSHIP?
WIPRO LTD HAS ENTERED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH KONGSBERG DIGITAL TO JOINTLY DEPLOY NEXT-GENERATION AI-POWERED DIGITAL TWIN SOLUTIONS FOR THE ENERGY AND UTILITIES SECTOR. THE COLLABORATION WILL PROVIDE A UNIFIED ENVIRONMENT INTEGRATING PHYSICS-BASED ENGINEERING MODELS, REAL-TIME OPERATIONS, AND ENTERPRISE AI.
WHAT ASSET SALE HAS MASTEK ANNOUNCED?
MASTEK HAS ENTERED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR THE SALE OF A COMMERCIAL BUILDING WITH APPROXIMATE BUILT-UP AREA OF 1,57,233 SQ. FT. ON 15.50 ACRES OF LEASED LAND AT MAHINDRA WORLD CITY SEZ, CHENGALPATTU, CHENNAI. THE AGGREGATE CONSIDERATION IS ₹60 CRORE, TO BE RECEIVED IN TWO TRANCHES AS PER THE MOU TERMS.
WHAT MINING LICENCE HAS NLC INDIA RECEIVED?
NLC INDIA LIMITED HAS RECEIVED A COMPOSITE LICENCE FROM THE MINERAL RESOURCES DEPARTMENT, GOVERNMENT OF CHHATTISGARH, FOR THE SEMHARDIH PHOSPHORITE AND LIMESTONE BLOCK AND THE RAIPURA PHOSPHORITE AND LIMESTONE BLOCK IN BALOD DISTRICT, CHHATTISGARH. NLCIL EMERGED AS THE PREFERRED BIDDER IN THE TRANCHE-V AUCTION OF CRITICAL AND STRATEGIC MINERALS CONDUCTED BY THE MINISTRY OF MINES.
WHAT IS THE PINE LABS-SHOPFLO ACQUISITION?
PINE LABS HAS ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF SHOPFLO, A NEXT-GENERATION ONLINE CHECKOUT OPTIMISATION PLATFORM TRUSTED BY OVER 1,000 E-COMMERCE BRANDS AND POWERING SEAMLESS EXPERIENCES FOR MORE THAN 60 MILLION CONSUMERS. THE DEAL MARKS A DEFINING STEP IN PINE LABS' EVOLUTION INTO A FULL-STACK PAYMENTS AND COMMERCE PLATFORM SERVING MERCHANTS ACROSS BOTH OFFLINE AND ONLINE CHANNELS.
