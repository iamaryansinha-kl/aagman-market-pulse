Aastha Spintex IPO Subscribed 52% on Day 1; GMP at 4% Suggests Modest Listing Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Aastha Spintex IPO subscribed 52% by mid-session on Day 1, with NIIs leading at 87% while retail lagged at 26%
  • 02/The ₹170 crore fresh issue is priced at ₹125-136 per share; GMP at 4% indicates modest estimated listing price of ₹141
  • 03/Anchor book closed June 25; subscription closes July 1, allotment July 2, listing on BSE and NSE expected July 6

Aastha Spintex Ltd opened its ₹170 crore IPO for subscription on Monday, June 29, 2026. By 2:10 pm, the issue was subscribed 52% with 70.72 lakh bids received against 1.36 crore shares on offer.

The Non-Institutional Investor (NII) segment led the demand, subscribed 87% with 47.33 lakh bids against 54.40 lakh shares reserved. Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) bid for 36% of their quota, while Retail Individual Investors (RIIs) subscribed 26% with 14.14 lakh bids against 54.40 lakh shares allocated.

The Grey Market Premium (GMP) hovered around 4%, or ₹4-5 per share, indicating modest listing expectations. At the upper price band of ₹136, the estimated listing price works out to approximately ₹141. The IPO carries no offer-for-sale (OFS) component and is entirely a fresh issue.

The Gujarat-based cotton yarn manufacturer, founded in 2013, priced the issue between ₹125-136 per share. The face value is ₹10 per share and the lot size is 110 shares, requiring a minimum retail investment of ₹14,960. Promoter holding will drop from 74.23% pre-IPO to 53.21% post-listing.

Financially, total income rose to ₹352.2 crore in FY25 from ₹239.7 crore in FY23. Net profit jumped sharply to ₹22.9 crore from ₹1.1 crore over the same period. The company reported a return on equity of 12.8% and the IPO is valued at approximately 18.8 times FY25 earnings.

The company plans to use ₹111.51 crore of the proceeds to acquire the remaining 84.79% stake in Falcon Yarns Private Limited. This acquisition is expected to more than double spinning capacity from 7,700 MT to 17,457 MT annually. An additional ₹10 crore will fund Falcon Yarns' working capital.

Swastika Investmart has assigned a "Subscribe" rating for medium- to long-term investors. The brokerage noted that investments in solar and wind power now meet nearly 80% of energy requirements, which should help reduce production costs. At 18.8x FY25 earnings, the valuation is considered reasonable for the growth profile.

Risks include exposure to cotton price fluctuations, dependence on a single manufacturing facility at Halvad, Morbi, and customer concentration through a key reseller. Demand shifts toward synthetic fibres also remain a long-term concern for the cotton yarn business.
