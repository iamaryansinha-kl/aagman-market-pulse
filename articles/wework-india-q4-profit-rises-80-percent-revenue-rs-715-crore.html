WeWork India Q4 Profit Surges 80% to Rs 65.55 Cr, Revenue at Rs 715 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/WeWork India Q4 net profit rises 80% to Rs 65.55 crore versus Rs 36.51 crore in year-ago period
  • 02/Total income grows 29% to Rs 715.35 crore; FY26 revenue reaches Rs 2,490.42 crore
  • 03/Company operates 76 centres across 8 cities; managed office demand remains strong

WeWork India Management Ltd reported consolidated net profit of Rs 65.55 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks an 80% jump from Rs 36.51 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Total income rose to Rs 715.35 crore in the March quarter. The figure stood at Rs 554.87 crore in Q4 FY25.

For the full financial year 2025-26, net profit came in at Rs 74.43 crore. This was lower than Rs 127.39 crore reported in FY25.

Total income for FY26 increased to Rs 2,490.42 crore. The prior year saw income of Rs 2,024 crore.

WeWork India operates 76 co-working centres. These span 8.6 million sq ft across eight cities.

The portfolio covers Bengaluru, Mumbai, Gurugram, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Noida and New Delhi. Demand for managed office spaces has been rising steadily.

The company was launched in 2017. It has grown to become one of India's largest flexible workspace operators.

The revenue growth reflects strong occupancy and expansion. Higher rentals in key markets also contributed.
