LTM Launches BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru to Scale Enterprise Agentic AI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LTM Limited launched BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026, to accelerate enterprise agentic AI adoption and close the gap between AI pilots and production deployment
  • 02/The company reported Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹11,291.7 crore, up 15.6% YoY, with net profit surging 23.4% to ₹1,392.3 crore, signalling a pivot toward high-margin AI services
  • 03/BlueVerse Studio joins existing AI hubs in London and Mumbai; execution and client adoption will determine how much the initiative contributes to LTM's stated goal of doubling revenue in five years

LTM Limited launched its BlueVerse Studio in Bengaluru on April 28, 2026. The facility is designed as a collaborative hub to help enterprises scale AI solutions from experiments to practical, day-to-day applications.

The studio unites LTM's AI capabilities under one ecosystem. This includes BlueVerse CraftStudio, physical AI showcases, industry-specific offerings, and a framework for rapid prototyping with reusable components.

Clients can run separate agentic AI pilots with clear plans for scaling, governance, and ROI measurement. The setup uses client data and KPIs to demonstrate end-to-end transformation and multi-agent collaboration.

"The BlueVerse ecosystem is central to our vision to be our clients' gateway to the agentic enterprise era," said Venu Lambu, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, LTM. "More than a showcase of technology, BlueVerse Studio is a collaborative space where our best ideas are tested, solutions are accelerated, and clients gain the confidence to scale AI responsibly."

The launch comes on the back of strong Q4 FY26 numbers. Revenue reached ₹11,291.7 crore, up 15.6% YoY. Net profit climbed 23.4% to ₹1,392.3 crore, with EBITDA margin at 15.1%.

For the full year ended March 31, 2026, LTM posted consolidated revenue of $4.76 billion, a 6% rise in dollar terms and 5.3% in constant currency. Operating EBIT grew faster than topline, reflecting margin expansion.

The company has already deployed over 1,500 "digital employees" into core finance and operations. This marks a deliberate shift away from headcount-linked growth toward non-linear, AI-driven revenue models.

LTM's name change from LTIMindtree was completed on March 17, 2026. The rebrand is part of a broader strategic pivot to position itself as an AI-centric "Business Creativity" partner within the Larsen & Toubro Group.

The Bengaluru studio joins existing AI innovation hubs in London and Mumbai. LTM has also partnered with IIT Kharagpur and MIT Open Learning to upskill its workforce through platforms like BlueVerse SkillSphere.

Shares of LTM Limited traded around ₹4,368 on the NSE on April 28, 2026, roughly flat for the session. The modest price move suggests investors are tracking the AI pivot but waiting for proof of sustained revenue contribution.
