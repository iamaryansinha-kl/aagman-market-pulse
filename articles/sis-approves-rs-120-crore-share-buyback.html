SIS Approves Rs 120 Crore Share Buyback at Rs 478.50 Per Share

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SIS Board approves Rs 120 crore share buyback at maximum price of Rs 478.50 per share, a 10% premium to the previous closing price of Rs 435
  • 02/This marks the fifth buyback since the August 2017 listing; cumulative capital returned to shareholders will rise to approximately Rs 720 crore
  • 03/Around 25 lakh shares could be repurchased; final mode, timeline and terms await board, shareholder and regulatory approvals

SIS Ltd (NSE: SIS) has approved a share buyback of up to Rs 120 crore. The board cleared the proposal on June 29, 2026.

The maximum buyback price is fixed at Rs 478.50 per share. This represents a 10% premium to the previous closing price of Rs 435.

Based on the maximum price, SIS could repurchase around 25 lakh shares. The final number depends on the actual buyback price.

This marks the fifth buyback since listing in August 2017. SIS has already returned roughly Rs 600 crore to shareholders through buybacks and dividends.

The Rs 600 crore includes around Rs 420 crore from four prior buybacks and about Rs 180 crore through dividends. Cumulative returns will reach Rs 720 crore.

The company has bought back nearly 86 lakh shares across previous programmes. Including this proposal, cumulative repurchases could exceed 1.11 crore shares.

"The proposed buyback is expected to be accretive to earnings per share and return on capital," said Group Managing Director Rituraj Kishore Sinha.

The mode, timeline and terms await approvals from the board, shareholders and regulators. SIS shares closed at Rs 435 with a market cap of Rs 6,152 crore.
