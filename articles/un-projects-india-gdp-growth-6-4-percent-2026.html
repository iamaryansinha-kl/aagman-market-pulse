UN Projects India GDP Growth at 6.4% in 2026, 6.6% in 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/UN ESCAP projects India's GDP to grow 6.4% in 2026, moderating from 7.4% growth recorded in 2025
  • 02/India's exports to the US declined 25% in H2 2025 after 50% tariffs were introduced in August 2025
  • 03/Inflation is projected at 4.4% in 2026 and 4.3% in 2027; services sector remains the key growth driver

India's economy is projected to grow at 6.4% in 2026, according to the UN Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific. The report was released on April 21, 2026.

Growth is expected to edge up to 6.6% in 2027. South and South-West Asia grew 5.4% in 2025, up from 5.2% in 2024.

India recorded 7.4% growth in 2025, supported by robust consumption and GST rate cuts. Export frontloading ahead of US tariffs also provided a temporary boost.

Economic activity moderated in the second half of 2025. Exports to the US declined 25% after 50% tariffs were introduced in August 2025.

The services sector remained a key growth driver throughout the period. It helped offset weakness in merchandise exports.

Inflation for India is projected at 4.4% in 2026 and 4.3% in 2027. This stays within the Reserve Bank of India's target range.

India attracted $50 billion in greenfield FDI in the first three quarters. It was the largest recipient in the Asia-Pacific region.

The country received $137 billion in remittances in 2024. A new 1% US tax on remittances from January 2026 could reduce inflows.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT GROWTH RATE HAS THE UN PROJECTED FOR INDIA IN 2026?
THE UN ESCAP PROJECTS INDIA'S ECONOMY TO GROW AT 6.4% IN 2026, MODERATING FROM 7.4% GROWTH RECORDED IN 2025. GROWTH IS EXPECTED TO RECOVER SLIGHTLY TO 6.6% IN 2027.
WHY DID INDIA'S ECONOMIC GROWTH MODERATE IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2025?
ECONOMIC ACTIVITIES MODERATED IN H2 2025 AS EXPORTS TO THE UNITED STATES DECLINED BY 25% FOLLOWING THE INTRODUCTION OF 50% TARIFFS IN AUGUST 2025. THE SERVICES SECTOR, HOWEVER, REMAINED A KEY GROWTH DRIVER.
WHAT IS THE INFLATION PROJECTION FOR INDIA?
INFLATION FOR INDIA IS PROJECTED AT 4.4% IN 2026 AND 4.3% IN 2027. BOTH FIGURES STAY WITHIN THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA'S TARGET RANGE.
HOW MUCH GREENFIELD FDI DID INDIA ATTRACT?
INDIA ATTRACTED USD 50 BILLION IN GREENFIELD FDI IN THE FIRST THREE QUARTERS OF 2025. IT WAS THE LARGEST RECIPIENT IN THE ASIA-PACIFIC REGION, AHEAD OF AUSTRALIA (USD 30 BILLION) AND SOUTH KOREA (USD 25 BILLION).
WHAT RISK DO REMITTANCES FACE?
INDIA, THE WORLD'S LARGEST REMITTANCE RECIPIENT WITH USD 137 BILLION IN 2024, COULD FACE A SIZEABLE LOSS. THE UNITED STATES HAS LEVIED A 1% TAX ON ALL REMITTANCES SINCE JANUARY 2026.
