Q4 Results April 28: Leela Palaces Profit Up 153%, IFCI Down 92%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Leela Palaces Q4 standalone net profit jumps 153% YoY to Rs 91.2 crore; FY26 PAT surges to Rs 275.79 crore
  • 02/IFCI Q4 standalone net profit crashes 92% YoY to Rs 21.36 crore from Rs 272.54 crore; shares down 3%
  • 03/Coal India Q4 profit rises 12% to Rs 10,907.7 crore; UltraTech Cement profit up 20% to Rs 2,983 crore

Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd (NSE: THELEELA) reported standalone net profit of Rs 91.2 crore for Q4 FY26. The figure marks a 153% jump from Rs 36 crore in the same quarter last year.

For the full FY26, Leela Palaces PAT stood at Rs 275.79 crore. This compares to Rs 34.23 crore in FY25. The board approved investment in BPBKC Properties Private Limited.

IFCI Ltd (NSE: IFCI) posted standalone net profit of Rs 21.36 crore for Q4 FY26. This is down from Rs 272.54 crore in Q4 FY25, a drop of roughly 92%. FY26 full-year PAT was Rs 51.71 crore against Rs 43.80 crore in FY25.

Coal India Ltd (NSE: COALINDIA) reported Q4 FY26 profit of Rs 10,907.7 crore, up 12% YoY. Revenue came in at Rs 46,490 crore, a 5.8% increase. The board declared a dividend of Rs 5.25 per share.

UltraTech Cement Ltd (NSE: ULTRACEMCO) posted Q4 net profit of Rs 2,983 crore, up 20% from Rs 2,482 crore YoY. Revenue from operations grew 12% to Rs 25,799 crore. Goldman Sachs maintained a Buy rating with target price Rs 13,230.

AU Small Finance Bank Ltd (NSE: AUBANK) reported Q4 net profit of Rs 832 crore, surging 65% YoY. Net interest income rose 31% to Rs 786 crore. NIMs expanded 24bps QoQ to 5.96%.

SBI Cards and Payment Services Ltd (NSE: SBICARD) reported Q4 net profit of Rs 609.30 crore versus Rs 556.64 crore YoY. However, shares traded 2% lower as brokerages cut earnings estimates on subdued receivables growth.

City Union Bank Ltd (NSE: CUB) posted Q4 net profit of Rs 360 crore, up 25% YoY. Net interest income jumped 31% to Rs 786 crore. The board approved a bonus issue. Shares surged 5% to Rs 285.09.
