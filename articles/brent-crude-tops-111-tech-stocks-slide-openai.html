Brent Crude Tops $111 as Tech Stocks Slide on OpenAI Miss

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent crude rose 3% to $111.53/barrel, extending its rally to a seventh straight day as the Strait of Hormuz stayed shut
  • 02/Nasdaq 100 futures slipped 0.6% after the Wall Street Journal reported OpenAI missed its goals for new users and sales
  • 03/SoftBank slumped 9.9% in Tokyo while Oracle, AMD and CoreWeave fell in US premarket trading on AI demand concerns

Brent crude advanced 3% to $111.53 per barrel on April 28. The benchmark gained for a seventh consecutive day as the Strait of Hormuz remained closed.

Technology stocks led global equity markets lower. The Nasdaq 100 futures slipped 0.6% after the Wall Street Journal reported OpenAI missed its targets for new users and sales.

SoftBank Group Corp., a major OpenAI investor, slumped 9.9% in Tokyo trading. Oracle Corp. fell 5.5%, while AMD and CoreWeave dropped roughly 4% and 5.4% in US premarket.

S&P 500 futures fell 0.2% while Dow Jones futures rose 0.3%. The MSCI Asia Pacific Index shed 0.4% and the emerging markets benchmark lost 0.7%.

Tech stocks had rallied nearly 10% in April after an 18-day chipmaker advance. Resurgent AI optimism drove the charge as rising oil prices dragged the rest of the market.

"The single most important line item isn't revenue or margins; it's capex," said Amanda Lyons, IT-sector lead at Energy Group Capital. She warned any slowdown in AI spending would hurt the ecosystem.

Euro-area consumers expect inflation at 4% over the next 12 months, up from 2.5% in February. Traders now expect the ECB and Bank of England to hike rates as soon as June.

"The rising oil price is starting to feature in macro data," said Anna Macdonald at Hargreaves Lansdown. She warned the longer the Middle East crisis rolls on, the more severe the impacts will be.
