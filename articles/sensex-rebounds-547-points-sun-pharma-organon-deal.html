Sensex Rebounds 547 Points To 77,211 On Sun Pharma $11.75B Deal Boost

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex jumps 546.64 points to 77,210.85 and Nifty climbs 169.55 points to 24,067.50 in early trade on April 27, 2026
  • 02/Sun Pharma surges over 4% after announcing $11.75 billion all-cash acquisition of U.S.-listed Organon & Co at $14 per share
  • 03/Markets rebound after three days of decline; on April 24 Nifty fell 275 points and Sensex dropped 982 points amid FII selling worth ₹8,827 crore

Indian benchmark indices rebounded on April 27, 2026 after three consecutive days of losses. The 30-share BSE Sensex jumped 546.64 points to 77,210.85 in early trade. The 50-share NSE Nifty climbed 169.55 points to 24,067.50.

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (NSE: SUNPHARMA) surged over 4%. The company announced an all-cash deal to acquire U.S.-listed Organon & Co for an enterprise value of $11.75 billion.

Sun Pharma will pay $14.00 per share for Organon. This represents a 24% premium to Organon's closing price on Friday.

The acquisition marks the largest-ever outbound deal by an Indian pharmaceutical company. Post-integration, the combined entity is expected to generate $12.4 billion in annual revenue. Combined EBITDA is projected at $3.7 billion.

The deal is expected to close in early 2027. It requires regulatory approvals and Organon shareholder approval.

Broader markets also joined the rally. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 1.3% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 gained 1.2%.

The rebound follows sharp losses on April 24 when the Nifty fell 275.10 points and the Sensex dropped 982 points. Foreign Institutional Investors sold equities worth ₹8,827 crore on Friday.
