Vodafone Idea Shares Rally 80% in 3 Months: Buy or Avoid for Investors?

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vodafone Idea shares jumped nearly 80% in under three months from April low of ₹8.13 to June high of ₹15.34
  • 02/Rally driven by 27% AGR dues reduction to ₹64,046 crore, Kumar Mangalam Birla's return as chairman, and ₹1,182 crore promoter infusion
  • 03/Experts caution stock remains a binary trade with structural challenges; traders may target ₹18-20 zone

Vodafone Idea Ltd (NSE: IDEA) shares have surged nearly 80% in less than three months. The stock climbed from a multi-month low of ₹8.13 in April 2026 to a 52-week high of ₹15.34 earlier this month.

The telecom operator has also gained 110% over the past year. It is currently trading around ₹14.20 on the NSE.

The rally was triggered by multiple tailwinds in quick succession. In early May, the Department of Telecommunications slashed Vodafone Idea's adjusted gross revenue dues by 27% to ₹64,046 crore from ₹87,695 crore.

Later, the company named billionaire Kumar Mangalam Birla as non-executive chairman. This marked his return roughly five years after resigning amid financial stress.

Vodafone Idea also raised ₹1,182 crore from promoter Aditya Birla Group through a warrant issue this month. The fresh capital bolstered near-term liquidity.

The company posted a consolidated net profit of ₹51,970 crore in Q4 FY26, its first quarterly profit in nearly six years. However, this was driven by a one-time accounting gain of ₹57,491 crore linked to the AGR recalculation.

Excluding exceptional items, the quarterly loss narrowed to ₹5,515 crore from ₹7,167 crore in Q4 FY25. Revenue rose 3.2% year-on-year to ₹11,303 crore.

Average revenue per user improved to ₹190 in Q4 FY26 from ₹175 a year ago. This still trails Reliance Jio's ₹214 and Bharti Airtel's ₹257 by a wide margin.

The total subscriber base stood at 192.8 million, with 128.9 million on 4G or 5G networks. Monthly subscriber additions turned positive from February 2026 after years of decline.

"The 80% rally should be read as a sentiment-and-positioning event rather than a fundamental re-rating," said Harshal Dasani, Business Head at INVasset PMS. "Survival improving is not the same as the investment case improving."

Hitesh Rathi, Technical Analyst at Angel One, noted that while the primary trend remains neutral to bearish, the stock could test ₹18-20 in the near term. A decisive breakout above ₹20 could open the door for a move towards ₹29-30.

The company remains saddled with elevated debt and lagging 5G capex compared to Jio and Airtel. As of March 2026, bank debt stood at ₹726 crore while cash and bank balances were ₹3,715 crore.
