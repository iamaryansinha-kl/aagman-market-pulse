NSE Gets Sebi Nod for Corporate Bond Index Futures, RBI Nod Awaited

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi has issued a no-objection certificate to NSE for futures contracts on a corporate bond index, a first for India's exchange-traded corporate bond derivatives market
  • 02/The product is not live — launch remains subject to approval from the Reserve Bank of India, and no launch date has been announced
  • 03/Watch for the RBI decision and the eventual disclosure of the underlying index, lot size and settlement terms

The National Stock Exchange of India has received a no-objection certificate from the Securities and Exchange Board of India. The approval clears NSE to introduce futures contracts linked to a corporate bond index.

The development was reported on October 1, 2026. It marks the first time an Indian exchange has been cleared to list exchange-traded derivatives on corporate bonds.

The product is not yet cleared to go live. According to the report, the launch remains subject to approval from the Reserve Bank of India.

No launch date has been announced. NSE has also not confirmed which corporate bond index will serve as the underlying for the contracts.

The move extends NSE's derivatives complex beyond equity index products and government securities-linked contracts. It takes the exchange into corporate bonds for the first time.

For institutional investors, the contracts offer an exchange-traded tool to hedge credit and interest-rate exposure. Such hedging has historically been available mainly in the over-the-counter market.

Regulators have been pushing for years to deepen India's corporate bond market. A key part of that effort is opening exchange-traded access for both retail and institutional participants.

A corporate bond index future lets investors hedge portfolios and take duration or credit exposure through a single contract. That is simpler than trading baskets of individual bonds.

The development comes as NSE advances toward its long-awaited initial public offering. Sebi issued a no-objection certificate for the exchange's listing on January 30, 2026, per earlier reports.

NSE is valued at roughly ₹5 lakh crore in the unlisted market. Its IPO is expected to be one of the largest in the country's capital market history.

Contract details remain undisclosed. Lot size, expiry structure and settlement method will be announced only after regulatory clearance.

The RBI's role is central here. Corporate bond derivatives sit at the intersection of securities regulation and debt-market oversight, where the central bank holds final authority.

Market participants expect exchange-traded futures to improve price discovery in a market where most bond trading happens off-exchange. Liquidity could deepen if banks, mutual funds, insurers and primary dealers participate.

For now, institutions and investors can only track the regulatory process. Trading begins only after RBI sign-off and a formal launch communication from the exchange.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HAS NSE LAUNCHED CORPORATE BOND INDEX FUTURES?
NO. NSE HAS RECEIVED ONLY A NO-OBJECTION CERTIFICATE FROM SEBI. THE LAUNCH STILL REQUIRES APPROVAL FROM THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA, AND NO LAUNCH DATE HAS BEEN ANNOUNCED.
WHICH INDEX WILL UNDERLIE THE FUTURES CONTRACTS?
THE SPECIFIC CORPORATE BOND INDEX HAS NOT BEEN CONFIRMED IN AVAILABLE COVERAGE. NSE HAS NOT DISCLOSED THE UNDERLYING INDEX OR CONTRACT SPECIFICATIONS SUCH AS LOT SIZE AND SETTLEMENT.
WHY DOES THIS MATTER FOR INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS?
CORPORATE BOND INDEX FUTURES WILL GIVE INSTITUTIONS AN EXCHANGE-TRADED TOOL TO HEDGE CREDIT AND INTEREST-RATE EXPOSURE AND TO TAKE DURATION OR CREDIT POSITIONS THROUGH A SINGLE CONTRACT.
HOW DOES THIS FIT INTO REGULATORY EFFORTS ON THE BOND MARKET?
REGULATORS HAVE BEEN PUSHING TO DEEPEN INDIA'S CORPORATE BOND MARKET, INCLUDING EXCHANGE-TRADED ACCESS FOR RETAIL AND INSTITUTIONAL PARTICIPANTS. THIS IS THE FIRST EXCHANGE-TRADED DERIVATIVES CLEARANCE FOR CORPORATE BONDS IN INDIA.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?
NSE MUST OBTAIN RBI APPROVAL BEFORE THE PRODUCT CAN GO LIVE. ONLY AFTER THAT WILL THE EXCHANGE ANNOUNCE THE UNDERLYING INDEX, CONTRACT SPECIFICATIONS, LOT SIZE, EXPIRY AND SETTLEMENT DETAILS. THERE IS NO ANNOUNCED TIMELINE.
