Sun Pharma to Acquire Organon for $11.75 Billion in India's Largest Pharma Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sun Pharmaceutical Industries will acquire US-based Organon & Co for $11.75 billion, marking India's largest-ever overseas pharmaceutical acquisition.
  • 02/The all-cash deal values Organon at $14 per share, a 24% premium to its April 24 closing price, and ranks as India's second-largest outbound deal after Tata Steel's $12 billion Corus takeover in 2007.
  • 03/The transaction is expected to close in early 2027, pending regulatory approvals and Organon shareholder clearance.

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd has agreed to acquire US-listed Organon & Co for an enterprise value of $11.75 billion. The boards of both companies approved the all-cash transaction on April 27, 2026.

Sun Pharma will pay $14.00 per share for Organon, representing a premium of more than 24% over the stock's closing price on April 24. Organon shares had already doubled since April 8, when reports of the deal first surfaced.

The acquisition marks the largest overseas buy by an Indian pharmaceutical company. It also ranks as the second-largest outbound deal by an Indian firm after Tata Steel's $12 billion takeover of Corus Group Plc in 2007.

Post-integration, the combined Sun-Organon entity is expected to generate $12.4 billion in annual revenue. This is nearly double Sun Pharma's standalone revenue of $6.2 billion in FY25, with combined EBITDA rising to approximately $3.7 billion.

Organon, spun off from Merck in 2021, brings a portfolio of over 70 products across women's health and general medicines. The company operates across 140 countries and reported 2025 revenue of $6.2 billion with adjusted EBITDA of $1.9 billion.

Sun Pharma Executive Chairman Dilip Shanghvi said, "This transaction represents a significant opportunity for Sun Pharma to build on its vision of reaching people and touching lives." Organon Executive Chair Carrie Cox said the deal offers "compelling and immediate value to Organon stockholders."

Sun Pharma shares rose 8.5% on April 27, heading for their biggest single-day gain since August 2021. The stock had closed at ₹1,618.5 on April 24, down 3.7% from the previous session.

The deal will be funded through internal cash resources and committed bank financing. Sun Pharma's debt stood at roughly $198.4 million as of December 31, 2025, against a profit of $1.16 billion.
