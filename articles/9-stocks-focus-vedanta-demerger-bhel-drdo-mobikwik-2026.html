9 Stocks in Focus: Vedanta Demerger, BHEL-DRDO Deal, MobiKwik Sale

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta shares go ex-date for demerger today; eligible shareholders to receive four new company shares for each share held
  • 02/BHEL signs technology transfer agreement with DRDO's NSTL for naval gas turbine infrared suppression system
  • 03/Peak XV exits MobiKwik via secondary sale of 62.15 lakh shares worth ₹133.01 crore at ₹214.01 per share

Vedanta Ltd shares will go ex-date for the proposed demerger today. Eligible shareholders will receive one share each of Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd, Vedanta Power Ltd, Vedanta Oil & Gas Ltd, and Vedanta Iron & Steel Ltd for every share held.

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (BHEL) has signed a Licensing Agreement for Transfer of Technology with DRDO's Naval Science and Technological Laboratory. The pact covers the LM2500 Gas Turbine Infrared Suppression System for naval vessels.

Peak XV Partners has exited its 7.89 per cent stake in MobiKwik through a secondary market bulk deal. It sold 62.15 lakh shares for ₹133.01 crore at ₹214.01 per share.

Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd (RVNL) has received a Letter of Award from NMDC Limited. The order is worth ₹39.21 crore including GST for construction of a residential campus in Hyderabad.

EIE Renewables, a wholly owned subsidiary of Enviro Infra Engineers, will acquire Suyog Urja for ₹311 crore. It will buy 51 per cent equity immediately and the remaining 49 per cent by July 31, 2028.

OneSource Specialty Pharma's Bengaluru facility received an EU GMP Certificate from German authorities. The certificate covers sterile products and aseptically prepared small volume liquids.

Wanbury's Patalganga site completed an MFDS regulatory inspection with zero observations. The company is adding a new manufacturing block at its Andhra Pradesh site for new APIs.

Solara Active Pharma Sciences' Puducherry facility received a US FDA Establishment Inspection Report with Voluntary Action Indicated status. The unannounced inspection was conducted between February 2 and 6.

Adroit Infotech has won a ₹23 crore order from PT Supra Primatama Nusantara for SAP BRIM-managed services. The project includes one year of functional, technical, and onsite/offshore support.
