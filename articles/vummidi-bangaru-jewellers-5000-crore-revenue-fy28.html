Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers Targets ₹5,000 Crore Revenue by FY28

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Chennai-based Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers aims to double revenue to ₹5,000 crore by FY28 from ₹2,700 crore in FY26
  • 02/The 125-year-old retailer will invest ₹500 crore internally to expand into Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Hyderabad
  • 03/VBJ also plans to launch a digital gold investing mobile app within the next two weeks

Vummidi Bangaru Jewellers (VBJ) has set a revenue target of ₹5,000 crore by financial year 2027-28. The Chennai-based retailer closed FY26 with a topline of approximately ₹2,700 crore.

The 125-year-old company is renowned for crafting the Sengol sceptre installed in the new Parliament building. It currently operates two stores in Chennai and one in the United States.

VBJ plans to invest ₹500 crore internally to fund its retail expansion. New stores are planned in Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Hyderabad in the coming years.

The company launched a new 14,000 sqft flagship store in Chennai on Tuesday. The multi-level showroom has dedicated floors for gold jewellery, diamonds, platinum and sapphire.

Store formats will vary by market size according to Managing Partner Amarendran Vummidi. "Chennai and Coimbatore are large markets, so they can take huge stores. But tier 2/3 cities need more flexible formats," he said.

VBJ's business mix comprises roughly 65 per cent gold, 30 per cent diamonds and 5 per cent platinum and other precious metals. The company posted 17-20 per cent growth in FY26 despite lower volumes.

Jithendra Vummidi, Managing Partner, announced that VBJ will launch a digital gold investing mobile app within two weeks. Rising gold prices have also driven marginal demand for 18-carat design-led contemporary pieces.

Amarendran Vummidi stated that sourcing and imports have faced no major challenges so far. "Demand in India remains largely resilient due to the strong cultural and investment linkage with gold," he added.
