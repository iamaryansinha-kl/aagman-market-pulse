Royal Enfield to Invest ₹2,200 Crore in Andhra Pradesh Plant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Royal Enfield approves ₹2,200 crore investment in two phases for a new manufacturing plant at Satyavedu in Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh
  • 02/The facility marks the company's first major expansion outside Tamil Nadu since 1901 and will add nearly 9 lakh units to annual capacity
  • 03/First phase targets completion by 2029 with a dedicated vendor park; second phase expected by 2032

Royal Enfield will invest approximately ₹2,200 crore to build a manufacturing facility at Satyavedu in Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh. The project will roll out in two phases.

The Andhra Pradesh State Investment Promotion Board approved the project on Wednesday. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu chaired the meeting.

The state government has allocated 267 acres across Vanelluru and Rallakuppam villages for the plant. This marks Royal Enfield's first major expansion outside Tamil Nadu since its founding in 1901.

The facility will add nearly 9 lakh units to Royal Enfield's manufacturing capacity. It is the company's second manufacturing destination in India after its Tamil Nadu base.

The project will generate around 5,000 direct and indirect jobs. A dedicated vendor park in the first phase will bring the supplier ecosystem to Andhra Pradesh.

The first phase is targeted for completion by 2029. The second phase is expected to be completed by 2032.

"This investment is a strong endorsement of Andhra Pradesh's ease of doing business and robust infrastructure ecosystem," Nara Lokesh, Minister for Education, IT & Electronics, and Human Resources Development, Government of Andhra Pradesh, said.
