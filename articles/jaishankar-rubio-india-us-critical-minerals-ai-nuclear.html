Jaishankar-Rubio Talks: Critical Minerals, AI, Nuclear Energy in India-US Review

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar met US Secretary of State Marco Rubio for talks reviewing India-US strategic partnership
  • 02/Key agenda items included critical minerals supply chain, artificial intelligence cooperation, and civil nuclear energy ties
  • 03/The discussions mark an early bilateral engagement as both nations deepen economic and defence alignment

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar held extensive discussions with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio. The meeting took place on May 24, 2026.

The talks covered critical minerals, artificial intelligence, and nuclear energy. These are priority sectors for both governments.

Critical minerals are essential for semiconductor and battery manufacturing. India and the US seek supply chain diversification away from China.

AI cooperation was another major focus. Both countries are advancing bilateral frameworks on emerging technology governance.

Nuclear energy ties were also on the agenda. Civil nuclear cooperation remains a long-standing pillar of the India-US relationship.

The review signals continued momentum in the bilateral partnership. Strategic alignment has deepened across defence, trade, and technology.

New Delhi and Washington have been strengthening supply chain resilience. The Indo-Pacific Economic Framework is a key vehicle for this.

The meeting comes amid global efforts to secure rare earth supply chains. India holds significant reserves of lithium and rare earth elements.
