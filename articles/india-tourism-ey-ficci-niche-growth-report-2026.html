India Tourism at ₹21 Trillion GDP: EY-FICCI Niche Growth Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EY-FICCI report says India's tourism contributes approximately ₹21 trillion to GDP and supports over 46 million jobs
  • 02/Coldplay concert in Ahmedabad generated ₹641 crore economic impact, showing event tourism potential
  • 03/Niche segments including wellness, culinary, space and women-centric tourism seen as key growth drivers

India's tourism sector contributes approximately ₹21 trillion to GDP. It supports over 46 million jobs across the country.

Foreign tourist arrivals reached about 9.9 million in 2024. This remains modest compared to competing global destinations.

Event tourism is emerging as a major growth driver. The Coldplay concert in Ahmedabad generated an estimated ₹641 crore in economic impact.

The EY-FICCI report highlighted concert tourism's multiplier effect. It boosts destination visibility and supports hospitality, transport and retail.

"Event tourism not only drives direct revenue but also enhances destination visibility, strengthens brand perception, and stimulates allied sectors such as hospitality, transport, and retail" - EY-FICCI report.

Culinary tourism, spiritual tourism and wellness travel offer strong potential. India's yoga, meditation and Ayurveda practices already attract millions of international visitors.

Emerging segments include space tourism linked to ISRO and astro-tourism in Ladakh. Women-centric travel and sports tourism also show growing demand.

The report was released on April 28, 2026. Strategic development of these niche segments could significantly boost India's inbound tourism numbers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES THE EY-FICCI REPORT SAY ABOUT INDIA'S TOURISM SECTOR?
THE EY-FICCI REPORT STATES THAT INDIA'S TOURISM SECTOR CONTRIBUTES APPROXIMATELY ₹21 TRILLION TO GDP AND SUPPORTS OVER 46 MILLION JOBS. FOREIGN TOURIST ARRIVALS STOOD AT ABOUT 9.9 MILLION IN 2024.
WHAT IS EVENT TOURISM AND WHY DOES IT MATTER?
EVENT TOURISM INVOLVES LARGE-SCALE EVENTS LIKE CONCERTS THAT ATTRACT DOMESTIC AND INTERNATIONAL VISITORS. THE COLDPLAY CONCERT IN AHMEDABAD GENERATED AN ESTIMATED ₹641 CRORE ($68.6 MILLION) IN ECONOMIC IMPACT, DEMONSTRATING ITS POTENTIAL TO BOOST TRAVEL DEMAND AND ALLIED SECTORS.
WHAT OTHER NICHE SEGMENTS DOES THE REPORT HIGHLIGHT?
THE REPORT HIGHLIGHTS CULINARY TOURISM, SPIRITUAL AND WELLNESS TOURISM (YOGA, MEDITATION, AYURVEDA), WILDLIFE AND NATURE TOURISM, SPACE AND SCIENCE TOURISM, WOMEN-CENTRIC TOURISM, GOLF TOURISM AND SPORTS TOURISM AS HIGH-POTENTIAL GROWTH DRIVERS.
HOW CAN INDIA DEVELOP SPACE TOURISM?
WITH INSTITUTIONS LIKE ISRO GAINING GLOBAL RECOGNITION, INDIA CAN DEVELOP EXPERIENTIAL TOURISM AROUND SPACE MISSIONS. REGIONS LIKE LADAKH ARE ALSO EMERGING AS ASTRO-TOURISM HUBS DUE TO CLEAR NIGHT SKIES.
WHAT SHOULD POLICYMAKERS FOCUS ON TO BOOST TOURISM?
THE REPORT RECOMMENDS STRATEGICALLY DEVELOPING NICHE SEGMENTS AND INTEGRATING EVENT TOURISM WITH MICE INFRASTRUCTURE TO POSITION INDIA AS A GLOBAL HUB. DEVELOPING SAFE AND INCLUSIVE OFFERINGS FOR SOLO WOMEN TRAVELLERS CAN ALSO HELP TAP EXPANDING DEMAND.
