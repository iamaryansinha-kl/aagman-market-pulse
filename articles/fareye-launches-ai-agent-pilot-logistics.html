FarEye Launches AI Agent PILOT to Cut Logistics Dispatch Time by 95%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FarEye launches PILOT, an AI product with 11 specialised agents to autonomously manage last-mile logistics dispatch
  • 02/PILOT reduces dispatcher active labour from 10 hours to 60 minutes daily, driving a 95% cut in dispatcher hours
  • 03/Early deployments show 17.5% reduction in cost-per-delivery and 3-5 times greater dispatcher productivity

FarEye has launched PILOT, an AI product for autonomous last-mile logistics dispatch. The system was unveiled on April 24, 2026, in Noida.

PILOT replaces manual dispatch with 11 specialised AI agents. These handle route planning, driver management, failed delivery recovery, and invoice reconciliation.

The system uses Human-In-The-Loop governance to ensure oversight. It automates routine dispatcher tasks at scale while keeping humans in control of decisions.

Dispatchers typically spend 10 hours daily managing manual errors. PILOT cuts active human labour to just 60 minutes each day.

FarEye claims a 95 per cent reduction in dispatcher hours. Early deployments show a 17.5 per cent drop in cost-per-delivery.

PILOT-enabled businesses report 3-5 times greater dispatcher productivity. This compares against traditional firms paying a legacy tax of manual routing.

"As the industry evolves towards more intelligent and agentic operations, we see technology as a critical enabler in reinforcing the reliability, trust and operational discipline that set us apart." - Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart
"In an industry where dispatchers typically spend 10 hours a day firefighting manual errors across multiple systems, PILOT reduces active human labour to just 60 minutes while driving a 95 per cent reduction in dispatcher hours. The dispatcher is the most underserved role in logistics, burdened by spreadsheets while carrying the entire operation." - Gaurav Srivastava, Co-Founder & CPTO, FarEye

PILOT also deploys sub-agents for dynamic slot booking, geocoding, real-time safety, and invoice reconciliation. FarEye is a global leader in last-mile delivery technology.
