BMW Group India Plans to Double MINI Sales in 2026 with Wider Portfolio

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BMW Group India targets doubling MINI sales volume in 2026
  • 02/Three-pronged strategy: wider portfolio, localisation, retail expansion
  • 03/10 new MINI models planned including special editions and new-generation variants

BMW Group India has announced ambitious plans for its MINI brand. The company aims to double MINI sales volume in 2026 through a comprehensive three-pronged strategy.

The first pillar involves expanding the product portfolio. BMW plans to introduce 10 new MINI models including special editions and new-generation variants. This diversification targets younger urban professionals who prioritize design and driving dynamics.

Localisation forms the second pillar. The company is working to increase local sourcing beyond the current 50% threshold. Manufacturing more components at its Chennai plant will help shield pricing from import duty volatility.

Retail expansion constitutes the third pillar. BMW plans to add 19 new outlets across 18 cities including Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets like Jammu, Amritsar, and Bhopal.

MINI currently accounts for a small fraction of BMW Group India's total sales. The brand is positioned as a lifestyle offering distinct from mainstream luxury vehicles.

The strategy aligns with BMW's broader 2026 roadmap of 27 product actions. The German automaker is closing the gap with Mercedes-Benz in India's luxury car market.

Electric variants may also feature in the MINI portfolio expansion. BMW's EV penetration in India has reached 21% and is targeted to hit 25% by year-end.
