India Drops to Sixth-Largest Economy, Nilesh Shah Calls It Temporary

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India has dropped to the sixth-largest economy in the world, a slip from its earlier ranking
  • 02/Nilesh Shah says the decline is a temporary blip and India will bounce back
  • 03/Watch for GDP growth data and quarterly macroeconomic indicators for confirmation

India has slipped to the sixth-largest economy globally. Market veteran Nilesh Shah says the drop is only a temporary blip.

Shah believes India will bounce back. He cites underlying economic fundamentals and growth momentum.

The ranking shift reflects currency fluctuations and GDP measurement revisions. Other economies have also seen rapid expansion.

India remains one of the fastest-growing major economies. The IMF projects over 6% growth for FY2027.

Foreign institutional investors continue to allocate capital. India is still a preferred emerging market destination.

The government has pushed manufacturing and infrastructure spending. These are long-term GDP drivers.

Short-term ranking moves do not alter India's structural growth story. Demographics and digital adoption remain tailwinds.
