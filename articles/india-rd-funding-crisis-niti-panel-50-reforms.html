India R&D Funding Crisis: Niti Panel Recommends 50+ Reforms to Boost Spending

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's GERD remains at 0.6-0.7% of GDP, far below China's 2.6% and South Korea's 5.3%
  • 02/Niti Aayog panel recommends 50+ measures including raising R&D spend to 2% of GDP over five years
  • 03/Application-to-grant ratio has fallen below 10% across several R&D funding agencies

India's Gross Expenditure on Research and Development (GERD) has stagnated at 0.6% to 0.7% of GDP. This is well below China at 2.6%, the US at 3.4%, and South Korea at 5.3%.

A high-level Niti Aayog panel has flagged the crisis in its 'Ease of doing R&D' report. The report identifies seven categories of challenges crippling India's research landscape.

The competition for grants has intensified sharply. The application-to-grant ratio has declined to under 10% across several funding agencies.

Funding is also heavily skewed. About 80% of ANRF funding flows to the IIT system, even though ANRF has a mandate to support state institutions.

The panel found heavy duplication across ministries. Both DST and CSIR back similar projects in areas like Hydrogen Energy and Carbon Capture.

Grants are often released 3-6 months after approval. The 'use it or lose it' budgeting rule disrupts research continuity at year-end.

The panel recommends raising GERD to 2% of GDP within five years. It also suggests tax deductions up to 125% for CSR R&D support and restoring a 5% GST slab for R&D procurement.

India has only 262 full-time equivalent researchers per million people. This compares poorly with Switzerland at 5,552, the UK at 4,821, and China at 1,585.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS INDIA'S CURRENT R&D SPENDING AS A PERCENTAGE OF GDP?
INDIA'S GROSS EXPENDITURE ON RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (GERD) HAS BEEN HOVERING AT 0.6% TO 0.7% OF GDP, ACCORDING TO THE NITI AAYOG PANEL REPORT.
HOW DOES INDIA'S R&D SPENDING COMPARE WITH OTHER COUNTRIES?
CHINA SPENDS 2.6% OF GDP ON R&D, THE US 3.4%, AND SOUTH KOREA 5.3%. INDIA'S 0.6-0.7% IS AMONG THE LOWEST FOR A MAJOR ECONOMY.
WHAT IS THE APPLICATION-TO-GRANT RATIO FOR R&D FUNDING IN INDIA?
THE RATIO HAS DECLINED TO UNDER 10% ACROSS SEVERAL R&D FUNDING AGENCIES, REFLECTING INTENSE COMPETITION FOR LIMITED GRANTS.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE NITI AAYOG PANEL?
THE PANEL HAS RECOMMENDED OVER 50 MEASURES INCLUDING RAISING GERD FROM 0.6% TO 2% OF GDP OVER FIVE YEARS, TAX DEDUCTIONS UP TO 125% FOR CSR R&D, RESTORING 5% GST FOR R&D PROCUREMENT, AND A SIX-MONTH DEADLINE FOR PROPOSAL CLEARANCE.
WHAT IS THE RECOMMENDATION FOR POST-DOCTORAL FELLOWSHIPS?
THE PANEL HAS CALLED FOR INCREASING POST-DOCTORAL FELLOWSHIPS BY 20% EVERY YEAR FOR THE NEXT FEW YEARS, ALONG WITH CREATING 'VIGYAN NIDHI' TO BOOST MANPOWER RETENTION.
