7 Stocks in Focus: Himadri Anode Entry, L&T AI Push, Godawari BESS Deal, Birla Coal Mining, Gujarat Gas Morbi Support, Rolex Buyback, Star Cement Green Push, Inventurus $565M US Buy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Himadri Speciality commences anode material production at 200 MTPA facility in West Bengal after 10 years of R&D
  • 02/L&T incorporates Vyoma.AI subsidiary for data centres and AI infrastructure services
  • 03/Godawari Power signs supply agreement with Roche Energy for 5 MWh BESS balance of system

Himadri Speciality Chemicals has commenced operations at its first anode material production facility at Mahistikry, Hooghly, West Bengal. The facility has an initial installed capacity of 200 MTPA and is the result of over 10 years of in-house research and development.

The facility covers the entire anode technology value chain from raw material processing to finished anode material. This development strengthens Himadri's capabilities in advanced battery materials for electric vehicles and energy storage systems.

Larsen & Toubro has incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary, Vyoma.AI, on April 22. The new entity will establish data centres and AI infrastructure to provide technology-enabled services.

Godawari Power and Ispat's subsidiary Godawari New Energy has entered into a supply agreement with Shanghai Shenyi Roche Energy. The deal covers supply of Balance of System for 5 MWh DC Block on CKD basis for its first phase of 20 GWh Battery Energy Storage System plant at Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra.

Birla Corporation has commenced coal mining operations at its Bikram Coal Mine in Burhan, Shahdol, Madhya Pradesh from April 23. The mine has an annual production capacity of 9.44 million tonnes of thermal coal.

Gujarat Gas Ltd has said it is supporting the revival of the ceramic industry in Morbi by ensuring uninterrupted gas supply and price stability amid recent geopolitical disruptions. Gujarat Gas is the authorised supplier of Piped Natural Gas in Morbi.

The board of Rolex Rings has approved a proposal to buy back equity shares worth up to ₹180 crore at a price of ₹180 per share, comprising the purchase of 1 crore shares. The promoters have indicated their intention not to participate.

Star Cement has acquired Jaitaran Renewable Power's shareholding from its shareholders. JRPPL has become a wholly owned subsidiary of Star Cement North East and a step-down subsidiary of Star Cement.

Inventurus Knowledge Solutions will acquire 100% of the shareholding in US-based TruBridge Inc for $565 million.
