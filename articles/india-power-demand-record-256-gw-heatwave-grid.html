India Power Demand Hits Record 256 GW as Heatwave Stresses Grid

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's peak power demand hit a record 256.11 GW on April 25, driven by intense pre-monsoon heatwaves across north and central India
  • 02/Night-time power shortfalls reached 5.4 GW since April, affecting 2.7 million rural homes; 21 GW of coal and nuclear capacity is offline
  • 03/IMD forecasts above-normal minimum temperatures in May; El Nino risks monsoon rainfall, threatening prolonged grid stress through June

India's peak power demand surged to a record 256.11 GW on April 25, 2026. The previous record of 252.07 GW was set just a day earlier on April 24.

Temperatures crossing 40-45°C across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Delhi drove the unprecedented load. Intense heatwaves gripped north and central India weeks before the usual June-July peak.

Night-time power shortfalls have reached as high as 5.4 GW since April. This equals the electricity needed to serve roughly 2.7 million rural homes across the country.

About 21 GW of coal and nuclear capacity was offline as of late April, mostly due to forced outages. LNG imports hampered by the Persian Gulf conflict have starved gas plants that provide flexible evening supply.

Solar energy provided critical daytime support, generating nearly 81 GW at noon on April 25. Solar accounts for about 30% of installed capacity, but output drops to zero after sunset.

"The impact of climate change is more on night temperatures," said Mrutyunjay Mohapatra, director general of meteorology at IMD. "Consumers need to be more diligent," added Sarnath Ganguly, senior vice president at Noida Power Co. Ltd.

Coal India's production dropped 1.7% to 768.1 million tonnes in FY26, down from 781.1 million tonnes in FY25. The IMD predicts El Nino during the June-September monsoon, risking below-normal rainfall and prolonged grid stress.

Blackouts have already triggered protests in Punjab, with the Shiromani Akali Dal organizing demonstrations against scheduled power cuts in one of the earliest affected states.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE RECORD PEAK POWER DEMAND INDIA HIT IN APRIL 2026?
INDIA'S PEAK POWER DEMAND REACHED A RECORD 256.11 GIGAWATTS ON APRIL 25, 2026, ACCORDING TO POWER MINISTRY DATA. THIS EXCEEDED THE PREVIOUS RECORD OF 252.07 GW SET JUST A DAY EARLIER ON APRIL 24.
HOW MANY HOMES ARE AFFECTED BY THE NIGHT-TIME POWER SHORTFALLS?
NIGHT-TIME POWER SHORTFALLS HAVE REACHED UP TO 5.4 GW SINCE TEMPERATURES ROSE IN APRIL 2026. THIS DEFICIT IS EQUIVALENT TO THE POWER NEEDED TO SERVE APPROXIMATELY 2.7 MILLION RURAL HOMES.
HOW MUCH COAL AND NUCLEAR POWER CAPACITY IS CURRENTLY OFFLINE?
ABOUT 21 GIGAWATTS OF COAL AND NUCLEAR POWER CAPACITY WAS UNDER MAINTENANCE SHUTDOWNS AS OF LATE APRIL 2026. MOST OF THESE OUTAGES ARE FORCED RATHER THAN PLANNED, REMOVING CRITICAL BASELOAD SUPPLY FROM THE GRID.
WHAT ROLE IS SOLAR POWER PLAYING IN MEETING THE RECORD DEMAND?
SOLAR GENERATION REACHED NEARLY 81 GW AT NOON ON APRIL 25, 2026, SUPPLYING ABOUT ONE-THIRD OF TOTAL ELECTRICITY AT THAT HOUR. HOWEVER, SOLAR ACCOUNTS FOR ROUGHLY 30% OF INSTALLED CAPACITY AND CANNOT SUPPLY POWER AFTER SUNSET, LEAVING EVENING PEAKS EXPOSED.
WHAT IS THE OUTLOOK FOR THE SUMMER MONTHS AND THE MONSOON?
THE POWER MINISTRY PROJECTS PEAK DEMAND COULD REACH 270 GW DURING THE SUMMER. THE INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT HAS ALSO PREDICTED EL NINO CONDITIONS DURING THE JUNE-SEPTEMBER MONSOON, RAISING THE RISK OF BELOW-NORMAL RAINFALL AND PROLONGED GRID STRESS.
