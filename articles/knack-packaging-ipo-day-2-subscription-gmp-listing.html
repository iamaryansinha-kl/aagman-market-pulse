Knack Packaging IPO Day 2: Issue Subscribed 2.60x, GMP at ₹26, Listing on July 8

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Knack Packaging IPO priced at ₹161-170 per share is subscribed 2.60x on Day 1, with NII portion leading at 4.89x
  • 02/The ₹439.5 crore issue raised ₹131.25 crore from anchor investors; listing scheduled for July 8 on BSE and NSE
  • 03/GMP stands at ₹26, indicating an estimated listing price of ₹196; Nirmal Bang and Anand Rathi have assigned 'Subscribe' ratings

Knack Packaging Limited's IPO opened on July 1, 2026, and is currently on Day 2 of bidding. The issue closes on July 3, 2026.

The price band is fixed at ₹161-170 per share with a face value of ₹10. The lot size is 88 shares, requiring a minimum investment of ₹14,960.

The IPO comprises a fresh issue of ₹380 crore and an offer-for-sale of 35 lakh shares worth ₹59.5 crore. The total issue size is ₹439.5 crore.

Ahead of the public opening, the company raised ₹131.25 crore from anchor investors. This signals institutional confidence in the packaging solutions provider.

On Day 1, the IPO received bids for 4,92,46,296 shares against 1,89,64,018 shares on offer. The overall subscription stood at 2.60x.

The retail portion was subscribed 1.31x, while the NII portion saw strong demand at 4.89x. QIBs bid 3.15x their allocated quota.

The grey market premium (GMP) today is ₹26. This indicates an estimated listing price of ₹196, a 15.29% premium over the upper price band of ₹170.

Knack Packaging commands approximately 10.1% market share in India's PLWPP bulk bags market. It operates a fully integrated value chain from polypropylene granules to finished bags.

The company delivered an EBITDA margin of around 18% in FY26, higher than listed peers such as TCPL Packaging and Time Technoplast. FY26 revenue was ₹823.43 crore with net income of ₹92.72 crore.

Nirmal Bang Institutional Equities has assigned a 'Subscribe' rating, citing the company's strong market position and reasonable valuation at 22.4x FY26 earnings. "At around 15x EV/EBITDA, the IPO is reasonably valued relative to peers," the brokerage noted.

Swastika Investmart has also recommended 'Subscribe' from a short-term perspective, citing healthy financial growth and robust operating margins. However, it cautioned that customer concentration and dependence on a new facility remain execution risks.

Anand Rathi Research assigned a 'Subscribe for Long Term' rating, noting the company is well-positioned to benefit from structural growth in flexible packaging. The post-issue market capitalisation is approximately ₹2,080 crore.

The basis of allotment will be finalised on July 6, 2026. Refunds and demat credits are expected on July 7, while listing on BSE and NSE is tentatively scheduled for July 8, 2026.

WHAT IS THE PRICE BAND OF THE KNACK PACKAGING IPO?
THE PRICE BAND IS FIXED AT ₹161 TO ₹170 PER EQUITY SHARE WITH A FACE VALUE OF ₹10 PER SHARE. THE MINIMUM INVESTMENT FOR RETAIL INVESTORS IS ₹14,960 FOR ONE LOT OF 88 SHARES.
WHAT IS THE SUBSCRIPTION STATUS OF KNACK PACKAGING IPO ON DAY 1?
THE IPO WAS SUBSCRIBED 2.60x OVERALL ON DAY 1. THE RETAIL PORTION RECEIVED 1.31x BIDS, THE NII PORTION WAS SUBSCRIBED 4.89x, AND THE QIB PORTION RECEIVED 3.15x BIDS. TOTAL BIDS WERE RECEIVED FOR 4,92,46,296 SHARES AGAINST 1,89,64,018 SHARES ON OFFER.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT GMP OF KNACK PACKAGING IPO?
THE GREY MARKET PREMIUM (GMP) TODAY IS ₹26. BASED ON THE UPPER PRICE BAND OF ₹170, THE ESTIMATED LISTING PRICE IS ₹196 PER SHARE, WHICH REPRESENTS A POTENTIAL LISTING GAIN OF 15.29%.
WHAT ARE THE KEY DATES FOR KNACK PACKAGING IPO?
THE IPO OPENED ON JULY 1, 2026, AND CLOSES ON JULY 3, 2026. THE BASIS OF ALLOTMENT WILL BE FINALISED ON JULY 6, REFUNDS AND DEMAT CREDITS ON JULY 7, AND THE LISTING IS SCHEDULED FOR JULY 8, 2026 ON BOTH BSE AND NSE.
HOW WILL KNACK PACKAGING USE THE IPO PROCEEDS?
THE COMPANY WILL UTILISE ₹320 CRORE FROM THE FRESH ISSUE TO SET UP A NEW MANUFACTURING FACILITY AT BORISANA, KADI, IN GUJARAT'S MEHSANA DISTRICT. THE REMAINING FUNDS WILL BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES.
