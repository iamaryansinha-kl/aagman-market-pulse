Auto Demand Upcycle to Continue for Next 2-3 Quarters, Growth Strong Till CY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Antique Stock Broking report says India's auto demand upcycle will continue for next 2-3 quarters with elevated growth through CY26
  • 02/Passenger vehicle domestic wholesale volumes grew around 20% YoY in April 2026; Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors led with 32% and 31% growth
  • 03/Geopolitical tensions, higher freight costs, and commodity inflation flagged as key risks for export volumes and profitability in 1HFY27

India's automobile industry demand upcycle is set to continue for the next 2-3 quarters. Growth will likely remain elevated through calendar year 2026 before normalising in CY27, according to a report by Antique Stock Broking.

The auto sector began FY27 on a strong footing. Volume growth was broad-based across passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, two-wheelers, tractors, and electric vehicles.

This growth has been supported by improving affordability following GST rationalisation. Healthy rural sentiment and ongoing premiumisation trends have also contributed.

In the passenger vehicle segment, domestic wholesale volumes grew around 20% year-on-year in April 2026. Maruti Suzuki led with 32% growth, followed by Tata Motors at 31%.

Hyundai Motor India reported 17% growth, while Toyota Kirloskar Motor grew 21%. Mahindra & Mahindra posted a more modest 8% increase in passenger vehicles.

In commercial vehicles, domestic volumes excluding Ashok Leyland grew about 16% year-on-year. Tata Motors outperformed with 28% growth, driven by strong demand in small commercial vehicle cargo and pickup segments which grew around 40%.

Two-wheeler volumes excluding Bajaj Auto rose around 30% year-on-year. Hero MotoCorp led the segment with an 85% surge, while Royal Enfield grew 37%.

TVS Motor Company reported 8% growth in two-wheelers, supported by 36% growth in EV volumes. However, motorcycle volumes declined 9% due to supply-chain constraints.

Tractor sales remained strong with domestic volumes growing about 23%. Escorts grew 28%, Mahindra & Mahindra 21%, and VST Tillers 17%.

Electric vehicle adoption continued to gain momentum. The electric passenger vehicle segment grew 74% year-on-year, with Tata Motors holding a 37% market share.

However, the report cautioned that escalating geopolitical tensions remain a key risk. Higher freight costs, commodity inflation, and potential supply-chain disruptions are expected to remain concerns particularly for export volumes and profitability in the first half of FY27.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW LONG IS THE AUTO DEMAND UPCYCLE EXPECTED TO CONTINUE?
ACCORDING TO THE ANTIQUE STOCK BROKING REPORT, THE ONGOING DEMAND UPCYCLE IN INDIA'S AUTOMOBILE INDUSTRY IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE FOR THE NEXT 2-3 QUARTERS, WITH ELEVATED GROWTH LIKELY TO SUSTAIN THROUGH CALENDAR YEAR 2026 BEFORE GRADUALLY NORMALISING IN CY27.
WHAT WAS THE PASSENGER VEHICLE VOLUME GROWTH IN APRIL 2026?
DOMESTIC WHOLESALE VOLUMES IN THE PASSENGER VEHICLE SEGMENT GREW AROUND 20 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR IN APRIL 2026. MARUTI SUZUKI LED WITH 32 PER CENT GROWTH, FOLLOWED BY TATA MOTORS AT 31 PER CENT, HYUNDAI MOTOR INDIA AT 17 PER CENT, AND TOYOTA KIRLOSKAR MOTOR AT 21 PER CENT.
WHICH TWO-WHEELER COMPANY POSTED THE HIGHEST GROWTH?
HERO MOTOCORP POSTED THE HIGHEST GROWTH IN THE TWO-WHEELER SEGMENT WITH AN 85 PER CENT SURGE IN VOLUMES. ROYAL ENFIELD GREW 37 PER CENT, WHILE TVS MOTOR COMPANY REPORTED 8 PER CENT GROWTH SUPPORTED BY 36 PER CENT GROWTH IN EV VOLUMES.
HOW FAST IS THE ELECTRIC PASSENGER VEHICLE SEGMENT GROWING?
THE ELECTRIC PASSENGER VEHICLE SEGMENT GREW 74 PER CENT YEAR-ON-YEAR. TATA MOTORS HOLDS A 37 PER CENT MARKET SHARE, FOLLOWED BY MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA AT 23 PER CENT AND MARUTI SUZUKI AT 5 PER CENT.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS TO AUTO SECTOR GROWTH?
THE REPORT FLAGS ESCALATING GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS AS A KEY RISK, PARTICULARLY FOR EXPORT VOLUMES AND PROFITABILITY IN THE FIRST HALF OF FY27. HIGHER FREIGHT COSTS, COMMODITY INFLATION, AND POTENTIAL SUPPLY-CHAIN DISRUPTIONS ARE EXPECTED TO REMAIN KEY CONCERNS FOR THE INDUSTRY.
