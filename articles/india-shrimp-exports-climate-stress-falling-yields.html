India Shrimp Exports Hit 5,47,422 Tonnes as Climate Stress Cuts Yields

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's shrimp exports reached 5,47,422 tonnes in FY 2024-25 with US absorbing 311,948 tonnes, but climate stress is disrupting coastal farming clusters
  • 02/Rising water temperatures and erratic weather are increasing disease outbreaks like White Spot Syndrome Virus, forcing farmers to spend more on inputs
  • 03/Adoption of recirculating aquaculture systems and biofloc technology is emerging, but high costs limit access for small and mid-scale farmers

India's shrimp industry shipped 5,47,422 tonnes in FY 2024-25. The US absorbed 311,948 tonnes, China 136,164 tonnes and the EU 99,310 tonnes.

Rising water temperatures are disrupting biological performance across Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Tamil Nadu and Gujarat. Even slight deviations increase metabolic stress without growth gains, weakening immunity.

Early Mortality Syndrome and White Spot Syndrome Virus are now more frequent during peak summer months. Outbreaks cause recurring crop losses and uneven yields across farming clusters.

Extreme weather compounds the damage. Cyclones, floods and erratic rainfall disrupt production cycles and damage farm infrastructure. Salinity fluctuations reduce feed conversion efficiency.

Input costs are climbing as farmers deploy more aeration, water exchange and chemical treatments. Production expenses are rising faster than farm gate prices, squeezing margins across the value chain.

Small and mid-scale farmers face the sharpest pressure with limited credit access and no risk hedging. They remain most exposed to repeated cost shocks and climate volatility.

Recirculating aquaculture systems and biofloc technology are emerging as adaptation tools. These reduce dependence on external conditions and improve survival rates, though high setup costs limit adoption.

Current insurance models cover production loss, not climate variability. Long-term resilience requires market diversification beyond the US, China and EU, plus value addition through processed shrimp products.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE CURRENT SCALE OF INDIA'S SHRIMP EXPORTS?
INDIA EXPORTED A TOTAL OF 5,47,422 TONNES OF SHRIMP IN FY 2024-25. THE UNITED STATES WAS THE LARGEST IMPORTER AT 311,948 TONNES, FOLLOWED BY CHINA AT 136,164 TONNES AND THE EUROPEAN UNION AT 99,310 TONNES.
HOW IS CLIMATE CHANGE AFFECTING SHRIMP FARMING IN INDIA?
RISING WATER TEMPERATURES, ERRATIC RAINFALL, CYCLONES AND FLOODS ARE DISRUPTING FARMING CYCLES ACROSS ANDHRA PRADESH, ODISHA, TAMIL NADU AND GUJARAT. SALINITY FLUCTUATIONS REDUCE FEED CONVERSION EFFICIENCY AND INCREASE MORTALITY RISK.
WHAT DISEASES ARE IMPACTING SHRIMP PRODUCTION?
EARLY MORTALITY SYNDROME AND WHITE SPOT SYNDROME VIRUS ARE BECOMING MORE FREQUENT DURING PEAK SUMMER MONTHS. THESE OUTBREAKS WEAKEN SHRIMP IMMUNITY AND CAUSE RECURRING CROP LOSSES.
WHAT TECHNOLOGIES ARE BEING ADOPTED TO ADDRESS CLIMATE STRESS?
RECIRCULATING AQUACULTURE SYSTEMS, BIOFLOC TECHNOLOGY AND CLIMATE-CONTROLLED POND SYSTEMS ARE BEING ADOPTED. THESE ALLOW GREATER CONTROL OVER WATER QUALITY AND REDUCE EXPOSURE TO TEMPERATURE AND SALINITY FLUCTUATIONS.
WHAT ARE THE KEY POLICY GAPS IN THE SECTOR?
CURRENT INSURANCE MODELS ARE DESIGNED AROUND PRODUCTION LOSS RATHER THAN CLIMATE VARIABILITY. THIS LEAVES MANY FARMERS UNDERINSURED DURING REPEATED CLIMATE SHOCKS. STRONGER ADAPTATION FRAMEWORKS INTEGRATING INFRASTRUCTURE RESILIENCE AND EARLY WARNING SYSTEMS ARE NEEDED.
