Subramaniam: June Quarter Earnings Will Set Market Direction, Stay Cautious

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sunil Subramaniam says June quarter earnings will be the key determinant of market direction in coming months
  • 02/He favours consumer durables, capital goods and PSBs; warns on pharma, energy and private banks
  • 03/Nifty closed at 23,719.30; volatility expected from crude prices, RBI policy and geopolitical risks

Market expert Sunil Subramaniam has advised investors to remain cautious in the near term. He cited rising geopolitical uncertainty, input cost pressures and the evolving impact of global conflict on corporate earnings.

Speaking to ET Now, Subramaniam said the previous quarter is not reflective of the current situation. He warned investors against extrapolating recent quarterly earnings into the future.

The full impact of geopolitical tensions will likely show up only in the June quarter earnings season. Higher costs for APIs, freight and supply chain disruptions are yet to fully hit company margins.

He remains positive on consumer durables due to strong summer demand for air conditioners and refrigerators. Premium automobiles and high operating leverage could help offset rising raw material costs in this segment.

On banking, Subramaniam prefers public sector banks over private lenders. He argued the recent rally in private banks was driven by short covering rather than genuine FII buying conviction.

FIIs continue to remain cautious on India and their flows play a major role in large private bank performance. With the RBI unlikely to cut rates soon, private banks face a less favourable environment.

PSBs are better positioned due to stronger CASA deposits, improving asset quality and greater alignment with the capex cycle. Government infrastructure spending is unlikely to fall sharply despite fiscal pressures.

On FMCG, he expects margin pressures to intensify from rising palm oil and fertiliser prices. Companies used lower-cost inventory earlier, but that buffer is now exhausted.

He called energy stocks "too hot to handle" due to extreme crude price volatility. He also remains cautious on IT due to persistent concerns around artificial intelligence impact on long-term growth.

For moderate investors, Subramaniam recommends balanced portfolios with roughly equal exposure to largecaps and mid or smallcaps. More aggressive investors can increase midcap exposure but must prepare for elevated volatility.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DOES SUNIL SUBRAMANIAM SAY ABOUT THE NEAR-TERM MARKET OUTLOOK?
SUBRAMANIAM ADVISES CAUTION IN THE NEAR TERM DUE TO RISING GEOPOLITICAL UNCERTAINTY, HIGHER INPUT COSTS AND THE EVOLVING IMPACT OF GLOBAL CONFLICT ON CORPORATE EARNINGS. HE EXPECTS VOLATILITY TO REMAIN ELEVATED.
WHICH SECTORS DOES SUBRAMANIAM FAVOUR CURRENTLY?
HE FAVOURS CONSUMER DURABLES, CAPITAL GOODS AND PUBLIC SECTOR BANKS. HE BELIEVES CONSUMER DURABLES WILL BENEFIT FROM STRONG SUMMER DEMAND, CAPITAL GOODS FROM CONTINUED GOVERNMENT AND PRIVATE CAPEX, AND PSBS FROM IMPROVING ASSET QUALITY AND STRONGER CASA DEPOSITS.
WHY DOES HE PREFER PSBS OVER PRIVATE BANKS?
HE ARGUES THE RECENT RALLY IN PRIVATE BANKS WAS DRIVEN BY SHORT COVERING RATHER than GENUINE FII BUYING. FIIS REMAIN CAUTIOUS ON INDIA, AND WITH THE RBI UNLIKELY TO CUT RATES SOON, THE ENVIRONMENT IS LESS FAVOURABLE FOR PRIVATE LENDERS. PSBS ARE BETTER ALIGNED WITH THE CAPEX CYCLE.
WHAT DOES HE WARN AGAINST IN THE CURRENT MARKET?
HE WARNS AGAINST EXTRAPOLATING RECENT EARNINGS INTO THE FUTURE, AS PREVIOUS QUARTER NUMBERS DO NOT FULLY CAPTURE THE IMPACT OF HIGHER INPUT COSTS. HE IS ALSO CAUTIOUS ON PHARMA, ENERGY, PRIVATE BANKS AND IT STOCKS.
WHAT PORTFOLIO ALLOCATION DOES HE RECOMMEND FOR INVESTORS?
MODERATE INVESTORS SHOULD MAINTAIN BALANCED PORTFOLIOS WITH ROUGHLY EQUAL EXPOSURE TO LARGECAPS AND MID OR SMALLCAPS. MORE AGGRESSIVE INVESTORS WITH LONGER HORIZONS CAN INCREASE MIDCAP AND SMALLCAP EXPOSURE BUT MUST PREPARE FOR ELEVATED VOLATILITY.
