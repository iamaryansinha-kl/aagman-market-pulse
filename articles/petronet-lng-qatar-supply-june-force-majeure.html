Petronet LNG Sees No Qatar Supply Clarity for June 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Petronet LNG MD Akshay Kumar Singh says there is no clarity on Qatar LNG shipments for June 2026 amid an ongoing force majeure
  • 02/India has not received Qatar cargoes since the Iran war disrupted flows through the Strait of Hormuz, which handles ~60% of India's LNG
  • 03/Petronet plans to add seven storage tanks to its existing ten to improve supply resilience, while spot LNG prices cool to $16/mmbtu

Petronet LNG Ltd (NSE: PETRONET) has no clarity on liquefied natural gas shipments from Qatar for June 2026. Managing Director Akshay Kumar Singh confirmed this at a media briefing on May 5, 2026.

QatarEnergy declared force majeure for May, effectively cancelling contractual obligations. "This month we are not receiving any cargo," Singh said. QatarEnergy has stated it will resume full supplies only after the Strait of Hormuz reopens.

The Strait of Hormuz handled about 60% of India's LNG flows before the Iran war disrupted maritime traffic. India's largest LNG buyer has not received a single Qatar shipment since the conflict began.

Petronet was contracted to receive roughly 10 cargoes in March and another 10 in April under its long-term deal to import 7.5 million tonnes annually from Qatar. Those volumes have not materialised.

The supply crunch has forced India to cut gas allocations to petrochemical plants and industries by 10% to 40%. The federal power ministry has deferred maintenance at coal-fired plants as gas-based generation remains constrained.

Spot LNG prices have cooled to about $16 per million British thermal units from $25 when the conflict began. However, Singh noted prices must fall to single digits for price-sensitive Indian buyers to return.

Petronet is now looking to diversify its supply base. The company plans to add seven storage tanks to its existing ten to improve resilience against future geopolitical disruptions.

Petronet shares have fallen sharply since the crisis began. The stock tanked 11.69% to ₹273 on the BSE on March 4, 2026, and traded near ₹238 by late March.
