ITC Sees India GDP at 6.9% in FY27 Despite West Asia, El Nino Risks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ITC projects India's real GDP growth at 6.9% for FY27, calling the economy "well-poised for rapid growth" in the medium term
  • 02/FY26 GDP grew 7.7% YoY, with Q4 at 7.8% beating the 7.3% Bloomberg economist median estimate
  • 03/West Asia conflict and emerging El Nino conditions pose risks to energy security, inflation, and monsoons

ITC has projected India's real GDP growth at 6.9% for FY27. The FMCG major believes the economy remains well-positioned despite near-term geopolitical and weather risks.

India's economy grew 7.7% in FY26, up from 7.1% in FY25. The fourth quarter clocked 7.8%, surpassing the Bloomberg median estimate of 7.3%.

Growth in the January-March quarter was slightly below the 8% seen in Q3. Still, it matched the pace recorded in the previous quarter.

ITC flagged the ongoing West Asia conflict as a key risk. A prolonged disruption threatens India's energy security and could fuel imported inflation.

Emerging El Nino conditions may weaken monsoons and intensify heatwaves. This poses risks to growth, inflation, and the current account balance.

Rural consumption is expected to stay robust, aided by resilient rural wages and declining unemployment. Urban demand should improve on higher disposable incomes and consumer credit recovery.

Corporate and bank balance sheets remain healthy. Low interest rates and ~75% capacity utilisation support a revival in private capital expenditure.

Recently negotiated trade agreements with the US, UK, EU, New Zealand, and Oman augur well for India's growth outlook. Goldman Sachs raised its India CY2026 forecast to 6.8%, up 30 bps.
