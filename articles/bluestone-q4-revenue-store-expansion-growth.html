BlueStone Q4 Revenue Jumps 38% to ₹688 Crore; Store Expansion Drives Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BlueStone reports standalone revenue of ₹687.7 crore, up 38% y-o-y, swinging to net profit of ₹36.4 crore
  • 02/Company operates 340 stores across 134 cities; existing outlets clocked 34% same-store growth
  • 03/Plans 20% annual store expansion; CEO says most outlets hit breakeven in 3-4 months

BlueStone reported standalone revenue from operations of ₹687.7 crore for the quarter, up 38 per cent year-on-year. The Bengaluru-based jewellery retailer swung to a net profit of ₹36.4 crore, compared to a loss of ₹48.6 crore in the previous quarter.

Founder and CEO Gaurav Singh Kushwaha said demand has strengthened as the company scaled. He noted no softening in consumer demand despite gold price volatility and macroeconomic uncertainty.

The company now operates around 340 stores across 134 cities. Existing outlets posted approximately 34 per cent same-store sales growth during the quarter.

BlueStone plans to expand its footprint by about 20 per cent annually. Kushwaha said most stores achieve operational breakeven within three to four months of opening.

A broader shift in consumer behaviour is underway across India's jewellery market. Buyers are moving from heavy occasion-led purchases to lighter, more wearable pieces at smaller ticket sizes.

India's gold jewellery demand fell about 26 per cent year-on-year in the first half of FY26, per an ICRA-Assocham Report. Demand for bars and coins rose 15 per cent, signalling a shift to investment-led buying.

"Instead of one large purchase, consumers are increasingly buying more frequently but at smaller ticket sizes," said Kushwaha. This trend supports BlueStone's product strategy focused on lighter jewellery.

Demand trends remain consistent across markets. Kushwaha said larger cities simply allow more store openings, with no significant difference in consumer behaviour between cities.
