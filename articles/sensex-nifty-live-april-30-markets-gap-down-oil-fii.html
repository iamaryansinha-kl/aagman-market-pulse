Sensex Nifty Live April 30: Markets Gap Down on Oil Surge, FII Selling

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex and Nifty opened sharply lower on April 30 as Brent crude nears $120 per barrel and FII selling persists
  • 02/Gift Nifty indicated a gap-down opening of 80-90 points; Friday is a market holiday for Maharashtra Day
  • 03/US Fed held rates at 3.5%-3.75% but shifted inflation language to "elevated"; watch crude and FII flows

Indian equity benchmarks opened sharply lower on Thursday, April 30, 2026. The gap-down start follows a volatile overnight session on Wall Street and rising geopolitical tensions.

Gift Nifty indicated an opening drop of approximately 80-90 points, trading near 24,145. Asian markets also opened largely in the red, tracking weakness across global peers.

Brent crude oil surged to nearly $120 per barrel as US-Iran tensions persist and supply disruptions loom. West Texas Intermediate crude traded at $107.18 per barrel.

The US Federal Reserve held its benchmark rate steady at 3.5% to 3.75% for the third consecutive meeting. It shifted its inflation language from "somewhat elevated" to "elevated," citing global energy prices and West Asia developments.

Foreign institutional investors sold ₹2,468 crore on Wednesday, extending their sustained outflow streak. Domestic institutional investors bought ₹2,262 crore, providing partial support to the indices.

"While domestic indices are holding near key levels, the broader setup remains fragile, shaped by elevated oil prices, global uncertainty, and a high-impact event calendar," said Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder, Livelong Wealth.

Friday, May 1, is a market holiday for Maharashtra Day. Traders are unlikely to carry overnight positions during this period of heightened volatility.

The Nifty had closed at 24,177 on Wednesday, up 0.76%, while the Sensex gained 0.79% to 77,496. Both indices pared gains from intraday highs due to late-session profit booking.
