Gold Edges to ₹1.51 Lakh on Rate Fears, Iran Crisis Caps Fall

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MCX Gold retreats to ₹1,51,000 per 10 grams from a multi-week high of ₹1,55,000 as hawkish central bank signals and a stronger US dollar weigh on sentiment
  • 02/Comex Gold holds above $4,600 but has shed over 2 per cent in two weeks; WTI Crude surged above $100 per barrel on Iran Strait of Hormuz tensions
  • 03/Near-term trading range expected at ₹1,51,000–₹1,56,000 per LKP Securities; watch US jobs data and PMI figures this week for Fed rate clarity

Gold prices extended their decline on Monday, with MCX Gold trading near ₹1,51,000 per 10 grams. The metal has pulled back sharply from a multi-week high of ₹1,55,000 reached earlier.

On international markets, Comex Gold is holding just above $4,600 per ounce. It has declined more than 2 per cent over the past two weeks on sustained selling pressure.

The primary headwind for gold is the hawkish pivot across major central banks. The US Federal Reserve held rates unchanged but signalled rising inflation risks, with Governor Jerome Powell cautioning markets about persistent inflationary pressures.

Surging crude oil prices have compounded the bearish outlook. WTI Crude has rallied more than 7 per cent over two consecutive weeks, settling above $100 per barrel. Energy-driven inflation fears have triggered fresh hawkish signals from central banks.

Iran has reiterated control over the Strait of Hormuz. Tehran submitted a revised peace proposal via Pakistan, but US President Donald Trump said he remained unsatisfied. The US confirmed it would maintain its naval blockade of Iranian ports.

Outflows from gold exchange-traded funds have added to the selling pressure. However, the metal found some footing on Friday after Iran signalled a proposal to open the Strait of Hormuz, which eased supply concerns temporarily.

Jateen Trivedi, VP Research Analyst at LKP Securities, said gold is expected to trade in a range of ₹1,51,000 to ₹1,56,000 in the near term. Immediate resistance on Comex sits at $4,640 to $4,670, with support at $4,570.

Silver is broadly flat near $75 per ounce and ₹2,50,000 per kg on MCX, with Axis Direct recommending buys around ₹2,47,000. Market focus this week shifts to PMI data and US jobs figures for clarity on the Fed's rate trajectory.
