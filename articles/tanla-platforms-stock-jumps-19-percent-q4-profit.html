Tanla Platforms Stock Jumps 19% on Q4 Profit Rise to ₹134 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tanla Platforms shares jumped 19% to ₹573.65 after Q4 FY26 profit rose 14.4% to ₹134.32 crore
  • 02/Revenue grew 15% to ₹1,177.53 crore; full-year profit flat at ₹509.14 crore versus ₹507.28 crore in FY25
  • 03/Board declared second interim dividend of ₹6 per share; record date fixed at April 30, 2026

Tanla Platforms Ltd (NSE: TANLA) shares surged 19% on April 27, 2026. The stock hit an intraday high of ₹577.70 and traded at ₹573.65 by 1:24 pm on the NSE, up from the previous close of ₹485.80.

The rally followed March quarter results announced on April 24, 2026. Consolidated net profit rose 14.4% year-on-year to ₹134.32 crore from ₹117.33 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations grew 15% to ₹1,177.53 crore against ₹1,024.36 crore in the year-ago quarter. EBITDA stood at ₹163.4 crore with the margin stable at around 16%.

For the full financial year FY26, profit was nearly flat at ₹509.14 crore versus ₹507.28 crore in FY25. Full-year revenue grew 9.7% and gross profit rose 11.8%.

The board declared a second interim dividend of ₹6 per share. The record date is fixed at April 30, 2026. The dividend represents 600% on the face value of Re 1.

"FY26 had been an eventful year with strong progress across innovation, customer success and financial metrics," said Uday Reddy, Founder Chairman and CEO, Tanla. Free cash flow stood at ₹477 crore, equivalent to 94% of profit after tax.

The company continued investing in AI, go-to-market and customer success teams. Its Wisely AI deployment with Indosat in Indonesia protected 100 million users from over 2 billion spam and scam communications since August 2025.

Tanla Platforms trades at a P/E of around 18.4x. The stock remains 31% below its 52-week high of ₹829.00, offering a potential entry point for investors tracking the CPaaS sector.
