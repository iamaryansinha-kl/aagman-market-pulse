GRSE Shares Rally 16% on Record Q4 Profit, ₹6.70 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/GRSE shares rallied 16% to ₹3,339 after reporting Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹303.19 crore, up 24.1% year-on-year
  • 02/FY26 full-year PAT surged 42% to ₹747.93 crore; revenue from operations rose 29% to ₹2,119.21 crore in Q4
  • 03/Board recommended final dividend of ₹6.70 per share; order book of ₹20,200 crore provides multi-year revenue visibility

Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd (NSE: GRSE) shares surged 16% in early trade on April 29, 2026. The stock hit an intraday high of ₹3,339 against the previous close of ₹2,875.30.

The rally followed strong March quarter results. Standalone net profit rose 24.1% year-on-year to ₹303.19 crore from ₹244.24 crore in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations jumped 29% to ₹2,119.21 crore. This compares with ₹1,642.03 crore in the year-ago quarter.

For the full financial year FY26, profit after tax climbed 42% to ₹747.93 crore. FY25 PAT stood at ₹527.40 crore.

The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹6.70 per share for FY26. The record date will be announced shortly.

GRSE carries an order book of approximately ₹20,200 crore. This provides four to five years of revenue visibility across defence and export contracts.

The stock has gained sharply from its 52-week low of ₹1,597.80. It trades at a market capitalization of nearly ₹29,900 crore.
