Supreme Industries Accumulate: Elara Cuts Estimates, Raises Target to ₹4,200

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Elara Securities downgrades Supreme Industries to Accumulate with target price of ₹4,200, raised from ₹3,980 earlier
  • 02/Q4FY26 volumes grew 16% YoY to 231,889 tonnes with EBITDA margin at 17.7%, up 391 bps aided by inventory gains
  • 03/FY27 volume growth guided at 12-13% overall with EBITDA margin of 14-14.5%; capex commitment exceeds ₹1,000 crore

Supreme Industries Ltd reported strong Q4FY26 results. Volumes reached 231,889 tonnes, marking a 16 per cent increase year on year.

The EBITDA margin expanded by 391 basis points to 17.7 per cent. This improvement was aided by an inventory gain of ₹70-80 crore from a PVC price spike in March.

Plastic piping revenue surged 23.3 per cent in Q4 with volume growth of 18.3 per cent. The segment benefited from capacity expansion and channel restocking by dealers.

Packaging revenue grew 7.2 per cent with volume increasing 10.1 per cent. The company is focused on value-added offerings to drive margin improvement.

Elara Securities has downgraded the stock to Accumulate. The brokerage also cut earnings estimates by 8 per cent for FY27E and 9.2 per cent for FY28E.

The target price was raised to ₹4,200 from ₹3,980. The revision reflects a roll-forward to March 2028E earnings with an unchanged P/E multiple of 40x.

The management has guided for FY27 volume growth of 12-13 per cent overall. Plastic piping volumes are expected to grow 15-17 per cent during the fiscal year.

Supreme Industries has committed over ₹1,000 crore in capex for FY27. Projects include greenfield piping plants at Patna, Jammu, and Gadegaon alongside a material handling facility at Malanpur. All expansion will be funded through internal accruals as the company maintains zero-debt status.
