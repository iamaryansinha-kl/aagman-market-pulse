Wonderla Q4FY26 Profit Jumps 49% to ₹16 Crore on New Parks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Wonderla Q4FY26 net profit jumps 49% to ₹16.42 crore from ₹11.10 crore in Q4FY25
  • 02/Revenue up 40.3% to ₹135.85 crore; EBITDA climbs 64% to ₹50 crore on new park openings
  • 03/Footfall rises 25% to 8.49 lakh visitors driven by Chennai park and Bengaluru resort

Wonderla Holidays Ltd (NSE: WONDERLA) has posted a 49% jump in net profit to ₹16.42 crore in Q4FY26. This compares to ₹11.10 crore in the same quarter last year.

Revenue from operations surged 40.3% year-on-year to ₹135.85 crore. The growth was driven by new assets coming online.

EBITDA climbed 64% to ₹50 crore from ₹30.54 crore in Q4FY25. Margin expansion signals strong operating leverage from the new properties.

Footfall across parks rose 25% to 8.49 lakh visitors in the quarter. The previous year quarter recorded 6.78 lakh visitors.

The company credits the opening of its Chennai park and 'The Isle' resort in Bengaluru. The resort opened in June 2025.

Arun Chittilappilly, Executive Chairman and Managing Director, said, "This quarter marks a significant milestone for Wonderla as we delivered our highest-ever Q4 performance, with strong growth across both revenue and profitability."

Looking ahead, he added, "As we move into FY27, with our new assets maturing and continued investments in guest experience and value expansion, we remain confident of sustaining this growth momentum and driving long-term value for our stakeholders."

Wonderla shares closed at ₹527 on the BSE on May 7, 2026. The stock was down 0.17% on the day.
