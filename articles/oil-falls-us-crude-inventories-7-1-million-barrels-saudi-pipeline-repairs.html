Oil Slips as US Crude Inventories Jump 7.1 Million Barrels, But Saudi Pipeline Repair Fears Cap the Fall

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent fell 93 cents (0.86%) to $107.82; WTI dropped 97 cents (0.92%) to $104.86.
  • 02/API data showed US crude inventories rose 7.1 million barrels in the week ended September 11, versus a Reuters poll forecast of a 1.6-million-barrel draw.
  • 03/Gasoline and distillate inventories also rose unexpectedly, Haitong Futures noted.
  • 04/Both benchmarks had settled more than $3 higher on Tuesday, at their highest since May 19.
  • 05/Repair estimates for the attacked East-West pipeline vary from days to five to six weeks.
