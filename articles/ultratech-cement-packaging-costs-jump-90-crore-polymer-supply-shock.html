UltraTech Cement Packaging Costs Jump ₹90 Cr on Polymer Supply Shock

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/UltraTech Cement CFO Atul Daga says packaging bag costs jumped ₹90 crore in Q4 due to West Asia conflict-driven polymer price surge
  • 02/Polymer and plastic raw material costs surged up to 70 per cent from supply chain disruptions; exchange losses from rupee devaluation added pressure
  • 03/Company targets double-digit volume growth in FY27 and plans ₹8,000-10,000 crore annual capex; 240 MTPA capacity target by FY28

UltraTech Cement Ltd (NSE: ULTRACEMCO) flagged rising packaging bag costs as a major headwind. The company incurred an incremental cost of approximately ₹90 crore on bags in the March quarter. CFO Atul Daga disclosed the figure during the latest earnings call.

The West Asia conflict has disrupted polymer and plastic raw material supply chains. Prices have surged up to 70 per cent due to soaring crude oil prices. This directly impacted cement manufacturers' packaging expenses.

Exchange losses from the rupee's devaluation in March added further pressure. Daga highlighted this as an additional cost factor beyond the packaging bag shock. The twin headwinds are squeezing margins.

Daga clarified that bag availability has not become a supply-side crisis. The issue is cost, not shortage. UltraTech works with nearly 150 suppliers across India.

The company's scale and diversified sourcing strategy provide negotiating power. Daga stated that volume advantage ensures suppliers prioritise UltraTech. Long-term fuel contracts will also turn favourable.

UltraTech crossed the 200 million tonnes per annum capacity milestone in FY26. It now targets 240 MTPA by FY28. Annual capex is planned at ₹8,000-10,000 crore.

For FY27, UltraTech is targeting double-digit volume growth. FY26 consolidated income stood at ₹89,089.04 crore. The management expects sustainable industry volume growth of 7 to 8 per cent annually.

On diesel and freight costs, Daga remained cautious. The full impact may surface in coming months. The company has already taken some price hikes in the March quarter.
