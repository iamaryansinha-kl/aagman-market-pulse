MAS Financial Q4: Profit Up 25% to ₹104 Cr, AUM Hits ₹15,304 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MAS Financial Services Q4 net profit rises 25% to ₹104 crore against ₹83 crore in the year-ago period
  • 02/Revenue climbs 23% to ₹542 crore; AUM crosses ₹15,304 crore mark, up 19% year-on-year
  • 03/Board declares dividend of ₹0.75 per share; company guides for 20-25% growth ahead

MAS Financial Services Ltd (NSE: MASFIN) has reported a 25% rise in March quarter net profit. The company posted ₹104 crore against ₹83 crore in the year-ago period.

Revenue for the quarter grew 23% to ₹542 crore. The MSME financing company had logged ₹439 crore in the same period last year.

The board has declared a dividend of ₹0.75 per share. The payout adds to returns for shareholders in the financial year.

Assets Under Management crossed ₹15,304 crore, up 19% from ₹12,868 crore. Disbursements during the quarter stood at ₹4,267 crore.

MSME loan book grew 20% to ₹5,738 crore while SME loans rose 16% to ₹5,213 crore. Two-wheeler loans jumped 35% to ₹1,063 crore.

Salaried personal loans increased 22% to ₹1,263 crore. Commercial vehicle loans climbed 11% to ₹1,086 crore.

"We have achieved important milestones of ₹15,000 crore in AUM and ₹500 crore in PBT on a consolidated basis," said Kamlesh Gandhi, Founder, Chairman & Managing Director.

The company remains committed to grow between 20-25% going forward. Shares of MAS Financial were up 5% at ₹341 on the BSE.
