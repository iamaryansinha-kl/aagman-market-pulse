Apollo Micro Systems Q4 Profit Jumps 163% to ₹36.8 Crore, Stock Up 7%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Apollo Micro Systems Q4 net profit jumps 163% YoY to ₹36.8 crore on revenue of ₹293.3 crore
  • 02/FY26 profit up 90% to ₹107.4 crore; order book stands at ₹1,432 crore as of March 31
  • 03/Stock rallies 7% to ₹334.55; trades at P/E of 124.43 with RSI at 61.6

Apollo Micro Systems Ltd (NSE: APOLLO) reported consolidated net profit of ₹36.8 crore for Q4FY26. This marks a 163% jump from ₹14 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations surged 81% year-on-year to ₹293.3 crore. The figure stood at ₹161.8 crore in Q4FY25.

For the full year FY26, profit climbed 90% to ₹107.4 crore. Revenue surged 61% to ₹904.3 crore against ₹562.1 crore in FY25.

The consolidated order book stood at ₹1,432 crore as of March 31, 2026. This provides strong revenue visibility for the coming quarters.

Managing Director Baddam Karunakar Reddy called FY26 a "breakthrough year." The company completed the IDL Explosives acquisition through ADIPL and received its DPIIT license for UAV manufacturing.

Apollo Micro Systems also secured its first export order during the year. Another acquisition through ADIPL is expected before the end of FY27.

Shares surged over 7% to ₹334.55 in Tuesday's trade. The stock has rallied 141% over the past year, making it a multibagger performer.

The stock commands a market capitalisation of ₹11,111 crore. It trades at a P/E of 124.43 and a P/B of 15.69, reflecting premium valuations amid strong growth expectations.
