DP Abhushan FY26: Profit Jumps 88% to ₹212 Crore, Revenue at ₹4,070 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/DP Abhushan FY26 PAT surges 88% to ₹211.84 crore from ₹112.70 crore in FY25, driven by strong store demand and network expansion
  • 02/Revenue grows 23% to ₹4,070.33 crore; EBITDA jumps 77% to ₹309.67 crore with margin expanding to 7.61% from 5.27%
  • 03/Company expanded showroom network to 12 outlets and initiated Gold Metal Loans and hedging to manage volatility

DP Abhushan Ltd reported an 88% jump in annual profit for FY26. Profit after tax reached ₹211.84 crore against ₹112.70 crore in FY25.

Revenue from operations rose 23% year-on-year to ₹4,070.33 crore. This compares with ₹3,312.35 crore reported in the previous financial year.

EBITDA increased 77% to ₹309.67 crore in FY26. The EBITDA margin expanded to 7.61% from 5.27% in FY25, reflecting improved operating leverage.

"FY26 has been a landmark year for DP Abhushan, marked by strong operational performance, disciplined execution, and continued strategic expansion," said Santosh Kataria, managing director of the firm. "Despite elevated gold prices and broader industry challenges impacting demand volumes, the company demonstrated remarkable resilience, delivering steady revenue growth supported by robust customer footfalls, strong conversion rates, and deep-rooted brand trust."

The company opened a new showroom in Dhar, Madhya Pradesh. It also opened its second outlet in Ratlam earlier in the year, bringing the total showroom count to 12.

The company operates primarily in Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan. It has initiated utilising Gold Metal Loans and hedging strategies to manage gold price volatility and protect margins.

The jewellery sector faces headwinds from elevated gold prices. DP Abhushan's margin expansion despite this environment signals strong execution and pricing power.
