Anthem Biosciences Block Deal: Portsmouth Sells Rs 250 Crore Stake

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Portsmouth Technologies LLC sold 30 lakh Anthem Biosciences shares at Rs 833 apiece in a BSE block deal on October 9, totaling Rs 249.90 crore.
  • 02/The sale represents roughly 19.49% of Portsmouth's 2.73% stake held as of June 30, 2026; the block price sat about 3.29% below the closing price of Rs 861.35.
  • 03/The buyer's identity was not immediately available; watch updated shareholding disclosures for Portsmouth's residual holding and for the counterparty to surface.

Portsmouth Technologies LLC sold 30 lakh shares of Anthem Biosciences in a block deal on the BSE on Friday, October 9. The shares changed hands at Rs 833 apiece, for a total transaction value of Rs 249.90 crore.

According to BSE data, Portsmouth held 1,53,96,327 shares, equivalent to a 2.73% stake in Anthem Biosciences, as of June 30, 2026. The 30 lakh shares sold represent approximately 19.49% of that holding.

Assuming no other changes in its shareholding since June 30, Portsmouth's estimated holding after the transaction stands at 1,23,96,327 shares. The actual remaining stake needs to be confirmed through updated shareholding disclosures from the company.

The buyer's identity was not immediately available. Block deals route large trades to institutional buyers without immediate disclosure of the counterparty, and the other side of the transaction typically surfaces later through exchange data.

Anthem Biosciences shares closed Friday at Rs 861.35, down 1.33% from the previous close of Rs 873. The stock traded in a range of Rs 833 to Rs 878.70 during the session, meaning the block deal price was approximately 3.29% below the closing price.

The sale comes roughly fourteen months after Anthem's debut on D-Street on July 21, 2025. The IPO comprised entirely of an offer for sale, with promoters and existing shareholders offering up to 5.96 crore equity shares at Rs 570 apiece, valuing the issue at approximately Rs 3,395 crore.

The stock listed at Rs 723.05, a premium of 26.85% over the issue price. At Friday's closing price of Rs 861.35, it trades at a premium of approximately 51.11% over the IPO issue price, framing the gains early holders such as Portsmouth have seen since listing.

The exit also follows a soft quarter for the company. Anthem Biosciences reported total income of Rs 443.11 crore for Q1FY27, down 21.3% year-on-year from Rs 563.02 crore in the corresponding period last fiscal.

Profit after tax declined 11.7% year-on-year to Rs 119.94 crore from Rs 135.79 crore, while profit before tax fell 22.4% to Rs 144.58 crore from Rs 186.25 crore. Earnings per share stood at Rs 2.11, compared with Rs 2.42 a year earlier.

Why Portsmouth trimmed nearly a fifth of its stake is not established by the available disclosures. The timing, however, is notable: an early investor reducing exposure after a weak quarterly print and a strong run in the stock.

Despite the soft quarter, the stock has gained 34.80% so far in 2026, according to BSE analytics. At Friday's closing price, the company's market capitalisation stood at Rs 48,539.49 crore.

For investors, the near-term markers are straightforward. Updated shareholding disclosures will confirm Portsmouth's residual stake, and exchange bulk-deal data will eventually identify the buyer on the other side of the trade.

The seller is a familiar type of shareholder. Portsmouth Technologies held more than 1.53 crore shares as of June 30, 2026, a 2.73% stake built before or around the company's market debut.

The listing itself was designed as an exit route. Anthem's IPO in July 2025 was entirely an offer for sale by promoters and existing shareholders, raising no primary capital for the company at all.

That structure mattered. It meant early backers got a liquid market from day one, and Friday's trade is the kind of sale that structure was built to absorb.

The discount on the block is standard market plumbing. At roughly 3.29% below the closing price, the buyer was compensated for taking on 30 lakh shares in one go — the price of immediacy for size.

Block deals are negotiated trades executed on the exchange between pre-agreed parties, with the price and quantity disclosed the same day. What they do not reveal immediately is who the buyer was.

That identity lands later, through bulk-deal data and quarterly shareholding filings. Until then, the market knows a large institutional counterparty found Rs 833 a fair entry price.

The timing invites questions the disclosures cannot answer. Anthem's first-quarter numbers were soft — income down 21.3%, profit before tax down 22.4%, earnings per share at Rs 2.11 against Rs 2.42 a year earlier.

None of that proves a connection. Quarterly numbers lag, block sales take weeks to arrange, and the seller may simply be rebalancing a position that has appreciated 51% since the IPO.

The stock's journey frames both sides of that argument. A 26.85% listing-day pop, a 34.8% gain this year, and a Rs 48,539 crore market capitalisation — against a quarter where every headline growth number went backwards.

What investors will watch next is the confirmation filing. Updated shareholding disclosures will show Portsmouth's exact remaining stake, and bulk-deal data may eventually name the buyer on the other side.

GLOSSARY: Block deal: A large, pre-negotiated trade in a listed stock executed on the exchange at an agreed price, with same-day disclosure of price and quantity. Offer for sale (OFS): An offer where existing shareholders sell their shares; unlike a fresh issue, the company receives no money. Market capitalisation: The total value of a company's listed shares, calculated as share price multiplied by the number of shares. Earnings per share (EPS): A company's profit divided by its share count, used to compare profitability across periods. Shareholding disclosure: The periodic filing in which investors above a threshold report their stake in a listed company. Listing-day premium: The percentage by which a stock's debut price exceeds its IPO issue price.
