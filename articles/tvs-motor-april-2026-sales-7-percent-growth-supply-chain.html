TVS Motor Sells 473,970 Units in April 2026, Flags Supply Chain Risks as EV Sales Jump 36%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/TVS Motor Company sold 473,970 units in April 2026, up 7% from 443,716 units in April 2025.
  • 02/EV sales jumped 36% YoY to 37,771 units, while three-wheelers soared 37% to 18,637 units.
  • 03/The company warned that supply chain constraints, raw material shortages, and limited container availability are hurting production and international dispatches.

TVS Motor Company (NSE: TVSMOTOR) reported total monthly sales of 473,970 units in April 2026, a 7% year-on-year increase from 443,716 units in the same month last year, the company said in a May 2 statement.

The two-wheeler segment, TVS's core business, grew 6% to 455,333 units in April 2026 from 430,150 units in April 2025. Motorcycles contributed 200,039 units, while scooters clocked 211,158 units during the month.

The three-wheeler segment posted the fastest growth at 37%, surging from 13,566 units in April 2025 to 18,637 units in April 2026.

Electric vehicle registrations grew a robust 36%, rising from 27,684 units in April 2025 to 37,771 units in April 2026, underscoring TVS's push into the EV space.

International business sales expanded 3% to 120,008 units in April 2026, compared with 116,700 units in April 2025.

However, TVS flagged significant supply-side headwinds. Dispatch volumes were hit by lower production due to workforce availability issues, raw material and consumable shortages at tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers, and limited container availability for timely international shipments.

The stock closed at ₹3,492.90 on May 1, 2026, down 1.7% from the previous close of ₹3,553.90. The 52-week range is ₹2,640 to ₹3,970, with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹1.65 lakh crore.

Among 37 analysts covering the stock, the consensus recommendation is BUY. Morgan Stanley has a price target of ₹4,327, JPMorgan at ₹4,440, Macquarie at ₹4,271, and HSBC at ₹4,000 with a Hold rating. TTM EPS stands at ₹60.94, up 29.51% YoY.

The supply chain warning is notable given that TVS derives roughly 25% of volumes from exports. Any prolonged disruption at tier-1 or tier-2 vendors could compress margins and delay international dispatches in the coming quarters.
