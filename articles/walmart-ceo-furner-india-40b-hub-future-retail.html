Walmart CEO Furner Calls India $40B Hub, Future Retail Model

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Walmart CEO John Furner, on his first India visit since taking charge in February 2026, called India a model for the future of retail with over $40 billion in annual sourcing
  • 02/PhonePe has received SEBI approval for listing while Flipkart is preparing for a domestic IPO by 2027 with an estimated valuation of ₹3.5 lakh crore
  • 03/Walmart's Vriddhi programme supports 115,000 entrepreneurs with digital training and marketplace access across Flipkart and Walmart platforms

Walmart Inc Chief Executive John Furner called India a model for the future of retail. He made the remarks during a visit to New Delhi on May 7, 2026.

Furner said India is already one of Walmart's most important sourcing markets. The company sources over $40 billion annually across key categories from Indian suppliers.

Furner took over as Walmart CEO in February 2026, replacing Doug McMillon. This marks his first visit to India since assuming the top role at the retail giant.

Walmart is building an ecosystem spanning Flipkart, PhonePe, and Myntra. Flipkart handles eCommerce, PhonePe drives financial inclusion, and Myntra leads fashion.

PhonePe has received SEBI approval for a public listing. Flipkart is also preparing for a domestic IPO by 2027 with an estimated valuation of ₹3.5 lakh crore.

"After more than 30 years in India, we still believe we're early in our journey," Furner said at a Delhi event. He cited entrepreneurship, sourcing capabilities, and digital innovation as key drivers.

The company's Vriddhi programme supports 115,000 entrepreneurs. It provides digital training, mentorship, and access to Walmart and Flipkart marketplaces.

Furner sees further growth as more MSMEs join global supply chains. He also highlighted women-led and digital-first businesses as expansion vectors.
