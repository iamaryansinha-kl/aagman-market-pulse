Oil Steadies Near $95 as Middle East Tensions Keep Markets on Edge

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Oil prices steadied near $95 per barrel amid Middle East tensions
  • 02/Iran-Israel conflict raises concerns about potential supply disruptions
  • 03/India faces inflation risk as major crude oil importer

Crude oil prices steadied near $95 per barrel on April 20, 2026. The market remained on edge due to escalating tensions in the Middle East.

The Iran-Israel conflict has raised concerns about potential supply disruptions. Any attack on oil infrastructure could send prices sharply higher.

Brent crude traded around $94-95 per barrel. West Texas Intermediate (WTI) was close to $90 per barrel.

India is particularly vulnerable as the world's third-largest crude oil importer. Every $10 rise in oil prices adds to the import bill and inflation.

The government has been monitoring the situation closely. Strategic petroleum reserves provide a buffer against short-term disruptions.

Aviation and logistics sectors face margin pressure. Higher fuel costs are passed on to consumers through increased ticket prices and freight charges.

Analysts warn of volatility ahead. Any escalation in the conflict could push prices above $100 per barrel.
