Brookfield India REIT Posts Record Q4: NOI Surges 53% to ₹743 Cr, Leasing Hits 1.6 MSF

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Q4 FY26 NOI surged 53% YoY to ₹743 crore; operating lease rentals rose 55.7% to ₹712.3 crore; quarterly distribution ₹5.50 per unit
  • 02/Record gross leasing of 1.6 million sq ft in Q4, with nearly half from global capability centres; re-leasing spreads at 15%
  • 03/FY26 full-year gross leasing hit 4 million sq ft (highest since listing); occupancy improved to 93% (up 5% YoY); annual distributions rose 11% to ₹21.40 per unit

Brookfield India Real Estate Trust reported its highest-ever quarterly performance in Q4 FY26 on May 12, 2026. The REIT's Net Operating Income surged 53% year-on-year to ₹743 crore. Operating Lease Rentals rose 55.7% to ₹712.3 crore.

Gross leasing in Q4 reached 1.6 million square feet. Nearly half of this leasing came from global capability centres. Demand was spread across tenant segments. Re-leasing spreads came in at 15%, reflecting healthy rental growth momentum.

Alok Aggarwal, CEO and Managing Director of Brookfield India REIT, said FY26 marked a strong year. This was supported by record gross leasing of 4 million square feet. Occupancy grew by 5% year-on-year.

For the full financial year, Operating Lease Rentals increased 22.9% to ₹2,146.6 crore. NOI grew 23.8% to ₹2,291.3 crore. Annual distributions rose 11% to ₹21.40 per unit. Occupancy improved to 93% as of March 31, 2026.

The year also saw strategic expansion. The REIT acquired Ecoworld, a 7.7 million square feet Grade A office campus in Bengaluru. This significantly enhanced scale and diversification.

Brookfield India REIT raised over ₹8,200 crore in equity since April 2025. This came from marquee domestic and global investors. It also raised ₹2,000 crore through a sustainability-linked bond anchored by IFC.

The portfolio now spans 37 million square feet across India's key gateway markets. The REIT positions itself as the "landlord of choice" for global and domestic tenants.

The gross asset value stands at ₹56,500 crore. Loan-to-value ratio is at 32.2%. The REIT remains India's only 100% institutionally managed office real estate investment trust.
