Global Crude Steel Output Drops 4.2% in March as China Drags Lower

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global crude steel production fell 4.2% YoY to 159.9 million tonnes in March 2026, led by China’s 6.3% decline
  • 02/India emerged as the fastest-growing major producer with output up 9.4% to 15.3 mt; US and Germany also posted gains
  • 03/Q1 2026 global output stood at 459.2 mt down 2.3%; demand concerns and supply adjustments continue to weigh on markets

Global crude steel production fell 4.2 per cent year-on-year in March 2026. Output reached 159.9 million tonnes across 69 countries, according to the World Steel Association.

The data covers nations accounting for about 98 per cent of global steelmaking. The decline reflects broad-based weakness in major producing regions.

China, the world’s largest producer, saw output drop 6.3 per cent to 87 mt in March. Its Q1 production fell 4.6 per cent to 247.6 mt amid ongoing demand concerns.

India bucked the trend with production rising 9.4 per cent to 15.3 mt in March. The US also grew 5.2 per cent to 7.2 mt during the same period.

Germany and Türkiye recorded increases of 7.5 per cent and 6.4 per cent respectively. India maintained 10.8 per cent growth in the first quarter.

Asia and Oceania output fell 3.9 per cent to 119.3 mt. The EU declined 4.6 per cent to 11.4 mt as regional trends remained mixed.

The Middle East saw the sharpest regional drop at 33.5 per cent. Russia, CIS and Ukraine fell 7.9 per cent combined in March.

Global Q1 output stood at 459.2 mt, down 2.3 per cent YoY. Continued demand softness and supply adjustments weigh on the sector outlook.
