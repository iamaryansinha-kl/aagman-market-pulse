South Korean Shares Surge 8% as Samsung Hits Record High on Labor Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/South Korean shares surge 8% as Samsung Electronics reaches record high after labor deal averts strike
  • 02/KOSPI index rallies on easing economic and chip supply chain concerns following wage agreement
  • 03/Samsung workers approved pay deal, avoiding disruption at world's largest memory chip maker

South Korean shares surged 8% on Thursday, May 21, 2026. The rally was led by Samsung Electronics shares reaching a record high on the day.

Samsung Electronics workers approved a labor deal that averted a planned strike. The agreement eased concerns about potential disruptions to chip supply chains.

The KOSPI benchmark index posted its biggest gain as economic worries faded. Samsung Electronics remains the world's largest memory chip manufacturer.

A work stoppage at Samsung would have threatened global semiconductor supply chains. The wage deal removes a major overhang for technology investors.

Samsung shares climbed to fresh record highs following the labor deal announcement. The stock has benefited from sustained demand for AI chips.

South Korean markets had faced pressure from ongoing global trade tensions. Thursday's rally signals renewed investor confidence in the technology sector.
