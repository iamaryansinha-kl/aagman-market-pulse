RVNL Q4 Results: PAT Plunges 43% YoY to Rs 212 Cr; Revenue Up 5%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RVNL standalone PAT plunges 43% YoY to Rs 212 crore in Q4FY26 despite 5% revenue growth to Rs 6,648 crore
  • 02/Board recommends Rs 0.71 per share final dividend for FY2025-26 subject to AGM shareholder approval
  • 03/Consolidated PAT falls sharper by 60% to Rs 182 crore; rising expenses and negative cash flow weigh on outlook

Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd (NSE: RVNL) reported standalone net profit of Rs 212 crore for Q4FY26. This marks a 43% decline from Rs 373 crore in the year-ago period.

Revenue grew 5% year-on-year to Rs 6,648 crore in the March quarter. This compares to Rs 6,345 crore posted in Q4FY25.

The board has recommended a final dividend of Rs 0.71 per share for FY2025-26. The payout is subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming AGM.

PAT fell 20% sequentially from Rs 264 crore in Q3FY26. Revenue however surged 48% quarter-on-quarter from Rs 4,504 crore.

For the full financial year FY26, standalone PAT declined 33% to Rs 800 crore. This is down from Rs 1,198 crore reported in FY25.

Expenses rose to Rs 6,535 crore in Q4FY26, up 7% YoY and 49% QoQ. Higher material costs and employee benefits drove the increase.

On a consolidated basis, PAT fell 60% YoY to Rs 182 crore versus Rs 455 crore in Q4FY25. Consolidated revenue rose 4% YoY to Rs 6,696 crore.

Total assets grew to Rs 20,588 crore in FY26 from Rs 19,485 crore in FY25. The company reported negative cash flow of Rs 1,923 crore. RVNL shares closed at Rs 272.45 on May 25.
