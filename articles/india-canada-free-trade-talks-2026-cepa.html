India-Canada Free Trade Talks Resume: Eye $50 Billion Trade by 2030

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India and Canada began the second round of CEPA negotiations on May 4, 2026, in New Delhi, targeting a year-end conclusion
  • 02/Bilateral goods trade stood at $8.66 billion in 2024-25; both nations aim to scale it to $50 billion by 2030
  • 03/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal is expected to visit Canada later in May to fast-track the trade deal

India and Canada kicked off the second round of talks for the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement on May 4, 2026. The five-day negotiations began in New Delhi with both sides targeting a year-end conclusion.

The first round took place in March 2026 following Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney's meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi. That summit had reset ties that had collapsed in 2023 over the Hardeep Singh Nijjar affair.

Bilateral goods trade between the two countries was $8.66 billion in 2024-25. India's exports to Canada stood at $4.22 billion during the same period.

India ships pharmaceuticals, machinery, iron and steel, electronic goods, textiles, seafood and auto parts to Canada. Imports from Canada include pulses, potash, mineral fuels, wood pulp, diamonds and aircraft parts.

The joint statement after the Carney-Modi meeting set a target of CAD 70 billion, or roughly INR 4.65 lakh crore, by 2030. That implies a nearly sixfold jump from current trade levels within five years.

"The attempt is to fast-track talks so that the pact can be concluded by the year-end," a person tracking the talks told businessline. The current round will also set the tone for Piyush Goyal's visit to Canada later this month.

India has concluded nine FTAs in the past three and a half years covering 38 countries. The Canada CEPA would add another developed economy to that list.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE INDIA-CANADA CEPA AND WHEN DID THE SECOND ROUND OF TALKS BEGIN?
THE INDIA-CANADA COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT (CEPA) IS A PROPOSED FREE TRADE PACT. THE SECOND ROUND OF NEGOTIATIONS BEGAN ON MAY 4, 2026, IN NEW DELHI AND IS SCHEDULED TO RUN FOR FIVE DAYS.
WHAT IS THE BILATERAL TRADE TARGET BETWEEN INDIA AND CANADA?
BOTH COUNTRIES AIM TO RAISE BILATERAL TRADE TO CAD 70 BILLION, OR APPROXIMATELY $50 BILLION / INR 4.65 LAKH CRORE, BY THE YEAR 2030.
WHAT WAS THE INDIA-CANADA BILATERAL TRADE VALUE IN 2024-25?
TOTAL BILATERAL GOODS TRADE STOOD AT $8.66 BILLION IN 2024-25. INDIA'S EXPORTS TO CANADA WERE $4.22 BILLION.
WHY DID INDIA-CANADA RELATIONS DETERIORATE IN 2023?
TIES HIT ROCK BOTTOM IN 2023 WHEN THEN-CANADIAN PRIME MINISTER JUSTIN TRUDEAU ACCUSED INDIAN AUTHORITIES OF INVOLVEMENT IN THE MURDER OF CANADIAN SIKH SEPARATIST HARDEEP SINGH NIJJAR.
WHAT ARE THE KEY ITEMS INDIA EXPORTS TO AND IMPORTS FROM CANADA?
INDIA EXPORTS PHARMACEUTICALS, MACHINERY, IRON AND STEEL, ELECTRONICS, TEXTILES, SEAFOOD AND AUTO PARTS. IT IMPORTS PULSES, POTASH, MINERAL FUELS, WOOD PULP, DIAMONDS, AIRCRAFT PARTS AND SCRAP METAL FROM CANADA.
