Dr Reddy's Gets Health Canada Nod for Generic Semaglutide Injection

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Reddy's Laboratories receives Health Canada approval for generic Semaglutide injection, becoming the first company to secure market authorization in Canada.
  • 02/The approval covers 2 mg and 4 mg pens at 1.34 mg per mL, targeting a market where 3.9 million Canadians live with diagnosed diabetes.
  • 03/Launch preparations are already underway and CEO Erez Israeli said the approval underscores the firm's expertise in peptide science and complex product development.

Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (NSE: DRREDDY) has received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for its generic Semaglutide injection. The company announced the approval on April 29, 2026.

The market authorization covers the 2 mg per pen and 4 mg per pen strengths, both at a concentration of 1.34 mg per mL. Dr. Reddy's becomes the first company to receive this approval in Canada, ahead of Health Canada's review target date.

Around 3.9 million people, or 9.7% of Canada's population over one year old, live with diagnosed diabetes. Over 6% of Canadian adults have prediabetes, a condition that increases the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

The Hyderabad-based company said launch preparations are already underway. It expects to make the treatment available to Canadian patients soon.

"The approval of our generic Semaglutide Injection represents a significant milestone in our GLP-1 journey," said Erez Israeli, Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Reddy's.

The number of people living with diabetes in Canada is expected to rise as the population ages. Demand for affordable GLP-1 treatments could grow, but pricing and reimbursement negotiations will determine commercial success.

GLP-1 drugs like Semaglutide have become a major revenue driver for global pharmaceutical firms. Dr. Reddy's entry into this segment in Canada could open additional regulated markets over time.

Investors should monitor the commercial launch timeline and any pricing announcements from Health Canada or provincial formularies.
