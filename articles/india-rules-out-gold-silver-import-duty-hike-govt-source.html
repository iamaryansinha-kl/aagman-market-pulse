India Rules Out Gold, Silver Import Duty Hike: Govt Source

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India has no plans to raise gold and silver import duties, a government source said on Monday, May 11, 2026
  • 02/Statement comes a day after PM Narendra Modi urged citizens to avoid buying gold for a year due to Iran war impact
  • 03/India is world's second-largest gold consumer and largest silver consumer globally

India has no plans to increase import duties on gold and silver imports. A senior government source confirmed this official position on Monday, May 11, 2026.

The statement comes a day after Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged citizens to avoid buying gold for one year. He cited the impact of the ongoing Iran war.

India ranks as the world's second-largest consumer of gold by volume. It also holds the position of largest consumer of silver globally.

Precious metal imports are a critical revenue source for the government. Import duties on gold and silver have been a key fiscal policy tool for years.

The government source did not specify the current duty rates. India typically levies basic customs duty plus agriculture infrastructure cess on gold and silver imports.

The clarification provides immediate relief to jewellers and bullion traders across India. They had been concerned about potential duty hikes following the Prime Minister's remarks.

Gold and silver prices in India are sensitive to both international rates and local import duties. Any duty change directly impacts retail jewellery costs for consumers.
