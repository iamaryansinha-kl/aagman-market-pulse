JSW Steel Breaks Ground on ₹16,350 Crore Low-Carbon Plant in Andhra

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JSW Steel breaks ground on ₹16,350 crore steel plant in Rayalaseema, Andhra Pradesh using electric arc furnace technology
  • 02/Phase 1: 1 mtpa capacity at ₹4,500 crore. Phase 2: 2 mtpa total with investment up to ₹11,850 crore
  • 03/Plant will use recycled steel scrap and DRI instead of blast furnace route for lower carbon emissions

JSW Steel Ltd has broken ground on a new steel plant in Andhra Pradesh. The ₹16,350-crore investment will expand domestic steelmaking capacity while advancing the company's push into lower-carbon production.

The project in the Rayalaseema region will be built in two phases. Phase 1 will establish a 1 million tonne per annum (mtpa) integrated steel plant at a cost of ₹4,500 crore. Phase 2 will double capacity to 2 mtpa, taking total investment to ₹16,350 crore.

The Rayalaseema plant will use electric arc furnace (EAF) technology. It will rely on recycled steel scrap and high-grade direct reduced iron (DRI) instead of the traditional blast furnace route. The facility is being designed to improve energy efficiency and lower carbon emissions.

"India is entering a significant phase of manufacturing and infrastructure expansion, and Andhra Pradesh is at the forefront of this growth. Steel will remain a critical enabler of this growth," said Jayant Acharya, joint managing director and chief executive of JSW Steel.

Sajjan Jindal, chairman and managing director of JSW Group, said: "The commencement of the Rayalaseema Steel Project reflects JSW's confidence in Andhra Pradesh's long-term industrial and economic potential. We aim to support the state's manufacturing-led growth while building globally competitive industrial capabilities."

The investment comes as JSW Steel accelerates capacity expansion. In May, the company said it plans to double steelmaking capacity to about 80 mtpa by 2031 through brownfield expansion and joint ventures. This would place India's largest steelmaker among the world's biggest producers.

The Rayalaseema project announcement comes three months after Tata Steel commissioned its first electric arc furnace at Ludhiana, Punjab. The 0.75 mtpa plant was built at an investment of approximately ₹3,200 crore.

The commencement ceremony was attended by Andhra Pradesh chief minister N. Chandrababu Naidu, senior state government officials, and Jindal. JSW said the project is expected to benefit from planned road, rail, power and water infrastructure.

The company expects the investment to spur ancillary industries and logistics activity. This will strengthen Andhra Pradesh's manufacturing base while supporting India's steel demand through a lower-carbon production process.
