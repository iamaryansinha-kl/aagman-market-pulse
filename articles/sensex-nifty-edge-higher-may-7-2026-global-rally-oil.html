Sensex, Nifty Edge Higher On May 7 Amid Global Rally, Softer Oil Prices

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex and Nifty opened marginally higher on May 7, tracking a global equity rally and softer crude oil prices
  • 02/Japan's Nikkei hit a record 62,243 (+4.6%), Hang Seng rose 1.2%, while India's derivatives data signaled cautious range-bound sentiment
  • 03/Watch PCR at 0.61, India VIX near 18, and Nifty resistance at 24,200-24,300 for directional cues

Indian equity markets opened with a positive bias on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The BSE Sensex gained 206.76 points to 78,165.28, while the NSE Nifty 50 rose 68.85 points to 24,399.80. The Sensex had opened at 78,339.24 from the previous close of 77,958.52.

The uptick tracked a broad rally across Asian markets. Japan's Nikkei 225 surged 4.6% to a record 62,243.88, breaking above 62,000 for the first time. Hong Kong's Hang Seng gained 1.2% to 26,531.35, and Australia's S&P/ASX 200 added 1.2% to 8,870.90.

South Korea's Kospi slipped 1.4% to 7,281.37 as traders booked profits after a nearly 7% jump in the prior session. Taiwan's Taiex climbed 1.7%.

Investor sentiment was lifted by easing Middle East tensions. Iran offered maritime support in the Strait of Hormuz after US President Donald Trump temporarily paused the Project Freedom initiative, raising hopes of a US-Iran peace deal.

Crude oil prices softened on those hopes. July Brent futures traded at $102.02 per barrel, up 0.74%, while June WTI was at $95.77, up 0.73%. On MCX, May crude oil futures were at ₹9,076, up 0.65% from the previous close of ₹9,017.

On the domestic derivatives front, indicators signaled caution. The put-call ratio hovered near 0.61, indicating a bearish undertone. Heavy call writing at Nifty strike levels of 24,200 to 24,300 suggests strong resistance and limited upside.

The India VIX was near 18, signaling range-bound trade expectations. Nifty support is at 24,000 to 23,800. Bajaj Auto led Nifty gainers at +1%, while Tata Consumer was the top loser at -0.89%.

The Indian rupee opened 0.13% lower at 94.7350 against the US dollar versus the previous close of 94.61. Gold prices climbed for a third straight session, with spot gold up 0.3% at $4,701.19 per ounce and MCX gold futures at ₹152,662, up ₹530.
