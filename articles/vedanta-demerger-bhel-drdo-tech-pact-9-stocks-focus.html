Vedanta Demerger, BHEL-DRDO Tech Pact: 9 Stocks in Focus Today

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) shares go ex-date for demerger on April 29; eligible shareholders get one share each of four new companies per existing share
  • 02/BHEL (NSE: BHEL) signs technology transfer pact with DRDO's NSTL for Gas Turbine Infrared Suppression System for naval vessels
  • 03/Peak XV Partners exits MobiKwik (NSE: MOBIKWIK) stake via bulk deal, selling 62.15 lakh shares for ₹133.01 crore at ₹214.01 each

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) shares go ex-date for the proposed demerger on April 29, 2026. Investors buying the stock today will be eligible for the demerger benefits.

Eligible shareholders will receive one share each of Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd, Vedanta Power Ltd, Vedanta Oil & Gas Ltd, and Vedanta Iron and Steel Ltd. This applies for every share held as on the record date.

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (NSE: BHEL) signed a technology transfer agreement with DRDO's Naval Science and Technological Laboratory. The pact covers the Gas Turbine Infrared Suppression System for naval vessels.

The agreement enables BHEL to fabricate and install the indigenously designed LM2500 GT-IRSS system. It focuses on strengthening domestic defence manufacturing capabilities.

EIE Renewables, a wholly owned subsidiary of Enviro Infra Engineers, will acquire Suyog Urja for ₹311 crore. The deal involves 51 per cent equity upfront, with the remaining 49 per cent by July 31, 2028.

Suyog Urja posted a turnover of ₹171.99 crore and net worth of ₹30.34 crore as of March 31, 2025. The company operates wind and solar projects across multiple states.

Peak XV Partners sold its entire 7.89 per cent stake in One MobiKwik Systems Ltd (NSE: MOBIKWIK) for ₹133.01 crore. The sale of 62.15 lakh shares was priced at ₹214.01 each in a secondary market bulk deal.

Buyers included Hong Kong-based Viridian Asia Opportunities Fund, Societe Generale, and Mathew Cyriac's Elimath Advisors. Viridian acquired 13.2 lakh shares while Elimath bought 30 lakh shares.

OneSource Specialty Pharma's Bengaluru facility received an EU GMP Certificate from German authorities. Wanbury's Patalganga site passed an MFDS inspection with zero observations, confirming cGMP compliance.

Solara Active Pharma Sciences' Puducherry facility received a US FDA VAI classification after an unannounced inspection. Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd (NSE: RVNL) secured a ₹39.21 crore order from NMDC for a residential campus at Banjara Hills, Hyderabad.

Adroit Infotech won a ₹23 crore order from PT Supra Primatama Nusantara for SAP BRIM-Managed Services. The project covers functional and technical support for one year.
