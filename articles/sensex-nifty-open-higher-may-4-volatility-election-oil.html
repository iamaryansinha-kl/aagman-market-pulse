Sensex, Nifty Open Higher May 4; Volatility Likely Amid Election, Oil

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex and Nifty open higher on May 4, 2026, with Gift Nifty signaling a gap-up near 24,270, up roughly 273 points from Friday's 23,997 close
  • 02/India VIX holds at 18.4 indicating elevated volatility; Brent crude recovered above $108 after a 2.4% drop to $105.55
  • 03/State election results from West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry begin today alongside Q4 earnings from Ambuja Cements and BHEL

Indian equity markets opened higher on Monday, May 4, 2026. Gift Nifty signaled a gap-up start near 24,270.

This represents a gain of roughly 273 points over Friday's Nifty close of 23,997. Sustainability of early gains remains uncertain.

Analysts warn of elevated volatility driven by state election results and West Asia tensions. India VIX, the volatility gauge, trades near 18.4.

Brent crude oil recovered above $108 per barrel after dropping 2.4% to $105.55. Global oil markets face a 4.8 mbpd supply deficit.

State election counting began at 8 am across West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry. Early trends in Assam show BJP leading in 10 seats and Congress in 6.

"Markets are poised to begin the week on a firm footing, but sustainability hinges on earnings and external stability," said Hariprasad K, Founder of Livelong Wealth.

Key Q4 results expected today include Ambuja Cements, BHEL, Tata Technologies, Godrej Properties, and Aditya Birla Capital. Their numbers will guide sectoral sentiment.

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 closed at record highs on Friday. MSCI's Asia Pacific Index gained 0.4%, led by a 2.4% surge in South Korea.
