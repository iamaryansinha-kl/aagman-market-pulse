Goldman Sachs India Portfolio: 8 Stocks Rally Up To 85% In CY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Goldman Sachs' India equity portfolio declined 6% in CY26 to ₹8,470 crore, down from ₹9,014 crore in December 2025
  • 02/Eight portfolio stocks delivered gains of 20-85%, led by GNG Electronics at 84%, while 18 stocks fell 10-44%
  • 03/The global investment bank made a fresh entry into Aye Finance in the March 2026 quarter, acquiring a 4.1% stake

Goldman Sachs' India equity portfolio declined 6% in calendar year 2026. The portfolio value fell from ₹9,014 crore in December 2025 to ₹8,470 crore as of June 25, 2026.

The portfolio comprised about 46 stocks as of the March 2025 quarter. Around 26 of these were negative performers during the period.

Eighteen stocks in the portfolio declined between 10% and 44%. The top six laggards fell 25-44%, led by Newgen Software Technologies which dropped 44%.

Despite the broad weakness, eight stocks bucked the trend. These delivered gains ranging from 20% to 85% over the same period, according to ACE Equity and Trendlyne data.

GNG Electronics topped the gainers list with an 84% rally, rising from ₹308 to ₹566. Goldman Sachs India holds a 1.90% stake in the company valued at approximately ₹122 crore.

TD Power Systems rallied 79%, climbing from ₹702 to ₹1,255. The bank holds a 2.40% stake worth about ₹470 crore, making it one of the largest holdings by value.

Inox India gained 65%, rising from ₹1,134 to ₹1,873. Goldman Sachs holds a 2.16% stake valued at approximately ₹367 crore in the cryogenic solutions provider.

Other notable gainers include Sai Life Sciences at 34%, Pearl Global Industries at 29%, Navin Fluorine International at 29%, SJS Enterprises at 26%, and Amber Enterprises India at 20%.

Goldman Sachs made a fresh entry into Aye Finance in the March 2026 quarter. It acquired a 4.1% stake in the fintech lender currently valued at approximately ₹168 crore.
