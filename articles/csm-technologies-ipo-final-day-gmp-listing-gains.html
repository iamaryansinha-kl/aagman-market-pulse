CSM Technologies IPO Final Day: GMP at ₹4 Signals 4% Listing Gains; Should You Subscribe?

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CSM Technologies IPO is in its final bidding session on June 29, 2026, with grey market premium at ₹4 per share indicating modest 3.5% listing gains
  • 02/The ₹145.78 crore fresh issue was subscribed 66% by Day 2, with retail at 75% and NIIs fully subscribed, but QIBs lagged at 38%
  • 03/Brokerage Swastika Investmart has a "Neutral" stance, flagging premium valuation and government contract dependence as key risks

CSM Technologies' IPO is in its final bidding session on June 29, 2026. The Bhubaneswar-based GovTech company aims to raise ₹145.78 crore through a fresh issue of 1.29 crore equity shares.

The price band is fixed at ₹107-113 per share. The lot size is 132 shares, requiring a minimum investment of ₹14,916 at the upper band.

The grey market premium (GMP) stands at ₹4 per share. This implies a 3.54% premium over the upper price band, with an estimated listing price of ₹117.

By Day 2, the IPO was subscribed 66% overall. Retail investors subscribed 75% of their 44.69 lakh share quota, while NIIs were fully subscribed at 1.09 times.

QIB participation remained weak at 38% against the 46.15 lakh shares reserved. Institutional demand typically shapes the final day momentum.

Established in 1998, CSM Technologies provides digital transformation and e-governance solutions. It serves government bodies and enterprises across mining, agriculture, healthcare, and tourism.

For FY25, the company reported revenue of ₹180.67 crore and profit after tax of ₹15.82 crore. Earnings per share stood at ₹3.64, pricing the IPO at a P/E of roughly 42.6x.

The order book was ₹357.63 crore as of March 2026. The company has raised anchor funds from Nova Global Opportunities Fund and Zeal Global Opportunities Fund at ₹113 per share.

Brokerage Swastika Investmart has assigned a "Neutral" rating. It notes the company's exposure to the growing GovTech segment but warns the valuation is steep compared to listed peers.

The brokerage also flagged high receivable cycles and heavy reliance on government contracts as risks. Competition in the IT services space remains intense.

Basis of allotment is expected on June 30, 2026. Shares are tentatively scheduled to list on NSE and BSE on July 2, 2026.
