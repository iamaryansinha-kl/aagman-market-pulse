BEL Q4 Results FY26: Profit Rises 5% to Rs 2,226 Crore, Rs 0.55 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bharat Electronics Ltd Q4 FY26 net profit rises 5% YoY to Rs 2,226 crore on 11% revenue growth to Rs 10,224 crore
  • 02/Full-year net profit reaches Rs 6,062 crore, up 14% annually, driven by sustained defense order execution
  • 03/Board declares dividend of Rs 0.55 per share; order pipeline and FY27 outlook remain key monitorables

Bharat Electronics Ltd (NSE: BEL) has reported a 5% rise in net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit stood at Rs 2,226 crore compared to the year-ago period.

Revenue for the quarter grew 11% to Rs 10,224 crore. The growth was supported by continued execution of defense projects.

For the full financial year FY26, net profit rose 14% to Rs 6,062 crore. The company maintained its trajectory of consistent profitability.

Full-year revenue crossed significant levels on the back of strong order book execution. Defense modernization programs continued to drive order inflows.

The board has declared a dividend of Rs 0.55 per share for the quarter. Shareholders will receive the payout subject to record date formalities.

BEL remains India's largest defense electronics company. Its order book visibility and execution pace are critical for FY27 earnings.

The stock performance and upcoming order wins from government defence contracts will guide investor sentiment. Valuation remains tied to order pipeline clarity.
