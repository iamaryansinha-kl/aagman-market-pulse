India-UK Trade Boost: Ministers Discuss Bilateral Growth Under CETA

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal and UK Business Secretary Peter Kyle hold virtual trade meeting on May 1, 2026
  • 02/Both sides aim to leverage India-UK CETA signed July 24, 2025, which targets doubling $56 billion bilateral trade by 2030
  • 03/Under CETA, 99% of Indian exports enter Britain at zero duty while tariffs on British cars and whisky are reduced in India

India and the United Kingdom held a virtual trade meeting on May 1, 2026. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal met with UK Secretary of State for Business and Trade Peter Kyle.

Both ministers explored ways to boost bilateral commerce and investments. They discussed leveraging opportunities under the India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA).

"We explored ways to further enhance our trade and investment ties while fully leveraging the opportunities under the India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA)," Goyal said in a social media post.

India and the UK signed CETA on July 24, 2025. The agreement allows 99% of Indian exports to enter the British market at zero duty.

Tariffs on British goods such as cars and whisky are reduced in India under the pact. CETA aims to double the current $56 billion trade volume by 2030.

Goyal and Kyle had previously met on the sidelines of the WTO Ministerial Conference in Yaoundé, Cameroon, on March 28, 2026. The May 1 virtual meeting builds on that earlier engagement.

The trade deal comes at a time when India is actively pursuing bilateral pacts with major economies. The UK represents one of India's largest export markets in Europe.
