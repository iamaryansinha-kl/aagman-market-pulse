Adani Power Sets Up CMAEL Subsidiary For Nuclear Energy Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Power's subsidiary AAEL incorporates Coastal-Maha Atomic Energy Limited (CMAEL) on April 13, 2026
  • 02/CMAEL becomes step-down subsidiary with ₹5 lakh authorised capital for nuclear generation, transmission, distribution
  • 03/Move follows SHANTI Act 2025 enabling private nuclear participation; India targets 100 GW nuclear capacity by 2047

Adani Power Limited (NSE: ADANIPOWER) has expanded its nuclear energy footprint through a new step-down subsidiary. Coastal-Maha Atomic Energy Limited (CMAEL) was incorporated on April 13, 2026.

CMAEL is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Adani Atomic Energy Limited (AAEL). AAEL itself was incorporated by Adani Power on February 11, 2026.

The new entity has an authorised capital of ₹5,00,000 divided into 50,000 equity shares of ₹10 each. Adani Power retains 100% indirect ownership through AAEL.

CMAEL's mandate covers nuclear and atomic energy generation, transmission, and distribution. This creates a dedicated structure for Adani's nuclear power ambitions.

The incorporation follows India's landmark SHANTI Act passed in December 2025. The Act opens nuclear power generation to private players for the first time in six decades.

India currently operates 24 nuclear reactors with 8.8 GW installed capacity. The government targets 100 GW by 2047 as part of its clean energy transition.

Adani Power is India's largest private thermal power producer with 18,150 MW portfolio across eight states. The nuclear foray marks strategic diversification beyond coal.

The SHANTI Act replaces the 1962 Atomic Energy Act and 2010 Civil Liability Act. It allows private firms to build, own, and operate nuclear power plants.

WHAT BUSINESS ACTIVITIES WILL CMAEL UNDERTAKE?
CMAEL IS AUTHORISED TO GENERATE, TRANSMIT, AND DISTRIBUTE ELECTRIC POWER DERIVED FROM NUCLEAR AND ATOMIC ENERGY SOURCES.

THE SHANTI ACT 2025 (SUSTAINABLE HARNESSING AND ADVANCEMENT OF NUCLEAR ENERGY FOR TRANSFORMING INDIA) PASSED IN DECEMBER 2025 ALLOWS PRIVATE SECTOR PARTICIPATION IN NUCLEAR POWER GENERATION FOR THE FIRST TIME, REPLACING THE 1962 ATOMIC ENERGY ACT.
