Micron Surges 19%, Overtakes Meta in Market Cap at $1.39 Trillion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Micron shares jump 18.5% to $1,244, giving it a market cap of $1.393 trillion and surpassing Meta's $1.391 trillion for the first time
  • 02/Q3 revenue more than quadrupled to $41.46 billion with adjusted EPS of $25.11, crushing analyst estimates of $21.05
  • 03/Company guided Q4 revenue at $49B-$51B and EPS at $30-$32; HBM memory chips are sold out through 2026

Micron Technology Inc (NASDAQ: MU) surged 18.5% to $1,244.4 per share. The stock rally lifted its market capitalization to $1.393 trillion.

The memory chip giant overtook Meta Platforms (NASDAQ: META) for the first time. Meta's market cap stood at $1.391 trillion.

The surge followed Micron's fiscal third-quarter earnings report on June 24, 2026. Revenue more than quadrupled year-over-year to $41.46 billion.

Adjusted earnings per share hit $25.11, well above the $21.05 analysts expected. Gross margin expanded to a record 84.6% from 37.7% a year ago.

"Micron's record fiscal Q3 financial results and even stronger outlook for Q4 reflect the strategic value of memory in the AI era," CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said. The company is investing at record levels to meet customer demand.

Micron's high-bandwidth memory chips are sold out for all of 2026. Only three companies globally can manufacture HBM at scale.

The company forecast Q4 revenue between $49 billion and $51 billion. Adjusted EPS guidance of $30-$32 also topped Wall Street projections.

Micron shares have risen roughly 270% in 2026. The stock has gained nearly 1,000% over the past twelve months.

UBS recently tripled its price target on Micron to $1,625. At that level, the company would be worth roughly $1.8 trillion.
