CESC Q4 FY26: Net Profit Surges 19% to ₹459 Crore, Revenue Crosses ₹4,100 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CESC Ltd reports 19% YoY jump in Q4 consolidated net profit to ₹459 crore for quarter ended March 31, 2026
  • 02/Total income rises to ₹4,129 crore from ₹4,030 crore in Q4 FY25; full-year revenue up 8.93% to ₹18,927 crore
  • 03/FY26 consolidated profit climbs 13.23% to ₹1,618 crore; company flags ₹35 crore labour law impact and margin pressure

CESC Ltd has reported a 19% year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit for the fourth quarter of FY2026. The power utility posted profit of ₹459 crore against ₹385 crore in the same quarter last year.

Total income for Q4 FY26 stood at ₹4,129 crore, up from ₹4,030 crore in Q4 FY25. The company crossed the ₹4,100 crore quarterly revenue mark for the first time.

On a standalone basis, Q4 revenue was ₹2,170 crore with profit at ₹223 crore. The standalone full-year profit came in at ₹852 crore.

For the full fiscal year FY26, consolidated net profit rose 13.23% to ₹1,618 crore from ₹1,429 crore in FY25. Consolidated revenue grew 8.93% to ₹18,927 crore from ₹17,375 crore.

However, operating performance weakened in Q4. EBITDA declined 8.5% to ₹743 crore from ₹812 crore in the year-ago quarter. Operating margin contracted to 18.14% from 20.94%.

The company recognised an impact of ₹35 crore on employee benefit obligations during FY26. This follows the implementation of four new labour codes effective November 21, 2025.

CESC continues to expand its renewable energy portfolio. It has signed power purchase agreements for 600 MW of wind-solar hybrid projects at tariffs of ₹3.74-3.75 per unit.

The agreements span 25 years with Vismaya Renewables, Hexa Climate Solutions, Purvah Green Power, and Sprng Energy. These projects will diversify CESC's generation mix beyond thermal power.

Shares of CESC ended at ₹184.69 on May 6, 2026, down 1.17% on the NSE. The stock has traded in a 52-week range of ₹119 to ₹203.80.
