Global Giants Commit $90B+ Investment In India Amid Geopolitical Risks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global corporations announce over USD 90 billion in fresh investment commitments to India despite rising geopolitical tensions
  • 02/Amazon raises India commitment to USD 48 billion by 2030; AirTrunk plans USD 30 billion for data centres
  • 03/Investments span AI, cloud, digital infrastructure, and manufacturing as firms diversify supply chains

Global corporations have announced over USD 90 billion in fresh investment commitments to India in recent months. The bets come despite rising geopolitical tensions and trade disruptions worldwide.

Amazon on Thursday raised its total India commitment to USD 48 billion by 2030. Chief Executive Andy Jassy discussed the plans with Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

The Amazon investment will support cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and digital services. It marks one of the largest corporate commitments to India.

Australian operator AirTrunk announced USD 30 billion for 5 gigawatts of data centre capacity by 2030. Google unveiled a five-year USD 15 billion AI infrastructure plan in February.

French firm Saint-Gobain pledged an additional 1 billion euros over five years. Canada Pension Plan Investment Board committed up to Rs 7,000 crore with CtrlS Datacenters.

ABB announced a USD 75 million manufacturing expansion in March. The planned investments span AI, cloud, digital infrastructure, and manufacturing.

Companies are diversifying supply chains amid conflict in West Asia and slowing growth elsewhere. India offers a large domestic market and improving infrastructure.

Sources said global boardrooms are doubling down on India despite economic uncertainty. The country is emerging as a strategic hub for technology and manufacturing.
