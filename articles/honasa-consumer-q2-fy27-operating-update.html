Honasa Q2 FY27 Update: Early-30s NSV Growth, Margin Lift

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Honasa Consumer expects Q2 FY27 net sales value growth in the early thirties year-on-year, with Mamaearth growing in high teens
  • 02/Younger brands are set to accelerate to around mid-forties growth, while offline channels lead the quarter on deeper distribution
  • 03/The company expects early double-digit EBITDA margin with strong year-on-year improvement; the update is provisional and unaudited

Honasa Consumer Ltd (NSE: HONASA), the parent of Mamaearth, released its Q2 FY27 operating update on October 6, 2026. The quarter ended September 30, 2026.

The company expects net sales value (NSV) to grow in the early thirties year-on-year. Shares responded immediately, rallying 6.74% to Rs 471.90.

Mamaearth, the largest brand in the portfolio, is expected to deliver high-teens YoY NSV growth. The company attributed this to improving brand affinity and a wider offline presence.

The younger brands are expected to accelerate to around mid-forties YoY growth. That means the House of Brands portfolio is now outpacing the flagship Mamaearth brand by a wide margin.

Offline channels are leading the quarter. Both General Trade and Modern Trade delivered strong growth, driven by deeper direct distribution and improved point-of-sale execution.

Online sales are maintaining their growth momentum alongside the offline push. The company did not break out channel-wise numbers in the update.

The bigger signal for investors is profitability. Honasa said the business is likely to deliver early double-digit operating margin in Q2 FY27, with strong year-on-year improvement.

Management has been focused on improving EBITDA margins through the year. An early double-digit print would mark a clear step-up from earlier quarters.

The stock has already priced in some of this recovery. Honasa shares are up roughly 60% over the past year.

The company's market capitalisation stands at Rs 14,638 crore. The stock trades at a P/E of 57.92 and a price-to-book of 10.19.

The 52-week high is Rs 509.80, set well above the current level of Rs 471.90. The rally after the operating update narrowed that gap.

One caveat matters for investors. The Q2 FY27 update is provisional and subject to limited review by the company's statutory auditors.

It is a voluntary disclosure. It does not constitute financial results, nor does it amount to earnings guidance.

Actual results may differ materially from the provisional numbers. The full reviewed financials will carry more weight than Monday's update.

The growth-with-margin combination is the story here. NSV compounding at roughly 30% or more, combined with a move into early double-digit EBITDA margins, is what drove the 6.74% single-day move.

Offline is the engine. General Trade and Modern Trade execution, not online marketplaces, did the heavy lifting this quarter.

The portfolio mix is also shifting. Younger brands growing in the mid-forties are gradually reducing the company's dependence on Mamaearth's high-teens growth.

Investors should treat the update as directional. The limited review is still pending, and the company itself has flagged that actual numbers may differ.

GLOSSARY: NSV (Net Sales Value): The revenue a company earns from selling its products after deducting discounts and taxes such as GST. It is a cleaner measure of underlying demand than gross billing. EBITDA Margin: Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation expressed as a percentage of revenue. It shows how much operating profit a company keeps from each rupee of sales. General Trade: Traditional retail channels such as neighbourhood kirana stores, chemists and small beauty outlets where brands sell through distributors. Modern Trade: Organised retail formats including supermarket chains, large grocery stores and department stores, typically served through direct company distribution. Limited Review: A lighter audit examination than a full audit, where statutory auditors check financial statements for obvious errors before results are published. Operating Update: A voluntary, unaudited business disclosure released ahead of formal financial results. It gives investors an early read on growth but can differ from final numbers.
