GAIL Q4 FY26: PAT Falls 15% to ₹1,485 Crore, ₹0.5 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/GAIL reports Q4 FY26 consolidated PAT of ₹1,485 crore, marking a 15% sequential decline from Q3
  • 02/Full-year FY26 consolidated PAT drops 39% to ₹7,582 crore amid challenging operating conditions
  • 03/Board declares ₹0.5 per share dividend; details on record date and payment timeline awaited

GAIL (India) Ltd has announced its fourth quarter results for FY2026. Consolidated net profit stood at ₹1,485 crore for the January-March period.

The quarterly profit represents a 15% decline compared to the preceding quarter. Sequentially, earnings momentum has weakened.

For the full financial year FY26, consolidated PAT fell sharply by 39%. The annual profit stood at ₹7,582 crore compared to the previous fiscal year.

The state-run gas utility declared a dividend of ₹0.5 per equity share. The board approved the payout alongside the quarterly results.

The company attributed the weak annual performance to challenging market conditions. Natural gas prices and volume dynamics impacted profitability during the year.

Investors await announcement of the record date for dividend eligibility. The payment timeline and ex-dividend dates are yet to be disclosed.

GAIL shares trade on the NSE under ticker GAIL. The stock has faced pressure amid volatile energy sector conditions.

GAIL REPORTED A CONSOLIDATED PROFIT AFTER TAX (PAT) OF ₹1,485 CRORE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF FY2026, ENDING MARCH 31, 2026.
HOW MUCH HAS GAIL'S PAT FALLEN ON A QUARTERLY BASIS?
THE Q4 FY26 PAT DECLINED BY 15% COMPARED TO THE PRECEDING QUARTER (Q3 FY26), REFLECTING SEQUENTIAL EARNINGS PRESSURE.
WHAT IS GAIL'S FULL-YEAR FY26 PROFIT PERFORMANCE?
FOR THE COMPLETE FINANCIAL YEAR FY2026, GAIL'S CONSOLIDATED PAT STOOD AT ₹7,582 CRORE, REGISTERING A SHARP 39% DECLINE FROM THE PREVIOUS FISCAL YEAR.
WHAT DIVIDEND HAS GAIL DECLARED?
THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF ₹0.5 PER EQUITY SHARE. THE RECORD DATE AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE ARE YET TO BE ANNOUNCED.

INVESTORS SHOULD MONITOR THE RECORD DATE ANNOUNCEMENT FOR DIVIDEND ELIGIBILITY, Q1 FY27 GUIDANCE, AND ANY MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY ON NATURAL GAS VOLUME AND PRICING OUTLOOK.
