AI, Semiconductors Top Global Themes for Indian Investors: Niteen Dongare

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Niteen Dongare recommends 20-30% global allocation for Indian investors via ETFs, direct stocks, and GIFT City funds
  • 02/AI and semiconductors remain the top investment themes; Micron Technology surged from $84 to $1,100 levels
  • 03/Space tech is emerging but AI and chips will dominate for next 3-4 years; LRS limit is $250,000 per investor

Niteen Dongare, Director & CEO of Anand Rathi International Ventures IFSC Pvt Ltd, says Indian investors should allocate 20-30% of portfolios to global markets. He recommends ETFs, direct stocks, and GIFT City funds as the primary routes.

The Liberalised Remittance Scheme allows Indian residents to invest up to $250,000 annually outside India. This creates diversification, USD asset exposure, and currency appreciation benefits.

The US dollar has appreciated 3-3.5% annually against the INR. Dongare started investing when the dollar was at 86 levels and has benefited as it reached 94-95.

AI and semiconductors remain the strongest long-term themes. Micron Technology grew from $84 to $1,100 levels. Nvidia, TSMC, and Hynix have also delivered strong returns.

Space technology is gaining interest after the $75 billion SpaceX IPO pushed Elon Musk's net worth past $1 trillion. However, AI and chips will dominate for the next 3-4 years.

Taiwan and South Korea offer strong opportunities beyond the US. TSMC and Hynix are leaders in chip manufacturing at lower costs than American peers.

"There is a concentration risk in AI because the top companies are delivering strong returns. But what about the other companies in the AI ecosystem?" - Niteen Dongare, Director & CEO, Anand Rathi International Ventures.

Healthcare innovation and defence are also worth tracking. But investors need sufficient knowledge before allocating to these sectors directly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT GLOBAL ALLOCATION DOES NITEEN DONGARE RECOMMEND FOR INDIAN INVESTORS?
DONGARE RECOMMENDS ALLOCATING 20-30% OF PORTFOLIOS TO GLOBAL MARKETS THROUGH ETFS, DIRECT STOCKS, AND GIFT CITY FUNDS.
WHAT IS THE LRS LIMIT FOR INDIAN INVESTORS INVESTING ABROAD?
THE LIBERALISED REMITTANCE SCHEME ALLOWS ANY INDIAN RESIDENT TO INVEST UP TO $250,000 PER FINANCIAL YEAR OUTSIDE INDIA.
WHICH GLOBAL INVESTMENT THEMES DOES DONGARE RANK AS THE TOP TWO?
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IS RANKED NUMBER ONE AND SEMICONDUCTORS IS NUMBER TWO. THESE THEMES ARE EXPECTED TO REMAIN ATTRACTIVE FOR THE NEXT 3-4 YEARS.
HOW MUCH HAS MICRON TECHNOLOGY'S STOCK PRICE APPRECIATED?
MICRON TECHNOLOGY GREW FROM AROUND $84 TO $1,100 LEVELS OVER THE PAST ONE TO ONE-AND-A-HALF YEARS, REPRESENTING A MORE THAN 13X RETURN.
WHAT ARE THE THREE BENEFITS OF GLOBAL INVESTING FOR INDIAN INVESTORS?
FIRST, DIVERSIFICATION ACROSS MARKETS AND CURRENCIES. SECOND, CREATION OF A USD ASSET BASE. THIRD, THE BENEFIT OF USD APPRECIATION AGAINST THE INR.
