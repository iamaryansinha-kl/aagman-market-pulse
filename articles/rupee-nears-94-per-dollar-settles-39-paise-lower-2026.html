Rupee Nears 94 Per Dollar, Settles 39 Paise Lower On Strong Greenback

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian rupee settled 39 paise lower, trading near the 94 per US dollar mark on April 22, 2026
  • 02/Strength in the US dollar index and sustained FPI outflows from Indian equity markets weighed on the currency
  • 03/RBI intervention expected near key levels; traders watch US Fed commentary and crude oil prices

The Indian rupee depreciated sharply against the US dollar on April 22, 2026. The domestic currency settled 39 paise lower, approaching the psychological 94 per dollar level.

The rupee opened at 93.72 and touched an intraday low of 93.94. It finally closed at 93.89 against the previous session's close of 93.50.

The US dollar index climbed to 99.80, its highest level in three weeks. Safe-haven demand for the greenback increased amid geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

Foreign portfolio investors sold Indian equities worth ₹3,200 crore during the session. Outflows have exceeded ₹18,000 crore in April so far.

Crude oil prices rose 2.5% to $74 per barrel. Higher oil imports widen India's trade deficit and increase dollar demand from oil marketing companies.

The Reserve Bank of India is widely expected to intervene near the 94 level. RBI has been actively selling dollars to prevent disorderly depreciation.

Bond yields rose 4 basis points to 6.82%. Higher US Treasury yields and rupee weakness have added pressure on the domestic debt market.

THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA IS EXPECTED TO INTERVENE NEAR THE 94 LEVEL BY SELLING DOLLARS FROM ITS RESERVES TO PREVENT DISORDERLY DEPRECIATION.

TRADERS SHOULD MONITOR US FEDERAL RESERVE COMMENTARY, CRUDE OIL PRICE MOVEMENTS, AND THE PACE OF FPI FLOWS. THESE FACTORS WILL DETERMINE NEAR-TERM RUPEE DIRECTION.
