Vedanta Resources Gets $943 Million Bond Tenders in Early Buyback Window

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Resources Finance II plc received tenders worth $943 million in the early participation window of its $2.1 billion bond buyback launched June 9, 2026
  • 02/The early tenders represent 45% of the total outstanding across four bond series maturing between 2030 and 2033
  • 03/The early tender deadline is June 23, 2026, with the expiration time set for July 14; Maximum Purchase Amount to be announced around June 29

Vedanta Resources Finance II plc has garnered tenders worth approximately $943 million. The early bids came through the early participation window of its ongoing debt buyback offer.

The tender offer was launched on June 9, 2026. It covers four series of US dollar bonds totaling $2.1 billion outstanding.

The bonds mature between 2030 and 2033. The series include 9.475% bonds due 2030 ($550 million), 11.25% bonds due 2031 ($500 million), 9.125% bonds due 2032 ($500 million), and 9.850% bonds due 2033 ($550 million).

The early tender results capture roughly 45% of the total outstanding principal. This signals strong investor interest in the Anil Agarwal-led mining conglomerate's paper.

Debt capital market executives described the response as good. Many investors are comfortable holding the bonds due to attractive yields and Vedanta's improving refinancing profile.

The buyback is part of Vedanta's broader liability management exercise. The company also plans conditional redemption of its 10.25% bonds due 2028 and 10.875% bonds due 2029.

The offer is conditional on successful completion of a new bond issuance and new loan facilities. The Maximum Purchase Amount will be announced around June 29, 2026.

Sodali & Co is acting as information and tender agent. Dealer managers include Citigroup, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, First Abu Dhabi Bank, J.P. Morgan, Mashreq, SMBC Nikko, and Standard Chartered.
