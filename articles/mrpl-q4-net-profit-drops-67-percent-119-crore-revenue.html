MRPL Q4 Net Profit Drops 67% to ₹119 Crore; Revenue at ₹28,493 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MRPL Q4 net profit falls 67% to ₹119 crore for the March quarter
  • 02/Revenue rises to ₹28,493 crore despite the sharp profit decline
  • 03/Results reported on April 24, 2026; stock trades on NSE under ticker MRPL

Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd (NSE: MRPL) reported a 67% decline in Q4 net profit. Profit fell to ₹119 crore for the quarter ended March 2026.

Revenue for the quarter rose to ₹28,493 crore. The top-line figure showed growth compared to the previous year.

The results were disclosed on April 24, 2026. The Hindu Business Line published the quarterly financial data.

MRPL is a public sector refinery and petrochemicals company. It operates in India's downstream oil sector.

The ₹119 crore net profit marks a sharp drop from the year-ago quarter. The 67% decline reflects a steep earnings contraction.

Revenue of ₹28,493 crore represents the company's quarterly top-line performance. The figure was reported for the March 2026 quarter.

The stock trades on the NSE under ticker symbol MRPL. Investors will track the company's financial trajectory in upcoming quarters.

The company has not disclosed specific reasons for the profit decline in the available data. Further details may be provided in the full earnings release.
