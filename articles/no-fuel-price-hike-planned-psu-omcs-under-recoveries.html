No Fuel Price Hike Planned as PSU OMCs Face ₹2,400 Crore Daily Loss

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoPNG confirms no proposal to raise petrol and diesel prices despite under-recoveries of ₹2,400 crore per day till March 27, 2026
  • 02/PSU OMCs bear ₹20-26 per litre under-recovery on petrol and ₹81-105 per litre on diesel as crude basket crosses $113 per barrel
  • 03/Prices remain frozen since April 6, 2022; adequate fuel stocks maintained but panic buying reported at some retail outlets

The Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas ruled out any increase in petrol and diesel retail prices on Tuesday. Prices have remained frozen since April 6, 2022.

Sujata Sharma, Joint Secretary at MoPNG, said there is no proposal to hike auto fuel rates. The statement comes amid rising global crude oil prices.

India faced a combined daily under-recovery of around ₹2,400 crore till March 27, 2026. PSU oil marketing companies are absorbing these losses.

Around March 27, under-recoveries stood at ₹26 per litre on petrol and ₹81.90 on diesel. By April 2, diesel under-recovery rose to ₹104.99 per litre.

The Indian crude basket averaged $70 per barrel in FY26. It surged above $113 per barrel in April 2026, marking a sharp increase.

Kotak Institutional Equities noted a 13-15 per cent decline in crude imports. Despite this, India's import bill rose by $190-210 million per day.

Sharma said all refineries are operating at high capacity with adequate crude inventories. Sufficient petrol and diesel stocks are being maintained nationwide.

Panic buying was observed at certain retail outlets due to rumours. The ministry clarified that no price increase is planned at PSU OMC outlets.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HAS THE GOVERNMENT PROPOSED TO RAISE PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES?
NO. THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM & NATURAL GAS HAS CONFIRMED THERE IS NO PROPOSAL TO INCREASE THE RETAIL PRICES OF PETROL AND DIESEL.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT DAILY UNDER-RECOVERY FOR PSU OIL MARKETING COMPANIES?
INDIA FACED A COMBINED DAILY UNDER-RECOVERY OF APPROXIMATELY ₹2,400 CRORE AS OF MARCH 27, 2026, ACCORDING TO THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM & NATURAL GAS.
HOW MUCH UNDER-RECOVERY ARE OMCs BEARING PER LITRE ON PETROL AND DIESEL?
AS OF APRIL 2, 2026, UNDER-RECOVERIES STOOD AT ₹24.40 PER LITRE ON PETROL AND ₹104.99 PER LITRE ON DIESEL. AROUND MARCH 27, THEY WERE ₹26 ON PETROL AND ₹81.90 ON DIESEL.
WHEN WERE PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES LAST INCREASED IN INDIA?
RETAIL PRICES OF PETROL AND DIESEL HAVE NOT BEEN RAISED SINCE APRIL 6, 2022, DESPITE A SHARP RISE IN THE INDIAN CRUDE BASKET FROM $70 TO OVER $113 PER BARREL.
ARE THERE ADEQUATE FUEL STOCKS AVAILABLE IN THE COUNTRY?
YES. ALL REFINERIES ARE OPERATING AT HIGH CAPACITY WITH ADEQUATE CRUDE INVENTORIES. SUFFICIENT STOCKS OF PETROL AND DIESEL ARE AVAILABLE AT ALL RETAIL OUTLETS, THOUGH PANIC BUYING HAS BEEN OBSERVED AT SOME LOCATIONS DUE TO RUMOURS.
