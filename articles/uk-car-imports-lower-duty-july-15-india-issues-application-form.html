UK Car Imports to Get Lower Duty From July 15; India Issues Application Form

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

UK car imports are about to get cheaper.

India will lower import duties on cars from the United Kingdom from July 15, and the government has issued the application form for importers to avail of the reduced tariff rates. The move is part of the broader India-UK trade agreement that aims to boost bilateral commerce and deepen economic ties.

The duty reduction is a significant concession. India currently imposes high import duties on automobiles to protect its domestic manufacturing industry. Under the trade agreement, UK car imports will face lower tariffs, making British brands like Jaguar Land Rover, Aston Martin, and Bentley more competitive in the Indian market.

The application form is a procedural step. Importers must submit the form to claim preferential tariff rates under the trade agreement. The process ensures that only eligible goods from the UK benefit from the lower duties, preventing transshipment from other countries.

The India-UK trade agreement has been negotiated over several years. The deal covers multiple sectors including automobiles, textiles, pharmaceuticals, and services. Both countries have made concessions to secure market access in areas of strategic importance.

For UK car manufacturers, the lower duties open a significant market. India is one of the world's largest automobile markets, with growing demand for luxury vehicles as incomes rise. British brands have a strong following in India, and the tariff reduction could boost sales substantially.

Indian car manufacturers are watching closely. Domestic companies like Tata Motors, Mahindra, and Maruti Suzuki have lobbied for protection against imports. The government has balanced the concession with other safeguards and phased implementation to minimise disruption to local industry.
