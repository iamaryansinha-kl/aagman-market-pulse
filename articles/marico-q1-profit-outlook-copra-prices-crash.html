Marico Q1 Profit Outlook Strong As Copra Prices Crash 45% From Peak

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Marico expects strong operating profit growth in Q1 FY27 driven by a 45% decline in copra prices from peak levels
  • 02/Consolidated revenue growth seen in early 20s with double-digit volume growth in India business at multi-quarter high
  • 03/Investors to watch Q1 results due July-end and management commentary on Saffola volume recovery and ad spend trajectory

Marico Ltd (NSE: MARICO) expects strong operating profit growth in the April-June quarter. The FMCG major cited robust business growth and softening copra prices as key drivers.

Copra prices declined 45% from peak levels during the quarter. This helped Parachute Coconut Oil deliver double-digit volume growth, its highest in several quarters.

Parachute brands contribute 36% of Marico's India revenue. The company passed on the copra price benefit to customers when prices fell in January.

The company expects consolidated revenue to grow in the early 20s. Growth is broad-based across core, digital and international businesses.

Saffola Oils recorded mid-single digit price-led revenue growth. Volumes declined as the company rationalized select variants to maintain profitability.

Marico's India business reported double-digit underlying volume growth. This marked a multi-quarter high for the domestic operations.

The company accelerated advertisement and promotion investments substantially. It invested in brand-building to strengthen franchise equity and drive portfolio diversification.

"Demand trends during the quarter remained steady, supported by resilient economic activity," Marico said in its quarterly update filed on Thursday. "We remain optimistic about consumption trends, while closely monitoring the evolving inflationary conditions."

The cost of crude-linked derivatives and vegetable oils rose sharply during the quarter. This partially offset the copra price benefit.

Marico shares gained 0.6% to ₹856 on the NSE at Thursday's close. The Nifty FMCG index gained 0.56%.

In Q4 FY26, Marico reported consolidated net profit of ₹391 crore, up 14% year-on-year. Revenue from operations grew 22% to ₹3,333 crore.

Analysts at Anand Rathi noted the FMCG sector maintained steady revenue growth during the June quarter. Some weakness was seen in the general trade channel, they said in a 30 June report.
