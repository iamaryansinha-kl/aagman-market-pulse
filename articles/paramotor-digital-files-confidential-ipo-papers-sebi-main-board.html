Paramotor Digital Files Confidential IPO Papers With SEBI for Main-Board Listing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •• Paramotor Digital Technology Ltd. has confidentially filed draft IPO papers with SEBI for a main-board listing
  • •• The company used SEBI's pre-filing route, allowing it to keep issue size and key details confidential for now
  • •• Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors and Equirus Capital are the book-running lead managers to the issue
  • •• The company operates SpendPro, RewardOn, yayyy.shop and DevStack across consumer and enterprise segments

Fintech and enterprise technology firm Paramotor Digital Technology Ltd. has confidentially filed draft papers with markets regulator Sebi. The company is seeking an initial public offering on the main board of stock exchanges.

The company submitted its draft papers under Sebi's pre-filing route. This allows issuers to keep details related to the issue size and other key information confidential until a later stage.

This route has been increasingly favoured by companies. It offers more flexibility in IPO preparations and allows firms to respond to market conditions before a public filing.

In a public notice, Paramotor said it filed the pre-filed draft red herring prospectus. This was done with Sebi and the stock exchanges in relation to the proposed initial public offering.

According to sources, Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors and Equirus Capital are the book-running lead managers to the issue.

Incorporated in 2016, Paramotor Digital Technology operates a diversified technology platform. Its business caters to both consumers and enterprises across India.

The company's portfolio includes SpendPro. This is a prepaid card-based spend management platform.

RewardOn is an enterprise rewards and loyalty management solution. The yayyy.shop platform is a direct-to-consumer digital gifting marketplace.

DevStack is the company's enterprise technology and software development services vertical. It focuses on developing business-critical software applications and enterprise platforms.

Paramotor's asset-light, technology-driven business model positions it well. It can benefit from increasing digital adoption in consumer spending, enterprise engagement and business process digitisation.

The yayyy.shop platform offers consumers access to prepaid and branded gift cards. These span multiple categories.

RewardOn enables enterprises to design and manage digital rewards programmes. These are used for employee engagement, customer loyalty and channel incentives.

DevStack helps organisations build customised and scalable digital solutions. The focus is on business-critical applications.
