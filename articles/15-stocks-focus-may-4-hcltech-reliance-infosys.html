15 Stocks In Focus: HCLTech AI Deal, Reliance Beauty Buyout, Infosys Vice Chairman

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HCLTech extends MetLife Stadium AI partnership, Tech Mahindra acquires Canadian payments firm Avant Tech Solutions
  • 02/Reliance Retail acquires Priyanka Chopra Jonas' haircare brand Anomaly; Infosys appoints Nitin Paranjpe as Vice Chairman
  • 03/NCC bags ₹1,703 crore orders, Siemens secures ₹1,825 crore deal; Vodafone Idea gets AGR dues relief to ₹64,046 crore

Fifteen major Indian companies are in focus on May 4, 2026, following a flurry of dealmaking and strategic moves. Key developments span IT, retail, pharma, energy, and infrastructure sectors.

HCLTech has extended its Cornerstone Partnership with MetLife Stadium. The company is now the Official AI Partner of the Stadium, the New York Giants, and the New York Jets.

Tech Mahindra has acquired Avant Tech Solutions, a Canada-based payments modernisation specialist. The deal adds real-time rail capabilities, ISO 20022 migration, and wealth management platforms to its portfolio.

Reliance Retail, a subsidiary of Reliance Industries, has acquired Anomaly. The haircare brand was founded by Priyanka Chopra Jonas. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Infosys has appointed Nitin Paranjpe as non-executive Vice Chairman, effective immediately. He currently serves as non-executive Chairman of Hindustan Unilever Ltd and non-executive Vice Chairman of Heineken NV.

Vodafone Idea received relief on adjusted gross revenue (AGR) dues. The Department of Telecommunications cut the payable amount to ₹64,046 crore as of December 31, 2025, down from an earlier estimate of ₹87,695 crore.

NCC Ltd secured four orders in April totalling ₹1,703.27 crore. The Buildings Division got ₹929.96 crore, the Electrical Division ₹603.41 crore, and the Transportation Division ₹169.90 crore.

Siemens received a new order worth over ₹1,825 crore. The internal work allocation covers manufacturing and supply of bogies, traction motors, and gearboxes.

Bharat Forge acquired a 30% stake in Fortuna Engineering Private Limited at an equity value of ₹129.60 crore. Fortuna manufactures precision-machined engine components for automotive and industrial applications.

Coal India received a Letter of Award from GRIDCO Limited, Odisha, for a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project. This marks a strategic pivot into renewable energy infrastructure for the coal giant.

Aurobindo Pharma subsidiary CuraTeQ Biologics received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for Bevqolva, its bevacizumab biosimilar. Two additional biosimilar applications are under review.

Lemon Tree Hotels opened Lemon Tree Premier in Biratnagar, Nepal. It is the city's first major branded hotel as the industrial hub undergoes modern transformation.

Sanofi India appointed Rahul Bhatnagar as Chairman, effective April 30. He has served on the board since 2020 and brings expertise in finance, strategy, and governance.

Deepak Chem Tech signed a long-term agreement with Praxair India, a Linde company. Praxair will build, own, and operate a dedicated HyCO plant for Deepak's upcoming polycarbonate facility at Dahej, Gujarat.

Shelter Pharma received international purchase orders. The company expects this to strengthen its global presence and contribute to revenue growth.

Diffusion Engineers secured a domestic order valued at approximately ₹8.16 crore. The order covers supply and maintenance of Roller Press Rolls for the cement industry.
