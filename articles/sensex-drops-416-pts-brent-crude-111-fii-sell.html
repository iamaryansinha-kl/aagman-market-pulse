Sensex Drops 416 Pts as Brent Crude Hits $111.4, FIIs Sell ₹1,151 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex declined 416.72 points (0.54%) to 76,886.91 and Nifty fell 97 points (0.40%) to 23,995.70 on April 28, 2026
  • 02/Brent crude surged 2.97% to $111.4 per barrel amid Strait of Hormuz disruption, while FIIs offloaded ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday
  • 03/Rupee closed at 94.56 against the dollar, down 41 paise; investors eye Fed meeting and Iran peace talks for cues

The BSE Sensex declined 416.72 points, or 0.54%, to settle at 76,886.91 on April 28, 2026. The 30-share index had lost 562.57 points intraday to 76,741.06.

The Nifty 50 dropped 97 points, or 0.40%, to end at 23,995.70. It managed to hold the 24,000 mark briefly during the session.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, jumped 2.97% to $111.4 per barrel. The surge follows continued disruption in the Strait of Hormuz.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded Indian equities worth ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday, exchange data showed. FIIs have sold a net ₹44,281 crore through April.

The rupee fell 41 paise to close at 94.56 against the US dollar. It touched an intraday low of 94.58.

Axis Bank, HCL Tech, InterGlobe Aviation, Maruti, SBI and ICICI Bank were the biggest Sensex laggards. Reliance Industries and Bharti Airtel bucked the trend.

"FIIs have been net sellers of ₹44,281 crore through April. We assume crude will remain north of $100 and FIIs will stay away," said Bruce Keith, Cofounder, InvestorAi.

Investors now await the US Federal Reserve meeting on April 28-29. Global markets remain on edge as Iran peace talks stall and oil prices stay elevated.
