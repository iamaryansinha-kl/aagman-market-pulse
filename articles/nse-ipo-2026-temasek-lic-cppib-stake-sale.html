NSE IPO 2026: Temasek, LIC, CPPIB Line Up $2.75 Billion Stake Sale

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE IPO will see existing shareholders sell a 5% stake worth $2.75 billion at a $55 billion valuation
  • 02/Temasek, LIC, SBI, ChrysCapital and Canada Pension Plan Investment Board are among 20 investors lining up to sell
  • 03/NSE will file its draft prospectus with SEBI by next month after declaring financial results

The National Stock Exchange of India is preparing for a $2.75 billion IPO through a pure offer for sale. Existing shareholders will offload a 5% stake in the exchange.

Singapore's state-owned investor Temasek and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board are among 20 investors looking to sell down stakes. Life Insurance Corporation, State Bank of India and homegrown private equity fund ChrysCapital are also participating.

The $2.75 billion valuation is based on a total exchange valuation of $55 billion. The figure comes from a platform that trades NSE's unlisted shares.

The NSE has been trying to list since 2016. Its key domestic rival BSE Ltd listed in 2017, leaving NSE as the last major Indian exchange without a public listing.

Monday was the deadline for expressions of interest to sell. Merchant bankers handling the IPO sought commitments from existing shareholders as part of the process.

NSE will now move to file its draft prospectus with SEBI by next month. The filing will follow the exchange's financial results declaration.

The exchange reported a 15% rise in quarterly after-tax profit to ₹2,408 crore for Q3 ended December 31. Consolidated revenue from operations rose nearly 7% from the previous quarter.

NSE is the world's most active equity derivative trading platform. It commands more than 75% of India's derivatives market and is the country's largest unlisted company with 177,807 shareholders.

A monetary settlement with markets regulator SEBI is likely to resolve lingering litigation. The settlement will open the way for the public offer after years of delay.

The NSE listing will be one of two major Indian share sales this year. Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Jio Platforms is also preparing a large public issue.
