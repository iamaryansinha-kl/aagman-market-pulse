CG Power Q4 Profit Jumps 32% to ₹363 Crore; Stock Hits 52-Week High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CG Power Q4 consolidated net profit climbs 32% y-o-y to ₹363 crore for quarter ended March 2026
  • 02/Shares surged over 4% to hit a 52-week high of ₹863.80; Motilal Oswal reiterates buy with ₹940 target
  • 03/Brokerage projects 25% revenue CAGR and 32% PAT CAGR over FY26-28 on strong power systems demand

CG Power and Industrial Solutions Ltd (NSE: CGPOWER) shares surged over 4 per cent on Thursday. The stock hit a 52-week high of ₹863.80 in early trade.

At 10:12 am, the shares were trading at ₹860.25. This compares with the previous close of ₹828.90 on the NSE.

The Murugappa Group company reported a 32 per cent year-on-year jump in consolidated net profit. Profit for Q4 FY26 stood at ₹363 crore, supported by healthy demand across power systems and industrial businesses.

Brokerage firm Motilal Oswal reiterated a buy rating on the stock. It maintained a target price of ₹940 per share, citing strong growth prospects across segments.

The brokerage revised its earnings estimates upward by 6 per cent for FY27 and 5 per cent for FY28. The revision factors in better margins in the power systems business and improved balance sheet metrics.

Motilal Oswal expects order inflows to post an 11 per cent CAGR over FY26-28. Growth is driven by robust demand for transformers and switchgear across domestic and export markets.

The brokerage models a revenue CAGR of 25 per cent over FY26-28. It projects EBIT margins of 13.9 per cent for FY27 and 15.1 per cent for FY28, leading to a PAT CAGR of 32 per cent.

However, the brokerage flagged execution risks. These include a potential slowdown in transmission and distribution capex, higher commodity prices, and weak motor demand.
