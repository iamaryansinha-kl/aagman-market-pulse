India Tech Investment Themes 2026: Six Sectors Backed by Real Force Functions

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Six technology themes are structurally compelling for Indian investors as force functions cross from early to unavoidable
  • 02/India's gig economy has created an estimated 15 million workers across delivery, ride-hailing, and home services with few dedicated products
  • 03/GST formalisation, UPI, and mandatory e-invoicing drove more B2B software adoption in two years than a decade of sales outreach

India's technology landscape is shifting from consumer hype to infrastructure-level opportunities. Six themes stand out as structurally compelling for investors right now.

Sandeep Gogia, MD – Tech and Digital at Equirus Capital, argues these themes are backed by force functions already in motion. GST formalisation, UPI, and mandatory e-invoicing did more for B2B software adoption in two years than a decade of sales outreach.

The data infrastructure space remains the unglamorous but essential bet behind the AI boom. Enterprise data in India sits in disconnected ERPs, WhatsApp threads, and Oracle databases.

Several early-stage Indian data infrastructure companies already generate more ARR from US customers than domestic ones. That signals how globally portable this category is.

Deep tech and manufacturing are seeing a real shift. Apple manufactures iPhones in Tamil Nadu. Semiconductor packaging is ramping up. Precision component clusters are emerging around Pune and Coimbatore.

This generates demand for shop-floor MES platforms, vision-based quality control, and predictive maintenance tooling. Sales cycles are longer than SaaS investors prefer, but once embedded, no factory manager replaces his production monitoring system in a rush.

Fintech's second act is less glamorous but more interesting. The consumer wave is largely written. The infrastructure layer — credit decisioning APIs, embedded lending rails, and fraud detection stacks — remains underbuilt.

India's Account Aggregator framework and OCEN have created a consent-based financial data-sharing architecture with no real parallel globally. The opportunity is the plumbing, not another consumer app.

The aggregation economy has shifted from platform-building to challenger dynamics. Rapido started with bike taxis, a segment Ola and Uber ignored, then expanded. Snabbit is collapsing the booking-to-arrival window to under an hour against Urban Company.

These platforms have built India's first truly large-scale gig workforce. An estimated 15 million workers across delivery, ride-hailing, and home services face irregular income, no formal credit, and patchy insurance. That gap is where the next wave of fintech and HR-tech investment lands.

Vertical-specific aggregators are applying the proven playbook to elder care, tutoring, pet services, and SME logistics. Founders skip the consumer education phase entirely. That is a meaningfully better risk profile than what Swiggy faced in 2015.

Climate tech benefits from regulatory pull. BRSR reporting mandates for listed companies took effect in FY23. Most compliance officers admit their emissions data is held together with spreadsheets and goodwill. Monetisation timelines run seven to ten years.

The supply chain pull is structural. Every tier 1 vendor of a listed manufacturer will eventually face downstream pressure to report emissions. The useful question is which specific companies have found the operational wedge that actually holds.

WHAT ARE THE SIX TECHNOLOGY THEMES INVESTORS SHOULD WATCH IN INDIA?
THE SIX THEMES ARE DATA INFRASTRUCTURE, DEEP TECH AND MANUFACTURING, FINTECH INFRASTRUCTURE, THE AGGREGATION ECONOMY, AND CLIMATE TECH. EACH IS BACKED BY A FORCE FUNCTION ALREADY IN MOTION, FROM GST FORMALISATION TO BRSR REPORTING MANDATES.
HOW LARGE IS INDIA'S GIG WORKFORCE?
INDIA'S GIG ECONOMY PLATFORMS HAVE COLLECTIVELY BUILT A WORKFORCE OF AN ESTIMATED 15 MILLION WORKERS ACROSS DELIVERY, RIDE-HAILING, AND HOME SERVICES. ALMOST NOBODY HAS BUILT SERIOUS FINANCIAL PRODUCTS FOR THEM.
WHAT IS DRIVING B2B SOFTWARE ADOPTION IN INDIA'S MID-MARKET?
GST FORMALISATION, UPI, AND MANDATORY E-INVOICING DID MORE FOR B2B SOFTWARE ADOPTION IN TWO YEARS THAN A DECADE OF SALES OUTREACH COULD. INDIA'S MID-MARKET HAS FINALLY BEEN FORCED TO DIGITISE.
