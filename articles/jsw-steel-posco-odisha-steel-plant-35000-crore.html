JSW Steel and POSCO Partner for ₹35,000 Crore Odisha Steel Plant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JSW Steel and POSCO partner for ₹35,000 crore steel plant in Odisha
  • 02/Project to be located in Paradip region with annual capacity of 5 MTPA
  • 03/Announcement coincides with South Korean President Lee Jae-myung's India visit

JSW Steel has entered into a strategic partnership with POSCO. The South Korean steel giant will collaborate on a new steel plant in Odisha.

The project represents an estimated investment of ₹35,000 crore. It will be located in the Paradip region with an annual capacity of 5 million tonnes.

The announcement coincides with high-level diplomatic engagements. South Korean President Lee Jae-myung is visiting India for bilateral talks.

POSCO is one of the world's largest steel producers. The company brings advanced technology and global expertise to the partnership.

The Odisha plant will add significant steel-making capacity. It supports India's goal of expanding domestic steel production to 300 MTPA by 2030.

The partnership reflects strengthening India-South Korea economic ties. Both countries are looking to deepen trade and investment relations.

JSW Steel has been actively expanding its capacity. The company aims to increase output to meet growing domestic demand from infrastructure and manufacturing.
