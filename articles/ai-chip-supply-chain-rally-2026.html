AI Chip Supply Chain Rally: Unimicron Up 770%, Prices Set to Rise

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The AI infrastructure boom is driving explosive gains in chip supply chain stocks, with Unimicron up 770% and Ibiden up 530% over 12 months
  • 02/AI servers consume 10-15 times more MLCCs than standard servers; production lines are running above 90% capacity
  • 03/Samsung Electro-Mechanics is considering a 10% MLCC price hike; JPMorgan raised targets on Murata and Taiyo Yuden

The artificial intelligence rally is spreading deeper into Asia's semiconductor supply chain. Investors are shifting focus from headline chipmakers to the lesser-known components that power them.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co., Samsung Electronics, and SK Hynix have dominated headlines as critical Nvidia suppliers. Their stocks have surged to record highs amid a global chip shortage.

Now the spotlight is turning to three critical categories. Multi-layer ceramic capacitors regulate power. Advanced chip substrates connect semiconductors. Thermal compression bonding fuses hardware together.

Within substrates, Unimicron Technology has surged around 770% over 12 months. Japanese peer Ibiden Co. has jumped approximately 530% in the same period.

MLCC producers Samsung Electro-Mechanics and Murata Manufacturing climbed to record highs this month. TCB leader Hanmi Semiconductor also hit an all-time high.

An AI server uses 10 to 15 times more MLCCs than a standard server. It uses about 30 times more than a smartphone, per Pictet Asset Management's Young Jae Lee.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics said this week it is considering raising MLCC prices by as much as 10%. Citigroup analyst Takayuki Naito highlighted growing expectations for increases across MLCCs, aluminum capacitors, and package substrates.

Production lines for MLCCs and substrates are running above 90% capacity. BofA's Simon Woo warned that even a slight AI demand ramp-up would sharply shrink conventional capacity.

JPMorgan analysts raised price targets for Murata and Taiyo Yuden last week. They cited tight supply and demand dynamics likely to persist for an extended period.

Aberdeen Investments' Kieron Poon sees pricing power firmly with suppliers. "Definitely still in the hands of the supplier," he said, given concentrated supply and expanding customer pools.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH COMPANIES ARE LEADING THE AI CHIP SUPPLY CHAIN RALLY?
SUBSTRATE MAKERS UNIMICRON TECHNOLOGY AND IBIDEN HAVE SURGED 770% AND 530% RESPECTIVELY OVER THE PAST 12 MONTHS. MLCC PRODUCERS SAMSUNG ELECTRO-MECHANICS AND MURATA MANUFACTURING, AND TCB LEADER HANMI SEMICONDUCTOR HAVE ALL HIT RECORD HIGHS THIS MONTH.
WHAT ARE THE THREE KEY COMPONENT CATEGORIES IN THIS RALLY?
THE THREE CATEGORIES ARE MULTI-LAYER CERAMIC CAPACITORS (MLCCS) THAT REGULATE POWER INSIDE ELECTRONIC SYSTEMS, ADVANCED CHIP SUBSTRATES THAT CONNECT SEMICONDUCTORS TO HARDWARE, AND THERMAL COMPRESSION BONDING (TCB) WHICH IS THE PRECISION PROCESS THAT FUSES EVERYTHING TOGETHER.
HOW MUCH MORE POWER DO AI SERVERS CONSUME COMPARED TO STANDARD SERVERS?
AN AI SERVER USES 10 TO 15 TIMES MORE MLCCS THAN A STANDARD SERVER, AND ABOUT 30 TIMES MORE THAN A SMARTPHONE, ACCORDING TO PICTET ASSET MANAGEMENT SENIOR INVESTMENT MANAGER YOUNG JAE LEE.
ARE COMPONENT PRICES EXPECTED TO INCREASE?
YES. SAMSUNG ELECTRO-MECHANICS IS CONSIDERING RAISING MLCC PRODUCT PRICES BY AS MUCH AS 10%. CITIGROUP ANALYST TAKAYUKI NAITO ALSO HIGHLIGHTED GROWING EXPECTATIONS FOR PRICE INCREASES ACROSS MLCCS, ALUMINUM CAPACITORS, AND PACKAGE SUBSTRATES.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT CAPACITY UTILIZATION FOR THESE COMPONENTS?
PRODUCTION LINES FOR MLCCS AND SUBSTRATES ARE ALREADY RUNNING AT MORE THAN 90% CAPACITY, ACCORDING TO BofA GLOBAL RESEARCH HEAD OF KOREA RESEARCH SIMON WOO. HE WARNED THAT IF AI DEMAND RAMPS UP EVEN SLIGHTLY FROM HERE, CAPACITY FOR CONVENTIONAL USE WILL SHRINK SHARPLY.
