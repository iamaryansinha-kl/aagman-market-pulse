BSE 100 Rejig: Paytm, Ashok Leyland, CG Power Enter Index

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/One 97 Communications (Paytm), Ashok Leyland, and CG Power enter the BSE 100 index effective May 22, 2026, replacing Ambuja, Tube Investments, and Colgate
  • 02/TVS Motor enters BSE Sensex 50 replacing Adani Enterprises; BSE Sensex Next 50 changes effective June 22, 2026
  • 03/Paytm gained 34% in the past year while Ambuja Cements fell 23%, reflecting performance-driven index reconstitution by BSE Index Services

One 97 Communications (NSE: PAYTM), Ashok Leyland (NSE: ASHOKLEY), and CG Power (NSE: CGPOWER) have entered the BSE 100 index. The changes became effective on May 22, 2026.

They replace Ambuja Cements (NSE: AMBUJACEM), Tube Investments of India (NSE: TIINDIA), and Colgate-Palmolive (NSE: COLPAL). The reshuffle is part of BSE's periodic index reconstitution.

Ambuja Cements is part of the Adani Group. Its shares have fallen over 23% in the past one year.

Paytm shares have risen 34% over the same period. Ashok Leyland gained 33% while CG Power delivered 24% returns.

Colgate-Palmolive eroded 13% in the past year. Tube Investments shares were up nearly 1%.

In the BSE Sensex 50, TVS Motor Company (NSE: TVSMOTOR) has replaced Adani Enterprises (NSE: ADANIENT). This change is also effective immediately.

The BSE Sensex Next 50 changes take effect on June 22, 2026. Adani Enterprises, Ashok Leyland, Paytm, and CG Power will be added.

They will replace TVS Motor, Ambuja Cements, Colgate-Palmolive, and Tube Investments. L&T Technology Services replaced Cyient in the BSE Focused IT index.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH COMPANIES ENTERED THE BSE 100 INDEX IN THE LATEST REJIG?
ONE 97 COMMUNICATIONS (PAYTM), ASHOK LEYLAND, AND CG POWER AND INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS HAVE ENTERED THE BSE 100 INDEX. THE CHANGES BECAME EFFECTIVE ON MAY 22, 2026.
WHICH COMPANIES EXITED THE BSE 100 INDEX?
AMBUJA CEMENTS, TUBE INVESTMENTS OF INDIA, AND COLGATE-PALMOLIVE (INDIA) HAVE EXITED THE BSE 100 INDEX. AMBUJA CEMENTS IS PART OF THE ADANI GROUP.
WHAT CHANGES WERE MADE TO THE BSE SENSEX 50?
TVS MOTOR COMPANY HAS ENTERED THE BSE SENSEX 50, REPLACING ADANI ENTERPRISES. THIS CHANGE BECAME EFFECTIVE ON MAY 22, 2026.
WHEN DO THE BSE SENSEX NEXT 50 CHANGES TAKE EFFECT?
THE BSE SENSEX NEXT 50 CHANGES WILL TAKE EFFECT ON JUNE 22, 2026. ADANI ENTERPRISES, ASHOK LEYLAND, PAYTM, AND CG POWER WILL ENTER, REPLACING TVS MOTOR, AMBUJA CEMENTS, COLGATE-PALMOLIVE, AND TUBE INVESTMENTS.
HOW DID THE ENTERING AND EXITING STOCKS PERFORM OVER THE PAST YEAR?
PAYTM ROSE 34%, ASHOK LEYLAND GAINED 33%, AND CG POWER DELIVERED 24% RETURNS. AMBUJA CEMENTS FELL 23%, COLGATE-PALMOLIVE ERODED 13%, AND TUBE INVESTMENTS ROSE NEARLY 1%.
