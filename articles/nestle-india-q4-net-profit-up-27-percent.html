Nestle India Q4 Results: Net Profit Jumps 27% to ₹1,111 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nestle India posts consolidated net profit of ₹1,110.9 crore in Q4 FY26, up 27.18% from ₹873.46 crore a year ago
  • 02/Revenue from operations grew 22.6% to ₹6,747.79 crore; domestic sales hit record ₹6,445 crore
  • 03/Board approves ₹5 per share final dividend; input cost pressures from edible oil, milk, and wheat remain a risk

Nestle India Ltd (NSE: NESTLEIND) posted consolidated net profit of ₹1,110.9 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 27.18% increase over ₹873.46 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Consolidated revenue from operations grew 22.6% to ₹6,747.79 crore. Total sales rose 23.4% during the March quarter.

The company's board approved a final dividend of ₹5 per share for FY 2025-26. This comes on top of an interim dividend of ₹7 declared earlier in the year.

Domestic sales hit a record high of ₹6,445 crore, growing 23.1% year-on-year. The growth was volume-driven across all product categories.

"In this quarter Nestlé India delivered high double-digit growth and recorded its highest-ever domestic sales," said Manish Tiwary, Chairman and Managing Director.

The company increased advertising spends by over 50%. This investment powered double-digit volume growth across Maggi, Kitkat, Nescafe, and other brands.

Nestle India expanded its export footprint to 28 countries with 127 million equivalent consumer units in FY26. Rural reach expanded to about 216,000 villages.

However, input costs remain a concern. Cocoa prices are subdued, but edible oil and milk prices have firmed. Wheat quality has also been affected by unseasonal rains.
