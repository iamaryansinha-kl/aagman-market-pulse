Air India Express FY26 Loss Narrows to ₹3,500 Crore, Down 35% YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Air India Express is expected to report a FY26 loss of around ₹3,500 crore, down roughly 35% year-on-year from FY25's ₹5,000 crore loss
  • 02/Revenue rose nearly 18% driven by passenger demand and network expansion, but fuel costs and rupee depreciation continued to pressure margins
  • 03/Board reviews financials on April 30; airline targets operating profitability in H2 FY27 through cost control and 737 MAX fleet induction

Air India Express is expected to post a loss of around ₹3,500 crore for FY26. The Tata Group-owned low-cost carrier's board will review the numbers on April 30, 2026.

The projected loss marks a decline of nearly 35% from FY25. The airline had reported an estimated loss of around ₹5,000 crore in the previous year.

Revenue is understood to have risen by nearly 18% during the fiscal. Stronger passenger demand and network expansion across domestic and international routes drove the growth.

However, elevated aviation turbine fuel prices and rupee depreciation weighed on margins. Increased operating expenditure and geopolitical instability in West Asia also disrupted the airline's Middle East operations.

The airline is India's largest operator of flights to the Middle East. Route diversions and longer flying durations during the second half added to costs.

Air India Express is aligning commercial and operational functions with parent Air India. The coordination covers scheduling, fleet planning, and revenue management.

The carrier expects to achieve operating profitability in the second half of FY27. It is phasing out older leased aircraft and adding fuel-efficient Boeing 737 MAX jets.

After the board review on April 30, the financials will be presented to the Air India Board on May 7, 2026.
