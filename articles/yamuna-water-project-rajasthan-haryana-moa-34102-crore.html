₹34,102 Crore Yamuna Water Project: Rajasthan, Haryana Sign MoA After 32 Years

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rajasthan and Haryana sign MoA for ₹34,102 crore Yamuna Water Project on June 29, 2026, ending a 32-year delay
  • 02/577 million cubic metres of Yamuna water will flow through a 295.5 km underground pipeline from Hathnikund Barrage to Churu district
  • 03/A Special Purpose Vehicle (RHYW-SPV) will oversee implementation; Haryana has granted in-principle approval for pipeline alignment

Rajasthan and Haryana signed a Memorandum of Agreement for the Yamuna Water Project in New Delhi on June 29, 2026. Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Patil witnessed the pact.

The project carries an estimated investment of ₹34,102 crore. It aims to transport Rajasthan's allocated share of 577 million cubic metres of Yamuna water through an underground pipeline network.

The 295.5 kilometre pipeline will run from the Hathnikund Barrage in Haryana to the Hansiyawas Reservoir in Churu district, Rajasthan. The network includes three underground pipelines, each with a diameter of 3.6 metres.

The agreement ends a 32-year wait for the project. Rajasthan Chief Minister Bhajanlal Sharma described it as a milestone in water security under Prime Minister Narendra Modi's Viksit Bharat vision.

The project is designed to benefit water-stressed regions of Rajasthan, particularly the Shekhawati area. It will also provide drinking water to ten locations in Haryana.

The Rajasthan government has prepared the Detailed Project Report and uploaded it to the Central Water Commission's e-PAMS portal. Haryana has already granted in-principle approval for the proposed pipeline alignment.

A Special Purpose Vehicle named the Rajasthan-Haryana Yamuna Water Project SPV (RHYW-SPV) will handle implementation, construction, operation, and maintenance.

Rajasthan faces chronic water scarcity with several regions classified as water-stressed. The project is expected to boost socio-economic and industrial development in these areas.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TOTAL COST OF THE YAMUNA WATER PROJECT?
THE ESTIMATED INVESTMENT FOR THE YAMUNA WATER PROJECT IS ₹34,102 CRORE. THIS COVERS THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 295.5 KM UNDERGROUND PIPELINE NETWORK AND ASSOCIATED INFRASTRUCTURE.
WHEN AND WHERE WAS THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT SIGNED?
THE MOA WAS SIGNED ON JUNE 29, 2026, IN NEW DELHI. UNION HOME MINISTER AMIT SHAH AND JAL SHAKTI MINISTER C.R. PATIL WITNESSED THE SIGNING ALONG WITH THE CHIEF MINISTERS OF RAJASTHAN AND HARYANA.
HOW MUCH WATER WILL BE TRANSPORTED UNDER THIS PROJECT?
THE PROJECT WILL TRANSPORT RAJASTHAN'S ALLOCATED SHARE OF 577 MILLION CUBIC METRES (MCM) OF YAMUNA WATER ANNUALLY FROM THE HATHNIKUND BARRAGE IN HARYANA.
WHAT IS THE LENGTH AND SPECIFICATION OF THE PIPELINE?
THE UNDERGROUND PIPELINE NETWORK WILL EXTEND APPROXIMATELY 295.5 KILOMETRES. IT INCLUDES THREE PIPELINES, EACH WITH A DIAMETER OF 3.6 METRES, ALONG WITH INSPECTION ROADS AND ARTIFICIAL RESERVOIRS.
WHAT ENTITY WILL IMPLEMENT AND MANAGE THE PROJECT?
A SPECIAL PURPOSE VEHICLE CALLED THE RAJASTHAN-HARYANA YAMUNA WATER PROJECT SPV (RHYW-SPV) WILL BE ESTABLISHED FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION, CONSTRUCTION, OPERATION, AND MAINTENANCE OF THE PROJECT.
