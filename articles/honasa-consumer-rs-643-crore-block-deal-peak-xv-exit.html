Honasa Consumer Rs 643-Crore Block Deal: Peak XV Likely Sells 4.4% Stake

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Honasa Consumer (NSE: HONASA) shares fell as much as 4.91% on September 29, 2026 after a Rs 643.4-crore block deal of about 1.4 crore shares at an average Rs 450 per share.
  • 02/Peak XV Partners, formerly Sequoia Capital India & Southeast Asia, is the likely seller; it held roughly a 14.8% stake in the company.
  • 03/The trade is secondary-market only, so Honasa receives no proceeds; investors should watch for further Peak XV stake trims after lock-in expiries.

Honasa Consumer Ltd (NSE: HONASA), the parent of Mamaearth, saw its shares slide as much as 4.6-5% on September 29, 2026. The drop followed a large block deal executed in early trade.

About 1.4 crore shares changed hands in the transaction. That equals roughly 4.4% of the company's total equity.

The shares were traded at an average price of Rs 450 per share. The total value of the block deal came to Rs 643.4 crore.

Peak XV Partners, formerly Sequoia Capital India & Southeast Asia, is the likely seller, according to exchange data. The sellers were not officially confirmed on tape, so the trade should be read as a likely exit rather than a confirmed one.

Peak XV held about a 14.8% stake in Honasa Consumer before this transaction. It was one of the earliest institutional backers of the company.

The stock declined as much as 4.91% during the session. It touched an intraday low of about Rs 443-445, with some sources pegging the day low at Rs 444.45.

By the close of the early session, the shares were still down 3-5%. The block deal accounted for a significant chunk of the day's traded volume.

CONTEXT: Honasa Consumer listed on the bourses in November 2023 at an IPO price of Rs 324 per share. The stock has more than doubled from that level, giving early-stage investors a profitable window to trim their holdings. The deal also fits a broader pattern in Indian markets. New-age consumer and D2C names have seen repeated PE/VC stake sales following lock-in expiries. Paytm, Zomato, Nykaa and Honasa have all carried a VC exit overhang through 2024-2026.

On fundamentals, the company reported a 116.5% rise in Q1 FY27 profit to Rs 90 crore, per a Kotak Neo recap of the earnings. Revenue for the June quarter rose 27% year-on-year to about Rs 756 crore, while EBITDA more than doubled to Rs 110 crore.

"We delivered our highest-ever quarterly revenue and profit, with revenue growing ~32 percent and EBITDA more than doubling to Rs 110 crore. What stands out for us is that this growth is coming from both our core and younger brands," Varun Alagh, Chairman and CEO and co-founder of Honasa Consumer, said after the Q1 FY27 print.

This is a secondary-market block trade. Honasa Consumer receives no proceeds from the transaction; the money moves between the selling shareholder and the buyers.

IMPACT: The near-term risk for shareholders is supply overhang, not operations. If Peak XV continues to pare its remaining stake, further block deals could keep the stock volatile in coming sessions. The counterweight is earnings momentum. Analysts tracked by StockAnalysis.com carry a consensus Buy rating on the stock with a 12-month price target of about Rs 557. At the block price of Rs 450, and with roughly 32.6 crore shares outstanding, Honasa's market capitalisation works out to approximately Rs 14,700 crore.
