Trent Q2 FY27: Revenue Up 23% to ₹5,788 Crore, Zudio at 1,000 Stores

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Trent's Q2 FY27 standalone revenue rose 23% YoY to ₹5,788 crore, versus ₹4,724 crore in Q2 FY26
  • 02/Zudio opened its 1,000th store during the quarter; total footprint reached 1,342 stores as of September 30, 2026
  • 03/Shares ended flat at ₹2,580 on Monday; the stock is down 9.95% YTD and 19.64% over the past year

Trent Ltd (NSE: TRENT) reported standalone revenue from operations of ₹5,788 crore for Q2 FY27. The figure excludes GST and was disclosed in the company's exchange filing.

Revenue from sale of merchandise, excluding other operating income, also grew 23% YoY in Q2. For the first half of FY27, that measure rose 21%.

Total standalone revenue for H1 FY27 stood at ₹11,454 crore. That compares with ₹9,505 crore in the same period a year earlier, a 21% increase.

The quarter's headline milestone was store count. Trent opened its 1,000th Zudio store during Q2, marking a scale breakthrough for its value-fashion chain.

As of September 30, 2026, the company operated 1,342 stores across Westside, Zudio and other lifestyle brands. This is per the exchange filing.

Net store additions in Q2 were 10 Westside stores and 17 Zudio stores. For H1 FY27, the company added 11 Westside and 36 Zudio stores on a net basis.

On the market, shares ended Monday flat at ₹2,580 on the NSE. The traded range for the session was ₹2,572.80 to ₹2,608.30.

The stock has declined 9.95% year-to-date and 19.64% over the past year. Its 52-week high of ₹3,399.70 was hit on July 3, 2026, and the 52-week low of ₹2,183.67 on March 30, 2026. Market capitalisation stands at ₹1,37,573.93 crore.

GLOSSARY: Standalone revenue: Revenue earned by the parent company alone, excluding subsidiaries. Trent discloses this figure excluding GST in its quarterly exchange filings. Net store additions: The number of new stores opened minus stores closed during a period, reflecting true expansion of the retail footprint.
