India to Resume Bangladesh Tourist Visas by June, Restart Technical Talks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India set to resume tourist visa services for Bangladeshi nationals by end of May or early June 2026
  • 02/Daily visa issuance to Bangladeshis dropped from 6,000-7,000 pre-2024 to 1,000-1,500 by early 2026; medical visas continue at ~1,000 per day
  • 03/Bangladesh is India's largest South Asian trade partner with FY25 exports at $11.46 billion; technical talks to cover trade, customs, water sharing

India is set to resume tourist visa services for Bangladeshi nationals by end of May or early June 2026. New Delhi currently issues approximately 1,000 medical visas daily to Bangladeshis.

The move follows Bangladesh Foreign Minister Khalilur Rahman's April visit to New Delhi. It marked the first high-level engagement since Dhaka's political transition to the Tarique Rahman government.

Bilateral ties had soured during Muhammad Yunus's 18-month interim tenure. Friction centered on India hosting deposed PM Sheikh Hasina and sovereignty rhetoric from Dhaka.

Tarique Rahman of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party was sworn in as Prime Minister in February 2026. The new government replaced the interim setup and initiated more pragmatic, trade-focused engagement.

India had sharply scaled back visa issuance following the 2024 political upheaval. Daily visas to Bangladeshis fell from approximately 6,000-7,000 to about 1,000-1,500 by early 2026.

New Delhi stopped issuing tourist visas entirely in December 2025 due to heightened security risks. Dhaka had also paused visas to Indians in December 2025 before resuming in late February 2026.

Bangladesh remains India's largest trade partner in South Asia. India's exports to Bangladesh in FY25 were estimated at $11.46 billion against imports of $2.05 billion.

Businesses on both sides expect restarted technical talks to address trade curbs and transhipment restrictions. Meetings will cover trade, customs, water sharing, border management and consular affairs.
