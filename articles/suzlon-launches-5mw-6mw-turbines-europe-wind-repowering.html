Suzlon Launches 5 MW and 6.3 MW Wind Turbines in Europe, Targets Repowering Boom

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Suzlon Group launched 5 MW and 6.3 MW wind turbine models in Europe on April 21, 2026, targeting the continent's repowering and new-build markets
  • 02/The company has ~510 MW of installed wind capacity in Europe and operates technology centres in Germany, Netherlands, and Denmark
  • 03/Suzlon appointed a new company president for Europe to drive deeper market engagement and faster expansion across key locations

Suzlon Group has launched its 5 MW and 6.3 MW wind turbine models in Europe. The announcement on April 21, 2026 targets the continent's growing repowering and new project markets.

The Pune-based company currently has approximately 510 MW of installed wind capacity across Europe. This launch marks a strategic push to capture share in one of the world's most competitive wind markets.

Suzlon operates technology centres in Germany, Netherlands, Denmark, and India. These facilities support the development of advanced turbine platforms tailored to European wind conditions.

The company has appointed a new company president for Europe. This move aims to ensure deeper engagement and faster market expansion across key locations on the continent.

Suzlon's global wind installations total approximately 21 GW across 17 countries. This includes 13,128 wind turbine generators installed worldwide as of March 31, 2025.

The company's order book stood at a record 6.4 GW as of Q3 FY26. It delivered 617 MW during the quarter and has 2.4 GW of capacity currently under delivery.

Suzlon reported its highest-ever revenue of ₹10,851 crore in FY25. Net profit surged to ₹2,072 crore, up from ₹660 crore in FY24.

The company maintains a debt-free balance sheet with a net cash surplus of ₹1,943 crore as of March 31, 2025. Manufacturing capacity has been augmented to 4.5 GW annually.

Europe's wind repowering market is expanding rapidly as older turbines reach end-of-life. Suzlon's higher-capacity models allow developers to replace aging units with fewer, more powerful machines.

Suzlon shares trade on NSE under the ticker SUZLON. The stock has gained significantly over the past year on strong execution and record order inflows.
