NLMC Recommends Monetisation of Government Assets Worth Over Rs 5,000 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The government's land monetisation drive is picking up pace. The board of the National Land Monetisation Corporation, at its 21st meeting on September 18, considered and recommended monetisation proposals involving assets valued at over Rs 5,000 crore, the company said in a statement.

The proposals cover surplus land and building assets identified for monetisation across central public sector enterprises and other government entities. The board stressed the need for accelerated asset monetisation, reviewing the pace of ongoing initiatives and directing that identified assets move forward through transparent mechanisms.

The meeting's other business included approval of NLMC's annual financial statements and directors' report for FY 2025-26, alongside a review of the corporation's institutional and operational capabilities.

NLMC, a wholly owned Government of India company under the Department of Public Enterprises in the Ministry of Finance, acts as the dedicated vehicle for unlocking value from idle public assets. Its mandate covers surplus land and buildings of CPSEs under closure, non-core assets of CPSEs being strategically disinvested, and advisory support to other government entities.

The process it follows is structured: working with asset-owning entities to identify suitable assets, complete due diligence, facilitate valuation, and structure the monetisation, whether through sale, lease or other routes. The emphasis, the board reiterated, is on transparency, efficiency and value realisation.

The scale of the pipeline matters. Public sector enterprises hold large tracts of surplus and under-utilised land, and earlier tranches have included realty assets of MTNL, BSNL and RINL. Converting these into productive use generates non-tax revenue for the exchequer while freeing balance-sheet-constrained CPSEs from carrying idle property.

The Rs 5,000 crore recommendation signals growing engagement in the monetisation programme rather than a one-off transaction. For the government, which has leaned on asset monetisation to part-fund its capital expenditure, a faster conversion pipeline directly supports the fiscal math.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID THE NLMC BOARD RECOMMEND?
Monetisation proposals involving surplus government land and building assets valued at over Rs 5,000 crore, at its 21st board meeting on September 18.
WHAT KINDS OF ASSETS ARE COVERED?
Surplus land and buildings of central public sector enterprises and other government entities, including assets of CPSEs under closure or strategic disinvestment.
WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Monetisation generates non-tax revenue, unlocks value from idle public assets, and supports the government's broader asset monetisation and capital expenditure objectives.
