Vodafone Idea Shares Jump 2% to Record High, Rally 100% in 1 Year

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vodafone Idea shares climb over 2% to hit fresh 52-week high of Rs 13.88; stock has doubled from Rs 6.12 in one year
  • 02/Q4 FY26 net profit at Rs 51,970 crore driven by one-time AGR dues reassessment gain; revenue up 3% YoY to Rs 11,332 crore
  • 03/Brokerages remain cautious despite rally; Nomura downgrades to 'Neutral' with Rs 12.60 target, only 3 of 21 analysts rate 'Buy'

Vodafone Idea Ltd (NSE: IDEA) shares jumped over 2% on May 22, 2026, to touch a fresh 52-week high of Rs 13.88. The stock closed at Rs 13.73, extending its meteoric rally.

The telecom operator's stock has nearly doubled from its 52-week low of Rs 6.12 hit in August 2025. The one-year gain stands at approximately 100%, making it one of the best-performing large-cap stocks.

The rally has been fueled by a three-part catalyst. Kumar Mangalam Birla returned as non-executive chairman on May 5, 2026. AGR dues were revised down from Rs 87,695 crore to Rs 64,046 crore. A fundraising board meeting on May 16 evaluated preferential allotment proposals.

Vodafone Idea reported a net profit of Rs 51,970 crore for Q4 FY26 against a net loss of Rs 7,166 crore in the year-ago period. However, this was primarily driven by a one-time accounting gain from AGR dues reassessment.

Revenue from operations rose 3% YoY to Rs 11,332 crore. EBITDA grew 4.9% to Rs 4,889 crore. ARPU improved 8.3% to Rs 190 from Rs 175 in the year-ago quarter.

The company's market capitalization now stands at approximately Rs 1.47 lakh crore. Vodafone Idea plans to invest Rs 45,000 crore over the next three years to expand its network and compete with rivals.

Despite the rally, brokerages remain divided. Nomura downgraded the stock to 'Neutral' with a target price of Rs 12.60. Nuvama maintained 'Hold' at Rs 13.50, while Motilal Oswal stayed 'Neutral' with a Rs 10 target.

"If VIL is able to raise bank funding, it would be a key positive," Nomura said. "However, we prefer Bharti Airtel among telecom stocks under our coverage."
