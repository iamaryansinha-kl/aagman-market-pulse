IGL Q4 Results FY2026: Net Profit Falls 25% YoY to Rs 341 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IGL Q4FY26 consolidated net profit drops 25% YoY to Rs 341 crore
  • 02/Revenue climbs 6% to Rs 4,585 crore despite higher expenses
  • 03/Expenses rise 8% during the quarter, squeezing margins

Indraprastha Gas Limited (IGL) has reported its Q4 FY2026 financial results. The company's consolidated net profit fell 25% year-on-year to Rs 341 crore.

Revenue for the quarter stood at Rs 4,585 crore. This marks a 6% increase compared to the same period last year.

Total expenses grew by 8% during the quarter. The faster rise in expenses outpaced revenue growth, pressuring profitability.

The profit decline reflects margin compression in the natural gas distribution business. Input costs and operational overheads appear to have tightened the company's financial performance.

IGL operates as a leading city gas distribution company in India. It supplies compressed natural gas (CNG) and piped natural gas (PNG) across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, and Ghaziabad.

The company holds a dominant market position in the National Capital Region. It benefits from government push towards cleaner fuel alternatives.

Full-year FY2026 results and segment-wise breakdown were not disclosed in the initial filing. Investors should await the detailed annual report for deeper analysis.
