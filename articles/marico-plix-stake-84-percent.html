Marico Raises Plix Stake to 84.09% for Rs 1,012.03 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Marico Ltd acquired an additional 24.09% in Plix owner Satiya Nutraceuticals for Rs 1,012.03 crore, completed October 5, 2026, taking its fully diluted stake to 84.09%.
  • 02/Aggregate consideration for the 84.09% stands at Rs 1,392.07 crore; the remaining 14.09% is slated for acquisition in July 2027 for up to Rs 592 crore plus milestone-linked consideration.
  • 03/Q2 FY27 update: double-digit underlying domestic volume growth, copra rangebound around 35% below peak, and operating profit expected to grow in the mid-twenties.

Marico Ltd (NSE: MARICO) has raised its fully diluted stake in Satiya Nutraceuticals Private Ltd, owner of plant-based nutrition and personal care brand The Plant Fix – Plix, to 84.09%. The acquisition of an additional 24.09% from founders and certain other shareholders was completed on October 5, 2026.

The additional stake cost Rs 1,012.03 crore, per the company's exchange filing. Definitive agreements cover the acquisition of a 38.18% stake in tranches; aggregate consideration for the 84.09% holding now stands at Rs 1,392.07 crore.

The remaining 14.09%, held by the founder and certain other shareholders, will be acquired in July 2027. That tranche carries a base consideration of up to Rs 592 crore plus milestone-linked additional consideration, subject to definitive-agreement terms.

Marico said the investment expands its total addressable market in value-added foods and nutrition, and strengthens its presence in personal care and wellness. Satiya Nutraceuticals was incorporated on February 13, 2020, is headquartered in Mumbai, and was previously 60% owned by Marico.

The same filing carried a Q2 FY27 business update: domestic demand stayed resilient in a volatile operating environment, with underlying volume growth in double digits. Parachute Coconut Oil posted early-teens volume growth.

Value Added Hair Oils grew volumes in the twenties for the sixth consecutive quarter, aided by mid and premium segment investments, Project SETU direct reach, and Almond category growth. Saffola Oils delivered mid-single-digit price-led growth while volumes declined, as the company focused on threshold profitability and rationalised select variants.

International business grew in the teens in constant currency, led by Vietnam, the Middle East and South Africa. Bangladesh improved marginally on a sequential basis, though it lapped a high base amid elevated inflation.

On margins, crude-linked derivative costs rose further while copra stayed rangebound around 35% below peak. That sets up strong year-on-year gross-margin acceleration on mix and the copra tailwind, even as ASP — advertising and sales promotion — investments rose significantly.

Operating profit is expected to grow in the mid-twenties, with consolidated revenue also seen in double digits; the company said it will likely surpass near-term guidance across key financial parameters. Marico shares settled 1.11% higher at Rs 790 on the NSE on Monday, in a session range of Rs 780-Rs 814.
