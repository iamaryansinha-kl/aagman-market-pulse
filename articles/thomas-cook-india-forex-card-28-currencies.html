Thomas Cook India Forex Card Expands to 28 Currencies, Adds 16 New Ones

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Thomas Cook India expands its prepaid forex card to 28 currencies, adding 16 new ones to its existing 12-currency portfolio on April 30, 2026
  • 02/Stock trades at ₹93.89, down 1.25% from previous close of ₹95.08; shares have shed over 30% in the past year
  • 03/New currencies target short-haul Asian and African destinations; card available on Visa and Mastercard platforms

Thomas Cook (India) Ltd on April 30, 2026 announced an expansion of its prepaid forex card to 28 currencies. The company added 16 new currencies to its existing portfolio of 12.

The newly added currencies include the Chinese Yuan, Vietnamese Dong, Malaysian Ringgit, Indonesian Rupiah, South Korean Won, and Philippine Peso. Other additions are the South African Rand, Kuwaiti Dinar, Bahraini Dinar, Qatari Riyal, Omani Riyal, Hong Kong Dollar, Bangladeshi Taka, Swedish Krona, Danish Krone, and Norwegian Krone.

These join existing currencies such as the US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, and Japanese Yen. The expanded suite is available on both Visa and Mastercard platforms.

The company cited shifting travel patterns among Indian consumers toward emerging short-haul destinations in Asia, Southeast Asia, and Africa. The card allows direct loading and spending in local currencies, avoiding layered conversion costs.

Customers can load forex through retail outlets, digital platforms, a WhatsApp service, or via a partnership with quick-commerce platform Blinkit in under an hour. The card creates one of the widest currency offerings on a prepaid forex card in India.

Thomas Cook India shares were trading at ₹93.89 on NSE at midday Thursday, down 1.25 per cent from its previous close of ₹95.08. The stock has shed over 30 per cent in the past year.

The stock touched a 52-week low of ₹86.35 in March 2026, well off its 52-week high of ₹188.29. The company's total market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹4,415 crore.

Fairbridge Capital (Mauritius), a subsidiary of Fairfax Financial Holdings, holds 63.83 per cent of TCIL's paid-up capital. The stock trades at a P/E of 17.74.
