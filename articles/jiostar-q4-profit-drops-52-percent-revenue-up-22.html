JioStar Q4 Profit Drops 52% to ₹419 Crore, Revenue Up 22%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JioStar Q4 net profit falls 52% sequentially to ₹419 crore despite 22% revenue growth to ₹9,784 crore
  • 02/FY26 full-year net profit stands at ₹3,210 crore on gross revenue of ₹36,248 crore
  • 03/JioHotstar averaged 500 million MAUs in Q4; digital subscriptions hit all-time high

JioStar reported a sequential profit decline of 52 per cent for Q4 FY26. Net profit stood at ₹419 crore for the quarter ending March 31.

Revenue increased 22 per cent sequentially to ₹9,784 crore. EBITDA for the quarter was ₹827 crore.

For the full financial year FY26, JioStar posted a net profit of ₹3,210 crore. Gross revenue for the year reached ₹36,248 crore.

The company maintained dominant TV entertainment viewership share at 34.2 per cent. It reached over 810 million viewers nationally during the quarter.

JioHotstar averaged 500 million monthly active users in Q4. The T20 Men's Cricket World Cup Final recorded 72.5 million peak concurrency, a global record.

Digital subscriptions grew sequentially with direct-to-consumer plans reaching an all-time high. New monthly plans were launched to improve affordability and flexibility.

"TATA IPL 2026 opened at record scale with deeper engagement across platforms, reaching over 515 million viewers on linear TV and digital during the opening weekend," the company said in its press release.

JioStar is the media arm of Reliance Industries. It was formed from the merger of JioCinema and Disney+ Hotstar operations in India.
