Supreme Industries Q4FY26: Elara Maintains ₹4,200 Target on Volume Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Elara Securities maintains Accumulate rating on Supreme Industries with a raised target price of ₹4,200, rolling forward to March 2028E at 40x P/E
  • 02/Q4FY26 volumes grew 16% YoY to 231,889 tonnes and EBITDA margin expanded 391 bps to 17.7%, aided by ₹70-80 crore inventory gain
  • 03/FY27 volume guidance of 12-13% overall and 15-17% for plastic piping; margin guidance at 14-14.5% as PVC prices normalize

Elara Securities has maintained an Accumulate rating on Supreme Industries Ltd. The broker raised the target price to ₹4,200 from ₹3,980 while cutting FY27E/FY28E earnings estimates by 8% and 9.2%.

The target price is based on an unchanged P/E multiple of 40x, rolled forward to March 2028E. The stock currently trades at ₹3,630.80.

Supreme Industries reported strong Q4FY26 results. Volumes grew 16% year on year to 231,889 tonnes.

EBITDA margin expanded by 391 basis points to 17.7%. This was aided by an inventory gain of ₹70-80 crore from a PVC price spike in March.

Plastic piping was the standout performer. Q4 revenue rose 23.3% and volume grew 18.3%, driven by capacity expansion and channel restocking.

Packaging revenue grew 7.2% with volume up 10.1%. The segment is focused on value-added product offerings.

Agri demand has picked up in Q1 post PVC price correction. There are no major inventory issues at distributor or retail levels.

The company has committed over ₹1,000 crore in capex for FY27. Projects include greenfield piping plants at Patna, Jammu, and Gadegaon, plus a material handling facility at Malanpur.

All capex will be funded via internal accruals. Supreme Industries maintains its zero-debt status.

Management has guided for FY27 volume growth of 12-13% overall. Plastic piping volumes are expected to grow 15-17%.

EBITDA margin guidance for FY27 stands at 14-14.5%. This factors in lower margins as PVC prices normalize after the March spike.

The stock has gained 10% in the past three months. This move came on the back of higher PVC prices and improved quarterly performance.
