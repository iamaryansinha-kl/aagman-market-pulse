NRI Buyers Drive 10-12% of Luxury Home Sales at Whiteland: Director

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NRIs from Middle East, Singapore, UK, Australia and Canada account for 10-12% of Whiteland's luxury home sales
  • 02/Dwarka Expressway has emerged as a prime luxury housing destination with metro connectivity and airport access
  • 03/Branded residences like Westin Residences Gurugram are driving demand from global buyers seeking managed communities

Non-Resident Indians now contribute 10-12% of luxury home sales at Whiteland Corporation. Sudeep Bhatt, Director – Strategy, disclosed the figure in an interview with ETMarkets.

NRI buyers are primarily coming from the Middle East, Singapore, the UK, Australia, and Canada. India's robust economic growth and improving infrastructure are key attractions.

Bhatt said this is not a cyclical surge but a structural transformation. Long-term infrastructure investments and rising household incomes are creating sustained demand.

Dwarka Expressway has evolved into one of India's strongest luxury housing corridors. Metro connectivity, airport access, and proximity to commercial districts have improved liveability.

The company is partnering with Marriott International for Westin Residences Gurugram. This brings hospitality-led services, wellness design, and long-term professional management to homeowners.

HNIs are now viewing luxury real estate as a long-term wealth planning tool. High-quality real estate serves as an inflation hedge and offers capital appreciation.

Branded residences are expected to become a significant part of India's residential landscape. Buyers prioritise quality, transparency, and long-term value over speculative inventory.

WHAT PERCENTAGE OF WHITELAND'S LUXURY HOME SALES COME FROM NRIS?
NRIS NOW ACCOUNT FOR 10-12% OF WHITELAND CORPORATION'S LUXURY HOME SALES, ACCORDING TO SUDEEP BHATT, DIRECTOR – STRATEGY AT THE COMPANY.
WHICH GLOBAL MARKETS ARE THE TOP SOURCES OF NRI BUYERS FOR WHITELAND?
THE MAJOR SOURCES ARE THE MIDDLE EAST, SINGAPORE, THE UK, AUSTRALIA, AND CANADA. THESE MARKETS ARE DRIVING SIGNIFICANT TRACTION FOR THE COMPANY'S BRANDED RESIDENCES.

THE TRANSFORMATION IS DRIVEN BY INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS, RISING HOUSEHOLD INCOMES, METRO CONNECTIVITY, AND BUYER PREFERENCE FOR CURATED LIFESTYLES AND PROFESSIONALLY MANAGED COMMUNITIES.
WHAT IS THE WESTIN RESIDENCES GURUGRAM PROJECT?
IT IS A COLLABORATION BETWEEN WHITELAND CORPORATION AND MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL. THE PROJECT INTEGRATES HOSPITALITY-LIVING WITH WELLNESS DESIGN, PERSONALISED CONCIERGE SERVICES, AND LONG-TERM PROFESSIONAL MANAGEMENT.
HOW ARE HNIS VIEWING LUXURY REAL ESTATE IN INDIA TODAY?
HNIS ARE TREATING LUXURY REAL ESTATE AS A LONG-TERM WEALTH PLANNING TOOL AND INFLATION HEDGE, SEEKING BOTH FINANCIAL RESILIENCE AND EXCEPTIONAL LIVING STANDARDS FROM THE SAME ASSET.
