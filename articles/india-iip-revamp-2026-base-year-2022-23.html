India IIP Revamp 2026: New Base Year 2022-23 With Expanded Sectors

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoSPI will release the new IIP series with base year 2022-23 on May 28, 2026, replacing the outdated 2011-12 base
  • 02/Coverage expands for the first time to include gas supply, water supply, sewerage, waste management, and minor minerals alongside mining, manufacturing, and electricity
  • 03/The product basket removes obsolete items like fluorescent tubes and kerosene while adding laptops, vaccines, LED bulbs, and aerospace components

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) is set to unveil a revamped Index of Industrial Production on May 28, 2026. The new series will shift the base year from 2011-12 to 2022-23 to better reflect India's current industrial structure.

The revision aligns the IIP with the newly updated GDP and CPI series already released earlier this year. The GDP base year was revised to 2022-23 on February 27, 2026, and CPI to 2024 on February 12, 2026.

For the first time, the IIP will cover six sectors instead of three. Gas supply, water supply, sewerage, and waste management will join the existing mining, manufacturing, and electricity components.

The product basket is undergoing a major overhaul. Obsolete items such as fluorescent tubes, CFLs, kerosene, and printing machinery are being removed. New additions include laptops, vaccines, LED bulbs, and aerospace components.

MoSPI reviewed 276 items previously classified as "not elsewhere classified." The new index assigns 95% of their weights to specific categories, sharply improving precision and reducing data distortion.

A systematic factory substitution mechanism will replace non-operational units with active factories of similar scale. Each replacement requires 12 months of overlapping data to ensure consistent index movement.

The revamped IIP also introduces a seasonally adjusted series for the first time. This will help analysts identify true cyclical patterns in industrial output, aligning India with international statistical standards.

The overhaul follows an IMF review that graded India's national accounts data a "C," the second-lowest rating, citing an outdated base year and methodological weaknesses. The government says the new series will address these gaps.

Chief Economic Advisor V. Anantha Nageswaran noted that while the IMF flagged deficiencies, growth comparisons remain valid year-to-year. He emphasized that the median rating across all Indian statistics is "B," not "C."

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE NEW BASE YEAR FOR INDIA'S INDEX OF INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTION?
THE NEW BASE YEAR FOR THE INDEX OF INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTION WILL BE 2022-23, REPLACING THE CURRENT BASE YEAR OF 2011-12 THAT HAS BEEN IN USE FOR OVER A DECADE.
WHEN WILL THE NEW IIP SERIES BE RELEASED?
THE MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION WILL RELEASE THE REVISED IIP SERIES ON MAY 28, 2026.
WHAT NEW SECTORS WILL BE INCLUDED IN THE REVAMPED IIP?
FOR THE FIRST TIME, THE IIP WILL INCLUDE GAS SUPPLY, WATER SUPPLY, SEWERAGE, WASTE MANAGEMENT, AND MINOR MINERALS ALONGSIDE THE EXISTING MINING, MANUFACTURING, AND ELECTRICITY SECTORS.
WHAT PRODUCTS ARE BEING ADDED OR REMOVED FROM THE IIP BASKET?
OBSOLETE ITEMS LIKE FLUORESCENT TUBES, CFLS, KEROSENE, AND PRINTING MACHINERY ARE BEING REMOVED. NEW ADDITIONS INCLUDE LAPTOPS, VACCINES, LED BULBS, AND AEROSPACE COMPONENTS.
WHAT OTHER METHODOLOGICAL IMPROVEMENTS ARE BEING INTRODUCED?
THE NEW SERIES INTRODUCES SYSTEMATIC FACTORY SUBSTITUTION FOR NON-OPERATIONAL UNITS, A SEASONALLY ADJUSTED INDEX, REVISED ITEM WEIGHTS BASED ON ASI 2022-23 DATA, AND THE USE OF AI/ML FOR DATA SCRUTINY.
