Bitcoin Near $76K as $1.07B Crypto Outflows End Six-Week Inflow Streak

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bitcoin trades at $76,861 amid worsening macro conditions and $982 million in fund redemptions last week
  • 02/Crypto investment products saw $1.07 billion in net outflows, the third-largest weekly outflow of 2026, ending a six-week inflow streak
  • 03/Repeated rejection near the $82,000-$83,000 resistance zone keeps Bitcoin under pressure; markets eye US inflation data and Fed policy

Bitcoin traded at $76,861 on May 19, 2026. It hovered near the $76,000 mark amid worsening global macro conditions.

Crypto investment products recorded $1.07 billion in net outflows last week. This marked the third-largest weekly outflow of the year.

Bitcoin funds alone saw $982 million in redemptions. Ether products recorded $249 million in outflows, ending a six-week inflow streak.

The global crypto market capitalisation stood at $2.56 trillion. It rose 0.1% according to CoinMarketCap data.

Bitcoin declined 0.1% in the past 24 hours. Ethereum gained 0.5% to trade at $2,132.

Over the past week, Bitcoin and Ethereum fell 5% and 8% respectively. Major altcoins corrected up to 12%.

"Bitcoin is trading above the $76,000 range after facing sharp rejection from the $82,000 zone, as rising macro uncertainty and geopolitical tensions continue to pressure market sentiment." - Avinash Shekhar, Co-Founder & CEO, Pi42

Riya Sehgal, Research Analyst at Delta Exchange, noted Bitcoin slipped below $77,000 after rejection near $82,000-$83,000. This level aligns with the 200-day moving average.
