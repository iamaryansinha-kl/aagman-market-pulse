Rupee Drops 28 Paise to 94.77 Against US Dollar on US-Iran Deal News

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee depreciated 28 paise to open at 94.77 against the US dollar on May 7, 2026
  • 02/The rupee had gained 69 paise to close at 94.49 on May 6; Brent crude trades at $101.83
  • 03/FII outflows of ₹5,834.90 crore on May 6 and US-Iran peace deal talks weighed on sentiment

The rupee depreciated 28 paise to 94.77 against the U.S. dollar in early trade on May 7, 2026. The domestic currency opened weaker at the interbank foreign exchange market.

On Wednesday, May 6, the rupee had appreciated 69 paise to close at 94.49. The sharp reversal came on renewed geopolitical uncertainty.

Market sentiment turned negative after reports of a U.S.-Iran 14-point peace deal MOU emerged. Brent oil prices, which had fallen to $98 on the deal news, edged higher to $101.83 per barrel.

"Markets are currently focused on the critical 48-hour window during which the U.S. expects Tehran's formal response through Pakistani mediators," said CR Forex Advisors MD Amit Pabari.

U.S. President Donald Trump threatened more bombing if Iran doesn't reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Axios reported both sides are close to a one-page MOU to end the war.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹5,834.90 crore on May 6, according to exchange data. Unabated capital outflows further dented investor sentiment.

The dollar index was trading at 98.01, down 0.01%. Meanwhile, the Sensex fell 160.24 points to 77,798.28 and the Nifty declined 30.25 points to 24,300.70 in early trade.

On the macro front, India's goods and services exports rose 4.6% to a record $863.11 billion in FY2025-26. Merchandise exports grew 0.93% to $441.78 billion.
