Moody's Upgrades Vedanta Resources to Ba3 From B1, Keeps Positive Outlook

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Moody's upgrades Vedanta Resources corporate family rating to Ba3 from B1 on May 7, 2026, maintaining a positive outlook
  • 02/Fitch had upgraded Vedanta to BB- in April 2026; Moody's action follows $7B annual EBITDA forecast and 2.5x debt ratio
  • 03/Demerger effective May 1, 2026 streamlined structure; watch for further rating action and bond pricing

Moody's Ratings upgraded Vedanta Resources Ltd's corporate family rating to Ba3 from B1 on May 7, 2026. The agency maintained a positive outlook on the global mining and natural resources firm.

The upgrade also covers senior unsecured bonds issued by Vedanta Resources Finance II Plc. These bonds were raised from B2 to Ba3.

"The upgrade reflects improvements in VRL's earnings and cash flow, supported by higher production, further vertical integration in its aluminium operations, and favourable commodity prices," said Nidhi Dhruv, Vice President and Senior Credit Officer at Moody's Ratings.

Moody's expects Vedanta to generate approximately $7 billion of EBITDA annually. Gross debt to EBITDA is projected at around 2.5x over the next two years.

The group holds over $2 billion in committed multi-year credit facilities. This supports capital spending at operating companies and mitigates refinancing risks.

Vedanta's demerger became effective on May 1, 2026. The split created multiple sector-focused entities with independent growth strategies and capital allocation.

The holding company directly owns all operating subsidiaries except Hindustan Zinc. This structure enables diversified dividend cash flows covering interest expense by more than 2.0x.

Fitch Ratings had upgraded Vedanta Resources to BB- in April 2026. Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) closed at ₹294.65 on May 5, 2026.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT RATING ACTION DID MOODY'S TAKE ON VEDANTA RESOURCES?
MOODY'S UPGRADED VEDANTA RESOURCES LTD'S CORPORATE FAMILY RATING TO BA3 FROM B1 ON MAY 7, 2026. THE AGENCY MAINTAINED A POSITIVE OUTLOOK ON BOTH VEDANTA RESOURCES AND VEDANTA RESOURCES FINANCE II PLC.
WHAT DID MOODY'S SAY ABOUT VEDANTA'S FINANCIAL OUTLOOK?
MOODY'S EXPECTS VEDANTA TO GENERATE ABOUT USD 7 BILLION OF EBITDA ANNUALLY WITH GROSS DEBT TO EBITDA OF AROUND 2.5X OVER THE NEXT TWO YEARS. THE GROUP HAS OVER USD 2 BILLION IN AVAILABLE MULTI-YEAR COMMITTED CREDIT FACILITIES.
WHAT HAPPENED TO VEDANTA'S BOND RATINGS?
MOODY'S UPGRADED THE RATING ON SENIOR UNSECURED BONDS ISSUED BY VEDANTA RESOURCES FINANCE II PLC FROM B2 TO BA3. THESE BONDS ARE GUARANTEED BY VEDANTA RESOURCES LTD.
HOW DOES THE DEMERGER IMPACT THE RATING?
VEDANTA'S DEMERGER EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2026 RESULTED IN A MORE STREAMLINED ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE. THE HOLDING COMPANY HAS DIRECT OWNERSHIP OF ALL OPERATING SUBSIDIARIES EXCEPT HINDUSTAN ZINC, ENABLING DIVERSIFIED DIVIDEND CASH FLOWS COVERING INTEREST EXPENSE BY MORE THAN 2.0X.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS WATCH NEXT?
INVESTORS SHOULD MONITOR VEDANTA LTD (NSE: VEDL) SHARE PRICE MOVEMENT AND ANY FURTHER RATING ACTIONS FROM MOODY'S OR FITCH. FITCH HAD ALREADY UPGRADED VEDANTA TO BB- IN APRIL 2026, MAKING THIS THE SECOND MAJOR UPGRADE IN TWO MONTHS.
