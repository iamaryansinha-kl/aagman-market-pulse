Emami Acquires 60% Stake in Vedix, SkinKraft Parent for ₹321 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Emami Limited signs definitive agreement to acquire 60% stake in IncNut Digital for ₹321 crore, gaining control of personalised beauty brands Vedix and SkinKraft
  • 02/Deal includes performance-linked adjustments over 24 months, with remaining stake to be acquired in two tranches over 4.5 years at performance-tied valuations
  • 03/Emami stock trades at ₹456.35, up 0.92% for the day but down roughly 28% over the past year, underperforming the Nifty FMCG index

Emami Limited has signed a definitive agreement to acquire a 60 per cent stake in IncNut Digital Pvt. Ltd. The Hyderabad-based company is the parent of personalised beauty brands Vedix and SkinKraft.

The transaction is valued at ₹321 crore. It includes performance-linked adjustments spread over a 24-month period.

Emami will acquire the remaining stake in two further tranches over the next four and a half years. The valuations for these tranches will be tied to future performance metrics.

IncNut was founded in 2011 and built its business on data-driven diagnostics and customised formulations. Vedix applies Ayurvedic principles alongside data analytics to deliver personalised haircare solutions.

SkinKraft takes a dermatology-led approach to skincare and haircare. Both brands operate direct-to-consumer platforms and have built subscription-based customer bases with high repeat purchase rates.

For Emami, the acquisition adds science-led, personalised offerings to its beauty and personal care portfolio. The company already owns The Man Company and Brillare alongside legacy brands such as Navratna, BoroPlus, and Kesh King.

"The deal addresses a gap in the market where few players offer genuinely personalised, outcome-driven solutions," said Harsha Vardhan Agarwal, Vice Chairman and Managing Director of Emami. He noted that consumer demand is increasingly moving in that direction both in India and internationally.

IncNut founder and CEO Chaitanya Nallan said the partnership would help accelerate innovation and expand the brands' reach. He described it as a turning point for the company.

Emami's stock was trading at ₹456.35 on the NSE on Thursday, up 0.92 per cent for the day. The scrip remains down roughly 28 per cent over the past year.

The company carries a total market capitalisation of approximately ₹19,920 crore. It has underperformed the Nifty FMCG index across most timeframes.
