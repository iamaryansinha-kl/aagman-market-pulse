Prudent Corporate Launches Edge+ AI Platform for Mutual Fund Distributors

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Ltd launches Edge+ AI platform in beta for mutual fund distributors on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Company operates 143 locations across 21 states with over 35,000 partners and is among top 10 MF distributors by AUM
  • 03/Board meets May 7, 2026 to approve FY26 results; stock trades at Rs 2,826 with market cap of Rs 11,679 Cr

Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Ltd (NSE: PRUDENT) has launched Edge+, an AI-powered business management platform for mutual fund distributors. The Ahmedabad-headquartered company announced the beta release on May 4, 2026.

Edge+ integrates goal-based planning, business analytics, marketing automation and research tools into a single interface. Distributors can generate asset allocation strategies across equity, debt and hybrid instruments based on client risk profiles and investment horizons.

The platform enables age-wise and AUM-wise client segmentation for targeted engagement. It tracks SIP trends and provides visibility into external assets held by clients, allowing more precise outreach.

Edge+ supports cross-selling beyond mutual funds into insurance, broking, PMS, AIFs, fixed deposits, bonds and NPS. Built-in communication tools allow distributors to reach clients with ready-made messaging and run campaigns.

Prudent Corporate, founded in 2003, operates through 143 locations across 21 states. It serves over 35,000 distribution partners and ranks among the top 10 mutual fund distributors in India by assets under management and commissions earned.

The company reported Q3 FY26 revenue of Rs 343.2 crore, up 20.4% year-on-year. Net profit rose to Rs 57.6 crore from Rs 48.2 crore in the same quarter last year, reflecting steady operational growth.

Prudent Corporate shares closed at Rs 2,826 on April 30, 2026. The stock has a 52-week high of Rs 3,098 and a low of Rs 1,750. Market capitalization stands at Rs 11,679 crore as of May 1, 2026.

The board is scheduled to meet on May 7, 2026 to approve FY26 audited results and consider a final dividend. Edge+ is currently available in beta version exclusively to the company's distribution partners.
