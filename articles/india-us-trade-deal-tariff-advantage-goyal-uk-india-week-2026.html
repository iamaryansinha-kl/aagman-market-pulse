India Won't Sign US Deal Without Tariff Edge: Goyal at UK-India Week 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal states India will not sign the bilateral trade agreement with the US without a framework guaranteeing comparative tariff advantage for Indian exports
  • 02/India and the US are racing to conclude the interim trade pact before July 24, 2026, when Washington's temporary 10% tariff regime expires
  • 03/Goyal met US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer on June 22, with both sides reviewing market access, digital trade and supply chain progress

Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal has drawn a firm line on India-US trade negotiations. Speaking at UK-India Week 2026, he said India will not sign the proposed bilateral trade agreement until a framework guaranteeing comparative tariff advantage for Indian exports is finalised.

Goyal met US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer on June 22, 2026. The two sides reviewed progress on the interim trade pact covering market access, digital trade and supply chains.

Both countries are working to conclude an agreement before July 24, 2026. This is the deadline for the expiry of Washington's temporary 10 per cent tariff regime on trading partners.

The US had earlier reduced additional tariffs on Indian imports to 18 per cent from 50 per cent. Of the 50 per cent tariffs, 25 per cent imposed for India's crude oil purchases from Russia was already removed.

India has concluded nine FTAs in the past three and a half years. These agreements bring 38 countries under their ambit, including the UAE, Australia, EFTA, UK, Oman, New Zealand and Mauritius.

India-EU bilateral merchandise trade hit $136.53 billion in FY 2024-25. The EU is India's top goods partner, accounting for 17 per cent of exports.

India has rejected US claims of excess production capacity in sectors such as steel and textiles. Officials maintain that manufacturing levels should be viewed in the context of domestic demand and development needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID PIYUSH GOYAL SAY ABOUT THE INDIA-US TRADE DEAL AT UK-INDIA WEEK 2026?
UNION COMMERCE MINISTER PIYUSH GOYAL STATED THAT INDIA WILL NOT SIGN THE PROPOSED BILATERAL TRADE AGREEMENT WITH THE US UNTIL A FRAMEWORK GUARANTEEING A COMPARATIVE TARIFF ADVANTAGE FOR INDIAN EXPORTS IS FINALISED.
WHAT IS THE JULY 24, 2026 DEADLINE FOR THE INDIA-US TRADE DEAL?
JULY 24, 2026 IS THE EXPIRY DATE OF WASHINGTON'S TEMPORARY 10 PER CENT TARIFF REGIME ON TRADING PARTNERS. BOTH COUNTRIES ARE WORKING TO CONCLUDE THE INTERIM TRADE PACT BEFORE THIS DEADLINE.
WHAT AREAS DOES THE INTERIM INDIA-US TRADE PACT COVER?
THE INTERIM TRADE PACT COVERS MARKET ACCESS, DIGITAL TRADE AND SUPPLY CHAINS. OFFICIALS FROM BOTH SIDES HAVE ALSO DISCUSSED TRADE IN GOODS, CUSTOMS PROCEDURES, NON-TARIFF MEASURES AND TRADE FACILITATION.
WHAT TARIFF CHANGES HAS THE US ALREADY MADE FOR INDIA?
THE US REDUCED ADDITIONAL TARIFFS ON INDIAN IMPORTS TO 18 PER CENT FROM 50 PER CENT. THE 25 PER CENT TARIFF IMPOSED FOR INDIA'S CRUDE OIL PURCHASES FROM RUSSIA WAS ALREADY REMOVED.
HOW MANY FTAS HAS INDIA CONCLUDED RECENTLY?
INDIA HAS CONCLUDED NINE FREE TRADE AGREEMENTS IN THE PAST THREE AND A HALF YEARS, BRINGING 38 COUNTRIES UNDER THEIR AMBIT. THESE INCLUDE THE UAE, AUSTRALIA, EFTA, UK, OMAN, NEW ZEALAND AND MAURITIUS.
