Stocks to Watch Tuesday: Suzlon, Hyundai, TVS Motor, HCL Tech, TCS, JSW Steel Among Key Names

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //

Suzlon Energy

Suzlon Energy is on the radar ahead of its Q4 FY26 results expected in May 2026. Analysts estimate revenue between ₹3,400 crore and ₹3,700 crore, with net profit projected at ₹540–600 crore. The stock closed at ₹52.93 on the NSE on April 20, 2026, well below its 52-week high of ₹86 and above its low of ₹43.

Hyundai Motor India

Hyundai Motor India is in focus as South Korean President Lee Jae Myung visits India from April 19 to 21, 2026. It is the first state visit by a South Korean president in eight years. Hyundai is targeting an annual production capacity of 1.5 million cars in India and listed a unit on the Indian stock market in 2024.

TVS Motor Company

TVS Motor has declared an interim dividend of ₹5 per share for FY26, with the record date set for April 22, 2026. The company is also scheduled to announce its Q4 FY26 results on Tuesday. The stock trades with a face value of ₹1 per share.

HCL Technologies

HCL Technologies is set to declare its Q4 FY26 results on Tuesday, April 22, 2026. Investors will watch for margin trajectory and deal wins in the quarter. The stock has been a steady performer in the IT pack amid a volatile start to 2026.

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

TCS signed a memorandum of understanding with South Korean tech giant NAVER Corp on April 20, 2026, at the India–Korea Business Forum in New Delhi. The MoU was one of 16 agreements signed at the forum aimed at strengthening bilateral cooperation. TCS had already declared its Q4 FY26 results on April 9, 2026.

JSW Steel

JSW Steel's board approved a 50:50 joint venture with South Korea's POSCO Group on April 17, 2026. The JV will build a 6 million tonnes per annum integrated steel plant in Odisha through Saffron Resources Private Limited, a JSW subsidiary. POSCO Group will invest approximately ₹508.8 crore in Saffron, and the deal is targeted for completion by December 31, 2026.

Saffron already holds 887 acres in Odisha — 595 acres freehold and 292 acres leasehold. The project is expected to generate around 8,000 jobs. JSW Steel's stock ended higher by 2.76% at ₹1,274.50 on the NSE on April 20, 2026.

RailTel Corporation

RailTel Corporation of India secured a Letter of Acceptance from the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai for BMC Cloud Services. The estimated order value is ₹86,36,21,334 (including tax), with execution until June 5, 2029.

The PSU had also bagged a ₹608-crore order from Rail Vikas Nigam Limited on April 15, 2026. RailTel's market capitalisation stood at approximately ₹10,902 crore as of April 16, 2026.

Other Stocks in Focus

Eimco Elecon, Carysil, and Thomas Cook India are also expected to remain active during Tuesday's session. Broader market sentiment may remain cautious as Brent crude trades near $96 a barrel amid Middle East tensions.
