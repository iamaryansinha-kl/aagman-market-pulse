India's Biofortification Startups Hit ₹14 Cr as Hidden Hunger Costs 1.18% GDP

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Better Nutrition, India's first biofortified food brand, grew revenue from ₹80 lakh to ₹14 crore in one year while fighting a crisis where 67% of Indian children are anemic.
  • 02/The 2025 Global Hunger Index ranks India 102nd out of 123 countries with a "serious" score of 25.8, even as the nation exports surplus grain.
  • 03/Watch PM POSHAN's fortified rice rollout, Better Nutrition's ₹100 crore FY26-27 target, and whether biofortified seeds can reverse soil mineral depletion at scale.

India produces enough food to export. Godowns overflow. Yet NFHS-5 data shows 35.5% of children under five are stunted, 19.3% are wasted, and 32.1% are underweight. The nation won the calorie war. It is losing the nutrient battle.

This is "hidden hunger." 67% of children aged 6-59 months and 57% of women aged 15-49 are anemic. The World Bank estimates micronutrient deficiencies affect approximately 70% of India's population. The damage is quiet but systemic: lower immunity, stunted growth, reduced cognitive ability.

The economic toll is measurable. India loses up to 1.18% of GDP annually due to iron deficiency anemia alone. The 2025 Global Hunger Index ranks India 102nd out of 127 countries with a score of 25.8, classifying the situation as "serious." Roughly 74% of Indians cannot afford a healthy, nutrient-dense diet according to FAO data.

The fix is not more food. It is better food. Biofortification breeds crops inherently richer in iron, zinc, and protein. No supplements. No powders. The crop becomes the medicine. Biofortified wheat now exceeds 38 ppm iron and 40 ppm zinc, up from baseline levels of 28-32 ppm and 30-32 ppm respectively.

Better Nutrition, founded in 2023 by husband-wife duo Prateek Rastogi and Aishwarya Bhatnagar in Lucknow, is India's first biofortified food brand. It works with over 15,000 farmers and offers biofortified atta, rice, bajra, and ragi. Revenue surged from ₹80 lakh to ₹14 crore within a year. In April 2025, the company raised ₹10 crore in seed funding from investors including Namita Thapar, PV Sindhu, and Aclr8.vc.

The government's response is scaling too. PM POSHAN serves 450-calorie, 20-gram-protein meals to primary school children and 700-calorie, 40-gram-protein meals to upper primary students. Fortified rice enriched with iron, folic acid, and vitamin B12 is being distributed through the scheme. The target is a 3% annual reduction in anemia prevalence.

But soil is the bottleneck. Decades of intensive farming have stripped Indian agricultural land of the very minerals biofortified seeds are bred to deliver. If zinc is not in the soil, no crop can conjure it. Regenerative agriculture and smarter farming practices must accompany the seed revolution.

The consumer shift is already visible. Better Nutrition's monthly revenue hit ₹1 crore within six months of consumer launch. The group run rate crossed ₹2.5 crore with production at 150 tonnes per month. The company targets ₹100 crore revenue in FY26-27, a 5X year-on-year jump.

The Green Revolution filled stomachs. The Smart Nutrition Revolution must fill them properly. What happens in India's fields over the next decade could once again change the world. The roti has not changed. But its power is about to.
