Q4 Results May 7: Bajaj Auto Revenue Surges 32% to Record ₹16,006 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Auto reports 32% YoY revenue growth to record ₹16,006 crore for Q4 FY26, with shares hitting a 52-week high of ₹10,656
  • 02/Over 20 companies including Britannia Industries, Pidilite, Lupin, MRF, and Biocon announce Q4 FY26 results on May 7, 2026
  • 03/Bajaj Auto warns motorcycle industry growth could slow sharply to 7-9% in FY27 despite strong Pulsar and export demand

Bajaj Auto Ltd reported a 32 per cent year-on-year jump in revenue from operations. The company clocked a record ₹16,006 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

The auto maker's shares surged 3.2 per cent to hit a 52-week high of ₹10,656 on the NSE. At 9:26 am, the stock traded at ₹10,579.

The record revenue was driven by robust demand for Pulsar motorcycles, strong export performance, and growing electric vehicle sales. Profit figures for the quarter are awaited.

However, Bajaj Auto has warned that India's motorcycle industry growth could slow to 7-9 per cent in FY27. This signals potential headwinds despite the strong Q4 show.

Several other major companies are announcing Q4 FY26 results today. The list includes Britannia Industries, Pidilite Industries, Lupin, Bharat Forge, Dabur India, Biocon, and MRF.

ACME Solar Holdings, Shakti Pumps, CarTrade Tech, Thermax, and Coromandel International are also declaring their quarterly earnings on May 7.

Key stocks in focus include Paytm (One 97 Communications), KPIT Technologies, KPI Green Energy, and Brigade Enterprises. Investors are tracking their quarterly performance closely.

The Sensex rose 90.82 points or 0.12 per cent to 78,049.34 at 9:17 am. The Nifty 50 gained 46.85 points or 0.19 per cent to 24,377.80.
