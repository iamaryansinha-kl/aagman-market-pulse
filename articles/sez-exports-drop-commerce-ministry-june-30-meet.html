SEZ Exports Drop 22%: Commerce Ministry Convenes June 30 Stakeholders Meet

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce ministry to hold stakeholder meeting on June 30, 2026, to address SEZ reforms and harmonisation of export promotion schemes
  • 02/SEZ exports declined to USD 133.45 billion in 2025-26 from USD 172.07 billion in 2024-25, a drop of over 22%
  • 03/A 17-member committee is preparing a concept paper recommending a roadmap for a comprehensive SEZ 2.0 policy

The commerce ministry has called a stakeholder meeting on June 30, 2026, to discuss issues related to special economic zones (SEZs). The meeting will focus on harmonisation of export promotion schemes and SEZ reforms.

The deliberations will cover INR payment for SEZ services to the domestic tariff area (DTA). Job work by SEZ units for DTA without linkage to exports will also be discussed.

Import substitution and free trade warehousing zone reforms are on the agenda. Further promoting ease of doing business in these enclaves will be a key priority.

Total exports from SEZs declined to USD 133.45 billion in 2025-26 from USD 172.07 billion in 2024-25. This represents a drop of over 22 percent year-on-year.

There are 276 operational SEZs with 6,695 units across the country. These zones are treated as foreign territories for customs, trade, and import duty laws.

A 17-member committee is conducting a background study on harmonisation of export promotion schemes. It is examining SEZs, EoUs, MOOWR, Advance Authorisation, EPCG, and DFIA.

The committee will submit a concept paper recommending a roadmap for broad-based reforms to formulate a SEZ 2.0 policy. The move is critical as the SEZ law was framed in 2005 when global trade conditions were vastly different.
