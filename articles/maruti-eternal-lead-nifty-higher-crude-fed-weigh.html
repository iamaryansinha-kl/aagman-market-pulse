Maruti, Eternal Lead Nifty Higher; Crude Oil and Fed Weigh on Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki led Nifty gainers with a 3.31% surge to ₹13,319 on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Brent crude hovers near $110-113 per barrel and the Fed policy meeting concludes today
  • 03/Analysts warn gains may be short-lived as global headwinds and foreign outflows persist

Markets opened in positive territory on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The Sensex gained 358.78 points to trade at 77,245.69. The Nifty 50 rose 122.55 points to 24,118.25.

Maruti Suzuki led the Nifty gainers, rising 3.31% to ₹13,319 from ₹12,892. Volumes stood at 47,606 shares worth ₹6,291.33 lakh.

Eternal, Zomato's parent company, was the second biggest gainer. It rose 3.11% to ₹260.94 on heavy volumes of over 1.80 crore shares.

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles added 1.53% to ₹356.15. ONGC climbed 1.43% to ₹305.60, and BEL rose 1.24% to ₹441.15.

On the losing side, Hindalco fell 0.92% to ₹1,064.40. Asian Paints slipped 0.58% and ICICI Bank edged down 0.46%.

Gaurav Udani of ThinCredBlu Securities described markets as range-bound. "23,900–24,000 remains immediate support, while 24,200–24,300 acts as key resistance," he said.

Brent crude is trading around $110-113 per barrel. Dr. V.K. Vijayakumar of Geojit Investments warned that elevated crude is negative for India's growth and inflation outlook.

Foreign institutional investors offloaded ₹2,100 crore on Tuesday. Domestic institutional investors partially offset this with purchases of ₹1,712 crore. The rupee depreciated 35 paise to 94.54.
