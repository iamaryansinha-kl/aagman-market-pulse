MFs Raise ICICI Bank Stake to 32.42% as FPIs Trim Holdings in FY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Mutual fund holdings in ICICI Bank rose to 32.42% as of March 31, 2026, an increase of 256 basis points from 29.86% a year earlier
  • 02/FPI ownership declined to 43.68% from 45.82%, while insurance companies pared holdings to 10.08% from 11.25%
  • 03/Total institutional holding stands at 90.54%; the bank continues to operate with zero promoter holding

Domestic mutual funds increased their stake in ICICI Bank Ltd (NSE: ICICIBANK) to 32.42% as of March 31, 2026. The holding rose 256 basis points from 29.86% in March 2025, data filed with stock exchanges shows.

The increase underscores sustained domestic institutional confidence in the lender's performance and growth outlook. Mutual funds have been steady buyers through the financial year.

In contrast, foreign portfolio investors trimmed their exposure during the same period. FPI ownership declined to 43.68% from 45.82% a year earlier, reflecting caution among overseas investors.

Insurance companies also reduced their holdings. Their combined stake, including Life Insurance Corporation of India, fell to 10.08% from 11.25% in March 2025.

SBI Mutual Fund held 5.50% of ICICI Bank as of March 2026. ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund owned 4.40%, while HDFC Mutual Fund held 3.47%.

Nippon Life India Mutual Fund and UTI Mutual Fund held 2.39% and 2.13% respectively. A total of 63 mutual fund schemes held positions in the stock.

The number of FPI investors in ICICI Bank fell to 2,142 from 2,208 in the previous quarter. This points to concentrated selling by some foreign accounts.

Total institutional holding, covering both foreign and domestic investors, stood at 90.54% as of March-end. The bank maintains a widely held structure with zero promoter stake.
