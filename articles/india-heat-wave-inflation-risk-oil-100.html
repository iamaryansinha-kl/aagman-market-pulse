India Heat Wave Threatens 5% Inflation as Oil Surges Above $100

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Heat waves and erratic monsoon may push India inflation above 5% in FY2026, exceeding RBI's 4.6% target
  • 02/Crude oil trades above $100/barrel due to Iran war; food has 37% weight in India's CPI basket
  • 03/RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra signals prolonged pause; economists warn of possible tightening pivot

India is facing inflation threats from heat waves and below-normal rainfall this year. Temperatures have hit 47°C in parts of northern India, driving power demand to a record high.

The government predicts below-normal monsoon rains between June and September. This season is crucial for farming activity across the country.

For most of last year, inflation stayed below the RBI's 4 per cent target. Declining vegetable prices kept price pressures in check.

This year's adverse weather will likely push inflation above 5 per cent in FY2026. That exceeds the RBI's projection of 4.6 per cent, according to economists.

Food has a weight of 37 per cent in the consumer price index. Although revised down from 46 per cent, it remains the largest inflation category.

More than 60 per cent of India's population lives in rural areas. Poor harvests could hit incomes and curb demand, weighing on economic growth.

Crude oil trades above $100 a barrel due to the ongoing Iran war. Farmers may need diesel-powered irrigation, raising costs further.

Bloomberg Economics's Abhishek Gupta sees inflation reaching 5.8 per cent if monsoon rains fall below normal. In 2023, a 5.4 per cent rainfall deficit pushed food inflation to 8 per cent.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW HIGH COULD INDIA'S INFLATION RISE IN FY2026?
ECONOMISTS EXPECT INFLATION TO AVERAGE CLOSE TO 5 PER CENT IN THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING APRIL 1, 2026. BLOOMBERG ECONOMICS'S ABHISHEK GUPTA SEES INFLATION REACHING 5.8 PER CENT IF MONSOON RAINS ARE BELOW NORMAL.
WHAT IS CAUSING THE INFLATION RISK?
INDIA IS FACING A COMBINATION OF SEVERE HEAT WAVES WITH TEMPERATURES UP TO 47°C AND BELOW-NORMAL MONSOON RAINS. CRUDE OIL IS ALSO TRADING ABOVE $100 PER BARREL DUE TO THE ONGOING IRAN WAR, RAISING ENERGY AND AGRICULTURAL INPUT COSTS.
WHAT IS THE WEIGHT OF FOOD IN INDIA'S CPI BASKET?
FOOD HAS A WEIGHT OF 37 PER CENT IN THE CONSUMER PRICE INDEX. ALTHOUGH REVISED DOWN FROM 46 PER CENT, IT REMAINS THE LARGEST CATEGORY IN THE INFLATION BASKET.
WHAT IS THE RBI'S CURRENT STANCE ON MONETARY POLICY?
RBI GOVERNOR SANJAY MALHOTRA SIGNALLED EARLIER THIS MONTH THAT THE CENTRAL BANK WILL STAY ON HOLD FOR LONGER. DEPUTY GOVERNOR POONAM GUPTA SAID THE RBI'S INFLATION PROJECTIONS ALREADY FACTOR IN A 7-9 PER CENT RAINFALL DEFICIT.
WHAT HAPPENED DURING THE LAST BELOW-NORMAL MONSOON IN 2023?
IN 2023, WHEN RAINFALL WAS 5.4 PER CENT BELOW NORMAL, CROP PRODUCTION FELL 3.5 PER CENT AND AVERAGE FOOD INFLATION SURGED TO 8 PER CENT.
