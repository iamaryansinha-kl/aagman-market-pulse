European Shares Rally on Micron, Qualcomm AI Forecasts; Tech Leads Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/European STOXX 600 rises 0.27% to 636.88 as strong Micron and Qualcomm forecasts revive the AI rally
  • 02/European tech stocks up 1.7%, led by Infineon +5.2% and STMicroelectronics +3.7%; sector up 30% this quarter
  • 03/Falling oil prices and easyJet rejecting a fourth takeover offer from Castlelake add to market sentiment

European shares opened higher on Thursday. The pan-European STOXX 600 index gained 0.27% to reach 636.88 points by 0711 GMT.

Technology stocks led the rally. Strong forecasts from US chipmakers Micron and Qualcomm eased fears about inflated AI valuations.

European tech stocks rose 1.7% on the day. The sector has already surged 30% this quarter.

Chipmaker Infineon gained 5.2%. STMicroelectronics added 3.7%.

Semiconductor equipment suppliers BE Semiconductor and ASML climbed more than 3.5% each. AI-equipment maker Siemens Energy rose 1%.

Falling oil prices provided additional support. More stranded oil tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz, easing supply concerns.

British budget carrier easyJet shares jumped 5.5%. The airline rejected a fourth takeover offer from US investment firm Castlelake.

Swedish retailer H&M fell 1.2% after reporting second-quarter operating profit below estimates. The broader retail sector was up 0.4%.

The AI rally has drawn investor scrutiny over valuations. The Micron and Qualcomm forecasts temporarily eased those concerns.
