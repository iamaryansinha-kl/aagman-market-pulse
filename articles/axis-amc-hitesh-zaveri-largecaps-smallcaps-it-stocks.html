Axis AMC's Hitesh Zaveri on Largecaps vs Smallcaps, IT Stocks Outlook

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Axis AMC's Hitesh Zaveri says market consolidation offers opportunities to accumulate quality businesses
  • 02/He discusses largecap versus smallcap allocation and IT sector positioning for investors
  • 03/Portfolio de-risking should align with individual risk appetite and long-term investment strategy

Axis AMC's Hitesh Zaveri believes current market consolidation should be viewed as an accumulation opportunity. Investors with appropriate risk appetite can build positions in quality businesses at reasonable valuations.

He weighs in on the ongoing largecap versus smallcap debate. Largecaps offer relative stability and lower volatility during uncertain phases. Smallcaps, while riskier, can deliver outsized returns in a broad-based rally.

Zaveri also shares his perspective on IT stocks. The sector has faced headwinds from global demand slowdown and margin pressures. Selective allocation within IT may be warranted based on individual portfolio positioning.

Portfolio de-risking remains a key theme in his outlook. He emphasizes that any rebalancing must align with an investor's specific risk appetite and investment horizon. One-size-fits-all approaches rarely work in volatile markets.

Market consolidation phases often separate quality businesses from speculative names. Zaveri's advice centers on using this period to strengthen core portfolio holdings. Patience and discipline matter more than timing the perfect entry.

Investors should review their asset mix rather than panic-exiting positions. A structured approach to de-risking can protect capital without missing eventual recovery upside.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS AXIS AMC'S HITESH ZAVERI'S VIEW ON CURRENT MARKET CONDITIONS?
ZAVERI VIEWS PERIODS OF MARKET CONSOLIDATION AS OPPORTUNITIES TO ACCUMULATE QUALITY BUSINESSES RATHER THAN REASONS FOR CONCERN. HE ADVISES INVESTORS TO USE SUCH PHASES TO STRENGTHEN THEIR CORE PORTFOLIO HOLDINGS.
HOW DOES HE COMPARE LARGE CAPS VERSUS SMALL CAPS?
ZAVERI ACKNOWLEDGES THAT LARGE CAPS OFFER RELATIVE STABILITY AND LOWER VOLATILITY DURING UNCERTAIN PHASES. SMALL CAPS CARRY HIGHER RISK BUT CAN DELIVER OUTSIZED RETURNS IN A BROAD-BASED MARKET RALLY. ALLOCATION SHOULD DEPEND ON INDIVIDUAL RISK APPETITE.
WHAT DOES HE SAY ABOUT IT STOCKS?
HE DISCUSSES IT SECTOR POSITIONING, NOTING THE INDUSTRY HAS FACED HEADWINDS FROM GLOBAL DEMAND SLOWDOWN AND MARGIN PRESSURES. INVESTORS MAY CONSIDER SELECTIVE ALLOCATION WITHIN IT BASED ON THEIR EXISTING PORTFOLIO WEIGHTS.
WHAT IS HIS ADVICE ON PORTFOLIO DE-RISKING?
ZAVERI EMPHASIZES THAT DE-RISKING MUST ALIGN WITH AN INVESTOR'S SPECIFIC RISK APPETITE AND INVESTMENT HORIZON. HE CAUTIONS AGAINST PANIC-EXITING AND RECOMMENDS A STRUCTURED, DISCIPLINED APPROACH TO REBALANCING.
WHAT SHOULD INVESTORS DO DURING CONSOLIDATION PHASES?
INVESTORS SHOULD REVIEW THEIR ASSET MIX, ACCUMULATE QUALITY BUSINESSES AT REASONABLE VALUATIONS, AND AVOID SPECULATIVE NAMES. PATIENCE AND DISCIPLINE ARE MORE IMPORTANT THAN ATTEMPTING TO TIME THE PERFECT MARKET ENTRY.
