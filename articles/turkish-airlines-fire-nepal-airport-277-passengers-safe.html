Turkish Airlines Jet Catches Fire at Nepal Airport: 277 Passengers Safe

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Turkish Airlines Airbus A330 jet caught fire on its right landing gear while landing at Kathmandu's Tribhuvan International Airport on May 11, 2026
  • 02/All 277 passengers and crew were evacuated safely with zero injuries; the airport was shut down and several inbound flights put on hold
  • 03/Turkish Airlines shares (BIST: THYAO) trade near TRY 315; this is the second serious incident on the Istanbul-Kathmandu route since March 2015

A Turkish Airlines Airbus A330 jet caught fire while landing at Kathmandu's Tribhuvan International Airport on Monday, May 11, 2026. The aircraft was arriving from Istanbul with 277 passengers on board. Fire and smoke emerged from the right landing gear during touchdown.

Emergency crews responded immediately and controlled the fire. All passengers were evacuated safely via emergency slides. No injuries were reported, according to airport officials.

Tribhuvan International Airport was shut down on Monday morning. Several inbound flights were put on hold while officials investigated the incident and attempted to clear the only available runway.

This is the second serious incident on the Istanbul-Kathmandu route in over a decade. In March 2015, another Turkish Airlines A330 skidded off the runway in dense fog. The nose gear collapsed and the aircraft was written off.

Turkish Airlines operates the Istanbul-Kathmandu route three to four times per week with Airbus A330-300 aircraft. The carrier has 37 A330-300s in its fleet, each configured for 289 seats in a two-class layout.

Turkish Airlines shares (BIST: THYAO) closed at TRY 314.75 on May 8, 2026. The stock has gained 20% year-to-date. Analysts will watch for any operational impact on the airline's Asia-Pacific network.

Nepal's mountainous terrain and variable weather make flying conditions challenging. The country has a history of aviation incidents due to these geographic factors.
