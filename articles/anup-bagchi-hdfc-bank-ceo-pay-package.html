Anup Bagchi HDFC Bank CEO Pay: Rs 35.9 Crore, India's Highest

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HDFC Bank has proposed an annual package of up to Rs 35.9 crore for incoming MD & CEO Anup Bagchi, making him India's highest-paid bank CEO on disclosed pay structures
  • 02/The package pairs Rs 8.97 crore fixed pay with variable compensation capped at 3x, and marks a roughly 90% jump from Bagchi's estimated Rs 18.9 crore FY26 pay at ICICI Prudential Life
  • 03/RBI has cleared the proposal and e-voting runs October 8 to November 6, 2026 — even as the bank navigates post-merger integration, a 25-bps repo hike, and a stock down about 9% in a year

HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK) has proposed an annual remuneration package of up to Rs 35.9 crore for incoming Managing Director & CEO Anup Bagchi. If shareholders approve, he becomes India's highest-paid bank CEO among leading private-sector lenders.

The Reserve Bank of India has already cleared the proposal. It now goes to shareholders through a postal ballot, with e-voting open from October 8 to November 6, 2026.

The structure pairs fixed pay of Rs 8.97 crore with performance-linked variable compensation of up to Rs 26.92 crore. That variable component is capped at three times the fixed salary.

The fixed portion includes a basic salary of Rs 3.32 crore, housing of Rs 1.66 crore, and a car allowance of Rs 54 lakh. It also covers other allowances, retirement benefits, and perquisites.

Of the variable pay, Rs 8.88 crore comes as cash, with half of that cash incentive deferred over three years. The remaining Rs 18.04 crore is delivered through employee stock options valued at Rs 253.42 per option under the Black-Scholes model, vesting over four years.

Bagchi also receives a one-time joining bonus of Rs 7.35 crore in stock-linked incentives. This comprises RSUs worth Rs 3.19 crore and ESOPs valued at Rs 4.16 crore, both vesting over four years.

The jump is steep. At ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, Bagchi's FY26 package was estimated at around Rs 18.9 crore including stock options, so the HDFC Bank offer is roughly a 90% increase.

For context, outgoing HDFC Bank CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan received Rs 28.65 crore in FY26 statutory returns, including Rs 14.71 crore gross salary and Rs 13.53 crore in stock options. Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha got Rs 26.32 crore, Kotak Mahindra Bank's Ashok Vaswani Rs 17.24 crore, and ICICI Bank's Sandeep Bakhshi roughly Rs 10.64 crore. One caveat: stock-option benefits for Jagdishan and Bharucha are measured differently from Bagchi's proposed grant-date valuations.

The timing is notable. The pay deal lands while HDFC Bank is still absorbing its merger with parent HDFC Ltd, adjusting to a 25-basis-point repo rate hike, and watching its stock slide around 9% over the past year.

The design itself signals the board's intent. Variable pay at 3x fixed, four-year vesting, and deferred cash tie Bagchi's rewards to multi-year performance — effectively stretching his incentives across the post-merger integration window rather than rewarding a single year's numbers.
