Centre Approves Procurement of Copra, Totapuri Mangoes from Tamil Nadu

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The Centre has approved fresh procurement from Tamil Nadu.

The government will procure copra and Totapuri mangoes from the state as part of its price support programme. The decision aims to ensure farmers receive fair prices for their produce and prevent distress sales during peak harvest season.

Copra, the dried kernel of coconut, is a key agricultural commodity in Tamil Nadu. The state is among India's largest coconut producers, and the procurement will provide a price floor for coconut growers who have faced volatile market prices.

Totapuri mangoes are a popular variety used for processing into pulp, juice, and other products. Tamil Nadu is a significant producer of this mango variety, and the procurement will support both fresh fruit sellers and processing units.

The procurement will be carried out by designated government agencies at minimum support prices. Farmers who struggle to get fair prices in the open market can sell to these agencies, ensuring a safety net against price crashes.

The decision comes ahead of the peak harvest season for both commodities. Timely procurement announcements help farmers plan their sales and reduce uncertainty about income.

The government's price support programmes cover a range of agricultural commodities. While cereals like wheat and rice receive the most attention, pulses, oilseeds, and horticultural products are increasingly included in procurement operations.
