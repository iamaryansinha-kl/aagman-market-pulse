Nifty Holds 24,000 As Sensex Gains 109 Points: 10 Key Market Triggers For Monday

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian markets closed flat Thursday after a volatile monthly expiry session, with the Sensex gaining 109 points to 77,100.47 and the Nifty rising 34 points to 24,056
  • 02/US stocks ended slightly lower Friday while European shares pulled back 0.7% from record highs; the Dow headed for a weekly gain but S&P 500 and Nasdaq posted weekly losses
  • 03/Analysts expect range-bound trade ahead with Nifty support at 23,800 and resistance near 24,500; FII outflows and monsoon progress remain key monitors

Indian stock markets closed marginally higher on Thursday after a volatile monthly expiry session. The Sensex gained 109 points to close at 77,100.47, while the Nifty 50 rose 34 points to end at 24,056. Both benchmarks had surged over 1% in afternoon trade before paring gains. Markets were shut on Friday for Muharram.

"The domestic market concluded the day flat as early intraday gains were offset by profit booking. While a sharp decline in crude oil prices supported the rupee and provided some cushion, it was insufficient to sustain upward momentum," said Vinod Nair, Head of Research at Geojit Investments. Auto stocks outperformed, driven by softer metal prices and improving retail demand.

On Wall Street, all three major indexes finished slightly lower in choppy trading Friday. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq posted weekly losses, while the Dow was headed for a weekly gain. Losses in industrials, technology and energy offset gains in healthcare and real estate stocks. European shares pulled back 0.7% from record highs, though the STOXX 600 narrowly marked gains for the week.

"The Nifty remained volatile during the session amid the BSE F&O expiry on Thursday, as the index failed to break out above the falling trendline on the daily timeframe. However, the overall trend continues to remain positive, with the index sustaining above the 50 EMA," said Rupak De, Senior Technical Analyst at LKP Securities. He sees Nifty support at 23,800 and resistance at 24,500.

HDFC Bank (₹3,734 crore), IndiGo (₹2,786 crore), and ICICI Bank (₹2,388 crore) were the most active stocks by value on the NSE. Vodafone Idea led volume trading with 34.52 crore shares, followed by Motherson Sumi Wiring India at 22.79 crore shares. Motherson Sumi Wiring India, Aegis Logistics, and M&M Financial saw strong buying interest.

Stocks hitting 52-week highs included Aegis Logistics, Motherson Sumi, Oracle, Welspun Corp, Emcure Pharmaceuticals, RR Kabel, and Radico Khaitan. Birlasoft was the only stock to hit a 52-week low. The market breadth was negative with 1,231 stocks advancing and 2,069 declining out of 3,418 traded.

Analysts expect sentiment to be influenced by a muted Q1 earnings outlook and an uneven monsoon in the near term. Continued FII outflows could limit upside, while a sharp decline in crude oil prices may provide some relief to the rupee and import-dependent sectors.
