Noida International Airport Appoints Nitu Samra as Interim CEO

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Noida International Airport appoints Nitu Samra as interim CEO effective April 24, 2026.
  • 02/Christoph Schnellmann steps down as CEO after serving since August 2020, moves to Board as Executive Vice Chairman.
  • 03/Leadership transition comes as the airport continues operational expansion in the Delhi NCR region.

Noida International Airport has appointed Nitu Samra as its interim Chief Executive Officer. The appointment was announced on April 24, 2026.

Christoph Schnellmann will step down from the CEO role. He has led the airport since August 2020, a tenure of nearly six years.

Schnellmann will join the Board of Directors as Executive Vice Chairman. This move keeps him involved in strategic decisions at the airport.

Nitu Samra takes charge during a critical phase for the airport. The facility serves the Delhi NCR region as a major aviation hub.

The leadership change represents a significant transition for the young airport. Operational expansion and route development remain ongoing priorities.

The airport continues to establish itself in India's competitive aviation market. It operates alongside Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport.
