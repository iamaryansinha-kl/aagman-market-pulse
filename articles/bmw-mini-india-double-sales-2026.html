BMW MINI India Targets Doubling Sales in 2026 With 10 New Models

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BMW MINI India targets doubling sales in 2026 after selling 730 units in 2025 and 213 units in Q1 2026, up 42% YoY
  • 02/The company plans 10 new models, expansion to 22 touchpoints from 13, and local production of MINI Countryman
  • 03/Petrol models now contribute 70% of sales, up from 61% last year; eight new Tier-2 cities to be added

BMW Group India aims to double MINI sales in 2026. The brand sold 730 units in 2025 and 213 units in Q1 2026, up 42% year-on-year.

Hardeep Singh Brar, President and CEO of BMW Group India, outlined the plan to PTI. He called 2026 "the best ever year for MINI" if targets are met.

The strategy rests on three pillars: wider product portfolio, localisation, and retail expansion. One new product and nine special editions are planned for the year.

The locally produced MINI Countryman will launch mid-2026. Brar called it "a pivotal enabler to scale volumes and improve accessibility."

Petrol models now account for 70% of MINI sales, up from 61% in 2025. The launch of the locally produced Countryman is expected to maintain this momentum.

MINI will expand from 13 to 22 touchpoints by year-end, entering eight new cities. Jaipur and Jodhpur are already operational.

Six more cities will be added: Lucknow, Ranchi, Guwahati, Surat, Vijayawada, and Cuttack. This targets demand beyond metro markets.

Aftersales service is available at 52 BMW Group touchpoints across 40 cities. Brar said MINI acts as a "brand shaper" for the group.
