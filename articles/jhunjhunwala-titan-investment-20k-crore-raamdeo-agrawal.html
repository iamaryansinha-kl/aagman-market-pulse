Jhunjhunwala's Titan Bet: How He Turned ₹30/Share Into ₹20,250 Crore Fortune

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rakesh Jhunjhunwala accumulated Titan shares at ₹30-₹35 during the 2001-2003 downturn when the stock hit an 8-year low of ₹29
  • 02/Raamdeo Agrawal reveals Jhunjhunwala's "natural gift" for spotting bargains while others sold; Titan's jewellery segment grew 30% even in those troubled years
  • 03/As of March 31, 2026, the Jhunjhunwala family holds a 5% stake in Titan worth approximately ₹20,250 crore; Titan shares now trade near ₹4,300

Rakesh Jhunjhunwala bought Titan Company shares when the stock had crashed to an 8-year low of ₹29. He accumulated shares at prices between ₹30 and ₹35 per share during 2001-2003.

Raamdeo Agrawal, Chairman of Motilal Oswal Group, recalled Jhunjhunwala's legendary contrarian instinct in a podcast. "Not because he was a CA. It was a natural trait, a gift that allowed him to sense or smell bargains," Agrawal said.

Titan was bleeding during that period. The company's profit after tax collapsed from ₹23.48 crore in FY2001 to just ₹6.21 crore in FY2003. A management-union dispute had led to a lockout at the key Hosur factory.

External headwinds compounded the pain. Gold prices were rising, competition was intensifying, and consumer demand was weak. The market had written Titan off.

But Jhunjhunwala saw something others missed. Even during those difficult years, Titan's jewellery business grew 30%. Its Tanishq brand had opened 52 stores across India and was rolling out innovative customer retention schemes.

Agrawal admitted his own mistake: he sold Titan shares at the same time Jhunjhunwala was buying. "Many people had bought Titan shares during the early days, but hardly anyone held on to them for 20 years," Agrawal noted.

Jhunjhunwala held on as Titan transformed from a watchmaker into India's dominant jewellery player. As of March 31, 2026, his family owns around 5% of the company.

That 5% stake is now worth approximately ₹20,250 crore. Titan shares currently trade near ₹4,300 on the NSE (NSE: TITAN), representing a roughly 130-fold return from his average entry price.

The stock has gained 23% over the past year and trades at a market capitalisation of over ₹3.8 trillion. Analysts maintain a 12-month price target of ₹4,200-₹5,600 for the counter.

Jhunjhunwala, who entered the stock market in 1985 with just ₹5,000, passed away in August 2022 at the age of 62. His Titan bet remains one of the most celebrated long-term investment stories in Indian market history.
