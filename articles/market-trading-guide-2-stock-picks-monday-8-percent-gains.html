Market Trading Guide: 2 Stock Picks For Monday With Up To 8% Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty broke below 23,800 on Friday as banking, metal and realty stocks dragged the index lower, ending a two-session winning streak
  • 02/Kirloskar Oil Engines and Wheels India flagged as technical buys with upside targets of ₹1,825 and ₹1,580 respectively
  • 03/Nifty faces resistance at 23,800 and support at 23,500; a breach below could drag the index towards 23,150

Nifty closed lower on Friday, breaking a two-session winning streak. Banking, metal and realty stocks dragged the index below the 23,800 zone.

Rupak De, Senior Technical Analyst at LKP Securities, sees a bearish setup on the daily chart. Nifty is forming lower tops with resistance at the 20-EMA daily and 50-EMA weekly.

India VIX surged nearly 9% from the day's low as Brent crude broke out. The RSI remains in a bearish crossover and continues to decline.

De warns that a breach below 23,500 could drag Nifty towards 23,150. On the upside, 23,800 is expected to act as a key short-term resistance level.

Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited (NSE: KIRLOSENG) broke out above the ₹1,680 resistance zone with a sharp bullish candle and robust volumes. The stock trades above all major EMAs with rebounding RSI.

Buy Kirloskar Oil at ₹1,740–1,750 for a near-term upside target of ₹1,825. Keep the stop loss at ₹1,680, the breakout zone.

Wheels India witnessed a strong bullish breakout supported by exceptional volume expansion. The stock is trading in lifetime-high territory and sustains above all key EMAs.

Buy Wheels India at around ₹1,467 for an upside target of ₹1,580. Place the stop loss at ₹1,410. The stock could extend towards ₹1,650.

Both picks were recommended by Virat Jagad, Sr. Technical Research Analyst at Bonanza Portfolio. He suggests a buy-on-dips approach for both counters.
