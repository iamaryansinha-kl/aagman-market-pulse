Sensex Surges Past 77,900; Maruti, Tech Mahindra, ITC Lead Midday Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex trades at 77,948.90, up 1,061.99 points or 1.38% as of 12:52 pm on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Nifty 50 breaches key 24,200-24,300 resistance zone, gains 327.30 points to 24,323
  • 03/Federal Reserve policy decision and state election exit polls due later today could cap aggressive bets

The Sensex surged to 77,948.90 by midday on April 29, 2026. It gained 1,061.99 points or 1.38 per cent from the previous close.

The Nifty 50 stood at 24,323, up 327.30 points or 1.36 per cent. It breached the key 24,200-24,300 resistance zone that analysts had flagged earlier in the session.

Market breadth was firmly positive on the BSE. A total of 2,486 stocks advanced against 1,575 declines and 169 unchanged out of 4,230 stocks traded.

A total of 144 stocks hit 52-week highs against 21 at 52-week lows. Meanwhile, 167 stocks were locked in upper circuits compared to 122 in lower circuits.

Maruti Suzuki was the standout Nifty performer, extending its morning gains. The stock traded up 4.68 per cent at ₹13,495 with volumes surging to 5,24,817 shares worth ₹70,523.12 lakh.

Tech Mahindra rose 3.69 per cent to ₹1,460 on volumes of 15,73,945 shares. Coal India climbed 3.50 per cent to ₹483.35 on heavy volumes of 1,38,95,069 shares, while ITC advanced 3.32 per cent to ₹314.55.

Eicher Motors gained 3.06 per cent to ₹7,288. On the losing side, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories fell 0.96 per cent to ₹1,341.60, making it the top Nifty laggard.

The rally spanned automobiles, IT, energy, FMCG, and two-wheeler sectors. However, the Federal Reserve policy decision due later today and state election exit polls expected this evening could cap further aggressive directional bets.
