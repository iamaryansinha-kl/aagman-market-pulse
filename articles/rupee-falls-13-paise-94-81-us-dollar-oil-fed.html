Rupee Falls 13 Paise to 94.81 Against US Dollar on Oil, Fed Jitters

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The Indian rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early Mumbai trade on April 29, 2026.
  • 02/Rising crude oil prices and month-end dollar demand dented sentiment, while traders await the US Federal Reserve policy tone.
  • 03/The rupee has weakened approximately 11.5% over the past year from around 85.03, reflecting sustained import and capital flow pressures.

The rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The currency opened at 94.79 before slipping to the day's low of 94.81.

Forex traders said rising global crude oil prices kept the rupee under sustained pressure. India imports over 80% of its oil needs, making the currency highly sensitive to energy costs.

Elevated month-end dollar demand from importers added to the weakness. A broader shift toward safe-haven assets also dented risk appetite in emerging market currencies.

Market attention has now shifted to the upcoming US Federal Reserve policy decision. While rates are expected to remain unchanged, the tone will carry more weight than the action, Forex traders said.

The rupee has depreciated roughly 11.5% over the past twelve months. It traded near 85.03 against the dollar in April 2025, according to market data.

Weakness in the domestic currency raises import costs for India. It also increases the rupee value of foreign debt held by Indian companies, adding balance sheet pressure.

However, a weaker rupee benefits export-oriented sectors such as IT and textiles. Software services firms earn a significant portion of revenue in dollars, cushioning the currency impact.
