Gokaldas Exports Q4 FY26: Net Profit Rises 146% to ₹36 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gokaldas Exports Q4 FY26 net profit surges 146% sequentially to ₹36 crore, up from ₹15 crore in Q3 FY26
  • 02/Consolidated revenue rises 9% QoQ to ₹1,087 crore, driven by Africa recovery and steady India operations
  • 03/Full-year FY26 revenue up 4% to ₹4,065 crore; investors should watch US tariff impact on FY27 margins

Gokaldas Exports Ltd reported a net profit of ₹36 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 146% sequential increase from ₹15 crore in Q3 FY26.

The company posted consolidated total income of ₹1,087 crore. This represents a 9% quarter-on-quarter rise from ₹998 crore in Q3 FY26.

On a year-on-year basis, profit declined from ₹53 crore in Q4 FY25. Total income grew 5% over the ₹1,035 crore recorded a year earlier.

EBITDA surged 40% sequentially to ₹135 crore from ₹96 crore in Q3 FY26. Margins expanded by 275 basis points to 12.4%, supported by productivity gains and tighter cost controls.

For the full year FY26, total income rose 4% to ₹4,065 crore. EBITDA increased 2% to ₹434 crore, with the margin at 10.7%.

India operations grew 2% year-on-year despite tariff headwinds. Africa operations jumped 17% year-on-year following AGOA renewal and favourable tariff treatment.

"Disruption due to penal US tariffs and volatile geopolitical events impacted our costs and margin during the year. Exceptional teamwork and relentless execution helped us deliver superior performance," said Sivaramakrishnan Ganapathi, vice chairman and managing director.

Apparel exports from India declined 10% during the quarter. The sector continues to navigate uncertainty from US reciprocal tariffs announced in April 2025.
