Yes Bank Q4 FY26: Net Profit Jumps 45% to ₹1,068 Cr, NPAs Hit Multi-Year Low

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Yes Bank Q4 FY26 standalone net profit surged 44.7% YoY to ₹1,068 crore, its highest quarterly profit since reconstruction
  • 02/NII grew 16% to ₹2,638 crore with NIM expanding 20 bps to 2.7%; asset quality improved with GNPA at 1.3% and NNPA at 0.2%
  • 03/No dividend declared; brokerages remain cautious with 8 of 12 analysts rating the stock a 'Sell'

Yes Bank Ltd (NSE: YESBANK) reported standalone net profit of ₹1,068 crore for Q4 FY26, up 44.7% from ₹738 crore in Q4 FY25. The board met on April 18, 2026 to approve the results.

Net interest income rose 15.9% YoY to ₹2,638 crore from ₹2,276 crore in the year-ago quarter. Net interest margin improved 20 bps to 2.7%, aided by a 60 bps drop in cost of deposits to 5.5%.

Asset quality hit multi-year lows. Gross NPA ratio fell to 1.3%, down 30 bps YoY and 20 bps QoQ. Net NPA improved to 0.2%, down 10 bps both YoY and sequentially.

Credit costs stayed contained at 0.17% of average assets, versus 0.30% in Q4 FY25. Provisions dropped sharply to ₹187.55 crore from ₹318.07 crore in the same quarter last year.

Deposits crossed the ₹3 lakh crore milestone to reach ₹3,18,969 crore, up 12.1% YoY. CASA deposits crossed ₹1 lakh crore at ₹1,11,959 crore, pushing the CASA ratio to 35.1%.

Net advances grew 11.1% YoY to ₹2,73,445 crore. Operating profit rose 23.1% to ₹1,618 crore. Return on assets hit 1.0% for the first time since the bank's 2020 reconstruction.

"YES BANK concluded FY26 on a strong footing, delivering a Q4 RoA of 1.0% in line with our guidance, supported by a 20 bps improvement in NIMs." - Vinay M. Tonse, Managing Director & CEO.

Brokerages remain divided despite the strong numbers. Nomura has a 'Neutral' rating with a ₹21 target. Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan both have 'Underweight' ratings at ₹15 and ₹18 respectively.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WAS YES BANK'S NET PROFIT FOR Q4 FY26?
YES BANK REPORTED A STANDALONE NET PROFIT OF ₹1,068 CRORE FOR Q4 FY26, UP 44.7% YEAR-ON-YEAR FROM ₹738 CRORE IN Q4 FY25. FULL-YEAR FY26 PROFIT STOOD AT ₹3,476 CRORE, UP 44.5%.
WHAT WAS THE NET INTEREST INCOME AND NIM?
NET INTEREST INCOME GREW 15.9% YOY TO ₹2,638 CRORE. NET INTEREST MARGIN IMPROVED 20 BASIS POINTS YOY AND 10 BPS SEQUENTIALLY TO 2.7%, SUPPORTED BY LOWER COST OF DEPOSITS WHICH FELL 60 BPS TO 5.5%.
HOW DID ASSET QUALITY PERFORM?
GROSS NPA RATIO FELL TO 1.3%, DOWN 30 BPS YOY AND 20 BPS QOQ. NET NPA STOOD AT 0.2%, DOWN 10 BPS BOTH YOY AND QOQ. RETAIL SLIPPAGES HIT A 9-QUARTER LOW OF ₹888 CRORE (2.8% OF RETAIL ADVANCES).
DID YES BANK DECLARE A DIVIDEND?
NO. YES BANK DID NOT DECLARE ANY DIVIDEND FOR Q4 FY26 OR THE FULL YEAR FY26. THE COMPANY CURRENTLY DOES NOT PAY DIVIDENDS AS IT CONTINUES TO REINVEST CAPITAL INTO GROWTH AND BALANCE SHEET STRENGTHENING.
WHAT IS THE BROKERAGE CONSENSUS ON YES BANK?
OF 12 ANALYSTS TRACKING THE STOCK, 8 RECOMMEND 'SELL', 2 HAVE A 'HOLD' RATING, AND ONLY 2 HAVE A 'BUY' RATING. TARGET PRICES RANGE FROM ₹15 (MORGAN STANLEY) TO ₹21 (NOMURA/ICICI SECURITIES).
