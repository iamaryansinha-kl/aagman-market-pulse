Copper Sulphate Prices Surge 70% to ₹450/kg, Coffee and Arecanut Growers Hit Hard

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Copper sulphate prices have surged 70% from ₹270 to over ₹450 per kg ahead of the monsoon season
  • 02/Coffee and arecanut growers face higher cultivation costs for Bordeaux mixture fungicide sprays
  • 03/Campco offers subsidised copper sulphate at ₹450/kg to members; migrant labour shortage adds to pressure

Copper sulphate prices have surged 70 per cent from ₹270 per kg last year to over ₹450 per kg now. The sharp increase is driven by higher copper and sulphuric acid prices.

The price spike directly impacts coffee and arecanut growers preparing for monsoon fungicide sprays. Copper sulphate is a key ingredient in Bordeaux mixture, used to prevent fungal diseases in Arabica coffee.

Sahadev Balakrishna, Chairman of UPASI Coffee Committee, confirmed the 70 per cent jump. He said Bordeaux mixture is imperative in high-rainfall areas to control leaf rust and black rot.

B S Jayaram, former president of Karnataka Growers Federation, said the input cost spike will add to cultivation expenses this year. Migrant labourers from Assam who left during recent elections are yet to return.

Jayaram warned that field operations like pruning, spraying, and manuring face delays if labour does not return within weeks. This compounds the financial pressure on growers already squeezed by rising input costs.

Mahesh Puchhappady, President of All India Arecanut Growers' Association, said prices now range from ₹450 to ₹530 per kg depending on quality. Last year's minimum was around ₹290 per kg.

Arecanut growers lost nearly 50 per cent of their yield to fruit rot disease last season. Puchhappady said small farmers may struggle to manage plantations at these elevated copper sulphate prices.

SR Satishchandra, President of Campco Ltd, said the cooperative is supplying copper sulphate at a subsidised rate of ₹450 per kg to grower members. Non-members pay ₹490 per kg.

Members can buy 2 kg at the subsidised rate for every 100 kg of arecanut sold to Campco last season. In 2025-26, Campco sold copper sulphate at ₹290 subsidised and ₹330 non-subsidised.
