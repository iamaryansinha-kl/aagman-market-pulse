Citi Sees India IPOs Rebounding to $22B+ in H2 2026 Despite Market Challenges

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Citigroup forecasts India IPO volumes for 2026 to match or exceed last year's $22.4 billion, with 60-70% of deals historically coming in Q4
  • 02/Only $3.5 billion raised in Indian IPOs year-to-date, a sharp drop from 2025 when India ranked as the world's third-biggest IPO market
  • 03/Jio Platforms' potential record-breaking listing and NSE's long-delayed IPO are crucial catalysts; Middle East war and rupee weakness pose risks

Citigroup Inc. expects Indian initial public offerings to rebound sharply in the second half of 2026. The bank forecasts full-year deal volumes to match or exceed 2024's record performance.

Arvind Vashistha, Citigroup's India head of equity capital markets, said volumes could rise 5% to 10% above last year. About $3.5 billion has been raised in Indian IPOs so far in 2026.

This compares with $22.4 billion in all of 2025. India was the world's third-biggest IPO market last year behind only the US and China.

"Over the past two years, 60% to 70% of issuance has been concentrated in the final quarter," Vashistha said. Current conditions suggest a similar trajectory for 2026.

Much depends on Jio Platforms Ltd.'s potential record-breaking IPO. The listing, however, is proving increasingly challenging amid volatile markets.

National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.'s long-delayed IPO is another critical deal investors are watching closely.

Foreign investor sentiment has weakened due to the Middle East war. The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively blocked for over two months, hurting India's energy imports.

The Sensex is down 11% this year. The rupee has hit a record low against the dollar.

"Markets have softened in the short term, but the broader expectation is for stabilization and recovery," Vashistha said.

Citigroup also sees India as an increasingly active buyer in cross-border M&A. Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. is acquiring Organon & Co. at an enterprise value of $11.75 billion.

"Indian companies retain both the capacity and the willingness to pursue large, strategic acquisitions," said Rahul Saraf, Citigroup's India head of investment banking.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH HAS BEEN RAISED IN INDIAN IPOS IN 2026 SO FAR?
APPROXIMATELY $3.5 BILLION HAS BEEN RAISED IN INDIAN INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERINGS IN 2026 TO DATE. THIS IS SIGNIFICANTLY LOWER THAN THE $22.4 BILLION RAISED IN ALL OF 2025.
WHAT IS CITIGROUP'S OUTLOOK FOR INDIA IPO VOLUMES IN 2026?
CITIGROUP EXPECTS FULL-YEAR IPO VOLUMES TO BE AT LEAST IN LINE WITH 2025 AND POTENTIALLY 5% TO 10% HIGHER. ARVIND VASHISTHA, CITI'S INDIA HEAD OF EQUITY CAPITAL MARKETS, SAID 60% TO 70% OF ISSUANCE HAS BEEN CONCENTRATED IN THE FINAL QUARTER OVER THE PAST TWO YEARS.
WHICH ARE THE KEY IPO LISTINGS TO WATCH?
JIO PLATFORMS LTD.'S POTENTIAL RECORD-BREAKING FIRST-TIME SHARE SALE AND NATIONAL STOCK EXCHANGE OF INDIA LTD.'S LONG-DELAYED LISTING ARE THE TWO MOST CRITICAL UPCOMING DEALS.
WHAT CHALLENGES ARE FACING THE INDIAN IPO MARKET?
THE ONGOING MIDDLE EAST WAR, BLOCKING OF THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ FOR OVER TWO MONTHS, SURGING ENERGY COSTS, FOREIGN INVESTOR OUTFLOWS, A RECORD-LOW RUPEE, AND AN 11% DECLINE IN THE SENSEX THIS YEAR.
WHAT IS CITIGROUP'S VIEW ON INDIA M&A ACTIVITY?
RAHUL SARAF, CITI'S INDIA HEAD OF INVESTMENT BANKING, SAID MORE DEALS WILL EMERGE AS MULTINATIONALS DIVEST NON-CORE ASSETS. INDIAN COMPANIES ARE INCREASINGLY PURSUING LARGE OVERSEAS ACQUISITIONS, SUCH AS SUN PHARMA'S $11.75 BILLION DEAL FOR ORGANON & CO.
