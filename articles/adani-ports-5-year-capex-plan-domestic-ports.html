Adani Ports Unveils ₹60,000 Crore 5-Year Domestic Port Expansion Plan

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Ports & SEZ plans ₹60,000-63,000 crore capex on domestic ports over FY27-31 to expand capacity from 653 million tonnes to 1,000 million tonnes by CY30
  • 02/Mundra, Vizhinjam and Dhamra will account for the bulk of physical capacity additions at 94MT, 62MT and 49MT respectively
  • 03/Nomura raised its target price to ₹1,930 from ₹1,850 with a 'Buy' rating, citing robust profit growth of approximately 19% annually through FY28

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd (NSE: ADANIPORTS) has unveiled a ₹60,000–63,000 crore capital expenditure plan for its domestic ports. The five-year investment covers FY2027 through FY2031.

The company aims to expand domestic port capacity to 1,000 million tonnes by calendar year 2030. Current capacity stands at 653 million tonnes as of FY26.

Mundra, Vizhinjam and Dhamra will drive the bulk of physical capacity additions. Mundra will add 94 million tonnes, Vizhinjam 62 million tonnes, and Dhamra 49 million tonnes.

Domestic port traffic is targeted to reach 850 million tonnes by CY30. This implies a 14% compound annual growth rate from 451 million tonnes handled in FY26.

Total group capex over FY27-31 could reach ₹1,00,000 crore. Domestic ports will absorb the largest share at ₹60,000-63,000 crore.

Marine fleet expansion will receive ₹11,000-13,000 crore. Logistics infrastructure including rakes, MMLPs and warehouses will get ₹7,000-9,000 crore.

For FY27, management has guided revenue of ₹43,000-45,000 crore, implying 11-16% year-on-year growth. EBITDA guidance stands at ₹25,000-26,000 crore, implying 9-14% growth over FY26's ₹22,900 crore.

Nomura raised its target price on the stock to ₹1,930 from ₹1,850. The brokerage maintained its 'Buy' rating and expects approximately 19% annual profit growth through FY28.
