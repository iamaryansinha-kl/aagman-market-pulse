Maruti Suzuki Q4 Earnings: Net Profit Falls 7% But Shares Jump 4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki Q4 FY26 standalone net profit fell 7% YoY to ₹3,590.5 crore from ₹3,857.3 crore
  • 02/Shares surged over 4% to ₹13,443 despite profit decline, driven by strong demand outlook and broker upgrades
  • 03/Brokerages remain constructive with targets up to ₹17,895; watch FY27 volume growth guidance and margin recovery timeline

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) reported a standalone net profit of ₹3,590.5 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 7% year-on-year decline from ₹3,857.3 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Despite the earnings miss, shares surged over 4% in early trade on April 29, 2026. The stock touched ₹13,443 at 9:32 am on the NSE.

Investor sentiment was lifted by strong underlying demand trends and forward guidance. The company has guided for around 10% domestic volume growth in FY27.

Global brokerage Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating with a target price of ₹17,895. It noted that exports were a key driver of FY26 outperformance.

Morgan Stanley highlighted management's FY27 volume guidance as roughly 200 basis points ahead of its own estimates. It cautioned that margins are likely to bottom out in Q1 before improving.

Investec maintained a buy rating but trimmed its target to ₹15,360 from ₹15,465. It lowered EPS forecasts for FY27-28E by 1-3% due to margin headwinds.

Investec expects upcoming models and export-focused vehicles such as the e-Vitara to drive growth. It projects a 17% CAGR in EBIT and 13% CAGR in PAT over FY26-28.

JM Financial reported Q4FY26 EBITDA and EBIT margins of 11.7% and 8.4% respectively. Margins were supported by lower employee costs, reduced discounts, and favourable forex movements.

JM Financial pointed to strong domestic demand, particularly in the entry-level segment. Dealer inventory stands at just 12 days with a robust order book of around 190,000 vehicles.

Motilal Oswal said a GST rate cut has helped revive demand for small cars. It expects 10% domestic volume growth in FY27E, aided by a strong order backlog and new launches.

Motilal Oswal reiterated a buy rating with a target price of ₹15,529. It values the company at 25x FY28E EPS and expects a 16% earnings CAGR over FY26-28.

Near-term margin pressures persist due to higher commodity costs and model launch expenses. However, brokerages remain constructive on growth underpinned by export momentum and operating leverage.
