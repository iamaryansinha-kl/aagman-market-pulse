AI Chip Rally Faces Reality Check, Bubble Fears Overdone: Adrian Mowat

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EM-Equity Strategist Adrian Mowat says South Korean chip stock volatility reflects a temporary dip after extraordinary gains, not an AI bubble bursting
  • 02/KOSPI has plunged 8-10% in recent sessions with Samsung and SK Hynix leading losses, yet the index remains up approximately 78% year-to-date in 2026
  • 03/Indian IT sector has not escaped the turmoil, with the tech sector down over 30% YTD on fears of AI disruption

South Korean chip stocks are experiencing a temporary correction, not an AI bubble burst. Adrian Mowat, EM-Equity Strategist, believes the recent sell-off reflects a pause after extraordinary gains rather than a fundamental collapse.

The KOSPI index plunged 8.37% on June 8, 2026, triggering a circuit breaker and a 20-minute trading halt. Samsung Electronics fell as much as 10.2% while SK Hynix dropped 7.7% during the session.

On June 23, the KOSPI fell another 5.69% to 8,596 points. The index hit a near 10% drop on June 23, with a sidecar trading curb temporarily halting programme trading.

Samsung and SK Hynix together account for roughly 40-50% of the KOSPI's total market value. Samsung carries a market capitalization of approximately $1.4 trillion, while SK Hynix is valued at around $1.2 trillion.

Mowat dismisses the bubble narrative. "I am not sure if the phrase 'bubble bursting' is correct," he said. "The movement in Micron, SanDisk, Hynix and Samsung has been driven by very dramatic positive earnings revisions because the supply-demand dynamics for semiconductors are so tight."

He noted that PE multiples for these chipmakers "actually look quite reasonable." He also pointed out there is "no clear evidence that there is a big supply increase coming in this sector."

Foreign investors have pulled approximately $48-62 billion from South Korean equities year-to-date. Retail margin debt in Korea has ballooned to a record level near ₩37.7 trillion, amplifying volatility through forced selling.

Mowat expects volatility to persist. "I do not think there is a short-term change in the flows from foreign investors," he said. "We are going to spend the rest of this year seeing high volatility in these memory names."

India has not been insulated from the AI-driven turbulence. "We have had the tech sector down something like 30-plus percent year-to-date because of AI and the fear of AI," Mowat said. TCS has fallen approximately 30% YTD while Infosys is down roughly 24%.

Mowat believes the next phase of the AI rally depends on companies translating investments into tangible results. "The next phase should be identifying which companies are actually increasing revenue or reducing costs by applying AI," he said.

For Indian IT, sentiment could improve if firms prove their AI value. "If we were to start seeing some of the Indian IT companies proving that they can help customers use AI effectively, then that would definitely change sentiment," Mowat added.

Goldman Sachs maintains a bullish outlook with a KOSPI target of 12,000, implying significant upside from current levels. The broader AI investment theme remains fundamentally intact despite the sharp correction.

HOW MUCH HAS THE KOSPI FALLEN IN RECENT SESSIONS?
THE KOSPI FELL 8.37% ON JUNE 8, 2026, TRIGGERING A CIRCUIT BREAKER. ON JUNE 23, IT FELL ANOTHER 5.69% TO 8,596 POINTS. DESPITE THESE SHARP DECLINES, THE INDEX REMAINS UP APPROXIMATELY 78% YEAR-TO-DATE IN 2026.

DURING THE JUNE 8 Selloff, SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS FELL AS MUCH AS 10.2% WHILE SK HYNIX DROPPED 7.7%. ON JUNE 23, SAMSUNG FELL 2.7% AND SK HYNIX FELL 2.0%. TOGETHER, THESE TWO COMPANIES ACCOUNT FOR ROUGHLY 40-50% OF THE KOSPI'S TOTAL MARKET VALUE.

THE INDIAN TECH SECTOR HAS FALLEN OVER 30% YEAR-TO-DATE IN 2026, WITH TCS DOWN APPROXIMATELY 30% AND INFOSYS DOWN ROUGHLY 24%. MOWAT BELIEVES SENTIMENT COULD IMPROVE IF INDIAN IT COMPANIES DEMONSTRATE TANGIBLE BENEFITS FROM AI ADOPTION AND PROVE THEY CAN HELP CUSTOMERS USE AI EFFECTIVELY.
WHAT DOES THE NEXT PHASE OF THE AI RALLY REQUIRE?
MOWAT SAYS THE NEXT PHASE SHOULD FOCUS ON IDENTIFYING WHICH COMPANIES ARE ACTUALLY INCREASING REVENUE OR REDUCING COSTS BY APPLYING AI. UNTIL BUSINESSES TRANSLATE AI INVESTMENTS INTO HIGHER REVENUES AND IMPROVED PROFITABILITY, VOLATILITY IS EXPECTED TO REMAIN ELEVATED EVEN THOUGH THE BROADER AI INVESTMENT THEME REMAINS FUNDAMENTALLY INTACT.
