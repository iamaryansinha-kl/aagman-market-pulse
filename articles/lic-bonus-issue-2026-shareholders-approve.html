LIC Bonus Issue 2026: Shareholders Approve 1:1 Bonus with 99.9998% Votes

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nearly 22 lakh LIC shareholders approved the 1:1 bonus issue with 99.9998% votes cast in favour
  • 02/LIC will capitalise Rs 6,325 crore from reserves to issue bonus shares by June 12, 2026
  • 03/Record date yet to be announced; Q4 FY2026 results scheduled for May 21, 2026

Life Insurance Corporation of India said shareholders approved its 1:1 bonus issue on Monday. Nearly 22 lakh shareholders voted in favour with 99.9998% approval.

The board had approved the bonus issue in April 2026. Eligible shareholders will receive one fully paid-up equity share of Rs 10 for every share held.

LIC will capitalise up to Rs 6,325 crore from its reserves and surplus to fund the bonus issue. Total reserves stood at nearly Rs 1.5 lakh crore as of December 31, 2025.

Bonus shares will be credited to eligible shareholders within two months. The crediting is expected on or before June 12, 2026.

The record date for the bonus issue is yet to be announced. Only shareholders holding shares as of the record date will be eligible.

LIC shares gained around 2% on Tuesday to hit an intraday high of Rs 805.5 on NSE. The stock has risen 42% over three years.

This is LIC's first bonus issue since its stock market debut in May 2022. The company has declared five interim dividends since listing.

LIC will announce its Q4 FY2026 results on May 21, 2026. The company currently commands a market capitalisation of over Rs 5 lakh crore.
