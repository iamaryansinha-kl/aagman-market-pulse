Sensex Drops 417 Pts, Nifty Below 24,000 as Oil Surges to $111.4

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex fell 416.72 points (0.54%) to close at 76,886.91; Nifty 50 shed 97 points (0.40%) to end at 23,995.70, slipping below the 24,000 mark
  • 02/Brent crude jumped 2.97% to $111.4 per barrel on US-Iran tensions and Strait of Hormuz disruptions, while FIIs offloaded ₹1,151.48 crore on April 27
  • 03/Bank Nifty crashed 864 points on RBI ECL norms; broader markets held firm with Midcap +0.3% and Smallcap +0.4%

Indian equity benchmarks declined on Tuesday, April 28, 2026, as surging crude oil prices and relentless foreign fund outflows dented investor sentiment.

The 30-share BSE Sensex fell 416.72 points, or 0.54%, to settle at 76,886.91. During the session, the index plunged as much as 562.57 points, or 0.72%, to touch an intraday low of 76,741.06.

The NSE Nifty 50 dropped 97 points, or 0.40%, to close at 23,995.70. It slipped below the psychological 24,000 support level for the second consecutive session.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, surged 2.97% to $111.4 per barrel. The rally was driven by ongoing US-Iran tensions and continued disruption in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global energy supplies.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded Indian equities worth ₹1,151.48 crore on Monday, April 27, according to exchange data. The sustained outflow has pressured the rupee, which fell 41 paise to close at 94.56 against the US dollar.

Banking stocks bore the brunt of the selling. The Nifty Bank index crashed 864 points to 55,400, while the Nifty PSU Bank index declined 2.15%. The selloff was triggered by the RBI finalising the Expected Credit Loss framework, which tightens provisioning norms from April 2027.

Axis Bank, HCL Tech, InterGlobe Aviation, Maruti Suzuki, State Bank of India and ICICI Bank were among the biggest Sensex laggards. In contrast, Reliance Industries, Coal India, ONGC, Bharti Airtel and Tech Mahindra closed in the green.

Broader markets showed resilience. The Nifty Midcap 100 index rose 0.3%, while the Nifty Smallcap 100 gained 0.4%, indicating selective buying in mid-tier stocks despite benchmark weakness.

Nifty opened at 24,049.90 and touched a high of 24,181.80 before succumbing to late-session selling. Technical analysts peg immediate support at 23,940 and resistance at 24,060.

India VIX, the volatility gauge, held around 18, below the 20 mark, suggesting cautious but not panicky sentiment. Traders are watching the US Federal Reserve meeting and any de-escalation in West Asia for the next directional move.
