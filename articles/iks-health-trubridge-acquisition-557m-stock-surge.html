IKS Health to Buy TruBridge for $557M; Stock Hits 10% Intraday High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IKS Health's US subsidiary will acquire TruBridge for $557M at $26.25 per share in cash, announced after market hours on April 23
  • 02/Stock surged nearly 10% to an intraday high of ₹1,573.80 before pulling back sharply to ₹1,460.10 by midday
  • 03/Deal funded via $600M term loan from Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, and Deutsche Bank; expected to close in Q2 FY2027

Inventurus Knowledge Solutions Limited announced a definitive agreement to acquire TruBridge, Inc. The US subsidiary of IKS Health will pay $26.25 per share in cash. The transaction values TruBridge at an enterprise value of approximately $557 million.

The company announced the deal after market hours on Thursday, April 23, 2026. The acquisition requires shareholder approvals from both companies and US antitrust clearance. It is expected to close in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027.

IKS Health shares opened at ₹1,476.90 on Friday. The stock touched an intraday high of ₹1,573.80, a near 10 per cent jump from Thursday's close of ₹1,435.30. It later pared gains to ₹1,460.10 by midday.

Traded volumes reached 33.35 lakh shares worth ₹503.84 crore. This is unusually heavy activity for a scrip with a free-float market cap of around ₹7,192 crore. Sell orders currently dominate at 65 per cent versus 35 per cent on the buy side.

TruBridge provides revenue cycle management and EHR solutions to over 700 rural and community hospitals in the United States. Its platform serves as a "system of record" for these clients. IKS Health will combine this with its AI-driven "system of action" care enablement platform.

Management is targeting combined EBITDA of approximately ₹3,000 crore by FY2030. This compares to a current consolidated figure of around ₹1,000 crore. The deal is projected to be earnings-per-share accretive from the first full year post-closing.

IKS plans to fund the acquisition primarily through roughly $600 million in new debt. Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, and Deutsche Bank are underwriting the term loan. Post-closing leverage will stand at approximately three times the combined entity's EBITDA.

IKS Health identified a cross-sell opportunity of over $575 million within TruBridge's existing client base. The stock remains well below its 52-week high of ₹1,876 hit in June 2025. It is down 13.21 per cent year-to-date.
