Sensex Rises 377 Points by Midday, SBI and Trent Lead Gains

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex surged 377 points by midday on April 20, reversing early losses to trade near 78,915
  • 02/SBI and Trent led sectoral gains while ICICI Bank shares rose following strong Q4 earnings
  • 03/Markets face headwinds from US-Iran tensions and crude oil above $95; volatility likely to persist

Indian equity markets reversed early losses to trade firmly in positive territory by midday on Monday. The BSE Sensex climbed 377 points as buying momentum picked up across key sectors.

At 12:54 p.m., the Sensex was up 421 points at 78,915. The NSE Nifty rose 126 points to 24,479. By 12:57 p.m., the Sensex stood at 78,911.41, up 0.53%.

State Bank of India and Trent emerged as the top gainers on the day. Their strong performance helped offset weakness in select midcap names.

ICICI Bank shares rose after reporting quarterly earnings. The lender joined HDFC Bank in providing support to the broader banking index.

However, gains remained capped by lingering geopolitical concerns. Renewed US-Iran tensions and elevated crude oil prices kept investor sentiment cautious.

Brent crude holding above $95 per barrel remains a key risk for India. Higher oil prices threaten to stoke inflation and trigger foreign portfolio outflows.

Analysts note that the Nifty faces strong resistance between 24,600 and 24,800. Immediate support lies at 24,300-24,200, with deeper support near 24,000.

Banking stocks showed relative strength, acting as a stabilising factor. The IT sector stayed range-bound, while oil & gas names traded with volatility.
