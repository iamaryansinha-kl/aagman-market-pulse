Trent FY26 Profit Rises 13% to ₹1,741 Crore; Board Approves 1:2 Bonus

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Trent reports FY26 consolidated PAT of ₹1,741 crore, up 13% year-on-year
  • 02/Q4 FY26 net profit surges 33% to ₹413 crore; revenue from operations up 19%
  • 03/Board approves 1:2 bonus issue and ₹6 per share dividend; Zudio store expansion continues

Trent Limited has reported strong financial results for FY26. The Tata Group retail company posted a consolidated profit after tax of ₹1,741 crore.

The full-year profit reflects a 13% increase over the previous fiscal year. Revenue from operations for FY26 stood at ₹20,074 crore.

In Q4 FY26 alone, Trent's net profit jumped 33% year-on-year to ₹413 crore. Quarterly revenue from operations reached ₹5,028 crore.

The company's EBITDA for FY26 rose 43%, indicating strong margin expansion. Store expansion across cities drove the robust performance.

Trent's board has approved a 1:2 bonus issue. Shareholders will receive one additional share for every two shares held.

The board also declared a dividend of ₹6 per share. Both the bonus issue and dividend are subject to shareholder approval.

Zudio, Trent's value fashion format, has been a key growth driver. The brand has rapidly expanded its store network across India.

Trent shares gained 8% in the two sessions ahead of the results. The stock has been among the best-performing retail plays in India.
