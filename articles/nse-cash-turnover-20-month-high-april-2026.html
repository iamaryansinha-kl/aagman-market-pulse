NSE Cash Turnover Hits 20-Month High at ₹1.34 Lakh Crore in April

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE cash segment average daily turnover hit 20-month high of ₹1,34,709 crore in April, up 8% from March's ₹1,25,169 crore
  • 02/Retail investors continued pumping money despite West Asia crisis and crude oil hitting $104/barrel as FPIs pulled out ₹60,847 crore
  • 03/Demat accounts exceed 24 crore and SIP flows hold at ₹30,000 crore per month; large-cap earnings growth of 12-14% expected in FY27

The average daily turnover in NSE's cash segment hit a 20-month high of ₹1,34,709 crore in April. This was up 8 per cent from March's ₹1,25,169 crore.

Overall cash market turnover on NSE rose 13 per cent to ₹26.94 lakh crore in April. It stood at ₹23.78 lakh crore in March.

BSE cash segment ADT dipped marginally to ₹9,323 crore in April from ₹9,375 crore in March. Overall BSE turnover still climbed to ₹1,86,462 crore.

Markets turned highly volatile in April. The Sensex tanked nearly 3,900 points across five sessions but recovered to end the month up 4,965 points or 7 per cent.

Foreign portfolio investors pulled out ₹60,847 crore in April. They have withdrawn ₹1.92 lakh crore over the last four months of 2026.

"The boost in cash market turnover during heightened volatility shows the maturity of retail investors," said Tanvi Kanchan, Associate Director at Anand Rathi Shares and Stock Brokers.

SIP investors who stayed invested regardless of headlines made the right decision, Kanchan said. Demat accounts now exceed 24 crore and monthly SIP flows stand at ₹30,000 crore.

Large-cap valuations have normalised after the market fell 15-16 per cent from peak in dollar terms. Earnings growth of 12-14 per cent is expected in FY27, said a broking firm analyst.
