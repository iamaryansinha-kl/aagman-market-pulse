Petrol Diesel Prices May Surge ₹25-28 Per Litre After April 29 Polls

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Petrol and diesel prices may surge by ₹25 to ₹28 per litre following the April 29 elections
  • 02/The government is expected to allow oil marketing companies to pass on rising crude oil costs
  • 03/Consumers should brace for a significant fuel price adjustment once the polling concludes

Petrol and diesel prices in India may rise sharply after the April 29 polls conclude. Industry sources indicate a potential surge of ₹25 to ₹28 per litre.

The government has reportedly kept fuel prices frozen during the election period. This is a standard practice to avoid public backlash during campaigning.

Oil marketing companies have been absorbing rising crude oil costs for weeks. They have been unable to adjust retail prices due to the election freeze.

Global crude oil prices have climbed significantly in recent months. This has squeezed margins for state-run fuel retailers across the country.

Post-election, the government is expected to permit price revisions. Companies will likely move quickly to recover accumulated losses.

Consumers should prepare for a substantial increase at the pump. The hike would affect transportation costs and potentially feed into broader inflation.
