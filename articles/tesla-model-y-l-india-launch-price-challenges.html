Tesla Model Y L India at ₹61.99 Lakh Faces Tough Market Reality

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tesla launches Model Y L in India at ₹61.99 lakh, with on-road price of ₹63.12 lakh
  • 02/The six-seater EV offers 681 km range but Tesla has sold only 342 units since September 2025
  • 03/Import duties of 100-110% and poor charging infrastructure remain key barriers to growth

Tesla has launched the Model Y L in India at ₹61.99 lakh, ex-showroom. The on-road price is ₹63.12 lakh according to the company's website.

The six-seater electric SUV comes with an 88kWh battery pack and dual motor setup. It offers a WLTP-certified range of 681 km and a top speed of 201 km per hour.

The Model Y L features a 2+2+2 seating layout with heated and ventilated front and second row seats. It includes a 16-inch touchscreen, dual wireless charging pads, and pillar-mounted rear AC vents.

Tesla entered India in July 2025 but has struggled to gain traction. The company recorded just 342 vehicle registrations in its first six months, averaging fewer than 60 units monthly.

By comparison, BMW sold an average of 295 electric cars per month in FY26. Mercedes-Benz retailed about 87 units monthly, both benefiting from local assembly.

Tesla imports its vehicles from China and pays import duties of 100 to 110 percent. This makes its cars significantly more expensive than in global markets.

The company has offered discounts of up to ₹2 lakh on 2025 inventory earlier this year. First deliveries of the Model Y L are scheduled for June 2026.
