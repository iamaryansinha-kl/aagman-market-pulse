4 SME IPOs to Raise ₹138 Crore This Week: Cloud Kitchen, Cashew Processor, and More

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •• Four SME IPOs plan to raise over ₹138 crore this week across food, construction, textiles, and packaged foods
  • •• NFP Sampoorna Foods opens May 18, Teamtech Formwork on May 19, Vegorama and Harikanta Overseas on May 20
  • •• Mainboard IPO activity remains subdued, but niche business models like cloud kitchens continue to attract retail investor interest

India's primary market activity is set to remain busy this week, with four SME public offers looking to raise more than ₹138 crore through fresh issues and offer-for-sale components across multiple sectors.

The first IPO to open is NFP Sampoorna Foods on May 18, closing May 20, with an NSE SME issue aiming to raise around ₹24.53 crore entirely through a fresh issue.

The company has fixed the price band at ₹52-55 per share, with retail investors needing to bid for a minimum of 4,000 shares, translating to an investment of ₹2.2 lakh at the upper price band.

Incorporated in 2019, NFP Sampoorna Foods operates in the dry fruits processing and distribution business, with a primary focus on cashew nuts imported mainly from African markets.

The second issue, Teamtech Formwork Solutions, opens on May 19 on the NSE SME platform, planning to raise ₹50.15 crore through a fresh issue.

Teamtech manufactures, refurbishes, and rents modular formwork systems used in the construction industry across infrastructure and real estate projects, operating a manufacturing facility in Telangana.

The company reported a 64% increase in revenue and a 48% rise in profit after tax in FY26, with IPO proceeds earmarked for a new manufacturing unit and repayment of borrowings.

On May 20, Vegorama Punjabi Angithi will open its ₹38.38 crore IPO on the BSE SME platform, consisting of a ₹30.7 crore fresh issue and a ₹7.68 crore offer-for-sale.

The company has fixed the price band at ₹73-77 per share, requiring retail investors to apply for a minimum of 3,200 shares, or ₹2.46 lakh at the upper end.

Vegorama operates restaurant and cloud kitchen businesses under the Punjabi Angithi brand, with 19 cloud kitchens and two fine-dining restaurants across Delhi-NCR, serving customers through dine-in, takeaway, and online delivery.

The fourth IPO is Harikanta Overseas, also opening on May 20 and closing May 22, with a BSE SME issue aiming to raise ₹25.63 crore entirely through a fresh issue.

Harikanta manufactures synthetic textile fabrics, including saree fabrics, garment fabrics, poly linen, and natural fibre products, operating a manufacturing facility in Surat and exporting to Bahrain, Singapore, and Thailand.

The company has fixed the price band at ₹91-96 per share, with a minimum retail investment requirement of approximately ₹2.3 lakh.

Mainboard IPO activity remains relatively muted, but the continued flow of SME offerings indicates sustained retail investor appetite for smaller public issues with niche business models.
