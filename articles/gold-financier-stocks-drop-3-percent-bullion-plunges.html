Gold Financier Stocks Drop 3% as Bullion Plunges Below $4,000

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Manappuram Finance tumbled nearly 3% to Rs 309.35, while Muthoot Finance and IIFL Finance fell over 2% each on June 25
  • 02/MCX gold futures for August 2026 crashed Rs 5,863 in two days to Rs 1,40,666 per 10 grams; spot gold hit lowest since November 2025
  • 03/Dollar index climbed to a one-year high around 101.5; CME FedWatch Tool shows 67% probability of a Fed rate hike in September

Gold financier stocks fell sharply on June 25, 2026 as a crash in bullion prices and a surging US dollar dented investor sentiment. Manappuram Finance shares tumbled nearly 3% to trade at Rs 309.35 apiece on NSE.

Muthoot Finance and IIFL Finance also declined over 2% each. The trio are India's largest gold-backed lenders and move in lockstep with gold prices.

MCX gold futures for August 2026 delivery plunged Rs 5,863 in just two days to Rs 1,40,666 per 10 grams. Spot gold slipped 0.4% to $3,985.89 per ounce after hitting its lowest level since November 2025 on Wednesday.

Bullion dropped below the key $4,000-an-ounce mark for the first time in seven months. US gold futures for August delivery were down 0.2% at $4,001.60 per ounce.

The dollar index climbed to a more than one-year high around 101.5. The US Federal Reserve held rates unchanged but a larger number of policymakers signaled higher borrowing costs later this year.

The first FOMC meeting under new Chairman Kevin Warsh noted inflation remained above the 2% target. Traders now expect three rate hikes this year per the CME FedWatch Tool.

Gold financiers face a double hit. Lower gold prices erode the value of pledged collateral. Borrowers may need to pledge additional jewellery to access the same loan amount.

"As investors face losses in equities, many are selling liquid assets such as gold to raise cash. Money is flowing into the US dollar, with the stronger dollar adding further pressure on bullion prices," said Jateen Trivedi, VP Research Analyst at LKP Securities.

Manoj Kumar Jain of Prithvi Finmart said gold and silver prices are likely to remain volatile this week. He sees gold support at $3,980-$3,920 per ounce and resistance at $4,040-$4,085.
