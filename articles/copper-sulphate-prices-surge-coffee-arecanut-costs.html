Copper Sulphate Surges 70% To ₹450/kg: Coffee, Arecanut Costs Spike

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Copper sulphate prices surged 70% from ₹270/kg to over ₹450/kg, driven by higher copper and sulphuric acid costs
  • 02/Coffee and arecanut growers rely on it for Bordeaux mixture fungicide ahead of monsoon; labour shortages add to pressure
  • 03/Campco offers subsidised copper sulphate at ₹450/kg to members; small farmers face income squeeze from dual cost pressures

Copper sulphate prices have surged 70% from ₹270 per kg last year to over ₹450 per kg now. The spike is driven by higher copper and sulphuric acid prices. Growers need it for fungicide sprays ahead of monsoons.

The chemical is a key ingredient in Bordeaux mixture. This organic fungicide protects Arabica coffee from leaf rust and black rot during heavy monsoon rains.

Arecanut plantations also depend on copper sulphate. It controls fruit rot disease, which wiped out nearly 50% of growers' yield last season.

"Copper sulphate prices have gone up by a whopping 70 per cent," said Sahadev Balakrishna, Chairman of UPASI Coffee Committee. The Bordeaux mixture is imperative in high-rainfall areas.

Mahesh Puchhappady, President of All India Arecanut Growers' Association, said the cost has almost doubled. Market prices now range from ₹450 to ₹530 per kg depending on quality.

Labour shortages are compounding the problem. Migrant workers who returned to Assam for elections have not fully returned to coffee estates yet.

B S Jayaram, former president of Karnataka Growers Federation, said lack of labour and rising input costs challenge field operations. Spraying and pruning face delays.

SR Satishchandra, President of Campco Ltd, said the cooperative has sufficient stock. Members buy at ₹450 per kg against ₹290 last year. Others pay ₹490 per kg.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH HAVE COPPER SULPHATE PRICES INCREASED?
COPPER SULPHATE PRICES HAVE SURGED 70% FROM ₹270 PER KG LAST YEAR TO OVER ₹450 PER KG NOW. DEPENDING ON QUALITY, PRICES RANGE UP TO ₹530 PER KG.
WHY DO COFFEE AND ARECANUT GROWERS NEED COPPER SULPHATE?
COPPER SULPHATE IS A KEY INGREDIENT IN BORDEAUX MIXTURE, AN ORGANIC FUNGICIDE USED TO PROTECT ARABICA COFFEE FROM LEAF RUST AND BLACK ROT DURING MONSOONS. ARECANUT PLANTATIONS USE IT TO CONTROL FRUIT ROT DISEASE.
WHAT LABOUR CHALLENGES ARE COFFEE GROWERS FACING?
MIGRANT LABOURERS WHO RETURNED TO ASSAM DURING RECENT ELECTIONS HAVE NOT FULLY RETURNED. THIS DELAYS VITAL FIELD OPERATIONS LIKE SPRAYING, PRUNING, MANURING, AND WEEDING.
WHAT SUBSIDIES ARE AVAILABLE FOR ARECANUT GROWERS?
CAMPCO SUPPLIES COPPER SULPHATE AT A SUBSIDISED RATE OF ₹450 PER KG TO MEMBERS WHO SOLD ARECANUT TO THE COOPERATIVE LAST SEASON. NON-MEMBERS PAY ₹490 PER KG. LAST YEAR, MEMBERS PAID ₹290 PER KG.
HOW WILL THE PRICE RISE AFFECT SMALL FARMERS?
SMALL FARMERS FACE HIGHER CULTIVATION COSTS AT A TIME WHEN ARECANUT YIELDS ALREADY DROPPED 50% DUE TO FRUIT ROT LAST SEASON. MANY MAY STRUGGLE TO MANAGE PLANTATIONS AS ALTERNATIVE FUNGICIDES CARRY UNCERTAIN RISKS.
