Q4 Results May 7 Live: MRF Profit Up 37%, BSE Jumps 61%, KVB Rises 41%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MRF Q4 standalone net profit jumps 36.6% to Rs 680.44 crore; BSE profit surges 61% to Rs 795.47 crore
  • 02/Bharat Forge PAT dips 17% to Rs 233.4 crore; Coromandel International Q4 standalone profit drops 60% to Rs 154 crore
  • 03/CarTrade Tech rallies 7% after 64% profit jump; Indoco Remedies surges 15% on return to profitability

MRF Ltd posted a 36.6% rise in standalone net profit to Rs 680.44 crore for Q4 FY26. The company crossed Rs 30,000 crore in annual sales for the first time.

BSE Ltd reported a 61% jump in consolidated net profit to Rs 795.47 crore. Revenue more than doubled to Rs 1,630 crore from Rs 926.38 crore in Q4 FY25.

Karur Vysya Bank's Q4 net profit rose 41% to Rs 724.96 crore. The private sector lender hit its highest-ever quarterly and annual profitability.

Craftsman Automation's Q4 standalone net profit surged to Rs 78.28 crore from Rs 27.89 crore a year ago. The stock gained 7% to Rs 8,312 on the NSE.

Bharat Forge's consolidated PAT dipped 17% to Rs 233.4 crore in Q4 FY26. The company had reported Rs 282.6 crore in the same quarter last year.

Coromandel International's Q4 standalone profit crashed to Rs 154.31 crore from Rs 388.90 crore. The fertiliser major's stock fell 3% to Rs 1,961.60.

CarTrade Tech's Q4 standalone profit jumped 64% to Rs 35.17 crore. The company engaged 76 million average monthly unique visitors during the quarter.

Indoco Remedies swung to a Q4 profit of Rs 27.35 crore from a Rs 26.24 crore loss. The stock rallied 15% to Rs 236.66.

Thermax posted a 19% rise in Q4 consolidated net profit to Rs 244 crore. The board approved a dividend of Rs 14 per share.

Bajaj Auto reported a record 32% rise in revenue to Rs 16,006 crore. The stock hit a 52-week high of Rs 10,656 on the NSE.

COROMANDEL INTERNATIONAL'S Q4 STANDALONE NET PROFIT FELL TO RS 154.31 CRORE FROM RS 388.90 CRORE IN Q4 FY25, A DECLINE OF ABOUT 60%. THE COMPANY'S FULL-YEAR PROFIT ALSO DECLINED 8% TO RS 1,898 CRORE DESPITE 30% REVENUE GROWTH.
WHAT WERE THE KEY HIGHLIGHTS OF KARUR VYSYA BANK'S Q4 RESULTS?
KARUR VYSYA BANK REPORTED A 41% RISE IN Q4 NET PROFIT TO RS 724.96 CRORE FROM RS 513.36 CRORE IN Q4 FY25. THE BANK ACHIEVED ITS HIGHEST-EVER ANNUAL PROFITABILITY WITH FY26 PAT AT RS 2,510.33 CRORE.
