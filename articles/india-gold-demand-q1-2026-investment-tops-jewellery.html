India Gold Demand Q1 2026: Investment Tops Jewellery for First Time Ever

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold investment demand in India hit 82 tonnes in Q1 2026, up 54% YoY, overtaking jewellery demand at 66 tonnes for the first time ever.
  • 02/Gold ETF inflows surged nearly 6-fold to ₹30,000 crore as prices climbed 81% to ₹1.51 lakh per 10 gram, denting jewellery affordability.
  • 03/WGC estimates full-year Indian gold demand at 650-750 tonnes; wedding season and Akshaya Tritiya may support jewellery recovery.

Investment demand for gold in India surpassed jewellery consumption for the first time ever in Q1 2026. The milestone reflects a fundamental shift in how Indians engage with the yellow metal.

Investment demand surged 54 per cent year-on-year to 82 tonnes. Jewellery demand declined 19 per cent to 66 tonnes during the same period.

Overall gold demand in India rose 10 per cent to 151 tonnes in Q1. In value terms, it nearly doubled to a record ₹2.27 lakh crore.

Gold ETFs led the investment surge with inflows rising nearly six-fold to ₹30,000 crore in value. Volume jumped 3 times to 20 tonnes. Bars and coins accounted for 62 tonnes.

Domestic gold prices climbed 81 per cent to a record ₹1.51 lakh per 10 gram. This dented jewellery affordability while boosting gold's appeal as a safe-haven asset.

Gold imports increased 39 per cent to 196 tonnes despite the price rally. Recycled gold supply also rose 19 per cent to 31 tonnes.

Sachin Jain, Regional CEO of World Gold Council India, said investment demand reflects confidence in gold as a strategic asset. He added that elevated prices may act as a near-term headwind for jewellery.

The WGC expects full-year Indian gold demand between 650 and 750 tonnes. This compares to 768 tonnes in 2025. The summer wedding season and regional festivals may provide support.

WHAT HAPPENED TO GOLD DEMAND IN INDIA IN Q1 2026?
INVESTMENT DEMAND FOR GOLD IN INDIA SURPASSED JEWELLERY DEMAND FOR THE FIRST TIME EVER IN Q1 2026. INVESTMENT DEMAND ROSE 54% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO 82 TONNES, WHILE JEWELLERY DEMAND FELL 19% TO 66 TONNES.
HOW MUCH DID GOLD ETF INFLOWS RISE IN Q1 2026?
GOLD ETF INFLOWS SURGED NEARLY SIX-FOLD TO ₹30,000 CRORE IN VALUE TERMS AND 3 TIMES IN VOLUME TO 20 TONNES. BARS AND COINS ACCOUNTED FOR AN ADDITIONAL 62 TONNES OF INVESTMENT DEMAND.
WHAT WERE DOMESTIC GOLD PRICES IN Q1 2026?
DOMESTIC GOLD PRICES CLIMBED 81% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO A RECORD ₹1.51 LAKH PER 10 GRAM. THIS COMPARES TO ₹83,375 LOGGED IN THE SAME QUARTER LAST YEAR.
WHAT IS THE WGC OUTLOOK FOR FULL-YEAR INDIAN GOLD DEMAND?
THE WORLD GOLD COUNCIL ESTIMATES FULL-YEAR INDIAN GOLD DEMAND IN THE RANGE OF 650-750 TONNES FOR 2026. THIS IS DOWN FROM 768 TONNES IN 2025, WITH INVESTMENT DEMAND EXPECTED TO REMAIN A KEY DRIVER.
WHAT FACTORS COULD SUPPORT JEWELLERY DEMAND GOING FORWARD?
THE SUMMER WEDDING SEASON AND REGIONAL FESTIVALS ARE EXPECTED TO PROVIDE SUPPORT TO JEWELLERY DEMAND. THIS BUILDS ON THE MOMENTUM SEEN DURING AKSHAYA TRITIYA, ACCORDING TO SACHIN JAIN, REGIONAL CEO OF WORLD GOLD COUNCIL INDIA.
