Canada Optimistic About Concluding CEPA Trade Talks With India by Year-End: Minister

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Canada has put a timeline on its trade ambitions with India. Trade Minister Maninder Sidhu said on Saturday that he is very optimistic and energetic about concluding a comprehensive economic partnership agreement in the coming months, aiming for the year-end target Prime Minister Mark Carney set during his New Delhi visit in March.

The mechanics are already moving. Indian trade negotiators are expected in Canada next month, Sidhu told Reuters in an interview during a visit to Mumbai, and leaders of the two countries are likely to meet on the sidelines of the G-20 gathering in December.

Energy sits at the centre of the talks. A Shell-led LNG export facility launched in Canada last year, with five more projects in various phases of development, and New Delhi is in conversations with Indian firms on how they can access and invest in those projects to secure supply. Beyond LNG, both countries want to expand nuclear cooperation as India pushes to significantly boost its power generation capacity.

Sidhu also revealed that large Indian business houses, from Reliance Industries to Mahindra and Mahindra and the JSW Group, are exploring investments in Canada's critical mineral projects as a way to secure supply chains. Companies are looking at co-investments at a time when supply chains are getting weaponised, he said.

The Canada track is one of several running in parallel for India. It follows the fourth round of CEPA negotiations concluded just days ago, and lands alongside the just-cleared India-New Zealand FTA and ongoing EFTA engagement, a flurry of trade diplomacy that stands in contrast to the tariff pressure coming from Washington.

For India, a Canadian deal would add energy security and critical minerals to its trade portfolio, while giving Canadian exporters and investors a firmer foothold in the world's fastest-growing large economy. The year-end clock is now ticking on both capitals.
