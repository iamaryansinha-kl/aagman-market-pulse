India-New Zealand FTA: Zero-Duty Deal Set for April 27 Signing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India and New Zealand will sign a comprehensive FTA on April 27, 2026, at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi
  • 02/New Zealand grants zero-duty access on 100% of Indian exports; bilateral trade target is $5 billion in five years
  • 03/Key beneficiaries include textiles, engineering goods, gems and jewellery, and leather sectors

India and New Zealand will sign a comprehensive Free Trade Agreement on April 27, 2026. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal and NZ Trade Minister Todd McClay will attend the ceremony at Bharat Mandapam.

The pact was concluded on December 22, 2025, after nine months of negotiations. It is one of the fastest trade deals sealed by New Delhi.

New Zealand will grant zero-duty access on 100% of Indian exports. India will liberalise tariffs on 70% of its tariff lines, covering 95% of bilateral trade.

Bilateral trade in goods and services currently stands at about $2.4 billion. The target is to double this figure to $5 billion over five years.

Engineering goods exporters currently ship about $150 million annually to New Zealand. Gems and jewellery exports stand at $16.61 million, expected to reach $50 million in three years.

New Zealand has committed to facilitate $20 billion in investments into India over the next 15 years. The deal also covers services, mobility, and regulatory cooperation.

"Indian exporters, having zero duty on all products of exports, see the India-New Zealand FTA as a positive and timely step. Exporters will look for smooth implementation and clear rules of origin," said Ajay Sahai, Director General, FIEO.

Risks remain. New Zealand's market is small with a population of just 5.29 million. Rules of origin compliance will be critical for exporters to claim tariff benefits.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN WILL INDIA AND NEW ZEALAND SIGN THE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT?
THE INDIA-NEW ZEALAND FTA WILL BE SIGNED ON MONDAY, APRIL 27, 2026, AT BHARAT MANDAPAM IN NEW DELHI. NEGOTIATIONS WERE CONCLUDED ON DECEMBER 22, 2025, AFTER NINE MONTHS OF TALKS INITIATED IN MARCH 2025.
WHAT TARIFF BENEFITS DOES THE FTA PROVIDE FOR INDIAN EXPORTERS?
NEW ZEALAND WILL GRANT ZERO-DUTY ACCESS ON 100% OF INDIAN EXPORTS. INDIA WILL LIBERALISE TARIFFS ON APPROXIMATELY 70% OF ITS TARIFF LINES, COVERING 95% OF BILATERAL TRADE VALUE. 57% OF NEW ZEALAND EXPORTS WILL BE DUTY-FREE FROM DAY ONE.
WHAT IS THE BILATERAL TRADE TARGET UNDER THE FTA?
THE TARGET IS TO DOUBLE BILATERAL TRADE IN GOODS AND SERVICES TO ABOUT $5 BILLION WITHIN FIVE YEARS, FROM AN ESTIMATED $2.4 BILLION CURRENTLY. INDIA'S GOODS EXPORTS TO NEW ZEALAND WERE VALUED AT $711 MILLION IN FY2025.
WHICH INDIAN SECTORS ARE EXPECTED TO BENEFIT MOST?
LABOUR-INTENSIVE SECTORS SUCH AS TEXTILES, ENGINEERING GOODS, GEMS AND JEWELLERY, LEATHER, FOOTWEAR, AND AUTO COMPONENTS WILL BENEFIT FROM ELIMINATION OF PREVIOUSLY APPLIED PEAK TARIFFS OF 10%. ENGINEERING GOODS SHIPMENTS ARE CURRENTLY $150 MILLION ANNUALLY.
WHAT ARE THE RISKS AND LIMITATIONS OF THIS FTA?
NEW ZEALAND'S MARKET SIZE IS LIMITED WITH A POPULATION OF ABOUT 5.29 MILLION. RULES OF ORIGIN COMPLIANCE IS CRITICAL FOR EXPORTERS. THE PACT MUST ALSO PASS PARLIAMENTARY SCRUTINY BEFORE FULL IMPLEMENTATION, INCLUDING ENABLING LEGISLATION.
