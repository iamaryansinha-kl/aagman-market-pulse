Bandhan Bank Q4 Profit Up 68% to ₹530 Crore; Stock Hits 52-Week High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bandhan Bank Q4 net profit jumps 68% YoY to ₹530 crore, beating JM Financial estimate by 115%
  • 02/Credit cost falls sharply to 2.0% from 3.9% YoY; provisions drop to ₹677 crore vs expected ₹1,008 crore
  • 03/JM Financial raises target to ₹200 from ₹160; stock surges 10% to 52-week high of ₹199.90

Bandhan Bank shares hit a 52-week high of ₹199.90 on the NSE on April 29. The stock surged over 10% to ₹196.76 by mid-morning. Trading volume exceeded 6.38 crore shares with a value of ₹1,249 crore.

The rally follows Q4FY26 results declared on April 28. Net profit rose 68% year-on-year to ₹530 crore. This beat JM Financial's estimate by 115% and grew 159% sequentially.

Net interest income reached ₹2,795 crore during the quarter. Net interest margin recovered to 6.2% from 5.9% in Q3FY26. The bank showed steady core earnings traction.

Asset quality improved significantly. Gross NPA ratio eased to 3.3%. Credit cost fell sharply to 2.0% from 3.9% a year ago, aided by lower slippages in the Emerging Entrepreneurs Business portfolio.

Provisions dropped to ₹677 crore against an expected ₹1,008 crore. The sharp decline indicates reduced stress in the loan book. Credit cost at 1.9% came well below JM's 3.1% estimate.

Nirmal Bang noted adjusted PAT of ₹534 crore versus their ₹410 crore estimate. The brokerage said the stock trades at 1.2x trailing adjusted book value. This suggests room for further re-rating.

JM Financial upgraded its target price to ₹200 from ₹160 while maintaining an ADD rating. The brokerage now values the bank at 1.1x FY28E book value. Brokerages are turning more constructive on the stock.

Management maintained FY27 exit ROA guidance of 1.6-1.8%. The stock has gained 34% over the past month and 38% year-to-date. It sharply outperformed the Nifty Private Bank index which is down 8%.
