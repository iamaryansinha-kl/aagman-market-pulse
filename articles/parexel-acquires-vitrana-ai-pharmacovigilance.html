Parexel Acquires Vitrana to Advance AI Pharmacovigilance Capabilities

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Parexel acquires Vitrana, an AI-enabled pharmacovigilance platform, for an undisclosed sum on April 30, 2026
  • 02/Noida-based Vitrana brings 600 employees and system-agnostic PV technology to Parexel's Patient Safety Services unit
  • 03/Global pharmacovigilance market is valued at ~$8.99 billion in 2025; Parexel now offers combined PV tech and services

Parexel International Corporation has acquired Vitrana, a provider of AI-enabled pharmacovigilance technology platforms. The deal was announced on April 30, 2026, for an undisclosed sum.

The acquisition gives Parexel an integrated patient safety platform powered by intelligent automation and AI. This accelerates end-to-end pharmacovigilance processes and enhances compliance for clients.

Vitrana's technology is system-agnostic. It integrates with any safety database, allowing customers to deploy capabilities that fit their specific needs without replacing existing infrastructure.

Noida-based Vitrana employs 600 people. Its team and technology will be aligned with Parexel's Patient Safety Services organisation. The company claims its AI reduces case processing time by over 60% and eliminates 75% of manual reconciliation tasks.

Parexel is a global clinical research organisation with approximately 22,000 employees worldwide. Around 6,000 of them are based in India. The company reported an estimated annual revenue of $3.8 billion in 2026.

The global pharmacovigilance market was valued at approximately $8.99 billion in 2025. It is projected to reach $16.62 billion by 2035, growing at a CAGR of 6.34%.

"With Vitrana, we can now offer customers a single partner for both PV technology and services," said Sanjay Vyas, President of Patient Safety Services & Clinical Logistics at Parexel. He also serves as Managing Director of India for the company.

"We're thrilled to join Parexel and further scale our proven technology," said Mohit Gupta, President and CEO of Vitrana. "Together, we can offer improved efficiency, quality and compliance to our customers worldwide."
