Sebi Approves IPOs of Defence Electronics Maker Tonbo Imaging and 3 Others

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Sebi is clearing the decks for more IPOs.

The Securities and Exchange Board of India has approved the initial public offerings of Tonbo Imaging, a defence electronics maker, and three other companies. The approvals come at a time when the primary market is seeing a surge in activity, with several high-profile issues scheduled for July 2026.

Tonbo Imaging is a Bangalore-based defence electronics company. The firm specialises in advanced imaging and sensor systems for military applications, including thermal imaging, night vision, and electro-optical systems. Its products are used by the Indian armed forces and defence public sector undertakings for surveillance, targeting, and reconnaissance missions.

The defence electronics sector is booming. The government's push for indigenisation under the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative has created a massive market opportunity for domestic defence manufacturers. Companies that can develop and produce sophisticated electronics and sensors locally are seeing strong order flows from the military and paramilitary forces.

Tonbo Imaging's IPO will fund capacity expansion and R&D. The company plans to use the proceeds to scale up manufacturing facilities, invest in next-generation sensor technologies, and expand its product portfolio to cover emerging requirements in areas like drone payloads, counter-drone systems, and border surveillance.

The three other companies approved alongside Tonbo span different sectors. Sebi has been processing IPO applications at a rapid pace, reflecting the strong demand from companies to tap public markets while investor sentiment remains favourable. The pipeline includes companies from healthcare, manufacturing, and technology sectors.

The IPO market is on track for a record year. With SBI Funds, Milky Mist, and several other large issues in the queue, 2026 could see the highest number of IPOs in Indian history. The strong retail participation and institutional demand have encouraged companies of all sizes to consider going public.
