SoftBank Shares Surge 20% on OpenAI and SB Energy IPO Plans

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SoftBank Group Corp shares surged nearly 20% in Tokyo, the biggest intraday gain since March 2020
  • 02/OpenAI and SB Energy, both SoftBank-backed companies, are preparing IPO filings in the United States
  • 03/SoftBank holds about 13% stake in OpenAI; analysts say IPOs will bring liquidity and clear market pricing

SoftBank Group Corp shares surged nearly 20% in Tokyo trading on Thursday. The rally followed reports that two SoftBank-backed companies are preparing for US initial public offerings.

OpenAI, creator of ChatGPT, is targeting a public debut this fall. The company is working with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley for a confidential IPO filing as early as Friday.

SB Energy, which builds energy infrastructure for data centers, said Wednesday it would file a confidential draft registration statement for a US IPO. The company has raised over $1.8 billion in the past year from SoftBank, OpenAI, and Ares Management.

SoftBank is one of OpenAI's largest shareholders with roughly a 13% stake. The IPO plans reverse losses from the past week following SoftBank's earnings report.

"This could potentially shrink its discount to net asset value, which is back in the high -20% range," said Bloomberg Intelligence analysts Kirk Boodry and Chris Muckensturm.

Ikuo Mitsui, a fund manager at Aizawa Securities, noted the shift toward liquid assets. "Once these companies go public, SoftBank will gain both liquidity and clear market pricing," he said.
