City Union Bank Broker Call: ICICI Securities Sets ₹325 Target, Retains Buy

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ICICI Securities retains BUY rating on City Union Bank with unchanged target price of ₹325, implying ~17.9% upside from CMP of ₹275.70
  • 02/Q4FY26 loan growth surged 26% YoY, the highest in 13 years, driven by gold loans and MSME focus at 55-60% of the book
  • 03/Bank guides RoA improvement to 1.65-1.7% by Q4FY27 and stable NIM; risks include execution challenges and gold price volatility

ICICI Securities has retained its BUY rating on City Union Bank Ltd (NSE: CUB) with a target price of ₹325. The current market price stands at ₹275.70, offering an upside of approximately 17.9%.

The brokerage issued the research note on April 28, 2026, following the bank's strong Q4FY26 results. The quarter marked a commendable farewell for the outgoing MD and CEO, under whose tenure from FY11-26 deposits, loans, and profit after tax grew 6-7 times each.

Loan growth jumped 26% year-on-year in Q4FY26, the highest in 13 years. Advances reached ₹66,699 crore, up 25.7% YoY, driven primarily by gold loans.

The MSME segment continues to dominate the portfolio at 55-60%, followed by gold loans at 30-35%. The management aims to cap the gold loan mix at about 30%, though it noted this is not a rigid limit.

The bank guided for stable net interest margin and a 10-15 basis point improvement in return on assets to 1.65-1.7% by Q4FY27. Q4FY26 RoA stood at 1.56%.

Asset quality showed multi-year improvement with SMA 2 levels at 0.7% and SMA 0+1+2 at multi-year lows. The bank highlighted its conservatism on per-gram gold pricing, deliberately detached from recent market price jumps.

ICICI Securities values City Union Bank at approximately 1.8 times FY28E adjusted book value. Key risks flagged include execution challenges and gold price volatility.
