DLF to Open Rs 500 Crore Goa Mall by March 2027, Eyes Over Rs 150 Crore Annual Rental Income

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

DLF is taking its mall business to Goa. The country's largest listed developer said its rental arm will open a seven lakh square foot premium shopping mall in the state by February-March next year, with a capital outlay finalised at Rs 500 crore.

The investment has grown with the project's ambition. Earlier estimates for the mall ranged between Rs 300 crore and Rs 350 crore, but the final capital outlay was set at Rs 500 crore, reflecting the scale of the build.

Demand appears to be keeping pace. The mall, named Promenade Goa, has already leased out 70-75% of its space, with PVR among the tenants signed so far. A senior company official said the asset is expected to generate more than Rs 150 crore in annual rental income once operational.

The Goa project fits a broader strategy. DLF's rental business is targeting Rs 10,000 crore in annual revenue by FY30, expanding beyond its National Capital Region stronghold into newer markets and formats. The company has been investing roughly Rs 5,000 crore annually across FY26 and FY27 to grow its rental portfolio, spanning both office and retail assets.

Annuity income is becoming central to DLF's story. While its development business rides the housing cycle, the rental portfolio delivers recurring cash flows, with the company's overall rental portfolio running at high occupancy and exit rentals guided higher each year.

Goa itself has been seeing premium real estate momentum, with luxury villa demand pushing North Goa prices past Rs 30,000 per square foot in some pockets, and the entry of a national-grade mall operator signals the state's growing pull for organised retail beyond the big metros.
