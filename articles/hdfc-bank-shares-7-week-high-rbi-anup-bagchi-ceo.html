HDFC Bank Shares Hit 7-Week High as RBI Weighs Anup Bagchi for CEO Post

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

HDFC Bank shares extended gains on Tuesday, rising to Rs 749.30 apiece, the highest level in nearly seven weeks. The stock has jumped 5% over three straight sessions of gains.

The rally follows reports that the Reserve Bank of India is weighing Anup Bagchi's candidature for the role of CEO and Managing Director at India's largest private bank.

RBI Seeks Feedback

The RBI has sought feedback from the insurance regulator IRDAI and ICICI Bank CEO Sandeep Bakhshi on the candidature of the 55-year-old Bagchi, according to sources familiar with the matter. Bagchi has been managing director and CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance for the past three years.

Before joining the insurer in 2023, he was an executive director at ICICI Bank from 2017, heading wholesale banking, transaction banking, markets group and the proprietary trading group.

Succession Process

HDFC Bank submitted the names of two candidates to the RBI last week, formally kicking off the succession process for Sashidhar Jagdishan, who is due to retire later this year.

The CEO search follows a governance cloud that emerged in March after former part-time Chairman Atanu Chakraborty resigned, saying some practices within the bank did not align with his personal values and ethics. The concerns triggered a sharp selloff, though the stock has recovered following leadership changes.

Brokerage Views

UBS recently added HDFC Bank to its 'APAC Key Call List', maintaining a Buy call with a target price of Rs 1,000, implying over 35% upside. UBS expects the bank's loan growth to improve to 15% over FY27-29, with margins recovering towards 3.5%.

Macquarie maintained its Outperform rating with a target of Rs 1,150, implying nearly 56% upside. The brokerage said an external CEO appointment is the primary catalyst for a stock re-rating.

Nomura maintained Buy with a target of Rs 950, stating that a credible external candidate could offer a longer runway and a cleaner slate for a strategic reset.

Stock Performance

HDFC Bank shares dropped to a fresh 52-week low of Rs 681.90 on September 11, marking a more than 33% fall from the record high of Rs 1,020.50 hit in October last year. The stock has recovered 4% in the past week.
