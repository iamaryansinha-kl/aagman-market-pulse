Capri Global Capital FY26 Profit Jumps 98%, AUM Hits ₹36,623 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Capri Global Capital FY26 PAT surged 98% YoY to ₹948.6 crore, driven by strong lending growth and improved cost efficiency
  • 02/Consolidated AUM expanded 60% to ₹36,623.3 crore as of March 31, 2026; gold loans doubled to ₹16,964.6 crore
  • 03/Company targets ₹55,000 crore AUM by FY28; cost-income ratio improved 1,056 bps to 49.4%

Capri Global Capital Limited (NSE: CGCL) reported a 98% jump in full-year profit. PAT for FY26 stood at ₹948.6 crore, up from ₹478.5 crore in FY25.

The Mumbai-based non-banking finance company saw its AUM surge 60% year-on-year. Consolidated Assets Under Management reached ₹36,623.3 crore as of March 31, 2026.

For Q4 FY26, profit after tax rose 59% to ₹282.8 crore. This was also up 11% sequentially from ₹255.4 crore in Q3 FY26.

Net Interest Income for the full year climbed 50% to ₹1,997.9 crore. Non-Interest Income surged 71% to ₹859.7 crore during the same period.

Operating Profit nearly doubled, rising 97% to ₹1,446.4 crore. The cost-income ratio improved sharply by 1,056 basis points to 49.4% from 59.9%.

Asset quality improved markedly over the year. Gross NPA fell to 0.9% from 1.5%, while Net NPA dropped to 0.5% from 0.9% in FY25.

Gold loans remained the fastest-growing segment, with AUM more than doubling to ₹16,964.6 crore. This now accounts for 46.3% of the total portfolio.

The company expanded its branch network to 1,429 branches. CGCL has set an AUM target of ₹55,000 crore by FY28, implying 25-30% annual growth.
