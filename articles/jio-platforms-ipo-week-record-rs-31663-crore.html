Jio Platforms to Lead Rs 31,663 Crore IPO Week, India's Biggest

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- Two IPOs will raise a combined Rs 31,663 crore next week, opening October 21.
  • •- Jio Platforms' Rs 30,213 crore issue would top Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,900 crore 2024 record.
  • •- Price band Rs 1,065–1,119; grey-market premium near 20%; valuation about Rs 10.3 lakh crore.
  • •- All fresh shares, no offer-for-sale; up to Rs 27,500 crore goes to repay Jio Infocomm debt.
  • •- Nine listings are also scheduled, including Zetwerk, Tonbo Imaging and Fusion CX.
  • •- Tuesday is a Dussehra market holiday; allotments land October 26, listings October 28.

India's primary market is about to find out whether it can absorb the largest cheque in its own history. Next week two companies will raise a combined Rs 31,663 crore through initial public offerings, anchored by Reliance Jio Platforms' Rs 30,213 crore issue — set to surpass Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,900 crore offering of 2024 as the country's biggest public issue ever. Both open for subscription on Wednesday, October 21 and close on Friday, October 23, with the anchor book for Jio opening a day earlier, on October 19. Between the subscriptions and the listings, the market takes Tuesday off for Dussehra — a holiday-shortened week carrying an unprecedented supply.

The Jio numbers are of a different scale from anything the primary market has processed. The price band is fixed at Rs 1,065 to Rs 1,119 per share, with a minimum bid of 12 shares taking the retail entry ticket to Rs 13,428 at the top of the band. At that level the company seeks a valuation of roughly Rs 10.3 lakh crore, which would place it among India's most valuable listed companies on day one. The grey market is already trading the issue at a premium of about Rs 220 — close to 20 per cent — a signal that the unofficial market expects allocation to be scarce. The issue is entirely fresh equity of 27 crore shares with no offer-for-sale component: every rupee goes to Jio Platforms, while Reliance Industries, Meta and Google all retain their existing stakes.

The use of proceeds is the story's second act. Up to Rs 27,500 crore of the net raise is earmarked to repay or prepay borrowings at the telecom subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm, and managing director Akash Ambani has said the debt reduction moves the company toward becoming net debt-free. That framing matters for how the market prices the issue: this is not an exit by insiders but a recapitalisation that converts the balance sheet ahead of the company's next investment cycle. Jio reported a profit of Rs 30,049 crore in FY26 and serves more than 530 million subscribers, and Ambani has anchored the growth pitch to a digital economy he expects to reach $1.4 trillion by FY31 — home broadband, enterprise services, cloud and artificial intelligence the named theatres.

Retail participation is being courted with unusual care. The issue carries a special reservation of up to Rs 2,500 crore for eligible Reliance Industries shareholders and Rs 500 crore for Jio employees, the latter receiving a Rs 54 per share discount. That structure effectively offers the Reliance family of shareholders a pre-allocated allocation in the conglomerate's crown jewel — a recognition that the demand will be overwhelming and that the company would rather its own shareholders were not squeezed out. Allotment is expected to be finalised on October 26 and listing on October 28, closing a week in which the primary market will have consumed most of the country's incremental equity attention.

The other issuer on the calendar is a study in contrast. Paras Healthcare is raising Rs 1,450 crore — Rs 500 crore fresh and Rs 950 crore of offer-for-sale — at a price band of Rs 660 to Rs 693, implying a market capitalisation of about Rs 7,498 crore. The hospital chain runs eight hospitals and 2,211 beds across Gurugram, Panchkula, Patna, Darbhanga, Kanpur, Udaipur, Ranchi and Srinagar, swung from a Rs 57.98 crore loss in FY25 to a Rs 43.83 crore profit in FY26 on income of Rs 1,628.78 crore, and plans to reach 3,011 beds by March 2028 with new hospitals in Gurugram and Ludhiana. Where Jio is a balance-sheet event, Paras is a sector bet: Indian hospital capacity, priced for scarcity.

Around the two issues sit nine scheduled listings, six of them mainboard. HD Fire Protect debuts October 21, Fusion CX on October 22, and four — Tonbo Imaging India, Zetwerk Manufacturing Business, Bonfiglioli Transmissions and Sahajanand Medical Technologies — list on October 23, with their subscriptions closing October 19. Three SME listings fill out the week. The test is twofold: whether the Rs 30,000 crore-plus Jio book builds the way the grey market implies, and whether the rest of the calendar survives the suction. A market that absorbs both is telling you something about the depth of India's retail bid; one that does not will hand the sceptics their best argument of the year.

For the secondary market, the week's significance is quieter but real. A successful Jio listing crystallises a Rs 10.3 lakh crore benchmark for India's digital-economy champions and gives index constructors a new top-tier weight almost immediately; a weak one would recalibrate every pending tech and consumer valuation in the pipeline. The precedents cut both ways — the mega-issues that priced fairly and listed soft have historically taken quarters to repair, while the ones that the grey market underpriced became the decade's great allocations. With a 20 per cent premium already baked in, the grey market has left itself no room for error, which is usually the moment the error arrives. Wednesday's opening print will tell you which version of history this market is about to join.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW BIG IS THE JIO PLATFORMS IPO?
Rs 30,213 crore at a price band of Rs 1,065–1,119, which would make it India's largest public issue, ahead of Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,900 crore in 2024.
WHEN DOES THE ISSUE OPEN?
Public subscription runs October 21–23, the anchor book opens October 19, and listing is tentatively scheduled for October 28.
WHERE WILL THE PROCEEDS GO?
Up to Rs 27,500 crore repays or prepays Reliance Jio Infocomm borrowings, part of the move toward a net debt-free balance sheet; there is no offer-for-sale.
WHAT ELSE IS HAPPENING IN THE IPO MARKET?
Paras Healthcare's Rs 1,450 crore issue opens the same day, and nine listings are scheduled during the week across mainboard and SME segments. GLOSSARY: - IPO: Initial public offering — the first sale of a company's shares to public investors. - Offer-for-sale: A component of an IPO where existing shareholders sell their stakes rather than the company issuing new shares. - Grey-market premium: The unofficial market price of an IPO's allotment before listing. - Anchor book: The portion of an IPO allocated to institutional investors a day before the public issue opens.
