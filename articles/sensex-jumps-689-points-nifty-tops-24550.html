Stock Market Live: Sensex Jumps 689 Points, Nifty Tops 24,550

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The BSE Sensex surged 689.77 points (0.88%) to close at 79,210.07 on April 21, 2026, while the Nifty 50 topped 24,550 intraday
  • 02/All 38 BSE sectors advanced with Realty leading at +3.09%; BSE 500 breadth was 416 advances to 82 declines
  • 03/Investors await Q4 results from Trent, Tech Mahindra, and SBI Life on April 22; US-Iran ceasefire deadline looms

The BSE Sensex surged 689.77 points or 0.88% to settle at 79,210.07 on April 21, 2026. The index opened 96.86 points higher and steadily built momentum through the session.

The Nifty 50 scaled above 24,550 during intraday trade. At 11:09 AM IST, the index stood at 24,554.05, up 189.20 points or 0.78% from the previous close of 24,364.85.

All 38 sectors on the BSE ended in the green. Realty was the top performer, surging 3.09%, while Capital Goods and Metals also hit fresh 52-week highs.

The BSE 500 advance-decline ratio stood at a robust 416 to 82, translating to a 5.07x ratio. This signals a broad-based rally rather than a concentrated move in a few heavyweights.

The S&P BSE 150 Midcap index gained 0.91%, and the S&P BSE 250 Smallcap index rose 1.12%. Lemon Tree Hotels soared 10.26%, the highest gain across BSE 500 stocks.

Among large caps, AU Small Finance Bank gained 3.80%. SBI Life Insurance was the top laggard, falling 3.43% ahead of its quarterly results scheduled for April 22.

Bank of Maharashtra surged 6.90% in mid-cap trade after reporting a 35% YoY jump in Q4 net profit to ₹2,014 crore. The stock had already risen 3.89% by mid-morning.

Foreign institutional investors sold Indian equities worth ₹1,060 crore on April 20. Domestic institutional investors offset this with net buying of ₹2,967 crore on the same day.

The Indian rupee opened lower at 93.30 per US dollar versus Monday's close of 93.12. Brent crude traded around $95 per barrel, staying below the $100 mark.

India VIX remained elevated near 18.79, up 9.21% from previous sessions. Investors are balancing optimism around potential US-Iran peace talks against the looming April 22 ceasefire deadline.
