Infrastructure Projects See Cost Overrun of Rs 2.88 Lakh Crore in August: MoSPI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Infrastructure projects worth more than Rs 150 crore each recorded a cumulative cost overrun of around Rs 2,88,122 crore, according to the monthly government report for August 2026 released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation.

A total of 1,731 ongoing infrastructure projects across 17 central ministries and departments were monitored. Their total revised cost has escalated to Rs 33,60,069 crore, compared with an original cost of Rs 30,71,947 crore.

The report did not specify the actual number of projects that faced cost overruns.

Cumulative expenditure incurred on these projects stands at Rs 16.33 lakh crore, accounting for approximately 48.59% of the revised project cost, indicating steady progress in implementation, MoSPI said.

A significant proportion of projects are at advanced stages, with 647 projects (38%) achieving over 80% physical progress, while 312 projects (18%) have crossed 80% financial completion.

The Transport & Logistics sector accounts for the highest number of ongoing projects at 1,191, with revised estimates of Rs 16.05 lakh crore, underscoring the priority to connectivity-driven infrastructure growth.

Among ministries, the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways has the highest number of projects at 982 (57%), with a share of total revised project cost of Rs 9.52 lakh crore (28%). The Ministry of Railways has 147 ongoing projects with a revised cost of Rs 3.14 lakh crore, while the Coal Ministry accounts for 122 projects worth Rs 2.22 lakh crore.

The 1,731 projects include 715 mega projects (cost of Rs 1,000 crore and above) with an original cost of Rs 25.89 lakh crore, and 1,016 major projects (Rs 150-1,000 crore) amounting to Rs 4.83 lakh crore.

The energy sector accounts for 32% of aggregated revised cost (Rs 10.95 lakh crore) across 223 projects, while Water & Sanitation projects account for Rs 2.04 lakh crore across 53 projects.
