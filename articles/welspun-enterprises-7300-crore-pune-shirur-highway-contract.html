Welspun Enterprises Bags ₹7,300 Crore Pune-Shirur Highway Contract from MSIDC

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Welspun Enterprises receives Letter of Award dated April 29, 2026, for a ₹7,300 crore six-lane partially elevated highway on NH-753F
  • 02/Order book expands 41% to ₹18,755 crore from ₹13,341 crore as of December 31, 2025, across water, tunnelling and transportation verticals
  • 03/Project spans 53.40 km on DBFOT toll model with 29-year concession including 4-year construction window; watch for financial closure

Welspun Enterprises Ltd (NSE: WELENT) has received a Letter of Award from Maharashtra State Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd (MSIDC). The contract, dated April 29, 2026, is for a six-lane partially elevated highway corridor on NH-753F.

The project covers the Pune-Shirur section from Km 10+600 to Km 64+000. The minimum design length is 53.40 km. The estimated project cost is approximately ₹7,300 crore.

It will be executed on a DBFOT toll basis with a 29-year sub-concession period, including a four-year construction window. The award is structured under a government-to-government arrangement between MoRTH and the Maharashtra Public Works Department, with MSIDC as the implementing agency.

The new contract pushes Welspun's outstanding order book to approximately ₹18,755 crore. This is up from ₹13,341 crore as of December 31, 2025, after adjusting for Q4 FY26 execution. The jump represents a 41% increase in backlog.

The order book is distributed across three verticals. Water contributes ₹10,813 crore, including ₹5,393 crore from O&M and asset replacement. Tunnelling stands at ₹1,791 crore, while transportation is now ₹6,152 crore.

Managing Director Sandeep Garg said the addition rebalances the order book across verticals and improves revenue visibility. Chairman B.K. Goenka noted that such projects are critical to unlocking regional economic potential and creating long-term value for communities.

The corridor will improve connectivity across Wagholi, Kharadi, Lonikand, Ranjangaon and Shikrapur. It is expected to ease congestion and improve freight movement across these key economic nodes.

Welspun shares were trading at ₹530.50 on April 30, down 1.78%. The stock has a 52-week high of ₹580 and a market capitalisation of approximately ₹7,476 crore. Waya Financial Technologies has a BUY rating with a target price of ₹750, expecting 44% upside.
