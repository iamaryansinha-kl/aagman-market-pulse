Sebi Bars Kore Digital Promoters Over Rs 541 Crore Revenue Misstatement, Flags Forged Audit Reports

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ Sebi has restrained MD Ravindra Doshi, CEO Chaitanya Doshi and CFO Kashmira Doshi from dealing in Kore Digital shares.
  • •- 02/ The regulator barred the company and the three executives from raising money from the public.
  • •- 03/ Consolidated financials were prima facie misstated by about Rs 541.3 crore in FY25 and FY26 — roughly 73% of revenue over the period.
  • •- 04/ Around 75% of revenue came from three recently acquired subsidiaries that site visits could not locate.
  • •- 05/ NSE has been directed to block Kore's migration from the SME platform to the main board; a forensic audit will cover June 2023 to March 2026.

## The order

Capital markets regulator Sebi has passed an interim order against SME-listed Kore Digital and three key managerial personnel after a probe found prima facie evidence of manipulated financial statements, allegedly non-genuine subsidiaries, suspicious accounting entries and diversion of preferential issue proceeds.

Managing director Ravindra Doshi, chief executive officer Chaitanya Doshi and chief financial officer Kashmira Doshi have been restrained from buying, selling or otherwise dealing in Kore Digital shares until further orders. The company and the three individuals are also barred from accessing the securities market to raise money from the public.

Sebi directed NSE not to allow Kore Digital to migrate from the NSE Emerge SME platform to the main board until regulatory clearance, and a forensic auditor will examine the company's books from its June 2023 listing through March 31, 2026.

## The growth story that wasn't

Kore's revenue from operations rose from Rs 21.27 crore in FY23 to Rs 408 crore in FY26 — a near-twentyfold jump. On average, around 75% of consolidated revenue came from three subsidiaries acquired along the way: Franken Telecom, Wolter Infratech and KDL Realinfra.

Sebi's interim order says consolidated financial statements were prima facie misstated by about Rs 541.3 crore during FY25 and FY26 — roughly 73% of total revenue over the period.

## The phantom subsidiaries

The red flags pile up. The three subsidiaries were incorporated only months before Kore acquired them, shared the same registered address, and had little or no filing history with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

GST registrations of Franken and Wolter were cancelled shortly after registration; KDL Realinfra's registration became inactive on the same day it was registered. Surprise site visits by NSE in June 2026 failed to establish the presence of these companies at their stated addresses — the same finding applied to several step-down subsidiaries.

## Forged audit trails

The audit records raised still more concerns. CA Riya Goyal, whose name appeared on the subsidiaries' FY25 audited accounts, told Sebi she had only certified provisional financial statements and had not conducted their statutory audit — prima facie indicating the audit reports carrying her signature and stamp were forged.

In another case, limited review reports attributed to CA Nikhil Gupta carried UDIN numbers generated only after Sebi sought information, though the reports were purportedly signed earlier.

## FAQ Q: Who has Sebi restrained? A/01/ Kore Digital's MD Ravindra Doshi, CEO Chaitanya Doshi and CFO Kashmira Doshi, plus the company's access to public fundraising.

Q: How big is the alleged misstatement? A/02/ About Rs 541.3 crore across FY25 and FY26 — roughly 73% of revenue over the period.

Q: What happens to the company's NSE listing? A/03/ Migration from the NSE Emerge SME platform to the main board is blocked pending regulatory clearance; a forensic audit will cover June 2023 to March 2026.

Q: How did the scheme work? A/04/ Revenue was consolidated from three recently created subsidiaries that shared one address, had defunct GST registrations, could not be found at their stated locations, and came with apparently forged audit reports.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS - Revenue grew 21 Cr to 408 Cr in three years; ~75% from three acquired subsidiaries. - Rs 541.3 crore misstatement ≈ 73% of two years' revenue. - Subsidiaries shared an address, lacked GST registrations, and weren't found at their stated sites. - Forged audit signatures and back-generated UDIN numbers flagged. - Main-board migration blocked; forensic audit ordered.
