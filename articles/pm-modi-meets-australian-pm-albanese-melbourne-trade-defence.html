PM Modi Meets Australian PM Albanese in Melbourne; Trade, Defence, Critical Minerals on Agenda

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is in Melbourne for high-stakes diplomatic talks.

PM Modi met Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese for bilateral discussions covering trade, defence cooperation, and critical minerals. The meeting is the centrepiece of Modi's visit to Australia and underscores the deepening strategic partnership between the two countries.

Trade is a priority. Both leaders discussed ways to expand bilateral trade, which currently stands at around $25 billion. A comprehensive economic cooperation agreement is under negotiation to reduce tariffs and improve market access in goods, services, and investment.

Defence cooperation has advanced significantly. The two countries are part of the Quad security dialogue alongside the United States and Japan. They have signed mutual logistics support agreements and conduct regular naval exercises. Australia sees India as a key partner in maintaining Indo-Pacific stability.

Critical minerals dominated the economic agenda. Australia is rich in lithium, cobalt, and rare earth elements that India needs for its electric vehicle and renewable energy transition. A supply chain partnership would reduce both countries' dependence on Chinese mineral processing.

Migration and education were also discussed. Australia is a popular destination for Indian students and skilled migrants. Both sides are working to streamline visa processes and recognise professional qualifications.

The meeting signals that India-Australia relations have moved beyond transactional diplomacy to a genuine strategic partnership. Both countries share concerns about Chinese assertiveness and are building a network of like-minded democracies in the region.
