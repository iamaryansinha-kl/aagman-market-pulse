Dunzo's Kabeer Biswas Raises $11 Million for AI Startup from Peak XV, Others

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kabeer Biswas, founder of troubled quick-commerce startup Dunzo, has raised $11 million for a new artificial intelligence venture
  • 02/Peak XV Partners and other investors participated in the funding round, backing Biswas in his pivot to the AI sector
  • 03/The move marks Biswas's return to entrepreneurship after Dunzo's operational and financial struggles in the hyper-competitive quick-commerce market

Kabeer Biswas has secured $11 million in funding for his new AI startup. The Dunzo founder is pivoting to artificial intelligence.

Peak XV Partners co-led the investment round. Other investors also participated in the early-stage funding.

Biswas founded Dunzo in 2015 as a hyperlocal delivery platform. The startup once commanded a valuation exceeding $700 million.

Dunzo later faced severe financial stress. Operational challenges and intense competition from Zomato's Blinkit and Swiggy Instamart strained resources.

The company struggled to raise fresh capital at viable terms. Employee departures and delayed salaries marked its final operational phase.

Biswas's new AI venture signals a sector shift. He joins a growing list of Indian founders moving from consumer tech to enterprise AI.

The $11 million raise provides runway to build and iterate. Specific product details remain undisclosed as the startup operates in stealth.
