Nifty Week Ahead: Crucial 24,500 Test To Define Market Direction

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty closed at 24,056 after a subdued week, gaining just 42.90 points (+0.18%) amid range-bound trade
  • 02/The index faces a critical resistance cluster between 24,160 and 24,500, where the 100-day and 100-week moving averages converge
  • 03/Pharma, Media, and Midcap indexes show relative strength, while Energy and IT lag; a decisive breakout above 24,500 could trigger short-covering

The Nifty50 ended the week at 24,056.00, barely changed with a gain of 42.90 points or 0.18%. The index oscillated in a narrow 476.65-point range, touching a high of 24,261.60 and a low of 23,784.95.

Volatility stayed contained. The India VIX rose a marginal 0.62% to 13.05, reflecting the lack of directional conviction among participants.

The index is now confronting a formidable resistance zone between 24,160 and 24,500. The 100-day moving average sits at 24,161, while the 100-week moving average is at 24,504. This cluster makes the zone technically significant.

Technical analyst Milan Vaishnav notes that unless Nifty registers a decisive move above this band, the ongoing rebound will remain tentative. A sustained breakout could trigger stronger short-covering and improve the medium-term outlook.

Immediate support levels are placed at 23,900 and 23,750. Failure to clear the 24,160-24,500 hurdle may keep the index confined to a broad consolidation phase.

The weekly RSI stands at 48.01, remaining neutral without any notable divergence. The weekly MACD stays bullish and above its signal line. The latest weekly candle is a Doji, reflecting indecision.

Sector rotation data from the Relative Rotation Graph shows Pharma, Media, and the Midcap 100 index in the leading quadrant. The Energy, PSE, Metal, and Infrastructure indexes have rolled into the weakening quadrant.

The IT sector continues to languish in the lagging quadrant, alongside Services, PSU Banks, Financial Services, and Auto. FMCG and Realty remain in the improving quadrant.
