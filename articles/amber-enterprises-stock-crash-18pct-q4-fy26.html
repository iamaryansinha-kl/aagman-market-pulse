Amber Enterprises Stock Crashes 18% After Q4: Key Reasons for Investors

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Amber Enterprises shares crash 18% on May 18, worst single-day drop since May 2022, after Q4 FY26 results
  • 02/Reported PAT rose 15% YoY to Rs 134 cr, but adjusted PAT fell 39% to Rs 70 cr due to heavy JV losses
  • 03/Management guides for 50-100 bps consolidated margin pressure ahead; Motilal Oswal retains Buy rating

Amber Enterprises India Ltd (NSE: AMBER) shares crashed nearly 18% on Monday, May 18, 2026. The stock fell to Rs 6,980 apiece, on track for its worst single-day decline in nearly four years.

The sharp selloff followed Q4 FY26 results announced on Saturday. Consolidated net profit rose 15% year-on-year to Rs 134 crore. Revenue from operations grew over 10% to Rs 4,148 crore against Rs 3,754 crore in the year-ago period.

Operating EBITDA increased 15% YoY to Rs 362 crore. Gross margins stood at around 19%. The consumer durables division grew 14% in FY26, while electronics surged 49%.

However, adjusted profit after tax declined 39% YoY to Rs 70.4 crore. Analysts at Motilal Oswal had estimated Rs 110 crore. The shortfall stemmed from heavy losses at a joint venture.

"Adj PAT declined mainly due to heavy losses booked by one of its JVs due to unexpected and unforeseen developments outside its control," Motilal Oswal said. A large customer suspended invoice payments, causing significant operational and financial stress.

During the earnings concall, management guided for 50-100 basis points of margin pressure on a consolidated basis going forward. This forward-looking caution spooked investors despite headline profit growth.

The stock has fallen 17% in one week and 11% in one month. It remains up 10% year-to-date in 2026 and has gained 235% over three years. Motilal Oswal maintains a 'Buy' rating on the stock.
