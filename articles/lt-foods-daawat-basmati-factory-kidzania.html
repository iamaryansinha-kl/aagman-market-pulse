LT Foods Opens Daawat Basmati Factory at KidZania for Children

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LT Foods launches experiential Daawat Basmati Factory at KidZania India for children aged 4 to 14
  • 02/Each 25-minute session simulates 7 stages from paddy procurement to packaging; participants get a 200g rice pack
  • 03/The FMCG major reported FY25 consolidated revenue of ₹8,773 crore with a 21% five-year PAT CAGR

LT Foods Ltd. has opened an experiential Daawat Basmati Factory at KidZania India. The Delhi-based FMCG company launched the installation on April 28, 2026.

The facility targets children aged 4 to 14. Each session runs 25 minutes and assigns participants the role of Production Engineer.

Children navigate a simulated processing line with seven stages. These cover paddy procurement, pre-cleaning, ageing, de-husking, de-stoning, colour sorting, and packaging.

Every child completing the session receives a 200g pack of Daawat Basmati. They also get a digital certificate to mark the completion.

Ashwani Arora, Managing Director and CEO of LT Foods, said the partnership builds consumer awareness from an early age. He added it educates children on quality standards and the production process behind the brand.

Sanjeev Kumar, Chairman of KidZania India, said the installation connects children with a product present in most Indian households. KidZania operates edutainment centres in Mumbai and Delhi NCR with over 100 occupation scenarios.

LT Foods reported consolidated revenue of approximately ₹8,773 crore for FY25. The company has delivered a five-year revenue CAGR of 16 percent and a PAT CAGR of 21 percent.

The company sells products in over 80 countries. It owns the Royal brand in North America and the Daawat brand in India. LT Foods shares traded at ₹420.50 on April 22, 2026, with a market capitalisation of ₹14,706 crore.
