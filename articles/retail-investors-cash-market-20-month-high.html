Retail Investors Shrug Off West Asia Crisis, Cash Market ADT Hits 20-Month High

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NSE cash segment ADT surged to ₹1,34,709 crore in April, highest in 20 months and 8% above March
  • 02/Retail investors continued buying despite West Asia crisis, with demat accounts now exceeding 24 crore
  • 03/SIP flows stayed strong at ₹30,000 crore monthly; large-cap earnings growth of 12-14% expected in FY27

Average daily turnover in the NSE cash segment touched ₹1,34,709 crore in April. This marks a 20-month high, rising 8% from ₹1,25,169 crore in March.

Overall cash market turnover on NSE stood at ₹26.94 lakh crore last month. This represents a 13% increase over March's ₹23.78 lakh crore.

Retail investors stayed invested despite volatility sparked by the West Asia crisis. Crude oil touched $104 a barrel, constrained by Strait of Hormuz transit.

The Sensex tanked nearly 3,900 points across five trading sessions in April. It still closed the month up 4,965 points or 7%, while Nifty gained 6%.

Foreign portfolio investors pulled out ₹60,847 crore in April. They have withdrawn ₹1.92 lakh crore in the last four months of 2026.

"The boost in cash market turnover during heightened volatility shows the maturity of retail investors," said Tanvi Kanchan, Associate Director at Anand Rathi Shares and Stock Brokers.

Demat accounts now exceed 24 crore, while monthly SIP flows hold steady at ₹30,000 crore. Kanchan noted that investors who stayed the course through uncertainty will benefit most.

Large-cap valuations have normalised after a 15-16% drop from peak in dollar terms. Earnings growth of 12-14% is expected in FY27, which bodes well for long-term investors.
