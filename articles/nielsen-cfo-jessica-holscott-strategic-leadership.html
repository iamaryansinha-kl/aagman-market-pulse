Nielsen CFO Jessica Holscott: Role Shifts From Financial Oversight to Strategic Leadership

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nielsen CFO Jessica Holscott says the CFO role has shifted from financial oversight to strategic leadership in media and entertainment.
  • 02/Nielsen has refinanced over $7 billion in debt in the past six months and paid down approximately $500 million.
  • 03/India serves as Nielsen's primary "test-and-learn" market for next-generation measurement solutions and workforce expansion.

Jessica Holscott, Chief Financial Officer at Nielsen, says the CFO role in media and entertainment has shifted from financial oversight to strategic leadership.

In an exclusive interview with businessline, Holscott described her daily focus as driving capital allocation toward innovation and growth.

She evaluates ROI across fragmented media channels and partners with the Board on long-term strategy.

Nielsen is over three years into private ownership under Elliot and Brookfield after a $16 billion buyout.

Holscott said private ownership has allowed the company to transform its business with key talent in India.

The company has generated cash to reinvest into growth products including Nielsen ONE Ads, streaming solutions, and Gracenote.

India is a key growth market and strategic hub for engineering, data science, analytics, and finance.

Nielsen uses India as a "test-and-learn" country for next-generation measurement solutions.

Holscott said the talent pool in India is world-class and the company plans to increase its workforce there.

Debt management has been a priority. Nielsen refinanced over $7 billion in debt in the past six months.

The company issued $1.2 billion notes in November 2025 and $1.5 billion notes in January 2027.

It also refinanced approximately $4.6 billion in term loan Bs, generating interest savings for reinvestment.

Nielsen used proceeds from asset sales and strong free cash flow to pay down about $500 million of debt.

Client retention remains high at 99%, reflecting trust in Nielsen's data quality.

The CTV audience base grew 85% last year, and Gracenote provides metadata for content discovery.

Holscott said Nielsen is preparing for optionality, whether through an IPO or a strategic sale.

The company has strong revenue growth, EBITDA margins, and free cash flow generation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW HAS THE CFO ROLE CHANGED IN MEDIA AND ENTERTAINMENT ACCORDING TO JESSICA HOLSCOTT?
HOLSCOTT STATES THE ROLE HAS SHIFTED FROM FINANCIAL OVERSIGHT TO STRATEGIC LEADERSHIP, FOCUSING ON CAPITAL ALLOCATION TOWARD INNOVATION, EVALUATING ROI ACROSS FRAGMENTED MEDIA CHANNELS, AND PARTNERING WITH THE BOARD ON LONG-TERM STRATEGY.
WHAT IS NIELSEN'S DEBT MANAGEMENT STRATEGY?
NIELSEN HAS REFINANCED OVER $7 BILLION IN DEBT IN THE PAST SIX MONTHS, INCLUDING $1.2 BILLION NOTES IN NOVEMBER 2025, $1.5 BILLION NOTES IN JANUARY 2027, AND $4.6 BILLION IN TERM LOAN B'S. IT ALSO PAID DOWN APPROXIMATELY $500 MILLION OF DEBT USING ASSET SALE PROCEEDS AND FREE CASH FLOW.
WHAT ROLE DOES INDIA PLAY IN NIELSEN'S GLOBAL STRATEGY?
INDIA IS NIELSEN'S KEY GROWTH MARKET AND STRATEGIC HUB FOR ENGINEERING, DATA SCIENCE, ANALYTICS, AND FINANCE. THE COMPANY USES INDIA AS A "TEST-AND-LEARN" COUNTRY FOR NEXT-GENERATION MEASUREMENT SOLUTIONS AND PLANS TO EXPAND ITS WORKFORCE THERE.
WHAT IS NIELSEN'S CLIENT RETENTION RATE?
NIELSEN'S CLIENT RETENTION RATE IS AS HIGH AS 99%, WHICH HOLSCOTT SAYS REFLECTS THE TRUST CLIENTS HAVE IN THE COMPANY'S DATA QUALITY.
IS NIELSEN PLANNING TO GO PUBLIC AGAIN?
HOLSCOTT SAID NIELSEN IS PREPARING FOR "OPTIONALITY" WHETHER THAT IS AN IPO OR A STRATEGIC SALE. THE COMPANY WANTS TO ENSURE STRONG REVENUE GROWTH, STRONG EBITDA MARGINS, AND FREE CASH FLOW GENERATION BEFORE ANY EXIT.
