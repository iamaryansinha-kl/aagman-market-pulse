Jio IPO 2026: How Ambani's Project Jupiter Built India's Biggest Listing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Reliance executed a secret initiative codenamed Project Jupiter to prepare Jio's IPO, filing the draft prospectus on June 19, 2026 with 19 banks
  • 02/The IPO structure shifted from an offer-for-sale to an all-primary issue, ensuring roughly $4 billion raised stays with the company in India
  • 03/Existing investors KKR, Meta, and Alphabet agreed to dilute about 8% on a pro-rata basis after some shareholders baulked at the initial valuation

Reliance Industries Ltd. ran a confidential initiative called Project Jupiter to prepare Jio Platforms Ltd. for India's biggest IPO. The draft prospectus was filed on June 19, 2026 with 19 investment banks on the mandate.

Only a small circle of executives knew the plan. Draft documents were circulated physically, emails were minimised, and meetings were restricted to the highest levels.

Chief Financial Officer V. Srikanth, KR Raja, and Jio executive Anshuman Thakur led the effort. Kotak Mahindra Capital and Morgan Stanley were the first banks brought in before the syndicate expanded.

The IPO structure changed significantly. Reliance originally planned an offer-for-sale where existing investors would sell about 2.8% of Jio. It switched to an all-primary issuance after some shareholders resisted the valuation amid weak markets.

Existing investors KKR & Co., Meta Platforms Inc., and Alphabet Inc. ultimately agreed to dilute roughly 8% of their holdings on a pro-rata basis. This preserved their relative ownership while meeting public float requirements.

In September 2025, Sebi eased minimum dilution rules for companies valued above ₹5 trillion ($53 billion). The threshold dropped from 5% to 2.5%, clearing a regulatory hurdle for the listing.

The government formally notified the revised norms on March 13, 2026. The filing was deferred twice earlier due to weak market conditions and pending rule changes.

Reliance shares closed flat ahead of the announcement. Jio's IPO is expected to raise roughly $4 billion, making it the largest stock market listing in Indian history.

WHAT IS PROJECT JUPITER?

ONLY A SMALL CIRCLE OF EXECUTIVES AND BANKERS KNEW OF THE PLAN, WITH DOCUMENTS CIRCULATED PHYSICALLY AND DIGITAL COMMUNICATION MINIMISED TO MAINTAIN SECRECY.

THE DRAFT PROSPECTUS FOR JIO'S IPO WAS FILED ON JUNE 19, 2026. THERE ARE 19 INVESTMENT BANKS ON THE MANDATE, INCLUDING KOTAK MAHINDRA CAPITAL AND MORGAN STANLEY AS THE FIRST BANKS BROUGHT INTO THE PROJECT.
HOW MUCH IS JIO EXPECTED TO RAISE THROUGH THE IPO?
THE IPO IS EXPECTED TO RAISE ROUGHLY $4 BILLION (APPROXIMATELY ₹33,600 CRORE). THE STRUCTURE WAS CHANGED TO AN ALL-PRIMARY ISSUANCE, ENSURING THE ENTIRE AMOUNT REMAINS WITH THE COMPANY AND STAYS IN INDIA.
WHAT REGULATORY CHANGE HELPED ENABLE JIO'S IPO?
IN SEPTEMBER 2025, SEBI EASED MINIMUM DILUTION REQUIREMENTS FOR COMPANIES VALUED ABOVE ₹5 TRILLION ($53 BILLION). THE THRESHOLD WAS REDUCED FROM 5% TO 2.5%. THE GOVERNMENT FORMALLY NOTIFIED THESE CHANGES ON MARCH 13, 2026.
WHICH EXISTING INVESTORS ARE DILUTING THEIR STAKE AND BY HOW MUCH?
EXISTING INVESTORS INCLUDING KKR & CO., META PLATFORMS INC., AND ALPHABET INC. AGREED TO DILUTE ABOUT 8% OF THEIR HOLDINGS ON A PRO-RATA BASIS. THIS ALLOWS THE COMPANY TO MEET PUBLIC FLOAT REQUIREMENTS WHILE PRESERVING THEIR RELATIVE OWNERSHIP PERCENTAGES.
