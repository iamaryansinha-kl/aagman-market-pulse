Nifty Slips Into Red By Midday; HDFC Life, Bajaj Auto Buck Trend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex drops 32.92 points to 77,925.60 by 12:30 PM on May 7, while Nifty50 barely holds green at 24,355.25 after a much stronger gap-up open
  • 02/HDFC Life Insurance leads Nifty50 gainers with a 3.61% rally to ₹628.25, followed by Bajaj Auto up 3.40% to ₹10,670
  • 03/Traders eye Nifty support at 24,100–24,000 and resistance at 24,600; Bank Nifty faces hurdles at 56,800–57,000

Indian markets surrendered their early morning gains by midday on Thursday. The BSE Sensex stood at 77,925.60, down 32.92 points or 0.04 per cent. The index had opened at 78,339.24.

The Nifty50 traded at 24,355.25, up just 24.30 points or 0.10 per cent. It was well below the gap-up open at 24,398.50 that traders had expected. The morning rally failed to sustain through the session.

HDFC Life Insurance was the top Nifty50 gainer, jumping 3.61 per cent to ₹628.25. It had closed at ₹606.35 on Wednesday and opened at ₹611. Bajaj Auto surged 3.40 per cent to ₹10,670 from ₹10,319.

Mahindra & Mahindra added 2.42 per cent to ₹3,380.80. Grasim Industries and NTPC each gained 1.62 per cent. On the losing side, Titan Company fell 1.73 per cent to ₹4,284.

Hindustan Unilever dropped 1.45 per cent to ₹2,283.60. TCS slipped 1.08 per cent to ₹2,409.20 and Nestle India shed 1.06 per cent to ₹1,470.40.

Broader market breadth remained positive with 2,685 advances against 1,313 declines. A total of 180 stocks hit 52-week highs while 16 touched 52-week lows.

Foreign Institutional Investors sold ₹5,834 crore on Wednesday. Domestic Institutional Investors bought ₹6,836 crore. Brent crude held in the $95–97 range.

Technical analysts say Nifty must hold 24,100–24,000 to preserve the recovery structure. A break above 24,600 could open the path toward 24,800–25,000.
