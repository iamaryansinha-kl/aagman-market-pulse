InCred Holdings IPO: Firm to Raise $132 Million via Fresh Issue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/InCred Holdings plans to raise ₹1,250 crore ($131.8 million) through a fresh issue as part of its IPO
  • 02/Existing shareholders KKR and V'Ocean Investments will sell about 99 million shares in the offering
  • 03/Proceeds will be invested in subsidiary InCred Financial Services; market conditions remain challenging

InCred Holdings Ltd plans to raise ₹1,250 crore ($131.8 million) through a fresh issue of shares. The company filed draft IPO papers on Thursday.

The offering will also include an offer for sale. Existing shareholders KKR India Financial Investments and V'Ocean Investments will sell about 99 million shares.

InCred Holdings is the parent of InCred Financial Services. The non-banking financial company offers personal, student and secured business loans.

Since its inception in 2016, the company has disbursed more than ₹25,000 crore. It serves over 400,000 customers through a network of more than 140 branches.

The company's profit rose nearly 21% to ₹373 crore in fiscal year 2025. The draft papers were filed with India's markets regulator.

The IPO proceeds will be invested in subsidiary InCred Financial Services. This is stated in the draft prospectus filed by the company.

The offering comes amid slowing Indian equity markets. Geopolitical uncertainty, tightening liquidity and sustained foreign outflows have pressured sentiment.

InCred had confidentially filed draft IPO papers with SEBI in November. The public filing follows the confidential submission process.
