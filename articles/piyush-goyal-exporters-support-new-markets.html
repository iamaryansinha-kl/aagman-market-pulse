India Exports FY26 at $441.78B: Goyal Pledges Support for New Markets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal assured exporters of sustained support to reduce entry barriers and enhance ease of doing business on April 27
  • 02/India's goods exports in FY26 grew barely 1% YoY to $441.78 billion amid West Asia crisis and rising shipping costs
  • 03/Progress under the Export Promotion Mission was reviewed; Goyal encouraged EPCs to increase the number of active exporters

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal met industry representatives on Monday, April 27. He assured sustained government support to reduce export market entry barriers and enhance ease of doing business.

Exporters from gems & jewellery, apparel, leather, engineering goods, textiles, and pharmaceuticals attended the meeting. They raised concerns over high compliance costs and stringent testing requirements facing MSMEs.

India's goods exports in FY26 grew barely 1% year-on-year to $441.78 billion. The prolonged West Asia crisis has disrupted sea routes and pushed up shipping and insurance costs.

"The Minister reaffirmed the government's commitment to strengthening a facilitative trade ecosystem through ongoing reforms, targeted support measures and close collaboration with industry" — Commerce Department statement.

Discussions reviewed progress under the Export Promotion Mission, the government's flagship exporter support scheme. Goyal encouraged Export Promotion Councils to increase the number of active exporters.

Representatives from carpets, handicrafts, agriculture, processed food, IT and software also participated. The government pledged to accelerate export growth and position India as a trusted global supply partner.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID PIYUSH GOYAL ASSURE EXPORTERS DURING THE MEETING?
COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY MINISTER PIYUSH GOYAL ASSURED EXPORTERS OF SUSTAINED GOVERNMENT SUPPORT, INCLUDING FACILITATION UNDER ONGOING SCHEMES, TARGETED INTERVENTIONS TO REDUCE ENTRY BARRIERS, AND STEPS TO ENHANCE EASE OF DOING BUSINESS.
WHICH SECTORS PARTICIPATED IN THE MEETING WITH PIYUSH GOYAL?
REPRESENTATIVES FROM GEMS & JEWELLERY, APPAREL, LEATHER, ENGINEERING GOODS, COTTON TEXTILES, MAN-MADE AND TECHNICAL TEXTILES, CARPETS, HANDICRAFTS, AGRICULTURE, PROCESSED FOOD, PHARMACEUTICALS, AND IT AND SOFTWARE ATTENDED THE MEETING.
WHAT WERE THE KEY CONCERNS RAISED BY EXPORTERS?
INDUSTRY REPRESENTATIVES RAISED CONCERNS OVER HIGH COMPLIANCE COSTS, STRINGENT TESTING REQUIREMENTS, AND OTHER CHALLENGES FACED BY MSMES IN ENTERING EXPORT MARKETS.
WHAT IS INDIA'S EXPORT PERFORMANCE IN FY26?
INDIA'S GOODS EXPORTS IN FY26 REGISTERED BARELY 1 PERCENT GROWTH YEAR-ON-YEAR TO $441.78 BILLION, AFFECTED BY THE PROLONGED WEST ASIA CRISIS THAT DISRUPTED SEA ROUTES AND INCREASED SHIPPING AND INSURANCE COSTS.
WHAT IS THE EXPORT PROMOTION MISSION?
THE EXPORT PROMOTION MISSION (EPM) IS THE GOVERNMENT'S FLAGSHIP SCHEME TO SUPPORT EXPORTERS. PROGRESS UNDER EPM WAS REVIEWED AT THE MEETING, AND GOYAL ENCOURAGED EXPORT PROMOTION COUNCILS TO TAKE STEPS TO INCREASE THE NUMBER OF ACTIVE EXPORTERS.
