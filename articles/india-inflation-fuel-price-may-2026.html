India Inflation May 2026: Fuel Hike to Push CPI Higher as WPI Hits 8.3%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's retail inflation rose to 3.48% in April 2026 while wholesale inflation surged to 8.3%, a 42-month high
  • 02/Fuel and power inflation jumped to 24.71% from 1.05% in March after petrol and diesel prices were hiked by Rs 4 per litre
  • 03/RBI expected to hold repo rate at 5.25% as it monitors the pass-through from wholesale to retail prices and monsoon impact

India's retail inflation, measured by CPI, rose modestly to 3.48 per cent in April 2026. This is up from 3.40 per cent in March, remaining below the RBI's medium-term target of 4 per cent.

Wholesale inflation painted a dramatically different picture. WPI inflation surged to 8.3 per cent in April from 3.88 per cent in March. This marks a 42-month high, driven largely by soaring fuel and energy prices.

The biggest jump came from the fuel and power category. Inflation in this segment shot up to 24.71 per cent from just 1.05 per cent a month earlier. Crude petroleum inflation climbed more than 88 per cent.

Petrol and diesel prices were raised by Rs 4 per litre in two installments. This is the first fuel price hike in four years, adding significant inflationary pressure across the economy.

Economists estimate the fuel price hike will impact retail inflation by around 20 basis points. The full impact, along with recent milk price increases, will likely be reflected in the June CPI data.

Economists expect wholesale inflation to touch 9 per cent in May as the West Asia crisis keeps global crude prices elevated. The high global prices will gradually feed into domestic fuel, manufacturing and transport costs.

The RBI is expected to maintain a cautious pause on interest rates. The repo rate will likely stay on hold at 5.25 per cent as the central bank monitors global crude prices, monsoon impact, and the pass-through of wholesale inflation to retail prices.

The inflation outlook also depends on the monsoon and any El Nino impact. Weather-related disruptions may further pressure food prices, which carry a high weight in the CPI basket.

WHAT IS THE CURRENT RETAIL AND WHOLESALE INFLATION IN INDIA?
INDIA'S RETAIL INFLATION (CPI) ROSE TO 3.48 PER CENT IN APRIL 2026 FROM 3.40 PER CENT IN MARCH. WHOLESALE INFLATION (WPI) SURGED TO 8.3 PER CENT IN APRIL FROM 3.88 PER CENT IN MARCH, REACHING A 42-MONTH HIGH.
HOW MUCH WERE PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES INCREASED?
PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES WERE RAISED BY RS 4 PER LITRE IN TWO INSTALLMENTS. THIS IS THE FIRST FUEL PRICE HIKE IN FOUR YEARS.
WHAT DROVE THE SHARP RISE IN WPI INFLATION?
THE FUEL AND POWER CATEGORY WAS THE PRIMARY DRIVER. INFLATION IN THIS SEGMENT JUMPED TO 24.71 PER CENT FROM 1.05 PER CENT IN MARCH. CRUDE PETROLEUM INFLATION CLIMBED MORE THAN 88 PER CENT AMID THE WEST ASIA CRISIS AND SUPPLY DISRUPTIONS.
HOW WILL THE FUEL PRICE HIKE IMPACT RETAIL INFLATION?
ECONOMISTS ESTIMATE THE FUEL PRICE HIKE WILL IMPACT RETAIL INFLATION BY AROUND 20 BASIS POINTS. THE FULL EFFECT, ALONG WITH RECENT MILK PRICE INCREASES, WILL LIKELY BE FULLY REFLECTED IN THE JUNE CPI DATA TO BE RELEASED IN MID-JULY.
WHAT IS THE EXPECTED RBI MONETARY POLICY RESPONSE?
THE RBI IS EXPECTED TO HOLD THE REPO RATE AT 5.25 PER CENT IN THE NEAR TERM. THE CENTRAL BANK WILL CLOSELY MONITOR THREE FACTORS: GLOBAL CRUDE OIL PRICE TRAJECTORY, MONSOON IMPACT ON FOOD INFLATION, AND THE EXTENT TO WHICH WHOLESALE INFLATION PASSES THROUGH TO RETAIL PRICES.
