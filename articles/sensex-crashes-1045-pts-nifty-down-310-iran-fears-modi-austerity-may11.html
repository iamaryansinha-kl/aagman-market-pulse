Sensex Crashes 1,045 Pts, Nifty Down 310 on Iran Fears, PM Modi Austerity Push

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex crashed 1,045.23 points to 76,282.96 and Nifty 50 fell 310.50 points to 23,865.65 during morning trade on Monday, May 11, 2026
  • 02/Sell-off triggered by PM Modi's austerity push and US-Iran tensions sending Brent crude surging past $105 per barrel
  • 03/SBI shares fell over 3% after Q4 margin miss; rupee crashed 139 paise to 94.90 against the US dollar

Sensex crashed 1,045.23 points to 76,282.96 on Monday, May 11, 2026. Nifty 50 fell 310.50 points to 23,865.65 as selling intensified through the morning. At 10:00 AM, the Sensex was down 1,056.53 points or 1.37% at 76,271.66.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi called for economic self-reliance and asked citizens to avoid purchasing gold for one year. He also urged reduced consumption of petrol, diesel, and cooking gas.

US President Donald Trump rejected Iran's response to a peace proposal as totally unacceptable. Brent crude surged past $101, with July futures hitting $105.44, up 4.10%.

The rupee crashed 139 paise to 94.90 against the US dollar in early trade. At the interbank exchange, it opened at 94.97 before inching up marginally.

SBI shares fell over 3% after a weaker-than-expected Q4 on net interest margin pressure. Titan dropped 6.06%, while Tata Consumer Products gained 4.24%.

Motilal Oswal Financial Research said the Nifty-50 trades at 19.1x forward earnings. That is roughly 9% below its long-period average, making large-caps relatively attractive.

"Nifty is expected to open lower around 24,000, down nearly 170 points," said Gaurav Udani, Founder of Thincredblu Securities. He flagged 23,800-23,900 as key support and 24,250-24,400 as resistance.

SBI Securities expects Q4FY26 real GDP growth at 7.2% and FY27 at 6.6%. The brokerage noted resilient economic activity despite global headwinds and elevated fuel prices.
