Brokerages Stay Bullish on Laurus Labs as CDMO Revenue Surges 36%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Laurus Labs stock has gained 30% in two months after strong FY26 results driven by a booming CDMO business
  • 02/The CDMO segment grew 36% YoY to ₹2,080 crore, with EBITDA margin expanding 670 bps to 26.8%
  • 03/The company has guided for ₹3,000 crore capex over two years, with CDMO revenue share expected to hit 50% by FY30

Laurus Labs Ltd has seen its stock surge 30% in two months following robust FY26 financial results. The rally is driven by growth in its contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) segment and improved product mix.

The CDMO segment grew 36% year-on-year to ₹2,080 crore in FY26. This was driven by late-stage pipeline progress, higher commercialisation of novel molecules, and strong outsourcing demand from global pharma companies.

CDMO now contributes over 30% to total revenue, up from just 13% six years ago. The company expects this share to reach 50% by FY30 as it continues its structural shift towards higher-value segments.

The operating margin before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA margin) expanded 670 basis points year-on-year to 26.8%. This was driven by higher operating leverage as the product mix improved.

The company has outlined capital expenditure of ₹3,000 crore over the next two years. Over 90% of this will go towards expanding mid and large-scale manufacturing capacities, including the greenfield Unit 7 facility with over 2,000 cubic meters of reactor capacity.

Laurus Labs is also expanding into non-pharma segments such as crop science and animal health. From a current base of about ₹150 crore, Motilal Oswal Financial Services expects these segments to scale beyond ₹1,000 crore over time.

"CDMO growth has been supported by both development projects and commercialised molecules. We expect the segment to maintain momentum, projecting a 22% annual growth over FY26-28," Motilal Oswal said. The brokerage has maintained a 'BUY' rating on the stock and raised earnings estimates for FY27 by 8% and FY28 by 6%.

The company has reduced dependence on antiretroviral (ARV) therapies, with their contribution declining to about 41% from 67% previously. Laurus Labs shares closed 0.2% lower at ₹1,450.6 on Thursday on the BSE.
