L&T Industrial Electronics: Coimbatore Campus Targets Scale by Early 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Larsen & Toubro (NSE: LT) forays into industrial electronics with new vertical LTEPS, targeting meaningful scale at Coimbatore by early 2027
  • 02/Two PCB assembly lines are operational on a 40-acre campus; workforce at 400 employees with plans to double to ~800
  • 03/New G+3 building of ~20,000 sq meters under planning; focus spans EV motor control units, power electronics, and defence systems

Larsen & Toubro Ltd (NSE: LT) has launched a new business vertical named L&T Electronic Products & Systems (LTEPS). It will anchor manufacturing operations at the company's 40-acre campus in Coimbatore.

The campus has commissioned two PCB assembly lines. These are producing DC-DC and AC-DC converters for chargers and fulfilling orders from sister companies.

L&T is planning a ground-plus-three building of approximately 20,000 square meters in the same campus. The project is currently in the planning and permit stage.

The aim is to complete the G+3 complex by the end of 2026. Prashant Chiranjive Jain, Head of Corporate Centre at L&T, told businessline he expects "meaningful scale" by early next year.

The division currently employs about 400 people. Jain expects this to grow to around 800 employees as the business ramps up over the coming quarters.

LTEPS will focus on power electronics, automotive electronics, industrial robotics, and ESDM. It will also leverage L&T's existing precision engineering and defence manufacturing strengths in Coimbatore.

One of the first products is an EV motor-control-unit platform. It is launching in three variants ranging from 60 kilowatt to 300 kilowatt, suitable for three-wheelers and four-wheeler commercial vehicles.

L&T has secured an anchor customer for the MCU platform. Jain said the company has received "very positive response" for this product.

The division will benefit from synergies with L&T Technology Services and L&T Semiconductor Technologies. This creates a full-stack capability from chip design to system manufacturing.

The Indian electronics ecosystem is largely low-IP manufacturing today. L&T is positioning LTEPS as an IP- and R&D-led business to avoid commodity competition.

Jain anticipates it will take about 2 years to qualify and test products across all standards. Key challenges include access to specialised R&D talent and building a supplier ecosystem around Coimbatore.

L&T shares closed at ₹4,060 on April 29, 2026. The stock has traded in a range of ₹3,539 to ₹4,149 in April, with a market capitalisation of approximately ₹5.55 lakh crore.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS L&T ELECTRONIC PRODUCTS AND SYSTEMS (LTEPS)?
LTEPS IS A NEW BUSINESS VERTICAL LAUNCHED BY LARSEN & TOUBRO LTD (NSE: LT) TO ENTER THE INDUSTRIAL ELECTRONICS SEGMENT. IT FOCUSES ON POWER ELECTRONICS, AUTOMOTIVE ELECTRONICS, INDUSTRIAL ROBOTICS, AND ELECTRONICS SYSTEM DESIGN AND MANUFACTURING (ESDM).
WHERE IS THE LTEPS MANUFACTURING CAMPUS LOCATED?
THE MANUFACTURING OPERATIONS ARE ANCHORED AT L&T'S 40-ACRE CAMPUS IN COIMBATORE, TAMIL NADU. THE CAMPUS ALREADY HOSTS TWO OPERATIONAL PCB ASSEMBLY LINES.
WHAT IS THE EXPANSION PLAN FOR THE COIMBATORE CAMPUS?
L&T PLANS TO BUILD A GROUND-PLUS-THREE COMPLEX OF APPROXIMATELY 20,000 SQUARE METERS IN THE SAME CAMPUS. THE BUILDING IS CURRENTLY IN PLANNING AND PERMIT STAGE, WITH A TARGET TO COMPLETE BY END OF 2026.
HOW MANY EMPLOYEES DOES LTEPS CURRENTLY HAVE, AND WHAT IS THE TARGET?
THE DIVISION CURRENTLY HAS ABOUT 400 EMPLOYEES ON THE GROUND. PRASHANT CHIRANJIVE JAIN, HEAD OF CORPORATE CENTRE AT L&T, EXPECTS THE WORKFORCE TO GROW TO AROUND 800 AS THE BUSINESS RAMPS UP.
WHAT IS L&T'S FIRST PRODUCT IN THE ELECTRONICS SEGMENT?
ONE OF THE FIRST PRODUCTS IS AN EV MOTOR-CONTROL-UNIT PLATFORM LAUNCHING IN THREE VARIANTS RANGING FROM 60 KILOWATT TO 300 KILOWATT. THIS IS SUITABLE FOR SMALL THREE-WHEELERS AND FOUR-WHEELER COMMERCIAL VEHICLES, AND L&T HAS ALREADY SECURED AN ANCHOR CUSTOMER FOR IT.
