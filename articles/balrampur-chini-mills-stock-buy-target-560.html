Stock to Buy Today: Balrampur Chini Mills Target ₹560-₹565

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Balrampur Chini Mills stock surged over 6% on Monday, trading at ₹516 with a fresh technical buy signal
  • 02/Price action since December last year is happening inside a bull channel; the recent rise is from the lower end
  • 03/Target set at ₹560-₹565 with support in the ₹500-₹490 region; stop-loss recommended below channel support

Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd (NSE: BALRAMCHIN) is the stock to buy today at ₹516. The counter surged over 6% on Monday, indicating the up move is gaining fresh momentum.

The price action since December 2024 is unfolding inside a bull channel. The recent rise is happening from the lower end of this channel, a classic continuation pattern.

Support for the stock lies in the ₹500-₹490 region. A sustained hold above this zone keeps the bullish structure intact for traders.

The stock has a 52-week high of ₹627.80 and a low of ₹393.55. It is currently trading well above the midpoint of this range.

Balrampur Chini Mills is India's largest sugar manufacturer with a cane crushing capacity of 80,000 tonnes per day. It has diversified into ethanol, distillery, and cogeneration operations.

The company reported revenue of ₹5,504.2 crore for FY2025-26 with profit at ₹401.73 crore. Promoters hold 42.85%, while FIIs and DIIs together own 38%.

Analysts have an average price target of ₹616 on the stock. Six analysts on Investing.com rate it a Strong Buy with a 26.78% upside potential.

The ₹560-₹565 target represents the upper end of the bull channel. Traders should watch the ₹500 support closely for any sign of breakdown.
