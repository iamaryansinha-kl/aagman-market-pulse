Energy Security to Drive Multi-Year Capex Cycle in India: 360 ONE

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/360 ONE Capital Markets says energy security will drive a multi-year capex cycle across India's power, transmission, storage and fuel substitution sectors
  • 02/India's crude oil imports stand at 89%, natural gas at 50% and coal at 23%, exposing the economy to external supply shocks
  • 03/The brokerage advises long-term investors to accumulate on dips even though most stocks in the space already trade close to target prices

360 ONE Capital Markets sees energy security evolving into a structural, policy-driven investment theme. The brokerage predicts a broad domestic capex cycle across power generation, transmission, storage and fuel substitution.

India's high import dependence remains a key vulnerability. Crude oil imports stand at 89 per cent, natural gas at 50 per cent and coal at 23 per cent.

This exposes the economy to external account pressure, imported inflation and currency volatility during supply disruptions. Energy demand is rising structurally on urbanisation, industrialisation and digital infrastructure growth.

The policy lens has shifted from affordability and decarbonisation to resilience, reliability and reduced import dependence. 360 ONE said this creates opportunities across the entire energy value chain.

Coal still accounts for over 70 per cent of electricity generation. India is likely to add up to 80 GW of new coal capacity by 2032 alongside renewable expansion.

Non-fossil capacity has crossed 220 GW, with India on track for its 500 GW target by 2030. Solar remains the most compelling segment due to cost competitiveness and scalability.

Battery Energy Storage Systems are becoming critical, with India targeting 236 GWh by 2032. Key beneficiaries include NTPC, Coal India, JSW Energy, PFC and REC.

"What markets are confronting is not just the risk of temporary supply disruption, but the emergence of a more fragmented, security-driven global energy order, in which resilience, redundancy and domestic capacity creation carry a higher policy premium than before" — 360 ONE Capital Markets.

WHAT DOES 360 ONE SAY ABOUT INDIA'S ENERGY SECURITY THEME?
360 ONE CAPITAL MARKETS BELIEVES ENERGY SECURITY IS EVOLVING INTO A STRUCTURAL, POLICY-DRIVEN INVESTMENT THEME THAT WILL UNDERPIN A MULTI-YEAR CAPITAL EXPENDITURE CYCLE ACROSS POWER GENERATION, TRANSMISSION, STORAGE AND FUEL SUBSTITUTION.
WHAT IS INDIA'S CURRENT ENERGY IMPORT DEPENDENCE?
INDIA IMPORTS 89 PER CENT OF ITS CRUDE OIL, 50 PER CENT OF NATURAL GAS AND 23 PER CENT OF COAL. THIS EXPOSES THE ECONOMY TO EXTERNAL ACCOUNT PRESSURE, IMPORTED INFLATION AND CURRENCY VOLATILITY DURING SUPPLY DISRUPTIONS.
HOW MUCH NEW COAL CAPACITY IS INDIA EXPECTED TO ADD BY 2032?
INDIA IS LIKELY TO ADD UP TO 80 GIGAWATTS OF NEW COAL CAPACITY BY 2032. COAL STILL ACCOUNTS FOR OVER 70 PER CENT OF ELECTRICITY GENERATION AND IS CRITICAL FOR BASELOAD AND PEAK-DEMAND MANAGEMENT.
WHAT IS INDIA'S RENEWABLE ENERGY TARGET?
NON-FOSSIL CAPACITY HAS CROSSED 220 GW, WITH INDIA ON TRACK FOR ITS 500 GW TARGET BY 2030. SOLAR REMAINS THE MOST COMPELLING SEGMENT DUE TO COST COMPETITIVENESS AND SCALABILITY.

DIRECT BENEFICIARIES INCLUDE NTPC, JSW ENERGY, NLC INDIA, ADANI POWER, CESC AND COAL INDIA. POWER FINANCIERS LIKE PFC AND REC PROVIDE A FINANCIAL PROXY, WHILE PIPE MAKERS SUCH AS JINDAL SAW AND WELSPUN CORP ALSO BENEFIT.
