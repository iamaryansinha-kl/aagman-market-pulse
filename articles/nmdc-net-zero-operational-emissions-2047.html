NMDC Targets Net-Zero Operational Emissions by 2047; Shares Rally 2%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

NMDC, India’s largest iron ore miner, has set a target to achieve net-zero operational emissions by 2047. The company disclosed the target in an exchange filing on Monday.

Shares rose up to 2% to a day’s high of Rs 80.85 following the announcement. The stock has fallen 5% in the last month and is down 4.09% in calendar 2026. Over the past year it has gained 4.85%, with three-year returns of 67.24%.

What does the target cover? The target covers Scope 1 emissions from direct fuel consumption and Scope 2 emissions from electricity use. The company said the decision reflects its commitment to progressively reduce the carbon footprint of its mining operations and transition towards lower-carbon practices.

Six key strategies have been identified: energy efficiency, renewable energy integration, fleet electrification, low-carbon fuels, Carbon Capture Utilisation and Storage (CCUS), and demand-side management.

The roadmap is divided into three phases. The short-term phase runs FY2026 to FY2030. The medium-term phase spans FY2030 to FY2040. The long-term phase runs FY2040 to FY2047.

Energy efficiency, renewable adoption, and electrification form the initial transition. Deeper decarbonisation measures and emerging technologies follow in subsequent phases.

Beyond Scope 1 and 2, NMDC’s logistics infrastructure is part of the decarbonisation plan. An upcoming slurry pipeline will provide greener downstream transportation by reducing dependence on conventional road transport and warehousing. The company also plans to increase iron ore movement through rail freight, supported by railway line doubling and supply infrastructure around its operations.

Existing initiatives include a 10.5 MW wind energy facility at Chitradurga and solar power installations across its projects.
