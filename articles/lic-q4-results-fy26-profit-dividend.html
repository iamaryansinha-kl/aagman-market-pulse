LIC Q4 Results FY26: Profit Surges 23% to Rs 23,467 Crore; Rs 10 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LIC Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 23% YoY to Rs 23,467 crore from Rs 19,039 crore in Q4FY25
  • 02/Board recommends final dividend of Rs 10 per share; net premium income grows 12% to Rs 1.65 lakh crore
  • 03/FY26 full-year PAT at Rs 57,453 crore up 19%; solvency ratio improves to 2.35 from 2.11 year ago

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) (NSE: LIC) reported a 23% year-on-year growth in consolidated net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit stood at Rs 23,467 crore against Rs 19,039 crore in the year-ago quarter.

The company's board has recommended a final dividend of Rs 10 per share for FY2025-26. The payout will be subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming annual general meeting.

Net premium income for the quarter rose 12% to Rs 1.65 lakh crore from Rs 1.48 lakh crore in Q4FY25. Growth was broad-based across all premium segments.

First-year premium income grew 17% to Rs 13,009 crore. Renewal premium rose 14% to Rs 82,233 crore, while single premium collections surged 22% to Rs 70,119 crore.

Income from investments, a key earnings driver, jumped 17% to Rs 1.09 lakh crore. This compares with Rs 93,443 crore in the corresponding quarter last year.

LIC's solvency ratio improved to 2.35 as of March 31, 2026 from 2.11 a year ago. The ratio remains comfortably above the regulatory minimum requirement.

Expenses of management rose to Rs 20,699 crore from Rs 16,526 crore in Q4FY25. Employee remuneration and welfare expenses jumped sharply to Rs 8,891 crore from Rs 5,943 crore.

The 13th month persistency ratio slipped to 67.77% from 68.62% a year earlier. The 61st month ratio also fell to 54.13% from 58.54%, indicating policy retention challenges.

LIC shares closed at Rs 800.20 on May 21, down 0.05%. For FY26, the insurer posted full-year PAT of Rs 57,453 crore, up 19% from Rs 48,320 crore in FY25.
