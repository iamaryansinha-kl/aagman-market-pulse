Rupee Falls 13 Paise to 94.81 vs Dollar on Oil Surge, Fed Watch

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on April 29, 2026, after opening at 94.79.
  • 02/Brent crude remains elevated at $111.03 per barrel and the dollar index gained 0.04% to 98.67 amid safe-haven flows.
  • 03/All eyes are on the US Federal Reserve policy decision due later today, with the 94.50-94.80 band acting as key resistance for the rupee.

The rupee depreciated 13 paise to 94.81 against the US dollar in early trade on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The domestic currency opened at 94.79 and lost ground in initial deals.

On Tuesday, the rupee had closed 53 paise lower at 94.68. The cumulative two-day decline now stands at 66 paise.

Rising global crude oil prices kept the rupee under sustained pressure. Brent crude was trading at $111.03 per barrel, down marginally by 0.21% but still at elevated levels.

Forex traders cited month-end dollar demand and a broader shift toward safe-haven assets as additional headwinds. Investor sentiment remained cautious ahead of the US Federal Reserve policy decision.

"The oil is above $111 with no resolution to the US-Iran crisis," said Anil Kumar Bhansali, Head of Treasury at Finrex Treasury Advisors LLP. He flagged the non-passage of ships from the Strait of Hormuz as a key concern keeping oil and the dollar firm.

The dollar index, which tracks the greenback against six major currencies, edged 0.04% higher to 98.67. "Broadly, the dollar Index is range-bound with war-related uncertainty supporting the safe-haven dollar," Bhansali added.

Meanwhile, the UAE announced it will leave OPEC effective May 1, 2026. This unexpected exit threatens to shake the oil cartel's grip on global supply dynamics.

"The rupee is navigating through a phase where global uncertainty, oil prices, and capital flows are all pulling at once," said Amit Pabari, Managing Director at CR Forex Advisors. He identified 93.50-93.80 as a strong demand zone and 94.50-94.80 as resistance.

On the domestic front, Foreign Institutional Investors sold equities worth ₹2,103.74 crore on Tuesday. India's industrial production growth also slowed to a five-month low of 4.1% in March 2026.

The domestic equity market showed resilience despite the weak currency. The Sensex climbed 358.92 points to 77,245.83 in opening trade, while the Nifty gained 101.2 points to 24,096.90.
