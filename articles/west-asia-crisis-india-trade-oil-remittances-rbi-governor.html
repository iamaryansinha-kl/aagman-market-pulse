West Asia Crisis Hits India Trade, Oil, Remittances: RBI Governor

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra warns West Asia crisis poses significant risk to India's economy via trade, oil, fertiliser and remittances
  • 02/West Asia contributes 1/6 of exports, 1/2 crude oil, 2/5 fertilisers and nearly 2/5 inward remittances; India diversifying sources
  • 03/RBI keeps repo rate at 5.25% in wait-and-watch mode; FY27 GDP cut to 6.9%, inflation seen at 4.6%

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra has warned that the ongoing West Asia crisis poses a significant risk to India's economy. Speaking at Princeton University on April 18, 2026, he highlighted impacts on trade, crude oil imports, fertiliser supply and remittances.

West Asia accounts for approximately one-sixth of India's exports and one-fifth of imports. The region supplies half of India's crude oil imports and two-fifths of fertiliser imports.

Nearly two-fifths of India's inward remittances come from West Asia. India received $135.4 billion in total inward remittances in FY25, making it the world's largest recipient.

India imports 80-85% of its crude oil requirements, with 50-55% coming from West Asia. Every $10 rise in crude prices adds $12-18 billion to the import bill.

In response, India is diversifying import sources and boosting domestic oil and gas production. The country now imports energy from 41 countries compared to 27 a decade ago.

India has rerouted 70% of crude imports outside the Strait of Hormuz. Strategic petroleum reserves of 5.3 million metric tonnes are being expanded by 6.5 mmt more.

Malhotra said there is no oil shortage given India's reserves, but some gas rationing is being imposed for industrial use. Oil marketing companies absorbed part of the price pressure.

The RBI kept the repo rate unchanged at 5.25%. GDP growth for FY27 is projected at 6.9%, down from 7.6% in FY26, with inflation seen at 4.6%.

"The appropriate monetary policy response to such a supply shock is to look beyond the first-round effects, provided they do not lead to longer-term second-round dynamics" - Sanjay Malhotra, RBI Governor.

India averaged 6.1% annual growth over the past decade versus the global economy's 3.2%. China's growth was 5.6% and Indonesia's 4.2% in the same period.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW DOES THE WEST ASIA CRISIS IMPACT INDIA'S ECONOMY?
WEST ASIA ACCOUNTS FOR ONE-SIXTH OF INDIA'S EXPORTS, ONE-FIFTH OF IMPORTS, HALF OF CRUDE OIL IMPORTS, TWO-FIFTHS OF FERTILISER IMPORTS, AND NEARLY TWO-FIFTHS OF INWARD REMITTANCES. RBI GOVERNOR SANJAY MALHOTRA WARNED THESE LINKAGES CREATE SIGNIFICANT VULNERABILITY.
WHAT SHARE OF INDIA'S CRUDE OIL COMES FROM WEST ASIA?
INDIA IMPORTS 80-85% OF ITS CRUDE OIL REQUIREMENTS, WITH 50-55% COMING FROM WEST ASIA. EVERY $10 RISE IN CRUDE OIL PRICES ADDS $12-18 BILLION TO INDIA'S IMPORT BILL.
HOW IS INDIA RESPONDING TO THE SUPPLY RISKS?
INDIA IS DIVERSIFYING IMPORT SOURCES AND BOOSTING DOMESTIC OIL AND GAS PRODUCTION. THE COUNTRY NOW IMPORTS ENERGY FROM 41 COUNTRIES VERSUS 27 A DECADE AGO. 70% OF CRUDE IMPORTS HAVE BEEN REROUTED OUTSIDE THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ.
WHAT IS THE RBI'S MONETARY POLICY STANCE?
THE RBI HAS KEPT THE REPO RATE UNCHANGED AT 5.25% AND MAINTAINS A NEUTRAL, WAIT-AND-WATCH STANCE. MALHOTRA EMPHASISED BEING AGILE AND DATA-DEPENDENT AMID UNCERTAINTY.
WHAT ARE THE GROWTH AND INFLATION PROJECTIONS?
RBI HAS CUT THE FY27 GDP GROWTH FORECAST TO 6.9% FROM 7.6% IN FY26. CPI INFLATION FOR FY27 IS PROJECTED AT 4.6%. INDIA'S FOREX RESERVES STOOD AT $697.1 BILLION AS OF APRIL 3, 2026.
