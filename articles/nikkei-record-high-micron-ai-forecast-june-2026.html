Nikkei Hits Record 72,366 on Micron AI Forecast, Soars 4.6%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Japan's Nikkei index surged 4.6% to a record closing high of 72,366.34 on June 25, snapping two sessions of declines
  • 02/Micron forecast quarterly profit and revenue well above expectations, with customers committing $22 billion for memory chip supply
  • 03/AI chip stocks led gains: Advantest jumped 15%, Kioxia surged 12.27%, Tokyo Electron rose 7.78%; energy stocks fell on lower oil prices

Japan's Nikkei share average soared 4.6% to close at a record high of 72,366.34 on Thursday, June 25. The rally snapped two straight sessions of declines and was driven entirely by AI-related stocks.

U.S. memory chipmaker Micron Technology forecast quarterly profit and revenue well above analyst expectations. The company also disclosed customers had committed $22 billion to secure long-term memory chip supply.

"The Nikkei's sharp gain is simply due to Micron's earnings," said Takamasa Ikeda, senior portfolio manager at GCI Asset Management. He added the U.S. semiconductor index, highly correlated with the Nikkei, would likely rise later in the day.

SoftBank Group reversed course from prior weakness to jump 8%. Chip-testing equipment maker Advantest surged 15%, while chip-making equipment maker Tokyo Electron rose 7.78%. Memory chipmaker Kioxia advanced 12.27%.

AI-data centre component makers also rallied. Murata Manufacturing climbed 7.21%, while Taiyo Yuden jumped 11.2%. "Investors tried to lock in profits in technology stocks in the previous session ahead of Micron earnings, but they scooped up the shares today," said Daisuke Hashizume, senior strategist at Daiwa Securities.

The broader Topix index rose 1.33% to 4,016.47, a more modest gain reflecting sector divergence. Energy-related stocks fell as oil prices extended declines, with the mining sector dropping 3.18% and Inpex down 3.35%. Oil refiners fell 1.38%.

Market breadth was positive. Of the more than 1,500 stocks on the Tokyo Stock Exchange's prime market, 66% rose, 30% fell, and 3% were flat. Japanese shippers slipped 2%, with Kawasaki Kisen down 3.87%.
