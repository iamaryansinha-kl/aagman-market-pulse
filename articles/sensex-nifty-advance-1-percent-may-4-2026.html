Sensex, Nifty Advance 1% on May 4: Election Results, Crude Keep Caution Alive

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex surged 870 points (1.13%) to 77,783.53 and Nifty 50 gained 232 points (0.96%) to 24,229.50 in late morning trade on May 4, 2026.
  • 02/The advance follows a volatile April 30 close where Sensex fell 583 points; April overall saw Sensex rise 5.2% and Nifty climb 5.81%.
  • 03/Investors are closely watching state election results from Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, and Puducherry, plus elevated Brent crude at $107-108 per barrel.

Indian benchmark indices opened higher on Monday, May 4, 2026, after a three-day extended weekend. The Sensex gained 870.03 points (1.13%) to 77,783.53, while the Nifty 50 added 231.95 points (0.96%) to 24,229.50 in late morning deals.

Gift Nifty had signaled a positive start, trading around 24,208.50 early Monday. It stood 0.97% above the April 30 Nifty close of 23,997.55.

However, analysts expect a volatile, news-driven week ahead. Investors are tracking state assembly election results, crude oil prices, West Asia tensions, and Q4 earnings.

Brent crude futures traded near $107-108 per barrel on Monday. July Brent was at $108.60, up 0.40%, while WTI crude was at $102.12, up 0.17%. Elevated oil prices remain a key macro concern.

The rupee hit a fresh record low of around 95.1 per dollar last week. Rising crude and FII selling pressure continue to strain the currency.

Foreign institutional investors sold a net Rs 59,619-70,135 crore in April 2026. Domestic institutional investors bought Rs 45,315-51,064 crore, providing a partial cushion.

April delivered strong gains despite late-month volatility. The Sensex rose 3,779 points (5.2%) and the Nifty surged 1,318 points (5.81%) for the month.

Ankit Jaiswal, Senior Research Analyst at Univest, said 24,200 is the key pivot for Nifty. "A decisive breakout above the 50-DMA near 24,140 could pave the way towards 24,500," he noted.
