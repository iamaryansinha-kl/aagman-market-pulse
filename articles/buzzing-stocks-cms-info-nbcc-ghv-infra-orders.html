Buzzing Stocks: CMS Info, NBCC, GHV Infra Bag Orders Worth ₹7,252 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CMS Info Systems wins 5-year integrated ATM managed services contract from HDFC Bank with Vision AI solution Hawkai
  • 02/NBCC secures ₹252.80 crore PMC contract from OSEPA for 46 schools; GHV Infra bags €630 million Cameroon tyre plant EPC deal
  • 03/JSW Steel declared Preferred Bidder for Goa iron ore block with 96 million tonnes projected resources at 118.10% value of mineral

CMS Info Systems Ltd has bagged an integrated ATM managed services outsourcing mandate from HDFC Bank. The 5-year contract covers currency forecasting, logistics, and the Hawkai Vision AI solution.

NBCC (India) Ltd has secured a project management consultancy contract worth approximately ₹252.80 crore from the Odisha School Education Programme Authority. The contract covers 46 schools and excludes GST.

GHV Infra Projects Ltd has received a Letter of Intent for EPC works on a greenfield tyre manufacturing plant in Cameroon. The project value is €630 million, approximately ₹7,000 crore, with a 36-month completion timeline.

JSW Steel Ltd has been declared the Preferred Bidder for the Pissurlem Mineral Block in Goa. The company offered the highest final price at 118.10 per cent of value of mineral dispatched.

The projected iron ore resources at the Goa block stand at 96 million tonnes. JSW Steel is among India's largest steel producers and has been expanding its raw material security.

Texmaco Rail & Engineering has received an order worth ₹27.82 crore from Hindalco Industries. The order covers supply of one BTAP rake and brake van.

Om Freight Forwarders has acquired a new warehousing facility at Mauje Sarade in Raigad district. The total investment is approximately ₹32 crore, adding 1,98,000 sq. ft. of open and closed warehousing space.

Steelco Gujarat Ltd has approved a revised rights issue to raise about ₹14.90 crore. The company will issue up to 13.30 crore shares at ₹112 each in a 5:1 ratio.
