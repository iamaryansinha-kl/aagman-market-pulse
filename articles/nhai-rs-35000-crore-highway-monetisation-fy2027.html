NHAI Plans ₹35,000 Crore Highway Asset Monetisation For FY2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NHAI plans ₹35,000 crore highway asset monetisation for the financial year ending March 2027
  • 02/Haryana accounts for the highest number of highway stretches on the monetisation list
  • 03/Uttar Pradesh ranks second in the number of assets identified for monetisation

The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) has set a ₹35,000 crore target for highway asset monetisation in FY2027. The plan involves leasing or divesting operational road assets to unlock capital.

NHAI has prepared a detailed list of highway stretches for the monetisation programme. This marks a continuation of the government's push to raise infrastructure funds without adding to fiscal debt.

Haryana accounts for the highest number of assets on the monetisation list. Uttar Pradesh follows as the state with the second-largest pipeline of highway assets.

The specific stretches, bidders, and deal structures have not been disclosed yet. NHAI is expected to release further details as the fiscal year progresses.

Highway asset monetisation typically involves toll-operate-transfer (TOT) models or infrastructure investment trusts (InvITs). These structures allow NHAI to recycle capital into new greenfield projects.

The ₹35,000 crore target signals sustained government commitment to infrastructure-led growth. Investors will watch for auction timelines and asset quality disclosures in the coming months.
