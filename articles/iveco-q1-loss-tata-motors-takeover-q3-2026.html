Iveco Posts €74M Q1 Loss, Tata Motors Takeover Now Expected in Q3 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Iveco swung to an adjusted net loss of €74 million in Q1 2026 from a €60 million profit a year earlier
  • 02/Industrial operating loss hit €90 million vs €82 million profit in Q1 2025 on €2.8 billion revenue
  • 03/Tata Motors' tender offer now expected to close in Q3 2026, delayed from prior Q2 estimate

Iveco Group reported an adjusted net loss of €74 million in the first quarter of 2026. This compares to a profit of €60 million in the same period last year.

The Italian truckmaker said results were impacted by the sale of its defence unit to Leonardo. The transaction was finalized in March 2026.

Adjusted operating loss from industrial activities widened to €90 million. A year ago, the same metric showed a profit of €82 million.

Net revenue from industrial activities came in at €2.8 billion for the quarter. The company did not provide a year-on-year revenue comparison in its release.

Iveco also revised the timeline for its acquisition by Tata Motors. The tender offer is now expected to close in the third quarter of 2026.

This marks a delay from the previous estimate of the second quarter. The companies have not cited specific reasons for the revised timeline.

The defence unit sale to Leonardo was completed in March. Iveco had previously guided an extraordinary dividend of €5.5-6.0 per share from the proceeds.

The payout was anticipated in April 2026 after contractual adjustments. The ex-dividend date was set at April 20 on the Italian Stock Exchange calendar.
