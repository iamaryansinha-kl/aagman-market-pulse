Govt Rules Out Petrol, Diesel Price Hike After Assembly Polls End

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Petroleum Ministry rules out petrol, diesel price hike after assembly polls conclude on April 29
  • 02/Retail fuel prices frozen since April 2022; state-run retailers losing ₹2,400 crore daily
  • 03/Crude averaged over $114/bbl this month vs $70 last year; no timeline for price revision

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas has ruled out any immediate increase in petrol and diesel prices. Joint Secretary Sujata Sharma dismissed speculation of a post-election hike on Tuesday.

Retail petrol and diesel prices have remained unchanged since early April 2022. This marks a record fourth year of frozen fuel prices at the pump.

State-run fuel retailers are incurring heavy losses from selling below market rates. Daily losses are estimated at about ₹2,400 crore across the sector.

Global crude oil prices have surged over 50 per cent in the past two months due to West Asia tensions. Crude averaged over $114 per barrel this month compared to $70 last year.

Rumours of price hikes triggered panic buying in Andhra Pradesh and other states. Some outlets saw demand rise by 30-33 per cent, with over 400 petrol pumps running dry on Sunday.

Sharma urged consumers to avoid panic buying and rely on official sources. She confirmed adequate inventories of petrol, diesel, LPG and aviation fuel are available nationwide.

Analysts had earlier flagged potential increases of ₹25-28 per litre after elections. However, the government maintains there is no proposal to raise prices despite mounting financial pressure.

Petrol is currently priced at ₹94.77 per litre in Delhi. Diesel comes for ₹87.67 per litre in the national capital.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HAS THE GOVERNMENT APPROVED A PETROL AND DIESEL PRICE INCREASE?
NO. THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS HAS EXPLICITLY RULED OUT ANY IMMEDIATE INCREASE IN PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES. JOINT SECRETARY SUJATA SHARMA STATED THERE IS NO PROPOSAL TO RAISE FUEL PRICES.
HOW LONG HAVE RETAIL PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES REMAINED UNCHANGED?
RETAIL PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES HAVE REMAINED FROZEN SINCE EARLY APRIL 2022. THIS MARKS A RECORD FOURTH YEAR OF UNCHANGED PUMP PRICES.

STATE-RUN FUEL RETAILERS ARE INCURRING LOSSES OF ABOUT ₹20 PER LITRE ON PETROL AND ROUGHLY ₹100 PER LITRE ON DIESEL. DAILY LOSSES ACROSS THE SECTOR ARE ESTIMATED AT ABOUT ₹2,400 CRORE.

RUMOURS OF AN IMMINENT PRICE HIKE AFTER ASSEMBLY ELECTIONS TRIGGERED PANIC BUYING IN ANDHRA PRADESH AND OTHER STATES. SOME OUTLETS SAW DEMAND RISE BY 30-33 PER CENT, WITH OVER 400 PETROL PUMPS RUNNING DRY ON SUNDAY.

PETROL IS CURRENTLY PRICED AT ₹94.77 PER LITRE IN DELHI. DIESEL IS PRICED AT ₹87.67 PER LITRE IN THE NATIONAL CAPITAL.
