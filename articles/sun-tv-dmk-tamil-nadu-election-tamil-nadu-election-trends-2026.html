Sun TV Network Crashes 9.4% as DMK Trails in Tamil Nadu Election Results

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sun TV Network shares plunged 9.4% to ₹548.10 intraday as DMK trailed rivals in Tamil Nadu election counting on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam led in 106 seats versus DMK's 44, signalling a potential political shift in the state
  • 03/Analyst Kunal Kamble of Bonanza warns stock has broken 20 & 50 DMA support; further downside likely unless averages are reclaimed

Sun TV Network Limited (NSE: SUNTV) crashed 9.4% on Monday, May 4, 2026. The stock hit an intraday low of ₹548.10, down from the previous close of ₹605.60.

At around 1:00 pm, the stock was trading at ₹563.80. It was the top mid-cap loser on the NSE as Tamil Nadu election counting began.

The sell-off was triggered by early election trends showing the ruling DMK trailing behind rivals. The Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), led by actor-politician Vijay, surged ahead.

As of 12:35 pm, TVK was leading in 106 constituencies. The AIADMK was in second place with 63 seats. The DMK was third with just 44 seats.

Sun TV Network, founded by Kalanithi Maran, has deep historical ties with the DMK. The Maran family shares close links with the party leadership, making the stock sensitive to political outcomes.

"The stock has broken below its short-term uptrend and key moving averages (20 & 50 DMA), signaling a loss of bullish momentum. A high-volume bearish breakdown and RSI near 40 indicate weakening strength, though not yet oversold," said Kunal Kamble of Bonanza.

Repeated rejection in the ₹650–670 zone has formed a lower-high pattern. Unless the stock reclaims its moving averages, further downside or consolidation is likely.

The sharp decline reflects investor fears that a weaker DMK could reduce the company's influence in its core Tamil Nadu market. Volatility is expected to persist as final results emerge.
