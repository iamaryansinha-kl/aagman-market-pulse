Hexaware Q1 FY2026: Net Profit Rises 7.4% to ₹351.6 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hexaware Technologies reports 7.4% YoY net profit growth to ₹351.6 crore in Q1 FY2026, with revenue up 12.6% to ₹3,613 crore
  • 02/EBIT margin improved 133 bps annually to 13.3%, while QoQ profit surged 20.6% and revenue rose 3.9%
  • 03/Company declared first interim dividend of ₹8.5 per share and targets $3 billion US revenue by calendar 2029

Hexaware Technologies Ltd reported net profit of ₹351.6 crore in Q1 FY2026. The figure marks a 7.4% increase over the year-ago period.

Revenue for the quarter stood at ₹3,613 crore. This represents a 12.6% rise compared to the same quarter last year.

On a sequential basis, profit grew 20.6% and revenue climbed 3.9%. The quarter ended March 31, 2026.

EBIT for the period came in at ₹480.1 crore. The EBIT margin stood at 13.3%, reflecting an annual expansion of 133 basis points.

"Q1 continued to reflect the strength and discipline of our financial engine," said Vikash Jain, CFO, Hexaware. The company declared an interim dividend of ₹8.5 per share, backed by LTM OCF to PAT conversion over 125%.

Two more customers joined the $10 million-plus category, taking the total to 34 from 32 in the prior quarter. CEO R Srikrishna said AI will displace some core services spend.

Most verticals declined sequentially except healthcare, up 9.8%, and professional services, up 1.3%. Srikrishna attributed this to seasonality and expects a Q2 reversal.

The company reiterated its $3 billion US revenue target for calendar 2029. It aims to accelerate growth through AI-led transformation and legacy modernization deals.
