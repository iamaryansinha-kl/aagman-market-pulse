Amazon Now to Expand to 100 Indian Cities, 1,000 Micro-Fulfilment Centres

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Amazon plans to scale Amazon Now to 100 cities across India, backed by over 1,000 micro-fulfilment centres
  • 02/The expansion is part of Amazon's previously announced ₹2,800 crore investment in its India operations network
  • 03/More than 16,000 farmers will gain access to Amazon's platform to sell fresh produce directly to customers

Amazon will expand its ultra-fast delivery service, Amazon Now, to 100 cities across India. The company announced the scale-up on April 27, 2026.

The rollout will add metro and non-metro cities including Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kanpur, Chandigarh, Ahmedabad, Meerut, Mysuru, Panipat, Kochi, Amritsar, Mangaluru and Visakhapatnam. Existing markets Mumbai, Delhi-NCR and Bengaluru are already live.

Amazon Now offers thousands of daily essentials delivered within minutes. The range includes groceries, fresh produce, frozen foods, personal care, beauty, fashion, small appliances, baby products, pet supplies and healthcare supplements.

The company plans to scale to over 1,000 micro-fulfilment centres to support the expansion. This dense network enables the rapid pick-and-pack operations needed for sub-hour delivery.

"Customers continue to enjoy Amazon Now, appreciating our delivery speed, value and selection—especially the quality of fresh produce sourced directly from farmers," said Harsh Goyal, Vice President, Everyday Essentials, Amazon India.

The expansion will enable more than 16,000 farmers to sell directly to customers via sellers on the platform. This farm-to-door link removes intermediaries and should improve farmer margins.

The rapid scale-up is part of Amazon's previously announced investment of over ₹2,800 crore (around $300 million). The capital is aimed at strengthening operations, with a focus on associate safety, health and financial wellbeing.

Customers can check Amazon Now availability through the Amazon.in app by accessing the 'Now' icon on the homepage. Service coverage varies by pincode within the listed cities.
