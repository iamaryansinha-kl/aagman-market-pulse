Dr Reddy's Gets Health Canada DINs for Semaglutide Injection

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Reddy's Laboratories receives Drug Identification Numbers for semaglutide injection from Health Canada, advancing its Canadian market entry
  • 02/The company was first in India to launch generic semaglutide as Obeda in March 2026, priced at ₹4,200 per month for type 2 diabetes patients
  • 03/Stock surged 10% on Thursday on Canadian approval hopes but declined 1.73% on Friday; investors await final regulatory clearance

Dr Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (NSE: DRREDDY) has received Drug Identification Numbers for its semaglutide injection from Health Canada. The company informed the Bombay Stock Exchange on April 24, 2026.

The DINs represent a key step in the regulatory approval process for the Canadian market. The company said it continues to engage constructively with Health Canada for final clearance.

Dr Reddy's was the first Indian company to receive DCGI approval for generic semaglutide. It launched the product under the brand name Obeda in India in March 2026 upon patent expiry.

Obeda is priced at ₹4,200 per month in India for both 2 mg and 4 mg strengths. The company aims to sell 12 million injectable pens in the first year of launch.

The generic is priced up to 60% below Novo Nordisk's branded Ozempic. India's GLP-1 market is valued at approximately ₹1,446 crore according to industry estimates.

Dr Reddy's shares gained 10% on Thursday, April 23, on reports of Canadian regulatory progress. The stock declined 1.73% on Friday to trade at ₹1,310.05.

The company has backed Obeda with Phase III data from 312 participants showing non-inferior efficacy versus the innovator. It also launched the SemaKare patient-support program in India.
