Sensex Rebounds 359 Pts to 77,246, Nifty Up 101 on April 29 Open

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex surged 358.92 points to 77,245.83 in opening trade on April 29, recovering from Tuesday's 416-point decline.
  • 02/Nifty gained 101.2 points to 24,096.90; buying in blue-chip stocks and Asian market rally drove the rebound.
  • 03/FIIs offloaded ₹2,103.74 crore on Tuesday while DIIs bought ₹1,712.01 crore; Brent crude traded at $111 per barrel.

Indian equity benchmarks rebounded in early trade on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The BSE Sensex climbed 358.92 points to 77,245.83 in opening trade.

The 50-share NSE Nifty gained 101.2 points to reach 24,096.90. Both indices had ended lower on Tuesday amid geopolitical uncertainty.

On Tuesday, the Sensex declined 416.72 points or 0.54 per cent to settle at 76,886.91. The Nifty dropped 97 points or 0.40 per cent to end at 23,995.70.

Buying in blue-chip stocks and a rally in Asian markets fueled the recovery. Domestic institutional investors also offered support, analysts said.

From the 30-share Sensex pack, Maruti, ITC, Tech Mahindra, Infosys, Mahindra & Mahindra and Reliance Industries were the biggest gainers.

Tata Steel, Asian Paints, ICICI Bank and Axis Bank were among the laggards. Most other sectoral indices traded in the green during early trade.

"On the flows front, foreign institutional investors remain net sellers amid global risk aversion, while domestic institutional investors continue to offer some support," Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, said.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹2,103.74 crore on Tuesday. Domestic Institutional Investors bought stocks worth ₹1,712.01 crore, according to exchange data.
