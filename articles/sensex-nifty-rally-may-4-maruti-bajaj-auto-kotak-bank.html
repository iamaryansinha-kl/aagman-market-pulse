Sensex Nifty Rally May 4: Maruti, Bajaj Auto Surge, Kotak Bank Drops 4%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex rises 563.98 points (0.73%) to 77,477.48 and Nifty gains 179.45 points (0.75%) to 24,177.00 shortly after opening on May 4, 2026
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki surges 4.17% to ₹13,869 and Bajaj Auto climbs 3.90% to ₹10,383.50, while Kotak Mahindra Bank drops 4.21% to ₹367.15
  • 03/Crude oil stays elevated at $108.60 Brent; FII selling streak hits 9 sessions at ₹8,000 crore; state election results due today

Indian benchmark indices opened sharply higher on Monday, May 4, 2026. The BSE Sensex jumped 563.98 points (0.73%) to 77,477.48 shortly after 9:16 am.

The NSE Nifty 50 gained 179.45 points (0.75%) to 24,177.00. It had closed at 23,997.55 on Friday.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) surged 4.17% to ₹13,869. The stock opened at ₹13,559 against Friday's close of ₹13,314.

Bajaj Auto Ltd (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO) climbed 3.90% to ₹10,383.50 from its previous close of ₹9,994. Auto sector gains follow robust monthly sales data.

Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd (NSE: KOTAKBANK) fell 4.21% to ₹367.15 from ₹383.30. Over 50.49 lakh shares worth ₹186.48 crore changed hands.

"Today's market action may be unduly influenced by state election results," said Dr. VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments. He added that crude oil prices will guide the real trend.

Foreign institutional investors extended their selling streak to nine sessions on April 30, offloading ₹8,000 crore. Domestic institutional investors bought ₹3,487 crore for six straight sessions.

GST collections rose 8.7% year-on-year to a record ₹2,42,702 crore in April 2026. The rupee slipped past 95 per dollar, adding pressure on import-heavy sectors.
