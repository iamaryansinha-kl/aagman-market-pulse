Gold Price Crashes Rs 1,834/10g: Biggest Monthly Fall Since October 2008

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold futures with August expiry on MCX fell Rs 1,834 per 10g to Rs 1,40,568, down 1.3% in a single session
  • 02/International spot gold has shed 12.7% this month, heading for its biggest monthly decline since October 2008 and fourth straight monthly fall
  • 03/Analysts expect gold to trade in the Rs 140,500–145,500 range on MCX ahead of US payroll data this week

Gold futures on the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) fell sharply on Tuesday. The August expiry contract dropped Rs 1,834 per 10 grams, or 1.3%, to settle at Rs 1,40,568.

Silver futures also declined. The September expiry contract fell Rs 1,960 to Rs 2,20,674 per kg. December expiry contracts also lost more than 1%.

In international markets, spot gold fell 1.5% to $3,956.92 per ounce. The metal has shed 12.7% so far in June.

This marks the biggest monthly decline for gold since October 2008. It is also on track for a fourth consecutive monthly fall.

Gold is set for its first quarterly decline since 2024. It will also be the worst quarterly drop since June 2013.

Platinum lost 1.1% to $1,557.21 and palladium slipped 0.4% to $1,208.17. All precious metals are heading for monthly and quarterly losses.

Traders now expect three Federal Reserve rate hikes this year. The CME FedWatch Tool prices in a 64% probability of a September increase.

"You have high inflation, high interest rate expectations and a strong dollar, and that is overriding all other bullish factors," said Edward Meir, analyst at Marex.

Gold faced strong resistance near $4,100 on COMEX and around Rs 145,500 on MCX, said Jateen Trivedi, VP Research Analyst at LKP Securities.

Trivedi expects gold to trade in the Rs 140,500–145,500 range on MCX. Rallies will face selling pressure unless US macroeconomic data weakens the dollar.
