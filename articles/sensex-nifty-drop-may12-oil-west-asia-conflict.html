Sensex Nifty Drop May 12: Falls 525 Pts on Oil, West Asia Conflict

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex fell 525.44 points to 75,489.84 in early trade on May 12 as crude oil prices surged
  • 02/Brent crude traded at $105.2 per barrel, up 0.93%; Trump called Iran ceasefire "on massive life support"
  • 03/FIIs offloaded ₹8,437.56 crore on May 11; TCS, Infosys, Tech Mahindra led Sensex laggards

Equity benchmarks tumbled in early trade on Tuesday, May 12, 2026. The 30-share BSE Sensex fell 525.44 points to 75,489.84. The 50-share NSE Nifty dropped 164.5 points to 23,651.35.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, traded 0.93% higher at $105.2 per barrel. Rising crude prices and West Asia conflict uncertainty dragged market sentiment lower.

U.S. President Donald Trump said the ceasefire with Iran was at its "weakest" on May 11. He rejected Tehran's peace proposal as "totally unacceptable."

"The ongoing U.S.-Iran conflict continues to weigh on global sentiment, keeping risk appetite subdued across financial markets," Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, said.

Foreign Institutional Investors offloaded equities worth ₹8,437.56 crore on Monday, May 11, according to exchange data. Unabated foreign fund outflows pressured the markets further.

From the 30 Sensex firms, Tata Consultancy Services, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, HCL Tech, Adani Ports and Bajaj Finserv were among the major laggards. Bharti Airtel and NTPC were the winners.

On Monday, the BSE benchmark tanked 1,312.91 points or 1.70% to settle at 76,015.28. The Nifty dropped 360.30 points or 1.49% to end at 23,815.85.

In Asian markets, South Korea's Kospi and Shanghai's SSE Composite traded lower. Japan's Nikkei 225 and Hong Kong's Hang Seng quoted higher. U.S. markets ended positive on Monday.
