Global Gold Demand Up 2% in Q1 2026: Bar, Coin Buying Surges 42%: WGC

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global gold demand rose 2% YoY to 1,231 tonnes in Q1 2026, with bar and coin investment surging 42% to 474 tonnes amid geopolitical tensions
  • 02/The value of gold demand hit a record $193 billion, up 74% YoY, as average prices soared 81% to $4,873 per ounce
  • 03/Jewellery demand fell 23% to 300 tonnes; central banks added 244 tonnes; higher-for-longer rates may pose headwinds ahead

Global gold demand rose 2% year-on-year to 1,231 tonnes in the January-March quarter of 2026, according to the World Gold Council's Q1 report released on April 29.

The value of demand surged to a record $193 billion, up 74% year-on-year. The average gold price during the quarter jumped 81% to $4,873 per ounce.

Bar and coin investment demand drove the overall increase, surging 42% to 474 tonnes. Geopolitical tensions and record prices attracted retail investors globally.

China led bar and coin buying with a record 207 tonnes, up 67% year-on-year. India, South Korea, Japan, the US, and Europe also posted gains.

Jewellery demand fell sharply by 23% to 300 tonnes. China dropped 32%, India fell 19%, and the Middle East declined 23% amid higher prices.

Central banks added 244 tonnes to global reserves. The Reserve Bank of India purchased 300 kg of gold during the quarter.

Gold ETF holdings rose by 62 tonnes overall. Asian-listed funds added 84 tonnes, while US-listed funds saw outflows in March.

"Geopolitical risk premium should continue to support investment demand, though higher-for-longer interest rates may present headwinds," said WGC analyst Louise Street.
