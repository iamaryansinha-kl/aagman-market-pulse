IMD Forecasts 92% Monsoon, Govt Says El Nino Risk To Agri Manageable

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IMD forecasts below-normal monsoon rainfall at 92% of the long-period average for 2026, flagging El Nino risk during the season
  • 02/Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan says El Nino impact on agriculture will be limited compared to previous episodes due to improved infrastructure
  • 03/Reservoir storage stands at 127% of normal levels; seed stocks secured above requirements; final IMD forecast due in late May

The India Meteorological Department has forecast below-normal monsoon rainfall at 92% of the long-period average for 2026. It has also flagged the risk of El Nino conditions developing during the season.

Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan chaired a preparatory meeting for the Kharif crop season on April 18, 2026. He told officials that farmers need not have any concerns about El Nino.

Reservoir storage across India currently stands at 127% of the normal level for this time of year. This provides a significant buffer for irrigation needs through the growing season.

Seed stocks for both the Kharif and Rabi seasons have been secured above requirements. Emergency reserves are in place should adverse weather force farmers to replant or switch crops.

Officials contrasted current conditions with El Nino episodes between 2000 and 2016. Crop losses were more severe then because farmers relied heavily on rainfall and had fewer tools to cope.

India has since expanded micro-irrigation networks, introduced climate-resilient seed varieties, and strengthened early-warning systems. These changes have materially reduced the sector's vulnerability.

Paddy, the main Kharif crop, was cited as showing greater stability now. Contingency plans are being drawn up for crops more exposed to rainfall shortfall.

Chouhan directed state governments to activate contingency plans down to the district level. He asked officials to promote drought-tolerant varieties and delayed sowing strategies if conditions worsen.

A crop-weather monitoring mechanism is already operational. The Centre and states coordinate regularly to enable quick responses during the season.

India is the world's second-largest producer of rice and wheat. Farm output directly affects the livelihoods of hundreds of millions of people.

El Nino is a periodic warming of Pacific Ocean surface waters. It typically disrupts monsoon patterns over South Asia and has historically been linked to weaker-than-normal rainfall in India.

WHAT IS THE IMD FORECAST FOR MONSOON 2026?
THE INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT HAS FORECAST BELOW-NORMAL MONSOON RAINFALL AT AROUND 92% OF THE LONG-PERIOD AVERAGE. A FINAL FORECAST IS EXPECTED IN LATE MAY 2026.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF WATER RESERVES IN INDIA?
RESERVOIR STORAGE ACROSS THE COUNTRY CURRENTLY STANDS AT 127% OF THE NORMAL LEVEL FOR THIS TIME OF YEAR. THIS PROVIDES A SIGNIFICANT BUFFER FOR IRRIGATION NEEDS DURING THE KHARIF SEASON.
HOW HAS THE GOVERNMENT PREPARED FOR POTENTIAL EL NINO CONDITIONS?
THE GOVERNMENT HAS SECURED SEED STOCKS ABOVE REQUIREMENTS FOR BOTH KHARIF AND RABI SEASONS. IT HAS ALSO EXPANDED MICRO-IRRIGATION NETWORKS, INTRODUCED CLIMATE-RESILIENT SEED VARIETIES, AND STRENGTHENED EARLY-WARNING SYSTEMS.
WHAT DID AGRICULTURE MINISTER SHIVRAJ SINGH CHOUHAN SAY ABOUT EL NINO?
CHOUHAN SAID FARMERS NEED NOT HAVE ANY CONCERNS AND THE GOVERNMENT WAS MOVING AHEAD WITH COMPLETE PREPAREDNESS. HE SAID THE EFFECT ON AGRICULTURE WOULD BE RELATIVELY LIMITED COMPARED TO PREVIOUS EL NINO EPISODES.
WHAT CONTINGENCY PLANS ARE IN PLACE FOR FARMERS?
STATE GOVERNMENTS HAVE BEEN DIRECTED TO ACTIVATE CONTINGENCY PLANS DOWN TO THE DISTRICT LEVEL. OFFICIALS WILL PROMOTE DROUGHT-TOLERANT VARIETIES AND DELAYED SOWING STRATEGIES IF CONDITIONS WORSEN. A CROP-WEATHER MONITORING MECHANISM IS ALREADY OPERATIONAL.
