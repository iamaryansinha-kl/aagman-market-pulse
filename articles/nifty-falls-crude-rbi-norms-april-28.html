Nifty Falls 97 Points to 23,996 as Crude, RBI Norms Hit Sentiment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 closed at 23,995.70, down 97 points or 0.40%, while Sensex shed 416.72 points to 76,886.91 on April 28.
  • 02/Brent crude surged past $110/barrel and RBI's new ECL provisioning norms triggered broad-based selling in banking stocks.
  • 03/Midcaps and smallcaps bucked the trend, gaining 0.28% and 0.42% respectively, with broader market ratios hitting fresh highs.

Equity benchmarks closed lower on Tuesday as a triple whammy of surging crude oil, a weakening rupee, and fresh RBI regulatory pressure dampened sentiment. The Nifty 50 fell 97 points, or 0.40%, to settle at 23,995.70. The BSE Sensex dropped 416.72 points, or 0.54%, to close at 76,886.91.

The session was volatile from the opening bell. The Nifty started at 24,050.65, climbed to an intraday high of 24,181.80, but failed to hold gains. It drifted to a low of 23,957.05 before settling near the 24,000 mark.

On the daily chart, the index formed a small-bodied candle with a prominent upper wick. This was the fourth consecutive session of such formations, pointing to a strong supply zone overhead near 24,200.

Banking stocks were the session's biggest drag. The Reserve Bank of India's finalisation of the Expected Credit Loss (ECL) framework triggered broad-based selling. PSU banks declined nearly 2%, while private banks slipped close to 1%. Axis Bank and State Bank of India were among the notable laggards.

The Bank Nifty fell 863.95 points, or 1.45%, to close at 55,400.35. This marked its fifth consecutive session of decline and a fresh breakdown below last week's low. A recovery above 56,475 is needed to trigger any meaningful upside.

On the other side, Oil & Gas emerged as the top sectoral gainer, rising around 1.5%. ONGC and Adani Enterprises led the charge. Metals, chemicals, and select pharma stocks also attracted buying interest.

The broader market notably decoupled from the benchmarks. The Nifty Midcap index gained 0.28% after breaking above a key resistance zone of 60,350–60,400. The Smallcap index advanced 0.42%, clearing the 17,900–17,920 range. Both the Midcap/Nifty and Smallcap/Nifty ratio charts scaled fresh highs.

Geopolitics remained the dominant macro overhang. Continued disruptions at the Strait of Hormuz, now into the ninth week of the Iran conflict, kept energy markets on edge. Domestic crude futures rose over 3% to near ₹9,400, while natural gas jumped more than 7%.

Foreign institutional investors have been net sellers of ₹44,281 crore through April, adding to the liquidity pressure. The Indian rupee weakened further, crossing the 94.5 level against the US dollar. India VIX eased marginally by 1.80% to 18.04, though 293 of the Nifty 500 stocks ended in the red.

Precious metals came under pressure despite the risk-off backdrop. MCX gold fell over 0.8% and silver dropped around 1.8%. "...crude oil competing for the same pool of inflation-hedge capital..." pulled flows away from metals, though the structural case for gold remains intact.

The near-term outlook hinges on a single variable: geopolitics. "...the U.S. response to Iran's proposal...could serve as a key trigger for market direction..." Technically, the Nifty is consolidating in a broad 23,600–24,400 band. A move above Friday's high of 24,206 could open upside toward 24,400, while a break below 23,813 risks a slide toward 23,600.

HOW MUCH DID THE NIFTY 50 AND SENSEX FALL ON APRIL 28, 2026?
THE NIFTY 50 FELL 97 POINTS, OR 0.40%, TO SETTLE AT 23,995.70. THE BSE SENSEX DROPPED 416.72 POINTS, OR 0.54%, TO CLOSE AT 76,886.91.
WHAT TRIGGERED THE SELLING IN BANKING STOCKS?
THE RESERVE BANK OF INDIA'S FINALISATION OF THE EXPECTED CREDIT LOSS (ECL) FRAMEWORK, WHICH SHIFTS BANKS TO A FORWARD-LOOKING PROVISIONING MODEL, TRIGGERED BROAD-BASED SELLING. PSU BANKS DECLINED NEARLY 2% AND PRIVATE BANKS SLIPPED CLOSE TO 1%.
WHICH SECTORS GAINED DESPITE THE BROADER MARKET DECLINE?
OIL & GAS ROSE AROUND 1.5% WITH ONGC AND ADANI ENTERPRISES LEADING. METALS, CHEMICALS, AND SELECT PHARMA STOCKS ALSO SAW BUYING. THE NIFTY MIDCAP INDEX GAINED 0.28% AND THE SMALLCAP INDEX ADVANCED 0.42%.
WHAT IS THE IMPACT OF RISING CRUDE OIL PRICES ON THE MARKET?
BRENT CRUDE SURGED PAST $110 PER BARREL, DOMESTIC CRUDE FUTURES ROSE OVER 3% TO NEAR ₹9,400, AND NATURAL GAS JUMPED MORE THAN 7%. THIS, COMBINED WITH FII OUTFLOWS OF ₹44,281 CRORE IN APRIL, PUT SIGNIFICANT PRESSURE ON SENTIMENT AND THE RUPEE, WHICH CROSSED 94.5 AGAINST THE US DOLLAR.
