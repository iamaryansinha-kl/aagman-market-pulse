Gold, Silver Jump Up to 2% on MCX as US Jobs Data Eases Rate Hike Bets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MCX gold August futures jump 1.44% to ₹1,47,860 per 10 grams; silver up 1.80% to ₹2,37,494/kg
  • 02/US nonfarm payrolls at 57,000 vs 1,10,000 expected; private payrolls at 98,000 vs 1,18,000 expected
  • 03/September Fed rate hike probability drops to 53% from 75%; gold eyes ₹1,48,000 resistance

Gold and silver prices jumped on the MCX on Friday as softer US jobs data eased expectations of aggressive rate hikes by the Federal Reserve. MCX gold August futures were 1.44% up at ₹1,47,860 per 10 grams. MCX silver September contracts surged 1.80% to ₹2,37,494 per kg.

Gold prices in the US also climbed by more than 1%. The metal is looking set for its first weekly gain in five weeks. Softer-than-expected US jobs data raised expectations that the Fed may delay rate hikes.

The dollar index was down about half a per cent for the week. This made greenback-denominated gold cheaper for buyers in other currencies.

ADP's national employment report showed that private employment in the US rose by 98,000 jobs in June. This was below expectations of a 1,18,000 rise. Nonfarm payrolls increased by just 57,000 in June, below the expected 1,10,000. April and May payrolls were also revised down by 74,000.

"Gold prices steadied after weaker-than-expected US nonfarm payrolls data reduced expectations of another Federal Reserve rate hike this year. The softer labour market data provided relief after gold posted its worst quarterly performance in 13 years," said Ravi Singh, Chief Research Officer at Master Capital Services Limited.

Singh added that hawkish remarks from Fed officials and their commitment to the 2% inflation target continue to limit the metal's broader upside potential.

As per Manoj Kumar Jain of Prithvifinmart Commodity Research, gold has support at $4,084 and $4,040. Resistance is at $4,180 and $4,240 per troy ounce. Silver has support at $59.40 and $58, while resistance is at $63 and $64.40 per troy ounce.

MCX gold has support at ₹1,44,400 and ₹1,43,350. Resistance is at ₹1,47,100 and ₹1,48,800. Silver has support at ₹2,33,000 and ₹2,26,600, with resistance at ₹2,37,700 and ₹2,41,000.

Singh pointed out that MCX gold has formed a strong base near the ₹1,40,500 support zone. The daily chart indicates a bullish reversal from lower levels. "As long as ₹1,40,500 remains intact, the overall outlook remains positive. On the upside, immediate resistance is placed near the 21-day EMA around ₹1,48,000. A sustained move above this level could extend the rally towards the 55-day EMA near ₹1,51,000," he said.

On the geopolitical front, the US and Iran concluded their indirect negotiations in Doha. However, key bones of contention remain unresolved, which continues to support safe-haven demand for gold.
