Bank of Baroda Pays $600 Million to Settle NMC Health Litigation

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bank of Baroda pays $600 million (~₹5,700 crore) to settle NMC Health litigation with no admission of liability
  • 02/Settlement resolves Abu Dhabi and UK court proceedings stemming from 2020 collapse of UAE healthcare giant
  • 03/Bank shares fell 4.3% to ₹259.85 on NSE; Q4 FY26 profit stood at record ₹5,616 crore

State-owned Bank of Baroda has paid $600 million (about ₹5,700 crore) to settle litigation linked to the collapse of NMC Health. The bank disclosed the out-of-court settlement in an exchange filing on Thursday, July 2, 2026.

The payment was made by the bank's Abu Dhabi branch under an agreement with joint administrators of NMC Health PLC, NMC Healthcare Ltd, and NMC Holding Ltd. The settlement resolves all claims without any admission of liability or wrongdoing.

NMC Health was the UAE's largest private healthcare provider before its collapse in April 2020. A report by US short-seller Muddy Waters alleged inflated asset values and hidden debt, triggering the downfall.

Administrators later discovered $6.6 billion in total debt against the $2.1 billion shown in audited statements. The gap of roughly $4.5 billion was concealed through forged documents and fabricated invoices.

The litigation was active in both the Abu Dhabi Global Market Court and the High Court of England and Wales. The Abu Dhabi trial had commenced on March 23, 2026, while English proceedings were stayed pending the outcome.

Following the settlement, the Abu Dhabi proceedings have been discontinued. The English proceedings are in the process of being withdrawn, according to the bank's filing.

Bank of Baroda shares closed 4.3% lower at ₹259.85 on the NSE on July 2. The lender had reported record Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹5,616 crore, up 11% year-on-year, with gross NPA at 1.89%.

THE ABU DHABI GLOBAL MARKET COURT PROCEEDINGS HAVE BEEN DISCONTINUED. THE ENGLISH HIGH COURT PROCEEDINGS ARE IN THE PROCESS OF BEING WITHDRAWN, THE BANK SAID IN ITS FILING.

THE BANK'S SHARES FELL 4.3% TO CLOSE AT ₹259.85 ON THE NSE ON JULY 2, 2026. THE STOCK DECLINED DESPITE THE BANK REPORTING RECORD Q4 FY26 NET PROFIT OF ₹5,616 CRORE.
