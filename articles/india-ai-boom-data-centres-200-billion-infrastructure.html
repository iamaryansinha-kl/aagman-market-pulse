India's AI Boom Turns Data Centres Into $200 Billion Infrastructure Play

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India ranks second globally in AI/ML transactions with 82.3 billion logged between June and December 2025
  • 02/Data centre capacity set to surge from 1.5 GW to 9 GW by 2030 as global majors commit $78.5 billion
  • 03/Government expects $200 billion investment; Budget 2026-27 offers tax holiday till 2047 for foreign cloud workloads

India is witnessing an unprecedented surge in data centre investments driven by artificial intelligence adoption and digital transformation. The government expects total investment to cross $200 billion.

Current data centre capacity stands at approximately 1.5 gigawatts. Industry projections indicate this will expand to 9 GW or more by 2030.

Between October and December 2025, global technology majors announced multi-billion-dollar commitments. Google pledged $15 billion, Microsoft $17.5 billion, Amazon $35 billion, and Digital Connexion $11 billion.

India ranks second globally in enterprise AI and machine learning transactions, behind only the United States. Indian enterprises logged 82.3 billion AI/ML transactions between June and December 2025.

This volume accounts for 46.2 per cent of all AI activity in the Asia-Pacific region. The surge is attributed to government-led digital transformation and cloud-first architectures.

The Union Budget for 2026-27 announced a tax holiday until 2047 for global cloud revenues routed through Indian data centres. This is expected to accelerate foreign workload migration.

Data consumption in India has grown from 8 exabytes in FY17 to 229 exabytes in FY25. Proposals worth $90 billion have been received, with $70 billion entering the construction phase.

"AI is now centre stage. While almost all customers are initiating AI, you need one place to host the infrastructure, and that is where data centres come in," said Ashok Mysore, President - Sales, CtrlS Datacentres.
