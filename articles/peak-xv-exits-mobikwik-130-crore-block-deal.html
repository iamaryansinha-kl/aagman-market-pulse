Peak XV Exits Mobikwik in ₹130 Crore Block Deal at 3x Return

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Peak XV Partners sold 61 lakh Mobikwik shares at ₹214 apiece in a ₹130 crore block deal, fully exiting its 7.7% stake
  • 02/The venture capital firm, an early investor, exited at three times its original investment value after holding the stake for several years
  • 03/The deal comes a day after RBI granted an NBFC license to Mobikwik's new subsidiary, MobiKwik Financial Services Pvt Ltd

Peak XV Partners has fully exited One Mobikwik Systems Ltd (NSE: MOBIKWIK) through a block deal. The venture capital firm sold around 61 lakh shares at an average price of ₹214 apiece.

The total deal size amounts to over ₹130 crore. The transaction represents about 7.7% of the company's total share capital.

Peak XV was among the early investors in the fintech firm. It exited at three times its original investment value, according to a source aware of the development.

"Peak XV Partners has sold around 61 lakh shares, about 7.7 per cent of the company share capital, at an average sale price of ₹214 share apiece. The total deal size is close to ₹130 crore. With this sale, Peak XV has completely exited from the firm," a source said.

Florintree, Viridian Asset Management, Dymon Asia, and Karma Capital were the buyers. These institutional investors acquired the entire stake from Peak XV in the block deal.

The exit comes a day after Mobikwik announced that the Reserve Bank of India granted a non-banking financial company (NBFC) license. The license was for its new subsidiary, MobiKwik Financial Services Pvt Ltd.

One Mobikwik Systems listed on the bourses in December 2024 at ₹528, a 90% premium over its IPO price of ₹279. The stock has since declined sharply and trades near the block deal price of ₹214.

The company reported revenue of ₹866.98 crore and a profit after tax of ₹8.94 crore for FY2024. Promoters Bipin Preet Singh and Upasana Taku together hold about 25% of the company.

A query sent to both Peak XV Partners and One Mobikwik Systems did not elicit an immediate response.
