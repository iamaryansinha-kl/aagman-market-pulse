Haryana Signs MoU for 1 Lakh Acres in Tanzania to Boost Farmer Exports

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Haryana government signed an MoU for one lakh acres of farm land in Tanzania to enable agricultural ventures for state farmers
  • 02/Entrepreneur Shiv Kumar Kamboj says wood costs in Tanzania are one-fourth of Indian prices, labour is significantly cheaper
  • 03/Haryana delegation will visit Tanzania for seven days later this year for the 50th Dar-es-Salaam International Trade Fair

The Haryana government has signed a Memorandum of Understanding for one lakh acres of farmland in Tanzania. Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini announced the pact with Centre support on May 3, 2026.

Saini said trade cooperation with African nations is being expanded across mining, plywood, agriculture, and IT sectors. The state aims to create overseas avenues for its farmers and entrepreneurs.

Somveer Ghasola, 58, from Dadri near Bhiwani has already bought land and set up a mining business near Dodoma. He visited Tanzania in 2024 as part of a state entrepreneur delegation.

"The land is mineral-rich, and whatever you grow, you will have a very good crop," Ghasola told PTI. His land lies between Dar es Salaam and Dodoma, suitable for rice, sugarcane, and cashew.

Shiv Kumar Kamboj, 49, from Yamunanagar acquired a factory from a Chinese national in Tanzania months ago. He processes eucalyptus wood into core veneer for plywood manufacturing.

Kamboj said the cost of wood in Tanzania is one-fourth of Indian rates. Labour is also substantially cheaper, making the venture highly profitable.

Haryana Industry and Commerce Minister Rao Narbir Singh said the state is building a strong global identity. A Haryana delegation will visit Tanzania for seven days to participate in the 50th Dar-es-Salaam International Trade Fair.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH LAND HAS HARYANA SECURED IN TANZANIA FOR FARMERS?
THE HARYANA GOVERNMENT HAS SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR ONE LAKH ACRES (100,000 ACRES) OF FARM LAND IN TANZANIA WITH THE SUPPORT OF THE CENTRE.
WHICH HARYANA ENTREPRENEURS HAVE ALREADY INVESTED IN TANZANIA?
SOMVEER GHASOLA, 58, FROM DADRI NEAR BHIWANI HAS BOUGHT LAND AND SET UP A MINING BUSINESS. SHIV KUMAR KAMBOJ, 49, FROM YAMUNANAGAR ACQUIRED A FACTORY PROCESSING EUCALYPTUS WOOD INTO CORE VENEER.
WHAT ARE THE COST ADVANTAGES OF DOING BUSINESS IN TANZANIA?
ACCORDING TO ENTREPRENEUR SHIV KUMAR KAMBOJ, THE COST OF WOOD IN TANZANIA IS ONE-FOURTH OF WHAT IT IS IN INDIA. LABOUR COSTS ARE ALSO SIGNIFICANTLY LOWER.
WHAT CROPS CAN BE GROWN ON THE TANZANIAN LAND?
GHASOLA STATED THE LAND IS SUITABLE FOR GROWING RICE, SUGARCANE, CASHEW, AND OTHER CROPS. THE SOIL IS MINERAL-RICH AND YIELDS VERY GOOD CROPS.
WHAT IS THE NEXT MAJOR EVENT FOR HARYANA-TANZANIA TRADE TIES?
A HARYANA GOVERNMENT DELEGATION WILL VISIT TANZANIA FOR SEVEN DAYS LATER THIS YEAR TO PARTICIPATE IN THE 50TH DAR-ES-SALAAM INTERNATIONAL TRADE FAIR, WHERE A HARYANA PAVILION WILL BE SET UP.
