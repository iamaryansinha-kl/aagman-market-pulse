India's Million-Plus Cities Lead in Job Quality, Earnings: NSO Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Workers in India's 46 million-plus cities earn more across all job categories, with self-employed making Rs 30,858 vs Rs 23,013 elsewhere
  • 02/58.5% of workers in larger cities hold regular salaried jobs, far above 42.9% in other urban areas
  • 03/Unemployment in million-plus cities fell to 4.9% in 2025 from 7.9% in 2017-18, while labour force participation rose to 52.4%

Workers in India's million-plus cities earned higher wages across all job categories in 2025. The statistics ministry report released on Monday highlights the urban employment divide.

Self-employed workers in larger cities reported average earnings of Rs 30,858 over 30 days. This compares with Rs 23,013 in the rest of urban India.

Regular salaried employees earned Rs 28,808 monthly in million-plus cities. Workers in other urban areas earned Rs 26,258 on average.

Casual labourers earned Rs 624 per day in larger cities versus Rs 550 elsewhere. The data covers 46 cities including Bengaluru, Chennai, Ahmedabad, and Patna.

Nearly 58.5% of workers in million-plus cities held regular salaried jobs. This is significantly higher than the 42.9% in other urban areas.

Casual labour accounted for only 6.3% of employment in larger cities. This is less than half the 14.4% recorded in smaller urban areas.

Unemployment in million-plus cities stood at 4.9% in 2025. The rate has declined steadily from 7.9% in 2017-18 and 5.8% in 2021-22.

Labour force participation reached 52.4% in larger cities. Female participation rose to 27.2% from 19.8% since 2017-18.
