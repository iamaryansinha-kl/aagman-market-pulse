Kotak Q2 FY27: Advances Up 24.7% to Rs 5.77 Lakh Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kotak Mahindra Bank's net advances stood at Rs 5,77,094 crore as of September 30, 2026, up 24.7% from Rs 4,62,688 crore a year earlier
  • 02/Total deposits rose 23.2% YoY to Rs 6,51,491 crore; CASA deposits were up 11.3% at Rs 2,49,105 crore
  • 03/Watch average balances and the $5.78 billion of FCNR(B) deposits included in period-end figures

Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd (NSE: KOTAKBANK) on Monday released provisional figures for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Net advances stood at Rs 5,77,094 crore, up 24.7% from Rs 4,62,688 crore a year earlier.

Sequential growth was also strong. Net advances rose 12.7% from Rs 5,12,249 crore at the end of June 2026.

Total deposits came in at Rs 6,51,491 crore, up 23.2% YoY from Rs 5,28,776 crore. That is a 13.7% jump from Rs 5,72,820 crore at June-end.

CASA deposits — current and savings account balances, the low-cost funding base — stood at Rs 2,49,105 crore. That is up 11.3% YoY from Rs 2,23,791 crore and 7.8% higher than the Rs 2,31,017 crore at June-end.

The bank also disclosed average balances, which smooth out quarter-end effects. Average net advances were Rs 5,46,963 crore, up 22.1% YoY, while average total deposits were Rs 6,06,234 crore, up 18.8% YoY.

One disclosure matters for reading the period-end numbers. End-of-period balances include $5.78 billion (about Rs 55,344 crore) of FCNR(B) deposits raised under the RBI swap facility, converted at the September 30 exchange rate.

Against those deposits, the bank's international branches extended advances of $1.68 billion, roughly Rs 16,110 crore. These sit inside the headline period-end figures.

On the market, the stock closed Monday 0.56% lower at Rs 416 on the NSE. The previous close was Rs 418.35, and the session ranged between Rs 413.75 and Rs 424.45. The shares are down 6.21% year-to-date, per NSE data.

All figures are provisional and subject to limited review by the bank's statutory auditors.
