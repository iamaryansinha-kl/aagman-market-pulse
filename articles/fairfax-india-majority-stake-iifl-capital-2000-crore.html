Fairfax India to Take Majority Stake in IIFL Capital with ₹2,000 Crore Investment

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Fairfax India to invest ₹2,000 crore in IIFL Capital at ₹350 per share through preferential allotment, raising stake to 51%
  • 02/Deal executed via FIH Mauritius subsidiary; triggers mandatory open offer under SEBI regulations; founders continue as co-promoters
  • 03/Stock surged 5.40% to ₹350.45 on NSE; up 33% past month and 51.75% over past year; subject to regulatory approvals

Fairfax India Holdings Corporation will invest ₹2,000 crore in IIFL Capital Services Limited. The board approved the preferential allotment at ₹350 per share on May 7, 2026.

The deal will raise Fairfax India's stake to a minimum of 51 per cent. It will be executed through wholly owned subsidiary FIH Mauritius Investments Ltd.

The transaction combines a preferential allotment, an open offer, and arrangements with existing promoters. It triggers a mandatory open offer under SEBI regulations.

Fairfax India and HWIC Asia Fund Class A will join the existing promoter group. Founders Nirmal Jain and R. Venkataraman will continue as co-promoters.

FIH Mauritius will have the right to nominate two directors to the board. The company was formerly known as IIFL Securities Limited.

The ₹2,000 crore infusion will strengthen IIFL Capital's balance sheet. It will fund growth across capital markets, wealth management, asset management, and investment banking.

IIFL Capital shares closed at ₹350.45 on the NSE on Thursday. The stock gained 5.40 per cent on traded volumes of approximately 33.78 lakh shares.

The stock has risen 33 per cent in the past month and 51.75 per cent over the past year. The company's market capitalisation stands at roughly ₹10,902 crore.
