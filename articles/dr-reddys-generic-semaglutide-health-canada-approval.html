Dr Reddy's Gets Health Canada Nod for Generic Semaglutide in Canada

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Reddy's Laboratories receives Health Canada NOC for generic Semaglutide Injection, becoming the first company authorized in Canada
  • 02/Authorization covers 2mg and 4mg pen doses; Canada has 3.9 million diagnosed diabetes patients representing 9.7% of population
  • 03/Novo Nordisk's Canadian semaglutide patents recently expired; nine companies filed submissions; generic pricing expected 70-85% below brand

Dr Reddy's Laboratories Ltd has received a Notice of Compliance from Health Canada for its generic Semaglutide Injection. The Hyderabad-based company announced the approval on April 29, 2026.

Dr Reddy's becomes the first company to secure market authorization for generic semaglutide in Canada. The approval covers the 2 mg per pen and 4 mg per pen formulations.

The authorization was granted ahead of Health Canada's original review target date. Launch preparations are already underway for the Canadian market.

Canada represents a significant opportunity with 3.9 million people living with diagnosed diabetes. This equals 9.7% of the population over one year old.

Brand-name Ozempic currently costs $253 to $308 per month in Canada. Generic semaglutide is expected to price at $40 to $160 monthly, a discount of roughly 70%.

The Canadian patents for Novo Nordisk's semaglutide treatments expired recently. Health Canada had received nine submissions from generic makers including Sandoz, Apotex, and Teva.

"The approval represents a significant milestone in our GLP-1 journey," said Erez Israeli, CEO of Dr Reddy's. He cited the company's expertise in peptide science and complex product development.

Novo Nordisk's stock has fallen 75% from its 2024 peak amid generic competition. The company guided for a 5-13% sales decline in 2026.

Dr Reddy's shares closed around Rs 1,316 on April 29, down 1.13%. Citi maintains a Sell rating with a target price of Rs 1,070 per share.

The company will announce Q4 and FY2026 results on May 12, 2026. The earnings call is scheduled for 19:30 IST.
