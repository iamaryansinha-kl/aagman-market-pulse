Nifty Rangebound At 24K; SBI's Sudeep Shah Picks 7 Stocks For Next Week

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI Securities analyst Sudeep Shah predicts Nifty will remain rangebound with support at 23,750-23,800 and resistance at 24,250-24,300
  • 02/FIIs turned net buyers at ₹1,309 crore between June 15-25; FII long-short ratio improved from 12.70% to 15.70%
  • 03/Shah picks CG Power, M&M, Maruti Suzuki, Shriram Finance, M&M Finance, TVS Motors, and JB Chem Pharma for the week ahead

Indian stock markets closed the week with marginal gains. Sensex added 109 points to finish at 77,100.47 while Nifty 50 rose 34 points to settle at 24,056.

The benchmark indices had surged over 1% intraday on monthly expiry Thursday. Most of those gains were erased by the closing bell.

Sudeep Shah, Vice President and Head of Technical & Derivatives Research at SBI Securities, expects Nifty to remain rangebound. The index has been stuck in a 477-point band over the past nine sessions.

On the upside, Shah identifies 24,250-24,300 as the immediate hurdle. A sustained move above 24,300 could push Nifty towards 24,500 and then 24,700.

On the downside, the 23,800-23,750 zone is critical support. It aligns with the 20-day EMA and the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement. A breach below this zone could trigger further correction.

The daily RSI is moving sideways and the MACD histogram shows fading bullish momentum. The ADX has slipped to 15.27, confirming the absence of a strong trend.

Derivatives data points to short covering rather than fresh buying. Between June 15 and June 25, Nifty gained 0.78% while open interest fell 1.25%. Net index futures shorts dropped from 2,41,241 contracts to 2,23,809 contracts.

The Put-Call Ratio has largely stayed above 1, signalling stronger put writing. It recovered sharply to 1.21 after dipping to 0.78 on June 23, before moderating to 1.02 on June 25.

FIIs were net buyers to the tune of ₹1,309 crore during this period. The FII long-short ratio improved from 12.70% to 15.70%, indicating gradual unwinding of bearish bets.

Bank Nifty continues to outperform. It faces resistance at 58,700-58,800 and support at 57,500-57,400. Sectors like Private Bank, Financial Services, Auto, Pharma, and Healthcare are expected to lead.

Shah's stock picks for the coming week include CG Power, M&M, Maruti Suzuki, Shriram Finance, M&M Finance, TVS Motors, and JB Chem Pharma. He notes these names are showing favourable price action with improving momentum.

The Nifty IT index remains weak despite this week's recovery. It trades below key moving averages on both daily and weekly charts. A decisive move above 28,200-28,300 is needed for a meaningful reversal.

IndiGo broke out of a symmetrical triangle on June 12 and continues to show strong bullish momentum. SpiceJet, in contrast, remains below key moving averages with a weak technical structure.
