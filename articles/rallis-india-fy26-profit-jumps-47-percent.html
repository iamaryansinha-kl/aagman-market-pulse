Rallis India FY26 Profit Jumps 47% to ₹184 Cr on ₹2,897 Cr Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rallis India FY26 net profit surges 47% to ₹184 crore on ₹2,897 crore revenue, up 9% from FY25
  • 02/Q4 FY26 loss narrows to ₹15 crore from ₹32 crore; March quarter revenue grows 6% to ₹456 crore
  • 03/Board recommends ₹3 per share dividend pending AGM; investors await monsoon and product pipeline outlook

Rallis India Ltd reported FY26 net profit of ₹184 crore, up 47% from ₹125 crore in FY25. Revenue for the full year rose 9% to ₹2,897 crore. The board approved the audited results on April 27, 2026.

The Q4 FY26 net loss narrowed to ₹15 crore from ₹32 crore in Q4 FY25. Revenue for the March quarter grew 6% to ₹456 crore on higher volumes in crop care and seeds segments.

The board has recommended a dividend of ₹3 per equity share. The payout is subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming Annual General Meeting.

"FY26 reflects our continued focus on strengthening the business through focused execution, portfolio expansion, and customer engagement," said Dr Gyanendra Shukla, Managing Director & CEO, Rallis India Limited.

During FY26, the Crop Care business grew 8% and the Seeds segment expanded 15%. B2B exports including Custom Synthesis Manufacturing rose 17%, while Soil and Plant Health grew 8%.

The company launched two new insecticide products, Fiplam and Alstor, during the year. Rallis said Q4 reflected mixed demand conditions, with lower pest pressure in certain crops impacting demand.

Rallis India shares (NSE: RALLIS) closed at ₹261.75 on April 28, marginally higher. The stock has gained roughly 3% over the past year, trailing the broader market.

The company, a subsidiary of Tata Chemicals, faces risks from erratic weather, regulatory scrutiny on pesticide molecules, and global export market volatility. Investors will watch the southwest monsoon and new product launches for FY27 momentum.
