PM Modi Inaugurates ₹9,377 Crore Infrastructure Projects in Telangana

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PM Modi inaugurated and laid foundation for development projects worth ₹9,377 crore in Telangana on May 10, 2026
  • 02/Telangana's railway budget allocation jumped to ₹5,500 crore from ₹1,000 crore for undivided Andhra Pradesh earlier
  • 03/Centre is focusing on energy security amid West Asia crisis; Zaheerabad Industrial Area to create jobs for youth

PM Modi inaugurated and laid foundation stones for development projects worth ₹9,377 crore in Hyderabad, Telangana, on May 10, 2026. The projects span infrastructure and industrial development.

The Centre is according top priority to basic infrastructure creation across the country, Modi said at a public meeting. He emphasized that state development is vital for national progress.

The Zaheerabad Industrial Area was among the key projects for which the foundation stone was laid. Modi stated this would generate employment for Telangana youth.

Telangana's railway budget allocation now stands at ₹5,500 crore. This is up sharply from the ₹1,000 crore allocated to undivided Andhra Pradesh earlier.

Modi highlighted the Centre's focus on energy security. The government is working to ensure the West Asia crisis does not adversely impact India's energy supply.

Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy said the state's Telangana Rising initiative aligns with the Centre's Viksit Bharat vision. He sought continued central support for the state's development.

The event signals sustained central government capex push. Infrastructure spending remains a key policy lever for employment generation and regional economic growth.
