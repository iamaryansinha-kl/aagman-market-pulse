L&T Divests Entire Hyderabad Metro Rail Stake for ₹1,461 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/L&T signs agreement to divest its entire stake in L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) for ₹1,461.47 crore to Telangana government-owned Hyderabad Metro Rail
  • 02/LTMRHL contributed only 0.43% of L&T's consolidated revenue and 0.83% of net worth in FY25 with revenue of ₹1,100.13 crore
  • 03/Deal expected to close by June 30, 2026; buyer will refinance existing debt, releasing L&T's corporate guarantees and letters of comfort

Larsen & Toubro (NSE: LT) has signed a share purchase agreement to divest its entire stake in L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited. The deal value is ₹1,461.47 crore.

The buyer is Hyderabad Metro Rail, a Telangana government-owned entity. The agreement was executed on April 29, 2026.

Upon completion by June 30, 2026, L&T Metro Rail will cease to be a subsidiary. L&T will fully exit the urban metro rail project.

LTMRHL contributed a small portion to L&T's consolidated financials. It accounted for 0.43% of revenue and 0.83% of net worth in FY25.

The subsidiary reported revenue of ₹1,100.13 crore in FY25. Its net worth stood at ₹807.49 crore during the same period.

As part of the deal, the buyer plans to refinance the existing debt of LTMRHL. This will release corporate guarantees and letters of comfort issued by L&T.

The divestment aligns with L&T's strategy to monetise non-core assets. It will recycle capital into core EPC and technology-driven businesses.

The Hyderabad Metro project is one of India's largest PPP metro ventures. It has faced financial challenges from lower ridership and high debt levels.
