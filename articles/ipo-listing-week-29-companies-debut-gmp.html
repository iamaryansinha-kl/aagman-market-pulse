IPO Listing Week: 29 Companies to Debut, GMPs Up to 59%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Twenty-nine companies are lined up for market debuts this week as the primary market shifts from fresh issues to listings
  • 02/Vans Electroengineerings shows the strongest grey market signal at 59.32% GMP after its issue was subscribed 628.73 times
  • 03/At least ten debutants show zero GMP, indicating flat listings; grey market premiums are unofficial and guarantee nothing

Twenty-nine companies are scheduled to make their stock market debuts this week. The listing rush follows a busy September for IPO fundraising.

One name sits outside the tally. RK Fashion Accessories opens for subscription next week, so it is not part of this listing group.

The week's calendar marks a clear shift in the primary market. After weeks dominated by fresh issues and subscription windows, attention now moves to allotment, listing-day price discovery, and early trading.

Grey market signals ahead of these debuts are mixed. A handful of names trade at strong premiums, several sit in the mid-tier, and at least ten show zero grey market premium at all.

A note of caution applies across the board. Grey market premiums are unofficial indicators, and they do not guarantee listing performance.

The strongest signal belongs to Vans Electroengineerings. Its GMP of Rs 70 implies a possible 59.32% listing gain over the issue price of Rs 118. The issue was subscribed 628.73 times, making it one of the most oversubscribed offerings in the group.

Black Opal Consultants follows with a GMP of Rs 95. That translates to 48.22% over its issue price of Rs 197, with subscription at 73.8 times.

EverestIMS Technologies commands a GMP of Rs 40, or 47.06%, over its issue price of Rs 85. Subscription was comparatively modest at 1.7 times, showing that grey market pricing and institutional demand do not always move together.

Orient Cables carries a GMP of Rs 115, implying 42.28% over the issue price of Rs 272. The Rs 552 crore IPO was subscribed 97.28 times.

SRIT India rounds out the strong-signal group. Its GMP of Rs 52 works out to roughly 40% over the issue price of Rs 130, and the issue was subscribed 125.16 times.

The mid-tier is led by Bench Mark Infotech Services, showing a 22.73% GMP with subscription of 103.12 times. Acme Universal Safezone 9 follows at 18.31% GMP and 23.41 times subscription.

Acme India Industries shows a 15.31% GMP on a Rs 121.69 crore issue that was subscribed 1.97 times. Sai Urja Indo sits close behind at 15.04% GMP with 4.95 times subscription.

At the flat end of the spectrum, ten companies are showing zero GMP. These include Paramount Syntex, Dove Soft, Eventions, Sollfege Smart Electronics, Shree TNB Polymers, SJP Ultrasonic, Omara Ventures India, Green Asia Impex, Papadmalji Agro Foods, and Himalayan Solar.

Runwal Enterprises is a notable case. The Rs 500 crore issue, priced at Rs 305 and subscribed 2.64 times, is currently showing no GMP after earlier indications of a premium.

Smaller premiums are visible in a cluster of names. Vishal Nirmiti shows a 9.09% GMP on a Rs 178 crore issue that was subscribed just 0.6 times, meaning it remained undersubscribed. Nityas Gems shows 4% GMP with 0.69 times subscription, also below the full-coverage mark.

Shah Investor's Home carries a 9.28% GMP with 38.12 times subscription. German Green Steel shows 7.55% GMP against 30.42 times subscription.

TNA Solutions displays a 14.29% GMP on a Rs 37.86 crore issue subscribed 1.71 times. Shivchem Agro shows 2.42% GMP with 2.2 times subscription.

Acevector trades at a 1.88% GMP on a Rs 420 crore issue subscribed 5.07 times. Dudani Retail shows 1.72% GMP on a small Rs 10.54 crore issue subscribed 1.42 times, while Pind Hospitality sits at 1.01% GMP with 2.71 times subscription.

The split signals underline a familiar pattern in the primary market. Heavy subscription and grey market premiums frequently diverge, and neither is a reliable predictor of how a stock will trade once listed.

For market participants, the week offers a live test of demand across 29 debutants spanning strong premiums, middling signals, and flat indications. Listing-day outcomes will also depend on broader market conditions, which no grey market number can capture.
