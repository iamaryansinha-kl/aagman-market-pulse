Parexel Acquires Vitrana for Undisclosed Sum, Adds 600 Staff to AI Drug Safety

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Parexel acquires Vitrana for undisclosed sum on April 30, 2026, adding 600 employees and AI pharmacovigilance technology
  • 02/Global pharmacovigilance market valued at $9.35 billion in 2025, projected to reach $31.56 billion by 2034 at 14.69% CAGR
  • 03/Watch how Parexel integrates Vitrana's system-agnostic platform with its 16,000+ employee global clinical network

Parexel International has acquired Vitrana, an AI-enabled pharmacovigilance technology provider, for an undisclosed sum. The deal was announced on April 30, 2026.

Noida-based Vitrana brings 600 employees and a system-agnostic platform that integrates with any safety database. The technology uses intelligent automation and AI.

The acquisition gives Parexel an integrated patient safety and pharmacovigilance platform. It aims to accelerate processes, improve accuracy, and enhance regulatory compliance.

Parexel is a global clinical development partner with over 16,000 employees and approximately $3.8 billion in annual revenue. It was acquired by EQT and Goldman Sachs for $8.5 billion in 2021.

"With Vitrana, we can now offer customers a single partner for both PV technology and services," said Sanjay Vyas, President of Patient Safety Services at Parexel and Managing Director of India.

Vitrana CEO Mohit Gupta said the companies can together offer improved efficiency, quality, and compliance to customers worldwide. Vitrana was founded in 2014 and is based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

The global pharmacovigilance market was valued at $9.35 billion in 2025. It is projected to grow to $31.56 billion by 2034 at a 14.69% CAGR.

The India pharmacovigilance market reached $358.6 million in 2025. Rising regulatory demands and AI adoption are driving consolidation among clinical research service providers.
