HDFC Bank Appoints Ex-Finance Secretary Rajiv Kumar As Part-Time Chairman

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HDFC Bank board appoints Rajiv Kumar as part-time chairman for 3 years, subject to RBI approval
  • 02/Kumar is a 1984-batch IAS officer who retired as Finance Secretary and served as Chief Election Commissioner
  • 03/Former chairman Atanu Chakraborty resigned in March over ethics concerns, wiping Rs 1.6 lakh crore in 3 days

HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK) has appointed Rajiv Kumar as its Part-time (Non-Executive) Chairman. The board approved the appointment on Monday, ending a months-long search after the abrupt exit of Atanu Chakraborty in March.

Kumar will serve as an Additional (Independent) Director for four years from June 30, 2026. His role as Part-time Chairman for three years is subject to Reserve Bank of India approval.

The appointment as Independent Director will additionally require shareholder approval. The bank announced the decision through an exchange filing after its board meeting.

Kumar is a 1984-batch IAS officer who retired as Finance Secretary in February 2020. He is widely known for steering the clean-up of public sector bank balance sheets between 2017 and 2020.

His tenure saw the rollout of the "4R strategy" of Recognition, Resolution, Recapitalisation and Reforms. Capital infusion exceeding Rs 3 lakh crore was pumped into public sector banks during this period.

The consolidation of 27 public sector banks into 12 entities was also completed under his watch. He later served as the 25th Chief Election Commissioner of India, overseeing the 2024 General Elections.

Chakraborty resigned on March 18, stating practices within the bank did not match his personal values. The stock lost 12% in three days, wiping around Rs 1.6 lakh crore from market value.

Keki Mistry was appointed interim part-time chairman for three months following Chakraborty's exit. The bank also announced Puneet Sharma as CFO-Designate from September 1 and full CFO from December 1.

HDFC Bank shares have gained 3% in one week and 7% in one month. The stock has fallen around 20% in 2026 so far.

The company currently commands a market capitalisation of nearly Rs 12.28 lakh crore.
