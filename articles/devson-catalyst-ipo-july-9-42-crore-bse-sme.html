Devson Catalyst IPO Opens July 9: ₹42 Crore Raise, Price Band ₹112-118

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Devson Catalyst IPO opens July 9-13, 2026, aiming to raise ₹42 crore at ₹112-118 per share
  • 02/Issue comprises ₹39.39 crore fresh issue and ₹2.95 crore offer for sale
  • 03/Shares to list on BSE SME platform on July 16; allotment finalises July 14

Devson Catalyst Limited has announced its SME IPO. The issue opens for subscription from July 9 to July 13, 2026.

The company aims to raise ₹42 crore through the public issue. The price band is fixed at ₹112 to ₹118 per share.

The IPO comprises a fresh issue of 0.33 crore equity shares aggregating to ₹39.39 crore. Additionally, there is an offer for sale of 0.03 crore shares worth ₹2.95 crore.

The company has reserved 50% of the issue for Qualified Institutional Buyers. Non-Institutional Investors get 15%, and retail investors get 35%.

In addition, 64,800 equity shares are reserved for employees. The market maker reservation is 1,80,000 shares.

The lot size is fixed at 1,200 shares. Retail investors need a minimum investment of ₹1.41 lakh at the upper price band.

The basis of allotment is expected on July 14. Shares are tentatively scheduled to list on the BSE SME platform on July 16.

JJ IPO Advisors is the book-running lead manager. MUFG Intime India is the registrar, and MNM Stock Broking is the market maker.

Proceeds will fund capital expenditure for a new manufacturing facility. Working capital and general corporate purposes are also included.

Patel Savan Prahladbhai, Managing Director, said the IPO supports capacity expansion. He highlighted growing opportunities in India's refining and petrochemical sectors.

Founded in 2004, Devson Catalyst manufactures catalysts, adsorbents, and ceramic balls. Its products serve petroleum refining, petrochemicals, fertilisers, and specialty chemicals industries.

The company operates manufacturing facilities in Gujarat. It serves both domestic and international customers.
