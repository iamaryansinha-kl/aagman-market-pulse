9 Stocks Brokerages Initiated Coverage On: Up to 42% Upside Potential

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CLSA, Citi, Jefferies, Motilal Oswal and Equirus initiated coverage on 9 Indian stocks with mostly Buy ratings
  • 02/Arvind Fashions has the highest upside target at 42%, followed by Gokaldas Exports and Ethos at 29% each
  • 03/Sectors covered include metals, textiles, power, e-commerce and luxury retail with strong earnings growth forecasts

Domestic and global brokerages have initiated fresh coverage on nine Indian stocks. The sectors span metals, textiles, power transmission, e-commerce and luxury retail.

CLSA started coverage on Vedanta Aluminium with an Outperform rating. The target price of Rs 540 implies over 16% upside from the previous close.

Citi initiated coverage on Meesho with a Buy call and Rs 210 target, implying 12% upside. The company has 264 million annual transacting users, up 33% YoY.

Jefferies initiated coverage on GE Vernova T&D with a Hold rating. The Rs 6,000 target implies 19% upside, with EPS growth forecast at 35-36% CAGR over FY26-29.

Motilal Oswal initiated coverage on four textile stocks with Buy ratings. Gokaldas Exports has the highest upside at 29%, with a target of Rs 1,110.

Arvind Fashions has the highest upside target among all nine stocks at 42%. Motilal Oswal set a target price of Rs 670 on the garment-focused company.

Pearl Global Industries has an 11% upside target at Rs 2,300. Indo Count has a 27% upside target at Rs 550, with revenue CAGR forecast at 20%.

Welspun Living has an 18% upside target at Rs 200. Equirus initiated coverage on Ethos with a 29% upside target, citing India's luxury market growth from $10 billion to $30 billion by FY30.
