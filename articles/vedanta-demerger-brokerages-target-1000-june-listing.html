Vedanta Demerger: Brokerages Set ₹1,000 Target as Split Entities Eye June Listing

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Leading brokerages raise Vedanta target price to ₹1,000 per share, more than triple its current market price of ₹305.40
  • 02/Nuvama expects demerged entities including aluminium, steel, oil & gas and power to list in June with 19-42% EBITDA CAGR through FY28
  • 03/Investec values aluminium business at ₹606 per share and flags value crystallisation as increasingly visible through the demerger

Vedanta Ltd (NSE: VEDL) closed at ₹305.40 on the NSE on Thursday. Leading broking firms have raised target prices to ₹1,000 apiece, over three times the current market price.

Brokerages have turned increasingly constructive on the company. They cite improving fundamentals, stronger free cash flow generation, cost reductions, and significant value unlocking potential from the proposed demerger.

Vedanta delivered record FY26 performance. Annual revenue rose 15 per cent year-on-year to ₹1,74,075 crore. EBITDA increased 29 per cent year-on-year to ₹55,976 crore.

The performance was supported by strong operational execution, margin expansion and continued deleveraging. The numbers beat consensus estimates for the March quarter.

Nuvama expects aluminium, steel and iron ore, oil and gas and power entities to be listed in June. It expects some demerged entities to benefit from firm commodity prices and volume growth.

Nuvama projects a 19-42 per cent EBITDA compounded annual growth rate between FY26 and FY28 for select demerged businesses. Pure-play entities typically command valuation premiums over diversified miners.

Investec has assigned a fair value per share of ₹1,000 to the pre-demerger entity on a sum-of-the-parts basis. It values the aluminium business at ₹606 per share and the flagship Vedanta base metals business at ₹319 per share.

"With value crystallisation increasingly visible and no deterioration in underlying operations, we reiterate our buy stance on Vedanta and recommend staying invested through the demerger process," said Investec.

Kotak Institutional Equities maintained a target price of ₹940. It noted that Vedanta's aluminum business is well-positioned for volume growth and cost reductions from backward integration over FY27-28E.

The commissioning of captive coal mines Kurloi (8 mtpa) and Ghogharapalli (20 mtpa) and the Sijimali bauxite mine (12 mtpa) should drive costs lower over FY27-28E, Kotak said.

CLSA, which has a 12-month price target of ₹835, highlighted improving cost trajectory, capacity expansion and ongoing deleveraging as key re-rating drivers. The restructuring could sharpen investor visibility into each vertical's financial profile.

Emkay Global Financial Services said the restructuring may drive upside through potential valuation re-rating. Pure-play entities typically command a premium over diversified miners, and focused management teams should improve capital allocation.
