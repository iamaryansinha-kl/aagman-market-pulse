IndiGo Resumes Direct Delhi-Guangzhou Flights, Expands China Network

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/IndiGo's flight 6E 1701 from Delhi landed in Guangzhou on April 28, 2026, marking resumption of direct India-China services
  • 02/Air China and China Eastern also restarted routes; Air China Beijing-Delhi fares start at $523 with 3 weekly flights
  • 03/IndiGo stock trades near ₹3,800, down 21% in 1 year; watch for Q4 FY26 results and international yield impact

IndiGo resumed direct flights between Delhi and Guangzhou on April 28, 2026. Flight 6E 1701 departed Delhi at 21:45 IST and arrived in Guangzhou at 04:50 local time.

The return flight 6E 1702 departs Guangzhou at 05:50 and reaches Delhi at 10:10 IST. The route uses Airbus A320neo aircraft.

This follows IndiGo's earlier resumption of Kolkata-Guangzhou and Kolkata-Shanghai routes. The airline now operates multiple daily services to China.

The restoration comes amid warming bilateral ties. Prime Minister Narendra Modi's SCO visit to Tianjin last year marked a diplomatic thaw.

Air China has resumed Beijing-Delhi flights with 3 weekly services on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays. Economy fares start at approximately $523 (₹43,500).

China Eastern Airlines resumed Kunming-Kolkata flights last month. It had reinstated New Delhi-Shanghai services in November 2025.

IndiGo holds 64.5% domestic market share as of June 2025. Revenue from operations grew 4.7% YoY to ₹20,496 crore in Q1 FY26.

Profit after tax dipped 20% YoY to ₹2,176 crore in Q1 FY26 due to geopolitical headwinds and airspace restrictions.

InterGlobe Aviation shares closed at ₹3,800 on April 28, 2026. The stock has declined 21% over the past year from a 52-week high of ₹5,200.
