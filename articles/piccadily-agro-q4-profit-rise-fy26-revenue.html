Piccadily Agro Q4 Profit Up 13.6% to ₹45.2 Cr, FY26 Revenue ₹1,110 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Piccadily Agro Industries Ltd reports Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹45.22 crore, up 13.6% year-on-year from ₹39.80 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/FY26 consolidated income rises 28% to ₹1,142.84 crore; distillery segment revenue surged 65.6% while sugar declined 7.9%
  • 03/Company is demerging sugar business by FY27 end; stock trades at ₹627.75, up 6.92%, on strong premium spirits demand

Piccadily Agro Industries Ltd has reported a 13.6 per cent rise in net profit to ₹45.22 crore for Q4 FY26. The maker of Indri single malt whisky and Camikara Rum had posted ₹39.80 crore profit in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue from operations stood at ₹359.56 crore in the March quarter. This marks a 32.37 per cent jump from the corresponding period last fiscal.

Total income for Q4 FY26 climbed 32.76 per cent to ₹363.63 crore. The Siddhartha Sharma-promoted company continues to ride strong demand for premium spirits.

The distillery segment drove growth with a 65.6 per cent revenue surge in Q4. Sugar operations declined 7.9 per cent during the same period.

Distillery products now account for 68.5 per cent of operating revenue. This reflects the company's pivot toward higher-margin premium alcohol.

For the full FY26, net profit rose 33.8 per cent to ₹137.40 crore. Total consolidated income gained 28 per cent to ₹1,142.84 crore.

"The exceptional 62.6 per cent Q4 growth in our IMFL segment highlights the strength of our premiumisation strategy," said CFO Natwar Aggarwal. He noted the company crossed the ₹1,100 crore revenue milestone in FY26.

Managing Director Harvindar Chopra expects revenue growth to strengthen further. Higher capacity utilisation at Indri and Chhattisgarh facilities will support expansion, alongside new IMFL offerings and white spirit categories.

Shares of Piccadily Agro closed at ₹627.75 on April 29, up 6.92 per cent. The company is demerging its sugar business, with completion expected by end of FY27.
