HUL Q4 Profit Jumps 21% to ₹2,994 Crore; Revenue Up 8% to ₹16,172 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HUL Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 20.96% to ₹2,994 crore from ₹2,475 crore in the year-ago quarter
  • 02/Revenue from operations climbs 8.13% to ₹16,172 crore, driven by 6% underlying volume growth — the best in 12 quarters
  • 03/Shares fell 3% to ₹2,243 on BSE despite strong numbers; FY26 full-year profit at ₹15,059 crore aided by Nutritionalab stake sale

Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) reported a 20.96% jump in consolidated net profit to ₹2,994 crore for Q4 FY26. The profit was aided by volume growth and proceeds from the Nutritionalab stake divestment.

Revenue from operations rose 8.13% to ₹16,172 crore in the January-March quarter. The company called this its highest revenue growth in 12 quarters.

Underlying volume growth stood at 6% during the quarter. This reflects improved demand across both urban and rural markets.

Total income for Q4 FY26 came in at ₹16,580 crore, up 5.01% year-on-year. Total expenses were ₹16,615 crore, rising 7.2% from the previous year.

For the full financial year FY26, HUL posted a profit of ₹15,059 crore. Total income for the year rose 4.6% to ₹65,219 crore.

HUL completed the sale of its 19.8% stake in Nutritionalab for ₹307 crore in March 2026. This divestment contributed to the higher full-year profitability.

"Financial Year 2026 witnessed an improved demand environment driven by supportive macro-economic policies," said Priya Nair, CEO and Managing Director. She warned that heightened geopolitical tensions have led to commodity and currency volatility.

HUL shares closed at ₹2,243.35 on BSE, down 3.01% on April 30. The stock fell despite the strong earnings, reflecting broader market caution.
