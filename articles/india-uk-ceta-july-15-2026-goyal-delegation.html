India-UK CETA From July 15: Goyal Leads 170-Strong Delegation as $120B Trade Target Nears

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India-UK CETA comes into force on July 15, 2026, with 99% of Indian exports receiving zero-duty access to Britain
  • 02/Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal leads 170 businesspersons, the largest-ever Indian delegation to the UK, for a 3-day review visit
  • 03/Bilateral trade target of $120 billion by 2030, up from current $56 billion, as Scotch whisky tariffs drop from 150% to 75%

The India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) takes effect on July 15, 2026. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal announced the date during a business plenary in London on Friday.

Goyal is leading a 170-strong business delegation, the largest ever to leave India. The group spans sectors from textiles to technology, big and small, across the length and breadth of the country.

The CETA was signed on July 24, 2025, after 14 rounds of negotiations spanning three years. It survived four UK prime ministers and two Indian general elections before reaching the finish line.

Under the pact, 99% of Indian exports will enter the UK market duty-free. India opened 89.5% of its tariff lines, covering 91% of UK exports, while safeguarding sensitive sectors like dairy, cereals, and gold jewellery.

Only 24.5% of UK exports will enjoy immediate duty-free access. Strategically important products under Make in India or PLI schemes will see gradual tariff reduction over 5, 7, or 10 years.

Tariffs on Scotch whisky will drop from 150% to 75% immediately upon implementation. They will decline further to 40% by 2035. Automobile duties, currently as high as 110%, will fall to 10% over five years under a managed quota system.

Current bilateral trade in goods and services stands at approximately $56 billion. The agreement targets $120 billion by 2030, more than doubling the existing flow.

"This is the time, the right time," Goyal said, echoing Prime Minister Narendra Modi. "I feel redeemed that what we have finalised as the India-UK CETA is a partnership that is good for both countries, is full of potential, goes beyond the typical trade in goods and services."

"This agreement is very comprehensive, and it will hopefully open the floodgates to not only greater trade, greater business but also strengthening the UK-India partnership," Goyal added.

The treaty spans roughly 20,000 pages and covers 26 sectors. It includes a first-of-its-kind chapter on women and gender, expected to benefit female-led enterprises, especially in West Bengal where 23% of businesses are women-led.

Alongside CETA, the Double Contributions Convention (DCC) will enter into effect. It prevents double payment of social security for up to three years, benefiting approximately 75,000 Indian professionals in the UK.

UK Business and Trade Minister Chris Bryant described the deal as "landmark" and said it goes "well beyond India's precedent in opening the door for UK businesses."

Goyal also addressed ongoing Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA) discussions with the US. He said broad agreement exists on most aspects, with India awaiting reassurances on "comparative advantage" over ASEAN competitors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHEN DOES THE INDIA-UK CETA TAKE EFFECT?
THE COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC AND TRADE AGREEMENT (CETA) BETWEEN INDIA AND THE UNITED KINGDOM COMES INTO FORCE ON JULY 15, 2026. THE AGREEMENT WAS SIGNED ON JULY 24, 2025, AFTER 14 ROUNDS OF NEGOTIATIONS.
WHAT PERCENTAGE OF INDIAN EXPORTS WILL GET DUTY-FREE ACCESS TO THE UK?
99% OF INDIAN EXPORTS WILL ENTER THE UK MARKET AT ZERO DUTY. THIS COVERS TEXTILES, LEATHER, MARINE PRODUCTS, GEMS AND JEWELLERY, TOYS, ENGINEERING GOODS, CHEMICALS, AND AUTO COMPONENTS.
WHAT IS THE BILATERAL TRADE TARGET UNDER THE CETA?
THE AGREEMENT AIMS TO DOUBLE BILATERAL TRADE IN GOODS AND SERVICES FROM THE CURRENT APPROXIMATELY $56 BILLION TO $120 BILLION BY THE YEAR 2030.
HOW WILL THE CETA IMPACT SCOTCH WHISKY AND AUTOMOBILE TARIFFS?
TARIFFS ON SCOTCH WHISKY WILL DROP FROM 150% TO 75% IMMEDIATELY UPON IMPLEMENTATION, FALLING FURTHER TO 40% BY 2035. AUTOMOBILE IMPORT DUTIES, CURRENTLY UP TO 110%, WILL REDUCE TO 10% OVER FIVE YEARS UNDER A MANAGED QUOTA SYSTEM.
WHAT IS THE DOUBLE CONTRIBUTIONS CONVENTION (DCC) AND WHO BENEFITS?
THE DCC IS A PARALLEL TREATY PREVENTING DOUBLE PAYMENT OF SOCIAL SECURITY CONTRIBUTIONS FOR INDIAN PROFESSIONALS WORKING IN THE UK FOR UP TO THREE YEARS. IT IS EXPECTED TO BENEFIT APPROXIMATELY 75,000 INDIAN PROFESSIONALS, PRIMARILY IN IT AND HOSPITALITY SECTORS.
