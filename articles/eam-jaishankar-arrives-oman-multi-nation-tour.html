EAM Jaishankar Arrives in Oman as Part of Multi-Nation Tour

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's top diplomat is touring the Gulf.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has arrived in Oman as part of a multi-nation tour that includes visits to several countries in the region. The trip focuses on strengthening bilateral ties, reviewing economic cooperation, and discussing regional security issues.

Oman is a strategically important partner for India. The Gulf nation sits at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz, through which a significant portion of India's oil imports pass. Oman also hosts a large Indian diaspora, with over 700,000 Indians living and working in the country.

Trade between India and Oman has been growing steadily. Bilateral trade crossed $10 billion in recent years, with India exporting textiles, machinery, and chemicals, and importing oil, gas, and petrochemicals. Both countries are looking to expand this relationship further.

Investment ties are also deepening. Indian companies have invested in Oman's ports, infrastructure, and manufacturing sectors. Oman has been encouraging Indian investment as part of its diversification strategy away from oil dependence.

The visit follows PM Modi's recent tour of Australia. Jaishankar's multi-nation tour is part of India's broader diplomatic outreach to strengthen relationships with key partners amid shifting global geopolitics. The Gulf region is particularly important given India's energy security needs.

Defence and security cooperation are also on the agenda. India and Oman conduct joint naval exercises and share intelligence on maritime security. The region has become more volatile following recent tensions between Iran and the United States, making security cooperation even more critical.
