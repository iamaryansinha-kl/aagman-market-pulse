SEBI Bars Ashok Maheshwari, 7 Others in Front-Running Case; Orders ₹1.29 Cr Disgorgement

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI bars Ashok Maheshwari and 7 others from securities markets for 24 to 48 months in a front-running case involving Unifi Capital trades
  • 02/Regulator orders disgorgement of ₹1.29 crore unlawful gains with 12% annual interest plus total penalties of ₹1.52 crore
  • 03/Order follows interim action from April 2024; noticees must pay within 45 days to Investor Protection and Education Fund

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has barred eight entities from the securities market for front-running trades of a portfolio management service provider. The final order was issued on April 28, 2026.

Ashok Maheshwari and Darshan Bakul Shah face the strictest penalty. They have been debarred for 48 months, including time already served under an interim order.

Benzer Department Stores Pvt. Ltd. and Mihir Dhirajalal Savla are barred for 24 months. CHL Stock Concepts Pvt. Ltd. and Chirag Mahendra Shah face a 36-month debarment.

Khusboo Darshan Shah and Darshan Bakul Shah (HUF) have already completed their 24-month market restraint. No further debarment was imposed on them.

The front-running occurred between April 1, 2021 and May 31, 2022. The entities traded in common scrips proximate to orders of the "Big Client," identified as Unifi Capital Pvt. Ltd.

"Noticees 1 and 2 collaborated to execute a carefully crafted scheme of front running the orders of the Big Client," said SEBI whole-time member Amarjeet Singh in the 78-page order. He called it "fraudulent, manipulative, and an unfair trade practice."

SEBI has directed all noticees to disgorge ₹1.29 crore in unlawful gains. They must also pay simple interest at 12% per annum from May 31, 2022 until payment.

Individual penalties range from ₹15 lakh to ₹25 lakh. The total penalty amount is ₹1.52 crore. Both disgorgement and penalties must be paid within 45 days to the Investor Protection and Education Fund.

Additionally, Ashok Maheshwari, Darshan Bakul Shah, and Chirag Mahendra Shah are barred from acting as directors or key managerial personnel in listed companies or SEBI-registered intermediaries for two years.

The order comes in continuation of SEBI's interim action from April 2024. It takes immediate effect.

SEBI HAS ORDERED DISGORGEMENT OF ₹1.29 CRORE IN UNLAWFUL GAINS PLUS SIMPLE INTEREST AT 12% PER ANNUM FROM MAY 31, 2022. ADDITIONALLY, TOTAL PENALTIES OF ₹1.52 CRORE HAVE BEEN IMPOSED, WITH INDIVIDUAL FINES RANGING FROM ₹15 LAKH TO ₹25 LAKH PER ENTITY.

ASHOK MAHESHWARI AND DARSHAN BAKUL SHAH ARE BARRED FOR 48 MONTHS. CHL STOCK CONCEPTS PVT. LTD. AND CHIRAG MAHENDRA SHAH ARE BARRED FOR 36 MONTHS. BENZER DEPARTMENT STORES PVT. LTD. THE ENTITIES FRONT-RANNED TRADES OF A PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT SERVICE PROVIDER IDENTIFIED AS UNIFI CAPITAL PVT. LTD., REFERRED TO AS THE "BIG CLIENT" IN SEBI'S 78-PAGE ORDER. THE FRONT-RUNNING OCCURRED BETWEEN APRIL 1, 2021 AND MAY 31, 2022.

ALL NOTICEES MUST PAY THE DISGORGEMENT AND PENALTIES WITHIN 45 DAYS TO THE INVESTOR PROTECTION AND EDUCATION FUND.
