Rubio's India Mission: US Aims to Repair Ties Hit by Trump Tariffs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/US Secretary of State Marco Rubio arrives in India on May 23, 2026, for a four-day trip covering New Delhi, Kolkata, Agra, and Jaipur
  • 02/The visit aims to repair ties strained by Trump's tariffs, renewed US-Pakistan closeness, and Trump's recent engagement with China
  • 03/A February interim trade deal cut tariffs on Indian goods to 18% from 50%, but talks have stalled since the Supreme Court brought duties to 10%

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio lands in India on Saturday, May 23, 2026. The four-day trip includes stops in New Delhi, Kolkata, Agra, and Jaipur.

The visit aims to stabilize a partnership battered by President Donald Trump's tariffs. Washington has also grown closer to Pakistan and China, alarming Indian policymakers.

US presidents have long courted India as a counterweight to rising Chinese influence. Trump imposed some of the highest US tariffs on Indian goods last year.

In February, both nations reached an interim trade deal. It lowered tariffs on Indian goods to 18% from a punishing 50%, half tied to India's Russian oil purchases.

The US Supreme Court struck down Trump's tariffs in late February. That move effectively brought the duty rate on Indian goods down to 10%.

Talks to finalize a comprehensive trade deal have since slowed. New Delhi has been weighing its options as the Trump administration pursues new investigations.

Rubio said energy exports will be a major focus. "We want to sell them as much energy as they'll buy," he told reporters on Thursday ahead of the trip.

"They're a great ally, a great partner. We do a lot of good work with them," Rubio said. He will meet Quad foreign ministers in Delhi next week.

Former State Department expert Basant Sanghera said Trump's approach had "created a perfect storm of anxiety" in India. He added that ties have now stabilized.

Richard Rossow of the Center for Strategic and International Studies struck a cautious note. He said the lack of a finalized trade deal clouds other areas of engagement.

MARCO RUBIO LANDED IN INDIA ON SATURDAY, MAY 23, 2026, FOR A FOUR-DAY OFFICIAL VISIT. HIS ITINERARY INCLUDES NEW DELHI, KOLKATA, AGRA, AND JAIPUR.
WHAT IS THE MAIN PURPOSE OF RUBIO'S VISIT TO INDIA?
THE TRIP AIMS TO REPAIR US-INDIA TIES STRAINED BY TRUMP'S TARIFFS, WASHINGTON'S RENEWED ENGAGEMENT WITH PAKISTAN, AND TRUMP'S RECENT VISIT TO BEIJING WHICH AMPLIFIED CONCERNS IN NEW DELHI.
WHAT IS THE CURRENT STATUS OF US-INDIA TRADE TALKS?
THE TWO COUNTRIES REACHED AN INTERIM TRADE DEAL IN FEBRUARY THAT CUT TARIFFS ON INDIAN GOODS TO 18% FROM 50%. THE US SUPREME COURT LATER STRUCK DOWN THE TARIFFS, EFFECTIVELY BRINGING THE DUTY RATE TO 10%. TALKS TO FINALIZE A COMPREHENSIVE DEAL HAVE SINCE SLOWED.
WHAT DID RUBIO SAY ABOUT US-INDIA ENERGY COOPERATION?
RUBIO SAID ENERGY WOULD BE A KEY FOCUS OF HIS VISIT. "WE WANT TO SELL THEM AS MUCH ENERGY AS THEY'LL BUY," HE SAID ON THURSDAY. THE US IS ALREADY IN TALKS TO EXPAND ITS SHARE OF INDIA'S ENERGY SUPPLY.
WHAT ARE ANALYSTS SAYING ABOUT THE OUTLOOK FOR US-INDIA RELATIONS?
BASANT SANGHERA OF THE ASIA GROUP SAID TIES HAVE STABILIZED AND BOTH SIDES ARE TRYING TO BUILD MOMENTUM. HOWEVER, RICHARD ROSSOW OF CSIS SAID RUBIO IS UNLIKELY TO CHANGE THE DOWNWARD TRAJECTORY WITHOUT A FINALIZED TRADE AGREEMENT.
