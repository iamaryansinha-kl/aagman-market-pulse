Oil Price Today May 18: Crude Above $110 as Iran Tensions Escalate

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent crude climbed above $110 per barrel on May 18 as hopes for Iran peace deal dimmed after UAE nuclear plant attack
  • 02/President Trump is reportedly reviewing military action options; drone attacks on UAE and Saudi Arabia increase
  • 03/Energy markets remain on edge as geopolitical tensions threaten global oil supply routes and stability

Crude oil prices climbed above $110 per barrel on Monday, May 18, 2026. Hopes for a peace deal with Iran dimmed sharply following a major escalation.

A drone attack targeted a nuclear plant in the United Arab Emirates over the weekend. The strike raised immediate fears of broader regional conflict spreading across the Gulf.

US President Donald Trump is now reviewing military action options in response. The White House has not confirmed a timeline for any potential response.

Drone attacks on energy facilities in both the UAE and Saudi Arabia have increased in recent days. These strikes directly threaten the heart of global crude production and export infrastructure.

Brent crude has been volatile for weeks as Iran-related headlines shift market sentiment. Supply disruption fears have kept prices elevated despite concerns about slowing demand.

The Strait of Hormuz remains a critical chokepoint for roughly 20% of global oil shipments. Any military escalation near this corridor would send immediate shockwaves through energy markets worldwide.

Analysts warn that sustained prices above $110 could increase inflationary pressure. Major economies including India and China are heavily exposed to rising import costs.

Traders are watching for any signs of de-escalation or further military response. The next 48 hours could determine whether crude stabilizes or breaks toward new multi-year highs.
