Q4 Results April 30 Live: Adani Ports, HUL, Bajaj Finance Profit Up

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Ports Q4 cons profit rises 9% y-o-y to ₹3,308.30 crore; cargo volumes drive growth
  • 02/HUL posts highest growth in 12 quarters with 8% revenue at ₹16,207 crore and 7% USG
  • 03/Bajaj Finance net income up 22% to ₹54.6 billion; AUM crosses ₹5.1 trillion mark

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd (NSE: ADANIPORTS) reported consolidated net profit of ₹3,308.30 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 9% rise from ₹3,023.10 crore in the year-ago quarter, driven by higher cargo volumes and revenue growth.

The company's standalone profit fell sharply to ₹198.83 crore from ₹798.21 crore in Q4 FY25. FY26 consolidated PAT stood at ₹12,782.03 crore against ₹11,061.26 crore last year. The board recommended a dividend of ₹7.50 per share.

Hindustan Unilever Ltd (NSE: HINDUNILVR) posted consolidated revenue of ₹16,207 crore, up 8% y-o-y. Underlying sales growth hit 7% with 6% volume growth, the highest in 12 quarters. "Financial Year 2026 witnessed an improved demand environment driven by supportive macro-economic policies," said Priya Nair, CEO and Managing Director, HUL.

Reported PAT rose 20% to ₹3,002 crore, including proceeds from the Nutritionalab stake sale. Standalone net profit was up 17.5% at ₹2,930 crore. EBITDA margin improved sequentially by 40 bps to 23.7%.

Bajaj Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJFINANCE) reported Q4 net income of ₹54.6 billion, up 22% y-o-y. Revenue rose 18% to ₹216.1 billion, while assets under management crossed ₹5.1 trillion. Gross NPAs improved to 1.01% from 1.21% q-o-q.

Morgan Stanley maintained Overweight on Bajaj Finance with a target price of ₹1,120. Citi upgraded to Buy, citing 22% PAT growth and improving core credit costs. The company declared a dividend of ₹6 per share.

Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Q4 profit jumped 30% y-o-y to ₹1,640.71 crore. Vedanta reported an 89% surge in net profit to ₹9,352 crore on higher aluminium and zinc prices.

Adani Enterprises standalone profit plunged 92% to ₹343.56 crore. Waaree Energies shares fell 10% despite profit doubling to ₹10.6 billion, as EBITDA margin contracted to 18.58% from 23.04%. The Sensex closed 582.86 points lower at 76,913.50.
