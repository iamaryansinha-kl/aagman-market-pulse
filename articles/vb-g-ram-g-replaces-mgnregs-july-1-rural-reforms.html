VB-G RAM G Replaces MGNREGS July 1: ₹1.51 Lakh Crore Rural Push

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan urged states to propose new reforms and implement existing schemes effectively for India's 2047 developed nation vision
  • 02/VB-G RAM G programme will replace MGNREGS from July 1 with Centre releasing ₹95,692 crore and total FY27 allocation exceeding ₹1.51 lakh crore
  • 03/PMAY-G budget raised 69% to ₹54,917 crore; Cabinet approved ₹3.06 lakh crore for 30 million rural houses over five years

Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan on Sunday called on states to propose fresh reforms. He asked them to also implement existing rural schemes more effectively.

Addressing a two-day ministry event in New Delhi, Chouhan said India's growth story is tied to village progress. He stressed states must take benefits to beneficiaries in the furthest corners.

The push comes amid the West Asia war disrupting global supply chains. India is looking to pull all domestic growth levers to beat external headwinds.

Chouhan said nearly all states have made budgetary provisions to adopt the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission-Gramin (VB-G RAM G). This programme replaces the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS) from July 1.

The Centre this month released its FY27 budgetary outlay of ₹95,692 crore to states for VB-G RAM G. Combined with mandatory state contributions, the total FY27 allocation will exceed ₹1.51 lakh crore.

The Centre had separately allocated ₹30,000 crore for MGNREGS this fiscal year. This covers the period until the new programme replaces it from the second quarter.

VB-G RAM G aims to align the rural job pledge with the "developed India by 2047" vision. It focuses on four verticals: core rural infrastructure, water security, livelihood, and climate mitigation.

On rural housing, Chouhan said beneficiary lists are almost finalised after a new survey. He asked states to submit any remaining names quickly.

The government raised the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Gramin budget to ₹54,917 crore for FY27. This is up 69% from the previous year's revised estimate of ₹32,500 crore.

The Cabinet in 2024 approved ₹3.06 lakh crore for building 30 million new houses over five years. Of these, 20 million will be built under PMAY-G.

Higher rural spending is expected to spur economic growth. It will also support rural demand and job creation across the hinterlands.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS VB-G RAM G AND WHEN DOES IT REPLACE MGNREGS?
VIKsit BHARAT – GUARANTEE FOR ROZGAR AND AJEEVIKA MISSION-GRAMIN (VB-G RAM G) IS THE NEW RURAL EMPLOYMENT PROGRAMME THAT WILL REPLACE THE MAHATMA GANDHI NATIONAL RURAL EMPLOYMENT GUARANTEE SCHEME (MGNREGS) FROM JULY 1, 2026. IT AIMS TO CREATE EMPLOYMENT AND DURABLE ASSETS IN RURAL AREAS.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL BUDGETARY ALLOCATION FOR VB-G RAM G IN FY27?
THE CENTRE HAS RELEASED ₹95,692 CRORE TO STATES FOR FY27. COMBINED WITH MANDATORY STATE CONTRIBUTIONS, THE TOTAL ALLOCATION FOR VB-G RAM G WILL EXCEED ₹1.51 LAKH CRORE.
HOW MUCH HAS THE PMAY-GRAMIN BUDGET BEEN INCREASED?
THE PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA-GRAMIN (PMAY-G) BUDGET HAS BEEN RAISED TO ₹54,917 CRORE FOR THE CURRENT FISCAL YEAR, UP FROM ₹32,500 CRORE IN THE PREVIOUS YEAR'S REVISED ESTIMATE — A 69% INCREASE.
WHAT ARE THE FOUR PRIORITY VERTICALS OF VB-G RAM G?
THE FOUR PRIORITY VERTICALS ARE: (1) CORE RURAL INFRASTRUCTURE, (2) WATER SECURITY, (3) LIVELIHOOD, AND (4) CLIMATE MITIGATION. THESE VERTICALS AIM TO SPUR BOTH EMPLOYMENT AND DURABLE ASSET CREATION IN RURAL AREAS.
WHAT IS THE SCALE OF THE RURAL HOUSING SCHEME APPROVED BY THE CABINET?
THE CABINET APPROVED ₹3.06 LAKH CRORE FOR BUILDING 30 MILLION NEW HOUSES ACROSS THE COUNTRY OVER FIVE YEARS. OF THESE, 20 MILLION HOUSES WILL BE BUILT UNDER THE PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA-GRAMIN (PMAY-G).
