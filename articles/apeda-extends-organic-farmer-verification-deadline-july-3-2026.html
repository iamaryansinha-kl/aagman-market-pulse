APEDA Extends Organic Farmer Verification Deadline to July 3, 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/APEDA extends deadline for 100% physical verification of organic farmers to July 3, 2026, covering 19.29 lakh certified farmers across 4,712 grower groups
  • 02/Only 52% of farmers verified so far since the process began on November 3, 2025; this marks the second extension granted by the authority
  • 03/Unverified farmers face Tracenet system blocking from July 4; grower groups with under 20% verification must achieve 50% coverage by June 3

APEDA has extended the deadline for 100% mandatory physical verification of Indian organic farmers to July 3, 2026. The authority cited network issues, record updation delays, and temporary farmer migration as reasons.

This is the second extension for the verification process. It originally began on November 3, 2025, with an initial three-month deadline.

So far, 52% of total farmers have been physically verified. APEDA Chairman Abhishek Dev confirmed the progress figure in an official communication.

As of July 29, 2025, there were 19,29,243 farmers certified under NPOP across 4,712 active organic grower groups. The government had earlier reported 25 lakh organic farmers in July 2022.

After July 3, the Tracenet system will block all operations for unverified farmers. This includes yield updation and sourcing from their lots.

Grower groups that have verified less than 20% of farmers must reach 50% coverage by June 3. Transaction Certificates for defaulters will be withheld from June 4 until full compliance is achieved.

An industry source said verification costs including fees and travel could reach ₹2 lakh per farmer group. The expense burden may explain delays among smaller grower groups.
