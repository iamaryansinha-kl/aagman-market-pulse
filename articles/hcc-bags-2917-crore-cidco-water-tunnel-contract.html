HCC Bags ₹2,917 Crore CIDCO Water Tunnel Contract in Joint Venture

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/HCC secures ₹2,917.6 crore water infrastructure contract from CIDCO, with its JV share at approximately ₹1,100 crore
  • 02/Project includes 22.21 km raw water tunnel and 250 MLD treatment plant in Raigad using TBM technology under DBO model
  • 03/HCC shares fell 3.49% to ₹19.89 on the announcement day amid heavy sell-side pressure

Hindustan Construction Company (NSE: HCC) has bagged a ₹2,917.6 crore water infrastructure contract from CIDCO. The City and Industrial Development Corporation of Maharashtra awarded the project on Friday.

The contract covers a 22.21 km raw water tunnel and a 250 MLD water treatment plant in Raigad district. The tunnel will carry water from Kondhane Dam in Karjat to Village Mohope in Panvel.

The project has been awarded to the LCESPL-HCC Joint Venture. HCC's share of execution is pegged at approximately ₹1,100 crore.

The tunnel will use Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM) technology. The scope extends beyond construction to include design, supply, erection, and commissioning.

HCC will also handle 15 years of operation and maintenance. The contract follows a Design, Build and Operate (DBO) model, adding long-term revenue visibility.

CIDCO has positioned the project as central to its water supply strategy for Navi Mumbai and surrounding areas in Raigad district.

HCC shares traded at ₹19.89 on Friday, down 3.49% from the previous close of ₹20.61. About 76% of the traded volume was on the sell side.

The stock has gained over 35% in the past month. It remains approximately 13% lower on a year-on-year basis.
