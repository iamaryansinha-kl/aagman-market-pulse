Gold ETF Inflows Rebound $6.6 Billion in April; India 11-Month Streak

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global physically backed gold ETFs recorded inflows of $6.6 billion in April, reversing March outflows
  • 02/India added $297 million, marking its 11th consecutive month of inflows; Asia total reached $1.8 billion
  • 03/Total AUM rose to $615 billion with holdings at 4,137 tonnes, the third-highest level on record

Global physically backed gold ETFs recorded inflows of $6.6 billion in April. This reversed the notable outflows seen in March, according to the World Gold Council.

All regions registered positive flows during the month. European funds led with $3.7 billion, flipping the region's year-to-date total from negative to positive.

The April expansion lifted global gold ETF assets under management to $615 billion. Collective holdings rose 1 per cent to 4,137 tonnes, the third highest ever.

India recorded positive flows of $297 million, marking its 11th consecutive month of inflows. Asia extended its streak to eight months with $1.8 billion in total.

Hong Kong funds added a record $732 million. Mainland China continued to draw inflows of $498 million amid elevated geopolitical tensions.

North America reversed course with $1 billion in inflows. The rebound was concentrated in the first half of April as gold recovered from March lows.

"Flows softened again in the back half of April as the US-Iran conflict showed signs of further escalation and higher opportunity costs re-emerged through a stronger dollar and higher yields," the WGC report noted.

Global gold market trading volumes fell 24 per cent month-on-month to $398 billion per day. Despite the decline, volumes remained above the 2025 average of $361 billion.
