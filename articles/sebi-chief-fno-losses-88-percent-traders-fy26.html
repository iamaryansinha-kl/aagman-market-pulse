Sebi Chief Pandey: 88% of Individual F&O Traders Lost Money in FY26 Even After His Reforms

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- 01/ Individual traders' aggregate F&O losses fell to about Rs 91,685 crore in FY26 from Rs 1.12 lakh crore in FY25.
  • •- 02/ Still, 88% of individual traders lost money during FY26.
  • •- 03/ Participation dropped about 20%, with 46 lakh traders exiting — versus 26 lakh exits a year earlier.
  • •- 04/ Proprietary traders made gross profits of about Rs 44,000 crore; FPIs made Rs 14,000 crore.
  • •- 05/ Sebi says 99% of FPI and proprietary profits came from algorithmic trading entities.

## Losses shrink, but the odds stay terrible

Sebi Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey on Thursday said the regulator's derivatives-market reforms have cut aggregate losses, but individual traders are still losing money — often after years in the segment.

The numbers make his point. A Sebi study released in August showed individual traders' aggregate net losses in equity derivatives fell to about Rs 91,685 crore in FY26 from roughly Rs 1.12 lakh crore in FY25. Yet 88% of individual traders still lost money during the year.

Many had been at it for a while. Pandey noted that traders continue to lose money even after three to four years of participation, making it important for investors to honestly assess whether derivatives trading works for them.

## The reforms did bite

Part of the loss reduction is simply fewer traders. Individual participation in equity derivatives fell about 20% in FY26, and exits accelerated: 46 lakh traders who participated in FY25 did not return in FY26, compared with 26 lakh exits the previous year.

Sebi's tightening measures included higher contract sizes for index derivatives, fewer weekly index expiries and upfront collection of option premiums. The stated aim was to cool excessive retail speculation.

## Who is making the money

The same study sketches a sharply divided market. Proprietary traders recorded the highest gross trading profit at about Rs 44,000 crore in FY26, followed by foreign portfolio investors at about Rs 14,000 crore.

Sebi said 99% of the profits earned by FPIs and proprietary traders came from algorithmic trading entities.

That is the structural reality individual traders face: larger, faster and better-capitalised participants running machines. The regulator said it will continue to monitor derivatives trading.

## FAQ Q: Did Sebi's F&O reforms reduce losses? A/01/ Yes — aggregate individual losses fell from about Rs 1.12 lakh crore in FY25 to Rs 91,685 crore in FY26, helped by a 20% drop in participation.

Q: What share of individual traders lost money in FY26? A/02/ 88%. Many kept losing even after three to four years in the segment.

Q: Who profits in the F&O market? A/03/ Proprietary traders (about Rs 44,000 crore gross) and FPIs (about Rs 14,000 crore), with 99% of those profits coming via algorithmic trading.

Q: What did Sebi change in derivatives rules? A/04/ It raised index-derivative contract sizes, cut weekly expiries and made option premiums payable upfront.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS - Losses fell to Rs 91,685 crore, but 88% of individual traders still lost money. - 46 lakh traders exited the segment in FY26 — nearly double the prior year's exits. - The profits pool sits with pro desks and FPI algos, not retail. - The Sebi chief's message: assess whether F&O trading works for you — most often, it does not.
