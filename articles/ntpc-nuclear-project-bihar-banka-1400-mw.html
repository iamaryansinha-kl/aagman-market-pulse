NTPC Plans ₹25,000 Cr Nuclear Project: 1,400 MW in Bihar's Banka

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NTPC is exploring two 700 MW nuclear units in Banka, Bihar, with an estimated investment of ₹25,000 crore
  • 02/The project requires around 1,000 acres of land; NTPC officials are currently conducting a feasibility study
  • 03/NTPC targets 30 GW of company-owned nuclear capacity by 2047, aligning with India's 100 GW nuclear goal

NTPC Ltd (NSE: NTPC) is planning two nuclear power units of 700 MW each in Banka district, Bihar. The combined 1,400 MW project could entail an investment of around ₹25,000 crore.

NTPC officials are currently conducting a feasibility study for the two units. The district is located approximately 250 km from Patna.

The Bihar government has assured full support for the project. It has also committed to ensuring water availability for the nuclear plant.

Initial estimates suggest around 1,000 acres of land will be required. A government official told PTI that a detailed project report will follow the feasibility study.

Industry estimates indicate a 1 GW nuclear plant needs ₹15,000-20,000 crore in investment. Such projects typically take at least three years from concept to commissioning.

NTPC is targeting around 30 GW of company-owned nuclear projects across India. This aligns with the government's ambition of achieving 100 GW nuclear capacity by 2047.

The power PSU is eyeing at least 2 GW of nuclear capacity as early as 2032. It is already building a ₹42,000 crore nuclear JV with NPCIL in Rajasthan.

NTPC shares closed at ₹401.85 on the NSE on April 24, 2026. The stock has gained 29% over its 52-week low of ₹315.55.
