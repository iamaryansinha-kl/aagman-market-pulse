CIL Coal Auction Volumes Dip 6% in April: 30.5 MT Offered

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CIL offered 30.5 MT coal in April via SWMA auctions, marking a 6% sequential decline from March's 32.5 MT
  • 02/FY26 production fell 1.7% to 768.1 MT; early FY27 output down ~6% YoY; allocation rate at 39%
  • 03/Cross-border buyers from Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal now have direct SWMA access since January 1, 2026

Coal India Ltd (NSE: COALINDIA) offered 30.5 million tonnes (MT) of coal through online SWMA auctions in April 2026. This marks a 6% decline from 32.5 MT offered in March.

The dip reflects softer demand signals rather than supply constraints. Power plants entered April with improved coal stockpiles of roughly 54 MT as of late March 2026.

Of the total April auction volume, Mahanadi Coalfields Ltd (MCL) contributed 9.4 MT. South Eastern Coalfields Ltd (SECL) offered 5.6 MT, followed by Central Coalfields Ltd (CCL) at 4.6 MT.

CIL allocated approximately 11.8 MT of the offered volume, reflecting a 39% allocation rate. The average premium over notified prices held firm at roughly 51%.

CIL's FY26 full-year production dropped 1.7% to 768.1 MT from 781.1 MT in FY25. The company missed its FY26 production target of 875 MT.

Early FY27 data shows cumulative April output declining approximately 6% year-on-year to 229.8 MT. This pre-monsoon softness warrants monitoring through the post-monsoon recovery window.

Effective January 1, 2026, CIL opened SWMA auctions directly to buyers from Bangladesh, Bhutan, and Nepal. The move aims to boost transparency and utilise surplus coal.

CIL shares traded at ₹456.40 on May 11, 2026, down 2.2%. The Maharatna PSU holds a market capitalisation of approximately ₹282,000 crore with a 5.81% dividend yield.
