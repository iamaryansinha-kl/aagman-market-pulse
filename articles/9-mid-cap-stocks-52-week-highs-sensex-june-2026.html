9 Mid-Cap Stocks Hit 52-Week Highs as Sensex Drops 372 Points

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nine BSE Midcap stocks touched fresh 52-week highs on June 29, 2026, even as the Sensex fell 372 points to 76,728
  • 02/Bank of Maharashtra led the pack with an 18% monthly rally to ₹94.5, followed by Thermax at 15% and Bharat Forge at 13%
  • 03/Pharma names dominated the list with four stocks, signaling resilient investor appetite for defensive sectors amid broader weakness

Nine mid-cap stocks hit fresh 52-week highs on Monday, June 29, 2026. The benchmark Sensex fell 372 points to settle at 76,728 during the session.

Bank of Maharashtra Ltd (NSE: MAHABANK) topped the list. The stock touched a new 52-week high of ₹94.5 and gained about 18% in the past month. Its current market price stands at ₹93.82.

Thermax Ltd (NSE: THERMAX) hit a 52-week high of ₹5,277, rallying 15% over the past month. The stock's CMP is ₹5,106.45.

Bharat Forge Ltd (NSE: BHARATFORG) reached ₹2,236.95, its highest level in a year. The auto components maker gained 13% in the past month with a CMP of ₹2,172.25.

Oracle Financial Services Software Ltd (NSE: OFSS) touched ₹11,055, gaining 12% in one month. The IT stock trades at ₹10,855.8.

Laurus Labs Ltd (NSE: LAURUSLABS) hit ₹1,530.3, up 11% monthly. The pharma stock's CMP is ₹1,502.9.

Pharma names dominated the list with four entries. Ajanta Pharma Ltd (NSE: AJANTPHARM) hit ₹3,476.7, while Aurobindo Pharma Ltd (NSE: AUROPHARMA) reached ₹1,585.55. Both gained around 8-10% in the past month.

GMR Airports Infrastructure Ltd hit ₹111.6, up 10% monthly. Radico Khaitan Ltd (NSE: RADICO) touched ₹3,910.5, also gaining 10% in the past month.

The breadth of sectors represented — banking, capital goods, IT, pharma, aviation, and FMCG — suggests selective buying interest. Investors are chasing momentum in specific mid-cap names despite broader market weakness.

Hitting a 52-week high is considered a bullish technical signal. It reflects strong investor confidence and potential for further upside. However, traders should watch for profit-taking after such sharp monthly rallies.
