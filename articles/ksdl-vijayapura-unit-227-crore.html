Mysore Sandal Soap Maker KSDL to Build ₹227 Crore Vijayapura Unit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Karnataka cabinet approves ₹227.91 crore KSDL manufacturing unit in Vijayapura on 50 acres to boost North Karnataka capacity and exports
  • 02/KSDL posted record ₹2,016 crore turnover and ₹507 crore net profit in FY2025-26, its highest in 110 years of operation
  • 03/New unit breaks ground in Ittangihal Industrial Area; company targets ₹3,000 crore turnover by 2028 and ₹5,000 crore by 2030

Karnataka Soaps and Detergents Limited (KSDL) will build a new manufacturing unit in Vijayapura. The Karnataka state cabinet approved the ₹227.91 crore project on May 1, 2026.

The unit will come up on 50 acres in the Ittangihal Industrial Area. Land acquisition is budgeted at ₹30 crore at ₹59.30 lakh per acre.

The project includes ₹77.88 crore for construction, ₹70.80 crore for machinery, and ₹24 crore for infrastructure. Minister M. B. Patil announced the cabinet clearance.

KSDL posted a record turnover of ₹2,016 crore in FY2025-26, its highest in 110 years. Net profit stood at ₹507 crore with production at 47,494 tonnes.

The company took 105 years to hit ₹1,000 crore turnover. It added the next ₹1,000 crore in just four years.

Product range has expanded from 34 to 94 items in three years. Modern retail channels generated ₹420 crore and e-commerce added ₹120 crore this fiscal.

KSDL targets ₹3,000 crore turnover by 2028 and ₹5,000 crore by 2030. The Vijayapura unit will also create jobs and expand reach into Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE KSDL VIJAYAPURA PROJECT APPROVED BY THE KARNATAKA CABINET?
THE KARNATAKA STATE CABINET HAS APPROVED A ₹227.91 CRORE PROJECT TO ESTABLISH A NEW KSDL MANUFACTURING UNIT IN THE ITTANGIHAL INDUSTRIAL AREA OF VIJAYAPURA. THE UNIT WILL SPAN 50 ACRES AND PRODUCE THE COMPANY'S RANGE OF 94 PRODUCTS INCLUDING THE ICONIC MYSURU SANDAL SOAP.
WHAT IS THE PROJECT COST BREAKDOWN FOR THE KSDL VIJAYAPURA UNIT?
OF THE TOTAL ₹227.91 CRORE, ₹30 CRORE IS FOR LAND ACQUISITION, ₹77.88 CRORE FOR CONSTRUCTION, ₹70.80 CRORE FOR MACHINERY, ₹24 CRORE FOR INFRASTRUCTURE, ₹8.63 CRORE FOR TENDER PREMIUM, ₹8.63 CRORE FOR CONTINGENCIES, AND ₹7.97 CRORE FOR OTHER EXPENSES. LAND IS PRICED AT ₹59.30 LAKH PER ACRE.
WHAT ARE KSDL'S LATEST FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FY2025-26?
KSDL ACHIEVED A RECORD TURNOVER OF ₹2,016 CRORE FOR THE FIRST TIME IN ITS 110-YEAR HISTORY. NET PROFIT STOOD AT ₹507 CRORE AND TOTAL PRODUCTION REACHED 47,494 TONNES DURING THE FINANCIAL YEAR.
HOW HAS KSDL'S PRODUCT RANGE AND DISTRIBUTION EXPANDED?
KSDL EXPANDED ITS PRODUCT PORTFOLIO FROM 34 TO 94 ITEMS IN THREE YEARS WITHOUT ADDING NEW MACHINERY OR WORKFORCE. IN FY2025-26, IT GENERATED ₹420 CRORE FROM MODERN RETAIL CHANNELS AND ₹120 CRORE THROUGH E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS.
WHAT ARE KSDL'S FUTURE GROWTH TARGETS?
KSDL HAS SET A TARGET OF ₹3,000 CRORE TURNOVER BY 2028 AND ₹5,000 CRORE BY 2030. THE COMPANY IS ALSO EXPANDING INTO EXPORT MARKETS INCLUDING AUSTRALIA AND THE UAE, AND WILL PAY ₹157 CRORE AS DIVIDEND TO THE STATE GOVERNMENT FROM ITS FY26 PROFITS.
