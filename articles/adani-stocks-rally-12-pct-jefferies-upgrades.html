Adani Group Stocks Rally Up to 12% as Jefferies Backs Energy, Power and Ports Growth Plans

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Adani group stocks rallied sharply on Friday, led by a more than 12% surge in Adani Total Gas, after Jefferies published positive views across the group's energy, power and ports businesses.

Adani Energy Solutions, Adani Ports & SEZ, Adani Green Energy, Adani Power and Adani Enterprises gained in the range of 1-3%. The moves came as the brokerage retained upbeat stances in its latest company reports.

The highest implied upside is on Adani Energy Solutions, where Jefferies has a target price of Rs 2,060, implying 53% upside from the previous close of Rs 1,350. The brokerage said management remains upbeat on the trading business and India's transmission outlook, and reiterated a medium-term annual capex run-rate guidance of Rs 200-250 billion. Smart meters and the trading business are ramping up well, Jefferies said, with a 2.5 GW round-the-clock power supply tie-up with MSEDCL expected to lift trading volumes to 57% of capacity in FY28E from 20% at the end of Q1FY27. It expects revenue and EBITDA CAGRs of 22% and 29% respectively over FY26-30E.

On Adani Green Energy, Jefferies has a target of Rs 1,695, implying 34% upside from Rs 1,269.6. Management is confident of adding 5 GW of capacity in FY27E, with battery energy storage capacity on track to rise from 3.6 GWh to more than 10 GWh. The brokerage expects capacity of 44 GW by FY30E against the company's 50 GW target and EBITDA growth at a 30% CAGR.

Adani Power gained on a maintained Buy rating with a target of Rs 270, implying 33% upside from Rs 202.35. Management reiterated a plan to expand capacity 2.5 times to 45 GW by FY32; of the upcoming 23.7 GW, 56% is already locked under long-term power purchase agreements. Jefferies expects 22% EBITDA CAGR over FY26-30E, free cash flow turning positive by FY30E, and net debt-to-EBITDA peaking at 3.6 times in FY28E before declining to 2.3 times by FY30E, among the lowest leverage among generation companies.

Adani Ports & SEZ rose on an unchanged target of Rs 2,160, implying 25% upside from Rs 1,725.95. The brokerage said the company remains confident of its 1 billion tonne cargo target by 2030, expects 13% volume CAGR, and sees the company potentially turning net cash positive by FY31E. FY27 year-to-date volume growth of 16% is running ahead of its estimate, with container cargo and the Vizhinjam ramp-up as key drivers.

The rally comes as Indian equities extended their recovery, with broader markets sharply outperforming benchmarks and risk appetite improving on moderating crude prices and global yields.
