India Soju Market Booms: 3 Brands Launch as Gen Z Embraces K-Culture

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Three soju brands — Jinro, Seoulmate, and So Good — launched in India between December 2025 and April 2026, targeting Gen Z consumers
  • 02/India's soju market was valued at $251.9 million in 2024 and is projected to reach $367.4 million by 2030, growing at 8.8% CAGR
  • 03/Brands are rolling out across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Goa, and Hyderabad; flavoured variants are driving first-time trial among younger drinkers

India's soju market is accelerating as Korean cultural influence meets evolving drinking habits. Three brands have launched since December 2025, signalling serious category momentum.

The market was valued at $251.9 million in 2024. It is projected to hit $367.4 million by 2030, growing at an 8.8% CAGR that outpaces the global average of 5.4%.

Monika Alcobev partnered with South Korea's Jinro, the world's top-selling soju label with 96.8 million cases sold in 2024. The brand launched in India in December 2025 and is now available across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Goa, Haryana, Chandigarh, and Uttar Pradesh.

Nuvola Spirits launched Seoulmate Soju in Karnataka in April 2026. The company has since expanded to Goa and Delhi, with Haryana, Maharashtra, and Assam lined up next. Bengaluru is its priority market given the city's young, globally exposed consumer base.

Penguin Overseas introduced So Good Soju in India in April 2026. The brand is manufactured locally and currently retails in Maharashtra and Haryana, with Karnataka, Goa, Delhi, Arunachal Pradesh, and Mizoram planned.

"Soju became aspirational through culture before it was commercially available at scale," said Raghav Sachdeva, Co-founder and CEO of Nuvola Spirits. He noted that Indian consumers prefer lower ABV and higher-flavour-profile drinks, creating a natural opening for soju.

Jinro's India portfolio includes Chamisul Fresh alongside Green Grape, Plum, Strawberry, and Peach variants. Kunal Patel, MD of Monika Alcobev, said flavoured soju has played a key role in attracting new consumers globally, and India is following the same pattern.

Soju typically carries an ABV of 16% to 25%, lower than whiskey or vodka. Jaspreet Singh, Founder & CEO of Penguin Overseas, said it pairs naturally with pan-Asian cuisine, which has gained significant traction in India over the past few years.

India remains a whisky-first market, and soju is still at an early stage compared to Southeast Asia. Sachdeva noted that scaling requires a state-specific approach due to India's complex regulatory and distribution landscape.

WHAT IS THE CURRENT SIZE OF INDIA'S SOJU MARKET?
THE INDIA SOJU MARKET WAS VALUED AT USD 251.9 MILLION IN 2024 AND IS PROJECTED TO REACH USD 367.4 MILLION BY 2030, ACCORDING TO INDUSTRY ESTIMATES.
WHICH SOJU BRANDS HAVE LAUNCHED IN INDIA RECENTLY?
THREE BRANDS HAVE ENTERED THE MARKET: JINRO (LAUNCHED DECEMBER 2025 BY MONIKA ALCOBEV), SEOULMATE SOJU (LAUNCHED APRIL 2026 BY NUVOLA SPIRITS), AND SO GOOD SOJU (LAUNCHED APRIL 2026 BY PENGUIN OVERSEAS).
WHAT IS DRIVING SOJU'S POPULARITY AMONG INDIAN CONSUMERS?
THE SURGE IN K-DRAMAS, K-POP, AND KOREAN CULTURE ON STREAMING PLATFORMS HAS FAMILIARISED GEN Z AND YOUNG MILLENNIALS WITH SOJU. INDIAN CONSUMERS ARE ALSO SHIFTING TOWARD LOWER-ABV, FLAVOUR-FORWARD, AND EXPERIENCE-DRIVEN DRINKING FORMATS.
WHERE ARE THESE SOJU BRANDS CURRENTLY AVAILABLE IN INDIA?
JINRO IS AVAILABLE IN DELHI, MUMBAI, BENGALURU, HYDERABAD, GOA, HARYANA, CHANDIGARH, AND UTTAR PRADESH. SEOULMATE IS IN KARNATAKA, GOA, AND DELHI. SO GOOD IS IN MAHARASHTRA AND HARYANA, WITH EXPANSION PLANNED TO KARNATAKA, GOA, DELHI, ARUNACHAL PRADESH, AND MIZORAM.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS FOR SOJU BRANDS SCALING IN INDIA?
INDIA IS A WHISKY-DOMINANT MARKET WITH COMPLEX STATE-SPECIFIC ALCOHOL REGULATIONS AND DISTRIBUTION BARRIERS. RETAIL WINE STORES AND HORECA CHANNELS ARE CRITICAL FOR SCALING, BUT ON-TRADE PENETRATION REMAINS BEHIND MARKETS LIKE SINGAPORE AND THAILAND.
