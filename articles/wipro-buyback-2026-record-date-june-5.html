Wipro Buyback 2026: Rs 15,000 Crore at Rs 250/Share, Record Date June 5

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Wipro board approves Rs 15,000 crore buyback at Rs 250 per share, representing 5.7% of total paid-up equity
  • 02/Record date fixed at June 5, 2026; promoters intend to participate; first buyback in nearly three years
  • 03/Retail investors could see 11-13% pre-tax returns based on expected 50-60% acceptance ratio

Wipro Ltd (NSE: WIPRO) has announced a share buyback worth up to Rs 15,000 crore. The board approved the programme on April 16, 2026.

The company will buy back up to 60 crore equity shares at Rs 250 each. This represents approximately 5.7% of the total paid-up share capital.

The buyback price of Rs 250 implies a premium of around 23% over the recent market price. The stock closed at Rs 202.97 on the NSE on May 22, 2026.

The record date to determine eligible shareholders is Friday, June 5, 2026. Investors must hold shares in their demat accounts by this date to participate.

This is Wipro's first share buyback in nearly three years. The last buyback was in June 2023 for Rs 12,000 crore at Rs 445 per share.

Promoters and promoter group members have indicated their intention to participate in the tender offer. The buyback will be conducted through the tender route as per SEBI regulations.

Market regulator SEBI mandates that 15% of the total offer size be reserved for small shareholders. This means approximately 9 crore shares worth Rs 2,250 crore are reserved for retail investors holding shares worth up to Rs 2 lakh.

"Retail investors looking for short-term opportunities can buy Wipro shares. We expect the acceptance ratio to remain high in the range of 50-60%, which could give a potential return of 11-13% pre-tax within 2-3 months," Motilal Oswal Wealth Management said in a note.

Brokerage SAMCO Securities estimates more modest returns of 5-7% at a 30-40% acceptance ratio. At 50-60% acceptance, SAMCO sees returns of 9-10%.

"We view Wipro's buyback as a tactical opportunity rather than a guaranteed arbitrage. The risk-reward appears balanced, with limited downside and attractive upside in favourable participation scenarios," SAMCO said.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE BUYBACK PRICE AND TOTAL SIZE OF WIPRO'S SHARE BUYBACK?
WIPRO HAS ANNOUNCED A BUYBACK PRICE OF RS 250 PER EQUITY SHARE WITH A FACE VALUE OF RS 2. THE TOTAL BUYBACK SIZE IS RS 15,000 CRORE FOR UP TO 60 CRORE SHARES, REPRESENTING 5.7% OF THE TOTAL PAID-UP EQUITY SHARE CAPITAL.
WHAT IS THE RECORD DATE FOR THE WIPRO SHARE BUYBACK?
THE RECORD DATE TO DETERMINE THE ELIGIBILITY OF SHAREHOLDERS FOR THE BUYBACK IS FRIDAY, JUNE 5, 2026. INVESTORS MUST HOLD SHARES IN THEIR DEMAT ACCOUNTS AS OF THIS DATE TO BE ELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE.
WHAT PREMIUM DOES THE BUYBACK PRICE OFFER OVER THE MARKET PRICE?
THE BUYBACK PRICE OF RS 250 REPRESENTS A PREMIUM OF APPROXIMATELY 23% OVER WIPRO'S NSE CLOSING PRICE OF RS 202.97 ON MAY 22, 2026. THE PREMIUM WAS AROUND 19% WHEN THE BUYBACK WAS FIRST ANNOUNCED AT AN APRIL 16 CLOSING PRICE OF RS 210.
WHAT RETURNS CAN RETAIL INVESTORS EXPECT FROM THE BUYBACK?
ACCORDING TO MOTILAL OSWAL WEALTH MANAGEMENT, RETAIL INVESTORS COULD SEE ACCEPTANCE RATIOS OF 50-60%, GENERATING PRE-TAX RETURNS OF 11-13% OVER 2-3 MONTHS. SAMCO SECURITIES ESTIMATES 9-10% RETURNS AT SIMILAR ACCEPTANCE LEVELS. RETURNS DEPEND HEAVILY ON THE FINAL ACCEPTANCE RATIO.
HOW CAN SHAREHOLDERS PARTICIPATE IN THE WIPRO BUYBACK?
ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS HOLDING SHARES IN DEMAT FORM MUST INFORM THEIR BROKER OF THE QUANTITY THEY WISH TO TENDER. THE BROKER WILL TRANSFER THE SHARES TO A SPECIAL ACCOUNT OF THE CLEARING CORPORATION AND PLACE AN ORDER ON THE STOCK EXCHANGE. FOR PHYSICAL SHARES, SHAREHOLDERS MUST APPROACH THEIR BROKER WITH ORIGINAL SHARE CERTIFICATES AND SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS.
