Sebi Show Cause Notice to 6 Capital Group FPIs Over Front-Running Leak

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sebi issues show cause notice to six Capital Group FPIs for alleged trade confidentiality lapses enabling front-running network
  • 02/Two traders James Cheng and Terence Tsai handled 90% of India trading and shared order details with Rohit Salgaocar
  • 03/Investigation covers January 2021 to June 2023; Sebi used Bloomberg chat logs and WhatsApp communications as evidence

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) has issued a show cause notice to six foreign portfolio investors run by Capital Group. The US-based asset manager manages over $3.3 trillion in assets globally.

Sebi alleges the FPIs failed to maintain trade confidentiality. Two traders reportedly shared sensitive order details with Singapore-based Rohit Salgaocar.

Salgaocar allegedly passed the information to market operator Ketan Parekh. This enabled a front-running network to profit from illicit gains.

The two Capital Group traders are James Vincent Cheng and Terence Tsai. They handled about 90% of the firm's India-related trading activity during the investigation period.

Sebi's probe covers January 1, 2021 to June 20, 2023. The regulator analysed Bloomberg chat logs and WhatsApp communications to build its case.

The notice names six funds including Smallcap World Fund, American Funds Fundamental Investors, The Growth Fund of America, and AMCAP Fund. The 17-page notice is dated February 2.

Sebi had already banned Salgaocar in January 2025 over the front-running matter. Parekh was allegedly involved in the 2001 stock market manipulation.

Capital Group declined to comment on the notice. Sebi didn't respond to queries either. Legal proceedings have been initiated against all six FPI entities.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH SIX CAPITAL GROUP FPIs RECEIVED THE SEBI SHOW CAUSE NOTICE?
THE SIX FPIs ARE: SMALLCAP WORLD FUND, AMERICAN FUNDS INSURANCE SERIES GROWTH-INCOME FUND, AMERICAN FUNDS FUNDAMENTAL INVESTORS, THE GROWTH FUND OF AMERICA, AMCAP FUND, AND CAPITAL GROUP AMCAP FUND (LUX).
WHAT IS THE MAIN ALLEGATION AGAINST CAPITAL GROUP IN THE SEBI NOTICE?
SEBI ALLEGES THAT CAPITAL GROUP FAILED TO MAINTAIN ABSOLUTE CONFIDENTIALITY REGARDING TRADING INTENTIONS AND PARAMETERS, ALLOWING INTERNAL TRADE SECRETS TO BE LEAKED TO UNAUTHORISED THIRD PARTIES.
WHO ARE THE TWO TRADERS NAMED IN THE SEBI INVESTIGATION?
THE TWO TRADERS ARE JAMES VINCENT CHENG AND TERENCE TSAI, WHO HANDLED ABOUT 90% OF CAPITAL GROUP'S INDIA-RELATED TRADING ACTIVITY.
WHAT IS THE TIME PERIOD COVERED BY SEBI'S INVESTIGATION?
SEBI'S INVESTIGATION COVERS THE PERIOD FROM JANUARY 1, 2021 TO JUNE 20, 2023.
WHO ARE ROHIT SALGAOCAR AND KETAN PAREKH IN THIS CASE?
ROHIT SALGAOCAR IS A SINGAPORE-BASED TRADER AND DIRECTOR OF STRAIT CROSSING PTE LTD WHO RECEIVED THE LEAKED INFORMATION. KETAN PAREKH IS AN ALLEGED MARKET OPERATOR WHO USED THE INFORMATION TO FRONT-RUN CAPITAL GROUP'S ORDERS. SEBI BANNED SALGAOCAR IN JANUARY 2025.
