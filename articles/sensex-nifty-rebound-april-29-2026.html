Sensex Jumps 609 Points to 77,496; Nifty Rebounds 0.76% on April 29

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Sensex surged 609.45 points (0.79%) to 77,496.36 and Nifty climbed 181.95 points (0.76%) to 24,177.65 on April 29, 2026
  • 02/ITC, Tech Mahindra and Maruti Suzuki led Nifty gainers; InterGlobe Aviation and Dr Reddy's were among top losers
  • 03/Technical resistance seen at 24,400-24,500 with support at 24,000; geopolitical tensions and $114 Brent crude remain key risks

Indian equity benchmarks rebounded nearly 1% on April 29, 2026. Bargain hunting in FMCG, auto and telecom stocks drove the recovery.

The 30-share BSE Sensex jumped 609.45 points, or 0.79%, to settle at 77,496.36. During the session, it surged 1,095.6 points to an intraday high of 77,982.51.

The 50-share NSE Nifty rose 181.95 points, or 0.76%, to close at 24,177.65. It hit an intraday peak of 24,334.70 before late-session profit-booking trimmed gains.

Signs of potential de-escalation in geopolitical tensions aided sentiment. Traders also drew comfort from upbeat earnings and a rally across Asian markets.

ITC led Nifty gainers with a 3.88% surge. Tech Mahindra rose 3.68%, Maruti Suzuki climbed 2.84%, Coal India added 2.77%, and Reliance Industries gained 2.63%.

Maruti Suzuki advanced after reporting a record FY26 consolidated net profit of ₹14,679.5 crore. The carmaker sold over 24.22 lakh units, its highest annual tally.

"The core driver of today's strength remained earnings," said Hariprasad K, Research Analyst at Livelong Wealth. "Strong results reinforced confidence in domestic demand."

Vinod Nair of Geojit Investments noted financials lagged due to regulatory tightening. PSU banks, media and consumer durables indices fell between 0.5% and 1%.

The Nifty Smallcap 100 gained 0.6%, while the Nifty Midcap 100 ended marginally lower. The advance-decline ratio on the NSE was nearly balanced at 1:1.

Brent crude jumped 2.85% to $114.4 per barrel. The rupee closed near a record low of 94.82 against the US dollar, adding macro pressure.

Nagaraj Shetti of HDFC Securities sees Nifty resistance at 24,400-24,500. Immediate support is placed at the 24,000 level on the daily charts.
