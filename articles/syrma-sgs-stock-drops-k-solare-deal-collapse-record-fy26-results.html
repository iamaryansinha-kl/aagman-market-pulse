Syrma SGS Stock Drops 2.6% as K-Solare Deal Collapse Offsets Record FY26 Results

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Syrma SGS shares drop 2.57% to ₹1,084 on May 12 after the company scraps its planned 49% acquisition of K-Solare Energy
  • 02/FY26 revenue hits record ₹4,856.9 crore up 27% YoY, PAT surges 87% to ₹345.8 crore, EBITDA margin expands to 12% from 9.7%
  • 03/Stock opened at ₹1,155 and touched a 52-week high of ₹1,188 intraday before sell orders dominated 70:30; heavy profit-booking likely after 100% annual gain

Syrma SGS Technology Limited shares fell 2.57% to ₹1,084 on the NSE as of 10:15 am on May 12, 2026. The stock opened sharply higher at ₹1,155 before reversing course.

The intraday sell-off came a day after the electronics manufacturer posted its strongest annual results to date. The stock had touched a fresh 52-week high of ₹1,188 in early trade before sellers took over.

Sell orders outnumbered buy orders roughly 70:30. There were 3,85,274 units on the sell side against 1,62,527 on the buy side. Traded volume crossed 63.49 lakh shares with value at ₹722.90 crore.

A key drag is the scrapping of the proposed acquisition of K-Solare Energy Private Limited. Syrma SGS and Premier Energies Limited had announced plans to jointly acquire a 49% equity stake in October 2025. On May 11, 2026, Syrma SGS informed exchanges the deal was called off as conditions precedent were not fulfilled.

For FY26, Syrma SGS reported total revenue of ₹4,856.9 crore. This is up 27% year-on-year. PAT surged 87% to ₹345.8 crore. EBITDA margins expanded to 12% from 9.7% in FY25.

The company also turned net cash positive. It ended FY26 with net cash of ₹467.2 crore against net debt of ₹263.9 crore a year earlier.

For Q4 FY26, revenue grew 56% year-on-year to ₹1,476.8 crore. PAT was up 67%. The company posted a consolidated profit of around ₹119 crore against a loss of ₹71.45 crore in Q4 FY25.

The stock has gained over 100% in the past year and 50.68% year-to-date. Its P/E stands at 62. This leaves it vulnerable to profit-booking on negative news flow despite strong operational delivery.
