Hyundai Motor Union Demands 30% Net Profit Bonus, $102 Monthly Hike

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hyundai Motor labour union demands 30% of the company's 10.36 trillion won ($7.06 billion) 2025 net profit as performance bonuses, totaling over 3 trillion won ($2 billion)
  • 02/Union also seeks monthly base salary hike of 149,600 won ($101.90) and standard bonus increase from 750% to 800% of base pay
  • 03/Hyundai's union is following a broader pattern across South Korean conglomerates, with Samsung Electronics union demanding 15% of operating profit and SK hynix allocating 10%

Hyundai Motor Company's labour union has demanded performance bonuses equivalent to 30% of the automaker's 2025 net profit. The payout would exceed 3 trillion won ($2 billion) after Hyundai posted record net profit of 10.36 trillion won ($7.06 billion) last year.

The union's proposal, finalized last week, also includes a monthly base salary increase of 149,600 won ($101.90). It seeks to raise standard bonuses from 750% to 800% of base pay.

Union members want the profit-based bonuses distributed equally to employees at Hyundai Motor's partner firms. This broadens the scope beyond the roughly 42,000 unionized workers at the company.

The demand is not new. Hyundai's union has pushed for 30% of net profit as bonuses in prior negotiations, including during the 2021 wage talks that ended in a breakdown and strike preparations. Management had then offered a far smaller package.

The timing matters. Hyundai Motor saw record-high revenue in 2024, driven largely by U.S. market growth. Strong earnings give the union leverage it did not have during weaker years.

This is part of a broader spring labour offensive across South Korea's largest conglomerates. Samsung Electronics' union is demanding performance bonuses funded by 15% of operating profit with no upper cap.

SK hynix, which reached a labour agreement in September 2025, allocated 10% of operating profit to bonuses. With operating profit forecast at 200 trillion won, its bonus pool could hit 20 trillion won.

Hyundai Motor trades on the Korea Exchange under ticker 005380.KS. The union and management have a history of tense negotiations, with partial strikes in September 2025 over similar wage and retirement age demands.
