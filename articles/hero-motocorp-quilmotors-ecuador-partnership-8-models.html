Hero MotoCorp Partners Quilmotors for Ecuador Entry: 8 Models Launched

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hero MotoCorp partners Quilmotors as exclusive distributor for Ecuador, launching eight two-wheeler models in the South American market
  • 02/The tie-up covers assembly operations, network development, sales, after-sales service and spare parts distribution across Ecuador
  • 03/Hero MotoCorp will open its first flagship outlet in Guayaquil; this expands its Latin America footprint beyond Colombia and Argentina

Hero MotoCorp Ltd (NSE: HEROMOTOCO) on Thursday announced a partnership with Quilmotors as its exclusive distributor for Ecuador. The agreement covers assembly operations, network development, sales, after-sales service and spare parts distribution in the South American nation.

The partnership will introduce eight models in Ecuador through Quilmotors. The lineup includes motorcycles Eco Deluxe and Eco 150, Naked Sports Range Hunk 125R and Hunk 160R 4V, Dual Purpose motorcycles Xpulse 200 4V and Xpulse 200 4V Pro, and Urban Mobility Scooters Xoom 110 and Xoom 125R.

"Ecuador presents a strong opportunity for us, driven by evolving customer aspirations and increasing demand for reliable, high-quality mobility solutions," Sanjay Bhan, Executive Vice-President of Global Business at Hero MotoCorp, said.

Bhan added that the partnership combines Quilmotors' deep local market expertise with Hero's global capabilities. The company will inaugurate its first exclusive flagship outlet in Guayaquil on Avenida de las Americas, a key automotive hub in the city.

"Hero MotoCorp is a globally respected brand and we are confident that its technology, quality and performance will strongly resonate with customers in Ecuador," Quilmotors Representative Felipe Estrada said.

The Ecuador entry expands Hero MotoCorp's footprint in Latin America, where it already operates in Colombia, Argentina, Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Guatemala, El Salvador and Mexico. The company has sold over 100 million two-wheelers globally and currently operates in more than 40 countries.

Hero MotoCorp shares closed at ₹5,114.50 on April 29, 2026. The stock has gained approximately 20% from its 52-week low of ₹4,000, though it remains below the 52-week high of ₹6,390.
