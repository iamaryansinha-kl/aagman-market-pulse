DMart Q2 FY27: Revenue Rises 18.4% to Rs 19,206 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Avenue Supermarts, which owns and operates the D-Mart retail chain, reported standalone revenue from operations of Rs 19,206.18 crore for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026.
  • 02/The figure marks an 18.4% rise over the Rs 16,218.79 crore reported a year earlier, and a 4.7% sequential increase from Rs 18,343.49 crore in the June 2026 quarter.
  • 03/D-Mart's store count stood at 518 as of September 30, 2026, including the Sanpada store in Navi Mumbai that remains closed to customers due to reconstruction.

Avenue Supermarts Ltd (NSE: DMART), which owns and operates the D-Mart supermarket chain, has released its revenue update for the second quarter of financial year 2026-27. The company made the disclosure in a regulatory filing on Saturday, October 3. The update covers standalone revenue from operations for the July-September period.

Standalone revenue from operations stood at Rs 19,206.18 crore for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. A year earlier, the comparable figure was Rs 16,218.79 crore. That translates into year-on-year growth of 18.4% for the September quarter.

The growth was visible on a sequential basis as well. Revenue climbed 4.7% from the Rs 18,343.49 crore that the company had recorded in the June quarter of financial year 2026-27, its first quarter of the fiscal.

In its statement, the company said: "Standalone Revenue from operations for the quarter ended (QE) September 30, 2026, stood at Rs 19,206.18 crore." The disclosure is limited to standalone revenue and the store count. No other financial parameters were part of the filing.

As of September 30, 2026, the total number of D-Mart stores stood at 518. This count includes the Sanpada store in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, which is currently closed to customers due to reconstruction work at the location.

D-Mart is promoted by Radhakishan Damani and his family. The chain retails basic home and personal products, and its store network spans markets including Maharashtra, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, NCR, Tamil Nadu, Punjab and Rajasthan.

The Saturday filing gives the first official snapshot of the company's top line for the September quarter. It arrives ahead of the full quarterly results, which will carry the complete set of financials for the period.

The store network is the other number to track alongside revenue. One of the 518 stores is temporarily shut for rebuilding, and its reopening after reconstruction will restore selling space in the Navi Mumbai market.
