India Turns Net Exporter of Finished Steel in FY26: Exports Jump 36%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India turned net exporter of finished steel in FY26, shipping 6.6 million metric tons of finished steel, up 35.9% from a year ago
  • 02/Imports dropped 31.7% to 6.5 million tons; Italy, Vietnam, Belgium, UAE and Spain were the biggest buyers of Indian steel
  • 03/Crude steel production hit 169.2 million tons in FY26, up 11.2%; consumption rose 8% to 164.2 million tons

India became a net exporter of finished steel in the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026. Provisional government data reviewed by Reuters showed exports of 6.6 million metric tons.

This marks a 35.9% jump from the previous fiscal year. Imports during the same period fell 31.7% to 6.5 million tons.

Italy, Vietnam, Belgium, the United Arab Emirates, and Spain were the biggest buyers. South Korea, China, Japan, Vietnam, and Russia supplied most of India's imports.

Crude steel production reached 169.2 million tons in FY26. This represents an 11.2% increase over the prior year.

Finished steel consumption stood at 164.2 million tons. It rose 8% from a year earlier, indicating strong domestic demand alongside export growth.

The data positions India as a rising force in global steel markets. Lower imports and higher exports reflect improving domestic production capacity.
