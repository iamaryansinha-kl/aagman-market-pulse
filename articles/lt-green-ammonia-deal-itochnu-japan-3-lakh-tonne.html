L&T Bags 3 Lakh Tonne Green Ammonia Export Deal With Japan's ITOCHU

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/L&T signs MoU with Japanese trading giant ITOCHU for 3 lakh tonne per annum green ammonia export
  • 02/The deal supports Japan's national hydrogen strategy and India's renewable export push
  • 03/L&T shares rose 2% on the news, reflecting investor optimism around green energy pivot

Larsen & Toubro Ltd has signed a memorandum of understanding with ITOCHU Corporation. The Japanese trading house will procure 3 lakh tonnes of green ammonia annually from L&T's planned production facility.

The deal is one of the largest green ammonia export agreements announced by an Indian engineering firm. It aligns with Japan's national hydrogen roadmap that targets 3 million tonnes of annual ammonia imports by 2030.

Green ammonia is produced using renewable energy to electrolyse water and combine hydrogen with nitrogen. It is considered a carbon-free fuel for power generation and shipping.

L&T is developing a green hydrogen and ammonia complex at an undisclosed location. The company has not revealed the project cost or commissioning timeline yet.

The partnership gives L&T a credible offtake anchor for its clean fuel venture. ITOCHU brings decades of experience in commodity trading and Japanese utility relationships.

India aims to become a green hydrogen exporter under its National Hydrogen Mission. The government has approved $2.4 billion in incentives for electrolyser manufacturing and green hydrogen production.

L&T shares closed at ₹3,142.50, up 2.1% on April 22. The stock has gained 18% over the past year, outperforming the Nifty 50 index.
