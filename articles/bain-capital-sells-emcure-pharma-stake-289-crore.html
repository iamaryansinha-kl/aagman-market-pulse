Bain Capital Sells 0.95% Emcure Pharma Stake for ₹289 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bain Capital offloads 18 lakh shares (0.95% stake) in Emcure Pharma for ₹289.47 crore at ₹1,608.20 per share via block deal
  • 02/Norway's Government Pension Fund Global buys the entire block; BC Investments IV holding falls to 2.93% from 3.87%
  • 03/Emcure shares fell 2.31% to ₹1,690 on April 30; stock trades near 52-week high of ₹1,770 after 82% rally from May 2025 lows

Bain Capital has sold a 0.95% stake in Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd for ₹289.47 crore. The US-based private equity firm offloaded 18 lakh shares through open market block deals on April 29, 2026.

The shares were sold at an average price of ₹1,608.20 apiece. BC Investments IV Ltd, a Bain Capital affiliate, executed the transaction on the NSE.

The buyer was Norway's Government Pension Fund Global, the world's largest sovereign wealth fund. It acquired the entire 18 lakh share block at the same price.

Post-sale, BC Investments IV's holding in the Pune-based drug maker dropped to 2.93%. It previously held 3.87% as of March 2026.

This is Bain Capital's second stake reduction in Emcure within a year. In July 2025, it sold a 2.4% stake for ₹563 crore at ₹1,225 per share.

Emcure shares fell 2.31% to ₹1,690 on April 30. The stock had hit a 52-week high of ₹1,770.05 just a day earlier.

The stock is up over 82% from its May 2025 low of ₹950. It has sharply outperformed broader pharma indices in the past year.

Emcure reported a 48% YoY jump in Q3 FY2026 PAT to ₹231 crore. Revenue rose to ₹2,363 crore from ₹1,963 crore in the year-ago period.
