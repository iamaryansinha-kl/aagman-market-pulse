Q4 Results 28 Apr: AWL Agri, Eternal Profit Up; Maruti, REC Down

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/AWL Agri Business Ltd (NSE: AWL) Q4 standalone net profit jumps 70.7% to ₹268.01 crore, while Eternal Ltd (NSE: ETERNAL) Q4 PAT rises 22% to ₹705 crore.
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) Q4 standalone profit declines 7% to ₹3,590.5 crore and REC Ltd (NSE: RECLTD) consolidated net profit falls 22% to ₹3,375.08 crore.
  • 03/Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd, City Union Bank and UltraTech Cement Ltd post double-digit profit growth; brokerages flag near-term margin headwinds in cement and autos.

On April 28, 2026, several major companies announced Q4 FY26 earnings. AWL Agri Business Ltd (NSE: AWL) and Eternal Ltd (NSE: ETERNAL) posted strong profit growth, while Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) and REC Ltd (NSE: RECLTD) saw declines.

AWL Agri Business Ltd standalone net profit surged 70.7% to ₹268.01 crore. Revenue hit a record ₹21,465 crore for the quarter. The board declared a final dividend of ₹1 per share.

Eternal Ltd reported a 22% rise in standalone net profit to ₹705 crore. FY26 PAT jumped to ₹2,655 crore from ₹1,960 crore a year ago.

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd standalone Q4 profit fell 7% to ₹3,590.5 crore due to mark-to-market impact. However, the company recorded its highest-ever quarterly sales of 676,209 units.

REC Ltd consolidated net profit dropped 22% to ₹3,375.08 crore on lower interest income. Dalmia Bharat Ltd standalone profit slipped to ₹73 crore from ₹77 crore.

Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd (NSE: THELEELA) standalone net profit more than doubled to ₹91.2 crore. City Union Bank net profit rose 25% to ₹360 crore. UltraTech Cement Ltd net profit gained 20% to ₹2,983 crore.

The Sensex closed 416.72 points lower at 76,886.91 amid mixed Q4 outcomes. Brokerages expect near-term margin pressures in cement and autos to persist.

REC LTD'S CONSOLIDATED NET PROFIT FELL 22% TO ₹3,375.08 CRORE IN Q4 FY26, MAINLY DUE TO LOWER INTEREST INCOME WHICH DROPPED TO ₹14,119.11 CRORE FROM ₹14,947.26 CRORE IN Q4 FY25. TOTAL INCOME DECLINED TO ₹14,583.39 CRORE FROM ₹15,348.37 CRORE.

THE BOARD OF MARUTI SUZUKI INDIA LTD RECOMMENDED A FINAL DIVIDEND OF ₹140 PER EQUITY SHARE FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDING MARCH 31, 2026.
