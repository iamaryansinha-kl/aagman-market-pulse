Top-10 Firms Shed ₹1L Cr Mcap; SBI Biggest Laggard At ₹44,722 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Four of India's top-10 most valued firms shed ₹1 lakh crore in combined market cap during the week ended May 9, 2026
  • 02/SBI was the biggest laggard with a ₹44,722.34 crore valuation drop to ₹9,41,107.62 crore; six firms gained ₹46,685 crore combined
  • 03/Sensex climbed 414.69 points or 0.53% and Nifty rose 178.6 points or 0.74% in a volatile, range-bound week

Four of India's top-10 most valued firms shed ₹1 lakh crore in combined market cap last week. State Bank of India took the biggest hit.

SBI's valuation dropped by ₹44,722.34 crore to ₹9,41,107.62 crore. It remained the fourth most valued firm despite the decline.

Bharti Airtel's valuation declined by ₹31,167.1 crore to ₹11,18,055.03 crore. TCS lost ₹28,456.26 crore to settle at ₹8,66,477.69 crore.

Larsen & Toubro edged lower by ₹5,371.84 crore to ₹5,46,621.21 crore. It was the smallest loser among the four declining firms.

Six firms gained ₹46,685.21 crore combined. HDFC Bank jumped ₹15,425.09 crore to ₹12,02,699.26 crore, becoming the second most valued firm.

Bajaj Finance surged ₹11,486.89 crore to ₹5,94,610.02 crore. Hindustan Unilever added ₹8,763.97 crore to reach ₹5,37,562.98 crore.

Reliance Industries gained ₹6,563.28 crore to retain top spot at ₹19,42,866.58 crore. LIC rose ₹2,751.37 crore to ₹5,07,549.44 crore.

"Indian equity markets witnessed a volatile and range-bound week, with sentiment remaining cautious despite intermittent recovery attempts," Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, said.
