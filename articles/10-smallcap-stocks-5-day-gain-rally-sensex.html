10 Smallcap Stocks Gain 5 Days Straight: Sensex Falls 0.4% But These Rally 7-19%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/10 smallcap stocks posted gains on all 5 trading sessions from June 19 to 25, delivering cumulative returns of 7% to 19%
  • 02/Pearl Global Industries topped the list with a 19% gain, followed by TV Today Network at 18% and SKF India at 17%
  • 03/The Sensex fell 0.40% or 310 points to close at 77,100 over the same period, making this smallcap rally stand out

Despite the Sensex slipping 0.40% or 310 points over the five trading sessions that ended June 25, a select group of smallcap stocks bucked the trend. Around 20 stocks in the BSE Smallcap Index advanced in each of the five consecutive sessions between June 19 and 25.

Of these, 10 stocks posted gains on all five trading days. They delivered cumulative returns ranging from 7% to 19% over the period. Data was compiled by ACE Equity.

Pearl Global Industries led the pack with a 5-day gain of 19%. The stock closed at Rs 2,078 on the last trading day of the period.

TV Today Network followed closely with an 18% rally over the five sessions. The media stock closed at Rs 126.

SKF India (Industrial) gained 17% over the same period. It closed at Rs 2,911.

VA Tech Wabag was next with a 16% gain, closing at Rs 2,111. Talbros Automotive Components and Fermenta Biotech both rose 15%, closing at Rs 407 and Rs 366 respectively.

Stove Kraft added 14% to close at Rs 783. Nintec Systems gained 13% to close at Rs 779. Kirloskar Electric Company rounded out the top 10 with a 10% gain, closing at Rs 113.

Other stocks that advanced on all five days included Dr. Agarwal's Health Care (up 9% to Rs 486), Rubicon Research (up 9% to Rs 1,416), Sansera Engineering (up 9% to Rs 3,146), RR Kabel (up 9% to Rs 2,491), Automobile Corporation of Goa (up 8% to Rs 2,255), and Himatsingka Seide (up 7% to Rs 90).

The smallcap segment often outperforms in periods of broader market consolidation. The Sensex registered gains in three of those five sessions but still ended the period lower at 77,100.
