Ashiana Housing Invests Rs 560 Cr in Jaipur Residential Project 'Ashiana Oma'

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Ashiana Housing Ltd (NSE: ASHIANA) launches Phase 1 of 'Ashiana Oma', a Rs 558.60 crore premium residential project in Jaipur
  • 02/The 10.35-acre project will develop 490 units total, with Phase 1 comprising 280 units at Rs 186.78 crore
  • 03/The project is a joint venture with revenue-sharing arrangement, targeting end-user demand for spacious premium residences with lifestyle amenities

Ashiana Housing Ltd (NSE: ASHIANA) will invest nearly Rs 560 crore to develop a premium residential project in Jaipur. The company launched the first phase of 'Ashiana Oma' on May 22, 2026.

The project spans 10.35 acres and will house a total of 490 units. Phase 1 will develop 280 units with an estimated investment of Rs 186.78 crore.

The total estimated project cost stands at Rs 558.60 crore. This is a joint venture with a revenue-sharing arrangement between Ashiana Housing and its development partner.

"The project has been designed to cater to end-user customers in Jaipur, where demand for spacious premium residences with integrated lifestyle amenities continues to strengthen." - Ankur Gupta, Joint Managing Director, Ashiana Housing Ltd

Delhi-based Ashiana Housing is a leading listed real estate developer. The company specialises in developing homes for senior citizens across India.

The Jaipur expansion signals Ashiana's push into premium residential segments beyond its senior living niche. It marks a strategic diversification in one of India's emerging real estate markets.

Realty demand in Jaipur has been strengthening for spacious homes with integrated amenities. Ashiana Oma targets this growing segment of end-user buyers.
