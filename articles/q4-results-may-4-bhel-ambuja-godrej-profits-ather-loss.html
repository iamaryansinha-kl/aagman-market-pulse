Q4 Results May 4 Live: BHEL Profit Jumps 155%, Ambuja Net Up 196%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BHEL Q4 standalone profit jumps to ₹1,282.68 crore from ₹504.05 crore YoY, while Ambuja Cements net profit nearly triples to ₹1,643.66 crore
  • 02/Godrej Properties delivers record FY26 with PAT up 32% to ₹1,840 crore and Q4 profit surging 70%; Ather Energy narrows Q4 loss to ₹100 crore
  • 03/Exide Industries Q4 profit slips to ₹213 crore; Tata Technologies, Sobha and KEI Industries are among companies scheduled to announce results today

Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd (NSE: BHEL) reported standalone Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹1,282.68 crore. The figure marks a 155% jump from ₹504.05 crore in Q4 FY25. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.40 per share.

Ambuja Cements Ltd (NSE: AMBUJACEM) posted standalone net profit of ₹1,643.66 crore for Q4 FY26. This represents a 196% surge from ₹555.07 crore in the year-ago quarter. The company achieved its highest-ever annual volume of 73.7 million tonnes.

Godrej Properties Ltd (NSE: GODREJPROP) reported consolidated Q4 net profit of ₹645.44 crore, up 70% from ₹378.44 crore in Q4 FY25. FY26 PAT rose 32% to ₹1,840.66 crore, marking the highest annual profit in the company's history.

Ather Energy narrowed its Q4 FY26 net loss to ₹100.23 crore from ₹234.36 crore in Q4 FY25. FY26 loss reduced to ₹517.17 crore from ₹812.28 crore in the prior year. The company ended FY26 with 700 Experience Centres and over 6,000 charging points.

Aditya Birla Capital Ltd (NSE: AB CAPITAL) posted Q4 standalone net profit of ₹777.04 crore against ₹653.62 crore in Q4 FY25. FY26 PAT stood at ₹3,109.32 crore. The board approved raising the NCD borrowing limit to ₹2,00,000 crore.

Exide Industries Ltd (NSE: EXIDEIND) reported Q4 standalone net profit of ₹213.44 crore, down from ₹254.60 crore in Q4 FY25. FY26 PAT improved to ₹1,111.33 crore from ₹1,076.93 crore. The board recommended a dividend of ₹2 per share.

Pirojsha Godrej, Executive Chairperson of Godrej Properties, said the company targets residential bookings exceeding ₹39,000 crore in FY27. He flagged potential global security-related disruptions as a risk to watch. Tata Technologies, Sobha and KEI Industries are also scheduled to declare results today.

BHEL shares hit the upper circuit at ₹387.65, gaining 10%. Godrej Properties rose 4% to ₹1,909.40. Ambuja Cements traded 2% higher at ₹454. The Nifty 50 closed up 114.60 points at 24,112.15.
