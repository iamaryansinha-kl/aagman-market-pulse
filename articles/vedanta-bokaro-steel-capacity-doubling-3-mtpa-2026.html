Vedanta Bokaro Steel Capacity Doubling to 3 mtpa by End 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Iron & Steel aims to double Bokaro steel capacity to 3 mtpa by end 2026, nearly a year ahead of FY28 guidance
  • 02/The $1.3 billion capex programme targets 5 mtpa by FY29; regulatory clearances are expected within six months
  • 03/At 3 mtpa, Vedanta would rival mid-sized steelmakers NMDC Steel and Evonith Steel

Vedanta Iron & Steel plans to double Bokaro steelmaking capacity to 3 million tonnes per annum by end 2026. The board has accelerated the timeline from the FY28 guidance in the annual report.

The Bokaro expansion was delayed by regulatory hurdles since Vedanta acquired ESL Steel in 2018. Pending environmental clearances and consent-related approvals held back the project.

The company is now awaiting final regulatory clearance, expected within the next six months. Once approved, the expansion will proceed on an accelerated schedule.

The plan involves debottlenecking the existing plant to add 0.5 mtpa and installing a 1.5 mtpa blast furnace. Vedanta has also outlined a next phase to reach 5 mtpa by FY29.

At 3 mtpa, Vedanta Iron & Steel would match mid-sized domestic steelmakers like NMDC Steel at 2 mtpa and Evonith Steel at 1.4 mtpa. The standalone listed entity can now make faster investment decisions post-demerger.

Vedanta Iron & Steel has outlined a $1.3 billion capex programme through FY29. The target is 5 mtpa steel capacity and 18.5 mtpa iron ore production by FY29, with EBITDA forecast at $526 million.

The 5 mtpa expansion will be funded through internal accruals and borrowings. CFO Ajay Goel said the steel arm will assume only a "very small" portion of parent Vedanta Ltd's $6.7 billion debt.

Four demerged Vedanta entities listed on June 15. Chairman Anil Agarwal said each of the five companies could eventually be valued at $100 billion.
