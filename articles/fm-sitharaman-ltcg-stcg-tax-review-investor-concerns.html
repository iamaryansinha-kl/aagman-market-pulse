FM Sitharaman Open To Review LTCG, STCG Tax Rates After Investor Pushback

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said the government is willing to listen to investor concerns on LTCG and STCG taxation at the TEXPROCIL event on May 25
  • 02/Current rates: STCG at 20% (up from 15%) and LTCG at 12.5% (up from 10%) after Budget 2024; markets remain volatile amid global tensions
  • 03/No formal review announced yet; investors view statement as signal of potential engagement on capital gains tax framework

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said the government is open to hearing investor concerns on capital gains taxes. She spoke on May 25, 2026, at the TEXPROCIL Export Awards event in New Delhi.

"On this specific issue, and on any issue, we are always ready and willing to listen to the people. We will certainly take their inputs," Sitharaman told reporters.

Short-Term Capital Gains tax is 20% on equity sold within 12 months. Long-Term Capital Gains tax is 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh for holdings beyond 12 months.

Both rates were raised in Budget 2024. STCG climbed from 15% to 20%, and LTCG rose from 10% to 12.5%, effective July 23, 2024.

Market participants have been demanding a review amid rising volatility. Domestic equities have faced pressure from geopolitical tensions, crude oil prices, and foreign investor outflows.

Sitharaman did not announce any formal review or change in the tax structure. Her remarks only indicated openness to feedback from stakeholders.

She also addressed petrol and diesel price hikes, clarifying they are operational not policy-driven. The government absorbed a ₹1 lakh crore fiscal hit to insulate consumers for over two months.

Investors are watching for any signals in the upcoming budget or policy announcements. A rate rollback could boost market sentiment and equity participation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT DID FM SITHARAMAN SAY ABOUT LTCG AND STCG TAXES?
ON MAY 25, 2026, FINANCE MINISTER NIRMALA SITHARAMAN STATED THE GOVERNMENT IS "ALWAYS READY AND WILLING TO LISTEN" TO INVESTOR CONCERNS ON CAPITAL GAINS TAXATION. SHE DID NOT ANNOUNCE ANY FORMAL REVIEW BUT SIGNALED OPENNESS TO FEEDBACK.
WHAT ARE THE CURRENT LTCG AND STCG TAX RATES IN INDIA?
SHORT-TERM CAPITAL GAINS ON LISTED EQUITY ARE TAXED AT 20% FOR HOLDINGS UNDER 12 MONTHS. LONG-TERM CAPITAL GAINS ARE TAXED AT 12.5% ON GAINS EXCEEDING ₹1.25 LAKH PER FINANCIAL YEAR FOR HOLDINGS OVER 12 MONTHS.
WHEN WERE THE CURRENT CAPITAL GAINS TAX RATES IMPLEMENTED?
THE CURRENT RATES WERE INTRODUCED IN THE UNION BUDGET 2024, PRESENTED ON JULY 23, 2024. STCG ROSE FROM 15% TO 20%, AND LTCG INCREASED FROM 10% TO 12.5% WITH THE EXEMPTION RAISED FROM ₹1 LAKH TO ₹1.25 LAKH.
WHY ARE INVESTORS CONCERNED ABOUT LTCG AND STCG TAXES?
INVESTORS ARGUE THE HIGHER RATES REDUCE POST-TAX RETURNS AND DAMPEN EQUITY MARKET PARTICIPATION. THIS COMES AMID DOMESTIC MARKET VOLATILITY DRIVEN BY GLOBAL GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS, CRUDE OIL PRICES, AND FOREIGN INVESTOR FLOWS.
WHAT SHOULD EQUITY INVESTORS DO NOW?
INVESTORS SHOULD MONITOR BUDGET AND POLICY ANNOUNCEMENTS FOR ANY POTENTIAL ROLLBACK. UNTIL THEN, HOLDING EQUITY BEYOND 12 MONTHS SAVES 7.5 PERCENTAGE POINTS IN TAX (20% STCG VS 12.5% LTCG), AND THE ₹1.25 LAKH ANNUAL LTCG EXEMPTION REMAINS AVAILABLE.
