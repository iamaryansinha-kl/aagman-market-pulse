India Engineering Exports Hit: UAE Shipments Crash 66.8% in March

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's engineering exports to UAE plunged 66.8% to $237.4 million in March amid West Asia crisis disrupting cargo movement
  • 02/Overall engineering exports still rose 1.13% to $10.94 billion in March, supported by strong US and European demand
  • 03/FY2025-26 engineering exports hit record $122.43 billion; sector targets $250 billion by 2030

India's engineering exports to the UAE crashed 66.8% to $237.4 million in March 2026. Shipments to Saudi Arabia also dropped 45% to $247.7 million. The West Asia crisis has disrupted cargo ship movement.

EEPC India data shows UAE exports fell from $714.8 million in March 2025. Saudi Arabia shipments declined from $450.5 million a year earlier.

Despite the West Asia blow, overall engineering exports rose 1.13% to $10.94 billion in March. Strong demand from the US and Europe offset the Gulf decline.

For FY2025-26, engineering exports hit a record $122.43 billion. This marks a 4.86% increase over the previous year. The sector targets $250 billion by 2030.

The US remained the largest market with shipments up 2.3% to $19.6 billion. Germany grew 14.6% to $4.96 billion and the UK rose 18.2% to $4.74 billion.

China saw a 32.4% jump to $3.53 billion. Korea exports increased 16.7% to $3.03 billion. Asian and European markets cushioned the Gulf shock.

Engineering goods now account for 27.71% of India's total merchandise exports. The share improved to 28.11% in March 2026. It remains the top export segment.

"Going forward, the combination of sectoral resilience, market diversification, and targeted policy interventions will remain critical for sustaining export momentum and progressing towards long-term export targets," EEPC India stated.
