Reliance Power CFO Among Three Held in ₹136 Crore Fake Bank Guarantee Case

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Delhi Police Economic Offences Wing arrested Reliance Power CFO Ashok Kumar Pal and two associates
  • 02/The trio allegedly submitted forged bank guarantees worth ₹136 crore to Solar Energy Corporation of India for a tender
  • 03/Reliance Power says it is a victim of fraud and the arrested executive has been removed from his position

Delhi Police's Economic Offences Wing arrested three individuals on April 21, 2026. Those arrested include Ashok Kumar Pal, Chief Financial Officer of Reliance Power Ltd.

The arrests relate to an alleged ₹136 crore bank guarantee forgery. Investigators claim Pal and two associates submitted fake guarantees to Solar Energy Corporation of India to secure a battery storage tender.

The other arrested individuals are Partha Sarthi Biswal and Amar Nath Dutta. Police allege they acted as intermediaries in preparing and submitting the forged documents.

Reliance Power issued a statement to stock exchanges. The company said it and its subsidiaries acted bonafidely and are victims of a fraud, forgery, and cheating conspiracy.

The company clarified that Anil Ambani has not been on the Reliance Power board for over 3.5 years. It stated he is not concerned with this matter in any manner.

Pal has been removed from his position as Executive Director and CFO pending investigation. Reliance Power said it will take all legally appropriate steps in the matter.

The case relates to a tender floated by SECI for battery energy storage systems. Police allege the accused submitted two forged bank guarantees worth ₹68.20 crore each.
