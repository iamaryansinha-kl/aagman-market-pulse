Gold Imports From Dubai May Surge After India Hikes Duty to 15%: GTRI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India hiked gold import duty from 6% to 15%, creating a wider tariff gap for Dubai-routed bullion
  • 02/Gold imported under India-UAE CEPA quota enters at 14%, just 1 percentage point below the new MFN rate
  • 03/GTRI warns the arbitrage opportunity could encourage greater routing of global bullion through Dubai

India raised the import duty on gold from 6 per cent to 15 per cent. The move sharply alters the economics of precious metal imports from the UAE.

Think tank GTRI said the widening gap could boost Dubai gold imports. Global bullion may increasingly route through the UAE despite it not being a gold miner.

Under the India-UAE CEPA, gold imports enter at tariffs one percentage point below the normal MFN rate. The concessional rate now stands at 14 per cent versus the new 15 per cent standard.

The Tariff Rate Quota began at 120 tonnes annually in 2022. It is set to rise to 200 tonnes by 2027, covering nearly one-fourth of India's yearly gold imports.

GTRI Founder Ajay Srivastava highlighted the arbitrage opportunity. "That margin is scheduled to widen further each year until CEPA tariffs fall to zero by 2031," he said.

The duty gap for silver has widened to 8 percentage points. Concessional silver imports from the UAE currently stand at 7 per cent against the new 15 per cent general tariff.

In FY 2025-26, India imported nearly $72 billion worth of gold. This marks a 25 per cent increase over the previous fiscal year.

Silver imports crossed $1 billion, recording an extraordinary 150 per cent annual increase. The surge comes amid record demand for precious metals.

GTRI urged the Finance Ministry to simplify tariff notification language. Importers currently must trace references to customs notifications issued over the past 26 years.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE NEW GOLD IMPORT DUTY IN INDIA?
THE IMPORT DUTY ON GOLD HAS BEEN RAISED FROM 6 PER CENT TO 15 PER CENT. INCLUDING 3 PER CENT IGST, THE EFFECTIVE IMPORT DUTY HAS SURGED FROM 9.18 PER CENT TO 18.45 PER CENT.
HOW DOES THE INDIA-UAE CEPA AFFECT GOLD IMPORTS FROM DUBAI?
UNDER THE INDIA-UAE COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT, GOLD IMPORTED UNDER THE UAE QUOTA ENTERS AT 14 PER CENT. THIS IS ONE PERCENTAGE POINT BELOW THE NEW 15 PER CENT MOST-FAVOURED-NATION RATE, CREATING AN ARBITRAGE OPPORTUNITY.
WHAT IS THE SIZE OF THE UAE GOLD IMPORT QUOTA?
THE TARIFF RATE QUOTA BEGAN AT 120 TONNES ANNUALLY IN 2022 AND IS SET TO RISE TO 200 TONNES BY 2027. THIS COVERS NEARLY ONE-FOURTH OF INDIA'S YEARLY GOLD IMPORTS.
HOW MUCH GOLD DID INDIA IMPORT IN FY 2025-26?
INDIA IMPORTED NEARLY $72 BILLION WORTH OF GOLD IN FY 2025-26. THIS REPRESENTS A 25 PER CENT INCREASE COMPARED TO THE PREVIOUS FISCAL YEAR.
WHAT DID GTRI SAY ABOUT THE TARIFF NOTIFICATION PROCESS?
GTRI URGED THE FINANCE MINISTRY TO SIMPLIFY THE LANGUAGE OF TARIFF NOTIFICATIONS. IMPORTERS, LAWYERS AND CONSULTANTS MUST CURRENTLY TRACE REFERENCES TO CUSTOMS NOTIFICATIONS ISSUED OVER THE PAST 26 YEARS TO DETERMINE APPLICABLE DUTIES.
