India Climbs Global Manufacturing Rankings Amid China+1 Shift

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

India's manufacturing profile is rising globally.

The country has climbed in international manufacturing rankings, reflecting its growing role as an alternative production hub to China. The improvement comes as multinationals accelerate supply chain diversification under the China+1 strategy.

Several global companies have announced or expanded manufacturing operations in India over the past year. Sectors including electronics, automobiles, textiles, and chemicals have seen the most significant investments.

The government's production-linked incentive schemes have played a key role. PLI schemes for semiconductors, mobile phones, textiles, and solar panels have attracted billions in committed investments from both domestic and foreign firms.

India's large domestic market provides an additional pull. Companies setting up manufacturing facilities can serve both export markets and India's growing consumer base of 1.4 billion people.

The ranking improvement also reflects infrastructure upgrades. New industrial corridors, improved port capacity, and expanded highway networks have reduced logistics costs for manufacturers.

Challenges remain. Labour productivity, regulatory complexity, and land acquisition continue to hinder faster manufacturing growth. India still lags China in scale and cost competitiveness across several sectors.

Supply chain diversification away from China and government incentive schemes.

A strategy where multinationals diversify manufacturing away from China to reduce risk.

Electronics, automobiles, textiles, and chemicals.

Production-linked incentive schemes for semiconductors, mobile phones, textiles, and solar panels.

Labour productivity, regulatory complexity, and land acquisition.
