Kashmir Handicraft Exports Stalled as West Asia Conflict Hits Demand

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Kashmir's handicraft sector faces crisis as West Asia conflict stalls exports to Gulf markets, which account for 60% of total trade
  • 02/Fresh orders have dropped 30-40%, freight charges surged 30-50%, and payments remain delayed across the sector
  • 03/Indian Carpet Expo and multiple international exhibitions postponed, with J&K exports at ₹733.59 crore in FY25

Kashmir's handicraft sector is facing a deepening crisis nearly two months after the outbreak of conflict in Iran. Exporters say consignments to Gulf markets remain suspended and fresh orders have dried up.

Trade representatives report that payments have been delayed and inventories are piling up. Uncertainty linked to the conflict continues to disrupt trade routes and dampen buyer sentiment across West Asia.

Javed Ahmad Tenga, President of the Kashmir Chamber of Commerce and Industry, said consignments meant for Gulf markets have been stranded since the conflict began. "Even bulk shipments destined for West Asian markets during Ramadhan remain stalled in the Valley," he said.

The Gulf region accounts for approximately 60 percent of Kashmir's handicraft exports. The ongoing disruption has triggered a liquidity crunch affecting thousands of artisans, weavers and small exporters.

Official data shows the sector employs more than 4.4 lakh artisans. It is the second-largest employment generator in the region after agriculture, with exports touching ₹733.59 crore in 2024-25.

Handicraft exports had nearly doubled over the past five years, rising from about ₹1,000 crore to ₹2,000 crore by industry estimates. Exporters warn the current disruption threatens to slow that growth momentum.

Freight charges on the India-West Asia route have climbed 30 to 50 percent since hostilities intensified. Industry leaders estimate fresh orders have declined by 30 to 40 percent since tensions escalated.

Asia's largest handmade carpet exhibition, the Indian Carpet Expo in New Delhi, has been postponed along with shows in Copenhagen, China, Netherlands and the UAE. The KCCI has sought credit extensions from the DGFT to ease the cash flow crisis.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW IS THE WEST ASIA CONFLICT AFFECTING KASHMIR'S HANDICRAFT SECTOR?
THE ONGOING CONFLICT INVOLVING IRAN, ISRAEL AND THE UNITED STATES HAS STALLED EXPORTS TO GULF MARKETS, WHICH ACCOUNT FOR APPROXIMATELY 60 PERCENT OF KASHMIR'S TOTAL HANDICRAFT EXPORTS. FRESH ORDERS HAVE DROPPED 30-40 PERCENT, PAYMENTS ARE DELAYED, AND CONSIGNMENTS REMAIN STRANDED IN THE VALLEY.
HOW MANY ARTISANS ARE EMPLOYED IN KASHMIR'S HANDICRAFT SECTOR?
OFFICIAL DATA SHOWS THE SECTOR EMPLOYS MORE THAN 4.4 LAKH ARTISANS AND CRAFTSMEN. IT IS THE SECOND-LARGEST EMPLOYMENT GENERATOR IN THE REGION AFTER AGRICULTURE.
WHAT IS THE SIZE OF KASHMIR'S HANDICRAFT EXPORT TRADE?
EXPORTS FROM JAMMU AND KASHMIR TOUCHED ₹733.59 CRORE IN 2024-25, ACCORDING TO THE ECONOMIC SURVEY 2025-26. INDUSTRY ESTIMATES PLACE OVERALL HANDICRAFT EXPORTS AT NEARLY ₹2,000 CRORE, HAVING DOUBLED FROM ABOUT ₹1,000 CRORE OVER THE PAST FIVE YEARS.
WHAT HAS HAPPENED TO SHIPPING AND FREIGHT COSTS?
FREIGHT CHARGES ON THE INDIA-WEST ASIA ROUTE HAVE CLIMBED 30 TO 50 PERCENT SINCE HOSTILITIES INTENSIFIED. INSURERS HAVE ALSO INTRODUCED WAR-RISK PREMIUMS FOR VESSELS PASSING THROUGH SENSITIVE ZONES NEAR THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ AND YEMEN'S COAST.
WHAT STEPS ARE BEING TAKEN TO SUPPORT THE SECTOR?
THE KASHMIR CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY SUBMITTED A REPRESENTATION TO THE DIRECTOR GENERAL OF FOREIGN TRADE ON MARCH 25, SEEKING EXTENSIONS OF PRE-SHIPMENT AND POST-SHIPMENT CREDIT FACILITIES. MULTIPLE INTERNATIONAL EXHIBITIONS INCLUDING THE INDIAN CARPET EXPO HAVE BEEN POSTPONED DUE TO THE CONFLICT.
