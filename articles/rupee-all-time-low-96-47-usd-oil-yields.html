Rupee Hits All-Time Low of 96.47 vs USD on Oil, US Yield Pressure

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian rupee hit an all-time low of 96.47 per US dollar on Tuesday, May 19, 2026
  • 02/Stubbornly high crude oil prices and rising US Treasury yields drove the currency lower
  • 03/State-run banks sold dollars in the forex market, signaling likely RBI intervention

The Indian rupee fell to an all-time low of 96.47 against the US dollar on Tuesday. The currency breached its previous record low amid broad-based weakness across emerging markets.

Stubbornly high crude oil prices kept India's import bills elevated. The country imports over 85% of its crude requirements, leaving the rupee highly exposed to energy cost shocks.

Weaker Asian currencies added to the pressure on the rupee. The dollar gained ground across regional peers as risk appetite faded and capital flowed toward US assets.

Higher US Treasury yields drove further dollar strength. The widening interest rate differential reduced the rupee's carry appeal for foreign portfolio investors.

State-run banks were spotted selling dollars in the spot forex market. This signals likely intervention by the Reserve Bank of India to contain excessive volatility.

The rupee has depreciated sharply against a resurgent dollar through 2026. It has ranked among the worst-performing Asian currencies in recent months.

Analysts expect the RBI to intervene more aggressively if the rupee slides past 97 per dollar. Sustained weakness could fuel imported inflation and strain the current account deficit.
