EMS Players Rally After Government Extends Customs Duty Sops

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

EMS stocks are surging on government support.

Electronic manufacturing services companies have rallied after the government extended customs duty exemptions on key components and inputs. The move supports the government's broader push to make India a global electronics manufacturing hub and reduce dependence on imports, particularly from China.

The EMS sector has been a major beneficiary of the production-linked incentive scheme. Companies like Dixon Technologies, Amber Enterprises, and Kaynes Technology have seen their order books swell as global brands shift manufacturing to India. The customs duty sops further improve their competitiveness.

The extension covers components used in smartphones, laptops, and other electronic devices. By waiving import duties on certain inputs, the government reduces the cost of manufacturing in India, making domestic production more competitive against imports from China and Vietnam.

Stocks in the sector have rallied sharply. Dixon Technologies, the largest listed EMS player, has hit fresh highs. Other companies in the sector have also seen strong gains as investors price in continued government support and the long-term growth trajectory of Indian electronics manufacturing.

The government's electronics manufacturing push has been remarkably successful. Smartphone production in India has crossed $50 billion annually, and the country is now the world's second-largest manufacturer of mobile phones. The next frontier is expanding into laptops, servers, and semiconductors.

However, challenges remain. The EMS sector is still heavily dependent on imports for high-value components like semiconductors and display panels. Achieving true self-sufficiency will require significant investment in upstream manufacturing capabilities, which will take years to develop.
