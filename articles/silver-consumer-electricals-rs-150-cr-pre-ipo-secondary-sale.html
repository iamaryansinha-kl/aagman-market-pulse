Silver Consumer Electricals Raises Rs 150 Cr in Pre-IPO Secondary Share Sale

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Silver Consumer Electricals completes Rs 150 crore pre-IPO secondary sale at Rs 205.05 per share, valuing the transaction at a premium
  • 02/Investor Riyaz Suterwala acquires 73.15 lakh shares, representing 2.59% pre-offer equity from promoter Dharamshibhai Bediya
  • 03/Company filed DRHP for Rs 1,400 crore IPO in August 2025; fresh issue of Rs 1,000 crore and OFS of Rs 400 crore planned

Silver Consumer Electricals Ltd has completed a pre-IPO secondary share sale worth Rs 150 crore. Promoter group member Dharamshibhai Mohanbhai Bediya transferred 73,15,288 equity shares to investor Riyaz Suterwala on Friday.

The transaction was executed at Rs 205.05 per equity share. Following the acquisition, Suterwala now holds a 2.59 per cent stake in the company's pre-offer equity share capital.

Silver Consumer Electricals filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus with Sebi in August 2025. The company plans to raise up to Rs 1,400 crore through its maiden public offering.

The proposed IPO comprises a fresh issue of equity shares worth Rs 1,000 crore. An Offer For Sale of up to Rs 400 crore will be made by promoter Vinit Dharamshibhai Bediya.

The Rs 1,000 crore fresh issue proceeds will primarily repay debt. Rs 865 crore is allocated for existing borrowings and Rs 35 crore for subsidiary debt clearance.

Based in Rajkot, Gujarat, Silver Consumer Electricals manufactures pumps, motors, fans and lighting. The company operates under the 'Silver' and 'Bediya' brands and also supplies to major OEMs in India.

The company reported revenue from operations of Rs 1,586.38 crore in Fiscal 2025. Revenue grew at a CAGR of 95.17% between Fiscal 2023 and Fiscal 2025, making it the fastest-growing company in its sector.

The company's manufacturing facility spans 138,821 square metres in Rajkot. It is India's largest single-location vertically integrated plant for electrical consumer durables and agricultural equipment.

The IPO is being managed by Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors, ICICI Securities, JM Financial and Choice Capital Advisors. Listing dates and price band are yet to be announced.
