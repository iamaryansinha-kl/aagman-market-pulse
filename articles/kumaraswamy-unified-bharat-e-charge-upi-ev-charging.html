Kumaraswamy Unveils Unified Bharat e-Charge: 'UPI for EV Charging'

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Union Minister H.D. Kumaraswamy launched Unified Bharat e-Charge on May 12, 2026, a national interoperable platform for EV charging backed by NPCI
  • 02/₹503.86 crore sanctioned for 4,874 chargers under PM E-DRIVE; 6,200 of 9,100 approved chargers already energised across India
  • 03/The "UPI for EV charging" aims to unify BPCL, Indian Oil, HPCL and private networks under one digital interface

Union Heavy Industries Minister H.D. Kumaraswamy unveiled Unified Bharat e-Charge (UBC) on May 12, 2026. He called it the "UPI for EV charging" at the National Conference on Enabling Nationwide EV Charging Infrastructure in Bengaluru.

The platform is backed by the Government of India and NPCI. BHEL developed the system using open protocols to display real-time charger availability, ownership, power output and location.

So far, ₹503.86 crore has been sanctioned for 4,874 chargers across states and PSUs. Of 9,100 approved chargers, 6,200 have already been energised while 1,800 await grid connectivity.

India now has more than 67,000 chargers including private installations. Over 25,000 are fast chargers. Heavy Industries Additional Secretary Hanif Qureshi said a charger is now available within 30 km on most highways.

Karnataka leads with 8,600 charging stations. EV sales in the state grew 17-fold between FY21 and FY26 with 76.15 per cent CAGR. The state has 13.65 per cent EV penetration.

Oil marketing companies are transforming fuel retail into charging networks. BPCL has built 225+ Bharat K Stations. Indian Oil operates around 6,000 DC fast chargers and 1,450 battery-swapping stations.

Nearly 90 per cent of EV charging in India still happens at homes or workplaces. Tata Motors is advocating a statutory "right to charge" for apartment residents to install home chargers.

"Just as UPI revolutionised digital payments, Unified Bharat e-Charge is set to revolutionise EV charging in India," Kumaraswamy said.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS UNIFIED BHARAT E-CHARGE?
UNIFIED BHARAT E-CHARGE IS A NATIONAL INTEROPERABLE PLATFORM FOR EV CHARGING UNVEILED BY UNION HEAVY INDUSTRIES MINISTER H.D. KUMARASWAMY ON MAY 12, 2026. IT ALLOWS USERS TO LOCATE, BOOK AND PAY ACROSS CHARGING NETWORKS THROUGH A SINGLE INTERFACE.
HOW MUCH FUNDING HAS BEEN SANCTIONED UNDER PM E-DRIVE FOR CHARGERS?
₹503.86 CRORE HAS BEEN SANCTIONED FOR 4,874 CHARGERS ACROSS STATES AND CENTRAL PUBLIC SECTOR ENTERPRISES INCLUDING BPCL, INDIAN OIL AND HPCL.
HOW MANY CHARGERS ARE OPERATIONAL IN INDIA?
OF 9,100 APPROVED CHARGERS, 6,200 HAVE ALREADY BEEN ENERGISED. INDIA NOW HAS MORE THAN 67,000 CHARGERS INCLUDING PRIVATE INSTALLATIONS, OF WHICH OVER 25,000 ARE FAST CHARGERS.
WHICH STATE HAS THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF EV CHARGING STATIONS?
KARNATAKA LEADS WITH MORE THAN 8,600 CHARGING STATIONS. THE STATE HAS SEEN 17-FOLD EV SALES GROWTH BETWEEN FY21 AND FY26 WITH A 76.15 PER CENT COMPOUND ANNUAL GROWTH RATE.
WHAT IS THE "RIGHT TO CHARGE" PROPOSED BY TATA MOTORS?
TATA MOTORS IS ADVOCATING A STATUTORY "RIGHT TO CHARGE" THAT WOULD GIVE EV OWNERS IN APARTMENT COMPLEXES A LEGAL ENTITLEMENT TO INSTALL HOME CHARGERS, SIMILAR TO THE RIGHT TO AN ELECTRICITY CONNECTION.
