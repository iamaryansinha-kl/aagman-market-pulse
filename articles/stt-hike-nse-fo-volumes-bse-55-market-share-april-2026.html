STT Hike Hits NSE F&O Volumes as BSE Captures 55% Market Share in April

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE F&O average daily turnover jumps 20% to ₹269 lakh crore in April while NSE's drops 26% to ₹216 lakh crore after the April 1 STT hike
  • 02/BSE notional F&O market share surges to 55% in April from 44% in March, overtaking NSE for the first time in segment turnover
  • 03/NSE options premium turnover falls 35% to ₹61,417 crore daily as BSE charges zero on futures and ₹5 per lakh on options

The April 2026 Securities Transaction Tax (STT) hike has triggered a sharp divergence in derivatives trading between India's two exchanges. BSE's average daily notional turnover in the Futures & Options segment surged nearly 20% month-on-month to ₹269 lakh crore. NSE, the long-time market leader, saw its notional ADT plunge about 26% to ₹216 lakh crore in the same period.

The STT on futures contracts rose to 0.05% from 0.02% effective April 1. Options premium tax climbed to 0.15% from 0.10%, and options exercise tax moved to 0.15% from 0.125%. These changes were announced in Union Budget 2026 by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman.

BSE mitigated the cost shock by keeping its own charges far below NSE's. BSE levies zero transaction fees on futures contracts, while NSE charges ₹1.83 per ₹1 lakh. In options, NSE charges ₹36 per ₹1 lakh compared with BSE's ₹5 per ₹1 lakh on stock options.

BSE's share in notional F&O turnover jumped to 55% in April from 44% in March. NSE's corresponding share dropped to 45% from 56%, marking the first time BSE has overtaken NSE in this segment.

In the options premium segment, NSE's total monthly turnover fell 28% to ₹12.9 lakh crore. BSE grew 6% to ₹6.63 lakh crore. NSE's average daily premium turnover collapsed 35% to ₹61,417 crore, while BSE's held steady at ₹33,150 crore.

"Better liquidity from market-making and a shift in trader behaviour after changes in weekly expiry rules have helped BSE cross 50 per cent market share," said Feroze Azeez, Joint CEO at Anand Rathi Wealth. SEBI's rule limiting each exchange to one weekly expiry has worked in BSE's favour.

"The impact of increase in STT on F&O has been more on NSE in April, as the number of index option contracts traded in BSE rose," said Anand James, Chief Market Strategist at Geojit Investments. "Though we are not sure if this trend will continue."

"For retail participants, particularly high-frequency and low-margin traders, the math simply does not work after the STT hike," said Hariprasad K, Research Analyst and Founder at LIvelong Wealth. "It is not just a slowdown, but a filtration with weaker hands gradually stepping out."

Despite the turnover shift, NSE still dominates the options premium market with a 66% share versus BSE's 34%. BSE's gains have come primarily from index derivatives like Sensex, not stock options where NSE remains entrenched.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE STT HIKE ON F&O TRADING EFFECTIVE APRIL 2026?
STT ON FUTURES CONTRACTS WAS RAISED TO 0.05% FROM 0.02%. ON OPTIONS PREMIUM, IT CLIMBED TO 0.15% FROM 0.10%, AND ON OPTIONS EXERCISE, IT INCREASED TO 0.15% FROM 0.125%. THESE CHANGES TOOK EFFECT ON APRIL 1, 2026, FOLLOWING THE UNION BUDGET 2026 ANNOUNCEMENT.
HOW MUCH DID BSE AND NSE F&O TURNOVER CHANGE IN APRIL 2026?
BSE'S AVERAGE DAILY NOTIONAL TURNOVER IN F&O ROSE NEARLY 20% MONTH-ON-MONTH TO ₹269 LAKH CRORE. NSE'S NOTIONAL ADT FELL ABOUT 26% TO ₹216 LAKH CRORE IN THE SAME PERIOD.
WHAT IS BSE'S CURRENT MARKET SHARE IN F&O TURNOVER?
BSE'S SHARE IN NOTIONAL F&O TURNOVER ROSE TO 55% IN APRIL 2026 FROM 44% IN MARCH 2026. NSE'S SHARE DECLINED TO 45% FROM 56%, THE FIRST TIME BSE HAS OVERTAKEN NSE IN SEGMENT TURNOVER.
WHY DID TRADERS SHIFT TO BSE AFTER THE STT HIKE?
BSE LEVIES ZERO TRANSACTION FEES ON FUTURES CONTRACTS VERSUS NSE'S ₹1.83 PER ₹1 LAKH. ON OPTIONS, BSE CHARGES ₹5 PER ₹1 LAKH COMPARED WITH NSE'S ₹36 PER ₹1 LAKH. THESE LOWER CHARGES OFFSET THE HIGHER STT BURDEN FOR ACTIVE TRADERS.
WHAT SHOULD F&O TRADERS WATCH GOING FORWARD?
ANALYSTS CAUTION THAT THE SHIFT MAY NOT BE PERMANENT. NSE STILL HOLDS 66% OF OPTIONS PREMIUM TURNOVER. TIGHTER RBI LEVERAGE NORMS COULD MODERATE OVERALL VOLUMES BY 8-10%. TRADERS SHOULD MONITOR WHETHER BSE'S NON-EXPIRY DAY VOLUMES CONTINUE IMPROVING.
