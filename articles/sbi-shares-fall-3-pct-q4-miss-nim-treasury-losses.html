SBI Shares Fall 3% After Q4 Miss on NIM Pressure, Treasury Losses

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SBI shares fell over 3% to ₹984 after Q4 FY26 profit of ₹19,684 crore missed analyst estimates of ₹20,312 crore
  • 02/Domestic NIM dropped 18 bps QoQ to 2.93%, while treasury losses hit ₹4,520 crore vs ₹8,991 crore gain a year ago
  • 03/Full-year FY26 net profit rose 12.88% to ₹80,032 crore; dividend of ₹17.35 per share declared with record date May 16

State Bank of India (NSE: SBIN) shares declined over 3% on Monday, May 11, 2026. The stock hit an intra-day low of ₹984 after opening at ₹1,007, down from the previous close of ₹1,019.30.

The lender reported Q4 FY26 standalone net profit of ₹19,684 crore. This represents a 5.6% year-on-year increase from ₹18,643 crore in Q4 FY25.

However, the profit figure missed Street estimates. Reuters-LSEG data showed consensus expectations of around ₹20,312 crore for the quarter.

Net interest income rose 3.8% YoY to ₹44,380 crore. The figure declined 2% sequentially as margins compressed sharply during the quarter.

SBI’s whole bank net interest margin fell 17 basis points QoQ to 2.81%. Domestic NIM dropped 18 basis points QoQ to 2.93%, reflecting repo-linked loan repricing and MCLR resets following rate cuts.

Treasury losses were the biggest drag on earnings. The bank reported treasury losses of ₹4,520 crore during the quarter. This compares to treasury income of ₹8,991 crore in Q4 FY25.

Non-interest income fell 29% YoY to ₹17,314 crore. The sharp decline was driven by mark-to-market bond losses and lower trading income.

Asset quality remained stable. Gross non-performing assets improved to 1.49% from 1.82% a year ago. Net NPA dropped to 0.39% from 0.47% in March 2025.

Loan growth came in strong at 17% YoY, with gross advances reaching ₹49.32 trillion. Deposit growth improved to 11% YoY, while the CASA ratio held steady at 39%.

For the full year FY26, SBI reported net profit of ₹80,032 crore, up 12.88% YoY. The board declared a dividend of ₹17.35 per share with a record date of May 16, 2026.

Bernstein maintained a market perform rating with a target price of ₹1,300. The brokerage noted the 18 bps QoQ fall in domestic NIM was the steepest among large banks.

CLSA retained its outperform rating and target price of ₹1,275. It stated that while the top line was weak due to margin pressure, the quarter was otherwise healthy.

Kotak Institutional Equities maintained a buy rating with a target price of ₹1,250. It said the earnings miss could lead to a near-term pause in the stock’s re-rating until margin recovery becomes visible.

Morgan Stanley maintained an equal-weight rating with a target price of ₹980. It trimmed FY27 and FY28 earnings estimates and lowered NIM forecasts by more than 20 basis points.

SBI Chairman CS Setty said the bank expects to maintain a domestic NIM above 3% in FY27 assuming no further repo rate changes by the Reserve Bank of India.
