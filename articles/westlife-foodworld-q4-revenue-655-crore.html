Westlife Foodworld Q4: Revenue ₹655 Cr, Up 8.7%; Adds 21 Restaurants

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Westlife Foodworld Q4 revenue grew 8.7% YoY to ₹655 crore with operating EBITDA rising 9.6% to ₹87 crore
  • 02/Company added 21 new restaurants in Q4 taking total network to 478 outlets across 78 cities
  • 03/Westlife targets adding over 60 restaurants in FY27 and reaching 580-630 outlets by 2027

Westlife Foodworld Ltd (NSE: WESTLIFE) reported quarterly revenue of ₹655 crore for Q4 FY26. The figure marks an 8.7 per cent rise year-on-year. Results were announced for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Operating EBITDA rose 9.6 per cent to ₹87 crore. The EBITDA margin held broadly stable at 13.3 per cent. Cash profit after tax stood at ₹48.7 crore, representing 7.4 per cent of sales.

Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) came in at 1.5 per cent for the quarter. The company noted that momentum built progressively through Q4. Restaurant operating margins improved by approximately 70 basis points year-on-year.

On-premise channels accounted for 58 per cent of overall sales. Dine-in and takeaway together grew 9 per cent year-on-year. Off-premise sales including McDelivery rose 6 per cent year-on-year.

Digital channels contributed approximately 76 per cent to total sales. The company reported around 3.5 million monthly active users and roughly 52 million cumulative app downloads. Gross margins expanded about 60 basis points sequentially.

With 21 new restaurants added in Q4, Westlife's network now stands at 478 outlets across 78 cities. The company targets adding over 60 restaurants in FY27. The medium-term goal is reaching 580-630 restaurants by 2027.

"Chairperson Amit Jatia attributed the performance to value offerings, digital engagement, and cost discipline," and said the company is focused on building an omni-channel ecosystem.

Westlife shares closed at ₹500.95 on Thursday, up 1.31 per cent. The stock remains down about 28 per cent over the past year against a market capitalisation of approximately ₹7,751 crore.
