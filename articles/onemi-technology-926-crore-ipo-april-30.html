OnEMI Technology Solutions' ₹926 Crore IPO Opens April 30 at ₹162-171

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/OnEMI Technology Solutions sets a price band of ₹162-171 per share for its ₹926 crore IPO opening on April 30, 2026
  • 02/The fresh issue was trimmed to ₹850 crore from ₹1,000 crore proposed in the DRHP, while the OFS was halved to 44.4 lakh shares worth ₹76 crore
  • 03/At the upper price band, the company is valued at ₹2,881 crore; shares are proposed to list on BSE and NSE on May 8

OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd, which operates the digital lending platform Kissht, has fixed a price band of ₹162-171 per share. Its ₹926 crore initial public offering opens for subscription on April 30, 2026, and closes on May 5.

The IPO comprises a fresh issue of ₹850 crore and an offer-for-sale of 44,39,788 equity shares worth ₹76 crore. At the upper end of the price band, the Mumbai-based fintech is valued at nearly ₹2,881 crore.

Anchor investor bidding is scheduled for April 29. The shares are proposed to be listed on the BSE and NSE, with listing expected on May 8, 2026.

Of the fresh issue proceeds, ₹637.5 crore will augment the capital base of subsidiary Si Creva. The remaining amount is earmarked for general corporate purposes.

Founded in 2015 by former McKinsey executives Ranvir Singh and Krishnan Vishwanathan, Kissht provides digital credit to young consumers. As of March 31, 2025, the platform had 53.23 million registered users and 1.90 million active customers.

The company reported FY25 revenue from operations of ₹1,337 crore and profit after tax of ₹160.6 crore. Its assets under management stood at ₹4,087 crore, growing 55% from ₹2,604 crore in FY24.

For the nine months ended December 2025, revenue rose to ₹1,570 crore and PAT jumped to ₹199.3 crore. This signals a sharp recovery after FY25's 20% revenue decline and 18% profit drop.

The lot size is fixed at 87 shares, requiring a minimum investment of ₹14,877 from retail investors. Allocation is split 50% for QIBs, 35% for retail, and 15% for NIIs.
