Indian Stocks Flat Open on Wednesday as IIP Growth Slows to 4.8% in Q4

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian stocks set for flat opening on April 29 as Gift Nifty signals 24,090; IIP growth slows to 4.8% in Q4 FY2026 from 5.3% in Q3
  • 02/Brent crude hovers at $110-113 per barrel; FPIs remain net sellers while DIIs cushion downside amid geopolitical tensions
  • 03/Bajaj Finance earnings in focus today alongside Federal Bank, Adani Power, and Vedanta results

Indian stocks are expected to open flat on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The new monthly July series begins on the NSE today. Gift Nifty is signaling around 24,090.

The Nifty closed at 23,995.70 on Tuesday, down 97 points. The Sensex fell 416.72 points to 76,886.91. Both indices retreated from recent levels.

IIP growth slowed to 4.8 per cent in Q4 FY2026 from 5.3 per cent in Q3. The decline came entirely from slower manufacturing output. Mining and electricity sectors improved quarter-on-quarter.

March 2026 IIP touched a five-month low of 4.1 per cent. This was well above ICRA's estimate of 1.5 per cent growth. Manufacturing rose 4.3 per cent and mining climbed 5.5 per cent in March.

Brent crude remains firm in the $110-113 per barrel range. The UAE will exit OPEC from May 1, reshaping supply dynamics. The US-Iran conflict continues to disrupt the Strait of Hormuz.

Foreign portfolio investors remain net sellers amid global risk aversion. Domestic institutional investors are providing support to cushion downside moves. The rupee continues to weaken against the dollar.

Bajaj Finance earnings are the key domestic trigger for Wednesday's session. Federal Bank, Adani Power, and Vedanta also report results today. Strong numbers could offset global weakness in financials.

"Overall, sentiment remains fragile and highly sensitive to news flow," said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money. He cited elevated crude, geopolitical tensions, currency pressure and foreign outflows as near-term concerns.
