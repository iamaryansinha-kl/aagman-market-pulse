Infra Projects Cost Overrun Rs 5.65 Lakh Crore in April: MoSPI Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Central sector infra projects monitored by MoSPI report cumulative cost overrun of Rs 5.65 lakh crore as of April 2026
  • 02/Total revised cost of 1,941 projects stands at Rs 41.50 lakh crore against original cost of Rs 35.89 lakh crore
  • 03/Cumulative expenditure reaches Rs 19.93 lakh crore; 40% of projects achieve over 80% physical progress

Central infrastructure projects worth above Rs 150 crore each have accumulated a cost overrun of Rs 5.65 lakh crore. The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) released its latest Flash Report for April 2026.

The revised cost of 1,941 ongoing projects stands at Rs 41.50 lakh crore. Their original cost was Rs 35.89 lakh crore.

Cumulative expenditure on these projects has reached Rs 19.93 lakh crore. This accounts for 48.02 per cent of the total revised project cost.

The transport and logistics sector leads with 1,428 projects worth Rs 22.66 lakh crore. It represents 55 per cent of the total revised cost across all sectors.

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways accounts for 1,120 projects at 58 per cent of the total count. Its share of revised cost is Rs 10.61 lakh crore.

The Ministry of Railways implements 244 projects with a revised cost of Rs 8.37 lakh crore. This represents 20 per cent of the total project cost.

Around 777 projects, or 40 per cent, have achieved over 80 per cent physical progress. Another 261 projects have crossed 80 per cent financial completion.

During the month, 25 projects were commissioned across various ministries and sectors. The data indicates steady implementation progress across all central sector infrastructure projects.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TOTAL COST OVERRUN REPORTED BY MOSPI FOR INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS IN APRIL 2026?
THE CUMULATIVE COST OVERRUN FOR CENTRAL SECTOR INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS WORTH RS 150 CRORE AND ABOVE IS RS 5.65 LAKH CRORE AS PER THE APRIL 2026 FLASH REPORT.
HOW MANY PROJECTS ARE BEING MONITORED BY MOSPI?
MOSPI IS MONITORING 1,941 ONGOING INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS ACROSS 17 CENTRAL MINISTRIES AND DEPARTMENTS THROUGH ITS PAIMANA PORTAL.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL REVISED COST OF THESE PROJECTS?
THE TOTAL REVISED COST OF THE 1,941 PROJECTS STANDS AT RS 41.50 LAKH CRORE, COMPARED TO THEIR ORIGINAL COST OF RS 35.89 LAKH CRORE.
WHICH SECTOR ACCOUNTS FOR THE HIGHEST SHARE OF PROJECT COST?
THE TRANSPORT AND LOGISTICS SECTOR ACCOUNTS FOR THE HIGHEST SHARE WITH 1,428 PROJECTS AND A REVISED COST OF RS 22.66 LAKH CRORE, REPRESENTING 55 PER CENT OF THE TOTAL.
WHAT PERCENTAGE OF EXPENDITURE HAS BEEN INCURRED SO FAR?
CUMULATIVE EXPENDITURE STANDS AT RS 19.93 LAKH CRORE, ACCOUNTING FOR APPROXIMATELY 48.02 PER CENT OF THE TOTAL REVISED PROJECT COST.
