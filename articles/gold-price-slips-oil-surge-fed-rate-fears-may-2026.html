Gold Price Slips to $4,525 as Oil Surge and Fed Rate Fears Weigh on Metal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gold spot price drops to $4,525.70 per troy ounce on May 22, 2026, falling 0.21% for the day and marking a second consecutive weekly decline
  • 02/Escalating oil prices with Brent above $101 and WTI near $97, driven by Strait of Hormuz disruptions, are stoking inflation and rate hike fears
  • 03/Gold remains up 34.7% year-over-year despite the pullback, with investors watching the June 2026 Fed meeting for directional cues

Gold prices slipped to $4,525.70 per troy ounce on May 22, 2026. The metal fell 0.21% in the session, extending losses into a second straight week.

The 24K gold rate stood at $145.52 per gram. Prices have now retreated 3.71% over the past month from highs above $4,700 per ounce.

Brent crude futures traded around $101.20 per barrel. WTI crude held near $96.66 as the Strait of Hormuz remains largely blocked since February 2026.

The Hormuz chokepoint handles roughly 20% of global oil and LNG traffic. Sustained disruption has pushed global inventories to their tightest level in eight years.

Rising oil costs are fueling inflation expectations across major economies. Markets now fear the U.S. Federal Reserve may hold rates higher for longer to contain price pressures.

The Fed funds rate currently sits at 3.75%. Futures markets are pricing no rate cuts through the remainder of 2026 given the energy-driven inflation backdrop.

Despite the near-term pullback, gold is still up 34.7% compared to a year ago. The metal hit an all-time high of $5,608.35 per ounce in January 2026.

Analysts see immediate support for gold near $4,400 per ounce. Resistance remains at the psychological $4,700 level, with the metal trading well above its 200-day moving average.
