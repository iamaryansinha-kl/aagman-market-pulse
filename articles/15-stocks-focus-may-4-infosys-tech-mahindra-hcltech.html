15 Stocks in Focus on May 4: Infosys, Tech Mahindra, HCLTech Lead

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tech Mahindra acquired Canada-based Avant Tech Solutions; Reliance Retail bought Priyanka Chopra's Anomaly haircare brand on May 4
  • 02/Vodafone Idea's AGR dues cut to ₹64,046 crore; NCC bagged orders worth ₹1,703 crore; Siemens secured ₹1,825 crore order
  • 03/Infosys appointed Nitin Paranjpe as Vice Chairman; HCLTech extended AI partnership with MetLife Stadium

India Inc saw a flurry of corporate activity on May 4, 2026. Fifteen stocks across IT, telecom, retail, and infrastructure sectors attracted investor attention.

HCLTech (NSE: HCLTECH) deepened its global AI partnership with MetLife Stadium. The company is now the Official AI Partner of the stadium and NFL teams New York Giants and New York Jets.

Tech Mahindra (NSE: TECHM) acquired Canada-based Avant Tech Solutions. The deal expands Tech Mahindra's payments modernisation and wealth management platform capabilities.

Reliance Retail, a subsidiary of Reliance Industries (NSE: RELIANCE), acquired Anomaly. The haircare brand was founded by Priyanka Chopra Jonas. Reliance took ownership of all trademarks, assets, and digital properties.

NCC Limited (NSE: NCC) bagged four orders worth ₹1,703.27 crore in April. The orders span buildings (₹929.96 crore), electrical (₹603.41 crore), and transportation (₹169.90 crore) divisions.

Siemens (NSE: SIEMENS) received a new order worth over ₹1,825 crore. The work allocation from a group company covers manufacturing bogies, traction motors and gearboxes.

Vodafone Idea (NSE: IDEA) received relief on AGR dues. The Department of Telecommunications cut payable dues to ₹64,046 crore from an earlier estimate of ₹87,695 crore as of December 31, 2025.

Infosys (NSE: INFY) appointed Nitin Paranjpe as non-executive Vice Chairman. The board approved the appointment with immediate effect.

WHAT ARE THE MAJOR ORDER WINS REPORTED?

WHAT HAPPENED WITH VODAFONE IDEA'S AGR DUES?
THE DEPARTMENT OF TELECOMMUNICATIONS CUT VODAFONE IDEA'S PAYABLE AGR DUES TO RS 64,046 CRORE AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2025. THE EARLIER ESTIMATE WAS RS 87,695 CRORE, REPRESENTING A REDUCTION FOLLOWING A REASSESSMENT.

INFOSYS APPOINTED NITIN PARANJPE AS NON-EXECUTIVE VICE CHAIRMAN EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. SANOFI INDIA APPOINTED RAHUL BHATNAGAR AS CHAIRMAN EFFECTIVE FROM APRIL 30, 2026.
