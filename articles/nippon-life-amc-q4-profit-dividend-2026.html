Nippon Life India AMC Q4 Profit Up 29% to ₹385 Crore, Declares ₹12.50 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nippon Life India AMC Q4 FY26 net profit jumps 29% YoY to ₹385 crore on higher AUM and fee income
  • 02/Mutual fund QAAUM surges 30% to ₹7.25 lakh crore; ETF assets up 57% to ₹2.42 lakh crore
  • 03/Board declares ₹12.50 final dividend; record date June 26; total FY26 payout at ₹21.50 per share

Nippon Life India Asset Management Ltd (NSE: NAM-INDIA) reported a 29% year-on-year rise in net profit for Q4 FY26. Profit after tax stood at ₹385 crore against ₹299 crore in the year-ago quarter.

The board declared a final dividend of ₹12.50 per equity share for FY26. The record date is fixed at June 26, 2026, with payment scheduled for July 10.

Including the interim dividend of ₹9 already paid, the total FY26 payout amounts to ₹21.50 per share. This translates to a payout ratio of approximately 91.5% of profit after tax.

Total income for the quarter stood at ₹705 crore. Revenue from operations increased to ₹739 crore compared to ₹567 crore in Q4 FY25.

The company's mutual fund quarterly average assets under management rose 30% year-on-year to ₹7.25 lakh crore. Total closing AUM stood at ₹7.73 lakh crore, up 18%.

Equity mutual fund QAAUM reached ₹3.30 lakh crore, up 25%. ETF QAAUM surged 57% to ₹2.42 lakh crore.

Systematic investment flows remained robust with quarterly SIP inflows at ₹1.09 lakh crore, up 12% year-on-year. Retail AUM grew 20% to ₹1.96 lakh crore, while HNI assets jumped 45% to ₹2.41 lakh crore.

Digital transactions climbed 44% year-on-year to 50.4 lakh. Managing Director and CEO Sundeep Sikka said the fund house gained market share across categories in FY26. For the full year, net profit rose 20% to ₹1,497.8 crore.
