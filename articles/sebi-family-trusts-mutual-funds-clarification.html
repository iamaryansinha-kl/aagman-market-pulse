SEBI Clarifies Family Trusts Cannot Sponsor Mutual Funds Under New 2026 Rules

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SEBI issued interpretive letter on April 20, 2026, stating family trusts cannot sponsor mutual funds as they are not body corporates under Regulation 2(1)(xx)
  • 02/Clarification came in response to First Water Capital Advisory LLP's query under new MF Regulations 2026 effective April 1
  • 03/SEBI declined to answer a related query on Route-2 net worth composition citing policy concerns under the Informal Guidance Scheme

SEBI has clarified that family trusts cannot act as sponsors of mutual funds. The regulator issued an interpretive letter on April 20, 2026, under the Informal Guidance Scheme, 2025.

The clarification was sought by First Water Capital Advisory LLP. The Mumbai-based firm asked whether a family trust could qualify as a sponsor under the new SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026.

SEBI cited Regulation 2(1)(xx), which defines a sponsor as a body corporate. Since a family trust is not a body corporate, it cannot establish a mutual fund or mutual fund lite.

The MF Regulations 2026 came into force on April 1, 2026. They replaced the 1996 regulations and introduced simplified eligibility criteria for sponsors.

The applicant also asked whether Route-2 net worth of ₹150 crore could include ₹100 crore in redeemable preference shares. SEBI declined to respond, citing policy concerns under paragraph II(h) of the IG Scheme 2025.

Route 2 requires an AMC to maintain ₹100 crore net worth until achieving five consecutive years of profit. The threshold then drops to ₹50 crore.

The interpretive letter carries Issue No. 1/9760/2026. It was signed by SEBI's Nodal Co-ordination Cell and approved by the competent authority.

SEBI noted the guidance is limited to specific facts and does not constitute a binding decision. Different conditions could produce different outcomes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
CAN A FAMILY TRUST SPONSOR A MUTUAL FUND IN INDIA?
NO. SEBI HAS CLARIFIED THAT UNDER REGULATION 2(1)(XX) OF THE MF REGULATIONS 2026, A SPONSOR MUST BE A BODY CORPORATE. A FAMILY TRUST DOES NOT QUALIFY AS A BODY CORPORATE AND THEREFORE CANNOT ACT AS A SPONSOR OF A MUTUAL FUND.
WHICH ENTITY SOUGHT THIS CLARIFICATION FROM SEBI?
FIRST WATER CAPITAL ADVISORY LLP SOUGHT THE CLARIFICATION. THE QUERY WAS REPRESENTED BY MR. AMISH JHANGIANI AND SUBMITTED ON FEBRUARY 17, 2026.
WHEN DID SEBI ISSUE THIS INTERPRETIVE LETTER?
SEBI ISSUED THE INTERPRETIVE LETTER ON APRIL 20, 2026, BEARING ISSUE NO. 1/9760/2026 UNDER THE INFORMAL GUIDANCE SCHEME, 2025.
WHAT WAS SEBI'S RESPONSE TO THE NET WORTH COMPOSITION QUERY?
SEBI DECLINED TO RESPOND TO THE QUERY ON WHETHER ROUTE-2 NET WORTH OF ₹150 CRORE COULD INCLUDE ₹100 CRORE AS REDEEMABLE PREFERENCE SHARES. THE REGULATOR CITED POLICY CONCERNS UNDER PARAGRAPH II(H) OF THE IG SCHEME 2025.
ARE THE NEW MF REGULATIONS 2026 ALREADY IN EFFECT?
YES. THE SEBI (MUTUAL FUNDS) REGULATIONS, 2026 CAME INTO FORCE ON APRIL 1, 2026, REPLACING THE 1996 REGULATIONS. THE GUIDANCE IS BASED ON THESE NEW REGULATIONS AND DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A BINDING DECISION.
