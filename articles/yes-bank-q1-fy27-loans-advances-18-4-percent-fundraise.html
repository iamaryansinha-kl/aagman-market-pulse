Yes Bank Q1 Loans Surge 18.4% to ₹2.85 Lakh Crore; Board Approves ₹16,000 Cr Fundraise

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Yes Bank reports loans and advances of ₹2.85 lakh crore for Q1 FY27, up 18.4% YoY from ₹2.41 lakh crore
  • 02/Deposits increased 14% YoY to ₹3.15 lakh crore; CASA deposits rose 14.3% to ₹1.03 lakh crore
  • 03/Board approved raising up to ₹16,000 crore via ₹7,500 crore equity and ₹8,500 crore debt

Yes Bank has released its Q1 FY27 business update. Loans and advances stood at ₹2.85 lakh crore, marking an 18.4% year-on-year rise from ₹2.41 lakh crore in Q1 FY26.

Sequentially, loans rose 4% from ₹2.73 lakh crore in Q4 FY26. The bank's deposits increased more than 14% YoY to ₹3.15 lakh crore from ₹2.76 lakh crore a year earlier.

However, deposits declined more than 1% quarter-on-quarter from ₹3.19 lakh crore in Q4 FY26. CASA deposits rose 14.3% YoY to ₹1.03 lakh crore but fell 7.8% QoQ from ₹1.12 lakh crore.

The CASA ratio, including certificates of deposit, stood at 32.7%. This compares with 35.1% in the previous quarter and 32.8% a year earlier. Certificates of deposit declined to ₹6,604 crore from ₹6,831 crore.

The credit-to-deposit ratio improved to 90.5% as of 30 June 2026. This was up from 85.7% at the end of March and 87.4% a year earlier.

The bank's average quarterly Liquidity Coverage Ratio on a consolidated basis stood at 138.5%. This compares with 119.0% in Q4 FY26 and 135.8% in Q1 FY26.

Yes Bank said these figures are provisional and released ahead of its financial results. Actual earnings will be announced after the audit.

This week, the bank's board approved a proposal to raise up to ₹16,000 crore. The plan includes up to ₹7,500 crore through eligible equity securities and up to ₹8,500 crore through eligible debt securities.

The equity fundraising, together with any dilution from convertible debt, will not exceed 10% aggregate dilution of existing share capital. The proposals require shareholder and regulatory approvals.

Yes Bank shares closed at ₹24.39 on Friday. The stock has gained 13% in 2026 and 21% over the past year.
