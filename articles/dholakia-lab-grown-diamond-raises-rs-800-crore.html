Dholakia Lab Grown Diamond Raises Rs 800 Crore Led by Abakkus, ICICI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dholakia Lab Grown Diamond raises over Rs 800 crore in a funding round led by Abakkus PE and ICICI Ventures' IAF5 fund on April 29, 2026
  • 02/The Surat-based company serves 100+ retailers across 25 countries and plans to expand into precision diamonds for defence and semiconductors
  • 03/Proceeds will scale production, bolster working capital, and build a pan-India retail network

Dholakia Lab Grown Diamond Pvt Ltd (DLGD) has raised over Rs 800 crore. The funding round was led by Abakkus PE and ICICI Ventures' IAF5 fund. Investor Amal Parikh also participated.

The Surat-based company announced the fundraise on April 29, 2026. It is the jewellery arm of the Hari Krishna Group.

Proceeds will scale diamond production, bolster working capital, and build a pan-India retail network. DLGD is fully vertically integrated across Surat, Mumbai, and New Jersey.

DLGD plans to expand into precision single-crystal diamonds for defence, semiconductor, and quantum applications. This spans optical and thermal uses beyond jewellery.

The company serves over 100 retailers and brands across 25 countries. It introduced lab-grown diamonds to major US retailers in 2018.

The global lab-grown diamond market is valued at $33.54 billion in 2026. It is projected to reach $91.85 billion by 2034 at a 13.42% CAGR.

"The world's growing confidence in lab-grown diamonds" drove the investment, said Hasu Dholakia, CEO of DLGD. Global retailers and luxury brands are increasing commitments to the category.

Ankit Aggarwal of Abakkus PE said consumer attitudes had "reached a tipping point". Gagandeep S Chhina of ICICI Venture cited DLGD's value chain control as a key differentiator.
