Adani Group Stocks Rise 3.5% as US DOJ Drops All Charges Against Gautam Adani

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/US Department of Justice drops all criminal charges against Gautam Adani and Sagar Adani, ending a 19-month legal battle
  • 02/Adani Group stocks surge up to 3.5%; Adani Enterprises, Adani Green, Adani Power among gainers
  • 03/Adani Enterprises separately agrees to $275 million settlement with US Treasury for sanctions violations without admitting guilt

The US Department of Justice has dropped all criminal charges against Gautam Adani and his nephew Sagar Adani. The court ordered the indictment dismissed "with prejudice" on May 19, 2026.

Shares of Adani Group companies surged up to 3.5% in early trade. Adani Enterprises, Adani Green, Adani Power, Adani Ports, and Adani Total Gas were among the notable gainers.

The DOJ filed a motion seeking dismissal, stating it would not devote further resources to the criminal charges. The decision ends a 19-month legal overhang on the conglomerate.

The SEC and DOJ had alleged a $265 million bribery scheme linked to Indian officials for solar power contracts. Prosecutors claimed the arrangement was concealed from US investors and lenders.

Separately, Adani Enterprises agreed to a $275 million settlement with the US Treasury for apparent OFAC sanctions violations against Iran. The company said this does not constitute an admission of guilt.

Adani had also agreed to pay $18 million in a separate US civil court case connected to corruption allegations. The combined resolutions remove significant uncertainty over the group's expansion plans.

The legal team, led by Trump personal lawyer Robert J. Giuffra Jr., reportedly proposed $10 billion in US investments and 15,000 jobs. This followed meetings at DOJ headquarters last month.

The group had faced fundraising challenges and contract losses during the 19-month period. The dismissal lifts a major barrier to the conglomerate's international capital-raising activities.
