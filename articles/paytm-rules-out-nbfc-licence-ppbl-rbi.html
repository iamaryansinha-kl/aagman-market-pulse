Paytm Rules Out NBFC Licence After RBI Cancels PPBL Banking Licence

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/One97 Communications rules out NBFC licence plans during Q4 FY26 earnings call on May 7, preferring partnership-based lending model
  • 02/RBI cancelled Paytm Payments Bank licence on April 24, 2026; PPBL held ₹1,395 crore in deposits and posted ₹94.64 crore loss in FY25
  • 03/Paytm Q4 profit at ₹183 crore versus ₹545 crore loss YoY; FY26 revenue grew 22.2% to ₹8,437 crore

One97 Communications Ltd (NSE: PAYTM) has ruled out applying for a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) licence. Paytm President and Group CFO Madhur Deora announced the decision during the company's Q4 FY26 earnings call on May 7, 2026.

"We're not super excited about going for an NBFC licence," Deora said. He explained Paytm prefers a partnership model where it handles distribution, technology, and collections while lending partners manage capital and risk.

The announcement comes weeks after the Reserve Bank of India cancelled the banking licence of Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL) on April 24, 2026. RBI acted under Section 22(4) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

RBI cited multiple grounds for cancellation. The regulator said PPBL's affairs were conducted in a manner detrimental to depositors and that management was prejudicial to public interest. PPBL had ₹1,395 crore in customer deposits and a loss of ₹94.64 crore as of March 2025.

Paytm reported a consolidated profit of ₹183 crore in Q4 FY26. This marks a turnaround from a loss of ₹545 crore in the same quarter a year ago.

Revenue from operations grew 18.4 per cent to ₹2,264 crore in Q4 FY26 from ₹1,912 crore in Q4 FY25. For the full year FY26, profit stood at ₹552 crore against a loss of ₹663 crore in FY25.

Annual revenue rose 22.2 per cent to ₹8,437 crore in FY26 from ₹6,900 crore in FY25. The company said it has no exposure to PPBL after fully impairing the investment as of March 31, 2024.

One97 Communications shares closed at ₹1,088 on the BSE on May 6, 2026. The stock had recovered from an initial 8.38 per cent drop after the RBI order on PPBL.

Jefferies maintains a "Buy" rating with a price target of ₹1,350. Bernstein sees 31 per cent upside potential. Analysts view the PPBL cancellation as having limited impact on Paytm's core payments and lending distribution business.
