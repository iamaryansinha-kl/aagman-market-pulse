Sensex Rises 390 Pts to 77,278; Nifty Up 110 Pts to 24,105

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex opened 390 points higher at 77,278.65 and Nifty 50 gained 110 points to 24,105.15 in early trade on April 29, 2026
  • 02/Maruti Suzuki led Nifty gainers rising 3.31% to ₹13,319; crude oil futures declined on news of UAE's exit from OPEC effective May 1
  • 03/Analysts eye 23,900-24,000 as immediate support and 24,200-24,300 as resistance; traders await a breakout for directional momentum

Sensex opened 390 points higher at 77,278.65 on April 29, 2026. The Nifty 50 gained 110 points to 24,105.15 in early morning trade.

Maruti Suzuki led the Nifty gainers, rising 3.31% to ₹13,319 from a previous close of ₹12,892. Trading volume stood at 47,606 shares worth ₹6,291.33 lakh.

The rebound follows a weak session on April 28. The Sensex had dropped 416 points to 76,886 and the Nifty shed 97 points to 23,995.

Foreign portfolio investors sold ₹2,103.74 crore on April 28 while domestic institutional investors bought ₹1,712.01 crore. The rupee continues to face pressure.

Crude oil futures declined after the UAE announced its exit from OPEC effective May 1. July Brent oil futures were at $104.02, down 0.36%.

IIP growth eased to 4.8% in Q4 FY2026 from 5.3% in Q3 FY2026. The slowdown was driven by weaker manufacturing output, even as electricity and mining improved.

"Indian markets are poised to open on a constructive note," said Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder of Livelong Wealth. Gift Nifty indicated early resilience around 24,090.

Gaurav Udani, Founder of ThinCredBlu Securities, said 23,900-24,000 remains immediate support for Nifty. Resistance stays at 24,200-24,300 and a breakout is needed for momentum.
