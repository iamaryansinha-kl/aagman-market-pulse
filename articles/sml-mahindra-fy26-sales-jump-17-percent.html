SML Mahindra FY26 Sales Jump 17% to 16,632 Units; Cargo Up 28%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/SML Mahindra sold 16,632 commercial vehicles in FY26, a 17% increase over 14,215 units sold in FY25
  • 02/Cargo vehicles drove growth with a 28% surge to 5,412 units, while passenger vehicles rose 12% to 11,220 units
  • 03/March 2026 sales stood at 2,457 units, up 6% YoY; management cautions on fuel cost volatility and supply chain risks ahead

SML Mahindra Limited reported FY26 commercial vehicle sales of 16,632 units, marking a 17% year-on-year jump from 14,215 units in FY25. The numbers cover sales for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026.

The cargo vehicle segment was the standout performer. Sales surged 28% to 5,412 units in FY26, up from 4,232 units in FY25. This reflects strong demand from logistics and e-commerce sectors.

Passenger vehicles also posted healthy growth, rising 12% to 11,220 units from 9,983 units in the previous fiscal year. Both segments contributed to the double-digit annual expansion.

For March 2026 alone, total sales came in at 2,457 units, up 6% from 2,318 units in March 2025. Cargo vehicle sales for the month rose 2% to 606 units, while passenger vehicles climbed 8% to 1,851 units.

Vinod Sahay, Executive Chairman — SML and President — Aerospace, Defence, Trucks, Buses & CE, M&M, said, "Despite macroeconomic and geopolitical challenges, the commercial vehicle sector has demonstrated resilience. While replacement demand and government-led projects are expected to support volumes in FY27, volatility in fuel costs and supply chain constraints could temper sentiment."

The FY26 performance positions SML Mahindra to capture emerging opportunities in India's light and medium-duty commercial vehicle market. However, intense competition from Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, and Eicher Motors remains a key risk.

Investors will watch FY27 volume trends and any commentary on new product launches, BS-VI Phase 2 readiness, and infrastructure-driven demand.
