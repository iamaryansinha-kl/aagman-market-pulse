South Korea KOSPI Crashes 8.18%: Chipmakers Trigger Trading Halt

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/South Korea's KOSPI index crashed 8.18% to 8,199.81 on June 26, 2026, triggering a 20-minute trading halt after circuit breakers were activated for the second time this week
  • 02/Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, which account for over half of KOSPI's market cap, each fell more than 9% on renewed fears about AI spending and US tech weakness
  • 03/The index has lost 9.4% this week, its worst weekly drop since early March, though it remains up approximately 95% year-to-date after a 76% rally in 2025

South Korea's benchmark KOSPI index plummeted 8.18% to close at 8,199.81 on June 26, 2026. Trading was suspended for 20 minutes after circuit breakers kicked in.

This marked the second circuit breaker activation this week. It was also the fifth such instance this year and only the 11th in Korean stock market history.

The selloff was driven by weakness in US technology shares overnight. Investors reassessed artificial intelligence spending levels despite strong demand signals from the sector.

Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix led the rout, each falling more than 9%. The two chipmakers together account for over half of the KOSPI's total market capitalisation.

Losses spread beyond semiconductors. LG Energy Solution dropped over 7%, while automakers Hyundai Motor and Kia also declined sharply during the session.

Market breadth was deeply negative, with 848 stocks declining against just 61 advancing issues. Foreign institutional investors dumped shares worth approximately 3 trillion won.

Despite the brutal week, the KOSPI remains up about 95% in 2026. The index posted a 76% gain in 2025, fueled by the AI and semiconductor boom.

Samsung Group is expected to unveil a major domestic investment plan next week. The plan reportedly involves up to 1,000 trillion won over the next decade.
