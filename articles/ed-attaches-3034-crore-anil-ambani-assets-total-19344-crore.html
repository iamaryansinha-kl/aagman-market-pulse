ED Attaches ₹3,034 Cr Anil Ambani Assets, Total Now ₹19,344 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/ED attaches fresh ₹3,034 crore assets of Reliance Anil Ambani Group companies, taking total to ₹19,344 crore
  • 02/Assets include Mumbai flat, Khandala farmhouse, Sanand land, and 7.71 crore R-Infra shares
  • 03/Probe covers bank fraud and diversion of ₹40,185 crore in outstanding loans across group companies

The Enforcement Directorate has attached fresh assets worth ₹3,034 crore in its money laundering probe against Reliance Anil Ambani Group companies. The provisional order was issued on April 28, 2026, under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.

The attached assets include a flat in Mumbai, a farmhouse in Khandala, and land parcels in Sanand near Ahmedabad. They also cover 7.71 crore shares of Reliance Infrastructure Ltd.

The properties belong to Reliance Communications and Reliance Infrastructure. The ED is investigating the group on charges of bank fraud and diversion of public funds.

With this action, total attachments against the Reliance Anil Ambani Group now stand at ₹19,344 crore. Earlier actions included a ₹3,084 crore attachment in October 2025 and the Pali Hill 'Abode' seizure in February 2026.

CBI registered an FIR on August 21, 2025, against RCOM and Anil Ambani. SBI alleges wrongful loss of ₹2,929.05 crore from loans that turned non-performing.

RCOM and group companies availed loans totalling ₹40,185 crore from domestic and foreign lenders. Nine banks have declared the loan accounts as fraudulent.

ED detected diversion of over ₹13,600 crore for evergreening loans and ₹12,600 crore to connected parties. Some funds were also siphoned offshore through foreign remittances.

The probe agency indicated that further attachments may follow. The investigation covers transactions across 50 companies and 355 individuals linked to the group.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT ASSETS DID THE ED ATTACH IN THE LATEST ACTION AGAINST ANIL AMBANI'S GROUP?
THE ENFORCEMENT DIRECTORATE ATTACHED A FLAT IN MUMBAI, A FARMHOUSE IN KHANDALA, LAND PARCELS IN SANAND (AHMEDABAD), AND 7.71 CRORE SHARES OF RELIANCE INFRASTRUCTURE LTD. THE TOTAL VALUE OF THESE FRESH ATTACHMENTS IS ₹3,034 CRORE.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL VALUE OF ALL ASSETS ATTACHED BY ED AGAINST THE RELIANCE ANIL AMBANI GROUP?
THE TOTAL VALUE OF ATTACHED PROPERTIES ACROSS ALL CASES AGAINST THE RELIANCE ANIL AMBANI GROUP NOW STANDS AT ₹19,344 CRORE. THIS INCLUDES THE LATEST ₹3,034 CRORE ATTACHMENT AND EARLIER SEIZURES INCLUDING THE ₹3,084 CRORE ACTION IN OCTOBER 2025.
UNDER WHICH LAW DID THE ED ISSUE THE ATTACHMENT ORDER?
THE CENTRAL AGENCY ISSUED A PROVISIONAL ATTACHMENT ORDER UNDER SECTION 5(1) OF THE PREVENTION OF MONEY LAUNDERING ACT (PMLA), 2002. THE ORDER WAS ISSUED ON APRIL 28, 2026.
WHAT ARE THE ALLEGATIONS AGAINST THE RELIANCE ANIL AMBANI GROUP?
THE ED IS INVESTIGATING THE GROUP FOR BANK FRAUD AND DIVERSION OF PUBLIC FUNDS. RCOM AND GROUP COMPANIES AVAILED LOANS OF ₹40,185 CRORE, OF WHICH NINE BANKS DECLARED ACCOUNTS AS FRAUD. ED DETECTED DIVERSION OF OVER ₹13,600 CRORE FOR EVERGREENING AND ₹12,600 CRORE TO CONNECTED PARTIES.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT WITH THE ATTACHED ASSETS?
THE PMLA ADJUDICATING AUTHORITY MUST CONFIRM THE ATTACHMENT ORDER WITHIN 180 DAYS. IF CONFIRMED, THE ED CAN CONFISCATE THE PROPERTIES AND INITIATE PROCEEDINGS FOR RESTITUTION TO BANKS AND OTHER RIGHTFUL CLAIMANTS. FURTHER ATTACHMENTS MAY FOLLOW AS THE PROBE CONTINUES.
