ECLGS 5.0 Crosses 4.11 Lakh Guarantees, Extends Rs 1.55 Lakh Crore Support to MSMEs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

The government's flagship MSME credit scheme has reached a new milestone.

ECLGS 5.0 has crossed 4.11 lakh guarantees, extending Rs 1.55 lakh crore in credit support to micro, small and medium enterprises. The numbers reflect the scheme's continued importance as a credit lifeline.

The Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme was launched during the pandemic to provide collateral-free loans to businesses facing cash flow stress. Under ECLGS 5.0, the government guarantees up to 75% of the loan amount, reducing risk for banks and making them more willing to lend.

MSMEs account for roughly 30% of India's GDP and employ over 110 million people. Access to credit has historically been a challenge for these businesses, which often lack the collateral and credit history that banks require.

The Rs 1.55 lakh crore disbursed under ECLGS 5.0 has helped businesses meet working capital needs, pay salaries, and maintain operations. The scheme has been particularly valuable for sectors like hospitality, retail, and manufacturing that were hit hardest by the pandemic.

The government has extended the ECLGS framework multiple times since its initial launch. Each iteration has refined the eligibility criteria and expanded the scope to cover more businesses and larger loan amounts.
