Maruti Suzuki Market Share Hits 42% in April FY27 on Record Sales

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Maruti Suzuki India captures 42% market share in April FY27, up nearly 3 percentage points from 39% in FY26
  • 02/Company posts record domestic sales of 1,91,122 units in April, surpassing previous high of 1,82,165 in December 2025
  • 03/SUV sales hit all-time high of 55,065 units with 141.6% YoY growth; passenger car sales at 96,725 units

Maruti Suzuki India Ltd (NSE: MARUTI) started fiscal 2026-27 with a 42 per cent market share. The figure rose from 39 per cent in the previous fiscal year.

The company posted record domestic sales of 1,91,122 units in April 2026. This surpassed the previous high of 1,82,165 units set in December 2025.

Industry estimates show the carmaker gained nearly 3 percentage points in market share during the month. The broader passenger vehicle market sold an estimated 4.5 lakh units, up 27 per cent from 3.54 lakh in April 2025.

Passenger car sales stood at 96,725 units in April 2026 against 68,244 units in April 2025. Small cars including Alto, S-Presso, Celerio and WagonR grew 74.4 per cent year-on-year.

SUV sales hit an all-time high of 55,065 units in April. The segment grew 141.6 per cent over the same month last year.

"We started the new fiscal with a bang in April with record sales and also gained market share," said Partho Banerjee, Senior Executive Officer, Marketing & Sales, Maruti Suzuki India.

Banerjee noted the company is "almost close to the pole position" in SUVs. The growth has been led by passenger cars with SUVs making a significant contribution.

The strong start to FY27 comes amid a recovering passenger vehicle market. Carmakers sold an estimated 4.5 lakh units in April, up 27 per cent year-on-year.
