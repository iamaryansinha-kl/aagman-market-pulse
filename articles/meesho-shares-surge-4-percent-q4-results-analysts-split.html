Meesho Shares Surge 4% on Strong Q4 Results; Analysts Split on Valuation

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Meesho shares surged over 4% to ₹204.20 after Q4 FY26 results showed NMV up 43% YoY to ₹11,371 crore
  • 02/Annual transacting users grew 33% to 26.4 crore; contribution margin recovered to 4% of NMV from 2.3% in Q3
  • 03/Analysts split on valuation: Choice Institutional ADD at ₹210, JM Financial REDUCE at ₹180, flagging 46x FY29 EBITDA multiple

Shares of Meesho Limited opened sharply higher on Thursday, rising over 4 per cent to trade at ₹204.20 on the NSE by 10:31 am. The stock touched an intraday high of ₹211.34 amid heavy volume of over 5.16 crore shares worth ₹1,063 crore.

Sell-side pressure remained elevated with 61.79 per cent of orders on the sell side against 38.21 per cent on the buy side. The stock, listed in December 2025 at an IPO price of ₹111, has significantly outperformed the broader market.

Meesho is up 37.55 per cent over the past month and 12.15 per cent year-to-date, compared to the Nifty 500's decline of 2.98 per cent. Its 52-week high stands at ₹254.40, while the low of ₹125.56 was hit in March 2026.

The Q4 FY26 results announced May 6 showed net merchandise value rising 43 per cent year-on-year to ₹11,371 crore. Contribution margin recovered to 4 per cent of NMV from 2.3 per cent in the previous quarter.

For the full year FY26, annual transacting users grew 33 per cent to 26.4 crore. The e-commerce platform continues to expand its seller and buyer base across tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

Analyst reaction has been mixed. Choice Institutional Equities maintained an ADD rating with a target price of ₹210, citing logistics recovery and steady user growth as positives. However, the brokerage noted near-term upside appears largely priced in.

Swapnil Potdukhe at JM Financial maintained a REDUCE rating with a target of ₹180. He argued that while Q4 results were strong, the stock trades at approximately 46x FY29 estimated adjusted EBITDA, among the highest multiples in internet coverage.

Potdukhe also flagged that NMV growth could moderate to around 34 per cent YoY in FY27 due to a tough base. Choice expects EBITDA breakeven by the second half of FY28, a key milestone for the loss-making e-commerce firm.
