Nifty Surges 1.3% to 24,032 on US-Iran Hopes, Crude Below $100

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nifty 50 surged 1.3% or 312.40 points to 24,031.70, reclaiming the 24,000 mark after about two weeks
  • 02/Brent crude dropped over 5% below $98 on hopes of US-Iran agreement to reopen Strait of Hormuz
  • 03/Banking stocks led the rally with PSU banks gaining 2.9%; India VIX fell 6.7% to 16.7 signaling easing risk

The NSE Nifty 50 advanced 1.3%, or 312.40 points, to close at 24,031.70 on Monday. The index reclaimed the 24,000 level after about two weeks.

The S&P BSE Sensex climbed 1.4%, or 1,073.61 points, to 76,488.96. This marked the Nifty's strongest single-day gains since April 1.

Sentiment was lifted by reports that the US and Iran were nearing an agreement to ease tensions. Brent crude declined more than 5% to around $98 a barrel.

"With every day of delayed truce, there is a chance that the inflation can be higher, so the earlier we have a solution, the better," said George Thomas, equity fund manager at Quantum AMC.

The banking sector led the broad-based rally. Bank Nifty rose 2.3%, PSU banks gained 2.9%, and private banks added 2.1%.

The rupee climbed to 95.23 per dollar, its highest in over two weeks. The 10-year bond yield fell to 7.025% from 7.088%.

Volatility eased with the India VIX declining 6.7% to 16.7. "Nifty witnessed a decisive breakout and closed strong driven by short covering," said Rajesh Palviya, Head of Research, Axis Securities.

Asian markets rallied in tandem, with Taiwan gaining 3.3%, Japan 2.9%, and China 1%. Palviya said further gains toward 24,800 could materialise with positive triggers.
