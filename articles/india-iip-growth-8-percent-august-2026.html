India's Factory Output Growth Rises to 8% in August from 7.4% in July

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Index of Industrial Production (IIP) grew 8% in August 2026, accelerating from 7.4% growth in July
  • 02/The pickup signals sustained momentum in manufacturing and capital goods output ahead of the festive season
  • 03/Strong industrial growth adds to the case for the RBI to keep monetary policy tight at its review next week

India's industrial production growth rose to 8% in August 2026. The figure was released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation on Monday.

The August reading compares with 7.4% growth recorded in July 2026. It marks one of the strongest monthly prints in recent quarters.

The acceleration points to sustained momentum in manufacturing. Capital goods and consumer durables output have been key drivers of the recent uptrend.

The data lands ahead of the festive season, when factory output typically picks up further. It also comes a week before the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy review.

Strong industrial growth strengthens the case for the central bank to stay hawkish. A majority of respondents in a Reuters poll expect the RBI to hike its policy rate next week.

Robust IIP numbers, combined with sticky inflation, suggest the economy is running hot. That reduces the room for rate cuts in the near term.

Investors will watch the September IIP print for confirmation of the trend. The next release is due at the end of October.
