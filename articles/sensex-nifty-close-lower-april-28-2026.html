Sensex Nifty Close Lower April 28: Sensex Drops 417 Pts, Nifty Below 24,000

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex settled 416.72 pts or 0.54% lower at 76,886.91; Nifty 50 fell 97 pts or 0.4% to 23,995.70 on April 28
  • 02/Brent crude held above $102 on Iran-Hormuz tensions; rupee weakened 24 paise to 94.39 against the dollar
  • 03/FIIs have sold ₹44,281 crore through April; Nifty faces resistance at 24,300 with support at 23,800

Indian equity markets closed lower on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. The BSE Sensex shed 416.72 points or 0.54% to settle at 76,886.91.

The NSE Nifty 50 dropped 97 points or 0.4% to end at 23,995.70. The index slipped below the 24,000 mark during the session.

Markets opened cautious as Nifty's April derivative contracts expired. Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and elevated crude oil prices kept investors on edge.

Brent crude traded around $102.59 per barrel. WTI crude was near $97.45 as stalled US-Iran talks over the Strait of Hormuz fueled supply concerns.

Coal India led Nifty gainers, surging over 4% after beating Q4 earnings estimates. ONGC and Adani Enterprises also rallied on the back of strong commodity flows.

State Bank of India fell 1.16% to ₹1,098.90, making it the worst Nifty performer. Axis Bank, Sun Pharma, Eternal Ltd and Bajaj Auto also closed in the red.

Foreign institutional investors remained net sellers, offloading ₹1,151 crore in the cash market on April 27. Domestic institutional investors offset some pressure with ₹4,124 crore in buying.

"FIIs have been net sellers of ₹44,281 crore through April," said Bruce Keith, Cofounder of InvestorAi. "With VIX cooling to 18.4, our platform has moved measurably defensive over the last 30 days."

The rupee depreciated 24 paise to 94.39 against the US dollar. Month-end demand and stubbornly high oil prices added pressure on the currency.

Maruti Suzuki reported a 6.45% decline in Q4 consolidated net profit to ₹3,659 crore. The stock closed 2% lower at ₹12,980 on the NSE.

Nifty technical support is seen at 23,800. Resistance remains near 24,300, a level the index has tested multiple times without a sustained breakout.
