SBI Funds Management IPO to Open on July 14

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

SBI Funds Management is heading to the public markets.

The asset management company's initial public offering will open on July 14, 2026. The IPO is among the most anticipated public issues in India's financial services sector, given the company's position as one of the country's largest fund houses.

SBI Funds Management is a joint venture between State Bank of India and Amundi, a European asset manager. The company manages assets across equity, debt, hybrid, and solution-oriented schemes, with a significant presence in both retail and institutional segments.

The IPO comes at a time when India's mutual fund industry is experiencing unprecedented growth. Assets under management have surged to record levels, driven by systematic investment plans and rising retail participation. SBI Funds Management is well-positioned to capitalise on this trend.

The company has filed its draft red herring prospectus with Sebi. The issue will comprise a fresh issue of shares and an offer for sale by existing shareholders, including SBI and Amundi.

The proceeds from the fresh issue will be used to augment the company's capital base and support future growth. The offer for sale component will provide partial liquidity to existing shareholders.

Market participants expect strong demand for the IPO. The mutual fund sector has attracted significant investor interest, and SBI Funds Management's brand recognition and distribution reach are key strengths.
