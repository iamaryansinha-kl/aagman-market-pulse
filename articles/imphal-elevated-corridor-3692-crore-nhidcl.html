₹3,692-Crore Imphal Elevated Corridor: 25-Km Project to Reshape Manipur Capital

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/MoRTH transfers DPR for ₹3,691.81 crore, 25-km four-lane elevated corridor to NHIDCL for implementation
  • 02/Route runs from Koirengei Junction along NH-02, bifurcating toward Manipur University and Imphal Airport
  • 03/DPR approval targeted before March-April 2027 polls; project first proposed by Manipur PWD in 2020

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has transferred the Detailed Project Report to NHIDCL for implementation. The ₹3,691.81 crore Imphal Elevated Corridor is a 25-km, four-lane elevated road project.

It is the largest urban road infrastructure project ever undertaken in Manipur. The corridor promises to reshape the capital's skyline and redefine mobility across the city.

The route starts at Koirengei Junction along NH-02. It bifurcates near Raj Bhavan Rotary into two arms.

One arm extends toward Manipur University along NH-102. The other proceeds toward Imphal International Airport along NH-02.

An additional two-lane flyover will connect CM Traffic Rotary to the City Convention Centre. This creates a seamless urban transport network with a vertical clearance of 5.5 metres.

NHIDCL Deputy General Manager (Manipur) Rafique Ahmad Choudhury said the DPR is likely to be approved before the March-April 2027 state assembly elections. Multiple video conferences have been held with MoRTH, NHIDCL, and Nagpur-based consultant M/s K&J Projects Private Limited.

The State Public Works Department first proposed the project in early 2020 to ease traffic congestion. The Centre sanctioned consultancy services in 2021, and the DPR was submitted in November 2022.

Union Minister Nitin Gadkari first assured the project during his 2022 Manipur visit. It is part of the nearly ₹15,000 crore National Highway development package for the state.

During a June 2026 meeting with Chief Minister Y. Khemchand Singh, Gadkari reportedly reiterated his commitment to early sanction. This signals the Centre's resolve to fast-track the landmark initiative.

Urban planners compare the project to Guwahati's transformation into a "city of flyovers." The corridor is expected to cut peak-hour commutes from over an hour to a fraction of that time.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TOTAL COST OF THE IMPHAL ELEVATED CORRIDOR PROJECT?
THE ESTIMATED COST IS ₹3,691.81 CRORE. THIS MAKES IT THE LARGEST URBAN ROAD INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECT EVER UNDERTAKEN IN MANIPUR.
HOW LONG IS THE ELEVATED CORRIDOR AND WHAT ARE ITS SPECIFICATIONS?
THE CORRIDOR IS 25 KILOMETRES LONG WITH FOUR LANES. IT HAS A VERTICAL CLEARANCE OF 5.5 METRES AND WILL RUN AS AN ELEVATED STRUCTURE ABOVE EXISTING ROADS.
WHAT IS THE ROUTE OF THE IMPHAL ELEVATED CORRIDOR?
IT STARTS AT KOIRENGEI JUNCTION ALONG NH-02 AND BIFURCATES NEAR RAJ BHAVAN ROTARY. ONE ARM HEADS TO MANIPUR UNIVERSITY VIA NH-102, AND THE OTHER TO IMPHAL INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT VIA NH-02. AN ADDITIONAL TWO-LANE FLYOVER WILL CONNECT CM TRAFFIC ROTARY TO THE CITY CONVENTION CENTRE.
WHEN IS THE DPR EXPECTED TO BE APPROVED?
NHIDCL DEPUTY GENERAL MANAGER RAFIQUE AHMAD CHOUDHURY SAID THE DPR IS LIKELY TO RECEIVE APPROVAL BEFORE THE MANIPUR LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY ELECTIONS SCHEDULED FOR MARCH–APRIL 2027.
WHEN WAS THE PROJECT FIRST PROPOSED AND WHO IS IMPLEMENTING IT?
THE MANIPUR STATE PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT FIRST PROPOSED IT IN EARLY 2020. THE CENTRE SANCTIONED CONSULTANCY SERVICES IN 2021, AND THE DPR WAS PREPARED BY NAGPUR-BASED M/S K&J PROJECTS PRIVATE LIMITED. THE PROJECT IS NOW BEING IMPLEMENTED BY THE NATIONAL HIGHWAYS AND INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION LIMITED (NHIDCL) UNDER THE MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS.
