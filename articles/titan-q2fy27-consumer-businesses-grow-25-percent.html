Titan Q2FY27 Update: Consumer Business Grows 25%, Jewellery Up 21%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Titan Company Ltd's consumer businesses grew 25% YoY in Q2FY27, with domestic businesses up 22% and international business up 97%
  • 02/Jewellery grew ~21%, watches ~30% and EyeCare 28%; demand softened late in the quarter as the festive calendar shifts into Q3 FY27
  • 03/Total consumer store network stands at 3,758; the 97% international growth includes Damas consolidation, and the Q3 festive quarter is the key monitorable

Titan Company Ltd (NSE: TITAN) reported 25% year-on-year growth in its consumer businesses for Q2FY27. The update for the July-September 2026 quarter came through an exchange filing on October 6, 2026.

Domestic businesses grew 22% YoY. International business surged 97% YoY, a number that includes the consolidation of Damas Jewellery, in which Titan holds 67%, from January 2026.

Jewellery, the largest segment, grew ~21% YoY. Demand stayed healthy through most of the quarter before softening towards the close, as the festive calendar shifted into Q3 FY27.

Studded jewellery grew in the early thirties, helped by the 'Festival of Diamonds' campaign and brand promotions. Plain gold jewellery grew ~20% YoY. Gold coins were the weak spot: investment-led demand tapered from a high base, falling in high single digits YoY.

Within the jewellery portfolio, buyer growth was in mid-single digits while average ticket sizes grew in double digits. Tanishq, Mia, Zoya and beYon together grew 20%, while CaratLane recorded 32% growth. The segment added 42 net stores, taking the total to 1,269.

Watches grew ~30% YoY, driven by premiumisation. Analog watches grew in the early thirties, and the smartwatch business recovered to high single-digit growth. The segment added 34 net stores, ending the quarter with 1,379 stores.

EyeCare grew 28% YoY on a multi-brand approach, store upgrades and sharper merchandise. The division operates 847 stores.

Emerging businesses grew 21% YoY: fragrances grew in the mid-thirties, women's bags in the twenties, and Taneira in high single digits. The segment operates 99 stores, adding just one net store in the quarter.

Internationally, Tanishq, Mia and CaratLane kept up strong double-digit momentum in North America. The GCC held up in a volatile geopolitical environment, with Tanishq improving and Damas showing early recovery signs.

Titan shares closed 0.66% lower at Rs 4,550 on the NSE on October 6, against a previous close of Rs 4,580. The stock moved in an intraday range of 4,600-4,530.30. Market capitalisation stood at Rs 4,03,942.70 crore; the 52-week range is Rs 3,401-5,186.70.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHICH TITAN BUSINESSES GREW FASTEST IN Q2FY27?
INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS LED WITH 97% YOY GROWTH, FOLLOWED BY WATCHES AT ~30%, EYECARE AT 28% AND EMERGING BUSINESSES AT 21%. THE OVERALL CONSUMER BUSINESSES GREW 25% YOY.
WHY DID GOLD COIN DEMAND DECLINE DESPITE JEWELLERY GROWTH?
GOLD COIN DEMAND FELL IN HIGH SINGLE DIGITS YOY BECAUSE INVESTMENT-LED BUYING TAPERED FROM A HIGH BASE. COINS ARE A PRICE-DRIVEN, INVESTMENT PRODUCT RATHER THAN AN OCCASION-LED ONE, SO THEY DO NOT MOVE WITH FESTIVE JEWELLERY DEMAND. JEWELLERY DEMAND, BY CONTRAST, REMAINED HEALTHY FOR MOST OF THE QUARTER, GROWING ~21% YOY.
HOW BIG IS TITAN'S INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS AND WHAT DROVE THE 97% GROWTH?
TITAN'S INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS HAS 164 STORES. THE 97% YOY GROWTH REFLECTS STRONG DOUBLE-DIGIT MOMENTUM FOR TANISHQ, MIA AND CARATLANE IN NORTH AMERICA, A RESILIENT GCC, AND THE CONSOLIDATION OF DAMAS JEWELLERY (67% TITAN HOLDING) FROM JANUARY 2026.
WHAT DOES THE FESTIVE-CALENDAR SHIFT MEAN FOR Q3?
DEMAND SOFTENED TOWARDS THE CLOSE OF Q2FY27 BECAUSE FESTIVALS SHIFTED INTO Q3 FY27. THE FESTIVE QUARTER NOW CARRIES THE PENT-UP DEMAND, MAKING Q3 THE KEY QUARTER TO WATCH FOR JEWELLERY MOMENTUM.
HOW LARGE IS TITAN'S STORE NETWORK AFTER Q2FY27?
TITAN'S TOTAL CONSUMER STORE NETWORK STOOD AT 3,758 STORES AS OF SEPTEMBER 2026, WITH 78 NET ADDS IN THE QUARTER. JEWELLERY HAS 1,269 STORES, WATCHES 1,379, EYECARE 847, AND THE INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS 164. GLOSSARY: Studded jewellery: Jewellery set with diamonds or gemstones, as opposed to plain gold pieces. Titan's studded segment grew in the early thirties in Q2FY27. Premiumisation: The shift of consumers towards higher-priced, higher-margin products. It drove ~30% growth in Titan's watches business. Average ticket size: The average amount a customer spends per transaction. Titan's jewellery ticket sizes grew in double digits even as buyer growth was mid-single digits. Net store adds: The number of new stores opened minus stores closed in a period. Titan added 42 net jewellery stores and 78 net stores overall in Q2FY27. Consolidation (accounting): Including a subsidiary's full revenues and results in the parent company's financials once control is acquired. Titan's international numbers now include Damas Jewellery, its 67%-held subsidiary consolidated from January 2026.
