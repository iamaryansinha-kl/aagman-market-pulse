Hexaware Technologies Shares Jump 8% on Anthropic Reseller Status for Amazon Bedrock

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Hexaware Technologies shares surged 8% to ₹534 after securing Anthropic Authorised Reseller status for Amazon Bedrock on June 25, 2026
  • 02/The designation makes it one of a select group globally authorised to sell, integrate, and support Claude AI models for enterprise clients
  • 03/Stock remains down 29.58% in 2026 so far despite the rally, with 52-week high of ₹1,005 and low of ₹620

Hexaware Technologies shares surged as much as 8% to ₹534 on Monday. The rally followed the company's announcement on June 25, 2026, that it secured Anthropic Authorised Reseller status for Amazon Bedrock.

The exchange filing made Hexaware one of a select group of companies globally. It is now authorised to sell, integrate, and support Anthropic's Claude AI models for enterprise customers through Amazon Bedrock.

The partnership aligns with Hexaware's AI-focused strategy. It enables the company to offer services across the AI lifecycle, including model access, customisation, implementation, and managed services.

"This authorisation reflects the Foundational AI capability that we've built and the trust our clients have placed in us. Claude's safety-first design is what highly regulated industries need—and Hexaware has the domain knowledge, engineering excellence, and delivery scale to take it from a model to a working solution," said Siddharth Dhar, President & Global Head - Digital IT Operations & AI, Hexaware.

The authorised reseller status strengthens Hexaware's ability to deliver Claude-powered solutions across key use cases. These include intelligent document processing, automated compliance, advanced customer service, clinical data summarisation, supply chain intelligence, and AI-assisted software engineering.

Hexaware is scaling these Claude-first solutions for clients. It is prioritising AI in the software development life cycle (SDLC), private equity transformation, and cybersecurity. The company has also established a dedicated AI centre of excellence (CoE) to support its AI strategy globally.

Despite Monday's 8% rally, the stock remains under pressure for the year. In 2026 so far, Hexaware shares have declined 29.58%. The stock has crashed 27.49% in the past six months, from a 52-week high of ₹1,005 to the current level.

In the past three months, the shares have rallied up to 20.53%. The past one month saw a gain of 4.97%. The company's market capitalisation stands at approximately ₹40,800 crore.
