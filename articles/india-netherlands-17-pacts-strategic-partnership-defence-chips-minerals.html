India and Netherlands Elevate Ties to Strategic Partnership with 17 Pacts on Defence, Chips, and Minerals

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •• PM Modi and Dutch PM Rob Jetten elevated India-Netherlands ties to a strategic partnership with 17 agreements
  • •• Key focus areas include defence manufacturing, semiconductors, critical minerals, AI, quantum computing, and green hydrogen
  • •• Bilateral trade touched $27.8 billion in 2024-25, with the Netherlands ranking as India's fourth largest investor at $55.6 billion cumulative FDI

India and the Netherlands have elevated their bilateral relationship to a strategic partnership, signing 17 agreements to boost cooperation across defence, critical minerals, semiconductors, and other key sectors.

The elevation came during talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his Dutch counterpart Rob Jetten in The Hague on Saturday evening, as part of Modi's four-nation European tour.

The two leaders expressed deep concern over the situation in West Asia, particularly disruptions to global energy supplies and trade networks through the Strait of Hormuz, which carries roughly one-fifth of global energy supplies.

Modi and Jetten called for freedom of navigation and global flow of commerce through Hormuz while opposing any restrictive measures, according to a joint statement.

The strategic partnership roadmap unveiled by both nations focuses on trade and investment, defence and security, critical and emerging technologies including semiconductors, space, artificial intelligence, and quantum computing.

A dedicated India-Netherlands roadmap on green hydrogen development was also launched, reflecting both countries' commitment to clean energy transition.

The two sides agreed to explore a defence industrial roadmap for joint manufacturing of defence equipment, systems, and components through technology transfer and joint ventures.

Bilateral trade between the two nations touched $27.8 billion in 2024-25, with the Netherlands serving as India's fourth largest investor with cumulative FDI of $55.6 billion.

The Port of Rotterdam functions as a strategic gateway to Europe for Indian exporters, leveraging the Netherlands' world-class logistics network.

A key pact on migration and mobility was signed to facilitate greater movement of Indians to the Netherlands for jobs and education.

Both leaders welcomed an initiative to connect the Dutch Semicon Competence Centre to the Indian Semiconductor Mission, aiming to strengthen the semiconductor sector through shared expertise.

On critical minerals, the two prime ministers expressed mutual interest in strengthening cooperation across the entire value chain, including exploration and integration.

The leaders also discussed the situation in Ukraine and agreed to support efforts toward a comprehensive, just, and lasting peace through dialogue and diplomacy.

Jetten strongly condemned the Pahalgam terrorist attack from April 2025 and extended unwavering support to India in its fight against terrorism, including cross-border terrorism.
