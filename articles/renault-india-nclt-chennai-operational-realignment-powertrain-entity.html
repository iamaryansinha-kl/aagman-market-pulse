Renault Group India Files NCLT Chennai for Powertrain Spin-Off

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Renault Group India has filed with NCLT Chennai to approve a structural realignment that separates powertrain manufacturing into a dedicated entity while integrating vehicle manufacturing and sales operations
  • 02/The move supports Renault's ambition to achieve €2 billion in annual exports from India by 2030, backed by a Rs 5,400 crore investment and full ownership of the Chennai RNAIPL plant
  • 03/The company confirmed no disruption to ongoing operations, with zero impact on employees, customers, dealers, suppliers, or partners

Renault Group India has approached the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) in Chennai. It seeks approval for a structural realignment. Powertrain manufacturing will become a dedicated entity. Vehicle manufacturing and sales will be integrated under one operating structure.

The realignment reflects distinct industrial requirements of each business segment. It is designed to support Renault's long-term strategy in India and strengthen the country as a manufacturing and export base. The company targets exports of up to €2 billion annually by 2030.

The French automaker holds full ownership of the Chennai plant. It acquired Nissan's 51% stake in Renault Nissan Automotive India Pvt Ltd (RNAIPL) in 2025. The Chennai facility has produced over 2.8 million vehicles since 2010.

The plant has also manufactured 4.6 million engines and gearboxes. A total of 1.2 million vehicles have been exported to more than 100 countries. It is supported by an ecosystem of nearly 300 local suppliers.

Renault has invested Rs 5,400 crore in India. It operates with close to 15,000 employees and over 600 sales and service touchpoints nationwide. "India is a key market for Renault Group. With full ownership of our plant in Chennai, we now have all the means to accelerate in India," said François Provost, CEO of Renault Group.

Stéphane Deblaise leads Renault Group India as CEO since September 1, 2025. He previously served as CEO of Renault Korea and held senior roles in China and Latin America. The proposed changes will not disrupt business operations. Employment terms and existing relationships with all stakeholders remain unchanged.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE PURPOSE OF RENAULT GROUP INDIA FILING WITH NCLT CHENNAI?
RENAULT GROUP INDIA HAS APPROACHED THE NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL (NCLT) IN CHENNAI TO SEEK APPROVAL FOR A STRUCTURAL REALIGNMENT. THE PLAN SEPARATES POWERTRAIN MANUFACTURING INTO A DEDICATED ENTITY AND INTEGRATES VEHICLE MANUFACTURING AND SALES OPERATIONS UNDER ONE UNIT.
WILL THE REALIGNMENT AFFECT EMPLOYEES, DEALERS, OR SUPPLIERS?
NO. RENAULT GROUP INDIA HAS EXPLICITLY STATED THERE IS ZERO IMPACT ON EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS, DEALERS, SUPPLIERS, OR PARTNERS. EMPLOYMENT TERMS, SERVICE CONTINUITY, AND EXISTING RELATIONSHIPS REMAIN UNCHANGED.
WHAT IS RENAULT'S EXPORT TARGET FROM INDIA?
RENAULT AIMS TO ACHIEVE EXPORTS OF UP TO EURO 2 BILLION ANNUALLY FROM INDIA BY 2030. THE CHENNAI PLANT HAS ALREADY EXPORTED 1.2 MILLION VEHICLES TO OVER 100 COUNTRIES.
HOW MUCH HAS RENAULT INVESTED IN INDIA?
RENAULT HAS INVESTED RS 5,400 CRORE IN INDIA. THE COMPANY OPERATES WITH CLOSE TO 15,000 EMPLOYEES AND OVER 600 SALES AND SERVICE TOUCHPOINTS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
WHO IS THE CURRENT CEO OF RENAULT GROUP INDIA?
STEPHANE DEBLAISE WAS APPOINTED CEO OF RENAULT GROUP INDIA EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 1, 2025. HE PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS CEO OF RENAULT KOREA AND HAS HELD SENIOR ROLES IN CHINA AND LATIN AMERICA.
