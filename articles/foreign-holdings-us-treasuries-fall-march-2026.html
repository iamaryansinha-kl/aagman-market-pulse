Foreign Holdings of US Treasuries Fall to $9.348 Trillion in March

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Foreign holdings of US Treasuries fell 1.5% to $9.348 trillion in March, down from a record $9.487 trillion in February
  • 02/Japan's holdings dropped nearly 4% to $1.192 trillion, while China's fell 6% to $652.3 billion — lowest since September 2008
  • 03/Overall net capital inflows into the US stood at $150.7 billion in March, with strong demand for corporate bonds

Foreign holdings of US Treasuries declined to $9.348 trillion in March. This marks a 1.5% drop from the record $9.487 trillion set in February.

The data was released by the US Treasury Department on Monday. Despite the monthly decline, holdings remain 3.3% higher than a year ago.

Japan retained its spot as the largest foreign holder. Its holdings fell nearly 4% to $1.192 trillion in March from $1.239 trillion in February.

Japan's holdings are now well below the peak of $1.325 trillion reached in November 2021. The decline signals a broader shift among major creditors.

China, the third-largest overseas holder, reduced its exposure sharply. Its holdings dropped 6% month-on-month to $652.3 billion from $693.3 billion.

This marks China's lowest Treasury holdings since September 2008. China's portfolio has declined over 14% since the beginning of 2025.

The United Kingdom bucked the trend by increasing holdings. UK Treasury holdings rose 3.3% to $926.9 billion from $897.3 billion in February.

The UK is seen as a key custody center for global investors. Flows through the country often reflect hedge fund positioning.

Demand for US corporate bonds strengthened further in March. Corporate bond inflows rose to $76.8 billion from $53.9 billion in February.

Treasury securities recorded net inflows of $13.5 billion. This is up from just $2.6 billion in the previous month.

US equities attracted $10.5 billion in foreign investment. This was a slowdown from $23.8 billion seen in February.

Overall net capital inflows into the United States stood at $150.7 billion in March. This compares with $182.7 billion in February, showing a moderation in foreign capital movement.
