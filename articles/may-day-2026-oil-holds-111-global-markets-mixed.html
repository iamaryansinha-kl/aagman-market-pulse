May Day 2026: Oil Holds $111 Per Barrel, Global Markets Trade Mixed

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Brent crude rises 83 cents to $111.23 per barrel; US benchmark crude adds 12 cents to $105.19 on May 1, 2026
  • 02/Peak Brent contract hit $119.50 last month, up from roughly $70 per barrel before the Iran war began
  • 03/S&P 500 closed at record 7,209.01 on Thursday; US futures edge 0.1% higher as May Day shutters most markets

Brent crude rose 83 cents to $111.23 per barrel on Friday. The US benchmark crude added 12 cents to trade at $105.19. Oil prices held steady despite thin trading volumes across global markets.

Most global share markets were closed for May Day holidays on Friday. Britain's FTSE 100 fell 0.6 per cent to 10,319.24. In Tokyo, the Nikkei 225 gained 0.7 per cent to 59,678.31.

Australia's S&P/ASX 200 surged 0.9 per cent to 8,743.70. US futures for the S&P 500 and Dow Jones edged 0.1 per cent higher after Thursday's record-setting session.

The S&P 500 rallied 1 per cent Thursday to close at 7,209.01. The Dow Jones leapt 1.6 per cent to 49,652.14. The Nasdaq composite climbed 0.9 per cent to a record 24,892.31.

Brent crude was roughly $70 per barrel before the Iran war began. The most actively traded contract peaked at $119.50 last month. June delivery briefly touched above $126 early Thursday before pulling back.

"Investments in artificial intelligence are lighting up every part of the business" - Sundar Pichai, Alphabet CEO.

Prospects for a ceasefire deal in the Iran war remain clouded. Iran's supreme leader said it will protect its nuclear and missile capabilities. US President Donald Trump floated a plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

The dollar bought 156.56 Japanese yen, down from 156.61 yen late Thursday. Japanese officials had warned of intervention if the yen fell further. The euro rose to $1.1733 from $1.1731.

Alphabet rallied 10 per cent after profits nearly doubled expectations. Meta Platforms tumbled 8.7 per cent on higher AI spending forecasts. Microsoft fell 3.9 per cent after raising capital spending guidance.
