Sensex Crashes 1,200 Pts to 76,302; Nifty Tanks 370 Pts to 23,808

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/The BSE Sensex crashed 1,200 points to 76,302 and the Nifty 50 sank 370 points to 23,808 on April 30, 2026, as all sectoral indices turned red.
  • 02/Brent crude surged past $119.94 per barrel amid US-Iran tensions, while the Indian rupee hit a record low of 95.27 against the dollar.
  • 03/With the Maharashtra Day holiday on May 1, traders are unlikely to carry positions amid elevated volatility and persistent FII selling pressure.

Indian benchmark indices opened sharply lower on April 30, 2026, and selling accelerated through the session. The BSE Sensex crashed 1,200 points to settle at 76,302, while the Nifty 50 tanked 370 points to 23,808.

All sectoral indices traded in the red. Nifty Midcap and Smallcap indices declined around 1% each, reflecting broad-based risk aversion across the market.

Brent crude futures surged to $119.94 per barrel, extending a nine-day rally. The spike follows US President Donald Trump's firm stance on maintaining the naval blockade on Iranian ports until a nuclear deal is reached.

The Indian rupee hit a record low of 95.27 against the US dollar. Surging oil prices and sustained foreign portfolio investor outflows weakened the currency further.

The US Federal Reserve held its benchmark interest rate steady at 3.5% to 3.75% for the third consecutive meeting. It shifted its inflation characterization from "somewhat elevated" to "elevated," citing global energy prices.

"While domestic indices are holding near key levels, the broader setup remains fragile, shaped by elevated oil prices, global uncertainty, and a high-impact event calendar," said Hariprasad K, SEBI-registered Research Analyst and Founder, Livelong Wealth.

Major Nifty losers included Eternal Ltd, InterGlobe Aviation, Adani Enterprises, Mahindra & Mahindra, and Shriram Finance. Bajaj Finance, Coal India, and Infosys were among the rare gainers.

The GIFT Nifty had signaled a gap-down opening of 80-90 points ahead of the session. With markets closed on May 1 for Maharashtra Day, traders preferred to lighten positions rather than carry risk overnight.
