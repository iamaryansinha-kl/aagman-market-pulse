Tata AMC Launches Titanium Equity Long-Short SIF: NFO Closes May 11

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Tata Asset Management launches Titanium Equity Long-Short SIF, its second specialised fund; NFO closes on May 11, 2026
  • 02/Minimum investment is ₹10 lakh with net equity exposure dynamically adjustable between -25% and 100%
  • 03/Fund keeps minimum 80% gross allocation to listed equity and can take unhedged short positions up to 25%

Tata Asset Management has launched the Titanium Equity Long-Short Fund. It is the asset manager's second Specialised Investment Fund (SIF). The NFO closes on May 11, 2026.

The minimum investment required is ₹10 lakh. This positions the fund in the high-ticket SIF category between traditional mutual funds and AIF or PMS structures.

The fund aims to generate medium-to-long-term capital appreciation. It actively manages both long and short exposures in equities and equity derivatives.

Net equity exposure can be adjusted dynamically between -25% and 100%. This flexibility allows the fund to navigate bull, bear, and range-bound market phases.

The fund maintains a minimum 80% gross allocation to listed equity. It can hedge equity exposure through derivatives and take unhedged short positions up to 25% of portfolio value.

"The SIF framework has opened up a very strategic middle ground between traditional mutual funds and AIF or PMS structures, allowing fund houses to bring more sophisticated strategies in a tax-efficient manner," said Anand Vardarajan, Chief Business Officer, Tata Asset Management.

"Traditional equity product keeps the net equity high at every valuation of the market, but the long-short fund is designed to do dynamic equity allocation as per the valuations of the market," said Suraj Nanda, Fund Manager, Titanium SIF.

The strategy seeks to deliver performance less dependent on market direction. It aims to convert volatility into opportunity across different market cycles.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE TATA AMC TITANIUM EQUITY LONG-SHORT SIF?
IT IS A SPECIALISED INVESTMENT FUND (SIF) LAUNCHED BY TATA ASSET MANAGEMENT. THE FUND ACTIVELY MANAGES BOTH LONG AND SHORT EXPOSURES IN EQUITIES AND EQUITY DERIVATIVES TO GENERATE MEDIUM-TO-LONG-TERM CAPITAL APPRECIATION.
WHEN DOES THE NFO CLOSE AND WHAT IS THE MINIMUM INVESTMENT?
THE NEW FUND OFFER (NFO) CLOSES ON MAY 11, 2026. THE MINIMUM INVESTMENT REQUIRED IS ₹10 LAKH.
WHAT IS THE NET EQUITY EXPOSURE RANGE OF THE FUND?
THE FUND CAN DYNAMICALLY ADJUST NET EQUITY EXPOSURE BETWEEN -25% AND 100% BASED ON MARKET CONDITIONS. IT MAINTAINS A MINIMUM 80% GROSS ALLOCATION TO LISTED EQUITY.
WHAT IS THE MAXIMUM SHORT POSITION THE FUND CAN TAKE?
THE FUND CAN TAKE UNHEDGED SHORT POSITIONS OF UP TO 25% OF THE PORTFOLIO VALUE. THIS ALLOWS IT TO CAPTURE OPPORTUNITIES IN BOTH RISING AND FALLING STOCKS.
HOW IS THE SIF FRAMEWORK DIFFERENT FROM TRADITIONAL MUTUAL FUNDS?
THE SIF FRAMEWORK SITS BETWEEN TRADITIONAL MUTUAL FUNDS AND AIF OR PMS STRUCTURES. IT ALLOWS MORE SOPHISTICATED STRATEGIES SUCH AS DYNAMIC LONG-SHORT POSITIONING IN A TAX-EFFICIENT MANNER COMPARED TO ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENT FUNDS.
