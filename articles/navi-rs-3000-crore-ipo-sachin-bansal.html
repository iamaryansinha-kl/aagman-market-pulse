Sachin Bansal-Backed Navi Plans Rs 3,000 Crore IPO; Sebi Filing Expected by March Quarter

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Navi is preparing to go public. The fintech company co-founded by Sachin Bansal plans to raise approximately Rs 3,000 crore through an initial public offering.

Draft IPO papers will be filed with the Securities and Exchange Board of India by the March quarter of FY27. The exact timing within that quarter has not been disclosed.

The issue structure will include a mix of fresh equity and an offer for sale. The final split between new shares and existing shareholder stake sales is yet to be decided.

Kotak Investment Banking has been appointed as the lead manager for the issue. The bank will handle the regulatory filings, investor outreach, and book-building process.

Navi operates in lending, insurance, and wealth management. The company was founded by Bansal after his exit from Flipkart, which was sold to Walmart in 2018.

The IPO will test investor appetite for fintech listings. Several fintech firms have gone public in India over the past two years with mixed results.
