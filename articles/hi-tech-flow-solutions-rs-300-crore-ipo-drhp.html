Hi-Tech Flow Solutions Files DRHP for Rs 300 Crore IPO to Fund HSAW Pipe Expansion

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Hi-Tech Flow Solutions has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus with the Securities and Exchange Board of India for an initial public offering. The IPO comprises a fresh issue of up to Rs 300 crore at a face value of Rs 2, alongside an offer-for-sale by promoters Ajay Kumar Bansal (up to Rs 75 crore) and Vipul Bansal (up to Rs 25 crore). Investor The Wealth Company Alternates Trust - India Inflection Opportunity Fund is offering up to 20,268,225 shares.

The company may also undertake a pre-IPO placement of up to Rs 60 crore, which would reduce the fresh issue size if completed.

Use of proceeds is concrete. Of the fresh issue, Rs 125.9 crore will fund an expansion to set up an API-grade helical submerged arc welded (HSAW) pipe manufacturing unit along with a 3LPE coating unit within the existing Nagpur facility. Another Rs 90 crore will go towards repayment or pre-payment of certain bank borrowings, with the balance for general corporate purposes.

The business is a pure B2B play in water infrastructure. Incorporated in 1993, Hi-Tech Flow manufactures and supplies HSAW pipes across 17 states and two union territories from facilities at Sanand in Gujarat and Nagpur. It primarily serves private engineering, procurement and construction contractors executing large-scale pipeline and infrastructure projects, with applications in bulk water supply, irrigation networks, inter-basin river-linking pipelines, municipal distribution, dam-based systems and sewerage projects. It is also expanding into oil and gas applications.

Capacity is expanding. The company has a total installed capacity of 160,000 metric tonnes per annum, of which 40,000 MTPA is at Sanand and 120,000 MTPA at Nagpur, and it supplied 154,000 MT of pipes in aggregate over the last three fiscals. An expansion at Nagpur funded from internal accruals, expected to be completed by June 2027, will add 30,000 MTPA, taking total annual capacity to 190,000 MTPA.

The demand backdrop is supportive. According to the CareEdge report cited in the filing, rapid expansion of data centre infrastructure, particularly hyperscale and AI data centres, requires supporting water supply and utility infrastructure, which could create additional demand for HSAW pipes.

The offer will be made through the book-building process, with not more than 50% of the net offer allocated to qualified institutional buyers, not less than 15% to non-institutional bidders and not less than 35% to retail individual bidders.
