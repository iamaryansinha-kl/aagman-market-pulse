Nykaa Q2 FY27 Update: GMV Growth Seen in High 20s YoY

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/FSN E-Commerce Ventures, parent of Nykaa, said consolidated GMV growth is expected in the high 20s YoY in a provisional Q2 FY27 business update
  • 02/The Fashion vertical led the quarter with NSV growth seen in the early 40s, while Beauty posted high-20s growth and added 14 net-new stores to reach 338
  • 03/Shares rose 4.12% to Rs 338.35 on October 5; a larger share of the festive season falling in Q3 shifts some festive-led growth to the December quarter

FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd (NSE: NYKAA), the parent company of beauty and fashion platform Nykaa, released a provisional business update for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 on Monday, October 5. The update pointed to continued growth momentum across both of its core verticals.

According to the company, consolidated gross merchandise value (GMV) growth is expected to come in the high 20s year-on-year for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Net sales value (NSV) growth is seen in the early 30s, while consolidated net revenue growth is also expected in the high 20s.

The company attributed the consolidated growth to the scaling-up of its Fashion vertical alongside steady growth in the Beauty business. All growth figures cited in the update are on a year-on-year basis.

Within Beauty, NSV and net revenue growth are both expected in the high 20s for the quarter. The performance was driven by strong omnichannel growth, supported by new customer additions and repeat engagement across the platform.

Nykaa's offline expansion continued through the quarter. The company added 14 net-new stores, taking its total store count to 338 as of September 30, 2026.

The existing store base also performed well. Like-for-like store sales growth stood in the low 20s during the quarter, marking the strongest such performance in the last six quarters.

The House of Nykaa portfolio continued to grow faster than the overall Beauty business during the quarter. This extends the in-house brand mix's run of outperformance within the beauty segment.

Fashion remained a key growth driver for the company in Q2 FY27. NSV growth for the vertical is expected in the early 40s, with net revenue growth seen in the low 40s.

Customer acquisition in Fashion remained strong through the quarter. More than 250 brands were added across categories, widening the selection available to shoppers on the platform.

The company's partnership with Nike continued to gain traction during the quarter, helped by exclusive product drops. The tie-up contributed to the vertical's customer acquisition momentum alongside the new brand additions.

Nykaa also flagged a calendar effect that shifts some growth between quarters. A larger portion of the festive season falls in the third quarter this year, which means some festive-led growth moves from Q2 to Q3.

Despite this shift, the company said it remains confident about its underlying growth drivers and its long-term growth ambitions. The update offered no change to the company's assessment of demand conditions across either vertical.

The update was issued voluntarily and carries a detailed disclaimer. The revenue figures for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 are provisional and subject to limited review by statutory auditors.

The company also clarified that the update does not constitute financial results or earnings guidance. Notably, no absolute rupee figures for GMV, net sales value, or revenue were disclosed, and no profit numbers were shared; all metrics were given as year-on-year growth ranges.

On the stock market, shares of FSN E-Commerce Ventures rose 4.12% to Rs 338.35 on Monday following the update.

The stock has gained around 41% over the past year, taking the company's market capitalisation to Rs 95,938 crore. The shares trade close to their 52-week high of Rs 349.55.
