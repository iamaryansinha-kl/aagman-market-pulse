West Asia Conflict: India Adds Record 10.69 Lakh PNG Connections

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India added a record 10.69 lakh PNG connections since March 2026 as the West Asia conflict disrupted LPG supplies
  • 02/March 2026 alone accounted for 6.1 lakh connections, with over 10,000 new connections provided daily since March 15
  • 03/The government now targets 30 lakh PNG connections in pipeline-ready areas and has extended the National PNG Drive 2.0 till June 2026

India has added a record 10.69 lakh piped natural gas (PNG) connections since March 2026. The surge comes as the West Asia conflict disrupts liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) imports.

The Oil Ministry confirmed the milestone on April 21, 2026. March alone saw 6.1 lakh connections added, the highest monthly tally on record.

Since March 15, 2026, city gas distribution companies have provided over 10,000 new PNG connections on average each day. Of the total, 5.01 lakh connections have been gasified and 5.68 lakh customers registered for new connections.

India had approximately 1.67 crore domestic PNG connections as of February 2026. This is against a Pro-rata Target of almost 3.99 crore, meaning achievement stands at 42 per cent of the goal.

The West Asia conflict has severely impacted India’s LPG supplies. The country imports nearly 60 per cent of its LPG from the region, and the Strait of Hormuz blockade triggered supply fears.

The Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB) launched the National PNG Drive 2.0 in January 2026. CGD entities developed infrastructure for 6.5 lakh additional connections by March 31, and the drive has now been extended till June 2026.

The government is targeting nearly 30 lakh PNG connections in areas where pipeline infrastructure already exists. An estimated 60 lakh LPG connections sit near operating CGD networks and can migrate to piped gas.

Sujata Sharma, Joint Secretary at the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, said the crisis has unlocked unprecedented momentum in PNG adoption. The government has also linked additional commercial LPG allocations to states that expedite CGD network expansion.

PNG is priced lower than LPG cylinders and offers uninterrupted supply. The government aims to raise natural gas share in India’s energy mix from 6 per cent to 15 per cent by 2030.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MANY PNG CONNECTIONS HAS INDIA ADDED SINCE MARCH 2026?
INDIA HAS CUMULATIVELY ADDED MORE THAN 10.69 LAKH PNG CONNECTIONS SINCE MARCH 2026, INCLUDING BOTH GASIFIED CONNECTIONS AND NEW REGISTRATIONS. OF THIS, 5.01 LAKH HAVE BEEN GASIFIED AND 5.68 LAKH CUSTOMERS HAVE REGISTERED FOR NEW CONNECTIONS.
WHAT IS THE DAILY RATE OF NEW PNG CONNECTIONS?
SINCE MARCH 15, 2026, CITY GAS DISTRIBUTION COMPANIES HAVE PROVIDED MORE THAN 10,000 NEW PNG CONNECTIONS ON AN AVERAGE DAILY BASIS. IN MARCH 2026 ALONE, OVER 3.25 LAKH CONNECTIONS WERE GASIFIED AND OVER 2.85 LAKH NEW CONNECTIONS WERE INSTALLED.
WHY IS INDIA PUSHING PNG EXPANSION NOW?
THE ONGOING WEST ASIA CONFLICT HAS DISRUPTED LPG IMPORTS, WITH INDIA SOURCING NEARLY 60 PER CENT OF ITS LPG FROM THE REGION. THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ BLOCKADE TRIGGERED SUPPLY CONCERNS, PUSHING THE GOVERNMENT TO ACCELERATE THE SHIFT FROM LPG CYLINDERS TO DOMESTIC PNG.
WHAT IS THE GOVERNMENT'S PNG EXPANSION TARGET?
THE GOVERNMENT IS TARGETING NEARLY 30 LAKH PNG CONNECTIONS IN AREAS WHERE PIPELINE INFRASTRUCTURE IS ALREADY IN PLACE. THERE ARE AN ESTIMATED 60 LAKH LPG CONNECTIONS NEAR OPERATING CGD NETWORKS THAT CAN EASILY MIGRATE TO PNG.
WHAT IS THE NATIONAL PNG DRIVE 2.0?
THE PNGRB LAUNCHED THE NATIONAL PNG DRIVE 2.0 IN JANUARY 2026 AS A THREE-MONTH CAMPAIGN TO EXPAND CGD INFRASTRUCTURE. IT YIELDED INFRASTRUCTURE FOR 6.5 LAKH ADDITIONAL CONNECTIONS BY MARCH 31, 2026, AND HAS NOW BEEN EXTENDED TILL JUNE 2026 TO SUSTAIN MOMENTUM.
