Sensex, Nifty Set for Positive Open as Oil Slips Below $110

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Gift Nifty indicates gains of around 100 points at 24,550 ahead of May 7 market open
  • 02/Brent crude oil slipped below $110/barrel as US paused aggressive moves around Strait of Hormuz
  • 03/Derivatives data signals caution with PCR at 0.61 and aggressive call writing capping upside

Indian equity markets are set for a positive opening on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The Gift Nifty is trading at 24,550, indicating gains of around 100 points.

Crude oil prices slipped below $110 per barrel overnight. The drop came after indications that the United States paused aggressive moves around the Strait of Hormuz.

Asian markets are trading sharply higher today. Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore and Taiwan all posted strong gains amid improved global sentiment.

Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, said global sentiment strengthened on expectations of de-escalation in Middle East tensions. "Indications from Donald Trump that the U.S. has paused its assertive stance around the Strait of Hormuz have supported a recovery in risk appetite," he said.

Norbert Rücker, Head of Economics and Next Generation Research at Julius Baer, noted that oil markets moved past the initial shock reaction. "Politics aside, the oil market has settled in a regime of deficit absorption by inventory draws," he said.

However, derivatives data continued to signal caution in domestic markets. The put-call ratio near 0.61 suggests a cautious-to-bearish undertone despite the relief rally.

Aggressive call writing at the 24,200–24,300 level continues to cap upside potential. Meanwhile, the put base at 24,000–23,800 reinforces support for the Nifty.

ICICI Prudential Alternates remains constructive on Indian equities. The firm said favourable domestic macro indicators support bottom-up stock selection in a more differentiated environment.
