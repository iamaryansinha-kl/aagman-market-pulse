India MEI Sector Hiring 2026: 70% Firms Plan Expansion as Growth Hits 6.6%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's Manufacturing, Engineering and Infrastructure sector posts 6.6% net employment growth for H1 FY2026-27, up from 5.5%
  • 02/70% of MEI employers plan workforce expansion, ranking the sector among top three nationally by hiring intent
  • 03/Semiconductor investments in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka expected to generate 1 million jobs between 2026 and 2028

India's Manufacturing, Engineering and Infrastructure (MEI) sector has recorded a Net Employment Change of 6.6% for the first half of FY2026-27. This marks a rise from 5.5% in the previous half-year, according to the TeamLease Employment Outlook Report.

The report, based on a survey of 1,268 employers across 23 industries, indicates that 70% of MEI employers plan workforce expansion. This places the sector among the top three industries nationally by hiring intent.

Balasubramanian A, Senior Vice President at TeamLease Services, said the MEI sector has crossed the recovery phase. He added that the high NEC reflects sustained investment pipelines across semiconductors, clean energy and advanced manufacturing.

Semiconductor investments in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka are likely to create around 1 million jobs between 2026 and 2028. These roles span fabrication, ATMP operations, chip design and supply chain functions.

Hiring growth is concentrated in electric vehicles, renewable energy and advanced manufacturing segments. Engineering hiring remains steady with 33% of employers planning expansion and 46% maintaining current headcount.

Chennai leads city-level hiring intent at 24%, followed by Pune at 20% and Bengaluru at 18%. Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra and Gujarat remain the primary drivers of manufacturing employment.

Average salary growth in the MEI sector is projected at 9.4% for FY2026-27. Chennai and Pune lead with 9.8% each, ahead of IT, banking and insurance sectors.
