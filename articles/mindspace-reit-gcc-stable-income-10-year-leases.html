Mindspace REIT GCCs Drive 50% Portfolio With 10-Year Lease Stability

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Global Capability Centres account for over 50% of Mindspace REIT's portfolio with average 10-year lease tenures
  • 02/Re-leasing spreads hit 40% this quarter but sustainable rate seen at 20% going forward
  • 03/Chennai acquisitions closing in 5-10 days to add Rs 500 crore NOI once stabilised

Mindspace Business Parks REIT derives over 50% of its portfolio from Global Capability Centres. CEO Ramesh Nair said GCCs are "stickier" and sign 10-plus year leases with longer lock-ins.

GCC demand spans technology, BFSI, manufacturing and engineering sectors. India currently hosts around 2,900 GCCs, projected to grow to 4,400 by 2030.

Employment in GCCs is expected to rise from 1.9 million to 2.8 million by 2030. The industry size is forecast to expand from $65 billion to $105 billion.

Re-leasing spreads reached 40% this quarter. Nair said a more sustainable number would be around 20% over the next couple of years.

Hyderabad is Mindspace's strongest market with 46% of new GCCs setting up there. Vacancy in key micro-markets like Madhapur is around 2%.

Rental growth in Hyderabad has exceeded 25%, driven by infrastructure, talent availability and social amenities. Mumbai's BKC vacancy is below 1%.

"GCCs are willing to give us longer tenures, which means there is more stability of income for us," Nair told businessline.

The REIT's LTV ratio is currently around 24%, rising to 29% after recent acquisitions. It has 7.3 million sq ft under development or planned.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT SHARE OF MINDSPACE REIT'S PORTFOLIO COMES FROM GCCs?
GLOBAL CAPABILITY CENTRES ACCOUNT FOR OVER 50% OF MINDSPACE REIT'S LEASING PORTFOLIO. IT SERVICES MAKE UP 20% AND LARGE INDIAN DOMESTIC ORGANISATIONS CONTRIBUTE 30%.
WHAT IS THE AVERAGE LEASE TENURE FOR GCCs AT MINDSPACE?
GCCs TYPICALLY SIGN 10-PLUS YEAR LEASES WITH LONGER LOCK-IN PERIODS COMPARED TO IT SERVICES CLIENTS, PROVIDING GREATER INCOME STABILITY AND LOWER CYCLICALITY.
WHAT WERE THE RE-LEASING SPREADS THIS QUARTER?
RE-LEASING SPREADS WERE ABOUT 40% THIS QUARTER. MINDSPACE CEO RAMESH NAIR SAID A MORE SUSTAINABLE NUMBER WOULD BE AROUND 20% OVER THE NEXT COUPLE OF YEARS.
WHAT ARE THE CHENNAI ACQUISITION TERMS?
MINDSPACE ACQUIRED TWO PREMIUM ASSETS FROM K RAHEJA CORP AND CAPITALAND IN CHENNAI. THE DEALS SHOULD CLOSE IN THE NEXT 5-10 DAYS AND GENERATE AROUND Rs 500 CRORE IN NOI ONCE STABILISED.
HOW MUCH NEW SUPPLY DOES MINDSPACE HAVE UNDER DEVELOPMENT?
MINDSPACE HAS 7.3 MILLION SQ FT UNDER DEVELOPMENT OR PLANNED. IT ALSO HAS ABOUT 1.3 MILLION SQ FT OF VACANT SPACE TO LEASE, WITH CONTRACTUAL ESCALATIONS OF 5% EVERY YEAR.
