Navin Fluorine Hits 52-Week High on 76% Q4 Profit Jump, ₹8.60 Dividend

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Navin Fluorine International Ltd Q4 standalone net profit surges 76.2% to ₹135 crore on revenue of ₹626.5 crore, up 29% year-on-year
  • 02/Board recommends final dividend of ₹8.60 per share; FY26 full-year PAT more than doubles to ₹487.7 crore from ₹241.9 crore
  • 03/Brokerages split: Jefferies and DAM Capital maintain buy with targets up to ₹8,385, while Citi maintains sell at ₹6,500 citing priced-in upside

Navin Fluorine International Ltd (NSE: NAVINFLUOR) jumped 6.5 per cent in early trade on April 30, 2026. The stock hit a 52-week high of ₹7,200 before paring gains to trade at ₹6,752.50 by 10:40 am on the NSE.

The company reported standalone net profit of ₹135.03 crore for Q4 FY26, up 76.2 per cent from ₹76.61 crore in the year-ago period. Revenue from operations grew 29 per cent year-on-year to ₹626.51 crore.

On a full-year basis, FY26 profit after tax more than doubled to ₹487.67 crore from ₹241.93 crore in FY25. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹8.60 per share for FY26.

Jefferies maintained a 'buy' with a target price of ₹8,385, noting EBITDA and PAT beat its estimates by 19 per cent and 30 per cent respectively. It expects a 24 per cent EPS CAGR over FY26-FY28 driven by specialty chemicals and CDMO strength.

Citi maintained 'sell' with a target of ₹6,500, arguing the stock's 50 per cent rally over the past year has priced in much of the growth outlook. It flagged potential downside risks to refrigerant pricing from industry capacity additions.

DAM Capital retained 'buy' with a target of ₹7,769, highlighting the sixth consecutive quarter of revenue and profitability growth. It noted management remains confident of sustaining EBITDA margins around 30 per cent.

Elara Capital also reiterated 'buy' with a revised target of ₹8,681, citing improved earnings visibility and a transition into a multi-year compounding phase driven by CDMO ramp-up and favourable industry tailwinds.
