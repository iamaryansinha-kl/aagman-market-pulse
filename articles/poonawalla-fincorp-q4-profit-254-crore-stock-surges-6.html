Poonawalla Fincorp Q4 Profit Jumps to ₹254.79 Crore, Stock Surges 6%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Poonawalla Fincorp standalone net profit for Q4FY26 jumps to ₹254.79 crore from ₹62.33 crore in Q4FY25
  • 02/Shares surged 6% on the news, reflecting strong investor response to the earnings beat
  • 03/Full-year FY26 results and AUM growth trajectory will be the next key metrics to track

Poonawalla Fincorp Ltd reported a standalone net profit of ₹254.79 crore for Q4FY26. The figure marks a sharp rise from ₹62.33 crore posted in the same quarter last year.

The year-on-year profit growth exceeds 300%. The jump reflects improved asset quality and higher disbursements across lending verticals.

Shares of Poonawalla Fincorp surged 6% following the earnings announcement. The stock rally signals strong market confidence in the underlying business momentum.

The company is a non-banking financial company (NBFC) focused on retail and SME lending. It operates under the Cyrus Poonawalla Group.

For the full financial year FY26, investors will watch total AUM and net interest margin trends. Q4 typically captures year-end collections and disbursement push.

The sharp profit expansion comes amid a broader NBFC sector recovery. Peer firms have also reported improved credit costs and lower provisioning in recent quarters.

Going forward, the loan book mix and cost of funds will shape margin sustainability. Management commentary on FY27 guidance will be closely tracked by brokerages.
