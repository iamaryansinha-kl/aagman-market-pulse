Eternal Shares Jump 5% on Q4 Profit Surge; Brokerages Raise Targets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eternal shares surged 5% to an intraday high of ₹265.40 after Q4 net profit jumped 346% YoY to ₹174 crore
  • 02/Consolidated revenue rose 196% to ₹17,292 crore; brokerages including Investec and Morgan Stanley raised target prices
  • 03/Quick commerce growth outlook remains strong with $1 billion adjusted EBITDA target by FY29, though competitive intensity is flagged as a risk

Eternal Ltd shares jumped 5% in early trade on April 29, 2026. The stock hit an intraday high of ₹265.40, up from the previous close of ₹253.07.

The company, which owns Zomato and Blinkit, reported a sharp rise in profitability. Consolidated net profit surged 346% year-on-year to ₹174 crore in Q4 FY26.

Revenue from operations rose 196% YoY to ₹17,292 crore. This compares to ₹5,833 crore in the corresponding quarter last year.

Global brokerage Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating. It raised its target price to ₹347 from ₹345, citing focus on profitable growth.

Investec maintained a buy rating with a target of ₹375. It flagged rising competitive intensity as a near-term risk, even as execution remains strong.

UBS also kept a buy rating with a target of ₹310. It noted food delivery growth is approaching 20%, while quick commerce profitability continues to improve.

Macquarie stood out with an underperform rating and a ₹200 target. It cautioned that moderating sequential growth and competitive pressure could hurt unit economics.

HDFC Securities and Motilal Oswal both maintained buy ratings with a ₹340 target. They expect quick commerce growth to normalise with improving unit economics and a clearer path to profitability.
