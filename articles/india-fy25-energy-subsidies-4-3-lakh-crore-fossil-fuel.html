India FY25 Energy Subsidies at ₹4.3 Lakh Crore; Fossil Fuel Aid 3x Clean

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India spent ₹4.3 lakh crore on energy subsidies in FY25, with fossil fuel support roughly three times higher than clean energy aid
  • 02/Electricity subsidies stood at ₹2.41 lakh crore (58% of total) and LPG at ₹71,718 crore, while renewables got only ₹26,406 crore
  • 03/IISD warns LPG under-recoveries could exceed ₹60,000 crore in FY27 if global prices stay elevated amid Gulf tensions

India spent ₹4.3 lakh crore ($51 billion) on energy subsidies in 2024-25. The data comes from a new IISD report released on April 29, 2026.

Electricity and LPG consumption subsidies accounted for 75% of the total bill. Electricity subsidies alone stood at ₹2.41 lakh crore, or 58% of all energy support.

LPG subsidies reached ₹71,718 crore in FY25. Nearly half of this burden came from under-recoveries, where oil marketing companies bear losses from below-cost retail prices.

By contrast, renewable energy subsidies stood at ₹26,406 crore. Support for electric vehicles was ₹16,812 crore. Together, clean energy got only about 10% of total subsidies.

"The recent tensions in the Gulf highlight India's exposure to global LPG price volatility," said Sunil Mani, policy advisor at IISD. He warned under-recoveries could cross ₹60,000 crore in FY27.

Electricity subsidies have nearly doubled over the past decade. They are growing faster than consumption itself, partly due to higher eligibility limits for subsidised consumers in some states.

In several states, electricity subsidies equal nearly 20% of annual revenues. A rising share covers routine operating costs of power distribution companies rather than efficiency improvements.

The IISD report called for better targeting of electricity and LPG subsidies. It also urged stronger support for rooftop solar, electric cooking, biogas and EVs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MUCH DID INDIA SPEND ON ENERGY SUBSIDIES IN FY25?
INDIA SPENT AT LEAST ₹4.3 LAKH CRORE ($51 BILLION) ON ENERGY SUBSIDIES IN THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2024-25, ACCORDING TO AN IISD REPORT.
WHAT SHARE OF ENERGY SUBSIDIES WENT TO ELECTRICITY AND LPG?
ELECTRICITY AND LPG CONSUMPTION SUBSIDIES ACCOUNTED FOR 75% OF THE TOTAL. ELECTRICITY SUBSIDIES STOOD AT ₹2.41 LAKH CRORE (58%) AND LPG SUBSIDIES AT ₹71,718 CRORE.
HOW MUCH DID CLEAN ENERGY SUBSIDIES GET IN FY25?
RENEWABLE ENERGY SUBSIDIES STOOD AT ₹26,406 CRORE AND ELECTRIC VEHICLE SUPPORT AT ₹16,812 CRORE. TOGETHER, CLEAN ENERGY SUBSIDIES ACCOUNTED FOR ONLY ABOUT 10% OF TOTAL SUPPORT.
WHAT IS THE RISK FROM LPG UNDER-RECOVERIES?
NEARLY HALF OF THE FY25 LPG SUBSIDY BURDEN CAME FROM UNDER-RECOVERIES. IISD POLICY ADVISOR SUNIL MANI WARNED THAT IF GLOBAL PRICES REMAIN ELEVATED, UNDER-RECOVERIES COULD EXCEED ₹60,000 CRORE IN FY27.
WHAT DOES THE IISD REPORT RECOMMEND?
THE REPORT CALLS FOR BETTER TARGETING OF ELECTRICITY AND LPG SUBSIDIES, AND STRONGER FISCAL SUPPORT FOR ALTERNATIVES SUCH AS ROOFTOP SOLAR, ELECTRIC COOKING, DECENTRALISED BIOGAS AND ELECTRIC VEHICLES.
