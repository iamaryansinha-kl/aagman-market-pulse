JSW Steel-POSCO 50:50 JV for ₹35,000 Crore Odisha Steel Plant

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/JSW Steel and POSCO Group approve 50:50 joint venture to set up a 6 MTPA integrated steel plant in Odisha's Keonjhar district
  • 02/Odisha government cleared the project at ₹35,000 crore; POSCO will invest ₹508.8 crore for its equity stake in Saffron Resources
  • 03/Transaction targeted for completion by December 31, 2026; commercial operations potentially by 2028-2029 after feasibility and construction

JSW Steel Ltd (NSE: JSWSTEEL) has approved a 50:50 joint venture with South Korea's POSCO Group. The board cleared the Share Subscription and Joint Venture Agreement on April 17, 2026.

The JV will develop a greenfield integrated steel plant with 6 MTPA capacity in Odisha. The project revives POSCO's re-entry into the state nearly two decades after its earlier $12 billion proposal was withdrawn in 2017.

Saffron Resources Pvt Ltd, a wholly owned JSW subsidiary, will become the JV entity. Saffron already holds 887 acres in Odisha — 595 acres freehold and 292 acres leasehold.

POSCO Group will invest approximately ₹508.8 crore through equity subscription for its 50% stake. The transaction is targeted for completion by December 31, 2026.

The Odisha government cleared the project with an estimated ₹35,000 crore investment as of December 2025. Around 2,445 acres across 12 villages in Patna tehsil, Keonjhar district, have been identified for acquisition.

The partnership combines JSW's domestic execution capabilities with POSCO's advanced steelmaking technology. The facility will produce high-grade steel for automotive and advanced applications.

JSW Steel currently operates 35.7 MTPA crude steel capacity. It aims to scale to 50 MTPA in India by FY31. The new plant adds approximately 17% to its existing capacity.

Feasibility studies are expected to span 12-18 months. Construction will add another 2-3 years. Commercial operations may begin by 2028-2029.

The project is expected to generate approximately 8,000 direct and indirect jobs. It strengthens Odisha's position as India's major steel manufacturing hub.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE JSW STEEL-POSCO JOINT VENTURE ABOUT?
JSW STEEL LTD AND POSCO GROUP HAVE APPROVED A 50:50 JOINT VENTURE TO SET UP A 6 MTPA INTEGRATED STEEL PLANT IN ODISHA'S KEONJHAR DISTRICT. THE ODISHA GOVERNMENT CLEARED THE PROJECT WITH AN ESTIMATED INVESTMENT OF ₹35,000 CRORE. THE ORIGINAL MOU SIGNED IN OCTOBER 2024 ENVISIONED A 5 MTPA PLANT, WHICH HAS SINCE BEEN SCALED TO 6 MTPA.
HOW MUCH IS POSCO INVESTING IN THE JOINT VENTURE?
POSCO GROUP WILL SUBSCRIBE TO EQUITY SHARES OF SAFFRON RESOURCES FOR APPROXIMATELY ₹508.8 CRORE, SUBJECT TO CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS. THIS REPRESENTS ITS 50% STAKE IN THE JV ENTITY. THE TOTAL PROJECT CAPEX IS ESTIMATED AT ₹70,000-80,000 CRORE, WITH EACH PARTNER CONTRIBUTING ROUGHLY ₹35,000-40,000 CRORE OVER THE PROJECT LIFECYCLE.
WHERE WILL THE STEEL PLANT BE LOCATED?
THE PLANT WILL COME UP IN KEONJHAR DISTRICT, ODISHA. SAFFRON RESOURCES ALREADY HOLDS 887 ACRES (595 ACRES FREEHOLD AND 292 ACRES LEASEHOLD). AN ADDITIONAL 2,445 ACRES ACROSS 12 VILLAGES IN PATNA TEHSIL HAVE BEEN IDENTIFIED FOR ACQUISITION, COVERING BOTH GOVERNMENT AND PRIVATE LAND.
WHAT IS THE TIMELINE FOR THE PROJECT?
THE BOARD APPROVED THE JV AGREEMENT ON APRIL 17, 2026. THE EQUITY TRANSACTION IS TARGETED FOR COMPLETION BY DECEMBER 31, 2026. FEASIBILITY STUDIES WILL TAKE 12-18 MONTHS, FOLLOWED BY 2-3 YEARS OF CONSTRUCTION. COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS MAY BEGIN BY 2028-2029.
WHAT TECHNOLOGY AND PRODUCTS WILL THE PLANT FOCUS ON?
THE PARTNERSHIP COMBINES POSCO'S ADVANCED STEELMAKING TECHNOLOGY WITH JSW'S DOMESTIC EXECUTION CAPABILITIES. THE FACILITY WILL PRODUCE HIGH-GRADE AND SPECIALISED STEEL PRODUCTS, INCLUDING STEEL FOR AUTOMOTIVE APPLICATIONS. THE COLLABORATION ALSO EXPLORES OPPORTUNITIES IN BATTERY MATERIALS AND RENEWABLE ENERGY FOR CAPTIVE REQUIREMENTS.
