India's Mining Sector to Add $500B GDP, 25M Jobs by 2047: Report

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's mining sector could add $500 billion to GDP and create 25 million jobs by 2047, per a Deloitte-ICC report released May 9, 2026
  • 02/The "Mining 5.0" report calls for AI, digital twins, and autonomous systems to drive the structural transition beyond Mining 4.0
  • 03/Current mining contribution is just 2-3% of GDP; achieving the 2047 targets requires integration of fragmented digital capabilities

India's mining sector can contribute an additional $500 billion to GDP and create up to 25 million incremental jobs by 2047. This is according to the report "Mining 5.0 - Emerging Mining Technologies by 2030" released May 9, 2026.

The report is authored jointly by Deloitte and the Indian Chamber of Commerce (ICC). It was published as India aims to become a $30 trillion economy by 2047.

Currently, the mining sector contributes around 2-3 per cent to India's GDP directly. It also supports downstream industries including steel, cement, power, automobiles and infrastructure.

The report states India is moving beyond Mining 4.0 toward Mining 5.0. This next phase shifts from volume-centric extraction to value-driven, technology-enabled, sustainable operations.

Key technologies include AI-based predictive safety systems, digital twins, autonomous operations, real-time monitoring, and hybrid cloud-edge architectures.

The report warns that many Indian miners have adopted Mining 4.0 elements in isolation. "Without integration, digital investments risk remaining isolated pilots with limited enterprise value," it said.

Policy reforms, rising steel demand, critical mineral requirements, and Atmanirbhar Bharat are driving the sector forward. The government is pushing for energy security and self-reliance in minerals.

"Mining 5.0 is not a technology roadmap; it is a leadership agenda," the report stated. Success depends on aligning operating models with value over volume.
