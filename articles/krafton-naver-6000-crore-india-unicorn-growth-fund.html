Krafton, Naver Launch ₹6,000 Cr India Unicorn Growth Fund With Mirae Asset

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Krafton, Naver Corporation, and Mirae Asset formally launched the ₹6,000 crore Unicorn Growth Fund for Indian startups on April 21, 2026
  • 02/The fund will begin with over ₹3,000 crore and deploy capital over four years, with Krafton committing ₹1,230 crore at first close
  • 03/Investments will target four themes: technology platforms, consumer discretionary, AI and software, and deep tech

KRAFTON Inc. and Naver Corporation have formally launched the Unicorn Growth Fund, a ₹6,000 crore investment vehicle dedicated to Indian startups. Mirae Asset Venture Investments will manage and advise the fund.

The announcement follows a meeting attended by Union Minister Piyush Goyal and South Korea's Minister of Trade Jung Kwan Kim. Senior executives from KRAFTON and Naver were also present.

The fund will begin operations with over ₹3,000 crore. It aims to reach the full ₹6,000 crore target over a four-year deployment period.

KRAFTON will contribute ₹1,230 crore at the first close. Naver's capital commitment is expected to be broadly comparable, at roughly $137 million.

The fund targets four sectors: technology platforms, consumer discretionary, AI and software, and deep tech. Typical investments will range between $10 million and $30 million per company.

Portfolio companies will gain access to KRAFTON and Naver's expertise in product, AI, gaming, and platforms. They will also receive entry into Korean and broader Asian markets.

KRAFTON has invested over $200 million in India to date. Its flagship title Battlegrounds Mobile India has crossed 240 million downloads in the country.

South Korea's cumulative foreign direct investment in India reached roughly $6.8 billion by mid-2025. The fund marks the first large-scale collaboration between the three South Korean majors in India.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE UNICORN GROWTH FUND AND WHO HAS LAUNCHED IT?
THE UNICORN GROWTH FUND IS A ₹6,000 CRORE INVESTMENT VEHICLE FORMALLY LAUNCHED BY KRAFTON INC., NAVER CORPORATION, AND MIRAE ASSET VENTURE INVESTMENTS ON APRIL 21, 2026. IT IS DEDICATED TO FUNDING GROWTH-STAGE STARTUPS IN INDIA.
HOW MUCH WILL THE FUND BEGIN WITH AND WHAT IS KRAFTON'S CONTRIBUTION?
THE FUND WILL BEGIN OPERATIONS WITH OVER ₹3,000 CRORE. KRAFTON WILL CONTRIBUTE ₹1,230 CRORE (APPROXIMATELY $137 MILLION) AT THE FIRST CLOSE. NAVER'S COMMITMENT IS EXPECTED TO BE BROADLY COMPARABLE.
WHICH SECTORS WILL THE UNICORN GROWTH FUND INVEST IN?
THE FUND WILL TARGET FOUR THEMES: TECHNOLOGY PLATFORMS (CONSUMER INTERNET, DIGITAL MARKETPLACES), CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY (DIGITALLY NATIVE BRANDS), AI AND SOFTWARE (GENERATIVE AI, ENTERPRISE SAAS, DEVELOPER TOOLS), AND DEEP TECH (SEMICONDUCTORS, ROBOTICS, SPACE TECHNOLOGY, ADVANCED MATERIALS).
WHAT EXPERTISE WILL PORTFOLIO COMPANIES RECEIVE FROM KRAFTON AND NAVER?
PORTFOLIO COMPANIES WILL GAIN ACCESS TO PRODUCT, AI, GAMING, AND PLATFORM EXPERTISE FROM KRAFTON AND NAVER. THEY WILL ALSO RECEIVE SUPPORT FOR ENTRY INTO KOREAN AND BROADER ASIAN MARKETS.
HOW MUCH HAS KRAFTON ALREADY INVESTED IN INDIA?
KRAFTON HAS INVESTED OVER $200 MILLION IN INDIA TO DATE. THIS INCLUDES BACKING COMPANIES SUCH AS CASHFREE, NODWIN GAMING, LOCO, PRATILIPI, KUKU FM, AND NAUTILUS MOBILE. THE COMPANY ALSO PLEDGED TO INVEST AT LEAST $50 MILLION ANNUALLY IN INDIA.
