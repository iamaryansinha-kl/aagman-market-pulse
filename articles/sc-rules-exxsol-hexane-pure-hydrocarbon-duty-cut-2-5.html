SC Rules Exxsol Hexane Pure Hydrocarbon, Cuts Customs Duty to 2.5%

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Supreme Court rules exxsol hexane is a pure hydrocarbon, reducing customs duty from 10% to 2.5%
  • 02/Reliance Industries contended n-Hexane purity at 63.93%; tribunal order upheld by Justices Kumar and Varale
  • 03/Revenue implications are significant for India's import-export trade status; customs department cannot be unjustly enriched

The Supreme Court on Monday ruled exxsol hexane is a pure hydrocarbon. Imported exxsol hexane will now attract a 2.5% customs duty. Previously, customs officials wanted it classified as motor spirit at 10%.

A bench of Justices Aravind Kumar and Prasanna B Varale delivered the ruling. The court upheld a tribunal order favoring lower duty on the product. The judgment impacts the nation's import-export status significantly.

Reliance Industries imports n-Hexane or Exxsol Hexane as a processing solvent. The customs department wanted it classified as petroleum oil. RIL argued it was a separate chemical compound under tariff headings.

RIL contended the major component n-Hexane remained at 63.93% purity. The company stated the presence of impurities did not change the composition. The show cause notice failed to prove n-Hexane was used as fuel.

The court said Exxsol hexane is a saturated acyclic hydrocarbon in pure commercial form. No other substance is deliberately added to classify it as a mixture with impurities. The department did not discharge its burden of proof.

The apex court said customs cannot be unjustly enriched due to wrong classification. It cited the Harmonized System Nomenclature Notes in its ruling. The revenue implications are far and wide for Indian trade.

THE SUPREME COURT RULED THAT EXXSOL HEXANE IS A PURE HYDROCARBON, NOT MOTOR SPIRIT. IMPORTED EXXSOL HEXANE WILL NOW ATTRACT A LOWER CUSTOMS DUTY OF 2.5% INSTEAD OF THE 10% LEVIED ON MOTOR SPIRIT.
WHICH COMPANIES IMPORT EXXSOL HEXANE AND WERE IMPACTED BY THIS RULING?
RELIANCE INDUSTRIES (RIL) IS AMONG THE COMPANIES THAT IMPORT EXXSOL HEXANE OR N-HEXANE. THE PRODUCT IS USED AS A PROCESSING SOLVENT IN VARIOUS INDUSTRIAL PROCESSES.
WHAT WAS THE CUSTOMS DEPARTMENT'S POSITION ON EXXSOL HEXANE CLASSIFICATION?
THE CUSTOMS DEPARTMENT WANTED EXXSOL HEXANE TO BE CLASSIFIED AS PETROLEUM OIL OR MOTOR SPIRIT, WHICH WOULD ATTRACT A 10% CUSTOMS DUTY. THE DEPARTMENT'S SHOW CAUSE NOTICE FAILED TO PROVE N-HEXANE WAS USED AS FUEL IN SPARK IGNITION ENGINES.
WHAT WAS RELIANCE INDUSTRIES' ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF LOWER DUTY?
RIL CONTENDED THAT EXXSOL HEXANE IS A PURE HYDROCARBON EXISTING AS A SEPARATE CHEMICAL COMPOUND UNDER CUSTOMS TARIFF HEADINGS. THE COMPANY ARGUED THAT THE MAJOR COMPONENT N-HEXANE REMAINED AT 63.93% AND IMPURITIES DID NOT CHANGE THE COMPOSITION.
WHAT IS THE BROADER IMPACT OF THIS SUPREME COURT RULING?
THE SUPREME COURT STATED THAT CUSTOMS CANNOT BE UNJUSTLY ENRICHED DUE TO WRONG CLASSIFICATION OF PRODUCTS. THE REVENUE IMPLICATIONS ARE FAR AND WIDE, HAVING SIGNIFICANT IMPACT ON THE IMPORT-EXPORT STATUS OF THE NATION.
