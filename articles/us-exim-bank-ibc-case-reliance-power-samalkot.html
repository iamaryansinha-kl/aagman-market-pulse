US Exim Bank Files ₹1,300 Cr IBC Case Against Reliance Power

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/US Exim Bank has filed a Section 7 IBC petition against Reliance Power alleging a $165.41 million default by subsidiary Samalkot Power Ltd
  • 02/Reliance Power shares fell 1.21% to ₹28.64 on April 30 after the company disclosed the development to exchanges on April 29
  • 03/Samalkot Power has contested the debt before the London Court of International Arbitration since June 2025, arguing the amount is not yet due

US Exim Bank has filed an insolvency application under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 against Reliance Power Ltd (NSE: RPOWER). The petition alleges a debt default of US$165.41 million, equivalent to over ₹1,300 crore.

The default relates to Samalkot Power Ltd, a subsidiary of Reliance Power. Reliance Power had provided a corporate guarantee for the loan. The company disclosed this development to stock exchanges after market hours on April 29, 2026.

Reliance Power shares traded at ₹28.64 on the NSE at 11:40 am on April 30. The stock was down ₹0.35 or 1.21%.

The company maintains the petition lacks legal merit. It stated that Samalkot Power had proactively filed arbitration proceedings on June 29, 2025 before the London Court of International Arbitration against US Exim Bank and Citibank N.A.

Samalkot Power is contesting that the debt is not yet due. The arbitration proceeding remains pending. Reliance Power said it has been legally advised that the Section 7 petition is not tenable.

The matter was first flagged to exchanges by the company on July 1, 2025. If the NCLT admits the plea, it could trigger the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process against Reliance Power.

This is the latest in a series of legal and financial challenges facing the Anil Ambani-promoted power company. Reliance Power said it will take all steps necessary, as advised by its legal counsel, to protect its interests.
