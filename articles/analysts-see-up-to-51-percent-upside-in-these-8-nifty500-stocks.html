Analysts See Up to 51% Upside in These 8 Nifty500 Stocks

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eight Nifty500 stocks show analyst consensus upside of 42% to 51%.
  • 02/Newgen Software and Lemon Tree Hotels top the list at 51% upside each.
  • 03/Strong Buy ratings cluster in IT, hospitality, real estate, and FMCG.

Brokerages have identified eight Nifty500 stocks with consensus analyst upside targets ranging from forty-two to fifty-one percent. These ratings reflect recent earnings updates and revised forward estimates across multiple sectors.

Newgen Software Technologies Ltd (NSE: NEWGEN) trades at ₹472 per share against a consensus broker target of ₹713. Seven analysts covering the stock maintain a Buy rating.

Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd (NSE: LEMONTREE) commands a Strong Buy consensus from twenty analysts. Their average target of ₹168 implies fifty-one percent upside from the current ₹111.

Info Edge (India) Ltd (NSE: NAUKRI) has a twelve-month consensus target of ₹1,392 from twenty analysts rating it Buy. The stock closed at ₹928, reflecting fifty percent implied upside.

JK Tyre & Industries Ltd (NSE: JKTYRE) carries a Buy rating from six analysts with a consensus target of ₹561. At ₹378, the implied upside stands at forty-eight percent.

Swiggy Ltd (NSE: SWIGGY) has twenty-seven analysts covering it with a Buy consensus and a target of ₹376. The current price of ₹255 implies forty-seven percent upside potential.

Aditya Birla Real Estate Ltd (NSE: ABREL) trades at ₹1,292 with a Strong Buy consensus from ten analysts. Their average target of ₹1,896 implies forty-seven percent upside.

Eternal Ltd (NSE: ETERNAL), formerly Zomato, has thirty-two analysts covering it with a Buy consensus. The consensus target of ₹347 against the current ₹241 implies forty-four percent upside.

Emami Ltd (NSE: EMAMI) has a Strong Buy consensus from twenty-three analysts with a target of ₹608. At ₹428, the implied upside is forty-two percent.

The list spans IT services, hospitality, internet platforms, auto ancillaries, food delivery, real estate, and consumer goods. Analysts have revised several targets after the March 2026 quarter earnings season.

Consensus targets are not guarantees of future returns. Execution risks, macroeconomic shifts, and sector-specific headwinds could delay or materially reduce projected gains.

Several names including Swiggy and Eternal trade on growth narratives rather than current earnings. Investors should weigh valuation premiums against the path to profitability.

The data reflects analyst opinions as of mid-May 2026. Investors should verify current prices and read latest brokerage notes before making decisions.
