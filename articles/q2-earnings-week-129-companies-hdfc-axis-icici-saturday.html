Q2 Earnings Week: HDFC Bank, Axis, ICICI Results on Rare Saturday

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/As many as 129 companies announce Q2 FY27 (July-September) results between October 12 and October 17, spanning IT, banking, FMCG and NBFC names
  • 02/The week closes on a rare Saturday session — HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and Yes Bank all report on October 17
  • 03/Bajaj Broking's Pabitro Mukherjee warns relentless FII selling and global headwinds will keep markets volatile, though record DII buying limits the downside

The second quarter of fiscal 2027 results season enters its busiest week. As many as 129 companies are set to announce Q2 FY27 (July-September) results between October 12 and October 17.

The lineup spans heavyweight private lenders HDFC Bank, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, IT majors HCL Tech and Wipro, FMCG major Nestle India and NBFC Jio Financial Services. Several others report through the week.

The earnings season kicked off last week with TCS announcing its results. Investors will track the upcoming announcements after the stock market snapped an eight-week losing streak last week, although multiple headwinds continue to warrant caution.

Monday, October 12: HCL Technologies opens the week — the second major IT services firm to report after TCS. RBL Bank and ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company also announce results.

Tuesday, October 13: Recently listed Hero Motors announces its first set of quarterly results. Tata Elxsi, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, Transformers and Rectifiers (India), and Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers also report.

Wednesday, October 14: Around 25 companies report, the heaviest midweek slate. PSU major BHEL, Groww parent Billionbrains Garage Ventures, Canara Bank, Tata Technologies, HDB Financial Services, ICICI Lombard General Insurance, Ceat, Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts and Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals (MRPL) are on the calendar.

Thursday, October 15: IT majors Wipro, Tech Mahindra and Persistent Systems report. FMCG major Nestle India and NBFC Jio Financial Services also announce. Polycab India, HDFC Life, Indian Railway Finance Corporation (IRFC), Angel One, Cyient DLM, HDFC Asset Management, Heritage Foods, IIFL Finance, Tata Communications, Oriental Hotels and L&T Finance round out the day.

Friday, October 16: LTI Mindtree, Federal Bank, Bajaj Housing Finance, Crompton Greaves and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank are among the notable reporters heading into the weekend.

Saturday, October 17: The standout day. Four heavyweight private lenders — HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and Yes Bank — report on a single day, a rare Saturday clustering. India Cements also announces results.

Why does the Saturday banking cluster matter? Private lenders carry the heaviest weight in the Nifty and Bank Nifty indices. Their commentary on loan growth, net interest margins and asset quality will set the tone for benchmark indices heading into the following week.

The backdrop is mixed. Pabitro Mukherjee, Deputy Vice President of Research at Bajaj Broking, said relentless FII selling and global headwinds are likely to keep markets volatile in the coming week, although record buying by domestic institutional investors should help limit the downside.

Mukherjee flagged the specific cues investors will watch: US and Indian inflation data, US bond yields, Brent crude prices and developments in US-Iran geopolitical tensions.

It is a busy week in the primary market too. Five companies are set to tap the primary market, led by two mainboard issues worth a combined Rs 1,414 crore.

For investors, the calendar offers a full read on India's corporate economy in one week: IT demand from HCL Tech and Wipro, consumption from Nestle India, and credit growth from the big four private lenders on Saturday.

Monday sets the technology tone. HCL Technologies will be only the second major IT services firm to report after TCS opened the season last week, and its numbers will be read as an early verdict on tech spending by global clients.

Tuesday brings a different kind of milestone. Hero Motors, the recently listed company, will announce its first set of quarterly results — the first time public investors get a look inside its books since listing.

Wednesday is the busiest single day, with around 25 companies reporting. The mix is striking: a PSU engineering major in BHEL, the parent of stock-broking platform Groww in Billionbrains Garage Ventures, and lenders including Canara Bank and HDB Financial Services all on the same day.

Thursday puts the IT sector back in focus. Wipro, Tech Mahindra and Persistent Systems report together, giving investors a three-way snapshot of how mid-tier and large-tier tech services are faring. FMCG bellwether Nestle India and Jio Financial Services round out the day's majors.

Friday is quieter but not empty. LTI Mindtree and Federal Bank lead a list that also includes Bajaj Housing Finance, Crompton Greaves and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank.

Saturday is the week's real event. Four heavyweight private lenders — HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and Yes Bank — report on the same day, an unusual clustering that will effectively set the tone for banking stocks into the following week.

GLOSSARY: Earnings season: The quarterly period when listed companies announce their financial results. DII (domestic institutional investor): Indian institutions such as mutual funds and insurers that buy and sell stocks in local markets. FII (foreign institutional investor): Overseas investors who trade in Indian equities and bonds. Mainboard IPO: A public offering listed on the primary exchange, as opposed to a smaller SME listing. NBFC (non-banking financial company): A lender that does not hold a full banking licence and cannot accept public deposits.
