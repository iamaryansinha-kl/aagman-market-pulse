Sensex Surges 940 Points, Nifty Hits 24,330 on Global Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Sensex rallies 940.73 points to 77,958.52 and Nifty gains 298.15 points to 24,330.95 on May 7, 2026
  • 02/Crude oil prices slip below $110 and US pauses Strait of Hormuz action, easing Middle East supply fears
  • 03/Derivatives data signals caution with PCR at 0.61 and heavy call writing at Nifty 24,200-24,300 levels

Indian equity markets opened sharply higher on May 7, 2026. The BSE Sensex surged 940.73 points to 77,958.52. The Nifty 50 gained 298.15 points to 24,330.95.

The rally was driven by positive global cues and softer crude oil prices. Brent crude slipped below $110 per barrel. Investor sentiment improved after the US indicated a pause in aggressive action around the Strait of Hormuz.

Gift Nifty signalled gains of around 100 points ahead of the open. Major Asian markets including Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore and Taiwan traded strongly higher.

However, derivatives data reflected underlying caution among traders. The put-call ratio stood near 0.61, indicating a cautious-to-bearish undertone. Heavy call writing at Nifty 24,200-24,300 levels suggests strong resistance ahead.

Immediate support for Nifty lies at 24,000 and 23,800 based on put bases. India VIX hovered near 18, reflecting controlled volatility and expectations of range-bound movement.

On the commodities front, MCX crude oil fell ₹684 to ₹9,014. Gold rose ₹2,422 to ₹152,175. Silver surged ₹9,284 to ₹253,600.

Several stocks buzzed in early trade. Hero MotoCorp, Motilal Oswal, Thyrocare, Motherson and Zaggle were in focus. ADIO backed Motherson in Abu Dhabi, while Clean Max revised its Apple deal.

Brigade announced a bonus issue and Thyrocare expanded its genomics portfolio. Analysts note India's domestic macroeconomic indicators continue to support equities despite lingering global volatility.
