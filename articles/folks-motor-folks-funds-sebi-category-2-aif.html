Folks Motor Registers Folks Funds With SEBI as Category II AIF

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Folks Motor registers Folks Funds with SEBI as a Category II Alternative Investment Fund under Registration No. IN/AIF2/25-26/2100
  • 02/The fund targets a ₹500 crore corpus with a sector-agnostic strategy focusing on EV ecosystem, warehousing, data centres, and real estate
  • 03/Folks Funds will deploy capital via equity, debt, and convertible instruments through portfolio companies and SPVs

Folks Motor has registered Folks Funds with SEBI as a Category II Alternative Investment Fund. The registration number is IN/AIF2/25-26/2100.

The fund will adopt a sector-agnostic investment strategy. It will target high-growth opportunities across warehousing infrastructure, the electric vehicle ecosystem, data centres, and real estate.

Folks Funds will invest through a diversified mix of equity, debt, and convertible instruments. Capital will be deployed via portfolio companies and Special Purpose Vehicles rather than direct ownership of physical assets.

The fund has a target corpus of ₹500 crore. It secured ₹100 crore in its first close in May 2025 and aims for full closure by end of 2025.

Folks Funds is India's first ESG-focused investment fund dedicated to the EV supply chain. It targets a minimum of 50% domestic value addition in its portfolio companies.

A significant allocation will go to "The Blue IP," a warehousing and industrial park in Sonipat, Haryana. This facility will support the EV ecosystem with manufacturing and logistics infrastructure.

India's EV market sold over 2 million units in 2024. This represents a 25.4% year-on-year growth, underscoring the opportunity in the sector.

Nikhil Anand Khurana, Managing Director of Folks Motor and CIO of Folks Funds, said: "We believe the next decade of value creation will be driven by sectors underpinning India's energy transition, industrial expansion and logistics backbone. Through Folks Funds, we are defining the EV supply chain as an investible asset class."

Category II AIFs constitute 55-60% of total AUM among all AIF categories in India. They are close-ended funds with investment periods typically ranging from 2 to 5 years.
