Alcoholic Beverage Industry Seeks 15-20% Price Hike Amid West Asia Crisis Costs

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CIABC and BAI have asked State governments to allow alcoholic beverage suppliers a price increase of 15-20% to offset rising input costs.
  • 02/Rising can and bottle costs stem from supply chain disruptions triggered by the ongoing West Asia crisis affecting global trade routes.
  • 03/State-level excise policies will determine whether IMFL, wine, and beer manufacturers receive interim pricing relief.

Alcoholic beverage makers have sought a 15-20% price hike from State governments. The request comes from the Confederation of Indian Alcoholic Beverage Companies (CIABC) and the Brewers Association of India (BAI).

The industry bodies want revisions to prices of IMFL, wine, and beer products. They have also asked for interim relief measures to help manufacturers cope.

Rising can and bottle costs are the primary driver behind the plea. Supply chain disruptions triggered by the West Asia crisis have pushed up packaging material prices.

India imports a significant share of its glass bottle requirements. Any disruption to West Asian trade corridors affects freight and raw material availability.

The West Asia crisis has already impacted global shipping lanes. Brent crude has traded near $110 per barrel, keeping pressure on manufacturing and logistics costs.

The industry argues that without a price revision, profit margins will compress further. Several smaller distilleries and breweries may face working capital stress.

State governments control excise policy and retail pricing for alcohol. Each State will need to evaluate the request independently through its excise departments.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT PRICE INCREASE HAS THE ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE INDUSTRY REQUESTED?
THE CONFEDERATION OF INDIAN ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE COMPANIES (CIABC) AND THE BREWERS ASSOCIATION OF INDIA (BAI) HAVE ASKED STATE GOVERNMENTS TO ALLOW SUPPLIERS A PRICE INCREASE OF 15-20% TO PARTIALLY OFFSET THE IMPACT OF RISING INPUT COSTS.
WHY DOES THE INDUSTRY NEED A PRICE HIKE NOW?
SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTIONS TRIGGERED BY THE WEST ASIA CRISIS HAVE SHARPLY INCREASED THE COST OF CANS AND GLASS BOTTLES. THE INDUSTRY FACES COMPRESSED MARGINS AS PACKAGING MATERIAL COSTS RISE WHILE RETAIL PRICES REMAIN FROZEN BY STATE EXCISE POLICIES.
WHICH ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE CATEGORIES ARE COVERED IN THE REQUEST?
THE REQUEST COVERS INDIAN MADE FOREIGN LIQUOR (IMFL), WINE, AND BEER PRODUCTS. BOTH CIABC AND BAI HAVE SOUGHT PRICE REVISIONS AND INTERIM RELIEF MEASURES ACROSS THESE CATEGORIES.
WHO DECIDES WHETHER THE PRICE HIKE WILL BE GRANTED?
STATE GOVERNMENTS AND THEIR RESPECTIVE EXCISE DEPARTMENTS CONTROL ALCOHOL PRICING IN INDIA. EACH STATE MUST EVALUATE THE REQUEST INDEPENDENTLY BASED ON ITS OWN EXCISE POLICY AND REVENUE CONSIDERATIONS.
WHAT IS THE WEST ASIA CRISIS IMPACT ON INDIAN MANUFACTURERS?
THE WEST ASIA CRISIS HAS DISRUPTED GLOBAL SHIPPING LANES AND PUSHED ENERGY COSTS HIGHER. BRENT CRUDE HAS TRADED NEAR $110 PER BARREL, RAISING FREIGHT AND RAW MATERIAL COSTS FOR MANUFACTURERS THAT RELY ON IMPORTED PACKAGING MATERIALS AND ENERGY-INTENSIVE PRODUCTION.
