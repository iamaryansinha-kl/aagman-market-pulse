Small & Midcap Stocks Surge Up to 7% on May 19; IT Leads Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Small and midcap stocks surged up to 7% on Tuesday, with Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 both gaining over 1.4%
  • 02/Coforge Ltd led midcap gains with a 6% jump; Ola Electric Mobility topped smallcaps with a 7% rally
  • 03/IT stocks rebounded on attractive valuations after the recent AI-led crash; macro concerns on growth and rupee persist

Small and midcap stocks surged sharply on Tuesday, May 19, outperforming benchmark indices. The Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 indices gained over 1.4% each. The largecap Nifty 50 was up a modest 0.5%.

Coforge Ltd (NSE: COFORGE) led the midcap rally, jumping around 6%. IT peers Mphasis, Oracle Financial Services Software (OFSS), and Persistent Systems followed with 5% gains each.

IT stocks rebounded as persistent rupee depreciation and cheaper valuations after the recent AI-led crash boosted investor sentiment. Geojit Investments maintains a Buy call on Coforge with a target price of Rs 1,670 per share.

"As per market consensus, Coforge is trading at a 1-year forward P/E of 23x (3-year average P/E of 34x), offering an attractive valuation," said Geojit Investments. The brokerage highlighted the company's strong order book and AI-led capabilities.

Ola Electric Mobility (NSE: OLAELEC) was the top smallcap gainer, rallying nearly 7%. FirstCry-parent Brainbees Solutions, Angel One, and Tata Technologies jumped over 5%.

VK Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments, warned that macro concerns on growth, inflation, and currency depreciation persist. He recommended focusing on pharmaceuticals, power, and defence stocks as the least affected by a potential slowdown.

Q4 results have been broadly better than expected, indicating the economy had started recovering before the energy crisis impact. A quick resolution of the Hormuz crisis could lead to a faster-than-expected recovery.
