Gold Rebounds 0.7% to $4,573 as Oil Above $120 Caps Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Spot gold rises 0.7% to $4,573.09 per ounce after falling to its lowest level since March 31 in the prior session
  • 02/Brent crude above $120 per barrel keeps inflation fears alive; Fed holds rates steady in most divided decision since 1992
  • 03/Traders price out Fed rate cuts for 2026; markets now see 30% chance of a hike by March 2027, up from 5% a day prior

Spot gold climbed 0.7% to $4,573.09 per ounce as of 0229 GMT on April 30. The metal bounced after dropping to its lowest level since March 31 in the previous session.

U.S. gold futures for June delivery rose 0.5% to $4,585.10. Dip buyers stepped in after the recent selloff.

"Gold is representing a value proposition for traders at current levels. So, dip buying is playing a part in gold's recovery efforts today," said Tim Waterer, chief market analyst at KCM Trade.

Gains remained capped as Brent crude hovered above $120 a barrel. Deadlocked U.S.-Iran negotiations have raised fears of prolonged disruptions to Middle Eastern supply.

Elevated oil prices add to inflation risks, increasing the likelihood of higher interest rates. While gold is a traditional inflation hedge, high rates weigh on its appeal as a non-yielding asset.

The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady on Wednesday in its most divided decision since 1992. Three officials dissented from a policy statement that noted rising inflation concerns.

Traders are now pricing out Fed rate cuts entirely for 2026. Markets see a 30% chance of a rate hike by March 2027, sharply up from roughly 5% a day prior.

In India, MCX gold traded at 149,054, down 973. 24K gold in India stood at 15,043 per gram, while 22K gold was at 13,789 per gram.

Spot silver rose 1.6% to $72.63 per ounce, platinum gained 1.8% to $1,913.86, and palladium was up 0.4% at $1,465.14.
