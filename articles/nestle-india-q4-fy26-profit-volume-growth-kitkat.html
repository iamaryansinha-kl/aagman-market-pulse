Nestle India Q4 FY26: Profit Up 27% to ₹1,111 Cr, India Becomes Largest KitKat Market

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Nestle India Q4 FY26 net profit surges 27.2% YoY to ₹1,110.9 crore on revenue of ₹6,747.79 crore
  • 02/India becomes the largest global market for KitKat in March quarter, ahead of the UK
  • 03/CMD Manish Tiwary warns Q3 FY27 will need close watch due to West Asia conflict and below-normal monsoon risks

Nestle India Ltd (NSE: NESTLEIND) reported its strongest quarterly results in nine years for Q4 FY26. Net profit jumped 27.2% year-on-year to ₹1,110.9 crore.

Revenue from operations rose 22.6% to ₹6,747.79 crore, beating analyst consensus estimates by 7.9%. The EBITDA margin expanded sharply to 26.3%, well above the 22.7-25.1% forecast range.

The company declared a final dividend of ₹5 per share, taking total FY26 payout to ₹12 per equity share. The record date is fixed at July 10, 2026.

For the full year FY26, standalone revenue stood at ₹23,071.5 crore and net profit at ₹3,544.6 crore. Cash generated from operations reached ₹5,047.6 crore.

India became the largest market for KitKat globally in the March quarter, overtaking the UK. Confectionery remains Nestle India's fastest-growing category, followed closely by coffee and prepared dishes.

Rural distribution expanded aggressively in the past year. Wholesale hub count rose to 45,000 from 25,000, covering 220,000 villages. Rural now contributes 22-27% of sales and is growing faster than urban.

The company absorbed all cost inflation so far and has not passed price hikes to consumers. Rising packaging costs linked to the West Asia conflict remain a concern alongside predictions of below-normal monsoons.

"We are fortunate that from the top-end the quick-commerce piece is growing, on the other end, rural is growing. So I'm equally excited by both the opportunities" - Manish Tiwary, Chairman and Managing Director, Nestle India.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WERE NESTLE INDIA'S Q4 FY26 REVENUE AND PROFIT NUMBERS?
NESTLE INDIA REPORTED Q4 FY26 REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS OF ₹6,747.79 CRORE, UP 22.6% YEAR-ON-YEAR. NET PROFIT ROSE 27.2% TO ₹1,110.9 CRORE, WHILE EBITDA MARGIN EXPANDED TO 26.3%.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL DIVIDEND DECLARED BY NESTLE INDIA FOR FY26?
THE BOARD DECLARED A FINAL DIVIDEND OF ₹5 PER SHARE FOR FY26. ALONG WITH THE INTERIM DIVIDEND OF ₹7 PER SHARE, THE TOTAL FY26 DIVIDEND PAYOUT STANDS AT ₹12 PER EQUITY SHARE.
WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF INDIA BECOMING THE LARGEST KITKAT MARKET?
IN THE MARCH QUARTER, INDIA OVERTOOK THE UK TO BECOME THE LARGEST GLOBAL MARKET FOR KITKAT. THIS REFLECTS STRONG VOLUME-DRIVEN GROWTH IN THE CONFECTIONERY CATEGORY, WHICH IS NESTLE INDIA'S FASTEST-GROWING SEGMENT.
HOW HAS NESTLE INDIA EXPANDED ITS RURAL PRESENCE?
THE COMPANY GREW ITS RURAL WHOLESALE DISTRIBUTOR HUBS TO 45,000 FROM 25,000 IN ONE YEAR. IT NOW COVERS 220,000 VILLAGES WITH TECH-ENABLED DISTRIBUTION. RURAL CONTRIBUTION TO SALES IS CURRENTLY 22-27% AND GROWING FASTER THAN URBAN.
WHAT RISKS DID THE CMD FLAG FOR THE COMING QUARTERS?
CMD MANISH TIWARY HIGHLIGHTED RISING PACKAGING COSTS DUE TO THE WEST ASIA CONFLICT AND PREDICTIONS OF BELOW-NORMAL MONSOONS AS KEY RISKS. HE STATED THE INDUSTRY WILL NEED TO WATCH Q3 FY27 CLOSELY FOR THE IMPACT OF THESE FACTORS ON DEMAND AND COSTS.
