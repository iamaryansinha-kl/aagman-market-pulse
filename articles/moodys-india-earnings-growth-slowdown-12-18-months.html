Moody's Warns India Earnings Growth To Slow Down Over Next 12-18 Months

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Moody's Ratings warns India's corporate earnings growth will slow over the next 12-18 months due to rising input costs and supply disruptions
  • 02/Capex growth expected to moderate to 4% over next two years, down from 11% CAGR recorded between FY2022 and FY2026
  • 03/Airlines, autos, metals, cement and oil marketing companies face the steepest earnings pressure from commodity prices and weak demand

Moody's Ratings has warned that India's corporate earnings growth will slow over the next 12 to 18 months. Rising input costs, supply-chain disruptions, and rupee depreciation are the primary drivers.

The agency identified three key risks. First, the US-Iran conflict has pushed up commodity prices and created supply disruptions for crude oil, natural gas, and fertilisers.

Second, AI adoption is creating labour market uncertainty. Job displacement and skill mismatches in services could slow income growth and hurt consumption demand over time.

Third, companies are delaying capital spending to preserve liquidity. Capex growth is expected to moderate to 4% over the next two years, down sharply from 11% CAGR between FY2022 and FY2026.

Airlines and automobiles remain exposed to elevated crude oil prices. Passenger vehicle sales have stayed resilient, but higher fuel costs may weaken demand for internal combustion engine vehicles.

State-run oil marketing companies face severe earnings pressure. Retail fuel prices have not kept pace with higher feedstock costs, squeezing margins at Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum and Hindustan Petroleum.

Steel, cement and metals demand could slow as weak consumer sentiment delays real estate investments. Companies like Tata Steel, JSW Steel and UltraTech Cement may face softer orders ahead.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHY DOES MOODY'S EXPECT SLOWER EARNINGS GROWTH IN INDIA?
MOODY'S RATINGS WARNS THAT RISING INPUT COSTS, SUPPLY-CHAIN DISRUPTIONS FROM THE US-IRAN CONFLICT, RUPEE DEPRECIATION, LABOUR MARKET UNCERTAINTY FROM AI ADOPTION, AND DELAYED CAPITAL SPENDING WILL WEIGH ON INDIA'S CORPORATE EARNINGS OVER THE NEXT 12 TO 18 MONTHS.
HOW MUCH WILL CAPITAL EXPENDITURE GROWTH SLOW DOWN?
CAPITAL EXPENDITURE GROWTH IS EXPECTED TO MODERATE TO AROUND 4% OVER THE NEXT TWO YEARS, COMPARED WITH AN 11% COMPOUND ANNUAL GROWTH RATE RECORDED BETWEEN FISCAL YEAR 2022 AND 2026.
WHICH SECTORS ARE MOST VULNERABLE TO EARNINGS PRESSURE?
AIRLINES, AUTOMOBILES, OIL MARKETING COMPANIES, STEEL, CEMENT AND METALS ARE MOST EXPOSED. ELEVATED CRUDE OIL PRICES HURT AIRLINES AND AUTO MAKERS, WHILE WEAK REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT REDUCES DEMAND FOR STEEL AND CEMENT.
HOW DOES AI ADOPTION AFFECT INDIA'S EARNINGS OUTLOOK?
MOODY'S FLAGGED THAT ACCELERATING AI ADOPTION RAISES THE RISK OF JOB DISPLACEMENT AND SKILL MISMATCHES, PARTICULARLY IN SERVICES AND WHITE-COLLAR SEGMENTS. THIS COULD SLOW INCOME GROWTH AND HURT CONSUMPTION DEMAND OVER TIME.
WHAT IS THE IMPACT ON OIL MARKETING COMPANIES?
STATE-RUN OIL MARKETING COMPANIES INCLUDING INDIAN OIL CORPORATION, BHARAT PETROLEUM AND HINDUSTAN PETROLEUM ARE WITNESSING SEVERE EARNINGS PRESSURE. RETAIL FUEL PRICES HAVE NOT INCREASED IN LINE WITH HIGHER FEEDSTOCK COSTS, SQUEEZING THEIR MARGINS.
