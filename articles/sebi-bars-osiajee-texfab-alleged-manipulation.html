Sebi Bars Osiajee Texfab for Alleged Manipulation

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Sebi has taken action against another alleged market manipulator.

The Securities and Exchange Board of India has barred Osiajee Texfab and its promoters from accessing the securities market, citing irregularities in trading patterns that artificially inflated the stock price. The action is the latest in the regulator's ongoing crackdown on market manipulation and price rigging.

Osiajee Texfab is a small textile company listed on the stock exchanges. The company and its promoters were allegedly involved in creating a false market for the stock through coordinated trading, circular transactions, and misleading disclosures.

Sebi's investigation found that certain entities connected to the promoters were trading the stock in a coordinated manner. The trades were designed to create the impression of genuine buying interest, attracting unsuspecting retail investors who then bought at inflated prices.

The regulator used its surveillance systems to detect the patterns. Sebi's sophisticated market monitoring tools track unusual price movements, trading concentrations, and linked entities. The system flagged Osiajee Texfab for detailed investigation.

The promoters have been barred from buying, selling, or dealing in securities. The company may also face restrictions on raising capital, and existing shareholders who suffered losses could potentially seek legal remedies.

The action is part of a broader Sebi crackdown. The regulator has been increasingly aggressive in pursuing market manipulation, using both surveillance technology and whistleblower inputs to identify wrongdoing. Several small-cap companies have been targeted in recent months.
