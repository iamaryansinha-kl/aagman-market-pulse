bb.q Chicken India Entry: 150 Outlets by 2031, Bengaluru Launch June

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/bb.q Chicken opens first two India outlets in Bengaluru in June 2026, with Hyderabad following in July
  • 02/Genesis BBQ Group targets 150 Indian stores by 2031 and 15-20% of global business from India in five years
  • 03/Korean food wave is accelerating in India with Nestle, HUL, ITC and KFC all launching Korean-inspired products

bb.q Chicken, the flagship brand of South Korea's Genesis bb.q Group, will open its first two India outlets in Bengaluru this June. The brand has named SrikG Group as its master franchisee for the Indian market.

A Hyderabad outlet will follow in July 2026. Stores in Chennai, Kochi, Vizag, and Vellore are planned, taking the total to 7-8 outlets by year-end.

Globally, bb.q Chicken operates over 2,300 stores in South Korea and nearly 1,000 overseas. The US is currently its largest international market.

The group plans to scale to around 30 Indian stores by 2027. Over the next four years, it is targeting more than 60 outlets across southern markets before expanding north.

"If there are three strongest countries in the world, it would be the US, China, and India," said Honggeun Yoon, Chairman of Genesis BBQ Group. He cited India's large, young population as a key growth driver.

Duhyeon Yun (Chris), Global CEO, said India could contribute 15-20% of the group's global business within five years. The target is driven by a young, digitally savvy consumer base and growing exposure to global food trends.

Korean cuisine is rapidly gaining traction in India beyond niche appeal. FMCG majors Nestle, HUL, and ITC have launched Korean-inspired products, while QSR player KFC has introduced Korean-flavoured fried chicken.

The real challenge will be adapting bb.q Chicken's premium fried chicken format to India's highly competitive and price-sensitive quick-service restaurant market.
