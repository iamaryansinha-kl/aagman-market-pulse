India Overtakes UK as Sri Lanka's Second-Largest Export Market in 2026

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India overtakes the UK as Sri Lanka's second-largest export destination with USD 364.15 million in exports during January-April 2026
  • 02/Sri Lanka's total exports reached USD 5,784.38 million in Jan-April 2026, reflecting 4.3% growth over the same period in 2025
  • 03/The US remains the top destination accounting for 22% of merchandise exports; apparel and tea sectors face headwinds

India has surpassed the United Kingdom to become Sri Lanka's second-largest export market. The shift took place during January-April 2026, according to the Export Development Board of Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka's exports to India reached USD 364.15 million in the first four months of 2026. This marks an 8.9 per cent increase compared to the previous period.

However, April 2026 alone showed a marginal 1.41 per cent year-on-year decline in exports to India. The monthly fluctuation signals uneven momentum in bilateral trade.

The United States remains Sri Lanka's largest export destination, accounting for approximately 22 per cent of merchandise exports. US-bound shipments stood at USD 196.37 million in April 2026, up 3.15 per cent year-on-year.

Sri Lanka's total exports, combining merchandise and services, touched USD 1,380.93 million in April 2026. This reflects a solid 6 per cent year-on-year growth for the month.

On a cumulative basis, total exports for January-April 2026 stand at USD 5,784.38 million. The figure represents 4.3 per cent growth over the corresponding period in 2025.

Coconut-based products surged 23.49 per cent year-on-year in April 2026. Activated carbon, a key value-added product, jumped 58.32 per cent to USD 20.55 million.

Apparel and textiles declined 4.88 per cent to USD 346.67 million. Tea exports also fell 6.82 per cent to USD 100.02 million, dragged by weak demand from Iraq, Libya, and the UAE.
