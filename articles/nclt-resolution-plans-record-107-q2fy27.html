NCLT Approves Record 107 Resolution Plans Worth ₹11,000 Crore in Q2

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NCLT approved 107 resolution plans worth over ₹11,000 crore in Q2 FY2026-27 — the highest quarterly count since the Tribunal's inception
  • 02/Q2 approvals nearly doubled from 60 in the year-ago second quarter; H1 FY2026-27 total reached 185 plans
  • 03/A new case-listing framework prioritises older pending matters, including IBC admissions and resolution plan approvals, to sustain the pace

The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) approved 107 Resolution Plans in the second quarter of FY2026-27, the three months ended September 30, 2026. The aggregate approved plan value exceeded ₹11,000 crore, according to an official statement.

This is the highest number of resolution plans approved in any quarter since the Tribunal was established. It is also the highest ever count for a second quarter — nearly double the 60 plans approved in Q2 FY2025-26.

The acceleration is visible across recent years. Earlier second quarters saw 57 approvals in FY2024-25, 82 in FY2023-24, 47 in FY2022-23 and just 20 in FY2021-22. The Q2 tally is up roughly 78% from a year earlier.

The pace builds on a strong first quarter. The Tribunal approved 78 plans in Q1 FY2026-27, taking the first-half total to 185 — the fastest opening half since the insolvency regime began.

The cumulative number of resolution plans approved by the NCLT across all Benches has reached 1,735 as on September-end 2026. The H1 run rate signals the resolution engine is now operating at record throughput.

For creditors, the speed matters as much as the volume. Resolution plans cleared quickly generally deliver better recovery values than liquidation, where realisations are typically lower and stretched over longer periods. For banks carrying stressed exposures, that gap directly affects recovery ratios and provisioning outcomes.

Distressed companies gain too. Faster approvals mean quicker exits from insolvency proceedings, earlier release of capital by resolution applicants, and less erosion of enterprise value from prolonged litigation. The watch item for Q3 is whether the run rate holds — a second strong quarter would confirm the backlog is structurally clearing.

"Together with 78 Resolution Plans approved during the first quarter of the current financial year, the Tribunal has approved 185 Resolution Plans during the first half of FY 2026-27" - NCLT official statement. The Tribunal has also introduced a new framework for registration and listing of cases, aimed at greater uniformity in Registry practices and more systematic Cause List preparation. It explicitly prioritises older pending matters, including IBC admission matters and applications relating to approval of Resolution Plans — a design meant to keep the current momentum intact heading into Q3.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
HOW MANY RESOLUTION PLANS DID THE NCLT APPROVE IN Q2 FY2026-27?
THE NCLT APPROVED 107 RESOLUTION PLANS IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF FY2026-27, THE HIGHEST QUARTERLY COUNT SINCE THE TRIBUNAL'S INCEPTION. THESE PLANS CARRIED AN AGGREGATE APPROVED PLAN VALUE OF MORE THAN ₹11,000 CRORE, ACCORDING TO AN OFFICIAL STATEMENT FROM THE TRIBUNAL.
IS Q2 FY2026-27 A RECORD QUARTER FOR RESOLUTION PLAN APPROVALS?
YES. THE 107 APPROVALS REPRESENT THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF RESOLUTION PLANS APPROVED IN ANY QUARTER SINCE THE NCLT'S INCEPTION. IT IS ALSO THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF RESOLUTION PLANS APPROVED IN ANY SECOND QUARTER SINCE THE TRIBUNAL WAS ESTABLISHED, SURPASSING EVERY COMPARABLE PERIOD ON RECORD.
HOW DOES THE Q2 FY2026-27 TALLY COMPARE WITH PREVIOUS SECOND QUARTERS?
THE NCLT APPROVED 60 PLANS IN Q2 FY2025-26, 57 IN FY2024-25, 82 IN FY2023-24, 47 IN FY2022-23 AND 20 IN FY2021-22. THE LATEST QUARTER'S 107 APPROVALS ARE NEARLY DOUBLE THE YEAR-AGO SECOND QUARTER FIGURE OF 60, AND WELL ABOVE EVERY PRIOR Q2 ON RECORD.
WHAT IS THE TOTAL NUMBER OF RESOLUTION PLANS APPROVED BY THE NCLT TO DATE?
AS ON SEPTEMBER-END 2026, THE CUMULATIVE NUMBER OF RESOLUTION PLANS APPROVED BY THE NCLT ACROSS ALL BENCHES STOOD AT 1,735. THIS TOTAL INCLUDES 185 PLANS APPROVED DURING THE FIRST HALF OF FY2026-27 ALONE — 78 IN Q1 AND 107 IN Q2 — MAKING THE OPENING HALF OF THE CURRENT FINANCIAL YEAR THE STRONGEST IN THE TRIBUNAL'S HISTORY.
WHAT DOES THE NEW CASE-LISTING FRAMEWORK INTRODUCED BY THE NCLT DO?
THE TRIBUNAL HAS INTRODUCED A NEW FRAMEWORK FOR REGISTRATION AND LISTING OF CASES, ANNOUNCED IN THE SAME OFFICIAL STATEMENT AS THE Q2 DATA. THE OBJECTIVE IS TO BRING GREATER UNIFORMITY IN REGISTRY PRACTICES AND ENSURE MORE SYSTEMATIC PREPARATION OF CAUSE LISTS. THE FRAMEWORK PROVIDES FOR APPROPRIATE PRIORITISATION OF OLDER PENDING MATTERS, INCLUDING PRIORITY TO INSOLVENCY AND BANKRUPTCY CODE (IBC) ADMISSION MATTERS AND APPLICATIONS RELATING TO APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION PLANS — A DESIGN MEANT TO KEEP THE CURRENT MOMENTUM IN PLAN CLEARANCES INTACT. GLOSSARY: NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal): India's specialised tribunal handling insolvency and company-law matters, including approval of resolution plans under the IBC. Resolution Plan: A revival proposal for a distressed company under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, typically offering creditors a share of dues in exchange for keeping the business running. IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code): India's 2016 law consolidating insolvency rules, giving lenders a time-bound process to resolve stressed loans or liquidate assets. Cause List: The daily schedule of cases a tribunal bench hears. A systematic cause list ensures older matters get heard rather than pushed back.
