Bitcoin Selling Pressure Builds; $400M Liquidated in 24 Hours

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bitcoin and Ethereum extended losses as macroeconomic concerns and ETF outflows weighed on investor sentiment
  • 02/Over $400 million in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated within a 24-hour period
  • 03/Markets remain vulnerable despite easing U.S.-Iran tensions; traders should watch ETF flow data and support levels

Bitcoin and Ethereum extended losses on May 23, 2026. Macroeconomic concerns and ETF outflows weighed heavily on investor sentiment.

More than $400 million in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated in a single day. The scale of liquidations signals significant downside pressure across the market.

Geopolitical uncertainty continues to trouble investors despite easing U.S.-Iran tensions. Markets have not fully absorbed the improvement in diplomatic relations.

ETF outflows have added to the selling pressure. Institutional flows remain a critical driver of short-term price action.

The liquidations impacted both long and short positions. High leverage in the derivatives market amplified the price swings.

Crypto markets remain vulnerable to macro shocks. Traders should monitor ETF flow data and key technical support levels.
