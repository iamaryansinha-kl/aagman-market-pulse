Polycab India Surges 5% to Record High on FY26 Results; Analysts Raise Targets

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Polycab India shares surge 4.93% to hit a fresh 52-week high of ₹8,940 after posting record FY26 revenue of ₹28,884 crore, up 29% YoY
  • 02/Full-year PAT rises 32% to ₹2,708 crore; Q4 revenue grows 27% to ₹8,865 crore while PAT climbs 7% to ₹786 crore, beating estimates
  • 03/Motilal Oswal and JM Financial raise price targets to ₹9,800 and ₹9,700 respectively; dividend of ₹47 per share proposed for FY26

Polycab India Ltd (NSE: POLYCAB) shares jumped nearly 5 per cent on the NSE on May 7, 2026. The stock touched a fresh 52-week high of ₹8,940 before easing to ₹8,830.

The surge follows FY26 results announced on May 6, which marked the company's highest-ever annual revenue and profit. Consolidated revenue hit ₹28,884 crore, crossing the ₹285 billion mark for the first time.

Full-year PAT rose 32 per cent to ₹2,708 crore, while Q4 revenue grew 27 per cent to ₹8,865 crore. Q4 PAT came in at ₹786 crore, up 7 per cent and ahead of analyst estimates.

Brokerages maintained buy ratings with upgraded targets. Motilal Oswal raised its target to ₹9,800, valuing the stock at 40x FY28 earnings, up from ₹9,350.

JM Financial set a revised target of ₹9,700 at 42x March 2028 estimated EPS, up from ₹9,200. Both lifted FY27 and FY28 EPS estimates by 3-5 per cent.

Market share in the organised cables and wires segment improved to 30-31 per cent in FY26 from 26-27 per cent the previous year. This represents a gain of roughly 300-400 basis points.

The board proposed a dividend of ₹47 per share for FY26, up from ₹36.4 in FY25. The payout ratio stands at 27.2 per cent, and net cash is ₹4,190 crore.

The stock has returned 50 per cent over the past year. It outperformed the Nifty Midcap 50 index, which gained 13 per cent in the same period.
