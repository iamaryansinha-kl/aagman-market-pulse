Pine Labs Q4 Results: Rs 59 Crore Profit on 17% Revenue Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Pine Labs reports consolidated net profit of Rs 59 crore in Q4 FY26, reversing Rs 29 crore loss in the year-ago quarter
  • 02/Revenue from operations rose 17% to Rs 701 crore; FY26 revenue up 19% to Rs 2,711 crore
  • 03/Company recorded first full year of profitability with Rs 113 crore PAT; stock trades below IPO price

Pine Labs Ltd posted a consolidated net profit of Rs 59 crore in Q4 FY26. The result marks a turnaround from the Rs 29 crore net loss recorded in Q4 FY25.

Revenue from operations grew 17% year-on-year to Rs 701 crore. This compares with Rs 599 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.

On a sequential basis, profit after tax jumped 40% from Rs 42 crore in Q3 FY26. However, topline slipped nearly 6% from Rs 744 crore in the October-December quarter.

For the full financial year FY26, the company reported revenue of Rs 2,711 crore. This represents a 19% increase over the Rs 2,274 crore posted in FY25.

The bottom line for FY26 stood at Rs 113 crore. This marks Pine Labs' first full year of profitability, reversing the Rs 145 crore loss in FY25.

The company recorded its highest-ever quarterly operating cash flow of Rs 676 crore in Q4. Full-year operating cash flows reached Rs 395 crore, growing eightfold year-on-year.

Pine Labs processed $194 billion in gross transaction value during FY26. UPI volumes grew 68% while the platform handled over 20 million daily transactions.

The company's shares closed at Rs 146.20 on May 25. The stock trades below its IPO price band of Rs 210-221. Shares gained 2.35% ahead of the results.

"These numbers tell us we are winning transactions, winning merchants, and winning in new markets," said Amrish Rau, Chief Executive Officer, Pine Labs. "When GTV grows at 50% while revenue grows at 19%, it reflects the platform depth we have built."
