Rupee Crashes to Record Low of 95.23 Against US Dollar on May 4

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Rupee fell 39 paise to close at record low of 95.23 against US dollar, breaching previous all-time low.
  • 02/Brent crude oil hovering around $108-110 per barrel due to West Asia tensions at Strait of Hormuz.
  • 03/Watch RBI intervention and FII flow reversal; forex reserves at $640 billion provide buffer.

The Indian rupee crashed to an all-time low of 95.23 against the US dollar on May 4, 2026, falling 39 paise from its previous close of 94.84. This marks the weakest level the domestic currency has ever recorded against the greenback.

Global oil markets remain on edge as Brent crude trades between $108 and $110 per barrel. West Asia tensions, particularly the US-Iran conflict around the Strait of Hormuz, are driving fears of supply disruptions.

India imports 85 to 90 percent of its crude oil requirements, making it extremely vulnerable to price shocks. The surge in oil prices is widening the trade deficit and putting additional pressure on the rupee.

Foreign institutional investors continue pulling capital out of Indian markets. Recent data shows FII outflows have reached Rs 13,944 crore, reflecting risk aversion among global investors.

The Reserve Bank of India is reportedly exploring measures to mobilize dollar inflows and stabilize the currency. However, direct market intervention remains limited as the central bank balances multiple objectives.

India VIX, the volatility index, currently hovers around 18, down from recent highs but still elevated. This suggests markets remain cautious even as immediate panic has subsided slightly.

Forex reserves stand at approximately $640 billion, providing the RBI with significant ammunition to defend the currency. Analysts note that while reserves are ample, sustained pressure could test the central bank's resolve.

Jateen Trivedi of LKP Securities describes the rupee trend as weak, with resistance at 94.50 and support at 95.40. The breach below 95 suggests sentiment remains firmly negative and further depreciation cannot be ruled out.
