Himadri Speciality Targets FY28 Profit Doubling on ₹4,660 Cr Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd (NSE: HSCL) reports FY26 revenue of ₹4,660.7 crore and highest-ever EBITDA of ₹1,006 crore, up 19% YoY
  • 02/Company unveils roadmap to more than double profitability by FY28 through speciality carbon black, battery materials and tyre segments
  • 03/LFP cathode plant phase 1 operational by Q3 FY27; anthraquinone and carbazole facilities to complete by Q2 FY27

Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd (NSE: HSCL) has laid out an aggressive expansion roadmap. The company aims to more than double profitability by FY28 across three strategic pillars.

For FY26, consolidated revenue from operations reached ₹4,660.7 crore. The company posted its highest-ever annual EBITDA of ₹1,006 crore, reflecting 19% year-on-year growth.

Profit After Tax surged 36% to ₹755 crore for the full year. Profit Before Tax rose to ₹1,001 crore, marking a 24% increase over FY25.

In the fourth quarter, revenue stood at ₹1,288 crore, up 13.5% YoY. Q4 PAT came in at ₹208 crore, while EBITDA was ₹280 crore, up 21% YoY.

For FY27, the company targets full-year operations of its expanded speciality carbon black capacity. A new 70,000 MTPA line takes total capacity to 250,000 MTPA at Mahistikry.

The company plans to complete anthraquinone and carbazole facilities by Q2 FY27. Phase 1 of the LFP cathode active material plant is scheduled for Q3 FY27.

Birla Tyres will ramp up in the off-highway and commercial vehicle segments during FY27. By FY28, Himadri expects to enter passenger car radials including EV and SUV tyres.

"Growth at Himadri continues to be shaped by purposeful innovation," said Anurag Choudhary, Chairman and CEO. He added that disciplined capital allocation will maintain a robust ROCE profile.
