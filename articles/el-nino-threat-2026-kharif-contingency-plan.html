El Nino Threat 2026: Centre Rolls Out Kharif Contingency Plan To Shield Crops

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Agriculture ministry launches contingency plan for kharif 2026 amid El Nino threat and below-normal monsoon forecast at 92% of LPA
  • 02/IMD warns of 35% probability of drought-like conditions; states directed to prepare district-wise crop strategies and promote short-duration crops
  • 03/Government distributing drought-resistant seed varieties and region-specific weather advisories to minimise crop failure and economic losses

The agriculture ministry has activated a contingency plan for India's kharif season as El Nino threatens to disrupt the southwest monsoon. The India Meteorological Department has projected rainfall at 92% of the long-term average.

IMD has warned of a 35% probability of drought-like conditions developing during the monsoon season. El Nino, a climate pattern linked to weak monsoons, has historically cut kharif output by over 10% in many districts.

The ministry has directed all states to prepare district-wise crop strategies. Short-duration crops such as pulses, millets, and vegetables are being promoted because they require less water and mature quickly.

In districts where El Nino impact is expected to be severe, the government is distributing drought-resistant seed varieties. This marks a shift from routine kharif preparations toward mission-mode weather monitoring.

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan had last month directed officials to ensure seed and fertiliser availability. He also ordered alternative crop options, delayed sowing strategies, and drought-tolerant varieties to be readied.

The government has issued region-specific sowing and weather advisories to farmers. The goal is to avoid crop failures from erratic weather and minimise economic losses through targeted, localised measures.

State agriculture departments have been told to monitor irrigation and reservoir levels closely. Reservoir storage currently stands at 127% of normal, which will help meet irrigation needs if rainfall falls short.

A study by ICAR-affiliated scientists shows kharif output drops more than 10% during El Nino years. The current preparedness drive aims to limit that damage through better water management and crop diversification.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE MONSOON FORECAST FOR 2026?
THE INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT HAS PROJECTED SOUTHWEST MONSOON RAINFALL AT 92% OF THE LONG-TERM AVERAGE FOR 2026. THE FORECAST ALSO INDICATES A 35% PROBABILITY OF DROUGHT-LIKE CONDITIONS DUE TO EMERGING EL NINO CONDITIONS.
WHAT CROPS ARE BEING PROMOTED UNDER THE CONTINGENCY PLAN?
THE AGRICULTURE MINISTRY IS DIRECTING STATES TO PROMOTE SHORT-DURATION CROPS SUCH AS PULSES, CERTAIN MILLETS, AND VEGETABLES. THESE CROPS REQUIRE LESS WATER, MATURE QUICKLY, AND HELP FARMERS MITIGATE THE VAGARIES OF ERRATIC WEATHER.
WHAT IS THE GOVERNMENT DOING IN DISTRICTS MOST AT RISK?
IN DISTRICTS WHERE EL NINO IMPACT IS EXPECTED TO BE MORE PRONOUNCED, THE GOVERNMENT IS DISTRIBUTING DROUGHT-RESISTANT VARIETIES OF SEEDS. STATES ARE ALSO PREPARING DISTRICT-WISE CROP STRATEGIES AND MONITORING IRRIGATION AND RESERVOIR LEVELS CLOSELY.
HOW DOES EL NINO AFFECT KHARIF PRODUCTION?
ACCORDING TO A STUDY BY ICAR-AFFILIATED FARM SCIENTISTS, OUTPUT OF KEY KHARIF CROPS DIPS MORE THAN 10% IN MANY DISTRICTS DURING EL NINO YEARS. THIS NECESSITATES REMEDIAL MEASURES SUCH AS CONTINGENCY PLANS AND ALTERNATIVE CROP OPTIONS.
WHAT ADVISORIES HAVE BEEN ISSUED TO FARMERS?
THE GOVERNMENT HAS ISSUED REGION-SPECIFIC SOWING AND WEATHER ADVISORIES TO INCREASE AWARENESS AMONG FARMERS. THESE MEASURES AIM TO AVOID CROP FAILURES FROM ERRATIC WEATHER AND MINIMISE ECONOMIC LOSSES THROUGH TARGETED, LOCALISED ACTION.
