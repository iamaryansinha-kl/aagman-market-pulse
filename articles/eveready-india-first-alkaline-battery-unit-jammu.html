Eveready Commissions India's First Alkaline Battery Unit in Jammu

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Eveready Industries commissions India's only operating alkaline battery manufacturing facility in Jammu
  • 02/The greenfield unit was set up with an investment of approximately ₹200 crore and inaugurated by the Jammu Lieutenant Governor
  • 03/Eveready targets capturing 25% of India's alkaline battery market over the next three years

Eveready Industries India Limited has commissioned India's first alkaline battery manufacturing unit. The state-of-the-art greenfield facility is located in Jammu.

The plant was inaugurated by the Lieutenant Governor of Jammu and Kashmir. It represents a major milestone in India's battery manufacturing landscape.

The facility was set up with an investment of approximately ₹200 crore. Eveready acquired the land as part of the Make in India campaign.

The company is now India's only domestic producer of alkaline batteries. All other alkaline batteries sold in India are currently imported.

Eveready aims to capture 25% of the Indian alkaline battery market within three years. The market has been growing rapidly as consumers shift from carbon-zinc batteries.

In Q3 FY26, Eveready's alkaline battery segment grew 72% year-on-year. This outpaced the overall battery business growth of 11.1%.

The company's consolidated Q3 FY26 revenue stood at ₹367.2 crore. EBITDA increased to ₹33.3 crore from ₹29.5 crore in Q3 FY25.

Net debt stood at ₹317 crore, including ₹167 crore capex for the Jammu facility. The company expects long-term cost efficiencies from the new plant.
