NCC Bags Rs 1,076.71 Crore Andhra Pradesh Drinking Water Project Order

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/NCC Limited received a Letter of Acceptance dated September 26, 2026 for a Rs 1,076.71 crore drinking water project in Andhra Pradesh, excluding GST
  • 02/The order is part of NCC's 2026 winning streak that includes five orders worth Rs 2,456.89 crore in January and two orders worth Rs 534.85 crore in July
  • 03/NCC shares rose about 5% on September 29 after the September 28 disclosure; execution risk and state payment cycles remain watch items

NCC Limited has secured a Rs 1,076.71 crore order from the Rural Water Supply department of Andhra Pradesh. The Letter of Acceptance is dated September 26, 2026.

The company disclosed the order win to exchanges on September 28, 2026. NCC shares traded up roughly 5% on September 29 following the announcement.

The project covers drinking water supply under the Multi Village Scheme on the Yeleru Reservoir. It serves the Anakapalli segment in Anakapalli district, Andhra Pradesh.

The order value of Rs 1,076.71 crore excludes GST. The execution period is fixed at 24 months.

This is a domestic order for NCC, one of India's leading construction and EPC companies. Its portfolio spans buildings, water, transport, and electrical segments.

CONTEXT: Andhra Pradesh is running a large pipeline of rural drinking-water projects linked to the Jal Jeevan Mission. State governments are funding multi-village schemes, and that spending has become a steady source of orders for water-focused EPC contractors.

The Yeleru Reservoir supplies the Visakhapatnam and Anakapalli region. Multi Village Scheme projects of this scale typically involve intake works, treatment plants, pumping stations, and bulk distribution pipelines across multiple habitations.

This win extends NCC's order-winning streak through 2026. In January 2026, the company bagged five orders worth a combined Rs 2,456.89 crore.

In July 2026, NCC added two more orders worth Rs 534.85 crore. Together, the January and July wins alone total nearly Rs 3,000 crore before this Andhra Pradesh award.

The water segment has become a structural growth driver for NCC. State-funded drinking water and irrigation programs now sit alongside its traditional buildings and transport backlog.

NCC's consolidated order book stood at Rs 81,214 crore as of June 30, 2026. The new LoA adds roughly 1.3% to that backlog.

The company's Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue came in at Rs 5,842.48 crore, up 12% year on year. Net profit for the quarter was Rs 216.40 crore.

Management has guided for FY27 order inflows between Rs 22,000 crore and Rs 25,000 crore. This Andhra Pradesh award is an early contribution to that target.

IMPACT: For investors, the order reinforces revenue visibility over the next two fiscal years. It also strengthens the water segment's share of a backlog that management says is fully executable.

The 24-month execution window means revenue from this project should begin flowing in once mobilization is complete. Margins on government water contracts typically track NCC's guided EBITDA band of 8.5% to 9%.

Risks remain. Execution depends on site conditions, client fund availability, and state payment cycles. Payment delays from select state governments have hurt Indian EPC players before.

The Rs 1,076.71 crore figure excludes GST, so the all-in contract value will be higher. Investors should also note that a single order of this size moves the needle gradually rather than immediately.

NCC stock has reacted positively, gaining about 5% on September 29. The market is rewarding consistent order inflow more than any single award at this stage.
