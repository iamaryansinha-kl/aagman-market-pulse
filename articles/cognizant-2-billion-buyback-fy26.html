Cognizant $2 Billion Buyback: Board Doubles FY26 Repurchase Target

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Cognizant board doubles FY26 share buyback target to $2 billion from $1 billion, adding $1 billion repurchase in Q2 2026
  • 02/Stock surged nearly 10% to $51.40 on Nasdaq; company plans to use $1 billion from revolving credit facility for the buyback
  • 03/FY26 revenue guidance stands at $22.1-22.6 billion; stock still down 37% over past year amid broader IT sector pressure

Cognizant Technology Solutions has doubled its FY26 share buyback target to $2 billion. The board approved an additional $1 billion repurchase programme in the second quarter of 2026.

The announcement was made on Monday, May 18. Cognizant shares rallied nearly 10% on the Nasdaq, closing at $51.395.

The company now holds approximately $3.45 billion in total remaining buyback authorization. The extra $1 billion repurchase is expected to execute during the September quarter.

Cognizant plans to utilise $1 billion from its existing revolving credit facility. This is partly in anticipation of the previously announced Astreya acquisition.

The stock has declined roughly 39% over the past year. Market capitalisation had fallen to nearly $22.3 billion as of last Friday.

FY26 revenue guidance is set between $22.1 billion and $22.6 billion. The sharp correction brought valuation close to the lower end of this band.

"Our plan to increase share repurchases reflects our strong conviction in the long-term opportunity AI creates," CEO Ravi Kumar S said. He believes the current share price significantly undervalues Cognizant's prospects.

CFO Jatin Dalal said the strong balance sheet and robust free cash flow provide flexibility. The company can return more capital without compromising growth investments.
