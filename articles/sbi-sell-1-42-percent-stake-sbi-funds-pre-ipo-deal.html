SBI to Sell 1.42% Stake in SBI Funds in Pre-IPO Deal

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

SBI is trimming its stake ahead of the IPO.

State Bank of India will sell a 1.42% stake in SBI Funds Management through a pre-IPO deal, ahead of the asset management company's public offering scheduled to open on July 14. The sale is part of the IPO preparation process and will bring in institutional investors.

SBI currently holds the majority stake in the fund management company. After the 1.42% sale, the bank will remain the controlling shareholder, with Amundi, the European asset manager, holding the remaining significant stake.

Pre-IPO placements are common ahead of public offerings. They allow companies to bring in strategic or institutional investors at a negotiated price, often providing a benchmark for the IPO pricing. The pre-IPO investors are typically subject to a lock-in period.

The stake sale comes after SBI Funds filed its IPO papers and announced the issue would open on July 14. The company is one of India's largest asset managers, with a diversified portfolio of equity, debt, and hybrid schemes.

SBI's 38 lakh shareholders have a reserved portion in the IPO. The company is offering a specific allocation for SBI shareholders, giving them preferential access to the issue.

The IPO is expected to attract strong demand. India's mutual fund industry has grown rapidly, and SBI Funds benefits from the SBI brand and extensive distribution network across the country.
