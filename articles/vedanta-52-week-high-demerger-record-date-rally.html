Vedanta Hits 52-Week High at ₹795 as Demerger Record Date Triggers Rally

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Vedanta Limited shares surged to a 52-week high of ₹795 in early trade on April 21, 2026, after the board announced May 1 as the demerger record date
  • 02/The stock has returned nearly 90% over the past year versus the Nifty Next 50's 8.33% gain, with year-to-date returns at 29.59%
  • 03/Shareholders will receive one equity share each in four newly listed companies for every Vedanta share held on the record date

Vedanta Limited (NSE: VEDL) shares surged to a 52-week high of ₹795 in early trade on Tuesday, April 21, 2026. The rally followed the company's board meeting on April 20, which fixed May 1, 2026 as both the effective date and record date for its long-awaited demerger.

The stock opened sharply at ₹791.70 before pulling back slightly. It was trading at ₹778.50 as of 9:46 am, up ₹7.50 or 0.97% from Monday's close of ₹771. Traded volume crossed 109.55 lakh shares with turnover exceeding ₹860 crore.

The demerger will carve out Vedanta's aluminium, merchant power, oil and gas, and iron ore businesses into four separate listed entities. Shareholders on the record date will receive one share each in Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd, Talwandi Sabo Power Ltd, Malco Energy Ltd, and Vedanta Iron and Steel Ltd for every share held.

The residual Vedanta Limited will retain the base metals business. This includes its significant stake in Hindustan Zinc, India's largest zinc producer and a major silver producer globally.

Vedanta also approved transferring its stake in Bharat Aluminium Company (BALCO) to Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd. BALCO reported turnover of ₹15,909 crore in FY2025, roughly 10% of Vedanta's consolidated revenue. BALCO's net worth stood at ₹12,088 crore, contributing 39% to consolidated net worth.

The stock has been a strong outperformer, returning nearly 90% over the past year. The Nifty Next 50 gained just 8.33% in the same period. Year-to-date returns stand at 29.59%.

The demerger received NCLT Mumbai bench approval on January 6, 2026. Kotak Institutional Equities has a 'buy' rating with a target price of ₹965 per share. Nuvama has set a target of ₹806.

The order book showed mild selling pressure with 52% sell orders versus 48% buy orders. Vedanta's market capitalisation stood at approximately ₹3.03 lakh crore. The 52-week low was ₹391.10.
