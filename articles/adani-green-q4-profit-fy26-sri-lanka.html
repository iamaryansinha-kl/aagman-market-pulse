Adani Green Q4 Profit ₹514 Crore; Sri Lanka Write-off Drags FY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Adani Green Energy reports Q4 profit of ₹514 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026
  • 02/FY26 full-year earnings dragged by Sri Lankan project write-off and refinancing costs
  • 03/Company added 5.1 GW capacity in FY26, taking total operational portfolio to 19.3 GW

Adani Green Energy Ltd reported a Q4 profit of ₹514 crore. The result was announced for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

The full-year FY26 performance was impacted by a Sri Lankan project write-off. Refinancing costs also weighed on the annual numbers.

The company added 5.1 GW of greenfield renewable energy capacity during FY26. This expansion takes its total operational portfolio to 19.3 GW.

The capacity addition reflects continued execution on the company's growth strategy. Renewable energy demand in India remains robust.

FY26 marked a year of portfolio expansion despite financial headwinds. The Sri Lankan write-off reflects asset-specific challenges.

Management has not provided specific forward guidance. Investors will watch Q1 FY27 for signs of margin recovery.
