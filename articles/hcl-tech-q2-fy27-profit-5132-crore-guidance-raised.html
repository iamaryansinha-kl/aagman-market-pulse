HCL Tech Q2 Profit Up 21% to Rs 5,132 Crore, FY27 Guidance Raised

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

HCL Tech delivered a standout September quarter on Monday, posting profit growth well ahead of estimates and lifting the lower end of its full-year revenue guidance, in the clearest signal yet that India's third-largest IT services firm is converting its artificial intelligence bets into real revenue.

Consolidated net profit for the July-September quarter came in at Rs 5,132 crore, up 21.2% from the year-ago period and 11% higher than the Rs 4,626 crore reported in the June quarter. The figure beat the CNBC-TV18 poll estimate of Rs 4,963 crore. Revenue from operations grew 3% sequentially and 11.5% year-on-year to Rs 35,620 crore, edging past the Bloomberg consensus of around Rs 35,547 crore.

The margin story was the quarter's quiet highlight. Operating margin, measured as EBIT as a share of revenue, expanded 131 basis points sequentially and 71 basis points year-on-year to 18.2%. That is a striking recovery for a company that absorbed restructuring costs in the previous quarter, and it sits comfortably within HCL Tech's guided band of 17.5% to 18.5% for the full year, which the management retained.

Deal momentum provided the fireworks. Net new total contract value wins for the quarter stood at $3.24 billion, and the pipeline included a $1.14 billion mega AI deal from Mercedes-Benz that underscores how large enterprises are committing serious budgets to production-scale AI deployments. Chief executive and managing director C Vijayakumar said new bookings of $3.3 billion were up 27% year-on-year, taking first-half bookings to a record high. Services revenue grew 3% sequentially with expansion across verticals and geographies, he added.

The advanced AI franchise is now a measurable business rather than a slide in an investor deck. Advanced AI revenue reached $219 million in the quarter, up 28.4% sequentially and 78% year-on-year in constant currency. "Our AI propositions are resonating well with our clients, giving us confidence in our growth trajectory," Vijayakumar said.

That confidence translated into guidance. HCL Tech raised its FY27 revenue growth forecast to 2.5-4% in constant currency, from 1-4% earlier, citing the strong order book that will flow through in subsequent quarters. The move is notable at a time when global enterprise spending remains uneven, and it positions HCL Tech more aggressively than peers that have kept guidance unchanged. The EBIT margin band of 17.5-18.5% was left untouched, suggesting management sees profitability holding even as it chases larger transformation deals.

Alongside results, HCL Tech announced a global premier partnership with Anthropic to accelerate enterprise adoption of agentic AI, with a focus on safety, cybersecurity and governance. The tie-up gives the company a seat at the table in one of the fastest corners of the AI stack, where clients are moving from pilots to governed, production-grade deployments. HCL Tech also rolled out 20 industry-specific AI solutions across key sectors and continued to rebuild headcount, adding 6,450 employees during the quarter and stepping up fresher hiring.

Shareholders received a sweetener in the form of a Rs 12 per share interim dividend on equity shares of face value Rs 2 each. The stock closed 0.5% higher at Rs 1,223 on Monday before the results were announced in post-market hours, and the guidance upgrade sets up an active session on Tuesday as investors reprice the stock against a stronger-than-expected first half.

The broader context makes the print more impressive. Indian IT has spent much of the past two years navigating cautious discretionary spending from US and European clients, with growth concentrated in cost-takeout and vendor-consolidation deals. HCL Tech's numbers suggest the mix is shifting: AI-led transformation deals are not only winning but also expanding margins, a combination the sector has been promising for quarters. If the raised guidance holds through the second half, the September quarter could be remembered as the point where AI stopped being a narrative and started being a numbers line.

Investors will watch execution on the Mercedes-Benz-class mega deals, the pace of AI revenue conversion, and whether margin holds near 18% as fresher hiring accelerates. For now, though, HCL Tech has given the market exactly what it wanted: growth, margin, bookings and a raised outlook in the same print.

GLOSSARY: EBIT margin: Earnings before interest and tax expressed as a percentage of revenue, a measure of operating profitability. Constant currency (CC): Growth measured after stripping out the impact of exchange-rate movements between the rupee and foreign currencies. Total contract value (TCV): The full value of a signed deal over its entire duration, used to gauge order-book strength. Advanced AI: HCL Tech's reporting segment for revenue tied to artificial intelligence engagements, separate from traditional digital and engineering services. Agentic AI: AI systems that can autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks, a step beyond generative chat-style tools. Interim dividend: A dividend declared and paid during the financial year, before full-year results are known. Basis points: One hundredth of a percentage point, used to describe changes in margins and interest rates. Post-market hours: The trading window after the regular market close, when companies typically release results.
