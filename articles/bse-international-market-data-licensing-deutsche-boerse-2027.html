BSE Takes Over International Market Data Licensing From Deutsche Börse From Jan 2027

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/BSE Ltd will directly license its market data products to international clients starting January 1, 2027, replacing Deutsche Börse AG
  • 02/The 13-year partnership began in October 2013 when Deutsche Börse became exclusive international licensor for BSE market data
  • 03/Domestic Indian clients face no changes; BSE and Deutsche Börse are coordinating for a smooth transition

BSE Ltd (NSE: BSE) announced it will directly manage licensing of its market data products for international clients from January 1, 2027. The function is currently handled by Deutsche Börse AG under a long-standing agreement.

The exchange said international clients will continue receiving BSE Market Data Products starting January 1, 2027, with no interruption in service. Both organizations are working together to ensure a smooth transition throughout the migration process.

There will be no change for clients based in India. Domestic clients will continue to access market data products directly from BSE as they do currently.

The original agreement between BSE and Deutsche Börse AG was signed in October 2013. Under that arrangement, Deutsche Börse was the exclusive licensor responsible for sales and marketing of all BSE market data products to customers outside India.

BSE serves over 5,000 listed companies on its exchange platform. The exchange generates the majority of its revenue from stock exchange operations within India.

BSE shares closed at ₹3,898.90 on June 25, 2026. The stock has seen significant volatility in recent sessions, with volumes exceeding 1.5 million shares on the last trading day.

The move to direct licensing represents a strategic shift for Asia's oldest stock exchange. It signals BSE's intent to build closer relationships with its international client base and capture full value from its data products.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS BSE CHANGING ABOUT ITS INTERNATIONAL MARKET DATA LICENSING?
BSE LTD WILL DIRECTLY MANAGE THE LICENSING OF ITS MARKET DATA PRODUCTS FOR INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS STARTING JANUARY 1, 2027. THIS FUNCTION WAS PREVIOUSLY HANDLED BY DEUTSCHE BÖRSE AG UNDER A 13-YEAR AGREEMENT SIGNED IN OCTOBER 2013.
WILL INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS FACE ANY SERVICE DISRUPTION?
NO. BSE HAS CONFIRMED THAT ALL INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS WILL CONTINUE TO RECEIVE BSE MARKET DATA PRODUCTS FROM JANUARY 1, 2027, WITHOUT ANY INTERRUPTION IN SERVICE. BSE AND DEUTSCHE BÖRSE AG ARE COORDINATING TO ENSURE A SMOOTH TRANSITION.
WHAT HAPPENS TO DOMESTIC CLIENTS IN INDIA?
DOMESTIC CLIENTS BASED IN INDIA WILL SEE NO CHANGE. THEY WILL CONTINUE TO ACCESS MARKET DATA PRODUCTS DIRECTLY FROM BSE AS THEY DO CURRENTLY. THE CHANGE ONLY AFFECTS INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS.
WHAT WAS THE ORIGINAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN BSE AND DEUTSCHE BÖRSE?
IN OCTOBER 2013, BSE AND DEUTSCHE BÖRSE AG ENTERED INTO A MARKET DATA SERVICE AGREEMENT. UNDER THIS DEAL, DEUTSCHE BÖRSE ACTED AS THE EXCLUSIVE LICENSOR FOR SALES AND MARKETING OF ALL BSE MARKET DATA PRODUCTS TO CUSTOMERS OUTSIDE INDIA.
WHY IS THIS CHANGE SIGNIFICANT FOR BSE?
THE MOVE ALLOWS BSE TO BUILD DIRECT RELATIONSHIPS WITH ITS INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS AND CAPTURE FULL VALUE FROM ITS DATA PRODUCTS. AS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST STOCK EXCHANGES WITH OVER 5,000 LISTED COMPANIES, BSE'S MARKET DATA IS A VALUABLE ASSET FOR GLOBAL INVESTORS AND FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS.
