India's Textile Exports Rise 2.1% in FY26 to ₹3.16 Lakh Crore on Cotton, MMF Growth

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's textile exports reached ₹3.16 lakh crore in FY26, marking a 2.1% increase over the previous year
  • 02/Cotton and man-made fibre (MMF) segments were the primary growth drivers in the export basket
  • 03/Global headwinds including weak demand and geopolitical tensions posed challenges that the sector largely navigated

India's textile exports have crossed ₹3.16 lakh crore in FY26. The sector grew 2.1% despite challenging global conditions.

Cotton textiles led the charge. India's competitive advantage in cotton production supported steady demand from key export markets.

The man-made fibre segment also contributed meaningfully. MMF exports gained traction as buyers sought cost-effective synthetic alternatives.

Global headwinds were significant. Weak consumer demand in Europe and the US, plus shipping disruptions, created headwinds for exporters.

The 2.1% growth is modest but notable. Many peer manufacturing nations reported flat or declining textile exports in the same period.

Government production-linked incentive schemes have helped. PLI benefits for MMF and technical textiles are beginning to show results.

The sector employs over 45 million workers directly. Export growth translates to sustained rural and semi-urban livelihoods.

Going forward, the industry targets 10% annual growth. This will require faster clearance of pending PLI claims and trade agreement expansion.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WERE INDIA'S TEXTILE EXPORT FIGURES FOR FY26?
INDIA'S TEXTILE EXPORTS REACHED ₹3.16 LAKH CRORE IN FINANCIAL YEAR 2026, REPRESENTING A 2.1% INCREASE COMPARED TO THE PREVIOUS YEAR.
WHICH SEGMENTS DROVE THE GROWTH?
COTTON TEXTILES AND MAN-MADE FIBRE (MMF) SEGMENTS WERE THE PRIMARY GROWTH DRIVERS. COTTON LEVERAGED INDIA'S RAW MATERIAL ADVANTAGE, WHILE MMF BENEFITED FROM COST-COMPETITIVE SYNTHETIC DEMAND.
WHAT GLOBAL CHALLENGES DID THE SECTOR FACE?
THE SECTOR FACED WEAK CONSUMER DEMAND IN EUROPE AND THE US, SHIPPING DISRUPTIONS, AND GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS THAT SQUEEZED MARGINS AND SLOWED ORDER FLOWS.
HOW DOES THIS COMPARE TO OTHER COUNTRIES?
THE 2.1% GROWTH IS MODEST BUT NOTABLE. MANY PEER MANUFACTURING NATIONS REPORTED FLAT OR DECLINING TEXTILE EXPORTS DURING THE SAME PERIOD, MAKING INDIA'S GROWTH RELATIVELY RESILIENT.
WHAT DOES THE INDUSTRY NEED FOR FASTER GROWTH?
THE SECTOR NEEDS FASTER CLEARANCE OF PENDING PRODUCTION-LINKED INCENTIVE (PLI) CLAIMS, EXPANDED TRADE AGREEMENTS WITH KEY MARKETS, AND CONTINUED GOVERNMENT SUPPORT TO ACHIEVE ITS 10% ANNUAL GROWTH TARGET.
