Powergrid Board Approves ₹4,000 Crore Unsecured Loan From SBI

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Power Grid Corporation of India board approves raising up to ₹4,000 crore through an unsecured rupee term loan from State Bank of India
  • 02/The company is India's largest electric power transmission utility under the Ministry of Power, with a market cap of ₹2.95 lakh crore
  • 03/The funding signals aggressive capex plans; investors should watch the upcoming Q4 FY26 results for utilization guidance

Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd (NSE: POWERGRID) announced on Thursday, April 30, 2026, that its board has approved raising up to ₹4,000 crore. The funds will come through an unsecured rupee term loan or line of credit from State Bank of India.

"The Board of Directors considered and approved the raising of funds through unsecured rupee term loan or line of credit of up to ₹4,000 crore from State Bank of India," the company informed exchanges.

The loan is unsecured, meaning Powergrid will not pledge specific assets as collateral. This reflects SBI's confidence in the company's creditworthiness as a government-owned entity.

Powergrid operates under the Ministry of Power and is India's largest electric power transmission utility. It manages the inter-state transmission system and has a total works-in-hand order book of approximately ₹1,455 billion.

The stock closed at ₹319 on April 28, 2026, after hitting a high of ₹322.80. The 52-week range is ₹250 to ₹323.05, and the market capitalization stands at around ₹2.95 lakh crore.

The company reported Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹12,395 crore and profit after tax of ₹3,676 crore, with an operating margin of 77.3%. Capital expenditure in Q3 FY26 came in at ₹11,376 crore, up 48.7% year-on-year.

Brokerages remain largely positive on the stock. Motilal Oswal has a Buy rating with a target of ₹340, while YES Securities also has a Buy with a target of ₹335. The consensus recommendation from 23 analysts is Buy.

The Q4 FY26 results are expected in May 2026. Analysts estimate revenue of ₹5,100-5,400 crore and PAT of ₹3,900-4,100 crore. Management guidance on FY27 capex and the final dividend will be key triggers.
