India Sports Retail Heats Up: PlayBlue Raises ₹21 Crore Seed Round

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/PlayBlue raises ₹21 crore seed round on 20 June backed by Centre Court Capital and MIXI Global Investments
  • 02/India's sports market to grow from $37 billion in 2023 to $99 billion by 2028 at 15% CAGR per Lodha Capital
  • 03/Total Sports plans ₹100 crore raise; Agilitas posted ₹1,015 crore revenue in FY25 and eyes Sports365 acquisition

PlayBlue, founded by Jayam Vohra and Satyam Trivedi, raised ₹21 crore in a seed round on 20 June. The round was backed by Centre Court Capital, MIXI Global Investments, WEH Ventures, and angels including Abhishek Goyal and Arjun Vaidya.

Vohra built Fitternity, sold to Curefit in 2021. Trivedi held roles at GMR Sports, RPSG Sports and Adani Sports. Both founders bring over a decade of sports ecosystem experience.

Mumbai-based Total Sports & Fitness is preparing to raise ₹100 crore for expansion. The company has 23 stores across 13 cities and posted about ₹100 crore revenue in FY26.

Total Sports founder Niteen Shah said its own brand revenue grew from 3% to 21% in the past year. The company expects it to hit 30% within two years.

India's sports market was worth $37 billion in 2023. It is projected to reach $99 billion by 2028, clocking a 15% CAGR, according to a Lodha Capital Markets report.

"Sports is rapidly evolving into a lifestyle choice and serves as a high-engagement community building tool," said Paras Shah, partner & co-head of investment banking at Lodha Capital. "Consumers are now demanding a professional and structured retail environment."

Agilitas Sportsyard launched a 40,000 sq ft multi-brand outlet in Bengaluru. It turned profitable within two months. The company plans 10 more stores by FY-end and a multi-brand outlet in October.

Agilitas raised ₹225 crore led by Nexus Venture Partners and Rainmatter. Virat Kohli invested ₹40 crore. The company posted ₹1,015 crore in FY25 revenue and is acquiring Sports365.
