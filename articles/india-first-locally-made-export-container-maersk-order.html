India Rolls Out First Locally Made Export Container; Maersk Orders 1,000 More Units

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's first domestically manufactured export-import shipping container handed over to Maersk at Concor's Dadri depot
  • 02/Manufactured by DCM Shriram Group; Maersk places additional order for 1,000 containers
  • 03/Part of ₹10,000 Cr container manufacturing promotion scheme to build 7.5 lakh TEU annual capacity

India unveiled its first domestically manufactured export-import shipping container on Friday. It was handed over to global shipping major Maersk at Concor's inland container depot in Dadri, Uttar Pradesh.

The container was manufactured by DCM Shriram Group. It comes 16 months after Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Maersk chairman Robert Maersk Uggla in February 2025.

Maersk placed an order for 1,000 additional containers. This signals early confidence in India's manufacturing capabilities, the ministry of ports, shipping and waterways said.

India has long depended on imports, particularly from China, for shipping containers. Supply disruptions during covid-19 exposed vulnerabilities in global container supply chains.

The initiative is backed by the ₹10,000-crore container manufacturing promotion scheme (CMPS). Announced in the Union Budget for 2026-27, it aims to create a globally competitive container manufacturing ecosystem.

Under CMPS, India's annual container manufacturing capacity will increase nearly tenfold to about 7.5 lakh TEUs. Both greenfield units and existing facility expansions are supported.

The first India-made container complies with international ISO specifications and CSC standards. It is eligible for deployment across global shipping routes.

Ports Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said the policy will generate employment, facilitate technology transfer and reduce import dependence.

The government has also announced a ₹70,000-crore financial assistance package for shipbuilding. It is working on plans to establish a national container shipping line.

Major port infrastructure projects include Vadhavan Port, Galathea Bay International Container Transshipment Port, Tuna Tekra and Outer Harbour Container Terminal.
