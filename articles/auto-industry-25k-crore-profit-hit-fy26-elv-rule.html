Auto Industry ₹25k Cr Profit Hit in FY26 Due to End-of-Life Vehicle Rule

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian auto industry faces ₹25,000 crore hit on profits for FY26 due to Environment Protection (End-of-Life Vehicles) Rules 2025
  • 02/Rule 4(6) triggers IND AS 37 requiring provisions for EPR certificates on vehicles sold over past 15-20 years
  • 03/SIAM sought amendment before EC cost notification but ministry did not alter the clause in March 27, 2026 notification

The Indian automobile industry faces a ₹25,000 crore hit on profits for FY26. The Environment Protection (End-of-Life Vehicles) Rules 2025 triggered the accounting impact.

Rule 4(6) of the January 2025 notification requires automakers to comply with Extended Producer Responsibility if operations cease. This triggers IND AS 37 accounting standards.

Automakers must make financial provisions for EPR certificates covering all vehicles sold over the past 20 years for private use. Commercial vehicles face a 15-year lookback period.

Industry body SIAM wrote to the ministry highlighting the financial impact. Preliminary estimates indicate ₹25,000 crore on gross basis and ₹9,000 crore on discounted basis for FY26.

Four-wheeler manufacturers face an estimated impact of ₹14,623 crore. Two and three-wheeler makers face a combined hit of ₹9,650 crore for the financial year.

The ministry amended the rules on March 27, 2026, but did not alter Rule 4(6). SIAM had sought clarity that cumulative budgetary provisioning may not be required.

"Once the provision is realised in the accounting books, it would significantly reduce the profits of that year for the entire auto industry," an industry executive said.

The rule may affect manufacturers' ability to invest in new technologies. One executive said it could wipe off ₹25,000 crore from industry profits and constrain growth plans.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT IS THE ESTIMATED PROFIT IMPACT ON THE INDIAN AUTO INDUSTRY FOR FY26?
THE INDIAN AUTOMOBILE INDUSTRY FACES AN ESTIMATED ₹25,000 CRORE HIT ON PROFITS FOR FY26 DUE TO THE ENVIRONMENT PROTECTION (END-OF-LIFE VEHICLES) RULES 2025.
WHICH RULE TRIGGERED THE ACCOUNTING PROVISION REQUIREMENT?
RULE 4(6) OF THE JANUARY 2025 NOTIFICATION REQUIRES PRODUCERS TO COMPLY WITH EXTENDED PRODUCER RESPONSIBILITY IF OPERATIONS CEASE. THIS TRIGGERS IND AS 37, REQUIRING FINANCIAL PROVISIONS FOR EPR CERTIFICATES.
WHAT IS THE BREAKUP OF THE FINANCIAL IMPACT ACROSS VEHICLE SEGMENTS?
FOUR-WHEELER MANUFACTURERS FACE AN ESTIMATED IMPACT OF ₹14,623 CRORE. TWO AND THREE-WHEELER MAKERS FACE A COMBINED HIT OF ₹9,650 CRORE FOR FY26.
WHAT ACTION DID SIAM TAKE REGARDING THIS RULE?
SIAM WROTE TO THE MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE SEEKING AMENDMENT OF RULE 4(6) TO CLARIFY THAT CUMULATIVE BUDGETARY PROVISIONING MAY NOT BE REQUIRED BEFORE EC COST NOTIFICATION.
DID THE GOVERNMENT AMEND THE RULE IN RESPONSE TO INDUSTRY CONCERNS?
THE MINISTRY ISSUED AN AMENDMENT NOTIFICATION ON MARCH 27, 2026, BUT DID NOT ALTER RULE 4(6). THE CLAUSE REMAINS IN EFFECT, REQUIRING AUTOMAKERS TO MAKE PROVISIONS FOR PAST VEHICLES SOLD.
