Bajaj Finance Q4 Results: Net Profit Rises 22% to ₹5,465 Crore

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bajaj Finance Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit rises 22% YoY to ₹5,465 crore, in line with analyst estimates
  • 02/Stock surges around 5% to ₹975 in early trade on April 30, bucking the broader market decline
  • 03/Eight of ten global and domestic brokerages maintain buy or overweight ratings with target prices up to ₹1,210

Bajaj Finance Ltd (NSE: BAJFINANCE) reported a 22% year-on-year jump in consolidated net profit to ₹5,465 crore for Q4 FY26. The result was largely in line with the analyst consensus of ₹5,490 crore compiled by LSEG.

Standalone net profit rose 23% to ₹4,839.5 crore. The company benefited from lower credit costs and steady loan growth during the quarter.

Consolidated revenue increased 18% to ₹21,606 crore against ₹18,294 crore in Q4 FY25. Net interest income grew 20% to ₹10,716 crore from ₹8,911 crore a year ago.

Assets under management expanded 21% to ₹3,72,986 crore as of March 31, 2026. Gross NPA stood at 0.27% and net NPA at 0.11%, both showing stability.

The board declared a dividend of ₹6 per share. The record date is June 30, 2026, and includes a special payout of ₹0.60 from the sale of Bajaj Housing Finance shares.

Rajiv Bajaj will step down as Non-Executive Director at the AGM on July 30, 2026. The board noted his long association and contribution to the firm.

The stock jumped around 5% in early trade on April 30, climbing to ₹975 from the previous close of ₹930. It traded at ₹965.25 on the NSE around 9:44 am despite weak broader markets.

Morgan Stanley maintained an overweight rating with a target of ₹1,120. Jefferies set the highest target at ₹1,210, while Macquarie remained cautious with an underperform rating and ₹860 target.
