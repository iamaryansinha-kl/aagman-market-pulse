Indian Railways Plans 18,262 Special Trains for Summer Peak Season

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Indian Railways will operate 18,262 special train trips during the peak summer period from April 15 to July 15, 2026
  • 02/So far, 11,878 train trips have been notified, with remaining trains being notified progressively for passenger benefit
  • 03/Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw reviewed summer special train operations in New Delhi on April 24

Indian Railways will operate 18,262 special train trips during the peak summer travel season. The plan covers the period from April 15 to July 15, 2026.

The Ministry of Railways announced the deployment on April 24. Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw reviewed summer special train operations in New Delhi.

Till date, 11,878 train trips have been notified. The remaining special trains are being notified progressively for passenger convenience across high-demand corridors.

Special focus is on stations like New Delhi, Mumbai, Surat, Ahmedabad, and Bangalore. Western Railway has implemented real-time crowd management at Udhna station.

RPF personnel assisted passengers boarding Train No. 19045 Udhna-Thave Tapti Ganga Express. Boarding was managed systematically to maintain crowd control and ensure safety.

The large-scale deployment reflects improved rake availability and optimised scheduling. Continuous monitoring will support smoother connectivity during the summer rush.

Indian Railways said all efforts are being taken to ensure smooth passenger movement. Special attention was given to travelers heading to their hometowns.

The ministry remains committed to seamless passenger mobility through proactive planning. These targeted interventions aim to ensure safe and hassle-free journeys.
