Coal India Q4 Profit Jumps 11% to ₹10,839 Crore on Higher Revenue

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Coal India reports Q4 FY26 consolidated net profit of ₹10,839.18 crore, up 11.1% from ₹9,751.64 crore in the year-ago quarter.
  • 02/Revenue from operations rose to ₹46,490.03 crore from ₹43,961.56 crore, but FY26 full-year profit dropped 12.4% to ₹31,094.29 crore.
  • 03/The board declared a final dividend of ₹5.25 per share; production and offtake both declined for the full fiscal year.

Coal India Ltd (NSE: COALINDIA) reported an 11.1 per cent rise in consolidated net profit to ₹10,839.18 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026. The state-owned Maharatna firm had posted a net profit of ₹9,751.64 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal year.

Revenue from operations grew to ₹46,490.03 crore in Q4 FY26 from ₹43,961.56 crore in Q4 FY25. The company disclosed the numbers in a filing to the BSE on Monday, April 27, 2026.

Total expenses during the fourth quarter increased to ₹37,107.07 crore from ₹34,999 crore in the year-ago period. The rise in costs was more than offset by higher top-line performance during the March quarter.

For the full financial year 2025-26, net profit declined to ₹31,094.29 crore from ₹35,505.79 crore in FY25. Annual revenue marginally slipped to ₹1,68,400.29 crore from ₹1,69,177.37 crore in the previous year.

The board of directors declared a final dividend of ₹5.25 per equity share for FY26. The payout is subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing annual general meeting.

Coal production for FY26 stood at 768.19 million tonnes, down from 781.06 million tonnes in FY25. Coal offtake also fell to 744.88 million tonnes from 762.98 million tonnes during the same period.

Closing coal stock as on March 31, 2026 was 129.96 million tonnes, up from the opening stock of 107.16 million tonnes on April 1, 2025. The inventory build-up reflects lower offtake relative to production during the year.

CIL is the single-largest coal producer globally and contributes to 80 per cent of total domestic coal production. The PSU operates 310 working mines across 85 mining areas in eight Indian states.

Equirus Securities had expected Q4 FY26 adjusted EBITDA to fall 8.7 per cent year-on-year to ₹10,250 crore. The brokerage maintains a SHORT rating on the stock with a March 2027 target price of ₹370.
