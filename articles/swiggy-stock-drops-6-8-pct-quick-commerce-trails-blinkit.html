Swiggy Stock Drops 6.8% as Quick-Commerce Growth Trails Blinkit

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Swiggy shares plunged as much as 6.8% on May 11, heading for the worst session in over a month on quick-commerce slowdown fears.
  • 02/Instamart GOV grew 68.8% YoY to ₹7,881 crore, well below Blinkit's 95.4% growth, signalling continued market-share loss per JPMorgan.
  • 03/Q4 FY26 consolidated loss narrowed to ₹800 crore from ₹1,081 crore YoY, but investors remain focused on quick-commerce profitability amid intense competition.

Swiggy Ltd (NSE: SWIGGY) shares fell as much as 6.8 per cent on Monday, May 11, 2026. The stock was heading for its worst session in over a month and biggest intraday drop since April 2.

The fall came after Swiggy reported Q4 FY26 results on Friday, May 8. Consolidated loss narrowed to ₹800 crore from ₹1,081 crore in the year-ago quarter and ₹1,065 crore in Q3.

Revenue from operations surged 45 per cent year-on-year to ₹6,383 crore. Total income for the quarter stood at ₹6,649 crore, up 46.75 per cent from the previous year.

JPMorgan flagged that Swiggy's quick-commerce arm Instamart grew slower than rivals. Gross order value rose 68.8 per cent, well below Blinkit's 95.4 per cent jump, pointing to market-share erosion.

Competition in quick commerce remains fierce. Players including Zepto, Amazon, and Walmart-backed Flipkart are aggressively expanding their dark store networks and discounting to capture market share.

Swiggy's food delivery business delivered its strongest growth in nearly four years. Gross order value climbed 22.6 per cent YoY to ₹9,005 crore, with adjusted EBITDA crossing ₹1,000 crore annually.

Morgan Stanley noted that while food delivery margins improved, investor focus stays on quick-commerce execution and profitability. Instamart's adjusted EBITDA loss was ₹858 crore in Q4, with margin at negative 10.9 per cent.

Swiggy shares closed at ₹282.80 on May 8, well below the IPO price of ₹390. The stock is down roughly 27 per cent year-to-date as of the last trading session.

SWIGGY REPORTED A CONSOLIDATED LOSS OF ₹800 CRORE FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2026, COMPARED WITH ₹1,081 CRORE IN Q4 FY25 AND ₹1,065 CRORE IN Q3 FY26.
HOW MUCH DID SWIGGY'S REVENUE GROW IN Q4 FY26?
REVENUE FROM OPERATIONS ROSE 45% YEAR-ON-YEAR TO ₹6,383 CRORE. TOTAL INCOME FOR THE QUARTER CAME IN AT ₹6,649 CRORE, UP 46.75% FROM THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR.
HOW DID INSTAMART PERFORM COMPARED TO BLINKIT?
INSTAMART'S GROSS ORDER VALUE GREW 68.8% YOY TO ₹7,881 CRORE, WHICH JPMORGAN FLAGGED AS LAGGING BLINKIT'S 95.4% GROWTH. SWIGGY OPERATES 1,143 DARK STORES ACROSS 129 CITIES.

MORGAN STANLEY SAID INVESTOR FOCUS REMAINS ON EXECUTION AND PROFITABILITY IN QUICK COMMERCE. INSTAMART'S CONTRIBUTION MARGIN IMPROVED TO -1.8%, WITH MONTHLY MARGIN AT -1.1% IN MARCH 2026, BUT ADJUSTED EBITDA LOSS WAS STILL ₹858 CRORE.
