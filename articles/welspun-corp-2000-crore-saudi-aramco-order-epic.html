Welspun Corp Bags Rs 2,000 Crore Saudi Aramco Order; Stock Up 232% This Year

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Welspun Corp's order book just got deeper. Shares of the pipe manufacturer rallied as much as 5% to a day's high of Rs 2,791 on Monday after its associate company secured a fresh order from Saudi Aramco for the manufacturing and supply of steel pipes.

In an exchange filing overnight, Welspun Corp said its associate East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry, or EPIC, which is listed in Saudi Arabia, signed a contract with Saudi Aramco, with parallel disclosure to the Saudi stock exchanges. The order is valued at 771 million Saudi Riyal, or nearly Rs 2,000 crore.

The contract will run for six months, with its financial impact expected to be reflected from the fourth quarter of the current financial year through the first quarter of financial year 2028. EPIC is a leading manufacturer of Helical Submerged Arc Welded pipes and operates fully integrated manufacturing facilities.

The order caps a remarkable run. Just a month ago, Welspun Corp secured its largest-ever order worth $1.8 billion, or nearly Rs 17,200 crore, for pipe supply from its US manufacturing facility, taking the company's order book to a record Rs 42,100 crore, or $4.4 billion. Earlier this month, the company also signed an MoU with Perma-Pipe International Holdings and the Government of Jordan to explore a joint venture for pipe manufacturing and advanced coating facilities, targeting water, oil and gas and infrastructure projects in the region.

The market has noticed. The stock has gained 232% so far in 2026, making it the best performer on the Nifty 500 index this year, ahead of HFCL's 216% rise.

The earnings back the momentum. Welspun Corp's Q1 FY27 net profit surged 200% year-on-year to Rs 1,047.9 crore, helped by an exceptional gain of Rs 548 crore from the sale of shares in an associate, while consolidated revenue rose 14.9% to Rs 4,081.1 crore. EBITDA rose 31.8% to a record Rs 692 crore, with the operating margin widening to 17% from 14.8% a year earlier.

For a company that spent years in the shadow of the oil-and-gas capex cycle, the combination of Middle Eastern energy majors and American infrastructure demand has turned Welspun into the year's standout industrial stock, and the Aramco order suggests the cycle is still paying out.
