Dr Reddy's Receives DINs for Semaglutide from Health Canada

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Dr Reddy's Laboratories Ltd receives Drug Identification Numbers for semaglutide injection from Health Canada as part of approval consideration
  • 02/Stock lost 1.73% to Rs 1310.05 on Friday after surging 10% on Thursday on reports of Canadian regulatory progress
  • 03/Company launched Obeda brand semaglutide in India last month; was first Indian firm to receive DCGI approval for generic version

Dr Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (NSE: DRREDDY) has received Drug Identification Numbers for its semaglutide injection from Health Canada. The DINs form part of the regulatory consideration for product approval in the Canadian market.

The company informed the Bombay Stock Exchange on Friday, April 24, 2026. It said it remains committed to bringing the product to Canada upon final approval.

"We continue to engage constructively with the regulatory authority and remain committed to bringing the product to the Canadian market upon approval," the company stated in its exchange filing.

Dr Reddy's shares lost 1.73% on Friday and were trading at Rs 1310.05. The decline followed a 10 percent surge on Thursday driven by reports of the Canadian regulatory progress.

Last month, the Hyderabad-based company launched its injectable semaglutide under the brand name Obeda in India. The product is indicated for the management of type 2 diabetes.

Dr Reddy's was the first Indian company to receive DCGI approval for generic semaglutide. It made a first-day entry into the segment upon patent expiry.
