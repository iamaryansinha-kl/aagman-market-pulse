EXIM Bank Targets 10% Loan Book Growth in FY27 Despite West Asia Tensions

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/EXIM Bank of India MD Harsha Bangari projects 10% loan book growth for FY27
  • 02/Growth outlook factors in ongoing geopolitical tensions in West Asia region
  • 03/Final lending target and sector allocation details awaited from board

Export-Import Bank of India expects its loan book to expand by 10% in FY2027. Managing Director and CEO Harsha Bangari announced the target on May 18, 2026.

The projection accounts for ongoing geopolitical tensions in West Asia. The region remains a critical corridor for Indian trade and energy imports.

EXIM Bank functions as India's official export credit agency. Its lending portfolio supports domestic exporters and overseas project financing across strategic sectors.

The 10% target signals cautious optimism amid volatile global trade conditions. State-backed lenders have faced rising risk premiums in emerging market financing.

The bank's board is expected to finalize the annual business plan in the coming weeks. Sectoral allocation and risk provisioning will determine actual disbursement.

Indian export credit growth has slowed in recent quarters due to uneven global demand. The FY27 outlook reflects confidence in a measured recovery of cross-border trade.

ONGOING GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS IN WEST ASIA WERE CITED AS A SIGNIFICANT EXTERNAL RISK FACTOR THAT COULD IMPACT THE BANK'S GROWTH TRAJECTORY.
