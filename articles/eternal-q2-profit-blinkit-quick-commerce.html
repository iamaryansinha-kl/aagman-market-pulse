Eternal Q2 Profit Jumps 92% as Blinkit Turns Profitable

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • •- Eternal's Q2 net profit rose 92.3% year-on-year to Rs 125 crore, on revenue that surged 77.6% to Rs 24,138 crore.
  • •- Blinkit's net order value grew 79.3% to Rs 20,940 crore, and its adjusted EBITDA margin improved for a sixth consecutive quarter to 0.9%.
  • •- Food delivery net order value grew 21.5%, with adjusted EBITDA up 25% to Rs 627 crore.
  • •- The company plans to expand Blinkit to 5,000 stores by March 2028 and launch Dark.AI globally by end-2026.
  • •- Eternal will hold about 4.4% of the merged Mygate entity, valued near Rs 156 crore, after swapping its Adonmo stake.
  • •- The stock still closed 2.71% lower at Rs 311.75 ahead of the results announcement.

The numbers say one thing and the stock said another. Eternal — the parent of Zomato and Blinkit — reported a 92.3% year-on-year jump in net profit to Rs 125 crore for the July-September quarter, on revenue from operations that surged 77.6% to Rs 24,138 crore. Sequentially, profit rose 36% from Rs 92 crore and revenue grew 19.4%. EBITDA climbed 26.6% quarter-on-quarter to Rs 752 crore, with margin expanding to 3.15% from 2.95%. Yet shares closed Wednesday down 2.71% at Rs 311.75, sliding as much as 5% intraday, because the market that had bid Eternal up through the results season wanted the print before paying for it.

The context for that caution is a stock that has carried heavy expectations all year. Quick commerce has attracted aggressive capital from competitors determined to buy share, and investors have spent two quarters debating whether the industry's economics favour the incumbent or the aggressor. Against that backdrop, a 92% profit jump on 78% revenue growth is table stakes rather than a beat — Eternal needs Blinkit's margin trajectory, not just its growth, to justify the valuation it entered the week with. Wednesday's results, in that light, are less a verdict on the quarter than ammunition for whichever side of that debate a holder already sits on.

Blinkit is the engine and the margin story. Quick commerce net order value surged 79.3% year-on-year to Rs 20,940 crore, accelerating 22.2% sequentially, as the network added 308 net new stores in the quarter to reach 2,751. The adjusted EBITDA margin improved for the sixth consecutive quarter — a streak that now spans every quarter of Blinkit's path from cash incinerator to contributor — reaching 0.9% of net order value and producing an adjusted EBITDA profit of Rs 184 crore, against a loss of Rs 156 crore a year ago. The 5,000-store target by March 2028, reiterated with the results, implies the buildout roughly doubles from here, and the company clearly believes the margin template proven at current scale travels with the expansion.

The rest of the portfolio is quietly compounding. Food delivery net order value grew 21.5% year-on-year to Rs 11,445 crore, accelerating for a fifth straight quarter, with adjusted EBITDA up 25% to Rs 627 crore at a 5.5% margin — a hair below the prior quarter, but on a much larger base. Going-out, the District business, grew net order value 58% to Rs 3,259 crore with losses narrowing; adjusted EBITDA margin improved to negative 2% from negative 3.1% a year earlier. Hyperpure, the B2B supplies arm, grew 22.7% on a like-for-like basis and flipped to a small adjusted EBITDA profit of Rs 10 crore. Even the experimental bucket — Bistro and Nugget — shrank its adjusted loss to Rs 91 crore from Rs 94 crore. Only the margin optics disappoint at group level: 3.15% EBITDA margin is thin by any standard, and the pre-results selloff suggests investors wanted firmer evidence that Blinkit's margin keeps climbing as stores multiply.

The corporate shuffle around the print is its own signal. Eternal is transferring its Going-out India business to wholly owned Zomato Entertainment through a slump sale, folding in Orbgen and Wasteland Entertainment to consolidate that structure; more strategically, it is swapping its entire Adonmo stake into Vivish Technologies — the Mygate parent — and will emerge with roughly 4.4% of the merged entity, a holding valued near Rs 156 crore against the Rs 112 crore invested in January 2022. A digital-advertising asset folded into a housing-society platform, at a modest mark-up, is less a venture triumph than housekeeping: Eternal is pruning adjacent bets to concentrate capital on the two machines that work. Dark.AI, slated for global launch by end-2026, is the next test of whether the company can make that discipline apply to new bets rather than only to old ones.

It is worth registering how complete the portfolio's turnaround is from the cash-burn era that defined this company's market reputation just three years ago. Every operating segment now either earns an adjusted EBITDA profit or is narrowing its losses on schedule; the only segment still in structural investment mode, the new-commerce experiments lumped under Others, lost Rs 91 crore on Rs 132 crore of adjusted revenue — small enough to be funded entirely by the profitable core. Capital allocation, once the central criticism of the Zomato story, is now its most defensible element.

The quarter's real question is competitive, not financial. Quick commerce is in a margin war with well-funded rivals, and Blinkit's sixth consecutive margin improvement is the strongest evidence yet that scale economics can win it. Food delivery's five straight quarters of order-value acceleration say the core franchise is untouched by the fight. If both hold through the 5,000-store march, the 2.7% Wednesday dip will read as the market's last chance to buy the print cheap; if the margin streak breaks while stores double, the same print will mark the top of the multiple. Eternal's management has made the bet explicit either way.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //
WHAT WERE ETERNAL'S Q2 NUMBERS?
Net profit rose 92.3% YoY to Rs 125 crore on revenue of Rs 24,138 crore, up 77.6%; EBITDA margin expanded to 3.15% from 2.95%.
HOW DID BLINKIT PERFORM?
Net order value grew 79.3% YoY to Rs 20,940 crore; adjusted EBITDA margin improved for the sixth straight quarter to 0.9%, delivering a Rs 184 crore profit versus a loss a year earlier.
WHAT IS ETERNAL'S BLINKIT EXPANSION PLAN?
The company targets 5,000 Blinkit stores by March 2028, from 2,751 at the end of September.
WHAT CORPORATE CHANGES DID ETERNAL ANNOUNCE?
It will consolidate its Going-out business into Zomato Entertainment, swap its Adonmo stake for about 4.4% of merged Mygate, and launch Dark.AI globally by end-2026. GLOSSARY: - Net order value: The total value of orders placed on a platform, before discounts and adjustments. - Slump sale: Transfer of a business undertaking as a whole, for a lump-sum consideration. - Quick commerce: Ultra-fast grocery and essentials delivery, typically in 10-30 minutes. - Like-for-like growth: Growth excluding the effect of new stores or acquisitions.
