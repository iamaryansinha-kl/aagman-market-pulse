Q4 Results May 8 Live: SBI, Titan, BoB, Swiggy, MCX, Kalyan Jewellers Declare Earnings

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Over 75 companies including SBI (NSE: SBIN), Titan (NSE: TITAN), Swiggy (NSE: SWIGGY) and Bank of Baroda declare Q4 FY26 results on May 8, 2026
  • 02/BSE Q4 profit jumps 61% YoY to hit 52-week high; KVB net profit surges 41.3% to ₹725 crore
  • 03/Brokerages await SBI earnings in ₹19,500-₹20,000 crore range; Titan and Swiggy profitability in focus

Over 75 companies are scheduled to announce Q4 FY26 results on May 8, 2026. State Bank of India, Titan Company, Swiggy, Bank of Baroda, Tata Consumer Products and ABB India headline the list.

The market opened weak ahead of the results. At 1:00 pm, Sensex was down 565.44 points (0.73%) at 77,279.08. Nifty 50 fell 162.50 points (0.67%) to 24,164.15.

Brokerages expect SBI's standalone net profit in the ₹19,500 crore to ₹20,000 crore range. Net interest income growth is likely healthy, but treasury losses on government securities may pressure other income.

BSE Ltd stole early attention with a 61% jump in Q4 net profit, sending its shares to a 52-week high. The exchange operator's strong numbers set a bullish tone for financial services.

Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) reported Q4 net profit of ₹725 crore, up 41.3% YoY from ₹513 crore. Net interest income grew 25% YoY to ₹1,359 crore. Despite the beat, shares erased early gains and declined 3% to ₹305.

MRF Ltd posted a 37% increase in consolidated Q4 net profit to ₹702 crore. The stock traded flat at ₹1,30,800 after an early 1.5% rise. Kotak Securities maintains a Sell with a target of ₹1,25,000, citing margin headwinds from rising crude and rubber prices.

Dabur India reported steady demand with rural outperforming urban growth. The company took a 4% price hike across its portfolio to counter ~10% raw material inflation. Quick commerce grew 54% YoY and now contributes 70-75% of Dabur's e-commerce business.

Lupin's Q4 consolidated profit after tax jumped 87.7% to ₹1,468.7 crore. Yet the stock declined nearly 3% to ₹2,394.90, suggesting investor expectations ran even higher.

Biocon's Q4 profit dropped 57% due to exceptional items. However, the biosimilars and generics segments showed strong underlying growth. Shares gained despite the headline profit decline.

ACME Solar reported a standalone Q4 net profit of ₹276.44 crore, reversing a ₹10.6 crore loss in the same quarter last year. FY26 PAT stood at ₹340.15 crore against ₹188.77 crore in FY25. The board appointed Arun Chopra as CFO after Rajat Kumar Singh's resignation.

CarTrade Tech extended its rally for a second session, rising 5% after Q4 earnings showed a 64% YoY profit increase. The auto classifieds platform is gaining investor traction.

Shakti Pumps shares tumbled over 8% after Q4 consolidated net profit crashed 65% to ₹38.3 crore from ₹110 crore YoY. Revenue rose to ₹857 crore, but EBITDA margin collapsed from 24.64% to 9.68%.

Thermax shares rallied 12.5% to ₹4,706.30 after posting Q4 consolidated PAT of ₹244 crore. The engineering major's strong execution drove buying interest.

Vedant Fashions reported Q4 net profit of ₹114.3 crore, up 13% YoY from ₹101.1 crore. FY26 PAT was ₹375.5 crore. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹7.75 per share.

Godrej Consumer Products delivered Q4 consolidated revenue of ₹3,900.4 crore, up 11% YoY. EBITDA grew 10.8% to ₹841.4 crore with stable margins at 21.6%. Adjusted PAT rose 19.5% to ₹516.2 crore.

Solex Energy's Q4 revenue surged 247.1% YoY. The solar EPC player crossed the ₹1,600 crore annual revenue milestone in FY26. Shares rose 2% to ₹1,358.

Sonata Software shares zoomed 7% to ₹290.40 after Q4 standalone net profit jumped to ₹76.23 crore from ₹18 crore in the year-ago quarter. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹4.15 per share.

Analysts will watch management commentary from Titan and Kalyan Jewellers for jewellery demand trends and pricing action. Swiggy's path to profitability remains the key question for the food delivery platform.
