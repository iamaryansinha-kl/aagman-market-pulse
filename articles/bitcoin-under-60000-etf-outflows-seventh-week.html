Bitcoin Under $60,000: ETF Outflows Stretch to Seventh Consecutive Week

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Bitcoin trades at $60,127, struggling below the $60,000 psychological level as spot ETF outflows extend to a seventh consecutive week
  • 02/Ethereum at $1,576, up 1% in 24 hours; global crypto market cap rises 0.6% to $2.08 trillion despite mixed altcoin performance
  • 03/Analysts warn Bitcoin could test $55,500-$56,000 support if it breaks below $58,000 amid rising US interest rate concerns

Bitcoin trades at $60,127, hovering below the key $60,000 mark. Spot Bitcoin ETFs have recorded outflows for seven straight weeks as investors pivot away from crypto.

In the past 24 hours, Bitcoin gained 0.3% while Ethereum rose 1% to $1,576. The global crypto market capitalization edged up 0.6% to $2.08 trillion, according to CoinMarketCap.

Weekly losses remain steep. Bitcoin fell 5.5% and Ethereum dropped 8.6% over the past week. Major altcoins including BNB, XRP, Solana, and Dogecoin corrected up to 10%.

Piyush Walke, Derivatives Research Analyst at Delta Exchange, said the decline in risk assets dampened investor appetite. Spot Bitcoin ETFs clocked a seventh straight week of outflows.

"If Bitcoin falls below $58,000, it could extend its decline toward the $55,500-$56,000 support area. Overall, the broader outlook remains cautious until key resistance levels are reclaimed." - Piyush Walke, Derivatives Research Analyst, Delta Exchange

WazirX market's desk noted crypto markets shifted from early-week resilience to late-week caution. Bitcoin slipped from $64,000 to $58,000 while Ethereum fell from $1,729 to $1,525.

Higher-than-expected US inflation and long liquidations weighed on sentiment. However, key support zones remain in focus for traders watching the next move.
