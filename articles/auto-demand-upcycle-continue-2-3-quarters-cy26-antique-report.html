Auto Demand Upcycle to Continue 2-3 Quarters, Strong Growth Till CY26

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/India's auto demand upcycle expected to continue for the next 2-3 quarters with elevated growth through CY26, per Antique Stock Broking report
  • 02/April 2026 saw broad-based volume growth: passenger vehicles up 20%, two-wheelers up 30%, tractors up 23%, and commercial vehicles up 16% year-on-year
  • 03/Geopolitical tensions remain a key risk for export volumes and profitability in the first half of FY27

India's automobile industry has entered FY27 on a strong footing. An Antique Stock Broking report expects the demand upcycle to continue for the next 2-3 quarters.

The report stated: "The ongoing demand upcycle to sustain over the next 2-3 quarters, with elevated industry growth likely through CY26." Growth is expected to gradually normalise in CY27.

The upturn has been broad-based across all segments. Passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, two-wheelers, tractors, and electric vehicles all showed growth in April 2026.

Key growth drivers include GST rate rationalisation, improving affordability, healthy rural sentiment, and ongoing premiumisation trends across vehicle segments.

In passenger vehicles, domestic wholesale volumes rose around 20% year-on-year in April 2026. Maruti Suzuki led with 32% growth, driven by utility vehicles, compact cars, and mini segments.

Tata Motors posted 31% growth in passenger vehicles. Hyundai Motor India grew 17%, Toyota Kirloskar Motor 21%, while JSW MG Motor India and Kia India saw modest growth of 4% and 3%.

Commercial vehicle volumes, excluding Ashok Leyland, grew about 16% year-on-year. Tata Motors outperformed with 28% growth, while its small commercial vehicle cargo and pickup segments surged around 40%.

VE Commercial Vehicles grew 9%, and Mahindra & Mahindra's light commercial vehicle business rose 7%. Infrastructure-led demand and steady freight activity supported the segment.

Two-wheeler volumes, excluding Bajaj Auto, jumped around 30% year-on-year. Hero MotoCorp led with 85% growth, while Royal Enfield rose 37%.

TVS Motor Company reported 8% growth, supported by a 36% surge in electric vehicle volumes. However, its motorcycle volumes declined 9% due to supply-chain constraints.

Tractor sales remained strong with domestic volumes growing about 23%. Mahindra & Mahindra grew 21%, Escorts 28%, and VST Tillers 17%. Favourable farm sentiment and good reservoir levels helped.

In three-wheelers, Mahindra & Mahindra and TVS reported strong growth of 81% and 61%, respectively. Export volumes across the industry grew 19%, though performance varied across companies.

Electric vehicle adoption gained further momentum. The electric passenger vehicle segment grew 74% year-on-year. Tata Motors held a 37% market share, followed by Mahindra & Mahindra at 23% and Maruti Suzuki at 5%.

The report flagged key risks ahead. Escalating geopolitical tensions could hurt export volumes and profitability in the first half of FY27. Higher freight costs, commodity inflation, and potential supply-chain disruptions remain concerns.

WHAT WERE THE PASSENGER VEHICLE GROWTH NUMBERS IN APRIL 2026?
DOMESTIC WHOLESALE PASSENGER VEHICLE VOLUMES GREW AROUND 20% YEAR-ON-YEAR IN APRIL 2026. MARUTI SUZUKI GREW 32%, TATA MOTORS 31%, HYUNDAI MOTOR INDIA 17%, TOYOTA KIRLOSKAR MOTOR 21%, JSW MG MOTOR INDIA 4%, AND KIA INDIA 3%.
HOW DID THE TWO-WHEELER AND TRACTOR SEGMENTS PERFORM IN APRIL 2026?
TWO-WHEELER VOLUMES, EXCLUDING BAJAJ AUTO, ROSE AROUND 30% YEAR-ON-YEAR, WITH HERO MOTOCORP UP 85% AND ROYAL ENFIELD UP 37%. TRACTOR DOMESTIC VOLUMES GREW ABOUT 23%, WITH MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA UP 21%, ESCORTS UP 28%, AND VST TILLERS UP 17%.
WHAT IS THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE MARKET SHARE BREAKDOWN IN PASSENGER VEHICLES?
THE ELECTRIC PASSENGER VEHICLE SEGMENT GREW 74% YEAR-ON-YEAR. TATA MOTORS HOLDS A 37% MARKET SHARE, MAHINDRA & MAHINDRA HOLDS 23%, AND MARUTI SUZUKI HOLDS 5%.
WHAT ARE THE KEY RISKS TO THE AUTO SECTOR OUTLOOK?
ESCALATING GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS REMAIN A KEY RISK, PARTICULARLY FOR EXPORT VOLUMES AND PROFITABILITY IN THE FIRST HALF OF FY27. HIGHER FREIGHT COSTS, COMMODITY INFLATION, AND POTENTIAL SUPPLY-CHAIN DISRUPTIONS ARE EXPECTED TO REMAIN KEY CONCERNS.
