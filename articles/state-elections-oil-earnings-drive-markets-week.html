State Elections, Oil at $100, Q4 Earnings to Drive Stock Markets This Week

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Counting of votes for five state assemblies begins May 4; West Bengal outcome is the most market-sensitive contest
  • 02/Brent crude sustained near $100/bbl; Barclays raised 2026 forecast to $100 from $85 on Hormuz supply risks
  • 03/Q4 earnings from Ambuja Cements, BHEL, Hero MotoCorp, Mahindra & Mahindra and Bajaj Auto to drive stock-specific moves

Counting of votes for five state assembly elections begins on Monday, May 4. Results for West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Assam, and Puducherry will shape market sentiment this week.

Exit polls suggest a close contest in West Bengal between the BJP and Trinamool Congress. Investors are watching whether the ruling party can make inroads into opposition-ruled Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

"The most immediate catalyst will be the outcome of key state elections," said Hariprasad K, Research Analyst and Founder of Livelong Wealth. Santosh Meena, Head of Research at Swastika Investmart, expects 1-2 days of volatility around the results.

Crude oil remains the single most critical macro variable for India. Brent crude has sustained levels near $100 per barrel amid ongoing tensions around the Strait of Hormuz.

Barclays raised its 2026 Brent forecast to $100 per barrel from $85. Goldman Sachs also forecasts Brent averaging above $100 as the Hormuz closure persists since February 28.

"With Brent prices sustaining elevated levels, inflation risks remain pronounced," said Hariprasad K. High crude prices pressure the rupee and weigh on corporate margins for an import-dependent economy.

Q4 earnings season enters a busy week with results from Ambuja Cements, BHEL, Hero MotoCorp, Mahindra & Mahindra, and Bajaj Auto. Stock-specific movements are likely to continue amid the macro backdrop.

The Sensex closed at 76,913.50 and the Nifty at 23,997.55 on April 30. FIIs sold Rs 70,135 crore in April while DIIs bought Rs 51,064 crore, reflecting a structural flow divide.
