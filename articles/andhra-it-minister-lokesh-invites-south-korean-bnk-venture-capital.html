Andhra IT Minister Lokesh Invites South Korean BNK Venture Capital to State

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • Key points unavailable

Andhra Pradesh is courting South Korean investors.

IT Minister Nara Lokesh has invited BNK Venture Capital, a South Korean investment firm, to explore opportunities in the state. The outreach is part of Andhra Pradesh's broader strategy to attract foreign capital into its technology and startup ecosystem.

BNK Venture Capital focuses on early-stage technology investments. The firm has backed companies in fintech, artificial intelligence, and enterprise software across Asia. Its presence in Andhra Pradesh would bring both capital and expertise to local startups.

Lokesh has been actively promoting Andhra Pradesh as a technology hub. The state government has been developing infrastructure, offering incentives, and creating special economic zones to attract technology companies and investors.

The invitation to BNK Venture Capital comes at a time when Indian states are competing fiercely for foreign investment. Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu have all been aggressive in courting technology investors, and Andhra Pradesh is working to catch up.

The state's Amaravati capital region project has faced challenges, but the government is pushing ahead with technology-focused development in other parts of the state. Visakhapatnam and Tirupati are being positioned as alternative technology hubs.

South Korean investment in India has been growing. Companies like Samsung and Hyundai have significant operations in the country, and venture capital firms are increasingly looking at Indian startups for growth opportunities.
