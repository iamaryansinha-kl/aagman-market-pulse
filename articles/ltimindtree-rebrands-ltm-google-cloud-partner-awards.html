LTIMindtree Rebrands as LTM, Bags Two Google Cloud Partner Awards

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/LTIMindtree announces rebrand to LTM, positioning itself as a "business creativity" partner rather than traditional IT services firm
  • 02/Company bags two Google Cloud Partner Awards, validating its cloud engineering capabilities
  • 03/The dual announcements come ahead of Q4 FY26 results, signalling confidence in strategic pivot

LTIMindtree Ltd unveiled its new brand identity on April 22, 2026. The company will now operate as LTM, dropping the "Indtree" suffix from its name.

The rebrand is part of a strategic repositioning. LTM wants to be seen as a "business creativity" partner that helps clients reimagine operations, not just an IT services vendor.

Alongside the rebrand, LTM announced two Google Cloud Partner Awards. The company won recognition in the categories of Infrastructure and Data Analytics.

Google Cloud awards are given to top-performing partners based on customer success stories, technical certifications, and revenue growth. LTM has been a Google Cloud partner since 2019.

The dual announcements come a day before LTM's Q4 FY26 board meeting. The company is expected to report revenue of approximately ₹9,400 crore for the March quarter.

LTM was formed in 2022 through the merger of Larsen & Toubro Infotech and Mindtree. It is India's fifth-largest IT services firm by revenue.

Shares of LTM were trading at ₹5,847.20, up 1.2% on April 22. The stock has declined 4% year-to-date amid weak sentiment for mid-tier IT stocks.
