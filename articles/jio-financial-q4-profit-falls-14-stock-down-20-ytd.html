Jio Financial Q4 Profit Drops 14% to ₹272 Cr; Stock Down 20% YTD

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Jio Financial Services reports Q4 FY26 net profit of ₹272.22 crore, down 13.88% from ₹316.11 crore in Q4 FY25
  • 02/Total income nearly doubles to ₹1,020 crore but expenses surge to ₹692 crore, squeezing margins
  • 03/Stock trades at ₹237-242, down ~20% year-to-date from January levels near ₹295-300

Jio Financial Services Ltd reported a consolidated net profit of ₹272.22 crore for Q4 FY26. This marks a 13.88% decline from ₹316.11 crore in the year-ago quarter.

Total income for the quarter jumped to ₹1,020 crore from ₹518 crore in Q4 FY25. The revenue growth was driven by strong momentum across lending and payments businesses.

However, operating expenses surged sharply to ₹692 crore from ₹145 crore in the prior year. The spike in costs offset the revenue gains and dragged down profitability.

For the full year FY26, net profit stood at ₹1,561 crore. This compares to ₹1,613 crore in FY25, reflecting marginal annual compression despite top-line expansion.

Jio Credit, the lending subsidiary, saw standalone net profit rise three-fold to ₹70 crore. Its assets under management grew 156% to ₹25,711 crore versus ₹10,053 crore YoY.

Jio Payments Bank reported an 11-fold jump in income to ₹87 crore. CASA customers grew 61% to 3.7 million and deposits rose 84% to ₹544 crore.

The board recommended a dividend of ₹0.60 per share on a face value of ₹10. The record date and payment date will be announced separately.

Jio Financial shares closed near ₹237 on April 20, 2026. The stock has shed approximately 20% since January when it traded near ₹295-300.

The 52-week high stands at ₹338.60 hit in August 2025. The 52-week low is ₹223.30 recorded on March 30, 2026.

Annapoorna Venkataramanan has been appointed Group CFO, effective May 11, 2026. The company described FY26 as a "pivotal inflection point" in its transition to scale.
