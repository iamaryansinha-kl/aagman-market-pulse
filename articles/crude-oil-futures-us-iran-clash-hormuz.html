Crude Oil Futures Surge After US-Iran Naval Clash Near Strait of Hormuz

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/July Brent oil futures rose 1.69% to $101.75 and June WTI crude surged 1.61% to $96.34 following a US-Iran naval exchange near the Strait of Hormuz
  • 02/MCX May crude oil futures traded at ₹9107, up 0.46% from previous close of ₹9065; June MCX contracts were at ₹8777
  • 03/US destroyers USS Truxtun, USS Rafael Peralta, and USS Mason transited the Strait of Hormuz under Iranian attack on May 7; oil market faces elevated geopolitical risk

Crude oil futures traded sharply higher on Friday morning, May 8, 2026. The surge followed a direct military exchange between the US and Iran near the Strait of Hormuz.

At 9:59 AM, July Brent oil futures stood at $101.75. That is a 1.69 per cent jump from the previous session.

June WTI crude oil futures were at $96.34. They gained 1.61 per cent in early trading.

On the Multi Commodity Exchange, May crude oil futures traded at ₹9107. This was up 0.46 per cent from the prior close of ₹9065.

June MCX crude oil futures were at ₹8777. They inched up 0.09 per cent from the previous close of ₹8769.

The spike follows a naval clash on May 7, 2026. US Navy guided-missile destroyers USS Truxtun, USS Rafael Peralta, and USS Mason were transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

Iranian forces launched missiles, drones, and small boats. No US assets were struck, the US Central Command confirmed in a post on X.

US Central Command said it eliminated inbound threats. It targeted Iranian military facilities including missile and drone launch sites.

Iran's military countered that the US attacked two ships entering the Strait. It also accused the US of carrying out strikes on Iranian territory.

Other commodities on MCX saw mixed moves. May nickel futures were at ₹1803, down 0.51 per cent from the previous close of ₹1812.20.

On the National Commodities and Derivatives Exchange, May guarseed contracts traded at ₹5633. That was down 0.34 per cent from the prior close of ₹5652.

May turmeric (farmer polished) futures were at ₹16498 on NCDEX. They fell 0.41 per cent from the previous close of ₹16566.

The Strait of Hormuz handles roughly one-fifth of global oil shipments. Any threat to this chokepoint immediately injects a risk premium into crude prices.
