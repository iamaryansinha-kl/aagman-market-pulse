CSM Technologies Hits 5% Lower Circuit After Flat Listing at ₹113

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/CSM Technologies debuted flat at ₹113 on NSE and BSE, matching its issue price, before locking in 5% lower circuit
  • 02/The ₹145.78 crore IPO was subscribed only 1.36 times, reflecting muted investor demand and premium valuation of 31x earnings
  • 03/Analyst Shivani Nyati recommends 'Neutral' stance, advising aggressive investors to hold with a stop-loss at ₹105

CSM Technologies Ltd made a flat debut on the Indian stock market on July 2, 2026. The stock opened at ₹113 on both NSE and BSE, exactly at par with its issue price.

Within minutes of listing, the shares hit the 5% lower circuit. The stock was locked in the lower band after a lacklustre market reception.

The company commanded a market capitalisation of ₹553.96 crore on the BSE at the time of listing. The ₹145.78 crore IPO was entirely a fresh issue of 1.29 crore equity shares.

The IPO was priced at a band of ₹107-113 per share. It received a muted response, getting subscribed only 1.36 times on the final day of bidding.

CSM Technologies is a GovTech and digital transformation solutions provider. It serves both government and private sector projects across mining, agriculture, education, healthcare, and public services.

Shivani Nyati, Head of Wealth at Swastika Investmart Ltd, maintained a 'Neutral' view on the stock. She noted that while revenue growth has been steady, earnings have remained volatile.

Nyati said the IPO was priced at a premium valuation of around 31 times earnings compared with peers. She advised aggressive investors to consider holding from a long-term perspective.

Nyati recommended a stop-loss at ₹105 for those who choose to hold the stock. The company plans to use ₹53 crore for working capital and ₹25.88 crore for debt repayment.

The remaining funds will be deployed toward inorganic growth initiatives, including potential acquisitions. Keynote Financial Services served as the book-running lead manager to the issue.
