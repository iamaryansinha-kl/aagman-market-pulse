Colgate-Palmolive India Q4 Profit Down to Rs 353 Cr; FY26 Revenue at Rs 6,124 Cr

BY AAGMAN DESK | EST. READ: 3 MIN
EXECUTIVE TLDR //
  • 01/Colgate-Palmolive India Q4 FY26 net profit dips marginally to Rs 353 crore against year-ago period
  • 02/FY26 full-year revenue falls to Rs 6,124 crore despite Q4 sales rising 9% to Rs 1,582.77 crore
  • 03/March quarter revenue up from Rs 1,452.02 crore YoY; margin pressure likely amid competitive oral care market

Colgate-Palmolive (India) Ltd reported a marginal decline in Q4 FY26 net profit. The figure stood at Rs 353 crore for the quarter ended March 2026.

The company recorded Q4 revenue of Rs 1,582.77 crore. This marks a 9% increase from Rs 1,452.02 crore in the same quarter last year.

For the full financial year FY26, total revenue slipped to Rs 6,124 crore. The figure reflects broader volume and pricing challenges in the oral care segment.

Despite top-line growth in Q4, profitability remained under pressure. Margin compression likely offset the benefits from higher sales volumes.

Colgate-Palmolive India is the market leader in toothpaste. It faces rising competition from Patanjali, Dabur, and newer organic brands.

The company derives bulk of its revenue from oral care products. It also operates in personal care and home care categories.

Investors will watch the June quarter for margin recovery signals. Management commentary on pricing power and rural demand will be key.
